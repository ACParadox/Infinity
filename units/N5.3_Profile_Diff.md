### Current-profile examples / structural notes

- **Índigo:** BS13 Initial; Enhanced reaches BS14, ARM4, 2W and X-Visor; 19–25 pts. The 19-pt AP SMG loadout is Irregular in vanilla PanO but Regular in sectorials.
- **Confessor:** Religious; Enhanced reaches ARM4, 2W, PH14; AP CCW across loadouts.
- **Ariadna Intel:** light profile with Mimetism/Trench-Hammer family; heavy Team-Ops counterpart retains Full Power/Battle Ravaged structure.
- **Haqq Husam:** Team-Ops heavy counterpart uses Regeneration/MSV1 and Viral weapon family.
- **Nomad Vortex:** light Mimetism; heavy counterpart adds ECM:Hacker/Deployable Repeater/MSV1.
- **Combined Nexus-7:** K1 weapon access; heavy counterpart reaches BTS9.
- **Morat Treitak:** Religious/Warhorse theme; heavy counterpart uses Immunity(AP)/MSV1/AP Red Fury(+1B).

These details describe the post-update/current profile, not a fabricated old profile that did not exist.

## Evidence boundary / why no fake N5.2→N5.3 numeric diff exists

InfinityDB's own N5.3 reconciliation explicitly notes that the historical Army archive does **not** contain a verified pre-September-1 comparison snapshot for the complete N5.3 Army change. Therefore:

- new-unit/current-profile data can be listed exhaustively;
- official corrections can be listed exhaustively;
- Sāchā's current five-sectorial result can be listed;
- a field-by-field “old numeric profile → new numeric profile” for every Spec-Ops/Team-Ops entry would be meaningless because those entries did not exist before N5.3;
- unrelated current-data fixes are not back-dated without evidence.

That boundary is intentional and prevents post-release current values from being misrepresented as a clean historical diff.
