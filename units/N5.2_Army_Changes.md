# N5.2 Army / unit changes

> **N5.2 Army change — October 15, 2025**
>
> This is the Army update released alongside the v5.2 rules PDF and N5 FAQ v5.0.0.

Official source: [Army Update October 2025 - Part 2](https://infinityuniverse.com/en/news/army-update-october-2025-part2)

Historical Army-data anchor: `646187ca89995e7091138c694498a684cb5c4ca3` (`army: N5.2`, 2025-10-15).

## Global profile change

- **Immunity (Shock) was removed from every Trooper with VITA 2+ or STR.**
