## Units below AVA — do not confuse with a mandatory shopping list

- **Thorakitai 3/6; Myrmidons 4/6; Myrmidon Officer 1/3; Makhai 2/3; Hoplites 1/3; Thyreos 1/2; Ekdromoi 1/4; Agêma 1/2; Yudbots 2/4.** These are numerical gaps only. A Steel Phalanx **Core Fireteam can contain at most 4 members**, so reaching unit AVA is not necessary to build a full Core.
- **Dactyls 2/2, Netrods 2/2**, and named characters recorded at 1/1 already meet their availability limits.
- The [Steel Phalanx tactical guide](../notes/factions/steel-phalanx.md) cites **K2 Auxiliars**, **Netrods** and **Lamedh Rebots** as useful low-cost support choices; a K2 model or practical proxy may be worth prioritizing before buying more elite duplicates.
- **Probot**, **Arjuna**, **Hippolyta** and **Sāchā** remain optional choices; no assertion of a universal must-buy is made.
