## Summary

- **23/23 standard Caledonia roster types accounted for. No standard roster unit was omitted** from the user's list. Optional `Intel Spec-Ops` and `Ariadna Team-Ops` are excluded.
- **19 Caledonia-specific physical miniatures newly reported**, not previously enumerated in the five inventories. **7 previously recorded physical models reused**: 1 Dozer, 1 motorized 112, 1 Wardriver, 1 Beasthunter, 1 Warcor, and 2 shared Traktor Mul/Kuryer chassis. Thus **26 physical models directly represent Caledonia roster selections**, not counting additional cross-sectorial proxy candidates.
- **Two Caledonia S5 dog proxy figures already appear in the TAK inventory** as part of its four-model S5-base dog pool (2 Caledonia + 2 USAriadna Devil Dogs on S5 bases). They are **not newly added** in this file and are **not assumed to be S6 Cameronians**.
- **195 confirmed distinct physical miniatures across all six inventories:** 176 previously recorded + 19 new Caledonia-specific bodies. Existing TAK Dog-Warriors and cross-faction proxies are not double-counted.
- **4 roster types lack dedicated models:** foot 112, William Wallace, Denma Connolly, and Scots Guards. User-suggested buy/proxy paths are recorded below.
- **The Caledonian Highlander Army is flagged “discontinued” in the pinned Army metadata**, though its full N5.3 roster remains in the repository's current reference. Model availability and proxying matter for buying decisions.
- **AVA `Total (T)`** on Volunteers and Galwegians is *not* a numeric maximum. Fireteam row caps are separate and do not dictate model ownership.

## Validation and caveats

- Source roster count **23**; all are present in the table. No omissions or non-roster unit rows.
- **112 (foot)** and **112 (motorized)** are separate entries. A single motorized 112 is shared with TAK; no foot 112 is recorded.
- A shared **Traktor Mul/Kuryer** pair is **two physical chassis total**, not 2 dedicated Traktor plus 2 Kuryer.
- The **2 Caledonia S5 dog figures** were already recorded in TAK. They are retained here as a cross-reference and **not counted twice**.
- **Amadeus** is recorded as an unconfirmed proxy suggestion, not a confirmed separately owned miniature or a standard Caledonia roster entry.
- The sectorial's discontinued flag applies to the current Army metadata; the N5.3 rules reference continues to provide the complete roster.
- The inventory does not assume ownership of other unreported HVTs, extra character proxies, or alternative loadout sculpts.
