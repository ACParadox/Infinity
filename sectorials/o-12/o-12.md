- Rules baseline: N5.3 + N5 FAQ v0.1.

Coverage notes:

- Covers the **49 standard Vanilla O-12 roster entries** whose current Army entry explicitly includes faction `1001`.
- `Ŝtormo Spec-Ops` (ID `1923`) and `O12 Team-Ops` (ID `1937`) are optional-mode hooks and are listed separately.
- Generic Soldier-of-Fortune / mercenary-catalog entries whose source `factions` array is empty are excluded. Entries explicitly assigned to O-12 by Army are included even when their canonical origin is another faction or the mercenary catalogue (for example Parvati, Hippolyta, Ioann Bann, Monstruckers, Warcors, Varangians, Raveneye, Yudbots, Dikpala and Kyra Sharma).
- Linked Reinforcement army ID: **1099**. Reinforcement profiles remain separate from this base roster.
- **Vanilla O-12 currently has a faction Fireteam chart:** no Core, up to one Haris, and up to two Duos. This is unlike Vanilla ALEPH and must not be discarded merely because O-12 is a vanilla faction.
- `FTO` profile markers are preserved exactly where they affect current Fireteam access.
- Army raw `AVA 255` is rendered as **Total**.
- Disabled subordinate profiles such as Zetbots, SideBots, SiriusBots, Devabots and Yudbot-B are documented with their parent entries rather than counted as separate roster selections.
