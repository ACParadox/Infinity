Coverage notes:

- Covers the **49 standard Vanilla O-12 roster entries** whose current Army entry explicitly includes faction `1001`.
- `Ŝtormo Spec-Ops` (ID `1923`) and `O12 Team-Ops` (ID `1937`) are optional-mode hooks and are listed separately.
- Generic Soldier-of-Fortune / mercenary-catalog entries whose source `factions` array is empty are excluded. Entries explicitly assigned to O-12 by Army are included even when their canonical origin is another faction or the mercenary catalogue (for example Parvati, Hippolyta, Ioann Bann, Monstruckers, Warcors, Varangians, Raveneye, Yudbots, Dikpala and Kyra Sharma).
- Linked Reinforcement army ID: **1099**. Reinforcement profiles remain separate from this base roster.
- **Vanilla O-12 currently has a faction Fireteam chart:** no Core, up to one Haris, and up to two Duos. This is unlike Vanilla ALEPH and must not be discarded merely because O-12 is a vanilla faction.
- `FTO` profile markers are preserved exactly where they affect current Fireteam access.
- Army raw `AVA 255` is rendered as **Total**.
- Disabled subordinate profiles such as Zetbots, SideBots, SiriusBots, Devabots and Yudbot-B are documented with their parent entries rather than counted as separate roster selections.

### Play plan: why Vanilla O-12 works

- **Build a delivery plan, not a catalogue of expensive toys.** O-12 has excellent *separate* answers—BS14 Gamma/Omega, the Zeta HMC, Wrecker rockets, MSV, hacking, midfield control and fast specialists—but Gamma/Omega/TAG costs quickly leave too few Orders, screens or mission scorers. Pick one primary attack package, one credible second angle, cheap midfield delay, and the specialist/network support needed for the actual mission. This is the main constraint highlighted in the June 2026 all-faction discussion, which put Vanilla O-12 around **B tier** despite praising its tools.
- **Exploit Vanilla's actual 2 Duo + 1 Haris allowance.** The Haris gives Gamma/Omega/Jackboot/Silverstar and other eligible FT-II profiles mobile support and potentially better attack/ARO output. Duos can save movement Orders pairing complementary models (e.g. eligible RoadBot/Oko movement, Jackboots, or other legal FT-I/II members). A Duo does *not* mean a Core, and formation still requires the canonical Fireteam table above.
- **Make gunfights fair *before* taking them.** Cyberghost **HD+ Pitcher/White Noise**, a repeater web, Varangian/Hippolyta smoke, MSV pieces and camo pressure each solve a different opponent. White Noise especially helps *non-MSV* Gamma/TAG guns bypass an enemy visor; an MSV attacker may lose a useful sighting advantage under the same White Noise. Do not rely on a single high-BS weapon to defeat every Mimetism/MSV/Template defense.
- **Win tempo with cheap pieces.** Moonrakers, Kyttä, inexpensive Kappas/FuzzBots, a selectively placed Peeler, and a mission-running Oko force the opponent to waste Orders before your main striker activates. O-12 has many specialists almost incidentally, but buying every premium utility upgrade wastes points unless the mission makes it useful.
- **Command structure is a strategic choice.** Alpha offers Strategos/Counterintelligence or CoC; Cho and Ment offer other continuity options; Sonya's Lieutenant option provides **+1 SWC**, while NCO alternatives can convert available Lt Orders. A Zeta Lt plan should budget CoC, engineer access and protection against Immobilized/Isolated/possessed situations instead of treating high ARM as complete safety. Verify how Orders reach each NCO under the current rules and profile.

### 2026-10-26: Robert Shepherd Vanilla O-12 faction focus

**Source:** [*Infinity N5 Faction Focus — O-12*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Nl8BMJ8E5U) (full YouTube transcript inspected; timestamps below). #### Faction-building thesis — availability is broad, budget is narrow (00:00–18:30)

- **Capability breadth:** ordinary Smoke+MSV shooting, White Noise, Disco Baller, HD/HD+/KHD, infiltration/Hidden Deployment, Combat Jump, CC, effective Vanilla **Duo/Haris**, and especially **high-mobility Specialists**. The usual bottleneck is not a missing special rule but needing to *pay for multiple components* of a plan. Special-case impersonation and enemy-DZ Parachutist were called out as exceptions, not proof that every other unusual tool is present.
- **A strong but expensive floor:** Moonrakers, Ment, Raveneye and Varangians are excellent *for 12–15 points* but cannot subsidize several elite attackers like 3–7pt orders in some other armies. Buy **one clear primary gun**, an objective path and sufficient protection before upgrading every support unit.
- **Reactive defence is a crumple zone:** two Moonraker markers/mines, potentially reinforced by Varangians and **Peeler Thunderbolt TR + Engineer** or a resilient RoadBot, but no automatic Swiss/Noctifer-style decisive lethal ARO anchor. Your own Turn 1 after going second must use surviving Orders decisively to score, retaliate or repair.
- **Nonlethal is most efficient in enemy turn:** Adhesive / Riotstopper stopping an attacker after they have committed Orders can nullify the plan; **an immobilized enemy is not a dead enemy** and may recover. In active turn, prefer actual killing or a specific objective-opening purpose over endless nonlethal shooting.

### Cross-faction disagreements and rules safeguards

1. **No evidence of a literal whole-unit universal “never take” in all three current rosters.** The clearest strong negative is **Torchlight ordinary Silverstar Rover-heavy construction**, followed by profile-specific **Torchlight Strider Red Fury**, **Starmada Casanova**, and **Starmada combat-role Tian Gǒu**.

The **Hellblazer** or **Silverstar Rover** supplies the required-source entry; Waverider and allowed Wildcards follow the chart. 5. **Only current FTO options actually listed on the chart link:** Torchlight Vidocq FTO 1/2/3, StormBot FTO 2/3, Jamie Rover FTO 1/3; Starmada Nyoka FTO 3/4, Tian Gǒu FTO 1/3/4/6, etc. The full repository chart is the authority, not these illustrative subsets.

7. **New N5.3 unit not graded without fresh recorded evidence:** **Ioann Bann** was introduced in September 2026. These key N5 discussions predate his arrival. Treat him as **ungraded**, not “never take” merely for absence from transcripts.
8. **N4 tournament evidence** (notably the 2024 Torchlight WIP12 winner) supports a *role architecture*—Moonrakers, camouflage, Prime, Hippolyta—but **does not verify N5.3 Fireteams or prove a current win-rate advantage**.

#### 4. Across O-12 — even excellent units may be better off as proxies or shared models

**Ownership check:** no current authoritative O-12-specific inventory was found at the expected repository paths for **Vanilla, Starmada or Torchlight**. Consequently **no owned/available body count is inferred**. The recommendations distinguish **first needed model**, **alternate non-simultaneous weapon sculpt**, **additional actual trooper**, and **companion/peripheral body**. **Practical shopping rule:** (1) Buy/represent the **first** efficient **Ment, Raveneye, Moonraker where legal, Kappa HD, Varangian, Oko/cheap Copperbot, mission specialist** you actually want; (2) choose **one main ranged platform** (e.g. Vanilla Gamma/Epsilon, Starmada Bronze/Epsilon, Torchlight Prime/Wrecker); (3) **proxy** a *new specialized expensive hero, VH/TAG, optional extra gun, SideBot/peripheral package* for multiple games; (4) buy only after your lists repeatedly need it *simultaneously* and a lawful same-size model isn't available. On all proxy choices, confirm **current ITS/event policy, silhouette, base, distinct physical figure per simultaneously present model, team eligibility, exact selected profile and friendly-game opponent agreement**. Proxies change plastic, **not Army legality, equipment, Orders or Fireteam privileges**.

**Buying verdict by army:** **Vanilla:** few whole-model skips; proxy-first **Cuervo, unplanned Wrecker/Zeta/Firebat, special Lynx/Nightshade, duplicate premium guns**, and skip the **Sirius Hacker / Razor Mono / Vidocq Sniper** default profile. **Starmada:** **Casanova** is strongest full-model defer; proxy **Tian Gǒu combat variant, Firebat, Zeta, extra Bronze/Betatrooper or Nyoka**, keeping Raveneye/Bronze/Bluecoat/Šarko objective engine. **Torchlight:** strongest deferrals **excess Silverstar Rover, Sonya, Jamie, Firebat**, plus the **Strider Red Fury/Raveneye CoC** profile and illegal **Prime/Core or Beasthunter FTO** assumptions. None of these conclusions turns a valuable unit's alternate, distinct use into a blanket ban.
