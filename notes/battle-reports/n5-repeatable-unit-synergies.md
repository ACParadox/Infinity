# Infinity N5 — Unit synergies that actually recur in battle reports

**Prepared:** 2026-11-01  
**Primary corpus:** [N5 battle-report master index #001–#241](n5-battle-reports-and-tournament-reports.md), the 25 numbered tactical-analysis files (#001–#240, plus the partial #241), and the related [unit-value / role-effectiveness synthesis](unit-value-role-effectiveness-n5.md).  
**Current costing / legality:** repository's \`sectorials/\` reference files, pinned to **N5.3 Army** as indicated in each source file. **The costs below are *current representative builds*, not claims that a historical player selected those exact N5.3 profile IDs, costs or Fireteam compositions.** The historical footage spans multiple N5 patches.

> **Main conclusion:** The recurring winning architecture is **(1) delivery or a safe attack route + (2) a way to disable/avoid a defender + (3) independent mission scoring + (4) Order/recovery insurance**. A Fireteam is one possible transport for that package, not the definition of a synergy. Nominally strong combinations are frequently defeated by *mission-specific* counterplay.

## How to read the evidence

- **R3 — independently recurring, observed:** two or more distinct game/event sources clearly show the same package *mechanism* working. We count one original game once, even if it is relisted. These are the most portable *role hypotheses*, not proven population-wide win-rate advantages.
- **R2 — repeated but qualified/mixed:** two independent sources identify substantially similar components or mechanisms, but the full named lineup was not always present, one side lost, or the missions/versions differ materially.
- **R1 — a concrete instance:** one primary match, or multiple rounds *within one event*, supplies the evidence. An external retelling of that same event does not promote it to R3.
- **R0 — theoretical/roster-only:** proposed combo without verified gameplay. Not ranked as a repeated success. Some R0 contenders are explicitly listed as **not yet established**.
- A report mentioning two units **on opposing sides** does not establish a synergy. A winner's list containing two units does not by itself establish that those units interacted.
- **Directly observed / participant retrospective / inference** remain separate. A historical mistake, rules dispute, abnormal dice, tutorial, custom mission or incomplete ending always stays attached to its source.
- **Points/SWC** are additive *only across independently selected units*. Included named peripherals (Jazz + Billie, Zoe + Π-Well, Szalamandra + RobbyBot, Rasail + its Chaksa) **are already in the selected profile cost**; do not charge them twice. Separately purchased servants, disposable REMs, link anchors and supporting Regular Orders are **not included unless named in the package**.
- **Fireteam label:** \`YES\` = the exact selected constituents can form the stated Fireteam on N5.3; \`PARTIAL\` = a subset links while other units operate independently; \`NO\` = no shared Fireteam in this package. Historical Fireteam membership may differ from this current illustrative build.
- The source reference's **canonical Fireteam section** takes precedence over a reverse index, player narration, and weapon/profile name. No universal Team-Ops/Spec-Ops or Reinforcement assumptions.
- **No aggregate empirical success rate is calculated.** There is no denominator of total times the combination was deployed, opponent skill/dice controls, or complete Order-by-Order activation ledger.

## Independently recurring or repeatedly observed role architectures

### SY-01 — Moran + Jazz + Zero: turn the midfield into a task-and-hacking network (R3)

**Evidence:**
- [#014 — Corregidor vs Torchlight, ITS18 B-Pong](011-020-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md): Moran obstructed the beacon route; Jazz and other Corregidor mission bodies retained/retook the objective after Moran was killed. **Observed**.
- [#034 — Vanilla Nomads, August Ambush 2025](031-040-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md): Moran/Interventor/Heckler layered denial; Zero retrieved the Capture & Protect beacon and used mobility/support for withdrawal. **Firsthand three-round recap**, not per-activation tracking.
- [#230 — Vanilla Nomads vs Kosmoflot, Uplink Center 3–3](221-230-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md): Jazz/Billie, Moran, Zero and FO Puppets presented competing center-control routes, but Kosmoflot mine/Drop Bear screen held the central bonus to a **tie**. **Observed negative/neutral outcome** for central control.
- [#021 — Corregidor 2026 tournament recap](021-030-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) corroborates narrator's **Moran + Jazz + Guided** *threat* and points out **Jazz single-point-of-failure**; not a fourth independently played scoring sequence with the same trio. #030 is not new evidence for the Varuna matchup it duplicates.

**Mechanism:** Moran's mines/Koalas and Repeater occupy the approach, Jazz threatens Hackable units and projects Pitchers, and the independently mobile **Zero FO** completes an objective; a cheap Puppet/REM may substitute as late courier. **Why it succeeds:** active shooting is optional if opponent must clear multiple denial zones; even losing Moran does not remove Zero's scoring agency.

**Cost:** 17 + 25 + 20 = **62 points / 1 SWC**, no shared Fireteam. Jazz & Billie count as **25 in total**, not 25 plus a servant.

**Counterplay / limits:** Non-Hackable Ariadna, E/M-immune or screened targets, deleting Jazz early, alternate access routes, or physically contesting the objective after Zero reaches it. #230 illustrates that **control can finish tied**, and Interventor/Guided is not free damage.

### SY-02 — Achilles + Patroclus + Pandora: one hard attack with smoke, cyber support and specialist redundancy (R3)

**Independent evidence:** [#001 — Borderbash Next Wave winner](001-010-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) (*retrospective multi-game*); [#027 — Next Wave vs Morats, Double Bind](021-030-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) (*played, conditional Pandora hack enables follow-up*); [#093 — Next Wave vs Tohaa, Hardlock](091-100-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) (*last-turn specialist button wins*). [#197 — Next Wave vs Tunguska, Akial](191-200-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) is essential **counterexample**: Achilles overextended into Interventor/Kulak/Cybermine coverage and the list lost **7–3** despite powerful pieces. Not every report confirms all three exact profiles in the same Fireteam.

**Mechanism:** Achilles removes real AROs/defenders; Patroclus supplies smoke, threat projection and optional specialist/Medic access; Pandora applies hacking or repair and preserves another mission route. The package is worth the cost **when its attack Orders allow someone to press the last button** (#093), rather than all being consumed by early hero combat.

**N5.3 exact Haris example:** Achilles **62/1.5**, **Patroclus FTO 35/0**, Pandora **26/.5** = **123/2**; **Team Achilles Haris**, with **No Wildcards**. The **43-point non-FTO Holoprojector/MediKit Patroclus** is *not* interchangeable within this specific legal Team Achilles construction. Optional independent **Puzzler Adhesive/E/Marat 13/0** raises this package to **136/2**; a Puzzler is not another Fireteam member.

**Counter:** layered Repeater/Oblivion/Cybermine, Panzerfaust/ML AROs, Specialist access denial and denying Achilles an efficient lane. #197 demonstrates that winning with a hard attacker alone is not guaranteed.

### SY-03 — Vanilla Ariadna: Voronin + Strelok/Tankhunter Camo + Dynamo mobility (R2)

**Evidence:** [#161 — CanCon champion interview](161-170-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) and [#237 — the same winner's five-round CanCon retrospective](231-240-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) are **one tournament/event**, not two independent wins; [#223 — separate Wallace/TAK-forward Vanilla Ariadna battle vs USARF](221-230-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) independently tests the same Voronin, twin Strelok, twin Tankhunter, Dynamo backbone in Corporate Appropriation. #223 shows Wallace's anti-materiel completion and a Tankhunter counterattack while the **Dynamo AP Spitfire itself did relatively little** in that game.

**Mechanism:** Voronin's command/initiative economy supports a dispersed game; Strelok Decoys mask Tankhunter attack origin and block access; Tankhunters deny heavy lanes; Dynamo smoke + fast Paramedic turns an attack opening into distant mission delivery. Wallace, Scouts, Dog Warriors and Kövlk are **mission-dependent alternative modules**, not all simultaneously part of this costed shell.

**Current exact shell:** Voronin LT 22/0 + 2×Strelok FO 17/0 + 2×Tankhunter portable Autocannon 36/1.5 + Dynamo AP Spitfire 25/1 + Dynamo Paramedic 16/.5 = **169 points / 4.5 SWC**. **Dynamo Duo** is a legal vanilla Ariadna Fireteam; Streloks and Tankhunters are independent Camo pieces.

**When it succeeds:** Area scoring against opponents who must respect marker ambiguity, Corporate Appropriation in which a Cutter can be bypassed, or distant tasks needing a bike. **Counters:** Sensor, cheap Decoy clearance, deploying to prevent direct anti-materiel access, mines on bike routes, and forcing the expensive double-Tankhunter investment to remain out of position. CanCon reports contain one concession and a creator-admitted consequential rules error; don't treat five wins as five uniform verified demonstrations of *every unit*.

### SY-12 — Hassassin Muyib/Yara/Leila Haris + McMurrough: actual anti-materiel win condition (R1 full lineup)

## Next analyses worth doing from original video-level action data

1. **Co-occurrence with same-side verification:** for every indexed game, record *team A/team B*, exact selected units, loadout, whether linked, and which pairing *interacted*. A mere name appearing twice in a report must not count as a combo.
2. **Order-cost and value conversion:** measure Orders used by smoke/enabler vs attacker vs scorer; targets actually disabled, active OP and opponent Orders consumed. Attribute linked successes to the combination, not each model independently.
3. **Negative exposure:** denominator is the **number of games deploying that package**, not the number of wins in which a commentator praised it. Track losses/failed launches and the tables where it was unnecessary.
4. **Attribution confidence:** record \`verified played\`, \`participant retrospective\`, \`proposed\`, \`counterfactual\`, \`custom\`, \`rules mistake\`, \`incomplete transcript\` and \`shared event\`. Require >=2 distinct observed matches before calling a *specific profile+FT configuration* independently repeatable.
5. **Mission-specific frequency:** tag Mindwipe/D-Charge tasks, Hardlock, Area of Interest, Supplies/Provisioning, Unmasking and Classifieds separately. Rank **the task solution** within each mission category; never average across incompatible OP schemes.
6. **Current legal list audit:** confirm up-to-date Army profile and actual composition independently from historical narratives before putting a purported Fireteam in a shopping/list-building guide.

**Bottom line:** The best-supported synergies repeatedly create **safe scoring agency**. The named full Fireteam success stories are valuable *case studies*, but most still need extra independent games with actual full lists and logged activations before they can be ranked on mathematical points/SWC efficiency.
