## #032 — White Company, Melbourne three-round Arcfinity practice tournament

**Video:** [N5 Tournament Report with WhiteCo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8Jp2CJjIlo4). **Published/indexed by Apr 19, 2025; played date unresolved. N5 initial/N5.1; ITS16-era.** **Local rules:** Reinforced Command (no normal Loss of Lieutenant on Lieutenant incapacitation/Isolation), Stealth Operations (report says largely forgotten), **custom Mega Turret**. **Missions/opponents:** Mega Turret vs **ALEPH Marut-led list**, Annihilation vs **Bakunin (Lizard)**, Highly Classified vs **Kestrel**. Narrator reports **two wins, one loss**.

### Unit packages and interactions

- **Observed / player assessment:** **Varangian Smoke + Shona Carano Duo** delivers a dangerous melee attacker through LoF blockers. Shona's high-quality close-combat weapon was chosen especially to damage the **custom Mega Turret**, a case where attack type/range can matter more than shooting Burst.
- **Observed:** **Nisse MSV2 sniper, Haidao/Hawkwood/Karu gun options and linked support** (list configurations vary between reported games) provide heavy long-range AROs, but **Marut** was able to eliminate the Nisse and then exploit the exposed **Shona/Varangian** support. **Do not infer an inherent WhiteCo weakness to all ALEPH TAGs** from this opener.
- **Observed:** **Marut first-turn attacks** destroyed multiple pieces protecting the button approach. The narrator attempted to damage/repair his own Mega Turret and reach buttons, but afterward says conserving **Command Tokens for Engineer rerolls** could have secured the tie or a more credible win. This is **custom-mission engineering**, not an ITS standard.
- **Observed:** **Bakunin Lizard multi-HMG + Orphan Engineer + Repeater**, with cheap Moderator hacker, Vertigo, Lunokhod/other REMs, is a powerful durable anti-infantry/hacking shell **if its opening deployment is protected**. The narrator argues the Lizard was **too exposed** and WhiteCo's **Liang Kai + Shona** attacked separated parts of the defense. The speaker discusses **MARS Spider** as an alternative support unit—**that is player theory, not the actual played combination**.
- **Observed:** **Kestrel Magistrate Strategos + Jackals**, a **Redeye** and **Firefly Engineer + Crux Knight FTO Duo** supplied order economy, independent flanking and hacking-resistant recovery. The narrator particularly values **Crux's built-in attack die plus an Engineer** over spending even more points to increase already strong shooting. A **Karu Feuerbach** traded against **Griffin**, while Kestrel's scattered specialists and midfield pressures made **Highly Classified** complicated.
- **Player assessment:** a **Karu Engineer** combined with high gun/forward utility is potentially more efficient than an isolated Engineer, but low WIP makes **retained Command Tokens** especially valuable. The report ends with **possible future list changes**, not observations of their success.

### Mission and matchups

- **Mega Turret:** reserve **specialist approach Orders, repairs and Command Token rerolls**, and do not deploy the close-combat team in a Marut-accessible cluster. The custom turret has unusual S7, button and scoring rules: don't extrapolate directly to official ITS scenarios.
- **Annihilation vs Bakunin:** high-value TAGs and support Engineers must be **kept in positions that do not collapse simultaneously** to a single Liang Kai/Shona attack lane.
- **Highly Classified vs Kestrel:** the **Crux/Firefly utility Duo + multiple Jackal/marker threats** is effective because it combines shooting **with specialist access**, rather than a single premium BS piece.
- **Caution:** final placement was not retrieved, and the exact play date (hence initial N5 versus April N5.1) is not verified.

**Reusable finding:** an **elite gun + specialist Engineer + complementary Smoke/CC threat** is more resilient than building only another high-Burst sniper team.

## #034 — August Ambush 2025, Vanilla Nomads three-round tournament

**Video:** [Quiet Contender — August Ambush](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka-PjI5tQSQ). [Forum listing](https://www.bureau-aegis.org/forum/index.php?topic=18005.0). **Played late August 2025; N5.1.1, ITS16-era/Burn City Brawl practice**, not N5.2 or ITS18. Eight players; author **6th**. **B-Pong:** Vanilla Nomads mirror, close **2–3 loss**; **Capture & Protect:** vs **Shasvastii**, reported **9–4 win**; **Firefight:** vs **JSA Oban**, reported **4–5 loss**.

### Packages and specific matchups

- **Player assessment:** **Mobile Brigada + MARS Spider Engineer Duo**, **Interventor Hacker + KHD/Fast Panda**, **Moran Koala/Repeater**, **Heckler E/Marat**, **Lunokhod**, and **Zero** provide a *distributed mission network* while **Kriza Borac/Sin-Eater HMG** supply ranged threat. The player deliberately chose terrain for the beacon routes, not the prettiest central sniper lane.
- **Observed, B-Pong mirror:** **Zero** needed a protected route to pick up and retreat with the beacon. An opponent's **Jazz Pitcher / hacking pressure** forced a player to spend Orders recovering from disabling states, while a **MARS Spider** stole the final beacon position by taking an under-defended lane. **Inference:** in this mission, **late beacon contact beats controlling more consoles early** if the specific packet scores that way.
- **Observed, Capture & Protect vs Shasvastii:** the opponent used a mostly **Camo/Hidden roster** with a heavy **Gwailo HRL / other team support**. A **Zero** carefully crawled/cautiously moved past an ARO to obtain the beacon and withdraw, while **Lunokhod/other mobile pieces** enabled handoff/retreat. This was a win because the Nomads player kept following the **objective delivery plan**, not because all the Shasvastii markers were eliminated.
- **Observed, Firefight vs Oban:** **Yojimbo, Aragoto/bikes, Tanko and Hidden Ninjas** threatened the Nomads **Mobile Brigada Lieutenant** and long guns. **Hellcat Spitfire** dropped to engage vulnerable bikes/defenders, and **Perseus** pressured a close-combat angle. However, **not recording Lieutenant/specialist kills correctly** left the author uncertain about the final objectives and contributed to the close loss.
- **Player assessment:** placing the **Sin-Eater HMG** on a roof can be appropriate *when the exact lane benefits the mission*, even if an earlier blanket deployment rule said to keep it on ground; deploy using **table geometry** rather than habit.

**Reusable finding:** use **Zero + mobility/remote handoff** for Capture & Protect and **Moran/Interventor/Heckler** for route denial; in Firefight maintain an explicit kill-classification scorecard.

**Caution:** forum's third-party automated summary says “late August 2024”, which is impossible for a contemporary N5 report preparing for **BCB 2025**. The author's own audio says **late August before September BCB**, and the contemporaneous forum thread labels **2025**. Do not propagate the erroneous 2024 date.

Three games: **Frostbyte vs TAK (3–9)**, **Highly Classified vs Torchlight Brigade (3–6)**, **Mindwipe** (opposing faction not identified with high confidence).

The alternative **Mobile Brigada ML/Hacker + Evader** helps fight long AROs while retaining actual Classified skills.

Nomads' **Zero** failed repeated console rolls and spent too much of Turn 1 attempting the same button, leaving heater timing and late **exclusion-zone presence** under-resourced. **Inference:** don't count on one WIP attempt; keep an alternate console/zone route.
- **Observed, Highly Classified vs Torchlight:** opposing **Raveneye Holomask disguising Lieutenant identity**, **Wrecker double-rocket/other durable bodies**, and **Jamie Arantes** forced the Nomads player onto damage control. The author states he had too little **Classified-specific planning** and spent too many Orders reacting to the TAG-like attackers rather than doing objectives.
- **Observed, Mindwipe:** the Nomads list was specifically built around **Zero as the Überhacker** and an **airborne Hellcat with D-Charges** to attack the Rogue AI in the opposing DZ. The mission objective was reportedly achieved **in Turn 1**, not Turn 3 as the presenter had planned. The opponent called the game after Turn 2; their roster wasn't sufficiently identified in the audio to assert a favorable sectorial matchup.
- **Player assessment:** he credits a **turn-by-turn pregame mission plan** with preventing decision paralysis, even if unforeseen turns force changes. **Highly Classified** needs a more flexible Classified-matrix plan rather than one single target.
- **Caution:** the event used **ITS16 Part 1**, not the later ITS16 Part 2/Stealth Operations, and the narrator says some historical Hazmat assignment/procedure was forgotten. Do not generalize those incomplete objective checks.

**Reusable finding:** **KHD/repeater/Engineer support is not a substitute for objective planning**; a lone Zero may be excellent at Mindwipe but fail repeatedly in Frostbyte.
