### Unit packages and interactions

- **Observed / player assessment:** **Varangian Smoke + Shona Carano Duo** delivers a dangerous melee attacker through LoF blockers. Shona's high-quality close-combat weapon was chosen especially to damage the **custom Mega Turret**, a case where attack type/range can matter more than shooting Burst.
- **Observed:** **Nisse MSV2 sniper, Haidao/Hawkwood/Karu gun options and linked support** (list configurations vary between reported games) provide heavy long-range AROs, but **Marut** was able to eliminate the Nisse and then exploit the exposed **Shona/Varangian** support. **Do not infer an inherent WhiteCo weakness to all ALEPH TAGs** from this opener.
- **Observed:** **Marut first-turn attacks** destroyed multiple pieces protecting the button approach. The narrator attempted to damage/repair his own Mega Turret and reach buttons, but afterward says conserving **Command Tokens for Engineer rerolls** could have secured the tie or a more credible win. This is **custom-mission engineering**, not an ITS standard.
- **Observed:** **Bakunin Lizard multi-HMG + Orphan Engineer + Repeater**, with cheap Moderator hacker, Vertigo, Lunokhod/other REMs, is a powerful durable anti-infantry/hacking shell **if its opening deployment is protected**. The narrator argues the Lizard was **too exposed** and WhiteCo's **Liang Kai + Shona** attacked separated parts of the defense. The speaker discusses **MARS Spider** as an alternative support unit—**that is player theory, not the actual played combination**.
- **Observed:** **Kestrel Magistrate Strategos + Jackals**, a **Redeye** and **Firefly Engineer + Crux Knight FTO Duo** supplied order economy, independent flanking and hacking-resistant recovery. The narrator particularly values **Crux's built-in attack die plus an Engineer** over spending even more points to increase already strong shooting. A **Karu Feuerbach** traded against **Griffin**, while Kestrel's scattered specialists and midfield pressures made **Highly Classified** complicated.
- **Player assessment:** a **Karu Engineer** combined with high gun/forward utility is potentially more efficient than an isolated Engineer, but low WIP makes **retained Command Tokens** especially valuable. The report ends with **possible future list changes**, not observations of their success.

### Mission and matchups

- **Mega Turret:** reserve **specialist approach Orders, repairs and Command Token rerolls**, and do not deploy the close-combat team in a Marut-accessible cluster. The custom turret has unusual S7, button and scoring rules: don't extrapolate directly to official ITS scenarios.
- **Annihilation vs Bakunin:** high-value TAGs and support Engineers must be **kept in positions that do not collapse simultaneously** to a single Liang Kai/Shona attack lane.
- **Highly Classified vs Kestrel:** the **Crux/Firefly utility Duo + multiple Jackal/marker threats** is effective because it combines shooting **with specialist access**, rather than a single premium BS piece.
- **Caution:** final placement was not retrieved, and the exact play date (hence initial N5 versus April N5.1) is not verified.

**Reusable finding:** an **elite gun + specialist Engineer + complementary Smoke/CC threat** is more resilient than building only another high-Burst sniper team.
