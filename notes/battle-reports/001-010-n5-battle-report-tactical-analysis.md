# Infinity N5 battle reports — tactical findings #001–#010

**Research date:** 2026-10-29. **Linked catalog:** [N5 battle-report index](n5-battle-reports-and-tournament-reports.md), entries **1–10**, in the **same numbering and order**. These are *ten catalog entries*, several of which recap multiple separate matches. The source evidence is YouTube video transcripts accessed via the connected YouTube transcript reader plus the referenced Corvus Belli forum descriptions. **Do not turn these observations into statistical win rates**.

## How to read these notes

- **[Observed]** = the presenter/player or livestream identifies a specific event, play or outcome. Narrated reports may reconstruct an opponent list imperfectly; direct match footage carries greater weight.
- **[Player assessment]** = a first-hand player's opinion about utility, choices or what should have happened. It is not automatically a generally true matchup advantage.
- **[Inference]** = a tactical implication drawn from an observed example, worth testing elsewhere; never a universal sectorial-versus-sectorial ranking.
- **[Caution]** = a rules/mission or evidence limitation, including mistakes acknowledged by the players or commentary. Opponent lists, scores and successes not actually recoverable from a source are **not invented**.
- Exact points, SWC, Fireteam compositions and legality **must be checked against the game-date Army build**. Current N5.3 rules/rosters are useful for *present-day adaptations*, not proof that a 2025 report used those profiles. Refer to [Kestrel](../../sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md), [Qapu Khalqi](../../sectorials/haqqislam/qapu-khalqi.md), [Military Orders](../../sectorials/panoceania/military-orders.md) and [White Company](../../sectorials/na2/white-company.md) for current legal options.

## #001 — Winning Borderbash 2026 — Next Wave multi-game tournament victory

**Primary:** [Infinity the Dante, full 2026 Borderbash report](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ux3_bYsFUQ4) (approximately 101 minutes). **Corroboration:** [Corvus Belli event retrospective](https://infinityuniverse.com/en/news/event-recap-borderbash). **Rules:** N5.2 + May 2026 Army; ITS17. **Opponents discussed:** including a Next Wave/Achilles mirror, updated Operations Subsection, and Corregidor Deepspace; report also describes a Su-Jian-centered opponent. **Packet:** Superiority, B-Pong, Akial Interference, Cutthroat and Radial Unmasking. **Result:** presenter won the event.

### Army lists and Fireteams — transcript reconstruction

**Source:** [Borderbash tournament report](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ux3_bYsFUQ4), opening list walkthrough. The narrator describes **two distinct Next Wave builds** and several *optional on-the-fly formations*; an option or practice-game experiment is not proof that it was deployed in a tournament round.

| **Achilles attack Haris** | Achilles + usually **2 Harbinger FOs** | **Explicit preferred alternative** to leave three FOs with Teucer; narrator also considers Achilles + three FOs, with the other two accompanying Teucer. Do not count these as separate played lists. |
| **Alternate Teucer-led Core / Achilles Duo** | Teucer with the Harbinger group; Achilles + Pandora elsewhere | **Explicit alternative** when the list needs Teucer shooting or Pandora's hacking/Pitcher. The player changes Fireteams in response to terrain and objectives. |

### Units and combinations

- **[Player assessment] Achilles as Key Ops / center of the aggressive Next Wave list.** High personal impact, survivability and close combat make the expensive centerpiece attractive on Superiority and Cutthroat. However, fitting Achilles into Next Wave takes so many points that the rest of the list has **less redundancy** than the player would like. Multiple **Harbinger Forward Observers** supply cheap(ish) Regular Orders and real specialist bodies to restore some coverage; they are not merely cheerleaders. See video **0–13 min**.
- **[Observed] Harbingers + defensive Puzzlers with E/M/adhesive-type denial.** The Puzzler exposed itself at a critical corner and repeatedly frustrated the Su-Jian; eventually the Su-Jian was stopped by adhesive control rather than an expensive direct gunfight. Another Puzzler deployment was intended to fork Achilles between a projectile, a hacking exposure and an ARO at a choke point. **Application:** cover an elite attack route with more than one response type. See roughly **26–40 min**.
- **[Observed] Defensive deployment of Ikadrons/repeaters, Glyph Warcor, Shrouder/Shooter, and specialist Harbingers.** An opponent must pay Orders to penetrate the corridors and find the truly valuable units. The Akial Interference list used **Pandora** as its only ordinary hacker/Spotlight source; the narrator specifically calls her a **single point of failure** alongside a small number of Classified-capable pieces. Pairing a premium assault package with thin Classified coverage is risky. See roughly **12–25 min**.
- **[Observed] OSS counter-matchup:** Asura hacking plus a **Dikpala** shooting duo, Artalis Engineer, and later an OSS package with **Tarkshya / Racerbots / Post-Human support** illustrates a very different way to handle a costly Achilles army: hacking, repeatable durable shooting, and recovery. The Cutthroat round hinged on whether Achilles could cross an exposed firelane to reach the Asura. It did, surviving an enemy critical hit and removing the Asura, but later succumbed to a recovered attack piece. See roughly **54–94 min**.
- **[Player assessment] Key Ops and reserve construction are mission-specific.** The player made one highly aggressive list for the Key Ops-heavy missions and a different list for Akial Interference/Classified work, rather than pretending one Achilles configuration solves all scoring formats.

### Matchup, mission, tactics

- **[Observed] Next Wave vs updated OSS is a real contest of expensive individual capability vs layered hacking/recovery**, not evidence of a generic faction advantage. Losing an Asura or Pandora can abruptly change the threat network; losing a high-impact attacking piece can collapse Next Wave's mission tempo.
- **[Observed] The presenter deliberately changed the final against Corregidor from a cautious mission-centered plan to an Achilles-forward aggression plan** after seeing the matchup. Corvus Belli's recap confirms this change and the final-round win.
- **[Inference]** In order-hungry objective scenarios, count how many Harbingers and genuinely competent specialists remain *after* the centerpieces fight. Avoid spending early turns trying to win every gunfight if the protected scoring floor disappears.
- **[Caution]** Several outcomes depended on failed button rolls, extraordinary critical hits and saves. Do not label Achilles an automatic win against Asura/OSS or Corregidor based on the final placement.

**Reusable lesson:** A premium attack piece should be surrounded by *diverse scoring bodies, network defense and denial*, not merely more high-cost guns.

**Primary:** [Maslow R1 video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gEHha2wc81Y); [tournament thread](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/n5-tournament-itc-2025.43503/). **Played:** Apr 12–13, 2025. **N5.1 / ITS16.** **Result:** Kestrel win, per narrator.

**Other identified Kestrel List A units:** Blink X-Visor Heavy Rocket Launcher, Banshee KHD, Yamabushi, Fugazi, baggage REM, Tech-Bee, Trauma-Doc and recovery support. These are roster pieces, **not automatically Core members**.

**Four-member evidence:** The **Kestrel Core** is confirmed. The White Banner link is not included in a strict four-/five-member count without a validated historical roster or clear team declaration.

### Units and combinations

- **[Player assessment] Black A.I.R. MULTI Sniper in a Fennec team** was the principal long-range, hard-to-ignore ARO; **Fennec FO** gives the same architecture mission-scoring capability. The player stresses that **Albedo or White Noise** are actual gear checks for the MSV-dependent sniper. It is not literally unbeatable.
- **[Player assessment] Griffin Feuerbach + Firefly Engineer/Deactivator** supplies anti-armor/anti-TAG reach, the ability to clear enemy Pitchers and a backup gun. **Blink X-Visor HRL** gives a *different* lane/response when the premium visor ARO is suppressed or denied vision. This is a layered ARO plan, not three identical guns.
- **[Player assessment] Hetkari as the midfield assault/fight-winning piece, Banshee as a flank/rear follow-up**, and a **Trauma-Doc** as recovery insurance. The list intentionally distributes threats so the opponent cannot defeat it by neutralizing the Black A.I.R. alone.

### Matchup, mission, tactics

- **[Observed] White Banner brought Camo/infiltration pieces and mobile specialist attackers**, and the narrator explains that Unmasking let the opponent attack HVTs **without challenging the premium ARO network head-on**. The two players largely raced HVTs/objectives instead of direct FtF engagements.
- **[Observed] This is not proof Kestrel beats White Banner in a firefight:** the player explicitly reports almost no meaningful opposed shooting between their models. The direct lesson is that an excellent gunline may fail to protect HVTs positioned in an accessible part of the board.
- **[Inference]** For Unmasking, deploy mines, close-range templates, repeater/marker detection and physically obstructed HVT approaches; do not value a beautiful Black A.I.R. sniper lane more than the HVT access route. White Banner's Camo trades can be *mission-efficient* even when they die.
- **[Caution]** The presenter reconstructs the **opponent's exact list from memory**. Treat opponent profile details as probable, not an independently verified Army import.

**Reusable lesson:** Excellent shooting can be strategically irrelevant if the opponent can score without exposing a valuable trooper.

## #003 — ITC 2025 Round 2, Frostbyte — Kestrel vs White Banner

**Primary:** [Maslow R2 video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TvQU61YsPWw); [ITC thread](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/n5-tournament-itc-2025.43503/). **Played:** Apr 12–13, 2025. **N5.1 / ITS16.** **Result:** Kestrel win, per narrator.

| White Banner | **Haris — 3**, later possible Duo | **Jing Qo** + **2 Shaolin Monks**; player recalls splitting one monk off | **Haris stated**; the later Jing Qo + Shaolin pair is a recollection, not a separately purchased roster. |

**Other White Banner units:** Long Ya Camo REMs/minelayers, Hundun long-range defender, Mech-Engineer and Yáozăo servant, additional support REMs and a protected Lieutenant. The four-person Core is described as providing **Pitcher/hacker, Jujak Firewall/Heavy Flamethrower and heavy-shooting options**, but the speaker himself is uncertain about **which hacker carried which program**.

**Counting note:** The confirmed four-model White Banner Core is **one game observation**. It is **not** evidence that every listed model contributes to Fireteam Level 4; counts-as and historical profiles require separate checking.

- **[Observed] White Banner Long Ya Camo AROs + Hundun sniper** act as an early defensive layer; **Haetae/Ye Mao/Jujak/Huáng fireteam** combined offensive weapons, TinBot, Disco Baller/vision control, a linkable Pitcher/hacker package and anti-armor utility. **Jing Qo + Shaolin Monks** supplied a dangerous low-cost movement/CC attack package.
- **[Player assessment] Long Ya is often valuable for its unrevealed state and first-use weaponry**, rather than because it can be repaired indefinitely. The narrator questions paying an Engineer/Servant to resurrect a Long Ya after it has already expended its disposable ARO weapons and lost its Camo uncertainty.
- **[Observed] Kestrel used Firefly/Griffin to clear a hostile Pitcher/repeater and important opposing team components**, opening a route for **Hetkari's direct attack**. Once Hetkari forced a defender's facing/dodge and threatened the middle, a **Banshee Parachutist** punished the exposed back angle.
- **[Observed] Jing Qo + Shaolin advanced through a vulnerable corner and killed several Kestrel support/order pieces**, threatening to reverse the game despite Kestrel's initial board advantage. The Griffin stopped Jing Qo after a lucky critical outcome; coverage gaps mattered more than the absolute quality of Black A.I.R.

### Matchup, mission, tactics

- **[Observed] Frostbyte requires correct heater/zone planning, not just tabling the opponent.** Kestrel used **repair/recovery → Sensor REM → console activation** to power a heater, while the White Banner threat of durable/key heavy troops in the central area remained relevant.
- **[Inference]** A premium Kestrel attack package can defeat White Banner's linked Hacking/Pitcher shell *if* the repeater is eliminated first. That is a **sequence-dependent** advantage; charging Hetkari into live TinBot/network coverage is a different matchup.
- **[Inference]** Check side lanes for Jing Qo/Shaolin and other fast close assault pieces even if the main sightline is guarded by the sniper. A single surprise flank may eliminate repair/support and force a change of plan.
- **[Caution]** Individual fireteam names and gear are reconstructed/narrated in imperfect speech-to-text; source yourself against the 2025 video, not N5.3 costs.

**Reusable lesson:** Clear enabling networks first; then commit expensive pieces; keep a cheap recovery route and scoring REM.

## #004 — ITC 2025 Round 3, Firefight — Kestrel vs Torchlight Brigade

**Primary:** [Maslow R3 video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=py5_R6TU-hw); [ITC thread](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/n5-tournament-itc-2025.43503/). **Played:** Apr 12–13, 2025. **N5.1 / ITS16.** **Result:** Kestrel win, per narrator.

### Army lists and Fireteams — transcript reconstruction

**Source:** [ITC round-three walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=py5_R6TU-hw). **Kestrel again reused the exact List A introduced in #002**, including the **Black AIR + Fennec Lt + Fennec Decoy + Fennec FO four-person Core**, Griffin/Firefly Duo and Hetkari/Pathfinder package.

| Side | Fireteam | Members | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Torchlight Brigade** | **Four-member linked attack team / Core** | **Jamie Arantes Lieutenant + 3 Hellblazers** | **Explicitly called a four-man Fireteam**; player describes Jamie moving the three Hellblazers forward. |
| Torchlight | **Duo — 2** | **2 Wreckers** | **Explicit**. |

**Other identifiable Torchlight units:** Moonrakers screening the midfield, Nightshade Minelayer, defensive REMs and Beast Hunter. The narrator emphasizes the opponent **deliberately took no Specialists** in this Firefight-focused list; that is a **reported tournament choice**, not a recommended all-missions build.

**Counting note:** The Jamie + three Hellblazers is a **confirmed physical four-person team**. Its exact formal Fireteam label and period-specific level/bonus should not be inferred from physical count alone.

### Units and combinations

- **[Observed] Torchlight tested an extreme zero-specialist list**: **Jamie + Hellblazers** aggressive link, **Wreckers** as durable heavy attackers, **Nightshade/Moonrakers** for approach/midfield pressure. It is presented by both players as a deliberate stress test for the **Firefight** mission, not a representative all-comers Torchlight list.
- **[Observed] Jamie found an unexpected line through the ARO network**, eliminated/disabled Kestrel pieces and threatened the scoring margin. The Kestrel player initially could not take ordinary fair engagements with the Wreckers efficiently.
- **[Observed] Fennec Disco Baller blocked the Wreckers' key line**, allowing **Hetkari** to reach Jamie. Jamie's adhesive/launcher interaction briefly stopped Hetkari. A **Speedball intervention and Sensor REM recovery** restored momentum; Hetkari subsequently killed Jamie. **Loss of Lieutenant** then reduced Torchlight's remaining action economy.
- **[Observed] Trauma-Doc recovered Black A.I.R.; Griffin/Firefly and Hetkari finished the remaining Wrecker pressure.** The player attributes the victory principally to **Griffin + Black A.I.R. + Hetkari**, with the recovery/vision-control pieces enabling them.

### Matchup, mission, tactics

- **[Player assessment] Zero-specialist Firefight can deny an opponent some specialist-kill scoring**, but becomes brittle if the attack centerpiece dies, and sacrifices flexibility/mission utility. The narrator thinks removing *all* specialists is too extreme even for this mission.
- **[Inference]** In Firefight, a specialized kill-focused squad may make sense; still retain enough Classified/opportunity coverage and independent attackers. Against a Jamie-led alpha team, hold **overlapping side/back angles**, not merely a straight sniper lane.
- **[Caution]** A lucky decisive FtF roll and failed/failed-recovered state checks affected the outcome; don't infer Kestrel inherently counters Torchlight or that the zero-specialist concept has been competitively validated.

**Reusable lesson:** Vision-blocking and repair can transform a trapped expensive gunfighter into a successful counterattack; losing the opposing LT can be more valuable than chasing every Wrecker.

## #005 — ITC 2025 Round 4, Supremacy — Kestrel vs Vanilla Combined Army

**Primary:** [Maslow R4 video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8aj3Ueyvp3s); [ITC thread](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/n5-tournament-itc-2025.43503/). **Played:** Apr 12–13, 2025. **N5.1 / ITS16.** **Result:** Kestrel comeback win, per narrator.

**Other identified Kestrel List B units:** Black AIR FTO, Blink X-Visor HRL, Yamabushi, Scarecrow, Redeye, Fennec FO, Fennec Paramedic, another Fennec Minelayer/Triangulated Fire profile, Trauma-Doc with Palbot, Tech-Bee, Fugazi and other mission support. **No separately confirmed four-/five-member Kestrel Core** in this list.

**Other reconstructed Combined units:** Sheskiin, Charontid (transcribed “currented”), Bit & Kiss, T-Drone Guided, Q-Drone, Daturazi, Ikadron/Imetron and support. The opponent's complete roster is **not verified from an Army export**.

**Important legality/data caveat:** The narrator reconstructed a **307-point / 16-model deployment** after the event and suspected that an **extra R-Drone** had been placed accidentally. This is **the narrator's hypothesis**, not a verified official illegal-list ruling; exclude that REM from confirmed roster totals and do not convert the anecdote into a legal precedent.

### Units and combinations

- **[Player assessment] Second Kestrel list was built for Supremacy/Supplies:** **Hidden Deployment Scarecrow** to start near a scoring zone/objective, **Yamabushi** as an independently deployed CC threat, **Crux FTO + Fennec + Bipandra Haris** for reliable mission access and high-value shooting, and **Redeye** as a fast reserve attacker/late-zone controller. Black A.I.R. + Blink still supplied defensive pressure.
- **[Observed] Combined Army used a classic Bit & Kiss / Anathematics-or-Charontid-level hacking shell, Q-Drone, Pitchers, remote repeaters and powerful active shooting**. The first-turn **Redeye** alpha looked promising on paper but burned most Orders and died trying to defeat the Q-Drone, leaving Combined's order pool mostly intact.
- **[Observed] Kestrel recovered by clearing active repeater/pitcher coverage**, using a **Disco Baller** to bypass a dangerous sightline, pushing **Crux** into exposed supports and **Bipandra/Crux/Fennec** into contesting positions. By the late game, the surviving Kestrel AROs and button control could restrict Combined's final scoring routes.

### Matchup, mission, tactics

- **[Observed] Supremacy rewards *quadrant weight, console access and timing*.** A fast expensive Redeye can contribute scoring points quickly, but an unsuccessful alpha strike is terrible when it leaves too few Orders for actual mission actions.
- **[Player assessment] The narrator believes Combined could have consolidated its advantage earlier by advancing a strong hacker/attacker more decisively**, rather than being distracted by additional Guided attacks. This is an opponent-decision critique, *not* an intrinsic Kestrel superiority.
- **[Important source-quality warning] The narrator suspects the Combined player mistakenly deployed an additional R-Drone that did not belong in the list**, and states its repeater coverage materially affected the Redeye's route. This is an allegation/after-the-fact observation, **not** an independently established rules infraction; do not use the round as clean statistical balancing evidence.
- **[Inference]** Build **independent scoring redundancy**: a failed Redeye attack need not end the game if Hidden Deployment specialists, durable Haris members and small consoles/zone controllers remain.

**Reusable lesson:** Never consume the scoring floor in a high-variance alpha attempt. Repeater destruction can be worth more than another low-percentage Guided attack.
