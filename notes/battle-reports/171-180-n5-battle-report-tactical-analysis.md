## #171 — Battle by the Beach 2025 winner: Torchlight Brigade

[Full MetaChemistry interview](https://open.spotify.com/episode/06tZZ7GsxKM7LbffYXVSIl) · [Headhunter list](https://bit.ly/TLBHeadhunterList) · [Jaeger list](https://bit.ly/TLBJaeger). **Derrek “Misderrektion” won in late February 2025; initial N5 / ITS16.**


### Army lists and Fireteams — Derrek's two Torchlight builds (podcast reconstruction)

**Primary evidence:** [MetaChemistry episode 112](https://open.spotify.com/episode/06tZZ7GsxKM7LbffYXVSIl), approximately **20–23 and 48–50 minutes**; the guest provides [Jaeger](https://bit.ly/TLBJaeger) and [Headhunter](https://bit.ly/TLBHeadhunterList) exports. The short links did not expose a readable roster in this review, so the following is a **partial spoken reconstruction**, not a 300-point export.

- **Shared shell — narrated:** Beast Hunter, Moonraker(s), Fuzzbot, Stormbot, Wave Rider, and flexible repair/mission coverage (Kluger or Lambda discussed). The guest says the lists are roughly 90% shared, rather than publishing a complete roll call of both combat groups.
- **Prime/Hellblazer variant — narrated:** SilverStar Prime and Hellblazer, with a **SilverStar/TinBot** option described during the comparison. **Do not assume all three are simultaneously in a particular deployed Fireteam**; the speaker contrasts parts that are substituted.
- **Hippolyta/Nimrod variant — narrated:** Hippolyta, Nimrod FTO and Kluger, plus another close-attack/utility option whose automated transcript sounds like “Occlusier.” The speaker calls the **Nimrod/Kluger grouping a Haris**; the **third member and exact profiles are not established**. Do not guess the remaining slot or assert five bodies.
- **Other discussed models:** Lambda with Deployable Cover is mentioned as a selection; Nightshade participates in the Last Launch account, but no complete deployment list or a verified team containing Nightshade is read out.
- **Fireteam status:** **Nimrod + Kluger Haris = partially confirmed**, missing third member; **Wave Rider/SilverStar pairing = likely list-building package**, not a verified team count; **no four-/five-member starting Core explicitly enumerated**. The host's generic comments about other O-12/Torchlight teams are not the winner's roster.

**Confidence:** High on named repeated units and two list archetypes; low on full profiles, exact grouping and which short-link name maps to which variant. Do not merge these 2025 lists with Derrek's separate **Krug 2025** championship (#168/#180).

- **Observed retrospective:** The five missions were **Armory, Panic Room, Highly Classified, Evacuation and Last Launch**. Three depend on heavily contested **objective rooms**, making **Torchlight Brigade's tough close-range, mostly S2 fighters** preferable to an alternative Vanilla O-12 or Starmada configuration optimized for longer lines.
- **Observed:** Both lists shared **Beast Hunter, Moonrakers, Fuzzbot, Stormbot, Wave Rider** and other compact mission pieces. One version added a **SilverStar Prime**; the alternative used **Hippolyta, Nimrod and close-attack support**. The guest favored enough *survivors in the room* rather than maximizing one Sniper's damage.
- **Observed — matchup:** In **Panic Room vs Dahshat**, opposing **Maghariba Guard + McMurrough** were difficult to clear; **Hippolyta** threatened the center and let Torchlight contest points despite a much tougher gunfight than the first-round **Armory vs Bakunin**. **Last Launch vs Vanilla Combined** required choosing a Prime list and anticipating **Nourkias/Teucer** attack lanes.
- **Player assessment:** **Nightshade Hidden Deployment Hacker** is versatile, but at this early-N5 event was a list-building option rather than guaranteed in every played match. The **Hellblazer** was described as serviceable but not universally compelling; test the exact profile before purchasing.
- **Inference:** **SilverStar/Prime and Moonraker close defenders + Wave Rider** suit tight objective-room mission packs; **Hippolyta/Nimrod** can mitigate Dahshat's Maghariba/McMurrough melee and zone weight. No verified generic TLB advantage against Dahshat.
- **Caution:** Interview is a tournament winner's recollection, not five independently observed recorded games; exact OP and all opposing lists not always stated.

**Mission lesson:** If three rounds have **central objective rooms**, choose a close-range specialist/HI shell instead of forcing a long-range faction onto cramped terrain.

- **Caution:** Source describes early N5 opponents unfamiliar with Shujae; future opponents who know the mine interactions will deploy differently.

**Mission lesson:** Against aggressive early-N5 sectorials, protect the mission path with **hidden E/M mines and Jammer layers**, then spend active Orders on surviving objectives rather than desperate LT assassination.

A **fast skirmisher list** is an alternative when randomized objective conditions make a single TAG too inflexible.
