## #171 — Battle by the Beach 2025 winner: Torchlight Brigade

[Full MetaChemistry interview](https://open.spotify.com/episode/06tZZ7GsxKM7LbffYXVSIl) · [Headhunter list](https://bit.ly/TLBHeadhunterList) · [Jaeger list](https://bit.ly/TLBJaeger). **Derrek “Misderrektion” won in late February 2025; initial N5 / ITS16.**

- **Observed retrospective:** The five missions were **Armory, Panic Room, Highly Classified, Evacuation and Last Launch**. Three depend on heavily contested **objective rooms**, making **Torchlight Brigade's tough close-range, mostly S2 fighters** preferable to an alternative Vanilla O-12 or Starmada configuration optimized for longer lines.
- **Observed:** Both lists shared **Beast Hunter, Moonrakers, Fuzzbot, Stormbot, Wave Rider** and other compact mission pieces. One version added a **SilverStar Prime**; the alternative used **Hippolyta, Nimrod and close-attack support**. The guest favored enough *survivors in the room* rather than maximizing one Sniper's damage.
- **Observed — matchup:** In **Panic Room vs Dahshat**, opposing **Maghariba Guard + McMurrough** were difficult to clear; **Hippolyta** threatened the center and let Torchlight contest points despite a much tougher gunfight than the first-round **Armory vs Bakunin**. **Last Launch vs Vanilla Combined** required choosing a Prime list and anticipating **Nourkias/Teucer** attack lanes.
- **Player assessment:** **Nightshade Hidden Deployment Hacker** is versatile, but at this early-N5 event was a list-building option rather than guaranteed in every played match. The **Hellblazer** was described as serviceable but not universally compelling; test the exact profile before purchasing.
- **Inference:** **SilverStar/Prime and Moonraker close defenders + Wave Rider** suit tight objective-room mission packs; **Hippolyta/Nimrod** can mitigate Dahshat's Maghariba/McMurrough melee and zone weight. No verified generic TLB advantage against Dahshat.
- **Caution:** Interview is a tournament winner's recollection, not five independently observed recorded games; exact OP and all opposing lists not always stated.

**Mission lesson:** If three rounds have **central objective rooms**, choose a close-range specialist/HI shell instead of forcing a long-range faction onto cramped terrain.

## #172 — Salt Lake Showdown 8 (2025): Vanilla Combined Army

[Full MetaChemistry interview](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6cDFcKyBpVEmbOZz4WEJNd). **Azoka wins with Vanilla Combined**, March 2025, **initial N5 / ITS16**, distinct from his June **2026** Salt Lake win covered in #155.

- **Observed retrospective:** Two lists addressed **Mindwipe, Supplies, Biotechvore, Acquisition and Firefight**. The aggressive list used **Achilles + Pandora Duo, Sheskiin, Mentor LT, Bit & Kiss, Shrouded KHD, Daturazi, Teucer and cheap Ikadrons/Imetrons**; the specialist/repair alternative included **Rindak Paramedic(s), Charontid MSV3 HMG, Med-Tech/Doctor Worm, Shrouded Hacker, Dukash and Noctifer**.
- **Observed — Mindwipe vs Vanilla O-12:** **Achilles and Sheskiin** threatened both sides, while a cheap defensive attack/contesting action secured **9–1 for Combined** (the opponent's point was an activated console). The winner noted repeatedly prioritizing mission damage over optional Classifieds.
- **Observed — Biotechvore vs Invincible Army:** Opponent chose first turn to escape the lethal zone; Combined had a **fast, tough center-occupying option**. The winner stresses that a list which camps in DZ loses the mission regardless of nominal gunpower.
- **Player assessment:** The **Rindak Paramedics and Doctor Worm** were included specifically because **Supplies demanded medical/specialist functionality**; the Achilles/Sheskiin all-in attack list could not reliably cover those jobs. **Charontid MSV3** is an answer to some marker/smoke AROs but costs much of the same budget as another elite.
- **Inference:** **Achilles/Pandora + Sheskiin dual flank alpha** can overwhelm underprepared Vanilla O-12 Mindwipe defenses, while **Rindak + Med-Tech + Charontid** is the more mission-safe option for Supplies/Biotechvore. The exact 9–1 is one game, not a faction win rate.
- **Caution:** These are **early initial-N5 Combined profiles**, not the Oct-2025/2026 Next Wave or N5.3 versions; do not count this as an Avatar or Next Wave tournament.

**Mission lesson:** Have a **dedicated Specialist/Paramedic list** for Supplies alongside an aggressive assassination list, and pre-plan how every model leaves Biotechvore's hazardous zone.

## #173 — Attack on Atlanta 2025 winner: Hassassin Bahram

[Full MetaChemistry interview](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6phvx1TPyyLAJMlvqSR5Yn). **Jake “Lobo” won with Hassassin Bahram** on February 1, 2025, **initial N5 / ITS16**. Mission pack **Unmasking, Supremacy, Decapitation, Capture and Protect, Frostbyte**.

- **Observed retrospective:** Designed **a defensive list able to survive going second** and a similar Decapitation variant, rather than an all-in Fiday alpha. **Two Shujae Hidden Deployment Minelayers with E/M mines, two Ghazi Muttawi'ah Jammers, a Rafiq Deactivator, Nasmat/Kameel resupply and other specialists**, plus **Asawira Spitfire, Killer/regular hackers, Repeater/Pitcher/Guided** covered both defense and offense.
- **Observed:** The winner **lost the Lieutenant roll in all five games** and went second five times, making the **Shujae E/M mine + Mutt Jammer** net central. Opponents unfamiliar with new Shujae Minelayer were surprised by **E/M mines that Isolated/IMM'd units even when their visors saw a marker**.
- **Observed — Unmasking vs Svalarheima Winter Force:** SWF's **Varg Haris/Shona and Beast Hunter** created a strong mobile threat; the winner had to sacrifice a high-value **Asawira to stop a cheap Beast Hunter**. **Guided missile support removed some Varg/Karhu defenders** after the expensive sacrifice, demonstrating how a small hidden attacker can force a big trade.
- **Player assessment:** New N5 **Berserk/CC and Pitcher changes** made multi-layer CC/Guided counterattacks viable; **Rafiq's Deactivator** was a contingency but was **not actually used during the event** because nobody presented a relevant turret threat.
- **Inference:** **Shujae E/M Minelayer x2 + Mutt Jammers + Asawira counterpunch/Guided** can help Hassassin survive **SWF Varg/Shona/Beast Hunter alpha attacks** while still contesting midboard. Do not extrapolate a universal Hassassin > SWF advantage from a single exchange.
- **Caution:** Source describes early N5 opponents unfamiliar with Shujae; future opponents who know the mine interactions will deploy differently.

**Mission lesson:** Against aggressive early-N5 sectorials, protect the mission path with **hidden E/M mines and Jammer layers**, then spend active Orders on surviving objectives rather than desperate LT assassination.

## #174 — Sin City Smackdown 2025: ResOps winner Rob CobraPrime

[Full MetaChemistry winner interview](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4cXBkdCyMkmGwNCnXNQgay). **Rob Paul won an early N5 tournament** in January/February 2025, **initial N5 / ITS16 ResOps**. The transcript discusses a **Maghariba (“Maggie”) army**; **exact faction/sectorial must be confirmed from original list**, not inferred from TAG alone.

- **Observed:** Tournament used **five rounds of Resilience Operations**, with **round conditions kept secret until play** but shared by all tables. Entrants drew special constraints; Rob made **both lists satisfy all six optional restrictions**, rather than optimize to known normal ITS missions.
- **Observed:** One list contained **Maghariba Guard**, the other multiple **midfield Skirmishers**. Both emphasized **close-range fights, zone control and the ability to occupy scenario scoring spaces**. Deployable turrets were used **as speed bumps** rather than expected killing pieces; one such turret helped stop **Yojimbo** from reaching Maggie.
- **Observed — direct counter:** Against **Miranda Ashcroft E/Mitter (+1 Burst) in cover/Mimetism**, Maggie was repeatedly **IMM/Isolated** and failed difficult Reset attempts. A defended **Engineer with Deactivator** removed a nearby mine without entering normal direct Line of Fire, complicating the TAG's counter-play.
- **Player assessment:** ResOps rewards **broad access to zones and small independent scorers** because cards may request disparate effects. Strong defensive pieces only pay off if the opponent actually must pass them; turrets are not a reliable first-turn answer to a premium assault.
- **Inference:** A **Maghariba-heavy list** benefits from **midfield screening and a second, TAG-independent specialist plan**, especially against **Miranda's E/M and strong active Hackers**. A **fast skirmisher list** is an alternative when randomized objective conditions make a single TAG too inflexible.
- **Caution:** Do not label Rob's early-2025 winner as **Bakunin** merely because he later won multiple Bakunin events. **Maggie appears in multiple Haqq-associated rosters**, and the source does not establish exact allegiance with sufficient clarity.

**Mission lesson:** In randomized ResOps, plan **multiple independent ways to reach/deny objectives**, and screen E/M lines before committing a TAG.

## #175 — CanCon 2025: Kestrel Colonial Force winner Hector

[Full Loss of Lieutenant winner interview](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2N7ARPCtt7FA08mKVaF68W) · [published lists/rounds](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1BC472NH5MVpjRdz2X48RyYMfxeGMwEeXu9nOLzchvhU/edit). **Hector won CanCon 2025 5–0 with Kestrel**, January 2025 **initial N5/ITS16**. Distinct from **Rob Shepherd's Vanilla Ariadna win in 2026** (#161).

- **Observed retrospective:** Five missions **Acquisition, Unmasking, Supplies, Firefight, Decapitation**. Hector divided lists by **three central-console objective missions versus two kill/LT missions**. One core was **Griffin MSV1 Feuerbach with Bipandra and Fennec including Triangulated Fire/Minelayer**, while a separate **Hekari + Firefly Minelayer Duo** advanced for active kills and mine placement.
- **Observed:** **Firefly** reloaded/deployed forward **mines/turrets** after the main Hekari gun had reached a useful position. This gave the attacker a safer exit without spending every subsequent Order on Suppressive Fire.
- **Observed:** Additional **Banshee KHD, Scarecrow KHD, Magistrate with Pal(s), Jackal Infiltration/Light Rocket Launcher, Yamabushi and Beast Hunter double heavy-flamethrower** gave **classified breadth, anti-Hacker reach and hard midfield roadblocks**. Hector specifically rated an unhackable Yamabushi/Beast Hunter specialist-free melee threat in his planned defense.
- **Player assessment:** Griffin's **high-quality reactive Feuerbach in a pure defensive Fennec link** was considered an efficient answer to elite shooters, while Firefly is a defensive escort so Hekari can focus on active shooting. The winner says both **practice against the same strong opponent and defending better** mattered more than a particular turn-one kill.
- **Inference:** **Griffin/Bipandra/Fennec Core + Hekari/Firefly Duo** is a tournament-winning initial-N5 Kestrel configuration for **Acquisition/Unmasking/Supplies**, with **Banshee/Scarecrow** broadening Hacker/Classified support. No evidence the exact Core remains legal/optimal under N5.3.
- **Caution:** Automatic audio transcription garbles **Hekari, Firefly, Griffin/Feuerbach**; validate linked list/period Army. This is not a 2026 N5.2 Kestrel report.

**Mission lesson:** Combine **one credible long-range defensive Fireteam** with **one gun + mine-laying Duo**; keep separate Hackers to actually press central buttons.

## #176 — CanCon 2025 multi-faction meta recap

[Full Loss of Lieutenant post-event episode](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2nYBdG1RefnMbJeiIxzOB8). **Initial N5/ITS16, January 2025**. Covers **same CanCon winner Hector** as #175, NOT a second tournament championship.

- **Observed — standings:** Top nine **1 Kestrel, 2–3 Invincible Army, 4 Ramah Task Force, 5 Tohaa, 6 Steel Phalanx, 7 Vanilla O-12, 8 Onyx, 9 TAK**. Multiple appearances in one top nine are **not a valid faction-wide win-rate estimate**.
- **Observed — Kestrel list:** **Griffin Feuerbach, Bipandra + Fennecs, Hekari + Firefly Minelayer, Banshee KHD, Scarecrow, Beast Hunter, Jackal, Yamabushi** appear in the champion's list review. **One Firefly positioning option** laid mines after the advance.
- **Observed — Invincible Army:** Participants discussed **high-burst AP/HRMC-heavy ranged fire, Tai Sheng/Zhuyong + Haidao link** and **Shang Ji** comparisons, with the tradeoff of heavy faction order costs versus a smaller roster of cheap scoring Specialists.
- **Observed — Onyx:** **Nourkias Lieutenant, Unidron Plasma Sniper/K1 with Firewall, Kurgat Engineer and Noctifer Spitfire** was one of the source top-nine lists. **Noctifer** provides hidden attack; **Kurgat** helps protect/recover the machine defense; **Unidrons** deter central advance. Does not automatically mean Onyx was better than TAK/Tohaa.
- **Player assessment:** Early-N5 **Super-Jump, new Special Dice, deployable turrets and defensive link deployment** changed expected attack lanes; players emphasized learning **actual range/modifier lines** rather than ranking by reputation from N4.
- **Inference:** **Kestrel linked anti-armor + Firefly midfield defense**, **IA Tai Sheng/Zhuyong linked gun**, and **Onyx Unidron/Kurgat/Noctifer** are distinct architectures worth testing in **Firefight/Decapitation/console rounds**; this dataset does not contain every head-to-head matchup.
- **Caution:** Transcript contains panel opinions, top-list commentary and occasional out-of-event jokes, not certified game outcomes. This is a **multi-army recap**.

**Mission lesson:** For initial N5, separate **anti-armor gun/ARO packages** from **button-capable specialists**, and use mission mix to choose between them.

## #178 — Amañecer 2025 tournament forum: five-round mission/roster analysis

- **Observed Top 2 list:** A mixed **ORC Feuerbach + Haidao MSV2 sniper + double Karhu Feuerbach/Engineer/Paramedic + Shona Carano + Beast Hunters + Varangian Smoke** heavy-gun/specialist shell, resembling **White Company-style mixed roster**. The thread’s list text is directly recorded; verify exact 2025 faction availability rather than infer it from unit labels alone.
- **Observed Top 3 list:** **Kosmoflot Volkolak ML + Kosmosoldat Portable Autocannon + Strannik Specialist/Doctor + Kibervolk + Traktor Mul Katyusha + 112 Motorized** presents complementary **anti-armor gun and Classified/Medic** options.

**Mission lesson:** For **Countermeasures/Highly Classified**, ensure a full **medical, hacking, engineering, anti-materiel and high-mobility skill matrix**, even when carrying expensive top-tier guns.
