## #201 — Vanilla Nomads vs vanilla Yu Jing: Last Launch, flyer showcase

[Full Fast Panda Gaming report](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tve5DXbtdUs). **Nomads 6–4 Yu Jing**, confirmed after the last active Orders. Both players deliberately brought their newly painted flyers; the flyers were an experiment, not evidence that vehicles are the strongest Last Launch archetype.


### Army lists and Fireteams — original opening roll call

**Source:** [Fast Panda Last Launch report](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tve5DXbtdUs), **roughly 1–9 min**, including both sides' initial roster walk-through. These are **vanilla Nomads and vanilla Yu Jing**, so a Yu Jing-linked group is not evidence that a Yu Jing *sectorial* was being played.

**Nomads — named opening list:**
- **GoPod flyer + GoBots** (vehicle-associated models; not a Fireteam).
- **Gecko TAG**, Mark 12; **Wolfgang Amadeus Wolff**; **Chimera with three Pupnik Peripherals**.
- **Interventor Lieutenant**, **an additional electronic-support unit** (nickname compared with Mary Problems; exact identity unclear), **Clockmaker with Zondbot**, and a midfield **Moran/CrazyKoala** package.
- **Two Morlocks** (one with Climbing Plus), **two Transductor Zonds**, **Thunderbolt Total-Reaction REM**, and **Warcor**.
- **Starting Fireteams:** **none named**. The Chimera's three Pupnik companions are **Peripherals** and do **not** make a four-model Fireteam. A lone Gecko is not a Duo.

**Yu Jing — named opening list:**
- **Zhuyong Invincibles Haris — three bodies, explicitly confirmed:** **Zhuyong MULTI Rifle Paramedic**, **Zhuyong HMG**, and **Zhuyong Boarding Shotgun/E/Mitter**.
- **Lù Duān** with Holoecho/E/M equipment; its three representations are **one unit**, not a linked trio.
- **Rui Shi Spitfire/MSV**, **Shaolin Monk**, **Thunderbolt Total-Reaction REM**, **Warcor**, **E/M-capable Hacker**, plus independently deployed Camo/engineer/support units and the **flyer with E/M Carbine/Pitcher**.
- **Other reported Fireteams:** none. Rui Shi and Lù Duān are separate REMs.

**Large-team audit:** **No four-/five-member Fireteam** is stated. **Yu Jing three-Zhuyong Haris** is a direct declaration; exact identities of unrevealed Yu Jing Camo tokens and a few Nomad support pieces cannot be certified from the opening alone.


- **Observed — Nomads package:** A **Moran with CrazyKoala**, **Chimera/Pupnik**, **Wolfgang Amadeus Wolff**, **Morlock smoke/E/M**, **Interventor** and support Hackers, **Gecko Mark 12**, **GoPod/GoBot**, **Clockmaker**, and cheap **flash-pulse/Thunderbolt AROs** created a layered midfield and objective-room defense. The **Moran successfully extracted through the mission mechanism on turn two**, generating enough early score to force Yu Jing to chase the clock.
- **Observed — Yu Jing package:** **Zúyǒng Invincible Haris** (HMG, Paramedic, close-range shotgun/E-M option), **Rúì Shī Spitfire/MSV**, **Shaolin smoke**, **Lù Duān decoy/E-M**, and Yu Jing vehicle/support tried to clear the central room and escort a final extraction. The Zúyǒng moved decisively in the last turn, killing or incapacitating multiple Nomad blocking pieces.
- **Observed — deciding turn:** Despite successful close-range exchanges, the Yu Jing carrier needed **approximately half an Order more** to complete the required final extraction. A **Wolfgang/Chimera/Pupnik/Koala-style body and template screen** made every crossing expensive. The Nomad player counted **6–4**, including its mission/secondary scoring.
- **Player opinion:** Both emphasized that **Moran extraction in turn two changed the rest of the game**; the Yu Jing player identified the conversion/availability of one extra Order as a realistic counterfactual.
- **Inference:** Against **Zúyǒng HMG/shotgun Haris** mission runners, **Nomad Moran/Koala plus durable short-range bodies and hacking/flash screens** can win *Last Launch* without winning the late gunfight. Conversely, Yu Jing has the tools to open the door, but should **budget the pickup, door crossing and final teleport action before committing Orders to kills**.
- **Limits:** Two vanilla forces, bespoke flyer choices and near-miss timing; no claim that Nomads in general counter Yu Jing or that the flyer profiles are cost-effective.

**Mission takeaway:** Score an early specialist extraction, then **count the exact move–pickup–exit sequence**; denying just one short action can protect a 6–4 lead.

## #202 — Druze Bayram Security vs Shasvastii: ITS17 Evacuation

[Full Fast Panda Gaming report](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cHi-W_7TeYI). **Druze 6–1 Shasvastii** by the closing discussion; Shasvastii's third turn was curtailed because its remaining four Orders could not meaningfully reverse the game.


### Army lists and Fireteams — Druze versus Shasvastii

**Source:** [Fast Panda Evacuation game](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cHi-W_7TeYI), **roughly 2–7 min** initial deployment and unit-by-unit declaration; **~22 min** confirms Scarface/Cordelia as linked.

| Side / formation | Explicit starting members | Count / confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Druze Core** | **Druze Hacker**, **Druze HMG**, **Druze Rifle/basic**, **Druze Paramedic** | **Core of 4 — confirmed verbatim**; four troopers, not five |
| **Druze Brawler Haris** | **Brawler Hacker**, **Brawler Rifle**, **Brawler MULTI Rifle/MSV2** | **Haris of 3 — confirmed** |
| **Druze Scarface/Cordelia** | **Scarface TAG + Cordelia Engineer** | **Duo of 2 — confirmed later in turn one**; Cordelia is an actual teammate, not a synchronized peripheral |
| **Shasvastii Haris** | **Gwailo HMG**, **Jayth Chain Rifle(+B)**, **Jayth SMG Specialist** | **Haris of 3 — confirmed**; the player describes the strong historical team bonus, not a five-man group |

**Other Druze roster pieces:** **Valerya Gromoz Hacker**, **Monstrucker Engineer**, **Thunderbolt(+B) TR REM**, several **Camo/hidden pieces** and **HVTs**; the initial camera pass does not identify every Camo token. **Scarface** is not the fifth Druze Core member.

**Other Shasvastii roster pieces:** **Agent Dukash/AP Marksman-style unit** (name/model caption uncertain), **multiple Taigha**, concealed midfield **Camo tokens**, possible unrevealed **Noctifer**, and **HVTs**. Neither Taigha count nor Camo-marker count establishes a Fireteam. The opening says **two Jayth and one Gwailo**, not three Jayth.

**Large-team audit:** **One four-model Core (Druze)**, two three-body Harises and one Duo. **No confirmed five-member team.** The opponent conceded early; roster completeness is bounded by what was actually shown or said.


- **Observed — Druze:** **Scarface + Cordelia**, **Valerya Gromoz Hacker** (name distorted as “Valyria”), **Monstrucker Engineer**, **Bashi Bazouk**, cheap AROs and a **Chain of Command / Counterintelligence backup** supported **evacuating civilians/HVTs while completing Classifieds**. Valerya completed **repeated Spotlight rolls for a Classified** late; Cordelia's mobility/specialist access enabled a civilian delivery.
- **Observed — Shasvastii:** A **Gwailo heavy gun**, Camo/hidden threats, and other fragile attack pieces failed to stop the evacuation. The opponent had to consider a possible **Noctifer missile-launcher ARO**, which constrained movement even without the feared missile being revealed at that spot; this is a **threat-of-presence** effect, not a verified Noctifer kill.
- **Observed — causation:** The Shas player lost attack pieces and Order economy in repeated adverse firefights. Druze did not merely table an opponent: the winning margin also came from **civilian delivery and late Classified actions**, which still needed a functioning specialist network.
- **Player opinion:** The Shasvastii player was unhappy with **Gwailo value relative to Sheskiin** and said Sheskiin *felt* like a near-auto-pick. This is a **postgame preference**, not comparative tested efficiency: the report does not directly test both profiles under equal conditions. The Druze player preferred a different long-range loadout in hindsight.
- **Inference:** **Druze Scarface/Cordelia + Valerya/Monstrucker + Bashi** offers distinct routes to **Evacuation** points: durability/repair, specialist hacking, and off-angle mobility. **Shasvastii marker deception** can slow a runner, but a failing frontline leaves the adversary enough Orders to complete multiple tasks.
- **Limits:** Early concession, severe dice swings and limited Shas roster experience; do **not** use a single 6–1 as Druze > Shasvastii competitive evidence.

**Mission takeaway:** Reserve **one uncommitted civilian courier and two viable Classified skill sets** after the first attack wave; mission actions are the score, not an afterthought.

## #203 — Varuna vs USARF: ITS17 Area of Interest

[Full Fast Panda Gaming report](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqkn0M7mrEU). **Varuna 6–4 USARF** after a **spoken score correction**: the players initially said 5–4, then recounted Varuna's special-area scoring across earlier rounds.


### Army lists and Fireteams — Varuna versus USARF

**Source:** [Fast Panda Area of Interest video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqkn0M7mrEU), **roughly 2–8 min** initial list and deployment presentation. Both sides explicitly call their Fireteam sizes.

| Army / link | Members as announced | Team size |
|---|---|---|
| **Varuna Fusilier Core** | **Fusilier Hacker**, **Fusilier Paramedic**, **Richard Quinn Marksman Rifle**, **two additional Fusiliers** | **Core — 5, confirmed** (“five-man group”) |
| **USARF Minutemen/Unknown Ranger Haris** | **Minuteman AP HMG**, **Unknown Ranger AP Spitfire**, **Minuteman Boarding Shotgun** | **Haris — 3, confirmed** |
| **USARF Maverick Duo** | **Maverick Forward Observer** and **Maverick Boarding Shotgun** | **Duo — 2, confirmed** |
| **USARF Grunt Core** | **Grunt AP Sniper**, **Grunt FO**, **Rosie Monroe Thunderbolt(+B)**, **Minuteman FO/Light Flamethrower**, **basic Grunt** | **Five-body Core — 5, confirmed**; includes one Minuteman as narrated |

**Other Varuna models:** **Cutter TAG** (reserve/Camo), **Squalo Mk II MULTI Marksman**, **Thunderbolt TR REM**, **Machinist + Palbot**, **Trauma-Doc**, **Zulu-Cobra Camo** and assorted objective/supply pieces. The double TAGs are independent and **not linked to the Fusilier Core**.

**Other USARF models:** **Grunt Heavy Flamethrowers** forward-deployed, Camo/mine markers (**Fox Rangers / Hardcase** inferred from later play), and additional specialists. The full identity of concealed markers is **not** exposed in the first roll call.

**Large-team audit:** **Two separate five-model Cores**, one per side, **not one combined team**. This is unusually direct evidence of **five-member starting formations**; the two TAGs on Varuna's side do not count as extra Core members.


- **Observed — Varuna:** A **Cutter camouflaged TAG** sat on the *special Area of Interest* to generate zone points without offering normal shooting targets, while a **Squalo Mk II**, **Fusilier Core**, **Machinist/Palbot**, and **Zulu-Cobra Camo** covered other lanes/areas. The **Cutter's extreme gun and point weight** made ordinary USARF assault attempts expensive; a **Zulu-Cobra's 23-point footprint** narrowly outvalued a last-turn **USARF 18-point marker** in a separate circle.
- **Observed — USARF:** **Unknown Ranger, Minutemen, Grunts, Maverick bikes/smoke, Fox Rangers and Hardcase** contested midfield and tried to score outside the Cutter's dominant lane. Mavericks attempted smoke/dodge routes to reverse a contested zone, but were removed by the TAG/support ARO. Other USARF bodies still preserved enough zone/Classified score to keep it close.
- **Observed — mission swing:** Varuna's **Cutter-as-hidden-zone-weight** scored over several turns. The final **Zulu-Cobra vs low-cost USARF marker** point comparison determined another Area, despite neither force needing to exterminate the other. The players explicitly corrected the final result to **6–4**.
- **Player opinion:** Varuna player built the list around the fact that **camouflage can secure areas**, intentionally bringing **two TAGs** as a test. USARF player praised **Unknown Ranger and Minuteman extra-die shooting**, which did not overcome the specific deployment geometry.
- **Inference:** **Varuna Cutter + cheap Camo zone holder + Fusilier/Machinist support** is a credible **Area of Interest** scoring architecture against USARF's **Maverick/Unknown Ranger/Fox Ranger** spread. A USARF counterplan needs **anti-TAG AP lanes, smoke that does not expose bikes to unopposed shots, and the exact troop-point total in each zone**.
- **Limits:** One unusual double-TAG, terrain-dependent game. Two TAGs are not automatically efficient in ordinary Varuna missions.

**Mission takeaway:** In Area of Interest, **marker state, scoring-zone placement, point weight and end-of-round scoring** matter at least as much as attrition.

**Mission takeaway:** Build **both an attack-route plan and a defended-room plan** before knowing who goes first; a last-turn Isolated elite cannot substitute for an independent objective specialist.
