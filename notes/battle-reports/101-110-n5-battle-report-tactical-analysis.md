## #101 — OSS vs Corregidor: Battleground

[Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mqVWehM9uBk). Fast Panda Gaming assembled 300-point armies from the Mazebreaker main/Beyond sets plus Ruby Monday, as a pre-order demo. Battleground: three scoring strips and Key Ops. OSS 8–3 Corregidor.

- Observed: OSS Asura provided high surviving army-point weight; Shukra, Naga, Ruby Monday and a cheap scoring REM/Warcor preserved useful zone coverage. Corregidor deployed Coyote, Wildcat/Shifter, Bandit, Moran and Bearcat Combat Jump.
- Observed: Bearcat successfully arrived behind the Asura facing and attempted to exploit Super-Jump; too few remaining Orders meant it could not dislodge the zone anchor. The Corregidor side misjudged elevated LoF for Coyote. Both players forgot to nominate Key Ops before starting.
- Inference: Asura plus independent Naga/Shukra/REM zone holders is effective against a Corregidor attacker who expends Orders on Coyote/Bearcat without changing strip totals. Corregidor should screen Asura's back and retain a second zone scorer.
- Caveat: These product-box rosters are not fully optimized tournament lists; the exact Key Ops scoring was affected by pregame error.

Tactic: Plan points in each strip before attacking, nominate Key Ops, and reserve the final Orders for sector occupation rather than merely eliminating a key gun.

## #102 — Starmada vs Neoterra: ITS17 Hardlock

[Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DJmXoX260Vw). Narrator states the game occurs after Autumn Assault 2025 and a holiday break. N5.2-era ITS17. Starmada 7–1 Neoterran Capitaline Army.

- Observed: Starmada used Cyberghost hacking, Varangian Smoke/short attacks, Shona and a fast Roadbot-style Specialist route. Cyberghost repeatedly Possessed the opponent's valuable TAG, depriving it of efficient normal play and turning it into an awkward low-WIP button option.
- Observed: Neoterra's Bolt Fireteam was resilient and remained threatening, but few actual console activations were completed. Starmada moved its mobile robot between Hardlock buttons and secured HVT objectives, scoring seven versus Neoterra's one.
- Inference: Cyberghost + projected Hacking Area + a protected independent Roadbot can be potent against Neoterra TAG/Bolt lists in Hardlock. A TAG-only response must protect against Possession and carry an unaffected Specialist.
- Caveat: Participants checked Warhorse, hacking, Firewall and TAG interactions live; use N5.2-period rule text for reproducing them.

Tactic: Separate the button runner from the premium TAG and keep a route for Engineer/Reset support.

## #103 — TAK vs Invincible Army: ITS16.5 B-Pong (2025 original, Redux video)

[Redux video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otGq22MnymQ), [contemporaneous April 2025 original listing](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/guerrilla-miniature-games-infinity-n5-battle-reports.41284/page-3). Original was preparation for the May 3, 2025 Spring Offensive. Result: Invincible Army 4–2 TAK. Patch was early N5.1–N5.1.1, not the reupload date.

- Observed: Invincible Army combined Shang Ji/Tai Sheng heavy linked presence with a Liu Xing/airborne shotgun attack. The airborne trooper pressured TAK's left flank, removing its remaining console-capable bodies. Shang Ji survived hard firefights and held useful B-Pong midboard space.
- Observed: TAK Dog-Warrior/Dogface and grenade/Chain Rifle attacks resisted repeated gun pressure, with transformation and Camo complicating the center fight; they did not compensate for loss of scoring routes.
- Inference: HI zone-weight plus a separate airborne attack on enemy button workers is a reasonable IA answer to TAK Dog-Warrior and reactive Camo in B-Pong. TAK needs an independent button Specialist and deployment-zone screening.
- Caveat: This edition's B-Pong entry rule distinguishes Airborne Deployment and Parachutist; players discussed it live, so verify the original packet.

Tactic: Target specialists who can move the B-Pong rather than spending all Orders on a very resilient Dog-Warrior.

## #105 — Corregidor vs JSA Oban: ITS16.5 Supremacy

[Original GMG video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tatfQSad8Bs). Early 2025 ITS16.5 Supremacy. Oban 7–2 Corregidor.

- Observed: Oban fielded two O-Yoroi TAGs, Tanko + Miyamoto Musashi melee and Disco/Deactivator support, plus Ryuken-9. Corregidor fielded Iguana, Jazz Hacker and Intruder/support guns. Jazz's side temporarily took control of an O-Yoroi, demonstrating an actual anti-TAG cyber counter.
- Observed: Tanko Disco Baller protected Musashi as he approached a premium enemy target; Musashi's explosive CC took down an expensive TAG. Corregidor lost Iguana while Oban retained enough TAG point weight for quadrants. The result was explicitly 7–2.
- Player assessment: The presenter praised Tanko's combination of Disco/Deactivator and efficient bodyguarding. Mine/Repeater deactivation and TAG Possession were discussed as tricky historical rules, not settled current precedent.
- Inference: Double O-Yoroi + Tanko/Musashi is strong conditional Supremacy zone control against a single-Iguana Corregidor roster. Corregidor can answer with Jazz Hacking only if it converts control of a TAG into durable scoring advantage.

Tactic: Spread the two expensive TAGs into separate scoring quadrants and keep Musashi/Disco mobile.

## #106 — Vanilla Ariadna vs Bakunin: Frontline and Margot/Duroc

[Robert Shepherd full report](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FLBdXj49w6A). Frontline; exact date/patch unverified. Author estimated outcome about 6–4 for Bakunin.

- Observed: Ariadna used Margot + Duroc as paired DZ parachutists and an infiltrating Camo Brigadier Bruant-style AP Spitfire Specialist, with other Camo/FO support. Duroc is intended to use Smoke and durability to create an opening Margot can exploit.
- Player assessment: The author says he held Margot/Duroc back too passively. This locked roughly 58 points and 2 SWC out of relevant active play, letting Bakunin preserve zone scoring. He considers the pair high-ceiling but practice-intensive, not weak.
- Inference: In Frontline, spending early Orders to force a rear breach with Duroc, then attack with Margot, may be better than waiting until the opponent already dominates the final strip. Bakunin's preserved independent bodies won this game's zone race.
- Caveat: One report is not evidence of general poor Ariadna balance or a guaranteed anti-Bakunin flank.

Tactic: Commit airborne pressure on a specific turn while retaining a distinct forward-deployed Frontline scoring model.

## #107 — Vanilla O-12 vs Vanilla PanO: B-Pong

[Robert Shepherd's video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dzNcYN_a-4) and [creator July 2025 index](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/robert-shepherd-index-batreps.43336/). PanO list was inspired by a winning Border Bash roster. Film result: O-12 6–3 PanO.

- Observed: O-12 used Razor Monofilament Minelayer + Razor Forward Observer, Moonraker mines, Roadbot Paramedic, Gamma and supporting hacker. FO Razor trades one Minelayer for D-Charges, Specialist access and Boarding Shotgun (+1B) with strong Camo/Mimetism.
- Observed: PanO had Swiss Guard, Banshee, Karhu with Blitzen/Feuerbach, and Fennec Hacker backed by Fugazi Repeaters. A cheap Hacker behind two Repeaters was enough to constrain O-12 Roadbot movement.
- Observed: Razors performed well with short-range gun attacks into Swiss Guard/Banshee; Monofilament mines triggered, but PanO passed the saves. The PanO Karhu came within roughly one inch/Order of moving B-Pong back and turning the score into a PanO win; an O-12 defense stopped it.
- Inference: Razor FO + midfield Camo and protected Gamma ball lane has useful match-specific tools against elite PanO guns, but the endgame was close. Do not promote Monofilament Mine kill reliability from this game.

Tactic: Keep one Razor FO to score and a final reactive shooter on the Karhu route that could return the ball.

## #108 — Vanilla Yu Jing vs Tunguska: Unmasking

[Robert Shepherd video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jOXa2tTEd50), [creator index confirms no seasonal extras](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/robert-shepherd-index-batreps.43336/). Initial N5 likely; Yu Jing won decisively, exact score unverified.

- Observed: Yu Jing used Su-Jian + Mowang NCO Duo, Yan Huo HRMC and Dokkaebi KHD/Pitcher. The Duo was a movement/Order synergy rather than a traditional Fireteam bonus platform.
- Player assessment: Mowang + Haidao Specialist/KHD would likely have fit Unmasking better; Haidao can push buttons as the Mowang spends NCO Orders. Dokkaebi is slightly more versatile alone while Haidao can be attractive in the appropriate link.
- Observed: Tunguska had Salamandra, Hollow Men/Perseus and Clockmaker. Clockmaker overextended and was removed, preventing efficient Hollow Men recovery. Yu Jing hit Unmasking antennas, opened lines with Yan Huo, and Su-Jian made an extremely long late run to an HVT.
- Player opinion: Despite winning, the author hypothesized Tunguska hacking could favor it in repeated games (an illustrative “60–40”), specifically against an Engineer-light HI list. This is not measured evidence.
- Inference: Su-Jian/Mowang can be powerful but Hackable; protect an Engineer and combine Mowang with an actual antenna Specialist when Unmasking objectives matter.

Tactic: Do not lose Clockmaker merely to get a better attack angle; prioritize antenna worker survival over big-HI gun duels.

## #109 — Corregidor Gecko Team vs JSA Oban: Evacuation, creator write-up only

Initial N5 and ITS16.2 Evacuation. Winner and detailed actions unverified.

- Creator-confirmed observation: This is a Gecko-based Corregidor vs Oban game in Evacuation. The mission is “deceptively Order intensive” despite lacking unusual timing for when points are awarded.
- Creator assessment: All three turns need a balance of attacks, advancement and preparations toward extracting CivVac/HVT targets. Players who spend the first two turns solely fighting can lack Orders to complete scoring.
- Inference only: Corregidor Geckos must leave Orders and safe paths for eligible CivVac units; Oban melee mobility may threaten routes. No actual Gecko-vs-Oban exchange, roster, victory or repair is verified from the accessible creator text.
- Caveat: Do not present invented tactical plays or compare this as a counted faction win.

Tactic: Map the extraction path and allocate movement Orders in turn 1 before the firefight begins.

## #110 — Imperial Service vs Bakunin: The Armory

[Robert Shepherd narrated report](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iut3OkyPBwM), [May 2025 forum listing](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/robert-shepherd-index-batreps.43336/). ITS16.2 The Armory; N5.1–N5.1.1 probable. ISS won decisively with nine reported points; opposing total not reliably captured. This was an expressly experimental, not optimized, ISS list game.

- Observed: ISS tested Adil Crane Rank, Celestial Guard Smoke, Pheasant CoC/E-Mitter, Kanren mines/MadTraps/Repeaters, Koga Hacker, Rui Shi, Panguling EVO/Baggage and Warcor. It combined smoke shooting and cheap room-scoring bodies, rather than only expensive armored attackers.
- Observed: Bakunin Penitent AP Continuous Spitfire + Orphan Engineer, Cenobite, Moira and support tried to contest The Armory. The Cenobite reached the room and removed the cheap Warcor, but Bakunin spent too many Orders cautiously resolving Camo and deployable threats.
- Observed: In a decisive late sequence, Adil advanced under Celestial Guard Smoke, took several CC attempts to defeat a Moira, entered the room with sufficient point weight, and scored while the Pheasant traded with an Orphan, denying late HVT points.
- Player assessment: The ISS pilot recognized suboptimal early Warcor/Kanren deployment and wished for a more forward mine/repeater screen. The Bakunin player needed an earlier sacrificial reveal/attack to prevent ISS accumulating Armory points.
- Inference: Celestial Guard Smoke + Adil + cheap Kanren/Warcor room presence plus Pheasant CoC is a good conditional Armory plan into a cautious Bakunin Penitent/Cenobite approach. Not a universal faction matchup.

Tactic: Score with cheap early occupants, then deliver an expensive CC occupant under Smoke without trying to eliminate every opponent.
