## #101 — OSS vs Corregidor: Battleground

[Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mqVWehM9uBk). Fast Panda Gaming assembled 300-point armies from the Mazebreaker main/Beyond sets plus Ruby Monday, as a pre-order demo. Battleground: three scoring strips and Key Ops. OSS 8–3 Corregidor.

**Large-team census:** **One confirmed Corregidor five-body Fireteam**. **OSS has an explicitly linked Dikpala/Sharvara unit with uncertain body count**. The later Corregidor three-member reformation is a **casualty-stage event**, not a second initial roster.



- Observed: OSS Asura provided high surviving army-point weight; Shukra, Naga, Ruby Monday and a cheap scoring REM/Warcor preserved useful zone coverage. Corregidor deployed Coyote, Wildcat/Shifter, Bandit, Moran and Bearcat Combat Jump.
- Observed: Bearcat successfully arrived behind the Asura facing and attempted to exploit Super-Jump; too few remaining Orders meant it could not dislodge the zone anchor. The Corregidor side misjudged elevated LoF for Coyote. Both players forgot to nominate Key Ops before starting.
- Inference: Asura plus independent Naga/Shukra/REM zone holders is effective against a Corregidor attacker who expends Orders on Coyote/Bearcat without changing strip totals. Corregidor should screen Asura's back and retain a second zone scorer.
- Caveat: These product-box rosters are not fully optimized tournament lists; the exact Key Ops scoring was affected by pregame error.

Tactic: Plan points in each strip before attacking, nominate Key Ops, and reserve the final Orders for sector occupation rather than merely eliminating a key gun.
