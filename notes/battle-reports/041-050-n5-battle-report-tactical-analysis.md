## #041 — Critical Intervention: O-12 / Starmada-style team against PanO heavy troops

**Primary source:** [Warp Charged live Infinity N5 tournament — Critical Intervention](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Td9Mx2oYJA4). **Factions:** an O-12 team with **Varangian Smoke, Epsilon MSV2, Betatroopers and Shona** versus a PanO force with **Tikbalang, Trinitarian, Bulleteer and Knights**. **Exact opposing sectorial, Army codes and N5 patch not independently verified**; label the PanO player **PanO (sectorial unconfirmed)** rather than asserting Military Orders or WinterFor. End-of-game players calculate **7–3**, but the recording's faction-to-score attribution has not been independently confirmed.

### Tactical evidence
- **Observed:** **Varangian Impetuous Smoke → linked Epsilon MSV2** opened firing against a **Tikbalang** that was otherwise protected by a very dangerous lane. One Varangian **chose not** to use an Impetuous move that would have walked directly into the TAG. This is the right type of *Smoke/visor + selectivity*, not “always advance your smoke warbands.”
- **Observed:** The Epsilon switched into the shooting link as leader while the other team members repositioned behind Smoke. The game includes explicit range/visibility calculations: MSV changes the attacker's odds, but enemy **Mimetism, cover and reactive fire** still make a TAG engagement costly.
- **Observed:** The PanO response used **Hidden/forward Trinitarian Boarding Shotgun**, **Bulleteer mobility** and remaining Knight bodies to regain room/objective space after losing frontal ARO access. The Bulleteer made a late run precisely because its movement could reach the room with very few Orders.
- **Observed:** **Shona + a linked robotic/heavy support piece** offered an alternate CC approach when the Epsilon sniper plan became awkward. The players discussed correct Fireteam composition and whether a troop could benefit from a three-member bonus; this is **not proof the debated composition was legal**.
- **Inference:** **O-12 vision-control/CC** can threaten a valuable PanO TAG if Smoke is placed *before* exposing the Epsilon and a distinct specialist/room occupant is preserved. Conversely **Trinitarian markers + Bulleteer speed** can contest the real **Critical Intervention objective** without winning an open MSV duel.
- **Caution:** Speaker discussions about **pure Fireteam composition, Climbing Plus and CC against peripherals** are in-game rulings to recheck, not authoritative N5.3 text. The first-person roster is incomplete.

**Use in list-building:** Smoke source + MSV gun + *independent* fast mission piece; retain a last-turn room attacker rather than spending all Orders on the Tikbalang.

## #042 — Next Wave vs Bakunin: Area of Interest

**Primary:** [Warp Charged N5 Tournament — Area of Interest](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rQbLzeGbGfw). Participants explicitly call the mission **Area of Interest**. The attacker identifies **Next Wave**; the opposing tools include **Bakunin Riot Grrl, Chimera/Eclipse, Camo/Paramines and Hacker**. **N5.2–N5.3, ITS17/18 era, exact date unknown.** Closing score reported **6–5**; winner-to-faction mapping is **not independently confirmed**.

- **Observed:** **Next Wave Skyhound MULTI Rifle/X-Visor** initially attempted an **11-inch movement/attack path** but deliberately checked Camo-marker and **Paramine** danger. At a poor range it could not simply outshoot a long-range reactive launcher despite its mobility. **Mobility without a safe approach angle is not a guaranteed alpha**.
- **Observed:** **Achilles + Pandora + Steeljaw/Raindancer support** formed separate active-fight and scoring routes. Achilles needed to traverse a missile ARO to attack Riot Grrl positions, and the player repeatedly reviewed whether **Eclipse** could let an objective carrier pass.
- **Observed:** Bakunin's **Chimera Eclipse + Riot Grrl/Paramine** created a conflicting defense: direct-fire templates/sightlines on one side, non-vision-blockable mine/marker uncertainty on another. A turn spent fighting the Riot Grrl was not necessarily a turn advancing a unit into the Area.
- **Observed:** The last turn hinged on moving a remaining marker/body into a scoring region rather than tabling the opponent; the final **6–5** was decided by *presence/timing*, not by the number of elite bodies killed.
- **Inference:** **Bakunin can obstruct a costly Next Wave elite push** through *Eclipse, mines and E/M or hacker threat*, but if too few late control bodies survive, **Achilles/Pandora pressure** can still create an opening. A strong unit match-up does **not** imply one sectorial inherently beats another.
- **Caution:** “Impersonation token” in speech at the end might refer to a marker/courtesy representation; the underlying unit and legal scoring eligibility were not positively identified. Do not make a unit-specific claim from that phrase alone.

**Use in list-building:** test **Skyhound vs Paramines**, **Achilles vs Riot Grrl launcher**, and whether a *third* specialist remains after Pandora/Steeljaw trades.

## #043 — ITS18: TAK mobility, Vystrel firelanes and a last-turn objective

**Primary:** [Warp Charged N5 S18 livestream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6xBwb53he5Q). **N5.3/ITS18 implied by title**. **Confirmed roster-side:** **TAK/Ariadna** using **Voronin, Line Kazaks, Veteran Kazaks/Frontoviks, Vystrel, Dynamo Duo, Carmen and Camo Scouts**. **Opponent's complete faction/sectorial not securely identifiable** in the auto-translated transcript: do not substitute an inferred “Greeks” matchup merely because the English transcript uses that word. Mission involves **beacon/objective capture, activated consoles and HVT/zone control**, but exact ITS18 scenario name is unresolved.

- **Observed:** **Dynamo Duo + Carmen Smoke** threatened to move around an exposed defensive frontage. TAK's **Voronin / Line Kazak / Frontovik linked shell** retained Orders and reliable medium-range shooting while mobile pieces used a separate pool.
- **Observed:** **Vystrel** was used for **Discover plus direct-template/launcher responses** against a marker and warband/close threat. The transcript describes a player deliberately **breaking the link** for a particular shot and considering the Command Token price of reforming it; this is a concrete **Fireteam flexibility/order-tax** example.
- **Observed:** **Scout Camouflage + late Veteran Kazak** produced a last-turn **capture/control and console** sequence. A final objective was taken or secured with a small remaining Order pool; this is evidence of *Camo specialists saving a scoring route* after the main firefight.
- **Player discussion:** the players explicitly debate whether a **single Vystrel and its AP/BS-penalty weapon** is more useful against certain opponents than a higher-cost heavy-gun fireteam. That is opinion in the stream, not a tested universal ranking.
- **Inference:** **TAK mobility + reliable veteran backline** supports late objective games, but the Dynamo/Carmen team is vulnerable if the table leaves a missile/launcher lane unblocked. Scorekeepers should log **what the Camo Scout can still do after the attack team dies**, not just the number of fighters.
- **Caution:** automated translation is poor, and the video begins without a clear mission slate. Do not record a winner, exact opponent or named ITS18 mission as verified from this source.

**Use in list-building:** **Voronin order economy + Vystrel lane + Scout endgame** can be a coherent TAK plan on objective/console maps.

Losing potential objectives was largely a **list construction problem**, not just poor dice.
- **Inference:** **Ariadna's high-impact CC/Smoke can achieve Predator-style missions**, but in **Countermeasures** a single Dozer, Paramedic and varied skill profiles may outperform buying an extra Cameronian if the active card pool demands precise specialist types.
- **Caution:** the stream uses unfamiliar card names and both players check the objective text while playing; rely on the **actual Countermeasures packet from the event**, not a current ITS18 card list.

**Use in list-building:** build a **Classified coverage matrix** by required *skills, deployables, Engineer, D-Charges, FO/Doctor and marker-detection*, not just army-killing capacity.
