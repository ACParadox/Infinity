- **Observed — ALEPH:** **Maximus with heavy gun/Suppressive Fire**, **Asura**, Warcor/Probot, flash/missile REMs and supporting Netrods were the core. **Asura went down early**, but the **Maximus** gun and a swarm of small **difficult-to-isolate six-point models** made a durable late control screen, pushing Shasvastii to kill cheap bodies to change scoring.
- **Observed — Shasvastii:** **Sphinx, Sheskiin, two Calibans, Malignos Hacker, Mentor, Nox Paramedic, M-Drone and Ikadron/Syndron** split between offense and pushing/stealing the antenna state. Do not treat 5–3 as ALEPH generally outmatching Shasvastii.
- **Caution:** Exact point allocation is interpreted from heavily translated postgame narration; verify ITS18 condition texts before exporting list-tech verbatim.

**Mission takeaway:** Neutralize items early, then **budget enough remaining Orders to touch/contest antennas**; losing a key attacker is recoverable, losing the late antenna swing is not.
