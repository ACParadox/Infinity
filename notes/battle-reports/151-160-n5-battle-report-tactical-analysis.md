## #151 — Tunguska vs Steel Phalanx: Hardlock

[Original full video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OdKrlYTWSRM). **Tunguska 5–0 Steel Phalanx.**

- **Observed:** Phalanx brought **Agamemnon, Phoenix, Atalanta, Ektros, Myrmidon Officer and Dactyl support**. Early Lieutenant-targeting attacks exposed its forward Fireteams; Atalanta guarded a firing line Tunguska largely avoided.
- **Observed:** Tunguska's **Interventor, Heckler/Hollow Man hacking network, Kriza Borac and Zellenkrieger** repeatedly inflicted **Isolated, Targeted and IMM** states, including unusually successful split-Burst hacking. Phalanx spent precious Orders Resetting instead of reaching consoles. **Kriza eventually put Phoenix down**.
- **Observed:** Tunguska activated two consoles and completed a Classified, for **5–0**. Phalanx's late eligible specialist was too far away or isolated to change the state of Hardlock.
- **Player assessment:** The Phalanx player thought a successful first-turn LT assassination could have changed the game, but regretted shifting his reserve plan after deploying other pieces, which left Atalanta disconnected from relevant combat.
- **Inference:** **Interventor/Hollow Man hacker + Kriza/Switcher mission runner** is a credible **anti-Hackable Phalanx Hardlock** package; Phalanx should guard repeater approaches, diversify its console workers and avoid committing all Orders to an uncertain LT hunt.
- **Caution:** Remarkably strong Tunguska Hacking rolls make the 5–0 result poor evidence of average faction superiority.

**Mission takeaway:** Keep **a surviving unisolated Specialist to push consoles** even after a failed alpha.

## #152 — Vanilla Combined Army vs White Banner: ITS17 Outbreak

[Original full video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tPAdd8PhlRo). **Combined Army 10–3 White Banner**.

- **Observed:** Combined's **Caliban Specialist Operative, Umbra Legate hacker/linked advance, Nexus Chain of Command, Bit & Kiss and Libertos mine/forward screen** deliberately covered **scan, stabilize, alpha escort and CivVac** duties. The Caliban profile was selected for mission eligibility instead of the usual fighting option.
- **Observed:** White Banner used **Tiger Soldier/medical movement, Shaolins, Gudan/linked shooters and other specialists**. Both armies **scanned three infected each**, but Combined additionally **stabilized more, stabilized the alpha, CivVac'd more including alpha and secured the infected**, taking every other category of points.
- **Player assessment:** Going second, **carry infected back toward a safe DZ before scanning/stabilizing** may work better than attempting repeated WIP rolls in contested midfield. The winner did not replay that alternative, so mark it hypothetical.
- **Inference:** **Caliban/Legate/HVT escort plus Libertos denial** can excel in Outbreak against a White Banner team unable to stop alpha evacuation. White Banner should retain **two qualified medical Specialists** and screen the Tiger Soldier route.
- **Caution:** Outbreak is a *new midseason ITS17 mission*: specialist eligibility and scenario points must be checked in that edition, not assumed from ITS18.

**Mission takeaway:** Secure **early alpha access** and separate **medical actions from CivVac escort**.

## #153 — Krug 2026: Vanilla JSA tournament winner

[MetaChemistry full transcript/episode](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3VWppA6FkPPN9v001kUS0j) · [winner's Samurai list](https://bit.ly/Krug_Kawaii_Samuraii) · [Shinobu list](https://bit.ly/Krug_Akial_Shinobu). **Azoka wins 7-round event, October 2026 N5.3/ITS18.**

- **Observed retrospective:** Main list used **two Karakuri, Miyamoto Musashi, Hatamoto and Tanko**. Alternate list included **Shinobu Kitsune and early infiltrators** for **The Dig and Provisioning**, which need forward starting positions and rapid objective access. The primary list emphasized **Akial/B-Pong and broad task coverage**.
- **Player assessment:** **Tanko Disco Baller escorting Musashi** on open tables was a central success factor. Practice in **Double Bind** showed Musashi's close-combat Sabotage could save Orders relative to a less accurate D-Charges attempt.
- **Observed retrospective:** In a match against **OSS in Area of Interest**, Musashi did button/zone work rather than chase kills. A **Hatamoto** separately cleared a shooting path for an attacker to reach an opposing Lieutenant.
- **Player assessment:** **Shinobu as anti-Next-Wave** was an idea developed in practice, **not a documented Next Wave unit killed by Shinobu during Krug**. Seven rounds demanded broader Classified skill coverage than either pure assassination or one narrowly tailored counterlist.
- **Inference:** **Karakuri durability + Tanko Disco/Musashi objective assault + Hatamoto ranged pressure** is a proven *tournament-winning JSA shell*, not proof of automatic sectorial advantage.
- **Caution:** Several round names in auto transcription are ambiguous; no seven fabricated match scores or assumed 7–0 record.

**List takeaway:** Split lists across **deep infiltration missions versus broad Classified/zone coverage**, and use Disco visibility to deliver CC scoring.

## #154 — Burn City Brawl 2026: OSS tournament winner

[Loss of Lieutenant full transcript/episode](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2Rtq6upCKjpArRl7G0qDT4). **Ivory (Zephyr), winner with OSS**; published Oct 1 2026, event date unresolved; **N5.3 probable**. Published episode includes two Army exports and explicitly notes **no flyer**.

- **Observed retrospective:** Mission lineup was **Raid the Kazarabu, Evacuation, Uplink Center, Critical Intervention and Last Launch**. The winning construction used **Asura Hacker/Lieutenant + Posthumans (Mk2/Mk4) + Dart** as a focused specialist/attacker web; a second alternative with **Kaira Sharma Optimate** addressed a different fast Specialist role.
- **Player assessment:** Asura has excellent abilities but **uses too many points if its list still needs three distinct noncombat specialists**. Kaira Sharma is a legitimate alternative to another roughly 45-point vehicle/flyer attacker when mission tasks matter.
- **Observed retrospective:** During **Evacuation versus TAK**, Dart's speculative **E/M grenade hit Voronin** and Isolated the Lieutenant. Player initially wanted to induce an exposed Tankhunter/Camo reaction, so the LT result was a bonus, not a reliably repeatable outcome.
- **Player assessment:** **Pilot X REM Driver** benefits depend on the target and can be redundant for an already BS13 REM. **Racerbots/Netrods** offer utility but do not replace a credible defensive gun.
- **Inference:** **Asura + Posthuman + Dart** is an effective OSS broad-utility team, while **Kaira Sharma/Optimates** may be better in rapid objective/evacuation scenarios. This is a decision between investments, not a must-buy list of both.
- **Caution:** Interview contains strong balance opinions; opponent-by-opponent final OP are not verified.

**List takeaway:** Choose **Asura or Kaira Sharma** according to scenario specialist and defensive redundancy requirements.

## #155 — Salt Lake Showdown 2026: Vanilla Combined winner

[MetaChemistry full transcript/episode](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2QCaMHdal57kzFgPLAzMCt) · [winner's list](https://bit.ly/AzokaSLS1). **Azoka wins with Vanilla Combined Army** on June 19–20 2026, N5.2/ITS17.

- **Observed retrospective:** Player prepared **Avatar** and a **fast Kodali/Kasuto/Combined list**, but emphatically **did not play the Avatar list in the event**. Do not count this as an Avatar victory.
- **Player assessment:** Avatar could be too risky into **Caledonian close combat**, a skilled anti-Avatar opponent, **Supremacy** or **Akial's diverse Classified demands**. A more versatile **Anathematic Hacker plus fast Combined threat** permitted broader scoring.
- **Observed retrospective:** In **Hardlock vs Starmada**, an opponent **M-Drone Sensor detected the Anathematic** and **Shona Carano** pushed aggressively, challenging the EI plan. The Combined player survived the unexpected pressure while still following his button/mission plan.
- **Inference:** **Anathematic/independent Specialists rather than Avatar** is a legitimate answer to broad ITS17 mission sets and elite close-combat matchups; these are *situational choices*, not evidence Avatar is weak in all other contexts.
- **Caution:** Some fast-Combined attack unit names are machine-transcribed inconsistently; use the linked list for exact profiles.

**List takeaway:** A tournament winner can legitimately leave its **premium TAG unplayed** when opponent expertise and Classified coverage make another list better.

## #156 — Border Bash 2026: Next Wave tournament winner

[Loss of Lieutenant full transcript/episode](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4Bhv6ktwZKp4iYjyR7EW8q). **Infinity the Dante wins with Next Wave**, June 2026 N5.2/ITS17. Episode explicitly **does not cover all round-by-round results**.

- **Player assessment:** **Achilles** was chosen for **two Key Ops missions** that reward his fast movement and reliable high-PH Dodge; author calls him **expensive and a poor automatic include** in other lineups.
- **Observed retrospective:** **Blackheart primary gun, Stinger backup, Pandora, Juicer/Achilles, Harbinger Core/Haris flexible Fireteams, Puzzlers and Skyhound** formed an aggressive but fragile network. Guest describes **floating Harbinger Fireteam and Achilles/Juicer Haris** choices.
- **Player assessment:** **Puzzlers** were a calculated obstacle to OSS hackable advancement and **Tarxia-type flyers**; Blackheart supplied strong ARO while Stinger could work in the active turn. **Pandora/Skyhound casualties** could collapse the plan, and player wrongly assumed some Skyhound repair equipment existed.
- **Inference:** **Achilles + Harbinger/Pandora + Blackheart** can be powerful for **Key Ops missions**, while Puzzlers and Skyhound provide independent threats. Not necessarily a sound list for other events.
- **Caution:** The winner explicitly calls his unusual list **a specific key for a specific lock**; verify historical Fireteam and GizmoKit rules before copying.

**List takeaway:** **Achilles Key Ops value is mission-specific**, and his supporting gun/repair roles must be budgeted realistically.

## #157 — Battle of the Bridges 2: Bakunin winner

[MetaChemistry full transcript/episode](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4vbWAkYlMp9f0rWkeN0LoJ). **Rob “CobraPrime” wins with Bakunin** (June 2026 N5.2/ITS17). Mission pack **Hardlock, Crossing Lines, Akial Interference, Provisioning, Area of Interest**.

- **Observed retrospective:** Winner kept a **well-practiced first Bakunin list** and changed his second, adding a **Go-Pod-style flyer** and **Penitent AP Continuous-Damage Spitfire** to counter increasingly mobile/flyer-heavy opponents.
- **Player assessment:** Penitent's armor/BTS and Continuous Damage offer credible **hard ARO and active-trade punishment**, but pure static defense is vulnerable to **Disco Ball/smoke and fast DZ incursions**. Flyer mobility is necessary to contest boxes carried deep behind gunlines.
- **Observed retrospective:** In **Provisioning**, cheap Specialists survived after expensive attackers had fallen; they still **transported and secured boxes in the proper zones** and the opponent could not recover.
- **Player assessment:** Rob prepared by rehearsing **brief mission-specific plans** repeatedly. Familiarity with one general-purpose list was preferable to changing every profile for every event.
- **Inference:** **Bakunin flyer/backup Penitent + cheap couriers** is an effective modern toolbox for Provisioning and Crossing Lines, not a universal anti-flyer guaranteed counter.
- **Caution:** Speaker acknowledges imperfect memory of all rounds and some unplayed options.

**List takeaway:** Maintain one **stable scenario list**, change the second for **flyer/ARA counter-pressure** without sacrificing objective couriers.

## #158 — AdeptiCon 2026: Kestrel winner

[MetaChemistry full transcript/episode](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3B1GrHPWtqQ3SubaiADxAL) · [list Falcon Paonch 2](https://bit.ly/FalconPaonch2) · [list Nintendo Legal Team 2](https://bit.ly/NintendoLegalTeam2). **Alex “KittenMarines” wins with Kestrel**, April 2026 N5.2/ITS17. Missions **Provisioning, Evacuation, Crossing Lines, Akial Interference, Area of Interest**.

- **Observed retrospective:** Familiar Kestrel was chosen over underpracticed Vanilla Combined. Lists featured **Black A.I.R. K1, Neurocinetics Sniper, Banshee regular Hacker, Redeye flyer, Croc Knight Strategic Deployment**, plus alternative **Maximus, Fennec FO and Bipandra**.
- **Observed retrospective:** Against **Achilles in Suppressive Fire**, **Banshee regular Hacker** inserted unexpectedly and threatened cyber attack; Achilles dropped suppression to deal with it, allowing **Redeye to gun it down over several Orders**. This is a directly recounted **Banshee vs Achilles + Redeye combo**.
- **Observed retrospective:** In **Crossing Lines against Caledonia**, **Redeye Sensor** revealed Camo and **Black A.I.R. K1** threatened a **Cameronian** despite its Smoke, illustrating distinct sensor and gun roles.
- **Player assessment:** The Banshee **standard Hacking Device, not only KHD**, also completes Spotlight Classifieds. **Croc Knight Strategic Deployment** permits better sniper/Black A.I.R. starting positions on open event tables.
- **Inference:** **Banshee hacker → Redeye shooter plus Black A.I.R. defender** can counter a Hackable elite/army with poor DZ Hacker screening. It is not proof Kestrel always beats Achilles or Caledonia.
- **Caution:** All lists predate May 2026 Army changes.

**List takeaway:** A **regular Hacker inserted from reserve** can make a supposedly safe elite Suppressive Fire posture unsafe.

## #159 — Battle by the Beach 2026: Shasvastii winner

[MetaChemistry full transcript/episode](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6FErkreAsQoUKosH4WkdPW) · [Day 1 list](https://bit.ly/BbtB-Day1) · [Day 2 list](https://bit.ly/BbtB-Day2). **Brian “Nazeen” wins with Shasvastii**, April 2026 N5.2/ITS17.

- **Observed retrospective:** The **sneak list** fielded **two Speculo Killers, three Calibans, Shrouded, cheap support and JTH Disco Baller** kept safely until the last turn; alternate more direct list had **Sheskiin, Nox, Gwailo and Victor Messer**.
- **Player assessment:** **Caliban Spitfire with MSV** and **Victor Hacker/Repeaters/Cybermines** were deliberate counters to growing **flyer pressure** after Oct 2025. Camo/Decoy marker density also makes some opponents waste whole Order pools discovering expendable nonthreats.
- **Observed retrospective:** Against **Achilles/Pandora/Patroclus in Superiority**, Shasvastii did not fight the entire hero link: it seized **other zones**, while the elite attacker wasted Orders on a Decoy. **Sheskiin deep attack** was useful for **Corporate Appropriation prototype assault**, with other Calibans/Victor as backups.
- **Inference:** **double Speculo + triple Caliban + JTH late Disco** can be excellent **zone/courier and stealth counterplay** against armies concentrating attack in one elite cluster. Sensor and cheap Discover actions are counterplay, not a universal opponent auto-loss.
- **Caution:** Retrospective round ordering/individual final scores not confirmed.

**List takeaway:** **Marker saturation consumes an opponent's Orders** while real Caliban/JTH specialists score elsewhere.

## #160 — Sin City Smackdown 2026: Bakunin vs Military Orders top table

[MetaChemistry full transcript/episode](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6DnrqIIJAgdFE07nhCcbXV). Tournament Jan 31–Feb 1 2026; N5.2/ITS17. **Rob “CobraPrime” won with Bakunin**, with guest Azoka **playing Military Orders** on a top table. Missions **Akial Interference, Crossing Lines, Annihilation, Provisioning, Area of Interest**.

- **Observed retrospective:** Rob used a **stable Bakunin core** practised for months; Azoka had **Military Orders Knight of Justice Hacker and other elite Knights**. The **Area of Interest** matchup put high importance on choosing/holding scoring zones, even when not actively killing opposing units.
- **Observed retrospective:** **Bakunin Riot Grrl Spitfire Fireteam** attacked MO support and cleared part of the defense. The MO player's E/M attempts and hard gun remained threatening, but the opponent had **multiple independent bodies to defend different areas**.
- **Player assessment:** Area placement and choosing first/second can determine the best opening route; **don't spend turns trading elite AROs if zones can be held/secured instead**. Longer **2h15 tournament rounds** reduced deadline pressure.
- **Observed retrospective:** Organizer used **the same deployment-zone side for Provisioning and Area of Interest**, a local scenario setup convenience differing from standard split descriptions.
- **Inference:** **Riot Grrl Fireteam + distributed Bakunin objective runners** can give a concentrated MO Knights force trouble in Area of Interest, but this interview **does not supply a reliable numeric top-table score**.
- **Caution:** Some tactical anecdotes refer to prior tournaments; do not attribute them all to Sin City or infer full MO matchup win rates.

**List takeaway:** **Mission-zone selection and dispersed specialists** are just as important as winning the Knight-versus-Riot-Grrl fight.
