# N5 Battle-Report Counterplay — TAGs, Heavy Infantry, Hacking Networks, Camo, Impersonators and Defensive Fireteams

**Reviewed:** 2026-11-02 · **Source:** [numbered battle-report tactical analyses](./) #001–#241 and [master report index](n5-battle-reports-and-tournament-reports.md). Related: [unit effectiveness](cross-report-unit-effectiveness-and-combinations.md), [mission performance](cross-report-mission-performance-by-sectorial.md), [repeatable synergies](n5-repeatable-unit-synergies.md). The present document covers **counterplay**, not unit ratings.

## Evidence, reproducibility and scope

- **O = directly observed** action described in one battle report; **R = tournament retrospective** containing first-hand reported actions; **I = inference** supported by actions but **not** an independently confirmed tactic. A second episode with similar tools strengthens a *mechanism*, not proof of matchup dominance.
- **Independent repetitions matter:** #161/#237 review the *same* CanCon champion; #169/#170 cover *one* Borderbash event; #006/#031, #009/#028, #010/#033, #011/#030 and #022/#026 are duplicates or re-uploads. Do not count as separate confirmations. #241 lacks verified action. Tourney recaps may cover five rounds without five independent indexed games.
- **Not clean normal-ITS evidence:** #208 has serious disputed Order counts; #226 is a one-TAG/single-group special format with a projected score and disputed LoF; #233 is projected, not completed; #235 uses Team Ops alterations; #237 includes a consequential rules error in a late round; #006 includes a Capture & Protect HVT error; #187 involved a critical E/M hit, not average reliability. A single lucky critical does not establish a reliable counter.
- **Rules baseline:** multiple N5 revisions, tournament packets and historical Army builds. Verify N5.3 profile, hacking programs, Firewall, equipment, status effects and legal Fireteams in this repository's current `sectorials/` and `rules/`. **Countered/disabled**, **removed**, **forced to Reset**, **delayed** and **outscored** are *different outcomes*.
- **Judge success by** (a) enemy Orders spent, (b) important enemy actions/specialists disabled, (c) own Orders cost, (d) mission access gained, (e) ability to repeat after counterplay or repair. Most counters fail when they **don't leave an independent scoring body**.

## 1 — TAGs and other premium targets

### A. Disable or divert first; kill only if necessary

**Observed:**
- **Scout E/M mine → Marut Lieutenant disabled**, opening vanilla Ariadna's forward attack before OSS conceded in CanCon Crossing Lines (#237 R). This is an **E/M trap + mission entry**, not a clean completed five-turn TAG-kill result; its late event rules issue also limits outcome use.
- **Sāchā E/M grenade → Skyhound disabled** (#187 O). The Shasvastii counter succeeded on a reported *critical* hit but the expensive flyer list still scored Provisioning; compare the **Order cost** of stopping a flyer with preserving normal scorers.
- **Interventor/Cybermine/Kulak web → Achilles stuck Resetting/disabled**, Tunguska then scored Akial Classifieds, 7–3 (#197 O). Similar layered Spotlight/Isolation into Shindenbutai's Hatamoto/HI occurred at #056 O; target is elite HI rather than a TAG.
- **Starmada Cyberghost repeatedly Possessed an NCA TAG** while a Roadbot-style unit flipped Hardlock buttons, 7–1 (#102 O). This directly demonstrates why control effects plus an **independent objective runner** are stronger than merely attempting TAG damage.
- **Kestrel Banshee hacker forcing Achilles to drop Suppression → Redeye firing lane** (#158 R); the named gunfighter was finished *after* cyber positional pressure, not by a stand-alone Redeye alpha (#005 O counterexample).
- **Bakunin Koala wounded Shang Ji** after failed Dodge (#060 O); cheap deployables can tax expensive HI in ways analogous to TAG protection. **Kriigel pheroware/E/M** made even Hatamoto wary of entering a Tohaa objective room (#224 O).

**Non-hacking solutions:** a **Tankhunter Autocannon** punished an exposed split-Burst Unknown Ranger (#223 O), **Black A.I.R. K1** threatened a Cameronian (#158 R), **Karakuri/Tanko Monofilament CC** punished an expensive threat after delivery in #099 O, and a **Kaeltar/Chaksa/Rasail direct-template corridor** delayed Shasvastii attacks (#189 O). Exact E/M, AP, K1, Monofilament and anti-materiel loadouts are profile-specific; do not assume one is universally best at every range/armor.

### B. Winning around the TAG can be the stronger counter

- **Vanilla Ariadna bypassed Varuna Cutter**, took Corporate Appropriation prototype using **Dynamo Smoke + Sasha** with minimal shooting, while the Cutter spent Orders on kills rather than getting a Specialist to the displaced objective (#237 R; consistent with #161, same event).
- A **hidden Cutter held Area of Interest zone weight** against USARF (#203 O), so *avoiding* it is not automatically correct when the TAG itself scores passively. Defeat the **zone/weight calculation** using another region or eligible contesting points, or actually remove/expel it.
- **Hassassin Bahram versus triple PanO TAGs**: Daylami/Panzerfaust and Muyib slowed the big guns, but the *win condition* was **three Classifieds for 5 OP** against PanO's 2 OP from kills (#214 O). This is **mission denial**, not a claim the Fidays killed all three TAGs.
- **Shasvastii Sphinx** killed ~four defenders but spent ~9–10 Orders while Torchlight **Jamie Parachutist + Moonraker** dismantled exposed Med-Tech/EVO/order support and took Battleground bands, 10–0 (#231 O). Deny the TAG's **support and score**, not necessarily its armor.
- **Critical counter-counterexample:** Double **Seraph + Defersen/repair** successfully attacked Tunguska TAG-hacking networks in Corporate/Hardlock (#181 R). A hacker grid without direct protection, own KHD/Firewall checks or safe objective access is not a guaranteed anti-TAG win.

**Practical sequence:** locate Repeater/Hacker and mine zones; choose **E/M/control, K1/AP/CC, flank backline, or bypass-to-score**; protect the enabler and reserve mission Orders; account for Engineer/Reset/repair; do not automatically chase the TAG to destruction.

## 2 — Heavy Infantry and defensive HI Fireteams

### A. Separate link bonuses, range and network access

- **Tunguska Pitcher → Spotlight → Guided → Oblivion** made **Shindenbutai Hatamoto-linked HI** spend Orders on Reset/Isolation rather than Battleground strips (#056 O).
- **Morat double Dartok linked Pitchers + Guided** removed/disabled Svalarheima protected guns while **Rodoks** pressed Unmasking; the Suryat surviving a reactive roll was lucky, not the reason the tactic works in general (#118 O).
- **Tunguska Interventor/Mary Problems → Guided** broke **TAK Veteran Kazak/Frontovik Suppression** (#166 R). **Exposed premium defenders are not automatically safe just because they win open fire lanes**.
- **Tunguska Interventor/Heckler/Hollow Man hacking** repeatedly Isolated/Targeted/IMM'd Steel Phalanx and prevented timely Hardlock consoles (5–0, unusually good rolls; #151 O).
- **Combined Pitcher → Bit & Kiss killed WhiteCo's Fusilier Hacker → a Fireteam lost described Sixth Sense protection → Charontid killed Hawkwood** (#112 O). The key here is *removing the defensive team member and changing the subsequent gun odds*, not directly shooting the highest ARM first.
- **Bakunin Repeater/Koala** wounded **Shang Ji** while pressing Superiority (#060 O). **Caledonian Wulvers/Mormaer and warbands** blocked White Banner heavy infantry from a Critical Intervention room by close templates and short-range CC rather than premium AP gun duels (#086 O).
- **Tunguska Grenzer/reactive gun + cheap Bounty Hunter** beat a Military Orders multiple-elite-Fireteam approach in Firefight 7–0; two Classifieds/Panoply mattered as much as the gunfight (#137 O).

### B. What fails

- Trying to trade one expensive HI in a perfect lane **without seeing the template/Koala or projected Pitcher** (#060/#056).
- Investing all Orders in **Guided** after the board has opened while forgetting the actual scoring pieces (#005 O, #229 O).
- Assuming links are weak simply because they are hackable: **double Seraph/Defersen** and **TinBot/Engineer** support can punish a poorly protected hacking network (#181 R); **Hassassin Asawira/Muyib/Govad** kept supplies against ALEPH despite enemy elite attackers (#205 O).

**Checklist:** Is the *member carrying TinBot/Engineer/Hacker/Second Number* exposed? Does destroying one Repeater change the Order path? Can one **template/Disco/Pitcher** reach multiple models without illegal assumptions about Coherency/line of fire? Can a non-hackable Specialist bypass the defended quadrant?

## 3 — Hacking networks, Pitchers and Guided

### A. Attack infrastructure rather than pushing hackable units through it

1. **Shoot or safely clear the forward Repeater/Pitcher** while keeping important HI/TAG outside the projected area. Kestrel **Firefly/Griffin removed White Banner's Pitcher** to let Hetkari work (#003 O); Kestrel likewise cleared Combined Repeaters and then took Supremacy consoles/zones (#005 O).
2. **Project your own Repeater into the vulnerable Hacker, then use a legal KHD/program.** **ISS Zhanying/Wu Ming Pitcher → Deva KHD Trinity killed Jazz** in Corregidor's Moran/Jazz network (#238 O), freeing Adil's lane, though Adil was subsequently punished for staying exposed. **Combined Bit & Kiss** similarly killed the exposed **Fusilier Hacker** protecting a WhiteCo team (#112 O).
3. **Use non-hackable pressure, vision screens or other routes** when the attacker's own HI/TAG would trigger several enemy AROs. **TAK Camo/Vystrel** caused trouble for double Combined Aspects, while Combined's Repeaters were much less effective against much of nonhackable TAK (#064 O). **O-12 Varangian Smoke plus durable advance** continued through a Nomad Jazzy network in Biotechvore and won 5–2, albeit with environmental time pressure on both sides (#128 O).
4. **Attack the backfield hacker, repair/order base or deployment edge**: Torchlight Jamie + Moonraker reached Shasvastii **Med-Tech/EVO** (#231 O); the general lesson is offensive access behind the front, not automatic immunity to a hacking net.
5. **Keep the mission Specialist alive after the net changes:** #078 O Starmada needed a narrow route between **Moran Repeaters** for CivVac even while Corregidor Spotlight+Vertigo punished links. #238 O **Taowu backup** scored after Adil's exposed team was lost.

### B. Counter-network failure examples

- **Banshee KHD vs Interventor KHD**: Banshee won rolls but **Firewall/BTS prevented a meaningful wound and Banshee was lost** (#115 O). “Bring any KHD” is not a reliable answer.
- **Tunguska network suppressed Achilles** (#197 O), whereas **MO double Seraph/Defersen** reached and attacked Tunguska hacking support (#181 R). Network *delivery* and coverage matter as much as cyber statline.
- **Tunguska three Interventors + Guided** killed large portions of NCA (#122 O), but **the early console action** still determined the final objective difference; do not equate damage with scoring.
- **Kestrel #005** showed another Guided volley may be inferior to securing a quadrant; count all Pitcher, program, missile, clearance and mission Orders.

**Audit:** Identify *every* Repeater path, who can hack through it, Firewall and BTS, attacker's vulnerability to enemy KHDs, **which specific Repeaters can be destroyed**, whether safe bypass exists, and the remaining scoring route. Consult [hacking and network projection](../list-building/hacking-and-network-projection.md) for N5.3 mechanics.

## 4 — Camo, Hidden Deployment and Decoys

### A. Reveal selectively and otherwise deny the marker's mission

- **Redeye Sensor → reveal Camo; Black A.I.R. K1 pressure** against Caledonian threats (#158 R). **Shasvastii M-Drone Sensor** revealed a hidden Anathematic in a Combined tournament game (#155 R); separate Sensor success from the later game winner.
- **Asura MSV3** shot a camouflaged mine during the Hardlock final (#208 O, **score/Order-count disputed**). The reusable idea is **right visibility tool versus the right marker**, not treating a contested match as clean competitive data.
- **WhiteCo/linked MSV vs Smoke** and **Yojimbo/Eclipse/Disco** illustrate *specific* visor and line-of-fire interactions (#010 R, #041 O, #233 early stop). **MSV is not automatically Sensor**. Use exact N5.3 ARO/Discover and visibility rules.
- **Cheap model or expendable REM into a mine** can save the actual button operator: in Onyx #212 O, an **E-Drone deliberately absorbed a mine so Kurnau could reach the Classified** (6–0 Countermeasures). In #232 O, **Daylami** absorbed/cleared a Shasvastii minefield on the actual Sabotage route.
- **Attack the mission while Camo is elsewhere:** #196 O **quad-Shrouded/double Malignos** won zone occupancy from Next Wave through hidden markers and Dazer delay, but Next Wave still held a console for part of the game. Camo is strongest when it *scores or prevents scoring*; forcing every marker to reveal can be the wrong objective.
- **Minelayer/Decoy defenses tax both sides:** #067 O TAK's ~13 apparent markers slowed Ikari; #161/#237 R Ariadna's Strelok Decoys screened Tankhunters; #159 R Shasvastii marker saturation diverted Achilles/Pandora from zones. These illustrate **why selective Sensor/Discover, route choice and marker triage** matter.

### B. Detection can be less valuable than preserving Orders

Choose the marker to handle by **what it can score or block now**. A Camo shell without an actual valid specialist path can be outscored. Avoid indiscriminate Discover loops that give up last-turn consoles (#189 and #208 mission lessons). **Do not invent abilities** such as a particular visor automatically ignoring Impersonation or Holoecho.

## 5 — Impersonators, Fidays, Speculos and DZ alpha attackers

### A. The reliable principle is *deployment geometry* rather than a single counter-model

- **Do not expose Lieutenant + sole Hacker + Engineer/CoC in one accessible attack pocket.** Hassassin **Fiday** threatened Shindenbutai's **Tanuki Chain of Command/Hacker and Panguling** before Frontline scoring (#088 O). **Fiday + Haytham** repeatedly pressured high-value Combined army actors when the cheap **Ikadron/Dāturazi/minelayer screen was mispositioned** (#147 O).
- **Keep cheap templates, mines, Koalas, warbands and a reserve shooter** in positions forcing an Impersonator to cross **two separate threats**, not merely one impressive sniper. JSA **Hidden Oniwaban + Minelayer (2)** punished an airborne **Liu Xing** arrival (#051 O): **it is an anti-drop example, not proof the Oniwaban counters Impersonation**. In #223 O, mines/guards stopped Van Zant from *finishing* the prototype after he killed Voronin.
- **Protect the actual mission object/carrier:** Shasvastii **Sheskiin/Speculo** penetrated WinterFor shooting in Critical Intervention (#182 O), but WinterFor **Peacemaker mine, deployable turrets and last-turn REM/room worker** won **9–5**. Defending/going second gave this build time to punish the mandatory retrieval path.
- **Cheap anti-assassin deterrent:** #215 O players praised cheap **Gakis** for making Fiday/McMurrough-like approaches costly even when they did not directly kill those pieces. This is **deterrence**, not documented successful Fiday destruction. **Shujae Hidden E/M Minelayers + Ghazi Jammers** helped Hassassin survive alpha attacks in a winner retrospective (#173 R).
- **Counterexample:** Shasvastii **double Speculo/triple Caliban** exploited insufficiently protected hero lists and marker confusion in a tournament recap (#159 R). A hypothetical one-line mine screen is not guaranteed to prevent a coordinated infiltration.

**Use current rules to distinguish** Impersonation versus Camouflage, normal Discover, Biometric Visor, Sensor, enemy deployment restrictions and what triggers a valid ARO. **#143** includes a real Fiday versus Biometric Visor/Impersonation interaction, but the report does not support a universal “one visor removes all impersonators” rule.

## 6 — Defensive Fireteams, MSV snipers, entrenched reactive gunlines

### A. Change the type of fight

- **LoF/visibility:** **Fennec Disco Baller → Hetkari** crossed a **Torchlight Wrecker/Jamie** firing lane (#004 O); **Varangian Smoke → linked Epsilon MSV2** created a more favorable fight with a **Tikbalang** (#041 O); **Shinden Yojimbo Smoke + Shizoku rocket** removed exposed Torchlight **Vidoc/Moonraker** AROs (#233 O, result projected). Check which screens affect which visor and model.
- **Network instead of shooting:** **Dartok linked Pitcher/Guided** attacked protected WinterFor guns (#118 O), **Tunguska Spotlight/Guided** broke TAK Veteran Kazak/Frontovik Suppression (#166 R), and **Bakunin White Noise/Repeaters → Guided** threatened Nisse/Uma in Frostbite (#098 O).
- **Break the structural Fireteam member:** **Bit & Kiss KHD killed WhiteCo Fusilier Hacker**, dropping the recorded link bonus and opening Charontid vs Hawkwood (#112 O). Think “remove TinBot/Hacker/Doctor/bonus-enabler” rather than “duel the highest BS gun”.
- **Hit a side/back angle:** **Banshee Parachutist** punished White Banner's facing after Hetkari pressure (#003 O); **Jamie Parachutist + Moonraker** hit Shasvastii Med-Tech/EVO behind a Sphinx (#231 O); **Raoul** is a fielded selective airborne tool in Tunguska lists (#166 R), not proof any random AD is safe.
- **End the turn with a scorer, not another gunfight:** **Hassassin Daylami denial + protected Muyib supplies** stopped ALEPH Maximus/Penny converting Provisioning (#205 O); **Tohaa Rasail/Chaksa templates** made a Data Harvest delivery corridor expensive despite Shasvastii Sphinx/Sheskiin guns (#189 O); #214 O triple PanO TAG force could shoot but had no Classified scoring breadth.
- **Hide or take another path:** #002 O White Banner could complete Unmasking HVT tasks around Kestrel's Black A.I.R. ARO; #161/#237 R Ariadna went *around Cutter* in Corporate. This fails when the gun itself must physically dominate the scored zone (#203 Cutter Area of Interest O).

### B. When the counters failed

- **WinterFor Nisse spent 5–6 Orders clearing one Cenobite HRL** instead of securing Supplies, then lost on two-box majority to Bakunin (#194 O). Even a successful ARO removal can be strategically bad.
- **Kestrel Redeye spent most Orders failing Q-Drone** (#005 O); #158 R instead used a Banshee hacker to open Redeye's shot.
- **White Company Haidao/Hawkwood mixed vision core** was disrupted by Combined Pitcher/Bit/Fusilier Hacker death (#112 O); redundancy in vision weapons cannot protect an exposed supporting hacker by itself.
- **Caledonia's room defense** used Wulvers/Mormaer/templates to make White Banner waste Orders clearing Scottish fighters instead of reaching the server (#086 O). A premium linked gun still needs a **protected button/room actor**.
- **No universal hard counter:** #181 R **Defersen + twin Seraphs** broke some Tunguska hacking defenses, and #187 O anti-flyer Shasvastii investment still failed to stop normal ground scoring. Adapt to **opponent composition and mission**, not unit labels alone.

## List-building and turn-by-turn counter checklist

1. **Identify** the *real obstacle*: armor and wounds, hackability/state, visor/line of fire, hidden marker, impersonator path, or opponent's **mission action**. A “TAG problem” often is a **scoring-geometry problem**.
2. **Pick one safe primary and one fallback mechanism**: e.g., E/M + K1/anti-materiel versus TAG; Pitcher + nonhackable flanker versus HI; clear Repeater + protected KHD against network; Sensor + sacrificial mine-clearing versus Camo; mines + CoC/spacing versus impersonator; Disco/Smoke + lateral scorer versus ARO Fireteam.
3. **Protect infrastructure**: own Hacker/Engineer, TinBot carrier, LT/CoC, and any delivery Specialist. Expect the opponent to hunt exactly those components.
4. **Count the complete Order line**: deploy/clear smoke or Pitcher, target/reset/hit, eliminate/displace obstacle, move specialist, roll WIP/plant D-Charge, move to safe scoring zone and survive. Avoid solving an obstacle with more Orders than the objective is worth.
5. **Check mission and legality**: an anti-TAG tool is not automatically an objective action; a Fireteam composition recorded in N5.1 may not exist in N5.3; an impersonator is not ordinary Camo; a Compulsory/Team Ops rule may change relevance.
6. **Record outcome honestly**: **removed**, **immobilized**, **isolated**, **Forced Reset**, **Order tax**, **outscored** or **failed**. Never classify “never activated but forced caution” as zero impact or classify “killed many, lost mission” as strategic success.
