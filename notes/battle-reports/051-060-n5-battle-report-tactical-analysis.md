## #051 — Firefight: Invincible Army vs Vanilla JSA

**Primary:** [Episode 12](https://www.miniwargaming.com/content/invincible-army-vs-jsa-infinity-n5-battle-report-ep-12) · [original full video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FB_Dd43SI-s). **Greg: Invincible Army; Skari: Vanilla/mixed JSA**, published July 16, 2025; likely N5.1.1/ITS16. **Reported final: JSA 5–4 Invincible Army.**

- **[Observed]** JSA deployed **Oniwaban Hidden Deployment + a Camo Minelayer (2) trap** with two Mines, making apparent free arrival routes uncertain. An Invincible Army airborne **Liu Xing** arrived directly near the Hidden Oniwaban and was killed in close combat. This demonstrates **hidden anti-parachutist defense**, not a guaranteed anti-Liu Xing counter.
- **[Observed]** IA fielded **Daoying Lieutenant, Lei Gong, multiple airborne troops and EVO Hacker support**. The drop package could reach JSA support from the rear but also encouraged the opponent to keep **Oniwaban/Camo close-range interceptors** for the vulnerable landing flank.
- **[Observed]** A JSA **Hatamoto** suffered **Isolation/state pressure**, later escaped, and preserved its final-turn attack capability. The decisive late **Panoply interaction by a surviving Irregular/specialist body** produced a **5–4 JSA win**, despite serious casualties.
- **[Inference]** Firefight's **Specialist kills, Lieutenant kills, army points and Panoplies** demand more than good alpha shooting. A last-turn **cheap Panoply carrier** may decide a game when both forces have lost their premium attackers.
- **[Caution]** The host and guest were checking rules while playing. Verify **Minelayer (2), drop-zone placement, Impetuous/Irregular Panoply actions and recovery** with the 2025 profile/rules, not current N5.3.

**Useful build:** **Hidden Oniwaban + credible Camo mines + a surviving Panoply runner** creates a threat/scoring package even when playing against a high-cost Invincible HI list.


## #052 — Countermeasures: Torchlight Brigade vs Steel Phalanx

**Primary:** [Episode 11](https://www.miniwargaming.com/content/greg-is-back-torchlight-brigade-vs-steel-phalanx-infinity-n5-battle-report-ep-11) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tn6_u2OH4Tw). **Josh: Torchlight; Greg: Steel Phalanx.** Published June 18, 2025, likely N5.1.1/ITS16. **Steel Phalanx won on missions even after taking heavy casualties**; no exact certified result retrieved.

- **[Observed]** Steel Phalanx's **Agamemnon TAG, Scylla Hacker, Hector, Eudoros, Acmon, Myrmidon, Ajax, Netrods** presented extremely dangerous targets, but the win came from **Classified actions** rather than surviving with more troops.
- **[Observed]** Torchlight brought **Silverstar, Strider Red Fury, double Clutchers and a Wave Rider hacker**. The player eliminated important Greeks including **Hector/Eudoros**, then acknowledged that he had **killed enemies he needed alive** to finish remaining Countermeasures objectives.
- **[Player view]** A **double-Clutcher utility Fireteam** could perform several **Engineer/GizmoKit/other task-specific actions** if the right injured/eligible enemy remained. Spending Orders on a tempting Strider Red Fury kill removed that scoring opportunity.
- **[Inference]** For **Countermeasures**, one must **build a complete specialist/target matrix and sometimes deliberately avoid lethal shots**. Torchlight's direct offensive strength did not compensate for losing the task race, but no generic Steel Phalanx advantage is established.
- **[Caution]** The players stress they were learning mission timing. Do not repeat contested **GizmoKit or Classified objective** mechanics as a current rules ruling.

**Useful build:** **Clutchers as mission specialists + just enough guns to deny the opponent's own Classified**, rather than kill-maximizing a Silverstar/Strider turn.


## #053 — Frontline: Torchlight Brigade vs Vanilla Nomads

**Primary:** [Episode 10](https://www.miniwargaming.com/content/torchlight-brigade-vs-nomads-infinity-n5-battle-report-ep-10) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7CRSjQ-nEao). **Josh: Torchlight; Logan: Vanilla Nomads.** Published June 4, 2025, likely N5.1.1/ITS16. **Result: Nomads 7–4** as reported in video.

- **[Observed]** Vanilla Nomads used **double Gecko + Zoe/Pi-Well Engineer**, **Jazz/Billie**, **Interventor Lieutenant** and an additional **Kriza** threat. **Zoe repaired a wounded Gecko and removed a disabling/adhesive state**, securing a **Combat Support Classified** during the same recovery strategy.
- **[Observed]** **Jazz hacking/Carbonite + White Noise** attacked Torchlight's **Wave Rider and HI gun lanes** without committing the Geckos to dangerous fair fights. Nomads chose **when the shooting happened**, while Torchlight spent Orders on Reset/repositioning.
- **[Observed]** **Frontline** awarded **1/2/3 points** for control of the increasingly distant 8-inch strips, plus Ranked/Classified goals. The Nomads retained **durable Gecko points in scoring areas** while Torchlight's **Hellblazer/Clutcher/Wave Rider** attacks often failed to convert casualties into sector dominance.
- **[Player view]** Logan describes Nomads as a faction that **makes fights unequal** through hackers and visibility control. Josh agrees many Torchlight attacks were technically sensible, but they cost too many Orders for too little scoring effect.
- **[Inference]** **White Noise + Jazz + Zoe + Geckos** is a compelling **list-level answer to direct Torchlight HI/visor gunplay** on that table, but one game does not establish that Vanilla Nomads generally beat Torchlight.
- **[Caution]** The speech-to-text sometimes misnames status programs and unit profiles; use the actual recorded interaction with the period-correct weapon rules.

**Useful build:** **Gecko shooter/zone holder + Zoe engineer/mission card + Jazz hacking/visibility**, maintaining a separate final-turn scoring budget.


## #054 — Panic Room: Shasvastii vs Bakunin

**Primary:** [Episode 9](https://www.miniwargaming.com/content/shasvastii-vs-bakunin-infinity-n5-battle-report-ep-9) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A46wS1RAm08). **Logan Shasvastii Expeditionary Force; Luka Bakunin**; published May 21, 2025; likely N5.1.1/ITS16. The players **recalculate draw/win outcomes in the last attack**; do not enter an unverified exact final score.

- **[Observed]** Shasvastii **Sphinx Hidden/Camo TAG + Mentor Lieutenant Decoy + Speculo Impersonator + Caliban Chain of Command** protected commander information, with **Nox Duo/Disco Ballers, Dazer terrain effects, and E-Drone/Q-Drone Thunderbolt defense**.
- **[Observed]** Bakunin **Stigmata TAG + MARS Spider Engineer**, **Custodier Lieutenant**, **Penitent/Orphan Engineer** and **Cenobite** had a distinct alternative to guessing which Shas Camo marker was real: **put substantial hard-to-remove points and essential personnel in the room**.
- **[Observed]** The **Panic Room contamination zone advanced each round**, while **room occupation and Essential Personnel** both affected scoring. Bakunin protected the **Custodier LT inside the room**, sacrificing a Doctor/other support when needed.
- **[Observed]** A **Cenobite attack on a durable room piece** created a real draw-to-win swing near the end. One unlucky/lucky critical result could alter the scoreboard; this is not a general troop power ranking.
- **[Inference]** Shasvastii deception forces **Discover/approach Order taxes**; Bakunin **Stigmata/Custodier/Engineer** can still contest if it keeps the *right* bodies alive until the end-of-round scoring.
- **[Caution]** Check the **historical Dazer, contamination, Essential Personnel and Hidden Deployment wording**. The players were learning this mission, and some final points were disputed during live calculations.

**Useful build:** **hidden command + independent room-entry force** versus **durable TAG/Essential Personnel + Engineer**; score presence, not backfield kills.


## #055 — Uplink Center: Imperial Service vs Vanilla JSA

**Primary:** [Episode 8](https://www.miniwargaming.com/content/imperial-service-vs-jsa-infinity-battle-report-ep-8) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WHTcxO7wZV0). **Greg ISS, Skari mixed Vanilla JSA**; published May 7, 2025. **ITS16-era Uplink Center; actual N5 revision unknown within initial N5–N5.1.1.** Avoid claiming a final score solely from the **joking “ISS wins” after the Initiative roll**.

- **[Observed]** ISS brought **Longwang NCO Heavy TAG/vehicle, Pheasant + Crane Duo, Wu Ming E/Mitter duo, Koga Ninja KHD, Celestial Guard FO/Hacker/Lieutenant Haris, Yingyao Paramedic**. The complementarity is **armored gun, anti-HI E/M, actual Hacker/FO specialists and hidden pressure**.
- **[Observed]** JSA deployed **Hatamoto NCO, Shizoku AP HMG, Senku Paramedic, Jizamurai E/M CC profiles, Yamabushi, Raiden, Mechazoid TAG, Metsuke**. Japanese E/M melee threatened a valuable ISS armored approach even if JSA lacked superior long-range Burst.
- **[Observed]** The active player spent **roughly seven Orders and several Command Tokens** on a **Vita Pack recovery / Fireteam reform**; the two players count the expense explicitly. Repairs must result in **a functional scoring attack**, or this is an inefficient use of mission time.
- **[Observed]** A **flamethrower template** threatened **three closely deployed JSA attackers**, forcing several Dodges. Uplink Center's **three midboard scoring/activation points** also required Hacker/specialist access, not simply killing the TAG.
- **[Inference]** **ISS Longwang + Crane/Pheasant + specialists** can pressure JSA center shooting, while **JSA Camo/E-M/close attacks** can bypass a heavy gun. No broad sectorial advantage is supported.
- **[Caution]** Player discussions about **NCO, Fireteam repair, Uplink console actions** require historic ITS16/N5 verification.

**Useful build:** anti-armor **Wu Ming E/Mitter + specialist Celestial Guards + Longwang gun** must retain enough *console Orders* after the attack.


## #056 — Battleground: Shindenbutai vs Tunguska

**Primary:** [Episode 7](https://www.miniwargaming.com/content/shindenbutai-jsa-vs-nomads-ep-7) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UADKTi95X_s). **Skari Shindenbutai; Jordan Tunguska**. **Published Apr 2, 2025: initial N5, explicitly ITS16 Part Two Battleground p.63.** Final numerical winner not clearly preserved by closing transcript.

- **[Observed]** Tunguska **Pitcher + Repeater + Spotlight/Targeted + Guided Missile**, followed by **Oblivion/Isolation**, targeted the **Hatamoto/elite Shindenbutai link**. The recording actually shows a **Guided Missile at Targeted defenders**, so this is not hypothetical anti-HI counterplay.
- **[Observed]** Shindenbutai had **Raiden surprise Rocket Launcher**, **Senku Paramedic + Jizamurai/Hatamoto linked attack**, **Yojimbo Smoke and motorcycle**, **Yamabushi** and supporting specialists. The **Raiden launcher** hurt a grouped defensive team, but did not make the player immune to later hacking.
- **[Observed]** Hatamoto had to **Reset / deal with Targeted or Isolated states** and the player regretted leaving his **Hacker too far from a useful support position**. Senku's extra **Paramedic/scoring utility** was praised in the postgame.
- **[Observed]** The Tunguska side also used **Super-Jump with unusual rooftop geometry** to access a firing angle, demonstrating movement efficiency apart from the Guided network.
- **[Inference]** **Tunguska projection + Guided** can severely delay Shindenbutai's expensive HI, unless JSA keeps **Engineer/Reset access and a second independent zone-holder**. **Battleground scores three contested strips**; destroying the hacker is only valuable if a JSA scorer crosses those strips.
- **[Caution]** Published **before Apr 3 N5.1**. Do not apply later guided/hacking revisions to this game's calculations.

**Useful build:** **Yojimbo Smoke / Raiden corner removal / Senku backup specialist** addresses part of a Tunguska network, but needs an actual Engineer route.


## #057 — Supremacy: Torchlight Brigade vs USAriadna

**Primary:** [Episode 6](https://www.miniwargaming.com/content/o-12-vs-ariadna-infinity-n5-battle-report-ep-6) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UNW13DIH2wQ). **Josh Torchlight Brigade, Jared USAriadna**, confirmed introductions despite generic O-12/Ariadna title. Published Mar 5, 2025; initial N5/ITS16 Supremacy. Closing discussion gives USARF late quadrant/Classified points but not a clean certified numeric result.

- **[Observed]** Torchlight **Silverstar HI gun + link/support Hacker** tried to open lanes with superior direct shooting. It could remove defenders, yet **USARF still occupied the scoring quadrants** with multiple independent bodies.
- **[Observed]** USARF listed **Unknown Ranger, Rosie, Hardcase Camo tactical bow, Marauder MSV1 Sniper and Paramedic, Minuteman NCO/AP HMG, Grunts and Van Zant Parachutist (Deployment Zone)**. This is a **spread of resilient guns, Camo cheap trades, rear entry and specialist capacity**.
- **[Observed]** **Van Zant** entered near the enemy DZ and **used NCO/Lieutenant orders** to reach a heavy enemy TAG/troop and complete a **Capture-type Classified**. A late flank can be an objective action rather than an assassination.
- **[Observed]** Cheap **Hardcase/Camo** eliminated a key **Torchlight Hacker** required for **HVT Espionage**, denying a Classified action disproportionate to the Hardcase's price. USARF **Marauder/Minuteman/Unknown Ranger** subsequently retook quadrants.
- **[Inference]** **USAriadna's multiple order-efficient scoring bodies + Van Zant** can be a conditional advantage in **Supremacy** against a Torchlight elite gunline that has few surviving specialists. Not proof USAriadna outshoots Torchlight.
- **[Caution]** Verify historical **Parachutist DZ and Capture/NCO** wording before rating Van Zant a guaranteed Classified specialist.

**Useful build:** **quadrant weights + two console/HVT-capable troopers + Van Zant back-entry** rather than one silver-star-dependent attack route.


## #058 — Frontline: Vanilla JSA vs TAK

**Primary:** [Episode 5](https://www.miniwargaming.com/content/infinity-jsa-vs-ariadna-infinity-n5-battle-report-ep-5) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1RRxODQGHsw). **Skari Vanilla JSA; Jared Tartary Army Corps**, from introductions. Published Feb 19, 2025, initial N5/ITS16 Frontline. **TAK win 9–1** explicitly announced.

- **[Observed]** TAK **Frontovik MSV sniper + Line/Veteran Kazak Fireteam, Tankhunter, Spetsnaz Parachutist and Forward Observer** created long-range lanes and **reliable endgame scoring bodies**. The linked Kazaks moved forward to occupy the Frontline strips.
- **[Observed]** A **Spetsnaz Parachutist** arrived **directly on/near Hidden Deployment Oniwaban** and was **killed in CC**. Hidden Deployment as **anti-parachutist defense** is a genuine one-play success, but it did not undo TAK's objective advantage.
- **[Observed]** TAK **Forward Observer completed multiple HVT/FO Classified tasks**, creating **three Classified points**; this is far more useful than the destroyed attacker in deciding the final margin.
- **[Observed]** JSA relied on **Daiyokai HMG/Panzerfaust, Raiden Rocket Launcher, Kempe Chain of Command, Hatamoto, Oniwaban, Ryuken-9**, and the player admitted **too few Doctor/Engineer/FO specialists** to recover or score.
- **[Inference]** **TAK Frontovik/Kazak link + specialists** is mission-effective in Frontline, but the score reflects an under-specialized JSA learning roster and dice, not general matchup odds.
- **[Caution]** The announcer explicitly says he's learning larger-point Fireteam play; do not classify it as competitive statistics.

**Useful build:** a **TAK Frontovik line holds scoring strips while a Camo/FO does Classifieds**; JSA must retain specialist alternatives.


## #059 — Acquisition: Bakunin vs USAriadna

**Primary:** [Episode 4](https://www.miniwargaming.com/content/nomads-vs-ariadna-infinity-n5-battle-report-ep-4) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LtA_FClXCX0). **Luka Bakunin; Jared USAriadna**, not generic Nomads and Ariadna. Published Feb 5, 2025; initial N5/ITS16 Acquisition. **Bakunin conceded** early rather than completing a normal three-turn scoring sequence.

- **[Observed]** Bakunin **Riot Grrl linked formation + Custodier Hacker / support** faced **USAriadna Marauder MSV sniper + linked Minuteman Missile Launcher/T2/Grunt AP Sniper** at exposed firing angles. Long-range missile/templates eliminated important Riot Grrls and reduced order economy.
- **[Observed]** One USARF template threatened a **second already-Unconscious trooper**, illustrating how clustered expensive link members can lose multiple pieces to one blast and follow-up wounds.
- **[Observed]** The map's **QAZ anomaly** also punished Bakunin positioning. The mission requires **two antenna Specialist activations plus Tech-Coffin control**, which early casualty losses left increasingly difficult.
- **[Player view]** Luka describes the bad rolls and poor exposed positions, then **concedes**; this is not evidence of a scored full tournament 10–0 or proof of a weak Bakunin profile.
- **[Inference]** **USAriadna long-range blast/MSV links** can punish a compact Bakunin Core on open high-ground tables. Alternative **Nomad hacking/Camo approaches** could change that interaction on a different table.
- **[Caution]** There were live rules/cover checks, so use the lesson of **Blast spacing and protecting antenna specialists**, not specific disputed modifier calculations.

**Useful build:** in Acquisition, protect **two antenna specialists and a Tech-Coffin holder** separately from the exposed main gun.


## #060 — Superiority: Invincible Army vs Bakunin

**Primary:** [Episode 3](https://www.miniwargaming.com/content/invincible-army-vs-bakunin-infinity-n5-battle-report-ep-3) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fis91SB7oqA). **Greg Invincible Army; Luka Bakunin.** Published Jan 22, 2025; initial N5/ITS16. **Mission is Superiority (not Supremacy)** with quadrants, consoles and Key Ops. Bakunin host discussed concession/accelerating late turns while IA led; numeric final result unverified.

- **[Observed]** Invincible Army **Shang Ji high-value HI + Tactical Awareness and NCO/LT order conversion** advanced to fight and **dominate quadrants by army-point value**.
- **[Observed]** Bakunin **Crazy Koala, Sin-Eater ARO, Custodier LT and other Repeater/support** could deny the route to a much more expensive enemy. A **Koala wounded Shang Ji** after it failed a Dodge, demonstrating real **cheap deployable vs premium HI** order/damage value.
- **[Observed]** Bakunin **Custodier Lieutenant died**, undermining its Order pool and capacity to activate consoles late. The host said he had exposed an obvious Lieutenant; afterward the players debated **Retreat and Loss of Lieutenant** interactions.
- **[Observed]** A heavily-armored Invincible model could still provide large **quadrant point weight** while a cheap Bakunin piece needed a **correct console Specialist action**. Damage to the HI is not equivalent to scoring a quadrant.
- **[Inference]** **IA durable multi-order HI** fits Superiority sector weight if the LT/Order support survives; **Bakunin Koala/hacking** can make the attack inefficient, but must be followed with *scoring units*.
- **[Caution]** The microphone failed during late play, and the host was still learning; not reliable for a precise terminal score or a faction tier ranking.

**Useful build:** preserve **LT, Key Ops, and quadrant occupancy**, while using cheap Koalas as *order tax* rather than sole anti-HI solution.
