## #051 — Firefight: Invincible Army vs Vanilla JSA

**Primary:** [Episode 12](https://www.miniwargaming.com/content/invincible-army-vs-jsa-infinity-n5-battle-report-ep-12) · [original full video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FB_Dd43SI-s). **Greg: Invincible Army; Skari: Vanilla/mixed JSA**, published July 16, 2025; likely N5.1.1/ITS16. **Reported final: JSA 5–4 Invincible Army.**


### Army lists and Fireteams — full-transcript / closing-showcase reconstruction

**Source:** [Invincible Army vs vanilla JSA, Firefight](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FB_Dd43SI-s). **Crucial:** the players deliberately hide marker and airborne information until the **postgame army showcase**, so that closing walkthrough is stronger roster evidence than the opening table view. The JSA player calls his force **vanilla**; do not reclassify it as Shindenbutai merely because it contains Hatamoto, Senku and other JSA models.

| Player | Units and confirmed profiles | Linked formations |
|---|---|---|
| **Vanilla JSA (Skari)** | **Senku Lieutenant**, **two Senku Paramedics** (one in each part of list), **Hatamoto**, **Daiyokai** HMG/Panzerfaust-type gunfighter, **Jizamurai**, **Nokizaru** midfield specialist, **Kempeitai Chain of Command**, **Oniwaban** (failed risky Infiltration roll), **Saito Togan**, **Raiden**, **Ryūken-9**, and other camo/mines; **two Combat Groups**. | A **Daiyokai/Jizamurai pair** is referred to as a **“Duo”** in the closing recap (grammar and exact members uncertain). The narrator explicitly says he is **not using a sectorial's broader Fireteam options** and discusses *wanting* future Hatamoto Cores/Haris. **Do not promote these future ideas into a played Core.** |
| **Invincible Army (Greg)** | **Dàoyīng Lieutenant, Boarding Shotgun**, **Gŭidān** (proxy), **2 Zhēnchá** (KHD and Minelayer 2, names normalized cautiously), **Hac Tao HMG**, **3 Liú Xīng Combat Jumpers** (two represented by Tiger Soldier sculpts), **Léi Gōng**, **Pangguling EVO** supporting jumps. | **No fielded four-/five-model Core is named in the closing list**. The three Liú Xīng are **separate airborne units, not a Haris**. |

**Other evidence:** JSA Hatamoto deployed in Suppressive Fire; the IA player initially shows only remote/Camo pieces, then launches airborne attacks. Distinguish a **coordinated/multiple-airborne** turn from a Fireteam activation.

**Large-team census:** **No confirmed four- or five-member starting Fireteam**. JSA's possible Duo needs period-specific chart validation before using it as a legal list template; the after-game description names no deployed Hatamoto Core. The reported score is **JSA 5–4**.



- **[Observed]** JSA deployed **Oniwaban Hidden Deployment + a Camo Minelayer (2) trap** with two Mines, making apparent free arrival routes uncertain. An Invincible Army airborne **Liu Xing** arrived directly near the Hidden Oniwaban and was killed in close combat. This demonstrates **hidden anti-parachutist defense**, not a guaranteed anti-Liu Xing counter.
- **[Observed]** IA fielded **Daoying Lieutenant, Lei Gong, multiple airborne troops and EVO Hacker support**. The drop package could reach JSA support from the rear but also encouraged the opponent to keep **Oniwaban/Camo close-range interceptors** for the vulnerable landing flank.
- **[Observed]** A JSA **Hatamoto** suffered **Isolation/state pressure**, later escaped, and preserved its final-turn attack capability. The decisive late **Panoply interaction by a surviving Irregular/specialist body** produced a **5–4 JSA win**, despite serious casualties.
- **[Inference]** Firefight's **Specialist kills, Lieutenant kills, army points and Panoplies** demand more than good alpha shooting. A last-turn **cheap Panoply carrier** may decide a game when both forces have lost their premium attackers.
- **[Caution]** The host and guest were checking rules while playing. Verify **Minelayer (2), drop-zone placement, Impetuous/Irregular Panoply actions and recovery** with the 2025 profile/rules, not current N5.3.

**Useful build:** **Hidden Oniwaban + credible Camo mines + a surviving Panoply runner** creates a threat/scoring package even when playing against a high-cost Invincible HI list.


## #052 — Countermeasures: Torchlight Brigade vs Steel Phalanx

**Primary:** [Episode 11](https://www.miniwargaming.com/content/greg-is-back-torchlight-brigade-vs-steel-phalanx-infinity-n5-battle-report-ep-11) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tn6_u2OH4Tw). **Josh: Torchlight; Greg: Steel Phalanx.** Published June 18, 2025, likely N5.1.1/ITS16. **Steel Phalanx won on missions even after taking heavy casualties**; no exact certified result retrieved.

| **Torchlight Brigade** | **Silverstar-heavy linked group — 5 likely** | **2 Hellblazers + Silverstar Rover HMG + Silverstar Rover Paramedic + Waverider Hacker** | Opening calls these a “nice little team” and lists five component bodies; the closing presentation groups them together. **Five-person membership strongly reconstructed**, but the initial “Core” label and full legal Army export are not shown. |

- **[Player view]** A **double-Clutcher utility Fireteam** could perform several **Engineer/GizmoKit/other task-specific actions** if the right injured/eligible enemy remained. Spending Orders on a tempting Strider Red Fury kill removed that scoring opportunity.
- **[Inference]** For **Countermeasures**, one must **build a complete specialist/target matrix and sometimes deliberately avoid lethal shots**. Torchlight's direct offensive strength did not compensate for losing the task race, but no generic Steel Phalanx advantage is established.
- **[Caution]** The players stress they were learning mission timing. Do not repeat contested **GizmoKit or Classified objective** mechanics as a current rules ruling.

**Useful build:** **Clutchers as mission specialists + just enough guns to deny the opponent's own Classified**, rather than kill-maximizing a Silverstar/Strider turn.

| Nomads | **Peripheral / support pairs (not Fireteams)** | **Zoe + Π-Well**, **Jazz + Billie**, and **Puppetmaster + one Puppetbot** | **Paired units explicitly listed**, but these are their own unit/peripheral relationships, **not three more Duos**. |

**Large-team census:** **Torchlight five-model Core/team confirmed by physical count**; **Nomads only a confirmed two-Gecko Duo**. The documented Nomads 7–4 result is a mission outcome, not evidence that a four-model defensive link was used.



- **[Observed]** Vanilla Nomads used **double Gecko + Zoe/Pi-Well Engineer**, **Jazz/Billie**, **Interventor Lieutenant** and an additional **Kriza** threat. **Zoe repaired a wounded Gecko and removed a disabling/adhesive state**, securing a **Combat Support Classified** during the same recovery strategy.
- **[Observed]** **Jazz hacking/Carbonite + White Noise** attacked Torchlight's **Wave Rider and HI gun lanes** without committing the Geckos to dangerous fair fights. Nomads chose **when the shooting happened**, while Torchlight spent Orders on Reset/repositioning.
- **[Observed]** **Frontline** awarded **1/2/3 points** for control of the increasingly distant 8-inch strips, plus Ranked/Classified goals. The Nomads retained **durable Gecko points in scoring areas** while Torchlight's **Hellblazer/Clutcher/Wave Rider** attacks often failed to convert casualties into sector dominance.
- **[Player view]** Logan describes Nomads as a faction that **makes fights unequal** through hackers and visibility control. Josh agrees many Torchlight attacks were technically sensible, but they cost too many Orders for too little scoring effect.
- **[Inference]** **White Noise + Jazz + Zoe + Geckos** is a compelling **list-level answer to direct Torchlight HI/visor gunplay** on that table, but one game does not establish that Vanilla Nomads generally beat Torchlight.
- **[Caution]** The speech-to-text sometimes misnames status programs and unit profiles; use the actual recorded interaction with the period-correct weapon rules.

**Useful build:** **Gecko shooter/zone holder + Zoe engineer/mission card + Jazz hacking/visibility**, maintaining a separate final-turn scoring budget.
