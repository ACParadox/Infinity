# Infinity N5 — battle reports and tournament matchups

**Compiled:** 2026-10-29. **Period:** N5 era, late 2024–2026. **Priority:** ITS tournaments, satellites, actual matchups and first-hand game reports.

## Data integrity

- **Date** is the published date when known. Qualifiers **(event)**, **(forum)**, **(Reddit)**, **(article)** denote other dates. **Not verified** means no reliable video upload date was exposed by the video search connector; a year appearing in a title is NOT upload date.
- **Sectorials/factions** come from an explicit report title/description; a broad faction remains broad when the source does not establish a specific sectorial. Some video rows extract only names appearing in titles; these are *leads* for match analysis, not independently verified list-level matchups.
- Forum links go to the relevant thread indexing/reporting the battle where embedded direct video links were not available as text. A single tournament report can discuss 3–5 games.
- Podcast dates are **episode publication** dates and podcast rows usually do not disclose all opponents. Do not use them as game outcome data without checking audio/transcripts.
- Event statistics indexes are listed separately, not falsely described as battle reports. Earlier N4/pre-N5 MiniWarGaming videos are excluded.
