# Cross-report N5 unit effectiveness and combinations (battle-report synthesis)

**Reviewed:** 2026-11-01. **Evidence:** [master index](n5-battle-reports-and-tournament-reports.md), entries #001–#241, and the [numbered tactical dossiers](./). **Purpose:** derive repeatable list-building lessons, not unit win rates or definitive faction tiers.

## Method, scope, and confidence

- This is a **qualitative cross-report synthesis**, not a controlled sample. The 241 index entries are not 241 independent full games: some are multi-game winner recaps, duplicates/re-uploads (#009/#028, #011/#030, #006/#031, #010/#033, #022/#026), repeated event coverage (#161/#237; #169/#170), tutorials, special Team Ops or narrative missions, and unverified footage. #241 has **no verified play-by-play** and supplies **no observed unit performance**.
- Read **[Observed]** as a recorded match action, **[Player assessment]** as a first-hand opinion, and **[Inference]** as a reusable hypothesis. Winning a tournament with a unit is not proof that it caused every win; a unit appearing on the losing side is not proof it is bad. Do not derive win percentages or count duplicate reports as independent corroboration.
- The dossiers span **N5.0–N5.3**, multiple historical Army builds and ITS packets. This document describes **battle-era observations**. Current unit profiles, legal Fireteams, skills and costs come from the repository's `sectorials/` and `rules/` references. Battle reports never override those.
- **Impact test:** (1) did it remove an important enemy Order, Specialist, Lieutenant or blocking ARO; (2) create safe passage / deny a route; (3) score or preserve a scoring action; (4) do so without spending an unsustainable number of Orders? Track those separately from kills.

## Recommendations for applying this to lists

1. For each expensive attacker record a **delivery enabler**, a **specialist left after the attack**, a **defensive/recovery backup**, and the **turn/Orders reserved to score**. A gun with no resulting mission access is a *threat*, not demonstrated value.
2. Prefer **two independent scoring paths** to a single perfect Fireteam. Test the list after one critical piece dies, after its Pitcher is destroyed, and after being forced to go second.
3. Treat **smoke + MSV**, **Pitcher + hacker + Guided**, **TAG + engineer**, and **Fireteam + TinBot** as *dependent* packages, not automatic upgrades; budget all component points and actions and verify exact current legality.
4. Note attractive-but-different roles: **Raoul/Jamie/Banshee** for arrival-angle assassination; **Trinitarian/Strelok/Shrouded** for hidden denial/specialist redundancy; **Asura/Swiss/Hatamoto** for selective elite shooting; **Makaul/Varangian/McMurrough** for vision/assault delivery. Do not rank them by kills alone.
5. When reusing evidence in a sectorial or unit-category page, cite **numbered reports** and clearly label observations versus player opinion versus inference. Revisit when N5.3 rules or mission definitions meaningfully change a combo.
