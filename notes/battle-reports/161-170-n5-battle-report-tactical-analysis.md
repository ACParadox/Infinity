## #161 — Cancon 2026 champion Rob Shepherd: Vanilla Ariadna

[Full Loss of Lieutenant winner interview](https://open.spotify.com/episode/0X4QIoP24cKONtltxClHMH) · [original creator Cancon 2026 after-action report](https://buymeacoffee.com/robertshepherd/infinity-n5-event-report-ariadna-cancon-2026). **N5.2 / ITS17; five rounds; winner Vanilla Ariadna, NOT just TAK.**

- **Observed retrospectively:** Both lists shared **Colonel Voronin, two Streloks, two Tankhunters, a Scout** and about **nine Camo markers**. Streloks' **Decoys were deployed around the DZ Tankhunter ARO markers**, making the opponent guess which of four similar markers was actually a deadly gun. The Streloks also remained competent midfield specialists.
- **Observed:** **Dynamo Paramedic + Dynamo AP Spitfire Duo**, available in Vanilla Ariadna as well as TAK at that time, offered a fast linked smoke/mine-dispensing gunfighter and medical rescue. Cheap **Baggage/Dozer/Visceral** support kept the order economy effective.
- **Player opinion:** He picked **Vanilla Ariadna over TAK** because he did not want to pay for the TAK-specific Courier or larger linked Visceral package; his preferred Tankhunter/Scout/Dynamo toolkit stayed accessible in Vanilla. **Kibervolk Climbing Plus** was misplayed in this broader report; it **did not have Climbing Plus**, so do not repeat that movement claim.
- **Observed:** Against **Ivory's Varuna Cutter**, played **Corporate Appropriation**, he **played around the Cutter and scored the mission** rather than trying an unfavorable anti-TAG gunfight. He explicitly says **Crossing Lines** would have been much tougher since Cutter could dominate shifting zones. Against Nick's **five-Umbra limited-insertion attack**, radial/table distance helped keep the Umbra from collapsing on his Ariadna pieces.
- **Inference:** **Tankhunter hidden ARO + Strelok Decoy screen + Dynamo AP Spitfire/Paramedic** is a proven N5.2 Vanilla Ariadna cross-role shell. Avoid the Cutter by prioritizing **prototypes/mission tasks in Corporate Appropriation**; do not presume that plan handles a Cutter controlling Crossing Lines.
- **Caution:** A practice-game seven-wound Tankhunter attack was a reason to select the faction; it was **not a seven-wound Cancon tournament attack**.

**Tactical lesson:** Use **Camo ambiguity to preserve Tankhunters**, avoid unfavorable TAG duels when the mission permits, and keep Dynamo specialists for late objectives.

## #162 — Cancon 2026 multi-player meta debrief

[Full Loss of Lieutenant Cancon recap](https://open.spotify.com/episode/7JKMUgGOagK439SBWu969I). **N5.2 / ITS17**, multiple hosts' armies and tournament experiences, not a separate winner interview.

- **Observed retrospectively:** One participant's **Vanilla Yu Jing** list featured **Yan Huo HRMC, Rui Shi MSV2, Daoying Lieutenant +1 Order, Gudan, Zhencha hacker, Pangguling EVO, Shaolin, Hac Tao HMG and cheap REM support**, with many Hidden/Camo threats that produced a deceptively short courtesy list.
- **Observed:** Players discussed **Next Wave Blackheart** deleting an opponent's valuable first-turn forward attacker, illustrating why exposing a high-pricepoint model to Blackheart ARO can be a poor trade. Some opponents switched to an **Anaconda** later in the weekend rather than continue presenting the same weakness.
- **Player opinion:** **ITS17 and October updates** made previously ignored **Sensor/Camo control, counter-hacking and forward-deployed mission bodies** more relevant. The panel's “best meta yet” is opinion, not comparative faction win-rate data.
- **Inference:** **Vanilla Yu Jing Yan Huo/Rui Shi ranged package + Hac Tao hidden attack + Zhencha WIP** is a coherent mixed-range tournament construction; maintain different ways to uncover Hidden/marker pieces before exposing expensive active guns.
- **Caution:** Panel is recounting several participants' games, not one named 1v1 matchup. Do **not attribute all list elements to Cancon champion Rob Shepherd** (he played Vanilla Ariadna), or claim a measured YJ vs Next Wave win rate.

**Tactical lesson:** Build **reactive anti-Camo/Blackheart options** and a scoring specialist package rather than a courtesy-list-only alpha plan.

## #163 — Beantown Beatdown 2025: Bakunin winner

[Full MetaChemistry winner interview](https://open.spotify.com/episode/1S2sIe6nlefj4TZlZuII9g) · [Close Range list](https://bit.ly/BeatdownCloseRange) · [Long Range list](https://bit.ly/BeatdownLongRange). **Rob “CobraPrime” wins with Bakunin, N5.2/ITS17**. Missions **Hardlock, Corporate Appropriation, Supremacy, Provisioning, Area of Interest**.

- **Observed retrospectively:** Winner explicitly **did NOT use a hacker-heavy Guided/Gator/Go-Pod stereotype**. He used **melee/CC-centric Bakunin** and selected lists by table firing distances: **long-range Riot Grrl missile, Cenobite Sniper, long hard ARO and TR bot**, or **closer-range Orphans, Chimera/Pupniks, Barspider and warbands**.
- **Observed:** He did retain **a small credible hacker package: Riot Grrl with TinBot and Uhahu**, enough to cover key electronic tasks. A mostly CC list still requires some Mission Specialists and counter-hacking.
- **Observed:** In **Provisioning**, he **Eclipse-smoked Chimera + Pupniks to retrieve the first box and retreat to DZ**, then surrounded the second box with **mines, Suppression and multiple CC interceptors** before collecting it later. The opponent killed substantial Bakunin material yet could not recover the boxes.
- **Observed:** Against Lobo in **Supremacy**, contesting the zone mattered more than eliminating the opponent; the winner says the round was a difficult, possibly decisive match. Reported approximate individual round OP include nine in Hardlock, ten in Corporate Appropriation and seven in Supremacy, but **he admits he did not check the exact numbers**.
- **Inference:** **Chimera/Pupnik Eclipse courier + Cenobite/Riot Grrl ranged ARO + Orphan/Barspider close defenders** can excel on **short-room/zone and box mission boards**. This is a field-tested alternative to the fashionable Bakunin hacking/Guided archetype.
- **Caution:** Near-score arithmetic is retrospective and uncertain; do not invent complete 5-round opposition or exact OP.

**Tactical lesson:** Choose **short versus long ARO version by table geometry**, and use **Eclipse/CC delivery to secure cargo before risking a general firefight**.

## #164 — Kentuckiana Fight Club 2025: Vanilla Haqqislam winner

[Full MetaChemistry winner interview](https://open.spotify.com/episode/0LGgTnDoYFXhEYswjRdjbL) · [Sticky Stuff list](https://bit.ly/KYStickyStuff) · [Pound Town list](https://bit.ly/KYPoundTown). **Fess wins KFC with Vanilla Haqqislam, N5.2/ITS17**.

Winner warns Saladin's extra efficiency can tempt overexposure and suggests **Bashi Bazouk Holomask decoys** for LT protection.
- **Observed:** **Sticky Stuff** used **Ragik and Mukhtar high Classified breadth, jammers/Mutt defensive denial and Repeater projection** for **Akial Interference versus NCA** with **Hexa Hidden Sniper and full Bolt Core**. Winner directly reports **10–2 Haqq** despite not having the straight gunpower to beat the NCA TAG list if it had been chosen.
- **Observed:** In **Area of Interest vs USARF**, the **Maghariba Guard / aggressive support** competed on zone presence; a different round's opponent included **two Shikami**, but precise matchup/round mapping must be verified before a sectorial counter claim. Another reported **8–2** came from the **Pound Town** list.
- **Observed:** **Critical Intervention vs Vanilla Combined** featured **Achilles + Anathematic**. Haqq won a useful deployment/second-turn choice, used **Mutt/Jammer and Daylami screens** and a resilient Maghariba to weather the first elite push. Combined **Anathematic** could hit its WIP button, showing that hard defense must still protect the actual mission terminal.
- **Player opinion:** Stress tests should identify **the one model whose survival is necessary on turn three**—sometimes Mukhtar, Maggie, or a nominally humble specialist—and sacrifice other models to preserve it.
- **Inference:** **Saladin extra-order economy + Mukhtar/Ragik Classifieds + Mutt/Jammer screens**, with a separate **Maghariba/close-range punishment list**, creates robust Haqq mission coverage against Bolt-heavy NCA and elite CA. The speaker never claims it outshoots every TAG.
- **Caution:** Source sometimes pronounces Ragik and Maghariba imperfectly; **10–2** against NCA is explicitly stated, but unconfirmed round scores should not be manufactured.

**Tactical lesson:** Into **Akial, specialist breadth beats superior gunlines**; protect Saladin and your last task-capable worker with deception/Jammer coverage.

## #165 — Arizona Armageddon 2025: Next Wave winner

[Full MetaChemistry winner interview](https://open.spotify.com/episode/55JYmiBqixUiCVljnQLwSn) · [Defensive Dinos](https://bit.ly/DefensiveDinos) · [Achilles Limited Insertion](https://bit.ly/AchillesLE). **Ben/disbensable wins with Next Wave, N5.2/ITS17**. Missions **Crossing Lines, Battleground, Hardlock, Corporate Appropriation, Cutthroat**.

- **Observed retrospectively:** Built **two distinct lists**. **Achilles Limited Insertion** for **Cutthroat and Crossing Lines** included **Achilles + Patroclus/Juicer, Harbingers, a Gearhead Hacker, Puzzlers/Repeaters and Hidden Contrabandolero Killer Hacker**, with strong aggressive Lieutenant-cut potential.
- **Observed:** **Defensive Dinos** had **four Steeljaws split between two groups, Patroclus/Drakios Fireteam supports, two Harbinger/Wraith Duos and Ironside** to **hold Battleground/Hardlock/Corporate Appropriation zones** rather than overreach for kills.
- **Observed:** Against **StarCo in Battleground**, the Dinos moved up and occupied the right zones; an enemy **Anaconda TAG remained durable on a roof** but could not force enough scoring weight into the Next Wave midfield.
- **Observed:** Versus **Military Orders**, a **Sasha E/M grenade threat** made a **Knight of the Sepulchre** Dodge/lose its advantageous Suppression posture, while turrets denied easy movement. This is a **positional threat**, not a documented E/M knockout.
- **Player opinion:** **Patroclus offensive HRL** underperformed expectations; **Blackheart/Skyhound AP Thunderbolt and Stinger** can fill awkward range bands but not all should be bought simultaneously. **Steeljaw Sensor** is useful for protecting valuable TAGs from Hidden Ninjas.
- **Inference:** **Steeljaw/Harbinger zone weight + turret net** can blunt StarCo TAGs and Military Orders elite lists in missions favoring midfield presence, while **Achilles/Puzzler** serves pressure/assassination cases.
- **Caution:** This is a **post-N5.2 Next Wave tournament**, not evidence for launch N5.1 lists.

**Tactical lesson:** Choose **Achilles for kill/cross-board missions** and **Steeljaw sensor/turret saturation for zone scoring**.

## #166 — Burn City Brawl 2025: Tunguska winner

[Full Loss of Lieutenant tournament recap and winner interview](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3bC5DmFnk3eFiNlZhBZBA3). **Val wins with Tunguska**. Published Oct 6 2025, so **pre-Oct-15 N5.2**, most plausibly **N5.1.1 with an ITS16/ITS17 transitional/custom packet**. Five missions: **CUSTOM Hammer of Dawn, B-Pong, Evacuation, Firefight, Capture and Protect**.

- **Observed retrospectively:** One list (“1.0”) emphasized **Mary Problems and Raoul Spector Parachutist, Interventor Hacking Device Plus Lieutenant, Missile bot, Clockmaker, Puppetmaster/Red Fury bots, Zellenkrieger and a forward repeater network**. A second (“2.0”) used **Cyclone, Salamandra Pilot KHD, Securitate, Hollow Men including Hacker/Missile Launcher, Perseus, Interventor, EVO, Ludacat and Meteor Zond** to create thicker defense.
- **Observed:** Against **TAK**, the aggressive list did not initially want to challenge **Veteran Kazaks and Frontoviks in Suppression** with direct guns, so **Spotlight + Guided missiles** broke their resistance. Against **Bakunin**, the second list's **multiple hackers and forward Repeater/defensive screen** made a straightforward Interventor assassination much harder.
- **Observed:** Val picked the **more defensive Cyclone/Salamandra setup** when he wanted board control or feared counter-infiltration, and **Mary Problems/Raoul** when scalpel strikes could take a disproportionate bite. Source confirms **Val first, Ryan Kirby second**, and discusses other top-five lists without making those lists the champion's.
- **Player opinion:** **Repeater “fork” AROs**—forcing opponents into bad hacking versus shooting decisions—are what makes this Tunguska construction work. Restoring REMs/positioning their silhouettes behind cover matters as much as the initial Burst.
- **Inference:** **Mary Problems + Guided/Interventor + Raoul** is useful into **TAK exposed Suppression/HI**, whereas **Hollow Men/Cyclone hacker network** can challenge **Bakunin pitching/hacking pressure**. Both are source-era unit combos, not guaranteed faction counters.
- **Caution:** The episode explicitly references **ITS16-to-17 Evacuation changes**, so do NOT assume the local Evacuation followed later ITS17 wording; the episode's post-event discussion of subsequent profile changes is not proof the tournament used them.

**Tactical lesson:** Against heavily armored suppression, choose **Spotlight/Guided and separate parachutist scalpel** instead of sinking all Orders into normal gun duels.

## #167 — Krug 2025: Shindenbutai vs Imperial Service finalist debrief

[Full MetaChemistry participants' recap](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4ynlj3tX1cuiGPakiYuFly). Event **August 2025, N5.1.1/ITS16.5-era**; published September 22 **after** the competition. **Azoka's Shindenbutai vs Tim Chainsaw's Imperial Service** is a separately streamed final-table match; **the overall Krug champion was Derrek's Torchlight Brigade**. Do not conflate.

- **Observed retrospectively:** ISS player cites a historical **Sensei Counterintelligence upgrade** and squad-transfer order flexibility as a major reason he can launch multiple **waves of specialized gun/specialist pieces**. **Wu Ming Duo and ISS-exclusive Koga Ninja/Ken Fang-style tools** were specifically valued over what Vanilla Yu Jing offers.
- **Observed:** Shindenbutai fielded **two Kyogen Killers**. One advanced into **Adil's PARA close-combat ARO** and was immobilized, demonstrating a strong **Adil anti-CC screen**; the second crossed another part of the board and removed an important ISS Hacker.
- **Player assessment:** The Shindenbutai player needed to **remove Adil before sending successive Ninja/CC assassins into his protected zone**. ISS player says positioning even a cheap **Flash Pulse bot** to cover the route forced awkward Japanese Orders.
- **Inference:** **ISS Adil/PARA + Sensei order economy + Wú Míng/Camo specialists** can disrupt a **Shindenbutai Kyogen close-combat dive**, but **Kyogen's alternate lateral approach can reach the hacker** when Adil holds only one lane. This is a specific matchup pattern rather than a universal result.
- **Caution:** Source podcast is a recap by the two participants and does **not confirm a safe numeric match OP**. [Contemporaneous Krug footage and top-three source](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/n5-tournament-krug-2025.43602/) establish overall winner and pre-September date.

**Tactical lesson:** In Shindenbutai versus ISS, **clear Adil's PARA zone before launching Kyogen**, or send the second assassin around a different lane to kill the Hacker.

## #168 — Krug 2025: Torchlight Brigade overall winner

[Full MetaChemistry winner interview](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2sPL2OXtP91LsdDy7c7koC). **Derrek Misderrektion wins with Torchlight Brigade**, August 2025 **N5.1.1/ITS16.5-era**, NOT N5.2 nor automatically ITS17 based on Sept podcast.

- **Observed retrospectively:** His two list archetypes were **Headhunter**, with **Kappa Paramedic, Wave Rider hacker, SilverStar Rover and other reliable close attack/utility**, and **Stormtrooper**, with **Raveneye Lieutenant, Lambda Engineer/Yudbot, Vidoc, Wave Rider and high-Burst guns**. Different missions got different lists, rather than two near-identical all-comers lists.
- **Observed:** In **Supremacy versus TAK (round 4)**, **Nightshade Hidden Deployment** added unexpected zone weight; **Strelok Camo/Decoy and TAK Mimetism** made direct shooting unusually difficult. The winner used a **reactive mine/cyber solution plus hidden zone reinforcement** instead of committing to a questionable rush.
- **Observed:** In **Unmasking round 2**, an international opponent fielded a full set of threats; the speaker credits maintaining terminal access and protecting critical mobile Specialist WIP over pure damage. **Round 5** was against the Shasvastii specialist Nazeen, a hard high-level final pairing.
- **Player opinion:** **SilverStar Rover + hacker/doctor coverage** made mission adjustments easy; he prefers **head-hunting objective specialists or attacking from unexpected lane** where an opponent's premium sniper/hacker net cannot fully cover every button.
- **Inference:** **Torchlight's Raveneye/Wave Rider/hidden Nightshade and Rover mission utility** can be good into **TAK Camo zone control in Supremacy**, but the win involved fortunate dice and **not** universal Torchlight > TAK success.
- **Caution:** The transcript calls multiple game states “statistically anomalous” and discusses alternative list choices; don't turn reported four major wins into fabricated OP per round.

**Tactical lesson:** Hidden Deployment is not merely for killing; **save Nightshade for a final Supremacy zone swing** when Camo makes active fights unfavorable.

## #169 — Borderbash 2025: Vanilla PanOceania winner

[Full Loss of Lieutenant winner interview](https://open.spotify.com/episode/5OBRqMPLp9BBw6yYf13zhm). **Jordan wins with Vanilla PanO at Borderbash June 2025, N5.1.1/ITS16.5**; [contemporary forum results](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/n5-tournament-borderbash-2025.43529/) summarize a **5-game, 25 TP / 44 OP** first place.

- **Observed retrospectively:** One published list contained **Cutter**, but winner says the list **actually played in all five games** was his **Joan of Arc V2 Lieutenant AP Spitfire + two Helot Militiamen FTO + Swiss Guard HMG in Hidden Deployment**, backed by **Fugazi, Hacking, Fennec and other Specialists**. Do NOT record this as five Cutter wins.
- **Observed:** **Joan's Inspiring Leadership and Helot repositioning** generated substantial ARO coverage while **Swiss Guard BS15 Mimetism-6 HMG** emerged to remove opponents' key ranged assets. Host specifically praises **strong active Mimetism gun versus conventional unmodified HMG**.
- **Observed retrospectively:** Matchups discussed include **Vanilla Ariadna early, JSA Oban Mindwipe, Starmada, Morats and White Banner Armory**. Against **Oban**, a dangerous Japanese close-range Ninja/CC attack required careful spacing; in **Armory vs White Banner** the winner had a practical defensive setup for the room while the rival needed a dependable counter-Mimetism/Guided plan.
- **Player opinion:** **Swiss Guard HMG** was underappreciated at launch N5 and deserves respect; **Helot Sensor/FTO** is good for Camo identification and angle denial. The author cautions that a **Cutter-heavy list** might have been correct for an opponent vulnerable to Mimetism but was not viable for every cramped mission.
- **Inference:** **Joan V2 + Helots + Swiss Guard HMG** is a documented winning **Vanilla PanO** all-comer package versus mixed **JSA/White Banner/other faction** objectives, with hidden shooter and flexible sector coverage.
- **Caution:** The June 2025 Army lists and order/link legality are historical. Do not attribute tournament results to the unplayed Cutter list.

**Tactical lesson:** Combine **multi-lane Helot AROs, Joan's extra/order flexibility and a hidden Swiss Guard attacker** while maintaining real specialists for room/button missions.

**Tactical lesson:** For Mindwipe, identify and **protect two separate server-killing methods** so an opponent cannot win by deleting one Hacker and one Anti-Materiel model.
