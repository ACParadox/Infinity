## #071 — Corregidor Surface vs Morat Aggression Force: Ghost in the Feed

**Video:** [GMG x Tactical Awareness, Ground Corregidor vs Morats](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TPI9RSi4HmQ). **Mission:** CUSTOM *The Monday Signal / Monday Transmissions* **Ghost in the Feed**, written by Robert Shepherd, with **three consoles that reveal three opponent-side clues/boxes**. Only **Doctor, Paramedic, Chain of Command, Forward Observer, Hacker or Engineer** can activate them; generic Specialist Operative/“specialist” status is *not automatically enough*. **Reported result: Corregidor 6–0 Morats**.

The final win came from **one retrieved clue, additional consoles, protected HVT and Classified/telemetry scoring**, not eliminating every Morat.
- **[Observed]** Morats spent approximately **ten Orders** attempting to disable/kill the **Gator** according to the player, without generating a comparable scoring advantage. Its **survivability and continued hacking vulnerability** tied up attacks that could have gone into console control.
- **[Observed]** The Morat army included several units that were **Specialist Troops in the broad sense** but *lacked the scenario's narrow qualifying skill list*. At the end the player explicitly said **he could barely play the mission**, having insufficient eligible console activators. Morat **Dartok/Hacker** could still attempt a late button, but was blocked by the Corregidor reaction network.
- **[Inference]** **Gator + protected Jazz + multiple true FO/Paramedic/Hacker bodies** is excellent **CUSTOM Ghost in the Feed tech**: the Gator distracts the rival while eligible cheap models collect the only scoring items. This does **not** mean Corregidor generally counters Morats.
- **[Player assessment]** The Morat player said his list would have suited a more conventional **direct fight or table-edge assault mission**, not this narrow **console-skill-gated** mission.
- **[Caution]** The presenters dispute Fireteam/hacker/peripheral declarations. Do not use their first interpretation of **Possessed**, Firewall or who qualifies for a *custom* console as current N5.3 law.

**Actionable:** read the entire **scenario's exact Specialist whitelist before list-building**, then carry **two or more eligible, independently positioned buttons** instead of betting on your TAG's survival.
