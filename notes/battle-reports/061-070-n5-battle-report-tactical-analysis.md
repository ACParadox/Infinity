## #061 — Haqqislam vs Bakunin, Battleground

**Sources:** [MiniWarGaming N5 episode 2](https://www.miniwargaming.com/content/we-have-our-first-guest-haqqislam-vs-nomads-infinity-n5-battle-report-ep-2) · [full original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WWezK8sRLM0). **Published Jan 8, 2025; initial N5.** Jordan played **Vanilla Haqqislam**, Luka **Bakunin**, not Vanilla Nomads. **Battleground** contested three strips; narrator expressly called it an **N4 mission used with N5**. Haqqislam was positioned to control all strips, but a final certified score is not established.


### Army lists and Fireteams — complete deployment and closing-showcase reconstruction

**Video:** [Vanilla Haqqislam vs Bakunin, Battleground](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WWezK8sRLM0). The opening deployment **and end-of-video army showcase** identify most models. Correct an important first-impression ambiguity: **Jordan calls this “300 points of Ramah Taskforce” in the closing showcase**, whereas the opening calls his force simply Haqqislam and the pre-existing analysis labels it vanilla. The unit package strongly indicates **Ramah Taskforce**; record the **presenter's explicit sectorial identification**, but do not silently overwrite the earlier classification without a period Army code.

| Player | Fireteam | Members | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ramah Taskforce / Haqqislam (Jordan)** | **Khawarij/Tarik Haris — 3** | **Tarik Mansuri AP Spitfire + Khawarij NCO Mk12/MSV2 + Khawarij Paramedic Boarding Shotgun** | **Explicit opening deployment**: “Haris of Tarik Mansuri leading two Khawarij,” and the closing list names their profiles. A later NCO Lieutenant-Order activation is **the same** three-model team. |
| Haqqislam | **Maghariba/Leila Duo — 2** | **Maghariba Guard TAG HMG / double Heavy Flamethrower / Mine Dispenser + Leila Sharif Hacker** | **Explicit opening deployment and linked movement**; the Maghariba's Tactical Awareness Order moves its Duo. |
| **Bakunin (Luca)** | **No confirmed starting Fireteam** | **Reverend Custodier Lieutenant**, 2 Reverend Moiras (Combi and MULTI Sniper), Reverend Healer, 2 Cenobites (Chain Rifle and Light Shotgun), Sin-Eater, Penitent Red Fury/E/M Grenade Launcher, Initiate of the Observance SMG/Repeater, **3 Zeros** in a second Combat Group | **Full closing showcase confirms troops**, but neither deployment nor full list identifies a constituted Core/Haris/Duo. Nearby Reverend models are **not enough to claim a four-member Core**. |

**Other Haqqislam:** **2 Naffatûn** (Heavy Flamethrower and Light Flamethrower/Grenades), **Nahab Aeromobile Red Fury/Infiltration** with Decoy markers, **Ghulam Rifle**, **Kameel EVO REM**. The Nahab's three decoys **represent one model**, not three fighters.

**Large-team census:** **No confirmed four- or five-model Fireteam**; two named Haqqislam small teams and no declared Bakunin Fireteam. **Data inconsistency:** original analysis says *vanilla Haqqislam*, while the speaker's closing Army Showcase specifically says *Ramah Taskforce*; keep this discrepancy visible pending official list code. - **[Observed]** A **Maghariba Guard HMG, dual Heavy Flamethrowers and Mine Dispenser** took long/short range fights and penetrated the Bakunin backline. **Nahab Red Fury** provided a second hidden/flanking attack vector. The TAG gained far more value from clearing **Doctor/recovery support and scoring routes** than from winning a single frontal firefight.
- **[Observed]** Bakunin relied on **Reverend Healer + Cenobite + Zero Camo**, plus **Penitent** and reactive Mimetism. The **Healer revived both Cenobite and Zero** in an exceptional memorable turn. When the Maghariba subsequently eliminated her, the flank lost its recovery plan.
- **[Inference]** **Maghariba + Nahab** can force Bakunin to defend *open firelanes and rear specialist paths simultaneously*. Bakunin's recovered **Zero/Cenobite** might still contest strips if the Healer has cover and Orders.
- **[Caution]** Beginner game; template and old Battleground zone wording need the original period packet, not ITS18. Do not invent a verified “Haqq 10–0”.

**Mission application:** protect the **last sector-counting Camo/specialist** against a TAG reaching the rear, rather than merely protecting a sniper.
