## #061 — Haqqislam vs Bakunin, Battleground

**Sources:** [MiniWarGaming N5 episode 2](https://www.miniwargaming.com/content/we-have-our-first-guest-haqqislam-vs-nomads-infinity-n5-battle-report-ep-2) · [full original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WWezK8sRLM0). **Published Jan 8, 2025; initial N5.** Jordan played **Vanilla Haqqislam**, Luka **Bakunin**, not Vanilla Nomads. **Battleground** contested three strips; narrator expressly called it an **N4 mission used with N5**. Haqqislam was positioned to control all strips, but a final certified score is not established.

- **[Observed]** A **Maghariba Guard HMG, dual Heavy Flamethrowers and Mine Dispenser** took long/short range fights and penetrated the Bakunin backline. **Nahab Red Fury** provided a second hidden/flanking attack vector. The TAG gained far more value from clearing **Doctor/recovery support and scoring routes** than from winning a single frontal firefight.
- **[Observed]** Bakunin relied on **Reverend Healer + Cenobite + Zero Camo**, plus **Penitent** and reactive Mimetism. The **Healer revived both Cenobite and Zero** in an exceptional memorable turn. When the Maghariba subsequently eliminated her, the flank lost its recovery plan.
- **[Inference]** **Maghariba + Nahab** can force Bakunin to defend *open firelanes and rear specialist paths simultaneously*. Bakunin's recovered **Zero/Cenobite** might still contest strips if the Healer has cover and Orders.
- **[Caution]** Beginner game; template and old Battleground zone wording need the original period packet, not ITS18. Do not invent a verified “Haqq 10–0”.

**Mission application:** protect the **last sector-counting Camo/specialist** against a TAG reaching the rear, rather than merely protecting a sniper.

## #062 — Morats vs Bakunin, 200-point Domination

**Sources:** [MiniWarGaming episode 1](https://www.miniwargaming.com/content/baby-steps-morat-aggression-force-vs-nomads-infinity-n5-battle-report-ep-1) · [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gq6Ei35w1qY). **Published Dec 30, 2024**, initial N5, **200-point teaching match**. Actual factions **Morat Aggression Force vs Bakunin**; mission **Domination** (four quadrants and consoles). Morat shooter positions heavily constrained Bakunin; exact final score not confirmed.

- **[Observed]** **Yaogat MULTI Sniper + Spitfire**, **Vanguard HMG/Paramedic Fireteam**, **Kaitok Flammenspeer/Chain Rifle**, and **Raptor/Kyosot assault** gave Morats several firing angles. The high-placed **Yaogat Sniper** locked down Bakunin routes and consumed movement Orders.
- **[Observed]** Bakunin included **Reverend Moira Sniper/MULTI Rifle, Reverend Healer, a Killer Hacker, Penitent Red Fury/E/M, Sin-Eater Neurocinetics Sniper and Initiate deployable Repeaters**. The player explicitly regretted using Repeaters with **only narrow Killer Hacking payoff** against a relatively non-Hackable Morat enemy.
- **[Player view]** The Bakunin player called the **Sin-Eater/Penitent concentration** too expensive/fragile at 200 points and wanted more independent pieces, better support Hacking and ways to escape the Yaogat lanes.
- **[Inference]** **Morat Yaogat + Vanguard + Kaitok** fits a small four-quadrant shooting/control scenario well when the opponent's hacker grid has few eligible targets. This says **nothing** about 300-point Morats vs Bakunin generally.
- **[Caution]** Both players were returning after years; they consulted rules for **NCO, Fireteams, mission wording** live. Not a source for current rule legality.

**Mission application:** at 200 points, count **different console actors and alternative approaches** before buying a second expensive reactive sniper.

## #063 — Shasvastii vs OSS, Domination slow-grow league

**Original written eyewitness report:** [The Responsible One, Jan 29, 2025](https://the-responsible-one.blogspot.com/2025/01/infinity-n5-slow-grow-league-battle.html). **Initial N5. Confirmed Shasvastii 7–3 Operations Subsection**, with the author noting that the final OSS Doctor/button action could have reduced the margin to **6–5**.

- **[Observed]** Shasvastii's **Mentor Lieutenant, Med-Tech, Ikadron and Q-Drone Thunderbolt** were a protected command/ARO-recovery package. **Nox + Oktavia Grimsdottir + Aida Swanson** formed the attack link; **Shrouded Minelayer, Malignos Hacker and Taigha** covered midfield and close threats.
- **[Observed]** **Malignos first activated a console and re-Camouflaged** rather than remaining exposed; **Q-Drone** stopped a Deva Hacker. The Taigha's **Berserk attempt against Apsara** failed, illustrating limits of expendable warband pressure.
- **[Observed]** OSS **Dakini HMG + Trisha N33 Engineer + Deva Hacker** spent Orders repairing and reactivating a Dakini that lost another firefight. **Oktavia's rocket killed Trisha**, removing the recovery route.
- **[Observed]** OSS **Posthuman Proxy HMG eliminated Q-Drone** and Apsara exploited the opened lane, shot **Shrouded/Nox Hacker** and broke the Fireteam. **Med-Tech revived Q-Drone**; **Oktavia killed Apsara and the OSS Lieutenant**.
- **[Observed]** OSS **Proxy Doctor** reached a final console but **failed WIP and died to Dogged Oktavia**. This turned a close tactical contest into **7–3**, with the Shas author explicitly crediting favorable saves/Mimetism.
- **[Inference]** **Malignos safe console access + recoverable Q-Drone + Oktavia targeting Engineer/LT** worked here. OSS **Proxy HMG + Trisha repair** was a viable counter until support/LT disappeared.

**Mission application:** keep a backup console body and Lt-safe Order plan once the **Q-Drone/Trisha/Engineer** exchange begins.

## #064 — TAK vs Vanilla Combined, ITS17 Hardlock

**Sources:** [Robert Shepherd's Nov 6, 2025 report](https://buymeacoffee.com/robertshepherd/infinity-n5-battle-report-tartary-army-corps-vs-combined-army) · [embedded original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kgcdvpwZgU0). **N5.2/ITS17**, training for CanCon January 2026. **Final result: 5–5 DRAW**, despite TAK enjoying a large attrition lead.

- **[Player view]** TAK tested an extreme **Vystrel/Camo/Courier/Strelok/Scout-K9** shell, overinvesting in some pieces versus a more balanced **Frontovik sniper** and alternative scorers. **Vystrel layered defense** was difficult even for a **Charontid AP HMG with Daturazi Smoke** to clear.
- **[Observed]** Combined used **Charontid + Anathematic double Aspects, Sartroid Ranters, Puzzlers, cheap Warbands** and a projected **Deployable Repeater**. The **Repeater threat was weaker into non-Hackable TAK** than it would be versus Heavy Infantry: don't credit hypothetical successes.
- **[Observed]** TAK obtained early **beacon/HVT pressure** and inflicted serious damage, while the Combined player spent many Orders forcing a path past Vystrel fire. **Hardlock rewarded final buttons heavily**, so expensive losses did not eliminate the Combined scoring route.
- **[Observed]** The **Anathematic Hacker**, with **Camo, WIP16 and final-turn Orders**, crossed a route after eliminating/displacing a Strelok and **flipped a button on the last Order**, producing **5–5**.
- **[Player view]** Shepherd says **keeping one Strelok in HVT control rather than advancing it** would have secured a **6–5 TAK win**; speculative attacks on Anathematic were an alternative high-variance possibility.
- **[Inference]** TAK **Camo/Vystrel** can stall a costly Combined Aspect, but **Hardlock mission control and last-turn WIP** make **Anathematic** more important than the attrition tally.

**Mission application:** defend **last-turn WIP16 Anathematic paths** and the **HVT/control body** even while winning gunfights.

## #065 — White Banner vs PanO, Superiority

**Original:** [White Banner vs PanO full narrated video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gyg2ESiXC7Q). Reddit shared Mar 22, 2026; **N5.2 probable but played date unverified**. **White Banner vs Vanilla PanO-like force** featuring **Joan of Arc, Tikbalang, Swiss Guard, Banshee**; no verified PanO sectorial Army code. **White Banner narrates a win**, no certified score. Automatic translation is exceptionally poor.

- **[Observed]** **Liang Kai + Shaolin Smoke** supported mobile close engagement, while **Jujak + Hwarang/Haetae** supported **Disco Baller, HRL, Repeater and Fireteam control** to approach PanO guns without always fighting through their favored LoF.
- **[Observed]** The experimental **Dokkaebi Marksman + Yin-She Albedo Sniper + Engineer** provided a contrasting *Albedo/range/repair* axis. The player explicitly praises **Yin-She** for defeating **Swiss Guard, Tikbalang and sniper** threats by choosing suitable range bands.
- **[Observed]** **Joan's order advantage + Tikbalang, Swiss Guard Mimetism/Hidden Deployment, Banshee arrival** pressured White Banner's shooting teams. The **Banshee** damaged Fireteam/support from a flank and almost reversed the momentum.
- **[Observed]** **War Driver Total Control** temporarily affected a **Tikbalang**, then opposing War Driver action reversed control. **Both War Drivers came from a local event EXTRA**, not automatically available in ordinary sectorial rosters.
- **[Inference]** **Smoke + Albedo + Disco** is a legitimate **conditional counter configuration** to PanO visor/ARO-heavy formations, especially when a **surviving Engineer** can press Superiority antennas afterward.
- **[Caution]** Do not treat the local free-War-Driver interaction as normal list access, nor translate uncertain proper names into confidently legal mixed Fireteams.

**Mission application:** win **Superiority antenna/zone timing** after the Albedo gun has cleared the necessary lane.

## #066 — Morats vs Svalarheima, Provisioning

**Original:** [GMG Infinity x Tactical Awareness video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sflHHXvgtfI) · [publication archive dated Aug 13, 2026](https://www.patreon.com/cw/guerrillaminiaturegames/sitemap). **Late N5.2 + May Army / ITS17**. **Provisioning** uses central supplies/boxes and designated corner delivery. **Confirmed Svalarheima 10–0 Morats.**

- **[Observed]** Morats used **Bultrak HRMC NCO TAG, Daturazi Smoke, Zerat Hacker and Marksman, Suryat HMG/launcher Duo and EVO**. This enabled attacks but concentrated points in large fighters rather than the fast delivery infrastructure required by the mission.
- **[Observed]** Svalarheima employed **Oktavia + Fusilier/Paramedic Fireteam**, **Karhu Feuerbach + Karhu Paramedic**, **Varangians**, **Liang Kai**, **Peacemaker/Auxbot, Thunderbolt TR REM and Engineer**. The combination created **multiple carriers, firing lanes, and recovery routes**.
- **[Observed]** **Karhu/Paramedic and Fusilier/Oktavia** threatened the box routes while **Peacemaker templates and TR REM** kept an evacuation corner dangerous. A **Liang Kai flank/CC attempt** further diversified the attack.
- **[Observed]** The Morat **Bultrak** could kill things but could not replace **delivering the supplies** after lost or exposed specialists; the commentators total **10–0** and discuss the Interplanetario preview/ITS17-season end.
- **[Inference]** Svalarheima's **specialist redundancy and zone control** can be very strong against a Morat high-cost **TAG+Suryat** setup **in Provisioning**, not necessarily in Annihilation/Frontline.
- **[Caution]** A Tactical Support card/local scenario extra was used. Historical carrying rules and order interactions need exact event packet.

**Mission application:** prioritize **box carrier, delivery corner, covering ARO and specialist backup** over an expensive TAG's damage ceiling.

## #067 — TAK vs Ikari, Superiority

**Original:** [Tabletop Throwdown streamed game](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Laisi3IThYc). Stream mentions recently completed Krug but not play day; **probably ITS17-era**, exact N5.1.1 vs N5.2 unresolved. **Confirmed TAK 3–2 Ikari**.

- **[Observed]** TAK **Scout Minelayer (2) E/M Mines, Strelok FO, Baruk Decoys, Vasily LT and Decoys, Vystrels, Tankhunter Portable Autocannon, Courier and Dynamo** created a large *Camo/Decoy shell*. Commentators count about **13 apparent markers**, requiring many real Discover decisions.
- **[Observed]** Ikari included **Wu Ming, Tanko, Brawler, Yojimbo and heavy reactive REMs/Alpha Seed**. **Yojimbo** pressured the TAK side/rear, but the firepower/zone trade repeatedly exposed Ikari to **Tankhunter/indirect Speculative fire**.
- **[Observed]** Some TAK shots cost **three or four Orders per kill**, illustrating that a valid indirect counter is not always an efficient one. Camo/Decoy still forced Ikari to waste its own Orders finding actual targets.
- **[Observed]** Last-turn **quadrant point-weight and antenna/button actions** gave **TAK 3–2**; one or two failed Ikari Dodges/console rolls could have changed the result.
- **[Player view]** The panel considers **Vasily vs Voronin** and possible **Yojimbo/Dynamo linking**, but those are *unplayed future variants*; don't treat them as evidence of a successful combo here.
- **[Inference]** TAK's **Vystrel/Camo/FO/Tankhunter** package can screen quadrants while scoring from infiltrators, but **Ikari Yojimbo + Wu Ming/Brawler** can still swing a one-point Superiority match.

**Mission application:** use Camo to *protect real quadrant weight*, then reserve Orders for the final console; this was a **3–2**, not a rout.

## #068 — Invincible Army vs Svalarheima, Evacuation

**Original:** [Champion of Terra full video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jpf2mWB7xjE). **Actual N5 patch/played day unknown.** Explicit **Evacuation**: four civilians per half, enemy HVTs, CivVac actions, evacuation terminal and secret tasks. **The auto-translated closing score conflicts** (“five vs four,” then “4–1”); don't publish a definitive winner/score without original scoreboard.

- **[Observed]** Svalarheima had **Nisse sniper/Doctor, Karhu Feuerbach/Paramedic, Locust Killer Hacker, Liang Kai, Fusiliers and Fugazi REMs**. That gives **long-range removal + specialist CivVac + repeaters/hacker disruption**.
- **[Observed]** Invincible Army employed **durable HI and Camo/Libertos/Mine pressure**, forcing the PanO player to **Discover or Deactivate** dangerous approaches before extracting objectives.
- **[Observed]** A **Locust/Engineer/Deactivator** route interacted with deployed Repeaters and Mine danger. Valuable Orders spent clearing hackers instead of moving CivVac targets cost real mission tempo; **Liang Kai Super-Jump** can access a defender but is not a substitute for the *correct specialist skill*.
- **[Inference]** **Karhu/Nisse defense + independent fast specialist** is plausible Evacuation tech into HI-heavy IA, yet the sample's questionable final score, luck and exact unit profiles cannot support an unconditional matchup advantage.
- **[Caution]** The player says 5 and 4 in one count and 4–1 moments later; treat the source as **tactical only** until a human-checked result is obtained.

**Mission application:** **carry CivVac-qualified bodies and clear a path to the extraction terminal**, not only superior guns.

## #069 — CUSTOM Assassins: Svalarheima vs Oban

**Sources:** [Champion of Terra video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hshtP521nn4) · [creator's forum lists played date Mar 5, 2026](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/cot-battle-reports.43148/). **N5.2-era**, but explicitly **CUSTOM “Assassins”**, NOT an ITS scenario. Four HVTs per side can be **attacked as enemy troops**, with special placement/defensive rules. **Reported Svalarheima 10–1 Oban**; not comparable to standard ITS HVT play.

- **[Observed]** Svalarheima's **Jotum, Karhu and Liang Kai** generated heavy firepower and **mobile close-combat HVT killing**. **Liang Kai Super-Jump → CC into target HVT** was mission scoring *only because the custom packet made HVTs hostile*.
- **[Observed]** Oban **Yojimbo and Shakami Eclipse**, **Hidden Beast Hunter Panzerfaust**, Camo attackers and short-range CC pressure tried to bypass PanO guns. **Yojimbo Isolation** hampered tempo; a concealed **Beast Hunter Panzerfaust** altered a Smoke/Eclipse route.
- **[Observed]** Svalarheima's late mobile models scored repeatedly against those special hostile targets, producing the reported **10–1**.
- **[Inference]** **Liang Kai mobility + strong PanO ranged covering pieces** fits a **custom HVT-assassination** objective, while **Yojimbo/Eclipse/Camo** might cover approach; no standard ITS sectorial advantage follows.
- **[Caution]** A separate [participant account notes filming and rules mistakes](https://krank.ee/infinity-battle-report/). Never infer that official neutral ITS civilians are legal attack targets from this video.

**Mission application:** isolate this outcome as **custom-mission data**, not a normal Oban/Svalarheima win rate.

## #070 — Ariadna vs Shindenbutai, mission unverified

**Original:** [Infinity Gamer N5 video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bt6VRjpnJUk). **Played date, patch, scenario and numeric result unverified.** Auto-translation starts in the Impetuous phase and does not preserve a trustworthy opening mission explanation. **Voronin + Margot + Dynamo** suggests **Vanilla Ariadna**, opposed by **Shindenbutai**.

- **[Observed]** Ariadna **Voronin order support + Dynamo motorcycle + Margot grenade launcher/Suppression + Smoke/warband** attacked the Japanese defense from different bearings. Smoke placement blocked a dangerous **MSV/long-range lane** while another body approached.
- **[Observed]** Shindenbutai **Raiden surprise Rocket Launcher, Hatamoto, Tanuki linked guns, Kyojin-style flame/E/M fighter and deployable mines/Koalas** punished direct approaches. One **Raiden launcher** removed an early attacking body, while a **template/mine counterattack** forced bad saving-roll choices.
- **[Observed]** A **Dynamo tried an exposed flank under Mine/ARO danger** and used several Orders with Dodge/counterfire decisions. A later **D-Charge Sabotage** interaction with scenery completed a Classified/task; that is more durable mission evidence than the player's guesses about exact Mine trigger rules.
- **[Observed]** Late **Margot grenade and Suppression** restricted the remaining route. The other player declared only around **four Regular Orders** and no realistic way to score; they stopped without a verified full scoreboard.
- **[Inference]** **Voronin/Dynamo/Margot** can create three incompatible routes for Shindenbutai to defend, but **Raiden/Hidden/Camo + Hatamoto** may hold the lanes if the attacking bodies lack safe Smoke/specialist follow-through.
- **[Caution]** “Communications team,” “Kyoen Killer” and exact rules calculations are garbled; no reliable exact Fireteam composition or mission card is available.

**Mission application:** value **Sabotage/scorer access, Smoke timing and multiple lanes**, not simply a cavalry rush or a declared faction-wide advantage.
