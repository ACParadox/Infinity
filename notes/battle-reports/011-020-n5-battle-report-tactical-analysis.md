## #011 — MEGA 2025: Varuna Immediate Reaction Division, three rounds

**Source:** [Infinity the Dante: Varuna Tournament Report — MEGA 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QaAXC5Y332E) ([forum entry](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/n5-mega-2025-tournament.43763/)). **Rules:** N5.2; ITS17-era. **Missions:** Critical Intervention, Corporate Appropriation, Akial Interference. **Opponents:** Steel Phalanx, Invincible Army, Bakunin. **Result:** narrator **5th of approximately 21–22**, with a reported win against Steel Phalanx (roughly 9–1), a close win over Invincible Army (roughly 6–3), and a loss to Bakunin (roughly 8–4). **Rough scores are recalled by speaker, not an official database export.**

### Units / packages and why

- **[Player view]** Helots + Patsy Garnett command Core: a Red Fury/SMG Helot pair with Patsy converts otherwise Irregular utility into Regular link bodies under the then-current list construction. This buys *useful Orders plus AROs* rather than a pure line-infantry tax. Check N5.3 current Fireteam chart before reusing.
- **[Observed]** Camo MSV1 Heavy Rocket Launcher + two Knights of Montesa Haris adds Discover/ARO reach to mobile Knights, with the Knights covering close-range and positional fights. The reporter views this as a flexible attacking/defensive package for missions that force movement; the unit's value is not just its standalone launcher.
- **[Observed]** Squalos / ORC HMG + Pathfinder/Sensor REM + specialists in alternative list shapes give Varuna attack plus mission operations. The player repeatedly relies on speed/second route for moving beacons or reaching HVTs, not just a premium BS weapon.
- **[Observed]** Steel Phalanx opponent fielded Agêma sniper threats, Myrmidons/Hippolyta and a smoke-assisted midfield push. Varuna's layered Helot/Knights/visor AROs pressured that approach and forced slow Cautious Moves. [Inference] Varuna can punish compact assault links with multiple non-overlapping ranged responses *when the table offers real firelanes*. Not proof Steel Phalanx generally loses to Varuna.
- **[Observed]** Against Invincible Army, an expensive Yan Huo/Tai Sheng-style linked attack and specialist support were capable of fighting but had difficulty completing Corporate objectives once Varuna moved an HVT/objective to a safer side. The player calls the opponent new to tournaments and says the list was partly collection-limited; don't generalize.
- **[Observed]** Against Bakunin, Penitent/Observants, Chimera/Uberfall, Fiddler and Riot Grrls provided distributed scoring options, outpacing the Varuna list in a Classified-heavy Akial game.

### Missions / tactical takeaways

- **[Player view]** Critical Intervention: defensive second turn was good for this particular build (presenter says his practiced second-turn games were successful); do not universalize to every list. Preserve a response to Hippolyta or smoke attackers who can move behind sightlines.
- **[Player view]** Corporate Appropriation: once the opponent relocates the objective to a remote safe corner, Varuna's plan needs a truly fast carrier / airborne option (motorized Montesa, Echo-Bravo, Guided support considered), not merely a midrange HMG.
- **[Observed]** Akial Interference: a turn with no Classified scored is punishing; the narrator says a risky Helot suicide for a Classified was probably worse than retaining that specialist for a later opportunity. Bakunin won on superior task efficiency.

**Portable insight:** field **two lines of attack** (guns and actual long-range objective movement). Varuna's strong reactive shell doesn't itself answer a relocated objective.

## #012 — Road to Talvisota Satellite 2025: Svalarheima Winter Force

**Sources:** [Champion of Terra preparation Part 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uf2lN1TWC4M), [actual five-game recap Part 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HMz3Nnpi4E8), [Corvus Belli forum index and top-list pointer](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/n5-tournament-road-to-talvisota-satellite.43428/). **Played:** Mar 15–16, 2025, Helsinki. **Rules:** **initial N5 / ITS16**, before April rules changes. **Packet:** Biotechvore/“Tech-War”, Unmasking, Supplies, Frostbyte, custom Firefight.

### Units / packages and use

- **[Player view]** Nisse HMG + Uma Sørensen mobile Duo: HMG opens difficult lanes while Uma supplies close combat, specialist/mission access and aggressive follow-through. A second Nisse Sniper + WinterFor Engineer/turret package covers longer reactive lanes or repairs assets. Author dropped an older Fusilier Core in favor of two attacking teams.
- **[Player view]** Liang Kai / Shang Ji / Knight-of-Justice-style flexible threats were chosen for close-range objective attack, smoke/approach alternatives and a Biotechvore-specific escape list. These are the speaker's historical roster choices, not necessarily recommended current WinterFor N5.3 profiles.
- **[Observed]** Round 1 Biotechvore vs Kosmoflot: after advancing into the safe zone, the player left Liang Kai exposed; Kosmoflot capitalized and defeated him. Result narrated 1–9. The important mistake is leaving the *fast attack and mission body* visible after completing the obligatory advance.
- **[Observed]** Round 2 Unmasking vs Morat: relatively dense terrain and fast HVT/console play produced a close back-and-forth game, ending about 3–7 against WinterFor. [Inference] one successful early HVT kill does not compensate for failing to protect the other HVTs and future console access.
- **[Observed]** Round 3 Supplies vs Morat: Uma/Nisse took a Supply box early; patient retrieval and holding two boxes let the player win 9–1 despite the Morat opponent having a major armored threat and recovery support. The opponent could not recover his downed TAG without exposing the Doctor/Engineer to retained ARO coverage.
- **[Observed]** Round 4 Frostbyte vs Vanilla Combined: player was particularly familiar with the heater rules and used Uma aggressively against an Overdron, while contesting zones/heaters. Exact outcome should be checked directly before turning it into a matchup claim.
- **[Caution]**  This is a first-person reconstruction with spoken unit-name transcription errors, and custom Firefight rules; there is no evidence that Svalarheima *in general* has favorable Morat or Combined matchups.

### Mission / strategic lesson

- **Biotechvore:** advance *all* necessary bodies and park them **out of an alpha-strike angle**, not just barely outside the danger zone.
- **Supplies:** after stealing an objective, preserve the carrier; ARO positioning can deny enemy access to a damaged TAG and its repair package.
- **Frostbyte:** score the heater and qualifying-zone conditions at the correct turn, not simply by winning the shooting battle.
- **Cross-game:** the narrator lost several games despite successful starts; **the last safe Order / last exposed model** was decisive more often than the strongest gun.

**Portable insight:** Nisse/Uma's usefulness is a *mission-capable attack formation*, not a Nisse HMG kill count.

## #013 — Protocol Banshee 2026: 12-player event and tactical recap

**Sources:** [organizer: Banshee Errante Sept 23 report](https://bansheerrante.blogspot.com/2026/09/1er-torneo-de-infinity-protocol-banshee.html), [Ordre Impetuosa full event debrief](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3GE6FU6su0), [GravityPool tournament recap/list discussion](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FGCyfKNkj4M). **Play window:** Sept 2026; **N5.3 / ITS18**, based on contemporary mission packet. **Missions:** B-Pong, **The Dig**, Corporate Appropriation. **Winner:** event commentary names **Rolk**; club had 12 players.

**Source character:** This is **an event-wide retrospective**, not an extra independent matchup. #014 is one of its actual games.

### Units / packages and use

- **[Observed]** GravityPool piloted Torchlight Brigade, and discusses Silverstar attack/command structure, Wave Rider Hacker, Hellblazers and Hippolyta, with Raveneye deception/support. The mission spread needs not only attacking models but durable specialists and objective-moving pieces.
- **[Player view]** A Silverstar Lieutenant + Strider/NCO arrangement and a Psy-Cop Killer Hacker/Disco Baller Wildcard are proposed in the *post-event* list discussion. The Psy-Cop adds hacking protection and visibility tools instead of adding yet another solo gun. These are suggestions discussed in audio, not verified as the exact played list or current legal Fireteam.
- **[Observed]** Hellblazer Flammenspeer reactive rolls produced a striking series of critical hits against a Bakunin Riot Grrl and a Corregidor Intruder despite unfavorable odds. In one follow-up the Intruder was revived by a Coyote and challenged the Hellblazer again. This is a notable dice anecdote, not evidence that a Hellblazer should routinely face down premier shooters.
- **[Observed]** Table density varied heavily, affecting speed and whether long-range profiles or corner-based templates could cover B-Pong and Corporate routes.

### Matchups / mission / tactics

- **[Player view]** Against Bakunin, retaining an isolated Hellblazer Flammenspeer can still force a serious ARO response, but the narrator's unprecedented run of crits invalidates any ranking based on this alone.
- **[Observed]** Against Corregidor, dense cover and long-range Intruder fire meant the Torchlight player needed smoke, backup attackers and objective access; the tactical details of the actual R1 game are documented in #014, and an additional Corregidor game is discussed in GravityPool's recap.
- **[Inference]**  For B-Pong, prepare a specialist to move/control the beacon *after* the first attack run and a last-turn zone defense. For The Dig, take multiple button specialists and anti-contest threats. For Corporate Appropriation, ensure mobile access to a relocated/defended objective.
- **[Caution]**  The organizer explicitly says it is not publishing detailed per-game play-by-play. Do not turn the club's overall ranking into evidence for specific unit matchups.

**Portable insight:** distinguish *consistent list capabilities* from astonishing match outcomes caused by multiple improbable criticals.

## #014 — Protocol Banshee R1 B-Pong: Torchlight Brigade vs Corregidor

**Primary:** [GravityPool filmed tactical report: B-Pong Torchlight vs Corregidor (Rolk)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OVnBkDX7bVk), [organizer's linked video](https://bansheerrante.blogspot.com/2026/09/1er-torneo-de-infinity-protocol-banshee.html). **N5.3 / ITS18**. **Reported outcome:** strong **Corregidor/Rolk win**.

### Unit packages / observed interactions

- **[Observed]** Torchlight setup: Silverstar HMG + Hellblazer + Wave Rider Hacker Core, Clutcher + Nimrod + Hippolyta support Haris, and Raveneye/midfield Camo. The Wave Rider's hacking may protect a corridor and the Clutcher provides unusual movement/recovery routes.
- **[Observed]** Corregidor used Moran to control a B-Pong ball/beacon approach, Jazz for the hacking/late zone, an Intruder to threaten risky visible movement, and Iguana + Mobile Brigada + Lupe Balboa + Coyote as multiple hard-to-ignore attackers and scorers. A Moran's ability to control the *objective* can be more important than an early kill.
- **[Observed]** The Torchlight Silverstar HMG fell early; a Clutcher/Nimrod/Hippolyta route then tried to recover tempo. Clutcher removed the Moran, while Hippolyta used Smoke to interrupt Mobile Brigada's firelane, *not* simply to protect the primary attacker. This is an important use of smoke to protect a wounded objective/recovery path.
- **[Observed]** A Speedball-supported Hellblazer attempted to revive the Silverstar but was lost to an Iguana critical hit, compounding the original casualty. Clutcher subsequently managed medical/recovery plays, but Torchlight was spending Orders repairing the plan instead of scoring.
- **[Observed]** Iguana pilot, Lupe and Jazz retained or reclaimed beacon control. By Turn 3 Corregidor gathered multiple bodies around the center point; Torchlight could complete a late Classified but could not reclaim the objective margin.

### Matchup and tactical takeaways

- **[Inference]**  Against Corregidor's Moran/Jazz + heavy-weapon control, killing the Moran is only the first step; the follow-up must convert that opening into objective control before Lupe/Iguana/Jazz rotate in.
- **[Observed]** A failed recovery chain is a major hidden order cost. Before attempting a risky Speedball/Doctor route, evaluate whether the rescued attacker can still score or whether switching to a surviving specialist is better.
- **[Observed]** Corregidor's late-game center occupation illustrates B-Pong's last-scoring-timing advantage, while Torchlight's good firepower and smoke couldn't replace the multiple objective-capable bodies lost early.
- **[Caution]**  This is one game with significant critical rolls, not evidence that Corregidor always counters Torchlight Brigade. Geometry of smoke and line of sight matters.

**Portable insight:** remove a midfield objective guardian *and immediately replace it with your own controller*—otherwise the opponent's second layer simply retakes the point.

## #020 — Can'tCon26: Military Orders, three-game tournament

**Primary:** [Quiet Contender: Can'tCon26, ITS17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ybnQTkQBemE). **Played:** second weekend of **February 2026**, Western Australia, six players. **N5.2 / ITS17**. **Missions:** **Akial Interference, B-Pong, Area of Interest**. **Reported result:** author **6th of six**.

### Units and combinations

- **[Player view]** Original balanced list: Hospitaller HMG Core with De Fersen LT and TinBot, Santiago KHD + MSV2 Crosier HMG + Crosier Paramedic Haris, Crosier sniper + Knight Commander CoC Duo, and Curator/Palbot + 2 Trinitarians with deployable repeaters + airborne Crusader FO. This is the same core concept studied in #009, but the new report shows what happens when the early defensive pieces fall.
- **[Player experiment] Alternate **“spray and pray” multi-Duo / small Fireteam** list** deliberately lacked a heavy dedicated ARO, substituting offensive Knight duos and specialist flexibility. The player wanted to test **roles compressed into more expensive multi-role knights**; a few dead units quickly left too few Orders to convert any advantage.
- **[Observed]** Akial Interference vs ALEPH/Steel Phalanx-type attackers: a Black Friar Thunderbolt + Brother Konstantinos smoke/disco + Knight of Justice pushed toward HVT objectives, but the Trinitarian Forward Observer was sent to an objective requiring a Hacker, so the intended Classified interaction failed. Role mismatch wasted Orders.
- **[Observed]** B-Pong: the first-turn plan prioritized an alpha strike, but a strongly positioned enemy specialist/ARO could preserve objective control while the mobile Knights lost team structure. Santiago/De Fersen later had fewer Orders than needed to recover scoring tempo.
- **[Observed]** Area of Interest vs Bakunin: Denma Connelly, Puppets, Fiddler and counterattack utility punished a Knights-heavy list. The Crusader FO could arrive and activate a console even with the primary team depleted, but was subsequently eliminated; the final Knight Commander push ran out of Orders before reaching a second objective.

### Matchups / lessons

- **[Player view]** Military Orders' Trinitarian midfield network and Crusader reserve specialist are strong insurance against a collapsed visible front; however, *too many expensively linked knights* can leave only a couple safe mission activations per turn.
- **[Observed]** The Curator/Palbot repair is most valuable to remove Isolated/IMM states, but if the Engineer itself dies or a team cannot reach it, the Knights' printed ARM/BS will not save the scoring plan.
- **[Player view]** After this loss, the narrator argues for “alpha-striking objectives” rather than just enemy troopers, and strongly values choosing deployment over initiative because of side-choice information. Treat those as player learning points, not universal mathematical truths.
- **[Caution]**  The audio includes a failed Classified due to the wrong troop type and repeated unusually poor FtF dice. Do not blame the entire sectorial or rules edition for this event placement.

**Portable insight:** *a correctly typed, reachable specialist plus two spare Orders* can be worth more than an additional elite Knight or early kill.
