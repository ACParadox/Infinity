## #111 — Vanilla Yu Jing vs Kestrel Colonial Force: Evacuation

**[Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=giPfxybrbdI)**; [creator-index listing June 28, 2025](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/robert-shepherd-index-batreps.43336/). **Result: Yu Jing 5–1 Kestrel.**


### Army lists and Fireteams — original full-transcript reconstruction

**Video:** [Vanilla Yu Jing vs Kestrel, Evacuation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=giPfxybrbdI). The narrator explains both army builds **before deployment**, including his deliberate **Hsien Lieutenant(+1 Order) Specialist** experiment and the opponent's **two linked Kestrel groups**. Captions render Hsien as “Cien,” Mowang as “Moang,” Fennec as “Fenic.”

| Faction | Fireteam | Members, deployment and status |
|---|---|---|
| **Vanilla Yu Jing** | **Mowang/Pheasant Duo — confirmed 2** | **Mowang NCO Red Fury/Flammenspeer/Disco Baller + Pheasant Rank Agent Chain of Command/Minelayer**. Explicitly “duoed.” The Pheasant would **break the link** to synchronize a civilian; that possible future separation is **not a separate starting team**. |
| Yu Jing | **Celestial Guard Monitor/Kuang Shi package — 5 named, physical Fireteam uncertain** | **Celestial Guard Monitor + 4 Kuang Shi** explicitly selected as cheap Order/smoke infrastructure. The opening lists five bodies but **does not unmistakably declare all five as the same played Core**; do not promote to verified five-person Fireteam without an Army/deployment confirmation. |
| **Kestrel Colonial Force** | **Magistrate/Jackal Haris — confirmed 3** | **BCA Magistrate Lieutenant/Strategos + 2 Jackals**. The narrator explicitly identifies it as the efficient **Magistrate Haris**. |
| Kestrel | **Griffin/Fennec/Bipandra mixed team — confirmed 5** | **Griffin** long-range gunner + **Bipandra Doctor** + **3 Fennecs** (**Thunderbolt/Triangulated Fire**, **Hacker**, **Forward Observer/Disco Baller**). All five named as the **large Combat Group 2 Fireteam**; Bipandra can heal Griffin. |

**Other played Yu Jing:** **Hsien Lieutenant Specialist Operative MULTI Rifle**, **Yan Huo HRMC**, **Hac Tao Missile Launcher(+1B) Hidden Deployment**, **3 Shaolin Monks**, **Libertos**, **Zhanshi Yisheng Doctor + Yaozao** and other support. No fictitious Daoying Decoy was included: the creator specifically rejects that suggested alternate deception. **Other Kestrel:** **Squalo HMG/Grenade Launcher TAG**, **Peacemaker Minelayer**, **Machinist + turret**, **Yamabushi Paramedic**, **Fugazi**, **Mulebot**, support.

**Large-team census:** **One directly named five-body Kestrel group**. **Five Yu Jing Monitor/Kuang Shi troopers are present** but team membership not independently stated in the narration. Keep the distinction between an **Order package** and a **declared Core**. Source result **Yu Jing 5–1**.



- **[Observed]** The centerpiece experiment was **Hsien Warrior Lieutenant (+1 Order), Specialist Operative** as a solo Evacuation worker. **Shaolin Smoke, Kuang Shi order supply, Yan Huo ranged support, Hidden Hac Tao missile launcher, Libertos and Zhanshi Doctor/Yáozăo** filled out the list. The Hsien personally escorted **two enemy HVTs and a friendly civilian** to safety, generating **all five Yu Jing scenario points** despite being expensive.
- **[Observed]** Kestrel's **Squalos TAG, Griffin and Thunderbolt/F-X REM** threatened the evacuation lanes. The REM used **Triangulated Fire to ignore Hac Tao's Mimetism** and land hits, but the Hac Tao's armor saved it. The Hsien attempted to engage Squalos and died late; the *already banked extraction score* still won the mission.
- **[Player opinion]** The author strongly **dislikes the non-HMG Hsien Lieutenant as a normal purchase/list selection**: approximately 49 points for mediocre Burst without Fireteam bonuses, and a less costly **Daoying Lieutenant + Mowang NCO + Haidao Specialist** could provide similar or better objectives plus safer command. However this game proves the Hsien can work in a **very narrow, Order-rich Evacuation role**. He liked Hac Tao's unusual reactive threat.
- **[Inference]** **Hsien Lt + Shaolin Smoke + abundant Orders** can beat a Kestrel TAG gunline by **moving CivVac targets rather than fighting TAGs**. Kestrel's better counter is to kill the Hsien's safe extraction routes and reserve enough mobile scoring bodies, not simply shoot its gun.
- **[Caution]** A postgame “Hsien no-repeat” opinion is **not evidence the Hsien HMG profile is bad**, nor that the Hsien Lt is universally unusable; only this niche build was tested.

**Mission takeaway:** Bank Evacuation points early; once five are secured, denying the final TAG's access can matter more than completing another Classified.
