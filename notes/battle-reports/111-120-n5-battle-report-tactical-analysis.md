## #111 — Vanilla Yu Jing vs Kestrel Colonial Force: Evacuation

**[Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=giPfxybrbdI)**; [creator-index listing June 28, 2025](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/robert-shepherd-index-batreps.43336/). **Result: Yu Jing 5–1 Kestrel.**

- **[Observed]** The centerpiece experiment was **Hsien Warrior Lieutenant (+1 Order), Specialist Operative** as a solo Evacuation worker. **Shaolin Smoke, Kuang Shi order supply, Yan Huo ranged support, Hidden Hac Tao missile launcher, Libertos and Zhanshi Doctor/Yáozăo** filled out the list. The Hsien personally escorted **two enemy HVTs and a friendly civilian** to safety, generating **all five Yu Jing scenario points** despite being expensive.
- **[Observed]** Kestrel's **Squalos TAG, Griffin and Thunderbolt/F-X REM** threatened the evacuation lanes. The REM used **Triangulated Fire to ignore Hac Tao's Mimetism** and land hits, but the Hac Tao's armor saved it. The Hsien attempted to engage Squalos and died late; the *already banked extraction score* still won the mission.
- **[Player opinion]** The author strongly **dislikes the non-HMG Hsien Lieutenant as a normal purchase/list selection**: approximately 49 points for mediocre Burst without Fireteam bonuses, and a less costly **Daoying Lieutenant + Mowang NCO + Haidao Specialist** could provide similar or better objectives plus safer command. However this game proves the Hsien can work in a **very narrow, Order-rich Evacuation role**. He liked Hac Tao's unusual reactive threat.
- **[Inference]** **Hsien Lt + Shaolin Smoke + abundant Orders** can beat a Kestrel TAG gunline by **moving CivVac targets rather than fighting TAGs**. Kestrel's better counter is to kill the Hsien's safe extraction routes and reserve enough mobile scoring bodies, not simply shoot its gun.
- **[Caution]** A postgame “Hsien no-repeat” opinion is **not evidence the Hsien HMG profile is bad**, nor that the Hsien Lt is universally unusable; only this niche build was tested.

**Mission takeaway:** Bank Evacuation points early; once five are secured, denying the final TAG's access can matter more than completing another Classified.

## #112 — Vanilla Combined Army vs White Company: Unmasking

**[Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TMD51E8C6Js)**; [creator-index post Aug 30, 2025, describing ITS16.2](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/robert-shepherd-index-batreps.43336/). **Combined Army won decisively; exact score not reliably spoken.**

- **[Observed]** Combined used **Charontid MSV3 HMG Lieutenant + Hidden Anathematic Hacker**, **Bit & Kiss, Pandora Medikit**, **Daturazi Smoke/CC, Krakot Renegades, Imetrons and Ikadron**. The key combination was **aggressive forward Pitcher projection + Bit & Kiss Killer Hacking + Charontid HMG** supported by expendable Daturazi/Krakots.
- **[Observed]** White Company was testing **Emily counting as Fusilier in a Fireteam**, with **BS14/X-Visor E/M Grenade Launcher**, **John Hawkwood** and Fusilier Hacker support. The Combined player landed a long Pitcher, **Bit killed the Fusilier Hacker**, reducing the Fusilier link from five to four and causing **Hawkwood to lose Sixth Sense** as described; Charontid then eliminated Hawkwood through the newly favorable lane. Early loss of high-value defenders and hackers cascaded into Unmasking control.
- **[Player opinion]** A camouflaged Killer Hacker or camouflaged defender that can **decline to reveal while hostile Pitchers are launched** could resist this sort of highly telegraphed cyber alpha better than exposed Fusilier Hackers. The author calls **Daturazi + Krakot order/attack swarm** especially strong value alongside premium EI models.
- **[Inference]** **Charontid/Anathematic + Bit/Kiss + Warband mass** can punish a White Company force whose anti-TAG/Fireteam defenses depend on a single visible Hacker and active Sixth Sense link. It is **not a general proof Combined always counters White Company**.
- **[Caution]** Hacking-network strength and the exact link bonus are historical; the presenter notes fortunate rolls on the first Pitcher and gun duel.

**Mission takeaway:** Do not leave all Unmasking objective workers and the Fireteam Sixth Sense enabler visible to **Pitcher → KHD → HMG** sequencing.

## #113 — JSA Oban vs Tohaa: Supplies

**[Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P1txU6Z6P8w)**; [creator index identifies no seasonal extras](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/robert-shepherd-index-batreps.43336/), early January 2025. **Result: Tohaa 3–2 Oban.**

- **[Observed]** Oban fielded an aggressive **O-Yoroi TAG Lieutenant, Aragoto motorcycle Duo, Shikami, Ryuken-9 real/Decoy threats** and cheap utility; Tohaa ran **Sukeul K1/SymbioMate, smoke/linked Triads, Specialist/Paramedic and recovery**. Early Aragoto riding/shotguns/Smoke provided unexpectedly efficient routes under N5's changed motorcycle/base movement.
- **[Observed]** The O-Yoroi pushed deep into the Tohaa DZ, absorbed much of the opponent's following turn and died to an extremely durable **Sukeul supported by a Vita Pack/SymbioMate**, trading Heavy Flamethrower attacks against close weapons. Oban's **isolated Shikami spent Orders Resetting** instead of immediately positioning to threaten an HVT or supply carrier.
- **[Observed]** Both armies held **one Supply Box** late. Tohaa's final Paramedic attempted a **GizmoKit/Medikit task** while uncertain which apparent **Ryuken Camo/Decoy** marker was real. The marker was a Decoy; Tohaa successfully completed a Classified and won **3–2** rather than settling for a draw.
- **[Player opinion]** Oban should likely **withdraw O-Yoroi to defensible fire positions** after its successful push rather than leave it inside the opponent's DZ. The Shikami could **advance while isolated** to force a response instead of spending the whole turn resetting.
- **[Inference]** **Tohaa SymbioMate + Vita Pack + mobile Triad/Paramedic** gives strong *Supplies carrier resilience* against Oban TAG aggression; **Oban Aragoto Duo + Ryuken Decoy + O-Yoroi** can still put Tohaa under severe early pressure.
- **[Caution]** Vita Pack timing and SymbioMate/transmutation interaction reflect **initial N5** and the filmed rules discussion, not present-day guaranteed legality.

**Mission takeaway:** Do not sacrifice a secured box by overcommitting a TAG; preserve a separate final-turn **Paramedic Classified route and fake/real Camo screening**.

## #114 — ALEPH Operations Subsection vs Vanilla Yu Jing: Superiority

**[Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o94d5wFxu80)**; [creator posted Apr 18, 2025; ITS16.2 Superiority](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/robert-shepherd-index-batreps.43336/). **Result: OSS 10–3 Vanilla Yu Jing.** Post-Apr 3 N5.1 era, but actual filmed patch uncertain.

- **[Observed]** OSS deliberately tested **Garuda** as a fast Silhouette 2 flanking attacker under the revised **Super-Jump**, with **Posthuman Proxy Mk2/Mk5, Shukra, John Hawkwood and Lamdeh/cheap Order support**. The Garuda attacked from unusual rooftop/vertical geometry, **forced a Zhencha Hacker back** and **removed a Tiger Soldier**, then used a Speedball **Auto Repair Kit** on an upper-level route.
- **[Observed]** Vanilla Yu Jing brought **Yan Huo high-volume gun, Tiger Soldier, Zhencha, Libertos, Long Ya/Beast Hunter-style Camo**. Opponent had actually built for **Panic Room**, not Superiority; some defense/mission compromises were inherent to the mismatch. Yan Huo wounded Hawkwood and threatened a favorable volume-of-fire duel.
- **[Observed]** Despite hard shots and some wounds, OSS retained enough **Posthuman Specialist and Garuda movement** for quadrant weight and console scoring; **Posthuman pressed the last objective**, producing **10–3**.
- **[Player opinion]** Author recommends **one more Order in the second combat group** to maximize Mk2/Mk5 actions; says a **Liu Xing or another tactical airborne profile** could improve the opponent's particular list, but those were unplayed alternatives.
- **[Inference]** **Garuda vertical movement + Posthuman redundancy + Shukra** is very well suited to **Superiority**, especially when enemy Camo markers must defend multiple spread zones. **Not a fair proof of OSS superiority over optimized Vanilla Yu Jing**, because the opposing list was built for a different mission.
- **[Caution]** The Apr 3 Super-Jump/N5.1 changes are relevant; do not back-project this Garuda behavior to December 2024 initial N5.

**Mission takeaway:** Maximize **Posthuman Order pool and independent Garuda repositioning**, and avoid relying on a single Yan Huo to hold several quadrants simultaneously.

## #115 — Kestrel Colonial Force vs Tunguska: Supremacy

**[Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vRSawMjmiJ0)**; [Jan 16, 2025 creator-index record states no seasonal extras](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/robert-shepherd-index-batreps.43336/). **Initial N5. Result: Tunguska 7–6 Kestrel.**

- **[Observed]** Kestrel tested a broad package of **Griffin active gun, Banshee Killer Hacker, Black A.I.R. reactive gun/mines, several Hackers and bots**. The author deployed **Black A.I.R. far back** to survive an opposing **Kriza Borac** attack; this saved the unit but reduced its proactive zone pressure.
- **[Observed]** Tunguska used **Fiddler Engineer with Jackbots**, **Kriza Borac**, **Interventor Killer Hacker, Repeater-support REMs and mission specialists**. Fiddler advanced while **repairing/reconstituting Jackbots**, so effective attrition disappeared midgame. The Kestrel side's attacks on Tunguska pieces created a lead that failed to hold.
- **[Observed]** The late fight featured **Banshee KHD versus Interventor KHD**: Banshee won some hacking rolls but could not penetrate the target's **Firewall/BTS**, and eventually lost. Interventor pressed the last console, while the mine-protected **Black A.I.R.** still prevented a riskier three-zone Tunguska move. **Final 7–6 to Tunguska**.
- **[Player opinion]** Kestrel's list had more Hackers than necessary; the author suggests substituting **Yamabushi for extra close assault**. Tunguska **Fiddler** is particularly useful because repairing bots can be bundled with the assault itself.
- **[Inference]** **Tunguska Interventor + Fiddler Jackbots + Kriza** can outscore a Kestrel gun/hacker list in Supremacy if it has **multiple recoverable midboard pieces**. **Kestrel Griffin/Black A.I.R./Banshee** nearly won, so this is emphatically **not a runaway sectorial counter**.
- **[Caution]** This is an original **January 2025 initial-N5** battle, NOT an N5.2 Interventor/Kestrel data point.

**Mission takeaway:** Save a **final console Specialist and a safe counter-hacking approach**; strong Black A.I.R. AROs cannot score buttons alone.

## #116 — Vanilla Combined Army vs Shock Army of Acontecimento: Outbreak

**[Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W4JSxRzvgyw)**. Creator calls this his **first game of the new ITS17 midseason Outbreak**, saying next video comes in **early April 2026**. **N5.2-era ITS17.2**. **Result: Combined approximately 6–5 Acontecimento** (creator says “something like 6–5”); likely score, not a verified written card.

- **[Observed]** New Outbreak demands **Doctors/Paramedics or rare Specialist Operatives**, not generic hackers/FO/Chain of Command, with repeated **CivVac, infected scanning and stabilization**, special **alpha infected** control and radial deployment. The author warns it is **too easy for the first player to snowball points** with nearby infected.
- **[Observed]** Acontecimento fielded **Dart midfield Camo/mine control, Akal Commando mobile objective capture, Bag-Mari, Machinist and aggressively placed Fugazi Flash Pulse REMs**. **Dart's denial stalled the first Combined scoring push**, while **Akal extracted the alpha infected**. **Fugazi Stun/Flash Pulse repeatedly consumed Combined Orders**, preventing scans.
- **[Observed]** Combined relied on **Sasha/other medical scoring, Rodok/Suryat-style mobile fighters and Libertos**; a late **Rodok successful Dodge moved out of Zone of Control**, letting Combined secure the last infected condition. Author explicitly says otherwise the match would have been **5–5**.
- **[Player opinion]** For a tournament packet, consider requiring **at least eight inches between infected models**; this is the creator's *proposed house balancing change*, NOT an official Outbreak rule.
- **[Inference]** **Dart + Fugazi picket + Akal alpha runner** is a highly credible *mission-specific* anti-Combined setup; Combined can still win through **redundant scoring and late Rodok movement**. One close 6–5 result does not rank these factions.
- **[Caution]** Outbreak's exact midseason Specialist whitelist must be checked before importing this advice to future mission packs.

**Mission takeaway:** Before taking a TAG/hero duel, assign **two eligible infected handlers, an alpha carrier and Flash Pulse denial**.

## #117 — Dahshat Company vs JSA Shindenbutai: Firefight

**[Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T8Cz_rSinVM)**. Presenter describes being ~one month into N5 and flags **Dahshat Motorized Bounty Hunter profile data as possibly erroneous**. **Initial N5 probable; exact played date/ITS revision unknown**. **Result Dahshat 6–0 Shindenbutai**; player warns this included tactical mistakes.

- **[Observed]** Dahshat deliberately tested **two Uragan Traktor Muls** functioning as **Total Reaction/Red Fury defensive guns plus Guided Missile options**, rather than a passive one-REM list. **Turrets and mine/Repeater deployment**, a **Maghariba Guard**, and **midfield specialist/Hacker support** provided a layered defensive shell. One Uragan went down and was **recovered/repaired**, continuing to impose a reactive threat.
- **[Observed]** Shindenbutai used **Yojimbo Smoke + Rui Shi MSV2 Spitfire**, **Tanuki + Shizoku**, **Hidden Ninja Killer Hacker and nearby Repeater tools**, and could bypass ordinary Total Reaction with **Smoke/visor shooting**. Its Ninja removed a midfield Hacker via **Trinity** after dealing with Libertos/mine threat.
- **[Observed]** Dahshat's many **ARO guns and turrets** forced a very costly engagement (player mentions **14 saving rolls in one response sequence**). Shindenbutai committed into these rather than use as much **Pitcher → Hacking/Guided and anti-turret positional counterplay** as the player afterward believed possible.
- **[Player opinion]** The Uragans' **2 SWC combined investment** is high, but they demonstrated functional dual roles and the author would consider trying them again. He also liked **neutral terrain Eclipse visibility areas**, but these were **local experimental table features**, not normal ITS terrain defaults.
- **[Inference]** **Double Uragan TR/Guided + disposable midboard mines + high-value TAG** can be effective into Shindenbutai when the JSA player tries direct attacks. **Yojimbo Smoke/Rui Shi** is nevertheless a genuine counter to TR guns; more asymmetric Hacking could have reversed the course.
- **[Caution]** Dahshat pilot explicitly forgot a Command Token and states **6–0 is not a robust measure of relative sectorial strength**; terrain experiment also affects conclusions.

**Mission takeaway:** In Firefight, combine **cheap reactive REM area control with flexible missile/targeting**, but protect the Lieutenant and screen against Ninja hacking.

## #118 — Morat Aggression Force vs Svalarheima Winter Force: Unmasking

**[Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bvNTyBHsGXM)**. **N5 played patch not securely dated**. **Result: Morats 8–4 Svalarheima.**

- **[Observed]** Morats used **Rodok Fireteam, double Dartok linked Pitcher support, Guided Missile, Suryat Tempest Regiment reactive/active gun, Daturazi Smoke, Ikadron Repeater/Baggage and Gaki Order/assault bodies**. The **Dartoks projected Repeaters across the board**, giving Combat Group 2 Orders to **Spotlight and Guided-kill a protected Svalarheima gun**, while Rodoks pressed Unmasking buttons.
- **[Observed]** Svalarheima used **Shona Carano, Varangians Disco Ball/Eclipse, Beast Hunter, linked shooters and forward specialists**. Its opening attack was described as strong. **Suryat Tempest** survived a decisive reactive exchange and then created an unrecoverable attrition/mission opening.
- **[Observed]** **Rodok team activated buttons, revealed and shot HVTs**, and Morats ended with **real HVT killed, both decoys eliminated and more consoles activated**, scoring **8–4**. This is a **scoring and target-identity** victory, not just a high-damage game.
- **[Player opinion]** The author rates **linked Dartok Pitchers** favorably because dual launchers reduce the need to reload from the Ikadron and allow repeat Spotlight/Guided attempts without taking a fair ARO against Svalarheima guns.
- **[Inference]** **Rodok attack + Dartok guided/Repeater network + Suryat** is a coherent **Unmasking** package versus **Svalarheima Shona/Varangian/visor gunlines**; Svalarheima could still attack the actual Repeater/Hacker nodes instead of allowing repeat free Guided Orders.
- **[Caution]** Player notes a lucky Suryat defensive survival; a single 8–4 game is not a general Morats-vs-Svalarheima ranking.

**Mission takeaway:** Separate **HVT revealing, assassination and cyber protection** into redundant units; use Guided when it avoids a costly direct shot, not automatically.

## #119 — Vanilla Combined Army vs Tohaa: ITS17 Akial Interference

**[Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oi96gDzQAVw)**. ITS17 **AKIAL INTERFERENCE**, N5 exact patch/day unverified. **Vanilla Combined Army major victory**; narrator reports **four counted Classifieds versus Tohaa's three**, plus the bonus for finishing at least one each round; **exact terminal OP not calculated on camera**.

- **[Observed]** Combined brought **Sheskiin Protheion assault, Legate Specialist/Hacker with TinBot, Exrah/Datūrazi-type disposable attackers and backup Classified-capable units**. Tohaa instead built a **dedicated Highly Classified specialist matrix** with **Sukeul K1 Sniper, Triads and multiple Speculative Fire grenade launchers**, plus a **War Driver Cybermine** counter.
- **[Observed]** **Sheskiin gained wounds** by eating exposed targets and ended up an extremely resilient forward predator. An enemy **War Driver Cybermine** forced a real **Reset-vs-Dodge** decision and threatened **Stunned** even though Sheskiin was not ordinarily Hackable, briefly constraining the attack route.
- **[Observed]** Tohaa completed multiple Classifieds but allowed **Sheskiin to sweep several expensive targets in the first turn**, reducing available Orders and future Classified performers. Combined later **used an antenna to cancel previously scored enemy Classifieds**, helping it stay ahead despite playing aggressively.
- **[Player opinion]** Author argues Akial can sometimes be played **like Annihilation with sufficient minimal specialist breadth**; however, an early KHD kill on the **Legate** could leave Combined unable to do Hacker-dependent cards. He suggests Tohaa instead do one early task, withdraw, and **Speculative Fire grenades at the enemy warband/Imetron order base**.
- **[Inference]** **Sheskiin + minimal robust Classified coverage + antenna cancellation** can beat Tohaa specialist-overinvestment *on this table*. **Tohaa Speculative Fire + safe Triad and Cybermine** could be a real conditional counter to aggressive Sheskiin.
- **[Caution]** The reported counts are *completed Classifieds*, **not a verified 5–3 or 6–3 official OP total**; exact patch and some Cybermine rulings must be verified.

**Mission takeaway:** Do **one objective per round**, use the antenna to **remove an opponent's prior score**, and defend specialist Order economy against a Protheion snowball.

## #120 — Bakunin vs White Company: ITS16.2 Supplies

**[Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RkPOx3JWitA)**; [creator-index post Feb 23, 2025; ITS16.2](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/robert-shepherd-index-batreps.43336/). **Initial N5**, both players' first games with their sectorial in this edition. **Bakunin won with two Supply Boxes versus White Company's one, plus one Classified each**. Exact numeric victory points not calculated.

- **[Observed]** Bakunin fielded **Pure Duo of two Stigmaton TAGs** (special die bonus, high Tactical Awareness Order budget), **Reverend/Custodier, Cenobites, Morlocks/Smoke, Pitcher and Cyclone REM**. White Company tested **Guijia TAG Lieutenant, John Hawkwood reactive gun, Emily E/M Speculative Grenades, Laxmee Hacking, Machinist Engineer and other specialist support**.
- **[Observed]** Both Stigmatons initially spent too many Orders fighting **Hawkwood's excellent ARO profile**, taking wounds. The White Company player then **voluntarily offered an agreed rewind** of that disastrous first sequence. In the replay, Bakunin **prioritized an early Supply Box recovery and retreat** before risking spare Orders on Hawkwood; this changed the game. **The documented outcome is NOT a strict no-takebacks tournament result.**
- **[Observed]** Bakunin **Cenobites carrying boxes were durable enough to survive Emily's E/M/grenade pressure**, even if isolated; **Stigmaton/Custodier Repeaters and Hacking Possession** made the Guijia move into a dangerous ARO/repeater trap. The **Cyclone with 360° visor** ultimately landed the critical DA shots that killed Guijia during its final push.
- **[Player opinion]** The author would not automatically fight Hawkwood: **Smoke and objective movement** achieved the same conditions without a high-variance gun duel. White Company could have benefited from **non-Hackable close attackers** such as Liang Kai/Shona-style options, rather than concentrating all meaningful attack in Guijia and other Hackable pieces.
- **[Inference]** **Double Stigmaton + Custodier/Repeaters + durable Cenobite box carriers** can be effective in Supplies against an over-Hackable White Company TAG/elite roster; **Hawkwood remains a credible counter to early Stigmaton attacks**. This is a friendly replay result, not proof Bakunin generally wins this matchup.
- **[Caution]** Note exact **replay/rewind**, historical **Pure Duo/Stigmaton/Hacking** rules and that numeric scores were not announced.

**Mission takeaway:** Get a **box and retreat to safety before gambling both TAGs on Hawkwood**. The opponent should save a clean, non-Hackable path to the carrier.
