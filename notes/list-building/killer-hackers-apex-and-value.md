# Killer Hackers — KHD Apex and Value (N5.3)

Dedicated ranking of **Killer Hacking Devices (KHD)** for Trinity counterhacking, Cybermask, network defense and specialist work. Apex prioritizes cyber-duel performance; Value emphasizes affordable anti-Hacker coverage and mission utility. Hybrids with a printed KHD may appear here **and** in the hybrid guide.

**Related guides:** [HD / HD+ Hackers](hackers-apex-and-value.md) · [Hybrid HD/KHD programs](hybrid-hackers-apex-and-value.md). The [shared device comparison and N5.3 rule summary](hybrid-hackers-apex-and-value.md#what-the-current-rules-actually-do) is in the hybrid guide.

## Killer Hackers — KHD

**KHD Apex criteria (N5.3):** First assess **effective Trinity attacks** (WIP and printed program upgrades); second assess **protection from hostile hacking and return fire** (BTS, intrinsic Firewall/ECM, NWI, W2+/STR, Immunities, Hidden Deployment). Repeater/Pitcher reach, deployment, mobility, links and non-cyber weapons are **secondary**. Price/SWC are ignored for raw Apex. A standard KHD has Trinity/Cybermask, **not** Oblivion/Carbonite/White Noise unless expressly upgraded. [N5.3 hacking rules](https://infinitythewiki.com/Hacking_Programs).

**Job:** Fight opposing **Hackers** with **Trinity**, use **Cybermask** to stay alive/reach objectives, and provide a cheap specialist or anti-hacking protector. **Basic KHD cannot Oblivion/Carbonite a normal non-Hacker TAG/HI**. Listbuilding value changes sharply if there are no enemy hackers; forward deployment and scoring then matter more. KHD + non-KHD program upgrades appear in the [Hybrid Hacker guide](hybrid-hackers-apex-and-value.md), with only exceptionally good standalone KHDs cross-listed here.

**KHD purchasing threshold:** the best KHD may be a **20–26pt objective runner**, rather than the best raw cyber duelist. **Tuareg/Trinitarian/Ninja-class** models pay for midfield position; **Santiago** pays for a two-Wound Fireteam/TinBot package; **Deva/Apsaras/Barid/Cyberghost** concentrate the anti-Hacker role at lower cost. Shakti's enhanced Trinity is **still KHD-only**, not a Carbonite/Oblivion hybrid.

**Lower-return KHD profiles (not ranked):** **Corregidor Bandit KHD26/0** is an Irregular WIP12 infiltrator whose ambush/scoring value generally exceeds its sustained cyber effectiveness; **Ryūken Unit-9 KHD25/0.5** is a weak dedicated counterhacker investment compared with 0-SWC KHD alternatives. These evaluations are specific to their hacking role, not their broader utility.
