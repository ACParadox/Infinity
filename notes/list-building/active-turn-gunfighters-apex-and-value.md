- **Strongest lower-cost direct guns per investment:** **Skyhound33/0** (mobile plasma/AP and Mimetism−6), **Posthuman Mk.4 HMG30/1.5 _inside 2–3 Proxy purchase_**, **Kazak Spetsnaz Camo HMG36/1.5**, **Hetkari MULTI Red Fury48/1.5 _in a qualifying Kestrel team_**, **Squalos Mk-II MULTI Marksman55/1**, **Veteran Kazak AP HMG45/1.5**, **Kamau HMG26/1 _with a legal team_**, and **Kriza Borac HMG45/1.5**.

- **Best pure coverage counters:** **Marut/Asura MSV2–3** into Mimetism/smoke; **Hetkari/Spetsnaz Marksmanship** into common Cover; **Overdron Albedo** into MSV, **Skyhound/Vostok/Cutter** impose Mimetism themselves; **Mukhtar MSV2 Red Fury34/1** and **Epsilon MSV2 HMG34/1.5** are lower-budget anti-camo specialists.
- **Best low-cost attack + mission flexibility:** **Sukeul FO K1 combi29/0**, **Mukhtar viral rifle25/0 / MSV2 Red Fury34/1**, **Sheskiin MULTI rifle48/0**, **Asawira AP Spitfire38/1.5**, **Stinger NCO HMG30/1 in Next Wave**, and **OSS Posthuman Mk.5 Mk12(+1SD)21/0.5 _inside the package_**. These become superior when the main weapon also opens a scoring route.
- **LT and Chain of Command:** A **powerful LT gun** is an *army architecture*, not a free upgrade; CoC is normally the **safe second-layer insurance**, not another disposable assault piece. Separate LT/CoC rankings below, with exact-profile restrictions. For generic command superiority consult [LT/CoC guide](command-units-apex-and-value.md).

## How to compare real Order efficiency

**Do not rank by a paper 'damage per shot'.** Resolve six costs, in order:

1. **Enter the fight:** 0 Orders from an advantageous already-placed angle vs 2–4 movement/smoke/hacking Orders to reach good range.
2. **Win the Face-to-Face:** actual **active Burst**, modified BS/target values, range, target Mimetism, user's MSV/Marksmanship, Cover, Surprise, Fireteam bonuses, hidden ARO risk. **+1 Special Die (SD) improves consistency but is NOT +1 hit/Burst**: the extra die is discarded before resolution.
3. **Cause a meaningful consequence:** hit(s) surviving FtF, **PS, target ARM/BTS, AP/DA/EXP/Continuous/Plasma/K1, W/STR, Immunities**. In N5 **lower PS is more lethal**; 'higher damage' is bad shorthand.
4. **Choose next opponent:** speed, vertical access, Climbing, stealth, templates, side angles and ability to fight **different ranges** or delete exposed support **without another setup Order**.
5. **Avoid being traded down:** Wounds/STR, NWI, ARM/BTS, Immunity, Camo/Mimetism, TAG Engineer/servant/recovery, repositioning and **Orders budgeted for escape**.
6. **Pay the whole list cost:** gun + Fireteam members and legal composition + required program/smoke/hacking + Engineer + **SWC + Tactical/NCO/LT economy**, versus buying 2 medium guns and mission specialists.

**Practical relative score, not calibrated statistics:** `net useful Orders = (targets/positions meaningfully improved + opponent useful Orders denied + scoring opened) / (movement + setup + shooting + exit Orders)`; weigh failure branches and 300pt roster opportunity cost. Never publish invented expected kills or percentage-to-kill from a profile alone. **An unopposed BS attack can be better than a mathematically stronger frontal gunfight.**

**Important N5.3 interactions:**
- **Marksmanship** ignores the negative BS MOD for Partial Cover/Nanoscreen, **not** the defender's saving-roll benefit. Also becomes susceptible to White Noise/Albedo/Reflective interactions—do **not** claim it ignores Mimetism or is universally better than MSV.
- **MSV** helps against appropriate visibility/Mimetism problems; its utility is matchup-dependent. **White Noise/Albedo** may reverse expensive anti-camo investment.
- **Fireteam Level-2 +1SD** is *one extra die discarded* (not N4 +1 Burst); other levels grant specific bonuses according to valid composition. One Order can move the team and have **one elected leader attack**, not five independent active shots. Cohesion, movement of weakest member, forced shared AROs and team opportunity cost matter.
- **NCO** converts *available* Special LT Orders into NCO-only Tactical Orders and can spend one as **team leader** where legal. If Strategos already regularized them, **don't count the same Orders twice**.
- **TacAware** provides a personal Tactical Order usable to activate a legal Fireteam only when its owner is leader. Reserve retreat/scoring Orders rather than treating TA as extra generic pooled Orders.

**Final buying question:** _If I spend the next three Orders on this gun, exactly which defender can it remove/disable, which objective opens, what is my best alternative shooter, and where will it finish its move?_ The unit is inefficient if all four answers are weak, no matter how impressive its BS/Burst/ARM looks.
