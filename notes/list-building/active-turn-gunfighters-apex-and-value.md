_Updated 2026-11-04, strict non-TAG ranged-apex and Portable Autocannon re-audit. Ranged-role tier rebalance using battle-report synthesis and 2026 profiles; original source review begun 2026-10-23. Cross-faction **editorial, conditional** ratings, not official unit grades, measured tournament win rates, or simulated damage statistics. Current profile/points/AVA/Fireteam authority: September 2026 pinned Army references in [sectorials](../../sectorials); [N5.3 rules and FAQ](../../rules/README.md) supersede historical commentary. Prices shown are examples of a **specific profile in a specific legal roster**. A unit may rank S for peak impact and B for value._

**Scope:** **active-turn medium- and long-range shooting** (especially 16–32in, with meaningful 24–32in capability), **not all-purpose assault**. Close-range 0–16in sweeps, boarding shotguns, rifles, template attacks, melee, Protheion, assassination runs and mobility without a useful ranged engagement belong in [close-combat and short-range assault](../Strategy/close-combat-and-short-range-assault.md). This guide excludes defensive AROs, command structure or deployment tricks in isolation. **TAGs have their own apex and value categories below** and are **not mixed into infantry/REM/VH rankings**. **Apex** asks: _How effectively does the specified weapon/profile win medium/long ranged fights against realistic defenders?_ **Value** asks: _Is that ranged performance worth its own points + SWC + approach Orders + team/support/recovery + roster slot?_ **S** is an exceptional ranged ceiling, **A** strong, **B** conditional/secondary, **C** poor relative fit. **Apex and value are distinct**, and rows within a tier are not strict ordering.

## 1. Non-TAG gunfighters — **ranged apex** ceilings

Infantry/REMs/VH only. **Apex** means maximum credible active-turn gunfighting against realistic defenders in the stated range, not merely short-assault efficiency. VH still suffer **No Cover**; see [vehicles](vehicles-vh.md).

**Range policy:** Long-range means credible repeated **24–32in or farther** fire (HMG/HRMC/Feuerbach/sniper and 16–32in Thunderbolt). Medium-range means gunfights primarily **8–24in**, especially **16–24in** (MMR/Red Fury/Spitfire/Mk12) and exceptional HMG/Thunderbolt crossovers. A profile appears in both only when its *actual* 16–24in FtF, ammo and cost also justify a medium-lane choice; ordinary HMG overlap alone does not mandate duplication. Ratings reflect the listed profile in each context. **G:Jumper rankings must count the complete minimum legal Proxy package, not only the incremental gun body.** **Ranks restart within each range and follow grade first**, with qualitative tie-breaking within grades; no measured tournament win-rate ordering.

## 2. Non-TAG gunfighters — **ranged points / Order value**

Full cost includes an existing **legal** team/G:Jumper package, SWC, attack setup and usable mission Orders. **Value is for the named gun and range**, not all troop-family skills; cheaper 1W guns do not automatically outclass repeatable elite guns.

**Range policy:** Long-range means credible repeated **24–32in or farther** fire (HMG/HRMC/Feuerbach/sniper and 16–32in Thunderbolt). Medium-range means gunfights primarily **8–24in**, especially **16–24in** (MMR/Red Fury/Spitfire/Mk12) and exceptional HMG/Thunderbolt crossovers. A profile appears in both only when its *actual* 16–24in FtF, ammo and cost also justify a medium-lane choice; ordinary HMG overlap alone does not mandate duplication. Ratings reflect the listed profile in each context. **Ranks restart within each range and follow grade first**, with qualitative tie-breaking within grades; no measured tournament win-rate ordering.

### Which non-TAG unit wins its *particular* shootout?

- **Enemy in Cover:**
  - **Spetsnaz HMG**
  - **Hetkari**
  - **Territorial AP MMR**
  - *Context:* or a justified **Assisted Fire REM**.
  - **Mk.4** instead imposes BS Attack(−3) on the defender; don't conflate the two effects.
- **Mimetism−6 / Smoke ARO:** consider the following visor attackers:
  - **Asura**
  - **Intruder**
  - **Nisse**
  - **Hsien**
  - **Epsilon**
  - **MSV2 Mukhtar**

  The visor may be countered by White Noise/Albedo; plan a separate gun.
- **HI/TAG in high ARM:**
  - **Veteran Kazak AP HMG**
  - **Asawira AP Spitfire**
  - **Hatamoto's close-range alternate**
  - **TAG with AP HRMC/MULTI**, if the list can support one

  Pure high Burst without penetration may not finish.
- **Hidden ARO first-contact:**
  - **Swiss Guard / Hac Tao**
  - **Camouflaged Spetsnaz**
  - **Cheap expendable probe**
  - **Guided/indirect setup**, where available
  - **An attacker approaching behind Smoke**

  Do not feed your biggest body into an untested hidden defender.
- **Midfield heavy Cover / short corners:** **Sheskiin MULTI Rifle, Mukhtar Viral Rifle, Asawira BSG and Hatamoto Plasma Carbine** may be stronger than any HMG *at that job*. That is **short-range assault**, not a reason to promote their **long-range gunfighter** tier; see [the short-assault guide](../Strategy/close-combat-and-short-range-assault.md). **Vostok Mk12/Spitfire** remains a legitimate separate medium-range option.
- **Open long firefight:** HMG-range candidates include:
  - **Kriza**
  - **Gamma**
  - **Hac Tao**
  - **Swiss Guard**
  - **Spetsnaz**
  - **Hsien**

  Do not force Red Fury/Spitfire outside their positive range.
- **High vertical / weak hacking net:**
  - **Skyhound**
  - **Firebat**
  - **Redeye**
  - **Vostok**

  If terrain is flat and hacker coverage dense, favor a grounded shooter with better Cover/recovery.

## 7. Ranged evaluation framework and Order efficiency

### Ranged-first assessment rubric (revised 2026-11-02)

This is a **qualitative weighting policy**, not pretend measured percentages or unit win rates:

| Weight | Ranged evaluation axis | What counts / does not count |
|---|---|---|
| **40%** | **Range-specific ranged FtF: Long 16–32in (emphasis 24–32in); Medium 8–24in (emphasis 16–24in)** | Use printed weapon bands, real active Burst/SD, BS, Cover/Mimetism/MSV/Marksmanship and enemy ARO. Rate a Red Fury/Spitfire on its **actual medium bands**, not as a hypothetical HMG. Crossovers qualify only when the **same profile** is strong in both roles. |
| **25%** | **Damage/conversion and target diversity at that range** | AP/HRMC/MULTI or appropriate ammunition versus HI/TAG; ability to remove more than cheap light infantry. Close-range templates, Protheion and CC kill chains **earn no credit here**. |
| **15%** | **Survival for repeat ranged engagements** | ARM/Wounds/STR, Mimetism, Hidden entry, safe extraction/recovery. No Cover, hackers, E/M and exposed return fire are real penalties. |
| **10%** | **Reaching/repositioning between useful ranged lines** | Climbing, height, deployment and movement, **only** insofar as they create or improve 16–32in shots. Speed to reach base contact is short-assault value instead. |
| **10%** | **Ranged contribution per full roster cost** | Actual points/SWC, existing legal Fireteam, support and usable activations; value tier applies an additional explicit opportunity-cost check. |

**Range-specific threshold:** In Long-range, a model must credibly contest 24–32in; in Medium-range, it must credibly fight 8–24in, especially 16–24in. Do **not** deduct medium-grade credit for lacking HMG-range positive bands. **Hard separation:** A model that cannot credibly contest its declared medium/long firelane must **not** receive an S or A+ ranged ranking merely for having elite BS, CC, speed, a short-range gun, or a spectacular first-turn alpha. Rate **individual gun profiles**, not blended fictional combinations. For 0–16in or mixed close/midrange play, see the [short-assault comparison](../Strategy/close-combat-and-short-range-assault.md). Report evidence informs *context and confidence*, not unsupported statistical grades.

### Firepower versus **actual** Order efficiency

A good gunfighter wins the **right** Face-to-Face fight, converts hits to consequences, and still contributes after the first target dies. Compare six steps:

1. **Reach:** count Orders to get into positive weapon range and a workable angle. A 20-5 flyer, infiltrator, or Climbing unit may pay **zero or one** approach Order where a BS15 heavy gun pays three.
2. **Win the FtF:** use the **real active Burst**, modified BS/PH target number, range, Cover, Mimetism, MSV, Marksmanship and enemy ARO. High Burst improves *consistency*, while high target numbers help defeat a strong enemy die.
3. **Convert hits:** account for current **PS/ammunition** and target armor/BTS/Wounds/STR/Immunities. In N5 **lower PS is more lethal**; AP, DA, EXP, K1, Plasma, Viral and E/M have **different** ideal victims.
4. **Continue:** after target one, can the same position hit exposed support, Specialist, Repeater, Engineer, second gun or scoring lane—or must it move through another three Orders?
5. **Survive/exit:** ending prone, out of LoF, behind screen, or recoverable near Engineer may be worth an Order. An exposed 50pt one-wound shooter is not automatically value because it killed 20 points first.
6. **Pay full list cost:** add 2–4 required Fireteam fillers, smoke, hacking, Engineer, EVO, SWC and the fact that 90–122pt TAGs can prevent buying an independent second attack plan.

**Fast heuristic:** _useful enemy Orders denied + objectives opened + attacks won_, divided by _setup + movement + attack + exit Orders_; compare catastrophic losses and alternative shooters. This is a **decision aid**, not a fake precise score or unsupported mathematical kill percentage.

**Rules traps:**
- **+1 Special Die (SD) is not +1 Burst.** Roll the extra die and discard one before resolving; it improves the FtF but does not generate another effective hit. A legal Fireteam activates its members together, **one nominated leader makes the active attack**.
- **Marksmanship** ignores the negative BS MOD from Partial Cover/Nanoscreen, **not** Cover's improved save. It does **not** ignore Mimetism or range. Marksmanship itself is affected by White Noise/Albedo/Reflective interactions; do not silently score it as universal +3.
- **MSV** answers suitable visual MODs; **Albedo and White Noise** can invert the matchup. Mimetism has less value into the correct MSV level.
- **A profile with a personal extra Order** only increases efficiency if that activation is **actually usable** on the shooter or its legal team; it does not become a free pooled Order. Count Fireteam **composition** and mobility restrictions, not just printed bonuses.
- Avoid assigning fake weapon Burst or success probabilities from a video. Current [weapon chart and update](../../rules/4_Ammunition%20and%20Weaponry/Weapon_Chart.md) and explicit active/reactive dice decide a particular exchange.
