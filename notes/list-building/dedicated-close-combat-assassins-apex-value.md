# Dedicated Close-Combat Assassins and Fighters — N5.3 Apex / Points Value

*23 October 2026 · cross-game guide for 300-point Infinity N5.3. **Qualitative judgments, not simulated win rates.** Prices, profiles and legal sectorials from [pinned 2026 sectorial references](../../sectorials/); the latest current roster wins over older recordings. Rules reviewed: [CC Attack](../../rules/3_Combat/CC_Attacks.md), [Martial Arts](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Martial_Arts.md), [Berserk](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Berserk.md), [Guard](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Guard.md), [Natural Born Warrior](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Natural_Born_Warrior.md), [Protheion](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Protheion.md), [Smoke](../../rules/4_Ammunition%20and%20Weaponry/Smoke_Ammunition.md), [Eclipse](../../rules/4_Ammunition%20and%20Weaponry/Eclipse_Ammunition.md).*

**Role boundary:** This list ranks **getting a particular target into legal CC, winning the exchange, doing useful damage or disabling it, and retaining a useful attacker afterward**. It does **not** automatically award high rank to a troop with an excellent CC score that must cross half the table unprotected. For full techniques see [CC/short-assault doctrine](close-combat-and-short-range-assault.md), [Warband trades](warbands-and-cheap-trading-units-apex-value.md), [Airborne/DZ assailants](airborne-and-enemy-dz-attackers-apex-value.md), [Marker deception](marker-state-and-deception-strategy.md), [movement/vision](movement-positioning-and-vision-control.md) and [Orders](order-efficiency.md).

## 30-second recommendation

- **Best single-target assassination apex:** **Shinobu Kitsune44/0.5** (JSA/Ōban): CC25/MA5/**Monofilament**, Hidden Deployment, Infiltration with favorable target-specific profile, Climbing Plus, Smoke. **Oniwaban SMG29/0** is the **best less-expensive kill-the-TAG analogue**; CC24/MA4/Monofilament + E/M CCW, Hidden Deployment and Infiltration. **Saito Tōgan33/0** substitutes **EXP CCW + T2 Boarding Shotgun + Smoke/Bangbomb** for monofilament, effective against different target types.
- **Best raw melee dice for the price:** **Miyamoto Mushashi21/0 Irregular** or **25/0.5 Regular FTO** (Ōban), **CC25/MA5/EXP**. Almost unmatched face-to-face price/performance **once contact is made**; no Hidden Deployment or automatic safe entry, and 21pt version's Irregular Order is not interchangeable with Regular.
- **Best reliable mobile repeated assault:** **Shikami42/0** (JSA/Ōban) — MOV15-5, **Mimetism(-6), Eclipse, Super-Jump, Climbing Plus, Forward Deployment+10, 2W, MA3, AP+DA**, plus **Specialist Operative**. Not as devastating on a pure one-roll Monofilament hit as Shinobu, but reaches, scores and exits more consistently.
- **Best cheap *delivered* assassin:** **Fiday Boarding Shotgun23/0** (Bahram/Haqq, CC23/MA3, **Impersonation, Smoke, DA**) or **Speculo Killer BSG26/1** (Shas/Combined, **Impersonation, Monofilament, Regeneration**). **Hussein Al-Djabel33/0** raises Fiday reliability / target flexibility with CC23 MA4, WIP15 and **Viral CCW**, Climbing Plus. The Speculo's **1 SWC** and lower WIP14 carry a real cost; not always above cheaper Fiday.
- **Best anti-elite *midfield* bully:** **Andromeda30/0** (ALEPH/Steel, **Guard + MA3 + Infiltration + NWI + D-Charges**) wins a *distinct* game: project CC **inside LoF+ZoC without base contact**, force poor ARO commitments and retain Specialist utility. **Caliban BSG24/0** (Shas/Combined, **Camo, FD+20, MA3, Protheion**) is cheaper and can snowball on **VITA** targets but not on **STR**.
- **Best independent full-assault ceiling, not pure assassin value:** **Achilles62/1.5 AP Spitfire / 63/0 Plasma Rifle** (Next Wave) CC24 MA4, 3W, MOV15-10, high BS, EXP CCW; **Ajax39/0 FTO or 45/0 FD+20**, **Hippolyta37/0**, **Sheskiin48/0** and **Eudoros36/0** can also close fights after shooting/Smoke. A 60pt warrior must generate several impactful actions, not just stab one 15pt defender.
- **Best cost-effective fighting infantry:** **Domaru E/M CCW20/0 or AP19/0**, **Teutonic18/0**, **Dāturazi14/0 Regular+Smoke**, **Makaul14/0 Eclipse + Viral/DA**, **Myrmidon16/0 Smoke/Mimetism(-6)/MA3**, **Galwegian5/0** or **Diablo8/0** (cheap Berserk/Trench-Hammer). These are *trade/controller tiers* unless a legal team or geometry provides the approach.
- **Best Guard bubble/counter-assassination:** **Andromeda30** actively; **O-12 Alpha24** defensively. Guard **requires LoF + ZoC**, *does not* form Engaged at distance and **cannot target a non-Unconscious Aerial troop**.
- **TAGs are a different category**: **O-Yoroi, Sphinx, other melee-capable TAGs** are not ranked against a 21pt Musashi or a 29pt Oniwaban. They buy armor, gunfire, STR, Baggage/ancillary and a lot of points; melee is a **fallback/finisher**, not their primary purchased assassination economy.

**Tiers:** S = exceptional role ceiling/value, A = excellent but some target or approach restriction, B = effective when table/roster suits, C = tactical niche. **Apex** ignores some points pressure to ask how many target types one profile can reach/defeat; **value** counts cost *and the chance/orders of actually arriving*. Tier scores are **editorial** and never imply a universal percent chance to kill a TAG.

**Official N5.3 verification:** [Corvus Belli rules update](https://infinityuniverse.com/en/news/infinity-rules-update-5-3) · [CC/multiple troopers](https://infinitythewiki.com/Close_Combat) · [Guard](https://infinitythewiki.com/Guard) · [Berserk](https://infinitythewiki.com/Berserk) · [Natural Born Warrior](https://infinitythewiki.com/Natural_Born_Warrior). The official wiki confirms the N5.3 two-trooper CC participation threshold, Guard LoF+ZoC/Aerial exception, NBW negative-MOD rule and Berserk target-LoF + missed-distance Idle rules.

## 1) Actual lethality: what the melee roll does—and does not say

Current Martial Arts level is **one chosen printed level**, **not** every lower level combined:

| MA | Your CC MOD | Opponent CC MOD | CC dice in an ordinary active B1 duel |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **MA1** | 0 | −3 | 1 |
| **MA2** | +3 | −3 | 1 |
| **MA3** | +3 | −3 | **B1 +1SD**: roll 2, discard 1 |
| **MA4** | +3 | −3 | **B2**: 2 real attack dice |
| **MA5** | +3 | −3 | **B2 +1SD**: roll 3, discard 1 |
| **NBW counter** | The attacker keeps *positive* boosts | NBW defender who is **targeted and declares CC Attack** **ignores negative penalties imposed by that opponent**, including MA, Surprise and weapon CC MODs | NBW does **not** remove MA +3 or +1B/+1SD from the *attacker* |

**Worked, *unopposed* dice comparison, not kill chance:** **CC25/MA5** yields CC **28**, two effective attack dice chosen from three rolls, and critical results on **1–8 and 20** in current N5. Under independent d20, chance of **at least one critical across the three rolled dice ≈83.36%** (before the mandatory discard; an actual FTF can change the outcome). **CC24/MA4** yields CC **27**, B2 and crit faces **1–7 and 20**, giving **64% chance of at least one crit in two rolled dice**. **Critical ≠ guaranteed target death**: ammo, weapon PS, saving rolls, wounds/STR, Immunity and enemy reaction matter. An Mimetism−6 stat is **ranged defense**, not a direct CC modifier.

**Weapon must suit victim:**
- **Monofilament** is the premier **high-cost TAG/HI one-hit threat** (check actual Save/Immunity rather than assuming automatic kill). It does **not** add a ranged gun or safe route.
- **E/M CCW** prefers **Hackable HI/TAG/REM**, where **Isolated/IMM** can disable an otherwise hard target more usefully than raw wounds; E/M does not equal a generic win against nonhackable LI.
- **Viral** is for appropriate **VITA/BTS** targets, not a universal STR tank can-opener.
- **EXP/AP+DA/DA/T2** convert winning the contest into multiple or high-impact Saves; evaluate PS and ARM/BTS matchup, **not** merely CC number.
- **D-Charges** in legal CC/Guard are an excellent armor-target option, **not** the same as an EXP CCW under every profile/MA modifier. Some older podcast statements about damage bonuses refer to older MA rules.
- **Protheion** heals/grows from **Wounds inflicted on VITA models** in CC; **not** automatically from STR; excess wounds beyond Dead state do not generate infinite health.

### Four-order budget: use before ordering a CC assassin

For one target count: **(1) Orders to reach contact / legal Guard position + (2) Orders to block LoF / clear mines + (3) CC Orders expected against *that defender* + (4) Orders to escape or reposition**. Add **lost attacker risk** and next-turn objective need. Example: **Oniwaban in hidden midfield needing 1 Move + 1 CC, perhaps a follow-up** can beat **Musashi21 in own DZ needing 4 Moves + a Smoke enabler** even if Musashi has better pure CC dice. Opposite: if target moves into your defensive line **Musashi21 / Domaru19** may be the best bargain on the table.

**Practical approach success** depends on deployment and enemy AROs. No honest unconditional "Shinobu has an 83% chance of assassinating target" follows from dice math; that 83% is *unopposed critical opportunity* conditional on CC having been reached.

## 6) Guard and counter-CC — separate defensive category

**Guard** is not automatically "best melee": it bypasses **contact** only when the **target is in LoF and ZoC**, lets both participants use CC-contact skills, and can ARO with CC; but **does not physically pin/Engage** distant victim. It is a small local **control bubble**, not an unrestricted ranged Monofilament attack.

| Tier | Counter / price | Why it works | When it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S** | **Andromeda30** (ALEPH/Steel) | Guard + MA3 + D-Charges, NWI, Infiltration: offense and reactive melee-control in a mission corridor | Lacks LoF or facing Aerial, has to expose herself to get first ZoC + LoF, or opponent shoots from outside. |
| **A** | **Alpha24** (O-12) | Guard+MA2/CC21 + DA, **Biometric Visor** counters some impersonator positioning near protected DZ assets | Passive 24pt body with little long-range reach; CC attacker might instead kill Alpha with templates/remote shooting. |
| **A** | **Musashi21, Hatamoto34**, **Ajax39/45** | MA5 huge active/reactive quality (Musashi), **NBW** on Hatamoto/Ajax defeats attacker-applied **MA/Surprise penalty** | No LoF/position to countercharge; NBW does not grant auto-win or stop AP/Monofilament saves. |
| **A** | **Yojimbo19/20, Domaru19/20, Makaul14, Dāturazi14** | Minelayers/Smoke, strong CC or expendable body screens premium shooter from base contact | Screen can be bypassed with Smoke, speculated mines or templates; don't bunch defender/support in one template. |
| **B situational** | **cheap body joining melee / Coordinated assault** | Extra allied models **in base contact with target** can increase real CC Burst; one disposable body can delay a 70pt shooting machine without killing it | **Servant and Fireteam limitations** apply; not all 4 models can physically reach target; Command Token expense, geometry and AROs. |

**Counterplay decision:** **shoot** a dangerous assassin from outside its contact/Guard range, **Discover/Sensor** Hidden/Camo markers with screening, use **Biometric** where appropriate against impersonation, **mine the final approach**, maintain an uncommitted **NBW/Guard** defender and **template watch** to stop single Order multi-kills. Against Guard **keep target out of LoF + ZoC**; **Aerial exception** is narrow, not general "immune to CC".

### Where existing repository notes needed updating, not duplicating
- [CC doctrine](close-combat-and-short-range-assault.md) establishes route geometry, engagement and ganging-up. **This guide adds current precise units/costs, separate apex/value tiers and matchups.**
- [Warband tier](warbands-and-cheap-trading-units-apex-value.md) prioritizes cheap **smoke/trade** (5pt Galwegian, 8pt Diablo, 14pt Makaul/Dāturazi). They stay excellent *value* but do **not** outrank actual Hidden Monofilament assassins on target-removal ceiling.
- [Airborne enemy-DZ tier](airborne-and-enemy-dz-attackers-apex-value.md) prioritizes **Rasyat/Van Zant/Fiday/Speculo** for reaching the enemy rear; it is not a claim Rasyat is a better **CC duelist** than Shinobu/MA5 Musashi.
- Some older [Berserk](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Berserk.md) unofficial commentary describes the Long Skill and FTF correctly but other older discussions imply "automatic Normal-roll trade"; **not N5.3**.
- **Guard** and **Natural Born Warrior** require their own rule conditions; when facing both a monster assassin and cheap screen, it is often optimal to **shoot the screen, CC the undefended shooter, then leave** rather than duel a second monster at all.

## 9) Pick by situation

| Opponent/table/mission | Start with | When to reject |
|---|---|---|
| Enemy expensive TAG/HI with mediocre CC, room/corner reachable from Hidden | **Oniwaban29**, **Shinobu44**, **Speculo26** | Overlapping mines/screen; no close entry; NBW defense/Guard room |
| Enemy isolated 1W specialist / vulnerable medic in DZ | **Fiday23**, **Saito33** (if roster legal), **Rasyat25** via edge | Kill requires five Orders; multiple cheap interceptors |
| Room/objective likely needs 3+ attacks **and scoring** | **Shikami42**, **Hatamoto34**, **Caliban FO26** | List already has too few orders/scorers, hackable HI has no anti-hacker plan |
| Enemy close-combat monster with NBW/Guard | **shoot at range**, mine, E/M, immobilize, multi-model CC only when safe | Assuming MA −3/Suprise remains against reactive NBW; dueling blindly |
| Own DZ exposed to infiltrating assassination | **Musashi21**, **Domaru19/20**, **Alpha Guard24**, **Yojimbo19–20** | Bunching counters around one template or paying 30pt counter for no threatening enemy |
| Need smoke and a cheap door-opening attack | **Makaul14 Eclipse**, **Dāturazi14 Regular Smoke/MA4**, **Myrmidon16**, **Galwegian5**, **Jaguar10** | Enemy MSV into ordinary Smoke, route lacks actual safety |
| First-turn distant CC charge | **Usually don't**. Clear ARO with your gun, attack from new angle, or use a real marker/Impersonation delivery. | Count success only because "CC25" looks impressive. |

**Bottom line:** rank an assassin by **target relevance × achievable contact × winning contest × useful result**, divided by **list points, Orders, resources and survival risk**. In ordinary game plans, a hidden **29pt Oniwaban**, a **21pt Musashi kept as counter-charge** and a **42pt Shikami mission runner** may all be **S-tier**—but for three completely different reasons.
