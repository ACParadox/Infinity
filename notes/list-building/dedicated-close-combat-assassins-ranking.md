## 30-second recommendation — unified ranking

- **Best overall CC investments:** Oniwaban, Chimera + 3 Pupniks, Shinobu, Hatamoto and Dāturazi. The first and third excel at concealed assassination; Chimera and Dāturazi deliver inexpensive pressure; Hatamoto combines elite contact and useful secondary roles.
- **Premium sustained frontline choices:** Shikami, Ajax, Achilles, Ioann Bann and Sheskiin; choose for the actual mission and route, not raw CC alone.
- **Budget (≤22pt):** filter the unified ranking by `Budget`; a 29pt Oniwaban can still be `Value` without being `Budget`.
- **Guard / defensive denial:** Andromeda and Alpha for Guard; Hatamoto, Ajax, Mushashi, Señor Massacre, Valkyrie and Wulvers for contact counter-CC.
- **Role discipline:** Pavel is a Camo/Ohotnik marksman with dangerous AP+T2 melee, not a dedicated CC assassin. Ko Dali, de Fersen, Kunai and Machaon can excel elsewhere.

**One global ranking, different jobs:** a 6pt Shaolin can have exceptional efficiency without matching Achilles' repeated-combat ceiling. Guard is distinct from actual contact. ## 1) Actual lethality: what the melee roll does—and does not say

Current Martial Arts level is **one chosen printed level**, **not** every lower level combined:

| MA | Your CC MOD | Opponent CC MOD | CC dice in an ordinary active B1 duel |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **MA1** | 0 | −3 | 1 |
| **MA2** | +3 | −3 | 1 |
| **MA3** | +3 | −3 | **B1 +1SD**: roll 2, discard 1 |
| **MA4** | +3 | −3 | **B2**: 2 real attack dice |
| **MA5** | +3 | −3 | **B2 +1SD**: roll 3, discard 1 |
| **NBW counter** | The attacker keeps *positive* boosts | NBW defender who is **targeted and declares CC Attack** **ignores negative penalties imposed by that opponent**, including MA, Surprise and weapon CC MODs | NBW does **not** remove MA +3 or +1B/+1SD from the *attacker* |

**Worked, *unopposed* dice comparison, not kill chance:** **CC25/MA5** yields CC **28**, two effective attack dice chosen from three rolls, and critical results on **1–8 and 20** in current N5. Under independent d20, chance of **at least one critical across the three rolled dice ≈83.36%** (before the mandatory discard; an actual FTF can change the outcome). **CC24/MA4** yields CC **27**, B2 and crit faces **1–7 and 20**, giving **64% chance of at least one crit in two rolled dice**. **Critical ≠ guaranteed target death**: ammo, weapon PS, saving rolls, wounds/STR, Immunity and enemy reaction matter. An Mimetism−6 stat is **ranged defense**, not a direct CC modifier.

**Weapon must suit victim:**
- **Monofilament** is an exceptional **multi-Wound/STR TAG/HI one-hit *kill* threat**, not an automatic hit-to-death conversion. Its **PS8, ARM=0, one SR** means an ordinary uncancelled hit kills on a roll of 9–20 (**60%**, no crit/save modifiers/immunity). **Low printed PS can make DA, AP+T2 or EXP preferable against many 1–2 Wound targets.** Immunity (ARM) also removes its ARM=0 and State: Dead traits. It does **not** improve delivery or FTF odds.
- **E/M CCW** prefers **Hackable HI/TAG/REM**, where **Isolated/IMM** can disable an otherwise hard target more usefully than raw wounds; E/M does not equal a generic win against nonhackable LI.
- **Viral** is for appropriate **VITA/BTS** targets, not a universal STR tank can-opener.
- **EXP/AP+DA/DA/T2** convert winning the contest into multiple or high-impact Saves; evaluate PS and ARM/BTS matchup, **not** merely CC number.
- **D-Charges** in legal CC/Guard are an excellent armor-target option, **not** the same as an EXP CCW under every profile/MA modifier. Some older podcast statements about damage bonuses refer to older MA rules.
- **Protheion** heals/grows from **Wounds inflicted on VITA models** in CC; **not** automatically from STR; excess wounds beyond Dead state do not generate infinite health.

### Why some competitive players prefer low-PS DA/AP+T2 over Monofilament

**N5.3 check, independently verified from rules and unit profiles:** The generic [CC weapon chart](https://infinitythewiki.com/Weapon_Chart) lists **PS=8** for Monofilament, DA, AP+T2 and most melee weapons, but **unit-specific** entries override the generic PS: [Crux Father-Knight](https://www.human-sphere.com/Father_Knight) has **DA CC Weapon (PS=5)** and [Pavel McMannus](https://human-sphere.com/Pavel_Aleksei_McMannus) has **AP+T2 CC Weapon (PS=5)**. [Official modifier explanation](https://infinitythewiki.com/Rolls): an explicit Weapon (PS=5) uses PS5 rather than the generic weapon-chart PS. **Lower PS is stronger** because the defender passes an SR on d20 ≤ (effective ARM + PS + applicable MODs).

**Conditional saving-roll comparison:** one ordinary, uncancelled melee hit, no critical, no Cover/SR MOD, no immunity. Percentages refer to **different outcomes**, as labeled; they are *not* whole CC win/assassination probabilities.

| Target ARM | Monofilament **PS8, ARM=0**: immediate Dead (1 SR) | Crux **DA PS5**: ≥1 Wound (2 SRs) | Crux DA PS5: **2 Wounds** (both SRs fail) | Pavel **AP+T2 PS5**: **2 Wounds** (1 SR, ARM/2) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | **60%** | **84%** | **36%** | **65%** |
| 6 | **60%** | **69.75%** | **20.25%** | **60%** |
| 9 | **60%** | **51%** | **9%** | **50%** |

**Example, ARM3:** Monofilament passes its save on 1–8, so the target dies on 9–20 = **60%**. Crux's DA PS5 makes **two ARM+5 = 8** saves: each fails 60%, so at least one fails **1 − (0.4²) = 84%**, but both fail only **0.6² = 36%**. Pavel's AP halves ARM3 to **2** (round up), then adds PS5 for a save of **7**; it fails **65%** of the time, inflicting **2 Wounds** through T2. See [Saving Rolls](https://infinitythewiki.com/Saving_Rolls), [T2](https://infinitythewiki.com/T2) and the [ammo summary](https://infinitythewiki.com/Ammunition_Summary_Chart).

**Practical matchup decision:** Against many **1-Wound/VITA** models, low-PS DA removes a target from effectiveness more often after a hit than Monofilament (though results differ for Dogged/NWI, Shock and other states). Against **2-Wound** models, Pavel's AP+T2 can inflict both required Wounds on a single failed save; Crux needs **both** DA rolls to fail for two Wounds. Against a **3+ STR TAG or high-Wound elite**, **Monofilament's instant Dead** remains strategically special: two ordinary Wounds often do not eliminate that model. At very high ARM, Monofilament's 60% ARM-ignoring death chance becomes more attractive again. **Immunity (ARM)** strips Monofilament's ARM=0 and State: Dead traits, so verify the target's abilities before relying on it ([official Immunity examples](https://infinitythewiki.com/Immunity)).

**Do not compare the columns as equal 'kill chances':**

- the Crux percentages report wounds, Pavel reports 2 Wounds, and Monofilament reports outright Dead.
- Criticals generate additional saves; the **extra T2 critical save only inflicts 1 extra Wound if failed**.
- Actual value further depends on **winning FTF** (Crux **CC22/MA2**; Pavel **CC23/MA3**; Shinobu **CC25/MA5**), being able to reach contact, Orders, risk, and whether the remaining model can score.

**A profile with PS=5 does not automatically outrank a Hidden/Impersonation Monofilament specialist.**

**Evidence boundary:** This is a **rules-backed check of the objection** that Monofilament's PS8 can make it less attractive than low-PS alternatives. A particular YouTube speaker/timestamp making the comparison was **not independently identified**; do not attribute the precise comparison to an unnamed video.

### Delivery checks — numerical odds are **not** whole-assassination odds

| **Required** unopposed check, before ARO / movement / killing | Chance on one attempt | Practical meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Shinobu **Infiltration (PH=19)** roll, if the chosen placement actually calls for one | **95%** | High chance of *placement* only. Safe ordinary legal deployment may require no roll; opponent screening still matters. |
| Oniwaban **Infiltration (PH=18)** roll, where one is required | **90%** | Cheap hidden placement, **not** a 90% chance to kill a TAG or reach actual base contact. |
| One **PH14** unopposed Smoke roll in an allowed declaration | **70%** | Smoke success helps deny LoF; one failed placement can consume another Order or alter the route. |
| **Two independent PH14 Smoke rolls both succeeding first try** | **49%** (=0.70²) | Demonstrates how multiple required gate rolls compound; practical smoke can be face-to-face and can succeed after repeats. |
| Unmodified **PH11 Combat Jump** (e.g., applicable Yuan Yuan profile) | **55%** | A risky landing alone; safe legal Parachutist entry can be more reliable, but usually further from the target. |
| **Guard** target already in LoF **and** ZoC | No delivery die **to establish contact**, because contact is not required | Still must reach the 8-inch bubble/line of sight and survive opposition; its **CC Attack is still rolled**. |

There is **no universal percentage of reaching CC**: table layout, number of reactive models, markers/Discover, Stealth, Smoke, mines, Order pool, and whether the defender can Dodge are conditional variables. For a named target, count **approach Orders and individual mandatory checks first**, then compute target-specific CC Face-to-Face and Saving Rolls—not just high-CC crits.

### Four-order budget: use before ordering a CC assassin

For one target count: **(1) Orders to reach contact / legal Guard position + (2) Orders to block LoF / clear mines + (3) CC Orders expected against *that defender* + (4) Orders to escape or reposition**. Add **lost attacker risk** and next-turn objective need. Example: **Oniwaban in hidden midfield needing 1 Move + 1 CC, perhaps a follow-up** can beat **Musashi21 in own DZ needing 4 Moves + a Smoke enabler** even if Mushashi has better pure CC dice. Opposite: if target moves into your defensive line **Musashi21 / Domaru19** may be the best bargain on the table.

**Practical approach success** depends on deployment and enemy AROs. No honest unconditional "Shinobu has an 83% chance of assassinating target" follows from dice math; that 83% is *unopposed critical opportunity* conditional on CC having been reached.

## 3) Unified close-combat ranking

_Consolidated 26 November 2026 from the five existing ranked tables (assassination, frontline, budget, Guard and contact counter-assault). Editorial reranking, not a new Army audit or measured win rates._

**Scope:** credible CC delivery and useful target removal/disabling, repeated contact pressure or meaningful defensive CC. This is not a ranking of gunfighters merely carrying CCWs. Compare actual target relevance, approach Orders, contest and weapon effect, durability, total points/SWC and useful secondary roles. Suggested editorial priorities: CC performance and target relevance 30%, delivery/order efficiency 25%, points/SWC 25%, durability 10%, role compression 10%. These are priorities, not calculated scores. S+ = exceptional overall investment; S = outstanding; A+ = excellent; A = strong; B = situational. Rank descending within tiers; role-specific matchups can reverse the order.

**Tags (non-exclusive; profile-specific):**

- `Apex`: retained from former assassin/frontline ceiling rankings or standout defensive CC options; not a separate tier.
- `Value`: former budget-ranked profiles or particularly efficient relative-cost choices.
- `Unique`: unusually differentiated delivery, multi-body, weapon or control combination.
- `Toolbox`: useful role compression beyond CC, e.g. actual scoring, command, vision control, gunfighting or area defense.
- `Classified Runner`: meaningful evidenced Classified execution on the named option; see [Mission Specialists](mission-specialists-and-button-pushers-ranking.md).
- `Airborne`: actual Combat Jump or Parachutist on the named loadout.
- `Fireteam`: significant linked role or explicitly supported FTO/triad/Wildcard option; check actual roster.
- `Budget`: relevant option costs ≤22 points, including the printed multi-body package; does not apply to a more expensive alternate.

- `Anti-Tech`: credible response to enemy Mimetism, Camouflage/Hidden, Impersonation or visor/Marksmanship technology (Eclipse, Disco, Sensor, Biometric, MSV or Albedo as applicable). Ordinary Smoke, E/M, Monofilament and personal Mimetism alone do not qualify.
- `Mod-Stacking`: meaningful ranged BS asymmetry (exceptional Mimetism, BS Attack modifiers, Nanoscreen or conditional Albedo/SD). These do not modify CC rolls; MA and NBW alone do not qualify.

## 4) Guard and counter-CC — defensive mechanisms

**Guard is not the same as winning a base-contact duel.** It permits CC against a target within actual **LoF and ZoC**, without requiring base contact, and can be a reactive control bubble. A Guard model is not automatically the best **counter-assassin** in base contact. **Natural Born Warrior (NBW)** matters when the defender is targeted by a CC Attack and itself declares CC Attack: it ignores negative MODs imposed by that attacker; it does **not** cancel the attacker's positive MA bonuses.

**Screening remains relevant:** Dāturazi, Makaul, Yojimbo and inexpensive sacrificial bodies can prevent a premium CC attacker reaching the protected asset without themselves winning a duel. Only include other models in a CC at the actual N5.3 participation threshold; Fireteam/Peripheral/Coordinated actions do **not** automatically confer unrestricted +B.

**Counterplay:** Shoot dangerous assassins from outside contact/Guard range; layer mines, template coverage and Discover/Sensor around valuable targets. Against Guard, stay outside **LoF+ZoC**. Against NBW, do not rely on Surprise, MA negative MODs or PARA penalties to win the CC Face-to-Face; compare the defender's armor and the attacker's weapon.

## 7) Pick by situation

| Opponent/table/mission | Start with | When to reject |
|---|---|---|
| Enemy expensive TAG/HI with mediocre CC, room/corner reachable from Hidden | **Oniwaban29**, **Shinobu44**, **Speculo26**; compare **Pavel33 AP+T2 PS5** if 2 Wounds suffice | Overlapping mines/screen; no close entry; NBW defense/Guard room; **Monofilament only kills on a failed ARM=0+PS8 save** |
| Enemy isolated 1W specialist / vulnerable medic in DZ | **Fiday23**, **Saito33** (if roster legal), **Rasyat25** via edge | Kill requires five Orders; multiple cheap interceptors |
| Room/objective likely needs 3+ attacks **and scoring** | **Shikami42**, **Hatamoto34**, **Caliban FO26** | List already has too few orders/scorers, hackable HI has no anti-hacker plan |
| Enemy close-combat monster with NBW/Guard | **shoot at range**, mine, E/M, immobilize, multi-model CC only when safe | Assuming MA −3/Suprise remains against reactive NBW; dueling blindly |
| Own DZ exposed to infiltrating assassination | **Musashi21**, **Domaru19/20**, **Alpha Guard24**, **Yojimbo19–20** | Bunching counters around one template or paying 30pt counter for no threatening enemy |
| Need smoke and a cheap door-opening attack | **Makaul14 Eclipse**, **Dāturazi14 Regular Smoke/MA4**, **Myrmidon16**, **Galwegian5**, **Jaguar10** | Enemy MSV into ordinary Smoke, route lacks actual safety |
| First-turn distant CC charge | **Usually don't**. Clear ARO with your gun, attack from new angle, or use a real marker/Impersonation delivery. | Count success only because "CC25" looks impressive. |

**Bottom line:** rank an assassin by **target relevance × achievable contact × winning contest × useful result**, divided by **list points, Orders, resources and survival risk**. **Check printed weapon PS overrides and actual kill/Wound threshold before giving Monofilament automatic preference** over low-PS DA/AP+T2 alternatives. In ordinary game plans, a hidden **29pt Oniwaban**, a **21pt Mushashi kept as counter-charge** and a **42pt Shikami mission runner** can all be excellent investments—but for three completely different reasons.
