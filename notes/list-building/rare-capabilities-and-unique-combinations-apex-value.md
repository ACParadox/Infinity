**Question:** which units deliver a *qualitatively different answer*, not simply another +1 BS, because of a unique mechanic (G:Jumper, Protheion, RemDriver, Cyberplug) or an otherwise uncommon **combination** (Albedo+MSV, Eclipse+airborne/mono, two-role support, FO plus an independent FO partner)?

## 1. What the unusual rules *actually* do

| Mechanic | Unique leverage | Limits that determine value |
|---|---|---|
| **G:Jumper** | Deploy **2–3 Proxies** in distinct positions and choose which body acts each Order. One Proxy dying doesn't eliminate the Trooper while another is viable. | **One Trooper slot / one Order**, all same Combat Group. During each enemy Order, **only one Proxy can receive a normal unrestricted ARO**; other AROing Proxies **Dodge/Reset only**. Price and VP account for **every selected Proxy**. **No free second specialist Order**. |
| **Protheion** | In silhouette contact, **CC Attacks / Coup de Grâce** against an enemy with **VITA** heal existing damage first, then can add **up to +2 VITA over the original value**. | **Does not feed from STR models**, ordinary shooting or just sitting near an Unconscious model. A CC attack must really inflict a legal Wound or Coup de Grâce; overkill saves do not add extra growth. Reaching a VITA victim has an Order/ARO cost. |
| **RemDriver** | During deployment apply owner's printed **parenthesized MODs to one already-deployed eligible REM**, without an upkeep Order and with no stated range limit. | **Not a blanket +BS to all REMs**; only the actual printed modifiers, **one chosen REM**, max one RemDriver token per REM. Token removed at end of Order when driver enters a **Null state**; protect driver. **Different from TAGCom.** |
| **Peripheral Synchronized / Control** | Extra bodies create ARO forks, additional attack origins, objective routes while controller spends activation; **Control PuppetBots** have special activation/ravaged chassis. | Peripheral is not an independently generated Regular Order simply because its row has a points cost. **Being FO on the controller does not magically grant FO to the Peripheral.** |
| **Peripheral Cyberplug — N5.3** | Cyberplug may use **Connected** or **Autonomous** profiles chosen at start of each Order/ARO; **Autonomous may spend Order-pool Orders and ARO independently**, so it's *not* generic Synchronized. | Max **2** Cyberplugs/controller; max **one Connected** at a time; any other Autonomous. Connected controller Idles except permitted Doctor/Engineer exception. Check Fireteam Controller rules. |
| **Albedo + MSV** | Two **independent** anti-vision levers: MSV improves matchup against Mimetism/Smoke; Albedo penalizes enemy **MSV/Marksmanship** threat. | No automatic “ignore all vision”; not automatically shooting through White Noise/Eclipse; Albedo has a narrow matchup, and enemy can win by templates, normal guns/angles or no visors. |
| **Eclipse + mono/AD/fast CC** | Eclipse screens an approach **even against ordinary MSV**, while Monofilament/CC/AD threatens a target conventional Smoke attackers cannot safely approach. | Must **win grenade throw** and use orders to get from blind route to useful target. Enemy templates, Guard/CC counters, Mines and safe DZ deployment still work; **Rasyat's AD roll/insertion is a real cost**. |
| **FO + multiple bodies** | **Drummer + Drumbot-5 both FO**: synchronized pair can declare FO through two different LoF/target angles in one Order; FO controller + non-FO bot (Sirius/Coyote BambaBot) is a *different* fork. | Both must declare the same Short Skill and meet their own requirements; **Coherency** and **one shared Order** apply. In N5.3 FO is a LoF WIP BS Attack, does not use Repeater and directly causes Targeted on success (no saving roll). |
| **MSV3 + elite attacker** | MSV3 can automatically succeed on a valid Discover attempt against Camouflaged State, ignore Surprise Attack MODs, and in the Active Turn attack a visible CAMO Marker directly without Discovering first. Aquila/Asura/Charontid make this an offensive rather than merely detection tool. | **Direct BS Attack against CAMO still applies the Mimetism MOD printed on the Marker**, even for MSV3. It does not see through Total Cover, make a Marker target legal without LoF, or bypass White Noise/Albedo. Only the **Charontid's selected MSV3 options** have MSV3: do not give it to the Sepsitor Plus/HDplus options. |
| **MSV + Marksmanship** | MSV neutralizes Mimetism/visibility problems while Marksmanship ignores **the negative BS MOD** from Partial Cover/Nanoscreen. **Atalanta MSV2 + Marksmanship** and **Rounder MSV1 + Marksmanship** can pressure targets whose normal modifiers stack. | Marksmanship **does not remove the Cover bonus to Saving Rolls**, and both MSV and Marksmanship are vulnerable to White Noise, Reflective and Albedo. Atalanta **38/1.5 Hidden Deployment** and **41/1.5 Total Reaction** are different options. |
| **BS Attack (-3) + Burst / SR penalties** | **Kriza Boracs HMG45/1.5**, **Go-Pod HRL32/1.5** stack BS Attack (-3) with BS Attack (+1B); **Go-Pod's HRL option** also gains BS Attack (SR-1). **Rudras K1 MMR35/0** stacks BS Attack (-3) with BS Attack (SR-1) and K1. | BS Attack (-3) works in **Face-to-Face Rolls**, not as -3 to every opposing action; **Warhorse** ignores it. BS Attack (+1B) is Active Turn only; BS Attack (SR-1) changes **Saving Rolls**, not hit chance. Go-Pod's 33/0 MULTI Rifle is *not* the SR-1 option. |
| **Neurocinetics + amplified ARO weapon** | Neurocinetics allows the **weapon's full Burst against one target in the Reactive Turn**. **Sin-Eater MULTI Sniper + Marksmanship28/1**, **Black A.I.R. MSV2/Mimetism MULTI Sniper33/1.5**, **Drumbot-3 Feuerbach package40/1**, **Sunduqbut Feuerbach47/1.5**, **Vystrel Portable Autocannon32/1.5** offer different rare defenses. | All BS weapons are **B1 during the Active Turn**; B modifiers for Neurocinetics apply only Reactively. ARO full Burst goes **against one target** and remains vulnerable to flanking, Smoke/White Noise or bad range. Drumbot-3 is synchronized, not a standalone 40-point bot. |
| **HRMC + Albedo / protection / extra body** | HRMC gives **B5 AP or Shock** in standard shooting and an alternative **B1 DA** anti-materiel mode. **Overdron HRMC62/1.5 + Albedo(-6)/Tactical Awareness**, **Yān Huǒ HRMC47/1.5 + Immunity(ARM)/Warhorse**, and **Drummer + HRMC Drumbot-2 package42/1.5** combine this rare cannon with different survival/order or multiple-origin tools. | HRMC is **not AP+DA at B5**: modes are exclusive, choose one for the Burst. Overdron anti-visor MOD is situational; Yān Huǒ has no Albedo; Drumbot is a Peripheral and consumes the controller's activation, not a free extra Order. |
| **Plasma + atypical delivery / ARO / concealment** | Plasma forces **one ARM and one BTS Saving Roll per hit**, with an alternate Blast mode. **Anathematic Plasma + Camo/Hidden/Mimetism(-6)**, **Q-Drone Plasma + Total Reaction/Mimetism(-3)24/1**, **Overdron Plasma Sniper(+1B)59/1.5**, **Skyhound Plasma Carbine +1B/Mimetism(-6)33/0**, and **Vector Operator MSV2/Mimetism(-3)/jet-mobility Plasma Sniper29/1.5** are distinct packages. | ARM+BTS is **two possible failed saves, not two guaranteed Wounds**. Do not combine the Anathematic's separate Sepsitor versus Hacker options or mix Overdron HRMC with its Plasma Sniper profile. Plasma does **not** automatically ignore Immunity (Enhanced). |
| **K1 / Viral + cover-ignoring or vision tools** | **White Banner Yín-Shé K1 Sniper27/.5** has **Marksmanship + Albedo(-6)**; **Rudras K1 MMR35/0** carries BS Attack(-3)/SR-1; **Black A.I.R. K1 MMR FO29/.5** adds MSV2; **Odalisque Viral MMR26/0** and **Loup-Garou Viral Sniper24/1** add Marksmanship. K1 bypasses printed ARM via ARM=0; Viral pressures BTS. | K1 **does not ignore partial-cover Saving-Roll bonuses**, and the Yín-Shé K1 Sniper's Marksmanship is an option, not a blanket skill on every Yín-Shé. Viral is not Plasma; it does not force simultaneous ARM and BTS saves. |

## 4. Matchups: when a rare combination wins

| Situation | Prefer | Reason and failure case |
|---|---|---|
| **Scenario asks specialist work on two distant sides** | Posthumans Mk.1/Mk.5, Coyote + legal BambaDroid FO, PuppetBot FO | Choose existing body closest to task. **Posthuman only gives one Order**, but it does not have to walk from one end to another. **Drummer+Drumbot-5** gives genuinely **two FO attack origins in one synchronized Order**; Coyote+BambaDroid are separate units, not magic shared FO activation |
| **Premium VITA infantry / clustered Unconscious bodies** | Caliban / Ko Dali / Sheskiin / Samaritan | CC victory can heal/create up to +2 VITA; Coup de Grâce after kill can add further value. **No gain against STR REM/TAG.** Prioritize safe corpse access, not 4 Orders to farm a guarded model |
| **Opponent fields lots of MSV2 and Mimetism** | Black Friar, Satrah, Bào, Léi Gōng | Each has MSV **and** Albedo, enabling counter-camo offense and defense vs visor retaliation; **Albedo doesn't remove every normal ARO** |
| **Need to defeat MSV that is guarding a Smoke corridor** | White Banner Yín-Shé/Léi Gōng plus Shaolin Smoke, or Albedo gun | **Smoke + Albedo** forces visor defenders to solve two threats; a no-MSV opposing army reduces value |
| **Dangerous rooftop button / enemy cannot see through Eclipse** | Shikami or Kinnara | Eclipse + unusual mobility + specialist gets across and scores. Cheap Makaul does same *smoke screen* with no Super-Jump |
| **Enemy backline attack but must obscure extraction after landing** | Rasyat 25/0 Combat Jump+Eclipse | Drop/BG approach then screen next movement; if target is accessible without Eclipse a cheaper AD model saves resources |
| **Have one high-quality TR/attack REM** | Apsaras RemDriver or Pilot-X if bots also matter | Free source-specific MOD on chosen REM; 20pt driver must not be exposed for nothing. No stacking, nothing to buff if target REM dies |
| **Mission and board favor 3–4 bodies acting in concert** | Chimera/Pupniks, Drummer/Drumbot, Puppetactica | Synchronized/Control/FO capabilities create multiple angles and trade value; **not equal to three independent Orders** |
| **Need independent robotic screen that can survive controller loss/operate afar** | Next Wave Cyberplug | N5.3 **Autonomous** profile is distinct from Connected; don't treat it as old linked servant |
| **No enemy MSV, no VITA targets, low REM support** | **Ordinary inexpensive specialist/gun/warband** | The rare skill you paid for has no live target: prefer cheap regular Order + mission completion instead |

## 6. Five-minute list test

1. **Name the exact unusual interaction**: G:Jumper positions? Protheion vs VITA? Albedo vs *enemy* MSV? Eclipse vs uncovered lane? RemDriver MOD to *this* REM? What is the winning next action?
2. **Check profile exclusivity**: Ko Dali FD vs Combat Jump; Kinnara Infiltration Minelayer vs Eclipse Parachutist; Shikami's 42/47 gun differences; Caliban Engineer vs CoC vs FO; Black Friar anti-IMP option vs cheap Thunderbolt; Drakios FTO loses Kydoimos.
3. **Count the complete package**: Posthuman at least 2 Proxies; Puppet Master + PuppetBots; Pilot-X + RacerBots; Coyote + independently bought BambaDroid; Drummer already includes own bot; Cyberplug actual deployed elements.
4. **Count Orders, AROs, scoring** rather than bodies. One G:Jumper Trooper gives one Order; Synchronized peripherals don't independently generate it; some cyberplugs can spend the pool autonomously under N5.3.
5. **Choose the cheap ordinary alternative when the rare matchup does not exist.** The 16pt Bào, 24pt Kinnara, 13pt Mk.1 and 24pt Caliban only earn their rarity premium if their second function occurs.
