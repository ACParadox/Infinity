# Objective Holding / Zone Domination / Carried Objectives — N5.3 Apex & Value

**Independent defensive bodies versus shared activations:** [N5.3 Peripheral and Multi-Body apex/value](peripheral-synchronized-multibody-apex-value.md) distinguishes **Sirius+SiriusBot, Drummer+FO Drumbot, Raptor+Devabot, Puppetactica pickets and multi-Proxy Posthumans**, which can occupy different real physical places but do **not each produce pooled Regular Orders**. Count costs/Null state separately for zone control.

_23 October 2026 · whole-game **qualitative** rankings, not measured tournament win rates. Current Sep 2026 N5.3 profiles, AVA and legal Fireteams are sourced from [sectorial roster files](../../sectorials/); dated video/podcast transcripts supply tactical opinions, not roster authority. These cross-faction examples **cannot** be combined in one normal list._

**Different win conditions, different winners.** A **zone dominator** adds surviving **Victory Points** inside a scoring area; a **carrier** must be an **eligible Specialist** that collects, retreats and survives with an item; an **objective defender** prevents enemy interaction with guns, mines, hacking, templates, and layered positions. Never rank them as if the best TAG automatically is the best Supply Box carrier.

## 1. Exact scoring & survival rules — the traps

| Mission element | Correct N5.3 ruling | Practical consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Domination: quadrants** | At **end of each Game Round**, score relative **Victory Points**, not number of models or number of specialists. Only Troopers represented by Models/Markers qualify, **not Null models**, mines, Repeater tokens or other equipment. More than half of the base must lie in the quadrant. [Official Domination](https://infinitythewiki.com/Domination). | Put a cheap model just over a boundary *only* when it flips an actual VP differential; a 49pt TAG is 49VP of pressure if it is on table and non-Null. One strategically parked model may beat three irrelevant kills. |
| **Supplies: pick up/control** | Eligible **Specialist in Silhouette contact** uses a **Short Skill with no Roll** to pick up; **one box per Specialist**. At game end it must be a non-Null **Model** carrying the box, **not in Silhouette contact with an enemy Model**. [Official Supplies](https://infinitythewiki.com/Supplies). | Getting it is different from **keeping** it. Enemy contact can deny control even without killing the carrier. Retreat, hide from easy combat, defend final nearby base positions. |
| **Marker & Hidden Deployment** | A carried Supply Box belongs to a **Model, not a Marker**; Hidden not on table normally cannot contest a zone before reveal. | Infiltration/Hidden gets to the crate; once carrying, do **not** credit ongoing Camo/Hidden protection. |
| **G:Jumper Proxies** | **2–3 selected Proxy models**, one Trooper/one generated Regular Order. One unrestricted ARO among reacting Proxies per enemy Order; other reacting Proxies Dodge/Reset. Proxy Costs counted separately for Victory Points. | A 26/.5 Mk.1+Mk.5 package supplies distributed footprint and two different specialist positions, **not two Orders**; count the actual surviving Proxy/mission state in the appropriate area. |
| **Peripherals / Ancillary** | Connected/synchronized/Control/Ancillary have distinct activation, scoring and VP provisions. **Check points attributed to each printed body**, no double-counting controller total. Ancillary not deployed has a special FAQ rule for *Killed*; may not silently count as already in a quadrant. | A Drummer+Drumbot or Sirius+SiriusBot can defend different sides of an objective, but **Coherency, Null, and controller safety** determine whether the screen survives. |
| **Shasvastii Embryo** | **Domination specifically includes Shasvastii models in Shasvastii Embryo state** alongside Normal. [Domination](https://infinitythewiki.com/Domination). | Valuable *mission-specific* staying power even after opponent neutralizes a Shasvastii model; **do not extrapolate to every objective/carry mission**. |
| **Baggage** | Normal N5.3 Baggage recharges eligible Disposable equipment in ZoC. **Extra zone-VP value, or carrying extra items, applies only when a scenario explicitly provides it**; ordinary Domination lists no automatic +20VP for Baggage. [Baggage rule](https://infinitythewiki.com/Baggage). | Ikadron 11/0, FuzzBot/Probot/Pangguling ~8/0 are efficient **Regular Orders and corner guards**; in mission-specific Superiority rules they may score as higher VP, but **do not copy the bonus into Domination**. |
| **Dogged / NWI / Regeneration / Transmutation** | These are different ways to stay alive, not all equivalent to remaining non-Null at scoring time. | **Dogged is not reliable end-round holding** if the troop expires before scoring; NWI/Regeneration/Enhanced Immunity can keep a normal scoring model on table; verify exact States Phase and source skill. |
| **Zone versus carrier** | Objective protection from Mines/Repeater/DTW is useful without Specialist status; expensive non-specialist TAGs dominate quadrants but cannot pick up regular Supplies. | Build at least **a retriever, an actual non-Null carrier, and a separate removal-cost defense**; do not send TAG to perform a skill its profile lacks. |

### What counts as durable *during the opponent's turn*

- **Survival** = actual surviving effective VITA/STR, Mimetism/cover, Immunity/Firewall, Engineer/Doctor access, and ability to Dodge/Guts into Total Cover. A W3 TAG in view of 3 enemy attack vectors is less likely to survive than a 25pt NWI specialist already in Total Cover.
- **Deny recovery** = opponent must defeat *more than one* independent layer: corner mine/DTW → Flash/Repeater/KHD/ARO → actual carrier in total cover. **Guard the enemy's pickup/engage square**, not a scenic irrelevant firelane.
- **Carry safely** = enough movement to move, pick up (Short Skill), retreat from LoF/ZoC, and resist or evade a final-turn **base contact challenge**. Regular specialists outrank Irregular/non-specialists even if latter is physically tougher.
- **Distributed threat** = hidden/infiltrated troops already near two zones; synchronized duo covers two angles; two Proxy bodies cover separate places despite sharing Order. **Independent, cheap Regular units** can still be superior because each has its own Order and different reactive restrictions.

## 6. 20-second decision test

1. **Which scoring check?** End of each round **VP majority**, last-player-turn pickup, or end-game **non-Null Specialist box holder**? Check actual scenario, **Baggage** and **Shasvastii** exceptions.
2. **What do I need: reach, survive, or stop enemy touching me?** Shikami (reach), Karakuri (absorb), Asawira/Namurr (recover/immunity), Moran/Devabot (defend). Don't buy only one function for all three.
3. **How many real points survive in the zone, and what flips it?** A 31pt Karakuri may beat a 20pt deficit without exposing a 93pt Sphinx. Count separate Proxy/peripheral cost contributions legally, never duplicate parent total.
4. **Can the enemy contest without killing?** Supplies control fails if enemy Model in Silhouette contact; plan a guarded *contact square* and exit path, not simply Suppressive Fire into an irrelevant street.
5. **Which unit can actually pick up?** Specialist required; TAG/guard normally can't. A camouflaged Specialist must stay a visible Model to carry the box; check legal handoff.
6. **Survival through opponent turn:** model **Hacking Area / KHD / E/M / enemy CC / templates / ARO removal**, and whether separate allies can still recapture or protect if first holder dies.
