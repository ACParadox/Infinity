# Zone Domination & Protected Area Denial — N5.3 Apex / Value (non-TAG)

_11 November 2026 · whole-game qualitative rankings, not measured win rates. Non-TAG profiles only; exact costs/loadouts follow the main-branch [sectorial roster references](../../sectorials). Apex means strongest overall job performance; Value rewards denial/survival gained per point and SWC. Cross-faction examples cannot be combined into one normal army._

**Scope:** Two jobs only: **zone domination** (keep enough surviving troop Victory Points inside the scoring area) and **protected area denial** (make the relevant room, console, doorway, or last approach hazardous to enter). The same troop can excel at both, but a disposable mine-layer is not automatically a good VP holder. **No TAGs**, including Maximus in his TAG profile, appear in either ranking.

**Key distinctions before ranking:**

- **Domination checks VP, not bodies.** At the end of each Game Round, only qualifying Troopers represented by Models or Markers count, not Null troopers or equipment such as Mines; more than half the base must be in the quadrant. The mission expressly allows Shasvastii-Embryo to count. Consult the [N5.3 Domination scenario](https://infinitythewiki.com/Domination); do not assume a generic Baggage VP bonus.
- **Survival priority:** multiple W/STR or NWI, ARM/BTS and Immunities, Mimetism/cover, ability to retreat to Total Cover, and recovery support. A low-point body can be unusually efficient, but its VP contribution is still only its legal scoring value. Dogged does not guarantee a non-Null model at end-round scoring.
- **Denial priority:** a surviving guard covering the **actual entry/interaction square**, with overlapping threats such as **Guard**, E/Marat, Riotstopper, HFT, Shock/E/M Mines, perimeter weapons, or a supported Repeater network. Count what begins on the table versus what requires Orders to set up.
- **Guard is not a free omnidirectional melee zone:** the target must be in both **LoF and ZoC**; Guard permits CC without Silhouette contact, including as a suitable ARO. It does not apply against a non-Unconscious **Aerial** troop. See the [N5.3 Guard rule](https://infinitythewiki.com/Guard).
- **Minelayer ≠ carried mine/Mine Dispenser:** only a valid Minelayer option can preposition its permitted deployable at deployment; otherwise spend the relevant Orders. A Shock Mine or E/M Mine token never adds VP to a quadrant. See [Minelayer](https://infinitythewiki.com/Minelayer).
- **No double-counting synchronized bodies.** Peripheral and G:Jumper activation/scoring constraints still apply; a Raptor+Devabot, Rasail+Chaksa or other package does not place the full parent cost in two separate quadrants. See [multi-body mechanics](peripheral-synchronized-multibody-apex-value.md).

## Tactical verdict

**Best non-TAG heavy zone anchors:** **Gamma** for maximum durable shooting/VP, **Asura** when hacking is operationally relevant, **Jamie Handler** for forward Guard and durability, **Karakuri** for repeatable three-Structure holding at only 31pts, and **Rasail** for cheap multi-body close control. A cheap K2 is not a substitute for 30–60 surviving Victory Points, however good its removal tax.

**Best protected doors and objectives:** **Jamie Handler** if Guard can see the entry, **Raptor/Devabot** or **Rasail/Chaksa** for two template-relevant angles, **Karakuri** for surviving multiple attacks, **Andromeda** for an infiltrated Guard/Zapper threat, and **Draal Minelayer** for forward durable mine geometry. For a tiny budget, **11pt K2 E/Marat**, **11pt Kaauri E/M Minelayer**, **12pt Raveneye E/M Minelayer** or **15pt Moran** make the attacker solve different problems.

**Deployment test:** Identify (1) the precise end-round scoring position, (2) the door/corner the enemy must cross to contest it, (3) which **non-Null body** remains after the first failed defense, and (4) the enemy response to **Guard LoF+ZoC, Mines, templates, hacking, or a simple bypass**. Keep the heavyweight out of an unnecessary long-range duel; spread mines, templates and guards so a single blast or Discover/clearance sequence cannot dismantle the entire setup.
