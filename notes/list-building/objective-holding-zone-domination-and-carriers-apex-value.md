# Zone Domination & Protected Area Denial — N5.3 Apex / Value (non-TAG)

_11 November 2026 · qualitative whole-game rankings, not measured tournament win rates. Exact profiles and eligibility should follow the [main-branch sectorial rosters](../../sectorials); cross-faction examples cannot be combined into one normal army._

**One combined role, two rankings.** These tables reward troops that **stay non-Null and contribute surviving points inside a scoring zone while making the approach or protected area expensive to contest**. A great room defender that never survives the scoring check is incomplete; a durable VP brick with no useful counter to an arriving attacker is incomplete. **Specialist status is not required. TAGs are excluded.**

- **Apex:** absolute ceiling for **both** surviving/contesting a scoring zone and denying access, with point concentration and deployment/range constraints as tiebreakers. Durability and ability to reach/retain the actual area remain primary; close templates, Guard, mines, repeaters, multiple bodies, immunities, and recovery raise the ranking. An exceptional remote gun does not automatically outrank a resilient doorway guard.
- **Value:** the same **combined role per points/SWC and Orders required**. Cheap Minelayers can tax an approach enormously, but low VP and W1/STR1 lower their rank relative to affordable, resilient bodies. Rank one **specific profile/package**, not hypothetical equipment collected from different loadouts.
- **Scoring mechanics:** [Domination](https://infinitythewiki.com/Domination) scores surviving qualifying **Victory Points** in a quadrant at the end of the Game Round, not the number of models or mine markers. Null troops and equipment do not contribute; Shasvastii-Embryo has a mission-specific exception. Check whether the mission explicitly changes Baggage scoring; **do not assume an automatic Baggage bonus**.
- **Denial mechanics:** [Guard](https://infinitythewiki.com/Guard) requires the enemy in **LoF and ZoC**, not just nearby; [Minelayer](https://infinitythewiki.com/Minelayer) places the specified deployable at setup only on an eligible loadout. A carried mine, Drop Bear, Dazer, Repeater, or Mine Dispenser is **not automatically predeployed**.
- **Packages:** a synchronized Peripheral is not another independent Regular Order or a second copy of its controller's full VP contribution. Resolve separate bodies and Null states under the actual mission and [multi-body rules](peripheral-synchronized-multibody-apex-value.md).

**Value exclusions and profile boundaries:** **Asura 63** and **Gamma 50** have powerful Apex ceilings but cost too much to beat the best compact survival-plus-denial packages on pure Value. **Shang Ji 36** brings excellent Tactical Awareness/VP movement, but comparatively little automatic local area denial; it is covered in Apex instead. The **12pt K2 Mine Dispenser** is deliberately *below* the 11pt E/Marat K2: its mine must be placed with Orders and its loadout lacks E/Marat, making it conditional rather than a distinct top combined-role choice.

## Practical picks by the problem actually faced

| Situation | First combined-role picks | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **One central room/entry must survive multiple assault Orders** | **Karakuri**, **Rasail+Chaksa**, **Raptor+Devabot**, **Bronze** | Real surviving wounds/structure backed by templates, nonlethal tools or layered physical bodies. |
| **Protected midfield zone with a mine already in place** | **Draal Shock Minelayer**; supporting **Raveneye/Kaauri/Moran/Ryūken** | Draal combines forward survivability with a true Minelayer device; others are less durable but force a clearance tax per point. |
| **Enemy wants to melee/charge a tight entrance** | **Jamie Handler**, **Andromeda**; conditional **Alpha** | Guard threatens a legal target within **LoF+ZoC** before base contact, and Jamie/Andromeda are much more resilient than Alpha. |
| **Need several cheap defenders rather than one premium model** | **K2 E/Marat**, **Hollow Man**, **Silverstar**, **Sakiel** | Different levels of genuine surviving VP, with live disabling/template threats; 11pt K2 alone rarely swings a large VP deficit. |
| **Open quadrant with long shooting lane and high VP deficit** | **Gamma**, **Asura**, **Shang Ji** | Ranged dominance and heavy surviving VP can matter more than a mine that covers an irrelevant door. |
| **Enemy hacking or E/M pressure** | **Karakuri with support**, **Sakiel/Sukeul**, **Rasail**, **non-Hackable support troops** | Keep hackable HI/REM outside projected attack paths where feasible; Enhanced Immunity is not blanket immunity to hacking. |

**Deployment test:** (1) Where must the surviving non-Null *scoring model* actually end the Game Round? (2) Which **entry/interaction square** must the opponent use to contest it? (3) Which defense is already there **without spending setup Orders**, and which needs Orders or a live Repeater network? (4) After a mine is cleared or one guard goes Null, **what still survives and contests**? Do not group every defensive body so one template removes the entire position.
