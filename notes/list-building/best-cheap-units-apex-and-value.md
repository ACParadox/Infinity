# Best Cheap Units in Infinity N5.3 — Cross-Faction Apex and Value

**Additional transcript-verified Vanilla O-12 budget package (26 Oct 2026):** [Robert Shepherd, *O-12 Faction Focus* (18–32m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Nl8BMJ8E5U&t=1080s) treats **Moonraker Minelayer15/.5 ×2** (ambush/Adhesive entry lanes), **Ment CoC14/0** (fighting-LT insurance), **Varangian Chain+Smoke12/0 or Trench-Hammer13/0** (Regular PH14 smoke + Dogged screen), **Kappa HD13/.5** (cheap active repeater-net Hacker) and **Raveneye Lt12/0** (FO E/M Minelayer, NCO fuel) as O-12's premier **12–15pt** shell. Crucial trade-off: despite high **utility per model**, these relatively expensive floor pieces make it difficult to buy more than one or two premium guns—*not* the same economy as 5–7pt cheap Regular Orders in other factions. The presenter disfavors the **Varangian Boarding Shotgun13** without Chain template and prefers **Trench-Hammer13** when one extra point remains. This note is a **faction-specific shortlist, not a new universal ranking**; all prices/AVA from [current O-12 roster](../../sectorials/o-12/o-12.md).

_Research: 2026-10-26. **Community-informed editorial tiers, not tournament win rates or simulated combat odds.** Current profiles, points, Order training, availability and legal Fireteams must come from the [pinned September 2026 sectorial reference files](../../sectorials/) and [current rules/FAQ](../../rules/README.md), **not** older commentary. Videos and podcasts supply tactical arguments and counterarguments; they do not automatically endorse every ranking here._

**Scope:** `cheap` means **15 points or less, 0 SWC unless noted**. This is a cross-role shortlist of units that **outperform their cost in realistic lists**—not a claim that a 5-point warband replaces a 12-point Specialist. We separate **transferable Regular Orders**, **self-funded Irregular pressure**, **screening/defence**, and **scoring/support**. No TAG or premium-profile price comparisons disguised as a "cheap" category.

## How to choose between "cheap" troops

1. **First ask whether the unit adds a transferable Order.** A 6pt Irregular model that walks/throws Smoke for itself and a 7pt Regular Flashbot that funds a TAG are different resources. **Off-table deployment, G:Jumper bodies, PuppetBots, Peripherals, FT Master and Inspiring Leadership each have separate Order rules.**
2. **Then price its *second job*.** The 7pt Flashbot offers Repeater + Flash; the 8pt Baggage bot reloads; the 11pt Ikadron adds flamethrower corner denial; the 12pt Barid is a Specialist and Pitcher. Don't upgrade for a job the list won't actually use.
3. **Price delivery, not the attack headline.** A 5pt E/Marat only matters if it reaches the target; an 8pt Daylami marker can force caution from further away; an 8pt Diablo needs a safe corner/Smoke/team route.
4. **Protect the 15-unit cap and table geometry.** More 7pt REMs can mean more Orders but fewer independent attackers/specialists and more exposed S3/S4 footprints. Spend 2–7 extra points on a useful second role when it materially changes the game.
5. **Don't manufacture universal faction access.** The same named unit can differ in AVA, legal Fireteams, Order status, and priced profile between Vanilla and sectorials (especially Ghazi, Varangian, Jackal, Ikadron, Barid). Always check **its actual current roster**.
6. **Use mission and opponent to break ties.** Many buttons: preserve a Barid/Harbinger/Irmandinho. Enemy alpha/Impersonator: Kaauri/Warcor/Daylami/Jackal. Enemy MSV2 defence: Eclipse Makaul rather than default Smoke. TAG/HI: state threat or Panzerfaust, not simply more Chain Rifles.

### Contradictions and confidence

- **Confirmed roster distinctions:** Ghazi E/Marat and Jammer do **not** automatically include Smoke; White Company Varangians are **Irregular** unlike eligible O-12/Kosmoflot variants; Corregidor Diablos are printed **Irregular/Impetuous**. The price of a companion peripheral or Fireteam anchor is not included in the cheap model's price.
- **Top evidence confidence:** high for media-explicit packages **Ikadron, Puppet Master, Harbinger FO, Diablo, Irmandinho**, and for **Tactical Awareness's Makaul-versus-Mirrorball disagreement**. **Moderate (cross-guide synthesis)** for universal cross-faction ordering and the many specialized 5–8pt defenders. Nothing here is a measured win-rate ranking.
- **Changing roster caution:** video/podcast dates range 2025–Aug 2026; recheck the pinned repo's September 2026 exact legal **profile + faction** before treating a cost, Order or Fireteam combination as currently fieldable.

**Final selection principle:** the strongest 5–15pt unit is the one that *buys a necessary Order, saves movement/setup Orders, protects a critical model, or threatens something far more expensive* **without making the 300pt list unable to score or execute its primary attack**.
