### How ratings work

_Consolidated 26 November 2026 from the existing committed Fighting and Command Lieutenant apex/value tables. This is an editorial consolidation, not a new Army profile or balance-update audit._

One unified competitive rank per **role**: fighting and command. Evaluate combat or command ceiling, realized points/SWC and Order efficiency, survivability, succession costs, and secondary utility together. S = outstanding, A+ = excellent, A = strong, B+ = situational. Conditional excellence depends on the actual roster package. A hybrid can appear in both role tables but never twice in the same role. Distinct LT loadouts remain distinct in the profile descriptions; none gains equipment or skills from another option. Basic line-trooper LT placeholders without distinctive command utility remain excluded from the command ranking.

**Tags (non-exclusive):** `Apex` = retained membership of the former role-specific ceiling shortlist; `Value` = retained membership of the former role-specific efficiency shortlist; both can apply. `Unique` = rare meaningful capability or combination; `Toolbox` = genuine role compression; `Classified Runner` = especially useful, verified Classified coverage rather than LT status or generic specialist eligibility alone; `Airborne` = actual Combat Jump/Parachutist on the listed LT; `Fireteam` = meaningful team benefit or dependency on the actual profile; `Budget` = a listed non-TAG option at ≤25 points (or TAG at ≤60), counting mandatory peripherals/packages. Tags are profile-specific and do not automatically extend to every grouped weapon variant. Classified Runner and Airborne are retained in the vocabulary but not assigned without sufficient evidence from the existing LT guide.

**Cross-guide tags (26 November 2026):** [Anti-Tech](https://github.com/ACParadox/Infinity/blob/bfd46de8b157a80a70cbfb6db1620143cfb32401/notes/list-building/anti-tech-msv-mimetism-camo-impersonation-apex-value.md), [Rare Capabilities](rare-capabilities-and-unique-combinations-apex-value.md), [Toolbox Units](jack-of-all-trades-toolbox-units-apex-value.md) and [BS MOD mechanics](active-turn-gunfighters-ranking.md#bs-mod-mechanics-and-counterplay) supplement the tags above. `Anti-Tech` denotes an actual answer to Mimetism, Camouflage/Hidden, or visor/Marksmanship defenses (MSV, Sensor, Albedo, White Noise or Eclipse access); ordinary Smoke, own Mimetism and anti-armor ammunition alone do not qualify. `Mod-Stacking` denotes exceptional or combined BS modifiers (including strong Mimetism, Camo/Surprise, layered Nanoscreen, Albedo or opposed BS Attack(-3)); it does not mean every modifier applies at once. Rare Capabilities map to `Unique`; verified independent role compression maps to `Toolbox`. Shared chassis abilities may carry over only when retained by the stated LT option. Grouped options can have variant-specific tags (Santiago Toolbox refers to the KHD FTO, not the Spitfire alone). The rankings themselves are unchanged.

**Profile-specific cross-checks:** Marut MSV2; Asura AP Spitfire LT MSV3 (no HD+); Aquila Strategos FTO MSV3, Disco Baller and Specialist Operative; Hsien MSV2; Charontid MSV3 LT versus the separate HD+ LT(+1) with White Noise and Sepsitor Plus; Anathematic HD/Trinity LT with Mimetism(-6), not HD+; Maximus Albedo(-6); Voronin Sensor; Crane Sensor/Triangulated Fire; Myrmidon Officer Eclipse. Achilles and Hatamoto combine Nanoscreen with Mimetism; Sheskiin has Nanoscreen/Protheion, not Mimetism. Nanoscreen never stacks with physical Cover. Sun Tze's pinned November correction adds Surprise Attack(-3); its Decoy/Mimetism and opposed attacks are distinct mechanisms. Verify the exact legal profile in each roster before applying the tags.

**Existing profile discrepancy flagged during tag review:** TAK's pinned Voronin 22pt profile lists Lieutenant (+1 Command Token) and Sensor but does not print Strategos L1; the historical command ranking assumes Strategos. This ranking is not a fresh balance audit; verify current Army rather than treating that historical assumption as confirmed.

**Order accounting:** LT(+1 Order) means two Special LT Orders beyond the normal Regular Order. A personal LT Order, its NCO use, and its Strategos conversion are alternative uses of the same resource, not additive. Tactical Awareness is separate. Compare the actual LT loadout against the matching non-LT option and price the real succession/support package.

**Balance-review flag:** Saladin's historical evaluation depends on eligible Irregular conversions; the pinned September Haqq roster summary lists Strategos L2 and +1 Command Token but omits Inspiring Leadership, as noted in the existing rules audit. No new November balance/profile audit was performed here. Historical conversion-based judgments throughout this document are conditional and must be rechecked against the latest Army/rules before list selection.

Prioritize effective independent attack, repeatability, self-funded activations, useful secondary roles and real SWC/points savings, after allowing for LT exposure and backup costs. Raw ceiling alone does not outrank a better complete package.

### Rules audit traps

- **Loss of Lieutenant** is checked in the **Tactical Phase**. Normally affected models become Irregular until a new LT is chosen at end of turn; **Warhorse** remains Regular. CoC triggers during that check with the successor on-table and not Null/Isolated; previous LT doesn't regain command after successor promotion. **CoC (Non Specialist)** doesn't grant scenario specialist status.
- **Mnemonica** transfers at end of Order when Null is entered, potentially repeatedly through eligible Cube/REM hosts. **Isolated LT can't jump.** Thus it differs from CoC's Tactical Phase succession.
- **Inspiring Leadership** needs actual LT **on table and not Null**; excludes **Religious Troop** models. Saladin's Inspiring was explicitly added in Oct 2025 N5.2; the pinned [Haqq roster summary](../../sectorials/haqqislam/haqqislam.md) abbreviates his skill list to Counterintelligence, but the [N5 profile data](https://human-sphere.com/Saladin) and [October official Army change discussion](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/infinity-army-n5-updates-bugs.43295/page-10) confirm Inspiring Leadership. **Roster-summary omission flagged; do not silently erase his real ability.**
- **Strategos vs NCO** compete for the **same** LT resource: after Strategos converts to Regulars there is **no Special LT Order** for NCO. If the LT has +1 Order, both Special Orders get converted under Strategos.
- **NCO** can use a generated Special LT Order even if LT subsequently enters Null during the turn. An **Isolated NCO can't** convert/use it. Tactical Awareness Order is separate.
- **Warhorse** does **not** ignore Mimetism, range, Cover, templates, Immobilized, or hacking damage; only its specific LoL/Retreat/Isolation/BS Attack(-X) interactions. Morat `Religious Troop` is not a substitute for Warhorse.
- **SWC rebates** can improve command value when they unlock another useful expensive weapon. Check the exact Lieutenant option for:
  - **Joan**
  - **Aquila**
  - **Hsien**
  - **Hatamoto**
  - **Neema**

  The **+1 SWC option** and other LT options may be mutually exclusive.
- **Don't invent CoC** for these roster-specific profiles, despite some older upload claims:
  - **Quinn (Kestrel / Varuna)** — no CoC on pinned profiles
  - **Emily Handelman (Kestrel)** — no CoC on pinned profile
  - **Emily Handelman (White Company)** — **does** have CoC; it is a different roster.
- **No generic army-wide CoC**: the listed faction/model permission and specific Fireteam profile decide legality.

### Five questions before finalizing 300 points

1. If LT is Dead or Isolated before my Tactical Phase, **which actual troopers remain Regular** and how many pooled Orders are lost?
2. Is my LT Order **personal**, **NCO Tactical**, or **Strategos Regular**? Count each only once; does an NCO Fireteam move or Strategos group transfer solve the mission?
3. How many *genuinely* Irregular models does Inspiring Leadership convert? Are any excluded by Religious Troop?
4. Can CoC survive a separate assault/Template/repeater attack, and is its backup mission action worth its marginal price?
5. If the main LT gun and its support die turn one, is there still **another independent weapon and enough scoring specialists for turn three**?
