# Airborne Objective Specialists — N5.3 Points-Value Top 5

## Purpose and criteria

This is a **focused, role-specific Value** comparison of airborne **Specialist** profiles: how effectively a unit reaches a far console or Classified, scores with limited Orders and avoids being stranded. Rank weighs cost/SWC, Regular Order, legal Combat Jump or normal Parachutist access, mobility, meaningful scoring skills, and survival/secondary threat. **A specialized scoring rank is not a rank for killing enemy-DZ targets.**

**Counting policy:** Because this is a narrow/overlapping specialist subcategory, **do not add these positions to the sectorial Top 3/5/10 percentages** in [overall rankings](../sectorial-rankings-n5-3.md), which count broad roles. For example, Teishin is #9 in [general mission specialist Value](mission-specialists-and-button-pushers-apex-value.md#2-value-specialists--best-bang-for-buck-all-types) and #14 in [general airborne/DZ attack Value](airborne-and-enemy-dz-attackers-apex-value.md#3-best-points-value--repeatable-trade-up-or-surgical-disruption). Those established ranks remain intact.

## Interpretation

- **Choose Akalis** for compact shooting, scoring and deployable-network utility in Acontecimento.
- **Choose Garuda** when movement after arrival is the greatest scoring constraint.
- **Choose Gao-Tarsos** when survival after the initial action is worth the extra points.
- **Choose Teishin** when a 19pt Regular FO drop with Super-Jump and D-Charges best fits JSA's available Orders.
- **Choose Cliff-Jumper** for Next Wave's Mimetism and stronger unsupported Combat Jump odds.

**Source of truth:** These are editorial cross-faction comparisons; use the linked pinned **N5.3 sectorial roster profiles** for legality, exact costs, skills and mission permissions. The [broader airborne-attacker table](airborne-and-enemy-dz-attackers-apex-value.md) ranks a different job and is not replaced by this shortlist.
