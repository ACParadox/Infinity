# Active-Turn Gunfighters and Vehicles — N5.3 Unified Ranking

_Consolidated 26 November 2026 from the committed active-turn gunfighter and BS mod-stacking guides. Reranked selected HI profiles on 26 November using Irregular Order episodes 8–12 and the November 25 Army changes as secondary evidence. Qualitative editorial ranking, not measured win rates; unaffected profiles are carried forward, not freshly audited._

**Scope:** **active-turn medium- and long-range shooting** (especially 16–32in, with meaningful 24–32in capability), **not all-purpose assault**. Close-range 0–16in sweeps, boarding shotguns, rifles, template attacks, melee, Protheion, assassination runs and mobility without a useful ranged engagement belong in [close-combat and short-range assault](../Strategy/close-combat-and-short-range-assault.md). This guide excludes defensive AROs, command structure or deployment tricks in isolation. **TAGs have their own unified category below** and are **not mixed into infantry/REM/VH rankings**. Other relevant guides: [TAGs](tags-ranking.md) · [VH vehicles](#vehicles-and-aerial-platforms) · [ARO](aro-units-apex-and-value.md) · [weapons/ammunition](../Strategy/weapons-ammunition-and-engagement-selection.md) · [face-to-face odds](../Strategy/probability-and-face-to-face-decision-framework.md) · [Fireteams](../Strategy/fireteams-and-profile-evaluation.md) · [Order efficiency](../Strategy/order-efficiency.md) · [target sequencing](../Strategy/target-priority-and-attack-sequencing.md).

**Hard-armor matchup supplement:** [N5.3 non-hacking anti-TAG/anti-HI apex/value](anti-tag-anti-heavy-infantry-non-hacking-apex-value.md) separately ranks **cheap E/M/PARA lockdown, K1, Monofilament, Panzerfaust and repeatable AP/Autocannon** against actual armor and delivery, not general gunfight utility.

## Scope and ranking method

One unified competitive rank per engagement category: long-range non-TAG, medium-range non-TAG and TAG. VH profiles compete directly against other non-TAG gunfighters in their relevant range category; the [vehicle-family comparison](#vehicles-and-aerial-platforms) assesses the complete aerial package (including non-shooting support profiles) and must not be counted as an additional broad gunfighter ranking. Reward effective active-turn FtF, damage conversion, repeatability, useful reach, order efficiency, actual points/SWC and roster cost. Following the other consolidated guides, primary role, value and versatility are evaluated together, not as separate ranked lists. Raw shooting excellence does not excuse a poor roster investment; a cheap gun still needs to perform its job. S = outstanding, A+ = excellent, A = strong, B+ = situational. Ranks are editorial, descending within each tier.

**Tags (non-exclusive):** `Apex` = exceptional raw role performance; `Value` = unusually good points/SWC/Order efficiency; `Unique` = rare meaningful capability; `Toolbox` = genuine role compression; `Classified Runner` = particularly useful actual Classified coverage; `Airborne` = legal Combat Jump/Parachutist delivery (not VH flight); `Fireteam` = meaningful legal team benefit or dependency; `Budget` = ≤25pt non-TAG or ≤60pt TAG (mandatory packages count); `Mod-Stacking` = notable BS asymmetry formerly assessed in the BS mod-stacking guide, including combined offensive/defensive modifiers, exceptional Mimetism, opposed BS Attack(-3), or conditional anti-visor protection. It is **not** synonymous with Anti-Tech: it includes tools that impose modifiers and tools that cancel or exploit them. The tag alone neither guarantees a favorable matchup nor means every modifier stacks on the same die. See [BS MOD mechanics and counterplay](#bs-mod-mechanics-and-counterplay). `Anti-Tech` = a meaningful direct answer to enemy Mimetism, Camouflage/Hidden, or visor/Marksmanship-based defenses (e.g. MSV2/3, useful MSV1 against Mim3 or partially against Mim6, Albedo, or actual Sensor). Based on the [Anti-Tech guide](https://github.com/ACParadox/Infinity/blob/bfd46de8b157a80a70cbfb6db1620143cfb32401/notes/list-building/anti-tech-msv-mimetism-camo-impersonation-apex-value.md) and confirmed equivalent capabilities on the listed gun profile; MSV1 is only a partial counter, and Albedo only affects qualifying opponents. Mimetism or Marksmanship alone does not qualify. Ordinary anti-armor ammunition is not Anti-Tech in this sense. `Unique` and `Toolbox` also reflect applicable exact-profile or shared-chassis capabilities documented in [Rare Capabilities](rare-capabilities-and-unique-combinations-apex-value.md) and [Toolbox Units](toolbox-units-rankings.md). Mandatory multi-body packages count only with their real costs; optional team partners and different specialist variants do not confer their capabilities on the gunfighter. Tags are conservative and refer to the stated profile, not every loadout.

**Range and profile rules:** Keep long-range non-TAGs that contest 24–32in (including Thunderbolt and Portable Autocannon) separate from native medium guns (8–24in; Spitfire, MMR, Red Fury, Mk12, plasma). Crossover Thunderbolts are ranked once, in long-range, even where the old value table also listed them in medium. HMGs and Portable Autocannons remain long-range. A few geometry-dependent MULTI Rifles are retained from the prior medium tables, not treated as native 24in guns. TAGs remain separate because their full roster cost and recovery needs differ. An eligible gun may cost 40pt or more; the former under-40pt value cutoff is no longer an exclusion. G:Jumper and mandatory peripherals must be priced as a legal package. Distinct roster variants and weapons are not interchangeable.

- **Enemy in Cover:**
  - **Spetsnaz HMG**
  - **Hetkari**
  - **Territorial AP MMR**
  - *Context:* or a justified **Assisted Fire REM**.
  - **Mk.4** instead imposes BS Attack(−3) on the defender; don't conflate the two effects.
- **Mimetism−6 / Smoke ARO:** consider the following visor attackers:
  - **Asura**
  - **Intruder**
  - **Nisse**
  - **Hsien**
  - **Epsilon**
  - **MSV2 Mukhtar**

  The visor may be countered by White Noise/Albedo; plan a separate gun.
- **HI/TAG in high ARM:**
  - **Veteran Kazak AP HMG**
  - **Asawira AP Spitfire**
  - **Hatamoto's close-range alternate**
  - **TAG with AP HRMC/MULTI**, if the list can support one

  Pure high Burst without penetration may not finish.
- **Hidden ARO first-contact:**
  - **Swiss Guard / Hac Tao**
  - **Camouflaged Spetsnaz**
  - **Cheap expendable probe**
  - **Guided/indirect setup**, where available
  - **An attacker approaching behind Smoke**

  Do not feed your biggest body into an untested hidden defender.
- **Midfield heavy Cover / short corners:** **Sheskiin MULTI Rifle, Mukhtar Viral Rifle, Asawira BSG and Hatamoto Plasma Carbine** may be stronger than any HMG *at that job*. That is **short-range assault**, not a reason to promote their **long-range gunfighter** tier; see [the short-assault guide](../Strategy/close-combat-and-short-range-assault.md). **Vostok Mk12/Spitfire** remains a legitimate separate medium-range option.
- **Open long firefight:** HMG-range candidates include:
  - **Kriza**
  - **Gamma**
  - **Hac Tao**
  - **Swiss Guard**
  - **Spetsnaz**
  - **Hsien**

  Do not force Red Fury/Spitfire outside their positive range.
- **High vertical / weak hacking net:**
  - **Skyhound**
  - **Firebat**
  - **Redeye**
  - **Vostok**

  If terrain is flat and hacker coverage dense, favor a grounded shooter with better Cover/recovery.

## Vehicles and aerial platforms

**Ranking baseline:** 11 November 2026. **Consolidated:** 26 November 2026. **Format:** one cross-faction, ordinal **Best** ranking with non-exclusive descriptive tags for peak effectiveness, efficiency and other roles; no separate Apex or Value leaderboards. Eight current standard-roster VH families are covered, one row per family; representative profiles are examples rather than a claim of shared sectorial loadouts. This is editorial judgment for a typical **300-point game**, not tournament win-rate data.

**Ranking method:** weigh (1) ability to create otherwise impossible attack angles and remove important targets, (2) efficiency after points, SWC and faction opportunity cost, (3) durability / reactive-turn safety, (4) useful secondary roles such as NCO, Sensor, Repeaters, peripherals, or post-shell Operators, and (5) practical support and deployment requirements. This is **not** the arithmetic average of the former Apex/Value grades. **S** = exceptional all-round pick or offensive rate; **A** = excellent but with a more material list/matchup constraint; **B** = useful specialist or high-risk pick rather than a broadly efficient default. Within-tier gaps are small.

**Top-five cross-roster audit (11 Nov 2026):** The named **VH profile** is present in each linked standard roster below. Every listed top-five VH is **AVA 1** in those armies except **Redeye AVA 2** in Kestrel and Neoterra; Vanilla PanO Redeye is AVA 1. **Haytham is legally in Ramah Taskforce as well as Vanilla Haqqislam**—the former statement excluding it from Ramah was incorrect. No top-five VH acquires Fireteam access merely because its parent sectorial has Fireteams. This audit preserves **one combined Best ranking** rather than adding an incompatible second Apex/Value table.

**Primary profile authority:** current N5.3 / pinned Infinity Army material in this repository's `sectorials/` and `rules/` trees. Commentary from video and podcast tier discussions informs judgment, but outdated points or faction access do not override the current roster. The best dedicated **pre-Tarkshya** vehicle ranking is [Robert Shepherd's Oct-2025 aerial-vehicle analysis](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N6TYjMgU4ZM), which ranked the seven then-available *actual VHs* **Zeybek > Skyhound > Haytham > Redeye > Firebat > Go-Pod > Fēiquán** (Garuda is a REM, not VH). Its ranking deliberately emphasizes faction context. **Tarkshya was introduced later** and its placement below is an independent N5.3 judgment, not an attributed video placement.

### Ranking tags

Tags are non-exclusive and apply to the vehicle family only where supported by the named profile or explicitly conditional alternate state.

- `Apex`: exceptional peak effectiveness for the VH role, independent of cost.
- `Value`: particularly compelling effect for points, SWC and order/support burden in the baseline assessment; subject to reassessment after the November changes.
- `Unique`: differentiated role or combination of capabilities, not simply the universal Aerial rule.
- `Toolbox`: substantial secondary control, detection, support, command-order or mission utility beyond ordinary shooting.
- `Classified Runner`: explicit mission-relevant specialist capability, conditional on the legal Operator transition where applicable; not a guarantee of completing all Classifieds.
- `Airborne`: actual Combat Jump or Parachutist deployment; Aerial movement alone does not qualify.
- `Fireteam`: explicit, useful legal Fireteam participation; not inferred from army membership.
- `Budget`: a representative entry profile costing 34 points or less after the November corrections, a role-relative threshold.
- `Anti-Tech`: meaningful direct counter to visual/detection technology, following the [Anti-Tech guide](https://github.com/ACParadox/Infinity/blob/bfd46de8b157a80a70cbfb6db1620143cfb32401/notes/list-building/anti-tech-msv-mimetism-camo-impersonation-apex-value.md): MSV1 partially counters Mimetism (-6), Albedo conditionally counters MSV/Marksmanship, and Sensor detects eligible Camouflage/Hidden models in ZoC. E/M/AP alone do not qualify.
- `Mod-Stacking`: notable BS-roll asymmetry under [BS MOD mechanics](#bs-mod-mechanics-and-counterplay), including exceptional Mimetism (-6), visor combinations, and conditional Albedo; ordinary Mimetism (-3) alone is insufficient. No Cover prevents a VH from adding Partial Cover's MOD.

[Rare Capabilities](rare-capabilities-and-unique-combinations-ranking.md) and [Toolbox Units](toolbox-units-rankings.md) inform the existing `Unique` and `Toolbox` tags rather than duplicating them. The companion guides specifically rank Zeybek and Fēiquán as toolboxes; other tags use confirmed equivalent chassis/profile capabilities and the current committed November correction notes. These are role classifications, not an imported second leaderboard.

No ranked VH is documented here as qualifying for Airborne or Fireteam. Tarkshya's Classified Runner tag applies only to its Forward Observer Operator state.

### Ranking interpretation and useful swaps (historical baseline)

- **Skyhound vs Tarkshya:** Skyhound wins *first-attack lethality and weapon coverage*; Tarkshya wins *staying power, MSV matchup, operator continuity*. If a list is already light on repair, redundancy or ground specialists, Tarkshya may be the practical #1.
- **Zeybek vs Redeye:** Zeybek's Albedo, smoke and Operator make it unusually valuable in Haqq; Redeye is the better raw armored flyer and moves up in PanO lists exploiting Kestrel/NCA **AVA2**. Neither is an automatic replacement for a long-range ARO-breaker.
- **Redeye #4 vs Haytham #5, after Ramah correction:** Both are excellent, but Redeye retains a narrow overall edge on BS14/ARM6, Sensor and dual-AVA access in two sectorials; Haytham's **29/0** profile, MSV1, Continuous Damage and now-verified Ramah access make it a credible swap to #4 in lists where SWC efficiency or anti-Mimetism hunting dominates. New sectorial access strengthens a case for the unit but does not automatically reverse an all-round cross-faction rank.
- **Haytham vs Firebat:** **Haytham ranks higher overall, and is available in both Vanilla Haqqislam and Ramah:** the 29/0 entry is cheaper, while MSV1, Mimetism (-6) and its Continuous Damage attack suite make it a more compelling active-turn threat and anti-VH hunter. **Firebat** is better only for specific NCO/Lieutenant-Order plans, nonlethal weapon control or defensive Suppressive Fire. Shepherd preferred Haytham in his dedicated tier ranking and characterized Firebat as merely situational in his newer O-12 review.
- **Go-Pod vs Fēiquán:** Go-Pod has a more direct kill-and-disrupt plan; Fēiquán is exceptional **only when its aerial Repeater projection feeds actual hackers/guided weapons**. Treat the latter as role-dependent rather than universally weak.
- **Tradeoffs matter more than 1–3 places of rank.** A 0-SWC vehicle leaves a premium weapon available; a 1.5-SWC vehicle must improve your plan enough to justify the gun/SWC it displaces. Every VH also needs S7 staging space that might otherwise belong to a TAG.

### Employment, restrictions and counterplay

**Use a VH to make a new angle, not to substitute for a TAG's frontal gunfight.** They are large **S7, No Cover, Hackable** models that cannot go Prone, so park in **Total Cover**, not partial cover; consider the *entire Jump trajectory* for ARO exposure. Remove MSV/high-Burst AROs, Pitchers and Hackers before launching. Budget Orders not only for reaching and defeating a target, but also for retreating to Total Cover.

**They cannot make Silhouette contact while Aerial.** An active VH therefore normally cannot touch objectives, grab mission items, engage in CC, or receive normal contact-based Engineer repairs. **Ranged GizmoKit + Tech-Recovery** can remove eligible states; Unconscious repair uses ordinary GizmoKit recovery rather than the extra state-clearing effect. See [Aerial](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Aerial.md), [Tech-Recovery](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Tech-Recovery.md) and [GizmoKit](../../rules/5c_Equipment/GizmoKit.md) for details. The **Tarkshya/Zeybek Operator** may gain different scoring options *only after* its specified legal transition.

**Matchups:** into hard MSV, consider Zeybek's Albedo; into Mimetism-heavy targets, prioritize Tarkshya/Haytham MSV1; into prone rooftops, seek Redeye/Skyhound angles; into a thin hacking network, Fēiquán's Pitcher can open the map; into dense Repeaters and long-range covered AROs, **sometimes no VH is the correct answer**. Sensor is valuable, but a Sensor model with a poor combat route is still a poor attack piece.

**Legal roster boundaries:** Redeye is Vanilla PanO AVA1 but Kestrel/NCA AVA2; Skyhound belongs to Next Wave; Tarkshya to Vanilla ALEPH/OSS; Zeybek to Vanilla Haqq/Qapu; Haytham to Vanilla Haqq and Ramah Taskforce; Firebat to Vanilla O-12/Starmada/Torchlight; Go-Pod to Vanilla Nomads/Bakunin/Tunguska; and Fēiquán to Vanilla Yu Jing/White Banner/Imperial Service. Ordinary Fireteam permission does **not** follow from being a VH. Garuda Tacbot, motorcycles, TAGs and jumping HI are not VH entries.

## 7. Ranged evaluation framework and Order efficiency

Use a qualitative ranged-first rubric: range-specific FtF 35%, damage/conversion and target diversity 20%, full roster cost 20%, survival 10%, delivery and order efficiency 10%, useful versatility 5%. These weights guide editorial ordering, not computed probabilities or objective measurements. Apply all axes to the chosen weapon and legal roster. Close assault and melee do not earn ranged credit.

**Range-specific threshold:** In Long-range, a model must credibly contest 24–32in; in Medium-range, it must credibly fight 8–24in, especially 16–24in. Do **not** deduct medium-grade credit for lacking HMG-range positive bands. **Hard separation:** A model that cannot credibly contest its declared medium/long firelane must **not** receive an S or A+ ranged ranking merely for having elite BS, CC, speed, a short-range gun, or a spectacular first-turn alpha. Rate **individual gun profiles**, not blended fictional combinations. For 0–16in or mixed close/midrange play, see the [short-assault comparison](../Strategy/close-combat-and-short-range-assault.md). Report evidence informs *context and confidence*, not unsupported statistical grades.

### Firepower versus **actual** Order efficiency

A good gunfighter wins the **right** Face-to-Face fight, converts hits to consequences, and still contributes after the first target dies. Compare six steps:

1. **Reach:** count Orders to get into positive weapon range and a workable angle. A 20-5 flyer, infiltrator, or Climbing unit may pay **zero or one** approach Order where a BS15 heavy gun pays three.
2. **Win the FtF:** use the **real active Burst**, modified BS/PH target number, range, Cover, Mimetism, MSV, Marksmanship and enemy ARO. High Burst improves *consistency*, while high target numbers help defeat a strong enemy die.
3. **Convert hits:** account for current **PS/ammunition** and target armor/BTS/Wounds/STR/Immunities. In N5 **lower PS is more lethal**; AP, DA, EXP, K1, Plasma, Viral and E/M have **different** ideal victims.
4. **Continue:** after target one, can the same position hit exposed support, Specialist, Repeater, Engineer, second gun or scoring lane—or must it move through another three Orders?
5. **Survive/exit:** ending prone, out of LoF, behind screen, or recoverable near Engineer may be worth an Order. An exposed 50pt one-wound shooter is not automatically value because it killed 20 points first.
6. **Pay full list cost:** add 2–4 required Fireteam fillers, smoke, hacking, Engineer, EVO, SWC and the fact that 90–122pt TAGs can prevent buying an independent second attack plan.

**Fast heuristic:** _useful enemy Orders denied + objectives opened + attacks won_, divided by _setup + movement + attack + exit Orders_; compare catastrophic losses and alternative shooters. This is a **decision aid**, not a fake precise score or unsupported mathematical kill percentage.

**Rules traps:**
- **+1 Special Die (SD) is not +1 Burst.** Roll the extra die and discard one before resolving; it improves the FtF but does not generate another effective hit. A legal Fireteam activates its members together, **one nominated leader makes the active attack**.
- **Marksmanship** ignores the negative BS MOD from Partial Cover/Nanoscreen, **not** Cover's improved save. It does **not** ignore Mimetism or range. Marksmanship itself is affected by White Noise/Albedo/Reflective interactions; do not silently score it as universal +3.
- **MSV** answers suitable visual MODs; **Albedo and White Noise** can invert the matchup. Mimetism has less value into the correct MSV level.
- **A profile with a personal extra Order** only increases efficiency if that activation is **actually usable** on the shooter or its legal team; it does not become a free pooled Order. Count Fireteam **composition** and mobility restrictions, not just printed bonuses.
- Avoid assigning fake weapon Burst or success probabilities from a video. Current [weapon chart and update](../../rules/4_Ammunition%20and%20Weaponry/Weapon_Chart.md) and explicit active/reactive dice decide a particular exchange.

## BS MOD mechanics and counterplay

The former standalone mod-stacking reference is consolidated here. `Mod-Stacking` is a cross-role tag, not another ranking. Incoming-roll protection (Mimetism, Cover/Nanoscreen, conditional Albedo), outgoing accuracy (MSV, Marksmanship, BS, range, Burst and Special Dice), and opposed-roll suppression (BS Attack(-3)) affect different rolls and cannot simply be summed. Mimetism(-6) is meaningful even alone; ordinary Mimetism(-3) or Cover alone is not sufficient. Nanoscreen replaces rather than doubles Cover; Marksmanship ignores its shooting penalty, not its Saving Roll benefit or Mimetism. MSV1 reduces Mim6 to -3, MSV2/3 removes it; Albedo affects only opposing MSV/Marksmanship. Warhorse counters opposed BS Attack(-X), not Mimetism or Cover. Surprise Attack applies to eligible active marker openings, never ordinary ARO; active +1B is not reactive Burst. Assisted Fire is a funded, eligible REM buff, not native Marksmanship. Apply exact-profile and legal Fireteam restrictions.

**Benchmark:** BS13 at +3 range versus Cover hits on 13 normally, 10 against Mim3 and 7 against Mim6; MSV1 restores 10 and MSV2 restores 13 against Mim6. These are single-die target numbers, not FtF probabilities. Against Mim6 without MSV, bypass the lane, use templates or non-LoF pressure, exploit range/corners, or target the support and scoring plan; stacking one's own favorable MODs does not cancel enemy Mimetism. See [Mimetism](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Mimetism.md), [Nanoscreen](../../rules/5c_Equipment/Nanoscreen.md), [Marksmanship](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Marksmanship.md), [MSV](../../rules/5c_Equipment/Multispectral_Visor.md), [Albedo](../../rules/5c_Equipment/Albedo.md), [Warhorse](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Warhorse.md) and [Surprise Attack](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Surprise_Attack.md).
