**Scope:** Non-hacking answers to armored TAG/HI/REM/VH: **E/M, adhesive/PARA, K1, Monofilament, AP, AP+DA, AP+EXP, EXP, Breaker/BTS shots, ordinary missiles, mines, CC**. **Exclude Hacker/KHD hacking programs, Pitcher-to-hacking strategies, and Guided fire *requiring hacking Spotlight* as an independent self-contained kill plan.** Pure Forward Observer/Targeted setup and scenario targeting can be useful **only if all supporting resources/Orders are counted**. This guide scores **neutralizing the target, not necessarily killing it**; an IMM-B TAG for the rest of the last turn is often as useful as a dead one.

## Thirty-second results

**If the opponent has a 60–120-point TAG:**

1. **Cheapest *credible chance* to neutralize immediately:** **Ghazi Muttawi'ah E/Marat5/0 (Bahram/Haqq)**, **Minescorp Jackal Panzerfaust6/0 or infiltrating E/M Carbine10/0 (Kestrel)**, **Daylami Panzerfaust6/0 / Camo+Infiltration8/0 (Haqq/Bahram)**. The **E/Marat template** can disable on a failed BTS roll; **Panzerfaust AP+EXP** can cause damage. **Cheap does not mean guaranteed:** Ghazi has to reach template range and may die to return fire; 6pt Panzerfaust is BS11 and Disposable(2).
2. **Best disposable anti-TAG *ambush* value:** **Lóng Yá16/0 Panzerfaust(+1B), Camo+FD+10 (White Banner)**; **Beasthunter Panzerfaust+HFT9/0 FTO or Camo+FD+20 15/0** in eligible rosters; **Haqq Nadhir23/0 Hidden+FD+10 Panzerfaust**. Cheap enough to risk, with *delivery or threat timing* that an ordinary backline Panzerfaust lacks.
3. **Best *repeatable* cheap can-openers:** **Combined Unidron K1 Combi14/0** (**B3, ARM=0**; still must win BS11 gunfight), **Tohaa Reex K1 Marksman16/0** (**B3, ARM=0 at 16–24"**), **Yu Jing Lù Duān Thunderbolt+E/M Carbine+E/M Mine20/0** (also Holoprojector), **Tohaa Kosuil Engineer K1 Combi25/0 / Sukeul K1 Combi FO29/0** (more competent/specialist gunfighters).
4. **Best cheap *two-use* ranged electronic trap:** **Kestrel Jackal Infiltration E/M Carbine10/0**; **Tohaa Kaauri E/M Minelayer11/0** (zone denial, not direct immediate damage); **Military Orders Raveneye E/Marat + E/M Mine12/0**; **Corregidor Shifta E/Marat+E/M Mine17/0**. **E/Mitter** is B2 repeatable, **Blitzen** B1 Disposable(2), **E/M Carbine** reusable; distinguish delivery/attack roll from actual BTS effect.
5. **Best durable *active-turn lethal gun* without buying a TAG:** **Ariadna TankHunter AP HMG33/1.5**, **TankHunter Portable Autocannon(+1B)36/1.5** in Vanilla (TAK listing differs); **Azra'il AP HMG37/1.5 / Feuerbach BS Attack(+1B)41/1.5 (Haqq)**; **Combined Morat Kurgat Portable Autocannon30/1 (Onyx/Morat as legal)**. Sustained AP HMG B4 or AP+EXP autocannon B2 threatens multiple STR while fighting back. Hidden/Mimetism and support still determine who wins the Face-to-Face roll.
6. **Best hard-target *precision assassin*:** **Oniwaban SMG29/0 (JSA), Speculo Killer BSG26/1 (CA), Shinobu Kitsune44/.5 (JSA)** — Monofilament's **State: Dead on failed saving roll** can bypass the usual 3STR attrition plan, but **base contact/Impersonation/Hidden delivery** and defensive screening are the true cost.
7. **Best multi-role TAG-denial set:** **Kestrel Jackal10 E/M**, **White Banner Hwarang19 Panzerfaust+EXP CC**, **Corregidor Jaguar11/0 Adhesive Launcher Rifle+Panzerfaust+Smoke**, **Imperial Service Wú Míng19/0 E/Marat HI**, **Tohaa Kaauri11 E/M Mine / Sukeul29 K1**, **Morat Vanguard K1 Sniper17/1, Anyat K1 Combi+E/M Grenade22/0**. The correct faction/mission/geometry decides, not a universal all-roster purchase.

**Decision rule:** need the enemy TAG **unable to act right now** → E/M/PARA; need it **Unconscious/Dead before the Engineer arrives** → repeatable AP+EXP/K1/HMG or Monofilament; need to **prevent it stepping into a lane** → Panzerfaust, E/M Mine, inexpensive ARO; need to beat an **elite TAG actively shooting back** → aim first for **Burst, attack MODs, range, Mimetism and firing angle**, then ammunition.

## 1. What “reliable” means — four different tests

| Dimension | Ask before paying | Biggest trap |
|---|---|---|
| **Access / setup** | Deployment, movement Orders, Camo/Hidden, Irregular Order, S4–S7 geometry, template reach, ammo supply | A 5pt E/Marat has **zero effect** when the TAG is 30" away behind screening. |
| **Win the Attack / avoid ARO** | BS/PH, Burst, weapon range, enemy Mimetism/Partial Cover/MSV, team bonuses | A 6pt BS11 Panzerfaust may lose FTF to a BS15 TAG; its AP+EXP never gets to Save. |
| **Convert an uncancelled hit** | Target ARM, BTS, Cover, Immunity(ARM/BTS/AP), Warhorse, 3STR, engineer/Servant nearby | **AP does not halve Cover**; E/M doesn't deal ordinary Wounds; STR vs VITA matters. |
| **Persist after it lands** | Enemy self-Reset, Engineer reach, Remote Presence repair, time left in game | An isolated+IMM-B TAG may recover enough to fight later. Judge Orders and mission opportunity denied. |

**Cross-game tiers below rate the *carrier plus current weapon option*, not theoretical ammunition in a vacuum.** “Apex” is maximum reasonable battlefield swing at the correct matchup; “value” includes printed points, SWC, **Orders spent arriving**, risk of losing a Regular Order, ammunition limits, and remaining mission utility.

### Real weapon mechanics (current N5.3)

| Approach | Current key mechanics | Best target / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| **E/M: E/Marat, E/M Carbine, E/Mitter, Blitzen, grenades/mines** | Each successful E/M hit **forces 2 Saving Rolls at ceil(BTS/2)**. Any failed Save inflicts **Isolated**; **HI/TAG/REM/VH also IMM-B**. Ordinary E/M is **nonlethal**. | Strong **functional shutdown** even against ARM9–10, but needs legal reach/hit. **Warhorse prevents Isolated**, not necessarily every other state; inspect actual Immunity rules before promising a brick. |
| **Panzerfaust** | **AP+EXP**: hit makes **3 ARM/2 Saves**, **PS6**, **B1**, **Disposable (2)**. Usually a *reactive threat* first. | Excellent swing per point if uncancelled; BS11 and 2 charges make kill reliability poor by itself. Cannot claim all three Saves are failed. |
| **Feuerbach** | **B2 AP+DA** (2 ARM/2 Saves per uncancelled hit) vs **B1 EXP** (3 full-ARM Saves); PS6, long reach. | In active-turn armor attack usually choose **B2 AP+DA** over B1 EXP; reactive or non-AP matchup differs. |
| **Portable Autocannon** | **B2 AP+EXP**, **PS5**, 3 Saves per uncancelled hit; special source '+1B' overrides as printed. | Extremely damaging ranged anti-armor, but costs ~30–36pts and must win FTF / avoid range drawbacks. |
| **AP HMG / MULTI HMG AP** | **B4, PS5, ARM/2**, one Save each (plus Crit effects). | Repeatable strong **active** gun if cover/range/BS cooperate; 3STR usually needs sustained hits, unlike a single Mono save. |
| **K1 Combi / K1 Marksman** | **B3, PS7, ARM=0**, normal hit Save; cover still matters when applicable. | Great vs ARM7–10 irrespective of ARM value; loses to high-BS/Mimetism defenses when carrier has BS11, and against soft armor its PS7 can be underwhelming. |
| **Monofilament weapon/mine** | **ARM=0, PS8, State: Dead** on failed Save (follow exact immunity/cancel conditions). | One-hit **execution** versus expensive target only if delivered safely; screen, anti-marker and reactive templates stop it. |
| **Breaker rifle/marksman / Pulzar/Plasma** | Attacks **BTS**, with Breaker halving BTS. Plasma makes ARM+BTS-related saves. | Works when target's **BTS is the actual weak point**. Avoid blanket “Breaker always beats AP” versus BTS9 or Immunity (BTS). |
| **Adhesive Launcher Rifle / Akrylat / PARA** | On hit, **PH-based Saving Roll** (Adhesive Launcher Rifle **PH−6**); creates **IMM-A** effect, subject to recovery. | Excellent into high ARM/BTS with **poor PH**, but many TAGs have high PH and adequate engineers; different from permanent kill. |
| **T2** | Each failed ARM Save on standard T2 hit inflicts **2 Wounds**, but not ARM penetration. | Great against multi-Wound **VITA** infantry; high ARM + Cover can still shut it down, and T2 isn't a direct E/M or Mono substitute. |

**Important N5.3 override:** current [Trench-Hammer](../../rules/4_Ammunition%20and%20Weaponry/N5.3_Weapon_Updates.md) **is not Disposable(3)**. The large generated Weapon Chart still has the stale “Disposable(3)” row. Do **not** penalize **Corregidor Diablo8** or another Trench-Hammer user for nonexistent N5.3 charges. New **AP Red Fury (B4)** and **AP Thunderbolt (ARM/2)** likewise use N5.3 override values, not the old generic Thunderbolt row.

### One **conditional** probability example (not a unit kill-rate)

Against a TAG with **BTS6** and no applicable Cover/other Saving MOD, an **uncancelled E/M hit (PS7)** yields **two Saves at ceil(6/2)+7 = 10**. Each has a **50% chance to fail** on d20, so **at least one failed Save = 1−0.5² = 75%**. That is the **conditional chance to inflict E/M states AFTER the weapon has already hit**, *not* a 75% chance that your Ghazi successfully reaches and bricks a TAG. A target with BTS9, cover/Immunity or a different PS changes the calculation. A Warhorse victim may resist Isolated regardless of this roll; an Engineer or eligible Reset may subsequently clear the disability.

Against ARM8 in Partial Cover, AP reduces **ARM8→4**, **then** adds +3 cover (if the weapon/matchup permits Cover). An AP HMG at **PS5** then gives defender **Save SV = 4+3+5 = 12**, a **40% chance of losing 1 STR per uncancelled hit**. The HMG still needs to generate uncancelled hits in FTF and often to force several wounds; **don't turn 40% per Save into a complete TAG-kill figure**.

## 4. Which weapon/package beats *which* armor?

| Target state / defenses | Prefer (in descending *practical* order) | Avoid and why |
|---|---|---|
| **TAG ARM8–10, BTS6–9, Engineer distant**, accessible at short range | **E/Marat / E/M Carbine → IMM-B/Isolated**, then mission action; **K1** or AP+EXP if continued lethal removal necessary | Plain HMG repeated Saves; unsafe Impetuous charge; treating E/M status as Dead. |
| **TAG ARM8–10, BTS9 / Immunity(BTS) / Warhorse**, 24–40" away | **AP HMG B4**, **AP+DA Feuerbach B2**, **AP+EXP Autocannon B2**, **K1** with favorable FTF/line | Relying only on E/M against defensive immunity or high BTS; trying to get Ghazi there across many AROs. |
| **TAG in Partial Cover, BS15 and strong return fire** | **Outrange, flank, surprise, smoke, Marksmanship, Hidden ARO**, and then use AP/K1; multi-Order coordinated pressure only if justified | 6pt BS11 Panzerfaust exposed face-to-face on worst range; AP conversion can't rescue lost roll. |
| **Elite 2W HI (Hackable) accessible near midfield** | **Repeatable E/M** to Isolate/IMM-B, **Adhesive/PARA** if low PH, **AP+DA** to remove Wounds | Standard rifle at ARM5+ and favorable cover; explosive overkill if an E/M template secures objective. |
| **HI Warhorse / immunity to Isolated** | **AP/EXP/K1/Mono**, or PARA/IMM states **where legal**; inspect E/M's IMM-B separately | Claiming one E/M hit always fully disables or always does nothing. **Warhorse rules only explicitly prevent Isolated**. |
| **ARM-high / BTS-low** | **Breaker**, Plasma/Pulzar BTS, E/M for states | Assuming AP is always better than attacking lower BTS. MetaChem hosts cited a **Breaker pistol beating a TAG** through defense-type mismatch. |
| **VITA / BTS-high but ARM-low, or ARM3 HI** | **AP, T2, DA/EXP** according to save odds and Wounds; close Monofilament only if delivery is genuinely safe | K1 as a universal answer when its PS7 provides little edge over higher-Burst guns. |
| **Turn 3: 1 Order left, enemy TAG blocks objective** | **Any accessible E/M/PARA/Engaged/pinning threat** that creates one decisive disabled state; ignore full destruction where unnecessary | Spending remaining Orders to cause the *third STR loss* if immobilization would win now. |
| **Target has fast Engineer / Servant / Tech-Recovery** | Target **Engineer first**, cut route, attack from angle repeatedly, or deliver **Monofilament** permanent result | Stop after one IMM-B then assume it will stay gone all game. |

**Engineering caveat:** current N5.3 Engineer can clear the Engineer-removable conditions together under the single applicable success rule; Tech-Recovery has distinct long-range capabilities. Count attacker Orders against opponent's **repair Orders / route**, not fictitious one-Order-per-token taxes. [Recovery guide](doctors-engineers-and-recovery-apex-value.md).
