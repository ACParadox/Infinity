## Thirty-second results

**If the opponent has a 60–120-point TAG:**

The correct faction/mission/geometry decides, not a universal all-roster purchase.

**Tier interpretation:** an **S-value 5–10pt threat is not an S-reliability attacker**: the tier represents exceptional *potential swing per point*, not consistent solo results against prepared defenders. When the route is unsafe, a high-Burst AP gun can be the cheaper *total-Orders* solution.

**Decision rule:** need the enemy TAG **unable to act right now** → E/M/PARA; need it **Unconscious/Dead before the Engineer arrives** → repeatable AP+EXP/K1/HMG or Monofilament; need to **prevent it stepping into a lane** → Panzerfaust, E/M Mine, inexpensive ARO; need to beat an **elite TAG actively shooting back** → aim first for **Burst, attack MODs, range, Mimetism and firing angle**, then ammunition.

## 1. What “reliable” means — four different tests

| Dimension | Ask before paying | Biggest trap |
|---|---|---|
| **Access / setup** | Deployment, movement Orders, Camo/Hidden, Irregular Order, S4–S7 geometry, template reach, ammo supply | A 5pt E/Marat has **zero effect** when the TAG is 30" away behind screening. |
| **Win the Attack / avoid ARO** | BS/PH, Burst, weapon range, enemy Mimetism/Partial Cover/MSV, team bonuses | A 6pt BS11 Panzerfaust may lose FTF to a BS15 TAG; its AP+EXP never gets to Save. |
| **Convert an uncancelled hit** | Target ARM, BTS, Cover, Immunity(ARM/BTS/AP), Warhorse, 3STR, engineer/Servant nearby | **AP does not halve Cover**; E/M doesn't deal ordinary Wounds; STR vs VITA matters. |
| **Persist after it lands** | Enemy self-Reset, Engineer reach, Remote Presence repair, time left in game | An isolated+IMM-B TAG may recover enough to fight later. Judge Orders and mission opportunity denied. |

**Cross-game tiers below rate the *carrier plus current weapon option*, not theoretical ammunition in a vacuum.** “Apex” is maximum reasonable battlefield swing at the correct matchup; “value” includes printed points, SWC, **Orders spent arriving**, risk of losing a Regular Order, ammunition limits, and remaining mission utility.

### One **conditional** probability example (not a unit kill-rate)

Against a TAG with **BTS6** and no applicable Cover/other Saving MOD, an **uncancelled E/M hit (PS7)** yields **two Saves at ceil(6/2)+7 = 10**. Each has a **50% chance to fail** on d20, so **at least one failed Save = 1−0.5² = 75%**. That is the **conditional chance to inflict E/M states AFTER the weapon has already hit**, *not* a 75% chance that your Ghazi successfully reaches and bricks a TAG. A target with BTS9, cover/Immunity or a different PS changes the calculation. A Warhorse victim may resist Isolated regardless of this roll; an Engineer or eligible Reset may subsequently clear the disability.

Against ARM8 in Partial Cover, AP reduces **ARM8→4**, **then** adds +3 cover (if the weapon/matchup permits Cover). An AP HMG at **PS5** then gives defender **Save SV = 4+3+5 = 12**, a **40% chance of losing 1 STR per uncancelled hit**. The HMG still needs to generate uncancelled hits in FTF and often to force several wounds; **don't turn 40% per Save into a complete TAG-kill figure**.
