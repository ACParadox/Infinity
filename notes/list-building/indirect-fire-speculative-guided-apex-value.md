# Indirect Fire — Speculative Attack and Spotlight → Guided (N5.3 Apex and Value)

_Research: 2026-10-23. **Tactical relative tiers, not measured win rates or fixed damage-per-Order odds.** The latest committed [N5.3 sectorial references](../../sectorials) are authoritative for points/SWC, weapon mode, eligibility and Fireteams. This guide ranks **actual indirect attacks plus the whole enabling package**; media commentary (some pre-N5.3) is evidence for play patterns, not rules. ## The short verdict

| Award | Best choice | Why, and condition |
|---|---|---|

| **Best cheap offensive Speculative utility** | **Kestrel Jackal E/M GL 11pt/.5 SWC**, **FRRM infiltrating Métro GL 14pt/.5**, **Hunzakut GL 18pt/.5** | **Infiltration** creates immediate off-angle attacks; Jackal is Irregular with Minelayer, and the standard 10pt Métro GL is a *different, backfield* profile |
| **Best expensive attack+indirect hybrid** | **Redeye Continuous-Damage GL 38/1.5** (eligible PanO), **Squalos Mk-II HMG+GL 62/1.5**, **Maghariba Guard GL+MULTI HMG 71/1.5** | Primary attack plan and ability to threaten otherwise unreachable troops; inefficient if the premium chassis is bought *only* to lob B1 grenades |

| **Best cheap Guided payoff** | **Traktor Mul Katyusha Guided 9/1** (Ariadna); **Kuryer Katyusha 15/1** where permitted | Nine points is exceptionally cheap *if a forward Observer already creates Targeted*. Kuryer pays +6 for Warhorse/mine/Baggage/dual speculative and Guided capability |
| **Best standard Guided missile REM chassis** | **Vertigo, T-Drone, Clipper, Son-Bae, Samekh, Shaytaniyah, Millicent, Uran** (generally **16/1.5**) | The launcher is similar across factions; **rank the whole network, hacker/FO, repeaters and other uses**, not faction paint. **+1B/FTO alternative launchers are not automatically Guided** |
| **Highest network/Guided ceiling** | **Corregidor Moran×2 + Jazz HD+ + Vertigo**; **Nomads** with named hackers + Pitchers; **White Banner Dokkaebi + Haetae/Son-Bae** | Cheap early midfield Hacking Area / multiple attackers; **counter:** extra SWC, failure rolls and enemy Reset/anti-repeater play can make a conventional gun better |
| **Best Guided-without-expensive-hacking architecture** | **FRRM Para-Commando FO21/0 + Traktor9/1**; **TAK Chasseur/Strelok/Dynamo FO + Traktor/Kuryer** | WIP14/mobile/hidden or Flank FO already serves objectives; Guided becomes a low-investment second way to exploit Targeted |

**Important:** There is no such thing as a universal “best missile bot” without its **targeting support**. On-table work that does not need Targeted (Kuryer Speculative Fire or a forward PH grenade) and an off-table Guided strike solve different problems.

## 1. Rules that change decisions (N5.3)

| Rule | Practical implication |
|---|---|
| **Speculative Attack** | **Long Skill, Burst 1, −6 plus Range MOD**; ignores other negative visual/Cover/Mimetism MODs as specified. Requires the weapon's **Speculative Attack Trait**. A positive range band or good PH/BS matters disproportionately. **+1 Special Die does not apply to Long Skills**, even when available on an ordinary Fireteam shot |
| **Guided** | Needs the **BS Attack (Guided)** skill and an **enemy in Targeted State**. No LoF, but still weapon **maximum range / normal Range MOD**, and an eligible trajectory (not an enclosed room). **B1** unless an explicit exception. Target can **Dodge or Reset** in Face-to-Face; successful Reset also removes Targeted. Current rules limit active Guided attacks to **five per turn** |
| **Spotlight (Hacking)** | Active **B2** and **WIP vs Comms defense**; each hit inflicts **AP, PS5 against halved BTS**, with **Targeted only if the target fails a Saving Roll**. Can target **non-Hackable** troops; Reset is allowed against Comms Attack even for non-Hackables. **Never assume “one successful Spotlight roll = Targeted”** |
| **Targeted from FO / other tools** | Forward Observers create a *different delivery and angle problem* than a backfield Hacker; use the profile's actual current FO attack rules, WIP, position and any legal bonus. Targeted also aids **ordinary direct shooting**, so a launcher is not the only payoff |
| **Blast damage and counters** | Guided uses the prescribed circular/template mode; it does **not** universally let you choose single-target AP+EXP Hit Mode. Enemy spreading, Reset, Dodge, anti-network KHD, deleting Repeaters and shooting the exposed FO all reduce expected return |
| **Printed-profile trap** | FTO/+1B **Missile Launcher for a Fireteam** may lack **Guided**, even in the same unit entry as a Guided 16pt standalone REM. **ECM: Guided (-6)** on a TAG is *defence against* attacks, not an ability to make them |

Sources: [N5.3 Guided / BS Attack](https://infinitythewiki.com/Guided), [Spotlight](https://infinitythewiki.com/Spotlight), [Speculative Attack](https://infinitythewiki.com/Speculative_Attack), [repository Speculative guide](../Strategy/speculative-fire.md), [hacker rules/FAQ corrections](hackers-and-killer-hackers.md). **Do not carry N4 auto-Targeted Spotlight or flat +6 Guided into N5.3.**

## 3. **Speculative Fire POINTS VALUE** — prioritize useful starting range

The value list rewards **Infiltration, flanking Parachutist arrival, high mobility and strong secondary roles**. A cheap GL that starts in the DZ and moves only 10-10 often costs several Orders before its short band matters. **Kuryer is the deliberate exception**: long Katyusha range allows useful fire from the backfield. “Standard DZ” means the specific listed loadout does not print an advanced deployment skill.

| 2 | **S+** | **Jackal E/M GL + Minelayer 11/.5** | Infiltration; 10-10 | Cheap immediate E/M area denial + mine; Irregular and low-BS |

**Profile-specific deployment:** the **14pt Métro GL** has Infiltration and one-use Camouflage; its **10pt GL** does **not**. The **10pt Daylami GL** does not gain Camouflage from other Daylami options.

**Removed from Speculative Fire Value:** Squalos Mk-II and Maghariba Guard; they remain high-end combat hybrids in Apex, but are not points-efficient dedicated speculative attackers.

## 6. Pick by enemy / mission, not enthusiasm

| Situation | Better answer | Why / other side |
|---|---|---|
| Enemy Specialist/LT hides behind Total Cover **within a favorable Katyusha band**, no Targeted | **Kuryer speculative** | Zero setup, safe repeated attacks; try Guided if the target is far away and reliable Targeted **already** exists |
| Protected enemy gun has Mimetism(-6) + Cover | **Speculative** if in range / **Guided** if Targeted | Both avoid normal visual/Mimetism penalties; spec avoids needing separate spotter, Guided brings severe Blast consequence |
| Opponent presents **several troops in one circular template** | Spec/GUIDED template into center group | Biggest payoff when the enemy cannot scatter. One isolated single-wound cheerleader rarely justifies expensive setup |
| Enemy has strong **KHD, Repeater removal, and good Reset/Dodge** | **Direct gun / independent spec** | Guided may never fire successfully, and can be shut off by removing one network point |
| Enemy has high **ARM but low PH**, critical to remove | **Guided EXP Template** or strong AP direct shooting | Account for target Reset, actual missile range/Blast Mode and ARM saves; don't assume automatic damage |
| Mission needs buttons/zone control with few enemy hackers | **FO + spare Guided bot** only if Orders/SWC remain | FO scores even when no missile fires, unlike cyber net built for nothing |
| You have **1–2 spare Group-2 Orders every turn** | **Kuryer** or **low-cost speculative GL** | Independent potshots may have more real value than building an elaborate network with insufficient attack Orders |
| You have **live Pitchers/Repeaters and Spotlight ARO is routine** | **16pt Guided bot** | Cheap additional threat can force enemy Reset / kill units no gun sees; do not over-buy a premium launcher |
| Enemy is too far for good grenade range / no legal missile trajectory | **Long gun, closer FO or different objective route** | Neither mode ignores weapon maximum range or geometry |
| Enemy has a heavy **TAG with ECM Guided(-6)** | **Direct AP/K1/E/M or other anti-TAG** unless Targeted is exceptionally reliable | Avoid spending the turn on multiple avoidable low-odds layers |
| Your roster has **0.5 SWC left** | **Spec GL / cheap FO** when legal; skip 1–1.5SWC artillery | SWC cost can make a 9pt platform practically unaffordable |

## 7. Opposing opinions — retain both, then decide

1. **Kuryer Speculative vs Guided infrastructure:** [Robert Shepherd, TAK 2025 video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eM5cM_Y18vk) argues Katyusha's +3 band through 24″, affordable 15pt body and lack of a Targeted setup can make *several* speculative shots more Order-efficient than setting up Guided. **Counter:** at longer distances or against an already-Targeted high-value enemy, a Guided strike can be better; if the enemy doesn't need to sit still, long B1 chip misses may waste Orders. **This is the clearest direct side-by-side analysis**.
2. **Mandatory Guided in Corregidor?** [Tactical Awareness S3E23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RbNOBykfq_w) / [podcast](https://open.spotify.com/episode/1ngCemrrDVMMDgvWjNBMYY) debates a network built around:
   - **Moran** — midfield Repeater projection
   - **Jazz** — hacking/Spotlight component
   - **Vertigo** — Guided missile payoff

   One player considers the package capable of controlling the entire table; another emphasizes repeated deployment/Spotlight/save/missile/Reset rolls and weak impact against a **spread-out** enemy. [WIP12 E208, Jun 2026](https://open.spotify.com/episode/342lz18SRGSZ8QWf1Zg3Z9) adds that post-Mazebreaker Corregidor need not surrender half its SWC to the old network identity.
3. **Dedicated Guided-first Nomads vs multi-use hacking:** [Tactical Awareness S3E26, Vanilla Nomads](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2PpxuDu2WlxipiqpVzHTdv) contains explicit **Spotlight-and-missile-first** list and another remote-heavy alternative that likes Guided *because repeaters already exist*. **For:** powerful area denial and alternate attack vector. **Against:** little direct gunfire and overconcentration in Hackers means one anti-network plan can blunt everything.
4. **Kuryer versus Traktor:** [WIP12 E197 — FRRM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GmVfuKSIkXg) / [podcast](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3YNuQVF24bJmCGdxL1FjK5) compares:
   - **Kuryer — 15pt:** Warhorse and mine utility, but rostered **ETB-only** in FRRM rather than a generic Wildcard
   - **Traktor — 9pt:** saves six points when the task is only Guided
   - **Para-Commando FO:** favored as the active targeter for either artillery option

   Whether Kuryer's extras repay the six-point difference depends on the list's other needs.
5. **Speculative pressure wins without lethal kills:** [Shepherd — TAK](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eM5cM_Y18vk) argues safe Katyusha fire forces occupants to leave strong defensive setups, allowing the ARO triangle to exploit their movement. **Against:** if the opponent can tolerate missed B1 potshots or is already completing the mission, indirect doesn't magically win objectives.
6. **Guided as harassment versus matchup-specific waste:** [Shepherd — CanCon Ariadna 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTqYE_FddwQ) reports several hacking-heavy opponents got very limited actual kill return across five games against a mostly nonhackable, dispersed roster. **Counter:** a high-value stationary LT or clustered Hackable package in a well-covered Repeater net can still justify Guided.
7. **What does Shinden missile do?** [Shepherd — Shindenbutai N5](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=odeyiO-fm4E) views **Uran as a conditional payoff** when existing Pitchers/Spotlight are already present, preferring lethal **Trinity/cyber attacks** over playing every Order as a missile setup. Alternative: a 16pt missile in Group 2 magnifies Spotlight ARO and pressures otherwise safe troops.
8. **PanO coordinated missiles versus a normal gun:** [Tactical Awareness S3E07 — Vanilla PanO](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZbwXcQ_IK5A) experiments with **FTO +1B Clippers/EVO-assisted** direct attacks and Spotlight; do **not** import their conventional linked/direct bonuses to a **16pt unlinked Guided Clipper**. PanO gun-first advocates take a more efficient primary BS weapon and use the same repeaters for defenses instead.
