# Combined-Arms / Toolbox Fireteams — compact gunfighter + specialists Apex / Value

**Rebuilt 20 November 2026** from the current [source-backed Fireteam occurrence register](OBSERVED-COMPOSITIONS.md), current N5.3 [sectorial reference charts](../../../sectorials), and relevant cross-role [gunfighting](gunfighting-fireteams-apex-and-value.md) / [objective-play](mission-specialists-objective-play-apex-and-value.md) rankings.

**Selection rule:** start with a **top-quality gunfighter suited to the team's intended active or reactive plan, or a truly exceptional multi-role unit**, then ask whether its **actually linked** teammates make that gun stronger *and* supply useful mission/technical solutions: **Doctor/Paramedic, Engineer, hacker/KHD, Forward Observer, Minelayer, Disco/Smoke, strong counter-CC**. Reward relevant range-band and counter coverage separately. An ordinary weapon + several cheap bodies does not make an Apex toolbox. Apex prioritizes maximum all-comers team capability in a realistic 300-point list; Value prioritizes performance **per points/SWC and actual body count**, with unverified totals penalized rather than guessed.

**Positional compatibility rule:** Mixed role coverage is not genuine synergy if members want incompatible locations. A **dedicated backfield ARO sniper/launcher** and a **dedicated forward-driving active-turn shooter** often work against each other: one wants to hold a lane, the other to maneuver. Prefer specialists and **short-range defensive weapons** that travel with the attacker, or support that stays with a defensive anchor. A coherent **advance-then-hold** plan is the exception, not a reason to combine unrelated premium guns.

**Size rule:** **2–3 members only by default**. One explicitly documented **four-member exception** is included because every member solves a separate problem; there are **no five-body teams**. A four-body link with L2 should *not* be promoted on headcount. Fireteam Level depends on chart equivalence, not raw physical size, and **L2 BS Attack(+1 SD) is not +1 Burst**. [Official N5.3 rules](https://infinitythewiki.com/Fireteam_Bonuses) apply.

**Evidence rule:** every listed formation was used or participant-described, with its own indexed battle-report or video/podcast link. A played older-N5 link is *not proof of current Army export legality*; confirm every FTO, AVA, source-required anchor and profile against the pinned chart before copying. Weapons not identified by the report are **not filled in from guesses**. Modern costs shown are only those pinned with unambiguous current loadout matches; **—** means unverified, and Value rankings for those entries are qualitative, not calculated points-per-effect. Single reports (including tutorials/retrospectives/early-stops) do not establish win rates.

## Why the top entries changed

- **Best elite gun + practical support:** Sheskiin + Hacker/Disco, Penitent AP Continuous + Cenobite HRL + Orphan Engineer, Phoenix + Machaon + Myrmidon, Hoplite + Scylla + Alkê, and Asawira + Muyib Doctor + Govad KHD have credible independent threats and useful specialist/counter tools **inside** the Fireteam. They replace earlier large or low-information mixed links at the top.
- **Best affordable modules:** Yě Māo AP Spitfire + Yín-Shé Specialist (50/1.5), Karhu Feuerbach + Paramedic (58/1.5), Karhu + Engineer (59/1.5), and Griffin + Firefly Engineer (61/1.5) are compelling **two-person** routes. Three-model Harises must justify buying an extra body over these.
- **Four-member exception:** The **99/2.5 Asawira/Ghulam/Barid** Core is the only four-person entry, since the second Asawira's Disco/shotgun and the Ghulam NCO/Smoke plus Barid Pitcher/KHD supply genuine distinct attack/counter functions. It reaches only **L2**, and has a significant SWC footprint. No five-person link earns an exception here.
- **Why older leaders are out:** **Druze** 117/2.5 requires *five bodies* for a merely adequate shooter and useful but expensive E/M/hacking; **Dahshat** Brawler Sniper/Hacker/Digger has a split backline/forward mission; **Onyx** Kerr-Nau + two Unidrons has **unreported individual guns**. They remain documented in the [occurrence register](OBSERVED-COMPOSITIONS.md), but cannot outrank the above **fully identified compact elite gun + mission** packages. Likewise, unreported Neoterra Aquila/Bolt/Shona profile choices cannot be silently upgraded for an Apex score.

**Not included simply for theoretical legality:** Asura, Crux Knight, or other standout solo units do not become verified Fireteams because they have outstanding unit profiles. They require a sourced *specific linked* formation. The subsequently registered **Shikami/Shikami Duo** is an excellent double-scorer/CC module but lacks a distinct long-range gun; **Shona/Varangian** is a close-assault/Smoke module, not an elite gunfighter-plus-specialist team. The **Cenobite/Custodier** trio has a strong reactive/cyber plan but is documented in a 200-point game, and does not displace the stronger mixed gunfighter-led teams here. [Evidence policy](README.md) is unchanged. This is an editorial role ranking of the **observed sample**, not a statistically proven global win-rate order or exhaustive optimization of every legal N5.3 formation.
