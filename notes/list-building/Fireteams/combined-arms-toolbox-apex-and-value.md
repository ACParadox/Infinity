# Combined-Arms / Toolbox Fireteams — source-backed Apex and Value rankings

**Reassessed 20 November 2026.** Rank the **reported Fireteam itself**, not its sectorial's overall tier. Independent TAGs, infiltrators, repeaters and cheap Orders do not become linked members; an otherwise weak sectorial can still have one good Fireteam. The old Druze #1 Apex and Dahshat #1 / Onyx #2 Value placements underrepresented stronger recorded alternatives.

**Criteria:** complementary usable short/mid/long range bands, distinct anti-armor/anti-Mimetism/visibility/hacking counters, genuine in-link specialists and recovery, effective delivery, physical size and points/SWC. **Apex** rewards observed self-contained capability, **Value** practical roles per linked model and verified cost. Missing N5.3 prices make most Value positions **qualitative/provisional**, not numerical efficiency scores.

**Evidence and legality:** Every entry links to a [documented source-backed occurrence](OBSERVED-COMPOSITIONS.md); the register now holds **73** formations. A historical N5 played team is **not automatically N5.3 legal**. Level is listed only when current chart equivalence was documented, not inferred from size; **Not verified** means level/cost or loadout must be checked against [the pinned charts](../../../sectorials). See [Fireteam criteria](README.md). Tutorial, translated and early-stop reports have reduced competitive weight; one game is not a win rate.

## Why former leaders moved

- **Druze (#9 Apex, outside leading Value):** E/M GL + HMG + KHD/Pitcher + Paramedic is a legitimate **117/2.5** L5 toolbox, but has a heavy five-body footprint, limited delivery options and mediocre baseline gunfighting compared with smaller flexible links. It should not lead the whole-game toolbox Apex.
- **Dahshat (outside leading Value):** [#129](../../battle-reports/121-130-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) confirms **Brawler Hacker + MSV2 Sniper + Digger**, but the sniper's backfield role conflicts with the Digger's forward assault niche. Without verified prices and with translated source ambiguity, #1 Value was not justified.
- **Onyx (outside leading Value):** [#192](../../battle-reports/191-200-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) confirms **Kerr-Nau + two Unidrons**, but neither Unidron's weapon was reported. Claiming specific shooting bands or precise role/points efficiency would invent evidence; exclusion is **not** a declaration that the link is bad.
- **Higher-tier armies:** NCA, for example, has strong independent tools, but the archived Aquila/Bolt/Shona or Bolt/Hannibal/Shona teams lack confirmed complete loadouts. Overall army rank is not permission to insert hypothetical Fireteam weapons or specialists.

**Boundary:** These rankings compare observed exact-member formations, not all theoretically optimal N5.3 compositions. No undocumented filler, substituted profile or invented Fireteam is included.
