# Mission Specialists / Objective Play — Fireteam Apex & Value Rankings (N5.3 / ITS 18)

**Reviewed:** 19 November 2026. **Scope:** standard 300-point all-comers Infinity N5.3, sectorial Main-list Fireteams (including Tohaa's chart-specific Triads); **not** vanilla unsupported links, Reinforcement groups, Spec-Ops or an entire army list. **Status:** editorial cross-sectorial ranking of checked, costed, legal team *compositions*, **not** tournament win-rate or a claim that every exact selected loadout was played.

[Fireteams ranking conventions](README.md) · [N5.3 Fireteam chart rules](https://infinitythewiki.com/Fireteams_Chart) · [Fireteam Bonuses](https://infinitythewiki.com/Fireteam_Bonuses) · [Fireteam basic rules](https://infinitythewiki.com/Fireteams:_Basic_Rules)

## Reassessment under cross-role evaluation guidelines — 19 November 2026

**Re-ranking lens:** reward completing, retaining and revisiting objectives first, then **protected delivery** against long-range ARO, Mimetism and close screens, access to different specialist tasks, and survival of a casualty. Short/medium combat answers are more relevant than full sniper coverage unless they enable the objective route. Compact Duos/Haris are advantaged when their specialists and escort are independently useful; the two large five-model specialist Cores drop where they pay for headcount rather than additional mission reach. The 120-point Apex band remains a ceiling, not an efficiency target.

## Legality and practical-use checklist

1. **Formations:** only use the named sectorial family and its allowed types. Duo starts at 2, Haris at 3, Core at 3–5; a “Triad” label does **not** itself authorize five Tohaa models. Tohaa has **no Core**, with chart-limited Duos and unusually broad Haris access.
2. **Required members and options:** Wildcards do **not** bypass source-member requirements. Exact FTO status, option IDs, AVA limits and per-family member caps remain binding. Do not transfer a counts-as identity between different chart families, or infer transitive equivalence.
3. **Level:** recheck counts-as identities when changing any member. Mixed five-body Cores can be L1–L4 rather than L5; no “free” Sixth Sense from team size alone.
4. **Objective economy:** linked specialists do not all activate an objective at once. An extra Doctor/FO/Hacker is insurance against losses and broadens mission eligibility; it does not multiply one Order's objective attempts.
5. **Access and survivability:** a backfield static full Core may be less useful than a 2–3 model forward-moving team on spread-out objectives. Measure terrain, mission zones, specialists' starting position and likely hacking exposure before applying the ranking.
6. **Peripherals and state restrictions:** do not join a Controller with an active synchronized Peripheral where the Fireteam rules prohibit it; an Ancillary-specific exception may allow linking **only while undeployed/Disconnected/Null**. Marker/Hidden/Infiltration/Airborne and other restricted profiles/states need their exact N5.3 permission checked.
7. **Order and slot constraints:** Tohaa's Haris exceptions and Morat/Qapu variations are faction-specific; do not assume a global one-Haris rule. Irregular members and Fireteam Master interactions (notably Kestrel Jackals) require proper Order accounting. These ranking rows are **alternatives**, not a single list.
