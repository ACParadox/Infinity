# Mission Specialists / Objective Play Fireteams — Apex and Value

**Member tags:** `Mod-Stacking` records a current documented native or exact-profile BS MOD advantage on the named member; it does not confirm an unreported historical loadout or confer that ability on other team members.

**Reassessed 20 November 2026 · source-backed, N5.3-profile-aware.** This ranks the **strongest documented Fireteams for getting a qualified specialist to the objective and scoring**, rather than giving weak sectorials a representative slot. **All ranked Fireteams contain 2 or 3 physical troopers.** A fourth or fifth member would need a *separately documented* decisive gain in specialist redundancy, safe delivery or Order economy; none of the available larger examples beats the compact shortlist on that test. These are editorial comparative judgments, **not faction win-rate measurements**.

**Selection priority:** (1) a real eligible Specialist on the *recorded* member profile; (2) a second complementary Specialist/skill if possible; (3) practical route protection, speed and counter coverage (MSV versus Mimetism, Albedo/Disco versus visors, anti-HI, hacking protection, templates and Smoke); (4) resilience after losing one member; (5) points/SWC, movement footprint and Order cost. A mere Doctor/Engineer/CoC label is not enough: **CoC, an ordinary Lieutenant, and a gunner without a specialist skill are not generic button-pushers**. A high-ranked stand-alone specialist who was *not* documented in a particular Fireteam cannot be inserted just to manufacture a better composition.

The complete membership and source link for every row comes from the [observed-composition register](OBSERVED-COMPOSITIONS.md), checked against the [current sectorial profiles and Fireteam charts](../../../sectorials/) and [Fireteam guidelines](README.md). **Level** is based on actual same-unit/Counts-as groups where currently verifiable, not the number of models. **Pts/SWC*** are included only when the reported loadouts identify the unique matching modern profiles; **—** means incomplete, not free. A past report proves use in its own rules revision, **not automatic current legality**. The highlighted conditional Jazz FTO restriction must be checked when copying the composition.

## Why these replaced the previous leaders

- **Demoted/excluded from top objective-play rankings:** USAriadna Maverick/Motorized 112 (only one Doctor and limited complementary scoring); Ramah Zhayedan/Ghulam/Rouhani four-body Core (heavy recovery duplication and four-model movement tax); Kestrel Griffin/Bipandra/Fennecs five-body Core (shooting/ARO link with too little scoring specialization for its footprint); White Company Haidao/Kaplan/Beast Hunter **four-body** Core (the *documented* Hǎidào/Hǎidào/Karhu **three-body** Haris offers two technical specialists at lower footprint). These are recorded historical teams, just **not** best-in-game objective squads.
- **Why no invented top-tier Triads:** Draal Specialist Operative, Eudoros Specialist Operative, Shikami and other exceptional individual scorers are discussed in [individual specialist rankings](../mission-specialists-and-button-pushers-ranking.md), but **no exact corresponding 2–3-body membership in the evidence register** is established for these profiles. Their strength does *not* authorize making up a Fireteam. For example, an older reported Eudoros/K2/Myrmidons link is incompatible with the pinned Steel Phalanx Myrmidon-Core chart's absence of K2 membership.
- **Hacker caution:** Jazz, Pandora, Tanuki and Scylla can often project hacking through Repeaters without physically advancing their Fireteam. Rank the *linked* package for actual console/Classified access and protection, **not** for a network the rest of the roster supplies.
- **Sources vs projections:** Reports #121, #190 and #205 explicitly identify the three-model specialist teams; report #010 documents White Company's Hǎidào/Hǎidào/Karhu Haris. Several source-era games predating N5.3 contain loadout or naming uncertainty; keep those distinctions visible. A source-backed team is **not claimed to be the global optimal legal composition** across every N5.3 sectorial.

## Evidence and maintenance

Each entry has a primary game/player source **in its own row**. The [register](OBSERVED-COMPOSITIONS.md) contains the same recorded bodies and additional observed compositions, including many **not eligible for these rankings**. Future changes should discover and verify an actual played/player-discussed 2–3-model team first, check current chart membership/profile constraints second, and only then compare its mission efficiency. Do not turn a source-described Duo into a theoretical Haris or treat a periphery, pilot or Decoy as a linked trooper.
