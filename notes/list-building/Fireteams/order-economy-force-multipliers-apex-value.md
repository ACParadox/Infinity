# Order Economy / Force Multipliers — Fireteam Apex & Value (Infinity N5.3)

*Editorial whole-game comparison · 19 November 2026 · standard 300-point games, pinned September 2026 N5.3 Army data. Not a measured win-rate ranking. The latest [sectorial files](../../../sectorials) and [Fireteam rules](../../../rules/6_Command%20and%20Fireteams/Fireteams__Basic_Rules.md) govern legality, rather than this shortlist.*

**Question:** Which **legal Fireteams**, considered as complete purchased packages, best improve the army's *usable Orders, activations per Order and command/mission economy*? This is deliberately **not** a ranking of single cheap cheerleaders, nor of the biggest gunfighter brick with an incidental extra Order. Related: [Order Generators](../cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md), [Force Multipliers](../force-multipliers-special-abilities-apex-value.md), [Fireteam conventions](README.md), [command structure](../../Strategy/lieutenant-and-command-structure.md).

## Rules, counting method and ranking standard

- **Apex** = maximum credible all-comers impact from the **full Fireteam**, including its Order engine, what those Orders can accomplish, survival, team-level bonus and its effect on the rest of a list. **Value** = that impact *per total team points/SWC, Combat Group slots, mandatory anchor and foregone competing Fireteam*. These are **two distinct ranks**, not an average.
- **Preferred Apex: ≤120 points; Conditional: 121–135; Exceptional: 136–150; >150 normally excluded.** Every primary ranked team below fits the **preferred** band. The 110-point Invincible Haris is a *borderline expensive allocation* even though it meets the formal cap.
- **Pooled Regular** means an Order that may be spent on *other troopers in the same Combat Group*. **FT Master** makes **all members of its legal Fireteam Regular at Order Count**, not just the bracketed named unit. The bracketed name governs a special *automatic Fireteam cancellation* condition if that unit is present and the Master leaves. A converted Irregular is **one** Regular contributor, never an Irregular **plus** a Regular Order. Loss of Lieutenant is not prevented. [FT Master](../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/FT_Master.md) · [official rules](https://infinitythewiki.com/FT_Master).
- **Native Regular** troops are **not extra Orders generated by the link**. The Imperial Service Kuang Shi are already Regular; Pandora and Regular Stingers likewise do not create conversion surplus. Count native Orders separately from converted Orders.
- **Tactical Awareness** generates a personal Tactical Order, *not* a transferable Regular Order. If its user becomes Team Leader, it can activate their Fireteam. **NCO** replaces an available Lieutenant Special Order with a Tactical Order for its user, who may lead the team with it; NCO **does not generate** an additional Lieutenant Order. **Strategos L1** instead replaces the Lieutenant Special Order with a **pooled Regular** Order in the LT's Combat Group. Do **not** count the same LT resource for both NCO and Strategos. [Tactical Awareness](../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Tactical_Awareness.md) · [NCO](../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/NCO.md) · [Strategos](../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Strategos.md) · [official Fireteam activation](https://infinitythewiki.com/Fireteam_Order).
- All members must occupy the **same Combat Group** and consume their normal trooper slots. All totals below include **every listed member**, but **not** the rest of the army. The **Fireteam Level (FT L#)** counts same-Unit / explicit *counts-as* equivalence, **not physical member count**. Two different types in a 3-member Haris can mean **FT L2**. [FT bonuses](../../../rules/6_Command%20and%20Fireteams/Fireteam_Bonuses.md).
- Evidence labels distinguish **pinned roster/chart legality** from **reported played compositions**. A proposed combination that the chart permits is *not* a battle-report-tested unit. No tournament performance statistics are inferred from a video or roster.

## Why other attractive teams did not displace the Top 10

- **Hector LT + Steel Phalanx/Thorakitai linked troops** and **Kornak LT + Morat linked troops** can make *excellent total army command shells*, especially with extra LT Orders. However **Strategos already converts LT Orders without the Lieutenant being linked**. The incremental *Fireteam-specific* economic benefit must justify the hero's cost and risk before they beat the cheap conversion modules. No invented precise hero brick is ranked.
- **Other ordinary 5 × cheap Regular infantry Cores** have good passive 5-Order density and can sometimes be stronger mission shells; *linking them does not manufacture extra pooled Orders.* The ISS Core is included because its price, Smoke, FT L5, restrictive Monitor and recorded play make it a particularly instructive competitive benchmark.
- **Double TAG/Tactical Awareness Duos over 120–150 points** may activate powerfully, but a TA Order still belongs to its owner and the remaining army can be starved of affordable scoring tools. The 110-point IA Haris is preferred here because its paid resources supply **shooting, hacking, NCO, two TA activations and a real FT L3**.
- **FT Master with more expensive Irregular troops**, or one where the Irregular models do not start deployed/in the same Combat Group, is seldom a cheap Order farm. Always check the exact chart and whether the relevant troopers actually contribute at Order Count.

## Practical decision guide

1. **Need pooled Orders elsewhere in the Combat Group?** Prioritize **Morat Oznat Core/Haris**, **Kestrel Magistrate/Jackals**, or **ISS Kuang Shi Core**. Do not describe TA or NCO as a replacement for their freely transferable resources.
2. **Need extra activations specifically for a fighting Fireteam?** **IA's TA-heavy Invincible Haris** leads, with an honest **110-point/2-SWC price tag**. The TA users must lead when spending their respective Orders, and an NCO Order additionally needs a legitimate LT Special Order.
3. **Need cheap combat with order conversion?** **Liang Kai/Shaolin** is the most efficient aggressive cheap pack; **Lobo/Diablos** is a stronger durable close-trading alternative; **Taskmaster/Morlocks** brings the most resilient anchor but consumes SWC.
4. **Need specialist/network/ARO role compression?** **Patsy/Helots** and **Pandora/Steeljaws** give utility the cheapest Order farm does not, but lose the passive cost contest.
5. **Audit the full list**: include Core/Haris slot competition, 10-trooper Combat Group maximum, First Turn Order count, attacker's ability to spend the gained Orders, LT safety, FT Master survival and required team formation. Recompute at the next Order Count after losses.
