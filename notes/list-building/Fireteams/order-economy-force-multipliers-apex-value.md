# Order Economy / Force Multipliers — observed Apex and Value Fireteams

**Reassessed 20 November 2026 — cheerleaders and compact FT Master engines first.** These ranks are limited to [observed or participant-reported Fireteams](OBSERVED-COMPOSITIONS.md) with exact recorded member identities. A chart-legal composition with no observed use belongs in the **separate, unranked scouting table** below. Each ranked entry links to its battle report/video/podcast; unknown historical loadouts remain unknown.

## What wins this category

1. **FT Master Order conversion:** at the Order Count step, an FT Master **currently in the Fireteam** changes its Irregular members to Regular, generating pooled Orders that would otherwise be unavailable to the rest of their Combat Group. Record the **number converted**, not just the physical number of bodies. This matters more than generic link-bonus shooting.
2. **Cheap cheerleaders:** prioritize **three cheap native-Regular bodies** giving three usable pool Orders and credible Smoke, hacking, specialist or reactive utility. **Three Regular troops generate the same three Orders whether linked or not**; the Fireteam only adds tactical advantages and costs a Fireteam slot, with positioning constraints.
3. **Restricted-order leverage:** NCO, extra Lieutenant Orders and Tactical Awareness are useful if the reported profiles actually establish them. These **do not** increase the pooled Regular Order count. Do not assume a Tactical Awareness option or NCO configuration from a merely named trooper.
4. **Size discipline:** **three-member Core/Haris is the default**. Include four/five only for unusually cheap extra pooled Regular contributors, worthwhile L4/L5 coverage, or exceptional **FT Master multi-Order conversions**; no expensive five-body attack team on general 'Order Economy' grounds. A two-member team needs a compelling conversion/value reason to displace a three-member candidate.
5. **Net list cost:** count points, SWC, slots, opportunity cost and FT Master fragility. N5.3 exact totals cannot be assigned to observed records whose original profiles were not recovered; numerical examples in the *scouting* section are modern reconstructions, **not historical reports**.

The [current FT Master rule](https://infinitythewiki.com/FT_Master) changes the members' Order status **at Order Count**; it does not create permanent Regular status. Some FT Masters with a bracketed unit dependency automatically lose the Fireteam if the Master leaves. [Regular vs Tactical and Lieutenant Orders](https://infinitythewiki.com/Order) remain separate. Breaking a Fireteam after Order Count does not retroactively make earlier counts a permanent characteristic.

**Historical boundary:** Source-era membership does not by itself prove today's N5.3 FTO/AVA/composition legality.
