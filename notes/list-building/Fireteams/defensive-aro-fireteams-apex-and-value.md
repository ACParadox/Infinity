# Defensive / ARO Fireteams — compact, source-backed Apex and Value rankings

**Member tags:** `Mod-Stacking` records a current documented native or exact-profile BS MOD advantage on the named member; it does not confirm an unreported historical loadout or confer that ability on other team members.

**Reassessed 20 November 2026 (Infinity N5.3).** This ranking begins with **the actual ARO anchor**, then asks whether its **recorded, legal Fireteam** improves reactive reliability, visibility/armor counterplay, short-range coverage and resilience enough to justify the members. **Two-model Duos and three-model Haris/Core teams are the default.** A four- or five-body team qualifies only for an unusually strong, documented defensive benefit; a full Core is not a free ARO upgrade.

**Scope and evidence:** Every numbered entry is tied to a game, first-person tournament recap or source video and identified physical members. The shared [occurrence register](OBSERVED-COMPOSITIONS.md) is the evidence index; [N5.3 sectorial rosters](../../../sectorials) govern present-day eligibility. Historical source membership is **not** proof every old profile exists unchanged now. **No proposed chart-legal-only team enters the table.** Points/SWC are given only when the recorded roles resolve to pinned current profiles; **—** means not safely recoverable. The Value order is qualitative, **not** a points-efficiency calculation where cost is missing.

**ARO mechanics:** Only the eligible reacting trooper shoots in its ARO; linked guns do not all fire for free. L2 gives **BS Attack (+1 Special Die)** rather than +1 Burst; L3 also improves Discover/Dodge, L4 adds +1 BS, L5 adds Sixth Sense. A normal weapon **(+1B)** is not extra reactive Burst; Total Reaction/Neurocinetics specifically change reactive Burst. Favor exposed-lane quality, ARO durability, template/close defense, MSV versus Mimetism, alternative non-MSV lines, and actual defensive job of the supporting members. An Engineer does **not** medically heal a living sniper just by being linked to it.

**Positional compatibility rule:** Build around an ARO anchor and teammates that can **hold the same position**. A dedicated active-turn assault gun tends to push forward while a sniper/launcher wants to keep its reactive lane; pairing both in one Fireteam creates a movement and Coherency conflict even when both are individually excellent. A **short-range defensive gun or template** is different: it screens nearby approaches without abandoning the ARO anchor's position. A mobile **advance-then-hold** group is a possible exception where both models genuinely benefit from occupying the same final area.

## Historical strong ARO example — not ranked as an unchanged N5.3 Fireteam

**Kestrel · Black A.I.R. Neuro MULTI Sniper + three Fennecs (four-member Core):** [#002](../../battle-reports/001-010-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) explicitly describes **Black A.I.R. FTO MULTI Sniper, Fennec Lieutenant, Fennec Decoy and Fennec Forward Observer**. The FTO sniper's **Neurocinetics + MSV2 + Mimetism(-3)** is one of the best Fireteam-capable individual ARO profiles. **However, the recorded Fennec Decoy option is absent from the current N5.3 roster.** The source-based team remains historically important, but this literal exact-profile quartet cannot be represented as a verified present-day list. Current Kestrel guidance [constructs a legal four-member Fennecs Core](../../../sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md#recommended-fireteam-compositions--n53-audited) by changing the Decoy to a basic Fennec; **that replacement is proposed, not separately observed**, and therefore does not enter the evidence-only ranking. The chart offers **FENNECS Core/Haris, not a FENNECS Duo**, so do not invent a cheaper two-body Black A.I.R. pairing.

## Exclusions and size discipline

**USAriadna** Grunt/Rosie four-body Core and Blackjack Sniper/HMG Duo are genuine reported formations ([#057](../../battle-reports/051-060-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md), [#124](../../battle-reports/121-130-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md)), **not** credible world-leading compact ARO choices versus the available premium visor, Mimetism, Neurocinetics and +SD anchors. Both are **removed from the ranked shortlist**, not declared illegal or unusable. Similarly, the five-body **TAK Veteran/Frontovik**, **MO Crosier/Commander**, **Morat Suryat/Kaitok**, **White Company Hawkwood/Emily/Fusiliers** and four-body **Tunguska Hollow Men** observations consume too many bodies or have insufficient demonstrated *defensive* return to outrank the compact choices here. **A large group gets no size bonus for being reported once.**

**Evidence is narrower than theoretical optimality:** sources span N5.1–N5.3, a 200-point match and custom/asymmetric games. An observed lineup is not an experimentally proven strongest loadout. For building a competitive new list, recheck each [N5.3 roster](../../../sectorials), especially FTO limits, Fireteam identity/Counts-as, and profiles that changed after the video. Never combine members from different lists or turn a loadout suggestion into a reported deployment.
