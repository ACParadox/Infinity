# Defensive / ARO Fireteams — source-backed Apex and Value rankings

**Evidence-only reassessment (19 November 2026).** These are **rankings of observed or participant-described Fireteam memberships**, not invented optimal N5.3 combinations. Each ranked Fireteam is linked **in its own row** to a numbered actual battle report, and to the original video/podcast where the [source register](OBSERVED-COMPOSITIONS.md) supplies it. **No unsourced exact profiles, extra models or fifth-member fillers are assumed.**

**Role objective:** Reliable reactive lanes, visual counterplay, multiple weapon distances and persistence. Apex evaluates the strongest demonstrated role package; Value compares practical output with the physical footprint and cost **only where exact modern cost is recoverable**. Since source recordings often predate N5.3 and do not disclose every modern profile, **Value ordering is qualitative/provisional, not an exact points-per-effect calculation**. A dash in the price column is missing verified N5.3 information, **not zero cost**.

**Rules/legality caveat:** The historical source proves the combination was described/played in its own N5 environment; it **does not certify the identical formation under today's N5.3 Army chart**. Any modern list must be rechecked against [the sectorial guides](../../../sectorials), AVA, Counts-as, FTO restrictions and the current [Fireteam evaluation standards](README.md). A recorded custom/tutorial game or historical tournament report is not a win-rate statistic.

## Audit trail and exclusions

The [70 source-backed occurrence entries](OBSERVED-COMPOSITIONS.md) are the candidate pool; these tables intentionally include fewer than an artificially padded world-wide Top 10/15 where evidence is absent. Earlier versions mixed actual links with invented, chart-legal alternative profile selections. **Those uncorroborated constructions are not retained here.** A participant's Fireteam is not expanded from two to three or three to five just to improve bonuses. Rank direction is a qualitative judgment among documented cases, not proof of an optimal N5.3 result.
