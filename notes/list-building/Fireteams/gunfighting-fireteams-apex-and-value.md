### Tunguska: Hollow Men Core (L3; 116 pts / 1.5 SWC)

**Role:** Active / long-range. **Canonical source:** [Tunguska Fireteams and profiles](../../../sectorials/nomads/tunguska.md). **Evidence:** Constructed from the current Hollow Men chart and pinned loadouts.

- Kriza Borac HMG — **opt 2: 45 pts / 1.5 SWC**
- Hollow Man MULTI Rifle/Pitcher — **opt 2: 26 pts / 0 SWC**
- Hollow Man TinBot (-3) Combi — **opt 5: 24 pts / 0 SWC**
- Hollow Man BSG Specialist Operative — **opt 8: 21 pts / 0 SWC**

**Gunfight case:** Kriza's native +1B and enemy BS −3 are backed by L3 +1SD, three two-Wound Hollow Men, a Pitcher and TinBot. Strongest sustained primary HMG attack ceiling in the shortlist.

**Limitation:** Only L3, not L4; 116 points of Hackable infantry, no built-in MSV and no universal answer to White Noise or hacking.

## Reliability, legality, and comparison notes

1. **No fictional Burst:** L2 is a **Special Die**; ordinary +1B on a gun/skill is generally active-turn-only, not automatic extra ARO Burst. True Total Reaction/Neurocinetics cases must be checked against the applicable native rule. L4 gives BS+1, not an extra projectile.
2. **Gun-holder level:** A 5-body team with only four matching members is **L4** even if a non-equivalent high-end gun is the fifth member. In particular, the **Corregidor Vostok** build is L4 from four Alguaciles; the Vostok does not add the fifth equivalent. The Tunguska Kriza is **L3** from its three Hollow Men; the Kriza does not count as Hollow Men. An active gun can still benefit from a team level generated by its teammates.
3. **Multi-family constraints:** Use **Corregidor Surface**, not a blend with Deepspace. Team Achilles forbids Wildcards. **Steel Phalanx cannot field a five-person Core**. **Tohaa has zero Core and unlimited Triad Haris, but at most two Duos.** Do not import a different faction's lookalike team rule.
4. **N5.3 FTO fidelity:** The 34/1 Vostok FTO Spitfire lacks the non-FTO Spitfire's native Repeater; Patroclus FTO has its own MEDIKIT/MULTI Marksman profile rather than borrowing Smoke or Holoprojector; Shinden's Rui Shi is only eligible in the named Sayō context.
5. **Points/SWC:** Cost counts **all actual team members**, not just the primary gun or the incremental price for attaching a Wildcard. Use 300 points plus the actual scenario SWC rules. No team shown should be read as automatically fieldable with every other Fireteam in its sectorial.
6. **Evidence grades:** A cited **report** supports only the stated team *shape* unless its profiles are independently verified. An exact current-profile **constructed** candidate is not an exported tournament winner. For evolving Fireteam rulings, the [canonical sectorial Fireteam section](../../../sectorials) and [local Fireteam rules](../../../rules/6_Command%20and%20Fireteams/Fireteams__Basic_Rules.md) control.
