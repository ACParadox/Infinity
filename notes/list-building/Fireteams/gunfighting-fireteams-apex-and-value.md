# Gunfighting Fireteams — Apex and Value rankings (N5.3)

**Reassessed 20 November 2026.** Rank the **gunfighter first and the actual Fireteam second**, rather than awarding the top spots to the longest recorded Core or an already-weak sectorial. Fireteams with **2 or 3 physical troopers are the default**. A fourth trooper is considered only if it supplies a significant new counter or range band; five-body gunlines are deliberately omitted. A strong sectorial is not an automatic winner, but a single documented Druze or USAriadna Core is not evidence of game-wide Apex.

**Evidence gate:** Every row is a played or participant-described composition in the [source-backed occurrence register](OBSERVED-COMPOSITIONS.md), with its own report reference (and original media link when available). These are **the strongest evidence-qualified candidates**, not a fabricated ranking of every theoretically legal N5.3 Fireteam. The roster and Fireteam charts in [the sectorial references](../../../sectorials) determine whether a historical composition remains valid under N5.3. Source-era weapon selections are not silently replaced with modern options.

**Evaluation order:** (1) primary gun's winning Face-to-Face matchups (BS, MSV/Mimetism, weapon quality, damage/ammo); (2) **actual** Fireteam level, especially L2 **+1 Special Die**; (3) a second member giving a different range band or counter (Smoke/Eclipse/Disco, KHD/Firewall, AP/K1/E/M, close protection); (4) mobility, specialist follow-through and recovery; (5) total points, SWC and movement/Order footprint. More guns covering the same lane are not automatically more valuable.

**Cost confidence:** A modern pts/SWC total appears only where reported member loadouts can be matched with pinned profiles. **—** means not verifiably costed, not free. Level counts matching unit/Counts-as identities, **not bodies**; L1 receives no linked shooting die, and L2 grants **+1 SD, not +1 Burst**. See [official N5.3 Fireteam bonuses](https://infinitythewiki.com/Fireteam_Bonuses) and [Fireteams chart rules](https://infinitythewiki.com/Fireteams_Chart). A historically played group does not establish current win rates.

## Important exclusions and evidence limits

- **Druze E/M/HMG/KHD five-model Core:** A real and versatile recorded team, but **117/2.5**, five bodies and an HMG as the main direct-fire lead do **not** beat premium linked gunfighters above. Excluded from this gunfighting shortlist, not declared an illegal team.
- **USAriadna Grunt/Rosie (4), Blackjack Duo, Ikari Daiyōkai Haris:** Some have real value in their own factions, but their direct-fire ceiling or complementary tools are not strong enough to displace the best two-/three-body candidates; do not rank them merely because their profiles were fully documented.
- **Invincible Army Tai Sheng HMG/Shàng Jí Hacker/Shàng Jí HMG (#103):** Impressive historical Haris and now identified precisely in the register. **Not ranked as a current N5.3 exact formation:** the pinned Tai Sheng roster has Breaker Combi Rifle/Thunderbolt choices and **no HMG**. A different present-day Tai Sheng setup is a **new construction** that needs its own evidence before entering an evidence-only table.
- **Corregidor Mobile Brigada MULTI/TinBot + HMG + Jazz (#121):** Strong three-model direction, but the primary report did **not** identify Jazz as the **FTO** profile required by the current Corregidor chart. Logged for further confirmation, **not falsely certified and ranked**.
- **WinterFor Nisse HMG + Uma (#012):** A played early-N5 attacking Duo; the current chart links Uma **only through FTO options**, not confirmed by the source. The independent, fully identified Nisse HMG + Machinist Duo (#194) takes the current Value position instead.
- **Long-range ARO versus active gunfighting:** Nisse MULTI Sniper/Machinist, Varuna ORC/Kamau (88 pts / 4.5 SWC), and other strong sniper/rocket setups can be excellent lane defenders but are not automatically best *active-turn* gunfighting purchases; see [Defensive / ARO rankings](defensive-aro-fireteams-apex-and-value.md). A rank is not a claim of head-to-head victory against every opponent.
- **Unranked best-on-paper links:** The pinned sectorial files contain powerful **constructed** two-/three-model profiles and TAG/HI packages, but the repository's [no-invented-Fireteams policy](README.md) excludes unsourced exact combinations from the evidence-only tables. A chart-legal idea is not proof that someone actually used it.

## Reassessment audit

This revision replaces the previous emphasis on **five-model Druze/Kestrel/White Company packages** and restores compact premium attackers, supported by the already indexed battle reports. Previously omitted gunfighting evidence in reports **#012, #182, #190 and #194** was added to [OBSERVED-COMPOSITIONS.md](OBSERVED-COMPOSITIONS.md), and the reported #194 Nisse loadout and #103 Tai Sheng history were corrected. No four-model team is ranked solely for the L4 BS bonus; only the **Asawira/Ghulam/Barid four-body Core** passes the additional-tool test here. No five-model team is ranked.
