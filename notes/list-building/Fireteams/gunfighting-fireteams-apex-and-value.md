## Scope and ranking rule

A **gunfighting-focused Fireteam** is built to win meaningful ranged BS Attack engagements or force costly reactive gunfights. We reward a **real main gun** (HMG, AP Spitfire, MSV2 gun, Feuerbach, Missile Launcher, Plasma Sniper, etc.), practical range and ammunition, decisive FtF modifiers, durability, and the cost of preserving the team's power while fighting. Pure warbands, unarmed smoke engines, cheap cheerleaders and TAG/Engineer Duos that offer no meaningful linked gun advantage are **not** eligible simply because they are legal Fireteams. A strong ARO specialist may rank, but is marked as defensive rather than implied to be a premier active-turn sweeper.

- **Apex** = highest practical gunfighting ceiling **inside a complete 300-point all-comers army**. Points and SWC still constrain Apex: **preferred ≤120 points**, **conditional 121–135** (unusually strong capability required), **exceptional 136–150** (substantial justification), **over 150 normally excluded** unless extraordinary Order generation/mission flexibility demonstrably pays the tax. The cost bands are *admission hurdles*, not tier grades.
- **Value** = the strength and repeatability of actual ranged engagements **per total linked-team cost**, including SWC, surviving pooled Regular Orders, support opportunity cost, usable FT Level and mission flexibility; a cheap team without a credible gun does not win on cost alone.
- **Level is not headcount:** The Fireteam's L1–L5 comes from the largest group of matching Unit or **explicit chart counts-as** identities, with each family-specific identity applied carefully. L2 grants **+1 Special Die to BS Attack (not +1 Burst)**, L3 Discover +3/Dodge +1, L4 BS +1, and L5 Sixth Sense. Benefits are cumulative. Losing a qualifying member can drop the Level.
- **Rankings are separate:** Apex #1 and Value #1 are not required to be the same composition; two different legal builds from one sectorial can appear independently. An entry in the composition ledger is a **single proposed Fireteam**, not a suggestion to field all rows simultaneously.
- **Audit boundary:** Exact models/options below follow the present `status: current` sectorial references. New cross-sectorial combinations built from already-pinned chart+loadouts are marked **constructed**, not falsely presented as played. Reported unit-group architectures are not proof that identical September 2026 loadouts were fielded. The tables have not been supported by a full 45-army, match-by-match tournament result or FtF simulation; near-neighbor ordering is **provisional**.

## Reliability, legality, and comparison notes

1. **No fictional Burst:** L2 is a **Special Die**; ordinary +1B on a gun/skill is generally active-turn-only, not automatic extra ARO Burst. True Total Reaction/Neurocinetics cases must be checked against the applicable native rule. L4 gives BS+1, not an extra projectile.
2. **Gun-holder level:** A 5-body team with only four matching members is **L4** even if a non-equivalent high-end gun is the fifth member. In particular, the **Corregidor Vostok** build is L4 from four Alguaciles; the Vostok does not add the fifth equivalent. The Tunguska Kriza is **L3** from its three Hollow Men; the Kriza does not count as Hollow Men. An active gun can still benefit from a team level generated by its teammates.
3. **Multi-family constraints:** Use **Corregidor Surface**, not a blend with Deepspace. Team Achilles forbids Wildcards. **Steel Phalanx cannot field a five-person Core**. **Tohaa has zero Core and unlimited Triad Haris, but at most two Duos.** Do not import a different faction's lookalike team rule.
4. **N5.3 FTO fidelity:** The 34/1 Vostok FTO Spitfire lacks the non-FTO Spitfire's native Repeater; Patroclus FTO has its own MEDIKIT/MULTI Marksman profile rather than borrowing Smoke or Holoprojector; Shinden's Rui Shi is only eligible in the named Sayō context.
5. **Points/SWC:** Cost counts **all actual team members**, not just the primary gun or the incremental price for attaching a Wildcard. Use 300 points plus the actual scenario SWC rules. No team shown should be read as automatically fieldable with every other Fireteam in its sectorial.
6. **Evidence grades:** A cited **report** supports only the stated team *shape* unless its profiles are independently verified. An exact current-profile **constructed** candidate is not an exported tournament winner. For evolving Fireteam rulings, the [canonical sectorial Fireteam section](../../../sectorials) and [local Fireteam rules](../../../rules/6_Command%20and%20Fireteams/Fireteams__Basic_Rules.md) control.
