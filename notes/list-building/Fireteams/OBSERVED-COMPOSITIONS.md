# Source-backed Fireteam occurrence register — Infinity N5

This register replaces chart-legal **hypothetical compositions** with **Fireteams explicitly named as formed/used in a battle-report video, or explicitly described as used by a participant in a podcast/tournament retrospective**. A weapon or teammate not recovered from the source is written as **unconfirmed**, not filled with an N5.3 option from the Army builder. **Every row has a numbered battle report**, and selected rows also link directly to the original video/podcast. These are historical N5 observations, not independent confirmation of current N5.3 legality or an Army export.

**Source strength:** “Played” indicates a stated linked group or observed linked activation as summarized in the original report; “participant podcast” is first-person retrospective; “reported” may cover multiple separately submitted lists; custom/tutorial/translated material is identified. *No prospective-only formation is included.* Different submissions, modes and rounds are not automatically independent wins.

**FT level*:** The indicated level uses the same observed members with documented current chart equivalence where available. **Not verified** means that no defensible modern Level is assigned; a historically played Fireteam is not certified legal today. Physical headcount never determines FT Level by itself.

**Prices:** A few costs are supplied only where all unit weapon/specialist identities were explicitly described and a direct pinned N5.3 profile match is available. A dash means the exact **modern** profiles cannot be established from the source; it is not a zero-cost unit or an assertion of illegality. Prices are present-day comparisons, not historical list exports.

## Use in the category rankings

The role ranking files should refer only to source-backed memberships recorded here and include a separate battle-report/episode link on **every ranked row**. A historical recording confirms that a team was described or used; it does not demonstrate the best possible N5.3 loadout, a sectorial win rate, or whether those specific current point costs remain legal after chart changes. [Fireteam evaluation criteria](README.md) are applied qualitatively within this restricted evidence sample. Missing points, SWC, member names and specific FTO options must be resolved from the **original source**, never invented to make a ranking table appear complete.
