# Board Control / Area Denial Fireteams — source-backed Apex and Value rankings

**Evidence-only reassessment (19 November 2026).** The compositions in these tables were **played or explicitly described as used** in a numbered battle report or a participant YouTube/podcast report. **Every single ranked entry links to the actual recorded source**, and [the occurrence register](OBSERVED-COMPOSITIONS.md) identifies its evidence boundary and direct original video/podcast where recovered. No unmentioned filler members or theoretical legal loadouts are inserted.

**Role objective:** Persistent threat zones, disabling tools, credible reactive lanes, range and counter overlap. **Apex** compares the strongest observed role packages; **Value** compares practical contribution against team footprint and **confirmed** price, where available. Unknown modern profile prices prevent any precise points-per-effect ranking, so the Value positions are **qualitative, not a computed cost-efficiency statistic**. A dash means the recording does not identify all modern profile options, not zero cost.

**Historical/legality limit:** The source establishes the Fireteam in its *played N5 revision* (or participant retrospective). It does **not** certify today's N5.3 Fireteam-chart legality, AVA, exact SWC, member equivalence or profile options. No evidence here is a match-controlled win rate. Recheck the [pinned current sectorial chart](../../../sectorials) and the [Fireteam guidelines](README.md) before copying a team into Army.

## Evidence and former constructions

All ranked entries are drawn from the [source-backed Fireteam occurrence register](OBSERVED-COMPOSITIONS.md). Prior versions included **designed-but-unplayed combinations, extra profile choices not stated by the source, and sometimes incorrect team sizes**. Those are deliberately removed. No hypothetical team is retained merely because its N5.3 chart would allow it. A 200-point/custom scenario, a narrated list, an early concession or a participant podcast is identified as such by the linked dossier and should not be weighted as an equivalent tournament win.
