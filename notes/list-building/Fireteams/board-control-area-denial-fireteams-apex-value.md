# Board Control / Area Denial Fireteams — compact source-backed Apex and Value

**Rebuilt 20 November 2026 (N5.3).** This compares the strongest **observed two-/three-model Fireteams** built around a linkable **ARO anchor or actual denial device/effect**, not the overall strength or popularity of the parent sectorial. There is **one four-member exception and no five-member team**. The older Druze five-person Core, Ikari Wú Míng/Brawler, vague Onyx Unidrons, Torchlight all-Hacker Core and Gwailo/Jayth assault Haris are **not** the standards for best-in-game ARO/denial.

**Apex** = highest repeatable control of relevant approaches, objective lanes and enemy attack routes within a normal 300-point list; **Value** = denial per points, SWC, physical models and outside support. The two ranks are **independent** and the table sorts by Apex. A linked **Missile Launcher/HRL/MSV sniper** is relevant because it can *deny a lane*, but a strong weapon alone is **not** a minefield or Repeater network. Prefer a threat that makes the opponent pay multiple Orders to clear, reroute, hack through or approach safely.

**Evidence discipline:** Each composition below is explicitly used/described in the linked numbered [battle reports](../../battle-reports/n5-battle-reports-and-tournament-reports.md) or their participant video and indexed in [OBSERVED-COMPOSITIONS.md](OBSERVED-COMPOSITIONS.md). Names and physical headcount follow the **actual reported link**; no invented filler members, weapon substitutions, off-team support counted as teammates or theoretical-only legal formation. Costs and Levels are quoted only when matched against the [pinned N5.3 profiles and charts](../../../sectorials/); **—** means not confidently verified. **\*** denotes a modern reconstructive match that should be checked against a current Army list before tournament use. Several reports predate N5.3 or are custom/200pt: they establish *the historical composition*, not competitive win rates or automatically unchanged present-day legality.

**Mechanics:** [N5.3 Fireteam Level bonuses](https://infinitythewiki.com/Fireteam_Bonuses) give **BS Attack (+1 Special Die)** at L2 **including reactive shots** (roll one extra die, then discard one; **not +1 reactive Burst**), Discover/Dodge at L3, +1 BS at L4 and Sixth Sense at L5. Only members with the appropriate LoF, ZoC or hacking coverage may declare the applicable AROs; do not count every teammate as a simultaneous sniper or assume all Hackers share a Pitcher. A deployed or fired device needs the **actual equipped profile and legal placement/Order**. An Engineer cannot Doctor a living teammate.

## Role winners and size decisions

- **Best all-round compact ARO + cyber/vision fork:** **Shindenbutai Tanuki Haris (3)**. Its MSV2 sniper, Pitcher/Hacker, Disco and close CoC gun handle *different* opponent counters, but one visibility solution can interfere with another.
- **Best resilient linked rocket ARO:** **Steel Phalanx Phoenix/Machaon/Myrmidon (3)**. It combines a difficult FtF weapon with Doctor recovery and Smoke/close protection.
- **Best non-LoF route obstruction:** **Tohaa Sukeul/Kriigel/Kumotail (3)**, with the caveat that the documented Sukeul is the Lieutenant.
- **Best concentrated premium open-lane ARO:** **Varuna ORC/2 Kamau (3)**, explicitly **conditional** at 4.5 SWC. A cheaper **Kamau + Machinist Duo (2)** is often the practical list decision.
- **Best ARO plus a *separately* placed static tool:** **WinterFor Nisse sniper/Machinist turret Duo (2)**; a turret or mine must first be legally placed.
- **Best economical generated denial tools:** **Steel Phalanx Dactyl/K2 Duo (2)** at 32/0, but do not mistake a **Mine Dispenser** for Minelayer or predeployed mines.
- **Four-body exception:** **Tunguska Perseus/Hollow Men ML/Hacker/Pitcher Core (4)** only. Its extra body completes a uniquely complementary gun + network + hacking defense + close countercharge and brings a plausible L4 benefit. It still costs roughly 116 points before outside hacking support.

**Excluded or unranked despite strong parent-army control:** Corregidor's **Moran/Koala** and Hassassin's **Shujae/Ghazi/hidden ambush** dominate *army-wide* control but are not members of the cited teams. TAK's Scout E/M Minelayer and Shasvastii Shrouded Dazers are similarly independent. Do **not** give their Turn-0 mines, Jammers, Repeaters or hidden Orders to a Fireteam by association. [Army-level board control rankings](../../sectorial-rankings-n5-3.md#best-board-control--area-denial--top-10-armies-n53) answer a different question.

**Strong linkable ARO unit, but no separately verified current compact source formation:** **Kestrel Black A.I.R. FTO Neuro MULTI Sniper**, **WinterFor Knight of Justice ML(+1SD)**, **NCA Bolt ML** and other near-top entries in the [individual ARO ranking](../aro-units-apex-and-value.md). Do not invent a cheap Black A.I.R./Fennec Duo: the chart specifies a Fennecs Core/Haris, and the historical four-body [#002](../../battle-reports/001-010-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) uses a Fennec **Decoy** profile no longer pinned unchanged. The strongest individual ARO **Yān Huǒ Neuro ML** and **Teucer Neuro Plasma Sniper** likewise are **not** the same weapon/loadout as an eligible FTO alternative.

**Not every observed team deserves a rank.** In particular, the older five-body **Druze 117/2.5** and **White Company Hawkwood/Emily** are too large and mostly project single attack avenues for this compact role; **Ikari Wú Míng/Brawler** lacks proven independent denial tools, **Onyx Kerr-Nau/Unidron** lacks confirmed weapons, **Torchlight SilverStar/Waveriders/Kappa** is better analyzed as cyber concentration, and **Shas Gwailo/Jayth** is primarily close assault. These exclusions are editorial comparative decisions, **not** statements that those historical combinations were never played or are universally illegal.

### Evidence boundaries and unresolved verification

All quoted modern points/SWC and levels come from matching reported weapons with [pinned sectorial data](../../../sectorials/), **not** by retroactively claiming the game used N5.3. Report #012's **Nisse MULTI Sniper + turret Machinist** and #194's **Nisse HMG + Machinist** are *different* source-observed teams—do not import the sniper or turret into the latter. #226 is an atypical one-TAG event, #194 is 200 points, #200 is a custom mission, and #233 ended before a complete final turn. The [defensive ARO Fireteam companion ranking](defensive-aro-fireteams-apex-and-value.md) emphasizes pure long-lane defense, while this file gives additional weight to denial devices, short-range counterplay, disruption and objective geometry.

**Value ranks are qualitative when complete profile prices were not established.** No measured Orders-denied/game or win percentage can be inferred from one source, and the Fireteam's reach/availability is affected by terrain, deployment, opponent target type and external live Hackers. Recheck actual current Army chart legality, profiles, AVA, Fireteam limits and unit equivalence before fielding a report-derived team.
