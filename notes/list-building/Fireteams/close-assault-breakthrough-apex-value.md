# Close Assault / Breakthrough Fireteams — N5.3 Apex and Value

**Reassessed 20 November 2026.** Rank the **best linked assault and CC *troops*** and their complementary range, Smoke/Eclipse/Disco, hacking, anti-armor and mission tools—not the sectorial's overall tier, the largest possible Core, or the loudest battle report. This remains a **source-backed ranking**: each listed formation has a named-member appearance in a numbered report, first-person tournament retrospective or original recorded match. See the [occurrence register](OBSERVED-COMPOSITIONS.md) and [selection rules](README.md). Sources establish actual reported membership, **not** exact current profile choices, current rules legality, or measured win rates.

**Default size: Duo or three-model Haris/Core.** No five-model formation is recommended. The **only two four-model exceptions** below provide unusually distinct counters and support; neither earns its place merely by adding bodies. Four-body teams are judged severely for deployment footprint, cost and the missing independent attack pieces in the remaining 300pt army. Treat the 140pt White Banner reconstruction as an **exceptional Apex investment (136–150)**, never a default choice.

**FT level:** computed only where matching current chart identities can be verified, not equated with physical headcount. L2 is BS Attack (+1 SD), not +1 Burst; L3 improves Discover and Dodge; L4 adds +1 BS; L5 adds Sixth Sense. **Price notation:** a dash means the *played* source did not identify an exact reproducible set of current N5.3 options; modern **illustrative** options/costs in analysis are explicitly labelled, not invented as historical list exports.

## Eligibility audit and missing high-end Fireteam units

- **Steel Phalanx Eudoros/K2/two-Myrmidons** in historical [#011](../../battle-reports/011-020-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) is **not** a legal current N5.3 Myrmidon Core: K2 Auxiliar FTO belongs to Dactyl, Steel Phalanx or Thorakitai teams, **not** Myrmidons. Removed from the ranking; retained in the [historical register](OBSERVED-COMPOSITIONS.md) with this qualification.
- **Corregidor Massacre/Jaguar/Lobo/two-Diablos** and **Shindenbutai dual-Hatamoto five-member Core** were genuinely reported but omitted: five bodies and broad cost/footprint are not justified versus the compact elite options here. The Corregidor example was played in a **custom Megabeast** scenario, not a standard ITS matchup.
- **Achilles, Ajax FTO and Eudoros** should be priority **source-discovery targets** for truly top CC/close-assault links. The current [Next Wave chart](../../../sectorials/combined-army/next-wave.md) permits Achilles-led Team Achilles Fireteams; the [Steel Phalanx chart](../../../sectorials/aleph/steel-phalanx.md) permits Ajax **FTO only** and Eudoros in Myrmidon Cores. Their **specific present-day 2–3-trooper compositions** are not promoted into these observed-source tables without a qualifying original YouTube, podcast or battle-report formation.
- **Ōban Mushashi/Tankō/Tankō** is sourced as an opponent formation in tournament-recap report #164, **not** a detailed original Army export. The N5.3 Banzai chart requires **Mushashi's Regular FTO option 2** and native Tankō; these three count as Tankō for L3. Do not mistake a historical mention for the original selected modern profile.
- **Historical and current profiles are distinct.** Source-era scoring, weapons, FT Master regularization and FTO legality can change. The representative current costs quoted above come from the pinned 2026-09-03 sectorial references, not guaranteed report-era options. Future uncited builds belong in a separate legal-theorycrafting file, not these observed-source ranks.

**Scoring priorities:** (1) reliable approach through AROs, (2) actual close-range kill/anti-armor capability, (3) complementary range bands, (4) counterplay to MSV, Mim, armor, hackers and templates, (5) objective or recovery role after contact, (6) Orders and cost, then (7) Fireteam level. **A pure shooter without a breach route belongs in Gunfighting, not this ranking; a pure melee trio with no access belongs below a smoke-delivered finisher.**
