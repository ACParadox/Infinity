# Close Assault / Breakthrough Fireteams — Whole-game Apex & Value (Infinity N5.3)

*Last reviewed: 19 November 2026 · Standard 300-point ITS 18 lists · Editorial cross-sectorial comparison.*

> **Scope:** Fireteams that can **reach and force a contested short-range position**, break a screened approach, remove a hard defender through CC/templates/E/M/Disco or Eclipse, and ideally **keep enough survivors to score or hold the space**. The ranking is **not** a duplicate of the gunfighting or defensive/ARO rankings. Pure independent infiltrators, AD attackers, TAGs and 1-model heroes cannot be ranked as Fireteams.

**Source hierarchy:** pinned N5.3 standard-roster profile and canonical Fireteam chart in [sectorials/](../../../sectorials/) > audited example tables in those files > [indexed battle reports](../../battle-reports/n5-battle-reports-and-tournament-reports.md) > tactical/media commentary. The exact costed examples below are **legal reconstructions**, not assertions that their exact 2026 option IDs were used in a past recording. A played trio or Core is evidence of a formation, **not** a verified win rate. Rankings express a practical all-comers judgment, not measured tournament results.

## Method and point caps

- **Apex:** offensive ceiling **within a viable 300-point list**: delivery (Smoke, Eclipse, Disco, terrain/vertical mobility), protected approach, credible anti-heavy and anti-screen contact, counter-hacking/recovery, order access and ability to claim territory. An expensive five-body stack is not automatically stronger than a small assault Haris.
- **Value:** how much practical breach/score utility the package supplies **per point/SWC and order spent**, including opportunity cost of using the Core/Haris allowance. Disposable trading efficiency can outrank peak durability here.
- **Preferred Apex ≤120 points; Conditional Apex 121–135; Exceptional Apex 136–150; >150 normally excluded.** A category label is a cost warning, **not a second tier of strength**.
- **Fireteam Level (L1–L5) is not member count:** it depends on the largest eligible same-Unit or explicitly matching chart *Counts-as* group. L2 adds **BS Attack (+1 SD), not +1 Burst**; L3 adds Discover +3 / Dodge +1; L4 adds BS +1; L5 adds Sixth Sense. Bonuses accumulate and recalculate if members leave. Follow the [pinned Fireteam bonuses](../../../rules/6_Command%20and%20Fireteams/Fireteam_Bonuses.md), [creation](../../../rules/6_Command%20and%20Fireteams/Fireteams__Basic_Rules.md), and each source chart.
- Prices in the **catalogue** are **each selected member's points/SWC**. Listed total is **Fireteam members only**, never a complete list; FTO profiles, AVA, named foundation, Fireteam-slot limits, profile-specific Peripheral conditions, Regular/Irregular orders and mission legality still apply. A five-model team can have L2–L4, not necessarily L5.

### Avoid these category and legality traps

- **Do not count a Wildcard as matching FT Level by default.** Examples: Starmada Shona/Varangian = **L1**, Ōban Daiyōkai/Domaru/Tankō = **L2**, and Bahram's four-member Asawira/Ghulam Core = **L2**; conversely Tohaa's distinct Triad-equivalent troops genuinely reach **L3**.
- **Do not conflate Smoke, Eclipse and Disco.** Ordinary Smoke can enable compatible MSV firing; **Eclipse/Disco blocks normal MSV too**. Disco placement/ammunition and recovery are profile-dependent, not unrestricted Smoke spam.
- **Do not import independent assault stars into links.** Hidden/infiltrating and airborne profiles generally cannot begin in a Fireteam. Non-FTO Ajax, Mushashi or Patroclus, and non-FTO/Parachutist alternatives where restricted, are not substitutes.
- **Do not count an Irregular trooper as a new Regular Order merely for joining.** Apply Inspiring Leadership or FT Master only when the actual conditions are met; never give the Fireteam extra free Orders. A TA Order and LT Order have their own spending restrictions.
- **Do not treat a legal module as a complete 300-point army.** Leave room for independent long-range solving, specialists, hacking defense, command safety and mid-table scoring. Once a 136–150-point assault Core is purchased, the remaining 150–164 points must carry nearly every other responsibility.
- **Avoid >150-point default Apex bricks:** e.g. Next Wave five-member **Team Achilles 187/3.5 L5**, Ramah Tarik + four Khawarij **179/2.5 L5** (reported #227 defeat), and Shindenbutai deluxe Bushi-tai **155/2.5 L5**. Their ceiling does not automatically justify consuming half or more of a 300-point list.
