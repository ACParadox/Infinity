# Infinity N5.3 — Sectorial Capability & Rare Non-Access Matrix

*Created 24 October 2026. Covers **all 45 standard factions/sectorials** represented by the committed `sectorials/` references, including Vanilla and NA2. Source of truth: pinned Army N5.3 (generally `7.26246.158`, September 2026; White Company `7.26246.159`); checked against each roster and its canonical Fireteam chart. **This is a derived list-building index, not a roster/profile replacement.** Follow the linked source for exact AVA, profiles, Fireteam legality, SWC and Reinforcements boundaries.*

## How to read the ratings

- **Abundant:** multiple independent/sufficiently numerous **practical** ways to use the tool, or a strong repeatable engine. The army can commonly build around it.
- **Access:** at least one useful, legal standard-roster route; may demand a particular named unit/profile. It is **not** necessarily available on a cheap unit.
- **Limited:** scarce, expensive, narrow, conditional or meaningfully inferior access (e.g. **MSV1-only** smoke shooting; low-reliability Pitcher; peripheral/edge-only DZ access). Scarcity/price can make a binary “yes” misleading.
- **no-access:** no qualifying standard-roster method established by the cited reference. Does **not** include Soldiers of Fortune, Spec-Ops/Team-Ops, tournament extras or separate Reinforcement charts.
- **Smoke + MSV2+:** both **normal Smoke** and friendly **MSV2/MSV3** gun access in the *same army*, not necessarily the same trooper/team. `Limited` here means **MSV1 only**; Eclipse and Disco **do not** permit MSV smoke shooting.
- **Eclipse** means Eclipse grenades/launcher on the roster. **Disco** is separate: a Disco Baller generates an Eclipse special-ammunition template but operates via a disposable/activatable token and different deployment method.
- **White Noise** is normally provided by **Hacking Device Plus**, including appropriate upgrades; ordinary HD/KHD/EVO are not sufficient.
- **Enemy-DZ pressure** includes enemy-DZ-capable **Parachutist**, **Combat Jump**, **Impersonation**, or unusually deep **Infiltration (PH=18/19)** as *near-DZ staging*. **Infiltration never places inside the opponent's DZ** in N5.3. Ordinary Parachutist without specific enemy-DZ arrival or risky Combat Jump may be `Limited`.
- **Pitcher projection** grades Pitcher *quality*, particularly a **Fireteam-eligible** firing profile, X-Visor, +1B/+1SD, exceptionally cheap delivery, or multiple complementary carriers. A Pitcher on a unit with an FTO label **is not automatically linkable**; check the linked Fireteam chart.
- **Turn-0 repeater** means an **Infiltrating Repeater or deployable Repeater actually placed during deployment** (including eligible Infiltration + Minelayer), not a regular DZ remote or a Repeater carried by a separate infiltrating teammate. **Forward Deployment** alone is excluded.
- **Hackers** counts current standard hacking profiles, including Wardrivers; **Vehicles (VH)** counts the Army troop class **VH**, not ordinary motorcycle models; **Strategos L2** is not L1; **Inspiring Leadership** depends on the correct Lieutenant profile/being on the table.
- **CoC/CI** refer to **Chain of Command**/**Counterintelligence**. `Abundant` for command tools indicates multiple practical options, not that the effect stacks.

**Legend:** Every matrix cell spells out one of the four levels: **Abundant · Access · Limited · no-access**. The short notes name providers and important exceptions. Values are *tactical accessibility judgments grounded in the referenced profiles*, not quantified tournament usage.

## Rare absences and cross-faction consequences

1. **No standard TAG choices:** **USAriadna, Tartary Army Corps, Caledonia.** All other rows have at least one TAG (including named/mercenary options where the current main roster actually includes them). Having a TAG does **not** mean the army can efficiently support every TAG build.
2. **No ordinary Smoke:** **Operations Subsection, Onyx, Kestrel, Military Orders, Neoterra, Vanilla PanO, Acontecimento, Svalarheima, Varuna, Invincible Army**. Eclipse/Disco might still provide a *different* visibility screen. **Torchlight is only Limited** (Hippolyta).
3. **No MSV2+ smoke shooting despite abundant Smoke:** notably **Caledonia, Hassassin Bahram, Ōban, Druze, StarCo**. Ariadna lists with only **MSV1** are marked Limited, not falsely described as MSV2 smoke shooters.
4. **No reliable in-deployment network projection:** All Ariadna armies, Onyx, Shasvastii, Morat, Next Wave, Haqq Hassassin/Qapu, all three JSA lists, most O-12, etc. Some can **advance a Repeater Turn 1** with bikes/REM/FD, which is *not* Turn-0 Infiltration.
5. **No standard Hacker at all:** **none asserted**. Even Ariadna armies have Wardriver access in the current pinned roster; **Limited** marks the lack of a broad native suite rather than claiming they are literally hackerless.
6. **No standard VH / Vehicles:** common, **not rare**. Do not equate a **motorcycle**, **TAG** or **REM** with the newer **VH** troop type; the matrix tracks them separately.
7. **Strategos L2 is genuinely scarce:** **Vanilla ALEPH / OSS (Marut LT), Vanilla Combined (Avatar LT), Vanilla Haqqislam (Saladin LT), Vanilla Yu Jing / Imperial Service (Sun Tze)**. **Voronin, Hector, Alpha, Hannibal, Magistrates, Aquila and Metsuke are not Strategos L2** in the current local references.
8. **Inspiring Leadership is more common than Strategos L2 but still narrow:** **Vanilla Ariadna / Kosmoflot / Caledonia (Wallace), Vanilla PanO / Military Orders (Joan), NCA (Maximus LT profile)**. Do not count these profiles if taking a non-LT version.
9. **Impersonation is not generic camo:** available on **Vanilla Combined, Shasvastii, Onyx (limited Greif), Vanilla Haqqislam, Hassassin Bahram, Ikari and Tohaa**. Ordinary Infiltration/Decoy is not Impersonation.
10. **Eclipse and Disco Ball are separate counters:** an army can lack both (e.g. **Onyx**) or have only Disco (e.g. **Kestrel, Neoterra, Invincible Army**). Neither lets MSV see through the Eclipse zone. 

## Method and maintenance checks

- Each row links to the specific [repository-normalized Army reference](../../sectorials/REFERENCE_DESIGN.md). **Unit details** establish Smoke, Eclipse, Disco, HD+, Pitchers, deployments and command skills; the **canonical Fireteam** section determines whether a Pitcher can actually be linked. Weapons and skill grades are not transferred between vanilla and sectorial copies automatically.
- Network projection is **deployer/profile dependent**: `Infiltration` on one unit and `Deployable Repeater` on another does **not** create a turn-0 Repeater. Check whether each exact option includes or inherits both, and whether `Minelayer` applies to that type of Repeater.
- The **Vanilla Yu Jing** reference is explicitly marked `status: partial` for full per-profile inline data (though its roster membership and canonical Fireteams are complete). Its ratings use the roster + sibling Yu Jing references and should be reconfirmed for profile-specific details before exact list construction.
- Source versions may differ: **White Company** uses Army `7.26246.159`; other roster files generally use `7.26246.158`. An N5.3 update should re-run the access comparison, not just refresh points.
- Local rule definitions: [Infiltration](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Infiltration.md), [Hacking Device / White Noise](../../rules/5c_Equipment/Hacking_Device.md), [Disco Baller](../../rules/4_Ammunition%20and%20Weaponry/Disco_Baller.md), [Strategos](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Strategos.md). Above matrices summarize **standard main-roster availability**, not every possible upgrade/mercenary/ITS edge case.
