# Cheap Midfield Defenders & Speed Bumps — N5.3

**Budget defensive profiles; 5 November 2026.** To be ranked here the **exact selected profile** must have **Infiltration, Forward Deployment or specifically legal Strategic Deployment** letting it *begin* ahead of its normal deployment zone. Strategic Deployment only qualifies when actually granted by the legal list/setup; no such permission is presumed. **Being fast, Camo-only, Hidden-only, Parachutist or airborne does not qualify.** Non-qualifying variants belong in [Cheap DZ Defenders & Trade Pieces](cheap-dz-defenders-and-trade-pieces.md).

The job is to impose **Turn-0** detours, Discover/Dodge/clearance Orders, disabling E/M/Adhesive/PARA, mine or template commitments, and disposable anti-heavy shots **without spending your own active Orders**. Minelayer, native Repeater with a live allied Hacker, useful Camo/Decoy, DTWs and forward deployment are the highest-priority attributes. A Specialist, FO, Engineer, Doctor, GizmoKit, Sensor or other utility **improves an already credible defence**, but there is **no utility-only ranking**. Primarily 8–18pt profiles; up to 22pt for distinctive threats. Ranking is **qualitative** and cross-faction, not a measured win rate or mixed-faction legal list; [pinned sectorial references](../../sectorials/) remain authoritative.

## Deployment rubric and traps that actually work

- **Minelayer versus carried mine:** Moran15, Moonraker15, Jackal11/.5, Foxtrot18/.5, Zero18/.5, Libertos8/1 and Ryūken-9 22/.5 get a preplaced device only under their **specific Minelayer** rules. Gūdǎn12, Lóng Yá16, Konduktor12, Beasthunter15 and Jackal10 carry mines but must normally **spend an Order** to put them down.
- **Reveal timing:** Marker state taxes Discover and movement, but one-use Camo may not return. Hidden Deployment alone does not entitle a model to appear in midfield, and Hidden/off-table pieces have different Order-count consequences. Decoy, Camo, Mimetism and Infiltration are **different** abilities.
- **Template/reaction check:** Chain Rifle/Chain-colt, HFT, E/Marat and Zapper create direct-template or disabling forks; N5 **Boarding Shotguns do not**. Weapon (+1B) does not increase ordinary ARO Burst. Disposable Panzerfaust/Flammenspeer force a decision but lose value after shots are spent.
- **Repeaters:** A Moran native Repeater projects from setup; its actual payoff depends on live friendly Hackers and enemy targets. An undeployed Deployable Repeater, Mine Dispenser or Pitcher shot is **not** free Turn-0 area control. See [Network Projection](network-projection-repeaters-pitchers-apex-value.md).
- **Placement:** Defend a square an attacker must *finish on* or pass to score, not merely the prettiest rooftop. Place separate marker/mine, template and anti-heavy threats along **independent angles** so one attack cannot remove all layers. Pay attention to Infiltration failure, AVA, SWC, and whether clearing the device can be done by Sensor/Deactivator or expendable troops.

**Scope boundary:** Strong but generally more expensive infiltrators such as **Shujae, Dasyu, Guìláng, Clipsos and premium Shrouded/Naga** belong in [the full Midfield Area Denial & Speed Bumps guide](midfield-area-denial-and-speed-bumps-apex-value.md).
