# Cheap DZ Defenders & Trade Pieces — N5.3

**Best bang for buck — revised 38-entry ranking (5 November 2026).** This single table replaces separate *Apex* and *points-value* tables. It favors **low-cost defensive impact and trade-up potential** over absolute ceiling: a template or E/M stop, a Minelayer trap, an affordable Repeater/hacking layer, a non-lethal effect, or a Disposable launcher that can force an attacker to spend Orders or risk an expensive piece. Prioritize **immediate DZ stopping power**: live direct templates, E/M/PARA/Riotstopper disabling templates, legally preplaced mines and meaningful disposable anti-heavy shots. A carried mine, Repeater or Mine Dispenser **does not start deployed without Minelayer**. At comparable defensive effect, **cost, SWC, native Regular Order, survivability and Specialist/Engineer/Sensor/GizmoKit utility** settle ties. There is **no separate cheap-utility table**.

These selections begin in the ordinary **deployment zone**; no Infiltration, Forward Deployment or unverified Strategic Deployment is assumed for the ranked loadouts. **A complete Kuang Shi Core and a combined Pilot-X Team comparison are deliberate full-package exceptions** to the usual ≤15pt single-body focus. This is not a ranking of premium long-range ARO gunfighters. [Midfield Defenders & Speed Bumps](midfield-defenders-and-speed-bumps.md) covers advanced-deploying variants; [Cheerleaders](cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md) covers pure pooled-Order efficiency and complete FT Master packages; [ARO Apex/Value](aro-units-apex-and-value.md) covers high-end gunlines. [Committed sectorial profiles](../../sectorials/) are the legality/points source.

## Whole-game standouts — one combined defensive bang-for-buck ranking

**How to interpret:** Qualitative tactical judgment, not a measured win rate or a universal mixed-faction list. **S+** denotes outstanding stand-alone stopping power per point or unusually comprehensive anti-intrusion protection; **S** strong, efficient defenders; **A+** useful but more situational, pricier or dependent; **A** mainly aimed, short-reach or package-inefficient alternatives. **No fixed number of entries is assigned to a tier.** Native **Regular** creates a transferable Order; **Irregular** does not. A Fireteam Master conversion is conditional on a legal full team at Order Count. When one row mentions alternatives, its **preferred ranked profile is explicitly named** and the other is a comparison, never a pooled super-profile. Kuang Shi and Pilot-X use **full purchased team cost and actual Orders**; synchronized Auxbot is a second body, **not** a second Order. A close template wins on dense terrain; an LRL/launcher requires a real shot.

## How to build the defence

1. **Defend the destination, not the skyline.** Buy a 5–15pt Chain Rifle/E/Marat/Adhesive protector that covers the square an enemy must occupy to kill a Lieutenant, tag a Hacker, or claim a home objective. Most cheap guards should be safely hidden until that square is attacked.
2. **Preplace only legal Minelayer devices.** Raveneye12, Kaauri11, Fennec14 and Acontecimento Regular13 can begin with the named Minelayer device; a **carried** mine or Repeater does not predeploy itself. Konduktor7, both Barsuk10–11 variants, Gūdǎn FTO11, RacerBot Mk-I9, RacerBot Mk-II9, Tech-Bee9 and Pilot-X Mk-III8 do not get a free setup device. The 12–13pt Camo/FD Gūdǎn Shock Mine variants belong to midfield.
3. **Layer different answers.** A Repeater+Hacker, mine, cheap template, Disposable launcher and separate Flash Pulse guard require different counters. Two exposed ARO guns on the same roof can be pie-sliced individually.
4. **Trade intelligently.** A weak-BS line trooper usually should not pay major points/SWC for HMG/ML-style gunfighting. Its meaningful job is to impose a direct-template fork, E/M/PARA/Adhesive disablement or one-shot anti-heavy threat. In N5, **Boarding Shotguns are not direct-template weapons** and weapon (+1B) does not improve ordinary ARO Burst.
5. **Correctly account for Orders, packages and variants.** Ghazi, Beasthunter FTO, standalone Jackal, Helot, Galwegian, Shaolin, Morlock, Irmandinho, Preta, Desperado and Daylami are **Irregular** absent legal conversion. The full **Monitor13/.5 + four Kuang Shi6/0 = 37/.5** purchase yields **five native Regular Orders** and five slots; no extra FT Master Order is created. **PanO Pilot-X21/1 + two Mk-III8/0 = 37/1** or **ALEPH/OSS Pilot-X23/.5 + two Mk-III8/0 = 39/.5**, producing three native Regular Orders per package. Mk-III **cannot** be taken independently; Mk-I and Mk-II are **separate 9pt independent** variants where available, and begin in DZ. Auxilia15 includes an Auxbot but only one Regular Order. **6pt Daylami**, **7pt Konduktor FTO**, **11pt Gūdǎn FTO**, **11pt Barsuk Camo/Decoy** and **9pt Beasthunter FTO** are not their infiltrating/FD alternatives.
6. **Utilities are tie-breakers.** If two guards control the same corner for comparable cost, Specialist, Sensor, Baggage, FO, TAGCom or GizmoKit can make one better. A support-only Puppet Master, Doctor, empty beacon or costly sniper does not qualify merely by being inexpensive or useful in another role.

**Flashbots** (such as the **Lamedh Flash Pulse/Repeater REM** and faction equivalents) and the **3pt Irregular Warcor Flash Pulse** are of course excellent budget ARO/utility options **where available**, but **not all armies/sectorials can take them**. They are kept outside this template/mine/trade-piece ranking; the Warcor also does not provide a pooled Regular Order. A **16pt Garon TR** is a very economical conventional reactive gun, but is better compared as a **gunline ARO** in [the ARO tiers](aro-units-apex-and-value.md), not as an ordinary DZ trade-body. Full **Oznat/Gaki, Magistrate/Jackal and Liang Kai/Shaolin** formations stay in [Cheerleaders' complete-team packages](cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md); the requested **Monitor/four-Kuang-Shi** defense package is ranked here once at its full 37/.5 cost. Pilot-X PanO and ALEPH packages share a single row; the [network guide](network-projection-repeaters-pitchers-apex-value.md) compares their wider support role.
