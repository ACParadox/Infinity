# Cheap DZ Defenders & Trade Pieces — N5.3

**Best bang for buck — expanded 36-profile ranking (5 November 2026).** This single table replaces separate *Apex* and *points-value* tables. It favors **low-cost defensive impact and trade-up potential** over absolute ceiling: a template or E/M stop, a Minelayer trap, an affordable Repeater/hacking layer, a non-lethal effect, or a Disposable launcher that can force an attacker to spend Orders or risk an expensive piece. Prioritize **immediate DZ stopping power**: live direct templates, E/M/PARA/Riotstopper disabling templates, legally preplaced mines and meaningful disposable anti-heavy shots. A carried mine, Repeater or Mine Dispenser **does not start deployed without Minelayer**. At comparable defensive effect, **cost, SWC, native Regular Order, survivability and Specialist/Engineer/Sensor/GizmoKit utility** settle ties. There is **no separate cheap-utility table**.

These profiles begin in the ordinary **deployment zone**; no Infiltration, Forward Deployment or unverified Strategic Deployment is assumed for the named loadout. This is not a ranking of premium long-range ARO gunfighters. [Midfield Defenders & Speed Bumps](midfield-defenders-and-speed-bumps.md) covers advanced-deploying variants; [Cheerleaders](cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md) covers pure pooled-Order efficiency and complete FT Master packages; [ARO Apex/Value](aro-units-apex-and-value.md) covers high-end gunlines. [Committed sectorial profiles](../../sectorials/) are the legality/points source.

## Whole-game standouts — one combined defensive bang-for-buck ranking

**How to interpret:** The order is a *qualitative tactical judgment*, not a measured win rate, mathematically calculated score or universal list recommendation. **Regular** adds a transferable Order when eligible; **Irregular** does not. Irregular/Impetuous models do not provide a transferable Regular Order without the exact legal FT Master conversion, and **full package costs** matter. Kuang Shi are native Regular but require a paid Monitor; Auxilia's synchronized Auxbot is a second body, **not** a second pooled Order. Close-area template defenders may beat a long-lane launcher on dense maps and vice versa.

## How to build the defence

1. **Defend the destination, not the skyline.** Buy a 5–15pt Chain Rifle/E/Marat/Adhesive protector that covers the square an enemy must occupy to kill a Lieutenant, tag a Hacker, or claim a home objective. Most cheap guards should be safely hidden until that square is attacked.
2. **Preplace only legal Minelayer devices.** Raveneye12, Kaauri11, Fennec14 and Acontecimento Regular13 start with their explicitly legal mine deployment. Konduktor7, Barsuk11, Gūdǎn FTO11, Tech-Bee9 and other mine/Repeater carriers **do not** preplace devices without the relevant skill.
3. **Layer different answers.** A Repeater+Hacker, mine, cheap template, Disposable launcher and separate Flash Pulse guard require different counters. Two exposed ARO guns on the same roof can be pie-sliced individually.
4. **Trade intelligently.** A weak-BS line trooper usually should not pay major points/SWC for HMG/ML-style gunfighting. Its meaningful job is to impose a direct-template fork, E/M/PARA/Adhesive disablement or one-shot anti-heavy threat. In N5, **Boarding Shotguns are not direct-template weapons** and weapon (+1B) does not improve ordinary ARO Burst.
5. **Correctly account for Orders, support costs and variants.** Ghazi, Beasthunter FTO, standalone Jackal, Helot, Galwegian, Shaolin, Morlock, Irmandinho, Preta, Desperado and Daylami are **Irregular** unless legally converted. Kuang Shi are **native Regular**, but require **Monitor13/.5** and legal team/group overhead. Auxilia15 has an Auxbot, not a second Order. **9pt RacerBot Mk-I/II** starts in DZ despite MOV 20–10. **6pt Daylami**, **7pt Konduktor FTO**, **11pt Gūdǎn FTO**, **11pt Barsuk Camo/Decoy** and **9pt Beasthunter FTO** are not their infiltrating/FD alternatives.
6. **Utilities are tie-breakers.** If two guards control the same corner for comparable cost, Specialist, Sensor, Baggage, FO, TAGCom or GizmoKit can make one better. A support-only Puppet Master, Doctor, empty beacon or costly sniper does not qualify merely by being inexpensive or useful in another role.

**Flashbots** (such as the **Lamedh Flash Pulse/Repeater REM** and faction equivalents) and the **3pt Irregular Warcor Flash Pulse** are of course excellent budget ARO/utility options **where available**, but **not all armies/sectorials can take them**. They are kept outside this template/mine/trade-piece ranking; the Warcor also does not provide a pooled Regular Order. A **16pt Garon TR** is a very economical conventional reactive gun, but is better compared as a **gunline ARO** in [the ARO tiers](aro-units-apex-and-value.md), not as an ordinary DZ trade-body. Full **Oznat/Gaki, Monitor/Kuang Shi, Magistrate/Jackal and Liang Kai/Shaolin** formations stay in [Cheerleaders' complete-team packages](cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md), rather than duplicating a utility-specific table.
