# Cheap DZ Defenders & Trade Pieces — N5.3

**Budget deployment-zone defence; 5 November 2026.** Each ranked **exact profile** starts in the normal **deployment zone**; it has **no Infiltration or Forward Deployment** on that loadout and no unverified Strategic Deployment upgrade is assumed. These troops protect a Lieutenant, Hacker, Engineer, Order pool, deployment entry angle or home objective. They are **not expensive/super ARO gunfighters**. For *exact advanced-deploying variants*, use [Cheap Midfield Defenders & Speed Bumps](cheap-midfield-defenders-and-speed-bumps.md).

**What earns rank:** cheap direct templates (Chain Rifle/Chain-colt, Flamethrower, E/Marat, Zapper), **Minelayer**, mines, Repeater network, non-lethal Adhesive/PARA/Riotstopper, Disposable launcher, concealment/Decoy and the ability to trade up into a premium attacker. **Apex** rates credible protection and layered reaction; **Value** rates this protection per points/SWC and real Order type. Specialist, FO, Engineer, Doctor, Sensor, GizmoKit, Baggage and TAGCom are **valuable side benefits**, not a reason for a separate cheap-utility table. Pure Orders and FT Master packages are ranked in [Cheerleaders](cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md); premium TR/long-range guns in [ARO Apex/Value](aro-units-apex-and-value.md). Pinned [sectorial profiles](../../sectorials/) take priority; rankings are editorial and qualitative, not match statistics.

## How to build the defence

1. **Defend the destination, not the skyline.** Buy a 5–13pt Chain Rifle/E/Marat/Adhesive protector that covers the square an enemy must occupy to kill a Lieutenant, tag a Hacker, or claim a home objective. Most cheap guards should be safely hidden until that square is attacked.
2. **Preplace only legal Minelayer devices.** Raveneye12, Kaauri11, Fennec14 and Acontecimento Regular13 start with their explicitly legal mine deployment. Konduktor7, Barsuk11, Tech-Bee9 and other mine/Repeater carriers **do not** preplace devices without the relevant skill.
3. **Layer different answers.** A Repeater+Hacker, mine, cheap template, Disposable launcher and separate Flash Pulse guard require different counters. Two exposed ARO guns on the same roof can be pie-sliced individually.
4. **Trade intelligently.** A weak-BS line trooper usually should not pay major points/SWC for HMG/ML-style gunfighting. Its meaningful job is to impose a direct-template fork, E/M/PARA/Adhesive disablement or one-shot anti-heavy threat. In N5, **Boarding Shotguns are not direct-template weapons** and weapon (+1B) does not improve ordinary ARO Burst.
5. **Correctly account for Orders and variants.** Ghazi, Beasthunter FTO, standalone Jackal and Helot are **Irregular**. Kuang Shi are **native Regular** in the specific ISS roster, but Monitor/team costs must be counted for the complete package. **9pt RacerBot Mk-I/II** starts in the DZ despite MOV 20–10. **6pt Daylami**, **7pt Konduktor FTO**, **11pt Barsuk Camo/Decoy** and **9pt Beasthunter FTO** are not their infiltrating/FD siblings.
6. **Utilities are tie-breakers.** If two guards control the same corner for comparable cost, Specialist, Sensor, Baggage, FO, TAGCom or GizmoKit can make one better. A support-only Puppet Master, Doctor, empty beacon or costly sniper does not qualify merely by being inexpensive or useful in another role.

A **3pt Irregular Warcor Flash Pulse** can tax a lane, but is not a pooled Regular Order or a template/mine screen. A **16pt Garon TR** is a very economical conventional reactive gun, but is better compared as a **gunline ARO** in [the ARO tiers](aro-units-apex-and-value.md), not as an ordinary DZ trade-body. Full **Oznat/Gaki, Monitor/Kuang Shi, Magistrate/Jackal and Liang Kai/Shaolin** formations stay in [Cheerleaders' complete-team packages](cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md), rather than duplicating a utility-specific table.
