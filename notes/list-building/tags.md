# TAGs — N5.3 Best overall: battlefield strength + value

**Updated:** 11 Nov 2026. **Format:** one fully ordinal, 38-unit cross-faction **Best** ranking for a typical 300-point N5.3 game. The **position matters more than the tier**. This revision uses the [Loss of Lieutenant #142 (April 2025) full tier list](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dZo_gQWQYSY) as the starting order, then evaluates the **actual N5.3 on-table performance** of the ten requested comparison targets from newer YouTube and podcast discussions and the repository's current profiles.

**What counts:** first and foremost how well the TAG shoots, fights, chooses engagements, survives and suppresses threats across realistic missions and tables: BS, Burst, ammo, damage, defensive modifiers, Hidden Deployment/Camo, mobility, close defence, STR, ARM/BTS, and reliable usable Orders. Cost, SWC, support burden and ability to keep fighting after a bad exchange remain relevant. **Do not raise or lower a TAG just because of its faction, legal links, or the competition inside one sectorial**; those are background explanations, not the ranking metric. Extreme list-wide point concentration remains relevant when it affects realistic battlefield impact.

**Tier meanings:** S = exceptional and repeatedly game-defining; A = strong front-line choices; B = useful with significant ceiling or consistency limitations; C = a narrower, more fragile or less reliable investment; D = rarely a strong practical choice. This is a judgment, not measured win rates or a reprint of someone else's published ranking.

**Not silently omitted:** the three TAGs absent from the 2025 Loss of Lieutenant list—**Clausewitz Uhlan, Dragões, Juggernaut**—remain ranked alongside the 35 supplied names (38 total). **Maximus TAG and Iguana were changed after that 2025 episode:** their more recent profiles are evaluated rather than copying the obsolete D results. The current main-branch `sectorials/` N5.3 rosters govern points and weapons when sources differ. This update also corrects a former overstatement: **Zeta has NCO/Lt profiles but does not have Tactical Awareness in the current roster**.

## Reassessing over-rated and under-rated TAGs

**Marut raised to #3/S; O-Yoroi lowered to #14/A:** Marut's **BS15 MSV2 MULTI HMG**, ARM8/BTS9 and Tactical Awareness are a high-end, *repeatable* counter to hard-to-shoot and smoke-protected opponents, while Strategos L2 (on the 90pt LT variant) adds command value. Its 86–90pt investment still requires good support. LoL rated Marut lower partly because **OSS lacks native smoke enabling its own smoke-shooting tricks**; that faction caveat should not dominate a whole-game combat rating. O-Yoroi is an effective 78pt **BS14** MULTI HMG TAG with Stealth, Koalas and HFT but is not an elite mod-stacking gunfighter: **#4 over-rated it** even though LoL's high A was defensible. **Tikbalang #5 over Squalos Mk-II #6** remains appropriate: Mimetism (-3), Climbing Plus, Shock Mine and HFT give the Tik greater engagement control than the budget BS15 Squalos, although MULTI HMG and NCO make the Squalos attractive against armor.

**Blue Wolf:** LoL's panel upgraded it into A, praising the **Burst 5 AP Spitfire**, +1B HFT/Panzerfaust and credible CC deterrence; [Robert Shepherd's White Banner review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-hB_mGBSuMY) describes it as dangerous but a 70pt, ARM6/BTS6 commitment needing midrange access. **#13** is strong but below the better protected / longer range A TAGs. This is a comparison of its gun and delivery, not White Banner's other options.

**Maghariba, Chernobog, Shakush:** [LoL #142](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dZo_gQWQYSY) rated Maghariba in B: WIP14, durable MULTI HMG and weapon variety, but **S8 / 70mm footprint** really makes cover/routes harder. [Robert Shepherd's Haqq review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dmv4pfdTbc0) agrees Maghariba's mine dispenser and price are good while identifying S8 as its main disadvantage. **#18**. LoL penalized **Chernobog** for severe Transmutation / Battle-Ravaged degradation (not simply low ARM); **#30/C**. **Shakush** gets #21/B rather than LoL's C because its modern BS14 Continuous Damage HMG, SR-1 and low cost produce dependable firepower, as reflected in Shepherd's Haqq review. No promotion for Hassassin's equipment gaps.

**Wrecker, Scarface, Triphammer:** LoL put Wrecker near the B high end and highlighted the 51pt **HRL (+1B) AP Continuous Damage** threat. [Robert Shepherd's Torchlight review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PZKCQAupCMs) rates the extremely efficient HRL/Remote Presence package and possible two-Wrecker Duo but warns BS13 means inconsistent face-to-face gunfights. Therefore **#17/B**. LoL's **Scarface & Cordelia** is a legitimate B, and Shepherd sees an efficient TAG but recognizes the forced Engineer bundle; **#22/B** (judge it on **combined** 76–80pt spend). **Triphammer** has a cheap AP/zero-SWC role, but Shepherd considers it significantly less dependable than the other Haqq TAGs. **#34/C**, below Scarface and Wrecker.

**Cross-check, not independent proof:** the [January 2026 d6min Best Infinity TAG episode](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3GwiFRnpPyfAiXvd5GUDqd) praises Lizard's guns, ARM, pricing and Repeater as an all-round best; it is **not** a published full tier list. [Tactical Awareness's Qapu Khalqi N5 review](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6rTsUzDqv5PcCQtNTI1ZA6) discusses Scarface, Shakush and Triphammer as alternatives, with Shakush struggling for a place against alternatives—not evidence that its raw gun is bad.

**Further adjustments after checking 2026 N5.3 profiles and reports:** **Overdron #9→#12 (A)**: HRMC/Albedo efficiency is real but its BS13 and MSV-only Albedo utility are less broadly dominant than the prior ranking implied. **Wrecker #20→#17 (B)**: 51pt AP Continuous Damage HRL and Remote Presence deliver unusually strong practical attacks for a BS13 TAG. **Maximus #16→#20 (B)**: its modern AP HMG, 4 STR and Immunity (ARM) are good but the absence of Mimetism/marker state and premium cost still hurt repeatability. **Avatar #29→#23 (B)**: restoring some credit for superb individual battlefield lethality without discarding LoL's order-burden warning. **Xeodron #30→#27 (B)**: cheap K1/MULTI midrange attacks with Super-Jump and TacAware provide more value than the old near-bottom slot recognized.

**Contested but unchanged:** Jotum (#16/B) remains scary under ordinary fire but costs considerably more than alternatives for ARM10, which AP and hacking bypass; the [December 2025 community comparison](https://www.reddit.com/r/InfinityTheGame/comments/1pyanqc/how_do_we_rate_the_jotum_tag/) makes this case. **Sphinx (#33/C)** has an enormous Hidden Deployment/Mimetism (-6) surprise ceiling but is unreliable into armor due to its 93pt non-AP Spitfire. **Agamemnon (#1/S)** is excellent, but its ARM6 and lack of concealment mean it is a close contest with Zeta and Marut rather than an undisputed statistical #1; evidence does not justify an automatic reshuffle of those two without performance data.


## How to interpret the unified ranking

**The top-ranked TAGs are not interchangeable.** Pick Zeta/Szalamandra/Overdron for long HRMC or plasma lines; Tikbalang, Zeta, Sphinx or Uhlan for vertical routing / flank delivery; Marut for strong MSV2 versus smoke and Mimetism; Cutter or Sphinx for hidden marker-state attacks; Lizard, Stigmata or Gator where Nomad hacking and Engineer infrastructure makes the TAG much more resilient. **Albedo protects against MSV; it does not counter smoke like MSV2.**

**Mission pressure changes priorities.** In console/classified-heavy missions, a 50–70pt TAG plus separately funded specialists often beats a 95–122pt centerpiece. Ordinary TAGs are not specialists merely because they are large and expensive; ZetBot and a legally linked specialist must be assessed under the actual mission rules. For quadrants/zone control, Geckos, Xeodrons and Squalos can swing points efficiently, while a high-end TAG concentrates much more VP in one target. This is a mission tradeoff, not a tier bonus for cheap faction-specific scoring bodies. Zone mass is a consideration, but no longer raises Gorgos just because Tohaa has limited alternatives. See [zone domination and protected area denial](objective-holding-zone-domination-and-carriers-apex-value.md) and [mission specialists](mission-specialists-and-button-pushers-apex-value.md).

**Order economy is profile-specific.** Tactical Awareness generates its appropriate extra activation; NCO uses **available** Lieutenant Orders; Strategos may change how that economy is used. Do not double-count one Order or treat a Lieutenant's personal Orders as universal Duo activations. The Kestrel Tikbalang, MO NCO Tikbalang, NCO Squalos and vanilla/sectorial Zeta are not interchangeable builds.

**Survivability is more than ARM.** Maximus is an important post-May-2026 counterexample to older evaluations: BS14 AP HMG with BS Attack (SR-1), 4 STR, Immunity (ARM) against AP/EXP/Mono effects, CC Attack (-6) and Nbw are *substantial* strengths; immunity does not stop Hacking Programs, E/M/nonlethal states or all BTS-targeted attacks. Marker states, approach geometry, anti-CC screening, KHD/Firewall, Engineer access and having a second attacker can matter more than +2 ARM. A Repeater on Lizard/Iguana can help a friendly network **and** create exposure; avoid treating it as anti-hacking immunity. [Engineer and Servant recovery](doctors-engineers-and-recovery-apex-value.md), [anti-TAG non-hacking tools](anti-tag-anti-heavy-infantry-non-hacking-apex-value.md) and [force multipliers](force-multipliers-special-abilities-apex-value.md) give the relevant support tradeoffs.

**Deployment footprint matters.** A large S7/S8 TAG may have nominally dominant guns but no safe place to deploy, cross terrain or reach meaningful sight lines. Before selecting a second TAG, make sure it buys a different range/angle or a different mission role, not a second copy of the same 24–32-inch HMG plan.
