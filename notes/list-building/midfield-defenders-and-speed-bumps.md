# Midfield Defenders & Speed Bumps — N5.3

**Midfield defensive profiles; 5 November 2026.** To qualify, the **exact selected profile** must have Infiltration, Forward Deployment, or specifically legal Strategic Deployment allowing it to *begin* ahead of its normal deployment zone. Strategic Deployment only qualifies when actually granted in the legal list/setup. Speed, Camouflage alone, Hidden Deployment alone, Parachutist, and airborne deployment do not qualify. DZ-only profiles belong in [Cheap DZ Defenders & Trade Pieces](cheap-dz-defenders-and-trade-pieces.md).

**Separate evaluation axes:** **Apex** ranks the strongest credible forward-starting defensive problem regardless of price, including premium layered traps, durable blockers and aggressive reactive threats. **Value** ranks cheap/best bang-for-buck profiles, accounting for points, SWC, Regular versus Irregular Orders and how many enemy Orders they can force without your setup Orders. Minelayer, native Repeaters with allied Hacker coverage, Camo/Decoy, DTWs, disposable anti-heavy weapons and non-lethal E/M/PARA/Adhesive threats are especially valuable. Specialist or utility skills count when they reinforce real defence, not as a separate utility ranking. Rankings are qualitative cross-faction comparisons, not measured win rates or mixed-faction legal lists; the [sectorial references](../../sectorials/) determine each legal roster/profile.

**Research provenance:** Consolidates the 2026-10-23 area-denial research (N5.3 September 2026 sectorial snapshot, Army 7.26246.158/.159) with the 5 November 2026 forward-start Apex/Value rankings. The newer **forward-deployment eligibility and headline ranking order take precedence** where the older broader area-denial list included DZ-only or move-up-later units. Media are qualitative interpretations; current sectorial profiles control price, skills, AVA and Fireteam legality.

## Deployment rubric and traps that actually work

- **Minelayer versus carried mine:** Shujae27/0, Dasyu30/.5, Gangbuster22/0, Kinnara25/.5, Yaksha25/.5, Scout28/.5, Moran15, Moonraker15, Jackal11/.5, Foxtrot18/.5, Zero18/.5, Libertos8/1 and Ryūken-9 22/.5 get a preplaced device only under their **specific Minelayer** rules. Gūdǎn12, Lóng Yá16, Konduktor12, Beasthunter15 and Jackal10 carry mines but must normally **spend an Order** to put them down.
- **Reveal timing:** Marker state taxes Discover and movement, but one-use Camo may not return. Hidden Deployment alone does not entitle a model to appear in midfield, and Hidden/off-table pieces have different Order-count consequences. Decoy, Camo, Mimetism and Infiltration are **different** abilities.
- **Template/reaction check:** Chain Rifle/Chain-colt, HFT, E/Marat and Zapper create direct-template or disabling forks; N5 **Boarding Shotguns do not**. Weapon (+1B) does not increase ordinary ARO Burst. Disposable Panzerfaust/Flammenspeer force a decision but lose value after shots are spent.
- **Repeaters:** A Moran native Repeater projects from setup; its actual payoff depends on live friendly Hackers and enemy targets. An undeployed Deployable Repeater, Mine Dispenser or Pitcher shot is **not** free Turn-0 area control. See [Network Projection](network-projection-repeaters-pitchers-apex-value.md).
- **Placement:** Defend a square an attacker must *finish on* or pass to score, not merely the prettiest rooftop. Place separate marker/mine, template and anti-heavy threats along **independent angles** so one attack cannot remove all layers. Pay attention to Infiltration failure, AVA, SWC, and whether clearing the device can be done by Sensor/Deactivator or expendable troops.

## Selection principles: turn-zero versus active construction

The area-denial goal is to consume enemy Orders, detours, Discover attempts, Deactivator/Sensor actions, expendable bodies or bad engagements **without first consuming your own Active-Turn Orders**. Rate **Apex** by the strongest credible denial ceiling in a realistic matchup, and **Value** by disruption plus pooled Order/objective utility per points, SWC and setup cost. Neither column is a measured win rate.

1. **Genuine turn-zero pressure:** a legally forward-deployed Minelayer gives its particular preplaced mine/perimeter item; a prepositioned native Repeater threatens Hackable enemies **only with live allied hackers**. A camouflaged live HFT, Chain Rifle, Zapper, E/Marat, disposable weapon or close counterattack can deny a route *without* a prelaid mine.
2. **Order and marker accounting:** Regular on-table defenders contribute a pooled Order; Irregular ones do not. A Hidden model does not provide its Order while hidden. One-use Camo does not restore indefinite marker protection, and Decoy/Mimetism/Infiltration are not interchangeable skills.
3. **What costs setup Orders:** carrying a Mine, deploying a FastPanda/ordinary Deployable Repeater/Pitcher, firing a Mine Dispenser, dropping Bears, re-mining and entering Suppressive Fire normally require an activation unless a particular skill explicitly prepays the effect. Do not describe those constructed traps as turn-zero coverage.
4. **Measure the actual win:** enemy Orders diverted, angles abandoned, movement slowed, scoring delayed and counterattack made safe—not merely enemy kills. One inexpensive live template ambusher in a tightly constrained objective room may outperform a pricier multi-mine package on a wide-open map.

### Live forward ambushers — technical distinctions

The main Value table includes the cheap live Camo/FD trade pieces; they are **not all Minelayers**. In particular:

- **Beasthunter 15/0 HFT + Panzerfaust:** reusable Camo, FD(+20), a short-range HFT versus a ranged disposable shot; the AP Mine is not preplaced. Irregular, one W. **17/0 HFT(+1B)** is another close-range option.
- **Kyojin Killer 15/0 HFT + Flammenspeer:** FD(+20), Camo (1 Use), Super-Jump and a dangerous CC follow-up; Irregular and fragile. Not a permanent marker or minefield.
- **Gūdǎn 12/0 SMG + Zapper:** FD(+10), Regular Order, Camo (1 Use), **small E/M direct template**; carried Shock Mine is not free. **13/0 HFT** is a different damage-focused choice.
- **Heckler 20/0 E/Marat:** FD(+10), Regular Order and **large E/M direct template**. **23/0 Jammer + FastPanda** provides a different no-LoF ZoC/Order-fed node package; Jammer is Disposable (2), not a template.
- **Konduktor 12/0 Chain-colt + Flammenspeer:** Infiltration, Regular Order, one-use Camo; a cheap close direct-template plus lower-BS disposable ranged threat, **not** a full Strelok replacement.
- **Yamabushi** forward Chain Rifle + Panzerfaust / one-use-Camo variants: Dogged and strong melee increase the trade ceiling, but check the exact FD/Camo/profile cost before choosing; no inherent free Mine.
- **Moonraker 15/.5 Adhesive Launcher Rifle + Shock Minelayer:** uniquely combines a **prepaid mine** and live non-lethal Adhesive fire; its 14/0 version has no Minelayer.

**Deployment procedure:** Put blockers **behind/around** corners and objectives, covering *where enemies must move next*, not an irrelevant open sightline; separate multiple mine trigger areas when a single cheap clearing move would answer both. Use Camo/Decoy markers to make exact clearance uncertain; anchor the pocket with a dangerous *different* response (direct template, hidden ARO, live hacker). Keep the scoring Specialist alive while spending cheaper bodies to cause friction.

**N5.3 legal traps:** Minelayer's deployment mine/weapon must be within its permitted deployment area and ZoC; do **not** place a mine with any existing enemy model/marker in its Trigger Area; perimeter weapons have additional placement/ZoC checks. The fact that a unit has Mines/Koalas does **not** imply a free deployed device if the chosen profile lacks Minelayer. Hidden Deployment masks location but sacrifices its contributed Order while hidden. Do not imply a mine automatically destroys a TAG: Dodge, ARM/BTS/Immunities, Deactivator, Sensor, sacrificial troopers and Engineer support change results. ## Transcript-backed tradeoffs and opposing evidence

1. **“More mines” versus “more *credible kinds* of threat.”** [Robert Shepherd — Hassassin Bahram N5](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yHpAnRDw_E8) (**YouTube MCP transcript reviewed**) praises Shujae E/M Minelayer(2), but warns two deployed mines can reveal the possible Hidden model position; recommends mixing Farzan Viral/ordinary camo. **Choose repeated Shujae** into HI/TAG chokepoints; **mix** against experienced Sensor/Deactivator play.
2. **Shrouded mine saturation versus pricier Malignos.** [Robert Shepherd — Shasvastii faction focus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mtqHMuHmjjo) (**YouTube MCP transcript reviewed; N4-era**) explains how cheap Shrouded markers protect Caliban and Hidden attackers through ambiguity. Shrouded Dazer is much more situational; paying for Malignos Hidden is worthwhile only if its concealment wins the scenario. **Current prices above override old audio.**
3. **Ryūken high-ARO ambush versus SMG midfield option.** [Robert Shepherd — Ōban faction focus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AhicCreNCUQ) (**YouTube MCP transcript reviewed**) prefers the HRL's long-lived “discover-or-leave-it” pressure and often finds FD SMG harder to convert to value. **Counterview:** SMG/FD is cheaper in SWC and explicitly better for dense objectives and Suppressive Fire deployment.
4. **“Cheap regular mines fix DZ” versus attack/skirmisher investment.** [Robert Shepherd — Shock Army of Acontecimento](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RnP9fVZRb_Q) (**YouTube MCP transcript reviewed; older N4-era**) stresses 13pt Regular mine/Sensor protecting bad defensive tables. [MetaChemistry #135 — Acontecimento](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2J6ByFT0PJGKB0DiSQ55AC) (**Podcast MCP transcript reviewed, 2026**) finds it neat but gives much more attention to Sapper sniper and Naga/Dart attack options. **Use cheap DZ miners** when rear defense is fragile; otherwise spend the 3–4pt upgrade on mission roles.
5. **High-tech Yaksha/Kinnara versus cheaper bodies.** [Loss of Lieutenant E168 — Mazebreaker previews](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4MxU8qsoJlbZkYCLt1fo6d) (**Podcast MCP transcript reviewed, 2026; preview assumptions superseded by pinned profiles**) likes Naga's new Monofilament Minelayer and revised specialist networks. [Existing OSS analysis](../factions/operations-subsection.md) preserves these competing investments:
   - **Yaksha** — durable MadTrap/minefield, with armor, FD and E/M close fire
   - **Pilot-X + RacerBots** — a different multi-body network/screen investment
   - **Naga** — cheaper marker/minelayer route
   - **Dasyu** — alternate hidden/marker utility
   - **Dart** — alternate specialist/attack pressure

   Choose Yaksha only when its combined defenses justify paying 25+ points for a passive screen, not merely because it is new.
6. **Naga Monofilament Minelayer — peak consequence versus useful selection.** [Swiss Miss N5 SAA (~57:00)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hlmVXGHskS4&t=3420s) describes Mono as amusing but explicitly says it is probably not the one to take. [Infinity Dante (~39:30)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GKR_cd3oEFE&t=2370s) prefers a Peacemaker for Minelayer and Naga KHD/Combi utility. **Keep Mono a conditional apex outside the headline Top 10**. That preserves a credible anti-TAG/HI forced-choke use without presenting it as a high-value default; it is a reviewer-backed preference, not evidence the trap never works.
7. **Two Moran hacking setup vs SWC-efficient direct shooting.** [WIP12 E208 — Corregidor post-Mazebreaker](https://open.spotify.com/episode/342lz18SRGSZ8QWf1Zg3Z9) (**Podcast MCP transcript reviewed, 2026**) sees Moran/Jazz as a powerful but **SWC-expensive** path once Vertigo joins it. **Counterview:** take the 15pt Moran for cheap *passive clearance tax* even **without Guided**. CJC can run a gun-first list supported by physical Koalas/hacking AROs.
8. **Mine dispensers and deployable saturation can be oppressive but cost Orders.** [Tactical Awareness — N5 launch, Equipment/Deployables](https://open.spotify.com/episode/1XVRK5M5O8SLoQT099V2pu) (**Podcast MCP transcript reviewed, 2024 rules discussion**) emphasizes remotely generated minefields/Koalas/MadTraps and how multiple hazards force slow clearing. Its speculative coordinated-place interactions should **not** override N5.3 rules text. **Tradeoff:** invest Orders building a *harder future table* only if doing so prevents more enemy Orders or scoring.
9. **Chasseur/Foxtrot/Camo trap versus guns and proactive pressure.** [Robert Shepherd — Ariadna CanCon 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTqYE_FddwQ) (**YouTube MCP transcript reviewed**) emphasizes Strelok Decoy/Scout mine and passive camouflage saturation as a way to support aggressive guns and Speculative Fire. **Counterview:** too many nonlethal or one-shot roadblocks can leave no credible counterattack. [Defensive architecture](../Strategy/defensive-architecture-and-reactive-turn.md) favors **serial layers** rather than one massive uniform first line.

## Practical 20-second list check

- **Turn-zero obstacle?** Identify the precise printed *Minelayer* profile, free mine type and legal location, plus what enemy must do to cross it.
- **At least two different failure modes?** Mine/perimeter **plus** hidden ARO/DTW/Repeater/hacker/Deactivator denial; not three identical mines to the same door.
- **Real cost?** Include SWC, Irregular/Hidden Order loss, ability to score afterwards, AVA and Fireteam restrictions.
- **Opponent's cheapest answer?** Sensor, Deactivator, cheap warband, ranged gun, Dodge, immunity or bypass; deploy the second gate where that answer has a cost.
- **Score the mission.** If a minefield causes detours but protects no objective, gun or surviving Specialist, spend the points elsewhere.
