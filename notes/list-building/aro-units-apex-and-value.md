# ARO Units — N5.3 Apex, Points-Value and Defensive Field Guide

**Vanilla O-12 reactive architecture — transcript update (26 Oct 2026):**

- [Robert Shepherd, O-12 faction focus (19–23m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Nl8BMJ8E5U&t=1140s) describes **two Moonraker Minelayers 15/.5** as a *short-range crumple zone*, not as cheap long-range snipers.
- Position the **four initial Moonraker/mine camouflage markers** to overlap approaches **8–16in from meaningful enemy ingress**, so an opponent commits Orders before several possible hidden **Adhesive Launcher Rifle** AROs.
- Good reactive glue can **immobilize** a heavy attacker, but must be followed by removal/mission exploitation; it does not automatically destroy or isolate.
- Behind this: **Regular PH14 Varangians** as second screen, **Peeler TR Thunderbolt19/0 + Engineer** for a repairable long deterrent, or **RoadBot20 Paramedic Panzerfaust/Riotstopper** as tougher roadblock with mission mobility.

The presenter calls O-12's *lethal* ARO suite limited; don't treat every active Omega/Gamma as a permanent exposed sentry or copy Starmada/Torchlight links.

Best sniper is not automatically best zone guard.

## Executive verdict — expanded full-roster audit (2 November 2026)

This revision checks **current N5.3 roster profiles in every faction/sectorial**, including direct Total Reaction, Neurocinetics, Neurocinetics Peripherals, native/weapon BS Attack (+1 SD), elite hidden attacks, and legal Fireteam AROs. It deliberately ranks **maximum reactive threat (apex)** separately from **whole-list points/SWC efficiency (value)**. Source precedence: [sectorial profiles](../../sectorials), [Fireteam bonuses](../../rules/6_Command%20and%20Fireteams/Fireteam_Bonuses.md), [Total Reaction](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Total_Reaction.md), [Neurocinetics](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Neurocinetics.md), and [Peripheral](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Peripheral.md). This is a qualitative assessment; profiles are not interchangeable across factions.

- **S-apex exposed sniper previously omitted: Atalanta 41/1.5 (Steel Phalanx), Total Reaction MULTI Sniper.** BS15 + MSV2 + Marksmanship + Shock produces exceptionally reliable ranged FtF pressure against cover/Mimetism. **This is the 41pt TR profile, NOT the 38pt Hidden Deployment profile.** Only W1/ARM2: White Noise, Albedo, bypass and favorable cheap template trades can still end her. [Steel Phalanx roster](../../sectorials/aleph/steel-phalanx.md).
- **S-apex hidden commitment attacks:** Swiss Guard ML(+1SD) 60/1.5 and Noctifer ML 30/1.5. They win by threatening an otherwise apparently safe activation, not by supplying repeatable TR Burst. Agêma, Dasyu, Hexa and Raiden are relevant cheaper/other hidden alternatives.
- **S / A+ durable exposed anchors:** Yān Huǒ **42/1.5 Neurocinetics(+1B) Missile Launcher**, Knight of Justice **46/1.5 ML(+1SD)** in a legal WinterFor Fireteam, Hetkari **47/1.5 ML**, and Sunduqbut **47/1.5 Neurocinetics Feuerbach**. They have very different reactive dice, visors, team requirements and anti-armor ammunition.
- **A+ reactive-specialist omissions:** Teucer **37/1.5 Neurocinetics Plasma Sniper**; TAK Vystrel **32/1.5 Neurocinetics Portable Autocannon** or **26/0.5 Neurocinetics AP Sniper**; Drummer **40/1 with Drumbot-3 Neurocinetics Feuerbach Peripheral**; and Varuna **Helot Neurocinetics** options. At lower apex they may offer much better *value* than 40–60pt anchors.
- **Best Fireteam AROs are not simply the highest-BS models.** Black A.I.R. FTO Neuro MSR, Kamau MSV2 MSR, Nisse MSV2 MSR, Hǎidào MSV2 MSR, Riot Grrl ML, Neoterra Bolt ML, and Sukeul ML are serious **only in their legally supported team configurations**. Native +1SD (e.g. Al Fasid HRL, Naga/Dasyu sniper, Intruder sniper, Major Lunah) can function independently, but is not extra Burst.
- **Routine value remains cheap and layered:** 18–20pt 0SWC Thunderbolt TR REMs, 16pt JSA Garon smoke/Thunderbolt TR, 10/12pt Helot LRL, 6pt Panzerfaust roadblocks, 3pt Warcor, mines and reliable objective defenders. Do not spend the attacker's SWC and Orders on an exposed 1W sniper unless it protects a lane that matters.

**Core correction:** Total Reaction and Neurocinetics permit the weapon's **full native Burst** in ARO; Neurocinetics also cuts active BS weapon Burst to 1. **BS Attack (+1 SD) rolls an extra die and discards one; it does not add a hit, a target or a point of Burst**, and cannot improve a direct-template attack with no Roll. A Fireteam with the required same-Unit level gains +1 SD, later +1 BS and Sixth Sense under the N5 chart; a five-model team does not automatically receive all bonuses just from model count.

## What “best” means

| Metric | Question | Why it changes rankings |
|---|---|---|
| **Apex** | When the right threat must cross the right lane, how punishing is this defence? | Strong FtF, +SD/reactive Burst, hidden timing, armour/wounds, covered approach, crossfire |
| **Points-value** | How much *credible delay/denial*, surviving Regular Orders and secondary utility per point and SWC? | Cheap templates, Flash, mines and TR bots often beat premium snipers on whole-list return |
| **Survival & repeatability** | Does losing one FtF eliminate it, or can it Guts out / be repaired / be redeployed as a later-turn threat? | STR/Remote Presence with Engineer, 2W+ARM, Hidden Deployment, safe retreat square |
| **Scenario value** | Does it stop scoring/route access rather than merely win an irrelevant gunfight? | In Turn 3 a Flash Pulse or mine on the **console** can matter more than a Swiss ML on a far flank |
| **Counter-cost** | How many Orders/abilities must the opponent spend to get a safe attack or bypass? | Smoke, White Noise, MSV, Albedo, Sensor, hackers, mines, flank geometry, templates |

**S/A/B/C mean conditional tiers, not universal must-takes.** Apex ignores price more than value does. For a conventional 300pt list, estimate **Orders denied per defence cycle** (setup + movement + fight + retreat/retry) and whether the defender's lost Regular Order, SWC and mission contribution are tolerable.

**Do not mistake a weapon modifier for a new rule:** +1 SD is a keep-best-die mechanic, +1B is an applied Burst modifier, and native Total Reaction/Neurocinetics grants full Burst in the Reactive Turn. A **linked** model uses its *actual Fireteam level*, not the number of models nearby. Special dice may stack with native +1SD under the Fireteam rules; they remain special dice, not additional hits.

These are **not** full-Burst unless the printed profile actually has TR/Neuro.
- **Fireteam ARO due diligence:** 2 same-Unit members = +1SD; 4 = +1 BS; 5 = Sixth Sense, per [bonuses](../../rules/6_Command%20and%20Fireteams/Fireteam_Bonuses.md). Check canonical charts **for the profile**, not the generic unit: Black A.I.R. **FTO** joins Fennecs; KoJ Wildcard in WinterFor; Hǎidào Wildcard in IA; Kamau/Varuna, Nisse/WinterFor, Riot Grrl/Bakunin, Bolt/NCA, Sukeul/Tohaa Triad, Vystrel/TAK Gunnery. **Non-FTO Neuro Yān Huǒ** and **FTO ML(+1B) Yān Huǒ** are separate. **Escort Sergeant** is non-FTO; **Order Sergeant FTO HRL** is a distinct 15pt unit.
- **Weapons that change matchups:** AP/T2/K1/Feuerbach/Portable Autocannon against HI/TAG; Viral and Plasma against relevant saving profiles; blast ML/HRL against bunched targets; MSV2 against Mimetism, but White Noise/Albedo counters; Mimetism(-6) or one-use Hidden against active elite shooters; Marksmanship reduces cover protection **on its own attack**. The best number on a profile means nothing if a cheap enemy reaches the roof unseen.

### Short decision rules

1. If the target must take a long, covered gunfight **now**, consider Atalanta TR, a covered linked visor sniper, Yān Huǒ Neuro ML, Sunduqbut, Teucer or well-positioned Black A.I.R.; compare cost of winning to the danger of losing a premium body.
2. If the enemy must **commit before seeing you**, prefer Swiss/Noctifer/Dasyu/Agêma/Hexa/Raiden/Blink at the correct reveal moment.
3. If the defense must be **repairable/repeated**, prefer a Thunderbolt TR + Engineer, Vystrel STR2/Engineer, a protected Drumbot network or durable anchor that can Guts out.
4. If you need a **low-cost first layer**, prefer Helot, Warcor, Daylami, Jackal, Garon, cheaper linked HRL, mines and Repeaters. A 6pt Panzerfaust that costs two enemy Orders can be better than a 47pt exposed sniper that wins none.
5. If building a Fireteam only for ARO, pay the **whole Fireteam opportunity cost**, including loss of flexibility/Exposure and which exact members count toward the Level.

## TAG AROs — separate category

TAGs are **not ranked alongside non-TAG ARO specialists**: their main job is usually to win the active turn. A TAG holding a reactive lane must be judged against the chance of losing a costly primary attacker and its future Orders. See the separate [TAG tier guide](tags.md) for full cross-faction apex/value rankings.

| TAG / ARO use | Reactive strength | When it makes sense | Why not simply leave it standing? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Overdron Heavy Rocket Launcher, 51pt** (Combined/Onyx where legal) | Inexpensive-for-a-TAG long-lane HRL (+1B) threat; can serve as a tough emergency anchor while also providing active-turn pressure | The template controls an important required route, other ARO layers protect its approaches, and it has a safe exit/counterattack plan | Even a 51pt TAG is a much larger loss than a 20pt Q-Drone; mission and active-turn opportunity cost dominate a speculative defensive shot |
| **Squalos Mk-II — PanO/Kestrel native BS Attack(+1SD)** | BS15/ARM6/W3 long-gun reactive quality, far more durable than 1W standing sniper | Can hold a truly essential lane after its active-turn contribution | Expensive centerpiece with a large hackable silhouette; **native SD is not Total Reaction** |
| **Firebat VH — O-12/Torchlight native BS Attack(+1SD), Mim(-6)** | Strong mobile vehicle-style ARO with Thunderbolt/LRL or nonlethal weapon choices | Alternate lane/anti-approach guard when already fielded for NCO or mission mobility | **No Cover** and profile weapon bands still matter; excellent defense in one corridor needn't be worth losing a mobile active piece |
| **Other 70–100pt TAGs as static “towers”** | Heavy weapons, multiple wounds/STR and armor can impose a serious FtF or deterrence | A truly essential lane, limited enemy anti-TAG counters, recovery support and a planned counterpunch | **Low points-value for pure ARO**: risking the main active-turn attacker to win a single reactive exchange is generally an unfavorable trade |

**Default:** protect TAGs behind cheaper ARO layers; expose one reactively only when its lane meaningfully safeguards scoring or the survival of the army. This is a **role-specific ARO judgment**, not a downgrade to the TAG's overall apex/value tier in the [TAG guide](tags.md).

|---|---|---|

| **Enemy has MSV2 + smoke gunfighter** | Non-visor ML/Noctifer, DTWs, mines, Eclipse-resistant coverage, protected hack | A visor-based sniper may be disabled by White Noise/Albedo; the right response isn't always “another MSV” |
| **Enemy relies on Mimetism(-6)/Camo** | Black A.I.R. MSV2, Kamau MSV2, Nisse MSV2, Riot Grrl MSV1, cheap Sensor+DTW | Vision tech reduces attacker's protection; do not overbuy 1W MSV snipers that can be smoked/shot through a different lane |
| **Need a 0SWC standing guard** | 18–19pt Thunderbolt TR, 25pt Mk12 Sin-Eater (Bakunin), 13pt Bluecoat close guard | Preserves SWC and Regular pool; choose weapon bands for *real* corridor |
| **Against premium HI/TAG** | Panzerfaust, Feuerbach, AP/T2 sniper, E/M/Mine, Repeaters/Oblivion | Anti-armor saves or nonlethal immobilize/isolate; cheap Panzerfaust can force scary choice even if dies |
| **Against AD, Impersonation, bikes or rapid DZ penetration** | Kaauri E/M, Bluecoat Riotstopper/adhesive, cheap Chain Rifle/shotgun, mines, 360° Flash/TR | Cover *landing/exit and final destinations*, not only central rooftop firing lines |
| **Against Guided/Spotlight networks** | ARO covering the enemy's *Pitcher throw lane*, accessible Repeater, KHD/HD/Combat Instinct network, spread-out models | Stop network projection, not merely hunt a distant Guided missile bot. Do not bunch troops under one template |
| **Multiple objectives/corners** | Distributed mines, cheap DTWs, marker threats, two cheap TR/Flash screens | A single long-range premium sniper cannot watch every entrance; static defense should protect scoring |
| **Opponent will spend Smoke/White Noise bypassing** | Minelayer/perimeter, templates, hacking, corner guard, hidden timing | Vision-control bypass of a gun still costs resources, but only if it changes required route/mission timing |
| **You go first and need counterattack** | Hide the TAG/active HMG, show only cheap guard or appropriate TR | Surviving to spend your own Orders is more important than winning an uncertain reactive fight |
| **You go second and opponent deploys exposed first** | Reserve TR/linked sniper after studying enemy deployment; conceal hidden ARO covering its exit | Make the opponent *move/solve* before freely attacking your team |
| **End of Turn 2/3 protecting scoring** | Flash pulse, Bluecoat, Kaauri, cheap mine/DTW at console, Suppressive Fire network | Stunned/delay that stops one final objective movement may be enough. An irrelevant rooftop kill is not |
| **Have an Engineer nearby** | TR REM or durable HI/STR attacker in safe retrieval reach | Forcing a second recovery-clearance cycle is often stronger than one expensive irrecoverable ARO |
| **Dense terrain / blocked sightlines** | Cheap templates/mines/hidden short-range defender/Flash | Strong sniper rangebands rarely matter; don't spend 1.5 SWC to guard a six-inch corridor |

## Hidden ARO versus on-table guard

1. **Hidden Deployment is not a free Regular Order.** While off-table the trooper supplies no ordinary shared pool Order. Include this opportunity cost when comparing a 30pt hidden Noctifer to a 19pt visible TR REM.
2. **Ambush timing matters more than first possible target.** Often decline an early ARO and reveal when the attacker has committed to an exposed position, an objective or multiple threatened models. Observe all declaration/reveal timing and legal ARO requirements.
3. **Camouflage(1 Use) is not Hidden Deployment.** Daylami and Helot options can have a Camo marker and a deployment/timing advantage but should not be credited with unprinted Hidden Deployment.
4. **A marker/minelayer can be more valuable unrevealed.** The opponent may spend Discover, Sensor, Minesweeper/Deactivator, rerouting or a sacrificial body to clear what might be a dangerous ARO.
5. **Do not reveal merely to announce a shot.** The *unknown* Noctifer sometimes deters more Orders than the exposed survivor of its first attack. However, the right scenario-winning shot is worth taking even if the model then dies.
6. **Conceal your counterpunch.** A strong hidden ARO should defend your remaining mission-capable Orders or the square the opponent must end on, not expose every precious defender in one activation.

## How to build layered defence

**Serial gates beat two adjacent snipers.** Robert Shepherd's [stacking AROs demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bszE3AGzSuc) shows that two visible rooftop models are often **pie-sliced one at a time**. To force an expensive choice, protect one defender's approach with an independent **mine, different-angle sniper, hacking Repeater or hidden model**, or make the attacker cross a protected route before seeing the next defender. Vertical stacking can narrow horizontal pie-slicing but may still be beaten by vertical geometry.

**Reactive pieces should protect a destination.** Set up a cheap guard looking at the square a smoke-grenade thrower, pitcher model or objective runner **must occupy**, not just where it started. Place mines around *corners and objective exits*, not right beside a valuable model where one template removes both.

**Use four reactive layers, not four premium snipers:**
- **Information/route tax:** Camo marker, 3pt Warcor or safe 7pt Flash REM;
- **Approach gun:** 18–20pt TR Thunderbolt or 6pt disposable Panzerfaust on an alternate lane;
- **Commitment trap:** unseen Noctifer/Blink/Raiden/Kaauri mine or hacking ARO;
- **Mission/core stop:** S2 chain rifle / E/M / Sixth Sense / recoverable reactive anchor guarding objective and support pieces.

**Turn priority:** Turn 1: preserve primary attack Orders; Turn 2: create objective/exit network; Turn 3: stop decisive movement and take any trading opportunity that protects scoring.

## The disagreements that change list building

1. **Big hard ARO vs small disposable walls.** Some podcast hosts favor a **Knight of Justice ML** or Swiss as a visible/hidden anchor because the enemy must respect long sightlines. Other voices emphasize simply *not losing your active pieces*; cheap mines/Flash/Panzerfaust force enough Orders at much lower risk. Correct answer: long open tables favor hard guns; dense or objective-heavy maps favor distributed control.
2. **Swiss Guard Hidden ML: apex weapon or overpriced first reveal?** [Loss of Lieutenant Deployment Part 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=il2w0PSGr0U) discusses how revealing a Swiss early can immediately invite favorable follow-up or smoke shooting; a damaged Swiss sometimes should Guts/prone rather than keep standing. The [Part 2](https://open.spotify.com/episode/109wmskmaxqFierHIG89kN) discussion prizes **holding the unseen threat until a decisive activation**. A cheaper Noctifer loses less investment after its ambush but is much easier to kill.
3. **Kestrel Black A.I.R. vs Sierra/Jackals/Hetkari.** Existing Kestrel video/podcast syntheses record **Irregular Order hosts** actively favoring Black A.I.R. neuro-sniper or Hetkari ML, while **Robert Shepherd** regards expensive W1 Black A.I.R. as situational. Daboarder's visible **Sierra plus hidden Blink** offers a cheaper *layered* alternative. Long, smoke/camo-heavy lanes favor Black A.I.R.; mission-first compact lists may want the 18pt Sierra or 6pt Jackal.
4. **Bakunin Sin-Eater and Riot Grrl:** [Shepherd Bakunin faction focus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=firJVr1eDWg) prefers the Riot Grrl for sturdy visor/linked defence and a recoverable attack plan, but sees good use for Sin-Eater/cheap midrange threat in suitable lanes. Dante's older faction view was more enthusiastic about Sin-Eater as a specialty ARO. It isn't evidence every Sin-Eater must be taken.
5. **TR bots are better in N5, yet still solvable.** [LoL Deployment Part 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=il2w0PSGr0U) reports that 0SWC Thunderbolt versions and reactive-dice interactions make TR better than in N4; **their low BS and single STR** still make them disposable unless an Engineer covers a safe repair square. HMG 23–25pt/1SWC has longer-range/weapon utility but loses the 0SWC bargain.
6. **Varuna Helot saturation vs Order-pool cost.** [Infinity the Dante — Varuna focus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YPfLiJ8IKR0) argues two Helots are often a defining *defensive* ingredient, sometimes three. The cost is **Irregular** Combat Group capacity and limited active-turn contribution; Warcor/other cheap support may be better if the list already has enough traps. Clip is machine-translated/noisy; use current Varuna profiles.
7. **Hidden-delivered disposable launcher vs visible premium shooter.** A **Daylami/Jackal** can threaten TAGs for 6–8pt and be allowed to die; a **Noctifer** is far scarier once but costs 30pt/1.5SWC and gives up early Order. Pick the minimum tool that *actually stops* the intended route, not whichever weapon has highest printed damage.
8. **Shoot or Dodge against a fork?** [LoL — The Art of the Fork](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4AWYvbMuuQAeAYlaA26ghh) stresses that an ARO that Dodges by reflex may concede unopposed hits; sometimes **shooting despite danger** is the rational way to impose a failure state. Conversely, keep an elite survivor alive when its continued game-plan value exceeds a speculative trade.
9. **Concentrated firelane vs serial defence.** [Shepherd — stacking AROs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bszE3AGzSuc) shows that putting two ARO guns next to one another does not force two simultaneous reactions; [LoL Deployment Part 2](https://open.spotify.com/episode/109wmskmaxqFierHIG89kN) argues for **independent layers**, hidden traps and mining arrival squares. Both agree geometry beats buying yet another expensive standing gun.

## The 30-second ARO audit

1. **What absolutely must survive Turn 1?** (Lieutenant, primary gun, two Specialists, hacking/recovery support). Hide those unless intentionally used as defensive anchor.
2. **What enemy *route* must be slowed?** Locate the first important destination square, not just its opening deployment.
3. **Which piece is disposable?** One cheap Panzerfaust/Flash/DTW often suffices to force smoke, an extra Order or a risky first attack.
4. **What is the trap *behind* it?** Hidden ML, mine, Repeater/Combat Instinct Hacker, second-angle TR or a covered objective guard. Don't put both guns on the same pie-slice.
5. **If the main ARO loses one FtF, is the game still playable?** Guts/prone exit, Engineer/Servant recovery or preserved counterattacker.
6. **Which tool defeats your own ARO?** Smoke, Eclipse, White Noise, MSV/Albedo, marker state, Sense/Discover, Combat Jump, template or hacking. Include a different second layer.
7. **Will I be able to score in my own turn?** If not, replace the third 1.5SWC sniper with cheap defence and an actual specialist.

**Bottom line:** *Best ceiling is usually a dangerous hidden or hard reactive gun, but the best list value is often a cheap, layered and mission-focused defender that consumes Orders without sacrificing your active-turn plan.*
