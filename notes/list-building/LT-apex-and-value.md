## The fast verdict

- **Best whole-army command engine:** **Saladin LT 39/0** in Vanilla Haqq with multiple useful **Irregular** troops. Strategos L2 + Inspiring Leadership + Counterintelligence convert a normally scattered army into a flexible Order pool, add reserves and Command Token efficiencies. In a mostly-Regular army, pay much less for a normal Lieutenant.
- **Best inexpensive general Strategos:** **BCA Magistrate LT 19/0** in Kestrel or Vanilla PanO: Counterintelligence, Specialist Operative, Jackal Fireteam Master and Strategos L1 at a very low cost; fragility is the downside. **Magistrate CoC 21/0 is a mutually exclusive profile**, not simultaneously the LT.
- **Best combat/command ceiling:**
  - **Avatar LT 122/3 SWC** — Strategos L2, Mnemonica, Tactical Awareness and Sepsitor give the highest raw command/combat ceiling.
  - **Marut LT 90/2** — Strategos L2, MSV2, Tactical Awareness and a premium gun; often better **value** among command TAGs because more budget remains for specialists.
- **Best Morat fighting LT alternatives:**
  - **Sogarat LT 56/0.5–1** — discounted heavy gun; actual *Warhorse* allies remain Regular after a command disaster.
  - **Kornak LT 42/0** — Strategos L1 and LT(+1), preferable when two **flexible** Orders outperform another big gun.
- **Best cheap command insurance:**
  - **O-12 Ment CoC 14/0** (Vanilla/eligible O-12)
  - **Kempeitai CoC 17/0** (JSA)
  - **Raveneye CoC 18/0** (Torchlight)
  - **Hafza CoC 20/0** (Qapu/eligible Haqq)
  - **Shukra CoC 21/0** (ALEPH)
  - **Magistrate CoC 21/0** (Kestrel/Vanilla PanO).
- **Best CoC that also wins the mission:**
  - **Nexus 23/0** (Onyx/Combined)
  - **Farzan 24/0** (Haqq)
  - **Caliban 29/0** (Shasvastii)
  - **Kaeltar 21/0.5** (Tohaa); their actual gun, hacker/network, protection or specialist contribution reduces the *marginal* insurance tax.

### How ratings work

**Apex** rates the highest *fighting* or *command* ceiling in its respective table for a whole ~300-point force. **Fighting value** rates realized attack/defense and LT-specific points/SWC/Order return; **command value** rates extra *usable* Orders, deployment/control, specialist utility and safety *per cost* relative to legal alternatives after funding CoC, NCO and essential support. **S** exceptional in its stated condition, **A** excellent but less general, **B** solid/niche, **C** usually inferior unless a specific list needs it. **Conditional S ≠ automatic inclusion**. Compare **whole packages**, not head-to-head gun statistics.

**Role separation:** A **Fighting Lieutenant** is judged first by what the actual LT profile can kill, survive and do with its own or NCO-supported Orders; the fighting value table also rewards genuine LT price/SWC discounts and useful extra activations. A **Command Lieutenant** is judged first by Strategos, Inspiring Leadership, Counterintelligence, Command Tokens, succession/identity safety and how many *usable* activations its list gains. Strong hybrids may appear in both, but are ranked independently. **Basic line-trooper LTs, plain decoys and emergency low-cost choices without distinctive command utility are excluded from Command value.** No table assumes the LT, NCO or CoC variants are the same model or combines mutually exclusive options.

**Risk test:** estimate (A) probability the opponent removes/Isolates the LT, × (B) *number of useful pooled activations and scenario pushes you would lose* on the following turn, × (C) how safely a live CoC/Mnemonica host changes that outcome. This is a **decision aid, not a calibrated probability model**. When premium attacking LT cannot profitably use its extra Order or still needs a costly backup, a cheaper protected LT may outperform it.

**Chain of Command rankings:** [CoC apex, points-value and roster-specific successor tiers](chain-of-command-apex-and-value.md). The detailed successor table and additional options are maintained in that separate guide.

## Rules and command-package interactions

| Need or threat | Better architecture | The important why / failure condition |
|---|---|---|
| **At least 2–3 genuinely Irregular useful troopers** | **Inspiring Leadership LT** (Saladin/Wallace/Joan) | Army-wide Irregular-to-Regular conversion, Courage, Retreat prevention and LT-Order-only Coordinated Order. **Religious Troops excluded**; already-Regular models gain no additional regularization. |
| **Flexible orders on multiple guns in one group** | **Strategos L1/L2 LT** | Converts Special LT Orders into **Regular Orders in the LT's group**, not personal Tactical Orders. Two deployment reserves; L2 gives **one free Combat Group transfer including Peripherals** under N5.3. |
| **Safe LT(+1) and hard-working NCO** | **LT(+1 Order) + NCO** | Each Special LT Order becomes an **NCO-only Tactical Order**. NCO and LT may be in different groups; **NCO as Fireteam Leader** can activate its Fireteam with that Tactical Order. |
| **Strong gun must use its personal extra activations** | **Tactical Awareness** | Adds a personal Tactical Order, usable for a team **only if TA user is Team Leader**; a static TA model doesn't add generic transferable Orders. |
| **Premium LT plans to attack first turn** | **Active LT + separate protected CoC** | One personal attack activation is often worth risk, but CoC saves the **next turn's pool** if LT is Null/Isolated. Keep successors out of same line/template/hacking footprint. |
| **EI Aspect is LT with eligible Cube/REM allies** | **Mnemonica** | On entering **Null**, WIP/Mnemonica/LT and +LT modifiers jump to an eligible **on-table Cube/REM** at end of that Order. **Isolation is not Null**; cannot transfer. |
| **Many Morat soldiers have Warhorse** | **Sogarat or other active LT with reduced CoC tax** | Warhorse models remain Regular during LoL, immune to Isolation and BS Attack(-X); **Morat REMs / Gakis / Pretas do not inherit Warhorse**. If main gun relies on non-Warhorse pooled Orders, LoL still hurts. |
| **Hidden-looking backline command** | **Cheap LT + identical decoy + CoC if relevant** | Exploits Private Information and WIP identity ambiguity; even if LT Order unused, points buy a real specialist elsewhere. |
| **Need personal LT Order without breaking a Fireteam** | **NCO (as leader)** or **Strategos converted Regular** | An LT's personal Special Order generally **doesn't activate linked teammates and can cause it to leave the Fireteam**. |
| **High-end list loses its attack group early** | **Strategos L2** or spare Command Tokens | Transfer one surviving trooper and peripherals between groups while preserving Command Token for rebuilding/recovery; not unlimited moves. |
| **Aggressive attacker but objectives matter turn three** | **CoC that is also a real specialist** | CoC can score if LT survives; cheaper but useless backline backup can lose mission despite preventing LoL. |

### Rules audit traps

- **Loss of Lieutenant** is checked in the **Tactical Phase**. Normally affected models become Irregular until a new LT is chosen at end of turn; **Warhorse** remains Regular. CoC triggers during that check with the successor on-table and not Null/Isolated; previous LT doesn't regain command after successor promotion. **CoC (Non Specialist)** doesn't grant scenario specialist status.
- **Mnemonica** transfers at end of Order when Null is entered, potentially repeatedly through eligible Cube/REM hosts. **Isolated LT can't jump.** Thus it differs from CoC's Tactical Phase succession.
- **Inspiring Leadership** needs actual LT **on table and not Null**; excludes **Religious Troop** models. Saladin's Inspiring was explicitly added in Oct 2025 N5.2; the pinned [Haqq roster summary](../../sectorials/haqqislam/haqqislam.md) abbreviates his skill list to Counterintelligence, but the [N5 profile data](https://human-sphere.com/Saladin) and [October official Army change discussion](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/infinity-army-n5-updates-bugs.43295/page-10) confirm Inspiring Leadership. **Roster-summary omission flagged; do not silently erase his real ability.**
- **Strategos vs NCO** compete for the **same** LT resource: after Strategos converts to Regulars there is **no Special LT Order** for NCO. If the LT has +1 Order, both Special Orders get converted under Strategos.
- **NCO** can use a generated Special LT Order even if LT subsequently enters Null during the turn. An **Isolated NCO can't** convert/use it. Tactical Awareness Order is separate.
- **Warhorse** does **not** ignore Mimetism, range, Cover, templates, Immobilized, or hacking damage; only its specific LoL/Retreat/Isolation/BS Attack(-X) interactions. Morat `Religious Troop` is not a substitute for Warhorse.
- **SWC rebates** can improve command value when they unlock another useful expensive weapon. Check the exact Lieutenant option for:
  - **Joan**
  - **Aquila**
  - **Hsien**
  - **Hatamoto**
  - **Neema**

  The **+1 SWC option** and other LT options may be mutually exclusive.
- **Don't invent CoC** for these roster-specific profiles, despite some older upload claims:
  - **Quinn (Kestrel / Varuna)** — no CoC on pinned profiles
  - **Emily Handelman (Kestrel)** — no CoC on pinned profile
  - **Emily Handelman (White Company)** — **does** have CoC; it is a different roster.
- **No generic army-wide CoC**: the listed faction/model permission and specific Fireteam profile decide legality.

### Five questions before finalizing 300 points

1. If LT is Dead or Isolated before my Tactical Phase, **which actual troopers remain Regular** and how many pooled Orders are lost?
2. Is my LT Order **personal**, **NCO Tactical**, or **Strategos Regular**? Count each only once; does an NCO Fireteam move or Strategos group transfer solve the mission?
3. How many *genuinely* Irregular models does Inspiring Leadership convert? Are any excluded by Religious Troop?
4. Can CoC survive a separate assault/Template/repeater attack, and is its backup mission action worth its marginal price?
5. If the main LT gun and its support die turn one, is there still **another independent weapon and enough scoring specialists for turn three**?
