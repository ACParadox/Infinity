# Best Cheap Units in Infinity N5.3 — Cross-Faction Value

_Research: 2026-10-26. **Community-informed editorial tiers, not tournament win rates or simulated combat odds.** Current profiles, points, Order training, availability and legal Fireteams must come from the [pinned September 2026 sectorial reference files](../../sectorials) and [current rules/FAQ](../../rules/README.md), **not** older commentary. Videos and podcasts supply tactical arguments and counterarguments; they do not automatically endorse every ranking here._

**Scope:** individual `cheap` units mean **15 points or less, 0 SWC unless noted**; whole-team FT Master and Inspiring Leadership packages are explicitly priced exceptions. This is a cross-role shortlist of units that **outperform their cost in realistic lists**—not a claim that a 5-point warband replaces a 12-point Specialist. We separate **transferable Regular Orders**, **self-funded Irregular pressure**, **screening/defence**, and **scoring/support**. No TAG or premium-profile price comparisons disguised as a "cheap" category.

## How to choose between "cheap" troops

1. **First ask whether the unit adds a transferable Order.** A 6pt Irregular model that walks/throws Smoke for itself and a 7pt Regular Flashbot that funds a TAG are different resources. **Off-table deployment, G:Jumper bodies, PuppetBots, Peripherals, FT Master and Inspiring Leadership each have separate Order rules.**
2. **Then price its *second job*.** The 7pt Flashbot offers Repeater + Flash; the 8pt Baggage bot reloads; **K2 FTO 11–12pt buys NWI/Remote Presence plus E/Marat or an Order-consuming Mine Dispenser**; the 11pt Ikadron adds flamethrower corner denial; the 12pt Barid is a Specialist and Pitcher. Don't upgrade for a job the list won't actually use.
3. **Price delivery, not the attack headline.** A 5pt E/Marat only matters if it reaches the target; an 8pt Daylami marker can force caution from further away; an 8pt Diablo needs a safe corner/Smoke/team route.
4. **Protect the 15-unit cap and table geometry.** More 7pt REMs can mean more Orders but fewer independent attackers/specialists and more exposed S3/S4 footprints. Spend 2–7 extra points on a useful second role when it materially changes the game.
5. **Don't manufacture universal faction access.** The same named unit can differ in AVA, legal Fireteams, Order status, and priced profile between Vanilla and sectorials (especially Ghazi, Varangian, Jackal, Ikadron, Barid). Always check **its actual current roster**.
6. **Use mission and opponent to break ties.** Many buttons: preserve a Barid/Harbinger/Irmandinho. Enemy alpha/Impersonator: Kaauri/Warcor/Daylami/Jackal. Enemy MSV2 defence: Eclipse Makaul rather than default Smoke. TAG/HI: state threat or Panzerfaust, not simply more Chain Rifles.

  - **Ikadron**
  - **Puppet Master**

  - **Diablo**
  - **Irmandinho**

  Also high for **Tactical Awareness's Makaul-versus-Mirrorball disagreement**. Confidence is **moderate (cross-guide synthesis)** for universal cross-faction ordering and the many specialized 5–8pt defenders. Nothing here is a measured win-rate ranking.

**Final selection principle:** the strongest 5–15pt unit is the one that *buys a necessary Order, saves movement/setup Orders, protects a critical model, or threatens something far more expensive* **without making the 300pt list unable to score or execute its primary attack**.
