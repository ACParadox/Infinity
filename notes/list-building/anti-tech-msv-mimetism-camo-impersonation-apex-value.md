# Anti-Tech — MSV, Mimetism, Camouflage, Hidden Deployment and Impersonation (N5.3 Apex & Value)

*23 October 2026 · Qualitative tactical tiers, **not win rates**. Exact profiles, points, SWC, AVA and Fireteams: [pinned current September-2026 Army sectorial references](../../sectorials/), N5.3/FAQ v0.1 [rules](../../rules/README.md). Cross-faction examples are **not** a legal mixed army. Older media explains choices, never supersedes the current profile. This guide ranks **neutralizing the advantage** (safe crossing, revealed marker, usable attack angle, broken enemy attack plan) separately from **killing the bearer**.*

**Scope:** enemy MSV1/2/3, Marksmanship, Mimetism(-3/-6), ordinary Smoke/visibility, Camouflage markers, Hidden Deployment, Impersonation, Holoecho/Holomask, and tightly relevant detection/denial. Hacker **White Noise** is included because it is the central anti-MSV tool; this is **not** an anti-hacker/KHD or anti-TAG hacking tier. E/M on its own **does not switch off a visor** or strip Mimetism/Camo; target-specific states and attacks still matter.

Related retained work: [vision control](vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md), [rare Albedo/MSV combinations](rare-capabilities-and-unique-combinations-apex-value.md), [marker/deception](marker-state-and-deception-strategy.md), [DZ/IMP defense](dz-defenders-alpha-strike-and-impersonation-apex-value.md), [midfield screens](midfield-area-denial-and-speed-bumps-apex-value.md), [gunfighters](active-turn-gunfighters-apex-and-value.md), [ARO](aro-units-apex-and-value.md), [anti-armor](anti-tag-anti-heavy-infantry-non-hacking-apex-value.md).

## Rules that change the ranking (N5.3)

- **MSV1** ignores Mimetism(-3), reduces Mimetism(-6) to **-3**, and draws LoF through Zero Visibility at **-6**. **MSV2** negates Mimetism/visibility MODs and sees through ordinary Smoke without those MODs. **MSV3** adds automatic success for Discover against *Camo*, and may **shoot a Camo marker in LoF in the Active Turn** under its rule. None sees through Eclipse automatically. [Official MSV](https://infinitythewiki.com/Multispectral_Visor).
- **White Noise** denies LoF through the zone **to MSV and Marksmanship** users only; other shooters still see. Active-Trooper **HD+** program, WIP Normal roll, template wholly within Hacking Area, expires at start of States Phase; **NFB** matters to the casting hacker. [Official](https://infinitythewiki.com/White_Noise).
- **Albedo(-3/-6)** penalizes enemy **MSV/Marksmanship** LoF BS Attack/Discover against **the Albedo bearer**; it is neither Mimetism versus everybody nor team-wide Smoke. No benefit against CC attacks. [Official](https://infinitythewiki.com/Albedo).
- **Sensor** makes **one WIP+6 Normal roll** within ZoC, *without LoF* and without Range/Mimetism MODs, for all eligible **Camouflaged/Hidden** enemies; it also supplies +6 WIP on ordinary Discover versus Camo, and prevents recloaking in its ZoC. No analogous bulk IMP or Holo reveal. [Official](https://infinitythewiki.com/Sensor).
- **Biometric Visor** ignores the **IMP-1 Discover -3**, cancels Impersonation outright on a successful Discover against IMP-1, and ignores specified IMP/Holoecho Surprise Attack effects. It still needs a legal opportunity and success. [Official](https://infinitythewiki.com/Biometric_Visor).
- **Triangulated Fire** is a LoF-requiring **Long Skill** that ignores **attack roll** MODs except Burst-related MODs, including favorable range MODs; a legal gun and target are still needed. [Official](https://infinitythewiki.com/Triangulated_Fire).
- **Intuitive Attack** requires a weapon with that Trait, legal target/LoF conditions, **Long Skill, B1 and unmodified WIP**, with applicable defender reactions; do not automatically give it to every flamethrower or use it through arbitrary walls. [Official](https://infinitythewiki.com/Intuitive_Attack).
- **Do not equate Camo with Mimetism.** Sensor reveals markers; MSV counters visual modifiers *after the model is legally engageable*. **Mines can trigger from valid Camo/IMP Markers** subject to their rules, while Boost Koalas generally do not trigger on unrevealed markers. [Marker primer](marker-state-and-deception-strategy.md), [DZ primer](dz-defenders-alpha-strike-and-impersonation-apex-value.md).

## 3. Use the right counter, not just the highest tier

| If enemy relies on... | Prefer | Avoid / counter-view |
|---|---|---|
| Long-range **MSV2 sniper** covering the objective | **White Noise (with projection)** for movement, or **Eclipse/Disco** without hacking; **Albedo(-6)** if you need the **gun duel** | Paying for friendly MSV2 doesn't itself reduce enemy MSV advantage; own visor gun may be hurt by enemy White Noise |
| **MSV+Mimetism(-6)** (defensive stacked tech) | Eclipse/White Noise to deny LoF; **MSV2+Albedo** if you can obtain a favorable BS fight | Head-on BS11/no visor/no bonus is inefficient; a hard ARO may simply be avoided |
| A plain **Mimetism(-6) sniper**, no visor | **MSV2 gun** + favorable range/burst, or **Triangulated Fire** if a normal FtF is weak | Smoke alone may bypass lane but leaves actual gun alive; TF Long Skill often loses opportunity for Move+Attack |
| Many **Camo+Hidden** models behind terrain | **Advance mobile Sensor** within ZoC, sweep multiple in one WIP+6 activation; then have shooter/DTW ready | MSV3 cannot auto-Discover a model it has no LoF/awareness of; camping with sniper doesn't find Hidden |
| One CAMO marker at a corner | **Known legal Intuitive Attack** template or *one* cheap Discover/screen; do not necessarily reveal every Camo | Blowing multiple Regular Orders to Discover a 5pt decoy loses the exchange even if it gets shot |
| **Impersonator hunting LT** | **Kaauri/Bluecoat/Bào Biometric**, mines with legal triggers, **multiple spread defenders** and a covered retreat route | Sensor can reveal Camo/Hidden but **not IMP-1** by Sensor roll; Boost perimeter alone isn't equivalent to a mine |
| **Holoecho/HoloMask misdirection** | Correct declared status/public-info checks, multiple-arc screening and Biometric anti-Surprise where relevant | A Sensor activation is *not* universal identity scan. No blanket claim of targeting all echoes |
| Opponent tech roster unknown at list creation | One **cheap Sensor**, one independent **non-MSV gun**, and **Eclipse/White Noise only if you have a realistic route** | Overfitting three MSV or Albedo models into an opponent lacking them |
| Turn 3 scoring vs an unkillable visor ARO | **Disco/Eclipse/White Noise** if it allows the objective action now | Spend last Orders killing a gun you can already bypass |

## 4. Transcript checked — competing viewpoints, not one consensus

All the following media were **queried directly through the video/podcast MCP** for this pass, in addition to the project's existing research. Transcription errors ("Fenic", "mimmatism", and mistaken rules) are not unit/Army evidence.

1. **[The Dice Gods — Learn N5 ep5: Camo, Mimetism, MSV](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XS_NOPNdyIM)** — *YouTube transcript read*. Clear N5 order-of-operations primer: Mimetism affects both BS/Discover, while MSV tiers offer **different** cancellation. Good rules-first watch; **not** a present-day unit tier list.

**Where opinions really differ:** build several Albedo/visors to punish a predicted tech-heavy meta **vs** carry a 10–16pt adaptable tool and preserve points/Orders for universal scorers and guns. Neither is a universal tier winner. Re-evaluate after seeing opponent faction, table density, mission zones and whether the **counter can reach its effect range before Turn 3**.

## List audit in ten seconds

- **Anti-Camo:** Do I have **Sensor within realistic ZoC** or MSV3 with legal LoF; can another unit exploit the reveal?
- **Anti-IMP:** Is one **Biometric/mined guard** protecting the actual vulnerable LT, not just a random table edge?
- **Anti-Mimetism:** Can **MSV2 or a favorable attack angle** win a real fight, or does Triangulated Fire meaningfully help?
- **Anti-MSV/Marksmanship:** Do I have **White Noise projected into the needed area, Eclipse/Disco, or a plausible Albedo duel**? Count setup Orders and ammo.
- **Redundancy:** Can an **ordinary non-MSV gun** still defend against enemy White Noise/Albedo, and can I still score the mission?
