## Scope — what belongs here

Rank **unusual support packages that improve a chosen allied unit or provide valuable army-wide protection**. Reward a strong *incremental* effect on a model you already wanted to field, minimal SWC/Order tax, and a useful independent job for the support piece.

- **In scope:** RemDriver; EVO on a **nonstandard carrier with additional impactful abilities**; inexpensive, protected or multifunctional Counterintelligence. One unified ranking balances **support ceiling**, **incremental value** and independent utility.
- **Out of scope:** all **Inspiring Leadership** and **FT Master** units (ranked in the [Order economy guide](cheerleaders-ranking.md) and command/Fireteam studies); **ordinary 8pt Baggage bots, 14/.5 EVO Baggage bots, conventional reload carriers** (see [REMs](rems.md) and [Order economy](cheerleaders-ranking.md)). Do not fill a specialty ranking with the same generic bot under six faction names.
- **Also not re-ranked here:** ordinary TAGCom pilots, routine TinBot Firewall carriers (see [hackers](hackers-ranking.md) and [killer hackers](killer-hackers-ranking.md) and [network](network-projection-ranking.md)), Decoy alone, and Cyberplug/other unusual peripherals (see [rare abilities](rare-capabilities-and-unique-combinations-ranking.md) and [multi-body tactics](peripheral-synchronized-multibody-apex-value.md)). Their tactical value is real but belongs to another comparison.
- **Strategic Deployment:** only a cheap, conditional option remains at the **bottom** of the ranking; it has no separate section.

## Rules and eligibility that change the ranking

1. **RemDriver** is a Deployment Skill: place one token on **one already-deployed friendly REM**, which receives the **MODs in parentheses on that particular RemDriver profile**. There is no upkeep Order or stated range limit. The token is removed **at the end of the Order** in which the driver becomes Null. A REM cannot carry two RemDriver tokens; **TAGs and VHs are not eligible REMs**. [Local rule](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Remdriver.md).
2. **Apsaras has distinct profiles.** In **OSS** the RemDriver **20/0** has KHD with Firewall(-3), Deployable Repeater and printed RemDriver source attributes **CC=15, BS=13, WIP=14**; the TAGCom **18/0** is a separate profile. **Vanilla ALEPH RemDriver is 20/1**, not the OSS SWC cost. [OSS roster](../../sectorials/aleph/operations-subsection.md) · [ALEPH roster](../../sectorials/aleph/aleph.md).
3. **Agnes16/0** has **RemDriver (+1 BS, +5 CC)**, Sixth Sense and Specialist Operative. Her **13/0** non-RemDriver and **18/0** CoC are different choices. Only use her in [WinterFor](../../sectorials/panoceania/svalarheima-s-winter-force.md) or [Acontecimento](../../sectorials/panoceania/shock-army-of-acontecimento.md) when roster-legal.
4. **Pilot-X is not a universal profile.** PanO/WinterFor has **21/1, WIP13, Zero Pain, Immunity (Shock), RemDriver (BS=13, PH=13, BTS=6)** (fixed values, no CC modifier; corrected from user-provided Army screenshot on 2026-11-24); ALEPH/OSS has **23/.5, WIP14** and its own printed RemDriver source. A Pilot-X may buy **0–2 Mk-III RacerBots8/0 each**, all in the same Combat Group; each adds a **Regular troop** and mobile tools, not a free peripheral. Check the exact beneficiary and cost before buying the package. [PanO](../../sectorials/panoceania/panoceania.md) · [WinterFor](../../sectorials/panoceania/svalarheima-s-winter-force.md) · [OSS](../../sectorials/aleph/operations-subsection.md).
5. **EVO** offers **Assisted Fire, Enhanced Reaction, Fairy Dust and Controlled Jump**. A target can benefit from only **one Supportware at a time**, and each EVO Hacker sustains one. EVO **does not inherently grant Oblivion, Spotlight or Trinity**. **Scylla is exceptional because her profile explicitly adds Trinity (SR-2)**, Pitcher and Charybdis; do not generalize that upgrade to standard EVOs. [Device rules](../../rules/5c_Equipment/Hacking_Device.md) · [Steel Phalanx](../../sectorials/aleph/steel-phalanx.md) · [Acontecimento](../../sectorials/panoceania/shock-army-of-acontecimento.md).
6. **Counterintelligence** can reduce a first-turn enemy **two-Order** Command Token strip to **one**, or relax the relevant first-turn **one-Command-Token** limitation to **two**. It is neither a generated Regular Order nor an LT-succession rule. Safe survival, other useful equipment and actual likelihood of first-turn disruption determine value. [Rule](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Counterintelligence.md).
7. **Strategic Deployment** grants a legal Fireteam FD(+4″) only with the qualifying bearer deployed **first as Team Leader**. A teammate's personal FD(+4″) does not create +8″; only the specified scenario/tournament bonus can do that. The whole team must still have **safe places to deploy**. [Rule](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Strategic_Deployment.md).

## Unified force multiplier ranking

**One unified competitive ranking** for a typical 300-point game. Balance incremental support impact, points/SWC and dependent-model cost, reliability, independent utility and role compression. Tiers are editorial list-building judgments: S+ = exceptional/faction-defining, S = outstanding, A+ = excellent, A = strong, B = situational, C = generally outclassed, D = skip. Rank descending within tiers. One entry per profile, **no Lieutenants**. The ALEPH/OSS Pilot-X ranks above Apsaras for its broader package; Apsaras and Agnes remain outstanding pure REM-buff purchases. The more SWC-intensive PanO/WinterFor Pilot-X falls below the most economical support packages in the unified ordering. These different-faction entries cannot be combined into one legal list.

**Tags (non-exclusive):** `Apex` = exceptional raw role performance; `Value` = exceptional points/SWC efficiency; `Unique` = rare meaningful capability; `Toolbox` = genuine role compression; `Classified Runner` = particularly useful actual Classified coverage; `Airborne` = legal airborne delivery; `Fireteam` = significant legal team benefit or dependency; `Budget` = low-cost option (20 points or fewer for this guide). Tags do not create separate rankings. Apply them only to the selected profile; optional companions are not free and their cost must be included. No existing entry has a sufficiently established Airborne or Classified Runner role to warrant those tags. `Anti-Tech` = meaningful direct counter to opposing visual/marker technology; `Mod-Stacking` = notable BS-roll asymmetry from combined modifiers, not simply better base BS or a cyber/CC modifier.

**Cross-role audit (26 November 2026):** [Anti-Tech](https://github.com/ACParadox/Infinity/blob/bfd46de8b157a80a70cbfb6db1620143cfb32401/notes/list-building/anti-tech-msv-mimetism-camo-impersonation-apex-value.md) · [Rare Capabilities](rare-capabilities-and-unique-combinations-ranking.md) · [Toolbox Units](toolbox-units-rankings.md) · [BS MOD mechanics](active-turn-gunfighters-ranking.md#bs-mod-mechanics-and-counterplay). Rare capabilities and role compression use the existing `Unique` and `Toolbox` tags. A rare optional package qualifies only when purchased at its full cost. The Anti-Tech guide explicitly discounts Biometric/Discover alone; Shukra's tag denotes narrow anti-IMP/Holoecho utility, not a premium counter-tech ranking. The Taagma 15/0 has no MSV2 (the separate 22/1.5 sniper does). Scylla can give an eligible REM Assisted Fire (Marksmanship), but does not gain it herself, and her standard-HD/FTO toolbox entries are not the EVO profile. RemDriver modifies the recipient, not the driver's own BS rolls; ECM: Hacker, Firewall and Trinity modifiers are not BS Mod-Stacking.

## Matchup checklist

1. **Identify the benefiting model first:** Which deployed REM gets RemDriver? Which real combat REM / jumper merits EVO Supportware? Which opponent actually strips Orders?
2. **Calculate the cost of the full package**, including dependent RacerBots/Charybdis, the carrier's SWC, support Orders and deployment space. A **23/.5 Pilot-X** becomes **31/.5** with one 8pt RacerBot; **21/1** becomes **29/1** in PanO.
3. **Compare with the cheaper alternate profile:** OSS Apsaras17 vs 20; Agnes13 vs 16; Scylla normal HD32/.5 vs EVO32/.5. A free-feeling passive buff is not free when an alternate unit would do more.
4. **Check failure modes:** RemDriver carrier going Null; EVO being Isolated/Null; target using another Supportware; Counterintelligence with no enemy Command Token disruption.
5. **Do not duplicate other rankings:** Inspiring Leadership and FT Master belong to the [Order guide](cheerleaders-ranking.md); ordinary Baggage/EVO bots belong to [REMs](rems.md). This specialty list is intentionally shorter and more selective.
