# Peripherals, Synchronized & Multi-Body Packages — N5.3 Apex / Points Value / Matchups

**O-12 peripheral value check — [Robert Shepherd's O-12 focus (35–36m, 68–71m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Nl8BMJ8E5U&t=2100s), reviewed 26 Oct 2026:** **Zeta Lt70/2** includes **deployable ZetBot**: a small WIP12 Specialist can be dropped on the TAG's travel route and later reach a console, meaning the 20pt Zeta-vs-Gamma difference is not just armor/gun. As with any **Ancillary**, it is not an independent predeployment Regular Order. **Team Sirius FO21 + synchronized SiriusBot** offers an inexpensive *marginal peripheral body* with **Repeater, Mimetism and Heavy Riotstopper**: dangerous on a defended corner without losing an additional pool Order when destroyed, but the actual *buy-in* is **21pt controller+bot**, not 5pt stand-alone. The presenter is **skeptical of Sirius Hacker24/.5** because a KHD can kill the BTS0 controller and undermine the pair; the FO is his preferred useful variant. **Counterpoint:** older Tactical Awareness discussions are more enthusiastic about Sirius as a core two-body projection tool. Keep those opinions separate.

_Research: 2026-10-23 · **qualitative** whole-game tiers, **not simulated win rates**. Current September 2026 N5.3 [Army roster references](../../sectorials/) and [N5.3 Peripheral FAQ](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Peripheral_N5.3_Update.md) control profiles and legality. Video/podcast transcripts are **opinions**, often from older N5 versions; a 2024 podcast is not a 2026 points source. These are cross-army comparisons, **not** a legal mixed-faction list._

**Question:** which purchase creates the most **useful separate threats, ARO forks and physical defense per Order**, without misleadingly treating four bodies as four Independent Regular Orders? **Short answer:** **Chimera+3 Pupniks19/0** for cheap assault/vision control; **Antipode Handler+3 Antipodes26/0** for scouting/CC and **Regular+Tactical Awareness**; **Puppet Master11/0+3 PuppetBots27–48 additional** for rotating expendable active Spearheads and defensive pickets; **Drummer FO+FO Drumbot-5 21/0** for *two genuine synchronized FO origins*; **Gearhead13/0+one/two Puzzler12–13 each** for *Autonomous* remote defense; **ALEPH Posthumans (2–3 Proxies)** for unequalled alternate locations/roles on one shared Order. None wins every metric.

| What you need | Default pick | Why / where it beats alternatives | Main drawback |

| **Best fast Camo/CC reconnaissance per available Order** | **Antipode Handler + 3 Antipodes 26/0** | Sensor/Camo, Super-Jump and a printed Tactical Awareness activation; strong against midfield markers and elite models | One attacking Spearhead; clustered pack suffers templates |
| **Best genuine synchronized specialist fork** | **Drummer FO + FO Drumbot-5 21/0** | Two legal FO origins and a heavy template threat for one activation; excellent when two corners/objectives are close | Fragile W1/ARM1 parent; Coherency; not two Regular Orders |
| **Most adaptable remote pickets** | **Puppet Master + 2 FO PuppetBots 29/0** | Two expendable remote defense sites while Counterintelligence parent hides, with replaceable Spearhead | No simultaneous attacking Puppets; Controller assassination disconnects both |
| **Best independence even after Controller loss** | **Gearhead + 2 Puzzlers 37/0** | A Cyberplug can use pooled Orders or ARO independently in Autonomous mode; key for separated ambushes | No extra Orders generated; Autonomous profile loses stats |
| **Best pre-positioned role switching** | **Posthumans Mk.1 Engineer + Mk.5 FO 26/.5** | Spend a shared Order from whichever already-deployed Proxy fits the job; excellent split scoring | Not simultaneous attackers; only one unrestricted reacting Proxy per opposing Order |
| **Best durable close-range synchronized holder** | **Raptor HSG + Devabot 32/0 / Rasail BSG + Chaksa 27/0** | Parent survives longer than a W1 specialist while second body denies corners with a template | Higher cost and, in Raptor's case, hacking exposure |

**N5.3 Cyberplug reactive exception:** if the Gearhead **takes its own legal ARO**, both attached Cyberplugs may instead use **Autonomous** profiles and independently declare their own legal AROs. Choosing a **Connected** Peripheral instead makes the Controller Idle, while that bot reacts using its Connected profile. Thus *up to three distinct responding bodies* can exist in one enemy Order, unlike Control Spearheads or G:Jumper; this does **not** mean three generated Regular Orders. [Official Peripheral rule](https://infinitythewiki.com/Peripheral).

## Quick comparison: choose the architecture FIRST

| Architecture | Activation and fork | Real restriction |
|---|---|---|
| **Peripheral (Synchronized)** e.g. Auxbot, Drumbot, SiriusBot, Devabot, Fiddler JackBots, attached Coyote BambaBot, Tohaa Rasail/Gorgos | Controller and Synchronized bodies **activate together, declaring the same Skill**, may choose **different targets**. One defending Trooper reacting must select **one active body** as its ARO target, creating a real active-turn split-angle/template fork. Their individual reactive AROs use the same declared Skill unless exception. | **Coherency to Controller** checked start/end, Null/Isolated controller disconnects. Vulnerable to templates and pinning Controller. No new pooled Regular Order. |
| **Peripheral (Control)** e.g. PuppetBots, Antipodes, Chimera Pupniks | **Controller + up to 3 Control Peripherals**: declare common Skills; designate **one Spearhead** each Order. **When an attack Skill requires a target, only Controller and Spearhead use it**; others Idle for that portion, though all can often Move. Rotate Spearhead after a loss; useful multiple reserve bodies. | Control Unit maintains **Coherency to Spearhead**, not Controller; **no operating-distance limit Controller→Control Unit**. NOT three simultaneous attacking Pupniks or three full-Burst PuppetBots. Controller Null/Isolated disconnects by default. |
| **Peripheral (Cyberplug), N5.3** e.g. Next Wave Gearhead + Sartroid Puzzler/Ranter | **At start of each Order/ARO choose Connected or Autonomous**. Connected activates with Controller, Controller Idles (Doctor/Engineer exception). **Autonomous receives Orders from Order Pool and AROs independently**. With two, **max 1 Connected** at once. | Max **two per Controller**; each Cyberplug **generates no extra Order**, costs 12–19 points, but *can spend a pooled Order separately*. No operating-distance limit. Controller loss makes them Autonomous, **not inert**. Crucial N5.3 distinction. |
| **G:Jumper** ALEPH Posthumans | Deploy 2–3 different Proxies; spend **one Trooper's Orders from one chosen Proxy** at a time. Different prepositioned jobs can replace each other without travel Orders. Only one reacting Proxy per enemy Order may take **unrestricted ARO**; others restricted Dodge/Reset. | Not Synchronized / **no simultaneous active attack fork**. One Regular Order, one Combat Group slot; each Proxy's **individual points count** toward VP. Losing one Proxy often retains Order but loses its body/job. |
| **Peripheral (Servant)** e.g. Yudbot, Yáozăo, Zondbot, Nasmat, Chaksa | Doctor/Engineer stays behind while 3pt **remote contact body** performs repair/heal at another position using Controller's stat/Skill; up to **two Servants**. | **One patient per Order**, can activate only **one Servant at once**; no independent regular order or extra specialist. No max operating-distance for Servants. |
| **Peripheral (Ancillary)** e.g. TAG Crabbot/Robbybot/Jandab, Sphinx Scindron, O-Yoroi Kobaruto | **Starts off table**; Controller must spend **Place Deployable** to create position later. Can become objective/control/recovery tool. | Before deployment, Controller may Fireteam/Coordinate if otherwise eligible; after deploying active Ancillary, leaves Fireteam. **Undeployed Ancillary does not count as Killed** (FAQ); no free starting presence. |
| **Composite but NOT a Peripheral** e.g. **Polaris Team (Masha+Bearpode)**, Coyote+**separately purchasable** BambaDroid | Under their printed army relations these are **independent troops**, and some generate **multiple Orders**; not the same as a synchronized bot. | **Polaris Alpha32/0 = two Regular Orders**, Beta40/0 similarly, not one Controller Order. **Coyote FO+attached BambaBot27/0** (one order) vs **Coyote27+independent BambaDroid FO13=40/0** (genuine second troop) must never be conflated. |
| **Not physical Peripherals** e.g. CrazyKoala, mines, holoechoes, decoys | Deployable/marker threats can complicate AROs without extra independent bodies. | No additional Trooper/Regular Order; do not rank them as a live second attacker or assume decoy can generate a model ARO. |

| **Early disposable raid vs late zone score** | **Chimera/Pupnik/Antipodes** early; **Posthuman Proxies/Puppets/Betatrooper etc** late | A dead disposable can trade favorably early but may lose zone VP later; preserve parent to defend score | In zone missions per-body point contribution matters; count PuppetBots individually, not parent total in every zone |

## 5. 30-second list check

1. **Which subtype, exactly?** Synchronised = all legal bodies make fork; **Control = ONE Spearhead attacks**; **Cyberplug Autonomous = independent ARO/pool activation**; **G:Jumper = alternative locations, one active Proxy**; Servant = remote Doctor/Engineer; Ancillary = not on board until deployed.
2. **Count cost and Orders honestly:** 19/0 Chimera includes 3 pups; Puppet Master + bodies costs **20/29/38+**, NOT 9pt alone; Pilot-X must be bought before RacerBot Mk-III; no free extra Regular Orders unless explicitly listed (e.g. **Polaris32 gives two**).
3. **Can two bodies hit two distinct threats under the SAME declared Skill?** If yes, synchronized **Drummer FO/FO, Raptor HFT or Fiddler JackBots** are threatening; if one body must Idle because another fires as Spearhead, don't promise multiple shots.
4. **Protect parent / Spearhead Coherency:** Keep Controller in Total Cover, spread bodies enough to avoid one template, but obey Coherency **to Controller (Synchronized)** or **to Spearhead (Control)**. Cyberplug has no range limit.
5. **What is the opponent's shortest answer?** Shot on one W1 puppet-master/Drummer/Sirius, Mine/DTW spanning both bodies, enemy Hackers vs REM; if a component becomes Null, count loss of **zone VP, role, Orders** separately.
6. **Turn-3 scoring:** Peripherals and Proxies have **per-body VP** according to unit costs/rules; an undeployed Ancillary is not a killed bot, and a box holder must actually be legal Specialist. **Never credit all package cost to every scoring base**.
