# Killer Hackers — Trinity-capable profiles (N5.3)

Rank KHDs and other profiles with explicit Trinity for anti-Hacker combat, network defense, specialist work and useful secondary capabilities. Full HD/HD+ with Trinity and dual devices qualify on their printed programs. A plain HD without Trinity does not qualify. See [Hackers and shared hacking rules](hackers-ranking.md#what-the-current-rules-actually-do).

**One unified competitive ranking** for a typical 300-point game. Guiding weights: primary role 30%, points/SWC 25%, versatility and role compression 20%, delivery/order efficiency 10%, survivability/reliability 10%, unique capabilities 5%. These are editorial priorities, not computed scores. S+ = exceptional/faction-defining, S = outstanding, A+ = excellent, A = strong, B = situational, C = generally outclassed, D = skip. Rank descending within tiers. Full anti-heavy + Trinity access receives a substantial Toolbox bonus in both rankings; limited Spotlight/White Noise upgrades receive a smaller bonus and cannot substitute for Carbonite/Oblivion. Count only programs and equipment on the selected legal profile; alternatives never pool their abilities.

**Tags (non-exclusive):** `Apex` = exceptional raw role performance; `Value` = exceptional cost/SWC efficiency; `Unique` = rare meaningful capability; `Toolbox` = genuine role compression; `Classified Runner` = particularly useful actual Classified coverage; `Airborne` = legal airborne delivery; `Fireteam` = significant legal team benefit or dependency; `Budget` = low-cost option. `Anti-Tech` = meaningful counter to opposing visual/marker technology. Tags do not create separate rankings.

Cross-role audit (26 November 2026): [Anti-Tech](https://github.com/ACParadox/Infinity/blob/bfd46de8b157a80a70cbfb6db1620143cfb32401/notes/list-building/anti-tech-msv-mimetism-camo-impersonation-apex-value.md), [Rare Capabilities](rare-capabilities-and-unique-combinations-apex-value.md), and [Toolbox Units](jack-of-all-trades-toolbox-units-apex-value.md). `Anti-Tech` means a meaningful answer to MSV/Marksmanship, Mimetism, Camouflage/Hidden Deployment or Impersonation (e.g. White Noise, Eclipse/Disco, actual MSV, Sensor/Biometric or Albedo), not simply having a hacking device or a visual defensive MOD. `Unique` represents rare capabilities and `Toolbox` represents role compression; these are the existing tags, not additional duplicate labels. Credit only the selected profile's equipment; a non-hacker alternate is not a hacking candidate.

## Purchasing and profile caveats


**KHD purchasing threshold:** the best KHD may be a **20–26pt objective runner**, rather than the best raw cyber duelist. **Tuareg/Trinitarian/Ninja-class** models pay for midfield position; **Santiago** pays for a two-Wound Fireteam/TinBot package; **Deva/Apsaras/Barid/Cyberghost** concentrate the anti-Hacker role at lower cost. Shakti's enhanced Trinity is **still KHD-only**, not a Carbonite/Oblivion hybrid.

**Lower-return KHD profiles (not ranked):** **Corregidor Bandit KHD26/0** is an Irregular WIP12 infiltrator whose ambush/scoring value generally exceeds its sustained cyber effectiveness; **Ryūken Unit-9 KHD25/0.5** is a weak dedicated counterhacker investment compared with 0-SWC KHD alternatives. These evaluations are specific to their hacking role, not their broader utility.
