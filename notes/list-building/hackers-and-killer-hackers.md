# Hackers, Killer Hackers and hybrid programs — N5.3 cross-faction tier guide

## The 30-second decision

1. **Want to stop ordinary HI/TAG/REM/VH?** Start with **HD/HD+** for **Oblivion, Carbonite**, and possibly **Total Control vs TAGs**. The right choice depends on **Repeater reach**, not just WIP.
2. **Want to kill hostile Hackers, enable Impersonation-2 movement or score cheaply?** Start with **KHD** for **Trinity/Cybermask**. Plain KHD usually **cannot hack normal non-Hacker HI/TAG/REM/VH**.
3. **Want both roles with one slot?** Prioritize **HD(+)+Trinity** or **KHD+Carbonite/Oblivion/Spotlight/White Noise**. The program **actually printed on the profile** matters more than the device name. A 22pt hybrid may be a better 300-point choice than two 20pt specialists.
4. **Want to remove MSV AROs without killing them?** **HD+ White Noise** through a forward Repeater. If those visors are absent, White Noise adds little; a cheap standard HD often suffices.
5. **Want objective utility at low cost?** A **Camouflaged/Hidden/Infiltrating KHD** is often a better specialist than an expensive conventional HD, especially against nonhackable opponents.
6. **Want an actual infowar win condition?** Pair **high-WIP reliable Hackers + forward/Pitcher network + one hostile-hacker answer + backup scorer**, with an Order budget. A Hacker with no network often becomes an overpriced button-pusher.

## What the current rules actually do

| Device | Native programs | Primary role | Failure case |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HD** | Carbonite, Spotlight, Oblivion, Total Control | Stop Hackable heavies; Targeted/Guided setup; possess vulnerable TAGs | Exposed to hostile Trinity, lacks White Noise/Cybermask natively |
| **HD+** | HD suite **+ White Noise + Cybermask** | Network attack **and** anti-MSV vision control; safer specialist | Costs more; **still not a KHD** unless Trinity is explicitly upgraded |
| **KHD** | Trinity, Cybermask | Kill Hackers, protect your own network, mobile button pushing | No general-purpose anti-TAG/Hl programs unless upgraded |
| **HD + Trinity** | HD suite + explicit Trinity | Best role-compression: Carbonite/Oblivion against heavies and lethal anti-Hacker | Sometimes expensive, conspicuous or lacks a good network |
| **KHD + Carbonite/Spotlight/Oblivion** | KHD suite + **only the printed upgrade(s)** | Cheaper dual-purpose hunter or scorer; anti-HI/TAG if it has the right addition | **Do not assume it has the whole HD suite**; Spotlight alone is not Oblivion |
| **KHD + White Noise** | KHD suite + White Noise | Anti-Hacker and anti-MSV tool at **0 SWC** in an attack link | No automatic Carbonite/Oblivion; if no opposing MSV, White Noise is low value |
| **EVO** | Assisted Fire, Enhanced Reaction, Fairy Dust, Controlled Jump | REM combat buffs, HI/REM/TAG/VH firewall coverage, Combat Jump support | Not a substitute for HD/KHD; one lonely REM rarely supports the tax |

**Critical September-2026 N5.3 points:**

- **Spotlight** is **B2, PS5, AP, nonlethal**, giving Targeted on a failed Saving Roll; it is **not an automatic Targeted effect**.
- **Trinity** is **B3, WIP +3, PS6** versus an enemy **Hacker** and can wound; in reactive use follow the program/Burst rules and any **+SD** printed on a troop.

**Firewall** imposes the stated negative **WIP** MOD on the Comms attacker and grants the defended Trooper **+3 on Comms Saving Rolls**; its saving bonus is **+3 even for Firewall(-6)**. Device-based Firewall stops applying if the device is disabled as specified in the Sep 2026 FAQ. A **TinBot**'s Fireteam benefits depend on precise current rules and the roster's legal team, not being nearby. Source: [N5.3 official Hacking Device](https://infinitythewiki.com/Hacking_Device), [Firewall](https://infinitythewiki.com/Firewall), [program chart](https://infinitythewiki.com/Hacking_Programs_Chart), [Spotlight](https://infinitythewiki.com/Spotlight), plus repository `rules/`. Do not repeat historical “Firewall reduces damage by -3” or old “Spotlight requires no save” summaries.


**2026-11-06 reranking:** Apex emphasizes program scope, resilience, WIP and meaningful forward positioning; value emphasizes points/SWC, actually accessible Repeater/Pitcher support, and a second combat/scoring/Fireteam role. Low-return dedicated hackers are summarized in one paragraph after all tables.

**Ranking key:** **S:** game-defining ceiling / exceptional return per investment. **A:** powerful or efficient with normal support. **B:** solid situational role. **C:** niche or unfavorable opportunity cost. **Apex** rewards WIP, program breadth, durability, anti-hacker ability, deployment and utility. **Value** rewards **points + SWC + Order/network/Fireteam/support tax**. A nonhackable opponent can reorder the entire table. The ranking is **cross-game**, but “Value” assumes the unit can be legally fielded in its stated sectorial; unlisted options are not implied.

## 1. Hackers — standard HD / HD+

**Job:** Immobilize/Isolate enemy HI, TAG, REM or VH with **Carbonite/Oblivion**, provide **Spotlight** and (for HD+) **White Noise**. These rows evaluate the **Hacker profile itself**, not an alternate KHD loadout. **HD+ does not grant Trinity** unless the profile explicitly says so; those upgrades are ranked in Section 3. Some hybrid standouts are cross-listed here if their native HD suite is already exceptional.

### Strongest Hacker profiles — apex

**Hacker-first Apex assessment (November 2026):** Ranking priorities are **WIP and effective program coverage/upgrades** (including Trinity or Zero Pain against hostile Hackers), **network access and delivery reliability**, **BTS/Firewall/reactive protection**, then fighting power only as a bonus. A 56pt Swiss Guard/Dàofěi can be a strong assault/scoring unit without being a top pure Hacker. A genuine dual-device or HD+Trinity profile can rank here **and** in Hybrid if its HD performance warrants it. ## 2. Killer Hackers — KHD

**KHD-only evaluation:** Apex rewards high-WIP and upgraded Trinity, Firewall/BTS defense and cyberduel reach **before** guns/CC; Value rewards the same programs, projector access and redundancy per point/SWC. Hybrids with a *printed KHD* can also appear here; a plain HD with Trinity remains an HD/Hybrid, not an automatic KHD.

**Job:** Fight opposing **Hackers** with **Trinity**, use **Cybermask** to stay alive/reach objectives, and provide a cheap specialist or anti-hacking protector. **Basic KHD cannot Oblivion/Carbonite a normal non-Hacker TAG/HI**. Listbuilding value changes sharply if there are no enemy hackers; forward deployment and scoring then matter more. KHD + non-KHD program upgrades appear in Section 3, with only exceptionally good standalone KHDs cross-listed here.

## 3. Hybrid Hackers — HD(+)+Trinity / dual HD+KHD / KHD+HD programs

**Strict two-target hybrids:** A standard **HD/HD+ with a printed Trinity upgrade** can both **Trinity enemy Hackers** and **Carbonite/Oblivion hackable non-Hackers**. Likewise a **KHD with printed Oblivion or Carbonite** can do both; a genuine dual-device trooper carries both toolkits. These are the crucial **one-profile replacement for separate HD + KHD**. **Limited-program hybrids** (KHD+Spotlight or KHD+White Noise) gain flexibility but **do not acquire full anti-heavy Carbonite/Oblivion**. Their row explicitly distinguishes this narrower upgrade. A strong HD+Trinity may also appear in Section 1 **if judged as an HD alone**; similarly for standalone-quality KHDs in Section 2.

| Printed hybrid program access | What actually changes |
|---|---|
| **HD / HD+ + Trinity** | HD's Carbonite/Oblivion/Spotlight + Trinity versus Hackers; the printed **+1SD, +1B or other Trinity modifier** is specific to that profile. |
| **Full HD and KHD programs on one trooper** | Two native program packages on the **same profile** (e.g. Mary); do not mistake separately purchasable alternatives for one dual-device model. |
| **KHD + Oblivion or Carbonite** | Basic Trinity/Cybermask **plus only the specified anti-heavy upgrade**; not the entire HD suite. |
| **KHD + Spotlight / White Noise** | Powerful target-marking or vision utility, **not** general Isolated/IMM anti-TAG attacks. Included below as **limited-program hybrids**, distinct from the strict dual-target group. |

- **Absolute single-model cyber duel:** high-WIP options with exceptional ceilings when their upgraded **Trinity and defensive effects** apply:

  - **Asura**
  - **De Fersen**

  **Interventors** are a much cheaper network engine, not an equivalent frontal-dueling profile.
- **Best routine hacking investments** (confirm the exact legal roster profile):
  - **Interventor**
  - **Danavas**
  - **Cyberghost HD+**
  - **Jazz**
  - **Barid**
  - **Leila**
  - **Apsaras**
  - **Dokkaebi**
  - **Waverider**
  - **Deva KHD20pt**

  Build actual network coverage around the selected model. A 60–70pt apex Hacker is not the default optimal inclusion.
- **Against dense enemy Hacker opposition:** favor **KHD with Cybermask + good Firewall/+SD/BTS or a safe Repeater route** and a second hacker for redundancy. Consider **KHD + Carbonite/Oblivion** so those points still affect hostile TAG/HI.
- **Against few/no hackers:** plain KHD is mostly a **specialist/stealth** purchase. **HD Spotlight** remains relevant to non-Hackable enemies but uses the N5.3 **AP PS5 saving roll**; if available, cheap FO or pure specialist can be better.
- **Against multiple TAGs/HI/VH:** an **HD/HD+ with Oblivion/Carbonite**, good projected Repeater, and a KHD protecting its home is more valuable than three plain KHDs.
- **If opponents deploy behind MSV sniper/ML:** **HD+ White Noise** has a value independent of hacking attacks; a nominally mediocre WIP HD+ can solve the critical vision problem.
- **If long Pitchers are unreliable:** move cheap **Forward Deployment/Infiltration native Repeater or Minelayer/Deployable Repeater** first. **BS +1SD + X-Visor** on a *Pitcher carrier* is sometimes a bigger upgrade than +1 WIP on the hacker. Baggage reloads valuable disposable ammo.
- **If your main attackers are hackable HI/TAG/VH:** protect their network by **TinBot, correct Firewall rules, KHD/Trinity, engineer access and Repeater clearance**, not only an expensive EVO.
- **If you need multiple objectives in different places:** consider **two cheap specialists** rather than one 65pt hacker, even if one-model apex rating is higher.

## Tactical use: the sequence that matters

**Turn 0:** identify friendly Repeaters in the actual deployment area, Minelayer/forward Repeater locations, and the safe Hacker/KHD hiding positions. Do *not* assume an unseen enemy Repeater is a free friendly network node; hacking through it exposes you and invokes its relevant Firewall. Prepare a **first-loss fallback**.

**Turn 1:** project an accurate Pitcher / mobile Repeater; clear hostile killer hackers via **Trinity** if safe; **White Noise** the blocking MSV or **Carbonite/Oblivion** the exposed hackable target. Check **which enemy Reset, attack and hacking AROs** are available before committing to an Order. Calculate whether 2–3 Orders for setup/Spotlight are actually more effective than shooting directly.

**Turn 2:** expand/rotate network only into sectors needed for mission denial, then complete specialist or anti-heavy objectives. Keep an actual **KHD counterattack line**, not just one solo protected Hacker holding the whole network.

**Turn 3:** stop over-hacking when the win condition is pushing a button; **Cybermask** can preserve mobile KHD specialists, but it requires legal active use. In general a KHD is not an objective-game tax merely because the opponent has no heavy Hackable unit: it can still be a scoring specialist.

**+B versus +SD:** +Burst is most valuable **in active attacks** (stronger multiple attack dice). **+SD** adds reliability including the **reactive turn**; high-WIP +SD Trinity/Zero Pain can guard an existing network better than a model whose added Burst is spent only actively. Printed **SR-1 / PS / opponent MOD** upgrades are different effects: do not silently equate these with +B or +SD.

**Cyberplugs / Peripherals:** newer **Cyberplug** architectures exist in multiple current sectorial rosters; check each parent-and-child entry rather than counting a remote companion as another ordinary Hacker or Regular Order. Other packages with distinct Order/Peripheral arrangements include:

- **Jazz + Billie**
- **Zoe + Π-Well**
- **Bit + KISS**
- **Scylla + Devabot**
- **Coyote + BambaBot**

Do not extrapolate one package's child behavior to another. For full mechanics see [Peripheral and multi-body](../Strategy/peripherals-synchronized-and-multi-body-troops.md).

**EVO qualification:** 14pt/0.5-SWC EVO REM often looks efficient; **buy it because multiple programs have planned value** (e.g. REM Marksmanship, Combat Jump odds, an REM lacking Total Reaction, multi-HI/TAG/VH Fairy Dust). A TR REM already has its native reactive-Burst solution. EVO is *support*, not a high-WIP cyber assassin.
