### Rules that matter more than hype

- **Counterintelligence:** when the enemy uses the applicable first-turn **Strategic Command Token**, reduce an attempted **two-Order strip to one** *or* soften the one-Command-Token-use cap to **two**. It is **not** generic immunity to Loss of Lieutenant or any automatic extra Order. The skill is optional/private; value depends on tournament situation. [Rules](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Counterintelligence.md).
- **FT Master:** when in a legal Fireteam, makes **all members Regular at Order Count**. If they were *already* all Regular, instead extend the leader's Coherency ZoC **+2″**, non-cumulative. Parenthesized unit names specify a **team-cancellation dependency**, not universal eligibility. FT Master does **not** override Loss of Lieutenant, illegal Fireteam composition, or intra-turn Orders already spent. [Rules](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/FT_Master.md).
- **TinBot:** owner not Null/**Isolated**; the *whole legal Fireteam* benefits from its listed **Firewall, Guided defence, Discover, or Repeater** equipment. Two identical effects do **not** stack, take best. **Firewall(-6)** penalizes an enemy Comms attack by 6; the **Comms Saving Roll bonus remains +3** under current FAQ, *not* +6. A TinBot(Repeater) **does not grant Firewall**. [Rules](../../rules/5c_Equipment/TinBot.md); [hacker FAQ](hackers-and-killer-hackers.md).
- **EVO:** device offers **Assisted Fire**, **Enhanced Reaction**, **Fairy Dust** and **Controlled Jump**. A trooper can benefit from **only one Supportware at a time**, and each EVO Hacker sustains **one**. A new program on that target replaces its old program. If the Hacker enters Isolated/Null, its support ceases. **EVO is not a standard HD**: do not count on its device natively granting Oblivion/Spotlight or anti-Hacker Trinity. [Rules](../../rules/5c_Equipment/Hacking_Device.md), [Supportware](https://infinitythewiki.com/Category:Supportware).
- **Strategic Deployment:** the bearer must deploy **first and as Team Leader**; legal members then get FD(+4″) at setup. Personal FD(+4″) on a team member does *not* trigger +8″; the bonus is for **scenario/tournament** FD. Proximity at setup may improve objectives, but losing cover can outweigh every Order saved. [Rules](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Strategic_Deployment.md).
- **TAGCom:** only printed MODs for same-**Combat-Group** TAGs while carrier is deployed and non-Null; distinct effects from different TAGCom users can coexist but same MOD does not stack. **Not a universal +BS buff**; many current pilots grant **Dodge PH+3** and **GizmoKit PH+1**. [Rules](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/TAGCom.md).
- **Cyberplug N5.3:** at the **start of the Order/ARO** choose Connected or Autonomous. Connected acts on the Controller's Order while Controller generally Idles (Doctor/Engineer exception). Autonomous can spend Order Pool Orders and ARO independently. At most **two** Cyberplugs per controller and **one Connected** at a time; there is no operating-distance limit. **Controller death does not automatically erase an autonomous peripheral**; it remains a worse-stat, non-Order-generating model. [Update](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Peripheral_N5.3_Update.md).
- **Decoy:** static up-to-two false replicas, *not* real troops and *not* Holoprojector echoes; no extra Order, Fireteam membership or independent AROs. Does not reproduce physical deployed Mines/TinBots. [Rules](../../rules/8_Game%20States%20and%20Glossary/Decoy_State.md).
- **Baggage:** **Reload** by one eligible ally per Order when in ZoC, or **automatic States-Phase** reload for all eligible allies in ZoC. Does not replenish **Non-Reloadable** ammunition nor move/reload mines already deployed; stage carrier safely and preserve physical ZoC. [Rules](../../rules/5c_Equipment/Baggage.md).

## Which buffs to field: a five-question decision

| If your list contains... | Best support | Why / opposing alternative |
|---|---|---|
| **2–4 cheap Irregular models that can legally share one Fireteam** | Magistrate FT Master / Pandora / Patroclus | Converts whole team's future Orders into spendable Regular Pool; *do not* assume Irregular allies outside team become Regular (that's a different Inspiring Leadership mechanism) |
| **One expensive HI Fireteam in opposing Repeater coverage** | **TinBot Firewall(-6)**; a cheap -3 otherwise | Prevents cyber attrition on Hacker/HI and permits safe push; counter can still flank or **isolate the TinBot carrier** |
| **Marut/Maximus/Gator/Tik LT or a twin-TAG core** | **TAGCom** support pilot in **same group** | Improves Dodge/recovery across group; against a non-TAG roster it's an underpowered order and specialist |
| **1–2 Rui Shi/Vostok/Rudra/Puppet/REM guns scheduled to attack** | **EVO14/.5** | Assisted Fire/Enhanced Reaction can materially improve fights and shares Baggage; it should not replace your KHD if enemy Hacker pressure is the actual danger |
| **Multiple Combat Jump specialists/off-table scoring pieces** | **EVO Controlled Jump** | Buy for high-impact arrivals; if one cheap flyer arrives by Parachutist or avoids a contested Jump, it rarely pays |
| **Mines, Pitchers, Discoball, dispenser, repeatable E/M weapons** | **8pt Baggage** behind firing route | Automatic States-Phase reload after spending resources; do not buy an EVO just to reload |
| **Turn-1 contact objective with a safe corner inside FD(+4″)** | **Strategic Deployment** on legal Haris/Duo/Core | Saves whole team's moves and supports safe mine/scorer setup; if covered poorly, deploy deeper and use fewer models |
| **Identifiable LT and enemy DZ assassins** | **Decoy/Holomask shell; Sun Tze or Metsuke/Harbinger** | Hides real target and taxes enemy Discover, but a static model replica does not fight/ARO or shield from template |
| **Enemy routinely strips two orders on T1** | **Counterintelligence** on existing LT/specialist | Saves **one** Order; if enemy chooses not to strip or strips Command Tokens instead, the payoff varies |
| **Next Wave needs disposable surprise E/Marat threats across table** | **Gearhead+Puzzler/Ranter Cyberplug** | Connected reaches without Distance limit; Autonomous persists as separate Order-consuming threat after controller weakness, but at reduced profile |

### Cases where stacking buffs makes things *worse*

- EVO **Fairy Dust on a REM**, then **Enhanced Reaction on that REM**: the latter **cancels the former on that target**, not two buffs stacked.
- Two Firewall TinBots **-3 and -6**: use **-6**, not -9; Firewall Comms saving bonus remains **+3**.
- Two same-type TAGCom bonuses: no +6 Dodge PH from two +3 pilots; **distinct** listed modifiers may coexist.
- Baggage **does not** reload **Non-Reloadable** Armed Turrets, nor replace mines already deployed. It restores **uses of equipment** to a nearby still-eligible trooper.
- Cyberplug Peripheral **12pt Puzzler** is *not* a self-funded 12pt Regular Order. Add Gearhead13 + Puzzler12 for an actual **25pt/0** module, in a legal Combat Group; independent autonomous Orders come from the **pool**.
- **FT Master is bracket-unit sensitive:** Kestrel Master (Jackals) dying can break that dependent team. Check whether a given Fireteam is still legal and Regular in the next Tactical Phase.
- **Strategic Deployment's +8″** needs true scenario/tournament FD(+4″), not a friendly model simply possessing FD.

## Practical deployment checklist

1. Name the **specific allied beneficiaries** (Fireteam members, same-group TAGs, a gun REM, a flyer, disposable attacker) before buying a support unit.
2. Count the **incremental** upgrade over a useful cheaper unit (8 → 14/.5 EVO; 17 → 19 Magistrate LT; 17 → 21 CoC; 46 → 50 Crux Strategic; 13 Gearhead → 25 with Puzzler).
3. Check **Fireteam legality/parenthesized Master unit**; **Controller and Peripheral** rules; combat group; current profile's **exact listed** TAGCom and TinBot.
4. Assess **Turn-1 geometry** (safe +4″, opposing hacking zones, opponent's Order strip option) and **Turns 2–3 logistics** (actually reachable Baggage ZoC and viable ammo).
5. Check **failure mode** when TinBot is Isolated, Master dies, EVO is hacked, Baggage can't reach dispensers, or a Cyberplug runs in Autonomous mode.
6. Prefer the cheapest *functional* buff that amplifies an attack/mission plan you would run anyway. A support model that supports nothing is just a tax.
