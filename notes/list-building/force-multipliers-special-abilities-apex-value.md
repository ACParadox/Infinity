_Updated 2026-11-05 · Editorial, profile-specific **list-building judgments**, not measured win rates. Use the latest committed [sectorial rosters](../../sectorials), [N5.3 rules](../../rules/README.md), and actual profile/Combat Group rather than assuming skills transfer between profiles or factions. Related guides: [rare abilities](rare-capabilities-and-unique-combinations-apex-value.md), [REMs](rems.md), [hacking](hackers-and-killer-hackers.md), [network](network-projection-repeaters-pitchers-apex-value.md), [TAGs](tags.md), [command units](command-units-apex-and-value.md), [Order generators](cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md)._

## Scope — what belongs here

Rank **unusual support packages that improve a chosen allied unit or provide valuable army-wide protection**. Reward a strong *incremental* effect on a model you already wanted to field, minimal SWC/Order tax, and a useful independent job for the support piece.

- **In scope:** RemDriver; EVO on a **nonstandard carrier with additional impactful abilities**; inexpensive, protected or multifunctional Counterintelligence; TAGCom **only when its printed bonus is notably better than routine Dodge (PH+3) / GizmoKit (PH+1), or its carrier adds exceptional independent value**.
- **Out of scope:** all **Inspiring Leadership** and **FT Master** units (ranked in the [Order economy guide](cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md) and command/Fireteam studies); **ordinary 8pt Baggage bots, 14/.5 EVO Baggage bots, conventional reload carriers** (see [REMs](rems.md) and [Order economy](cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md)). Do not fill a specialty ranking with the same generic bot under six faction names.
- **Also not re-ranked here:** routine TinBot Firewall carriers (see [hackers](hackers-and-killer-hackers.md) and [network](network-projection-repeaters-pitchers-apex-value.md)), Decoy alone, and Cyberplug/other unusual peripherals (see [rare abilities](rare-capabilities-and-unique-combinations-apex-value.md) and [multi-body tactics](peripheral-synchronized-multibody-apex-value.md)). Their tactical value is real but belongs to another comparison.
- **Strategic Deployment:** small niche at the **bottom**. Forward Deployment(+4″) for a Fireteam can save zero Orders and introduce dangerous exposure. It is not an apex multiplier just because a costly character has it.

## Rules and eligibility that change the ranking

1. **RemDriver** is a Deployment Skill: place one token on **one already-deployed friendly REM**, which receives the **MODs in parentheses on that particular RemDriver profile**. There is no upkeep Order or stated range limit. The token is removed **at the end of the Order** in which the driver becomes Null. A REM cannot carry two RemDriver tokens; **TAGs and VHs are not eligible REMs**. [Local rule](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Remdriver.md).
2. **Apsaras has distinct profiles.** In **OSS** the RemDriver **20/0** has KHD with Firewall(-3), Deployable Repeater and printed RemDriver source attributes **CC=15, BS=13, WIP=14**; the TAGCom **18/0** is a separate profile. **Vanilla ALEPH RemDriver is 20/1**, not the OSS SWC cost. [OSS roster](../../sectorials/aleph/operations-subsection.md) · [ALEPH roster](../../sectorials/aleph/aleph.md).
3. **Agnes16/0** has **RemDriver (+1 BS, +5 CC)**, Sixth Sense and Specialist Operative. Her **13/0** non-RemDriver and **18/0** CoC are different choices. Only use her in [WinterFor](../../sectorials/panoceania/svalarheima-s-winter-force.md) or [Acontecimento](../../sectorials/panoceania/shock-army-of-acontecimento.md) when roster-legal.
4. **Pilot-X is not a universal profile.** PanO/WinterFor has **21/1, WIP13, Zero Pain, RemDriver (+1 BS, +5 CC, BTS=6)**; ALEPH/OSS has **23/.5, WIP14** and its own printed RemDriver source. A Pilot-X may buy **0–2 Mk-III RacerBots8/0 each**, all in the same Combat Group; each adds a **Regular troop** and mobile tools, not a free peripheral. Check the exact beneficiary and cost before buying the package. [PanO](../../sectorials/panoceania/panoceania.md) · [WinterFor](../../sectorials/panoceania/svalarheima-s-winter-force.md) · [OSS](../../sectorials/aleph/operations-subsection.md).
5. **EVO** offers **Assisted Fire, Enhanced Reaction, Fairy Dust and Controlled Jump**. A target can benefit from only **one Supportware at a time**, and each EVO Hacker sustains one. EVO **does not inherently grant Oblivion, Spotlight or Trinity**. **Scylla is exceptional because her profile explicitly adds Trinity (SR-2)**, Pitcher and Charybdis; do not generalize that upgrade to standard EVOs. [Device rules](../../rules/5c_Equipment/Hacking_Device.md) · [Steel Phalanx](../../sectorials/aleph/steel-phalanx.md) · [Acontecimento](../../sectorials/panoceania/shock-army-of-acontecimento.md).
6. **Counterintelligence** can reduce a first-turn enemy **two-Order** Command Token strip to **one**, or relax the relevant first-turn **one-Command-Token** limitation to **two**. It is neither a generated Regular Order nor an LT-succession rule. Safe survival, other useful equipment and actual likelihood of first-turn disruption determine value. [Rule](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Counterintelligence.md).
7. **TAGCom** applies **only printed bracketed MODs** to **TAGs in the carrier's Combat Group**, while the carrier is on table and non-Null. Distinct types of bonus can coexist, identical ones do not stack. Do not invent a BS, WIP or Reset bonus from the generic TAGCom label. [Rule](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/TAGCom.md).
8. **Strategic Deployment** grants a legal Fireteam FD(+4″) only with the qualifying bearer deployed **first as Team Leader**. A teammate's personal FD(+4″) does not create +8″; only the specified scenario/tournament bonus can do that. The whole team must still have **safe places to deploy**. [Rule](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Strategic_Deployment.md).

## TAGCom audit — which profiles are actually exceptional?

**No verified current roster profile in this comparison merits an apex TAGCom ranking for a stronger printed bonus than the routine Dodge/GizmoKit effect.** Do not invent one to populate the table.

| Profile / family | Verified status and extra job | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Tech-Bee9/0** — PanO, Kestrel, Military Orders and other roster-legal sectorials | **Dodge PH+3 / GizmoKit PH+1**, plus cheap Regular Specialist, Deployable Repeater / GizmoKit | **Good low-cost support/Order filler**, especially with a same-group TAG, but no exceptional TAGCom modifier; see Order/specialist guides |
| **OSS Apsaras TAGCom18/0** | Same **Dodge PH+3 / GizmoKit PH+1** plus KHD/Deployable Repeater | Usually select **Apsaras RemDriver20/0** when a combat REM needs the stronger distinct effect; TAGCom18 is a legitimate TAG-list alternative, not apex |
| **Tunguska/Corregidor TAG-Treiber, ISS Qíshì, JSA Taguraidā, Haqq TAG pilots** | Named cheap TAGCom specialists; source profiles typically show **Dodge PH+3 / GizmoKit PH+1** | Not ranked above unusual RemDriver or EVO; check **exact printed brackets** for each sectorial |
| **Ariadna TAG pilots** | Some current profiles show only **Dodge PH+1 / GizmoKit PH+1** | Even weaker as passive enhancement |
| **Next Wave Gearhead FTO13/0**, **Morat Faredak14/0** | TAGCom named with distinct **Minelayer/Wildcard** or pilot roles, but consulted roster summaries **do not expose a confirmed superior bracketed TAGCom MOD** | **Unranked pending printed bracket verification**; role compression alone is not proof of a greater TAG bonus |

If a future roster update adds **BS, Reset, WIP or other material TAGCom modifiers** beyond ordinary Dodge/recovery, compare that **specific option** and legal TAG pairing against RemDriver and Scylla. Until then, TAGCom is a solid cheap convenience in a TAG-heavy Combat Group, **not an exceptional force-multiplication category**.

## Strategic Deployment — deliberately low priority

- **Consider only if** the team has a safe upfield location, needs several moves toward scenario objectives, and the carrier was already worth taking. **Surface Shifta17/0** is the best low-cost example in this comparison.
- **Do not treat** Joan51–53, Crux50, Shizoku38, ISS Pheasant23–28, or similar profiles as apex simply because they move a Fireteam **4″** at deployment. Rank them on their **other jobs** (combat, command, scoring, mines), not on this modest bonus.
- **Deployment test:** compare actual **safe squares**, not theoretical distance. If 4″ makes one team member visible to a template/ARO or leaves fewer sheltered positions for a Core, the claimed multiplier can be **negative**.

## Matchup checklist

1. **Identify the benefiting model first:** Which deployed REM gets RemDriver? Which real combat REM / jumper merits EVO Supportware? Which TAG shares the carrier's group? Which opponent actually strips Orders?
2. **Calculate the cost of the full package**, including dependent RacerBots/Charybdis, the carrier's SWC, support Orders and deployment space. A **23/.5 Pilot-X** becomes **31/.5** with one 8pt RacerBot; **21/1** becomes **29/1** in PanO.
3. **Compare with the cheaper alternate profile:** OSS Apsaras17 vs 20; Agnes13 vs 16; Scylla normal HD32/.5 vs EVO32/.5. A free-feeling passive buff is not free when an alternate unit would do more.
4. **Check failure modes:** RemDriver carrier going Null; EVO being Isolated/Null; target using another Supportware; TAG in a different group; Counterintelligence with no enemy Command Token disruption.
5. **Do not duplicate other rankings:** Inspiring Leadership and FT Master belong to the [Order guide](cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md); ordinary Baggage/EVO bots belong to [REMs](rems.md). This specialty list is intentionally shorter and more selective.
