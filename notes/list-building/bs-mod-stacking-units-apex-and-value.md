*Researched 2 November 2026; full candidate/profile audit and table reranking 4 November 2026. Current profile, points, SWC and roster/Fireteam authority: the committed September 2026 N5.3 [sectorial references](../../sectorials/REFERENCE_DESIGN.md); local [N5.3 rules](../../rules/README.md) and FAQ v0.1. ## What actually stacks — and what does not

| Mechanic | Who gets the MOD or benefit? | When it matters | What it does **not** do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mimetism (-3/-6)** | Enemy shooting **at the bearer** with a LoF-dependent BS Attack (or Discover) | Persistent ranged defence, especially with Partial Cover and good range control | Does not affect CC, direct-template hits or all non-LoF attacks; MSV reduces/removes it |
| **Partial Cover** | Enemy BS attack usually suffers -3; defender gets +3 to relevant Saving Rolls | Nearly every conventional long-range duel | Does not stack twice merely because Nanoscreen is also active; a target is not necessarily in Cover just because terrain is nearby |
| **Nanoscreen** | Enemy BS attack -3 and defender's BS-attack Saving Roll +3 | Terrain-independent Cover-like defence, including exposed positions | No LoF-blocking terrain, no CC benefit, no Comms Attack benefit; Marksmanship ignores its shooting penalty |
| **BS Attack (-3)** | Target of the user's declared BS Attack suffers -3 to its **opposed FtF Roll** | Active Burst shooting and selected AROs against non-Warhorse targets | **Not passive protection** when the bearer is merely shot; not a -3 applied to unrelated enemy Normal Rolls. Warhorse negates it |
| **BS Attack (+1B)** | User's BS weapon Burst, **in Active Turn** | Consistent opposed shooting, particularly HMGs or strong short guns | Does not add a reactive die unless a distinct rule does so; respects maximum Burst |
| **Marksmanship** | User **ignores -3 from enemy Partial Cover/Nanoscreen** when declaring BS Attack | Covered AROs and repeated medium/long-range attacks | Does not ignore the target's +3 Saving Roll, Mimetism, range MODs or other penalties; user is exposed to Albedo/White Noise |
| **MSV1** | User ignores Mimetism (-3); reduces Mimetism (-6) to -3 | Cheap all-round accuracy versus ordinary mimetic enemies | Not a complete -6 answer; through ordinary Smoke still has the MSV1-specific penalty |
| **MSV2/3** | User ignores Mimetism/ordinary visibility penalties; MSV3 adds anti-Camo and anti-Surprise mechanics | Shooting Mimetism (-6), Smoke-supported attackers and hidden-marker disruption | White Noise, Albedo and Eclipse are different counters; MSV does **not** ignore Partial Cover |
| **Albedo (-3/-6)** | Only an enemy with **MSV or Marksmanship** shooting/Discovering the bearer suffers the MOD | Visor/Marksmanship-heavy opponents | Does nothing special against normal shooters without those traits, CC or direct templates |
| **Surprise Attack (-3)** | Target of an eligible marker/Hidden opening attack suffers -3 to the applicable FtF ARO | First attack from a legal marker state and later attacks after returning to marker | Not a permanent modifier on every subsequent shot; Combat Instinct and relevant MSV3 interactions counter it |
| **Warhorse** | **Negates enemy BS Attack (-X) MODs** applied to this trooper | Counter-Kriza, Rudras, Janissaries and other negative BS Attack skills | Does **not** cancel Mimetism, Surprise Attack, Cover, range, MSV, Albedo or ordinary ballistic risk |
| **Sixth Sense / Combat Instinct** | Reactive or anti-Surprise resilience under their respective rules | Breaks specific Smoke, flank, Stealth, Surprise or Dodge/Reset plans | Not a blanket +3 BS or unrestricted 360° shooting |

**Rule sources:** [Mimetism](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Mimetism.md), [Nanoscreen](../../rules/5c_Equipment/Nanoscreen.md), [Marksmanship](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Marksmanship.md), [MSV](../../rules/5c_Equipment/Multispectral_Visor.md), [Albedo](../../rules/5c_Equipment/Albedo.md), [Warhorse](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Warhorse.md), [Surprise Attack](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Surprise_Attack.md), [Combat Instinct](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Combat_Instinct.md), [Sixth Sense](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Sixth_Sense.md).

**ARO caution:** Surprise Attack is an **Active Turn** skill; a Hidden Noctifer/Swiss Guard revealing in ARO still benefits from Mimetism, position and the surprise of *where* the ARO came from, **not** a Surprise Attack (-3) MOD.

**Crucial distinction:** there are **three different kinds** of advantage:

1. **Incoming-roll protection:** Mimetism, Nanoscreen, Albedo against qualifying enemies; and temporary Cover/range/Surprise.
2. **Outgoing-roll quality:** MSV, Marksmanship, better BS, favorable range, extra active Burst or Special Dice.
3. **Opposed-roll suppression:** BS Attack (-3) applies **when the bearer attacks**; it can lower the enemy FtF return even when the bearer lacks Mimetism.

Don't sum MSV2 + Marksmanship + Albedo as “-9 defense”: they act on **different rolls or opponents**. Conversely, Mimetism (-3) + Nanoscreen can force **-6 on the incoming LoF shooting roll**, even while BS15, good weapon bands or extra Burst improve the user's own attack. A unit can have an excellent **apex** matchup and still be a poor points-value buy.

### Simple benchmark: target numbers, not fabricated win probabilities

Assume an ordinary BS13 attacker, target in real Partial Cover, weapon in its **+3 range band**, no other modifiers, and a target whose Mimetism is not canceled:

| Target's protection | Attacker's modified BS target number | Without the defender's own FtF dice |
|---|---:|---|
| No Mimetism; Partial Cover | **13** = 13 + 3 - 3 | Familiar covered gunfight |
| Mimetism (-3); Partial Cover | **10** = 13 + 3 - 3 - 3 | Three-point degradation |
| **Mimetism (-6); Partial Cover** | **7** = 13 + 3 - 3 - 6 | Six-point degradation; highly uncomfortable for non-MSV shooter |
| Mimetism (-6); Partial Cover; **attacking MSV1** | **10** | MSV1 reduces Mim (-6) to -3 |
| Mimetism (-6); Partial Cover; **attacking MSV2** | **13** | MSV2 cancels the Mimetism penalty |
| Mimetism (-6); Partial Cover; **attacking Marksmanship without MSV** | **10** | Marksmanship ignores Cover's shooting -3, **not** Mimetism (-6) |

These are **individual die target numbers, not a full Face-to-Face win percentage**. Burst, opponent's weapon range, criticals, Saves, Surprise, reactive options and whether a direct template bypasses the roll all alter the real choice. A BS14 attacker under the third scenario reaches only **8**. At a neutral range band the non-MSV result is worse again.

## BS Attack (-3) — opponent FtF suppression rather than passive Mimetism

**This is its own important category.** The -3 works on the target's opposing Face-to-Face Roll **when the bearer declares BS Attack**, not as an always-on -3 when the bearer is shot while Idle/Moving. ## Mimetism (-6) on its own — the no-MSV threat list

**Dedicated Mimetism (-6) ranking.** Here, evaluate the **-6 defensive layer as the primary advantage** rather than requiring an additional MSV/Albedo/Nanoscreen/Marksmanship combination. The strongest entries also exploit Camo/Hidden first contact, long weapons, armor, scoring utility, or efficient REM/warband pricing. Where one such model additionally has an offensive visor, that visor is **not** added to the -6 numerical example.

### How to defeat Mim6 when your list lacks MSV

1. **Do not default to a fair frontal FtF.** Remove the Mim6 unit's relevant ARO *without* exposing the main shooter, use a different side/range, or simply bypass its firing line to score.
2. **Use templates and non-LoF pressure.** A Direct Template bypasses the LoF BS hit roll; Speculative Fire, Guided, mines, hacking and E/M can force decisions that do not rely on landing a conventional shot. Calculate hit legality, Saving Rolls, Dodge and recovery separately.
3. **Exploit short range and corners.** A missile/sniper has a weak point-blank band; a heavy Mim6 gun may lose the useful ARO if approached along a protected route. Don't assume rushing a template carrier through a clear HMG lane is safe.
4. **Use your own stacked attacker:** high BS, positive range, legal Fireteam bonuses, extra active Burst and Surprise if available. The defender's Mim6 remains in the equation; you are compensating for it, not canceling it.
5. **MSV1 is a partial answer.** It makes Mim6 **-3**, which is good but still painful in Cover. **MSV2/3** removes Mimetism but invites Albedo/White Noise counterplay. Mix visor and ordinary-gun coverage instead of depending on one expensive MSV tower.
6. **Target the support or game plan.** Kill/restrain the Engineer, hacker or Order pool needed by a Mim6 TAG/REM/HI. Force the 35–120pt gun to spend Orders defending irrelevant territory rather than contributing to scoring.

**Five-question list test:**

1. What **negative MOD** does my target actually suffer, on which Roll, and **against which opposing equipment**?
2. Can this model win the required **16–32in or 8–24in** exchange with its selected weapon, or am I buying protection without reach?
3. Does the enemy have **MSV2, Warhorse, White Noise, Albedo, templates, hacking or a bypassing objective route**?
4. What are the actual **points + SWC + setup Orders + team/parent-model costs**?
5. After the first enemy reaction is cleared, does the unit still **score, preserve Orders or create new safe angles**?
