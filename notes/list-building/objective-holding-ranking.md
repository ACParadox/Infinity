# Zone Domination & Protected Area Denial — N5.3 Unified Ranking (non-TAG)

_11 November 2026 · unified ranking and tags 26 November 2026 · qualitative whole-game rankings, not measured tournament win rates. Exact profiles and eligibility should follow the [main-branch sectorial rosters](../../sectorials); cross-faction examples cannot be combined into one normal army._

**One combined role, one ranking.** This table rewards troops that **stay non-Null and contribute surviving points inside a scoring zone while making the approach or protected area expensive to contest**. A great room defender that never survives the scoring check is incomplete; a durable VP brick with no useful counter to an arriving attacker is incomplete. **Specialist status is not required. TAGs are excluded.**

- **Absolute ceiling:** performance in **both** surviving/contesting a scoring zone and denying access, with point concentration and deployment/range constraints as tiebreakers. Durability and ability to reach/retain the actual area remain primary; close templates, Guard, mines, repeaters, multiple bodies, immunities, and recovery raise the ranking. An exceptional remote gun does not automatically outrank a resilient doorway guard.
- **Efficiency:** performance in the same **combined role per points/SWC and Orders required**. Cheap Minelayers can tax an approach enormously, but low VP and W1/STR1 lower their rank relative to affordable, resilient bodies. Rank one **specific profile/package**, not hypothetical equipment collected from different loadouts.
- **Scoring mechanics:** [Domination](https://infinitythewiki.com/Domination) scores surviving qualifying **Victory Points** in a quadrant at the end of the Game Round, not the number of models or mine markers. Null troops and equipment do not contribute; Shasvastii-Embryo has a mission-specific exception. Check whether the mission explicitly changes Baggage scoring; **do not assume an automatic Baggage bonus**.
- **Denial mechanics:** [Guard](https://infinitythewiki.com/Guard) requires the enemy in **LoF and ZoC**, not just nearby; [Minelayer](https://infinitythewiki.com/Minelayer) places the specified deployable at setup only on an eligible loadout. A carried mine, Drop Bear, Dazer, Repeater, or Mine Dispenser is **not automatically predeployed**.
- **Packages:** a synchronized Peripheral is not another independent Regular Order or a second copy of its controller's full VP contribution. Resolve separate bodies and Null states under the actual mission and [multi-body rules](peripheral-synchronized-multibody-apex-value.md).

## Unified ranking — Zone Domination & Protected Area Denial

**Ranking policy (26 November 2026):** One continuous ranking balances surviving zone VP, realistic access to the scoring area, repeatable close or ranged denial, durability, points/SWC and setup Orders. Prior Apex and Value placements are inputs, not separate tiers. A strong affordable anchor can outrank a more expensive absolute-ceiling unit; low-VP defensive gates remain useful support rather than premier independent zone holders. Distinct loadouts/packages remain separate when their cost or mechanics materially differ. Existing profile data and caveats are retained; this is not a fresh balance-update audit.

- `Apex` — appeared in the former absolute-ceiling ranking.
- `Value` — appeared in the former efficiency ranking; both tags can apply.
- `Unique` — distinctive mechanic or unusual combination that materially affects zone control.
- `Toolbox` — meaningful additional control, mission, recovery or command utility.
- `Classified Runner` — ranked profile has an identified classified-relevant capability (e.g. FO, Doctor, Engineer, hacking or D-Charges); generic Specialist status alone is insufficient.
- `Airborne` — tactical airborne entry on this exact profile; none of the ranked profiles qualify.
- `Fireteam` — relevant legal linked use on the ranked profile in at least one listed roster; not permission in every faction or on alternate profiles.
- `Budget` — 21pts or less and 0SWC on the named profile or a named alternative; cheap support pieces still contribute only their qualifying VP.

**Cross-role tags (26 November 2026):** [Anti-Tech](https://github.com/ACParadox/Infinity/blob/bfd46de8b157a80a70cbfb6db1620143cfb32401/notes/list-building/anti-tech-msv-mimetism-camo-impersonation-apex-value.md) supplies `Anti-Tech` and the specific `Anti-Mimetism`, `Anti-Smoke`, `Anti-Camo`, `Anti-MSV`, `Anti-Impersonation` and `Anti-Holoecho` tags where the selected profile actually supports them. `Mod-Stacking` follows the [BS MOD reference](active-turn-gunfighters-ranking.md#bs-mod-mechanics-and-counterplay): meaningful native visual MODs or visor asymmetry, with Cover/Fireteam bonuses only when legal; Nanoscreen is Cover-like, not cumulative with terrain Cover. `Unique` and `Toolbox` incorporate [Rare Capabilities](rare-capabilities-and-unique-combinations-apex-value.md) and [Toolbox Units](toolbox-units-rankings.md) without duplicate categories. Do not import another loadout's equipment. Anti-Tech here is the vision/deception counter role, not generic anti-armor or anti-hacking. No rank changes in this pass.

**Profile boundaries and interpretation:** K2, Raveneye, Kaauri, Moran, Fennec and Ryūken-9 Minelayers remain economical defensive gates, but low VP and limited effective wounds/structure constrain their independent zone-holding ceiling. Teutonic Knights have excellent two-Wound cost but Impetuous/Limited Cover reduces exposed static survival. Alpha Guard is a conditional screen, not a premier garrison. Asura 63 and Gamma 50 retain their premium VP ceiling but are less efficient than compact anchors; Shang Ji 36 has useful Tactical Awareness and VP movement but less intrinsic close denial. The 12pt K2 Mine Dispenser remains an alternate, unranked loadout: it lacks E/Marat and needs Orders to place mines. The 19/0 Kaitok was added on 19 November 2026 (formerly Value #8 only); its inclusion rewards affordable ARM5/W2 rather than premium ranged or concealed scoring. Fiddler's 43pt two-JackBot and 36pt one-JackBot packages remain distinct.

## Practical picks by the problem actually faced

| Situation | First combined-role picks | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **One central room/entry must survive multiple assault Orders** | **Karakuri**, **Rasail+Chaksa**, **Raptor+Devabot**, **Bronze** | Real surviving wounds/structure backed by templates, nonlethal tools or layered physical bodies. |
| **Protected midfield zone with a mine already in place** | **Draal Shock Minelayer**; supporting **Raveneye/Kaauri/Moran/Ryūken** | Draal combines forward survivability with a true Minelayer device; others are less durable but force a clearance tax per point. |
| **Enemy wants to melee/charge a tight entrance** | **Jamie Handler**, **Andromeda**; conditional **Alpha** | Guard threatens a legal target within **LoF+ZoC** before base contact, and Jamie/Andromeda are much more resilient than Alpha. |
| **Need several cheap defenders rather than one premium model** | **K2 E/Marat**, **Hollow Man**, **Silverstar**, **Sakiel** | Different levels of genuine surviving VP, with live disabling/template threats; 11pt K2 alone rarely swings a large VP deficit. |
| **Open quadrant with long shooting lane and high VP deficit** | **Gamma**, **Asura**, **Shang Ji** | Ranged dominance and heavy surviving VP can matter more than a mine that covers an irrelevant door. |
| **Enemy hacking or E/M pressure** | **Karakuri with support**, **Sakiel/Sukeul**, **Rasail**, **non-Hackable support troops** | Keep hackable HI/REM outside projected attack paths where feasible; Enhanced Immunity is not blanket immunity to hacking. |

**Deployment test:** (1) Where must the surviving non-Null *scoring model* actually end the Game Round? (2) Which **entry/interaction square** must the opponent use to contest it? (3) Which defense is already there **without spending setup Orders**, and which needs Orders or a live Repeater network? (4) After a mine is cleared or one guard goes Null, **what still survives and contests**? Do not group every defensive body so one template removes the entire position.
