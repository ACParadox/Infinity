## How to rank real versatility (not simply count skills)

1. **Two or more competent, usable jobs.** Strong active gun, real WIP/remote hacking/Engineer, accessible Specialist, threatening melee, deployable denial, safe command or active mobility. A Pistol does not make a hacker a viable long-range gunfighter.
2. **Same route, same Orders.** A model going forward to attack can score a nearby objective or clear its own mines. An Engineer left in the DZ and a short-range gunfighter stranded away from REMs have **theoretical** roles.
3. **A plan if one subsystem is countered.** A competent gun still fights nonhackables after hacking fails; a KHD cannot automatically Oblivion TAGs without a printed program upgrade.
4. **Actual complete price.** Count SWC, legal teammates, extra delivery Orders, engineer targets, defensive support, group/Fireteam cost and **Specialist status of the chosen profile**. A linked module can have more total capability but lose on points efficiency.
5. **Surviving and completing objectives.** A 60pt hero with everything can monopolize Orders and expose a single failure point. Two 25–35pt independent toolboxes may win more missions.

**Tier meaning:** The unified S+ through C ranking balances maximum role breadth and points efficiency. B/C remain caution cases. Former Apex and Value tiers and ranks remain visible; units are not implied legal across every sectorial of a parent faction.

## Global toolbox ranking

_Consolidated 26 November 2026 from the committed Apex and Value tables; followed by a targeted editorial HI-informed reranking on 26 November 2026; no fresh profile audit or comprehensive balance-update reranking. The original 46 Apex and 68 Value assessments are represented by 85 entries, merging 29 overlaps. Different purchased packages (e.g. standalone Mukhtar specialists versus a Mukhtar Duo, or the 24pt versus 35pt Evader) remain separate._

**Method:** One global order rewards actual battlefield role compression and price efficiency without erasing premium options. Former tiers are scored S+ 1.05, S 1.00, S- .95, A+ .85, A .72, B .45 and C .20, with up to .06 for position in each original list. The stronger score plus .04 times the weaker score determines the merged order. Unified thresholds: S+ ≥1.08, S ≥.98, S- ≥.91, A+ ≥.84, A ≥.70, B ≥.40, otherwise C. This is an editorial consolidation, not measured performance. Original tiers and ranks remain visible. The previous Value cutoff remains **strictly below 45 points for the selected profile or entire package**; a low former Value tier is a caution, not an endorsement. Alternate profiles and paid packages are never combined into one fictional loadout.

**HI-informed editorial adjustment (26 November 2026):** The original consolidation score is the baseline, not a binding formula after this review. Promote De Fersen (#18), Charontid (#23), Swiss Guard HD (#36) and Hatamoto (#41) for their competent combat roles combined with hacking, scoring or close-combat utility. Move Krit Kokram (#28), Shikami (#30), Evader BSG Engineer (#45) and Evader AP Spitfire Engineer (#52) down for narrower combat reach or greater delivery/repair-target dependency, while retaining their specialist value. Shikami and Krit remain S because their distinctive utility still matters. This targeted judgment uses the merged HI assessment as a secondary signal; it does not transfer general HI tiers directly to toolbox tiers. Other entries retain their relative order and tiers; all historical Apex/Value ranks, tags and profile descriptions remain intact.

**Tags** apply only to the relevant stated profile or package, not automatically every alternative:

- `Apex`: appeared in the former Apex list.
- `Value`: appeared in the former Value list (including explicitly marked B/C caution entries).
- `Unique`: distinctive mechanic or combination supported by the [Unique Units ranking](unique-units-ranking.md).
- `Toolbox`: multiple usable battlefield roles; not assigned to the historical Wildcat caution example.
- `Classified Runner`: supported by the companion [Mission Specialists ranking](mission-specialists-ranking.md) or unique-units guide; does not imply an audited number of cards here.
- `Airborne`: the stated profile has Combat Jump or Parachutist; no transfer to FTO or FD alternatives.
- `Fireteam`: meaningful legal linked option or explicitly priced linked package in the stated roster.
- `Budget`: a relevant option at approximately 20 points or less.
- Existing `Anti-Tech`, `anti-tag/HI` and `Mod-Stacking` annotations are retained where present.

## Solo versus compact team: which wins?

| Scenario | Buy | Why / don't buy |
|---|---|---|
| Unknown mission, lots of cyber/vision problems, one elite centerpiece | **Asura HD+** | Hard to make completely irrelevant, but add cheap separate scorers to reduce failure risk |
| Vertical rooftop objective guarded by MSV/ARO and CC targets | **Shikami42** | Eclipse+Climbing/Super-Jump+2W+Specialist; ordinary durable attacker may fail to reach it |
| Enemy HI/TAG and Hackers share a central lane | **Nourkias48** or **Mary27** | Nourkias fights/CCs the exposed heavy; Mary is the safer, cheaper projected-cyber answer |
| Enemy midfield camo guards an objective and another attack lane is needed | **Ko Dali34/37**, **Black A.I.R. FO29**, **Nightshade KHD34** | BS14/MSV2 high-confidence active fight, MSV2/K1 linked attack, or Hidden CC/cyber ambush are distinct solutions; don't pay for all three |
| Shas midfield chaos, LT assassination threat, hard buttons | **Caliban selected FO/CoC/Engineer** | Pick **one profile** based on need; Camo/Protheion remains valuable even after job changes |
| Tohaa mission requires contesting and holding a dense midfield | **Draal Dazer or Viral Mine** | Deny movement while scoring, not just shooting a high-point gun |
| A Gecko/REM attack requires a close repairer with a second gun | **Evader Engineer** or **Gecko+Evader+Stempler** | Full module beats solo at redundancy; the 24pt Engineer often beats a 35pt Spitfire on value |
| PanO cheap Fireteam needs a scanner, scored button and safe approach | **Fennec13** | Cheap points/SWC superstar, but can't win a high-quality FtF gunfight |
| A durable infowar hacker also needs an attack profile in MO | **De Fersen Spitfire46** | MO's premium BS14/WIP14 hybrid; don't spend 46 to hack once then sit behind Total Cover |
| Need stealthy hacking reach from midfield | **Mary27** vs **Moran15** | Mary attacks Hacker/HI, Moran **projects** a safer Repeater and stalls movement; two distinct specializations |
| Need one versatile fast gun and nearby independent score/Doctor | **Mukhtar Duo** | Allows two specialists/jobs at different positions, but cannot claim one model owns both gun and Doctor |
| Need an all-in-one elite killer but not score | **Sheskiin** | More reliable proactive gunfights than many true toolboxes; pair with separate objective units |

**Practical construction test:** at list-writing time identify **two actions the unit will probably take during the same game**, and one plausible third fallback after the enemy counters the first. If you cannot name these, buy the simpler/cheaper profile. On the table, spend active Orders on the **mission**, not on using every weapon simply because it was purchased.

## Opposing opinions and evidence

1. **Broad skills ≠ a good toolbox.** [Infinity the Dante — Toolbox Units Guide N5](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q5kyCDufa8c) (YouTube MCP transcript checked) compares:
   - **Evader 35pt Engineer + AP Spitfire:** praised because both primary jobs meet a high competence bar
   - **De Fersen:** similarly praised for strong primary roles
   - **Wildcat Engineer:** criticized for role/position conflict
   - **Elena Kovac:** mixed evaluation

   **Counter:** an underwhelming solo unit can still be a legal Fireteam filler, objective specialist or matchup tech. A 2025 evaluation cannot determine updated roster legality.
2. **Asura versatility or cost bloat?** [Shepherd + Ollie — Mazebreaker OSS/Corregidor analysis](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NRRP4Fects8) (full YouTube MCP) calls Asura a premium infowar attacker and an efficient source of useful activations; observes she takes **fair gunfights** without Mimetism. [Loss of Lieutenant E168](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4MxU8qsoJlbZkYCLt1fo6d) (Podcast MCP) debates hacker vs AP gun and whether 60+pt role compression is worth it. Both: choose **HD+63** for toolbox, **AP Spitfire61** for anti-armor gun.
3. **Evader Engineer or simply specialist gun?** [WIP12 E208 — Corregidor after Mazebreaker](https://open.spotify.com/episode/342lz18SRGSZ8QWf1Zg3Z9) (Podcast MCP, esp. ~44–51m) discusses a small team combining:
   - **Gecko:** heavy attack platform
   - **Evader:** repair/network support plus attack
   - **Stempler:** additional team/network utility

   The package compresses repair, network, Fireteam and attack into one Order economy, **but** ties up a costly small team and Haris slot. The pinned roster separates **Evader 35pt Engineer AP Spitfire** from **31pt Specialist Operative AP Spitfire**; do not credit one profile with both.
4. **Shikami solo versus expensive double Shikami.** [Shepherd — Ōban faction focus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AhicCreNCUQ) praises Shikami's movement + Eclipse + specialist toolkit; [Tactical Awareness S3E16](https://open.spotify.com/episode/5EJe58gg1Lyo9xZxff2g75) discusses two Shikami and objective access. **For two:** redundancy and multiple approaches; **against:** same elite Order demand, fewer other tools. Take two **only** if both have separate work.
5. **Caliban swiss-army knife vs concentrated gun.** [MetaChemistry #139 — 2026 Shasvastii tournament winner](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6FErkreAsQoUKosH4WkdPW) (Podcast MCP, around ~18–49m) discusses triple Caliban, a returning MSV Spitfire, Protheion survivability and protected late scoring. **For:** Camo, multiple specialists, CC and recovery snowball; **against:** 1W and profile role split, need enough direct long-range shooting.
6. **Nourkias toolbox versus Sheskiin gunfighter.** [Shepherd — Combined Army N5](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qoyylw0p0JM) (YouTube MCP) contrasts:
   - **Nourkias:** hacking, CC and anti-TAG options; needs appropriate delivery and hackable/close targets
   - **Sheskiin:** better first gunfight and fewer delivery constraints; **not a scoring specialist**

   A wider toolbox is not always preferable to solving the immediate shooting problem.
7. **Faction toolboxes versus durable specialists.** [Shepherd — Haqqislam N5](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dmv4pfdTbc0) (YouTube MCP) favors fast Mukhtar and practical specialist Duos; some role-hungry coordinated-order lists prefer **unlinked** Mukhtars for activation flexibility. [Shepherd's Ariadna CanCon review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTqYE_FddwQ) and [Loss of Lieutenant #164](https://open.spotify.com/episode/0X4QIoP24cKONtltxClHMH) favor several independent cheap flexible specialists rather than a single deluxe hero.
8. **Ōban objective roles:**
   - **Karakuri:** durable midline **hold** anchor, with STR3 and repair potential
   - **Shikami:** independent scorer, better at **arriving safely** past MSV/height obstacles

   Paying for both makes sense only with sufficient pooled Orders and meaningful distinct roles.
