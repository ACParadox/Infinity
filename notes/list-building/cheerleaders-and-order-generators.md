## Short verdict

1. **Cheapest pure Regular Order:** **Traktor Mul 5/0** (eligible Ariadna, Baggage, native Regular; S4/Hackable and nearly unarmed) undercuts **6pt Netrods / Ímetrons** and **6pt Caledonian Volunteers**. The beacons have placement risk and no board utility; the Volunteer is the smallest armed S2 corner guard. Count protected Orders and faction AVA rather than comparing only sticker price.
2. **Best cheap Order-plus-utility Apex:** **Raveneye12** (FO/Minelayer/E/Marat), **Kaauri Sentinel11** (MSV1/Sixth Sense/E/M Minelayer), **Tech-Bee9** (Specialist/Repeater/Adhesive/TAGCom), **Gūdǎn12** (Camo/forward/Zapper/mine), **Barsuk10–11** (ARM3/HFT/LSG, FTO or Camo/Decoy) and **K2 Auxiliar FTO11** (NWI/E/Marat) lead the 12pt-or-less choices. [Apex table](#apex--best-total-battlefield-impact-in-the-order-generation-role).
3. **Best 9pt-or-less value fillers:** **Konduktor7**, **Rokot8**, **Métro8**, **Moderator BSG/E/M9**, **Keisotsu AP Rifle9** and **Line Kazak Rifle9**, plus the previously ranked Volunteer6 and Regular Kuang Shi6. **Tech-Bee9** stands above the **single tied family rank for every TAG support pilot** (Mekhamobilya, Sayiq, Qíshì, TAG-Treiber, Taguraidā, Faredak). Not every pilot costs 9pt or has the same skills.
4. **Best verified five-Order FT Master engine by sticker price:** **Morat Oznat FTO15 + 4 × Gaki4 = 31pts / 0 SWC / five Regular Orders** when formed as a legal Hungries Core at Order Count. This beats Imperial Service's **4 × Kuang Shi6 + Celestial Guard Monitor13 = 37pts / 0.5 SWC** on raw cost, but relies on an exposed 1W Master and four Irregular/Impetuous bodies; the Kuang Shi package has a different Smoke/Chain Rifle defensive role. Both consume **five Group slots**.
5. **Best conditional Irregular-to-Regular conversion:** Inspiring Leadership can regularize eligible Irregular troopers armywide; **FT Master** converts only its legal Fireteam members at Order Count. Particularly strong **three-order** packages are **Magistrate + 2 Jackal FTO (29pts)**, **Liang Kai + 2 Shaolin (31pts)** and **Lobo + 2 Diablos (36pts)**. **Pandora + Stingers does not generate extra Regular Orders**: Stingers are already Regular.
6. **Other inexpensive native Regulars:** **8pt Motorized Bounty Hunter** and independently fieldable **9pt RacerBot Mk-I/Mk-II** (Vanilla Haqq) trade safety for speed and area control. **8pt Mk-III RacerBot requires a paid Pilot-X**, not a standalone purchase. **12pt Monstrucker is Irregular**—excellent Engineer utility, but not transferable Order fuel.
7. **Not a Regular cheerleader:** **3-point Warcor**, standalone **Irregular** warband, peripheral/Servant, PuppetBot or off-table Combat Jump/Hidden Deployment trooper before it starts its Active Turn deployed on-table. They may be superb *defence or role compression*, but they cannot be counted as an ordinary shared Regular Order.

## What counts, and what does not?

- **Regular:** adds one transferable Order to its own Combat Group's pool **when eligible on the table at the relevant Order count**. The Group can spend it on a better attacker.
- **Irregular:** contributes an Order restricted to that troop, **not** a shared Order. A Command Token can sometimes convert it; Inspiring Leadership and FT Master have their own requirements. The Impetuous activation is similarly not a transferable pool Order.
- **Tactical Awareness:** extra **Tactical** Order reserved for that troop, with Fireteam-Leader use if legally linked. It is not a second generic Regular Order. Excellent on a unit that already wants to activate, mediocre as alleged passive filler.
- **Lieutenant (+1 Order), NCO, Strategos:** can create or redirect useful *special-order* capacity, but the resource stays conditional on the relevant rules and command shell; do not equate it to a 6-point cheerleader.
- **Puppet Master + PuppetBots / Controller + Peripherals / G: Jumper:** evaluate activation and table-coverage compression separately. The subordinate bodies are **not independent Regular Orders**.
- **Off-table models:** a Regular troop that is absent from the battlefield as Model or Marker does not provide the normal pool Order at the opening count. Do not pad turn-one calculations with undeployed Combat Jump troopers. A Combat Jump Netrod/Ímetron has mandatory deployment risk.
- **Slot and geometry matter:** the same 7-point REM can be best-in-class on an open DZ and the wrong third S3 bot on a packed TAG/VH/REM deployment zone.

## How to read the tiers

- **Apex:** **individual, usually 0SWC and ≤12pt native-Regular** units that contribute a pooled Order **plus a genuinely strong second job**. Irregular Monstrucker is labeled separately as a non-pooling exception. Complete conversion/team packages have their **own** audit section. A high Apex is **not** a claim of better standalone gunfighting.
- **Value:** useful, sufficiently safe **transferable Orders, utility and opportunity cost per points / SWC / Group slot**. An A-value support unit may beat an S-value pure filler in a particular list.
- **S:** benchmark/exceptional; **A:** strong; **B:** adequate/conditional; **C:** generally inefficient *if purchased mainly for passive Orders*. Cross-faction scores do not imply equal AVA or Fireteam permissions.

## FT Master — complete package cost and Order audit (2 Nov 2026)

**Mechanic:** At the **Order Count** step, if the FT Master is in a **legal Fireteam**, every member of that Fireteam is **Regular**. This is **conversion**, not one new Order per member on top of what they generate: a three-model package contributes **three**, not five, pooled Regular Orders. The “+2 converted” shorthand below compares the same troopers' **native** Order characteristics, not with another legal standalone Fireteam. All members must be in the **same Combat Group**, present and otherwise eligible for the Order Count. A purely Regular team instead gets **+2″ Leader Coherency ZoC**, non-cumulative; it **does not get any additional Regular Orders**. **FT Master failure and ranking cautions:**

- **Do not budget converted Orders from unlinked profiles.** No FT Master effect is granted to extra Jackals, Diablos, Gakis, Morlocks, Shaolin or Helots elsewhere on the table. The Master must be part of the team **during Order Count**.
- **Bracketed unit names impose special cancellation.** For example, if an FT Master **(Jackals)** leaves a team containing Jackals, that Fireteam is **automatically cancelled**; similarly for the listed Diablos, Helots, Shaolin, Morlocks and Stingers in their respective abilities. Other team integrity rules still apply. This is not a generic permission to form arbitrary cross-sectorial teams.
- **No retroactive Order multiplication or subtraction.** A mid-turn team break does not generate extra Orders and cannot undo the already completed Order Count. The engine’s loss affects subsequent counts and may end its linked activation immediately. Do **not** state that already-counted pool Orders instantaneously change into Irregular Orders.
- **Inspiring Leadership is separate.** It can affect eligible Irregular troops outside a Fireteam; FT Master is limited to legal linked members, and a converted member is counted **once**.
- **A five-order Core is not automatically superior to a Haris.** Oznat + 4 Gakis fills **five** slots, eats a precious Core allowance and offers little durable gunfighting. Oznat + 2 Gakis costs **23pts**, keeps more list slots free, and leaves points/slots for a better attacker. Choose the 31pt Core only when the list actually needs five cheap Orders and accepts its defensive weaknesses.
- **Regular-only teams are a different FT Master use case.** Pandora + Stingers is the prime example: +2″ Leader Coherency ZoC when *all members have Regular on their unit profile*, rather than an Order rebate. Adding a legal Irregular **Steeljaw or Blackheart** to the same Research team changes the effect to conversion, not both effects simultaneously.

**Practical ranking:** For **raw protected pooled Orders**, start with **Oznat + Gakis**, then **Magistrate + Jackal FTO**, **Liang Kai + Shaolin**, and **Lobo + Diablo**. The **Kuang Shi Monitor Core** is a competitive five-body *native-Regular* budget team, not a conversion engine. For **combined mission/attack/defence** rather than cheapest Orders, a **Patsy/Helot**, **Taskmaster/Morlock**, **Jing Qo/Shaolin** or **Pandora/mixed Research** team may justify its premium. Do not rank Pandora + all-Regular Stingers as an order-generator upgrade.

## Opposing opinions — preserved rather than forced into one ranking

1. **Max the cheap Orders vs buy fewer, more capable troops.** Tunguska Tactical Awareness presenters explicitly compare replacing a Transductor for a stronger hacker (Mary Problems) and accepting one fewer Order. Cheap orders increase an attacker's reach; better attack tools increase the chance that the spent Orders achieve anything. Resolve by *how many usable Orders the group needs after deployment/repositioning*.
2. **Kuang Shi / Volunteering swarms are overpowered vs consume too many slots.** The Imperial Service commentary praises 37pts for five regular bodies and smoke, but warns a five-slot module can obstruct necessary specialists in a 15-trooper army. Correct evaluation is at **whole-list** level, not cost per body.
3. **Saladin is a faction-defining economy engine vs investment and concentration risk.** Robert Shepherd's mid-2026 discussion praises regularized cheap Haqq irregulars and unusually strong Coordinated play; a co-discussant challenges the degree of the faction rating, and opponents can threaten Saladin. Strategos Regular-order conversion and LT-Order-based Inspiring Leadership coordination are alternatives—not simultaneously the same Order.
4. **Puppet Master/Bots are outstanding defence vs hidden Order inefficiency.** Tactical Awareness emphasizes 11pt Counterintelligence Master and multi-Puppet objective/Flash utility, but reminds listeners that PuppetBots generate **no Orders**, cost points and can fail if the Controller dies. Evaluate as *one Regular Order plus deployed defence*, not 3–4 Orders.
5. **Flashbot spam vs S3/S4 DZ congestion.** The 7pt Repeater/Flash profile is often a default, but one to two may be much better than taking the maximum in a list with multiple TAGs, Bikes, Engineers' Servants, and REMs. An extra S2 line soldier at +3–5pt can preserve a safe deployment position.
6. **Tactical Awareness 'extra Orders' vs misleading total count.** It is premium on a fast scoring unit/active Fireteam Leader; it cannot finance an unrelated attacker's move and may never be spent on a passive unit. Do not add Tactical Awareness directly to total transferable Order pool.

## A practical 30-second list audit

1. **Count your Group 1 Regular Orders after deployment**, not total bodies; separately label Irregular, Tactical, LT and off-table sources.
2. **Count the spending target:** Who can profitably consume six or more Orders? Is Group 2 a usable 3–5-Order attack/scoring module?
3. **Identify Order survivability:** Which fragile or accessible sources can be killed in Turn 1? Does Counterintelligence or a safe S2 deployment save a meaningful resource?
4. **Check density and slots:** A 37pt five-model package might be outstanding **or** block required Specialists; a 7pt REM might be mathematically best **or** physically impossible to hide.
5. **Evaluate the next upgrade marginally:** Does +2–6pts buy a real task (FO, Repeater, Minelayer, smoke, resilient guard, effective ARO) or just a fancy passive combi rifle?
6. **Recalculate after losses:** Can either Combat Group still achieve the mission without the principal attacker or the command engine?

**Key decision:** *The best cheerleader is the cheapest protected **transferable** Order that also does something you actually need.* Once that stops being the binding constraint, buy more capability—not more inert Orders.
