# Cheerleaders and Order Generators — N5.3 Cross-Faction Rankings

**Research: 23 October 2026.** This is a role guide, not an Army roster, legality document or tournament pick-rate study. **Authoritative prices, profile effects, AVA and Fireteams:** current committed [sectorial reference files](../../sectorials/) (September 2026 Army data, N5.3 + FAQ v0.1). The tier ratings are subjective comparisons of **what an Order-generating slot achieves**, not mathematical win rates. A particular sectorial may have different costs, AVA, FT permissions or availability from its vanilla parent. Recheck the exact chosen roster.

## Short verdict

1. **Best pure Orders for the points:** 6-point **Netrods / Ímetrons** (if legally available and deployed); **6-point Caledonian Volunteers** offer a much more usable *armed*, hideable Regular Order. An immobile Order source does not screen corridors or defend a Lieutenant.
2. **Best universally useful cheap bodies:** **7-point Flash Pulse + Repeater REMs** (Fugazi, Transductor, Fanous, Kyttä, Chaīyì, R-Drone and appropriate faction equivalents). They produce a Regular Order **and** a Repeater, mobile Flash ARO and sometimes Mimetism (-3). They are not interchangeable in AVA or source profiles.
3. **Best 8-point support filler:** basic **Baggage REM** (Mulebot, Salyut, Kameel, FuzzBot and equivalents). Cheap Regular Order + Repeater/Deactivator + reload support where the profile has them. One is often excellent; additional S4 bases may clog the DZ.
4. **Best mass-Order *engine*:** Imperial Service **Kuang Shi Core**: 4 × 6-point Kuang Shi + 13-point Celestial Guard Monitor = **37 points / 0.5 SWC for five Regular Orders**, smoke and multiple direct-template threats. Exceptional efficiency, but **five Combat Group slots**, a required Monitor and Fireteam constraints. AVA 8 does not let one Monitor sustain eight members in a five-trooper Core.
5. **Best command-fuel conversion:** an active **Inspiring Leadership Lieutenant** (e.g. Saladin in legal Haqq lists or Wallace where available) can make otherwise Irregular troopers generate Regular Orders. **FT Master** can regularize linked Irregular troops; these are *conditional package bonuses*, not unconditional profiles.
6. **Best cheap Order-plus-tempo alternatives:** 8-point **Motorized Bounty Hunters** where Regular and roster-legal; **9-point RacerBots** where present; modest-cost line Specialists, light templates or Minelayers when they substitute for a pure filler *without costing a valuable extra slot*.
7. **Not a Regular cheerleader:** **3-point Warcor**, standalone **Irregular** warband, peripheral/Servant, PuppetBot or off-table Combat Jump/Hidden Deployment trooper before it starts its Active Turn deployed on-table. They may be superb *defence or role compression*, but they cannot be counted as an ordinary shared Regular Order.

## What counts, and what does not?

- **Regular:** adds one transferable Order to its own Combat Group's pool **when eligible on the table at the relevant Order count**. The Group can spend it on a better attacker.
- **Irregular:** contributes an Order restricted to that troop, **not** a shared Order. A Command Token can sometimes convert it; Inspiring Leadership and FT Master have their own requirements. The Impetuous activation is similarly not a transferable pool Order.
- **Tactical Awareness:** extra **Tactical** Order reserved for that troop, with Fireteam-Leader use if legally linked. It is not a second generic Regular Order. Excellent on a unit that already wants to activate, mediocre as alleged passive filler.
- **Lieutenant (+1 Order), NCO, Strategos:** can create or redirect useful *special-order* capacity, but the resource stays conditional on the relevant rules and command shell; do not equate it to a 6-point cheerleader.
- **Puppet Master + PuppetBots / Controller + Peripherals / G: Jumper:** evaluate activation and table-coverage compression separately. The subordinate bodies are **not independent Regular Orders**.
- **Off-table models:** a Regular troop that is absent from the battlefield as Model or Marker does not provide the normal pool Order at the opening count. Do not pad turn-one calculations with undeployed Combat Jump troopers. A Combat Jump Netrod/Ímetron has mandatory deployment risk.
- **Slot and geometry matter:** the same 7-point REM can be best-in-class on an open DZ and the wrong third S3 bot on a packed TAG/VH/REM deployment zone.

## How to read the tiers

- **Apex:** how much *useful Order economy + free/passive utility + denial* the **unit or clearly specified package** can supply under favorable legal conditions. A high Apex is **not** a claim that it is a better gunfighter.
- **Value:** useful, sufficiently safe **transferable Orders, utility and opportunity cost per points / SWC / Group slot**. An A-value support unit may beat an S-value pure filler in a particular list.
- **S:** benchmark/exceptional; **A:** strong; **B:** adequate/conditional; **C:** generally inefficient *if purchased mainly for passive Orders*. Cross-faction scores do not imply equal AVA or Fireteam permissions.

| Apex | Unit or explicitly priced package | Why it earns the tier | Main limiting condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S** | **Kuang Shi Core + Celestial Guard Monitor** (37pts/0.5SWC for 5) | Five Regular Orders, smoke launcher and four cheap Chain Rifle defenders/traders; can force disproportionate enemy effort | Takes five slots, uses a mandatory Monitor and required FT; exposure/Impetuous movement must be managed |

### Points-value tiers — when buying a *passive transferable Regular Order*

| Value | Profiles and package | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|
| **S+ — pure minimum** | **6pt Netrod / Ímetron**, **6pt Volunteer** | Best points per Regular Order. Take the beacon only with acceptable deployment/zero-combat limitations; Volunteer if useful S2 defence or forward body matters |
| **S — cheap utility floor** | **7pt Flash/Repeater REM**, **8pt basic Baggage REM** | Usually first two or three filler buys, if present in that roster and if there is protected S3/S4 deployment space |
| **S — exceptional package** | **6pt Kuang Shi with Monitor**, **cheap Irregulars under active Inspiring Leadership** | Package rather than per-body economy. Include Monitor/LT, SWC and Group slots in the price; no double counting free Impetuous Orders |
| **A — unusually functional cheap Regular** | **8pt Motorized Bounty Hunter**, **9pt RacerBot**, **9pt Moderator**, **10pt Fennec/Fusilier/Alguacil**, **11pt Ghulam**, **11pt Ikadron** | Choose when extra mobility, non-hackable troop, Fireteam membership, smoke/guard duty, special missions or reusable Baggage is worth the premium |
| **A-/B — role compression** | **11pt Puppet Master**, **12pt Barid FO/Pitcher**, **13pt Securitate Repeater**, **13pt Dikpala Repeater/Akrylat**, **13pt Fennec Sensor/FO**, **Ghulam FO/Doctor** | These beat pure filler only if the second job or Command effect is *actually in your plan*. Puppet Master is often apex utility at a tiny extra price |
| **B/C — expensive for doing nothing** | Ordinary 14–20pt line infantry, over-upgraded Fireteam fillers, non-activated 20+pt troopers | Pay that premium for **proven** Specialist, ARO, link, anti-armor or mission function—not to generate one idle Order |
| **Not comparable** | Warcor, Irregular warbands, off-table undeployed AD, PuppetBots, Servants, TacAware bonus and LT Orders | Different restrictions and contribution; rank in distinct engine/tempo/support categories |

**Mechanical floor check:** 6pt = 0.167 Regular Orders/pt; 7pt = 0.143; 8pt = 0.125; 10pt = 0.100; 13pt = 0.077. That arithmetic **excludes** weapons, survivability, safe DZ space, threat, delivery and later-turn Order loss. One **6pt** model is not mathematically *twice as useful* as a **12pt** specialist.

## When each archetype wins

| Situation | Prefer | Why / what to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Need +1 Group 1 Order with 7pt spare** | 7pt Flash/Repeater REM (when legal) | No complicated package, Repeater/Flash value; avoid adding fourth large base that cannot hide |
| **Need 1–2 points less than a Flashbot** | 6pt Netrod/Ímetron or Volunteer (when legal) | Pure floor; only beacon if you accept its mandatory Combat Jump deployment and inability to screen |
| **Guided, Pitchers, Disposable weapons, Tech Recovery** | 8pt Baggage REM | Reload support makes already-chosen tools reliable; don't pay a large support package solely to justify the bot |
| **Going second vs aggressive AD, impersonation or bikes** | Cheap S2 Chain Rifle/Light Shotgun guards; dispersed backline support | Layer cheap bodies on **approach and destination corners**, not exposed lone Flashbots. Cheap troops with no passive stop power may be false economy |
| **Hacking or repeated objective access** | Flashbot/fast Repeater; FO/Pitcher upgraded line troop | Compare actual network geometry and objective reach against +5–6pts for upgrades |
| **Dense, large-base DZ with TAG, VH, 2+ REMs** | S2 infantry/Volunteer/Ghulam/Moderator/Alguacil rather than yet another bot | Deployment space is scarce; a model that cannot safely hide is a future lost Order |
| **Need a legal Fireteam base** | Cheapest required member *or* slightly dearer useful Specialist/Repeater | A 7pt REM cannot replace a mandatory 10–13pt line member. Count composition bonuses, paid SWC and team commitment |
| **Opponent relies on first-turn 2-Order Command denial** | Puppet Master with Counterintelligence (if legal) | Preserves one otherwise lost Order and provides its own; benefit low if you were not facing denial |
| **Premium active attack piece already has enough Orders** | Spend surplus on backup gun, Specialist, mine, counter-tech | Do not optimize Orders/point after the marginal Order cannot be used productively |
| **Group 2 with 3–5 Regular Orders** | Add second scalable consumer or Tactical Awareness unit, not only inert fillers | Four Orders with no worthwhile user are less valuable than three with a clear specialist attack path |
| **Lieutenant engine / order conversion list** | Cheap Irregular troops only when active Inspiring Leadership / FT Master legal | Check effects and minimum Fireteam requirements; losing LT or mandatory link changes later turn's economy |
| **Limited insertion / ten-slot packet** | Tactical Awareness on *productive* attackers, role-compressed Specialists and multiple bodies per activation | Slots, not simply points, are binding. Passive 6pt filler can displace a decisive unit |
| **Group-wide loss/attrition** | Several independent protected cheap Regulars instead of one exposed concentrated shell | Order survival through Turns 2–3 matters as much as Turn-1 headline count |

## Opposing opinions — preserved rather than forced into one ranking

1. **Max the cheap Orders vs buy fewer, more capable troops.** Tunguska Tactical Awareness presenters explicitly compare replacing a Transductor for a stronger hacker (Mary Problems) and accepting one fewer Order. Cheap orders increase an attacker's reach; better attack tools increase the chance that the spent Orders achieve anything. Resolve by *how many usable Orders the group needs after deployment/repositioning*.
2. **Kuang Shi / Volunteering swarms are overpowered vs consume too many slots.** The Imperial Service commentary praises 37pts for five regular bodies and smoke, but warns a five-slot module can obstruct necessary specialists in a 15-trooper army. Correct evaluation is at **whole-list** level, not cost per body.
3. **Saladin is a faction-defining economy engine vs investment and concentration risk.** Robert Shepherd's mid-2026 discussion praises regularized cheap Haqq irregulars and unusually strong Coordinated play; a co-discussant challenges the degree of the faction rating, and opponents can threaten Saladin. Strategos Regular-order conversion and LT-Order-based Inspiring Leadership coordination are alternatives—not simultaneously the same Order.
4. **Puppet Master/Bots are outstanding defence vs hidden Order inefficiency.** Tactical Awareness emphasizes 11pt Counterintelligence Master and multi-Puppet objective/Flash utility, but reminds listeners that PuppetBots generate **no Orders**, cost points and can fail if the Controller dies. Evaluate as *one Regular Order plus deployed defence*, not 3–4 Orders.
5. **Flashbot spam vs S3/S4 DZ congestion.** The 7pt Repeater/Flash profile is often a default, but one to two may be much better than taking the maximum in a list with multiple TAGs, Bikes, Engineers' Servants, and REMs. An extra S2 line soldier at +3–5pt can preserve a safe deployment position.
6. **Tactical Awareness 'extra Orders' vs misleading total count.** It is premium on a fast scoring unit/active Fireteam Leader; it cannot finance an unrelated attacker's move and may never be spent on a passive unit. Do not add Tactical Awareness directly to total transferable Order pool.

## Sectorial and faction selection notes (non-transferable roster permissions)

- **ALEPH / Operations Subsection / Steel Phalanx:** Netrods are the 6pt pure floor where available; compare 13pt **Dikpala** in OSS for actual ARO/repeater/FT functionality and Posthuman/G: Jumper slot compression. Do not use pre-Mazebreaker Dakini-era profile assumptions for OSS.
- **Combined / Onyx / Shasvastii / Next Wave / Morats:** Ímetron where available; **7pt R-Drone** where legal; **11pt Ikadron** trades pure low cost for Baggage, Repeater, Discover and a Light Flamethrower (+1B). Next Wave has expensive baseline Regular bodies and FT-Master cases—unit-count pressure is real. Don't assume one faction's AVA in another.
- **PanO / Kestrel / NCA / Varuna / WinterFor / Acontecimento / Military Orders:** **7pt Fugazi** and **8pt Mulebot** where legal, then S2 Fusilier/Fennec and legal FT fillers. A necessary **10pt Fennec** is cheaper than its upgraded 12–16pt toolbox cousins; evaluate FB requirement and Lieutenant-decoy identity. Pathfinder is a useful *second job*, not basic filler.
- **Nomads / Bakunin / Corregidor / Tunguska:** **7pt Transductor**, **8pt Salyut** where legal; **9pt Moderator**, **10pt Alguacil**, **11pt Puppet Master** in permitted lists. Tunguska **13pt Securitate** is a premium only if WIP/Warhorse/Repeater/team use is meaningful; 8pt Motorized Bounty Hunters are legal Regular tempo but not covert S2 guards.
- **Yu Jing / ISS / Invincible / White Banner:** **7pt Chaīyì** where legal; **ISS Kuang Shi Monitor Core** is a 37pt/0.5 package. For a non-ISS list, compare Zhanshi and cheap regular specialists instead; **do not transfer Kuang Shi Core to other factions**.
- **Haqq / Hassassin / Qapu / Ramah:** **7pt Fanous**, **8pt Kameel** where legal, **11pt Ghulam** and **12pt Barid FO/Pitcher** (vanilla) for role compression. Only count Ghazi/Daylami as Regular if actual Inspiring Leadership or other legal conversion applies.
- **O-12 / Starmada / Torchlight:** **7pt Kyttä**, **8pt FuzzBot** where legal; **12pt Kappa** small-base Generalist. Tactical Awareness on a linked/fast Waverider or mission piece can be worth much more than a parked extra Order.
- **Ariadna / Caledonia / TAK / Kosmoflot / USAriadna / FRRM:** no automatic PanO-style Flashbot/Baggage engine. **6pt Caledonian Volunteer** is an exceptional floor in its roster; other factions use their cheapest S2 line troops and Irregular warbands to guard/harass. Wallace Inspiring Leadership is a conditional force-multiplying engine. Do not equate a Warcor to a Regular order.
- **JSA / Oban / Shindenbutai:** evaluate Keisotsu / cheap required Team bases, FO and chain-rifle defence against scarce cheap Regular slots; cheap Smoke/Warbands may be Irregular, so split passive economy from independent activation tempo.
- **Tohaa:** assess Kamael and cheap supporting Triad/utility pieces as **Fireteam and battlefield enablers**, not just their base Regular cost; SymbioMates and peripherals do not produce extra pool Orders.
- **NA2 and other Non-Aligned:** roster-legal line infantry, Flashbot, Bounty Hunter and tech support vary dramatically; use each individual roster rather than assuming all mercenary profiles are allowed.

## A practical 30-second list audit

1. **Count your Group 1 Regular Orders after deployment**, not total bodies; separately label Irregular, Tactical, LT and off-table sources.
2. **Count the spending target:** Who can profitably consume six or more Orders? Is Group 2 a usable 3–5-Order attack/scoring module?
3. **Identify Order survivability:** Which fragile or accessible sources can be killed in Turn 1? Does Counterintelligence or a safe S2 deployment save a meaningful resource?
4. **Check density and slots:** A 37pt five-model package might be outstanding **or** block required Specialists; a 7pt REM might be mathematically best **or** physically impossible to hide.
5. **Evaluate the next upgrade marginally:** Does +2–6pts buy a real task (FO, Repeater, Minelayer, smoke, resilient guard, effective ARO) or just a fancy passive combi rifle?
6. **Recalculate after losses:** Can either Combat Group still achieve the mission without the principal attacker or the command engine?

**Key decision:** *The best cheerleader is the cheapest protected **transferable** Order that also does something you actually need.* Once that stops being the binding constraint, buy more capability—not more inert Orders.
