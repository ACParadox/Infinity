_Consolidated 26 November 2026 from the previous Apex and Value rankings. Qualitative list-building judgment, not tournament win rates. Profile and roster data are carried forward from the 5 November audit of the pinned September 2026 Army 7.26246.158/159 snapshots; this is not a new live Army audit._

**48 eligible or provisionally eligible named entries, ranked once, plus one explicitly excluded unit.**

## Scope and ranking method

One global ordinal ranking combines reliable succession, survivability/concealment, independent usefulness, points/SWC efficiency and role compression. Succession reliability is the primary purpose: reward protected support and cheap insurance as well as premium profiles that justify their cost without exposing the only backup. A powerful fighter can still rank below a cheaper, safer successor. The unified order is an editorial reconciliation of the former two rankings, not an arithmetic average or an independent profile re-audit. Grades: **S** exceptional, **A+** excellent, **A** good, **B** conditional/inefficient, **C** poor as succession insurance.

**Scope:** Chain of Command (CoC) succession **while the model is on the table and not Null/Isolated at the Loss of Lieutenant check**. A trooper's Lieutenant profile is not its CoC profile; never pool skills or equipment from different options. CoC alone does not confer generic Specialist status; use the native skills and mission-specific permissions in the [consolidated mission guide](mission-specialists-and-button-pushers-ranking.md). Always check exact faction/sectorial selection and Fireteam chart. See [Lieutenant command rankings and economy](LT-ranking.md), [local N5.3 rules](../../rules/README.md) and [official CoC rules](https://infinitythewiki.com/Chain_of_Command). Roster examples are verified examples, not exhaustive or transferable permissions. † denotes unresolved eligibility.

**Tags** are non-exclusive and describe at least one actual CoC option in the row:

- `Apex`: all 17 former Apex standouts, retained for exceptional independent defense, combat or support.
- `Value`: former S/A+ Value entries (23 units), representing genuinely strong succession efficiency; not every entry in the former full Value table qualifies.
- `Unique`: especially differentiated succession/support package.
- `Toolbox`: meaningful secondary combat, technical, scenario, defensive or command utility.
- `Classified Runner`: documented actual CoC profile with useful Classified execution or substantial audited coverage; not simply another profile of the same unit, or CoC eligibility alone.
- `Airborne`: actual CoC profile with Combat Jump/Parachutist; being off-table undermines early succession.
- `Fireteam`: documented useful legal linked CoC option; verify roster and FTO restrictions.
- `Budget`: at least one CoC option at 20 points or less, including low-SWC options.
- `Anti-Tech`: meaningful direct answer to enemy Mimetism, markers or visor-based defense; includes Eclipse/Mirrorball/Disco route control and MSV/Albedo where applicable. MSV1 is partial; mere Mimetism and ordinary anti-armor ammunition do not qualify. See [Anti-Tech](https://github.com/ACParadox/Infinity/blob/bfd46de8b157a80a70cbfb6db1620143cfb32401/notes/list-building/anti-tech-msv-mimetism-camo-impersonation-apex-value.md).
- `Mod-Stacking`: meaningful BS asymmetry from exceptional Mimetism, marker openings, combined offensive/defensive effects or conditional counter-MOD tools. See [BS MOD mechanics](active-turn-gunfighters-ranking.md#bs-mod-mechanics-and-counterplay); effects do not necessarily stack on the same roll.

`Unique` and `Toolbox` incorporate applicable exact-profile findings from [Rare Capabilities](rare-capabilities-and-unique-combinations-apex-value.md) and [Toolbox Units](jack-of-all-trades-toolbox-units-apex-value.md). Do not transfer alternate profile capabilities. Tag review 26 November 2026; rankings and inherited costs/eligibility unchanged.

## List-building takeaways

- **Best combined packages:** Kaeltar, Myrmidon Officer and Raveneye combine succession with substantial support, protection or economy. Kaeltar supplies SymbioMates; Myrmidon Officer brings Eclipse, Mimetism(-6) and NWI; Raveneye supplies Holomask, native FO and defensive equipment at 18/0.
- **Premium independent pieces:** Taqeul and Tai Sheng retain Apex status for durable technical and combat packages; their higher price is justified when their secondary roles matter.
- **Low-tax insurance:** Magistrate, Shukra, Ment, Tanuki, Kempeitai and Agnes are attractive when the rest of the list supplies the attack pieces. Farzan offers marker protection rather than generic objective scoring.
- **Role compression:** Pheasant, Pandora, Starmada Saladin, Hortlak and Caliban can justify more than the bare insurance tax. Distinguish actual mission skills from CoC and do not expose the sole backup unnecessarily.
- **Expensive fighters are conditional:** Vassily, Patroclus, Umbra and both Joans retain Apex recognition but cost and attack exposure reduce their succession value. Joan's Non Specialist status and LT-only Order effects remain important.
- **Protect the succession path:** separate LT and CoC across attack, template and hacking vulnerabilities. Kirpal Singh cannot provide early succession while off-table.

**Research status:** Single editorial ranking and inherited profile-coverage audit; no new live Army verification. Isobel remains provisional. Tags are selective, not exhaustive capability audits.
