## 1. The delivery cost — compare reliability, not just firepower

**The legal arrival matters more than nominal kills.** A Trojan horse outside the ARO layer may kill three 10-point Orders; a 50-point 'super dropper' failing insertion kills none.

| Method | Getting in | Worst common failure | Best targets/table | Why it wins / loses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Parachutist (DZ)** | **Automatic placement on a legal enemy-DZ table-edge surface**; no PH roll | **No Partial Cover on entry Order**, must respect 360/edge guards/mines/occupied footprint | Unprotected rear and side edge; LT/Engineer/EVO/cheerleaders | Most reliable **coordinate**; less geometric freedom if edge screened. |
| **Normal Parachutist** | Automatic legal **table-edge entry outside enemy DZ** | 1–3 follow-on Orders to actually reach support; vulnerable to edge guards on arrival | Side console, near-side support, weak flank | Better than Combat Jump when **approach exists** and no landing-roll risk needed. |
| **Combat Jump** | **PH roll** after placing on legal horizontal platform (no enclosed Objective Room); *no Partial Cover on landing Order* | **Failure places trooper at own DZ table edge as a Model**, removes associated deployables and marker/decoy opportunity | Protected rooftop/rear angle; avoid forced multi-ARO landing | Biggest choice of vectors; dramatic failure costs. Side/edge Parachutist on same model often better. |
| **Impersonation** | Deployment near opponent; **enemy-DZ deep entry needs legal roll/procedure** | Discover/Sensor/mine/template screen, opponent denies target access | Backline LT/Orders/premium TAG before fireline unfolds | Imposes **deployment tax even when never attacks**; catastrophic maximum but one-Wound floor. |
| **Infiltration, safe half** | **No roll** if in your half, often directly at midfield | Requires movement to breach enemy DZ; may be discovered | Central gate, approach to rear through blind lane | Usually strongest **reliable** way to use expensive Camo/Hidden assassins. |
| **Enemy-half Infiltration** | **PH−3 roll** or listed bracket PH substitute minus 3; cannot start *inside enemy DZ* | Failure to **own DZ edge** and loss of Marker/Hidden protection and deployables used | Direct weak flank to key target or objective | Only roll when immediate payoff decisively beats losing safe hidden attack potential. |
| **Fast ground / bike / flying VH** | Arrives from own deployment with movement Orders and legal LoF route | Repeated AROs, mines, Repeaters, Hackable/No Cover (for some VHs) | Poorly defended flank; second-turn attack after lane opens | Better **sustained threat** than one-shot drop once 2+ reachable targets exist, but cannot teleport past defenses. |
| **Hidden Deployment + forward position** | Usually safe marker/hidden in legal half; reveal on a chosen Order | Zero Order contribution while off-table, dangerous if your **LT** | Delayed assassination/turn-3 objective behind flank | Strong concealment, **not** free enemy-DZ deployment. |

### Success probabilities, *conditional arithmetic*, not battle simulations

For a single unopposed Normal D20 roll with eligible target value `T` between 1 and 20, **P(success)=T/20**. These are **delivery rolls only**: **not** the chance to survive an ARO, wound a target, or kill the LT.

| Example | Unsupported | Friendly Controlled Jump +3 | Opposing Controlled Jump −3 | Specific caveat |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **PH12 Combat Jump with no printed modifier** | **60%** | **75%** | **45%** | E.g. base PH12 Hellcat/Tiger/Delta *if no legal additional modifier*. |
| **PH13 Combat Jump** | **65%** | **80%** | **50%** | E.g. Rasyat; its **Parachutist (DZ)** alternative avoids all three risks. |
| **Caskuda Combat Jump(PH14)** | **70%** | **85%** | **55%** | **Pinned Onyx says PH14 on jump** despite physical PH16; don't use 80% base. |
| **Oniwaban enemy-half Infiltration(PH18−3)** | **75%** | **not applicable** | **not applicable** | Fails to own DZ edge and loses starting marker/hidden status. |
| **Shinobu enemy-half Infiltration(PH19−3)** | **80%** | **not applicable** | **not applicable** | Still cannot use Infiltration to *deploy inside enemy DZ*. |

**Video/source ambiguity:** [Dante Corregidor 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dNoJI8nPnIU) describes a **Hellcat 15/18 landing** (PH12 +3 on profile, then +3 EVO). The *abbreviated pinned Corregidor roster heading* names Combat Jump but **does not explicitly record its +3**. **Do not treat 75/90% as verified legal Hellcat odds until profile-specific modifier is checked in Army**; table uses conservative base PH12 60/75%. The +3 Controlled Jump rule itself is authoritative. The local [Combat Jump rule](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Combat_Jump.md) explicitly states **own-DZ edge on failure**; discard old 'scatter into worse position' commentary.

**Expected trade, not invented kill rate:** rough planning score  
`Net swing ≈ P(delivery) × [P(target removed | safe attack) × (target points + next-turn Orders denied + mission/support value) − attack/exit Order cost] − P(failed delivery) × (lost turn tempo + exposed own-DZ model's risk)`.  
This is a **decision framework**—no calibrated dice matchups or numeric casualties are claimed. If a successful 19pt BSG Hellcat *can* kill a 13pt Order and 20pt hacker but must trade itself, 33−19=**+14 printed points** before valuing removed Orders; at base PH12 the *maximum branch* is reached after 60%-chance placement **before** gunfight odds. A failed landing need not kill the Hellcat, but it can invalidate the **planned** strike. If the target is a 9pt flash REM whose loss removes no critical functionality, **three Orders spent chasing it may be a bad trade** even if kill succeeds.

### Ranking method — revised 2026-11-03

These are **strict ordinal recommendations for typical N5.3 tables**, *not measured tournament win rates*. A later number is not automatically a weaker fighter in other contexts. **Apex** asks whether the unit can actually threaten *valuable enemy-DZ targets* given realistic delivery, combat conversion, survival and follow-through; it does **not** rank a hypothetical perfect, undefended damage event. **Value** asks how often that result is worth the list points, SWC, Orders, support and failed-insertion risk. Give objective/scoring utility credit only when it is on the **same legal profile**.

| Factor | Apex emphasis | Value emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| **Entry and breach** | Legal enemy-DZ edge/IMP-1, safe markers/Hidden, landing roll, ARO/mine/hacker screen, movement Orders | Opportunity-cost of failed/late insertion and Orders to reach the first meaningful target |
| **Conversion** | Strong legal weapon band, Burst, ammo, surprise/CC and ability to affect real HI/TAG or high-value support | Realistic soft target/support trade-up, **not** invented average kills |
| **Follow-through** | Surviving/repositioning to threaten a second target, kill Order networks or turn control | Damage or scoring per full points+SWC commitment, repeatable usefulness and replacement options |
| **List fit** | Alternate mission/scoring path and defensive response imposed on opponent | Hidden SWC/Combat Group/EVO/repair taxes, overinvestment in a single gimmick |

**Tier order:** S+ (exceptional value), S (excellent), A+ (strong), A (credible/conditional), B (narrow), C (poor typical competitive investment); tiers are non-increasing down each ranking. **Special cases:** an A+ (niche) model can have extraordinary points-per-trade in a favorable matchup yet not be a reliable general enemy-DZ plan. **Caskuda deliberately stays B apex / C value**: its Explosion/high-STR ceiling is less relevant when a 30% base deployment failure, 64pt commitment and hack/ARO screens prevent safe payback.

**Delivery classes cannot be conflated:** **Parachutist (Deployment Zone)** is a direct *legal enemy-DZ table-edge* placement, not guaranteed survival; ordinary **Parachutist** is not; **Combat Jump** needs a landing roll; **IMP-2** is weaker deployment protection than IMP-1; **Hidden/FD(+20)** does not start inside enemy DZ; and a 20–15 bike or aerial VH must traverse a real route. Same-unit alternate profiles do **not** stack their weapon/skill/Fireteam benefits.

### Actual cost/expected-trade checkpoints

A fair comparison includes **(1) opportunity to deploy, (2) legal landing/deployment, (3) winning the attack, (4) damage/disable, (5) next-turn impact, (6) survival/reposition**. These are **six separate probabilities/resources**. Count an enemy trooper as killed **only** after applying burst, range, modifiers, saves and relevant immunity in actual game state. Disable (Isolated/IMM/Engaged/Targeted) may be as good as a kill for the mission, but is not points destroyed. *Avoid invented universal 'expected kills per unit'.*

**Mission and turn timing** alter trade value: On Turn 1, killing an 11pt Regular troop may remove its future Orders on Turns 2 and 3; on Turn 3 the same kill might only matter if it swings a zone or blocks scoring. A 33pt Van Zant that removes two 11pt troopers and one 15pt Hacker (37 printed) before dying is **+4 printed points**, but likely higher **tempo value** if 3 enemy Orders and hacking are removed. The same Van Zant killing one 10pt Regular before dying is **−23 printed points** despite the defender's activation cost. These **illustrative arithmetic scenarios** deliberately are **not forecasts**.

**Combat Group:** off-table airborne models **don't provide normal pooled Orders until on table** and spend their own Order to enter; [Tactical Awareness](../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Tactical_Awareness.md) on an entering Margot/Van Zant/Caskuda generates a **separate personal Tactical Order immediately on arrival**. A source may discuss more personal activations, but not extra unrestricted group Orders. Put **cheap one-and-done traders in a secondary group**, large sustained run in a group that can fund it, and preserve enough late Orders for mission.

## Quick army-build checklist

1. **Delivery mode legal?** Combat Jump PH/bonus, Parachutist **(DZ)** vs normal, Impersonation inside DZ roll, Infiltration **never into enemy DZ**, arrival LoF/cover restrictions.
2. **Preselect target chain:** LT **only if CoC/Warhorse/Mnemonica not defeating tempo goal**; otherwise Order generators, Engineer, Hacker, Fireteam Master, EVO, mission specialists.
3. **What is the exit Order?** A surviving 25pt striker that blocks 3 Orders next turn may outperform a 'hero' 60pt double kill that dies immediately.
4. **Can you achieve similar with cheaper model?** Bashi12 / Garuda18 / Hellcat19 / Teishin19 / Fiday23 / Rasyat25 vs premium Caskuda64 / Mirage60 / Sphinx93.
5. **Order pool allocated correctly?** Off-table no pooled Regular, **TA appears on entry**, Impetuous/Irregular/Coordinated use exact N5 rules; do not claim two free generic Orders from packaged team.
6. **Specialist or Lieutenant/CoC?** Verify exact profile, LT not left off-table/Hidden at LoL check, separate safe successor. **CoC shouldn't be the disposable attacker**.
7. **Is the goal mission scoring instead of killing?** Bring a genuine KHD/FO/Engineer/Paramedic option if target is a Classified/console, and reserve the turn-3 Orders to reach it.
