# Mission Specialists and Classified Runners — N5.3 Global Ranking

_Consolidated 26 November 2026 from the committed Mission Specialists and Classified Objective Runners guides. Editorial list-building ranking, not measured win rates. Profile data and coverage are carried forward from those guides; this is not a fresh Army/profile audit._

## Scope and ranking method

One global ordinal ranking combines practical objective scoring, points/SWC/Order efficiency and Classified versatility. Reward deployment, mobility, concealment, vision control, ability to clear defenders, survivability, WIP, role compression and real net Orders. Broad Classified coverage is a meaningful additional advantage, not a substitute for reaching an objective. A cheap backline Hacker can qualify for many cards without being a good hostile-console runner.

The initial unified order uses the previous four ranked lists as editorial evidence: mission tiers have base weights S+ 1.00, S .91, A+ .77, A .63, B+ .48, with a small within-list rank adjustment (up to .09). Classified rankings have a .82-to-.54 descending weight. The strongest mission or Classified score sets the baseline; add .10 times the weaker mission score, .055 times the weaker of the best mission and Classified scores, and .025 times the weaker Classified score. This rewards cross-role strength without letting theoretical card counts dominate. Unified tiers: S+ ≥ .985, S ≥ .88, A+ ≥ .73, A ≥ .59, otherwise B+. This is an initial consolidation heuristic, not a measured probability or an independent reassessment of every unit.

**Tags** describe strengths of at least one explicitly identified option in the row, not necessarily all options:

- `Apex`: retained from either former Apex ranking.
- `Value`: retained from either former Value ranking; former mission Value entries had a usable option costing at most 40 points.
- `Unique`: especially differentiated or uncommon mission package.
- `Toolbox`: significant secondary mission, combat, technical, command or network role compression.
- `Classified Runner`: previously ranked for Classifieds, or an audited exact option covering at least 10 of 20 cards. Card count is eligibility, not execution.
- `Airborne`: **Airborne Objective Specialist** — an actual Combat Jump or Parachutist scoring option, not merely a DZ attacker. Alternate FTO/forward-deployed profiles may lack it. Use this existing tag to filter the single ranking; no separate airborne-objective leaderboard.
- `Fireteam`: useful legal linked scoring option; check faction and exact FTO restrictions.
- `Budget`: a relevant affordable scoring option at approximately 20 points or less; not every option in the family qualifies.

**Profile rules:** FO, SO, HD, KHD, Doctor, Engineer and Paramedic qualify only as the actual mission specifies. CoC alone, Lieutenant alone, D-Charges and troop classification do not confer generic Specialist status. Separate loadouts remain separate; never stack their skills or transfer exact Classified counts between them. Non-Specialist profiles can still qualify for particular Classified cards. G:Jumper and other purchased packages obey actual Order and body restrictions. A dash or "Not audited here" does not mean zero eligible cards.

**Classified coverage:** retained from the supplied 20-card worksheet; count distinct eligible cards once and include cost ratios only where previously calculated. Veteran/Elite troop classification is distinct from the Veteran skill. Normal HD/HDplus includes Spotlight; a basic KHD does not unless explicitly upgraded. See the coverage rules and audit below.

**Companion-guide integration (26 November 2026):** [Rare Capabilities](rare-capabilities-and-unique-combinations-apex-value.md) and [Toolbox Units](jack-of-all-trades-toolbox-units-apex-value.md) supply additional mission-relevant profiles and `Unique`/`Toolbox` evidence. The prior ordinal sequence and tiers are retained for existing entries; newly admitted profiles are inserted editorially at comparable practical mission value. Non-specialist rare guns, command-only pieces, and utility packages without a qualifying scorer are not imported just for rarity. In particular, the Aquila SO is not the HMG, the Charontid HDplus is not the MSV3 option, and the Gearhead Cyberplug is not the Engineer/Hacker option. New entries without audited counts are not assigned speculative Classified totals. Tags on new entries reflect their scoring role rather than automatically inheriting combat-category tier placement.

**Airborne-objective integration (26 November 2026):** The former focused Value guide's five entries (Akalis, Garuda, Gao-Tarsos, Teishin and Cliff-Jumper) are all in the single ranking, with their specific scoring advantages and delivery caveats retained in their rows. Evaluate cost/SWC, Regular Order, actual jump/edge access, speed after arrival, survival, useful scoring skills and Orders in the correct Combat Group. Normal Parachutist does not permit enemy-DZ-edge entry. Do not independently count the `Airborne` tag in sectorial Top 3/5/10 standout percentages; it is part of the mission-specialist role. [Airborne/DZ attackers](airborne-and-enemy-dz-attackers-apex-value.md) remains a distinct attack-role guide, not an objective ranking.

## Choosing the actual scoring profile

**Do not stack separate options.** A Dasyu's FO29 with Deployable Repeater is not its KHD31; a Caliban's FO/SO/Engineer are distinct from its **non-scoring CoC29**. Raveneye **CoC+FO18**, BCA Magistrate **CoC+SO21**, and Tai Sheng **CoC+FO36/40** really do combine command succession with mission eligibility on the same selected profile. By contrast, Farzan CoC24, Caliban CoC29, Nexus CoC23, Myrmidon Officer CoC30–31, Kempeitai CoC17/20, Shukra CoC21, Léi Gōng CoC34, Kaeltar CoC, Hǎidào CoC24 and Hafza CoC20 are **not** generic button-pushers. Their separate FO/KHD/Engineer/SO options (when printed) may qualify.

**Mission scoring is not a single skill.** For regular consoles, prioritize the routes/Orders to physically interact. For Hacker tasks, a well-positioned HD/KHD may beat a superior cyber duelist that starts far away; a KHD also cannot automatically do all standard-HD programs. For Doctor/Engineer Classifieds, contact distance and the exact medical/repair skill matter; a Paramedic is not a Doctor. For carried objectives and zone holding, durability, cover, clearance, and the inability to keep a marker concealed while carrying an objective can reverse the order. Raw WIP affects the target roll but must not be confused with access or role legality.

**Important purchase caveats:** Posthuman Mk.1 and Mk.5 are a **legal G:Jumper purchase with shared Order rules**, not independent 13pt Regular troops. Drummer and Drumbot both have FO but act through synchronization; SiriusBot is a Repeater **without** FO. The Mirage-5 60pt package includes Margot's SO role and Duroc's separate Irregular body; no standalone 36pt Margot purchase. Irmandinho, Monstrucker, Hunzakut/Bashi and the airborne Raoul profile are affected by their Irregular status, while some sectorials change unit Order type or Fireteam options.

**Roster verification:** Scores and profile details follow the repository's pinned N5.3 sectorial rosters. Vanilla/sectorial permissions, printed specialist abilities and the exact costs may differ. The pinned Morat Zerat/Taryot summaries do not provide sufficient per-option Specialist qualification to rank those unit families as button-pushers. Basic infiltrating Rokot, Métro and Konduktor profiles are not their cheap FO/SO/Paramedic profiles. The Konduktor is a **Paramedic10**, not an Engineer.

## Rules and evidence guardrails

- **20-card source:** the supplied “List of Classifieds - Sheet1.csv” is the input inventory. This guide intentionally does **not** republish card text as an authoritative tournament document; check the current ITS packet and exact conditions before play.
- **Official FAQ:** [ITS FAQ](https://infinitythewiki.com/ITS_FAQ) says successful **Spotlight rolls** complete Telemetry/Designation as required, even without imposing Targeted. [Hacking Device rules](https://infinitythewiki.com/Hacking_Device) distinguish HD/HDplus from KHD upgrades.
- **Independent check:** [Infinity: The Classifieds profile leaderboard](https://www.infinitytheclassifieds.com/rankings) independently flags Zoe+Π-Well and many Veteran MI/Hacker combinations as exceptionally broad. Its public index identifies **v5.7.3**, whereas this repository's guides are pinned as **N5.3**. **Do not import website-specific point costs, troop upgrades or exact 20-card scores into the repository without reconciling the version.** These rankings prioritize the current **committed main-branch** profile text.
- **Classification audit:** linked roster summaries sometimes abbreviate unit data to MI/HI/SK. Veteran/Elite entries were checked against the current unit classification where explicit (e.g., Striders and Shikami) or cross-checked against the external unit/classification index; **reconfirm in the official Army builder for your active tournament version**, especially when moving a model across sectorials.
- **Concealment and position:** Hidden Deployment gives access but may withhold its Order until revealing. Camouflage does not eliminate the need to meet LoF/contact/HVT movement/target-state requirements.
- **Matchups matter:** An opponent with no valid enemy specialists, high-value HVT exposure, or isolated enemy-DZ scenery can make a theoretically broad candidate practically unhelpful. Build **two complementary runners** rather than relying on one expensive universal answer.


**Count rules:** distinct cards, not ways to solve the same card. Veteran/Elite classification is **separate** from the Veteran skill, Warhorse or Spec. Trained; MI/HI classification earns two additional cards. A normal HD/HDplus has Spotlight; a basic KHD does **not** unless the upgrade says so. KHD still counts as Hacker for Suspected Infiltration/Mapping/Data Scan/HVT: Espionage. **Predator** is counted for a damaging CC Weapon or D-Charges in CC Mode (the latter imposes Improvised **CC -6**); a **PARA-only CC Weapon** without D-Charges is *not* counted. The individual card can still fail for target, distance, timing, or enemy-interference reasons.

**Multi-body exception:** Posthuman **minimum 2, maximum 3 purchased Proxies** constitute a **single G:Jumper trooper** with shared activation/Order constraints. Both selected bodies can contribute *distinct qualifications* to a card union, but no extra Regular Orders or illegal combination of one Proxy's abilities with another's actions. Zoe and Π-Well are a distinct two-body paid package; only the actual bearer performs its qualified actions.
