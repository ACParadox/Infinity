## One-minute decisions

- **Best apex carrier and turn-3 closer:** **Shikami 42/0 or 47/0 (JSA)** — 15-5 MOV, 2W/ARM3, Mimetism(-6), Eclipse, Climbing Plus, Super-Jump and Martial Arts. Can **reach, take, leave and survive** a dangerous objective. **42** if mission-scoring comes first; **47** when additional gun/D-Charge matters. Expensive hackable HI: avoid making it the only scorer.
- **Best hidden/infiltrating opener:** **Dasyu FO 29/0 (ALEPH), Malignos FO 30/0 (Shas/Combined), Ninja KHD 26/0 (JSA)**. Hidden Deployment, Infiltration and Mimetism(-6) bypass early attack lanes and keep the option to push a distant button later. **A carrier generally cannot reenter Marker State while holding the mission object**; risk increases after pickup.
- **Best apex all-round combat hacker scorer:** **Asura HD+ 63/0.5 (ALEPH/OSS)** — WIP15, ARM5/NWI, MSV3, hacking/vision control and ranged presence. Outstanding **when those combat/cyber tools solve the same objective path**; otherwise two 20–30pt scorers are safer.
- **Best apex medical/technical scorer:** **Parvati Doctor+Engineer 36/0 (ALEPH)** for durable, mobile recovery and two specialist categories. **Best value two-role support:** **Sophotect 25/0 (ALEPH), Med-Tech Obsidon 21/0 (Combined)**. **Posthuman Mk.1 Engineer/Doctor 13/0** is exceptional *inside legal G:Jumper package*; not an independent 13pt troop plus free extra Order.
- **Best budget button infrastructure:** **Ghulam FO 12/0 (Haqq)**; **Pathfinder 14/0, TA 17/0 (PanO)**; **Daleth/M-Drone 15/0, TA 18/0 (ALEPH/Combined)**; **Barid FO 12/0/KHD 14/0 (Vanilla Haqq)**. These free points and slots while adding Sensor/Repeater/network or FO Classified tasks.
- **Best midfield CoC scorer:** **Farzan CoC 24/0** for infiltration and LT insurance; **Caliban CoC 29/0** for Camo/FD+20/CC threat; **Nexus CoC 23/0** for legal link/NCO/succession; **Kempeitai CoC 17/0** for lowest-cost JSA insurance.
- **Best specialist LT value:** **Magistrate Strategos LT 19/0 (Kestrel/Vanilla PanO)** — Specialist Operative/Counterintelligence + an Order converted into a Regular in LT group. **Best fighting specialist LT:** **Hatamoto LT 36–39/+1 SWC (JSA)** — mobility/combat + Specialist Operative with personal LT(+1) Orders; expensive to lose.

**Scoring rubric:** **Apex** = maximum realistic ability to **get there, legally activate, survive, keep scoring and do a second job**. **Points value** = *mission work per incremental points/SWC/Orders* versus the faction's available Regular alternative. **S** exceptional when conditions match, **A** strong, **B** situational, **C** not normally preferred. A list often needs a **midfield opener, mobile worker and protected turn-3 closer**, not three versions of its strongest attacker. WIP alone doesn't fix transit Orders or exposure.

## 7. When each specialist *wins* its role

| Situation | Best archetype, examples | Reason / counterplay |
|---|---|---|
| Permanent one-time console | **Cheap FO** Ghulam12/Pathfinder14 or already-near **Farzan22/Shrouded24** | Spending 40pt on survival of a model that need press once is often wasteful. |
| End-of-game carry/extract | **Shikami42**, durable Sukeul/hatamoto, protected fast bike when objective rules allow | Escape/survival, not the WIP, is the main cost; **carrier may lose Marker State**. |
| T3 surprise/objective steal | **Dasyu29/Malignos30/Ninja26**, independent group of Orders | Hidden needs legal deployment/reveal; reserve movement/console Orders, not merely hidden model. |
| Classified-heavy | **Doctor+Engineer** Sophotect25/Med-Tech21/Mk1 13, then FO and KHD | Actual task-category coverage beats four generic Specialist Operatives. |
| TAG/REM repair + buttons | **Engineer** Mk1/Artalis/Evader/Krit/Med-Tech and Servant | Valuable if repeated repair protects an 80pt attacker; one servant isn't a button pusher. |
| Heavy infantry/human recovery | **Doctor(+3)** Asawira35, Sophotect25, Ghulam15 | Heal VITA, not STR; need path/Servant and healthy Doctor. |
| KHD threat on console | **Deva20/Dokkaebi22/Tuareg28/Ninja26** | Kill/deny hacker, then score. Separate HD handles anti-TAG programs. |
| MSV ARO on lane | **Shikami Eclipse**, HD+ White Noise / legal smoke escort | Force view/approach advantage; no blanket immunity. |
| Enemy Camo/mines | **Pathfinder/Daleth/M-Drone Sensor** and Coyote MSV1 | Clear route instead of sacrificing a 30pt Hidden scorer to Discover attempts. |
| Attacking Fireteam already advancing | **Tai Sheng FO/NCO**, **Evader/Artalis Engineer**, legal linked Ghulam/Deva | Shared move pays for specialist delivery; **check chart anchors/team leader**. |
| LT vulnerable + mission button | **Farzan/Caliban/Nexus/Magistrate/CoC Raveneye** | CoC must still survive so choose separate angles. |
| 3 consoles far apart | **3 geographically separated Specialist types** | Independent approaches beat one “super-scoring” model isolated in wrong group. |

**Practical 300pt default:** one cheap FO/Sensor or backline Doctor; one infiltrating Camo FO/KHD; one Engineer/Doctor that also supports your real army; one preserved fast/durable closer. **3–5 specialists** is generally a useful starting band; count **independent routes, mission task types and last-turn Order pools**, not raw labels.

## 8. Opposing opinions with actual transcript evidence

1. **Expensive toolbox vs cheap button-pusher.** [Infinity the Dante — *Toolbox Units Guide N5*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q5kyCDufa8c) evaluates Evader AP Spitfire/Engineer and De Fersen as good **only if their gun + specialist/network tools both find work**: paying 30pt to do one cheap job is inefficient. [Dice Gods — *N5 List Building for a Mission*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vlqWQq7sllI) favors **the mission and combat-group Order plan**, multiple independent specialists and redundancies over collecting good units in isolation. **Both agree** to pay for second roles only when the route uses them.
2. **Hidden Camo scoring vs plain durable bodies.** [MetaChemistry E139 — Shasvastii event winner (Apr 2026)](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6FErkreAsQoUKosH4WkdPW) explains why double Speculo/triple Caliban/Shrouded can be great into **going-second late score**, but also says some missions **don't** favor Camo. **Counter:** multiple 24pt Shrouded FO/cheap bots may outperform 30pt hidden specialist if the mission requires repeated permanent consoles or a different set of lanes.
3. **Support piece follows attacker vs many independent scorers.** [Tactical Awareness — Adepticon winning ALEPH (Apr 2025)](https://open.spotify.com/episode/0YhA9K7K2xJkiCzqI7PcbV) says **Maximus clears the way and Danavas walks behind pressing successive buttons**. The [repository ALEPH analysis](../factions/vanilla-aleph.md) instead highlights independent Mk1/Naga/Garuda FO as score backups so expensive attacker loss doesn't end mission.

## 10. Fast scoring audit

1. Who is *legally* a Specialist for the **exact** mission? CoC (Non Specialist) and LT alone are traps; Specialist Operative does not cover type-specific Doctor/Engineer tasks.
2. Count **approach + clearing defenders + interacting + escaping Orders** at the actual table, not just WIP.
3. Bring at least **two independent routes** and a **preserved Turn-3 closer** with actual Combat Group resources.
4. Cover Classifieds across **FO/hacking, Doctor/Engineer, CoC, D-Charges, Sensor/MSV and body-type/CC**, rather than four versions of the same job.
5. Decide whether the unit's **other job** (repeaters, medical/tech recovery, NCO, CoC, mines, MSV, guns) will really get used.
6. Verify mutual exclusivity of loadouts, legal faction/Fireteam and **G:Jumper/Peripheral no-extra-Orders** caveats.
7. If first objective carrier dies or LT becomes Isolated, **can the list still score its last possible objective with a living Specialist**?
