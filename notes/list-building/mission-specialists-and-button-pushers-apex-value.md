# Mission Specialists / Button Pushers — N5.3 Global Apex and Value Rankings

**Two rankings, all specialist types together.** The same unit can rank very differently in the two tables. **Apex** measures maximum *realistic mission contribution*: legal scoring, reliable route to an objective (Hidden Deployment, Infiltration, FD, Parachutist/Combat Jump, marker and vision control), WIP, survival and ability to fight/repair/hack/guard after scoring. Cost is secondary, not irrelevant. **Value** measures the same mission utility **per points, SWC, and Orders**, including the value of a Regular Order and any compulsory support package. A more expensive gunfighter is not automatically an Apex button-pusher; a cheap backfield WIP specialist is not automatically an excellent midfield runner.

**Profile rules:** FO, SO, HD, KHD, Doctor, Engineer and Paramedic confer mission eligibility only as allowed by the specific mission. **WIP alone, CoC alone, Lieutenant alone, the “Specialist Trained Troops” classification and D-Charges do *not* grant generic Specialist eligibility.** A slash or “or” in a unit-family label means **separate selectable profiles** unless explicitly called out as a combined skill on the same profile. Multi-body purchases must obey their actual shared-Order and roster requirements.

**Scope:** All previously ranked *legal* specialist profiles across objective runners, FO/SO/Paramedics, Hacker/KHD scorers, Doctor/Engineers, eligible CoC scorers and qualified Lieutenant scorers have been consolidated at the **unit-family level**, with no role-specific leaderboard. Profiles that are only non-Specialist CoC support are excluded from the rankings. Tiers are relative across all types, not measured win rates; scenario-specific Classifieds and specialist permissions override an overall rating. **S = exceptional ceiling; A+ = excellent; A = useful; B = situational.** The Value table also uses **S+** for outstanding efficiency.

## Choosing the actual scoring profile

**Do not stack separate options.** A Dasyu's FO29 with Deployable Repeater is not its KHD31; a Caliban's FO/SO/Engineer are distinct from its **non-scoring CoC29**. Raveneye **CoC+FO18**, BCA Magistrate **CoC+SO21**, and Tai Sheng **CoC+FO36/40** really do combine command succession with mission eligibility on the same selected profile. By contrast, Farzan CoC24, Caliban CoC29, Nexus CoC23, Myrmidon Officer CoC30–31, Kempeitai CoC17/20, Shukra CoC21, Léi Gōng CoC34, Kaeltar CoC, Hǎidào CoC24 and Hafza CoC20 are **not** generic button-pushers. Their separate FO/KHD/Engineer/SO options (when printed) may qualify.

**Mission scoring is not a single skill.** For regular consoles, prioritize the routes/Orders to physically interact. For Hacker tasks, a well-positioned HD/KHD may beat a superior cyber duelist that starts far away; a KHD also cannot automatically do all standard-HD programs. For Doctor/Engineer Classifieds, contact distance and the exact medical/repair skill matter; a Paramedic is not a Doctor. For carried objectives and zone holding, durability, cover, clearance, and the inability to keep a marker concealed while carrying an objective can reverse the order. Raw WIP affects the target roll but must not be confused with access or role legality.

**Important purchase caveats:** Posthuman Mk.1 and Mk.5 are a **legal G:Jumper purchase with shared Order rules**, not independent 13pt Regular troops. Drummer and Drumbot both have FO but act through synchronization; SiriusBot is a Repeater **without** FO. The Mirage-5 60pt package includes Margot's SO role and Duroc's separate Irregular body; no standalone 36pt Margot purchase. Irmandinho, Monstrucker, Hunzakut/Bashi and the airborne Raoul profile are affected by their Irregular status, while some sectorials change unit Order type or Fireteam options.

**Roster verification:** Scores and profile details follow the repository's pinned N5.3 sectorial rosters. Vanilla/sectorial permissions, printed specialist abilities and the exact costs may differ. The pinned Morat Zerat/Taryot summaries do not provide sufficient per-option Specialist qualification to rank those unit families as button-pushers. Basic infiltrating Rokot, Métro and Konduktor profiles are not their cheap FO/SO/Paramedic profiles. The Konduktor is a **Paramedic10**, not an Engineer.
