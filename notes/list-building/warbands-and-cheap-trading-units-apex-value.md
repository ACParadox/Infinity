## Fast verdict — the role determines the winner

- **Best generic dirt-cheap value:** **Ghazi Muttawi'ah 5–6/0**, **Galwegian Chain Rifle 5/0**, **Shaolin Monk Chain Rifle 5/0**, **Morlock Chain Rifle + Smoke + E/M CCW 6/0**, **Yuan Yuan Chain+Smoke 6/0** (only eligible factions). For *one high-risk trade*, favor the **right weapon** over 1pt differences: Ghazi E/Marat attacks Hackable targets; a 5pt Galwegian cannot.
- **Best reliable anti-HI/TAG 5–10pt *state* threat:** **Ghazi E/Marat 5/0 (Haqq/Bahram profile-dependent Smoke access)**, **Morlock E/M CCW 6/0** and **Daylami Panzerfaust 6/0 / Camo+Panzerfaust 8/0**. The Daylami can **threaten at range**; templates and E/M CC generally require proximity and delivery.
- **Best *Regular* smoke + violent assault:** **Dāturazi Chain Rifle(+1B)14/0**, **Varangian 10/0 (Kosmoflot) or 12/0 (O-12/eligible)** and **Jaguar Chain Rifle+Smoke10/0 (Corregidor)**. They supply **Regular Order infrastructure**, not just sacrificial Irregular activations. Dāturazi also brings MA4/Warhorse/DA CC; Jaguar supplies Dogged/link/MSV2 Intruder smoke.
- **Best Eclipse enabler:** **Makaul HFT+Eclipse+Viral/DA CC14/0 (Tohaa)**. High **team utility** rather than guaranteed TAG kill; Eclipse blocks MSV, and Heavy Flamethrower can trade with more valuable enemies without needing a winning BS roll. **Myrmidon Officer CoC30/.5** adds Eclipse, Mimetism(-6) and NWI at a very different price.
- **Best budget close-combat suicide body in link context:** **Diablo 8/0 or 9/0** (Corregidor) — CC23, Berserk, **Trench-Hammer, Transmutation, nominal 2W**, high ceiling versus unprepared HI/TAG when FT Master Lobo/Correctional Fireteam makes the delivery practical. **Do not falsely count the Diablo's printed Irregular/Impetuous Order as a generic Regular**.
- **Best cheap resilient charge:** **Cameronian 18/0**, **Dog-Warrior21/0**, **McMurrough23/0** — smoke/chain/grenades plus multi-Wound close-in pressure; McMurrough offers CC23 and AP+DA, a credible sustained attacker. Higher apex/consistency than 5pt bodies but lower pure points ratio.
- **Best non-suicidal utility trader:** **Irmandinho Engineer + Chain Rifle + Smoke + D-Charges8/0** (Ariadna variants) — survives by contributing repairs/technical Classifieds even without attacking. **Carmen Johns + Bâtard17/0** includes fast Smoke, Mimetism/Dogged and synchronized Sensor/CC; **Desperado7/0** instead maximizes quick bike Smoke/Chain threat cheaply.
- **Sectorial-specific regularity:** **O-12 and Kosmoflot Varangians are Regular+Impetuous** in their pinned roster, whereas **White Company Varangians are explicitly Irregular+Impetuous**; same price does *not* mean same pooled Order value. **Shindenbutai Ioann Bann30** differs from 32pt Smoke-bearing Tohaa/Starmada options.

**Extra cheap contenders:** **Kum6/0** (Smoke motorcycle), **Libertos8/0** (one-use Camo+Dogged), **Minescorp Jackal FTO6/0** (Panzerfaust/LRL Irregular scaffold), **Motorized Bounty Hunter8–9/0** (bike Riotstopper/Chain-colt) compete on different movement/LoF angles. Their **actual sectorial roster** matters more than a broad nickname.
**Important exception:** **Muttawi'ah 5pt E/Marat in Hassassin Bahram printed current roster has NO Smoke on that particular option**; Vanilla Haqq's abbreviated roster says **E/Marat+Smoke**. Keep them faction/profile-specific until the underlying exact profile is reconciled. **6pt Jammer Ghazi in Bahram likewise has NO Smoke**, while the 7–8pt shotgun options do. Do not merge abilities into an imaginary 5–6pt all-in-one.
- **Not a warband but an apex cheap trade:** **Dahshat/Hassassin Daylami Camo Panzerfaust8/0** can force 50–100pt TAG/HI to expend Orders clearing/avoiding a concealed Disposable shot; 5pt Rokot/Flashbot is not the same job. Attack *functionality* (IMM/Isolated, Fireteam integrity, Orders and objective route), not only printed kills.

## What the tiers measure

**Apex** = total achievable *battle swing* on realistic terrain (breaking a premium unit, enabling a gunfighter with Smoke/Eclipse, denying the enemy orders, retaining utility/mission contribution). **Value** = contribution per *actual list* points, SWC, Order and opportunity cost. **S** standout for its stated role; **A** strong, **B** narrower, **C** only if specific counter/faction need. They are conditional across **different legal rosters**, not independent faction-strength ratings.

Reliability has 3 separate factors: **(a) delivery** (free Impetuous Move, speed, Smoke/Eclipse, safe lanes, peripheral/FT), **(b) attack effect** (Direct Template forcing saves, CC Face-to-Face or Berserk trade, E/M/Adhesive/Panzerfaust) and **(c) enemy response** (mines, cornerguard, Sensor, ARO, dodge, Warhorse, template, immobilization, surviving support). There is **no universal probability of '6pt Morlock kills a TAG'**; even 70pt victims may retain 3 Wounds/STR and repair support.

**Marginal-value check**: `EV of the exchange = P(reach) × P(effect|reach) × [enemy future functionality lost + Orders denied + mission swing] − our spent Orders − destroyed points/Orders/specialists`. Quantify the dice for a **specified target, range and ARO** before assigning numeric success. A 5pt attacker eating two 20pt pooled Orders to reach an 11pt cheerleader may still underperform; a 6pt E/M disabling a 70pt TAG for one turn may be a game-winner even when *zero points* are destroyed.

**Common trap:** a **Direct Template** avoids needing to win a BS Face-to-Face to *hit a model in its template*, but the target may Dodge, make Saves, ignore/mitigate specific Ammunition, or force the carrier to die before later missions. **E/M/Jammer/Adhesive produce disabling/Order denial, not automatically printed points destroyed**. An **E/M CCW does not have the reach** of E/Marat or Panzerfaust. Natural Born Warrior/Warhorse/Total Immunity/engineering and link support affect matchups.

## 3. Best into *which* valuable targets?

| Target/problem | First cheap answers | Why/what trade you seek | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **70–120pt Hackable TAG/HI on an exposed lane** | **Ghazi E/Marat5** if legal entry, **Morlock E/M CC6** if safely touching, **Jaguar Adhesive11**, **Daylami Panzerfaust6–8** at range | Immobilization/Isolated/E-M/adhesion may strip activation or attack plan; Engineer recovery then becomes target. | Rely on Chain Rifle damage alone to reliably delete multiple ARM7 Wounds/STR. |

| **MSV2 sniper or Smoke-counter gun castle** | **Makaul Eclipse14**, **Myrmidon Officer Eclipse30/.5**, other true **Eclipse** tools | Eclipse blocks LoF **even through MSV**; pair with a legal burst/CC assault route. | Pretend **ordinary Smoke** is absolute protection vs MSV or against all ARO mechanisms. |
| **Friendly MSV2 ranged gun needs favourable odds** | **Dāturazi14 + Yaogat**, **Jaguar10 + Intruder**, **Varangian + eligible MSV** | Repeated Smoke can turn a balanced shootout into low-odds opponent ARO. | Spend Smoke Orders when both players' visors/interactions nullify the benefit. |
| **Advancing an objective specialist past close ARO** | **Makaul14 Eclipse with Triad**, **Ghazi5 Smoke**, **Galwegian5 Smoke**, **Irmandinho8 Smoke**, **Myrmidon16 Smoke** | Low investment changes **mission geometry**; the attacker needn't kill. | Sacrifice the only late Specialist because a 5pt warband can theoretically reach CC. |
| **Enemy infiltration Camo/Impersonator** | **Carmen+Bâtard17 Sensor**, cheap Chain/template guard, **Gaki4** as expendable probe | Force opponent to spend Discover/movement or trade into low-value body. | Expose a premium attacker to an unidentified marker without a cheap screen. |
| **Enemy Warhorse-protected target** | **Panzerfaust/direct lethal, AP/DA/EXP, immobilizing states that actually apply** | Warhorse **cannot become Isolated**; rely on attacks or other disabling effects that apply. | Buy Ghazi Jammer with the assumption Isolated cripples Warhorse. |
| **Mission Turn 3 scoring dominates** | **Irmandinho8 Engineer**, **Makaul Triad**, **Myrmidon Officer**, **Carmen sensor**, preserved linked specialist | Preserve ability to **push buttons/recover/D-Charge/screen**; suicide only if it actually swings score. | Treat every affordable killer as interchangeable with a legal Specialist. |

## 4. Transcript-backed disagreements — don't force one consensus

1. **Dāturazi as independent warband versus list-wide Smoke/MSV engine.** [Robert Shepherd, *Morat N5 faction focus*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QwcqdXMtDXI) (full YouTube MCP transcript checked, 2025) stresses Smoke+Yaogat MSV2 to **win different gunfights rather than relying on BS12/13**; [Tactical Awareness S3E13 *Morats*](https://open.spotify.com/episode/7HZuzXo3FDUr6Eqp08TzjR) (full podcast MCP, ~10–15 and 27–37m) highlights **Oznat+Gakis/Pretas Fireteam Master** alternative and Krakot brute trades. **When choose Dāturazi?** Need independent *Regular* smoke+CC. **When Oznat/Hungries?** Cheapest legal order density/support launcher. Beware old talk of Morat Eclipse—pinned N5.3 Rasyat has Eclipse, Dāturazi normal Smoke.
2. **Makaul still premium versus Mirrorball replacing Smoke duty.** [Tactical Awareness S3E37 *Tohaa*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i1tcqWpV9jQ) / [podcast](https://open.spotify.com/episode/5zhPiR44EzbQ4GFKkICRvG) (both full MCP transcripts read; ~8–10 and 44m) makes *very strong pro-Kriigel Mirrorball* case (WIP14 linked repeatable Zone control), while discussing **Makaul** as a useful but less necessary traditional Eclipse Triad unit. **Choose Makaul** if HFT/CC/Eclipse and Triad movement also matter; **Mirrorball** if repeated safe screen itself wins objective approach. See [existing Tohaa faction analysis](../factions/tohaa.md) for the conflicting 2026 whole-faction reviews; do not treat an old episode as current Pheroware rules text.
3. **Cheap Diablo suicide versus deliver with a Correctional Lobo.** [WIP12 E208 *Corregidor post-Mazebreaker*](https://open.spotify.com/episode/342lz18SRGSZ8QWf1Zg3Z9) (full transcript checked, ~87–89, 115–125m) strongly praises **8pt Diablo Berserk+Trench-Hammer** and tests Jaguar+Diablo vs Lobo packages. Other players insist **Smoke via Jaguar and protected attack lane** matter more than rushing early. Pinned roster calls Diablo **Irregular+Impetuous**—transcript assumptions about easy pooled Orders cannot override it. **Choose one-shot Diablo** if the target is close; **Jaguar/Lobo** when it needs crossing and the rest of a team also benefits.
4. **Dog-Warrior as 21pt CC missile versus midboard speculative-fire tool.** [Robert Shepherd *Ariadna CanCon 2026*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTqYE_FddwQ) (full YouTube transcript checked) explicitly says Dog-Warrior's **PH-based grenade Speculative Fire** and self-funded approach matter **more** to his tournament list than simply charging into CC; [Tactical Awareness *Kosmoflot*](https://open.spotify.com/episode/0FZYrBdbYi3bNLX0Xbdl3l) (full podcast, ~2–12 and 39–49m) prefers many distributed smoke and sometimes a Bear/Varangian/Mirage threat. **Take Dog-Warrior** to dislodge targets that cannot be shot; **take 7pt Desperado/8pt Irmandinho** for cheapest lane control if there is no good speculative target.

## Quick list test

- **Pick one concrete trade first:** 5pt E/Marat into TAG, 6pt Chain into clustered Orders, 8pt Diablo into weak-CC HI, 14pt Eclipse for a scorer's safe crossing, or 21pt Dog-Warrior Specfire into dug-in ARO.
- **Pick a realistic path and Order source:** Irregular+Impetuous self-funds only itself; a Regular warband can contribute to *other models*, and a linked **FT Master** follows its specific chart and team leader restrictions.
- **Check weapon/profile:** E/Marat vs Jammer vs Smoke **not bundled**; Gaki 4 has AP CC and Explode **not Chain Rifle**; Preta7 has Chain+Dogged; Shaolin5 Shock versus Shaolin6 DA.
- **Check reactive counter:** 360°/templates/mines, MSV vs ordinary Smoke, Warhorse vs Isolated, hacker/Engineer repair, enemy MA/NBW/Guard. **Berserk extends threat but does not automatically survive AROs**.
- **Calculate the trade at mission time:** 2 killed cheap Orders on Turn 1 may deny ~4 later activations, but killing two on Turn 3 may score nothing. Does your attack **expose your last scorer or another irreplaceable unit**?
- **End the attack when value falls:** if it takes 3 pooled Orders for a fragile 6pt body to reach one 10pt Order, save it as a screen rather than feed an irrelevant target.
