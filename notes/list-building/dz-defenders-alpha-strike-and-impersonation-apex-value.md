# Deployment-Zone Defenders — N5.3 Apex, Value, and Anti-Alpha-Strike Guide

**Reactive hacking and Repeater placement:** [Network Projection apex/value](network-projection-repeaters-pitchers-apex-value.md) distinguishes passive DZ Repeaters from Turn-0 infiltration and long-range/airborne deployment. A cheap Repeater alone does not stop a nonhackable/IMP attacker; pair with actual HD/KHD target coverage and physical template/mine/Discover defenses.

_23 October 2026 · Tactical **qualitative tiers**, not tournament win rates. Exact costs, options, AVA, Training and Fireteams come from this repository's pinned **current N5.3** sectorial references (Army September 2026); the N5.3 rules/FAQ supersede dated video or podcast claims. Unit availability **cannot be transported** from sectorial to Vanilla or another sectorial._

**Purpose:** protect cheerleader Regular Orders, Lieutenant, hacker, Engineer, Doctor, support REMs and scoring reserve from **Parachutist (especially DZ-edge), Combat Jump, enemy-half Infiltration, fast ground or rooftop bypass, Impersonation and close-range alpha strikes**—preferably while providing a **Regular Order, FO/Sensor, recovery, hacker coverage or credible later counterattack**.

## Executive verdict — choose the *intrusion*, not one generic “best guard”

- **Best anti-Impersonator small defender (apex + value): Tohaa Kaauri 11/0 E/M Minelayer.** Biometric Visor, Sixth Sense, MSV1, enhanced Discover, Nanopulser(+1B), SMG and a turn-zero E/M mine all on an ordinary **Regular** body. Guards a *corner and reveal square*, not an entire back edge; 1 STR makes it fragile.
- **Best anti-Impersonator nonlethal alternative: O-12/Starmada Bluecoat Adhesive + Heavy Riotstopper 13/0.** Biometric, Sixth Sense, Discover(+6), Natural Born Warrior and close-range capture options; Regular Order. More about **punishing the reveal/CC approach** than killing an armored dropper at range. Starmada **AVA4**, vanilla O-12 **AVA2**.
- **Best cheap all-round rear/side ARO: Warcor 3/0 with 360º Visor.** A Flash Pulse from every arc can waste an elite attacker’s Order. **Irregular**: it does *not* provide a shared Regular Order. The **3pt Sixth Sense Warcor is another option**, **not** a simultaneous 360º+Sixth Sense Warcor.
- **Best persistent 360º escort/gun: Sierra or Hùsòng Thunderbolt(+1B) 18/0, Zayin/Reaktion 19/0, Q-Drone 20/.5** (army-dependent). **Total Reaction + 360º** contests normal flank and airborne attackers, remains a Regular Order and can be repaired; heavy expenditure/hacking/smoke or pie-slicing can still remove it. Put it in an actual crossfire, not on an isolated sacrificial roof.
- **Best mine + utility at home:** **O-12/Starmada/Military Orders Raveneye 12/0 E/M or Cybermine Minelayer+FO+Deactivator**, **Acontecimento Regular 13/0 Shock Minelayer+Sensor+Triangulated Fire**, **Kestrel Fennec 14/0 Shock Minelayer+Thunderbolt(+1B)**. All buy turn-zero denial and an on-table Regular Order for little or no SWC.
- **Best two-body close escort:** **NCA Auxilia 15/0 with Auxbot** (FO+Auxbot also 15/0; alternate FO 18/0), or **Tohaa Chaksa Auxiliar FTO 10/0 HFT+Sensor+Baggage**. Auxilia **Controller + synchronized peripheral** covers two angles but *not two independent Regular Orders*; Chaksa guards and reloads from the rear while supporting scoring/classified roles.
- **Best disposable “kill only a cheap thing” screen:** **Varuna Helot LRL 10/0 FTO**, or **12/0 one-use Camo LRL**, and **Hassassin Daylami LSG+Panzerfaust 6/0** / **5/0 LSG** or **Kestrel Jackal LRL/Panzerfaust 6/0**. **Irregular** units are excellent sacrifices but are not equal to 10–14pt Regular support troops for Order economy.
- **Best budget flexible two-body 360º option:** **Kestrel Drummer FO+Drumbot-5 21/0**: 360º controller, synchronized 360º/Mimetism bot, FO/Flash and midgame actions. Competes with **28/1.5 FO+MULTI Sniper** and premium 40pt gunbots. Use if the second body can actually cover a different angle or mission.
- **Best full defense is a *three-layer package*:** perimeter/marker/mine *before* arrival, 360º or short-ranged ARO at arrival/extraction, nonlethal/template + second defender covering the **next** square, with vital cheerleaders out of chained shotgun/template lines.

### Rules that materially change anti-Impersonation rankings

1. **360º Visor** merely extends the user's LoF from 180º to **360º**. It is not a Biometric Visor, Sixth Sense, or automatic Discover.
2. **Biometric Visor** ignores the IMP-1 WIP−3 Discover modifier; a **successful** Discover against IMP-1 cancels Impersonation outright, and it ignores specified Impersonation/Holoecho Surprise Attack modifiers. It does **not automatically detect** a marker or guarantee an ARO when one isn't legal.
3. **Mines trigger on eligible enemy Models **or Markers** in their trigger areas**, including when the relevant marker performs the triggering action and no exemption applies. They have 360º LoF **but a template/total-cover trigger geometry**: a mine beside a cheerleader may be much worse than one behind the entry corner.
4. **CrazyKoalas/MadTraps with Boost do *not* trigger from Camouflage or IMP-1/IMP-2 Markers**. They threaten once the target is a legal **Model**. Their path can be blocked. Thus **a CrazyKoala is not a substitute for a mine or Biometric guard against a still-masked Fiday/Speculo**.
5. **Minelayer** predeploys its eligible carried mine/perimeter weapon for no Active-Turn Order **only on the chosen profile**, within permitted deployment geometry/ZoC; N5.3 forbids enemy troopers/Camo markers in the deployable trigger area (or ZoC for Perimeter). **Carrying a Mine or Koala without Minelayer does not do this**; Place Deployable, Mine Dispenser, Drop Bears, Pitchers, and Suppressive Fire normally cost setup Orders.
6. Hidden Deployment can create a frightening second ARO, but a hidden model **does not contribute its normal Order** while off-table. **Nonlethal ≠ harmless:** Flash Stunned / Adhesive or Riotstopper hindrance can make the attack run fail without any Wound, depending on saving rolls.
7. **No layout grants absolute safety:** Combat Jump can find covered landing squares, enemy-DZ Parachutist accesses edges, Impersonation allows dangerous advanced starting positions, and ground assault bypasses exposed long AROs. **Deploy cheerleaders dispersed and facing different sectors** so one kill does not become four.

### Military Orders — unusually efficient home guards (not just Knights)

**Raveneye 12/0** (AVA1 in MO) is **Minelayer + FO + Deactivator**, with choice of initial **E/M Mine** or **Cybermine**: cheap Regular 0-SWC anti-AD/close-approach deterrence. **Order Sergeant ESCORT 17/0 with Auxbot** adds an independent *controller+peripheral* corner fork; unlike a normal FTO Order Sergeant it is **not Fireteam-legal by default**. **Coadjutor Crosier MULTI Sniper+E/M Mine Minelayer 19/1** is a dual long ARO + predeployed home trap, but costs 1 SWC. **Warcor 360º 3/0** watches DZ edges; **Sierra 18/0 TR+360º** provides a stronger repairable Regular gun. **Knight of Santiago FTO 31/0 KHD+TinBot** can protect its own legal team and provide 360º but is **not economical as a disposable DZ guard**, and the 36–38pt Combat Jump profiles cannot join Fireteams. Select **Raveneye + one angled cheap sentry/escort**, not a costly defensive knight pile.

## 3. Setup: defending actual cheerleaders, not decorative lines

**Deploy in depth:**

1. **Edge/landing layer:** face a 360º Flash/TR model across one *likely* DZ-edge entry and the rooftop/exit squares a Combat Jumper would exploit. Another cheap guard faces the *other* high-value side; don't try to cover an empty edge with five gunfighters.
2. **Breach layer:** put Minelayer mines around the **corner they must turn after arriving**, respecting N5.3 initial placement; pair with a separate Bluecoat/Kaauri/Ikadron/Auxbot DTW/nonlethal or Biometric threat. **One mine on the same table edge as the enemy arrival is not enough.**
3. **Asset layer:** place regular-order cheerleaders, LT, hacker/Engineer and Guided/EVO support **behind** overlapping guards, separated such that a single LSG/BSG template, chain attack or Speculo close-combat chain cannot hit/approach several. Give each important defender a retreat/out-of-LoF square; preserve a forward scorer and midgame counterattacker.

**Cheerleader exposure test:** for each trooper providing an Order, ask: *Could a 19pt Hellcat/Tiger/Delta/Teishin BSG or 33pt Van Zant enter one square and attack two support models before I can ARO?* If yes, rotate one sentry, move a mine, **split supports** or add an independent 360º guard. Save points for the attack and the mission after defensive coverage is adequate.

2. **Fennec Mine/Thunderbolt versus FO/Sensor/Disco.** [Robert Shepherd — Kestrel N5 faction focus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8lyUk12Ed28) (YouTube MCP transcript read) advocates **14pt zero-SWC Minelayer** as an unusually efficient DZ mine and backup gun. [Irregular Order #52 — Kestrel review](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4RNxiaI1k5xXBVzVHxwnB9) (podcast MCP transcript read) discusses an exposed 360º Drummer/Drumbot and 2026 gameplay: **different jobs**. **Pick Fennec** to close a DZ entry with a mine; **Drummer** when multi-angle and later mission activation justify 21–28+pt.
3. **Auxilia/Peacemaker controller-bot fork vs static mine.** [WIP12 #201 — NCA with Trent](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3FT1No7zFHzpj7WC63hcCb) (podcast MCP transcript read) values synchronized Auxbots and prefers the **27pt +1B Peacemaker** active attack package over the **24/.5 Minelayer** passive screen; extra movement/short-range burst can be worth more than one prepaid mine. **Counter:** if defending a predictable approach from turn zero, a mine can be better and needs no setup Order. 4. **Cheap Regular DZ mine versus Sapper/firepower spending.** [Swiss Miss — Shock Army N5 tier list](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hlmVXGHskS4) (YouTube MCP transcript read) likes the 13pt Regular's mine+Sensor but considers **FO/repeater and Sapper** alternative team slots. [MetaChemistry #135 — Acontecimento](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2J6ByFT0PJGKB0DiSQ55AC) (podcast MCP transcript read) argues that old **defensive Core castles** are less compelling in N5 and utility Regulars have more consistent impact. Take one 13pt guard when alpha-strike routes exist; don't blindly buy a static castle.
5. **Bluecoat wall and Raveneye mines vs exposed long guns.** [Tactical Awareness S3E27 — Starmada](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4gwxsTu15Mc2Tp3wAsuLHr) (podcast MCP transcript read, 2025) discusses **distributed Bluecoats** across the DZ, reserve combat teams between them, **Raveneye** mines and multi-arc defenders. **Counter:** deployment/mission/map geometry matters; one Bluecoat in each 12-inch segment may protect nothing if the real angle is a safe roof behind them. A **Raveneye Cybermine** better attacks Hacker/HI; E/M mine is another profile, and physical screens still matter.
6. **Varuna “all the Helots” versus Zulu Cobra counterpunch.** [Tactical Awareness S3E41 — Varuna](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4RsrJBsOlJ3qnkjW3HCmgU) (podcast MCP transcript read) praises Zulu-Cobra Sensor/Jammer and E/M Minelayer midfield options, but a **Helot** is much cheaper as a dedicated home-end trap. Choose **Helot** for sacrifice; **Zulu Cobra** for active follow-through and marker hunting.
7. **Hidden Noctifer as a second gate, not the only backline answer.** [Robert Shepherd — Shasvastii focus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mtqHMuHmjjo) (YouTube MCP transcript reviewed, **N4-era**) argues for hiding a Noctifer ML near the opponent's expected Q-Drone problem and waiting for the commitment. Strong psychology; **counterview:** expensive/off-table Hidden unit forfeits its normal Order until revealed and does little if an impersonator avoids that LoF. **Ikadron/Q-Drone+Noctifer** defense uses different jobs, not interchangeable units.
8. **The perfect DZ fortress may still lose the mission.** [Irregular Order #52](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4RNxiaI1k5xXBVzVHxwnB9) includes a 2026 game with **Speculo starting dangerously close** and a counterdeployed Ninja/close guard, but also shows the defenders being worked around later. **Counterview:** one exposed cheap sentry and 2–3 separated cheerleaders often buys enough turns; extra backline models can reduce meaningful forward pressure. **Qualitative anecdote**, not evidence of universal 360º coverage success.

## 5. Compact selection / deployment test

- **What attacks this army’s cheerleaders?** IMP? Pick Biometric + mine/DTW. Combat Jump/Parachutist? 360º + cross-coverage. Fast on-foot? layered corner template/Flash/nonlethal. Multiple? split support and cover **two distinct routes**.
- **Which troop also has a second real job?** Raveneye (FO/Deactivator), Fennec (Fireteam/Thunderbolt), Kaauri (Regular/MSV), Bluecoat (Regular + CC), Auxilia (FO/second body), Chaksa (Sensor/Baggage), 360 TR REM (Regular + gun + repair).
- **Is it genuinely zero-Order?** Minelayer = prepaid deployment. Mine/Koala alone = often setup Order. Repeater without defensive hacking = no actual cyber ARO. Watch separately the enemy's **first landing** and **second movement**.
- **What happens if defender dies?** The attacker should still meet a **different** guard and the surviving cheerleaders should not sit in one shotgun/template/CC kill chain.
- **Can you still score and attack?** If 50+ points of static insurance saves only one 10pt Order, cut an ARO and keep a specialist/counterpunch.
