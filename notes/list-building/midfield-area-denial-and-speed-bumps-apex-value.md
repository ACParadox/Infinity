**Question:** which deployment investment forces enemy Orders, detours, Discover attempts, Deactivator/Sensor work, low-value sacrifices or bad engagements **without spending your own Active-Turn Orders**?

## Fast verdict

- **Apex versus Hackable HI/TAG:** **Hassassin Shujae 27/0, Hidden + FD(+20) + E/M Mine Minelayer (2)**. Two prepaid E/M mines plus hidden Flammenspeer and Specialist Operative can demand a complete clearance plan. **But:** the Hidden model **does not supply an Order while hidden**; a Sensor/Deactivator sweep, Warhorse, or non-Hackable assault reduces value.
- **Apex against vulnerable biological troops:** **ALEPH Dasyu 30/.5 Viral Mine Minelayer (2)** — Hidden, Infiltration (ReRoll), Mimetism(-6), BS13, Pulzar. More murderous than ordinary mines **against suitable targets**, not a universal answer to STR-heavy armies.
- **Best network efficiency:** **Corregidor Moran Repeater + CrazyKoala + Minelayer 15/.5**. Infiltration, FO, Regular Order, perimeter deterrence **and a hacking entry point** if allied hackers are present. **Corregidor AVA2; Vanilla Nomads AVA1**, and its one-use Camo profiles **do not simultaneously have Repeater**. Two Morans cost **1 SWC**.
- **Best non-hacking pure cheap Camo mines:** **USA Foxtrot Minelayer 18/.5**, **Bakunin Zero Minelayer 18/.5**, **Chasseur Minelayer 20/.5**, **Tohaa Clipsos Minelayer 23/.5**, **Shasvastii Shrouded Minelayer 24/.5**. Pay the premium for template/mission utility and plausible marker ambiguity, not because every mine carrier is equally threatening.
- **Best zero-SWC cheap passive gadgets:** **O-12 Gangbuster KHD+MadTraps Minelayer 22/0**, **Tohaa Kaauri E/M Minelayer 11/0** (DZ, not midfield), **Kestrel Fennec Thunderbolt+Mine Minelayer 14/0** (DZ), and **Acontecimento Regular Mine+Sensor 13/0** (DZ).
- **Best contingent route denial:** **Shrouded Dazer Minelayer 21/0** slows a *route* rather than simply threatening damage; **Naga Monofilament Minelayer 26/.5** punishes expensive troops that must pass one choke. Both need terrain and mission geometry to justify their specialist tool.
- **Best zero-Order threat that is not merely a mine:** **Ōban Ryūken-9 HRL 23/1.5** — one-use Camo, Mimetism(-6), Decoy(1), deployment mine and scary reactive HRL. Strong **DZ/long-lane ambush**, not an infiltrating skirmisher; the 22/.5 SMG option is its **distinct FD(+20) midfield profile**.

## How to rate the role (avoid misleading rankings)

**Apex S / A / B** = maximum credible enemy plan disruption in its actual roster/matchup. **Value S / A / B** = disruption and useful Orders/objectives per points, SWC, slots and your **own setup cost**. Comparisons are cross-faction tactical benchmarks, **not legal mixed army lists**. An expensive piece can be S apex / B value, and a 13pt DZ mine can be S value / B apex.

1. **Turn-zero / zero-Order:** Infiltration, Forward Deployment or Hidden Deployment plus **Minelayer** creates immediate obstacles. **Decoy** adds identification tax; a threatened close-corner template creates a reaction fork. A regular on-table model still contributes its Regular Order. A Hidden model does not while hidden.
2. **Conditional zero-Order:** a prepositioned **Repeater only constrains Hackable models if suitable friendly hackers/network are live**; a Koala/MadTrap needs correct perimeter coverage and trigger geometry; a Camo marker is not a guaranteed successful ambush.
3. **Order-consuming construction:** ordinary **Place Deployable**, Mine Dispenser, Drop Bears, Pitcher/FastPanda, Turret deployment, Suppressive Fire and re-mining cost an activation or setup resource unless a specific deployment skill prepaid it. Rate them separately; do not falsely advertise an Order-fed minefield as turn-zero control.
4. **Measure the actual win:** Orders diverted + angles/routes given up + mission timing forced + opportunities for counterattack; *not just kills*. A 15pt blocker that forces three enemy activations then dies may beat a 35pt killer that needs three of your Orders to place one mine.

## 3. Pick by opponent and table

| Enemy/map problem | Prefer | Why / when worse |
|---|---|---|
| **Hackable TAG/HI must cross one objective** | Shujae **E/M (2)**, Scout **E/M (2)**, Jackal **E/M GL + Shock** if cheap; Moran/Jazz | Layer E/M with physical template and hacking. E/M is weaker against Warhorse/other valid immunities or easy Engineer repair, and a **Jackal's mine is only Shock**. |
| **Many biological 1–2W troops** | Dasyu **Viral (2)**, Farzan Viral, Chasseur LFT + Shock | Punish clusters/corners. Don't overpay for Viral against STR/immune targets; Sensor/Deactivator counter a predictable mine cluster. |
| **Premium ARM troops forced along narrow wall** | Naga **Monofilament**; suitable E/M tripwires | High-threat clearing tax. Against scattered 8pt warbands, cheap Shock/PARA or a disposable cornerguard is better. |
| **Opponent has several strong hackers** | Moran Repeater + live defensive hacker; Gangbuster KHD; prepaid Bandit/Initiated repeater | Avoid an unprotected repeater network that also helps the opponent. Non-Hackable smoke/warbands ignore much of a hacking-only perimeter. |
| **Enemy Camo-heavy Sensor sweep** | Kinnara Sensor/Decoy, Gangbuster MSV1, cross-covered Camo mine + close template | One isolated mine dies to Deactivator; **orthogonal** protections make their sweep hard. |
| **Densely built midfield objectives** | Shrouded Dazer, multiple Camo+Minelayer pockets, Chasseur LFT, Gangbuster traps | Route geometry makes target identification expensive. On broad open tables, long-range ARO and a hidden sniper can matter more. |
| **Enemy AD/Impersonation threatens DZ** | Kaauri 11 E/M, Regular 13, Fennec 14, Ryūken HRL/Decoy | Don't force a midfield infiltrator to act as a DZ guard. Cheap home-end mines often more efficient than moving a premium troop back. |
| **Need a *second* independent flank** | Two Morans where legal, Zero + Initiated, Foxtrot + Beasthunter, multiple Shujae/Shrouded markers | Splitting forces more independent clearance. Two mines protecting the *same* easily bypassed door is wasted redundancy. |
| **Expected enemy Deactivator/Sensor and expendable warbands** | Overlapping live template/ARO/hacker that punishes the clearing unit | Mines alone drop in value; kill the sweeper or deny its **safe Deactivator square**, not just a mine location. |
| **Going first, with orders to spend** | Add active **Mine Dispenser/Drop Bear/Perimeter/Pitcher** behind prepaid layers | Very strong *after* spending Orders; do not count the created coverage as free when ranking turn-zero pieces. |

**Deployment procedure:** Put blockers **behind/around** corners and objectives, covering *where enemies must move next*, not an irrelevant open sightline; separate multiple mine trigger areas when a single cheap clearing move would answer both. Use Camo/Decoy markers to make exact clearance uncertain; anchor the pocket with a dangerous *different* response (direct template, hidden ARO, live hacker). Keep the scoring Specialist alive while spending cheaper bodies to cause friction.

**N5.3 legal traps:** Minelayer's deployment mine/weapon must be within its permitted deployment area and ZoC; do **not** place a mine with any existing enemy model/marker in its Trigger Area; perimeter weapons have additional placement/ZoC checks. The fact that a unit has Mines/Koalas does **not** imply a free deployed device if the chosen profile lacks Minelayer. Hidden Deployment masks location but sacrifices its contributed Order while hidden. Do not imply a mine automatically destroys a TAG: Dodge, ARM/BTS/Immunities, Deactivator, Sensor, sacrificial troopers and Engineer support change results. Consult [Deployables and Area Control](../deployables-area-control.md), [Marker State / Deception](marker-state-and-deception-strategy.md), [Reactive-turn architecture](defensive-architecture-and-reactive-turn.md), and the [current rules](../../rules/README.md) for mechanics.

## 4. Transcript-backed disagreements (including opponents)

1. **“More mines” versus “more *credible kinds* of threat.”** [Robert Shepherd — Hassassin Bahram N5](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yHpAnRDw_E8) (**YouTube MCP transcript reviewed**) praises Shujae E/M Minelayer(2), but warns two deployed mines can reveal the possible Hidden model position; recommends mixing Farzan Viral/ordinary camo. **Choose repeated Shujae** into HI/TAG chokepoints; **mix** against experienced Sensor/Deactivator play.
2. **Shrouded mine saturation versus pricier Malignos.** [Robert Shepherd — Shasvastii faction focus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mtqHMuHmjjo) (**YouTube MCP transcript reviewed; N4-era**) explains how cheap Shrouded markers protect Caliban and Hidden attackers through ambiguity. Shrouded Dazer is much more situational; paying for Malignos Hidden is worthwhile only if its concealment wins the scenario. **Current prices above override old audio.**
3. **Ryūken high-ARO ambush versus SMG midfield option.** [Robert Shepherd — Ōban faction focus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AhicCreNCUQ) (**YouTube MCP transcript reviewed**) prefers the HRL's long-lived “discover-or-leave-it” pressure and often finds FD SMG harder to convert to value. **Counterview:** SMG/FD is cheaper in SWC and explicitly better for dense objectives and Suppressive Fire deployment.
4. **“Cheap regular mines fix DZ” versus attack/skirmisher investment.** [Robert Shepherd — Shock Army of Acontecimento](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RnP9fVZRb_Q) (**YouTube MCP transcript reviewed; older N4-era**) stresses 13pt Regular mine/Sensor protecting bad defensive tables. [MetaChemistry #135 — Acontecimento](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2J6ByFT0PJGKB0DiSQ55AC) (**Podcast MCP transcript reviewed, 2026**) finds it neat but gives much more attention to Sapper sniper and Naga/Dart attack options. **Use cheap DZ miners** when rear defense is fragile; otherwise spend the 3–4pt upgrade on mission roles.
5. **High-tech Yaksha/Kinnara versus simply buying cheaper bodies.** [Loss of Lieutenant E168 — Mazebreaker previews](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4MxU8qsoJlbZkYCLt1fo6d) (**Podcast MCP transcript reviewed, 2026; preview assumptions superseded by pinned profiles**) likes Naga new Monofilament Minelayer and revised specialist networks. 6. **Two Moran hacking setup vs SWC-efficient direct shooting.** [WIP12 E208 — Corregidor post-Mazebreaker](https://open.spotify.com/episode/342lz18SRGSZ8QWf1Zg3Z9) (**Podcast MCP transcript reviewed, 2026**) sees Moran/Jazz as a powerful but **SWC-expensive** path once Vertigo joins it. **Counterview:** take the 15pt Moran for cheap *passive clearance tax* even **without Guided**. CJC can run a gun-first list supported by physical Koalas/hacking AROs.
7. **Mine dispensers and deployable saturation can be oppressive but cost Orders.** [Tactical Awareness — N5 launch, Equipment/Deployables](https://open.spotify.com/episode/1XVRK5M5O8SLoQT099V2pu) (**Podcast MCP transcript reviewed, 2024 rules discussion**) emphasizes remotely generated minefields/Koalas/MadTraps and how multiple hazards force slow clearing. Its speculative coordinated-place interactions should **not** override N5.3 rules text. **Tradeoff:** invest Orders building a *harder future table* only if doing so prevents more enemy Orders or scoring.
8. **Chasseur/Foxtrot/Camo trap versus guns and proactive pressure.** [Robert Shepherd — Ariadna CanCon 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTqYE_FddwQ) (**YouTube MCP transcript reviewed**) emphasizes Strelok Decoy/Scout mine and passive camouflage saturation as a way to support aggressive guns and Speculative Fire. **Counterview:** too many nonlethal or one-shot roadblocks can leave no credible counterattack. [Defensive architecture](defensive-architecture-and-reactive-turn.md) favors **serial layers** rather than one massive uniform first line.

## Practical 20-second list check

- **Turn-zero obstacle?** Identify the precise printed *Minelayer* profile, free mine type and legal location, plus what enemy must do to cross it.
- **At least two different failure modes?** Mine/perimeter **plus** hidden ARO/DTW/Repeater/hacker/Deactivator denial; not three identical mines to the same door.
- **Real cost?** Include SWC, Irregular/Hidden Order loss, ability to score afterwards, AVA and Fireteam restrictions.
- **Opponent's cheapest answer?** Sensor, Deactivator, cheap warband, ranged gun, Dodge, immunity or bypass; deploy the second gate where that answer has a cost.
- **Score the mission.** If a minefield causes detours but protects no objective, gun or surviving Specialist, spend the points elsewhere.
