# Chain of Command — N5.3 Apex and Points-Value Tier Guide

**48 eligible or provisionally eligible named entries ranked individually, plus one explicitly excluded unit.** The guide separates a **curated 17-unit Apex shortlist** for exceptional standalone CoC quality from a **full 48-unit Value ranking** for effective succession insurance, followed by an audited two-column Top 10 cross-roster summary. These are qualitative list-building judgments, not tournament win rates.

**Scope:** Chain of Command (CoC) succession **while the model is on the table and not Null/Isolated at the Loss of Lieutenant check**. CoC (Non Specialist) does not count as a Specialist merely because of CoC. A trooper's **Lieutenant profile is not its CoC profile**; do not pool equipment/skills/bonuses from different options. Always check the actual faction/sectorial selection and Fireteam chart. See [Lieutenant command rankings and economy](command-units-apex-and-value.md), [mission specialists](mission-specialists-and-button-pushers-apex-value.md), and [local N5.3 rules](../../rules/README.md).

## Chain of Command — Apex ranking

**Apex is a selective ceiling ranking, not a list of cheap succession insurance.** A unit qualifies only when its **actual CoC profile** has exceptional independent battlefield quality in at least one of three categories:

- **Very defensible:** a genuinely resilient/hidden body or layered defences (e.g. 2W, NWI with Mimetism, Camouflage/Decoy, Eclipse), not simply a one-wound unit that can be tucked into the Deployment Zone.
- **Very useful:** uncommon, high-impact support even when succession never occurs (e.g. SymbioMates, strong hacking/Pheroware, multiple scenario/team roles, command infrastructure).
- **Strong on its own:** credible independent gunfighting, close combat or decisive defensive presence, with the CoC profile's **real** weapon loadout.

**Price efficiency and minimal insurance tax do not raise Apex rank**—those belong in the separate Value table. Being willing to keep a CoC alive matters, but a premium combat profile is not downgraded merely for being capable of attacking. **Only the 17 genuine standouts below are Apex-ranked.** The other 31 named entries remain fully ranked in Value; they are not called Apex by default. Grades are comparative across the selected standouts: **S** elite, **A+** exceptionally strong, **A** notable but more conditional. Rankings are qualitative judgments, not win-rate data.

## Chain of Command — Value ranking

**Value rewards CoC that provides reliable insurance and useful support for relatively little points/SWC.** Cheap alone is insufficient: survivability, concealment, defensive templates/mines, Counterintelligence and protected specialist/team use also count. This list deliberately penalizes expensive fighting CoC whose cost, SWC or temptation to advance makes them poor succession insurance, even when they are high Apex. Grades: **S** exceptional, **A+** excellent, **A** good, **B** inefficient/conditional, **C** poor as pure insurance.

## Eligibility and source-audit exceptions

- **Maximus, Optimate and HexaDome Legend — NOT RANKED.** The pinned [PanO TAG](../../sectorials/panoceania/panoceania.md) and [Optimate Agent Maximus](../../sectorials/aleph/operations-subsection.md) roster options have **no Chain of Command profile**. They have Lieutenant/Tactical Awareness options instead. A long cross-faction character listing does not create CoC legality.
- **† Isobel McGregor — provisional Value #34; not Apex-ranked.** The [Caledonian repository profile](../../sectorials/ariadna/caledonian-highlander-army.md) lists the 25/0 weapon loadout but **omits the CoC skill on the option**. The external [Human Sphere N5 profile](https://human-sphere.com/Isobel_McGregor) explicitly assigns **Chain of Command to 25/0**. Preserve and resolve the discrepancy against the actual Army export **before relying on her CoC eligibility**; her rank is conditional.
- **Emily Handelman and John Hawkwood are not globally selectable CoC profiles.** Emily CoC 28/0 is [verified in White Company](../../sectorials/na2/white-company.md). Hawkwood CoC 40/0 is verified there and in [ALEPH/OSS](../../sectorials/aleph/operations-subsection.md). An index naming nearly all sectorials is not proof of legal CoC availability in each.
- **Roster-specific SWC must not be normalized away.** Kulak CoC is 23/**0** in [Tunguska](../../sectorials/nomads/tunguska.md) but 23/**0.5** in [Vanilla Nomads](../../sectorials/nomads/nomads.md); Táowù CoC is 21/**0** in [Druze](../../sectorials/na2/druze.md)/[Ikari](../../sectorials/na2/ikari-company.md), versus 21/**0.5** in [Imperial Service](../../sectorials/yu-jing/imperial-service.md)/[White Company](../../sectorials/na2/white-company.md).
- **Joan v1 versus v2** are separately ranked, but the roster's single-Joan constraint prohibits taking both. Their CoC options are **Non Specialist**; the LT-only value of Inspiring Leadership's Order conversion cannot be credited to the CoC trooper before promotion.
- **Kirpal Singh's AD deployment is anti-synergistic with emergency CoC** if he is still off-table. The official skill requires the successor be on the game table as a model or marker when the Loss of Lieutenant check occurs; do not count a reserve-drop CoC as turn-one insurance.
- **Raveneye, Quinn, Nexus, Ment, etc.:** only attribute CoC to an actual option in that faction's roster. A common unit name, reinforcement/side-mode or cross-faction database category is not a blanket permission.
- **Specialist/command interactions:** CoC is generally a mission-specialist skill unless marked Non Specialist; a CoC option with no Strategos does **not** gain it from the same unit's LT option. Separate Fireteam variants (FTO) and profile weapons are not automatically interchangeable.

## List-building takeaways

- **Apex is a different question from Value.** Taqeul (Apex #1) offers 2W/Regeneration, Viral/Pheroware and Triad support; Tai Sheng (#2) combines 2W, NCO, Forward Observer, Warhorse, Sixth Sense and optional TinBot protection. They are premium independently useful models, not cheap backups.
- **Hard-to-kill Apex:** Myrmidon Officer (#3) offers Mimetism(-6), NWI and Eclipse; Vassily (#4) combines Camouflage, Decoy(2), Dogged and a T2 gun. Patroclus (#5), Umbra (#6), Caliban (#7) and Hortlak (#8) offer other strong defense/combat packages.
- **High-impact utility Apex:** Pandora (#9) contributes protected hacking and Research; Starmada Saladin (#11) brings Decoy(2), NWI and Counterintelligence; Kaeltar (#12) delivers actual SymbioMate protection.
- **Joan is powerful but not premier succession insurance:** Joan v1 (#10) and v2 (#14) are credible standalone fighters, while high cost and **Non Specialist** status keep them low in Value. Rank their actual CoC loadouts, not LT-only Inspiring Order benefits.
- **Value-only standouts stay valued:** Raveneye, Shukra, Magistrate, Ment, Kempeitai and Tanuki still score highly in Value for inexpensive, protected command coverage or passive support. They are not automatically Apex simply because they are excellent purchases.
- **Protect the succession path.** A strong CoC that is killed or isolated before the Loss of Lieutenant check cannot save the army; spread the LT and CoC across separate attack/template/hacking vulnerabilities.

**Research status:** Two separate tactical Apex and points-value rankings and profile-coverage audit only.
