# Chain of Command — N5.3 Apex and Points-Value Tier Guide

**48 eligible or provisionally eligible named entries ranked individually, plus one explicitly excluded unit.** The guide presents **two independent, complete tables**: Apex measures battlefield quality and Value measures succession efficiency. Each contains all 48 named entries. These are qualitative list-building judgments, not tournament win rates.

**Scope:** Chain of Command (CoC) succession **while the model is on the table and not Null/Isolated at the Loss of Lieutenant check**. CoC (Non Specialist) does not count as a Specialist merely because of CoC. A trooper's **Lieutenant profile is not its CoC profile**; do not pool equipment/skills/bonuses from different options. Always check the actual faction/sectorial selection and Fireteam chart. See [Lieutenant command rankings and economy](command-units-apex-and-value.md), [mission specialists](mission-specialists-and-button-pushers-apex-value.md), and [local N5.3 rules](../../rules/README.md).

## Chain of Command — Apex ranking

**Apex rewards CoC models that are exceptionally defensible, exceptionally useful, or individually strong.** High ratings can come from **defensibility** (Camouflage, Holomask, Mimetism, NWI/armor, defensive gear), **standalone utility** (Counterintelligence, SymbioMates, hackers, specialists, NCO/Fireteam support), **or combat power** (a credible independent gun/assault unit). The best CoC can achieve any of these without necessarily being cheap. Fighting power counts, but the risk of losing the successor while attacking still matters. Ranks evaluate the CoC **profile**, not a different Lieutenant/gun option. Grades: **S** exceptional, **A+** outstanding, **A** strong, **B+** situational, **B** comparatively limited.

## Chain of Command — Value ranking

**Value rewards CoC that provides reliable insurance and useful support for relatively little points/SWC.** Cheap alone is insufficient: survivability, concealment, defensive templates/mines, Counterintelligence and protected specialist/team use also count. This list deliberately penalizes expensive fighting CoC whose cost, SWC or temptation to advance makes them poor succession insurance, even when they are high Apex. Grades: **S** exceptional, **A+** excellent, **A** good, **B** inefficient/conditional, **C** poor as pure insurance.

## Eligibility and source-audit exceptions

- **Maximus, Optimate and HexaDome Legend — NOT RANKED.** The pinned [PanO TAG](../../sectorials/panoceania/panoceania.md) and [Optimate Agent Maximus](../../sectorials/aleph/operations-subsection.md) roster options have **no Chain of Command profile**. They have Lieutenant/Tactical Awareness options instead. A long cross-faction character listing does not create CoC legality.
- **† Isobel McGregor — provisional #29 apex / #34 value.** The [Caledonian repository profile](../../sectorials/ariadna/caledonian-highlander-army.md) lists the 25/0 weapon loadout but **omits the CoC skill on the option**. The external [Human Sphere N5 profile](https://human-sphere.com/Isobel_McGregor) explicitly assigns **Chain of Command to 25/0**. Preserve and resolve the discrepancy against the actual Army export **before relying on her CoC eligibility**; her rank is conditional.
- **Emily Handelman and John Hawkwood are not globally selectable CoC profiles.** Emily CoC 28/0 is [verified in White Company](../../sectorials/na2/white-company.md). Hawkwood CoC 40/0 is verified there and in [ALEPH/OSS](../../sectorials/aleph/operations-subsection.md). An index naming nearly all sectorials is not proof of legal CoC availability in each.
- **Roster-specific SWC must not be normalized away.** Kulak CoC is 23/**0** in [Tunguska](../../sectorials/nomads/tunguska.md) but 23/**0.5** in [Vanilla Nomads](../../sectorials/nomads/nomads.md); Táowù CoC is 21/**0** in [Druze](../../sectorials/na2/druze.md)/[Ikari](../../sectorials/na2/ikari-company.md), versus 21/**0.5** in [Imperial Service](../../sectorials/yu-jing/imperial-service.md)/[White Company](../../sectorials/na2/white-company.md).
- **Joan v1 versus v2** are separately ranked, but the roster's single-Joan constraint prohibits taking both. Their CoC options are **Non Specialist**; the LT-only value of Inspiring Leadership's Order conversion cannot be credited to the CoC trooper before promotion.
- **Kirpal Singh's AD deployment is anti-synergistic with emergency CoC** if he is still off-table. The official skill requires the successor be on the game table as a model or marker when the Loss of Lieutenant check occurs; do not count a reserve-drop CoC as turn-one insurance.
- **Raveneye, Quinn, Nexus, Ment, etc.:** only attribute CoC to an actual option in that faction's roster. A common unit name, reinforcement/side-mode or cross-faction database category is not a blanket permission.
- **Specialist/command interactions:** CoC is generally a mission-specialist skill unless marked Non Specialist; a CoC option with no Strategos does **not** gain it from the same unit's LT option. Separate Fireteam variants (FTO) and profile weapons are not automatically interchangeable.

## List-building takeaways

- **Best blend of defensibility and support:** **Raveneye** (Apex #1 / Value #1), **Kaeltar** (#2/#2), **Myrmidon Officer** (#3/#10), **BCA Magistrate** (#4/#5), **Shukra** (#5/#3) and **Pandora** (#6/#12). Not every one is physically durable: Magistrate and Shukra remain W1/ARM1 and require careful placement.
- **Apex rewards genuine standalone strength:** **Caliban** (#10/#28), **Hortlak** (#11/#21), **Umbra** (#14/#40), **Patroclus** (#15/#41), **Joan v1** (#18/#44) and **Joan v2** (#19/#46) rise relative to their Value rankings because they bring real capability independent of succession. Both Joans remain below the most defensible/versatile CoC and are costly insurance.
- **Efficient insurance over combat:** **Ment** (Value #4), **Tanuki** (#6), **Kempeitai** (#7) and **Agnes** (#9) are excellent economical choices, but not all belong at the top of an Apex power ranking.
- **Deployment discipline:** Keep the CoC outside the same template, hacking and attack footprint as the LT. Defensibility, utility and combat value do not excuse failing the actual succession check.

**Research status:** Two separate tactical Apex and points-value rankings and profile-coverage audit only. Sectorial data are pinned to September 2026, not claimed as post-snapshot live Army availability. Avoid expanding source-specific faction access without checking the exact current Army profile.
