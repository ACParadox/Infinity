## The fast verdict

- **Best whole-army command engine:** **Saladin LT 39/0** in Vanilla Haqq with multiple useful **Irregular** troops. Strategos L2 + Inspiring Leadership + Counterintelligence convert a normally scattered army into a flexible Order pool, add reserves and Command Token efficiencies. In a mostly-Regular army, pay much less for a normal Lieutenant.
- **Best inexpensive general Strategos:** **BCA Magistrate LT 19/0** in Kestrel or Vanilla PanO: Counterintelligence, Specialist Operative, Jackal Fireteam Master and Strategos L1 at a very low cost; fragility is the downside. **Magistrate CoC 21/0 is a mutually exclusive profile**, not simultaneously the LT.
- **Best combat/command ceiling:**
  - **Avatar LT 122/3 SWC** — Strategos L2, Mnemonica, Tactical Awareness and Sepsitor give the highest raw command/combat ceiling.
  - **Marut LT 90/2** — Strategos L2, MSV2, Tactical Awareness and a premium gun; often better **value** among command TAGs because more budget remains for specialists.
- **Best Morat fighting LT alternatives:**
  - **Sogarat LT 56/0.5–1** — discounted heavy gun; actual *Warhorse* allies remain Regular after a command disaster.
  - **Kornak LT 42/0** — Strategos L1 and LT(+1), preferable when two **flexible** Orders outperform another big gun.
- **Best cheap command insurance:**
  - **O-12 Ment CoC 14/0** (Vanilla/eligible O-12)
  - **Kempeitai CoC 17/0** (JSA)
  - **Raveneye CoC 18/0** (Torchlight)
  - **Hafza CoC 20/0** (Qapu/eligible Haqq)
  - **Shukra CoC 21/0** (ALEPH)
  - **Magistrate CoC 21/0** (Kestrel/Vanilla PanO).
- **Best CoC that also wins the mission:**
  - **Nexus 23/0** (Onyx/Combined)
  - **Farzan 24/0** (Haqq)
  - **Caliban 29/0** (Shasvastii)
  - **Kaeltar 21/0.5** (Tohaa); their actual gun, hacker/network, protection or specialist contribution reduces the *marginal* insurance tax.

### How ratings work

**Apex** rates maximum useful command, deployment and combat impact for a whole ~300-point force. **Value** rates the command/succession benefit *per extra point and SWC compared with the legal alternative* after funding decoys, NCO/CoC, specialists and protected Orders. **S** exceptional in its stated condition, **A** excellent but less general, **B** solid/niche, **C** usually inferior unless a specific list needs it. **Conditional S ≠ automatic inclusion**. Compare **whole packages**, not head-to-head gun statistics.

## Command economy versus risk — actual Order budget

**Orders shown are per relevant Active Turn while models/skills are valid**, not guaranteed extra generic pool Orders. A personal LT/Tactical Order can be worth less than a Regular Order on the right attacker; a CoC model costs points but normally adds its own Regular Order while alive.

| Package | Added command/TA activations beyond model's normal Regular Order | What is freely assignable? | LT exposure / practical risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Magistrate19 Strategos L1** | **1 LT → 1 Regular** | One flexible Order **in Magistrate's group**; two reserves + Counterintelligence | **Low deployment exposure, high assassination fragility:** W1/ARM1, so guard the backline |
| **Raveneye17 LT(+1) + NCO** | **2 Special LT → up to 2 NCO Tactical** | **Zero** extra generic pool Orders; only chosen NCO/team leader can use them | **Low until unmasked or NCO dies**; Holomask CoC alternative18 available separately |
| **Dāoyīng23 LT(+1) + Tai Sheng NCO** | **Up to 2 NCO Tactical** | **Zero** extra flexible pool; the proper NCO/team uses them | Camo makes LT harder to find; **NCO/Fireteam is economy chokepoint** |
| **Saladin39 Strategos2 + Inspiring** | **1 LT → 1 Regular**, plus **one converted pool Order per eligible existing Irregular trooper** | One extra Regular in Saladin's group **plus** every eligible Irregular conversion to its Combat Group; not extra trooper slots | **Highly concentrated:** lose Saladin and Regular conversions/command advantages vanish |
| **Sun Tze53 Strategos2 LT(+1)** | **2 LT → 2 Regular** | Both flexible in Sun Tze's group; free group transfer of one trooper (+ Peripherals) | Tough/disguised decoy relative to cheap LT; 53 points competing with guns |
| **Kornak42 Strategos1 LT(+1)** | **2 LT → 2 Regular** | Both flexible in Kornak's group | Protected by Warhorse-heavy MAF contingency; no NCO extra on top |
| **Marut90 Strategos2 + TA** | **1 LT → 1 Regular**, **+1 personal TA** | One flexible group Order; TA only on Marut/its led legal team | **High investment and hackable active LT**; Shukra21 CoC mitigates LoL, not TAG loss |
| **Avatar122 Strategos2 + TA/Mnemonica** | **1 LT → 1 Regular**, **+1 personal TA** | One flexible group Order; personal TAG TA; conditional Mnemonica succession | **Very high resource concentration**; neutralizing cheap pool or Isolating Avatar beats trusting raw armor |
| **Sogarat56 active LT + NCO Sogarat** | **1 LT → up to 1 NCO Tactical** | None in general pool; NCO can drive its actual Armored Duo | Large exposed combat LT, **but Warhorse heavy pool may remain Regular in LoL** |
| **Joan/Wallace Inspiring LT** | **1 personal LT Special Order**, plus **eligible Irregular→Regular conversions** and special Coordinated activation | Converted troopers produce flexible group Regular Orders; personal LT Order can Coordinate with LT | **High risk if converted warband pool reverts after command loss** |

**Risk test:** estimate (A) probability the opponent removes/Isolates the LT, × (B) *number of useful pooled activations and scenario pushes you would lose* on the following turn, × (C) how safely a live CoC/Mnemonica host changes that outcome. This is a **decision aid, not a calibrated probability model**. When premium attacking LT cannot profitably use its extra Order or still needs a costly backup, a cheaper protected LT may outperform it.

| Need or threat | Better architecture | The important why / failure condition |
|---|---|---|

| **Flexible orders on multiple guns in one group** | **Strategos L1/L2 LT** | Converts Special LT Orders into **Regular Orders in the LT's group**, not personal Tactical Orders. Two deployment reserves; L2 gives **one free Combat Group transfer including Peripherals** under N5.3. |

| **Strong gun must use its personal extra activations** | **Tactical Awareness** | Adds a personal Tactical Order, usable for a team **only if TA user is Team Leader**; a static TA model doesn't add generic transferable Orders. |
| **Premium LT plans to attack first turn** | **Active LT + separate protected CoC** | One personal attack activation is often worth risk, but CoC saves the **next turn's pool** if LT is Null/Isolated. Keep successors out of same line/template/hacking footprint. |

| **Hidden-looking backline command** | **Cheap LT + identical decoy + CoC if relevant** | Exploits Private Information and WIP identity ambiguity; even if LT Order unused, points buy a real specialist elsewhere. |

### Rules audit traps

- **Loss of Lieutenant** is checked in the **Tactical Phase**. Normally affected models become Irregular until a new LT is chosen at end of turn; **Warhorse** remains Regular. CoC triggers during that check with the successor on-table and not Null/Isolated; previous LT doesn't regain command after successor promotion. **CoC (Non Specialist)** doesn't grant scenario specialist status.
- **Mnemonica** transfers at end of Order when Null is entered, potentially repeatedly through eligible Cube/REM hosts. **Isolated LT can't jump.** Thus it differs from CoC's Tactical Phase succession.
- **Inspiring Leadership** needs actual LT **on table and not Null**; excludes **Religious Troop** models. Saladin's Inspiring was explicitly added in Oct 2025 N5.2; the pinned [Haqq roster summary](../../sectorials/haqqislam/haqqislam.md) abbreviates his skill list to Counterintelligence, but the [N5 profile data](https://human-sphere.com/Saladin) and [October official Army change discussion](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/infinity-army-n5-updates-bugs.43295/page-10) confirm Inspiring Leadership. **Roster-summary omission flagged; do not silently erase his real ability.**
- **Strategos vs NCO** compete for the **same** LT resource: after Strategos converts to Regulars there is **no Special LT Order** for NCO. If the LT has +1 Order, both Special Orders get converted under Strategos.
- **NCO** can use a generated Special LT Order even if LT subsequently enters Null during the turn. An **Isolated NCO can't** convert/use it. Tactical Awareness Order is separate.
- **Warhorse** does **not** ignore Mimetism, range, Cover, templates, Immobilized, or hacking damage; only its specific LoL/Retreat/Isolation/BS Attack(-X) interactions. Morat `Religious Troop` is not a substitute for Warhorse.
- **SWC rebates** can improve command value when they unlock another useful expensive weapon. Check the exact Lieutenant option for:
  - **Joan**
  - **Aquila**
  - **Hsien**
  - **Hatamoto**
  - **Neema**

  The **+1 SWC option** and other LT options may be mutually exclusive.
- **Don't invent CoC** for these roster-specific profiles, despite some older upload claims:
  - **Quinn (Kestrel / Varuna)** — no CoC on pinned profiles
  - **Emily Handelman (Kestrel)** — no CoC on pinned profile
  - **Emily Handelman (White Company)** — **does** have CoC; it is a different roster.
- **No generic army-wide CoC**: the listed faction/model permission and specific Fireteam profile decide legality.

## Opposing opinions — transcript-grounded, not forced into one verdict

1. **Morat Sogarat versus Kornak:** the sources disagree on the best LT purchase:
   - **Sogarat LT:** [Robert Shepherd (2025)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QwcqdXMtDXI) prefers the heavy gun, discounted SWC, disco-ball and Warhorse LoL resilience; choose for hard fighting or with an NCO armored Duo.
   - **Kornak LT 42/0:** [Tactical Awareness Morats S3E13 (Mar 2025)](https://open.spotify.com/episode/7HZuzXo3FDUr6Eqp08TzjR) praises Strategos(+1) and two pooled Orders in mixed-team lists, and considers Morat CoC often unnecessary.
2. **Saladin versus plain Haqq LT**: [Shepherd Haqq (Nov 2025)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dmv4pfdTbc0) calls Saladin one of the strongest commander archetypes while explicitly resisting a direct comparison to Marut. [Opponents discussing Saladin Dec 2025](https://www.reddit.com/r/InfinityTheGame/comments/1px4kn4/how_to_tackle_the_new_haqq_a_bit_of_a_rant/) describe oppressive cheap-Regular conversions. **Counter:** obvious decapitation point and less value without several useful Irregular allies; use Hafza/Hassassin alternatives.
3. **Cheap 'concession' LT vs brawler/commander LT**: existing [N5 transcript *A Guide to Lieutenants*](../../rules/Youtube%20rules-strats/A%20Guide%20to%20Lieutenants.txt) defines concession, synergizer, brawler and commander roles and favors high-ceiling brawlers/commanders. [WiseKensai's *Learning Lieutenants*](../../rules/Youtube%20rules-strats/Learning%20Lieutenants.txt) instead stresses that an obvious protected LT is sometimes too expensive to assassinate, and mission utility trumps reflexively hunting it. **Both depend on the enemy's approach and the LT's opportunity cost.**
4. **Avatar/Aspect versus inexpensive middleweight command:** [Shepherd Vanilla Combined Apr 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qoyylw0p0JM) highlights cheaper alternatives to a premium Aspect LT:
   - **Nexus** — midsize command/CoC option
   - **Vector** — midsize fighting piece
   - **Avatar / other expensive Aspect LT** — tremendous apex but can leave too little squad

   [Loss of Lieutenant N5 TAG tier panel Apr 2025](https://open.spotify.com/episode/1h5CJfZC5zBPxdg66o7xIN) likewise disputes Avatar's return once the opponent removes cheap Orders. **Apex ≠ points value**.
5. **Nexus CoC marginal value**: [Irregular Order #56 Onyx Oct 17 2026](https://open.spotify.com/episode/7b2QJ494pgPp6NY6WQ9QWF) explicitly praises **Nexus23 CoC + NCO + BS13/Mimetism**. **Counter:** if the list doesn't need Unidron Fireteam/member, the entire 23 points is real tax and a concealed cheaper backup may do better.
6. **Ariadna commander/CoC choice:** [Loss of Lieutenant #164 Feb 2026](https://open.spotify.com/episode/0X4QIoP24cKONtltxClHMH) distinguishes:
   - **Wallace CoC** — played as an independent attacker and successor, *not* automatically an army-wide Inspiring engine
   - **Wallace LT** — assess by the number of useful, genuinely Irregular models to convert
   - **Voronin 22 Strategos** — command alternative where flexible Orders or deployment control better serve the mission
7. **Next Wave cheap LT versus premium team:** [Loss of Lieutenant E172 Aug 2026](https://open.spotify.com/episode/5cVwqFnBxM9xYIskCHHuDt) discusses:
   - **Harbinger LT + decoy** — cheap command that funds other pieces
   - **Blackhearts** — one possible use of the saved points
   - **Achilles / Pandora premium teams** — early-win pressure, but risk reduced objective support
   - **Pandora 29 CoC** — useful when also providing Research/hacking, not the cheapest pure succession
8. **Strategos L2 FAQ detail**: [Loss of Lieutenant E174 N5.3 rules Sept 2026](https://open.spotify.com/episode/7jgbqpLcjwbOlbgZ94Ucxm) specifically notes group-transfer clarification **including Peripherals**. Recheck older claims that the controller moves alone.

### Five questions before finalizing 300 points

1. If LT is Dead or Isolated before my Tactical Phase, **which actual troopers remain Regular** and how many pooled Orders are lost?
2. Is my LT Order **personal**, **NCO Tactical**, or **Strategos Regular**? Count each only once; does an NCO Fireteam move or Strategos group transfer solve the mission?
3. How many *genuinely* Irregular models does Inspiring Leadership convert? Are any excluded by Religious Troop?
4. Can CoC survive a separate assault/Template/repeater attack, and is its backup mission action worth its marginal price?
5. If the main LT gun and its support die turn one, is there still **another independent weapon and enough scoring specialists for turn three**?
