# Hackers — HD / HD+ Apex and Value (N5.3)

Dedicated ranking of **standard Hacking Devices (HD)** and **Hacking Devices Plus (HD+)**. Apex prioritizes actual cyber effectiveness; Value emphasizes points, SWC and the support network. Strong hybrids can appear here for their **HD capability**, with their extra programs independently evaluated in the hybrid guide.

**Related guides:** [Killer Hackers (KHD)](killer-hackers-apex-and-value.md) · [Hybrid HD/KHD programs](hybrid-hackers-apex-and-value.md). The [shared device comparison and N5.3 rule summary](hybrid-hackers-apex-and-value.md#what-the-current-rules-actually-do) is in the hybrid guide.

## Hackers — standard HD / HD+

**Job:** Immobilize/Isolate enemy HI, TAG, REM or VH with **Carbonite/Oblivion**, provide **Spotlight** and (for HD+) **White Noise**. These rows evaluate the **Hacker profile itself**, not an alternate KHD loadout. **HD+ does not grant Trinity** unless the profile explicitly says so; those upgrades are ranked in the [Hybrid Hacker guide](hybrid-hackers-apex-and-value.md). Some hybrid standouts are cross-listed here if their native HD suite is already exceptional.

### Strongest Hacker profiles — apex

**Hacker-first Apex assessment (November 2026):** Ranking priorities are **WIP and effective program coverage/upgrades** (including Trinity or Zero Pain against hostile Hackers), **network access and delivery reliability**, **BTS/Firewall/reactive protection**, then fighting power only as a bonus. A 56pt Swiss Guard/Dàofěi can be a strong assault/scoring unit without being a top pure Hacker. A genuine dual-device or HD+Trinity profile can rank here **and** in Hybrid if its HD performance warrants it. Evidence: [Tactical Awareness OSS podcast / Asura and Danavas](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jh4jVsFoHN4), [Robert Shepherd on Tunguska / Interventors vs Mary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A2eKdenbcsw), [N5 hacking tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5epr8xME9I), [Military Orders Tactical Awareness podcast](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6xyHjc3R0N37bDHl0HiROO), and [cross-battle-report effectiveness analysis](../battle-reports/cross-report-unit-effectiveness-and-combinations.md). Judgments remain qualitative rather than measured win rates.

These are hacking-opportunity-cost assessments, not judgments on their other roles.
