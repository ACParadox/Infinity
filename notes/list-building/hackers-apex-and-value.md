# Hackers — HD / HDplus Apex and Value (N5.3)

Dedicated ranking of **standard Hacking Devices (HD)** and **Hacking Devices Plus (HDplus)**. **Apex** prioritizes maximum hacking attributes and innate defense (**WIP, BTS, ECM/Firewall, Wounds, NWI, protection against hostile hackers**); cost is **not** a penalty. Program breadth and offensive/defensive upgrades follow, while projection, Trinity and fighting/mission utility are secondary. **Value** separately emphasizes points, SWC and the support network. Strong hybrids can appear here for their **HD capability**, with their extra programs independently evaluated in the hybrid guide.

**Related guides:** [Killer Hackers (KHD)](killer-hackers-apex-and-value.md) · [Hybrid HD/KHD programs](hybrid-hackers-apex-and-value.md). The [shared device comparison and N5.3 rule summary](hybrid-hackers-apex-and-value.md#what-the-current-rules-actually-do) is in the hybrid guide.

## Hackers — standard HD / HDplus

**Job:** Immobilize/Isolate enemy HI, TAG, REM or VH with **Carbonite/Oblivion**, provide **Spotlight** and (for HDplus) **White Noise**. These rows evaluate the **Hacker profile itself**, not an alternate KHD loadout. **HDplus does not grant Trinity** unless the profile explicitly says so; those upgrades are ranked in the [Hybrid Hacker guide](hybrid-hackers-apex-and-value.md). Some hybrid standouts are cross-listed here if their native HD suite is already exceptional.

### Strongest Hacker profiles — apex

**Apex is selective:** a cheap useful Hacker is not automatically a strongest Hacker; lower-return cyber specialists appear in Value rather than being artificially rated Apex.

**Apex criteria (N5.3):** First assess **effective hacking WIP and upgrades** (including full HD control, native anti-Hacker programs and HD+ tools); second assess **staying power against enemy hacking and damage** (BTS, intrinsic Firewall/ECM, NWI, W2+/STR, Immunities, Zero Pain). **Network projection, mobility, Fireteams, weapons and mission utility are secondary**. Price/SWC never lower raw Apex strength. A TinBot helps only in a legal team and Mimetism does not directly modify cyber rolls. **HD / HDplus purchasing threshold:** invest in White Noise, upgraded Oblivion, a link or a premium body **only if it adds a second job**; ordinary 13–16pt HDs with projected Repeaters often outperform expensive, unnetworked hackers. **Charontid** is apex for durability/Sepsitor/direct combat, **not** an anti-Hacker Trinity duelist. **Cyberghost HDplus**, **Dokkaebi HD** and **Deva HD** are *different options* from the [KHD variants in the separate guide](killer-hackers-apex-and-value.md).
