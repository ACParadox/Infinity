# Hackers — HD / HD+ Apex and Value (N5.3)

Dedicated ranking of **standard Hacking Devices (HD)** and **Hacking Devices Plus (HD+)**. **Apex** prioritizes maximum hacking attributes and innate defense (**WIP, BTS, ECM/Firewall, Wounds, NWI, protection against hostile hackers**); cost is **not** a penalty. Program breadth and offensive/defensive upgrades follow, while projection, Trinity and fighting/mission utility are secondary. **Value** separately emphasizes points, SWC and the support network. Strong hybrids can appear here for their **HD capability**, with their extra programs independently evaluated in the hybrid guide.

**Related guides:** [Killer Hackers (KHD)](killer-hackers-apex-and-value.md) · [Hybrid HD/KHD programs](hybrid-hackers-apex-and-value.md). The [shared device comparison and N5.3 rule summary](hybrid-hackers-apex-and-value.md#what-the-current-rules-actually-do) is in the hybrid guide.

## Hackers — standard HD / HD+

**Job:** Immobilize/Isolate enemy HI, TAG, REM or VH with **Carbonite/Oblivion**, provide **Spotlight** and (for HD+) **White Noise**. These rows evaluate the **Hacker profile itself**, not an alternate KHD loadout. **HD+ does not grant Trinity** unless the profile explicitly says so; those upgrades are ranked in the [Hybrid Hacker guide](hybrid-hackers-apex-and-value.md). Some hybrid standouts are cross-listed here if their native HD suite is already exceptional.

### Strongest Hacker profiles — apex

**Apex reranking (November 2026):** Judge a Hacker on its maximum cyber strength, independent of price: **WIP and anti-hacking defense** (BTS, ECM: Hacker, Firewall when intrinsically applicable), **multiple Wounds/NWI/Remote Presence**, then HD/HD+ program coverage, upgrades, projected network, Trinity and ancillary fighting or mission utility. A Fireteam TinBot must be legally attached; Mimetism does **not** directly penalize hostile hacking rolls. This places **Charontid** and **De Fersen** well above cheaper fragile Hackers, while maintaining strong positions for well-protected WIP15/BTS9 Interventors. [N5.3 hacking rules](https://infinitythewiki.com/Quantronic_Combat_(Hacking)) · [Tactical Awareness OSS analysis](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jh4jVsFoHN4) · [Robert Shepherd on Tunguska](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A2eKdenbcsw). The result is a qualitative comparison, not measured win rates.
