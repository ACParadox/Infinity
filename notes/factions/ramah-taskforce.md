# Ramah Taskforce — List-building and tactical notes

## Overview

**What Ramah Taskforce does exceptionally well:** use **fast elite specialists and fighters** to take unusual firing angles, then keep scoring after the first attack. Tarik/Khawarij Super-Jump, Mukhtar mobility, Namurr Climbing Plus/E/M, and sturdy Janissary/Zhayedan guns produce distinct attack plans. The challenge is buying enough defense and Orders around expensive models.

### Particularly strong or unusual Fireteams

- **Ramah Haris/Core — fast Super-Jump combined arms:**
  - **Khawarij MSV2 Mk12/NCO or AP Spitfire:** select the actual offensive profile.
  - **Khawarij Doctor(+3):** linked frontline recovery.
  - **Tarik Wildcard:** counts as Khawarij, premium assault/scoring.
  - **Khawarij CoC/Disco:** an alternative LT-insurance/vision profile, not combined with the Mk12 loadout.
- **Budget Ramah team — reliable gun and Smoke:**
  - **Ghulam/Naffatûn:** cheap legal required-choice material.
  - **Zhayedan Marksmanship shooter:** counts as Ghulam.
  - **Ghulam NCO Smoke GL:** LT Order-funded lane creation.
  - **Yara/Leila/Valerya Wildcard:** choose MSV, KHD or Pitcher utility.
- **Mukhtar Duo/Haris:** **MSV2 Red Fury** alongside a separately purchased **FO, Doctor or Hacker**; not a Mukhtar Core.
- **Janissary Haris/Core or Armored Assault Duo:**
  - **Janissary + Hortlak/eligible Wildcard:** hard gun plus CoC/Disco or Engineer.
  - **Maghariba or Shakush + Rafiq FTO:** armored attack and separate Sensor/Repeater/support piece.

Current chart: **one Core, one Haris, unlimited Duos**, with required-choice limits and Rafiq **FTO-only** access. See [Ramah Fireteams](../../sectorials/haqqislam/ramah-taskforce.md#fireteams).

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **Mobility and scoring:** **Tarik/Khawarij Super-Jump**, **Mukhtar FD**, **Namurr Climbing Plus**, and **Nahab Infiltration *or* Parachutist** are different high-quality routes.
- **Disco/Eclipse:** **Khawarij CoC 33**, **Hortlak 28** and **Claire Doctor 33**; distinct from **Ghulam NCO Smoke GL** plus **Yara/Khawarij/Mukhtar MSV2** shooting.
- **Command/recovery:** **Khawarij/Hortlak CoC** can protect Tarik LT; frontline Doctors and Wildcards may recover premium attack pieces.
- **Unusually broad premium choices:** **Maghariba and Shakush TAGs**, **Haytham VH** and armored infantry. **Haytham is on the current pinned Ramah roster**, despite a contrary line in the broader Vehicle ranking note.
- **Asymmetric gap:** no **Fiday/Al-Djabel Impersonation** and no mass cheap **Ghazi/Daylami** screen. **Tuareg Hidden Deployment** and **Hunzakut AVA1** are not equivalent to Hassassin's marker saturation.
- **Economy risk:** stacking **Tarik, Namurr, Mukhtar and a TAG** can hollow out the Shihab/Fanous AROs, Monstrucker repair, and late mission budget.

### Played combinations worth testing

- **Four-model Ramah Core — Zhayedan HMG + Ghulam FO + Ghulam Doctor + Rahman Rouhani.** [#073 Outbreak](../battle-reports/071-080-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) explicitly names all four and reports the four-member linked bonus. The FO and two medical troops allow the shooter to be an **attack/ARO anchor without monopolizing specialist access**. The independent Tuareg Doctor used for the mission was **not a fifth link member**.
- **Three-model Khawarij Haris — Tarik Mansuri + Khawarij Mk12/MSV2 NCO + Khawarij Paramedic/Doctor.** Explicit deployment and activation in both [#073](../battle-reports/071-080-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) and [#061](../battle-reports/061-070-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md). Tarik's present-day **counts-as Khawarij** and NCO make this a mobile Lieutenant-Order outlet. #073 fielded the **Haris alongside the four-person Ghulam/Zhayedan Core**, not as one large team.
- **Maghariba Guard + Leila Sharif — Armored Assault Duo, #061 historical.** The linked TAG/Leila movement and TAG Tactical Awareness Order are narrated in [#061](../battle-reports/061-070-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md), with **Nahab Red Fury, two Naffatûn and Kameel EVO** supporting separately. The opening originally calls it Vanilla Haqqislam, but the player's closing showcase explicitly calls it **Ramah Taskforce**: record this provenance discrepancy pending historical Army export. Present chart: **Maghariba is an eligible Armored Assault anchor and Leila is Wildcard**; do not treat a TAG Pilot as a third Fireteam member.

### Failed architectures and mission safeguards

- **Tarik + four Khawarij Core — high ceiling, narrow scoring footprint.** [#227 NCA Evacuation](../battle-reports/221-230-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) reconstructs a five-person opening Core, later reduced/re-formed to four, with **two Namurr and a Nahab** outside an approximately **eight-trooper list**. Despite Tactical Awareness/NCO, Ramah lost **7–1** because early exposure consumed scarce models and the list lacked enough surviving **CivEvac carriers and task Orders**. Prefer a smaller attacking Haris and *independent* medical/scoring coverage when the mission spreads objectives.
- **Tarik + Janissaries + Leila + Nahab without a confirmed Fireteam.** [#216 Mindwipe](../battle-reports/211-220-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) does **not** declare Ramah's exact link count; don't certify an elite Haris from nearby miniatures. Starmada's Shona/Varangian pressure led to a **10–1** result when the forward rush failed. A second hacker/anti-materiel route and less exposed deployment are more important than assuming a Fireteam bonus.
- **Outbreak gunfight versus mission outcome.** [#073](../battle-reports/071-080-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) saw Ramah's linked HMG down an important attacker, but ALEPH won **8–5** through scans, stabilizations and CivVac. Reserve **more than one independent eligible medical/scoring body** and enough Orders for the actual task.

**Preferred trial blueprint:** **four-model Ghulam/Zhayedan/Rouhani Core + Tarik/Khawarij Haris + a separate Tuareg/Namurr-style mission runner**, trimming links to fit real points/SWC and the mission. The #061 TAG Duo is an **alternative architecture**, not a claim that the same played list also included #073's Core and Haris.

### Community tactics — N5 transcript-backed research (2026-10-22)

> **Precedence:** These are **community tactics, not Army data**. The authoritative roster, weapon options, AVA, costs and Fireteams are the pinned **N5.3 / Army 7.26246.158** sections above (verified 2026-10-15); current main roster has **37 entries**. **One Core, one Haris, unlimited Duos**. Match every suggested list against that chart. N5 videos from **August 2025** predate the October 2025 N5.2 update; Robert Shepherd's post-October N5 video and the November 2025 Tactical Awareness episode give useful later perspectives. Historical N4 podcast mechanics/points are NOT current legality.

#### One-minute read

- **Strength:** many excellent independent pieces, but each consumes real attack Orders:
  - **Tarik / Khawarij:** mobility, Super-Jump and combat.
  - **Namurr / Mukhtar / Nahab:** specialized mission and flank tools.
  - **Janissary / Zhayedan:** strong infantry shooting.
  - **Ghulam / Naffatûn / Fanous:** inexpensive Order economy.
  - **Haytham:** specialized fast aerial threat.
  - **Maghariba / Shakush:** two different TAG packages.
- **Weakness:** premium models are costly, and many fast super-soldiers are only one Wound or lack defense in the enemy active turn. Limited infiltrating midfield, thin expendable screening, narrow long-range anti-armor outside TAGs, and easy-to-attack expensive pieces make Ramah a **positioning and target-selection army**, not a simple shove-everything-forward elite faction.
- **Best general solution:** one premium active attacker **plus** a practical mission/defense backbone, not four unsupported premium units. Build deliberately for **going second**: a couple of Shihabs/Flash REMs, a Monstrucker/Nasmat to repair them, inexpensive template defenders, and depth protecting elite models from alpha strikes.
- **Sectorial identity disagreement:** Tactical Awareness emphasizes the unusually tough, efficient and fast *individual* super-soldiers, especially Tarik. Robert Shepherd considers post-October Ramah one of N5's **challenge factions** due to expensive profiles, structural gaps and required defense spending. Burlesford enjoys its improved N5 options but agrees it punishes sloppy list building. These are compatible observations: *excellent highlight models do not automatically make an effortless army*.

#### Fireteam/list architecture: legal scaffolds to test

- **Ramah primary:** Khawarij NCO/MSV2 Mk12 + Khawarij Doctor + Tarik (or a smaller Khawarij/Tarik Duo) to exploit Super-Jump and recovery. Optional Khawarij **CoC** if Tarik Lt. Core is possible but expensive; the pinned chart's FT-01 *required-choice* set must be met.
- **Budget fire support:** Zhayedan HMG/AP Sniper + Ghulam NCO smoke + Ghulam Doctor/Naffatûn/Leila, using counts-as **Ghulam** and the FT-01 set. The point is reliable function without 100+ pts of single attack package.
- **Independent objective unit:** Mukhtar **Red Fury MSV2 + FO/Doctor** Duo; or pure Mukhtar Haris with Hacker if scenario wants multiple WIP14 specialists. **Mukhtar cannot form Core.**
- **Janissary strike team:** Janissary HMG + Hortlak K1/CoC + Valerya/eligible Wildcard. If you want a defensive ML in Core, budget for it explicitly; Janissary/Hortlak are not an FT-01 wildcard substitute.
- **Armored Assault Duo:** **Maghariba/Shakush + Rafiq FTO** to bring Sensor/Repeater/Red Fury and a special-purpose team while the TAG goes active. **Rafiq 13-point non-FTO cannot take the FT-04 slot**. Alternatives include a TAG + a permitted Wildcard such as Namurr when the role and cost make sense.
- **Balanced 15-model concept:** main-group active gun and objective piece(s); **second combat group** Shihab(s), **Monstrucker (+Nasmat)**, three Fanous if affordable, plus Ghulam NCO smoke / Zhayedan for productive spare Orders. The 1-Haris limit means not stacking several Haris regardless of what a recorded example list says.

**Order-budget test:** count usable Regular Orders per group before purchasing additional attackers:
- **Monstrucker / Hunzakut:** Irregular, so their Orders are not freely spendable by other troopers.
- **Tarik + Namurr + Mukhtar + Maghariba:** a costly illustrative pile-up that can remove the deployment and ARO budget.
- **Tactical Awareness / NCO:** only fund permitted activations.

Multiple threats matter only when each can actually receive Orders.

#### Opposing opinions — keep both, choose by mission

1. **Ramah viability:** **Robert Shepherd (post-N5.2):** powerful individual attackers but one of N5's challenge factions, lacking affordable mid-priced synergy and passive defense. **Burlesford (pre-N5.2):** N5 markedly improved the faction; deeply versatile/enjoyable, but requiring serious skill and acknowledging competitive gaps. **Tactical Awareness (N5):** Tarik and elite soldier offense is exceptional; some regiments are outdated or hard to justify. **Implication:** build depth and repeatable defense before rewarding yourself with a third flashy unit.
2. **Tarik auto-include?** **Tactical Awareness:** Tarik is exceptional in fast attack and hard to counter; one host dislikes his near all-purpose profile. **Burlesford:** powerful, not mandatory; Lt or second-turn exposure can lose the game. **Robert:** serious offense, but 46–53 pts and relatively limited reactive defense need careful staging. **Implication:** choose an LT/CoC plan, win useful fights first, then withdraw Tarik.
3. **Khawarij vs Mukhtar:** **Burlesford** now defaults more often to Khawarij MSV/NCO because Super-Jump mobility + distinct CC roles trump some Mukhtar durability. **Robert** praises Mukhtars as unusually fairly priced and self-sufficient, though notes Vanilla can exploit them better. **Implication:** Khawarij when movement/CC and Lt synergy matter; Mukhtar when Forward Deployment, Mimetism and specialists matter.
4. **Maghariba vs Shakush vs Janissary:** **Burlesford** says Maggie is better than ever, but is dissatisfied with Shakush relative to **Janissary HMG**; **Robert** says both TAGs are good enough, yet expensive in a sectorial missing inexpensive mid-tier support. **Implication:** take Maggie for a specific S8 + Baggage/weapon use-case, Shakush for TacAware TAG fire, Janissary for efficient gunfighting.
5. **Haytham must-take?** **Robert's Ramah focus** recommends one in most lists because of an aggressive **MSV1/Mimetism/Continuous Damage** angle and elite enemy flyer hunting. **His dedicated Vehicle ranking** simultaneously says it offers almost purely gunfighting and Ramah already has mobile attackers. **Implication:** extremely strong into other VHs and exposed troops; omit it when mission needs specialists or anti-hard-target answers more than another gun.
6. **Al Fasid / Hortlak / Burkut:** **Tactical Awareness** generally questions Al Fasid's points next to faster super-soldiers; **Robert** finds Hortlak good on paper but hampered by Janissary link anchor, and Burkut can be pricey for BS12. **Burlesford** likes Burkut BSG in the close-fight niche. **Implication:** select a specific supporting E/Marat, CoC, TinBot/Engineer, blast or Disco role rather than treating them as direct upgrades.
7. **Heavy hacking or minimal?** **Tactical Awareness** includes Valerya + Leila + Fanous/Guided experiments; **Robert** often prefers one good hacker and repeaters because many fast MI are not hackable. **Implication:** expand hacking only if opponents, mission, and your own TAG/VH/HI load justify it.
8. **Full elite link vs redundancy:** Tarik + Khawarij Core can be very quick and imposing; solo/duo attack plus Mukhtar/other missions/screening has safer redundancy. Actual **one-Haris** constraint forces a choice between Khawarij/Ramah, Mukhtar, and Janissary Haris.
