## What is owned and what to buy first

**Exact owned:** one motorized Yojimbo and one foot Yojimbo (the same character; mutually exclusive in a list), Armand x1, Warcor x1, two pooled Yáoxiè chassis, four Dronbots, and possible Pangguling-compatible Mulebots. No Keisotsu, Wú Míng, Druze, Brawlers or Tankō recorded.

**P1** Buy 3–5 Keisotsu line bodies and one Wú Míng AP HMG, then a Druze KHD and a Tankō with Blitzen/Disco if building those teams.

**P2** First Yuan Yuan/Desperado, Karakuri durable FO only when mission/one-of matters; existing Yojimbo is already a smoke/close combat choice.

**Skip** both new Yojimbo variants, Armand, Warcor and additional Dronbot packs. Arslan is a low-value default buy but its MSV/Disco command niche remains; do not buy Al Fasid S5 as S2 Wú Míng proxy.

## Inventory-aware proxy options and cross-sectorial reuse

Under [the proxy policy](../miniature-proxy-guidelines.md), a proxy **must match exact S (base diameter plus height)**; prefer matching LI/MI/HI/SK/REM/TAG and suitable appearance. Don't proxy one unit using a model **whose own printed unit is also legal in this same sectorial**. A model can serve exactly one deployed body at a time. Robotic servants, motorbikes, creature companions, TAG Operators, transformations, and ancillary figures need their own suitable representation. Confirm tournament/event rules and explain distinct stand-ins to your opponent.

| Target family | Already-owned donor | Restriction |
