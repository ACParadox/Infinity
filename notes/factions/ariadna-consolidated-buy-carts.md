## Physical inventory and proxy rules — what is explicitly NOT bought

- **TAK already owned:** 32 dedicated troopers including **2 Dynamos, 2 S6 Dog-Warriors, 2 Scouts, 3 Tankhunters, 2 Spetsnaz, 3 Line Kazaks, 2 Konduktors, 1 Frontovik, 1 Strelok, 1 Ratnik, 1 Barsuk and 1 Vassily**, plus one Marauder-for-Barsuk proxy. **Two actual Traktor Mul/Kuryer chassis** are one common pool, not two of each.
- **Caledonia already owned:** 3 Volunteers, 2 Galwegians, 3 Highlander Greys, 2 Mormaers, 1 Wulver, 1 SAS, 1 Cateran, Uxía, Cadin, Isobel, Armand, McMurrough and Cameronian, with shared Beasthunter/Warcor/Dozer/motorized 112/Wardriver. Two TAK S6 Dog-Warrior figures can also proxy further **S6 Cameronians** if not otherwise used.
- **Dogs and peripheries:** The already documented **four physical S5 dog proxies** (two USAriadna Devil Dog sculpts *rebased to S5*, two Caledonian S5 dogs) are a single shared pool across **Bâtard, Strelok K-9 and Antipode Pack members**. They are **not S6 Dog-Warriors** and are not four for every named unit. An Antipode Assault Pack additionally needs a **handler + three S5 Antipodes**, so reserve distinct models. Carmen needs **a S4 motorcycle** (owned Penthesilea as proxy) plus separately allocated S5 Bâtard. Foot 112 (S2 LI) is **not** the motorized 112 (S4 bike).
- **Ordinary robots:** The **2 existing Traktor Mul/Kuryer** are already owned and cover usual Ariadna logistics REM requirements. Additional ordinary S1 Elektronik servants can be appropriately sized ALEPH Yudbots/Palbots. Where another conventional non-Combined-looking baggage/REM is needed, use existing matched **size, height and base** robot models rather than buying four of every REM identity. Rare uniquely shaped robot/animal packages are different and may require actual sculpts.
- **Skip:** Buying replacement TAK/Caledonia starters or duplicate Dynamos/Dog-Warriors/Spetsnaz/Greys/Mormaers/Volunteers just to fill AVA; buying an entire Merovingian/USARF force; costly retired/reissue collector premiums; a second McMurrough merely to represent a Cameronian (the owned S6 models already cover it); routine HVT/Warcor duplicates; unsuitable silhouette donor models.
- **No seller label overrides N5.3 roster membership.** The full 2024 ten-figure Ariadna Action Pack is **all Kosmoflot legal**, but only a handful of bodies are in Vanilla, while **the Scots Guard, Kosmosoldat and Volkolak are not automatically Vanilla-legal** merely because the box is called “Ariadna.” Consult the detailed 51/30/23/36-entry audits rather than assuming every boxed figure works everywhere. Optional Spec-Ops/Team-Ops and separately linked Reinforcements are excluded.
