# Yu Jing — consolidated collection-completion buy carts (N5.3)

**Prepared 2026-10-30.** Scope: [Vanilla Yu Jing](vanilla-yu-jing-buy-list.md), [Invincible Army](invincible-army-buy-list.md), [Imperial Service](imperial-service-buy-list.md), [White Banner](white-banner-buy-list.md) — **all four current Yu Jing armies** in this repository. This consolidates the four pre-existing, detailed full-roster buy/proxy/skip audits and newly added priced completion plans. Canonical [Yu Jing army references](../../sectorials/yu-jing/) were pinned to N5.3 Army `7.26246.158` (verified 2026-10-15); Vanilla membership is current but some individual full loadout projections are flagged *partial*. This is a **purchase plan**, not an inventory record.

**Policy:** Aim at least one usable physical representation of worthwhile units, and more than one **only when repeated bodies make sense** (e.g. 3 Zúyǒng, 2–3 Shàng Jí, 3 Wú Míng, 2 Bào, 2 Gūdǎn, 2 Shaolin, 4 Kuang Shi, 2 Hwarang/Sulyong). Use correctly sized **non-roster donor proxies** for other rarely used or expensive/OOP units, and do not buy entire rarely used unit identities just to finish a spreadsheet. Do not buy four separate families of standard Yu Jing Yáokòng remotes or baggage remotes when the owned ordinary PanO robots can proxy: the **physical chassis pool is shared**, not four of each named robot.

**Price basis:** The [104-item Miniatures Market Yu Jing archive](yu-jing-miniatures-market-prices-usd.md) and [Ultimate Toy Zone archive](ultimate-toy-zone-infinity-prices-usd.md) contain dated **user-captured USD** price leads (**U**, not live verified stock). CAD conversion uses **USD 1 = CAD 1.3985** on 2026-10-29, each line rounded to cents. **V** means a Canadian retailer catalogue/web price; 401 Games is in **Toronto/Vaughan, Ontario**, while UTZ dispatch origin and MM specific fulfillment location are not established. Tax, delivery, duty/broker fees and payment conversion spreads **excluded**. Some older MM links have failed and UTZ checkout/access was not verified, so figures are **planning estimates rather than a confirmed orderable cart**.

## Owned-model accounting, proxy and intentional skip policy

- **Actual Yu Jing-roster ownership:** From [Shindenbutai](../../inventory/shindenbutai.md), **2 physical Yáoxiè Rui Shi / Lù Duān chassis TOTAL**, usable as either type. From Kestrel, **1 Warcor and 1 Beasthunter**, both reused across armies where legal. Do not buy two more Rui Shi or Lù Duān simply to have their separate unit names on a shelf.
- **Normal robots:** The existing **4 PanO Dronbots (S3)** can depict the standard four Yáokòng roles (Wèibīng, Hùsòng, Son-Bae, Chaīyì), but **not** a stockpile of 4 of each. The **2 PanO Mulebots (S4)** can depict baggage/EVO Pangguling where height/base match; S1 PanO Palbots (2) / ALEPH Yudbots (2) are Yáozăo servants. **No regular REM or baggage purchase is required** for these cores, respecting the user's request.
- **Common non-Yu Jing human silhouettes:** 6 Kestrel Fennecs S2 LI make serviceable Zhanshi/Celestial/Guǐ Fēng proxies where not themselves legal in the played roster; Griffin/Hetkari S2 HI for Zúyǒng/Jujak or other S2 HI; Fireflies/Black A.I.R. S2 MI for Dāoyīng/Yě Māo etc.; Scarecrow S2 SK for Guìláng/Kǎnrèn; Banshee S2 MI for Tiger; Kurayami Ninja S2 SK for a vanilla or ISS Ninja. **Do not use a playable named donor from the same selected roster**, reuse one model for two simultaneous figures, or change size/class with wishful labeling. Check [proxy policy](../miniature-proxy-guidelines.md).
- **Physical exceptions:** S5 Mówáng/Yān Huǒ/Haetae and Sù-Jiàn S3/S5 paired forms merit real distinct models if played; ordinary S2 knights are wrong. S7 Gūijiă is not interchangeable with owned S6 PanO mecha; alien S7 Caskuda is also not a convincing Yu Jing model. ISS **Bayara S4** is unusual (in starter), and **Fēiquán S7 VH** is not a normal TAG. Special Lóng Yá mine robots and RacerBots may need separate adequate shape proxies when actually played.
- **Defer/skip:** Rare heroes (most Sun Tze/Adil/Léi Gōng/Bìxié/Jing Qo/Oktavia/Johnny Kao and other role-overlaps) and LE/OOP models are **proxy-first** unless recurring lists justify buying the sculpt. Avoid generic extra doctors/engineers, full AVA 8 Kuang Shi, additional standard bots, **second+ TAGs**, repeated ordinary troops when existing core quantity is sufficient, and costly special packages without clear tactical use. **Only buy matching legal units** for the selected roster: e.g. old White Banner Jujak/Yě Māo/Jing Qo not standard Vanilla, Yín-Shé not standard IA, and full 2025 Essentials is mostly *not* WB legal.
