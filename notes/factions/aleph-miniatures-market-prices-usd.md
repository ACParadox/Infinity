## Duplicate and package comparisons

- **New Operations Subsection / Mazebreaker:** **Operations Subsection of the SSS ($21.65)** is a three-model ALEPH-side release naming Dasyus Hacker and two Kinnara Scoutbots. **Beyond Operation Mazebreaker ($39.65)** is six models with the **Nomads Corregidor half** explicitly named, but its ALEPH half is truncated in the supplied text. These are plausibly alternate purchase routes for the same three OperationS figures, **not confirmed identical without the full SKU/box list**.
- **ALEPH Army Pack ($36.80, new)** has seven minis, but only some are named in the excerpt (Thorakites SMG, Agêma MSR, Apsara Hacker SMG). Older **Operation Blackwind ALEPH half ($24.00)** has **seven named models**, **Operation Coldfront ALEPH half ($27.30)** includes Dakini/Shukra/Yadu/Deva/Naga, and **Thorakitai ($18.80)** supplies four Thorakites. Match desired named sculpts rather than double-purchasing a troop just because it appears in multiple packs.
- **ALEPH Support Packs are materially different in the source:** **$15.50** contains **Dactyl Engineer, Dactyl Doctor, 2 Yudbots**, while **$16.50** contains **Sophotect, 2 Yudbots, 3 Netrods**. These are **not two prices for an interchangeable box**; the user already owns Sophotect and Trisha as previously recorded. Only buy any duplicated doctor/engineer/ancillary model if required for planned lists.
- **Achilles, Agamemnon, Phoenix and Thamyris variants:** Achilles thermoplastic with alternate arms **$6.60**; **Achilles v2 10th Anniversary, 2 models ($17.90)**; another **Achilles ($6.80)**. Agamemnon **Tournament Exclusive ($20.00)** vs regular **($16.70)**; Phoenix HRL **($9.50/$6.80)**; Thamyris **($13.90/$6.80)**. Separate source listings are not proof of different playable unique characters or identical sculpts.
- **Marut TAG / remotes / Posthumans:** **Marut resin $17.00** vs **Maruts TAG $33.50**; **Rebots Aleph $18.80** vs **Rebots $23.30**; **Posthumans 2G Proxies $27.30** lists **two Mk4 and two Mk5 weapon representations** while **Posthumans $21.90** lacks contents. Do not infer model generation, completeness or roster cost from the product name.
- **Steel Phalanx and named characters:** **Steel Phalanx Expansion Alpha ($18.50)** includes **Scylla, Charybdis and Alké**, while **Drakios and Scylla ($25.00)** may overlap character representation. **Myrmidons ($22.30)** is an explicit four-model box, versus an older/generic **Myrmidons ($6.80)** listing. Dart Convention Exclusive **$18.30** vs standard **$7.90**; Phoenix variations above.
- **Cross-faction items and accessories:** **Dire Foes Blindspot** has Hassassin Yasbir, Thrasymedes and a civilian; **Dire Foes Blocking Zone** includes Nomads Lupe Balboa. **Caskuda Exclusive ($18.60)** is a Combined Army TAG, not native ALEPH. The **40 mm Maximus scenic base ($3.50)** and **55 mm Caskuda scenic base ($4.50)** are **base-only** accessories, not complete miniatures. Seller faction filters do **not** establish ALEPH N5.3 legal roster access.
