## The practical answer: what Corregidor is good at

**Identity:** Flexible medium-infantry **Fireteams and repeaters** creating asymmetric routes for TAGs, Intruder firepower, close-range disruption and **off-table specialists/attackers**. Compared with vanilla, CJC trades some named elite hackers/units for **two Moran repeaters**, relevant FT bonuses, and specialist airborne options.

- **Go-to backbone:** **2 Moran repeaters** (AVA2, each 15-17 points / 0.5 SWC) for turn-zero midfield hacking/Koalas, **Jazz** to exploit that coverage, inexpensive **Transductor/Salyut**, a real **MSV/long-range attacker** (often **Intruder MULTI Sniper**), mission-dependent smoke screen, and 1–2 late-turn specialists.
- **Do not auto-buy Guided:** Moran ×2 costs **1 SWC**, Vertigo **1.5**, Jazz **0.5** = **3 SWC before a main SWC gun**. Post-Mazebreaker list flexibility allows strong non-Guided builds; include the missile only where repeated Spotlight or Coyote/BambaBot Forward Observer tagging is a credible plan.
- **Order discipline:** Hacking + landing forces + Irregular bandits/Diablos/McMurrough + Tactical Awareness and multiple small FTs produce impressive *apparent* order economy, but not a single transferable pool. Identify which group can pay for the decisive attacking sequence and which units actually generate Regular Orders.

## Three repeatable list designs (not final Army exports)

**A. Deepspace Gecko mission Haris**
- **Gecko MULTI Marksman Rifle / Mk12** → durable active midrange threat, Tactical Awareness.
- **Evader Engineer** (24–35 points depending on role) → repair + Specialist, or a TinBot/assault weapon package. The **35pt AP Spitfire Engineer** simultaneously provides **Engineer + Firewall(-3) + another serious gun**, but adds **1.5 SWC**; cheaper Engineers **do not always carry the TinBot**.
- **Stempler Tactical Awareness** (18pt) → Repeater, FO/Sensor/Deactivator, a second TacAware Order and FT utility. Source: WIP12 ~46–51 min, ~121–124 min. This three-person **Gecko + Evader + Stempler** construction is the strongest worked mixed armored block in reviewed transcripts. *Why*: less support-order tax, Engineer present in case TAG/REM takes damage, two gun ranges, and a button pusher.
- **Caution:** Haris uses its slot; SWC + 300pt cap make "Gecko, full Evader + Stempler" an investment. Check FT keyword/bonus level in official Army; a Wildcard does not necessarily contribute the same keyword bonus as two matching members.

**B. Surface mobile objectives and denial**
- **Coyote + BambaBot** (unlinked peripheral pairing), another legal **Surface Coyote FTO** or **Territorial + Stempler + Shifta** FT; **Moran x2**, Jazz, Intruder, Bearcat.
- *Why*: Coyote Forward Deployment/MSV1/Warhorse and peripheral FO creates **multiple approach/targeting lines**. A **BambaDroid is a distinct purchased REM**, not simply a free BambaBot: it requires a Coyote in the same Combat Group, and BambaDroids cannot outnumber Coyotes. The **Coyote FTO** profiles, in contrast, do **not** automatically include BambaBot peripherals. **Shifta Strategic Deployment** can move a Surface FT closer to scoring objectives. **Territorial Tactical Awareness** enables its own activity without consuming the main assault pool.
- *Caution*: WIP12 discusses coordinated specialist "double rolls"/FO forks. Exact simultaneous actions and scoring require verification against current Peripheral/Objective wording, especially for mission-specific interaction timing. Enemy shooting down the one-wound **Coyote turns off attached BambaBots**.

**C. Network + reserve attack**
- **Two Moran Repeater/Koala** + **Jazz FTO protected by Mobile Brigada TinBot(-6)**, **Intruder MULTI Sniper + smoke**, plus a **Vertigo or second direct gun**. Add **Bearcat (Surface)** or **Hellcat/Tomcat/Carlota (Deepspace)** as the unanticipated last-turn attack or scoring tool.
- *Why*: network punishes hackables and makes enemy deployment/advance expensive; sniper constrains their gunfighters; reserve entry attacks the part of the table where hacking is not decisive. **Jazz + Billie** supplies **2 Regular Orders** but is *not* the Jazz FTO team member; choose based on whether team Firewall/FT bonuses are worth losing Billie.
- *Caution*: If the mission mainly asks for buttons, Guided may not pay for itself; if the first-turn enemy can dismantle both Morans, backup hackers/repeaters and clean DZ defenses matter.

## Lieutenant, specialist and Order arrangements

- **Safe command:** cheap **Alguacil Lt** (verify exact option in [roster](../../sectorials/nomads/corregidor.md)) + **Lupe CoC** (both divisions), or **Shifta CoC in Surface**. Value: preserve LoL recovery without paying for a 33pt main-Lt body.
- **Protected hacking command:** **Mobile Brigada Lt TinBot(-6)** paired legally with **Jazz FTO** or other hacker; helps resist alpha-strike hacking and is a meaningful third-turn combat/guard body. Counterargument: ~33pts in the DZ may be better invested in a second attacker or engineer depending on mission.
- **Active Lt engine:** **Surface Territorial Lt Tactical Awareness +1** with AP Red Fury/Engineer alternative or **Mobile Brigada HMG Lt** (both) with Chain of Command. Invest only when the plan *really uses* Lt/TacAware Order and your fallback command exists.
- **NCO connection:** **Gator NCO** or **Wildcat Spitfire / Lobo Red Fury NCO** can spend Lt Orders if correctly available; paying 1.5 SWC for NCO weapons solely to use the LT order can be inefficient.
- **Dedicated last-turn reserve:** **Bearcat (Surface)** vs **Tomcat/Hellcat/Carlota (Deepspace)**; choose based on where mission buttons are and whether Combat Jump risk is preferable to predictable Parachutist board-edge entry. **Tomcat 17pt Paramedic** is an exceptionally inexpensive late-scoring engineer/medical-adjacent piece, not a general main gun.
- **Two order groups:** 2026 WIP12 example uses **large main group** for normal shooting and hacking, plus a **small Haris with Gecko + Evader + TacAware Stempler**; the latter has its own two TacAware activations without stealing as many Regular Orders from the main group. Always calculate *usable* orders, not raw bodies.

## Disagreements worth preserving

1. **Did the Surface/Deepspace split help?** WIP12 initial reaction: *negative*, Corregidor lost old remotes and access to half the new models compared to unsplit OSS. Later verdict: **the new Fireteam structure alone is a major improvement**, and two tactical versions broaden choices. Pros: distinctive builds, lower SWC commander tax. Cons: you cannot combine Bearcat+Coyote+Shifta with Evader+Gator+Hellcat; some opponent “hidden reserve” uncertainty is lost. [Official purpose and model-preservation argument](https://infinityuniverse.com/en/news/army-update-mazebreaker).
2. **Guided identity vs direct guns:** Old consensus: 2 Moran + Jazz + Vertigo is the core. WIP12 2026: **3SWC** for network/Guided constrains other guns, now less necessary thanks to new Fireteams/SWC-free options. Bring Guided when map/objectives let you repeat tag attempts, otherwise buy another shooter.
3. **Iguana good or weak?** Loss of Lieutenant **April 2025** negative; WIP12 **June 2026** positive after BS Attack(-3)/Escape changes. **Do not average ratings without dating the rules.** The 68pt gunfighter is materially different from the old version.
4. **Vostok “auto-include” or overvalued?** It was powerful in N4, awkward early N5, then revived in **June 2026 Haris wildcard/Fireteam** architecture. Good *because* Mimetism(-6) and link support mitigate BS12; vulnerable without anti-hacking/engineer support, and FTO loses Repeater.
5. **Gecko Vulkan vs MMR:** WIP12 contains **both opinions**. Vulkan is terrifying in 8-inch room/zone operations; a player who tried it found its approach cost made it dead weight. MMR/Mk12 suit midrange AROs and open tables. Decide from mission and terrain before selecting.
6. **Coyotes/Bamba utility vs exposure:** Enthusiasts: multiple FO/mine/repeater forks and premium MSV1/special die. Critics: 27–33pt one-wound Coyote is the controller and a single kill neutralizes its Peripherals. Always protect both the advance *and the controller*.
7. **Territorial AP Red Fury vs Marksman:** The Red Fury gives higher attack tempo; **WIP12 prefers 29pt AP MMR/X-Visor/0SWC**, saving 1.5 SWC without losing TacAware. AP Red Fury is for a list needing extra dice more than extra points/SWC.
8. **Intruder sniper vs HMG/KHD:** June 2026 reviewer calls **same-cost special-die sniper** a near-must; counterclaim: active HMG range and the infiltrating KHD's classification toolkit matter in certain missions. White Noise/Albedo and movement/corner exploitation still beat a static sniper.
9. **Vigilantes vs cheaper Moran:** pre-release advocates value Counterintelligence, unusual sensor/turret/Camo; post-release skeptics call them **overpriced Forward Deployment** troops competing with two already-efficient Morans. Buy only for the additional job.
10. **Lobo + Diablo melee raid:** Cheap disruptive melee units benefit from Correctional teams, but **current Diablos are Irregular/Impetuous** per source—do not blindly trust the transcript calling them Regular. That changes every group’s order arithmetic.

## Checklist for a 300-point pair of tournament lists

- [ ] Choose Surface or Deepspace **for each army list** and check all **exclusive units + Fireteam charts** against current Army.
- [ ] **2 Moran or deliberate alternative** protect objectives and feed real hacking reach; count their SWC.
- [ ] Hacking plan: **Jazz FTO/linked firewall** *or* **Jazz+Billie independent order package**, plus one protected secondary way to remove an enemy hacker.
- [ ] **Intruder Sniper / Vostok / Gator / Iguana / Gecko / Territorial** fills the real long-gun and active-attack job. Identify a second attacker that answers non-hackables, Impersonation/Stealth or White Noise.
- [ ] **SWC math before list polish:** Moran ×2 =1; Jazz=0.5; Vertigo=1.5; Intruder Sniper=1.5; remaining only 1.5 in a standard 6-SWC list. If Gator at 1.5 is also present, the budget is fully committed.
- [ ] If using **Gecko Haris**, verify exactly which division supports Evader/Wildcat and Stempler Wildcard; select an actual **Engineer** profile and check Firewall availability, not assumed TinBot.
- [ ] At least one **mission-credible specialist** that can still reach scoring at end of Round 3; prefer a cheap reserve if deployment lines are hard.
- [ ] Count **Regular vs Irregular vs TacAware vs Lt vs Impetuous** separately, especially **Diablos, Bandits, McMurrough** and unarrived drop troops.
- [ ] Plan who repairs the TAG/REM, which flank a lone Engineer is assigned, and whether the **Coyote/Bamba controller** can survive a counterattack.
- [ ] Test against **anti-hacking, Stealth, MSV/White Noise, mine-clearing, and boarding/airborne intrusion**, rather than a passive opponent.
- [ ] Do not field absent **Tsyklon, Lunokhod, Raveneye or Go-Pod** in current regular CJC lists. Older source army examples require rebuilds.

**Default starting pair (concepts, not verified Army codes):** **Deepspace:** Intruder + Jazz + 2 Morans + Gecko/Evader/Stempler Haris + cheap late Tomcat or Hellcat. **Surface:** Intruder + Jazz + 2 Morans + Territorial/Stempler/Shifta pressure + Coyote+BambaBot + Bearcat reserve; swap between that and **Iguana** if the mission demands heavy sustained direct fire. Adjust to actual mission objectives, terrain and legal points/SWC/group limits.

## Vision control — role/value update (23 October 2026)

- **Three distinct solutions:** **Jaguar Smoke 10/0 + Intruder MSV2** for aggressive shots; **Jazz FTO HD+ 22/.5 or Jazz+Billie 25/.5 + Moran Repeaters** for selective White Noise vs MSV/Marksmanship; **Shifta CoC + Disco 21/0** or **Lobo Disco 20–24/0** for MSV-proof crossing and close attack/mission progression.
