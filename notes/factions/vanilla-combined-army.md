**Legality/option corrections:** Vanilla Combined has **Duo/Haris (no standard Core)** from three distinct chart families: **Combined Army (Unidron/Base/Void/Nexus/Vector/EXO/Nourkias)**, **Morat (Vanguard/Yaogat/Rodok/Dartok/Rindak FTO)**, and **Overdron Duo (one mandatory Overdron + eligible Nexus/Nourkias)**. Only **Umbra Legate FTO** and **Victor Messer** are listed as the standard Vanilla Wildcards. For current TAGs, the roster offers **Avatar, Overdron, Sphinx and Caskuda** (often spelled “Cascuda” in the uploaded media notes): **no Raicho, Bultrak, Xeodron, Juggernaut or Skyhound VH**. Vanilla R-Drone AVA is **1**, not a general promise of abundant flash bots.

**Important profile distinction:** the **Charontid MSV3** gun profiles and its **Hacking Device Plus / Sepsitor** profiles are **distinct loadouts**; the 71-point LT(+1 Order) HD+ Sepsitor/Plasma option **does not list MSV3**. Do not budget a single Charontid as simultaneously an MSV3 gun, HD+ hacker, and Sepsitor user when its selected current option lacks one of those traits. The **Anathematic** likewise has LT and non-LT versions; legality of deployment state must follow the current rules and chosen option.

## What wins games in Vanilla Combined

**Make the opponent defend against more threats than you actually brought.** The roster can suggest an Impersonator, Hidden Deployment missile, airborne attack, monster hacker, MSV3/Smoke shooter, TAG or Guided threat *before any are identified*. After deployment, use the **right** one of those weapons, not necessarily the most expensive model, to remove the blocker. Keep enough cheap Orders, screening and specialists to finish the mission.

**Main change since old N4/early-N5 “top and bottom” lists:** October 2025 lowered cheap Regular-order efficiency / availability (some flash REMs), and made Ikadrons more expensive. An Aspects-plus-disposable-cheerleaders army can still work, but **you can no longer afford every apex threat and every screen**. Robert's April 2026 review now favors a much wider **middleweight** roster: **Vector/Unidron/Nexus**, Umbra Legate hacker, Sheskiin, Rasyat, Rodoks, Victor Messer, cheap Seed-Soldiers and Daturazi. This directly conflicts with treating the Avatar or Charontid + Anathematic as the only serious build.

**Always answer before submitting the list:**
1. **Command:** who is the LT, how many Orders does it *actually* add, and can it survive or hand off when killed?
2. **Primary gun:** can it beat hard AROs (MSV/Albedo/Smoke, range, AP or K1), not just easy targets?
3. **Infowar:** which hacker, where do the Repeaters/Pitchers come from, and is a specialist available for mission objectives?
4. **Attack geometry:** at least one flank/Hidden/AD/CC option not dependent on winning the same frontal FtF.
5. **Defense/recovery:** Q-Drone or Noctifer, area denial, Baggage, Engineer/Doctor, cheap Orders, and a plan if the centerpiece falls.
6. **Scoring:** at least two real mission-capable units; expensive guns with no route to the button are not enough.

## Lieutenant / primary threat decision

- **Maximum controlled infowar:** **Anathematic LT hacker + Bit & KISS + affordable Orders and a second gun**. Best when you expect an enemy hacking network to obstruct your movement.
- **Smoke/MSV + durable attack:** **Charontid MSV3 LT gun loadout** or **Vector/Unidron/Nexus** support (**not** an HD+ Sepsitor/MSV3 combined profile). Use MSV to create better FtF odds rather than charge high ARM with raw Burst alone.
- **Fast mission pressure:** **Sheskiin**, backed by Daturazi / Umbra HD+ / engineers, cheap specialists and a distinct hidden/off-table option.
- **Budget LT for premium roster:** **Nexus LT in a purposeful Unidron team**, with CoC/Warhorse contingency where sensible. Avoid treating its survivability as automatic.
- **Avatar:** deliberately devote most of the list to keeping Orders, protection and *independent scoring* around it; see TAG comparison.

**Important disputed legacy point:** old episodes advocating **Nourkias/Avatar as almost mandatory** predate post-October-2025 army construction. Keep those opinions as history and tactical alternatives, not current universal advice.

## Contradictory opinions to preserve, with dates

1. **“Big centerpiece + cheapest Orders” vs “build around versatile 25–50-point pieces”:** 2024 N4 top player Brooke prefers high-efficiency Sphinx plus cheap drones/smoke and careful withdrawal; 2025 TAG-panel criticizes Avatar's order dependence; **Robert Apr 2026** actively recommends stronger middleweight packages after Oct 2025 cheap-Order reductions. **Both win different matchups; 2024 points/AVA advice is obsolete.**
2. **Nourkias vs Sheskiin / regular Samaritan:** WIP12 Jan 2025 sees Nourkias as so good that an ordinary Samaritan often isn't worth taking; Robert Apr 2026 prefers **Sheskiin's standalone gun + NanoScreen** in many lists because Nourkias' post-nerf delivery/hacking protection costs add up. He remains a feared CC missile when supported.
3. **Anathematic vs Charontid LT:** Anathematic for **hacking dominance, Camo and safer network combat**; Charontid for **MSV3 range control/Sepsitor and flexible active/reactive gun**. Neither automatically makes the other obsolete; playing into enemy hackers changes the choice.
4. **Overdron HRMC vs Plasma Sniper:** one values **Burst and clearing hard AROs**, another the **Plasma save pressure and range/control**. Enemy MSV makes Albedo worth more; no MSV lowers the unique value.
5. **Sphinx overrated vs excellent:** detailed N4 winner favors **using 6 Orders to trade, 4 to retreat into safety**, hiding in reserve/through scoring; post-2024 changes increase difficulty with **limited AP**, poor economy and vulnerable approach. Consider if it will have targets it can finish and a path to escape.
6. **Avatar still scary vs poor competitive value:** no one disputes its direct FtF threat. The disagreement is *what happens after the opponent kills its support*; less self-generated order efficiency can strand 120+ points of hardware with few usable Orders.
7. **Bit & KISS vs Dartok vs Umbra Legate:** early 2025 lists leaned heavily on Bit & KISS Pitchers; N4 tournament winner preferred durable Dartok for Spotlight/Isolation; Robert Apr 2026 uses **Umbra Legate FTO HD+ as a more capable group-2 all-rounder**, finding Dartok comparatively less efficient today. Use separate lists/roles: Pitcher spam, reactive hacking/Warhorse or mission/White Noise flexibility.
8. **Specializing in Guided vs incidental missiles:** WIP12 early N5 advocates Charontid/Overdron/Bit/T-Drone as a robust pressure package; Robert Apr 2026 runs a **T-Drone incidentally** with Legate+Rodok+Nexus, often using reactive Spotlight. Both are valid if the player is realistic about network and Order costs.
9. **Noctifer/Speculo automatic vs matchup pick:** a stronger unknown threat can win without rolling dice, but actual deployment/timing and failed assassination attempts make them high-variance. A second specialist sometimes wins the mission more reliably.

## Mini-playbook and sample role structures

**A. “Minus-12” shooting and robust mission play**  
**Sheskiin + Vector Plasma Sniper / Unidron / Nexus CoC + Daturazi Smoke + Pitcher/KHD + cheap specialist + Q-/R-Drone.** Why: split two gun ranges, impose extreme FtF modifiers, have a genuine objective branch and protected LT. Reflects a tested Robert Apr 2026 concept; **not** a point-validated export.

**B. “Affordable elite” with Guided backup**  
**Nexus LT + Vector/Unidron + Achilles or other elite + Rodok gun/KHD + Umbra Legate HD+ + Daturazi + T-Drone + Orders.** Why: high-quality guns and hackers make the incidental Guided plan credible while keeping a flank/CC attacker. Ask if all those premium pieces leave enough scoring models.

**C. “Classic Aspect”**  
**Charontid or Anathematic LT + Overdron HRMC + Bit & KISS + Ikadron/R-Drone + M-Drone Sensor + Doctor Worm + optional Q-/T-Drone**. Why: layered guns, repeaters, LoF control and recoverable remotes. **Counter:** enemy kills cheap Orders/hackers and hides from both main guns; adjust to mission and current points.

**D. “Stealth assassin”**  
**Sphinx + Daturazi + Speculo/Noctifer + Caliban/Shrouded specialist + remotes + Engineer**. Why: initial hidden threats, trade cheaply and retreat, emerge late to outscore. **Counter:** too few Orders and anti-armor tools; likely harder in current N5.3 than in Brooke's 2024 victories.

**E. “Caskuda / Cascuda drop” (deliberately high variance)**  
**Cascuda + Overdron / ranged ARO remover + Bit & KISS KHD + optional E-Drone + budget Orders and one hidden scorer**. Why: airborne deployment can bypass all front-facing defenses after hackers and AROs are cleared. **Counter:** a single failed drop or hidden hacker can strand a 60+-point unit.

## Vision control — role/value update (23 October 2026)

- **MSV + Smoke attack engine:** **Dāturazi 14/0 Regular Smoke** enables **Vector MSV2 / Charontid MSV3** when the exact selected gun profile has a visor; **Rasyat Eclipse** is independent flank delivery. **Umbra Legate HD+** offers selective White Noise, not Eclipse; Overdron Albedo is personal anti-MSV tech.
- **Pick by task:** kill several guns with MSV-through-Smoke, bypass a lane with Eclipse, or project White Noise only when an MSV/Marksmanship defender is decisive. Charontid's HD+/Sepsitor is **not simultaneously** the MSV3 gun profile. [Global value/ceiling guide](../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).

## Toolbox / jack-of-all-trades assessment (2026-10-23)

**Most direct sources:** [Robert Shepherd, *N5 Combined Army faction focus*, April 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qoyylw0p0JM) (full YouTube MCP transcript rechecked); [Tactical Awareness, S3E15 Vanilla/CA Prime](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pTDaJJy86Sk), historical early-N5; [existing Vanilla research and opposing podcast summaries](vanilla-combined-army.md). [Current Vanilla profiles](../../sectorials/combined-army/vanilla-combined-army.md).

**Near-must / actual speaker defaults**
- **Dāturazi 14/0 with Chain Rifle, Grenades, Smoke Grenades** — **Direct:** Shepherd says he always takes this particular Dāturazi option. Regular **Smoke + lethal close-range trade** gives a cheap enabling piece for Vector MSV2/Charontid MSV3; one of Vanilla's best order-to-utility buys. **Exception:** a list that attacks through White Noise, Albedo or a dedicated non-Smoke plan; don't call the trooper compulsory.
- **Bit & KISS 22/.5 (KISS with Pitcher, Bit KHD/Oblivion)** — **Direct:** Shepherd says always choose the profile with **KISS Pitcher**; the two projection points and Killer Hacking Device justify its role. **Exception:** he himself sometimes cuts Bit when Umbra Legate HD+ supplies the network. **Crucial current-profile distinction:** the **25/.5 alternative includes KISS (Repeater), not KISS (Pitcher)**; the distinction is about teamwide projection, not saying all other hacking is impossible.
- **Libertos 8/1 Minelayer** — **Direct:** Shepherd says he *almost* always includes one: cheap disposable Camouflage/Minelayer speed bump protecting critical Orders and costly threats. **Exception:** SWC-pressure lists, or lists where its Irregular Order causes an unacceptable active-order deficit.

**Go-to, not compulsory**
- **Vector plasma sniper + Unidrons / Nexus short team** — MSV2 + Smoke and a compact Fireteam impose bad opposing shooting odds while buying useful link bodies; Robert's notable 2026 middleweight archetype. Its main risk is exposed one-wound/Exrah fragility, Smoke denial and Fireteam opportunity cost.
- **Umbra Legate FTO HD+38/.5, Tactical Awareness** — Forward projected hacking/White Noise/K1, actual extra activation and specialist use; competing substitute for Bit, not a reason to stack every pitcher user.
- **Sheskiin 48–50** — fast gun-to-CC Protheion snowball; more reliable open fighting than many dedicated close-combat heroes. **Nourkias** offers more hacker/anti-TAG breadth but needs careful delivery; both are credible.
- **Charontid LT or Anathematic LT** — choose **Charontid MSV3 gun OR different HD+/Sepsitor option**, never pretend one loadout grants all capabilities. Anathematic is the premium defensive hacker with Camo. Not both a mandatory centerpiece and affordable filler.
- **Nexus LT/CoC, Ikadron, Noctifer ML/Spitfire, Speculo, Rasyat and Overdron** — reliable role-dependent command/backbone, hidden threat, Impersonation, flank or hard-gun solutions; choose according to mission and opponent rather than automatic stacking.

**Avoid / never take (profile-qualified)**
- **Umbra Legate LT Spitfire 37/1.5** — **Direct strong avoid, Shepherd:** explicitly says not to take this LT because the cost/SWC crosses into the territory of significantly more compelling command/attack choices. **Not** a veto on the excellent non-LT Legate HD+.
- **Non-LT Charontid gun variants** — **Direct speaker preference:** Shepherd says he would *probably never* take one because near-identical LT options cost the same and are a stronger command investment. **Exception:** another deliberate LT plan; no universal ban.
- **Bit & KISS 25/.5 when bought solely to project KISS Pitchers** — **Direct:** Shepherd calls the *wrong KISS version* a bad value for that mission. It is a distinct HD/Trinity + KISS Repeater profile; retain it only when those **specific** benefits are intended.
- **Not “never take”: Avatar or Sphinx.** Shepherd calls Avatar arguably among the game's strongest models but says he almost never takes it **because of list-wide opportunity cost**, not because it is weak.

#### Confidence rules and conclusion

1. **Strict “never take” labels attach to exact current loadouts**, not to entire units. Use *speaker strongly avoids* rather than declaring a mathematical theorem from one opinion.
2. **Every faction has successful alternative list architectures.** At most a handful of troop choices are speaker-verified near-defaults; in Onyx and Shasvastii, **no individual unit has sufficiently strong all-build consensus**.
3. **Preserve opposing speakers.** Especially: Morat **Sogarat vs Kornak**, Shasvastii **Gwailo and Jayth skepticism vs 2026 winner**, Onyx **Unidron density**, Next Wave **Drakios**, Vanilla **Avatar effectiveness vs list cost**.
4. The roster is **N5.3, September 2026**, but most opinions are earlier; they are tactical heuristics, **not independently updated tournament win-rate data**. The Oct 2026 Onyx review and 2026 SEF tournament testimony receive more weight than older recordings where they conflict.

An **AVA maximum is not a shopping target**. “Rare/circumstantial” below is a *qualitative estimate of how often a particular job arises in the reviewed list plans*, **not a measured tournament pick rate**. **Decision labels:** **Defer/skip as a default dedicated miniature** = an explicit reviewer's negative judgment on that *usual role*, with exceptions explained. **Proxy first** = a specialized yet legitimate **inferred purchase priority**, *not* an invented direct quotation. **Avoid profile, NOT unit** = the named loadout/Fireteam plan is poor but other choices are strong. **Proxy the extra** = first copy or alternative sculpt may be a normal good purchase, but redundant physical duplicates need demonstrated simultaneous use. **Contested** = dated contrary opinions, successful tournament use or a distinct current-profile benefit make a universal “worst” label dishonest. **No verified complete-unit all-profile hard ban exists** in the present evidence.
