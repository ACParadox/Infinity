**N5.3 legality and profile distinctions:**
- **Combined Army Fireteams:** permitted options among Unidron, Base, Void, Nexus, Vector, EXO and Nourkias.
- **Morat Fireteams:** selected Vanguard, Yaogat, Rodok, Dartok and Rindak FTO.
- **Overdron Duo:** Overdron is mandatory; eligible Nexus/Nourkias support.
- **Fireteam sizes:** Duo/Haris, no standard Core.
- **Standard Wildcards:** Umbra Legate FTO and Victor Messer; observe the actual chart restrictions.
- **TAG options:**
  - Avatar
  - Overdron
  - Sphinx
  - Caskuda (Cascuda in older media)
- **Unavailable other-faction choices:**
  - Raicho
  - Bultrak
  - Xeodron
  - Juggernaut
  - Skyhound Vehicle
- **R-Drone:** AVA1, not a guarantee of multiple Flash bots.

**Important profile distinction:** the **Charontid MSV3** gun profiles and its **Hacking Device Plus / Sepsitor** profiles are **distinct loadouts**; the 71-point LT(+1 Order) HD+ Sepsitor/Plasma option **does not list MSV3**. Do not budget a single Charontid as simultaneously an MSV3 gun, HD+ hacker, and Sepsitor user when its selected current option lacks one of those traits. The **Anathematic** likewise has LT and non-LT versions; legality of deployment state must follow the current rules and chosen option.

## Overview

**What Vanilla Combined Army does exceptionally well:** present **more different attack and control problems than an opponent can screen at once**—Impersonation, Hidden Deployment, high-end hacking, MSV-through-Smoke, Eclipse, airborne insertion and four very different TAGs. Its identity is *toolbox breadth plus compact mixed teams*, **not** a sectorial-style five-model Core.

### Whole-game standouts — apex versus value

**Top 3 / Top 5 / Top 10** mean **qualitative, role-specific cross-faction shortlist candidates**, not official rankings or tournament win-rate measurements. A model can be apex for combat but poor value once its support, Orders and specialist displacement are counted.

### Particularly strong or unusual Fireteams

- **Combined Army Haris/Duo — MSV gun plus robotic/command tools:**
  - **Vector Operator MSV2 Plasma Sniper:** asymmetric ranged shooting.
  - **Unidrons:** inexpensive linked gun/mission bodies.
  - **Nexus:** selected Engineer, Hacker or command support.
  - **Nourkias, EXO, Base and Void:** additional legal choices in this source family, **not** a claim they all fit together or cost-effectively.
- **Morat Haris/Duo — mixed direct fire and hacking:**
  - **Yaogat MSV2:** primary visor shooter.
  - **Dartok:** hacking/Pitcher.
  - **Rindak FTO:** legal specialist on the explicitly permitted FTO profile.
  - **Dāturazi:** supplies normal Smoke **outside** the team; it is not a member of this Vanilla Fireteam.
- **Overdron Duo — unusually good heavy + utility pairing:**
  - **Overdron:** mandatory single native TAG.
  - **Nexus:** Engineer/command partner where selected.
  - **Nourkias:** alternative hacking/close-combat partner.
  - It is a **Duo**, so take **one** partner. Onyx's two-Overdron Duo composition does **not** transfer to Vanilla.
- **Structural caveat:** the pinned Vanilla chart allows **no standard Core, one Haris and two Duos**. Its mixed links support roles and movement, not giant sectorial Fireteam bonuses.

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **Several genuinely different visibility answers:**
  - **Dāturazi:** efficient **Regular normal Smoke**.
  - **Rasyat:** **Eclipse Grenades** for bypassing even MSV guns from a flank.
  - **Umbra Legate HD+:** **White Noise** in relevant visor matchups.
  - **Overdron:** **Albedo(-6)** against enemy MSV, a personal effect rather than a shared Smoke cloud.
  - **Charontid MSV3 gun profiles:** premium visor shooting. Its **HD+/Sepsitor LT option is a different loadout that does not have MSV3**.
- **Hidden/infiltration/Impersonation breadth:** **Speculo, Noctifer, Malignos** and other legally selected infiltrating threats impose different screening problems; this breadth is not available to visible Morat/Onyx armies in the same way.
- **Command insurance:** **Avatar/Anathematic/Charontid Mnemonica**, **Nexus CoC** and **Umbra Legate FTO CoC** permit distinct LT/recovery approaches. **Isolated is not Null** and does not trigger Mnemonica.
- **Four standard TAG families:** **Avatar, Overdron, Sphinx and Caskuda** (Combat Jump). No distinct standard-roster **VH/Skyhound**, nor **Raicho, Bultrak, Xeodron or Juggernaut** borrowed from sectorials.
- **Real gap:** unlimited small Fireteams or an efficient cheap Core are not available; an Aspect/TAG/Hidden-heavy roster can lack the flexible Regular Orders and surviving specialists required to score.


## What wins games in Vanilla Combined

**Visibility control (N5.3):**
- **Dāturazi Smoke + Vector MSV2:** active-through-Smoke shooting.
- **Charontid MSV3 gun:** alternative visor; distinct from its HD+/Sepsitor loadout.
- **Rasyat Eclipse:** bypass a lane from an arrival vector.
- **Umbra Legate HD+ White Noise:** selective anti-MSV tool, not Eclipse.
- **Overdron Albedo:** individual anti-MSV, not a projected Smoke zone.
Choose the enabling path based on the opponent rather than buying all of them. [Vision-control comparison](../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).

**Make the opponent defend against more threats than you actually brought.** The roster can suggest an Impersonator, Hidden Deployment missile, airborne attack, monster hacker, MSV3/Smoke shooter, TAG or Guided threat *before any are identified*. After deployment, use the **right** one of those weapons, not necessarily the most expensive model, to remove the blocker. Keep enough cheap Orders, screening and specialists to finish the mission.

**Main change since old N4/early-N5 “top and bottom” lists:** October 2025 lowered cheap Regular-order efficiency / availability (some flash REMs), and made Ikadrons more expensive. An Aspects-plus-disposable-cheerleaders army can still work, but **you can no longer afford every apex threat and every screen**. Robert's April 2026 review now favors a much wider **middleweight** roster: **Vector/Unidron/Nexus**, Umbra Legate hacker, Sheskiin, Rasyat, Rodoks, Victor Messer, cheap Seed-Soldiers and Daturazi. This directly conflicts with treating the Avatar or Charontid + Anathematic as the only serious build.

**Always answer before submitting the list:**
1. **Command:** who is the LT, how many Orders does it *actually* add, and can it survive or hand off when killed?
2. **Primary gun:** can it beat hard AROs (MSV/Albedo/Smoke, range, AP or K1), not just easy targets?
3. **Infowar:** which hacker, where do the Repeaters/Pitchers come from, and is a specialist available for mission objectives?
4. **Attack geometry:** at least one flank/Hidden/AD/CC option not dependent on winning the same frontal FtF.
5. **Defense/recovery:** Q-Drone or Noctifer, area denial, Baggage, Engineer/Doctor, cheap Orders, and a plan if the centerpiece falls.
6. **Scoring:** at least two real mission-capable units; expensive guns with no route to the button are not enough.

More expensive Caliban is better at attack/recovery and prolonged midfield work. |
| **Ikadron + Imetron + R-Drone** | Necessary Order infrastructure, **Baggage/reload, DZ repeaters and corner defense**; useful for protecting Bit & KISS/Legate Pitchers and a TAG's access points. | Their 2025 cost/AVA shifts weakened the old “buy all the free Orders” trick. Don't buy too much static infrastructure and then fail to score. |
| **Libertos / other irregular disruption** | Mines, inconvenient AROs and close-range trading protect expensive guns by making the opponent pay Orders to reach them. | Irregular Orders are not fungible Regular Orders. Count real troop-slot and activation constraints. |

**Toolbox profile comparisons:**
- **Nourkias 48/1:** WIP15 HD with Total Control/Trinity, K1, NCO, CC25/MA4/Protheion, Super-Jump and NWI.
- **Sheskiin:** direct gun/delivery advantage, but not a scoring Specialist or hacker.
- **Caliban:** lower-cost forward mission/tech role.
- **Bit & KISS 25/0.5:** economical Pitcher/hacking package.
Do not assume any one profile gets the attributes of the alternatives. [Toolbox rankings](../list-building/jack-of-all-trades-toolbox-units-apex-value.md).

## Lieutenant / primary threat decision

- **Maximum controlled infowar:** build a coherent network:
  - **Anathematic LT hacker:** primary protected hacking threat.
  - **Bit & KISS:** projected Repeaters/Pitchers.
  - **Cheap Orders:** enough activation resources.
  - **Second gun:** a separate response to nonhackable opponents.
  Especially useful when enemy infowar obstructs the midfield.
- **Smoke/MSV attack:** choose a real visor gun profile:
  - **Charontid MSV3 LT gun:** active-through-Smoke option.
  - **Vector/Unidron/Nexus:** more modular fire support.
  - **Dāturazi:** Smoke as applicable.
  **Do not conflate** the Charontid HD+/Sepsitor with the MSV3 gun loadout.
- **Fast mission pressure:**
  - **Sheskiin:** primary gun/midfield attack.
  - **Dāturazi:** approach Smoke.
  - **Umbra HD+:** infowar/White Noise.
  - **Engineer and specialists:** recover and score.
  - **Hidden/off-table threat:** a different attack axis.
- **Budget LT for premium roster:** **Nexus LT in a purposeful Unidron team**, with CoC/Warhorse contingency where sensible. Avoid treating its survivability as automatic.
- **Avatar:** deliberately devote most of the list to keeping Orders, protection and *independent scoring* around it; see TAG comparison.

**Important disputed legacy point:** old episodes advocating **Nourkias/Avatar as almost mandatory** predate post-October-2025 army construction. Keep those opinions as history and tactical alternatives, not current universal advice.

## Contradictory opinions to preserve, with dates

1. **“Big centerpiece + cheapest Orders” vs “build around versatile 25–50-point pieces”:** 2024 N4 top player Brooke prefers high-efficiency Sphinx plus cheap drones/smoke and careful withdrawal; 2025 TAG-panel criticizes Avatar's order dependence; **Robert Apr 2026** actively recommends stronger middleweight packages after Oct 2025 cheap-Order reductions. **Both win different matchups; 2024 points/AVA advice is obsolete.**
2. **Nourkias vs Sheskiin / regular Samaritan:** WIP12 Jan 2025 sees Nourkias as so good that an ordinary Samaritan often isn't worth taking; Robert Apr 2026 prefers **Sheskiin's standalone gun + NanoScreen** in many lists because Nourkias' post-nerf delivery/hacking protection costs add up. He remains a feared CC missile when supported.
3. **Anathematic vs Charontid LT:** Anathematic for **hacking dominance, Camo and safer network combat**; Charontid for **MSV3 range control/Sepsitor and flexible active/reactive gun**. Neither automatically makes the other obsolete; playing into enemy hackers changes the choice.
4. **Overdron HRMC vs Plasma Sniper:** one values **Burst and clearing hard AROs**, another the **Plasma save pressure and range/control**. Enemy MSV makes Albedo worth more; no MSV lowers the unique value.
5. **Sphinx overrated vs excellent:** detailed N4 winner favors **using 6 Orders to trade, 4 to retreat into safety**, hiding in reserve/through scoring; post-2024 changes increase difficulty with **limited AP**, poor economy and vulnerable approach. Consider if it will have targets it can finish and a path to escape.
6. **Avatar still scary vs poor competitive value:** no one disputes its direct FtF threat. The disagreement is *what happens after the opponent kills its support*; less self-generated order efficiency can strand 120+ points of hardware with few usable Orders.
7. **Bit & KISS vs Dartok vs Umbra Legate:** early 2025 lists leaned heavily on Bit & KISS Pitchers; N4 tournament winner preferred durable Dartok for Spotlight/Isolation; Robert Apr 2026 uses **Umbra Legate FTO HD+ as a more capable group-2 all-rounder**, finding Dartok comparatively less efficient today. Use separate lists/roles: Pitcher spam, reactive hacking/Warhorse or mission/White Noise flexibility.
8. **Specializing in Guided vs incidental missiles:** WIP12 early N5 advocates Charontid/Overdron/Bit/T-Drone as a robust pressure package; Robert Apr 2026 runs a **T-Drone incidentally** with Legate+Rodok+Nexus, often using reactive Spotlight. Both are valid if the player is realistic about network and Order costs.
9. **Noctifer/Speculo automatic vs matchup pick:** a stronger unknown threat can win without rolling dice, but actual deployment/timing and failed assassination attempts make them high-variance. A second specialist sometimes wins the mission more reliably.

## Mini-playbook and sample role structures

**Guided as secondary pressure:**
- **T-Drone Guided 16/1.5:** payoff for Targeted.
- **Umbra/Bit/Victor:** hacker routes.
- **R-/M-Drone and forward repeaters:** network.
- **Dāturazi Smoke + Vector/Overdron/Sheskiin:** direct-gun alternative.
- **Nox Guided sniper:** a separate unit/profile, not T-Drone.
Do not make every turn a series of Spotlight/Guided rolls when opposing KHD/Oblivion or ordinary shooting offer better returns. [Indirect-fire decisions](../list-building/indirect-fire-speculative-guided-apex-value.md).

**A. “Minus-12” shooting and robust mission play**

- **Sheskiin:** active midrange gun.
- **Vector Plasma Sniper + Unidron/Nexus CoC:** MSV and Fireteam support.
- **Dāturazi:** Smoke.
- **Pitcher/KHD:** hacking coverage.
- **Cheap specialist + Q-/R-Drone:** scoring and defense.

**Why:** split shooting ranges, impose bad opposing modifiers and keep a mission branch. Robert April 2026 concept, **not** a point-validated list.

**B. “Affordable elite” with Guided backup**

- **Nexus LT:** budget command.
- **Vector/Unidron:** firing support.
- **Achilles or another elite:** alternate assault.
- **Rodok gun/KHD + Umbra Legate HD+:** gun/hacker network.
- **Dāturazi:** Smoke delivery.
- **T-Drone + cheap Orders:** Guided option.

**Why:** multiple distinct routes, provided enough specialists and Orders remain.

**C. “Classic Aspect”**

- **Charontid or Anathematic LT:** main command.
- **Overdron HRMC:** long-range attack.
- **Bit & KISS:** repeaters/hacking.
- **Ikadron/R-Drone:** economical Orders.
- **M-Drone Sensor + Doctor Worm:** discovery/repair.
- **Optional Q-/T-Drone:** defensive or Guided.

**Why:** layered fire, hacking and recoverable REMs. **Counter:** opponents clear cheap Orders/hackers and avoid the guns.

**D. “Stealth assassin”**

- **Sphinx:** hidden TAG attack.
- **Dāturazi:** Smoke.
- **Speculo/Noctifer:** concealed/Impersonating disruption.
- **Caliban/Shrouded:** objective work.
- **REMs + Engineer:** economy and repair.

**Why:** trade from hidden angles and score late. **Counter:** thinner Orders and limited anti-armor, especially under N5 changes.

**E. “Caskuda / Cascuda drop” (deliberately high variance)**

- **Caskuda:** airborne TAG.
- **Overdron or equivalent gun:** remove facing AROs.
- **Bit & KISS:** KHD for landing-route security.
- **Optional E-Drone:** insertion support.
- **Cheap Orders + hidden specialist:** scoring backup.

**Why:** pressure from an uncovered flank. **Counter:** one failed drop or hidden hacker can strand the TAG.
