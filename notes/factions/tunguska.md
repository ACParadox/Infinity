# Tunguska — N5.3 Tactical and List-Building Guide

## Overview

**What Tunguska does exceptionally well:** it fields some of Nomads' strongest **hacker quality per point**, backed by excellent Pitcher/Repeater projection, recoverable mobile heavy-infantry teams, efficient Counterintelligence, and serious independent long-range guns. It can win by network control without forcing a Guided missile or TAG into every list.

### Particularly strong or unusual Fireteams

- **Hollow Men Core/Haris:** **Hollow Man Pitcher + Hacker/TinBot + Missile or specialist**, with a legal **Perseus** and/or **Kriza** addition. Combines vertical movement, hacking projection and resilient guns, but requires an **outside Engineer**; no Clockmaker/Fiddler in the Hollow Men chart.
- **Securitate + Interventor + Tsyklon FTO:** protective backline hacker/Repeater with a linked Pitcher gun; legal composition and Wildcard restrictions matter more than taking maximum bodies.
- **VATRA Duo — Kriza + Kulak KHD/CoC**, or an eligible **Anaconda + partner:** combines premium shooting with counter-hacking, command insurance or TAG mission utility. **Anaconda is not a Hollow Men member.**
- **Nayemnik/Spetskorp specialist combinations:** conditional **Fiddler FTO**, **Perseus**, **Stempler FTO** and eligible **Raoul FTO** give several small-team architectures, not universal Wildcards.

Pick one Core and one Haris in either list, keep Clockmaker external to Hollow Men, and reserve enough Orders for the final scored button.

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **Hacking plus visibility:** **Interventor HD+ White Noise** requires useful Repeaters/Pitchers; **Kulak KHD/Disco Baller 20/0** creates a projected Eclipse screen but **does not gain White Noise**; **Perseus/Zondnautica/Denma** provide normal Smoke routes.
- **Command and defense:** **Puppet Master Counterintelligence** is unusually cheap; **Kulak CoC** protects risky Lieutenants; **Heckler E/Marat or Jammer/FastPanda** threatens an advancing Hackable unit. These are profile-specific choices.
- **No Corregidor-style Moran start:** Tunguska lacks the **AVA2 infiltrating Moran Repeater+Koala** network; it builds reach through Fireteams, Pitchers, FastPandas and REMs instead. Cheap MSV2 answers are relatively limited.
- **Heavy/vehicle constraints:** **Szalamandra** is an unlinked HRMC TAG (NCO, not TacAware); **Anaconda** is a VATRA-linkable alternate TAG; **Go-Pod** is a real aerial **VH**. **Gator, Gecko, Iguana, Stigmata and Lizard** are not standard Tunguska options.
- **Fireteam budget:** one Core, one Haris and unlimited Duos, with actual [Tunguska required-choice chart](../../sectorials/nomads/tunguska.md#canonical-fireteam-chart).

## Executive recommendation

**Tunguska's main complementary modules:**

- **Interventor, Mary Problems and Heckler:** premium hacking and Repeater projection.
- **Tsyklon and Hollow Men Pitchers:** mobile network delivery from different positions.
- **Hollow Men/Perseus, Vostok and Kriza:** fast or vertical direct gunfighting.
- **Puppetactica, REMs and warbands:** cheap screening and distributed order control.
- **Mercenary Duo/Haris options:** alternate mission and assault architectures.

Not every good Tunguska list revolves around Guided missiles or a TAG.

**Three effective architectures, choose by mission and terrain:**

1. **Mobile Hollow Men Core + cheap network:** Hollow Men **Pitcher + Hacker/TinBot + Missile/short attacker + Perseus**; independent **Interventor HD+ + KHD**, 1–2 Transductors, 11-point Puppet Master or PuppetBot FO screen, **Clockmaker+Zondbots**, and a **Meteor/Raoul** endgame play. *Why:* the linked team moves vertically, places a powerful Pitcher, attacks or scores with multiple models, and recovers via Remote Presence. **Cost:** large team is a single target for hackers and hard close combat; engineer is outside the team.
2. **Flexible small-team/duo gunline:** a **VATRA Kriza Borac + Kulak** support Duo (or Kriza pair), cheap **Bounty Hunter+Miranda/Wolfgang** Duo, a **Tsyklon FTO** providing extended Pitcher/Feuerbach, Puppet master and **Heckler FastPanda**. *Why:* faster independent attack vectors and not committing ~120+ points to one Core. **Cost:** only one Core/Haris total; no guarantee every Wildcard is legal in every team, and too many premium weapons dry up SWC.
3. **TAG/hacking denial:** **Szalamandra HRMC** (main long gun) or **Anaconda VATRA Duo** (mission/shorter attack), **Interventor/remote/Heckler KHD screen**, cheap **Puppet FO** and **Meteor/ Spektr/ Heckler** specialist as backup. *Why:* TAG gives decisive ranged answers while hackers deny reprisal; 11pt Master plus 9pt Puppets protect softer Lieutenant/hacker DZ. **Cost:** Szalamandra 2 SWC **without Tactical Awareness**; TAG + Vertigo + Kriza may exceed SWC; suppressing an enemy TAG requires dealing with enemy hacker defenses first.

**Structural weaknesses to plan around:** expensive average Regular Order (13-point Securitate vs 7-point Transductor, 8–12-point mercenaries), limited free midfield presence compared with Corregidor Moran, highly hackable HI/TAG/REM investments, soft Lieutenant/hacker backline, sparse efficient MSV2, and non-elite CC on most of your main guns. **White Noise and smoke** can mitigate sightline mismatch, but do not substitute for actual mission Specialists.

**Vision control by job:**

- **Interventor HD+ 18–23/.5 + actual Repeaters/Pitchers/FastPanda:** White Noise against visors/Marksmanship; network reach matters.
- **Kulak KHD + Disco Baller 20/0:** projected Eclipse crossing, but **KHD has no White Noise**.
- **Perseus, Zondnautica and Denma:** normal Smoke or alternative approach and trading tools.

Disco can screen ordinary guns while White Noise selectively denies visors; do not pay for both without an attacker or scorer who uses them. [Whole-game vision-control comparison](../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).

## TAGs: Szalamandra versus Anaconda

**Szalamandra — a premium long-range TAG:**

- **Pinned profile:** 68pt/2SWC, AVA2, 3W, ARM8/BTS9, BS14, HRMC, HFT, NCO, ECM:Guided(-6) and RobbyBot; **no Tactical Awareness**.
- **Why take it:** the high-Burst/AP HRMC, long range and armor pressure hard AROs. Its **RobbyBot Ancillary** can score under the appropriate rules.
- **Order support:** NCO can use an available Lieutenant Order; **Interventor HD+** can provide separate hacking support, but it does not make the TAG a linked troop.
- **Why hesitate:** 2SWC, high cost, CC and hacker exposure, plus an opportunity cost versus **Kriza, Hollow Men and additional specialists**. Robert often prefers those wider packages.
- **Dated comparison:** N4 WIP12 favored the HRMC against **Gator's HMG**, but old comparative pricing is not current Fireteam authority.

**Szalamandra is not a Tunguska Fireteam member.**

**Anaconda — flexible TAG with Escape System:**

- **Pinned profile:** 59pt/1.5SWC, AVA1, 3W, ARM7/BTS6, BS13, Tactical Awareness, Immunity(AP), Warhorse and Escape System(-2).
- **Weapon choice:** AP Spitfire + HFT(+1B) + AP Thunderbolt **or** HMG + Light Shotgun(+2B) + Panzerfaust; prefer the former for nearer control and the latter for open lanes.
- **VATRA Duo partners:** eligible **Kriza, Kulak or Grenzer**, subject to the actual chart.
- **After Escape:** the Operator retains Specialist Operative, Tactical Awareness, Mimetism(-3) and Smoke Grenade Launcher.
- **Why take it:** saves 9pt/0.5SWC compared with Szalamandra; brings its own TacAware, anti-AP/Warhorse matchup utility and a surviving scoring operator.
- **Why hesitate:** Robert, Tactical Awareness and some MetaChemistry discussion dispute its reliability versus **Szalamandra HRMC** or a **45pt Kriza**.

Do not treat the current 59pt TAG as an old cheap sub-50-point option.

**TAG Order engines and Fireteams are distinct:**

- **Szalamandra:** NCO, **not** Tactical Awareness; always solo under the pinned Tunguska Fireteam chart.
- **Anaconda:** Tactical Awareness, **not** NCO; linkable only through the VATRA chart, not with the Securitate or Hollow Men team.
- **TAG-Treiber:** separately purchased 10pt Szalamandra Pilot/KHD in Securitate, not free firepower or an automatic Szalamandra Fireteam bonus.

**TAG and REM support choices:**

- **Clockmaker WIP15 18pt + Zondbot:** reach and repair for Szalamandra, Anaconda or REMs.
- **Interventor KHD/FastPanda:** hacker removal and midfield network coverage.
- **Smoke / White Noise:** protect the TAG's approach from opposing KHDs and long-range high-AP guns.

A bigger gun cannot replace an actual forward objective specialist.

## Vehicle: Go-Pod (and why speakers fight about it)

**Go-Pod is Tunguska's genuine AVA1 VH, not a Fireteam member:**

- **33pt/0SWC MULTI Rifle + E/Mitter.**
- **32pt/1.5SWC Heavy Rocket Launcher + AP SMG.**

Both have Aerial, 20–5 move, ARM2/BTS6, 2W, S7, Mimetism(-3), BS Attack(-3/+1B), No Cover, Super-Jump and a Go-Bot. **Zondnautica** is not a VH; it is mounted MI with a ZondMate Peripheral.

- **Strong positive view — Tactical Awareness (Jun 2025):** heavy rocket/AP SMG flies into odd angles, punishes enemy links, pairs with Kriza/Heckler deployable FastPanda pressure. The Go-Bot gives a synchronized extra template/Flash reaction. Fast late-game zone-changing can be decisive.
- **Strong negative view — Robert (Jul 2025, ~61–69m):** high ceiling but **very low floor**: S7 impossible to hide, **ARM2/No Cover** makes random TR/Feuerbach hits catastrophic, and enemy hacking locks it down before it starts. It cannot itself remove hard AROs reliably (three dice on BS13 can whiff). It is frequently **a finisher only once other troops already won the hard fights**.
- **Practical middle ground:** take **only** if you can name **(1) ARO-clearer, (2) enemy hacker-removal plan, (3) safe staging spot, (4) scoring or elimination target accessible by fast flight, (5) turn you intend to launch it**. Otherwise, **32–33pts** is often better as **Meteor + cheap guard**, **Puppets**, or another independent specialist. **0SWC rifle** is often easier to fit than **1.5SWC rocket** if running Szalamandra/Vertigo.

**Cross-Nomad availability check:**

- **Go-Pod:** also in [Bakunin](bakunin.md) and [Vanilla Nomads](vanilla-nomads.md); cross-faction experience is useful, but unit counts and Fireteams do not carry over.
- **Szalamandra:** also appears in Vanilla Nomads.
- **Gator, Gecko, Iguana, Stigmata and Lizard:** not ordinary Tunguska TAG options.

## Explicit opposing opinions and decisions

1. **Hacking dominance vs “enough hacking.”** Robert + MetaChemistry: **Interventor HD+ + KHD** is usually ample if guns/specialists matter; TA examples use **multiple Interventors + Mary/Kulak + Heckler FastPandas + Vertigo**. Heavy hacking is devastating to TAG/HI lists but has diminishing returns into non-hackable armies and can leave too few orders to score. **Pick one main Network delivery method and one counter-hacking piece**, then add redundancy only for matchup.
2. **Hollow Men full Core: exceptional or fragile/overcosted?** WIP12 **N4** says poor synergy and too high opportunity cost; Robert **N5** documents full 5-model Core with **two missiles + hacker + pitcher + Perseus** working in adverse mission pools; MetaChemistry 2025 respects its durability but notes CC11 and counter-hacking vulnerabilities. Use **2–3-person team** when you want mobility with lower single-point-of-failure; full Core when **each member** has a vital role.
3. **Kriza: amazing high-burst engine or overpriced?** TA likes **two 45pt Krizas with Kulak** for attack and Disco Baller protection; WIP12 N4 says it consumes HI-like points without AP and can bounce off real armor; Robert N5 calls **one great ARO-clearer**, second optional/fun. **Six dice is not worth two Krizas on its own**; split attack lanes or upgrade elsewhere.
4. **Go-Pod auto-include vs liability.** TA sees a flight path/strong links killer; Robert argues almost every **early** attack against hard AROs or hackers risks losing entire 32–33pt investment. **Take it as a planned finisher**, not automatic first-order engine.
5. **Szalamandra versus Anaconda/ordinary heavy infantry.** Szalamandra **HRMC/AP, BTS9, ARM8** wipes AROs; **2SWC, no TacAware**, and vulnerable to close hacking. **59pt Anaconda** is short/mid fire with **TacAware + specialist operator + VATRA**, but weaker direct ranged threat; 45pt Kriza can replicate the gun job for fewer points. Check whether first-turn ranged superiority or Round-3 specialist scoring wins the actual scenario.
6. **Puppets: Red Fury/EVO carry versus 9pt FO guards.** TA strongly advocates **16pt Red Fury+EVO** as an efficient offense; Robert/MetaChemistry prioritize **11pt Master Counterintelligence and 9pt 2W FO bodies** to soak enemy Orders and protect hackers, with **14pt AP MMR** if cracking armor. **1.5 SWC** Red Fury is less appealing when taking Szalamandra/Vertigo.
7. **Grenzer/Securitate vs Bounty Hunters.** A full quality-linked Securitate/Grenzer group looks flexible but expensive with weak keyword bonuses; Robert N5 favors **Bounty Hunter Duos, Hollow Men**. TA endorses some Grenzer sensor/NCO with smoke. Bring Grenzer when **MSV, NCO, Sensor** is needed, not just because it can link.
8. **Mary Problems great hacker or redundancy?** MetaChemistry often sees **Interventor + Mary** and respects both; Robert sees **27pts/0.5SWC** often unnecessary unless **Oblivion(+1B)/Trinity AP** solves a particular matchup. Do not turn midfield deployment into unsupported first-turn bait.
9. **Triggermen: intriguing ARO experiment versus trap.** TA proposes **Neurocinetics snipers and K1 forward-deployed decoy**; Robert strongly dislikes price/absence of irreplaceable role. One model/controlled-table test, not unproven 3-unit staple.
10. **Cheerkiller MSV2 team: cheap underused solution versus wishful thinking.** TA likes **Spetskorp MSV2+Fiddler/cheerkillers** for Super-Jump and smoke shooting. Robert finds **BS12/one wound/small CC gains** overpriced relative to stronger Hollow Men/mercenary teams. Useful if mission needs MSV2 and several close specialists, not a default full Core.
11. **Lunokhod, legacy Kulak/FTO and other outdated claims.** This is **not an opinion disagreement**.
