# Combined Army — consolidated purchase carts (Vanilla, Morat, Next Wave; N5.3)

**Prepared October 30, 2026, based on committed `main`.** Included: [Vanilla Combined Army](vanilla-combined-army-buy-list.md) (**52** standard roster entries), [Morat Aggression Forces](morat-aggression-forces-buy-list.md) (**35**), [Next Wave](next-wave-buy-list.md) (**35**). **Explicitly excluded as army completion projects:** Onyx Contact Force and Shasvastii Expeditionary Force. **Vanilla Combined Army can still legally take individual Noctifer/Malignos/Speculo/Unidron/Nexus models where the pinned Vanilla roster permits them**; their origin does not make them prohibited Vanilla purchases. All legal AVA/profile/Fireteam decisions use [pinned Vanilla N5.3](../../sectorials/combined-army/vanilla-combined-army.md), [Morat N5.3](../../sectorials/combined-army/morat-aggression-forces.md), [Next Wave N5.3](../../sectorials/combined-army/next-wave.md), Army `7.26246.158`, verified 2026-10-15. Existing per-army 52/35/35-entry *whole-roster* audits remain intact as the detailed record of buying, unit-wide skips, and specific profile decisions. This consolidated file is a **shopping plan, not inventory**.

**Budget principle:** At least one real physical model for recurring/useful unit identities, with sensible extra real **Vanguard (4 combined Morat)**, **Unidrons (3 physical, Next Wave AVA2)**, **Dāturazi (4 physical for smoke and Morat teams)**, **Yaogat (2)**, **Suryat (2)**, **Harbingers (3)**, **Raindancers (2)**, **Stingers (3)** and **alien Drone Remotes (4 across two boxes)**. Rare/weak/circumstantial characters and OOP/limited models are borrowed or proxied when a matched-size, recognizable physical donor exists. Ordinary-looking non-Combined robot pool can be proxied, **but Combined Army's distinctive alien bots are a deliberate exception**: buy or find a comparably alien-sized and styled stand-in. Skip filling AVA automatically and do not buy two or three competing TAGs as a prerequisite.

**User's actual inventory:** **1 Caskuda** already owned, **1 Armand 'Le Muet'** already owned in Caledonia, both **Vanilla legal**. **The Caskuda is NOT a Morat or Next Wave standard roster choice**, so do not count it as either army's TAG. **Staldron is not explicitly owned**. No dedicated Morat/Next Wave infantry or Combined Army alien Drone Remotes recorded; human Warcor is not a named **Glyph Warcor** alien body. Existing PanO/ALEPH and Ariadna human/robot figures only serve as *appropriate silhouette-compliant proxies*, not actual same-name ownership.

## Physical proxy constraints; no silent box/model duplication

- **Combined Army alien REM exception:** Different visual *CA* drones are not automatically ordinary human non-CA Dronbots. Modern CA two-metal-drone kit has **2** bodies; for 4 models buy two kits or supply additional distinct similarly alien and size-correct donors. Five CA drone **types** exist on N5.3 charts (Q/M/R/T/E), but you **do not automatically buy five physical robots**, only the number needed in planned lists. Nor do 2 **Ikadron** double as 2 **M-Drones** and simultaneously field themselves. *Sartroids Ranter/Puzzler* and *Slave Drone* servants have different roles, sizes and order accounting; do not clone model inventory by assigning the same physical model to several entries.
- **Owned and reuse:** **Caskuda 1** and **Armand 1** already cover two Vanilla roster entries; one *existing* Caskuda is no reason to buy another collector Caskuda. A Caskuda **Staldron** is not recorded; model this separately if chosen profile requires it. **Med-Tech + 2 Slave Drones** and **2 Ikadrons** can be shared in different armies at different times, not double purchased.
- **What the boxes really contain:** Morat 2022 Action Pack **9 soldiers**; Next Wave 2025 Action Pack **10 soldiers**; Combined Army 2026 generic Action Pack **10 soldiers** (optional alternative to some Vanilla core, 401 sold out), Morat Fireteam **3 soldiers + TinBot**, Morat Beta **Oznat+Tyrok+Preta+Skurgot**, Next Wave Alpha **Teucer+Patroclus+second Contrabandolero**, Next Wave Beta **Blackheart+Gearhead+Wraith-1**, CA Expansion Alpha **2 Ikadron +2 Ímetron**, support **Med-Tech+2 Slave Drones**, Bit+KISS **two separate physical units** (validate seller's actual package contents), Avatar+Staldron **two real models** (one TAG + ancillary).
- **Correct base/silhouette and faction style:** Human S2 figures are at best proxy donors for human Next Wave LI/MI, not a distinctive Morat head/armor sculpt, CA alien spider robot, Sogarat S5 or proper vehicle. User's S5-base Ariadna dog pool must **not** be assumed appropriate for Morat Preta/Gaki or differently sized alien beasts. Existing PanO TAGs/ALEPH HI cannot stand in for all CA TAGs and vehicles by merely sharing rough height.
- **No Onyx or Shasvastii armies:** Do **not** buy an Onyx starter or Shasvastii Action Pack to complete an excluded sectorial. A Vanilla-legal Shasvastii Noctifer/Malignos is a **single Vanilla purchase**, if chosen, not a sectorial purchase. Do **not** count the **Shasvastii 2026 Expansion Beta** as automatically three useful Morat/Next Wave soldiers — it isn't.
- **Optional/limited/released changes:** A model listed as “new” can be a **reissue rather than a new sculpt**; verify seller SKU/photos, weapon/equipment and exact counts. Old Ca Drone listing USD22.70 does not document same newer SKU's two-body count; count is contingent. No rare tournament exclusive, scenic base alone (Maximus/Caskuda 55mm base) or unrelated crossover collectible counts as a new roster troop merely because seller category shows “Combined Army”.
