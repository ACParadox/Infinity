## Duplicates, releases and purchase cautions

- **Kaauri weapon variants:** **Kaauri Sentinels $10.30** explicitly lists two bodies (SMG and Sniper), whereas **Kaauri Sentinels (Combi Rifle / Boarding Shotgun) $10.30** does not itemize body count. Compare product photos before inferring two independent troopers in both boxes.
- **Alternative individual sculpts:** **Kosuil Boarding Shotgun and K1 Combi Rifle** are both **$7.90**; **Igao Boarding Shotgun $9.20 vs DA CCW $7.90**; **Gao Rael Spitfire $7.20 vs Sniper Rifle $7.90**. Different weapons do not necessarily require duplicate purchases if proxy policy and actual list needs permit one distinct suitable figure.
- **Starter/force packs:** **Tohaa Starter Pack $29.70** and **Spiral Corps Army Pack $54.70** have **no itemized figures** in the pasted descriptions; do not guess the unit contents or falsely treat both as a single interchangeable box. Verify against [existing Tohaa roster references](../../sectorials/tohaa/) and current model inventory.
- **Potential companions and accessories:** **Kerail Preceptors $27.30**, **Rasail Boarding Team $28.40**, **Gorgos Squad $35.10** and **Tohaa Support Pack $21.00** lack complete physical counts. Do not equate the number of bodies, beasts, pilots, and companions with independent orders.
- **Mercenary and Spec-Ops:** **Armand “Le Muet” $16.50** is an existing cross-sectorial figure already noted in the user's Ariadna inventory; do not re-buy solely for Tohaa without checking actual shared use. **Aida Swanson $9.60** appears repeatedly in other sellers' faction filters. **Hatail Spec-Ops $6.40** is a specialty sculpt; storefront classification is not a legal roster claim.
- **Historical product photographs:** The provided image paths are from **2018, 2019 and 2021** for the items with pictures; these may be older miniatures, but photo URL age alone does **not** prove that a newer version exists. Note actual resculpts only when independently established.
