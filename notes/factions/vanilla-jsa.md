# Vanilla JSA — List-Building and Tactical Note

**Companion decision table:** [Source-backed JSA/Ōban/Shindenbutai must-have, go-to and avoid audit](../../sectorials/jsa/jsa.md). Specific `never take` claims are scoped to profiles and attributed to direct video/podcast transcripts.

## Overview

**What Vanilla JSA does exceptionally well:** combine cross-sectorial independent threats, concealed assassins, affordable command insurance and a few carefully chosen small Fireteams. Its distinctive jobs are:

- **Shinobu Kitsune:** premium Hidden Deployment / Infiltration assassination.
- **Oniwaban:** lower-cost concealed Monofilament assault.
- **Saito Tōgan:** Hidden assassin with Smoke and EXP melee.
- **Shikami:** self-carried Eclipse and difficult vertical objectives.
- **Tankō Disco:** projected Eclipse to open routes for others.
- **Hatamoto:** attacking Lieutenant and scoring gunfighter.
- **Kempeitai CoC:** inexpensive succession insurance for aggressive command.
- **Karakuri:** resilient STR3 mission holding.
- **Mechazoid and O-Yoroi:** distinct TAG options when a long-ranged heavy attack is needed.

The usual payoff is **concealment and route creation → one precise assault or ranged opening → mission conversion**. Buying every elite melee character at once often leaves too few Orders and scoring pieces.

### Whole-game standouts — apex versus value

**Top 3 / Top 5 / Top 10 are qualitative, job-specific cross-faction shortlist *candidates*, not official ranks, measured pick rates or claims that these are the best units overall.** **Apex** means exceptional ceiling at a given task; **value** means that effect relative to points, SWC and Orders. **Top CC threats are not interchangeable:**

- **Shinobu** — CC25/MA5/Monofilament: premium pre-positioned assassin.
- **Oniwaban** — CC24/MA4/Monofilament + E/M: cheaper concealed assassination value.
- **Miyamoto Mushashi** — CC25/MA5/EXP: exceptional melee value once contact is secured, but lacks Hidden Deployment.
- **Saito Tōgan 33/0** — CC24/MA4/EXP with Hidden Deployment, Infiltration and Smoke: different target/ammunition matchup.
- **Domaru 19–23/0** — CC24/MA3/Berserk, AP or E/M and 2W: cost-effective close-assault trades.
- **Hatamoto** — MA4/NBW plus Monofilament/E/M: credible CC without sacrificing ranged attack, Specialist or durability roles.
- **Ioann Bann 30/0** — Irregular 3W Dog-Warrior with Berserk/MA3/AP+T2 and short-range firepower; not a stealth infiltrator.

Printed CC alone does not determine the best target, delivery route or expected value.

**Concealed assassin distinction — rare Infiltration, *no Impersonation*.** These are different delivery mechanisms:

- **Shinobu Kitsune:** Hidden Deployment + Infiltration (PH19), Monofilament and Smoke.
- **Oniwaban:** Hidden Deployment + Infiltration (PH18), Monofilament/E/M.
- **Saito Tōgan:** Hidden Deployment + Infiltration, EXP and ordinary Smoke.
- **Ninja:** Hidden Deployment + Infiltration with specialist/hacker alternatives.
- **Shikami:** Forward Deployment, self-carried Eclipse and vertical mobility, primarily for repeatable scoring rather than one-hit assassination.

None has Fiday/Speculo-style **Impersonation**. Hidden positioning, Surprise Attack, Smoke and marker pressure do **not** bypass screening for free or grant Impersonation-state rules.

### Particularly strong or unusual Fireteams

Vanilla's N5.3 chart is **0 Core / 1 Haris / 2 Duos**, with only **FT-I Duo** and **FT-II Duo/Haris** families. These are **selective tools**, not the broad multi-link engines of the sectorials:

- **FT-II Haris — Domaru LT (+1 Order, 23/0) + Daiyōkai HMG NCO (50/1) + Karakuri FO (31/0):** an unusual **Lieutenant Order → individual NCO gun activation**, tough melee counter-charge and **STR3 scoring anchor** package. A strong but expensive ~104-point Haris; **the Lieutenant/NCO Order is not itself a free Fireteam activation**. Protect the team from hackers and avoid committing all three where one attack would do.
- **FT-II Haris — Daiyōkai HMG NCO + two Karakuri** (FO **31/0** or Mk12 **38/0**): **real long-range firepower + up to two independent STR3 objective bodies**. This is the durable Haris archetype, but costs enough to demand a clear mission and Order plan; vanilla **Karakuri AVA2**.
- **FT-II Haris — Rui Shi MSV2 Spitfire (23/1) + Karakuri FO + Yuriko Oda FTO:** **visor shooting + durable specialist + accompanying Engineer** in the *same legal team*. The **21/0.5 Rui Shi MSV1 Mine Dispenser** is a different control choice; Yuriko needs **FTO profile 1 or 2**, not her independent non-FTO version.
- **FT-I Duo — O-Yoroi or Mechazoid TAG + Yuriko FTO:** unusually practical **TAG + Engineer** mobility/repair pairing. Either TAG can instead operate independently, and **Tactical Awareness/LT Orders cannot be assumed to move both Duo members for free**; protect the repair model and maintain team coherency.
- **FT-I Duo — Miyamoto Mushashi + Domaru or Jizamurai:** a legitimate way to *deliver* one of the game's premier CC fighters alongside a second assault body. The **25/0.5 Regular FTO Mushashi** fits a cleaner Order economy; the vanilla chart also lists the **21pt Irregular** option, but don't silently treat its Order as Regular.

**Critical legality boundaries — do not import Ōban or Shindenbutai Fireteams:**

- **Tankō Disco:** not an FT-I or FT-II member in Vanilla.
- **Shikami:** no Vanilla Fireteam access.
- **Hatamoto:** no Vanilla Fireteam access.
- **Kempeitai:** no Vanilla Wildcard membership, even when providing CoC.
- **Oniwaban:** an independent Hidden assassin, not a vanilla link member.
- **Shinobu:** an independent Hidden assassin, not a vanilla link member.
- **Shizoku FTO:** FT-I Duo only; Strategic Deployment Shizoku is not FTO.
- **Miyamoto Mushashi / TAGs:** FT-I Duo family, not the FT-II Haris.
- **Yuriko FTO:** FT-I or FT-II; her Forward Deployment non-FTO profile is excluded.

### Unusual access, missing tools and meaningful weaknesses

- **Self-directed Eclipse — Shikami 42/0:** cross MSV-covered lanes and vertical terrain while completing an objective; the 47pt variant buys a different gun/tool suite.
- **Projected Eclipse — Tankō Disco 19/0:** block a key lane for another unit; it is a limited-use deployed tool, not ordinary Smoke or an Ōban linked throw.
- **Ordinary ranged Smoke — Garon Smoke GL 16/0:** creates routes or MSV2 firing opportunities; its short-lived fragile REM chassis requires protection.
- **Logistics — Pangguling Baggage 8/0:** reload eligible Disco ammunition only when the location and Order cost justify it.
- **Visibility decision:** choose Tankō for one cheap crucial crossing, Shikami for a mobile scoring route, or Garon for multiple normal-Smoke opportunities. See the [cross-faction vision-control tiers](../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).
- **Normal Smoke + MSV2 shooting:** **Saito, Shinobu and Garon Smoke GL** provide ordinary Smoke; **Rui Shi MSV2 Spitfire 23/1** and **Kempeitai MSV2 Shock Marksman 22/1** can exploit it against suitable opponents. **Yojimbo and Kuroshi Rider are absent from the standard Vanilla JSA roster**—do not import their Smoke packages from Ōban/Shinden.
- **High-quality command insurance:** **Hatamoto LT(+1 Order, +1 SWC)**, **Domaru LT(+1 Order)**, **Daiyōkai NCO** and **Kempeitai CoC 17/0** provide attractive, distinct command architectures. **No Tanuki CoC/Pitcher and no Metsuke Strategos** in the standard vanilla roster.
- **Real TAG choice and small-link support:** **Mechazoid** (cheaper **66/1.5 AP HMG**, Climbing Plus) versus **O-Yoroi** (**78/1.5 MULTI HMG**, ARM7, CrazyKoala/Minelayer), **AVA1 each**, both with **Kobaruto Ancillary** and legal FT-I Duo options. **No Triphammer**, and **Ōban's AVA2 O-Yoroi** does not transfer. Large Hackable platforms still need a repair and anti-hacking plan.
- **Strong but selective anti-marker/ARO tools:** **Ryūken** Decoy/Minelayer/HRL, **Raiden** Hidden heavy-weapon ARO, **Garon Total Reaction**, **Rui Shi MSV2** and **Atom Sensor** help defend the approach; these do not amount to a wide premium long-range gunline. **Shizoku FTO AP HMG 43/1.5** and **Daiyōkai HMG 50/1** are stronger conventional alternatives to forcing an assassin into a 32-inch firefight.
- **Mission access outside Fireteams:** **Shikami** for dangerous traversal; **Karakuri** for occupation; **Ninja/Kurayami** for Hidden specialist access; **Teishin** for Combat Jump/Parachutist FO/KHD; cheap **Robio/Pangguling** for essential Regular Orders and limited Repeater/Baggage support. **No Shinden Tanuki Pitcher** means a **Uran Guided Missile Launcher** is available but does not automatically form a deep, efficient hacking network.
- **Structural trade-offs:** no **Core**, only **one Haris and two Duos**, no standard **Impersonation**, and no Yojimbo/Tanuki. Several premier choices are **Hackable or Order-hungry**; the payoff is complementary independent threats, not sectorial-size Fireteam bonuses.
- **Shikami 42/47 toolbox:** rare independent Eclipse/CC/vertical scoring; poor value when the mission offers no difficult objective route.
- **Karakuri 31–38 toolbox:** durable holding and Forward Observer jobs rather than difficult traversal.
- **Kempeitai CoC 17:** inexpensive command insurance rather than a direct scoring or firefight replacement. Compare the [whole-game toolbox tiers](../list-building/jack-of-all-trades-toolbox-units-apex-value.md).

**Practical build test:** fund one job at a time:

- **Command:** Hatamoto LT plus Kempeitai CoC if choosing the aggressive-LT plan.
- **Approach:** one vision-control tool suited to the table.
- **Firepower:** a conventional ranged answer, not just concealed melee.
- **Mission:** a Hidden threat **or** an expensive Fireteam module with reliable scorers.
- **Economy:** enough Regular Orders and surviving specialists for Turn 3.

Add a second assassin or TAG only if it solves a particular matchup without hollowing out these jobs.

## N5.3 TL;DR — Vanilla JSA decisions (MCP transcript refresh, 2026-10-23)

**Build a job, not a museum of expensive heroes.** Vanilla's cross-roster advantage is combining independent units with different jobs:

- **Hatamoto:** command, gunfighting, scoring and counter-CC.
- **Shikami:** mobile Eclipse specialist.
- **Tankō:** projected Eclipse and short-range deterrence.
- **Ryūken:** defensive Decoy/mine/HRL pressure.
- **Karakuri:** durable objective control.
- **Shizoku:** conventional heavy ranged gun.
- **Teishin:** airborne specialist and attack angle.
- **Mechazoid / O-Yoroi:** two distinct TAG attack options.
- **Robio / Pangguling:** inexpensive Regular-order and logistical support.

Structural cost: **0 Core / 1 Haris / 2 Duos**, no standard **Tanuki** or **Yojimbo**, and a risk of too few usable Regular Orders after buying Hidden or Irregular elites.

**Practical templates, not fixed lists:**

- **Independent command/scoring:** Hatamoto LT + Kempeitai CoC + Shikami + 1–2 Ryūken + cheap mission/Order support.
- **Armored Haris:** Hatamoto LT acting separately, with Daiyōkai NCO + up to two Karakuri in a legal FT-II Haris.
- **TAG and repair:** Mechazoid **or** O-Yoroi + Yuriko FTO Duo + Doctor/Engineer and independent scoring bodies.
- **Hidden control:** Tankō Disco + one Shinobu/Teishin role + Ryūken/Raiden pressure.

Audit actual team membership, combat groups and the Order budget. A package named together here is not permission to combine arbitrary units into a Fireteam.

## TAGs, REMs and Vehicles — N5.3 Vanilla JSA (no invented sectorial access)

**VH vehicles: none** in the pinned 43-unit standard Vanilla JSA roster. The actual choices are:

- **Aragoto motorcycles:** LI motorbike troops, **not VH-class Vehicles**.
- **Mechazoid TAG:** AVA1; eligible for FT-I Duo.
- **O-Yoroi TAG:** AVA1; eligible for FT-I Duo.
- **Triphammer:** Shindenbutai-only in the relevant roster, **not standard Vanilla**.
- **Ōban's extra O-Yoroi:** its AVA2 permission does **not** transfer to Vanilla.

Vanilla has only **two total Duos**; a special Order spent on one TAG does not automatically move its partner.

## Core identity

Vanilla JSA is best understood as a **cross-sectorial toolbox built around strong independent pieces, Hidden Deployment / marker pressure, a powerful active Lieutenant package, and a small number of useful Fireteams rather than a conventional Core**.

The reason to choose vanilla is not simply "all the JSA units." Several sectorial-only options are absent. In particular:

- **Yojimbo is not in Vanilla JSA.**
- **Tanuki are not in Vanilla JSA.**
- Ōban and Shindenbutai Fireteam charts do **not** carry over into vanilla.

What vanilla does gain is the ability to combine high-value pieces from different JSA branches in combinations the individual sectorials cannot normally field together.

### Current Fireteam limits

- **Core:** 0
- **Haris:** up to 1
- **Duo:** up to 2

This pushes vanilla toward a list made of mostly independent threats, optionally supported by one meaningful Haris and/or a couple of mobility Duos.

## Competitive thesis

A strong starting model for Vanilla JSA is:

> **Efficient command engine + layered midfield / Hidden Deployment pressure + one durable or violent attack module + enough cheap support to keep the Order pool functional.**

The most convincing current tournament evidence supports the following shell:

1. **Hatamoto Lieutenant (+1 Order / +1 SWC)** as an active command piece.
2. **Kempeitai Chain of Command** to make that aggressive Lieutenant plan much safer.
3. **Two Ryūken Unit-9** for deployment pressure, mines, Mimetism (-6), and awkward ranged threats.
4. Add either a **durable Karakuri package** or a **Hidden Deployment / assassination package**.

This should be treated as a strong proven pattern, not as the only viable Vanilla JSA build.

## Command package: Hatamoto + Kempeitai

### Hatamoto Imperial Guard

The Hatamoto is one of the most important pieces defining current Vanilla JSA list construction.

Relevant traits:

- AVA 1.
- NCO on all current profiles.
- Lieutenant profiles have **Lieutenant (+1 Order)** and **+1 SWC**.
- Specialist Operative.
- Mimetism (-3).
- Nanoscreen.
- X-Visor.
- Martial Arts L4 + Natural Born Warrior.
- Strong ranged profiles including Plasma Carbine, Red Fury, and Spitfire.

The Lieutenant therefore does not need to hide as a passive tax. It can be a major active-turn attacker and mission piece while generating extra resources.

### Kempeitai Chain of Command

The Kempeitai gives the aggressive Hatamoto plan its safety valve.

Important current options include:

- **17 pts:** Boarding Shotgun + Chain of Command.
- **20 pts:** Combi Rifle + Chain of Command.

This creates a simple and powerful architecture:

> **Hatamoto spends Lieutenant resources aggressively; Kempeitai makes losing the Lieutenant much less catastrophic.**

The Kempeitai should still be deployed conservatively enough that the opponent cannot cheaply kill both pieces in sequence.

## Ryūken Unit-9: recurring backbone

Vanilla has **AVA 2 Ryūken**, and both Ryūken appeared in both lists used by the 2026 Krug winner discussed below.

Current profiles include:

- **HRL + Shock Mine** with Camouflage (1 Use).
- **SMG + Shock Mine + D-Charges** with Forward Deployment (+20).
- KHD version of the forward-deployed profile.

Common rules include:

- Mimetism (-6).
- X-Visor.
- Minelayer.
- Decoy (1).

### Why double Ryūken makes sense

They contribute several things without needing many Orders:

- deployment ambiguity;
- mine coverage;
- midfield access tax;
- disposable ARO pressure;
- awkward targets that require Discover / Sensor / MSV solutions;
- redundancy if one flank is compromised.

Two Ryūken can therefore improve the whole table without becoming the primary attack piece.

## Major build module A: Karakuri pressure package

Karakuri are a natural durable mission package for vanilla.

Current facts:

- AVA 2.
- STR 3.
- Remote Presence.
- Enhanced Immunity.
- Forward Observer.
- GizmoKit (PH=12).
- Access to JSA Fireteams II.

Notable weapons include Heavy Shotgun, MULTI Rifle, Mk12, E/Mitter, Chain Rifle, and Panzerfaust depending on profile.

A major vanilla-specific attraction is that they can coexist with pieces such as the Hatamoto and with other tools pulled from across JSA.

### Karakuri Haris concept

JSA Fireteams II can include:

- Karakuri;
- Domaru;
- Daiyōkai;
- Rui Shi;
- Yuriko Oda FTO;
- Neko Oyama;
- Shurayuki.

A **2 Karakuri + Yuriko** package is particularly attractive as a durable specialist/Engineer team. It advances, scores, fights through templates and attrition, and gives the opponent a very different problem from JSA's Hidden Deployment assassins.

The key list-building question is whether the Haris is creating real Order efficiency and mission value, or merely bundling three expensive models together. Vanilla does not need to force a Haris into every list.

## Major build module B: Hidden Deployment / assassination saturation

Vanilla can stack several concealed threats:

- Shinobu Kitsune;
- Oniwaban;
- Ninjas;
- Kurayami Ninja;
- Raiden;
- plus Ryūken marker/deployment pressure.

The goal is not necessarily to reveal everything and attack immediately. The value is also in forcing the opponent to move as if threats exist in multiple places.

### Shinobu package

Shinobu brings elite Hidden Deployment, Infiltration, Mimetism (-6), smoke, Climbing Plus, and lethal close combat.

Her strategic value includes:

- threatening priority targets behind the main gun line;
- forcing cautious movement around likely infiltration positions;
- preserving an unseen late-turn scoring/attack resource;
- punishing exposed Lieutenants, Engineers, TAG support, hackers, and isolated premium models.

Do not spend Shinobu just because a target is available. A 40+ point Hidden Deployment piece that survives until Turn 2/3 may be more valuable than an early trade into a merely decent target.

### Ninja package

Ninjas add cheaper Hidden Deployment specialists and hackers. They help turn the hidden-threat plan into mission play rather than pure assassination.

The KHD and Forward Observer profiles are particularly relevant when the list needs concealed specialist coverage.

## Tankō and disposable threat density

Vanilla has AVA 2 Tankō.

Useful profiles include:

- Blitzen variants;
- Flammenspeer/Contender;
- Missile Launcher;
- Disco Baller + Deactivator profile.

They are inexpensive enough to function as dangerous disposable pieces rather than precious anchors.

A pair can contribute:

- anti-TAG / anti-HI E/M threat;
- long-range missile pressure;
- disposable AROs;
- close-range deterrence;
- cheap heavy-body presence.

The important principle is not to treat every Tankō as a model that must survive. Their value often comes from forcing bad approach choices or trading above cost.

## Initial list-building framework

A practical first-pass checklist:

1. **Choose the Lieutenant plan.** Hatamoto + CoC is the current benchmark, but not automatically mandatory.
2. **Choose the main attack module.** TAG, Karakuri package, Daiyōkai, Shikami, Hidden Deployment assassins, etc.
3. **Choose midfield pressure.** Ryūken are an excellent default starting point; decide whether to add Ninja/Kurayami/Shinobu/Oniwaban.
4. **Cover anti-HI/TAG work.** E/M, monofilament, missile/HRL, Panzerfaust, hacking, or CC should exist in more than one place.
5. **Cover vision/marker problems.** Sensor, MSV, direct templates, mines, and Discover capability matter; JSA cannot assume CC solves every marker problem.
6. **Ensure specialists survive.** Do not let every button pusher also be a model whose job is to die attacking.
7. **Include cheap infrastructure.** Robio, Atom, Pangguling, Warcor, Keisotsu/Senku/Tokusetsu as appropriate keep expensive threats fed.
8. **Check range-band coverage.** Vanilla JSA can accidentally become overloaded with short/mid-range violence while lacking a clean long-range opener.
9. **Do not overbuy premium CC.** JSA has many excellent melee pieces; several of them in one list can duplicate the same job while leaving shooting, hacking, or scoring gaps.
10. **Decide what the opponent must spend Orders on before reaching the real pieces.** Mines, Ryūken, Tankō, hidden AROs, cheap REMs, and awkward approach geometry should make the defence degrade gradually rather than collapse at the first gunfight.

## Faction strengths

- Excellent close combat and assassination ceiling.
- Multiple Hidden Deployment options.
- Strong marker/deployment pressure.
- Very dangerous active Lieutenant option.
- Chain of Command support for aggressive LT play.
- Durable Karakuri mission package.
- Useful but limited Fireteams without needing to build around a Core.
- Strong E/M / monofilament / disposable anti-heavy tools.
- Good ability to present threats at multiple depths of the table.
- Access to fast specialist pressure through Shikami, Aragoto, airborne troops, Ninjas, and REMs.

## Faction weaknesses / traps

- No conventional Core; do not evaluate vanilla as if it were Ōban or Shindenbutai with all their link bonuses.
- Easy to overspend on flashy 30–45 point melee pieces.
- Some elite attackers overlap heavily in role.
- Hacking can be useful but is not automatically a dominant network; Repeaters/hackers need deliberate architecture.
- Long-range shooting must be planned rather than assumed.
- Premium Hidden Deployment pieces can leave the visible Order pool looking thin if too many are taken.
- Irregular pieces such as Ioann/Kyojin need Combat Group planning.
- Fast bikes and aggressive LT pieces can outrun their support if the attack plan is not staged.

## Working conclusions

Current starting assumptions for future Vanilla JSA analysis:

- **Hatamoto LT + Kempeitai CoC is a proven high-value command architecture.**
- **Double Ryūken deserves to be treated as a default consideration, not a niche gimmick.**
- Vanilla's key advantage is **cross-branch combination**, not access to every JSA model.
- The limited Fireteam chart is a tool, not the faction's central identity.
- Strong lists should combine **different kinds of pressure** instead of simply stacking more melee killers.
- A major strategic fork is **durable visible pressure (Karakuri/TAG/etc.) versus concealed pressure (Shinobu/Ninja/Raiden/etc.)**.

### Practical sequenced combinations

1. **Ryūken + Tankō + Hidden Raiden:** show the opponent several plausible ARO/deployable problems; keep at least one surprise threat unrevealed while the obvious lane is cleared. Goal: burn enemy Orders, not necessarily maximize reactive kills.
2. **Shikami + Eclipse:** eliminate/route around the important ARO only if necessary, Eclipse the objective lane, move and score, and stop in a position that does not donate the model next turn.
3. **Saito smoke + Rui Shi MSV2:** Saito supplies a smoke attack route; Rui Shi fires through ordinary Smoke with appropriate positioning and MSV interaction. This is a *dedicated* two-unit attack vector, not something to assume the Karakuri Haris will always have available.
4. **Hatamoto LT + optional NCO:** if opponent presents dangerous central targets, use Lieutenant resources on Hatamoto itself; if the HT is pinned or must remain safe, a Daiyōkai or Genbutai NCO gives another outlet.
5. **Karakuri + Yuriko:** fight while preserving Structure and allowing Engineer repairs; the two Karakuri remain Forward Observers and scoring pieces, not disposable cheerleaders.
6. **Uran GML + Spotlight:** identify the repeater/Spotlight activator, cover hacking retaliation, and decide whether the guided threat's deterrent value alone justifies the points; prefer this on Unmasking/Capture & Protect-like missions when the map/mission rewards it.
7. **Aragoto/Kyojin/Ioann group planning:** start with these troops where their own Orders have immediate targets. A cheap Irregular model positioned out of reach and behind your own army can be an expensive waste of a slot.

## Constructing a pair of tournament lists

**List A — Hidden/ambush:** consider the following independent jobs, **not one combined Fireteam**:

- **Hatamoto + CoC:** active Lieutenant and succession safety.
- **1–2 Ryūken:** marker/defensive lane taxation.
- **Tankō Disco:** crossing routes; **Ioann** can be the aggressive trade alternative.
- **Ninja KHD:** concealed anti-hacker / objective access.
- **One Hidden assassin:** target-dependent Shinobu or Oniwaban.

Fill remaining specialist, range and Order needs. Favors deployment uncertainty and exposed-support/LT punishment.

**List B — durable objectives:** preserve only the cheap command/defensive pieces the mission still needs, then select:

- **Two Karakuri:** durable midfield objective bodies.
- **Yuriko FTO:** Engineer and potential legal FT-II team partner.
- **Rui Shi:** optional visor/medium-range coverage when needed.
- **Independent fast specialist:** covers objectives beyond the Haris's reach.

Favors attrition, repeated scoring and opponents with strong early attack tools.

**Other viable competitive tests:**

- **Shikami + Shizoku + Hatamoto:** the WIP12 mobility/gun/command baseline, acting as independently legal pieces.
- **Double Shikami + TAG or Rui Shi:** high-mobility pressure explored in the 2025 Tactical Awareness lists, respecting vanilla AVA/teams.
- **Cheap Lieutenant + independent ranged and Hidden tools:** budget-conscious architecture with lower premium-piece concentration.

Not every list should resemble the 2026 tournament-winning shell.

1. **Promote FT-I Musashi + Ioann Bann Duo as a battle-report-backed candidate.** [#153](../battle-reports/151-160-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) actually discusses the pairing and Musashi's Classified/button contribution.
