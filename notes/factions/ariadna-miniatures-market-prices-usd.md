## Important comparisons and cautions

- **Ariadna Support Pack #2 ($28.00) vs #35 ($23.10):** #2 explicitly lists **Kazak Doktor, Dozer, 112 FTO on foot, and Elektronik**. #35 does **not** itemize its contents. Do not assume identical boxes, dates, figure count, or 112 equipment. Compare packaging.
- **Starter split overlap:** **Ariadna Starter Pack Operation Crimson Stone ($31.80)** and **Beyond Operation Crimson Stone — Ariadna pack ($18.30)** are different, complementary slices; **Coldfront Ariadna Starter ($27.70)** and **Beyond Coldfront ($15.80)** are older similarly complementary releases. Treat these as seller-quoted products, not current Army legality assumptions; reconcile against inventory.
- **Caledonia needs:** the user's [Caledonia buy list](caledonia-buy-list.md) prioritizes missing **Wallace and Scots Guard**, then optional second **SAS and Cateran**; this price file does not by itself establish whether #16 contains exactly the desired figures, whether #88 is purchasable, or whether second models are needed. Consult inventory and boxed contents before ordering.
- **Models with multiple listing variants:** **Wolfgang Amadeus Wolff (#12 standard / #23 exclusive)**; **Uxia McNeill (#25 Assault Pistol / #78 Covert Action)**; **Unknown Ranger (#41 GenCon LE / #46 regular)**; **Intel Spec-Ops (#28 / #42 GenCon / #43 Grunt / #87 unpackaged)**; **Van Zant (#53 / #71 / #73)**; **Mavericks (#47 / #69 / #56 dismounted)**; **Volunteer HMG (#44 box / #76 single)**; **USAriadna Grunts (#54 / #64)**; **Loup-Garous (#80 sniper / #83 box)**.
- **Devil Dogs and support:** **Devil Dogs Teams (#66)** says **two miniatures**, potentially overlapping **Devil Dog (#68)** and **K-9 Antipode (#67)**. **Traktor Muls (#72)** expressly says **two miniatures, *not four***. Remotes, ancillary models and separate physical forms are not automatically additional orders.
- **Cross-faction catalogue contamination:** **Hussein Al-Djabel, Korsan, Exos, Varangian, Cyberghost, Jazz, Aida Swanson, Penthesilea, and other narrative/RPG models** appear within the seller Ariadna filter. This does **not** establish legal Ariadna sectorial choices. Preserve their prices as seller data, and check relevant [Ariadna sectorial reference files](../../sectorials/ariadna/) for list building.
- **Exclusive / unusual packaging:** preorder-exclusive Korsan, exclusive Wolfgang, GenCon Unknown Ranger, GenCon Intel Spec-Ops, Go-Go Marlene limited edition, Penthesilea special edition; #87 Intel Spec-Ops explicitly **without box/package**. The final Scots Guards price appears **without a visible availability label**, not as an explicit in-stock offer.
