## The short answer

- **Identity:** A deep toolbox of *hackable but efficient* REMs supporting elite WIP hackers, a very cost-efficient Posthuman package, flexible Fireteams, and two very different TAG choices. Most lists cannot afford *every* good new named character.
- **What changed:** **Dikpala replaced old Dakini-centered plans**; **Pilot-X + two RacerBots** introduced cheap orders and very fast deployable-repeaters; **Sharvara** made close assault/trading realistic; **Kinnara** added Decoy/Sensor and *Eclipse*; **Tarkshya** supplies a serious Vehicle; **Maximus/Asura** gained meaningful offensive upgrades.
- **Recurring efficient base:** **Posthuman Mk1 Engineer + one/two other proxies**, **Lamedh 7pt**, **Probot Baggage 8pt**, **Pilot-X + RacerBots** where points permit, and a **Dikpala/Deva** or **Samarthan** link. Cheap orders must fit the *actual combat group* that will attack. Pilot-X's dependent RacerBots are in the **same group**.
- **Pick your expensive axis:** (a) **Asura HD+ Lieutenant(+1 Order)**, often plus **Yadu NCO**; (b) **Marut** gun/Strategos; (c) **Maximus TAG + Johnny Kao/Claire MAX-OP**; or (d) an **Optimates / Agent Maximus** specialist assault. The rest of the army should cover that axis's weaknesses.
- **Exposed weaknesses:** Repeater/Pitcher hacking is dangerous despite ECM; a crowded set of large REM bases is hard to conceal; most premium pieces are expensive; Lieutenant camouflage is awkward; OSS **lacks native ordinary smoke to smoke-shoot with the Marut/Asura**. Eclipse and Disco Balls *deny vision*, not the same as allied smoke-shooting.

## Arguments worth preserving, not flattening

1. **Asura hacker vs AP Spitfire:** LoL E168 guests overwhelmingly prefer **63pt HD+ / 67pt LT** for superior mission + hacking + MSV3 gun utility, but acknowledge **AP-Spitfire** is a genuine anti-armour gun now; Shepherd similarly thinks AP upgrade finally makes the pure-gun profile legitimate. Take gun if the network/hacker role is otherwise covered.
2. **Marut and MAX-OP Duo:** Tactical Awareness finds a supported TAG genuinely powerful, but LoL E168 cautions that adding a 33–42pt named companion purely for an extra FT benefit is too expensive; buy **Engineer/Doctor, scoring or board control** rather than bonuses alone.
3. **OSS vs Vanilla ALEPH:** May Tactical Awareness is enthusiastic about the newly broad OSS options and Fireteams; Shepherd's June post-update comparison leans **Vanilla ALEPH** because Penthesilea/smoke and out-of-sectorial tools solve matchups that a wall of hackable REMs cannot. Both call the sample of well-tested post-update lists small; no settled performance ranking.
4. **All-in Posthumans?** Shepherd describes them as exceptional slot/points economy, especially **Mk1 Engineer + Mk4 HMG**. The same discussion allows **2 Proxies instead of 3** to afford extra Naga/Dart or meet missions; a good package is not permission to neglect scoring.
5. **Yaksha vs cheaper counters:** Tactical Awareness hosts split between valuing the **Forward-Deployed ARM4 MadTrap/minelayer** and preferring **two RacerBots plus Pilot-X** or Dasyu/Naga/Dart defense. Compare *total* required package cost and exact deployment area; no universal winner.
6. **Hawkwood / Bounty Hunter teams:** Older OSS instructor favored **Riksha HRL + Artalis Engineer** for repair/reliability versus Hawkwood's improved immediate ARO odds; Hawkwood supporters value durable K1 sniper/chain of command. His Fireteam access is **Bounty Hunters only**; he isn't a free universal Wildcard.
7. **TAG Maximus:** LoL's April **2025** episode rates older Maximus poorly compared with then-stronger TAG choices; May **2026** reviewers emphasize the new **AP HMG/Thunderbolt + TAGCom/MAX-OP**. These are *not simultaneous conflicting current profiles*—record the historical change rather than averaging rankings.
8. **Hacking protection:** LoL argues ECM Hacker(-3) merely brings OSS up to a fair baseline against top-tier enemy hackers; Tactical Awareness/Shepherd praise the overlapping **Apsaras KHD/Zero Pain Pilot-X/Asura HD+ and repeaters** as active offensive capacity. Both are compatible: a strong network is still vulnerable if its nodes are forward in hostile KHD range.

## Deployment, target priority and spending discipline

- **First:** reserve a *safe base* of Lamedh/Probot, protected LT/CoC, Mk1 Engineer + REM repair routes; identify which two or three ARO pieces survive if opponent gets first turn. Hacking defenses are about **where the repeaters sit**, not just the ECM statistic.
- **Deployment/turn 1:** deploy **Dikpala or Pilot-X repeaters** where one activation gives the hacker a *valuable* hostile TAG/HI target; do not donate your own army to an opposing Pitcher network. **Kinnara/Dasyu/Naga/Dart/Yaksha** compete for midfield denial; choose one or two *different* functions rather than duplicate gimmicks.
- **Attack order:** if a Marut, Maximus, Mk4, Riksha or Rudra is your main gun, budget enough **Regular Orders in its own combat group**, a recovery route, then a second independent specialist/AD victory route.
- **Reactive:** choose **FTO Samekh for link ARO** *or* **non-FTO Guided** with a real Spotlight loop; don't claim both. **Zayin** plus **Riksha HRL/Engineer** are recoverable obstacles, not invulnerable sentries.
- **Endgame:** Garuda FO, Dart/Dasyu, Mk5 FO, Claire, Kyra, Agent Maximus and RacerBot repeater/denial can win objectives after the premium gun is spent. Keep a specialist alive rather than trading everything to kill another TAG.
- **Points/SWC:** cheap RacerBots/Lamedhs do not erase huge **high-price characters, SWC missile+sniper+HMG pressure**, group-slot constraints, or S3/S4 base congestion.

## Traps for readers of older videos

- **Dakini no longer form OSS's current Dakini Fireteams.** Use **Dikpala** and the September 2026 exact `OperationS` / `Samarthan` charts.
- May commentators say **Dikpala repeater Panzerfaust**; N5.3 reference lists **Akrylat-Kanone**.
- **Samekh FTO 15/1.5** lacks Guided; **Guided 16/1.5** is *not FTO*. Cannot assume link + Guided simultaneously.
- **Posthuman Mk4** carries HMG; **Mk5** is the fast FO/Mk12. Some casual transcript speech muddles the marks.
- **Pilot-X's 23/.5 OSS profile plus two 8pt RacerBots = 39pt**, not 36; and RacerBots aren't free independent roster choices. Vanilla PanO has different Pilot-X pricing.
- **Maximus Agent and TAG Maximus are mutually exclusive**; beware old previews claiming both.
- **Kinnara Eclipse does not substitute for ordinary smoke-shooting with an MSV Marut**; **Tarkshya is VH, not TAG** and TAGCom isn't a generic Vehicle buff.
- May 12 source videos are **pre-release conjecture**; refer to the [current canonical OSS Fireteam section](../../sectorials/aleph/operations-subsection.md#fireteams) for actual Wildcard/FTO/required-source access.

## Vision control — role/value update (23 October 2026)

- **Local alternatives to missing native ordinary Smoke:** **Yadu Disco 28/0** (FO/NCO/mission utility by specific profile), **Claire Lazhari Disco + Doctor(2W) 33/0**, **Kinnara Parachutist Eclipse 24/0**; **Asura HD+** projects *White Noise* when the target is MSV/Marksmanship and the Hacking Area reaches the intended zone.
- **2026 Tactical Awareness disagreement:** value the cheaper 22pt HD+ / Pitcher White Noise option against specific visors, but some hosts prefer paying for a more broadly useful unit if those targets are absent; Yadu is cheaper Disco than Claire, Claire repairs/heals valuable bodies. ## Toolbox / jack-of-all-trades assessment (2026-10-23)

**Asura HD+63/.5** is the top toolbox anchor (visors, strong guns, WIP15 hacking, scorer, resilience), but **Asura AP Spitfire61** is NOT the hacker. If the list needs redundancy or fewer hackable premium bodies, **Posthuman proxy+Danavas/Dawon/Claire/Artalis** can distribute repair, hacking, scoring and gunfighting at the cost of coordination. The OSS Fireteam bonuses cannot be imported to vanilla.
