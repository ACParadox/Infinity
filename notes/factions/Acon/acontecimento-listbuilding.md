## Choose an architecture, then buy units

| Plan | Practical spine | Why / tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| **Regular firebase + flanking threat** | Regulars Core with Sapper MSR 20/1, Minelayer/Sensor 13, paramedic 12, 10-point LT; Naga or Akali; mobile attack piece | Cheap, difficult to surprise, safe even on sparse DZ terrain; entrenched Sapper cannot move and retain its state. Do not give away the whole Core to an enemy template. |
| **Bagh-Mari assault/mission team** | Bagh-Mari HMG 30/1.5 or FO 22/0 + Bagh-Mari specialist/paramedic, Rao or **Machinist SAA**; other Regulars fill the order pool | MSV1 + Mimetism (-3), BS13, NCO, Warhorse, Number 2; good active fights and classifieds. Expensive for one-wound MI; mixed composition may forgo a desirable pure-bonus arrangement. |
| **Cheap mixed assault link** | One Regular in **Regulars** Core/Haris with up to two Bagh-Mari/ORCs, up to one Machinist and optionally the **9-point Beasthunter FTO** | Recruits a superior shooter or close-range deterrent without filling the list with expensive Bagh-Mari. Explicit Regular minimum and composition bonuses still matter. |
| **Montesa skirmishing** | Two **foot** Knights of Montesa in **Forward Montesa Duo** (e.g., FD Red Fury 32/1.5 + FD paramedic 26/0), or 20-point Montesa FTO in an Acontecimento link | Foot profiles have **BS Attack (+1 Special Die)** and Mimetism; Blitzens/shotguns/paramedic score and deter armored counterattacks. `Limited Cover`/Impetuous create positioning limits, and the **Forward Duo has exactly two foot Montesas**. |
| **TAG + useful partner** | Tikbalang 69/1.5 or Dragão 76/1.5 + **Pathfinder TacAware 17** in a legal Moto-mecanizados Duo; Machinist/Palbot support | Pathfinder brings its own useful FO/Sensor/Repeater/order and can activate with the TAG on eligible Fireteam Orders, making attack and objective runs economical. **AVA1 Pathfinder** is a real opportunity cost. |
| **Close-range combined arms** | Guarda de Assalto 40–44, Peacemaker 23–27, Bulleteer 19–22, Naga/Dart | Guarda's synchronized Auxbot provides **Eclipse** and threat splitting; FD Peacemaker deploys a forward presence; Bulleteer Mimetism (-6) exploits cleared approaches. Support with hackers/engineer; do not expose all hackable pieces through the same repeater net. |

## Specific picks, role and dissent

| Pick | Why spend the points | Competing views / warning |
|---|---|---|
| **Regular Sapper MULTI Sniper (20/1)** | Sapper brings its own cover and Mimetism, allowing effective ARO even where terrain is terrible; longer back-edge lanes strain hostile HMG range bands. **Dante; historical Shepherd; MetaChemistry favor/expect it.** | **Dice Gods 2026** says 20 points/1 SWC is often better invested elsewhere; this defense is static. Choose if your deployment/table needs an ARO anchor, not automatically. |
| **Regular Minelayer/Sensor (13/0)** | Zero-SWC DZ mine + Sensor and Triangulated Fire as backup gun; can cheaply deter Impersonation, infiltrators, AD and late-game sweeps. **Broad source agreement.** | Spending 3 points over a plain Regular is not free: specialist/extra Orders may be more relevant in an objective race. |
| **Regular hacker (17/0.5)** | WIP13 Hacking Device with FastPanda/Cybermine: a cheap forward network projection attached to an otherwise useful order/link body. | BTS0 and LI fragility: should not be the only hacker expected to win against dedicated cyberwar units. **Older 16-point/1-SWC grenade-launcher praise is outdated.** |
| **Bagh-Mari** | **BS13, MSV1, Mimetism (-3), Warhorse, NCO, Number 2** make a resilient-to-MOD *shooter* and flexible specialist package. **MetaChemistry** expects several for their classified/objective coverage; Tactical Awareness praises the upgrades. | Tactical Awareness notes **23+ points for 1-Wound MI** can be inefficient; ordinary ORCs cost only a little more for greater durability, and **Swiss Miss** sees some ORCs as largely redundant *when Montesa does the close-range job more cheaply*. Both perspectives depend on whether you need MOD control, wound count, or special-dice short-range defense. |
| **Rao (25 NCO; 27 LT)** | WIP14 and Wildcard **counts as Bagh-Mari**; **LT +1 Order** helps fund NCO Bagh-Mari attacks, or +1 Command Token gives strategic resilience; good mobile specialist. | An exposed LT in an aggressive link invites decapitation; cheap Regular LT + separate assault specialist may be safer. Rao's LT profiles are *not* NCO profiles themselves. |
| **Montesa foot FTO 20/0** | Two-wound, Mimetism (-3), Blitzen/boarding shotgun/paramedic and +1 Special Die for very little cost: excellent danger-zone coverage and post-clearance missions. **TA, MetaChemistry and Swiss Miss are enthusiastic.** | Impetuous and Limited Cover alter survivability; don't substitute motorcycle/FD loadouts into the FTO-only Acontecimento row. |
| **Motorized Montesa 21–29** | 20–15 MOV, aggressive boarding shotgun/Red Fury, paramedic choice; can threaten flank and objectives in Moto-mecanizados. | In the TA episode, hosts actively built around **foot** Montesas rather than motorcycles because of special dice, cheaper cost and links. Bikes win when terrain/mission rewards speed more than stable defense. |
| **Guarda de Assalto 40–44** | BS15/Mimetism backed by the synchronized Auxbot's **game-unique Eclipse Grenade Launcher** and flamethrower/shotgun creates an unusual PanO path around unanswerable front arcs. **Tactical Awareness loves double-Guarda pressure.** | **Swiss Miss** is more skeptical about cost/independent impact. Auxbot is the Eclipse source: kill or isolate it and the Guarda becomes a conventional expensive short/mid-range shooter. Cannot join standard SAA Fireteams. |
| **Scylla + Charybdis** | 32-point standard Hacking/EVO setup or **37/0.5 FTO package** with a separate Regular-order Charybdis FTO, Pitcher and strong Trinity SR(-2); adds meaningful active infowar and template/repeater leverage. **TA and Dante emphasize it.** | WIP13 is not top-tier infowar by itself; no guarantee the EVO profile pays off. **Do not split the FTO package** into invented independent selections. Protect/maintain actual network access. |
| **Naga / Dart** | **Naga WIP14 Infiltration/Camo**: **KHD 27/0** (E/M Mine, anti-hacker and specialist utility) or **FO 25/0** (mission scoring + Deployable Repeater) are the more defensible general picks. **Monofilament Minelayer 26/.5** is only a conditional anti-TAG/HI choke ambush, **not** a whole-game Top 10 standout. **Dart** 35–42 remains a separate FD hunter with viral bow, E/M grenades and mine/HoundBot options. | [**Swiss Miss (~57:00)**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hlmVXGHskS4&t=3420s) calls the Naga Mono variant amusing but not a recommended default; [**Dante (~39:30)**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GKR_cd3oEFE&t=2370s) prefers the Peacemaker Minelayer and Naga KHD/utility. The Mono mine can be Dodged, cleared, baited or bypassed. **TA/Meta** value Dart, but the reviewers debate its points cost. |
| **Akali / Kirpal Singh** | 21/0 **Akali BSG (+1B) specialist with repeater** is a serious enemy-DZ objective/attack contingency; 25/0.5 Hacking Device or 26/1.5 Spitfire for niches. Earlier notes mentioned a KHD; the pinned option table does not establish such an Akali loadout. **Kirpal 26/0 CoC** or 28/0 specialist offers an AD/command alternative. | **TA** notes newer PanO AD rivals can be more specialized. Off-table CoC is *not* an on-table security blanket until deployed; guard the LT/order budget and respect landing risk. |
| **Agnes CoC / RemDriver** | **18/0 CoC** safeguards aggressive Rao/Tik LT plans; **16/0 RemDriver** can improve a key REM shooting plan when its rules permit. Wildcard adds flexible linking. | Support cost and single-target focus: don't purchase the RemDriver profile for a TAG without checking valid targets; it is a **REM** plan, not +1 BS for every unit. |

**Vision-control choices:**

- **Guarda de Assalto (40–44 points) + synchronized Eclipse Auxbot:** creates an MSV-proof crossing while the Guarda remains a BS15 active shooter; delivery geometry, Orders and Auxbot survival matter.
- **Naga:** offers an alternative midfield approach when Eclipse is unnecessary.
- **Dart:** brings another independent approach and pressure route.
- **Peacemaker:** provides REM-led board access or conventional attack lanes without requiring the Guarda's full package.

Take Guarda when an objective or gunfighter actually needs Eclipse; otherwise compare those cheaper/different routes on their own merits. [Global vision-control comparison](../../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).

## TAGs, Vehicles and REMs (cross-PanO research)

### TAGs — Dragão versus Tikbalang is a genuine disagreement

| TAG | What it solves | Who argues against it, and why |
|---|---|---|
| **Tikbalang 69/1.5; LT +1 Order 73/1.5** | BS15 HMG with Mimetism (-3), Climbing Plus, mine, TacAware and smaller S6 body: finds alternate attack angles and can retreat into safety. As solo **LT +1 Order** it can use its personal LT Orders for exceptional self-funding. **Historical Shepherd and TA** especially value practical mobility/deployment. | **Swiss Miss** questions whether a plain HMG earns 69–73 points versus tougher armored targets; **Dice Gods and TA** emphasize that a Dragão's HRMC breaks heavy armor more decisively. TAG LT risks Loss of Lieutenant if lost. **Personal LT Orders don't automatically move a Duo partner**; a two-model Fireteam may break if spent solo. |
| **Dragão 76/1.5** | BS15 **Hyper-Rapid Magnetic Cannon** is the roster's premium high-burst anti-armor answer. ARM8, continuous-damage immunity, TacAware and heavy-flamethrower extra burst defend the investment. **Dante, Swiss Miss and TA** emphasize why the actual gun matters when normal HMGs bounce off armor. | **TA hosts explicitly dissent**: only +7 points but bulky S7 with no Mimetism or climbing, harder to hide or flank, and can be outplayed by visibility MODs. **MetaChemistry** instead describes it as a real heavy long-range threat; choose it when the mission/opposition needs raw armor killing more than Tik's movement and MODs. |

**Repair, command and REM support:**

- **Machinist (17–20 points):** Mimetism (-3) engineer with access to appropriate Fireteams.
- **Palbot (3):** extends the engineer's repair reach.
- **Tech-Bee (9):** TAGCom **Dodge PH +3 / GizmoKit PH +1**, *not* a BS boost.
- **Pathfinder Tactical Awareness (17 vs 14):** the 3-point premium is worthwhile only when its extra activation saves real Orders.

The TAG's own GizmoKit is not a substitute for a well-positioned Engineer.

**Vehicles:** SAA has **no VH entries**; don't copy **Redeye** (or other PanO flyers) into SAA lists. [Shepherd's PanO vehicle assessment](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N6TYjMgU4ZM) and [Dice Gods' aerial-vehicle discussion](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QscqX3hlUZU) are useful for *opponent threat assessment*, not SAA construction: flying side angles and Sensor can threaten exposed backfield pieces; flyers cannot rely on ordinary cover and can be vulnerable to hackers/long-range ARO. Both transcripts reviewed; no SAA vehicle was confirmed.
