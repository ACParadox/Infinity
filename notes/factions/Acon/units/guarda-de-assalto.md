# Guarda de Assalto — Shock Army of Acontecimento (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: B+ / situational. Not a must-have. Prefer Spitfire 44/1.5 for independent assault or Heavy Rocket Launcher (+1B) 40/1.5 for ranged clearance and anti-Fireteam work. The MULTI Rifle 40/0 is a niche SWC-saving support pick. Eclipse alone does not justify 40–44 points on a non-specialist.**

## What is worth paying for?

A BS15/Mimetism (-3), two-Wound HI with PH14, Combat Instinct, HFT and a second synchronized attack origin is a credible attacker. Both bodies ignore Surprise Attack MODs and negate opposing Stealth; neither has elite close combat. The Guarda is hackable with BTS3 and no native Firewall. Protect it against enemy repeater networks and dedicated melee threats.

The Auxbot's repeatable Eclipse Grenade Launcher is the distinctive benefit: it blocks MSV as well as ordinary LoF, allows speculative attacks, and has a neutral range MOD through 24 inches. Unlike disposable Disco Baller ammunition, it can keep creating screens while the bot survives. It rolls using the bot's own BS, not BS15. Prefer safe placement from cover; an Eclipse roll can oppose eligible attacks on the user, but does not automatically protect the controller during the same Order. Eclipse blocks friendly shooting too, so use it to bypass lanes or reach another firing angle, not as an MSV shooting combo. The two bodies also allow legal split-threat attacks (e.g., rifle versus flamethrower) under Synchronized rules. Losing the bot removes the Eclipse and second attack origin.

## Profile selection

### Spitfire 44/1.5 — A−, best conventional assault

B4, BS15 and Mimetism provide strong independent Face-to-Face performance at 8–24 inches. Eclipse lets the attacker avoid unfavorable fights, while the two flamethrowers and AP Heavy Pistol support close clearance. Use as a midrange opener or second-wave assault piece that can continue toward the enemy DZ. No AP on the Spitfire, MSV or FT bonuses; not an apex anti-armor shooter. The Tikbalang costs 25 points more but brings an HMG, an extra STR, ARM6, Climbing Plus and Tactical Awareness.

### Heavy Rocket Launcher (+1B) 40/1.5 — A−, equally worth testing for a different role

B3 in active turn, BS15, Mimetism and +3 range at 16–32 inches make a credible ranged clearance piece. At 8–16 inches the HRL is neutral; HFT/AP Heavy Pistol cover close range. Blast mode threatens clustered Fireteams with impact templates and Continuous Damage, while hit mode provides an alternative for single targets. It is less reliable than a B4 Spitfire for ordinary duels but extends reach and gives a different attack tool from Acon's HMGs. Prefer when the list already has short/midrange shooting but needs long-range template pressure.

### MULTI Rifle 40/0 — B, niche

B3 AP/Shock, DA anti-materiel mode, HFT, Eclipse and zero SWC. Retains the entire support package and is more attractive if TAGs or linked guns consume SWC and already open the long lanes. Otherwise 40 points is expensive for an unlinked non-specialist rifleman that needs Orders to reach its 0–16-inch positive range. Not the default simply because it saves SWC.

The Guarda is not a substitute for mission specialists. If the main requirement is just killing targets, Bagh-Mari, Montesa, Bulleteer or TAGs may deliver better order/points efficiency. If the requirement is just two synchronized attack origins, consider Peacemaker first.

## When to include

- Dense tables or missions with critical crossings held by hard AROs, particularly MSV.
- Forward Montesa assault/CC or specialist access where the Eclipse route genuinely changes the plan.
- Independent second attack vector when a linked team or TAG already supplies the primary gun.
- HRL template clearance against clustered links or a Spitfire assault run where the attacker can exploit its own Eclipse.

Usually 0–1. Double Guarda (80–88 points) is a deliberate vision-control/assault skew rather than a baseline: Tactical Awareness favors this pressure, while Swiss Miss is skeptical of its cost and independent impact. Prefer one and diversify with cheaper specialists, Montesas, REMs or a Bagh-Mari team.

**Collection:** one Guarda and its Auxbot if planning to test the unique package. No need for a second unless deliberately building double Guarda.
