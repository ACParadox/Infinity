# Kirpal Singh, Akalis Sergeant — Shock Army of Acontecimento (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: B+ overall — good situational pick, not mandatory.** The standard 21/0 Akali Specialist remains the default for inexpensive airborne scoring and disruption. Kirpal's 5–7 point premium buys NCO, credible E/M close combat, Shock Immunity and improved PH/BTS, plus either Chain of Command or AP ranged fire. Take him when those upgrades have an identified purpose, especially if Lieutenant Orders would otherwise go unused.

The ordinary Akali is the stronger default value: B3 active-turn shotgun, D-Charges, specialist utility and hacking network projection for only 21 points. Kirpal's BSG (+1SD) is a distinct modifier, not the same as +1B; the extra special die can improve reactive shooting as well as active shooting under the applicable special-die rules.

Kirpal's CC21/MA1 and E/M CCW make approaching isolated heavy troops a real option rather than a desperation attack. Stealth and Jet Propulsion can help reach favorable angles, but neither makes him immune to AROs or guarantees access to melee. Shock Immunity improves recoverability, not the number of Wounds he can sustain. BS13 remains unchanged: he is a selective attacker rather than an apex gunfighter.

## Profile selection

### Boarding Shotgun (+1SD), Chain of Command — 26/0

Combines a close-range airborne attacker, NCO and conditional command insurance. Boarding Pistol and E/M CCW give alternate close-range tools; D-Charges add demolition and relevant Classified utility.

**Critical limitation:** [Chain of Command](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Chain_of_Command.md) requires Kirpal to be on the table as a Model or Marker, neither Isolated nor in a Null State, at the Loss of Lieutenant check. An off-table Kirpal cannot save the army from Loss of Lieutenant. Deploy normally or arrive before exposing the Lieutenant if command insurance is essential. Normal deployment is legal but forgoes the unusual airborne attack angle.

His two jobs can conflict: aggressively spending Kirpal to disrupt the enemy can remove the command safeguard. Do not treat him as a disposable attacker if he is your only protection for an exposed LT.

### AP Combi Rifle, Specialist Operative — 28/0

AP ranged fire, E/M melee and D-Charges create multiple threats against armored enemies. Useful in Acon, where affordable independent non-TAG armor-piercing guns are relatively scarce. Assault Pistol adds a close-range option; WIP13 Specialist Operative provides a scoring fallback.

The premium is seven points above the Akali BSG specialist. He loses the cheaper model's Deployable Repeater and has no visual MOD or defensive extra Wound. Use airborne geometry to select favorable engagements rather than challenge enemy apex shooters head-on.

## NCO and deployment economy

- [NCO](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/NCO.md) converts already-generated Special Lieutenant Orders into Tactical Orders for Kirpal; the Lieutenant may be in another Combat Group. It does not create an extra Order or replenish the shared pool.
- Off-table deployment sacrifices Kirpal's pooled Regular Order until a subsequent eligible Order Count. His own Order pays for entry; NCO can pay for follow-up actions after arrival if Lieutenant Orders remain. Keep enough group Orders to exploit the entry.
- Acon's Bagh-Mari NCO and active Lieutenants compete for the same Lieutenant Orders. A protected Regular LT and otherwise unused Lieutenant Order favor Kirpal; a Bagh-Mari attack team that already spends it reduces his incremental value.
- PH12 [Combat Jump](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Combat_Jump.md) succeeds 60% unmodified, versus 55% for the standard Akali; with friendly Controlled Jump alone it is 75%, or 45% with opposing Controlled Jump alone. Failed jumps place the model at its own DZ edge. Normal [Parachutist](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Parachutist.md) is a roll-free alternative at a legal edge outside the enemy DZ; neither profile has Parachutist (Deployment Zone).
- Jet Propulsion permits unusual shooting angles, but legal trajectories and exposed AROs still matter. Arriving is a Long Skill, not a simultaneous attack or objective activation.

## Comparison with Agnes Chain of Command

| | Agnes CoC | Kirpal CoC |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | **18/0** | 26/0 |

| Fireteam | Wildcard | None |
| BTS | 6 | 3 |
| Airborne / NCO | No | Yes |
| Offensive role | Limited | Close-range flanker |

[Agnes](agnes.md) is cheaper and safer for routine command insurance, particularly with an aggressive Tikbalang LT. She can fill a Wildcard or stay protected independently. Kirpal's advantage is that he can combine CoC with a genuine airborne attack role, or let AVA1 Agnes take her 16/0 RemDriver profile instead.

**Agnes RemDriver + Kirpal CoC = 42/0.** Interesting in a REM-heavy list with a worthwhile buff target and a deliberate plan to bring Kirpal on-table before risking the LT. The package is not equivalent to always-available backline CoC, and RemDriver cannot buff a TAG.

**Recommended quantity: 0–1.** Prefer the 21/0 Akali specialist in a generic list. Upgrade to Kirpal when NCO, CoC or airborne anti-armor/CC capabilities materially improve the plan. The 26-point profile is particularly interesting alongside Agnes RemDriver, but not a substitute for reliably deployed CoC. Proxy with an appropriate Akali model before prioritizing a dedicated miniature.
