# Nagas — Shock Army of Acontecimento (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: Strong go-to (A overall).** One Naga is close to a default inclusion in objective-oriented Acon lists; two are reasonable. Prioritize the **KHD 27/0** and **FO 25/0** for independent midfield scoring and network utility. The HD is a good architecture-dependent alternative; dedicated Minelayers are situational, and the sniper is usually a skip.

## Why Acon wants Nagas

True Infiltration, repeatable Camouflage and WIP14 give independent objective access without requiring a Fireteam escort or spending several Orders moving from the deployment zone. These are relatively uncommon tools in PanO. BS12, Surprise Attack and Mimetism (-3) are adequate for selective short-range fights, not a substitute for apex gunfighters. Dogged can complete a crucial objective or attack in the current turn but is not NWI; ARM1/BTS0 leave the model fragile, especially against hacking or templates when applicable.

## Profile selection

- **KHD 27/0 — go-to:** WIP14 infiltrating specialist, anti-hacker utility, D-Charges and E/M Mine. The E/M Mine threatens expensive HI/TAGs, but is carried, not prelaid via Minelayer. The KHD cannot use ordinary HD anti-HI/TAG programs. Strong general-purpose choice when enemy hackers or heavy targets are likely.
- **FO 25/0 — go-to:** cheapest specialist option, WIP14, D-Charges, Flash Pulse and Deployable Repeater. Deploy the Repeater and re-enter Camouflage when legal, letting a protected Regular or Scylla project hacking. Especially attractive when the army already has enough hackers. Repeater is carried, not deployed automatically before play.
- **HD 29/0.5 — very good:** independent midfield anti-heavy hacking, Carbonite (+1SD), D-Charges and Deployable Repeater. The special die improves selection rather than adding Burst. Take when forward hacking and local scoring matter; the cheap Regular HD or Scylla can cover general network work instead. BTS0 warrants caution in hacker duels.
- **Monofilament Minelayer 26/0.5 — conditional:** Camo/Infiltration and a prelaid potentially lethal mine threaten a forced premium TAG/HI crossing. The opponent can clear, bait, Dodge or bypass it; no Specialist skill. At only one point below the KHD it is not a default purchase. Existing Acon reference notes explicitly treat this as conditional rather than Top 10 general area denial.
- **Shock Minelayer 25/0.5 — situational:** concealed infiltrating Boarding Shotgun/Nanopulser and a prepaid mine. Good on dense tables and concealed choke points, but generally prefer Peacemaker for dedicated forward defense or a specialist Naga for mission utility.
- **MULTI Sniper 29/1.5 — usually skip:** Camo, Surprise Attack, Infiltration and +1SD provide a useful surprise long-range angle, but expensive in SWC and no specialist role. Acon already has capable ranged pieces; spend Naga slots on capabilities the gunline lacks.

| Regular Sensor/Minelayer | 13/0 | Cheap Regular Order, Sensor and prelaid Shock Mine for DZ defense rather than midfield infiltration |
| Regular HD/FastPanda | 17/0.5 | Economical backline hacking and network projection rather than infiltrating scoring |
| Scylla HD + Charybdis | 32/0.5 | Stronger protected hacker/network platform, Pitcher and synchronized Repeater/HFT peripheral |
| [Pathfinder TA](pathfinder.md) | 17/0 | Fast FO/Sensor/Repeater with personal Order; different visible REM scoring approach |

Dart is the better premium midfield hunter, but the Naga KHD is eight points cheaper, has WIP14 and genuine Infiltration, and brings D-Charges and hacking utility. They are complementary rather than strictly interchangeable. For routine objectives, prioritize the Naga; buy Dart when her distinct combat package is needed.

## Example midfield package

- Naga KHD — 27/0.
- Naga FO — 25/0.
- Peacemaker Heavy Shotgun/Pulzar + Auxbot-3 — 23/0.

**Total: 75 points / 0 SWC**, three Regular Orders, two infiltrating camouflaged specialists, native and deployable Repeater coverage, close-range templates and an E/M Mine. The Auxbot does not generate an independent Order. This is an optional package, not a mandatory list core; ensure enough Orders and appropriate hacking support to use it.
