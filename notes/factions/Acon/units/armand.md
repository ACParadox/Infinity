## Profile selection

- **34/1.5 MSV1 — preferred:** only one point more than Minelayer. MSV1 removes enemy Mimetism (-3) and reduces Mimetism (-6) to -3, complementing his own Mimetism (-6). Acon lacks MSV2, making this a useful independent counter-Mimetism gun.
- **33/1.5 Minelayer — situational:** Shock Mine provides layered protection against close approaches, but Acon has cheaper Minelayers. Prefer only when the mine and defensive positioning are more important than visor utility.

Both have a MULTI Sniper Rifle, Nanopulser, Breaker Pistol (+1B), Viral CCW (PS 7), CC21 and Natural Born Warrior. The close-range equipment is valuable self-defense, not a reason to seek dedicated melee fighters.

## Active-turn role

Armand is best used to remove awkward long-range defenders or open a secondary attack lane independently of the main TAG or assault Fireteam. Mimetism (-6) plus cover can impose -9 on an opponent without relevant MOD cancellation. BS13 and MSV1 let him engage conventional and Mim3 targets efficiently within the sniper's favorable range band; Mim6 still imposes -3.

Ordinary B2 active shooting and no +1SD limit his reliability and order efficiency compared with B4 weapons. Favor isolated B1 defenders, range mismatches and favorable MODs rather than repeated even firefights. MULTI Sniper ammunition options provide flexibility against different target types. He has neither NCO nor Tactical Awareness.

## Reactive-turn role

**Do not buy Armand primarily as a standing ARO anchor.** B1 without a Special Die is rough against high-Burst attackers, particularly MSV2 shooters who negate his Mimetism. At 34 points and 1.5 SWC, losing him early is expensive. Transmutation costs him Mimetism (-6), reduces ARM/BTS to zero and removes Forward Deployment from the inactive profile.

Use him to cover a narrow secondary lane, preferably beyond normal HMG optimal range, or keep him protected for the next active turn. Two Wounds and emergency close-range defenses help, but do not make him a disposable roadblock.

Armand is not an automatic upgrade over Bagh-Mari or Naga: his native MOD advantage trades against Burst, Special Dice, order efficiency and marker protection. He preserves Naga slots for objective specialists and can provide a different attack angle from the main TAG.

## Final selection

**Priority: 0–1, B+.** A good independent precision gun when the list specifically needs long-range MOD stacking, but not a must-have or a priority purchase. Prefer the 34-point MSV1 option. If the list only needs a reactive roadblock, buy the Regular Sapper instead; if it needs sustained active firepower, prefer the Bagh-Mari HMG or a main TAG as appropriate.
