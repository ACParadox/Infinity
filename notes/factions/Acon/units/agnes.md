## Acon selection and use

- 18/0 CoC (+1B Nanopulser) is the primary insurance for aggressive Tikbalang LT (+1 Order), ORC or Bagh-Mari LT plans; WIP13 Specialist, BTS6 and Sixth Sense add utility. Protect her; AVA1 precludes simultaneously buying RemDriver.
- 16/0 RemDriver (+1 BS, +5 CC) costs only 3 points above the basic specialist. Bulleteer Spitfire becomes BS13 (38/1 package); Sierra Thunderbolt becomes BS12 (34/0); Peacemaker HSG becomes BS13 (39/0). Do not apply to TAGs or motorcycles. Token ends when Agnes enters Null State; no range upkeep.
- 13/0 basic Specialist is budget Wildcard glue if neither command insurance nor buff is required; native Regular/Bagh-Mari composition may be more efficient. Mixed Wildcard does not replace required native anchor. Usually 0–1.

### Flight Officer Agnes Ferreira — Svalarheima's Winter Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A tier — primarily a Chain of Command pick (A+ with an aggressive Lieutenant). Pilot-X is a near-mandatory listbuilding piece and already supplies a stronger RemDriver buff, so Agnes RemDriver is B/niche by default (B+ with a worthwhile second REM). Basic Specialist is B. Normally 0–1 copies, depending on the list.**

All profiles are Wildcards (max 1), with no Counts-as label. All are WIP13 Specialists. BTS6 and innate Sixth Sense are unusually good on a cheap LI, but ARM1, W1, BS11 and ordinary movement make Agnes a support piece rather than a frontline gunfighter. Sixth Sense is conditional reactive protection, not a permanent 360° Visor.

#### RemDriver — 16/0

**Downgraded for WinterFor specifically:** Pilot-X is treated as a near-mandatory inclusion for its hacking and RacerBot network-projection package, with RemDriver already included. Its +1 BS, +5 CC, BTS=6 buff is stronger than Agnes's. The following REM pairings remain individually efficient, but their target ratings do not imply that a second RemDriver is generally worth buying. Agnes's AVA1 makes choosing this profile an opportunity cost: it excludes her excellent CoC option.

For only **3 points above the basic Specialist**, Agnes grants one eligible REM +1 BS and +5 CC. Assign the token during deployment to an already deployed REM in the same army list. No Orders or range restriction are required for upkeep. The bonus applies to active and reactive rolls and is lost at the end of the Order in which Agnes enters a Null State. A REM cannot have multiple RemDriver tokens.

| Target | REM cost / SWC | Package with Agnes | Improved BS | Assessment |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Bulleteer Heavy Shotgun (+1B) | 19 / 0 | 35 / 0 | 13 | Excellent |
| Bulleteer Spitfire | 22 / 1 | 38 / 1 | 13 | Excellent |
| Sierra Thunderbolt (+1B) | 18 / 0 | 34 / 0 | 12 | Excellent |
| Sierra HMG | 23 / 1 | 39 / 1 | 12 | Very good |
| Peacemaker | 23–27 / 0–0.5 | 39–43 / 0–0.5 | 13 | Very good |
| Pathfinder Tactical Awareness | 17 / 0 | 33 / 0 | 12 | Situational |
| Fugazi | 7 / 0 | 23 / 0 | 9 | Poor |

- **Bulleteer:** preferred offensive target. BS13 with Mimetism (-6), fast movement and Spitfire or Heavy Shotgun makes a strong inexpensive secondary gunfighter. If Agnes already fills a specialist/order slot, the incremental buff costs only 3 points. Can legally Duo with Agnes, but the mixed Duo is FT1; normally keep Agnes safely in the backline instead.
- **Sierra Thunderbolt:** preferred budget defensive target. BS12, Total Reaction, 360° Visor and no SWC make a useful independent ARO package. Total Reaction gives normal weapon Burst in ARO; the printed (+1B) modifier is not an additional reactive Burst bonus under N5.3. The package gives two Regular Orders and one Specialist for 34 points.
- **Sierra HMG:** similar concept with better long-lane range, at five points and one SWC more.
- **Peacemaker:** BS13 combines with forward deployment and Mimetism (-3); useful if the main plan relies on active shotgun attacks rather than just its Auxbot, mines or repeater.
- **Pathfinder:** can benefit from BS when fighting but its primary value is Sensor, scoring and network projection, not shooting.
- **Pilot-X:** alternative RemDriver at 21/1 with Hacker/Zero Pain and a BTS=6 benefit, plus access to RacerBot Mk-III. Prefer Pilot-X as the default because its hacking and RacerBot package already earns its place in the list. Agnes RemDriver is a secondary choice only if another REM is worth buffing (e.g. Bulleteer plus Sierra), CoC is unnecessary, or Pilot-X is omitted. If both are taken, buff different REMs; the bonuses cannot stack.

**RemDriver cannot buff Jotum:** a TAG is not a REM. Keep Agnes protected; losing her also removes the buff. The +5 CC is secondary to the BS increase on most REMs.

#### Chain of Command — 18/0

Only **5 points above the basic Specialist**, this profile protects against Loss of Lieutenant if the LT is Isolated or in a Null State at the check and Agnes remains on the table, non-Null and non-Isolated. She becomes the new Lieutenant; this is succession insurance, not additional Lieutenant Orders.

| Lieutenant strategy | CoC value |
|---|---|
| Jotum LT 82/2 | Very high |
| Offensive ORC HMG LT 36/1 | Very high |
| Offensive Hospitaller HMG LT 32/1 | High |
| Protected HI LT | Moderate |
| Backline Fusilier LT 10/0 with decoys | Low |

With an aggressive Jotum LT, Agnes is strongly recommended: an inexpensive safeguard for the army's command structure while the TAG attacks. The extra active Nanopulser Burst is incidental rather than a reason to take the profile.

Alternatively, a protected Fusilier Lieutenant plus a Karhu NCO can use the Lieutenant Order productively without exposing the LT. That often makes the CoC investment unnecessary. Hannibal's Counterintelligence is useful but is not a substitute for Chain of Command. Avoid pushing Agnes alongside the very Lieutenant she is meant to replace.

#### Basic Specialist — 13/0

Useful budget Wildcard when a team needs a cheap button-presser but the other profiles' abilities have no meaningful target.

| Alternative | Pts / SWC | Distinct advantage |
|---|---:|---|
| Fusilier FO | 12 / 0 | Counts as Fusilier, Deployable Repeater, Flash Pulse |
| Agnes | 13 / 0 | WIP13, BTS6, Sixth Sense, Wildcard |
| Pathfinder | 14 / 0 | Mobility, Sensor, Repeater; independent FO |
| Agnes RemDriver | 16 / 0 | Passive REM enhancement |
| Blade-Ops FTO Engineer | 23 / 0 | Engineer, Tactical Awareness, Mimetism (-3), Counts-as Fusilier |

Normally prioritize the extra 5 points for CoC if the Lieutenant is exposed. Pay the extra 3 for RemDriver only if there is a worthwhile unbuffed REM after assigning Pilot-X's token, or Pilot-X is omitted. The 13-point profile remains useful in expensive mixed Harises.

#### Fireteam access and examples

Wildcard access covers Fusilier Core/Haris, Nisse Core/Haris/Duo, WinterFor Alpha Core/Haris/Duo, WinterFor Beta Haris/Duo and Bulleteer Duo. Native minimums and required-choice conditions still apply. Agnes does not count as Fusilier, Karhu, Nisse or WinterFor Alpha; she does not increase the matching-unit count.

| Composition | Pts / SWC | Level |
|---|---:|---|
| Karhu Feuerbach + basic Agnes | 47 / 1.5 | FT1 Duo |
| Karhu Feuerbach + Karhu Engineer + basic Agnes | 72 / 1.5 | FT2 Beta Haris |
| Bulleteer Spitfire + RemDriver Agnes | 38 / 1 | FT1 Duo |
| Four basic Fusiliers + basic Agnes | 53 / 0 | FT4 Core |

The Karhu Haris retains +1SD and adds a cheaper Specialist but misses the FT3 Discover/Dodge bonuses. The Fusilier Core retains FT4 shooting bonuses but loses FT5 Sixth Sense for other members (Agnes has it innately). Do not confuse the mixed Duo with FT2: two unrelated members do not grant +1SD.

Wildcard access is useful for cost reduction and moving a Specialist with an attacker, but keeping the RemDriver or CoC profile independent in safety often provides greater value than exposing it to frontline risk.

**Situational must-have, not universal.** Agnes compresses a Regular Order, a WIP13 Specialist and powerful passive support into a cheap zero-SWC slot. CoC is the default reason to take Agnes and is excellent with a risk-heavy Lieutenant plan. Pilot-X normally covers RemDriver; Agnes RemDriver is niche unless there is a meaningful second REM target or Pilot-X is omitted. Only take the basic profile if its cheap Wildcard Specialist role is specifically needed. AVA1 forces a choice between RemDriver and CoC.
