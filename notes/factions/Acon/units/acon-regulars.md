# Acontecimento Regulars — Shock Army of Acontecimento (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A+ / high-priority backbone.** The 13-point, zero-SWC Minelayer/Sensor is the standout utility profile and a near-default inclusion. The 20/1 Sapper MULTI Sniper is especially valuable for creating a unique ARO lane where ordinary snipers cannot obtain cover, rather than simply being another linked defensive sniper. Cheap Lieutenant, specialist and hacking options make Regulars more than filler.

## Standout: Minelayer + Sensor — 13/0

For only three additional points over a basic Regular, with **no SWC cost**, gain a predeployed Shock Mine, Sensor and Triangulated Fire while retaining a Regular Order, Combi Rifle and unrestricted native Regular Fireteam access.

- Minelayer places a mine during deployment, protecting a vulnerable corner, approach, Lieutenant or valuable gun before spending any Orders. It is not merely a trooper carrying mines for later use.
- Sensor provides inexpensive anti-Camouflage/Hidden Deployment utility when eligible targets are within reach. This is especially welcome given Pathfinder AVA1.
- Triangulated Fire is a situational answer to severe shooting MODs, not a substitute for a dedicated active-turn gunfighter.
- Works solo as a deployment-zone guard or in a Core/Haris without reducing matching composition bonuses.
- Useful against infiltrators, airborne attackers and follow-up attacks into the DZ; screening also helps against Impersonators, but do not assume a mine or Sensor automatically reveals or attacks an Impersonation Marker.
- No Forward Deployment, marker state or inherent defensive MODs: this is primarily home-zone insurance, not a substitute for a Naga or forward Peacemaker Minelayer.

**Selection:** near-default one per list. A second is reasonable if protecting separate approaches matters. At 13/0 it is one of Acon's best value support profiles, even without a Fireteam.

## Sapper MULTI Sniper — 20/1

**The main reason to buy this profile is to cover a unique line of fire from a position that lacks natural cover.** A conventional sniper's deployment depends on suitable cover; a Sapper in Foxhole can select the desirable lane first and obtain its own Partial Cover and Mimetism (-3). This opens otherwise exposed rooftops, overlooked angles, crossfires and objective approaches that other snipers cannot defend as efficiently.

- BS12, Mimetism (-3), Foxhole cover and DA ammunition are a credible independent ARO for 20/1. Cover plus Mimetism imposes -6 before range and other applicable MODs, unless negated.
- FT2+ adds one Special Die to eligible BS Attacks (in a B1 ARO, roll two and retain one, not B2); FT4 adds +1 BS; FT5 Sixth Sense.
- Long lanes outside the favorable range of opposing HMGs are useful, but apex attackers and appropriate visors can still remove a one-Wound Regular.
- Foxhole increases silhouette to S3 and restricts movement; leaving it sacrifices its benefits. Do not force the whole mobile team to revolve around a static sniper.
- A sniper on a conventional covered lane is useful but relatively replaceable. The distinctive advantage is access to angles that competing ARO units cannot use safely.

**Selection:** table- and list-dependent, not mandatory. Excellent when terrain makes the placement ability meaningful; do not automatically pay 20/1 if existing AROs already cover the important lanes. The existing research records both advocates (Shepherd, Dante, MetaChemistry) and Dice Gods' preference to spend the points/SWC elsewhere.

## Other profiles

- **Lieutenant 10/0:** default economical command choice. Another 10-point Combi Regular can act as a decoy. A Bagh-Mari NCO can use the Lieutenant Order, including as Fireteam Leader; keep the Lieutenant out of unnecessary fights. Rao LT (+1 Order) 27/0 and Tikbalang LT (+1 Order) 73/1.5 are more expensive command alternatives that supply two Lieutenant Orders.
- **FO 12/0:** for +2 points, gains WIP13 mission specialist, Flash Pulse and Deployable Repeater. Excellent low-cost network extension and scoring. Repeater is not predeployed by this profile.
- **HD 17/0.5:** FastPanda and Cybermine make this an unusually useful cheap hacker. Work through the Peacemaker, Fugazi, Pathfinder, FO/Naga deployable networks and Scylla support rather than expecting WIP13/BTS0 to defeat dedicated enemy Killer Hackers. No grenade launcher in the pinned N5.3 profile.
- **Paramedic 12/0:** inexpensive specialist and recovery support for a fallen linked gunner. PH10 on the Regular makes it a poor MediKit recipient compared with higher-PH allies; do not assume recovery is guaranteed.
- **Spitfire 16/1:** very cheap second gun; FT4/5 gives effective BS13 before situational MODs, with +1SD from FT2. No MSV, Mimetism or second Wound, so not an apex attacker. A Bagh-Mari HMG 30/1.5 is a stronger primary gun; an attack run may also expose other members of a defensive Regulars Core.
- **Basic 10/0:** useful cheap Order, decoy and matching member. Prefer the 12–13 point utility options when their capability is relevant and points permit.
- **Commlink 12/0:** primarily a Reinforcements-extra choice, not a standard listbuilding priority.

## Fireteams

One Core, one Haris, unlimited legal Duos.

- **Regulars Core/Haris:** at least one native Regular, up to five. ORC max 2, Bagh-Mari max 2, Machinist max 1, Beasthunter FTO max 1, plus legal Wildcards.
- **Acontecimento Core/Haris/Duo:** max two Regulars, anchored by an eligible native Bagh-Mari or ORC. Regulars do not count as Bagh-Mari. Machinist counts as Bagh-Mari only in this family.
- Wildcards do not replace the mandatory native anchor. See the pinned chart for package and FTO restrictions.
- FT level comes from matching composition, not merely headcount. FT2 +1SD BS Attack; FT3 +3 Discover/+1 Dodge; FT4 +1 BS; FT5 Sixth Sense. Bonuses apply to eligible members.

| Package | Pts / SWC | FT level | Use |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Sapper + Minelayer + LT + basic decoy + Paramedic | 65 / 1 | 5 | Static defensive Core with command and recovery |
| Minelayer + LT + decoy + FO + Paramedic | 57 / 0 | 5 | Utility/order Core without dedicated long ARO; free SWC for attackers |
| Spitfire + FO + HD + Paramedic + basic | 67 / 1.5 | 5 | Existing audited inexpensive mobile gun/utility Core |
| Bagh-Mari HMG + LT + decoy + FO + Paramedic | 74 / 1.5 | 4 | BS14 MSV1/Mim(-3) NCO gun; protect LT during movement |
| Sapper + HD + Minelayer + FO + Paramedic | 74 / 1.5 | 5 | Defensive/technical Core with Lieutenant elsewhere |
| Minelayer + FO + Paramedic | 37 / 0 | 3 | Cheap matching Haris if Core allocated elsewhere |

These are alternative constructed packages, not confirmed battle-report deployments. A Beasthunter FTO 9/0 adds Heavy Flamethrower, Panzerfaust, AP Mine and close-combat deterrence, but replacing one of five matching Regulars reduces FT5 to FT4 and adds an Irregular rather than Regular Order. Useful where close-range defense matters more than Sixth Sense and order economy.

## Final selection

**A+ overall; Minelayer S-tier value.** Start with the 13/0 Minelayer, then choose a cheap LT/decoy and appropriate specialists. Four or five Regulars make sense when building a Regular Core, but do not buy a full Core by default if the list needs independent orders or a Bagh-Mari attack Core instead.

The Sapper is a distinctive option, not an obligatory ARO tax: select it specifically when self-generated cover creates a better or otherwise unavailable firing lane. The zero-SWC Minelayer is the more broadly applicable purchase. Regulars' greatest strength is that inexpensive order generation simultaneously buys genuine technical, mission and defensive capabilities, leaving the budget for Acon's premium attackers.
