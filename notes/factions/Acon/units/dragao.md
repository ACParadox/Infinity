# Dragão — Shock Army of Acontecimento (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A+ / high-priority primary attacker.** Default TAG for conventional heavy assault and armor breaking; Tikbalang remains the better choice for terrain exploitation and flanking. For only 7 more points at the same SWC, the Dragão buys Burst 5 AP firepower, ARM8, a stronger flamethrower and better long-range bands, but sacrifices Mimetism (-3), Climbing Plus, mines and the smaller S6 silhouette.

## HRMC — why the upgrade matters

| Weapon / mode | Active Burst | PS | Ammo | Saving rolls per hit |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|
| HMG | 4 | 5 | Normal | 1 |
| AP HMG | 4 | 5 | AP | 1 |
| MULTI HMG AP / Shock | 4 | 5 | AP / Shock | 1 |
| HRMC AP / Shock | **5** | 5 | AP / Shock | 1 |
| HRMC Anti-materiel | 1 | 5 | DA | 2 |

BS Attack (SR-1) penalizes the target's Saving Roll by one, effectively PS4 for the HRMC. The Tikbalang also has this skill, so it is not an advantage exclusive to the Dragão.

| Weapon | 0–8" | 8–16" | 16–24" | 24–32" | 32–40" | 40–48" |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| HRMC | -3 | 0 | +3 | +3 | 0 | 0 |
| HMG | -3 | 0 | +3 | +3 | -3 | -3 |

- **Burst 5 AP:** excellent against HI/TAGs; improved Face-to-Face odds from an extra die and more potential hits than B4. AP makes each successful hit far more meaningful against high ARM.
- **Burst 5 Shock:** useful against vulnerable single-wound infantry; choose the mode according to the target.
- **32–48" range:** better at engaging distant ARO pieces than a conventional HMG, though not necessarily better than the opponent's sniper range.
- **DA anti-materiel mode:** B1 with two saves per hit is a useful conventional ARO option; still not EXP, and the Dragão lacks defensive Mimetism.
- **Order efficiency:** solves the normal HMG's difficulty cracking high ARM, reducing the need for a separate premium long-range anti-armor attacker. Keep inexpensive independent E/M/AP tools for redundancy and unfavorable engagements.

## Dragão versus Tikbalang

| Attribute | Dragão | Tikbalang |
|---|---|---|
| Cost / SWC | 76/1.5 | 69/1.5 |
| BS / BTS / STR | 15 / 6 / 3 | 15 / 6 / 3 |
| ARM | **8** | 6 |
| Silhouette | 7 | **6** |
| Primary gun | **HRMC B5 AP/Shock, B1 DA** | HMG B4 Normal |
| Mimetism | — | **-3** |
| Climbing Plus | — | **Yes** |
| Heavy Flamethrower | **+1B** | Standard |
| Mines | — | **Shock Mine** |
| Immunity (Continuous Damage) | **Yes** | — |
| Terrain | Jungle | **Total** |
| Tactical Awareness / Remote Presence / SR-1 | Yes | Yes |
| Lieutenant | — | 73/1.5 LT (+1 Order) |

- **Dragão:** preferred for direct engagements against heavily armored targets, opponents whose MSV negates the Tik's Mimetism, and long-range fights. Higher ARM absorbs conventional hits; S7 and lack of Mimetism make exposure a genuine risk.
- **Tikbalang:** preferred for choosing firing angles, fighting conventional shooters without MSV, exploiting vertical terrain, and using mines to protect its route and create forks. The smaller silhouette is easier to hide, and ordinary HMG remains its decisive weakness against heavy armor.

The Dragão is better at destroying difficult targets through brute force; the Tikbalang is better at finding favorable engagements. Neither is universally better against every MSV or infantry profile.

## Close-range and defensive limitations

Heavy Flamethrower (+1B) can place two direct templates in the active turn, potentially forcing multiple saves against each covered enemy. This is excellent for clearing clustered defenders or making a close opponent choose between dodging and trading damage. It does not become B2 in ordinary ARO and cannot win a Face-to-Face shooting roll by itself.

- No mines or Climbing Plus: less independent protection against close assault and fewer escape routes than the Tikbalang.
- CC18 is not a dedicated melee defense; avoid skilled CC attackers despite the AP CCW.
- HRMC is -3 inside 8"; short-range AP weapons can exploit this range and its large silhouette.
- BTS6 and ARM8 do not prevent hacking, E/M or isolation. ECM: Guided (-6) is not a hacking modifier.
- Avoid unnecessary exposure after the active-turn attack. Suppressive Fire can be useful if the position and threat ranges justify it; preserve an Engineer recovery route.

## Moto-mecanizados Duo

All different-unit combinations below are mixed **FT1**, not FT2: no +1 Special Die. The value is coordinated movement and each partner's own abilities. Tactical Awareness may activate the team with its owner leading; it cannot simply be transferred to another member's shooting.

| Partner | Combined pts / SWC | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Pathfinder Tactical Awareness opt 2 (17/0) | **93/1.5** | Preferred mobile utility |
| Bulleteer Heavy Shotgun (+1B) opt 1 (19/0) | 95/1.5 | Independent AP close-range fighter |
| Agnes CoC opt 3 (18/0) | 94/1.5 | Limited direct synergy; useful separately |
| Motorized Montesa Paramedic opt 4 (23/0) | 99/1.5 | Mobile objective and melee support |
| Tikbalang opt 1 (69/1.5) | 145/3 | Expensive two-TAG construction; not default |

**Pathfinder TA is particularly well suited to the Dragão.** Matching 6-4" movement and ground-level routes make coherency easier than with the Tikbalang. It brings FO, Sensor, Repeater, Flash Pulse and its own TA Order. The pair provides two Regular and two Tactical Awareness Orders; each TA owner leads its own tactical activation. The Pathfinder can detect camouflage, move the pair economically and score after the TAG clears lanes. Split the Duo when the objectives or TAG's attack route demand it. AVA1 Pathfinder means it must earn the allocation.

The Bulleteer offers an actual close-range AP Face-to-Face weapon, unlike the Dragão's direct template; do not buy it solely as protection. Agnes's CoC has no direct command-insurance relationship to the Dragão because it cannot be Lieutenant. The motorized Montesa is better when its separate speed and mission reach justify the investment.

## Support and list architecture

- **Tech-Bee 9/0:** high-priority support in the same Combat Group; TAGCom raises Dodge PH11 to PH14 and GizmoKit PH13 to PH14 while the skill remains active. Adds a Regular Order, specialist and Deployable Repeater. Not an Engineer replacement.
- **Machinist SAA 17/0 + Palbot 3/0:** preferred independent repair package. Remote Presence makes recovery worthwhile; ground-level movement makes access easier than for a climbing Tikbalang. Machinist cannot join Moto-mecanizados and a Servant controller cannot join a Fireteam.
- **Scylla 32/0.5 (HD or EVO):** optional cyberwar support with Trinity (SR-2), Pitcher and Charybdis. HD can contest hacking through friendly Repeaters; EVO can provide Fairy Dust for TAGs. Do not assume the EVO protects against ordinary E/M. The FTO package is separate and not needed for this support role.
- **Guarda de Assalto 40–44:** complementary close-range fighter whose synchronized Auxbot carries an Eclipse Grenade Launcher. Eclipse can facilitate movement across undesirable firing lanes; expensive alongside the TAG, so take only when the mission/terrain merits it.
- **Regulars / infiltrating specialists:** cheap orders, defensive mines and scoring allow Acon to afford the TAG without relying on it for every mission action.

Recommended starting package: **Dragão 76 + TA Pathfinder 17 + Tech-Bee 9 + Machinist 17 + Palbot 3 = 122/1.5**. The support pieces can also contribute independently. Add a safe Lieutenant, enough Regular Orders, hacking coverage and forward specialists; the full 122-point package is a starting module, not an obligatory tax.

## Broader PanO TAG comparison

These are cross-sectorial comparisons, not Acon alternatives:

- **Uhlan:** Camouflage, Surprise Attack and Feuerbach provide flexible ambush play and anti-armor firepower.
- **Cutter:** Hidden Deployment and Mimetism (-6) give superior concealment and defensive modifiers.
- **Jotum:** exceptional armor and MULTI HMG.
- **Squalos:** conventional heavy firepower; Dragão's B5 HRMC is its distinguishing advantage.
- **Tikbalang:** the actual Acon alternative; mobility and Mimetism rather than brute-force armor breaking.

## Final assessment

**A+ — excellent value as a primary attacker.** The premium over the Tikbalang is small for the gain in direct anti-armor firepower, durability, flamethrower Burst and range. Prefer the Dragão when the list needs to win conventional heavy-gun engagements; prefer Tikbalang when mobility, smaller silhouette, mines and Mimetism are central to the game plan. Both deserve consideration, but normally choose one main TAG and build complementary threats rather than buying redundant heavy firepower.
