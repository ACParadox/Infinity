# Knights of Montesa (foot) — Shock Army of Acontecimento (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: S-tier value for the 20/0 FTO Paramedic; A-tier for the 26/0 forward Paramedic; A− for forward assault guns.** Prioritize the FTO as durable close-range Fireteam support. The forward profiles are more specialized, and the motorcycle is a separate unit.

## Why the FTO is exceptional

- Two Wounds, ARM4, BTS6 and Mimetism (-3) for only 20 points; supplies a Regular Order, a Paramedic and strong close-combat deterrence (CC23, MA2, DA).
- Boarding Shotgun with AP and native +1SD is a reliable close-range weapon; Blitzen adds a disposable ranged E/M threat against heavy infantry and TAGs, including in ARO.
- Limited Cover removes the normal Partial Cover BS penalty imposed on enemies, but retains the Saving Roll cover bonus when applicable. It is not No Cover.
- Hackable HI with no MSV, no marker state and ordinary 4–4 movement. Avoid exposed long-range lanes and hostile Repeater nets. Does not possess the motorcycle's Shock Immunity or improved Dodge.
- The 20-point FTO has **no Forward Deployment**; the 26–35-point forward options have no Blitzen and cannot join the ordinary Acontecimento Fireteam.

| Specialist | Paramedic | No | FO |

ORC has higher BS, actual extra Burst and normal cover; Bagh-Mari has visor, NCO and different mission skills. Montesa wins as affordable durable escort, CC protector and backup anti-heavy piece. Do not replace every Bagh-Mari with Montesas: the team still needs a long gun and matching composition.

## Forward profiles

FD (+20 cm / 8") saves early movement and improves approach geometry. On a normal 48" table with 12" deployment zones it is still short of midfield, unlike Infiltration.

- **26/0 Paramedic:** default forward foot option. Compared with Naga FO 25/0, the Montesa has more durability, CC and direct fighting capability; Naga has WIP14, reusable Camouflage, deeper deployment, Surprise Attack and is not inherently Hackable. Prefer Naga for independent stealth scoring and Montesa for overt contesting and holding.
- **32/1.5 Red Fury:** B4 and native SD for sustained short/midrange shooting; Shock ammunition does not solve every heavy target. Consider cheaper Bulleteer Spitfire 22/1 for pure conventional shooting if FD/durability/CC are not required.
- **35/1.5 Spitfire + Drop Bears:** better range profile for many midfield engagements and deployable denial; three points over Red Fury. Drop Bears are carried, not prepaid by Minelayer. Expensive for a limited-cover non-specialist without MSV.

## Fireteam construction

Acontecimento Core/Haris/Duo requires a native Bagh-Mari or ORC anchor; only foot option 5 (FTO) can join (max 2). The separate Forward Montesa Duo requires exactly two foot Montesas; the canonical row has no FTO restriction, so FTO is also legal there. No bikes in this Duo. Foot and motorcycle AVA are separate.

| Package | Pts / SWC | FT level | Role |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 2× FTO Paramedic | 40 / 0 | 2 | Four Wounds, two Blitzens, two specialists; deploy normally |
| 2× FD Paramedic | 52 / 0 | 2 | Forward objective contest |
| FD Red Fury + FD Paramedic | 58 / 1.5 | 2 | Forward attack and scoring |
| FD Spitfire + FD Paramedic | 61 / 1.5 | 2 | Forward gun, mines and scoring |
| Bagh-Mari HMG + 2× FTO Paramedic | 70 / 1.5 | 2 | Mixed Acontecimento Haris with durable escort |
| Bagh-Mari HMG + Bagh-Mari FO + FTO | 72 / 1.5 | 2 | Broader specialist and NCO mix |

FT2's +1SD explicitly stacks with native +1SD. With a normal B1 rolled BS ARO, a foot Montesa in FT2 rolls three dice and discards two (retains B1); an independent foot Montesa rolls two and discards one. Extra dice improve reliability, not actual Burst, hits or Disposable expenditure. They do not apply to direct templates or Long Skills. FT2 is based on matching unit identities, not the mere number of models.

A linked Montesa gives up independent Impetuous activation while in the Fireteam. A mixed FD/non-FD Duo can be legal but loses much of the forward-deployment advantage when deployed in coherency.

## Final selection

**20/0 FTO is the standout and a priority inclusion in assault Fireteams.** Use 26/0 FD Paramedic if the list needs a robust forward specialist, and the FD gun variants only when building around the forward assault role. Two foot models are useful for the pure Duo or flexible mixed Haris. The separate [motorized Montesa](montesa-moto.md) is better for rapid independent flanking and late objective switches.
