# Motorized Knights of Montesa — Shock Army of Acontecimento (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A+ — 23/0 Boarding Shotgun Paramedic is the default; 21/0 basic is excellent raw value but normally find the two points for the specialist.** Fast, durable independent assault and scoring piece. Pure Moto Duo is useful, not mandatory; mixed Duos are situational.

## Independent mobility and combat

- Independent bike gets an optional Impetuous activation and contributes its normal Regular Order. Unobstructed legal Move + Move can reach 35 cm (14") per activation. One such Impetuous activation plus one ordinary Order can cover up to 70 cm (28"). Impetuous movement priorities and legal declarations constrain the route; skip it when unsafe.
- BSG's favorable 0–8" range and AP ammunition pair well with rapid flank movement. CC23/MA2/DA weapon threatens ordinary infantry, REMs and TAGs in close combat.
- ARM4/BTS6/W2, Mim(-3), PH13 and Dodge (+2.5) make it resilient for the price. Limited Cover removes the usual enemy BS penalty from Partial Cover, not the applicable Saving Roll bonus.
- S4, Motorcycle movement restrictions and Hackable HI status make narrow/vertical terrain, hostile Repeaters, mines and defended open lanes dangerous. No cheap Smoke or Disco Baller in Acon; Guarda's expensive synchronized Eclipse Auxbot can support a planned crossing.
- No native +1SD, Forward Deployment or Blitzen as on selected foot variants. The bike's advantage is speed, not superior gunfighting MODs.

### Profile selection

**23/0 Paramedic:** two points over the basic profile buys MediKit and mission-specialist eligibility with identical combat stats. Excellent late objective switch, secondary attack vector, flank pressure or emergency recovery; not a Doctor.

**21/0 basic BSG:** identical attack and movement; save two points only if the list already has sufficient scoring or literally cannot fit the Paramedic.

**29/1 Red Fury:** B4, useful 8–24" range, best motorcycle shooting profile for repeated engagements or a pure Duo attack leader. More costly and lacks the BSG's AP mode; buy for the separate medium-range role.

**26/0 MULTI Rifle:** flexible ammo and B3 midrange at zero SWC, but costs three points more than the Paramedic, loses specialist status, and BSG already brings AP. Conditional.

The bike quickly overtakes FD infantry on clear ground, particularly with an independent Impetuous activation; S2 infantry can traverse terrain the bike cannot. FD is valuable even when no movement is spent, including when going second.

## Moto-mecanizados Duo

The canonical required-choice set is Motorized Montesa (max 2), Bulleteer (max 2), Tikbalang (max 2), Dragão (max 1), with eligible Wildcards. No access to ordinary Acontecimento or Forward Montesa Duo. FT2 requires matching identities, not simply two bodies. A linked bike cannot use its independent Impetuous activation while linked.

| Package | Pts / SWC | FT level | Role |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 2× BSG | 42 / 0 | 2 | Cheap paired close attack |
| BSG + Paramedic | 44 / 0 | 2 | Close assault and scoring |
| 2× Paramedic | 46 / 0 | 2 | Two scorers; often better independent |
| Red Fury + Paramedic | 52 / 1 | 2 | Preferred offensive Duo |
| Bulleteer Spitfire + Paramedic | 45 / 1 | 1 | Mim(-6) midrange attack and CC/scoring |
| Pathfinder TA + Paramedic | 40 / 0 | 1 | Two specialist roles, Sensor and network |
| Tikbalang HMG + Paramedic | 92 / 1.5 | 1 | TAG gun and mobile scorer |
| Dragão HRMC + Paramedic | 99 / 1.5 | 1 | Heavy gun and scorer |

Pure bike Duo gets +1SD and joint movement, but independent bikes preserve separate free Impetuous activations and attack routes. Mixed Duos only get FT1 shared activation, not +1SD; slower partners and different terrain access can limit the bike. A TAG or Pathfinder Tactical Awareness Order may activate the team when its owner is the eligible leader.

## Comparison with cheap assault alternatives

| Attribute | Montesa BSG | Bulleteer HSG (+1B) | Peacemaker HSG + Auxbot |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pts / SWC | 21 / 0 | 19 / 0 | 23 / 0 |
| MOV | 20–15 | 15–10 | 15–10 |
| BS | 13 | 12 | 12 |
| ARM / BTS / W or STR | 4 / 6 / 2 | 1 / 3 / 1 | 1 / 3 / 1 |
| Mimetism | -3 | -6 | -3 |
| CC | CC23/MA2/DA | Poor | Poor |
| Other | Impetuous, optional Paramedic | Repeater, Remote Presence | FD(+20), Repeater, HFT Auxbot, Remote Presence |

Montesa wins speed, durability and CC, and has a specialist option. Bulleteer is better for cheap conventional MOD-based shooting. Peacemaker gives immediate forward presence, separate direct-template angles and network projection; 24/0.5 Minelayer package improves Turn-0 defense. All three are Hackable controllers/troopers, and only the Peacemaker brings the peripheral.

Acon's broad cheap assault options make the motorcycle excellent but not compulsory. Prefer the [foot FTO](montesa-foot.md) for close protection within a normal assault Fireteam: it is cheaper, S2, has native SD and Blitzen. The bike is the better independent fast-ground attacker and late-game scorer.

## Final selection

Start with one **23/0 Paramedic**. A second bike is useful for independent flanks or a deliberate FT2 Duo, but do not automatically buy both in every list. Prioritize the foot FTO for ordinary Fireteam construction. The earlier copied MO analysis is retained as historical reference, not the current Acon recommendation.
