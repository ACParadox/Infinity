# Bagh-Mari Unit — Shock Army of Acontecimento (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A-tier / high priority.** Excellent linked conventional gunfighter and mission backbone. Prioritize the HMG, Boarding Shotgun (+1B) Forward Observer and MULTI Sniper Minelayer. Karhu is the stronger individual all-rounder (S-tier), primarily because of its 6–2 movement, Climbing Plus, Feuerbach (+1B) and richer equipment.

## Why it works

- BS13, MSV1 and Mimetism (-3) combine offensive and defensive MODs. MSV1 negates enemy Mim(-3) and reduces Mim(-6) to -3; it is not MSV2.
- NCO spends existing Lieutenant Orders, including either Order from a Lieutenant (+1 Order). When the NCO is Team Leader it activates the entire Fireteam. Multiple NCOs improve allocation flexibility, not Order generation.
- Warhorse prevents Isolation, maintains Regular status in Loss of Lieutenant and Retreat, and negates enemy BS Attack (-X) skills. It does not ignore ordinary Mimetism, Cover, Surprise Attack or range MODs.
- Number 2 assists Fireteam continuity when the Leader falls.
- Non-hacker MI profiles are not inherently Hackable; the HD profile is Hackable. Spotlight and other applicable effects remain possible.
- Main limitations: one Wound, ARM2, standard movement, no marker state, limited anti-heavy capability on the HMG, and no cheap native vision-control tool.

## Preferred profiles

### HMG — 30/1.5

Default conventional infantry long gun. B4 HMG, MSV1, Mim(-3), NCO and Warhorse for less than an ORC HMG. FT2+ rolls five dice and retains four, FT4 adds +1 BS (effective BS14 before situational MODs). It wins ordinary MOD-based fights more efficiently than generic HI but is much less forgiving when it loses.

### Boarding Shotgun (+1B) FO — 22/0

Excellent complementary attacker and objective specialist. B3 active shotgun, AP ammunition, D-Charges, Flash Pulse, WIP13 FO and NCO. FT2+ rolls four dice and retains three with the B3 weapon mode. Cheaper than the 23-point basic Combi/Light Shotgun profile. Good default second Bagh-Mari or low-cost anchor.

### MULTI Sniper Minelayer — 29/1.5

A particularly interesting profile, not merely an inferior HMG. Costs one point less than the HMG while adding a predeployed Shock Mine for immediate route denial, MSV1/Mim(-3) reactive coverage and DA ammunition for punishing failed opposing Face-to-Face Rolls. FT2+ provides +1SD in ARO (roll two, retain one for a B1 shot); FT4 adds +1 BS. NCO can fund active-turn sniper engagements and repositioning. Warhorse negates applicable enemy BS Attack (-X) skills. Unlike the Karhu Feuerbach, it has a dedicated Minelayer capability rather than simply carrying mines for later placement. It is still a one-Wound model: avoid unnecessary exposure to apex guns.

Compare other ARO roles:

- Regular Sapper MSR 20/1: cheaper entrenched defense and native Regular composition.
- Armand Le Muet MSV1 MSR 34/1.5: independent premium sniper with active Mim(-6), Forward Deployment and Transmutation; no Fireteam access. Consult the corrected Armand analysis for the active/inactive statlines.
- Bagh-Mari: strongest justification is the combination of linked MSV sniper, NCO and prelaid mine, especially in an already useful Core.

## Other profiles and competition

- HD 29/0.5: strong mobile linked hybrid and classified runner, but no D-Charges. Regular HD 17/0.5 is a cheaper network piece with FastPanda; Scylla HD + Charybdis 32/0.5 offers Pitcher, Trinity and peripheral utility; Naga KHD 27/0 brings a different infiltrating anti-hacker role. Buy Bagh-Mari HD specifically for the linked hybrid role.
- Paramedic 25/0: useful for one-Wound allies, but Regular Paramedic 12/0 and foot Montesa FTO Paramedic 20/0 are economical alternatives in their respective legal teams.
- HMG Lieutenant 27/1.5: saves three points versus regular HMG; risk exposing command in an assault team. Agnes CoC is an available insurance option.
- Combi Lieutenant 21/0: Warhorse and better shooting protection than a Regular, but no extra Lieutenant Order. Two 10-point Regulars (Lieutenant plus decoy) are an economical alternative.
- Basic Combi 23/0: normally prefer the 22-point FO or a 17-point Machinist when their roles fit.

## Fireteam construction

Acon permits one Core, one Haris and unlimited legal Duos. Bagh-Mari can join Regulars Core/Haris (max 2, at least one native Regular required), or anchor the Acontecimento Core/Haris/Duo (max 5). Rao is a Wildcard counting as Bagh-Mari; Machinist SAA counts as Bagh-Mari in the Acontecimento family only. Neither replaces the native ORC/Bagh-Mari required-choice anchor. Only foot Montesa FTO option 5 can join Acontecimento, not the motorcycle or forward-deployed foot options.

FT2 gives +1SD, not extra actual Burst; FT3 adds +3 Discover/+1 Dodge; FT4 adds +1 BS; FT5 Sixth Sense. Composition bonuses apply to all eligible members, not only matching members. The HMG/FO/Machinist Haris is all one-Wound infantry; the Montesa alternative trades Engineering and FT3 for two Wounds, Blitzen, strong CC and native +1SD.

## Lieutenant economy

- Regular Lieutenant 10/0, with optional decoy: cheapest way to supply a protected Lieutenant Order to the Bagh-Mari.
- Rao Lieutenant (+1 Order) 27/0: supplies two Orders for Bagh-Mari NCOs, including an offensive linked leader. Rao is also a WIP14 specialist and counts as Bagh-Mari.
- Tikbalang Lieutenant (+1 Order) 73/1.5: another two-Order source, but those Orders are also valuable for the TAG itself. Allocate deliberately; NCOs do not create extra Lieutenant Orders.
- Bagh-Mari HMG Lieutenant 27/1.5: cheaper fighting LT, with command risk; not the default if other Lieutenant choices support the list better.

## Comparison with Karhu (WinterFor)

| Attribute | Bagh-Mari | Karhu |
|---|---|---|
| Shared | BS13, MSV1, Mim(-3), NCO, ARM2/BTS3/W1 | Same |
| Movement | 4–4 | **6–2, Climbing Plus** |
| WIP / CC | 13 / 16 | 12 / 14 |
| Command resilience | Warhorse, Number 2 | Courage (not Warhorse) |
| Main gun | HMG 30/1.5 | **Feuerbach (+1B), AP Mines 34/1.5** |
| Basic rifle | Combi + Light Shotgun 23/0 | MULTI Rifle + Blitzen or E/Marat + AP Mines 22/0 |
| Specialist | FO 22, HD 29, Paramedic 25 | Engineer 25, Paramedic 24 |
| Dedicated sniper | MSR Minelayer 29/1.5 | — |
| Matching teams | Acontecimento Core/Haris/Duo | WinterFor Beta Haris/Duo |

**Karhu is better overall.** The 6-inch first Move and Climbing Plus alone are a major advantage: superior angles, faster mine deployment and better order economy throughout the game. Its Feuerbach (+1B) is a stronger all-round active/ARO weapon for only four more points, especially versus armor (B3 AP+DA or B2 EXP in active turn, B1 in ordinary ARO). HMG B4 still has value for conventional firefights. Karhu also carries AP Mines on the Feuerbach profile and has excellent MULTI Rifle specialist loadouts.

Bagh-Mari's Warhorse, higher WIP, cheaper matching Fireteam members and ability to form FT4/5 are meaningful but more conditional. Its dedicated MSR Minelayer is a distinct upside: a prelaid mine plus a serious reactive gun at deployment, not merely a mine carried for later placement.

## Final selection

**A-tier, not Karhu's S-tier.** Start with two Bagh-Mari (HMG and FO); the MSR Minelayer is a worthwhile third model or a replacement for the HMG in lists prioritizing reactive defense. The sniper, HMG and FO are all legitimate choices rather than a single mandatory loadout. Prefer Bagh-Mari over generic ORC HMG unless the latter's two Wounds and ARM4 are specifically needed. Use cheaper Regulars and Machinists to avoid investing too heavily in fragile one-Wound infantry.
