## Selection and use

- **33/1.5 Minelayer:** default defensive variant if an independent sniper plus prepaid Shock Mine is desired. Forward Deployment (+10cm), Mimetism (-6) and active W2 make it a substantial long-range defender. Mine coverage helps against close approaches; Transmutation reduces ARM/BTS once the active Symbiont profile is lost.
- **34/1.5 MSV1:** only one point more, but trades away Shock Mine/Minelayer for visor utility. Prefer this version for active long-range attack into Mimetism targets; MSV1 does not negate all visibility penalties.
- Both have BS13, MULTI Sniper Rifle, Nanopulser and Breaker Pistol (+1B); Viral CCW and Natural Born Warrior are useful emergency close defense, not a reason to seek elite melee. BS Attack (Shock) adds Shock ammunition where applicable, not a Special Die. Without +1SD or Fireteam access, he has ordinary sniper Burst and a single reactive shot; Mimetism (-6) is lost after Transmutation. Position for favorable long-range engagements, with a fallback route; do not expose a premium independent gun to every opposing angle.

## Acon competition

- Regular Sapper MSR 20/1: cheaper static lane holder and native Regular FT composition; lower investment if only an ARO is needed.
- Bagh-Mari MSR Minelayer 29/1.5: MSV1, Mimetism (-3), NCO and link access; more economical when already building a Bagh-Mari team.
- Naga MSR 29/1.5: camouflaged infiltrating sniper with +1SD; different deployment and surprise angles, competes with Naga specialist slots.
- Armand adds independent Mimetism (-6), active W2 and optional MSV1 or Minelayer, but lacks the Naga sniper's +1SD and linked Bagh-Mari shooting bonuses. No need to spend a Haris/Core to obtain those modifiers. His SWC and points compete with main TAG or linked guns; avoid redundancy.

**Provisional priority:** 0–1, favor the MSV1 option for an independent active sniper or Minelayer for layered ARO. Not a mandatory purchase before testing on actual tables.
