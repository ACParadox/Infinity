# Scylla and Charybdis — Shock Army of Acontecimento (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A+ overall / near must-have in network-oriented Acon lists. The 37/0.5 FTO package is the default (S-tier points value); the 32/0.5 HD and EVO synchronized packages are excellent alternatives.** Scylla combines protected anti-heavy and anti-Hacker combat (HD), the sectorial's only Pitcher, a flamethrower Repeater body and, on the FTO package, two Regular Orders and flexible Wildcard access. Not mandatory in a low-tech list that only needs a cheap HD.

## Why Scylla is strong in Acon

- **HD + Trinity (SR-2):** one hacker covers Carbonite, Oblivion, Spotlight and Total Control as well as lethal anti-Hacker attacks. Trinity is B3, WIP+3 (effective 16 before other MODs), PS6, with the printed -2 to the target's Saving Roll. A standard HD lacks Trinity; Scylla's upgrade is explicit. Neither standard HD nor EVO grants Cybermask or White Noise.
- **Cyber resilience:** BTS6 and innate ECM: Hacker (-3) penalize hostile hacking while protecting against successful attacks. ECM is not a Firewall and does not grant a Saving Roll bonus itself; it can complement a legal Firewall source. Scylla is still W1 and can be removed by one failed damaging save. WIP13 is ordinary rather than elite.
- **Pitcher:** the only printed Pitcher in Acon, enabling ranged placement of a Repeater without physically walking the network into the enemy position. BS12 and terrain/range make it less automatic than a premium linked Pitcher carrier. Use existing forward Repeaters first when they already reach the target.
- **Physical backup:** E/Mitter threatens heavy targets independently of their hacking status when applicable; Light Shotgun and Charybdis's Heavy Flamethrower provide close-range options. Neither BS12 nor one Wound makes Scylla a frontline gunfighter. Protect her in total cover where possible.

## Synchronized HD and EVO packages — 32/0.5

Charybdis is a mobile HFT/Repeater threat that activates with Scylla. Move the bot into useful network or template positions while Scylla stays behind cover, within required Coherency. Controller and peripheral declare the same Skills; if only one meets the Requirements, the other Idles. Do not assume Scylla can Hack while the bot separately declares a BS Attack in the same synchronized Order. If Scylla becomes Isolated or Null, the synchronized bot disconnects. Neither controller nor peripheral can join a Fireteam, and the bot generates no extra Order.

**HD:** default synchronized option for area denial, counter-hacking and controlling enemy HI, TAGs and REMs. Good when moving two models with one Order and covering adjacent angles is important, or the list cannot spare another Order-generating slot.

**EVO:** exceptional force-multiplier profile because it retains Trinity (SR-2), Pitcher, ECM, BTS6 and the HFT/Repeater peripheral while replacing the normal HD suite.

| Supportware | Useful beneficiaries |
|---|---|
| Assisted Fire | Offensive Bulleteer or Peacemaker; Sierra when appropriate |
| Enhanced Reaction | Suitable defensive REMs; redundant on a Sierra's native Total Reaction |
| Fairy Dust | Eligible TAG, HI or REM class |
| Controlled Jump | Akalis and Kirpal Singh using Combat Jump |

Supportware requires activation and applicable targets; one sustained program per EVO Hacker and one Supportware per target. Assisted Fire and Agnes RemDriver can complement one another on a Bulleteer (not a TAG). EVO Scylla cannot use standard HD Carbonite, Oblivion, Spotlight or Total Control: add a Regular HD/FastPanda when those tools matter. An ordinary EVO Mulebot is 14/0.5, so the premium is justified by Scylla's hybrid defense, Pitcher, specialist and peripheral rather than support alone.

## FTO package — 37/0.5 (preferred general-purpose choice)

Only **5 points more** than the ordinary HD purchase gives a second Regular Order and an independent REM with its own activation, AROs and Repeater placement. Charybdis can cover a separate lane or move deep without obliging Scylla to accompany it; Scylla's loss does not automatically disconnect an independent FTO REM. Both can join legal Fireteams as separate Wildcards (max 1 each, no Counts-as). The two bodies consume two trooper slots; at a full roster cap the second Order is not necessarily an incremental gain. The extra Order is particularly valuable in a list that would otherwise struggle to fill its pool.

Trade-offs: outside a shared legal Fireteam, moving both costs separate Orders, unlike synchronized activation. Charybdis has ARM0, no Mimetism, and is not a specialist or a credible ranged gunfighter. Its HFT encourages template trades and the Repeater exposes it to enemy hacking when applicable.

Wildcard access is a bonus, not a reason to dismantle a high-level native Regular Core. Regulars Core/Haris must include a native Regular; Acontecimento teams need a Bagh-Mari or ORC anchor; Moto-mecanizados needs its own eligible anchor; Forward Montesa Duo remains exactly two foot Montesas. Neither Scylla nor Charybdis counts as Regular or Bagh-Mari for matching-unit bonuses. Do not form a standalone Scylla/Charybdis Duo without satisfying a chart family. Mixed teams can efficiently move a hacker or Repeater with an attack piece, but keeping Scylla safe and independent is often better.

| Naga FO | 25 / 0 | Infiltrating camouflaged specialist with Deployable Repeater |

Peacemaker is the most obvious synergy: start with hacking coverage in the midfield instead of spending Orders extending it. The Pitcher is then an additional route, not the entire network. Keep Repeater carriers and Scylla positioned so a single enemy attack or template does not dismantle the network; enemy hackers can also exploit Repeater interactions where legal.

**Example:** Scylla FTO package 37/0.5 + Peacemaker HSG/Pulzar with Auxbot-3 23/0 + Fugazi 7/0 = **67/0.5, four Regular Orders**, three independent native mobile Repeaters, a Pitcher, HD/Trinity and close-range template defense. The Peacemaker Auxbot does not generate an extra Order. The Minelayer Peacemaker alternative raises this to 68/1.

Other useful combinations: Scylla HD + Naga FO (57/0.5) for independent midfield mission access; Scylla EVO + Regular HD (49/1) for full network and Supportware coverage; Scylla EVO + Bulleteer Spitfire + Agnes RemDriver (70/1.5) for a focused REM attack plan.

**Near must-have for combined-arms/network lists, not universal.** Default to the **37/0.5 FTO package** for its second Regular Order and flexible independent Repeater. Prefer the **32/0.5 HD** for synchronized movement or tight roster space; prefer **32/0.5 EVO** when multiple relevant support targets justify losing the standard HD programs. Acon's unusually good REM ecosystem and lack of a cheap alternative Pitcher make Scylla particularly valuable. Existing repository global rankings remain unchanged: the hybrid HD profile is #6 in Apex and the EVO package is #1 in the combined force-multiplier assessment.
