**Niche pick/skip**

##### Clipper Dronbot (Missilebot) — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: C-tier for the 16-point Guided Clipper, full stop.** Do not upgrade its rating for hypothetical Forward Observer-heavy or hacker-heavy Kestrel builds. **The 19-point non-Guided FTO Missile Launcher (+1B) is also a poor Kestrel pick**: despite its FTO label, it has **no entry in Kestrel's Fireteam chart**. Neither Clipper is a recommended standard selection.

**Role:** Independent missile REM. Guided offers an occasional attack on a **previously Targeted** enemy without needing direct Line of Fire; without that setup, the same body is an exposed, unlinked BS12 direct-fire missile launcher. Kestrel's direct guns, mobile attackers, specialist utility, and 1.5-SWC competition make this a low-priority purchase.

###### Pinned N5.3 profiles

The [pinned Kestrel roster](https://github.com/ACParadox/Infinity/blob/main/sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md#clipper-dronbot) lists **AVA 1**, Regular REM, **MOV 15–10 cm (6–4")**, CC13, **BS12**, PH10, WIP13, **ARM0 / BTS3 / STR1**, S3; Remote Presence, Courage and PARA CCW (-3).

| Profile | Pts / SWC | Relevant capability | Kestrel judgment |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Missile Launcher, Guided** | **16 / 1.5** | B1 Guided strike against an eligible Targeted enemy; can also fire its launcher directly when appropriate | **C — avoid by default** |
| **Clipper FTO Missile Launcher (+1B)** | **19 / 1.5** | Higher native Burst for direct missile shooting and ARO; **not Guided** | **Poor — no legal Kestrel Fireteam access** |

**Do not combine the two profiles.** The 16-point profile does **not** gain the FTO's +1B; the 19-point FTO does **not** have Guided. The printed FTO suffix alone never authorizes membership in a Fireteam.

###### Why the Guided profile is C in Kestrel

1. **Targeted is the bottleneck.** The Clipper's 16 points purchase a potential finishing shot, not the means to create one. Kestrel has useful Forward Observers, but an FO generally needs a viable attack angle and its own Order/ARO. Kestrel can also use Spotlight with an appropriate Hacking Device, but its hacking and projected Repeater coverage are not comparable to the strongest Nomad/Combined-style networks.
2. **Spotlight is not a guaranteed mark.** In N5.3, success involves attack resolution **and** the target's saving roll; an enemy can also try to Reset. A Repeater existing somewhere on the table does not mean the Clipper has a reliable target.
3. **The cost is mostly opportunity cost.** **1.5 SWC** competes with Griffin HMG, elite AROs, TAG weapons, Firefly HRL and other impactful Kestrel guns. Spending several Orders on marking and Guided attempts may be worse than having a strong attacker solve the encounter directly.
4. **Its normal direct-fire backup is uninspiring.** BS12, B1, ARM0, STR1 and no Fireteam bonuses make it a weak primary active gun or standing ARO compared with purpose-built alternatives.
5. **The network is not a free army upgrade.** Buying a hacker, projector, escorts, and the Clipper specifically for Guided introduces fragile dependencies and steals Orders and points from conventional scoring and fighting.

**Even when the army already includes FOs, do not raise the Clipper above C.** The possibility of a lucky Guided shot does not outweigh the regular opportunity cost in a Kestrel list. FO models are selected for mission play, Sensor, utility or their own tactical functions—not to justify this missilebot.

###### What Guided still does, and why that is not enough

- **Potential upside:** attack a Targeted defender behind Total Cover without normal LoF; pressure static protected troops; punish clustered defenders with the legal Guided Blast/template mode.
- **Important restrictions:** requires a legal **Targeted** enemy, valid range and trajectory; attacks are subject to the N5 Guided rules, the target's defensive response, and the active-turn limit on Guided attacks. It is not an unrestricted table-wide auto-hit or a guarantee that a Targeted model dies.
- **Frequent practical result:** the army has no appropriate Targeted enemy, or getting one consumes too many valuable Orders. A dead or idle 16-point/1.5-SWC REM contributes little beyond its Regular Order.

The *threat* of Guided might force an opponent to spread out or address Repeaters, but in Kestrel that is an incidental benefit, not a sound reason to buy the piece.

###### Non-Guided FTO (+1B): why not take it?

**19 points / 1.5 SWC** buys a credible **B2 direct missile ARO**, but not the linked bonuses or defensive reliability that would make it an appealing specialized missile platform. Kestrel has **no Fireteam-chart entry for Clipper**, so it cannot join FENNECS, KESTREL, COLCORP or ARMORED teams merely by having FTO in its name.

| Alternative Kestrel ARO | Pts / SWC | Why usually preferable |
|---|---:|---|
| **Sierra Thunderbolt (+1B), Total Reaction** | **18 / 0** | Efficient sustained ARO, 360° Visor, no SWC requirement |
| **Sierra HMG, Total Reaction** | **23 / 1** | More reactive Burst when an important long shooting lane merits it |
| **Fennec Missile Launcher** | **15 / 1.5** | Cheaper and genuinely eligible for a FENNECS Fireteam |
| **Firefly Heavy Rocket Launcher** | **24 / 1.5** | BS13, Albedo and legal KESTREL Fireteam options |
| **Black A.I.R. Neuro MULTI Sniper / Griffin Feuerbach** | Higher investment | Much stronger specialized long-range defense, with relevant legal Fireteams |

The FTO Clipper's B2 EXP ARO can surprise something that must cross its lane, but the model remains a fragile **unlinked** reactive piece. **Do not select it just because 1.5 SWC is available.**

###### Combat Group 2, EVO, and repair do not rescue the rating

- **Group 2:** Clipper contributes a Regular Order and can spend spare Orders on an already-marked target, including a mark created from another group. But allocating unused Orders to Guided is not automatically valuable: the setup and target may never materialize. Do not build a Group 2 around it.
- **EVO Mulebot (14 / 0.5):** an EVO purchased for several other REMs or a separate deployment plan can sometimes provide support. **Do not buy the EVO for Clipper.** Assisted Fire/Marksmanship is largely redundant with the normal advantages of **Guided** and requires an activation. The unlinked FTO's native +1B and the Guided profile must still be evaluated separately.
- **Machinist/Palbot:** Remote Presence permits repair, but do not commit a dedicated Engineer and Servant just to keep a cheap, static, low-priority Clipper alive. Reuse existing support opportunistically.

###### Recorded PanO use: actual evidence vs. theoretical builds

**Reviewed sources:** the repository's **24 N5 battle-report analysis files (#001–240)**, plus relevant YouTube and podcast transcripts. These support a **rare/experimental PanO Guided Clipper** conclusion, **not** a claim that nobody takes it.

| Source | What is established | What is *not* established |
|---|---|---|
| [WIP12 E201 — NCA with Trent](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3FT1No7zFHzpj7WC63hcCb), ~43:10 | Player says he **has used Swiss Guard ML + two Guided Clippers** in experimental Neoterra lists, with Auxilia target marking and coordinated missile pressure; explicitly characterizes it as substantially a **meme list** | Not proof of a broadly successful competitive default; **two Clippers are possible in NCA, not Kestrel AVA1** |
| [Battle report #227 — NCA vs Ramah, Evacuation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NVN3qXXhsN4&t=570s) · [indexed analysis](https://github.com/ACParadox/Infinity/blob/main/notes/battle-reports/221-230-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md#227--neoterran-capitaline-army-vs-ramah-evacuation-nca-71) | An NCA **Guided REM is included** in a winning 7–1 roster; Blocker Spotlight attempts occur | No clearly documented Guided missile launch or demonstrated decisive Clipper contribution; victory is principally conventional shooting and objective delivery |
| [Robert Shepherd — N5 PanO faction focus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IqK-RwFt7fw&t=4455s), ~74:15 | Questions whether Guided MLs belong in PanO, calling them apparently redundant alongside its other guns; does not take one in his showcase | Not proof the profile is unusable |
| [Tactical Awareness S3E07 — Vanilla PanO](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZbwXcQ_IK5A&t=990s), ~16:30 | Theorycrafts **two Clipper (+1B)**, EVO, Peacemaker Repeater and Fennec FastPanda Hackers for Spotlight-assisted direct fire | **These are non-Guided FTO Clippers**, not evidence of two Guided launchers |
| [WIP12 E180 — Kestrel with Daboarder](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3JXkarf51Os0UUopYrM7IW) | Favors efficient Fireteams, direct ARO guns, and general-purpose specialist support | No specific Guided Clipper endorsement in the checked transcript |
| [Indexed N5 battle reports #001–240](https://github.com/ACParadox/Infinity/blob/main/notes/battle-reports/) | Several **other factions** use Guided missile REMs in live games, sometimes decisively | **No explicitly identified Kestrel Clipper Guided** was found in those indexed summaries |

**Evidence judgment:** There is genuine PanO Clipper use, especially experimental NCA lists. But the best recorded successes of Guided/Spotlight engines in this archive belong predominantly to factions with stronger network and hacking plans; little direct evidence shows **Kestrel Guided Clipper** paying for itself. An identified Guided REM in a winning PanO roster should never be mistaken for a recorded Guided kill.

###### Final list-building rule

| Choice | Tier | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Clipper Guided Missile Launcher 16/1.5** | **C** | **Usually omit.** No exception or rating upgrade for FO-heavy builds |
| **Clipper FTO Missile Launcher (+1B) 19/1.5** | **C / avoid** | Unlinked, non-Guided; other Kestrel AROs normally do the job better |

**Bottom line:** *Neither Clipper is a normal Kestrel pick.* Guided can occasionally punish a marked target that conventional guns cannot see, but Kestrel's unreliable target acquisition and strong alternatives make it a weak investment. The FTO has a legitimate B2 missile ARO but no Kestrel Fireteam access. Buy a more useful specialist, efficient ARO or independent gunfighter instead.
