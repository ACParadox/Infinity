# Akalis, Sikh Commandos — Shock Army of Acontecimento (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: Strong situational pick (A overall), not mandatory.** The **21/0 Boarding Shotgun (+1B) Specialist Operative with Deployable Repeater** is the clear default: a cheap independent airborne attacker, mission runner and network extension. Prefer one when an alternate attack/scoring angle matters; do not buy one merely to replace a conventional gunfighter or Naga. Kestrel's Banshee KHD is more versatile in isolation, but the Akali fills a comparatively scarce role in Acon.

## Why the 21-point specialist is good

- BS13 and B3 Boarding Shotgun in active turn make it a capable selective close-range attacker, especially against exposed support troops. Avoid contested apex gunfights.
- WIP13 Specialist Operative and D-Charges allow mission scoring and Sabotage even if the opponent denies profitable attack angles. A general Specialist is not automatically eligible for every specialist-type Classified.
- Carried Deployable Repeater opens a different Hacking Area for Scylla, a Regular HD, or a Naga HD/KHD as appropriate to their programs. It must be placed after arrival; no Minelayer or native Repeater.
- Unhackable on the non-Hacker profiles. E/M CCW offers a last-resort anti-heavy option but CC16 without Martial Arts is not a serious CC specialist.
- Jet Propulsion allows curved jumps and selective pop-up attacks from unusual angles, including returning to the starting point when legal. The declared trajectory cannot benefit from Partial Cover and can generate multiple AROs.
- Cheap at 21/0, independent of Fireteams and no additional SWC. The main weakness is a single Wound, BTS0 and no visual defensive MOD; the model relies on choosing engagements rather than surviving them.

## Other profiles

- **Combi + E/M Carbine 23/0:** a distinct airborne anti-heavy disruption option when enemy TAGs/HI/REMs justify E/M delivery. Costs two more points and loses the specialist, D-Charges and repeater. Not the default.
- **Spitfire 26/1.5:** B4 and better sustained fire outside shotgun range, useful when several accessible targets justify an airborne shooting run. Expensive SWC for a BS13 unmodified one-Wound attacker in a sectorial already rich in conventional guns. No specialist fallback.
- **HD 25/0.5:** surprise local Spotlight, Carbonite or Oblivion against eligible targets and hacker-specific mission utility. BTS0 and WIP13 make it a vulnerable hacker. Generally prefer the cheaper unhackable specialist plus existing protected hackers unless a direct airborne HD or Hacker-only objective is required.

## Deployment and Order economy

[Combat Jump](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Combat_Jump.md) and [Parachutist](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Parachutist.md) are optional; normal deployment remains legal and provides a pooled Regular Order from the first eligible Order Count.

| Mode | Reliability / cost | Best use |
|---|---|---|
| Normal deployment | No arrival roll; Order immediately available | Poor entry geometry, DZ support or later conventional movement |
| Parachutist | No roll; legal table-edge entry **outside enemy DZ** | Preferred safe flank or side objective route |
| Combat Jump | PH11: 55% base, 70% friendly Controlled Jump, 40% opposing Controlled Jump (individually) | High-payoff legal landing inaccessible from a safe edge |

Failed Combat Jump places the Akali at its own DZ table edge, not a scatter location. Neither airborne mode permits Partial Cover on entry, Prone placement or contact with objectives or enemy/neutral models. Combat Jump cannot place inside buildings/enclosed scenery. Entry is a Long Skill: no simultaneous shot, hacking or objective action.

An off-table Akali does not contribute to the pooled Regular Orders at Order Count. Its own Order pays for entry; subsequent actions that turn require the combat group's Orders. It contributes normally on later eligible Order Counts. Delaying until Turn 2–3 can exploit enemy repositioning or protect a late scorer, but sacrifices earlier pooled Orders. Keep sufficient group Orders to act after arrival. Do not purchase an EVO Mulebot 14/0.5 solely to improve a single Akali jump by 15 percentage points; existing EVO support or multiple intended jumps can change the calculation.

### Akali 21/0 vs Banshee KHD 23/0

For just two extra points, Banshee KHD brings MSV1, BTS3, PH12, B2 active-turn BTS-targeting Pulzar direct templates, Trinity, Cybermask and broader hacker-specific Classified access. The Pulzar is excellent against clustered enemies or low-BTS targets and ignores BS/Mimetism MODs, though targets can Dodge and return fire can kill the Banshee. Cybermask requires a separate legal Order and does not confer native Camo or Surprise Attack.

Akali brings a stronger direct-fire close-range BSG against ordinary targets, unhackability, a carried Deployable Repeater and cheaper general objective scoring. Its Spitfire option also offers a medium-range role absent from Banshee. Banshee's KHD does not replace a general HD or directly disable non-Hacker heavy troops.

**Overall comparison:** Banshee KHD is preferable in isolation (A+ vs A), with a more versatile chassis and additional technical options for only two points. Akali remains excellent value for its distinct unhackable scoring/network package. Kestrel has Black A.I.R., Scarecrows, Jackals and Redeye as additional unconventional attack vectors; Acon has Nagas, Dart and Montesas but few true airborne options beyond Akali/Kirpal. Consequently the Akali can be comparatively more important to its own sectorial despite being less versatile individually.

## Listbuilding and final assessment

Use a TAG or Bagh-Mari team to clear major AROs, then let the Akali score or exploit an isolated flank. A Regular HD or Scylla can use the Akali's repeater to threaten eligible targets without exposing the main hacker. Nagas and the Akali pressure different approaches. Acon's cheap Regulars can provide the follow-up Orders; an isolated underfunded combat group cannot.

**Recommended quantity: 0–1.** Two are a deliberate multi-vector experiment, not a default, because of the initial pooled-Order cost if both remain off-table. Start with the 21/0 specialist. Take the other options only for a clearly identified role. The existing cross-game ranking places this profile #5 in airborne points value, not in apex lethality. No need to force an Akali into every list, but it is a strong choice when the army needs a cheap alternative to frontal attack or midfield scoring.
