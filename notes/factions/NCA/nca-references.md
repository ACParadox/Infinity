### The 30-second listbuilding rule

**Start with a problem-solving chain, not an impressive Core:** (1) one premium gun to suppress the opponent; (2) a *different* approach if it fails (hidden attack, flank, Sensor, hacking or Redeye); (3) 1–2 Auxilia, cheap Fugazi, and a real objective plan; (4) recovery within reach; (5) optional small Fireteams if they improve the chain. NCA has inexpensive Regulars but its premium guns eat SWC and points. *Reason:* without an alternative opening, NCA can be shooting-locked when the first one or two engagements go wrong (Shepherd). Trent instead almost always starts with **a TAG + Auxilia + support**, a stronger emphasis on TAG-led construction.

| Decision | Use it when / why | Opposing view or price of the choice |

Trent sometimes fields **both** for complementary lanes | Swiss Miss ranks Swiss among the army's best; Trent says the KHD's flexibility can be worth its **47 points**, though prolonged Trinity exchanges can consume a whole pool. Fewer TAG wounds/repair options than a TAG-heavy approach |
| **Bolts + Hannibal / Black Friar** | Marksmanship, MSV sniper, Drop Bears, durable BTS and competent HD/KHD make a purpose-built defensive or midfield team; **Hannibal +1SD, Sensor and TacAware** can replace an expensive conventional gun/visor | Swiss Miss rates Bolts **S**, Hannibal **A**, while Trent often omits Hannibal because mission/SWC pressure and prefers a TAG first. Both FO bodies can try certain objective interactions and carry deployable repeaters; strong DZ corner defence from 360° Auxbot. **Specific source disagreement:** Swiss Miss (October 2025) placed **Swiss Guard, Bolts, Uhlan and AVA3 Fugazi in S**, **Auxilia, Hannibal and Aquila in A**, and viewed ordinary Fusiliers/Sierra/Redeye as circumstantial. Shepherd and Trent agree cheap Auxilia/Fugazi + specialist tools allow high-end threats, but differ about making **TAG first** a rule. Swiss Miss's tiering was a *launch reaction*, not demonstrated post-2026 tournament frequency.

### TAG, Vehicle and REM decisions (including other PanO sectorials)

| Chassis | Concrete selection test | Transcript-backed opposing argument |
|---|---|---|
| **Redeye VH: 37 Blitzen vs 38 Continuous-Damage GL** | Choose **Blitzen** for direct E/M deterrence; **GL** to punish prone/null deployment behind fire lanes, especially after another shooter suppresses AROs. Its 20-5 move, Mimetism (-6), Sensor and vertical angles make a *fast follow-through piece*, not just another Spitfire | **Pro:** Trent's NCA list uses Redeye to reach positions ordinary PanO guns cannot; Dice Gods calls double Redeye exceptionally oppressive for unprepared opponents. **Con:** Loss of Lieutenant dismisses it next to Garuda; Swiss Miss rated it **C** (but incorrectly discussed a 45-point profile); Shepherd's cross-faction flyer ranking puts it in the middle because **1.5 SWC, No Cover and other PanO guns** are real competition. Even Shepherd sees the GL as a meaningful situational enabler, not merely a Continuous Damage gimmick |
| **Peacemaker** | **27-point Boarding Shotgun +1B + Auxbot_3** for mobile close-range attack plus template/mine-capable screen; the **Auxbot_3B/forward mine** option for passive midfield roadblocks; **23-point +1B without Auxbot** if the cheap gun/Repeater itself suffices | **Trent:** actively prefers the 27-point attack package because Burst and Auxbot create close-range forks, and it can be supported with Marksmanship. **Loss of Lieutenant:** favors the mine/denial package because it imposes costs before the REM spends Orders. Do not confuse the current REM's own loadout with its Auxbot profile |
| **Bulleteer** | **19-point HSG (+1B)** is a cheap Mimetism (-6) angle attacker; **22-point Spitfire** offers longer attack bands. Both are Repeater Wildcards and can bring a Blade FTO Engineer or Blocker in a small purposeful link | Swiss Miss calls both good **B**, not always first pick because NCA's premium attack slots are crowded. Trent suggests **two independent Bolt-hacker / Blade-Engineer / Bulleteer modules** in non-TAG lists. The Fireteam must satisfy its required member, not merely be three Wildcards |
| **Pathfinder** | **17-point TacAware** gives Sensor, FO, Deactivator, Repeater, Triangulated Fire and an independently funded activation; particularly useful accompanying Squalos/ORC in a legal small team. **14-point** when saving 3 points matters more | Near-default *consideration*, not an auto-include if only paying for a Regular Order; large bases and movement restrictions can impede infantry/REM team movement |
| **Fugazi / Mulebot / Sierra** | **Fugazi AVA3 at 7 each** is premium cheap-order/Re​peater infrastructure; **8-point Mulebot Baggage**, **14-point EVO** only when the plan uses their jobs; **Sierra Thunderbolt 18/0 SWC** saves SWC versus **HMG TR 23/1** | Swiss Miss puts Fugazi **S** principally for AVA3, but Sierra low **C** because NCA has better hidden/linked defence; separate models can make the DZ congested. Do not default to HMG TR if the list's SWC is already committed |
| **Blade FTO Engineer vs Parachutist Engineer** | **23-point FTO** supports a legal Fireteam and repairs advancing REM/TAGs; **30-point Parachutist + E/Mitter + AP SMG + Decoy** is an independent side-entry threat and reserve recovery route, rather than obvious TAG-adjacent repair bait | Trent **deliberately pays +7** for off-table safety and the ability to reach a Redeye that has advanced far from a Squalos; many lists still prefer the cheaper FTO. A Palbot/Servant deployment and its coherency/arrival sequence must be checked against the current rules, rather than copied uncritically from the interview |
| **Garuda AD REM** | **20-point FO** can score from a surprising flank; **25-point Spitfire** can exploit a lane once the guns remove AROs; **18-point BSG/Chain-colt** is cheap enemy-DZ pressure | Loss of Lieutenant prefers Garuda's unexpected arrival to paying Redeye SWC; Trent prefers FO Garuda for mission jobs but frequently has room for neither when his TAG/Specialist shell is set |

**Shared-platform research:** For Redeye/Peacemaker/Pathfinder/Squalos outside NCA, use [Shepherd's aerial analysis](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N6TYjMgU4ZM), [Dice Gods' vehicle-counterplay discussion](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QscqX3hlUZU), and the Kestrel [video/podcast source map](../kestrel/README.md) (especially WIP12 #180 and Irregular Order #52).
