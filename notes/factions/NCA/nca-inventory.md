# Neoterran Capitaline Army — Sectorial Inventory (Infinity N5.3)

**NCA guides:** [Overview](nca-overview.md) · [Sectorial Inventory](nca-inventory.md) · [Listbuilding](nca-listbuilding.md) · [Fireteams](nca-fireteams.md) · [References](nca-references.md).

Role-indexed profile alternatives for NCA list building. **Do not combine weapons or skills from separate profiles.** Check the [pinned N5.3 roster](../../../sectorials/panoceania/neoterran-capitaline-army.md) for points, SWC, AVA, and eligibility. This is **not** an owned-miniatures count: see the [NCA purchasing and ownership audit](../buy-lists/nca-buy-list.md).

## Sectorial Inventory

### Lieutenant (LT)

- **Fusilier LT:** economical hidden/default command package for lists prioritizing premium weapons.
- **Auxilia LT:** low-cost command blended into ordinary Auxilia support; peripheral does not create a second Order.
- **Aquila Guard Strategos LT:** expensive command/BS15/MSV3 package with profile-specific Disco access.
- **Optimate Agent Maximus LT:** aggressive alternative with combat/command investment.
- **Bolt, ORC, and Uma LT profiles:** distinct team or fighting options when their profile role justifies exposed command.

### Chain of Command (CoC)

- **Richard Quinn CoC:** insurance for a high-risk LT, with utility and variant-dependent Disco.
- **Bolt CoC:** durable, Fireteam-eligible alternative that contributes to the defensive package.
- **Keep the LT and CoC separate from a shared exposed attack lane**, rather than losing both command layers together.

### NCO / Tactical Awareness / Activation Compression

- **Squalos Mk-II NCO MULTI HMG (66/1.5):** NCO and Tactical Awareness TAG that exploits otherwise unused Lieutenant Orders.
- **Pathfinder FO/Sensor/Repeater Tactical Awareness (17/0):** self-funded independent mobility or a legal Ultima Ratio Duo partner.
- **Uhlan Tactical Awareness:** independent premium TAG; does **not** obtain an NCA Fireteam merely from Squalos eligibility.
- **Hannibal Tactical Awareness:** flexible Bolt/ORC-equivalent Wildcard and team pressure.
- **Blade FTO Engineer Tactical Awareness:** recovery inside a legal link rather than another dedicated attacking gun.

### Long-Range Gunfighters / Apex Attackers

- **Swiss Guard HMG (56/1.5):** Hidden Deployment, Mimetism(-6), BS15; premier concealed first contact.
- **Uhlan Feuerbach (70/1.5):** Camouflage/Tactical Awareness, +1B long-range standoff, strong ARO potential.
- **Uhlan Spitfire + AP HRL (80/1.5):** more expensive close/midrange assault and unusual attack angles; distinct from Feuerbach loadout.
- **Squalos Mk-II HMG (62/1.5) or MULTI HMG NCO (66/1.5):** regular long-range TAG platform, separate ammunition/order economy choices.
- **Aquila Guard:** MSV3 precision attacker; check FTO restrictions before assigning team bonuses.
- **Bolt sniper / heavy launcher:** linked Marksmanship shooter and long-range ARO, not an independent Swiss-equivalent.

### Efficient Secondary Gunfighters

- **Bulleteer HSG(+1B) (19/0) or Spitfire (22/1):** Mimetism(-6) REM attack modules, distinct loadouts.
- **Peacemaker with Auxbot:** forward repeater and close-range synchronized attacker; select the actual Peripheral/loadout.
- **Redeye:** Mimetism(-6), vertical mobility, and either Blitzen/other anti-heavy or Continuous-Damage grenade-launcher exploitation; **No Cover and S7**.
- **Garuda Tacbot Spitfire (25):** airborne follow-through after initial long-range lanes are cleared.
- **ORC BSG(+1B), MULTI Rifle, HMG, or Feuerbach:** durable team body or paid gun as needed, not necessarily the primary attack slot.

### ARO and Long-Range Defense

- **Swiss Guard Hidden ML(+1SD) (60/1.5):** premier surprise reveal and high-impact ARO.
- **Bolt MULTI Sniper / Missile Launcher:** Fireteam-supported long-lane defense with Marksmanship and weapon-specific SWC.
- **Black Friar HRL / Thunderbolt:** MSV2, Albedo and different defensive range/template jobs.
- **Uhlan Feuerbach:** concealed TAG standoff/ARO, independent of Fireteam bonus.
- **Sierra TR Thunderbolt (18/0) or HMG (23/1):** 360º conventional REM defense; SWC competition matters.

### Midfield Defense and Marker Control

- **Locust forward/Hidden profiles:** mission approach, counter-hacking and camouflaged threat as printed.
- **Hexa:** hidden specialist/cyber or sniper defensive reserve with Counterintelligence.
- **Bolts with Drop Bears:** layered independent or linked area denial.
- **Blocker Specialist Operative/Pitcher (15/0):** Sensor, Adhesive Launcher, X-Visor and Repeater projection.
- **Peacemaker/Auxbot:** forward deployable mine/template angles and native Repeater.

### Deployment-Zone (DZ) Defense

- **Auxilia FO + Auxbot_2 (15 pts):** synchronized objective support and template perimeter protection.
- **Fugazi (7 pts, AVA3):** cheap Regular Repeater/Flash infrastructure.
- **Sierra TR:** home-lane defense; not automatically superior to hidden/linked NCA guns.
- **Warcor:** low-cost Flash defense but Irregular, not ordinary pooled Order fuel.
- **Blocker Hacker:** defensive Firewall/hacking support, rather than a premium offensive cyber duelist.

### Hackers

- **Blocker Hacker (19/0.5):** defensive cyber/Firewall and network presence; **does not carry the 15pt Specialist's Pitcher**.
- **Locust Hacker:** advanced-position cyber entry piece, with profile-specific deployment skills.
- **Swiss Guard HD + MULTI Rifle (56/0.5):** durable hidden Hacker/specialist, distinct from the Swiss HMG.
- **Deva HD:** WIP15 and Sensor utility, with eligible CSU team options.

### Killer Hackers

- **Swiss Guard KHD (47/0):** protected forward elite KHD with TinBot Firewall(-3), separate from HD/HMG/ML profiles.
- **Hexa KHD:** Hidden Deployment counter-hacking/specialist option, with a different budget and durability profile.
- **Bolt KHD:** legal Neoterra Fireteam cyber specialist.
- **Deva KHD:** independent/CSU utility where the loadout and required-choice rules fit.

### Network Projection / Pitchers / Repeaters

- **Blocker Specialist/Pitcher (15/0):** high-value X-Visor/BS Attack(+1B) Pitcher projection and objective utility.
- **Pathfinder FO/Sensor/Repeater (14 or TacAware 17):** mobile native Repeater and Sensor.
- **Fugazi (7) and Peacemaker:** rear and forward native Repeaters respectively.
- **Auxilia FO/Auxbot package:** specialist and deployable Repeater utility; follow actual Peripheral interactions.
- **Clipper Guided ML (16/1.5):** optional indirect payoff after establishing Targeted, not a legal NCA Fireteam member.

### Cheerleaders / Regular Order Infrastructure

- **Fusilier:** cheap Regular host and optional Lieutenant disguise; Fusiliers Fireteams require an actual Fusilier.
- **Fugazi AVA3 at 7 each:** efficient Regular Orders with Flash/Repeater coverage.
- **Auxilia:** Regular Order, specialist alternative and useful Auxbot body, not two independent Regular Orders.
- **Mulebot Baggage (8):** Regular order/reload support; EVO (14/0.5) only for an explicit supportware plan.
- **CSU and Blocker:** inexpensive linkable utility rather than pure order-filler purchases.

### Close Combat / Counterassault

- **Shona Carano:** high-impact close-combat counterassault Wildcard and potential Bolt/Hannibal teammate.
- **Maximus TAG / Optimate Agent Maximus:** premium close-assault bodies with much higher investment than a cheap perimeter guard.
- **Hannibal / ORCs / Bolts:** team protection and nearby deterrence; compare actual weapons and team composition rather than assuming all fill Shona's role.

### Vision Control / Anti-Camouflage

- **Aquila Guard MSV3 (including profile-specific Disco):** premium marker and visor solution.
- **Richard Quinn Disco:** alternative projected Eclipse and command-insurance utility on the appropriate loadout.
- **Black Friar MSV2/Albedo:** anti-visor and anti-Mimetism fire.
- **Bolt MSV1 MULTI Sniper:** efficient supported anti-marker long lane.
- **Blocker Sensor; Pathfinder Sensor; Deva Sensor; Hannibal Sensor:** complementary marker detection without paying for another premium HMG.

### Doctors, Paramedics and Engineers

- **Trauma-Doc / Palbot:** cheap Doctor and servant recovery for NCA's infantry.
- **Fusilier/Bolt Paramedic:** scenario utility in their actual relevant loadouts.
- **Machinist NCA:** Engineer, **counts as Bolt** in the Neoterra Fireteam.
- **Blade FTO Engineer (23):** in-team repair; **Blade Parachutist Engineer (30)** is a separate off-table repair route.
- **Tech-Bee TAGCom (9):** same-group TAG aid and GizmoKit, not a full Engineer.

### Airborne / Enemy-DZ / Flank Attack

- **Garuda (FO20, Spitfire25, BSG/Chain-colt18):** off-table surprise scoring or exploitation.
- **Blade Parachutist/Decoy Engineer (30):** alternate entry and emergency repair, not the 23pt FTO.
- **Redeye:** on-table fast vertical VH attack; not the same as Combat Jump.
- **Hidden Swiss/Hexa and forward Locust:** asymmetric starting positions and activation timing, rather than conventional off-table AD.

### Specialists and Mission Scoring

- **Auxilia FO + Auxbot_2 (15), Pathfinder Tactical Awareness FO (17):** inexpensive active mission work.
- **Blocker Specialist Operative/Pitcher (15), CSU, Deva, Hexa KHD:** alternate mission and cyber roles.
- **Blade Engineer / Machinist, Trauma-Doc, Bolt Paramedic:** role-specific classified and recovery access.
- **Garuda FO (20):** surprise flank scoring and late-game arrival.
- **Aquila/Uma/Hannibal/Quinn:** premium role compression only when the mission and army architecture justify it.

### Anti-TAG / E/M / Disabling

- **Uhlan Feuerbach, Squalos MULTI HMG, Bolt missile:** direct anti-armor solutions with distinct ammunition and positioning.
- **Redeye Blitzen:** E/M control from a fast but exposed vehicle.
- **Blocker Adhesive Launcher:** efficient immobilization/control where target geometry is favorable.
- **Peacemaker/Auxbot, Auxilia/Auxbot:** synchronized template and flank forks.
- **Offensive hacking plus Repeaters:** use actual Hacker programs; a Repeater is not itself an attack.

### Indirect Fire / Supportware

- **Clipper Guided Missile Launcher:** requires a real Targeted/network/FO route; FTO label does not confer NCA Fireteam access.
- **Mulebot EVO (14/0.5):** only worthwhile with planned eligible REM or Combat Jump supportware.
- **Mulebot Baggage (8):** disposable-weapon recharge infrastructure, not interchangeable with EVO.
