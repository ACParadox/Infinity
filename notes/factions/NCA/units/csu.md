# CSU — Neoterran Capitaline Army (N5.3)

**Near auto-include — take AVA 2 by default.** Excellent bang for buck (S-tier among inexpensive support infantry). Prefer CSUs to additional basic Fusiliers, except for Lieutenant concealment, the mandatory native Fusilier Fireteam anchor, or a specific specialist/equipment requirement. Their ability to form independent Duos makes them valuable even when the main Core is built around Bolts.

## Profiles and priorities

AVA 2; LI; Regular; MOV 4-4, CC16, BS12, PH10, WIP12, ARM0, BTS3, W1, S2. All profiles have **Sixth Sense, MetaChemistry, MULTI Pistol and PARA CCW (-6)**.

| 4 | Rifle, Light Shotgun, Nanopulser (+1B), Specialist Operative | 11 / 0 | S | Default first CSU; cheap mission scoring and close defense |
| 1 | Breaker Rifle, Nanopulser (+1B) | 10 / 0 | A+ | Best cheap Regular Order and DZ guard |
| 3 | Boarding Shotgun, Adhesive Launcher Rifle (+1B) | 11 / 0 | A+ | Disabling expensive enemies; complementary second CSU |
| 5 | Breaker Combi Rifle, Nanopulser (+1B), Specialist Operative | 13 / 0 | B+ | More capable conventional gun for an advancing Fireteam |

### Specialist Operative — 11 points

Best overall profile. One point over the basic CSU buys mission scoring and a versatile close-range weapon package. The Light Shotgun covers close-range gunfighting, while the Nanopulser (+1B) offers two direct templates in the Active Turn. A cheap, surviving specialist can be disproportionately useful in Turn 3. Two identical Specialists cost only 22/0 and give redundancy.

### Breaker Rifle — 10 points

Best pure cheerleader: same cost as a basic Fusilier with Sixth Sense, MetaChemistry, BTS3 and much better template-based close defense. Breaker ammunition targets BTS with AP; its Rifle range bands are less forgiving at very short range than the Combi Rifle, but the Nanopulser and MULTI Pistol cover that gap. Use it to protect valuable models and threaten trades rather than as a dedicated primary attacker.

### Boarding Shotgun / Adhesive Launcher Rifle (+1B) — 11 points

Excellent alternative second CSU when the list needs an inexpensive anti-heavy control tool. The Adhesive Launcher can impose Immobilized-A through PARA ammunition and a PH-based Saving Roll, rather than relying on penetrating heavy ARM/BTS. The Boarding Shotgun supplies conventional close-range fighting. This profile does **not** carry the Nanopulser. It is not a guaranteed TAG stopper: the BS roll, opposing ARO, target PH and applicable immunities still matter. Immobilization is not destruction.

### Breaker Combi Specialist — 13 points

A better conventional gun for a CSU expected to advance and shoot actively, but usually save the two points by taking the Rifle/Light Shotgun Specialist. At 15 points, Auxilia FO and Blocker Specialist offer different and often more useful capabilities.

### Combi Rifle — 11 points

Better close-range conventional rifle bands than the basic Breaker Rifle, but gives up Breaker ammunition and competes directly with the 11pt Specialist. Low priority.

## Why take both CSUs?

- **Regular Orders:** two inexpensive bodies for 20–22 points in the usual configurations.
- **Independent close defense:** Sixth Sense helps against Stealth and attacks from unexpected angles; it is not 360-degree LoF or unconditional immunity to Surprise Attack.
- **MetaChemistry:** random deployment improvement; adapt deployment and roles to the actual result rather than assuming a specific bonus.
- **Close-range trading:** Nanopulser (+1B) on four profiles, MULTI Pistol and PARA CCW (-6). Avoid confusing the Active Turn Burst bonus with automatic Burst 2 ARO.
- **Mission redundancy:** Specialist Operative is available at 11 points; WIP12 is ordinary, so preserve options for multiple attempts.
- **Flexible Fireteam architecture:** count as Fusiliers in that family, or form independent CSU Duos without using the Core or Haris slot. They cannot join a Neoterra Bolt/ORC team directly.

Compared with the basic 10pt Fusilier, the CSU sacrifices ARM1 for ARM0 but gains BTS3, skills and more close-defense options. It cannot replace a Fusilier Lieutenant, identical LT decoy, FO/Deployable Repeater or Paramedic where these roles are specifically needed. Fugazi are cheaper pure order fuel; Auxilia provide independent synchronized Auxbot utility; Blockers provide Sensor, Pitcher or hacking. CSUs complement those units rather than replacing all of them.

## Fireteams

The Fusiliers Core/Haris permits up to two CSUs, each counting as Fusilier; at least one actual Fusilier is still required. The separate CSU family is **Duo only**, with at least one actual CSU, up to two CSUs, Blockers counting as CSU, up to one Deva, and eligible Wildcards. NCA has unlimited Duos, one Core and one Haris.

Constructed alternatives verified against the pinned chart; totals are points/SWC, not battle-report exports. FT2 provides BS Attack (+1 SD), an extra rolled die followed by discarding one die, not an additional point of Burst or an extra Saving Roll.

### CSU + CSU — late-game objective-taking

A particularly attractive 21–22 point module when the main Fireteam is a Bolt/ORC Core. Keep both CSUs reasonably protected in Turns 1–2, generating Regular Orders and covering approaches. In Turn 3, advance both for the cost of one activation per movement order. Choose the most suitable leader to clear an approach, trade with templates, disable a threat or operate an objective. Two Specialists offer redundancy if one is lost; a Breaker or Adhesive partner offers a complementary combat loadout. Exploit useful MetaChemistry results, maintain coherency and avoid exposing both models to the same template.

### CSU + Blocker — cheap Pitcher-focused Duo

**25/0 with the basic CSU, or 26/0 with a CSU Specialist.** The Blocker counts as CSU, obtaining FT2 with only two bodies. The Blocker Specialist has BS Attack (+1B), X-Visor, Pitcher, Sensor, Pulzar and an Adhesive Launcher Rifle. When firing both available Pitcher shots, its Burst 2 plus FT2's selection die means rolling three dice and discarding one; this improves reliability without adding a third shot. Observe range bands, Disposable (2) and normal attack requirements. The CSU provides close defense, a second activation-efficient body and optional redundant scoring. The Blocker Hacker is a different profile and lacks the Pitcher.

A very cheap self-contained network-projection team that leaves the Core and Haris available for premium pieces. The network still needs actual Hackers elsewhere to exploit it. Two CSU + Blocker Duos are another option if both CSUs and both Blockers are allocated to them.

### Fusilier Core — replace excess basic Fusiliers

| Members | Pts / SWC | FT | Role |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Fusilier FO 12 + CSU Specialist 11 + CSU Adhesive 11 + Blocker Pitcher 15 + Bulleteer Spitfire 22/1 | 71/1 | 4 | Affordable offensive Core with network projection, three specialists and close defense |

The native Fusilier and the two CSUs plus Blocker give four same-family members; Bulleteer is a non-counting Wildcard. This provides the FT2/3/4 bonuses without wasting slots on extra basic Fusiliers. The Core is optional: independent Duos preserve flexibility, especially when a Bolt Core is preferred.

## Quantities and collection

| Requirement | First CSU | Second CSU | Total pts/SWC |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Balanced default | Specialist 11 | Breaker 10 | 21/0 |
| Anti-heavy control | Specialist 11 | Adhesive 11 | 22/0 |
| Objective-heavy mission | Specialist 11 | Specialist 11 | 22/0 |
| Cheap DZ defense | Breaker 10 | Breaker 10 | 20/0 |
| Active close-range team | Breaker Combi Specialist 13 | Adhesive 11 | 24/0 |

**Collection priority: two CSU models.** Represent the 11pt Specialist, 10pt Breaker and 11pt Adhesive loadouts as needed. Default to two CSUs in army lists; one or zero is viable if the mission, troop limit or a specific competing support package requires it. Do not spend an extra slot on a basic Fusilier unless it supplies a clear command or native Fireteam requirement.

**Preferred pairing:** 11pt Specialist + 11pt Adhesive when anti-heavy control is useful; 11pt Specialist + 10pt Breaker for a cheaper balanced list. Preserve the option to pair either CSU with a Blocker Pitcher instead of linking both together.
