# Optimate Agent Maximus — Neoterran Capitaline Army (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A-tier Lieutenant / all-rounder, B-tier conventional gunfighter. Not mandatory, but almost always choose the 47-point Inspiring Leadership Lieutenant if taking him.** His only native ranged face-to-face modifier is situational **Albedo (-3)** against MSV **or Marksmanship** opponents. He lacks Mimetism, Marksmanship, MSV and native BS Attack (+1SD). That keeps him below the Swiss Guard as an apex gunfighter. Almost everything else in his package is excellent: long-range AP shooting, immunity, non-Hackable S2 chassis, close combat, specialist, Tactical Awareness, wildcard flexibility and command economy.

## Why the package is excellent — except for native shooting MODs

| Attribute | Assessment |
|---|---|
| AP Thunderbolt (+2B) | B4 active, strong 16–32" AP gun, usable out to 48" |
| BS13, Albedo (-3) | Adequate BS, but no universal negative MOD or native spare die; Albedo is matchup-specific |
| Immunity (ARM) | Negates special ammunition effects on ARM saves (AP, DA, EXP, Shock, etc.) and applicable damaging traits; not immunity to BTS attacks, non-lethal traits or additional critical saves |
| W1 + NWI, ARM3/BTS3 | Good practical resilience, not equivalent to ARM5/BTS6 heavy infantry |
| Non-Hackable S2 MI | Can fight through enemy hacking coverage without being a normal anti-HI hacking target; can go Prone. His Repeater does not make him Hackable, but network security still matters |
| CC22, NBW, CC Attack (-6), AP+DA CCW | Excellent close-combat deterrent and a credible way to remove armored targets |
| E/M Carbine | Alternative against vulnerable HI, TAGs and REMs |
| MOV 6–2", enhanced Dodge | Useful mobility |
| Specialist Operative WIP13 | Can score objectives himself |
| Tactical Awareness | One personal Tactical Order each turn while eligible; can activate linked team as its leader |
| Inspiring Leadership, LT +1 SWC | Excellent list-building and order economy |
| Wildcard (ORC), Number 2 | Broad Fireteam access and useful link continuity |
| TinBot (Repeater) | Mobile hacking coverage; **not** a Firewall |

[Albedo](https://infinitythewiki.com/Albedo) also affects enemy **Marksmanship** users, including opposing Bolts, not only MSV. Against either type, Maximus imposes -3 on LoF-based BS Attacks and Discover against him. Against ordinary opponents it supplies no modifier. Avoid relying on him to beat high-Mimetism AROs, especially when a Black Friar or Aquila can handle them. [Immunity](https://infinitythewiki.com/Immunity) improves his survival when hit, not his chance to win the face-to-face roll.

## Lieutenant and Inspiring Leadership

For two points more than non-LT FTO, Maximus changes from costing 1 SWC to **granting +1 SWC (a two-SWC swing)** and gains Inspiring Leadership.

- **3-point Regular Warcor:** the main NCA conversion opportunity, since the sectorial has few Irregulars. All order-providing troopers become Regular and gain Courage (Religious Troops excepted from Courage) while the LT is on the table and not in a Null State.
- No Retreat while the relevant LT conditions hold.
- **10" Fireteam coherency** when Maximus belongs to a team whose members all have Regular Training natively on their profiles; this benefit applies to coherency checks only, not general ZoC for hacking/ARO, and does not stack with other ZoC extensions.
- **One Coordinated Order using the Lieutenant Special Order**, without spending a Command Token or another Order, provided Maximus participates. It is an alternative way to spend the LT Order, not an additional independent Order.

[Inspiring Leadership](https://infinitythewiki.com/Inspiring_Leadership) · [Coordinated Orders](https://infinitythewiki.com/Coordinated_Orders).

### How to use the Lieutenant Order

| Situation | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Solo FTO Lieutenant | Spend personally or coordinate movement/Dodge with other eligible solo units; FTO is allowed to operate solo |
| Linked Lieutenant | Spend Tactical Awareness to activate his entire team as leader. Do not confuse this with the LT Order |
| Squalos MULTI HMG NCO (66/1.5) | Best available NCO outlet; can spend the LT Order as its own Tactical Order, even in another combat group, without disrupting Maximus's team |
| Maximus personally spends LT Order while linked | Leaves the team on declaration; if leader, the team cancels. Cannot participate in a Coordinated Order while remaining linked |

A coordinated Move-Move or movement/Dodge order is more broadly useful than coordinated shooting in NCA. Coordinated Suppressive Fire is less attractive for Maximus than Joan because his AP Thunderbolt cannot enter Suppressive Fire. Do not automatically buy a 66-point TAG just to spend the LT Order; prefer the solo Maximus option or accept occasional unused LT Orders if the list otherwise has a strong architecture. CoC options **Quinn (24/0)** or **Bolt (26/0)** can protect succession, but do not preserve Maximus's army-wide benefits if he stops meeting the skill conditions.

[NCO](https://infinitythewiki.com/NCO) · [Tactical Awareness](https://infinitythewiki.com/Tactical_Awareness) · [Fireteam Integrity](https://infinitythewiki.com/Fireteam_Integrity).

### Recommended use

**Cheap Fusilier Haris (74 pts):** Probably the most cost-efficient default. Fusilier and Blocker supply the two matching bodies for FT2; Maximus gains +1SD, Paramedic can provide recovery support, Blocker offers Pitcher, Sensor and specialist utility. Three specialists, one Tactical Awareness activation and expanded coherency. The fragile support bodies should be kept out of unnecessary AROs.

**Hannibal forward Haris (95–99 pts):** Strategic Deployment gives the team +4" Forward Deployment when its requirements are met (Hannibal deploys first as team leader). Two Tactical Awareness Orders can activate the whole team, with their respective owners leading. Hannibal's Vulkan Shotgun and Nanopulser cover the short range where Maximus is less effective; Hannibal adds Sensor. The ORC version is FT3; the Bolt KHD version gives up FT3 but adds hacking, E/M Mines and a second specialist, generally preferred for mission play. The ORC Firewall BSG can replace the 26-point ORC for +1 point if relevant to protecting Hackable teammates. [Strategic Deployment](https://infinitythewiki.com/Strategic_Deployment).

**Black Friar pairing:** A particularly good complement, not a redundant gun. The 24/0 Thunderbolt Black Friar offers MSV2, Albedo (-6) and Nanopulser to attack Mimetism/MSV targets that Maximus is less efficient against. Maximus handles armored targets, command economy, specialists and CC. Add an actual ORC for a legal Neoterra Haris at FT2; alternatively place both in a legal Fusilier Core with sufficient Fusilier-equivalent support. A Black Friar does not count as ORC, and a Maximus + Black Friar pair by itself lacks a native anchor.

**Squalos NCO:** Two Regular Orders, two Tactical Awareness Orders and one shared LT Order source from the two units (five potential activations, only two unrestricted Regular Orders). The Duo is legal but FT1; often better to operate them on separate attack lanes, or put Maximus in a separate purpose-built team. NCO does not require being linked with Maximus.

**Do not force a large Core** merely to reach FT4/+1 BS or FT5/Sixth Sense. FT2 is the efficient reliability breakpoint; other members should justify their own cost.

## Minimus and final selection

The 48/1 solo profile adds synchronized Minimus with Flash Pulse, Repeater, E/Marat, Mimetism (-3) and DA CCW. The Peripheral provides an extra threat origin, not its own Regular Order; maintain coherency, and note the bot is Hackable even if Maximus is not. It cannot link and has no LT option in NCA. Generally skip unless the mission specifically rewards this synchronized package. NCA's other solo attack pieces are excellent.

**Bottom line:** Maximus is a very competent gunfighter attached to a superb leadership, specialist, order-generation, defensive and CC package. Lack of consistent native ranged MODs is the main reason he is not an apex gunfighter. Prefer the **47/+1 Lieutenant** rather than the 45/1 non-LT or 48/1 Minimus, use FT2 to improve shooting reliability, and pair him with a complementary anti-marker gun such as the Black Friar instead of expecting him to win every firefight. Use the smallest Fireteam that delivers the needed benefits.

## Original Kestrel analysis — retained reference copy

The original follows in full for transferable mechanics and reasoning. **Source-sectorial costs, AVA, Fireteams and recommendations are not NCA advice; the NCA section above and canonical NCA chart take precedence.** Links retain original source destinations.

### Optimate Agent Maximus — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A-tier premium gunfighter/specialist, especially as Lieutenant when the list can exploit the SWC swing and has an NCO.** The **45-point FTO Lieutenant** is genuinely compelling in a Fennec Fireteam: Maximus can spend **Tactical Awareness** to activate the whole team, while an **NCO elsewhere (Firefly or NCO Squalos) or inside the team (Sforza FTO)** can spend his Lieutenant Special Order **without Maximus leaving the Fireteam**. The **48-point Lieutenant + Minimus** is the preferred solo command option. Non-LT Maximus remains good, but loses the largest part of his exceptional list-building economy.

**Core role:** An **S2, non-Hackable, mobile 16–32\" AP gunfighter**, with useful fire to 48\", WIP13 Specialist Operative, E/M Carbine, potent close-combat deterrence, a self-funded Tactical Awareness activation, and protection against MSV via Albedo. He is not an infiltrator, a TAG, a dedicated reactive turret, or an expendable secondary gun.

Skills/equipment: **Albedo (-3)**, CC Attack (-6), Courage, Dodge (+2.5 cm / +1\"), Natural Born Warrior, Immunity (ARM), Number 2, Specialist Operative, Tactical Awareness and Cube 2.0. All four share **AP Thunderbolt (+2B), E/M Carbine, Heavy Pistol, AP+DA CC Weapon**.

| Profile | Points | SWC | Difference | Main recommendation |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Agent, non-LT | **48** | **1** | **Minimus** (Peripheral Synchronized) | Solo specialist/attacker with extra board-control model |
| Agent FTO, non-LT | **45** | **1** | **TinBot (Repeater)**; no Minimus | Linked attacker when another LT is preferred |

All four have **Tactical Awareness**. Lieutenant does **not** give Maximus a second Regular Order in the pool: it creates a **separate Lieutenant Special Order**. The +1 SWC LT profile versus the 1 SWC non-LT profile yields a **two-SWC list-building swing** for the same points.

Maximus Agent is **not a Wildcard** and cannot simply join KESTREL/COLCORP/ARMORED Fireteams. The separate Maximus TAG is a different unit sharing the **one-Maximus-per-list** limit.

[Human Sphere](https://human-sphere.com/Maximus) also lists a **47-point Inspiring Leadership FTO Lieutenant**, whereas the repository's pinned Kestrel source contains only the four options above. #### The AP Thunderbolt (+2B): a long-range AP HMG alternative

The [N5.3 weapon chart](https://infinitythewiki.com/Weapon_Chart) gives the **AP Thunderbolt** **B2, PS6, ARM/2**, with the unit-specific **+2B giving Maximus B4 in the Active Turn**. Its ARO is normally **B1** (the +B modifier does not turn into extra ARO Burst). Unlike an HMG or Spitfire, the Thunderbolt **does not offer Suppressive Fire**.

| Distance | AP Thunderbolt (+2B) | AP HMG | AP Spitfire |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0–8\" | -3 | -3 | 0 |
| 8–16\" | 0 | 0 | +3 |
| 16–24\" | **+3** | **+3** | **+3** |
| 24–32\" | **+3** | **+3** | -3 |
| 32–40\" | **0** | -3 | -6 |
| 40–48\" | **0** | -3 | -6 |
| Active Burst | **4** | **4** | **4** |
| PS (lower is better) | **6** | **5** | **6** |
| Suppressive Fire | **No** | Yes | Yes |

**Implications:** At **16–32\"**, the Thunderbolt is nearly an AP HMG but gives the opponent a **one-point better Saving Roll**; at **32–48\"** it has a valuable +3 effective BS advantage over an AP HMG. At **8–16\"**, the AP Spitfire is markedly more accurate. Maximus's BS13 becomes **BS16** in the +3 band before other MODs. His B4 AP profile is excellent against armor and enemy attack pieces, but he lacks natural MSV, Mimetism, Marksmanship and +1SD.

**In FENNECS FT2**, Maximus gains **BS Attack (+1SD)**: on the B4 shot he rolls **five dice, keeping four**, not five independent hits. This greatly improves the consistency of the primary gun. Maximus **does not count as Fennec** for bonus levels, so he needs **two Fennec-equivalents** beside him for FT2. A link of Maximus + Sforza + only one Fennec is **FT1**, not FT2. See [Fireteam bonuses](https://infinitythewiki.com/Fireteam_Bonuses).

**Secondary weapons and drawbacks:** The **E/M Carbine** offers non-lethal anti-HI/TAG/REM pressure at more appropriate close-to-mid distances and may be preferable to fishing for Thunderbolt wounds. His **AP+DA CCW**, CC22, Natural Born Warrior and CC Attack (-6) make him costly to charge. However, his **ARM3, BTS3 and one real wound plus NWI** do not make him an equivalent of a two-wound ARM5 HI, and direct templates, high BTS attacks, mines and multi-wound trades still matter. He has **no direct-template weapon on his own profile**.

#### Lieutenant vs non-Lieutenant: the NCO interaction is decisive

**Do not evaluate LT Maximus assuming he must personally spend the Lieutenant order.** N5.3 [NCO](https://infinitythewiki.com/NCO) allows an NCO to convert an available Lieutenant Special Order into a **Tactical Order usable by that NCO**. It explicitly **does not require NCO and LT to share a Combat Group**. If that NCO is in a Fireteam, they can spend the converted order **as Fireteam Leader** to activate **their team**. The order is **one resource**, used either by Maximus or an NCO: multiple NCO models do not each create a new LT order.

**Relevant Kestrel NCOs:**

| NCO | Legal role with Agent Maximus LT | Why consider |
|---|---|---|
| **Firefly** (e.g. **19pt Repeater** or **23pt Chain of Command**) | **Elsewhere**, solo or KESTREL/ARMORED Fireteam, **not in Maximus's FENNECS team** | Cheapest practical external NCO; **CoC 23** simultaneously protects an exposed Maximus LT |
| **Squalos Mk-II MULTI HMG, NCO (66pt / 1.5 SWC)** | **Elsewhere** (solo or ARMORED); **not in FENNECS** | If already taking the NCO TAG, convert Maximus LT order into another expensive TAG activation; don't purchase an extra 66pt TAG solely as an order sink |
| **Father Lucien Sforza FTO (23pt / 0 SWC; Albedo FTO 26pt / 0)** | **Wildcard may join Maximus's FENNECS Core/Haris**, or serve in another eligible Fireteam | Can spend LT order to **activate Maximus's team**, provided **Sforza leads that order**; offers Viral Rifle, Adhesive Launcher Rifle, X-Visor and Nanopulser |

**Order distinction in Maximus's FENNECS Fireteam:**

1. **Maximus Tactical Awareness order:** **can activate the entire Fireteam**, with Maximus as leader. The team stays intact and Maximus makes the attack if shooting is declared.
2. **Maximus personally spending Lieutenant Special Order:** **he leaves the Fireteam** on declaring the order. If he was Fireteam Leader, the team **cancels**. If another trooper was leader and two members remain, they can stay linked; rejoining at the next States Phase is possible subject to Coherency. See [Fireteam Integrity](https://infinitythewiki.com/Fireteam_Integrity).
3. **NCO Sforza in the same Fireteam spending the LT order:** **Sforza leads and activates the whole link**; Maximus stays linked. On a shoot order, only **Sforza** attacks while the others Idle; on movement, all move. This is a valuable *extra team activation*, not a second Maximus-led gun attack.
4. **NCO Firefly or Squalos outside the team spending LT order:** Maximus's link stays intact, but the **NCO (or the NCO's own Fireteam)** receives that order. The external NCO can even be in a **different Combat Group**.

References: [NCO](https://infinitythewiki.com/NCO), [Tactical Awareness](https://infinitythewiki.com/Tactical_Awareness), [Fireteams in the Active Turn](https://infinitythewiki.com/Fireteams_in_the_Active_Turn), [Fireteam Integrity](https://infinitythewiki.com/Fireteam_Integrity).

**Verdict on Lieutenant:** If the list already includes an NCO — particularly the **23pt Firefly CoC/NCO** — **LT Agent Maximus is often the best option even in a Fireteam**. His +1 SWC rather than 1 SWC produces the two-SWC swing, and the LT order remains productive without breaking his link. CoC is especially attractive because the expensive attacking Lieutenant is a plausible casualty. This is not permission to needlessly expose an LT: NWI and ARM immunity do not protect from all threats.

**When non-LT makes sense:** No desirable NCO, a list that already wants the **19pt Magistrate Strategos L1 Lieutenant**, or an assault plan that cannot safely protect Maximus despite CoC. Strategos L1 converts its Lieutenant order into a **Regular Order in the Magistrate's Combat Group** and grants an additional deployment reserve; it competes with, rather than stacks with, another Lieutenant's NCO/LT-order plan. Non-LT FTO is still a solid **45pt / 1 SWC** linked attacker with Tactical Awareness. Non-LT Minimus is a respectable **48pt / 1 SWC** independent attacker. Neither is an automatic skip.

#### Solo versus Fireteam

##### Solo with Minimus — 48pt (LT or non-LT)

**Use when:** You want an independently moving S2 gunfighter/specialist and want Minimus's extra threat origin. The synchronized bot is not an extra order or army-list slot, but supplies **Repeater, Flash Pulse, E/Marat, Mimetism (-3), DA CCW** and close-range/CC protection; Maximus and Minimus activate on the same order, may attack different legal targets and must maintain Peripheral Coherency. Minimus is **Hackable**, even though Agent Maximus himself is not; deploying a Repeater deep in the midfield has both opportunity and security costs.

The solo **LT** can use his Lieutenant order directly **without Fireteam-detachment complications**, or delegate it to an NCO on another part of the table. The **3-point premium over FTO** buys meaningful additional coverage and flexibility. Maximus's best personal shooting is at **16–32\"**; Minimus is more useful when the pair can approach contested areas and keep it out of casual fire.

**Avoid solo** if an FT2 Fennec link is central to getting enough reliability from the main gun, or if a similarly priced more durable/forward specialist better suits the mission.

##### FTO — 45pt (LT or non-LT)

**Use when:** You specifically want the Thunderbolt to get **FT2 +1SD**, want the valuable Tactical Awareness order to **move the whole link**, and want to carry a **Fennec Disco Baller/FO/Sensor and Paramedic** along. Giving up Minimus and occupying Core/Haris capacity are meaningful opportunity costs. **TinBot (Repeater)** adds hacking-network reach; it **does not provide a Firewall**.

**Recommended support package:**

| FENNECS Haris | Cost |
|---|---:|
| **Maximus Agent FTO LT** | **45 / +1 SWC** |
| Fennec **FO / Sensor / Disco Baller** | **13 / 0** |
| Fennec **Paramedic** | **12 / 0** |
| **Total** | **70 points / +1 SWC** |

**FT2**, because the two Fennecs match; Maximus receives **+1SD** on his B4 AP Thunderbolt. Tactical Awareness can activate the whole Haris. The FO supplies a second specialist, Sensor and **Eclipse** access to cross lanes (Maximus **cannot shoot through Eclipse**); the Paramedic offers a risky way to recover Maximus from NWI or assist either teammate. Fennecs are much squishier than Maximus; do not expose both just to give Maximus +1SD.

**Best LT version of this Haris:** Bring a **Firefly NCO** (ideally the **23pt Chain of Command** variant) **elsewhere**. Firefly spends the LT order on its own activation or own Fireteam. Maximus does **not** need to split from the Haris to utilize the Lieutenant resource. This directly addresses the concern that spending the LT order personally breaks his Fireteam.

**Sforza in the same link: alternative FENNECS Core.**

| Member | Cost |
|---|---:|
| Maximus Agent **FTO LT** | 45 / +1 |
| Sforza **FTO NCO** | 23 / 0 |
| Fennec FO/Disco/Sensor | 13 / 0 |
| Fennec Paramedic | 12 / 0 |
| **Total** | **93 / +1 SWC** |

This is a **four-member Core**, not a Haris (only 3 members permitted for Haris); its **two Fennecs give FT2**, not FT4, because Maximus and Sforza do not count as Fennecs. Two separate restricted-order sources can activate the team in a turn: **Maximus Tactical Awareness** (Maximus as leader) and **Sforza NCO conversion of Maximus's LT order** (Sforza as leader). It is **expensive** and consumes the one Core, but provides two complementary gunfighters, an anti-tech NCO, Disco, and medical support. If instead taking **Maximus + Sforza + one Fennec as a Haris**, remember the only matching Fennec makes it **FT1**: no +1SD. The external-Firefly NCO plus 70-point Haris generally has a cleaner role split.

**Fireteam opportunity cost:** Kestrel allows **one Core and one Haris**; spending Haris on Maximus/Fennecs prevents the **29pt Magistrate + two Jackals COLCORP Haris**, and a Maximus Core competes with a dedicated defensive Griffin/Black A.I.R. Core. Do not build a giant link simply because all members are legal. A non-LT **FTO + two Fennecs = 70/1 SWC FT2** when the separate Strategos Magistrate is already desirable.

| Preferred engagement | **16–32\" AP B4**; can shoot to 48\" at 0 MOD | **8–24\" MMR/Spitfire**; native **BS Attack (+1SD)** |
| Ranged reliability | BS13; needs FT2 for +1SD | **BS14 + native +1SD**; naturally efficient solo |
| Durability | ARM3/BTS3, W1 + NWI; **Immunity (ARM)** | **ARM5/BTS6, W2**; generally better basic survivability |
| Hacking | **Agent not Hackable**, but **Minimus is** | **HI is Hackable** |
| Positioning | No FD; **Tactical Awareness** accelerates approach | **Forward Deployment (+8\")** on applicable profiles; Hidden Deployment option |
| Specialty | **Lieutenant +1 SWC, NCO synergy**, E/M Carbine, Minimus or TinBot Repeater | Hidden deployment, D-Charges, Drop Bears/CrazyKoala, stronger immediate midfield presence |
| Fireteams | **FENNECS Core/Haris FTO only** | **FTO Wildcard** across eligible teams |

**Pick Maximus** for a long-range primary AP attacker, anti-MSV gunfighter, non-Hackable specialist, Tactical Awareness engine or SWC-positive Lieutenant backed by an NCO. **Pick Crux** when you need early-midfield scoring, a more reliable solo +1SD marksman, HI durability, D-Charges/deployables or wildcard flexibility. A **46/0 Crux MMR** is a real competitor to **45/1 non-LT Maximus FTO**, but is less direct competition to **45/+1 Maximus LT** with NCO support.

**Against TAG investment:** Solo Maximus 48pt is a legitimate secondary attacker with a distinct S2/non-Hackable footprint, but don't automatically spend another 48 points when a Tikbalang/Squalos is already consuming most offensive orders. The **55pt/1 Squalos MMR** is just seven more points than solo non-LT Maximus but has TAG size, Hackable/Total Control exposure, and different weapons. Maximus also **can go Prone** as S2 infantry, unlike a TAG.

#### When to take / when to skip

| Situation | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| NCO Firefly (especially 23pt CoC) or Sforza already desired; SWC pressured | **LT Maximus strongly favored** |
| Long lanes **16–32\"**, elite ARM targets, hostile MSV | **Strong pick** |
| Objective mission, want AP gun + FO/Disco + Paramedic moving together | **45pt FTO; 70pt Haris FT2** |
| Want whole team activation from Maximus Tactical Awareness | **FTO worthwhile** |
| NCO Sforza will move same Fireteam with the LT order | **Consider four-body Core**, not FT1 three-body Haris for gun bonuses |
| Already have secure Magistrate Strategos LT | **45pt non-LT FTO** or **48pt non-LT solo**, if Maximus adds a needed attack role |
| Sparse SWC, no NCO and high LT exposure risk | **Prefer non-LT or skip** depending on alternative LT |
| Dense table dominated by 0–16\" exchanges | **Crux MMR/Spitfire**, Bulleteer Heavy Shotgun, Peacemaker may offer better value |
| Need a midfield specialist from deployment, Hidden Deployment or D-Charges | **Crux / Scarecrow** rather than Maximus |
| Already have TAG, another expensive attacker and insufficient orders/defense | **Often skip** |
| Want a long-range reactive turret | **Skip as dedicated ARO**; B1 Thunderbolt isn't what you're buying |

#### Bottom line

**Rankings for Kestrel:**

1. **FTO LT 45 / +1 SWC — A**, if an NCO reliably spends his LT order and an attack team actually wants FT2; ideal with **Fennec FO/Disco + Paramedic** and **external Firefly NCO/CoC**.
2. **Solo LT + Minimus 48 / +1 SWC — A**, if you want an independent commander/attacker and can manage LT risk.
3. **FTO non-LT 45 / 1 SWC — B+**, excellent if you deliberately choose a **Magistrate Strategos LT** and want team activation/FT2.
4. **Solo non-LT + Minimus 48 / 1 SWC — B+**, flexible but faces stronger value comparisons against **46/0 Crux MMR**, **55/1 Squalos MMR**, and other premium attackers.

**Deciding question:** *Can the list put Maximus's Lieutenant order to work with an NCO without disrupting his Fireteam?* If yes, **LT Maximus is often clearly preferable to non-LT** because it gains **2 effective SWC** for zero additional points. If no, favor his non-LT profile when the plan depends on maximum freedom to attack, or use the Magistrate Strategos LT instead.
