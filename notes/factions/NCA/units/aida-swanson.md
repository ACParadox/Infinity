# Aïda Swanson — Neoterran Capitaline Army (N5.3)

**Skip: use Peacemaker instead.** The Peacemaker HSG + Auxbot_3B (24/0.5) already provides forward-deployed Minelayer coverage at exactly the same cost, with better mobility, a Repeater, Remote Presence and a synchronized second body. Aïda is a niche non-hackable Viral/Decoy alternative, not a structural NCA unit. Collection priority: skip (C-tier).

The Peacemaker with Auxbot_3B also establishes a mine during deployment, so Aïda does **not** uniquely supply order-free forward mine defense. It offers the controller, Auxbot and mine as three potential threats (the Auxbot is not an independent Order generator), plus Repeater coverage for NCA's hackers. Its larger silhouette and hacking vulnerability are genuine drawbacks, but the overall package is generally more useful.

## Aïda's remaining niches

- **Viral Mines:** particularly attractive against low-BTS biological attackers; different ammunition and save pressure from the Auxbot's Shock Mine. Do not treat Viral as equally effective against STR targets.
- **Decoy (2):** uncertainty about the real model during deployment; not Camouflage or Hidden Deployment.
- **Non-hackable LI:** useful where enemy infowar would make a forward REM vulnerable.
- **Close-range counterattack:** BS12, Mimetism (-3), SMG (+1B), Viral Pistol and MA2; credible against ordinary infantry, but fragile and short-ranged.
- **Booty:** incidental upside, not a reliable reason to buy the profile.

If selected, deploy her safely near an approach, use the initial mine to protect a blind corner or passage, and retain the real model for a later counterattack or additional mines. Forward Deployment (+10 cm) is only about four inches beyond the DZ, not true midfield infiltration. Avoid using her as a main gunfighter or dedicated melee attacker.

## Final verdict

Aïda is not strictly dominated: Viral ammunition, Decoy and a non-hackable chassis remain distinct. However, her practical battlefield roles are covered by stronger or cheaper NCA alternatives. Default to **zero Aïda**, and take the **Peacemaker with Auxbot_3B** if the list specifically needs forward Minelayer defense. Do not prioritize purchasing Aïda for the NCA collection.
