# Deva Functionaries — Neoterran Capitaline Army (N5.3)

**Near auto-include: HD 25/0.5 (S−) when taking a Blocker Pitcher. KHD 20/0 (A+) is highly recommended unless another KHD covers the role.** The Deva is NCA's economical WIP15 dedicated hacker, not primarily a gunfighter. Prefer independent backline deployment through NCA's exceptional Repeater network; strongly consider two Blocker Pitchers with both Devas. Collection priority: one recommended, two worthwhile for dedicated hacking lists.

## HD — the default dedicated hacker

The Blocker Pitcher at 15/0 makes a conventional HD particularly attractive. A KHD alone cannot exploit the network against ordinary HI, TAGs and REMs. The Deva HD brings WIP15 to Spotlight, Carbonite, Oblivion and Total Control against appropriate legal targets. Useful even without a Guided Missile Launcher: Targeted assists gunfighters, immobilization and isolation interrupt enemy attack pieces, and Possession threatens eligible TAGs.

Immunity (Isolated) prevents Isolation, including from applicable Oblivion/E/M effects, but does not prevent other effects of those attacks. NWI adds resilience against damaging attacks; the Deva lacks a native Firewall. WIP15 offers a 75% unmodified single-roll success rate compared with 65% for WIP13 and 60% for WIP12.

| HD | Pts / SWC | WIP / BTS | Differentiator |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Fusilier | 15 / 0.5 | 12 / 0 | Cheapest option; native Fireteam anchor |
| Blocker | 19 / 0.5 | 13 / 3 | Personal Firewall (-3), Sensor, composition |
| Bolt | 24 / 0.5 | 12 / 6 | E/M Mine, Marksmanship, Neoterra access |
| **Deva** | **25 / 0.5** | **15 / 3** | **High WIP, NWI, Isolation immunity** |
| Locust | 29 / 0.5 | 13 / 3 | Advanced deployment, Hidden Deployment, Pitcher, Firewall |
| Hexa | 30 / 0.5 | 13 / 3 | Camouflage, Hidden Deployment, Counterintelligence |

Prefer the Deva over the Blocker HD for dedicated proactive hacking when the list can afford six extra points. The Blocker's Firewall is valuable in direct cyber duels and the Bolt fits combat links; neither is strictly dominated. A standard HD is a high priority, not an unconditional requirement to use this exact unit in every list.

## KHD — the economical dedicated cyber attacker

At 20/0, the KHD is exceptional value: WIP15 Trinity (B3, WIP18 before other modifiers in the active turn), NWI, Immunity (Isolated), specialist status, Cybermask and a Boarding Shotgun. Use it to attack enemy Hackers before committing Swiss Guards, ORCs, TAGs or REMs, or deter hostile Hackers entering the network.

| KHD | Pts / SWC | WIP / BTS | Differentiator |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Deva** | **20 / 0** | **15 / 3** | Best economical independent dedicated hacker; NWI |
| Bolt | 22 / 0 | 12 / 6 | Marksmanship, BSG (+1B), E/M Mine, Shock Immunity, Fireteam |
| Locust | 27 / 0 | 13 / 3 | Forward deployment, Hidden Deployment, Pitcher, Firewall |
| Hexa | 27 / 0 | 13 / 3 | Camouflage, Hidden Deployment, Counterintelligence |
| Swiss Guard | 47 / 0 | 13 / 6 | Premium durable combat specialist; Firewall, camouflage |

Prefer the Deva KHD for independent backline hacking; prefer the Bolt KHD when the combat specialist, E/M Mine, BTS6 and Neoterra composition are important. Do not treat the KHD as a substitute for an HD: Trinity targets enemy Hackers, not every Hackable trooper.

## Backline operation and network projection

Both Devas can remain in total cover in the DZ and use the same friendly Repeater network without needing proximity to each other or to a Pitcher operator. The Blockers move or shoot Pitchers to project the network; Auxilia FO, Tech-Bees and native mobile Repeaters further extend coverage.

- Attack through friendly Repeaters against legal targets; LoF is not required.
- Total cover protects against conventional shooting, not hacking. Enemy Hackers in range of your Repeaters can use them to attack your Hackers subject to the Repeater rules and Firewall protection; avoid assuming a backline Hacker is immune to retaliation.
- Neither Deva has innate Sixth Sense or a native Firewall. Stealth can restrict reactive hacking when its conditions are met.
- Advance the KHD only when objectives, local hacking coverage, enemy Repeater opportunities or its Boarding Shotgun justify doing so. Cybermask can aid movement but is not unconditional protection.
- Preserve WIP15 specialists for late-game objectives. Do not spend Orders moving them merely to keep them near the Pitcher.

Rules: [Repeater](https://infinitythewiki.com/Repeater), [Firewall](https://infinitythewiki.com/Firewall), [Cybermask](https://infinitythewiki.com/Cybermask), [Stealth](https://infinitythewiki.com/Stealth).

## Preferred hacking packages

| Package | Pts / SWC | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| **Deva HD + Blocker Pitcher** | **40 / 0.5** | Default minimum network |
| Deva HD + Deva KHD + Blocker Pitcher | 60 / 0.5 | Dedicated cyber package |
| Deva HD + Bolt KHD + Blocker Pitcher | 62 / 0.5 | Integrated combat specialist alternative |
| **Deva HD + Deva KHD + two Blocker Pitchers** | **75 / 0.5** | Preferred strong network; two independent attack axes |

The 75-point package produces four Regular Orders, four specialists, two Sensors and four Pitcher ammunition uses, while leaving 225 points and 5.5 SWC in a standard 300/6 list. Two Pitchers offer redundancy after loss or destruction of a Repeater, coverage on both flanks, and scoring/control tools after ammunition is spent. Prefer both Pitchers over one Pitcher plus Blocker HD when both Devas already provide hacking. If native or deployable Repeaters already cover the required axes, one Pitcher can suffice.

## Fireteams — usually stay independent

Deva is not a Wildcard. It may join only CSU Duos, with a native CSU anchor, max one Deva per Duo. It does not count as CSU: a Deva/CSU Duo is FT1, without the FT2 +1 SD composition bonus.

| Duo | Pts / SWC | FT level |
|---|---:|---:|
| Deva KHD + CSU Breaker | 30 / 0 | 1 |
| Deva KHD + CSU Specialist | 31 / 0 | 1 |
| Deva HD + CSU Specialist | 36 / 0.5 | 1 |
| Deva Spitfire + CSU Specialist | 40 / 1 | 1 |
| CSU Specialist + CSU Specialist | 22 / 0 | 2 |
| CSU Specialist + Blocker Pitcher | 26 / 0 | 2 |

Attach a spare CSU to a Deva only if meaningful close defense, coordinated movement or late scoring warrants it. Generally leave both Devas solo in the backline and retain the CSU/CSU or CSU/Blocker Duo's +1 SD. Two independent CSU Specialist + Blocker Pitcher Duos cost 52/0 total, each FT2, without consuming Core/Haris slots. Alternatively place one Blocker in the offensive Core/Haris and the other with a CSU.

## Other profiles

**MSV2 Spitfire 29/1:** a viable secondary independent anti-Mimetism attacker with NWI, but Black Friar Thunderbolt (+1B) 24/0 has BS13, MSV2, Albedo (-6), zero SWC and Wildcard access. Prefer the Friar unless the Spitfire or second MSV2 specifically matters. Bolt Spitfire 25/1.5 and Bulleteer Spitfire 22/1 are other strong gunfighter alternatives.

**Devabot 24/0:** peripheral attack utility but NCA already has inexpensive Auxilia and advanced Peacemakers. No compelling default niche.

**Sensor 21/0:** Blocker Pitcher 15/0 and Pathfinder TacAware 17/0 offer Sensor plus more useful mission/network capabilities. Hannibal also provides Sensor. Triangulated Fire is situational.

**FO 21/0:** WIP15 scoring is useful, but the Deva hackers also score and offer much more strategic utility. CSU Specialist 11/0, Auxilia FO 15/0, Blocker Specialist 15/0 and Pathfinder are cheaper alternative scorers.

**Basic Combi and MULTI Rifle:** skip in normal lists. Prefer Blade-Ops, Bolts, Hannibal, Bulleteers or cheap CSUs according to role.

## Listbuilding takeaway

1. Start with Blocker Pitcher + Deva HD (40/0.5).
2. Add Deva KHD if a different KHD is not already filling the role.
3. Strongly consider AVA 2 Blocker Pitchers with both Devas; split the network axes.
4. Keep Devas solo in total cover by default. Use CSUs in composition-efficient Duos instead of stapling them to hackers without a reason.
5. Spend remaining SWC on dedicated gunfighters; do not purchase Devas primarily for shooting.
