# Clausewitz Uhlans — Neoterran Capitaline Army (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: S-tier Feuerbach (70/1.5); A− Spitfire/HRL (80/1.5). Must-own, not must-field. Prefer the Feuerbach almost exclusively unless deliberately building a forward assault TAG.** The Uhlan is an independent Camouflage hunter-killer, not a static gun platform. One of the strongest reasons to play NCA.

## Profiles

AVA2, Regular TAG, MOV 6-4", CC15, BS15, PH15, WIP12, ARM6, BTS6, STR3, S6. Camouflage, Mimetism (-3), Surprise Attack (-3), Remote Presence, Courage, Tactical Awareness, Warhorse, ECM: Guided (-6), enhanced Dodge/GizmoKit (consult Army for exact numeric modifiers). No Fireteam, NCO, Lieutenant or Hidden Deployment option. Both profiles have AP CCW and Crabbot.

| Opt | Pts/SWC | Weapons and modifiers | Rating |
|---:|---:|---|---|
| 2 | **70/1.5** | Feuerbach, Panzerfaust, Blitzen, MULTI Pistol; BS Attack (+1B) | **S** |

The 70-point profile is only four points more than the Squalos MULTI HMG NCO. It buys a fundamentally different attack mechanism: marker protection, Surprise Attack, persistent Mimetism, long-range anti-armour fire and Isolation immunity.

## Feuerbach — preferred hunter

| Weapon/mode | Active B | Ammo | PS | Positive range |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| Feuerbach Burst | 3 | AP+DA | 6 | 16–32" |
| Feuerbach alternate | 2 | EXP | 6 | 16–32" |
| Panzerfaust | 2 | AP+EXP | 6 | 16–32" |
| Blitzen | 2 | E/M | 6 | 16–32" |
| MULTI Pistol | 3 | AP or Shock | 7 | 0–8" |
| MULTI Pistol anti-materiel | 2 | DA | 7 | 0–8" |

- The Feuerbach retains a 0 range MOD at 32–48", where an ordinary HMG suffers −3. Its B3 AP+DA mode forces two saves against half ARM per ordinary hit; excellent against expensive HI and TAGs, though lower Burst than an HMG.
- Panzerfaust offers AP+EXP for a decisive shot; Blitzen disables vulnerable targets through E/M. Native BS Attack (+1B) is mandatory in the Active Turn when applicable: both shots consume both standard Disposable uses. Do not count multiple B2 attacks without reloading or remaining ammunition.
- The pistol provides genuine close-range flexibility but does not replace a direct template or CC deterrent.
- +1B is **active-turn only**; ordinary reactive shooting is B1, unlike +1SD which also applies reactively. The Feuerbach chart's alternate mode does not list an Impact Template trait; do not assume it behaves like a missile blast template.
- No weapon on this profile has Suppressive Fire. End attack runs in Camouflage or a protected position rather than planning suppression.

[Modifiers](https://infinitythewiki.com/Skills_and_Equipment_Module) · [Disposable](https://infinitythewiki.com/Traits) · [Weapons chart](https://infinitythewiki.com/Weapons_Chart).

### Example of asymmetric attack

Against a conventional BS14 HMG without MSV at 32–48", both sides in partial cover and no other MODs:

- Uhlan opening from Camouflage: B3 on 12 (BS15, range 0, cover −3).
- Reactive opponent: B1 on 2 (BS14, range −3, cover −3, Mimetism −3, Surprise Attack −3).
- After revealing, opponent instead shoots on 5 in the same conditions; Surprise Attack is not persistent.

These are illustrative target numbers, not universal face-to-face probabilities. Enemy MSV, Combat Instinct, Sixth Sense, different weapon ranges and other MODs can change the result.

### Practical use: attack, retreat, re-camouflage

1. Deploy as a Camouflage marker; unlike Hidden Deployment it can contribute its Regular Order while deployed and eligible. S6 often makes its identity obvious, but the marker protection still matters.
2. Select a favorable lane and exploit Surprise Attack on the opening engagement, ideally using range asymmetry. Avoid exposing multiple enemy AROs simultaneously.
3. Use the Feuerbach for repeated attacks or select Panzerfaust/Blitzen when the target justifies the limited ammunition.
4. Budget movement and an Order to re-enter Camouflaged State when requirements are met (including being outside enemy LoF and relevant Sensor restrictions). Do not assume a TAG can use Cautious Movement.
5. Keep a repair route and layered defensive coverage. Preserve the TAG rather than expose it to the entire opposing active turn.

ARO is an option, especially into an unfavorable enemy range band, but the Uhlan is normally worth more alive for the next active turn. Camouflage is not Hidden Deployment; a marker does not prevent an enemy from Discovering it, and MSV3 can circumvent normal marker protection.

## Spitfire / Heavy Rocket Launcher — forward assault

| Weapon/mode | Active B | Ammo/traits | PS | Positive range |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| Spitfire | 4 | AP, Suppressive Fire | 6 | 8–24" |
| HRL hit | 3 | AP, Continuous Damage | 5 | 16–32" |
| HRL blast | 3 | AP, Continuous Damage, circular impact template | 6 | 16–32" |

Native BS Attack (AP) applies to both guns; the HRL weapon-specific +1B makes it B3 in the active turn. Continuous Damage can inflict additional damage after failed saves. The HRL blast mode gives a genuine anti-cluster option, unlike the Feuerbach.

Best when the mission rewards advancing and occupying midfield space, engaging clustered Fireteams and using Spitfire Suppressive Fire. Camouflage and Suppressive Fire are alternative end states, not simultaneous protection.

**Why usually skip:** +10 points, shorter primary range, no disposable E/M or AP+EXP options, and greater exposure to hacking, mines and melee. It lacks a direct template, close-combat protection and native Firewall. The Feuerbach can often avoid those risks by staying farther away. Strong profile, but narrower value proposition.

## Warhorse, durability and vulnerabilities

Warhorse prevents Isolation (including Oblivion and the Isolated component of E/M), keeps the Uhlan Regular in Loss of Lieutenant and Retreat, and ignores enemy BS Attack (-X) special-skill modifiers. It does not ignore Mimetism, range, cover or Surprise Attack simply by being Warhorse.

- Carbonite, Total Control, Spotlight, Immobilization from E/M and ordinary damage remain relevant. Warhorse is not hacking immunity.
- Camouflage and range management are the main protection against enemy repeaters; no native Stealth or Firewall is listed.
- Remote Presence supplies additional Unconscious depth and Engineer reroll options; ARM6/BTS6/STR3 is a resilient but repair-dependent chassis.
- S6 cannot go Prone or declare Cautious Movement; terrain, narrow passages and hiding space are meaningful limitations compared with S2 Swiss Guard.
- MSV, templates, high-Burst attackers, dedicated CC troops and coordinated pressure can overcome the defensive modifiers.
- No native specialist on the TAG chassis; Crabbot can help with appropriate mission tasks but is not a substitute for multiple mobile specialists.

- **Versus Squalos:** prefer Uhlan for independent asymmetric attack and long-range anti-armour work. Squalos offers B4 MULTI HMG, SR-1, NCO, Suppressive Fire and Duo activation efficiency. Its Pathfinder/Engineer partners can change the listbuilding calculation.
- **Versus Swiss HMG:** Swiss has better Mimetism and B4, a smaller silhouette and lower price; excellent for ordinary gunfights. Uhlan adds anti-armour ammo, extended range, Tactical Awareness, Warhorse and recoverable TAG durability.
- **Versus Swiss ML:** complementary rather than redundant. Uhlan is the active hunter; Swiss ML is a hidden reactive threat and selective active counterattacker.
- **Versus Maximus TAG:** Uhlan is the better asymmetric ranged gunfighter; Maximus offers close-combat deterrence, Immunity (ARM/POS), occupation and Inspiring Leadership on the 0-SWC LT. Neither is universally superior.

Machinist + Palbot is another repair approach. A Blocker Hacker's linked Firewall cannot be imported onto the independent Uhlan via Fireteam; use network control, positioning and other legal Firewall sources if available. Warhorse reduces the urgency of anti-Oblivion support but not protection against Possession or Immobilization. Keep hackers and repeaters relevant to the attack route. Use Auxilia/Peacemaker to protect close approaches. Consider Redeye or an MSV/Disco unit to create a different attack solution.

### Premium pairings (300-point budget)

| Combination | Pts/SWC | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Uhlan Feuerbach + Swiss ML | 130/3 | Active hunter and hidden ARO; preferred complementary threat pair |
| Uhlan Feuerbach + Swiss KHD | 117/1.5 | Long-range hunter and protected midfield specialist |
| Double Feuerbach Uhlan | 140/3 | Two independent armoured hunters, two Tactical Orders |
| Uhlan Feuerbach + Squalos NCO | 136/3 | Distinct weapon/range and activation options |

Double Uhlan is viable, not mandatory. Both share counters and cannot link; avoid spending so much on redundant threats that specialists, recovery and network control disappear. Tactical Awareness supplies one personal Order per eligible TAG, not a free full attack run.
