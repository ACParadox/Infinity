### Maximus, Optimate and HexaDome Legend (TAG) — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A+ as AP HMG Lieutenant; A− as non-LT. Primary role: durable midfield zone-control anchor and heavy assault piece, not simply a premium gunfighter.** Maximus combines Immunity (ARM), exceptional CC, E/M, a four-Wound shared Escape System profile and Tactical Awareness. The **82pt / 0 SWC AP HMG Lieutenant** is particularly attractive with a protected Magistrate CoC + Jackal Duo. Prefer the cheaper Squalos or Tikbalang for pure gunfighting or mobile flanking.

> **Scope:** [Current Kestrel roster and canonical Fireteam chart](https://github.com/ACParadox/Infinity/blob/main/sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md#maximus-optimate-and-hexadome-legend), [Kestrel Fireteams](https://github.com/ACParadox/Infinity/blob/main/notes/factions/kestrel/kestrel-fireteams.md), [Human Sphere Maximus profile](https://human-sphere.com/Maximus), [N5.3 weapons](https://infinitythewiki.com/Weapons_Chart). Tactical conclusions are theorycraft, not evidence of exact tournament compositions. Reviewed 21 November 2026.

#### Why Maximus is a zone-control anchor

His advantage is **being hard to dislodge**, rather than imposing Mimetism on every ranged duel. Occupy an important piece of midfield terrain and make the opponent spend Orders removing him, bypassing him or accepting a scoring disadvantage.

- Immunity (ARM) neutralizes AP, DA, EXP, K1's ARM=0 effect, Continuous Damage and Monofilament's special lethality when the applicable save uses ARM. Full ARM6 remains meaningful, particularly in cover. Critical hits still cause their extra saving roll; normal save modifiers such as BS Attack (SR-1) still matter.
- **Immunity (POS)** prevents Possessed State (Total Control). It does not protect against Oblivion/Isolation, Carbonite/IMM-B, Spotlight/Targeted, E/M, or other unrelated effects. [Immunity](https://infinitythewiki.com/Immunity) · [Total Control](https://infinitythewiki.com/Total_Control).
- CC24, CC Attack (-6), Natural Born Warrior and AP+DA CCW (PS4 in TAG form) make melee attacks against him much less attractive than against conventional TAGs. NBW cancels opponent-imposed negative CC MODs in a CC Face-to-Face when targeted by a CC Attack; it does not cancel their positive bonuses.
- E/M Carbine threatens TAGs/HI at close range, allowing a disable-and-finish attack sequence. It is non-lethal and not a direct template.
- AP HMG in Suppressive Fire creates a B3 ARO and an additional enemy -3 in qualifying Face-to-Face rolls at 0–24". Combined with cover and immunity this can be an expensive obstacle.
- Escape System provides an additional chance to keep a scoring/command piece alive after the armor is lost.

**Distinction:** Tikbalang controls territory through mines, direct templates and mobility. Maximus controls it through durable physical occupation, conventional gun threat and melee deterrence. He has **no direct template or deployable**; do not confuse high durability with comprehensive area denial. Cheap warbands, peripherals and simultaneous threats can still exploit this gap.

##### Using him on the table

1. Clear key AROs while advancing toward a central approach, scoring zone or defensible objective.
2. Choose cover with restricted enemy firing angles; do not expose the entire S6 silhouette to the enemy army.
3. Screen approach routes with Jackal mines, inexpensive template troops or other AROs; use Sensor and friendly Repeater coverage as appropriate.
4. Finish in Suppressive Fire when relevant rather than chasing every available target. SF ends under its normal cancellation rules; it is not an invulnerable permanent state.
5. Preserve a route for engineering support and plan how to score on subsequent turns. Avoid spending the whole attack budget merely to stand in a zone that an enemy can bypass.

Best in central-objective, room/approach-control, quadrant, Frontline-style zone scoring and attrition missions, depending on scenario access and scoring rules. Check doors, terrain and mission-specific restrictions for S6 TAG access. Also strong for appropriate destructible objectives via the Anti-materiel CC weapon. Less efficient on widely dispersed objectives, extreme vertical tables, or missions requiring many independent specialists.

#### AP HMG vs AP Thunderbolt (+2B)

| | AP HMG | AP Thunderbolt (+2B) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Active Burst | 4 | 4 |
| PS | **5** | 6 |
| 0–8" | -3 | -3 |
| 8–16" | 0 | 0 |
| 16–32" | +3 | +3 |
| 32–48" | -3 | **0** |
| Save | ARM/2 | ARM/2 |
| Suppressive Fire | **Yes** | No |

Both benefit from BS Attack (SR-1). The AP Thunderbolt's official weapon chart describes Normal ammunition with ARM/2, functionally armor-halving for saving rolls. Its native B2 becomes B4 in active turn from the profile's +2B modifier. Neither is a close-range primary gun.

**Prefer AP HMG:** better PS, Suppressive Fire for zone occupation, and the zero-SWC Lieutenant option. The Thunderbolt saves two points and 0.5 SWC versus the non-LT HMG and is three range MOD points better at 32–48". Choose it only when these benefits matter more than persistent suppression.

**E/M Carbine:** B2, PS7, +3 at 0–16", two saves against half BTS. A viable way to stop a heavy target instead of grinding away STR. If safe and useful for the mission, follow up with AP+DA melee. It is not a substitute for template defense against cheap infantry.

#### Escape System and recovery

[Transmutation](https://infinitythewiki.com/Transmutation) shares accumulated Wounds and applicable states across profiles. On the externally documented Escape System (3) threshold, replace the TAG with its S2 operator after resolution and center an Eclipse circular template on the model. Three accumulated Wounds leave one remaining on the shared four-Wound profile; excess damage can still incapacitate or kill. There is no fresh four-Wound pool.

The Operator profile is documented with MOV6-2", CC22, BS13, PH13, WIP13, ARM3, BTS3, VITA4, S2, Specialist Operative, Immunity (ARM) and Tactical Awareness. The specific surviving normal-profile loadout and LT continuity should be checked in Army rather than substituting the aggregated Reinforcement operator entry. The small silhouette and Eclipse can help the surviving specialist hide or score; a surviving operator does not automatically mean the original TAG remains available.

Unlike Remote Presence Squalos/Tikbalang, Maximus lacks the extra Unconscious level and Remote Presence Engineer command-token rerolls. While in STR form, ordinary Engineer repairs and applicable state clearance remain important. After conversion to VITA use the appropriate recovery rules; do not assume an Engineer restores the original TAG chassis.

#### Lieutenant economics

The HMG LT costs **the same 82pts** as the standard HMG but saves **1.5 SWC**. No Lieutenant (+1 Order): only one native LT Special Order plus Tactical Awareness.

| Package | Points / SWC |
|---|---:|
| AP HMG 82 + Magistrate Strategos LT19 | 101 / 1.5 |
| AP Thunderbolt80 + Magistrate Strategos LT19 | 99 / 1 |
| **Maximus LT82 + Magistrate CoC21** | **103 / 0** |
| Standard HMG82 + Strategos19 + Jackal6 | 107 / 1.5 |
| **Maximus LT82 + Magistrate CoC21 + Jackal6** | **109 / 0** |

The last package is the preferred build: three pooled Regular Orders (Jackal conversion conditional on intact COLCORP at Order Count), one Tactical Awareness and one LT Special Order. Counterintelligence, protected WIP13 CoC specialist, and cheap deployment-zone template defense. Use the LT Order on Maximus where useful or an eligible NCO, not twice.

Trade-off: spend two more points for the CoC version of Magistrate, forgo Strategos' flexible LT-to-Regular conversion and two-reserve deployment advantage, and expose your actual Lieutenant. In return save 1.5 SWC for a Griffin HMG, Black A.I.R. sniper, hacking or other threats. Prefer non-LT when mission rules strongly penalize LT exposure, or when Strategos is central to the plan. CoC is insurance, not permission to throw away 82pts.

#### TAG comparisons

| | Maximus HMG | Tikbalang HMG | Squalos MULTI HMG NCO |
|---|---|---|---|
| Points / SWC | 82/1.5 (LT 82/0) | 69/1.5 (LT73/1.5) | 66/1.5 |
| BS | 14 | 15 | 15 |
| Wounds | 4 shared (escape after 3) | 3 | 3 |
| ARM/BTS | 6/6 | 6/6 | 6/6 |
| AP ranged fire | Yes | No | Yes |
| Mimetism | No | -3 | No |
| Climbing Plus | No | Yes | No |
| Immunity ARM / POS | Yes / externally documented | No | No |
| Melee | Elite | Poor | Poor |
| Direct template / mines | Neither | HFT / Shock Mines | Neither |
| E/M weapon | Yes | No | No |
| TA / Remote Presence | Yes / No | Yes / Yes | Yes / Yes |
| NCO | No | No | Yes |

- **Tikbalang:** superior mobile gunfighter, infantry removal and persistent area denial. Mimetism and Climbing Plus allow much better angles; HFT and mines punish cheap approaches. Ordinary HMG struggles with armor. Maximus is preferable where durability, melee and anti-armor matter more.
- **Squalos:** superior pure ranged gunfighter per point. BS15, MULTI ammunition (including EXP ARO), NCO and better repairability. Maximus trades 16 extra points for IMM ARM, escape, superior melee and E/M; LT also saves SWC. The Squalos MMR55 and HMG+GL62 are cheaper specialist variants, not equivalent zone anchors.
- **Other PanO TAGs:** Cutter favors camouflage/Mimetism and surprise; Uhlan camouflage and flexible engagement; Dragão high-Burst guns; Jotum raw armor; Seraph mobility and assault. These are broad role comparisons across sectorials, not Kestrel-legal alternatives.

Maximus is not necessarily better at winning ranged Face-to-Face rolls. His value is that the usual high-impact anti-TAG hits are less effective and he can fight in more ways at short range.

#### Fireteams: default solo

ARMORED requires a Tikbalang or Squalos; Maximus is an optional member, not a required-choice anchor. A Maximus + Pathfinder or Maximus + Firefly Duo alone is **illegal** under the current chart.

| Legal Duo | Cost / SWC with non-LT HMG Maximus | Assessment |
|---|---:|---|
| Maximus + Squalos MMR55 | 137 / 2.5 | Experimental expensive double TAG |
| Maximus + Squalos MULTI HMG66 | 148 / 3 | Redundant ranged investment |
| Maximus + Tikbalang69 | 151 / 3 | Contrasting routes and overlapping resource demands |

All are FT1, no FT2 +1SD. The LT HMG version reduces each cost by 1.5 SWC, not points. A personal LT Special Order breaks an intact team; Suppressive Fire also removes its user from a Fireteam. A solo Maximus avoids both problems. Do not spend over half the list on multiple TAGs simply to obtain a Duo.

#### Support priorities

- **Magistrate CoC21 + Jackal6:** preferred protected command module for Maximus LT.
- **Tech-Bee9:** cheap Regular Order, Specialist, Flash Pulse and Repeater. TAGCom assists eligible TAGs in her group; check her Dodge and GizmoKit interaction with Maximus's printed attributes, rather than applying the Tikbalang's fixed PH values.
- **Machinist15 + Palbot3:** engineering access and state recovery. No Remote Presence rerolls on Maximus.
- **Fennec FO/Sensor/Disco13:** camo clearing, objective play and Eclipse to facilitate safe positioning or CC approaches.
- **Fennec Hacker/FastPanda16:** activate the friendly Repeater network, threaten enemy hackable troops and deter approaches. Does not automatically make Maximus safe inside an enemy hacking network.
- **Infiltrating Jackal Minelayer:** pre-positioned persistent area denial and anti-approach screen. Fennec mines/templates or other disposable defenders are alternatives.
- **EVO Mulebot15:** conditional Fairy Dust/hacking support if the list has enough meaningful uses. Avoid spending a large support budget on redundant defense.

#### Opportunity cost: Agent Maximus

The 45pt Agent FTO and 82pt TAG cannot coexist. Agent has S2, Specialist Operative from deployment, Albedo (-3), TA, Immunity (ARM), AP Thunderbolt (+2B), E/M Carbine and excellent CC, and can join a FENNECS Core/Haris. Smaller silhouette and Prone provide much better concealment. The TAG has far greater concentrated durability, superior CC/BS, AP HMG/SF and a free LT/SWC option.

**82pt comparison:** Agent FTO45 + Griffin HMG37 = 82pts/2.5 SWC, two independently useful attackers, two pooled Orders, MSV1 and Fireteam options. Maximus TAG LT82 = 82pts/0 SWC, one pooled Order, TA and LT, concentrated staying power. Neither is universally superior.

#### Final selection

**A+ AP HMG LT:** preferred when building around a durable central anchor and needing SWC for complementary attackers. **A− non-LT HMG:** acceptable for protected command/Strategos plans. **A− Thunderbolt:** conditional long-lane/SWC alternative, less suited to Suppressive Fire zone control.

Take Maximus to hold territory, force expensive removal attempts and attack armor/melee targets. Do not take him just because a large gun is necessary: Squalos or Tikbalang will usually do that job more efficiently. Respect hacking and E/M, his lack of templates and deployables, and large silhouette.
