# Maximus, Optimate and HexaDome Legend (TAG) — Neoterran Capitaline Army (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A as AP HMG Lieutenant; B+ as non-LT AP HMG; B as AP Thunderbolt. Not a must-take.** Premium midfield occupation and close-assault TAG, rather than the most efficient ranged gunfighter. The 84/0 Lieutenant is the default if taking the TAG. Agent Maximus is the more economical way to obtain Inspiring Leadership.

NCA replaces Kestrel's 82/0 LT with the 84/0 Inspiring Leadership version. NCA Maximus is a required-choice anchor for Ultima Ratio Duo, unlike the optional ARMORED member in Kestrel. Maximus TAG and Agent Maximus are mutually exclusive.

## Battlefield role: durable midfield anchor

- Immunity (ARM) neutralizes special ammunition effects on applicable ARM saves, including AP, DA, EXP, K1 and Monofilament. ARM6 in cover remains meaningful; critical extra saves and ordinary save modifiers still apply.
- Immunity (POS) prevents possession via Total Control; it does not prevent Oblivion, Carbonite, Spotlight or E/M. Not a hacking-proof TAG.
- CC24, CC Attack (-6), Natural Born Warrior and AP+DA CCW make conventional melee removal unusually difficult. E/M Carbine is useful against TAGs and HI at close range.
- Escape System gives a shared four-Wound pool, not four fresh Wounds after ejection. The externally documented threshold is Escape System (3): after three accumulated Wounds the surviving S2 operator ordinarily has one Wound remaining and an Eclipse template is placed. Excess damage can still kill. Human Sphere documents the operator as a Specialist Operative; confirm surviving normal-profile equipment and LT continuity in Army. The pinned abbreviated roster omits the numeric threshold and Dodge modifier; do not infer changes without checking Army.
- No Remote Presence: no extra Unconscious level or associated Engineer rerolls. Engineer support is still important while in STR form. Do not assume engineering restores the TAG after escape to the VITA profile.
- AP HMG supports Suppressive Fire, useful for holding an approach at 0–24". No direct template or deployable on Maximus himself: use Auxilia, Peacemakers and other defensive pieces to protect approaches from cheap infantry.
- Large S6 silhouette, no Mimetism and BS14 mean the TAG does not win ranged Face-to-Face rolls as efficiently as Squalos or Uhlan.

Best missions: central objectives, zone control, quadrant scoring, attrition and suitable destructible objectives. Less attractive on very vertical tables, dispersed objectives or missions requiring numerous independent specialists. Clear important AROs, occupy a defensible position, screen approach routes, and retain engineering access. Do not expose the TAG to the entire enemy army.

Both benefit from SR-1. Thunderbolt saves 2 pts/0.5 SWC relative to standard HMG and is better at 32–48", but loses stopping power and Suppressive Fire. The LT HMG is better value overall. E/M Carbine is B2, PS7, +3 at 0–16", with two saves against half BTS; it is not a direct template. [Weapons chart](https://infinitythewiki.com/Weapons_Chart).

## Inspiring Leadership and order economy

The NCA Lieutenant costs only +2 pts, saves 1.5 SWC and adds the same army-wide leadership skill available to Agent Maximus.

- A 3pt Warcor becomes Regular under the skill conditions. NCA has limited Irregular options, unlike Joan-led MO.
- Order-generating troops gain Courage (Religious Troops excepted) and Regular training while Maximus is the active, on-table, non-Null Lieutenant.
- Spend the LT Special Order to coordinate up to four eligible troopers including Maximus, without spending a Command Token or pooled Regular Order. Alternatively use it on Maximus alone or on an eligible NCO; do not count the same order twice.
- +2" Fireteam coherency radius (normally 10" total) when every member has Regular printed in its profile. This particular effect is independent of LT status under the current skill wording, but does not stack with other coherency bonuses.
- No Retreat while the army-wide LT conditions hold.

**Useful coordinated actions:** Move-Move, Move-Dodge, or Suppressive Fire with eligible independent troops in the same combat group. Maximus, Auxilia, Tech-Bee, Peacemaker, Redeye, Uhlan and Locust are potential participants, subject to actual skill and equipment eligibility. Maximus cannot place deployables, limiting coordinated mine placement. Coordinated fire is situational because of shared target requirements and reduced Burst. Linked members cannot participate while remaining linked; plan to operate solo or cancel the Duo before coordinating.

| Immunity ARM/POS | Yes/Yes | No/No | No/No |

| Remote Presence | No | Yes | Yes |
| Close combat | Excellent | Poor | Poor |
| Leadership | Inspiring Leadership | NCO | None |

- **Squalos:** better conventional gunfighter per point, BS15, MULTI ammunition and improved repairability. NCO can spend the LT order when available. Prefer for open-lane ranged attack.
- **Uhlan:** better asymmetric ranged engagements, Camouflage, Mimetism and Surprise Attack. Feuerbach 70/1.5 is attractive as an independent long-range threat; Spitfire/HRL 80/1.5 is another option.
- **Maximus:** better concentrated durability against conventional anti-TAG ammunition, melee deterrence, E/M and occupation. Not necessarily better at winning the initial gunfight.

Complement Maximus with hidden-deployment Swiss Guard or other asymmetric attackers rather than assuming a second TAG is required.

## Ultima Ratio Duo

Maximus can anchor a Duo without Squalos. ORC max 1; eligible Wildcards can join. All examples below are FT1, not FT2 (no +1 SD bonus).

| Partner | Total with LT | SWC | Assessment |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Pathfinder Tactical Awareness 17 | 101 | 0 | Excellent general-purpose mobility/specialist |
| Blade-Ops Engineer FTO 23 | 107 | 0 | Excellent support/repair |
| ORC BSG Firewall 27 | 111 | 0 | Good hacking defense/close-range coverage |
| Quinn CoC 24 | 108 | 0 | Legal but exposes succession piece |
| Shona Carano 22 | 106 | 0 | Redundant melee role; provides templates |
| Squalos MULTI HMG NCO 66 | 150 | 1.5 | Expensive double TAG |

**Pathfinder TA:** Sensor, Repeater, FO and another Tactical Order. Each TA trooper can lead and activate the Duo with its own Tactical Order. Excellent for moving a specialist with the TAG; avoid exposing the fragile REM or creating unnecessary hacking exposure.

**Blade Engineer:** AP SMG, Mimetism (-3), D-Charges, GizmoKit, Engineer and TA. Can accompany and repair Maximus, or support objectives independently after leaving the Duo. The duo's two Tactical Orders improve movement economy. Avoid risking both models in one engagement.

**ORC Firewall BSG:** Firewall (-3) protects eligible linked members from hacking; BSG supplements short-range coverage. Durable but no TA or Engineer.

**Default: solo Lieutenant Maximus** with an independent Engineer/Palbot or Blade Engineer nearby. Ordinary personal LT Order and Suppressive Fire remove the user from the Fireteam; Coordinated Orders require independent participants. The Duo remains attractive if planning to spend TA and pooled orders primarily on linked movement; a non-LT Maximus is easier to keep linked. ## Command and support package

| Unit | Pts/SWC |
|---|---:|
| Maximus TAG LT | 84/0 |
| Quinn Chain of Command | 24/0 |
| Warcor | 3/0 |
| Tech-Bee | 9/0 |
| **Total** | **120/0** |

Four pooled Regular Orders while Inspiring Leadership is active, one TA, one LT Special Order, Chain of Command, TAGCom, Flash Pulse and deployment-zone defensive support. Leaves 180 pts/6 SWC in a normal 300pt list. Bolt CoC26 is an alternative in a Neoterra team (BTS6, Drop Bears). Keep succession protected; CoC avoids Loss of Lieutenant under its conditions but does not grant the successor Inspiring Leadership.

Other support: Machinist + Palbot or Blade Engineer; Blocker Pitcher/Hacker, Bolt KHD and friendly Repeaters to contest hacking; Auxilia or Peacemaker for close-range area denial. Tech-Bee TAGCom is useful, but verify exact Dodge/GizmoKit interactions rather than importing Squalos' fixed PH values. Hexa Counterintelligence can be useful if the list needs protection from strategic order reduction.

## Opportunity cost: Agent Maximus

| | Agent LT FTO | TAG LT |
|---|---:|---:|
| Points | 47 | 84 |
| SWC | +1 allowance | 0 |
| Inspiring Leadership / TA | Yes/Yes | Yes/Yes |
| Silhouette | S2 | S6 |
| Specialist from deployment | Yes | No |
| Fireteam scope | Wildcard (counts as ORC) | Ultima Ratio Duo |
| Albedo (-3) | Yes | No |
| Primary gun Suppressive Fire | No | Yes |
| Durability | Moderate | Excellent |

Agent LT47 + ORC Feuerbach35 = 82pts (net 0.5 SWC); Agent LT47 + Shona22 + Blade Engineer23 = 92pts (1 SWC allowance). These are point-budget comparisons, not necessarily single Fireteams.

Agent provides the same leadership benefits, costs 37 fewer points, grants +1 SWC allowance and fits a wider range of Fireteams. Choose Agent for infantry-heavy lists; choose TAG specifically for concentrated durability and occupation, not merely for Inspiring Leadership.

## Final selection

| Role | Rating |
|---|---|
| Midfield occupation/durability | A+ |
| Lieutenant/order efficiency | A |
| Close combat | A+ |
| Ranged gunfighter | B+ |
| Hacking resistance | B+ (possession immune only; ordinary hacking remains dangerous) |
| Listbuilding flexibility | B |

**Prefer 84/0 AP HMG LT almost exclusively if taking TAG Maximus.** Pair with a protected CoC and independent support, and use the saved SWC for complementary attackers. Non-LT AP HMG is viable when a different command plan is indispensable; Thunderbolt is a narrower long-lane option. Not a must-add: Agent, Squalos or Uhlan can be better according to the mission and army architecture.
