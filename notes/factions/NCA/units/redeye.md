# Redeye Close Air Support Squad — Neoterran Capitaline Army (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A — strong independent asymmetric secondary attacker; preferred over Garuda Spitfire for sustained fighting. Not a list must-have. Blitzen is the default.**

**Redeye is worth the 12-point premium over Garuda Spitfire.** For identical SWC, +2 BS, +6 ARM, +3 BTS and a second STR make a substantially better sustained fighter; the Chain Rifle and Blitzen add useful engagement options. Both have the same movement and Jet Propulsion, including pop-up Jump + Shoot when legal. Garuda can match Redeye's basic -6 defensive shooting MOD when in Partial Cover, but is much less resilient if hit (effective ARM3 in cover versus Redeye's ARM6). Redeye's large silhouette and No Cover are real disadvantages, and its Aerial skill prevents contact-based objective interaction while active.

**Garuda is principally an airborne flanker or a cheaper Redeye alternative, provided AVA is not already used by the FO.** Spitfire is appropriate if airborne entry saves significant movement Orders or the list cannot afford Redeye; otherwise prefer Redeye for repeated engagements. Boarding Shotgun (B2 AP, +6 at 0–8in) plus Chain-colt (direct template) is a distinct 18-point, zero-SWC backline assassin, not a cheaper sustained gunfighter. Use it against vulnerable support pieces, or to threaten armor at close range. Garuda FO is a strong alternative when the mission calls for airborne scoring; AVA 1 prevents taking it alongside a second Garuda.

## NCA list integration

- Prefer one Redeye as an exploitation piece after Swiss Guard HMG, Uhlan Feuerbach or Squalos opens the board. The premium guns and Redeye attack along different vectors; Redeye is not the default long-range ARO breaker.
- **Redeye + Garuda BSG (55/1.5)** is complementary: persistent mobile fighter plus cheap airborne threat, if Garuda AVA is free.
- **Garuda BSG + Bulleteer Spitfire (40/1)** is a worthwhile alternative when the priority is inexpensive separate attack vectors and saving SWC. Bulleteer alone (22/1) is an efficient linked/ground-level Mimetism (-6) alternative, but lacks Redeye's resilience and flight geometry.
- **Uhlan Feuerbach + Redeye (107/3)** or **Swiss HMG + Redeye (93/3)** are strong distinct attack pairs. All three cost 163/4.5; build around them rather than assuming every list needs them.
- No Tactical Awareness, NCO or Fireteam access on Redeye. Budget regular Orders for the attack and retreat. NCA's Repeater network and KHDs can help clear hostile hackers; Blade-Ops Engineer or Machinist provides repair support (ranged GizmoKit while Aerial is active).
- Zero Redeyes is legitimate when points/SWC or terrain favor other tools; one is preferred for mobile combined arms; two (74/3 with Blitzen) is deliberate skew. Sensor is useful but less unique in NCA given Blockers, Pathfinders and other Sensor access.

### Redeye Close Air Support Squad — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A overall; collection must-have, not a list must-have.** Blitzen **A (default)**; Continuous Damage Grenade Launcher **A (offensive alternative)**. Excellent asymmetric secondary attacker and flank exploiter, not the best conventional frontal ARO-breaker. One is a strong general-purpose option; two constitute a deliberate list archetype.

#### Profiles

**AVA 2 · Regular VH · MOV 20-5 (8–2in), BS14, PH13, WIP13, ARM6, BTS6, STR2, S7; no CC attribute.** Aerial, Courage, Dodge (-3), GizmoKit (PH=14), Mimetism (-6), Remote Presence, Super-Jump (Jet Propulsion), Terrain (Total), No Cover, Tech-Recovery, Sensor. No Fireteam access, Tactical Awareness or NCO.

| Opt | Loadout | Pts / SWC | Rating |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Spitfire, Chain Rifle, Blitzen | 37/1.5 | **A — default** |
| 2 | Spitfire, Chain Rifle, Grenade Launcher (Continuous Damage) | 38/1.5 | **A — backline disruption** |

#### Why take it?

- **Attack geometry rather than raw gun strength:** 10in Short Skill Jump (8+2), or 12in Long Skill Jump (8+4). Jet Propulsion allows direction changes, including popping up over a wall, firing and returning to the starting position within one Order if the entire trajectory fits. A horizontal landing surface large enough for the base is required. The whole declared trajectory can generate AROs; never assume a return to Total Cover prevents return fire.
- **BS14, B4 Spitfire, PS6, Normal ammo:** +3 at 8–24in. Very good against conventional enemies from unexpected angles, but lacks AP and MSV. Mimetism (-6) does not remove the target's Partial Cover; Redeye itself has No Cover.
- **ARM6/BTS6/STR2 and Remote Presence:** resilient for its cost, but exposed S7 and no Partial Cover's Saving Roll bonus mean it is not equivalent to a covered TAG. MSV, hacking and templates can undermine its defense.
- **Sensor:** Normal WIP+6 (19) roll to reveal all eligible Camouflage/Hidden Deployment enemies within ZoC, without LoF. Cannot substitute for a Pathfinder's specialist role. Fast movement makes it easy to sweep otherwise inaccessible areas; be mindful of hacking and templates.
- **Chain Rifle:** close-range direct-template trade option, particularly against enemies otherwise difficult to hit.
- **Aerial:** cannot enter Silhouette contact, go Prone, enter Engaged or declare Cautious Movement while active. Does not trigger Boost weapons through contact. Does not confer blanket immunity to mines or hacking. Cannot interact with objectives requiring contact; can contribute to applicable zone-control scoring according to mission rules.

#### Blitzen — default

37/1.5. B1, PS6, E/M, two BTS/2 Saving Rolls, Disposable (2), Non-Lethal. Range: -3 at 0–8in; 0 at 8–16; +3 at 16–32; -3 at 32–48.

Threatens expensive HI, TAGs and other VHs, including targets beyond favorable Spitfire range. Its major advantage is a credible reactive E/M threat without spending active Orders. Park at a difficult angle after the assault, if the opponent must cross that angle; do not leave it exposed to a superior active gun simply to use the Blitzen. It can also disable an armored target in the active turn when the Spitfire's Normal ammunition is inefficient. Two disposable shots limit prolonged use.

Daboarder (WIP12 #180) prefers the Blitzen because he rarely wanted to spend Orders on speculative grenades and values the awkward E/M ARO. This is the preferred general-purpose loadout, not a universal rule.

#### Continuous Damage Grenade Launcher — offensive alternative

38/1.5. Only one point more, same SWC. B1 circular Impact Template, Continuous Damage, Speculative Attack. Range 0 at 0–24in, -3 at 24–32, -6 at 32–48. BS14 gives a speculative roll of 8 at 0–24in before opposing Dodge; the -6 speculative MOD applies, but Mimetism and Partial Cover BS MODs do not. Continuous Damage can force repeated saves until a successful save or death.

Useful against clustered Fireteams, fragile cheerleaders, suspected Lieutenants that are legal targets, prone rooftops and troops hidden behind walls. Can also fire directly when a circular template catches multiple targets. Speculative Attack is a Long Skill, so cannot Jump + Speculative Attack in one Order. A valid trajectory and target are required; targets can Dodge. Do not spend several Orders on low-probability grenades when Jump + B4 Spitfire solves the same problem more efficiently.

Prefer this profile when seeking a dedicated backline disruption package or when running two Redeyes and diversifying their weapons. Shepherd's aerial analysis and WIP12 NCA discussion recognize the occasional high-value speculative attack, not a reason to use it every turn.

#### Midfield control and Suppressive Fire

A useful secondary role after the attack run: B3 Spitfire in Suppressive Fire, Mimetism (-6) plus suppression (-3) gives an applicable opponent **-9** in Face-to-Face Rolls, before range and other countermeasures. ARM6 makes ordinary return fire relatively unattractive.

SF range 0–16in at 0, 16–24in at -3; it is not the Spitfire's normal range profile. Entering SF requires a separate Long Skill. Blitzen and Chain Rifle AROs cancel SF, as do appropriate disabling states, other non-SF AROs and active Orders. MSV, direct templates, attacks beyond 24in, hacking and S7/No Cover remain important weaknesses. Prefer Total Cover when protecting the investment matters more than holding a lane. A Redeye is 10–11pts cheaper than the 48pt Hetkari Red Fury, and can reach awkward positions faster, but the Hetkari has AP/Shock and better cover-based conventional defense.

A Griffin HMG and Redeye (74pts/3 SWC with Blitzen) usually complement each other better than two Griffin HMGs in an offensive list: Griffin clears visible long-range threats, Redeye exploits the newly opened route and attacks otherwise inaccessible support pieces. Do not count the Redeye as a replacement for a premium long-range gun, an objective specialist or a self-funded TAG.

#### How to use it

1. Deploy fully concealed with enough room for the S7 base and planned landing paths; reserve deployment can exploit an enemy weak flank.
2. Remove priority MSV, long-range AROs, dangerous enemy Repeaters and hackers with the primary gun or other tools.
3. Jump + Shoot through the chosen opening; the whole trajectory determines exposure, including hacking areas and other legal AROs. Prefer isolated engagements and unexpected LoF, rather than exposing to multiple defenders.
4. Use Sensor when revealing nearby markers or Hidden Deployment meaningfully improves the attack or mission.
5. Finish in Total Cover, or deliberately choose a useful suppression/Blitzen position. Budget retreat Orders. A successful attack does not justify sacrificing 37–38pts unnecessarily.

**Repair:** [Firefly Engineer](https://github.com/ACParadox/Infinity/blob/main/notes/factions/kestrel/units/firefly.md) or [Machinist](https://github.com/ACParadox/Infinity/blob/main/notes/factions/kestrel/units/machinist.md) can provide ranged GizmoKit. While Aerial is active, physical Engineer contact is not possible; plan actual ranged LoF. Redeye rolls PH14 for GizmoKit. Tech-Recovery can remove eligible disabling states together on a successful roll, with no negative consequence for failure of that state-clearing roll. It does not itself remove Unconscious; normal eligible STR recovery is needed first, and ordinary GizmoKit repair has its own failure consequences. Remote Presence and Aerial becoming inactive in Unconscious can permit other normal repair approaches.

#### Zero, one or two?

| Count | Cost | Recommendation |
|---|---:|---|
| 0 | — | Entirely legitimate for specialist-heavy missions, SWC-constrained lists, unfavorable terrain or already crowded attack packages |
| 1 | 37–38/1.5 | Preferred starting point for a mobile/asymmetric list; default Blitzen |
| 2 | 74–76/3 | Specialized dual-flank pressure, two Blitzen or mixed Blitzen/GL (75/3). Needs deliberate Order pools, repair/anti-hacking and specialist coverage |

With two, consider cheap Magistrate/Jackal command support, a Fennec Hacker, Firefly Engineer and a conventional long-range weapon. Magistrate Strategos can convert the LT Order into a Regular Order usable by a Redeye in the appropriate Combat Group; they cannot directly use an unconverted LT Order via NCO. Do not assume every list needs this dedicated support package, particularly when one Redeye is a secondary threat.

**Bottom line:** buy/own one; start with Blitzen. Not automatic in every Kestrel list. Play it for its unique angles rather than as an ordinary Spitfire. The repository's cross-faction VH guide ranks Redeye #4 (A), and podcast sources disagree mainly over whether its mobility justifies the 1.5 SWC taken from conventional PanO guns.
