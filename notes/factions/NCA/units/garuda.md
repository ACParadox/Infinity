# Garuda Tacbots — Neoterran Capitaline Army (N5.3)

**A+ mission runner (FO); A-tier airborne attackers (Boarding Shotgun and Spitfire). Must-own, not must-field.** The FO is the default all-comers choice, but the Spitfire deserves serious consideration in NCA's high-SWC command builds. The defining feature is not merely airborne deployment: it is vehicle-like jump geometry on a small conventional REM that can take cover, go prone and interact with objectives.

## Profiles and priorities

## The miniature vehicle argument

Garuda is not a VH and does not have the Aerial skill. It nevertheless has the 8-inch first MOV and Jet Propulsion that make fast flying attackers so effective, on an S2 base without many of their restrictions.

| Capability | Garuda | Typical Aerial attacker |
|---|---|---|
| First MOV | 8 inches | Often 8 inches; profile-dependent |
| Short Jump + another skill | Up to 10 inches | Same with equivalent MOV and Super-Jump |
| Long Jump | Up to 12 inches | Same with equivalent MOV and Super-Jump |
| Change direction mid-jump | Yes | If Jet Propulsion |
| Silhouette | S2 | Often substantially larger |
| Ordinary Partial Cover | Yes, when eligible | Depends on profile and cover eligibility; Aerial is not itself a blanket No Cover rule |
| Prone | Yes, outside Jump | No while Aerial is active |
| Objective contact | Yes | No while Aerial is active |
| Cautious Movement | No (REM restriction) | No |
| Airborne Deployment | Parachutist + Combat Jump | Profile-dependent |
| Can be engaged in melee | Yes | No while Aerial is active |

Jet Propulsion lets the Garuda rise diagonally or vertically, obtain LoF, shoot and return to its original protected landing position within one Order. With 10 inches of total Jump path, a straight up-and-down path can theoretically rise 5 inches before returning, subject to legal geometry. It can instead land on a rooftop, cross a wall or change direction to find a new angle. Jump + Move can cover up to 12 inches in total (10 + 2) where legal. Ordinary Move + Move covers 10 inches.

Important limitations: declare the whole trajectory, account for AROs along it, and identify a legal landing surface. No Partial Cover during an Order declaring Jump, even if returning behind terrain. Returning to total cover protects against subsequent activations, not the AROs generated by the current Order. Jump cancels Prone; use a later eligible movement to go Prone again. The small base and access to normal cover on other Orders distinguish Garuda from many larger flying pieces. It does not gain the Aerial immunity to engagement or Boost weapons.

## FO — 20/0: exceptional objective access

**The default Garuda profile. It excels at reaching objectives rather than holding them.**

For just two points more than the shotgun:
- WIP13 specialist with a Burst 3 Combi Rifle (+3 at 0–16 inches), Flash Pulse and Forward Observer.
- Deploy from a suitable flank after seeing how the game develops; use Combat Jump for exceptional entry opportunities.
- Jump terrain rather than navigating the same lanes as conventional infantry; use Jump + objective interaction where the mission permits and the landing position satisfies the interaction requirements.
- Use normal cover and Prone after scoring; Suppressive Fire is another defensive option if appropriate.
- Can still attack exposed support troops, and Forward Observer can support Targeted-based plans.

The key distinction from the Pathfinder is not WIP or raw movement, but flexibility in choosing the entry angle and bypassing intervening terrain. It is particularly attractive in TAG-heavy lists, objective-heavy missions and late-turn scoring plans. Do not delay deployment automatically: the off-table Order is not in the shared pool, and a late Garuda may have too few Orders or legal entrances to finish its job.

## Boarding Shotgun — 18/0: cheap offensive disruption

- BS18 before other MODs in the +6 band (0–8 inches), Burst 2, AP ammunition, PS6.
- Chain-colt provides a direct template option against high-Mimetism targets or clustered defenders.
- Useful for attacking exposed cheerleaders, hackers, vulnerable command pieces and Fireteam members from the flank.
- Cheapest profile; reasonable expendable threat if the exchange benefits the list.
- Not a specialist, and the shotgun's range and Burst are limiting for extended attack runs.

Choose this over the FO when the Garuda's job is explicitly close assault, when AP/template utility matters, or when every point counts.

## Spitfire — 25/1.5: do not dismiss on SWC alone

**A-tier when the list has spare SWC and wants a genuine airborne gunfighter.** Burst 4, PS6 and +3 range from over 8 to 24 inches let the Garuda attack from much farther away than the shotgun, while maintaining better sustained shooting than the FO. Jet Propulsion provides vertical firing angles, allowing attacks on exposed rooftops and defenders without committing to close range. It is still BS12, Mimetism (-3), ARM0 and one STR, so select favourable engagements rather than challenging apex AROs head-on.

NCA has an unusually accessible SWC-expansion package:
- Aquila FTO Lieutenant with Strategos L1: 52 pts, +1 SWC.
- Agent Maximus FTO Lieutenant with Inspiring Leadership: 47 pts, +1 SWC.
- Hannibal MULTI Marksman Rifle with Strategic Deployment: 32 pts, +1 SWC.

A 300-point list has a baseline of 6 SWC. Combining **either** bonus Lieutenant (not both) with the +1 SWC Hannibal produces 8 SWC. Hannibal can also increase the budget to 7 with an ordinary Lieutenant. The bonus profiles have zero actual SWC cost and increase the allowance.

This does not make bonus SWC free in points: Hannibal and the premium Lieutenant must justify their places in the list, and their weapon/command packages should have independent value. Do not buy them just to fund the Garuda. However, in a list already taking those pieces, the 1.5 SWC is a much weaker objection than in a normal 6-SWC army. Even at 6 SWC it can be affordable with zero-SWC Black Friar Thunderbolt, Sierra Thunderbolt and other inexpensive utility pieces.

The relevant tradeoff is whether you want a **dedicated sustained airborne attacker** or a **20-point airborne specialist**. The Spitfire costs five more points and gives up FO/Flash Pulse, but adds a meaningful gun upgrade. It is not redundant with the Bulleteer merely because both use a Spitfire: their access to enemy positions is different.

## Airborne deployment and timing

- Prefer Parachutist when there is a safe legal table-edge entry outside the enemy DZ. No PH roll; the Garuda's subsequent speed and Jump can cover the remaining distance quickly.
- Combat Jump offers much greater placement flexibility including inside the enemy DZ, but PH11 means a 55% unmodified success chance. Friendly Controlled Jump alone raises this to 70%; enemy Controlled Jump alone lowers it to 40%; both cancel. Failure places the Garuda at the edge within its own DZ. No Partial Cover during Combat Jump; account for landing AROs.
- Neither deployment skill places the model directly in contact with a mission objective; budget an additional activation to approach and interact. Deployment is a Long Skill, not deployment plus shooting.
- While held off-table its presence is private and it does not contribute its Regular Order to the shared pool. It can spend its own Order to enter; remaining Orders for the attack or mission come from its group.
- Do not buy a 14-point/0.5 SWC EVO solely to enable one Garuda. Consider it if the rest of the REM/TAG architecture benefits from its supportware too.
- Deploy normally if immediate shared Order contribution and early Jump-based pressure are more important than airborne flexibility.

## Comparison and synergy

| Alternative | Pts / SWC | Prefer when |
|---|---|---|
| Pathfinder TacAware | 17/0 | Sensor, Repeater, self-funded utility, Fireteam access |
| Bulleteer Spitfire | 22/1 | Mimetism (-6), Repeater, linked conventional attack |
| Locust KHD | 27/0 | Hidden forward specialist, Pitcher, Firewall |
| Blade-Ops airborne Engineer | 30/0 | Airborne Engineering/D-Charges, Decoy |
| Redeye | Profile-dependent | BS14, Mimetism (-6), premium mobile exploitation |
| Swiss KHD | 47/0 | Durable, protected independent objective holder |

A Garuda complements the premium NCA attack pieces by forcing opponents to defend more than the obvious frontline. Swiss Guard, Uhlan or Squalos opens the long-range lanes; Garuda exploits a flank or vertical angle, attacks support units or reaches an objective. Unlike a Redeye or Bulleteer starting on the table, it does not commit to one initial approach. Pair with a resilient specialist or defensive screen if the mission requires holding the objective afterward.

## Weaknesses

- ARM0 and one STR: mines, templates and ordinary failed saves can remove it quickly.
- Hackable REM, BTS3, no Stealth or native Firewall; beware enemy Repeater coverage.
- No extra activation economy or Fireteam access; needs enough pooled Orders after arrival.
- Screened flanks, dense ARO coverage and limited landing surfaces can reduce its options.
- Remote Presence permits recovery, but an Engineer may not be able to reach a deep airborne attacker.
- Mimetism (-3) is useful, not apex protection; exploit geometry instead of relying on winning difficult face-to-face rolls.

**Bottom line:** FO first for unusually cheap and flexible mission access; Boarding Shotgun for close-range disruption; Spitfire for sustained airborne firepower, especially when NCA's +1/+2 SWC architecture already exists. All three exploit essentially vehicle-class jump geometry on an S2 REM with conventional cover and objective-contact privileges.
