# Neoterra Bolts — Neoterran Capitaline Army (N5.3)

**High priority: MSV1 MULTI Sniper (26/1.5), Killer Hacker (22/0), and Chain of Command (26/0) when needed.** The sniper is a must-own, not a must-field. Prefer a compact defensive sniper/CoC Duo and/or a KHD supporting another primary attacker. Do not automatically build a five-member Bolt Core or purchase ordinary Bolts as filler.

## Why Bolts are unusually reliable shooters

- **Marksmanship:** ignores the shooting penalty from Partial Cover and Nanoscreen; it does not remove the target's Saving Roll bonus.
- **Warhorse:** enemy BS Attack (-X), including BS Attack (-3), has no effect against the Bolt. Also prevents Isolated State and preserves Regular status during Loss of Lieutenant and Retreat. This is an offensive MOD advantage as well as command/E/M resilience, not immunity to all negative MODs or E/M effects.
- **MSV1 (sniper only):** cancels Mimetism (-3), reduces Mimetism (-6) to (-3), and sees through ordinary Smoke/Zero Visibility at -6. It does not defeat Eclipse. FT5 Sixth Sense can cancel the Zero Visibility penalty when targeted by a BS Attack through it, per FAQ.
- **BTS6:** strong protection against relevant BTS-targeting ammunition/programs; non-Hacker Bolts are not ordinarily Hackable merely for being MI. Hacker Bolts are vulnerable to anti-hacker programs. Warhorse does not protect against Trinity damage.
- **Immunity (Shock):** retains ordinary recovery opportunities. Does not confer a second Wound or NWI.

For a BS Attack in positive range (+3) against Partial Cover, excluding other MODs:

| Enemy | Ordinary Bolt | MSV1 Bolt sniper | Sniper FT4+ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| No Mimetism | 16 | 16 | 17 +1SD |
| Mimetism (-3) | 13 | 16 | 17 +1SD |
| Mimetism (-6) | 10 | 13 | 14 +1SD |

Enemy BS Attack (-3) does not change these numbers. FT2–3 adds +1SD without changing the target number. The Special Die is an extra roll followed by discarding one die, not an extra actual hit/Burst. Both Marksmanship and MSV are affected by White Noise, Albedo and Reflective; ordinary Bolts are not exempt simply because they lack MSV. A Bolt still has only ARM2, W1 and no defensive Mimetism.

## MSV1 MULTI Sniper — default precision ARO, not mandatory

For 26/1.5, this is one of NCA's strongest conventional long-range AROs against opponents stacking visual and shooting MODs. DA ammunition can threaten expensive targets, and the rifle remains useful as a secondary active-turn gun. The NCA Fireteam notes record tournament evidence of a linked Bolt sniper destroying a Squalos in two Orders; this illustrates its potential, not guaranteed performance.

| Alternative | Pts / SWC | Why choose it instead |
|---|---:|---|
| Bolt Missile Launcher | 24 / 1.5 | EXP and impact-template punishment against clustered/heavy targets; no MSV |
| Sierra HMG | 23 / 1 | Total Reaction B4, 360-degree coverage and Remote Presence |
| Sierra Thunderbolt | 18 / 0 | Cheap zero-SWC reactive coverage |
| Black Friar HRL | 22 / 1.5 | MSV2, Albedo (-6), impact template |
| Black Friar Thunderbolt | 24 / 0 | MSV2 and Albedo, especially valuable as an active anti-tech shooter |
| Swiss Guard ML | 60 / 1.5 | Hidden Deployment, Mimetism (-6), elite resilient surprise ARO |
| Uhlan Feuerbach | 70 / 1.5 | Concealed TAG with credible reactive and active use |
| Fugazi | 7 / 0 | Inexpensive disposable Flash Pulse/Repeater coverage |

Take the sniper when the rest of the list needs a dedicated long-lane defender, especially with a Swiss HMG, offensive TAG or active infantry team. Skip it if concealed AROs, Uhlan Feuerbach, Sierra REMs, or a cheaper layered defense already fulfill the role, or the mission favors aggression over static defense. It should tax enemy Orders rather than be expected to survive every engagement. Avoid exposing it to multiple angles or a superior high-Burst attacker when no meaningful lane is being protected.

### Preferred defensive Duo: Sniper + Bolt CoC

| Member | Pts / SWC |
|---|---:|
| Bolt MSV1 MULTI Sniper | 26 / 1.5 |
| Bolt CoC (Combi Rifle, Light Shotgun, Drop Bears) | 26 / 0 |
| **Total** | **52 / 1.5** |

**Neoterra Duo, FT2, two Regular Orders while operational.** Both count as native Bolts; the sniper gains +1SD in active and reactive shooting rolls. Does not consume Core or Haris.

- Keep the CoC hidden in Total Cover and within coherency; do not expose it to the same lane as the sniper or Lieutenant.
- CoC protects command, deploys Drop Bears along approaches, and supplies a close-range Light Shotgun template against flankers, airborne attackers and skirmishers.
- Drop Bears are placed/thrown during play, not deployed before the game by a Minelayer skill. They can protect the sniper's flank or retreat route.
- The CoC is particularly good value if command insurance was already required for an exposed Aquila/Maximus or another aggressive Lieutenant. If CoC is unnecessary, consider the sniper solo, Bolt BSG/Drop Bears (45/1.5 Duo), or Bolt KHD (48/1.5 Duo) if its own functions will actually be used. The KHD generally prefers a mobile team.
- Quinn CoC (24/0) also counts as Bolt and can form a 50/1.5 sniper Duo. He offers WIP13, MSV1, Immunity (ARM), Warhorse, SMG and E/Mitter, but lacks Drop Bears, Light Shotgun and BTS6. The Bolt is preferred for the specific defensive perimeter role; Quinn is an excellent alternative elsewhere. Neither CoC profile carries Disco Baller.

No need to expand this Duo into a Core unless each extra member justifies its independent value.

## KHD — preferred offensive Bolt

**22 points, zero SWC** for a BSG (+1B), Marksmanship, Warhorse, BTS6, E/M Mine, KHD and Hacker specialist access. This is not primarily a long-range attacker: it accompanies a stronger gunfighter and supplies close-range shooting, enemy-Hacker removal through friendly Repeaters, anti-heavy deployable control and objective scoring. Its WIP12 is ordinary, so the Deva KHD can be preferable for pure cyber duels. It does not grant Firewall to the team; protecting a HI/TAG from hacking still requires proper network management, removal of enemy Hackers and/or a suitable shared TinBot.

Prefer another main offensive piece (Hannibal, ORC, Aquila FTO, Maximus Agent, etc.) to open lanes while the Bolt handles short-range and specialist tasks. A KHD is not a replacement for a standard HD's anti-HI/TAG programs. With a Blocker Pitcher, Pathfinder or other network infrastructure, it can contest enemy Hackers without exposing itself directly. A solo or independent KHD can work, but the Bolt's combined capabilities and native Neoterra anchor are particularly attractive in a mobile link.

### HD and Paramedic comparison

| Feature | Bolt KHD | Bolt HD | Bolt Paramedic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pts / SWC | 22 / 0 | 24 / 0.5 | 23 / 0 |
| Main weapon | BSG (+1B) | BSG (+1B) | Combi, Light Shotgun |
| Deployable | E/M Mine | E/M Mine | Drop Bears |
| Specialist | Hacker | Hacker | Paramedic |
| Main utility | Anti-Hacker pressure | Standard hacking, anti-heavy control | MediKit recovery |
| BTS6 / network synergy | Yes | Yes | No comparable synergy |

Prefer KHD if standard HD coverage already exists. Prefer HD if the team or army needs conventional hacking programs. Both can provide useful actions and reactive threats throughout a game. The Paramedic has value for recovering Shock-immune infantry and mission-specific classifieds, but is generally less attractive: recovery requires an accessible casualty and successful MediKit resolution. A cheap independent Trauma-Doc can cover recovery without using an expensive Bolt slot. FT2 +1SD applies to ranged MediKit rolls, but that alone does not justify the Paramedic.

## Other profiles — generally optional

- **Spitfire 25/1.5:** BS13, B4, Marksmanship and Warhorse make a good budget attacker, especially in a linked team. However, NCA has plentiful stronger or more resilient dedicated guns: Swiss, Uhlan/Squalos, ORC HMG, Maximus Agent, Black Friar Thunderbolt (24/0), Hannibal MULTI Marksman (29/0), Bulleteer Spitfire (22/1). Do not take merely because it is a Bolt.
- **BSG 19/0:** strong dedicated close-range/mine defense with native +1B, but Blade BSG FTO (20/0) offers Mimetism (-3), Stealth and Tactical Awareness for one more point; Hannibal Vulkan (23/0) offers NWI, Sensor, +1SD and Tactical Awareness. The KHD adds specialist/cyber utility for three points and trades Drop Bears for E/M Mines. Prefer these alternatives for mobile teams; ordinary BSG still wins when the actual requirement is conventional mine coverage.
- **ML 24/1.5:** worthwhile when the EXP/impact-template threat is specifically needed. The MSV1 sniper is the usual preference for flexible long-range precision.
- **Basic Combi 21/0:** too expensive for ordinary filler; choose a dedicated profile or a Counts-as alternative.
- **Lieutenant 21/0:** cheaper Fusilier/Auxilia LT packages or more productive premium LTs generally win; Warhorse does not prevent Loss of Lieutenant for the rest of the army if this LT dies.

## Legal Fireteam architecture and examples

Neoterra Core/Haris/Duo requires at least one actual Bolt or ORC. Counts-as Bolts include Machinist NCA, eligible Blade FTO (max 2), Quinn, eligible Uma FTO and Hannibal. Blockers can join but do not count as Bolt or ORC. Agent Maximus counts as ORC; Aquila only via eligible FTO profiles. One Core, one Haris, unlimited Duos. No native Bolt required in an ORC-anchored team.

| Bolt KHD 22 + Blade Engineer 23 | 45 / 0 | 2 | Compact specialist Duo, one TacAware |

| Maximus Agent FTO 45 + Bolt KHD 22 + Blade Engineer 23 | 90 / 1 | 2 | Premium midrange/close assault, two TacAware, network |
| Hannibal MULTI Marksman 29 + Bolt KHD 22 + Blocker Pitcher 15 | 66 / 0 | 2 | Mobile network projection/anti-hacker team; HD elsewhere |
| Bolt Sniper 26 + Bolt KHD 22 + Blade BSG 20 + Blade Engineer 23 + Hannibal Vulkan 23 | 114 / 1.5 | 5 | Optional full-composition Core, three TacAware |

Constructed alternatives verified against the pinned roster and chart; not asserted to be played lists. FT levels reflect matching composition rather than headcount. Tactical Awareness Orders are personal; the holder must lead when activating the team, making them particularly useful for movement and their own relevant actions.

FT2 provides the most economical shooting improvement (+1SD); FT3 adds Discover/Dodge bonuses, FT4 adds +1 BS Attack attribute, FT5 adds Sixth Sense. Sixth Sense is useful on a Hacker to counter Stealth in its Hacking Area, but not a sufficient reason to purchase a five-model Core by itself. A large static Core costs points and may strand useful mobile members. The canonical roster's 108/1.5 four-Bolt + Machinist FT5 defensive Core remains a legal alternative when deliberately building a full defensive package; it is not the default recommendation here.

## Final listbuilding and collection recommendation

- **Defensive list:** one MSV1 sniper if a precision long-range ARO is needed; add Bolt CoC when using a high-risk LT and wanting mine/close-range protection.
- **Offensive list:** one Bolt KHD in a mobile team with another main gunfighter; substitute/add standard HD if required.
- **Combined architecture:** sniper + CoC defensive Duo and KHD in a separate offensive team = 74 points / 1.5 SWC for the three Bolts, leaving both Core and Haris slots available before assigning the KHD.
- **Collection:** prioritize the sniper and BSG Hacker representation; add a Combi/Light Shotgun CoC representation for the defensive package. Three models cover the preferred roles, not a mandatory three-model inclusion. Spitfire/ML and further ordinary bodies are optional.

**Bottom line:** use Bolts for their distinctive Marksmanship/Warhorse gunfighting, MSV1 sniper, CoC perimeter defense and BTS6 hacking support. Do not pay for generic Bolt filler or a Paramedic by default. The sniper is a must-own, not a must-field; the KHD is the preferred offensive Bolt.
