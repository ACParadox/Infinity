# Black Friar — Neoterran Capitaline Army (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A overall; Thunderbolt (+1B) 24/0 is the clear default (A+ as an active anti-tech counter). Usually skip the HRL and MULTI Rifle.** The Friar's defining advantages are matchup-dependent, so use it to actively select favorable engagements rather than relying on the opponent to expose a vulnerable gunfighter to its ARO. Excellent secondary gun for a TAG- or Swiss Guard-centric list; not mandatory.

## Why active turn is the better use

- **MSV2** cancels Mimetism, including -6, and permits shooting through ordinary Smoke under the visor rules (not Eclipse).
- **Albedo (-6)** penalizes enemy MSV or Marksmanship users when they declare LoF-requiring BS Attack or Discover against the Friar. It is not general Mimetism and does not penalize ordinary shooters.
- **Combat Instinct** ignores Surprise Attack MODs and prevents Stealth from denying otherwise eligible AROs. It does not grant extra LoF or the full benefits of Sixth Sense.

**Active turn: you choose the engagement.** Seek mimetic defenders, visor-equipped shooters and Marksmanship gunfighters (including Assisted Fire REMs). Against a mimetic MSV shooter, the Friar strips its Mimetism while imposing Albedo on its return fire. Move into the favorable range and firing angle, clear the specific obstacle and then return the main gunfighter to work.

**Reactive turn: the opponent chooses the engagement.** A capable opponent can avoid exposing vulnerable MSV/Mimetism troops, choose a normal heavy gun, attack from a different angle or bypass the lane. The Friar then becomes a BS13, ARM2, one-Wound model without a general defensive shooting MOD. Albedo/MSV2 still have deterrence value, but an ARO role leaves much of their potential dependent on the opponent's decisions.

This is the central reason to prefer Thunderbolt over HRL, even before the SWC saving. Use the Friar as a mobile, matchup-specific tool rather than a generic static ARO.

## Thunderbolt (+1B) — 24/0: first choice

Normal (not AP) Thunderbolt: B3 active, B1 reactive, PS6, +3 at 16–32 inches and flat at 32–48 inches. FT2 +1 Success Die improves the shooting roll without increasing actual Burst. Nanopulser covers close-range template engagements.

- No SWC expenditure: preserves SWC for TAGs, Swiss Guard and conventional defensive weapons.
- Affordable alternative to Aquila when a list already has a premium main attacker.
- Strong second-stage attacker, particularly against enemy Mimetism (-6), MSV and Marksmanship.
- Not a dedicated anti-armor gun; do not force repeated fights with conventional elite B4–5 shooters.

|---|---:|---|
| Deva MSV2 Spitfire | 29/1 | Friar is cheaper, saves SWC, has BS13 instead of 12, Albedo and broader FT access; Deva offers B4 and NWI |

## Heavy Rocket Launcher — 22/1.5: usually skip

MSV2 + Albedo with a Continuous Damage impact template can deter specific enemy shooters, but the role is reactive and consumes 1.5 SWC. The Friar only saves two points compared with its Thunderbolt profile and loses active Burst and control over its ideal matchups.

NCA already has strong general-purpose defensive options:

|---|---:|---|

Take the HRL only when the board, expected matchup or a specific defensive link justifies a specialized anti-visual ARO and SWC is available. Its impact template and Albedo deterrence are real, but do not make it the default Friar.

## MULTI Rifle + Biometric Visor — 27/0: usually skip

Biometric Visor and Discover (+3) are useful against IMP-1; Drop Bears and Nanopulser add local defense. Unlike MO, however, NCA has a 15/0 Blocker Pitcher Specialist with Biometric Visor, Sensor and Discover (ReRoll), plus hacking-network projection and scoring. The Friar's stronger direct combat and Drop Bears are distinct, but rarely justify buying a 27pt non-specialist for this role. Auxilia, Peacemakers and Bolts also cover local route defense. Default to the Blocker for anti-IMP utility and the Friar Thunderbolt for active anti-tech shooting.

## Listbuilding conclusion

- **Primary attack:** TAG, Swiss Guard, Aquila or another heavy gunfighter.
- **Secondary attack / visual-tech counter:** Thunderbolt Black Friar; engage when it has the right matchup, not simply because it is available.
- **Generic ARO:** Bolt Missile Launcher, Bolt MSV1 Sniper or Sierra.
- **Anti-Impersonation / network support:** Blocker.
- **Quantity:** 0–1. Default to Thunderbolt when taking a Friar; skip the other profiles in most lists.

The Friar is a 24-point, zero-SWC insurance policy against difficult visual-tech targets. Its value is maximized by actively finding favorable engagements, not hoping an opponent moves the right unit into its ARO.

### Black Friars — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A overall; A+ anti-visual-tech counter in its ideal matchups. In most MO lists, favor the 24pt/0SWC Thunderbolt (+1B) over the same-cost 24pt/1SWC Crosier MSV2 HMG.** Black Friars are **not mandatory**, but the 24pt/0SWC **Thunderbolt (+1B)** and 22pt/1.5SWC **Heavy Rocket Launcher** are particularly efficient ways to add **MSV2 + Albedo (-6) + Combat Instinct** to Military Orders. The 27pt MULTI Rifle/Biometric/Drop Bears specialist is genuinely useful against Impersonation, but usually a matchup pick rather than the default. Most lists should consider **0–1 Friar**, not automatically buy two.

#### Why MSV2 + Albedo is unusual and good

These abilities counter different pieces of the enemy's plan:

- **MSV2:** Negates Mimetism MODs, including Mimetism (-6), and can draw LoF through ordinary Smoke/Zero Visibility as prescribed by the MSV rule. **Do not** treat it as permission to see through **Eclipse**.
- **Albedo (-6):** A foe with **MSV or Marksmanship** takes **-6 to its Attribute** when it declares a **LoF-requiring BS Attack or Discover** against the Friar. It does *not* give the Friar general Mimetism; it does not affect ordinary shooters without those abilities, non-LoF attacks or CC Attacks. This distinction is vital.
- **Combat Instinct:** Ignores Surprise Attack MODs and stops enemy Stealth suppressing eligible AROs within the Friar's Zone of Control. **It does not give 360º LoF** or make shots through walls possible.

An opposing **MSV + Mimetism (-6)** marksman therefore loses its normal Mimetism protection against the Friar and suffers **Albedo (-6)** while shooting back. The Friar is an effective **tech-counter**, rather than simply a cheap generic shooter. Assisted-Fire **Marksmanship** can also expose an otherwise normal gunfighter to Albedo's penalty.

**Limit:** A plain BS15/B4–5 heavy gun without MSV/Marksmanship is not penalized by Albedo. The Friar is still W1/ARM2 with no native Mimetism, so don't repeatedly fight raw elite gunfighters head-on or assume MSV2 supplies durability.

#### Profile 2: Thunderbolt (+1B) — flexible first choice (24/0)

**Best for:** economical, zero-SWC **active-turn counterfire**, back-up gunfighting, or a moving Teutonic Fireteam.

The normal Thunderbolt is **B2, PS6, N ammo, ARM save**, with its preferred **+3 band at 16–32 inches** and no range MOD at 32–48 inches. This loadout's **+1B** makes it **B3 in the Active Turn** (normal ARO Burst remains 1). N5.3 also has a **different AP Thunderbolt** weapon, but the Black Friar roster explicitly lists **Thunderbolt**, not AP Thunderbolt: do not accidentally grant this unit AP.

**Why it works:**
- MSV2 opens Mimetism/camouflage matchups that many Knights and TAGs dislike.
- Albedo heavily penalizes the right enemy MSV/Marksmanship shooters.
- B3 BS13 at **0 SWC** is respectable for a 24pt specialist counter-gun.
- Nanopulser provides a short-range Direct Template threat; Combat Instinct discourages Surprise/Stealth ambushes.
- In a valid **FT2**, **BS Attack (+1SD)** means **roll B3+1 die, discard one**; it is *not* B4 for hit allocation or ammunition use.

**When not to bring:** If the only missing job is **anti-ARM/AP**, this non-AP Thunderbolt is not the solution. If the army already has MSV2/anti-Mimetism coverage and SWC is available for a more decisive heavy gun, 24 points may be better spent on another specialist, midfield obstacle, or Orders.

**Deployment/use:** A second-stage gun: let the TAG/HMG open the obvious long-range lane, then bring the Friar into a favorable 16–32-inch engagement with mimetic, visor-equipped or otherwise exposed troops. After fighting, avoid leaving the one-Wound Friar in a plain gunfight ARO.

#### Direct comparison: 24pt/0SWC Black Friar Thunderbolt vs 24pt/1SWC Crosier MSV2 HMG

**Default MO recommendation: Black Friar Thunderbolt (+1B) > Crosier MSV2 HMG for most lists.** They cost **exactly the same 24 points** and both have MSV2 and access to legal Military Orders Fireteams. Choosing the Crosier solely because it has an HMG overlooks the Friar's much stronger overall cost/tech package. The Crosier is not an automatic must-take or required Core filler; MO can build legal Core/Haris combinations around **Teutonics** or **Santiago FTO**, and a dedicated Order Sergeants Duo/Haris does not need any Crosier.

| Dimension | Black Friar Thunderbolt (+1B) | Crosier MSV2 HMG | Practical edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pts / SWC | **24 / 0** | **24 / 1** | **Friar saves 1 SWC**, very relevant with TAG, missile, or linked Knight gun |
| BS / weapon PS | **BS13 / PS6** | BS12 / PS5 | **Friar** |
| Active weapon Burst | B3 (native Thunderbolt B2 +1B) | **B4** | **Crosier**, especially into ordinary gunfighters |
| Reactive weapon Burst | B1 | B1 | Tie |
| 16–32" | +3 | +3 | Tie |
| 32–48" | **0** | -3 | **Friar**, which has *at least as useful* long range, not shorter range |
| MSV | MSV2 | MSV2 | Tie |
| Protection | **ARM2, BTS3, Albedo (-6), Combat Instinct** | ARM1, BTS0; no Albedo/Combat Instinct | **Friar**, especially versus MSV/Marksmanship opponents |
| Emergency tool | **Nanopulser** | None beyond pistol | Friar |
| Link families | Military Orders Core/Haris/Duo **and Order Sergeants Haris/Duo** | Military Orders Core/Haris/Duo | Friar for **join flexibility** |
| FT level contribution | **Not** a Crosier or Order Sergeant Counts-as | **Native Crosier** | Crosier for economical FT2/FT4/FT5 |
| Generic anti-ARM | **Normal Thunderbolt, not AP** | **Normal HMG, not AP** | Neither is a dedicated armored-target killer |

**Albedo caveat:** The -6 applies to the specified **MSV/Marksmanship** enemy BS Attack/Discover interactions that require LoF, **not** to every enemy gun. Even without Albedo triggering, the Friar retains higher BS, higher PS, slightly better ARM/BTS, Nanopulser, wider range utility and a whole-SWC saving. In a straight active-turn duel against an ordinary, non-MSV shooter, however, **Crosier B4** can outweigh those advantages by winning more Face-to-Face exchanges and causing more separate hits; there is no universal damage win rate without specifying target BS, ARM, cover and range.

**Fireteam rules matter as much as profile stats:**
- **Crosier HMG24 + Order Sergeant FTO Hacker/Pitcher18** is a **42pt / 1.5SWC Military Orders Duo at FT2**, because FTO Order Sergeants count as Crosiers *in this row*. The HMG gets **B4 +1 Success Die** (roll 5, discard 1), and the Hacker's eligible BS attacks can use FT2 as leader.
- **Black Friar TB24 + Order Sergeant FTO Hacker18** is a **42pt / 0.5SWC Order Sergeants Duo at FT1** (not a legal two-body Military Orders Duo on its own because neither model is a native required-choice member): **not FT2**, because the Friar does *not* count as a Sergeant or Crosier. The Friar keeps its natural BS13/B3/PS6/Albedo and saves 1 SWC; the Hacker loses the FT2 bonus in the two-body setup.
- **Black Friar TB24 + two Teutonic Knights18+19** is a **61pt / 0SWC Military Orders Haris at FT2**; the Friar benefits from FT2 **+1SD**, while the Knights provide two-Wound close assault, Panzerfaust threats and a Specialist on the 19pt profile. This occupies the only Haris, so the rest of the list must benefit from that assignment.
- **Black Friar TB24 + three basic Crosiers33 + Knight Commander CoC23** is an **80pt / 0SWC Military Orders Core at FT4**: Friar gun at **BS14, B3+1SD, PS6**, but **no FT5/Sixth Sense**. Replace Friar with **Crosier HMG24** for the same **80pts / 1SWC**, reaching **FT5** and an HMG at **BS13, B4+1SD, PS5**. This is the clearest real linked reason to prefer the Crosier.

**MO roster implications:** Unless the HMG's extra B and native Crosier FT equivalence are central to the list, buy the **Friar Thunderbolt** as the default economical MSV2 answer. The Crosier's narrower remaining jobs are (1) cheap **11pt Regular** or **12pt Blitzen** order/ARO filler; (2) **13pt Paramedic** specialist and native required-choice/FT-level filler; and (3) a deliberately built **FT2 HMG Duo or FT5 Core**. A Crosier Core is **optional**; do not consume the Core slot just because Crosiers have AVA Total. Cheap **7pt Fugazi, 8pt Mulebot, 9pt Tech-Bee, 12pt Raveneye** can supply more economical orders or different useful jobs within their AVA/availability constraints.

**Ranking:** **Black Friar Thunderbolt A+/first choice** as general MSV2 utility; **Crosier HMG A in a planned FT2/FT5 shooting team, B+ standalone**. Keeping both is justifiable if you need separate active-visor coverage or genuinely leverage a Fireteam; otherwise prefer the Friar's no-SWC flexibility.

#### Profile 4: Heavy Rocket Launcher — premier reactive choice (22/1.5)

**Best for:** economical **MSV2/Albedo ARO**, defending an access lane, and threatening grouped troops with a **Continuous Damage circular impact template**.

At **22 pts** it is only seven points above the **15/1.5 Order Sergeant FTO HRL**, while gaining **BS13 (rather than 12), MSV2, Albedo and Combat Instinct**. Against a relevant enemy tech profile those seven points are highly meaningful. Against ordinary non-MSVisor shooters, the Sergeant often supplies the same core template/Continuous Damage function more cheaply.

The standard HRL has **B2 in Active**, **B1 in ARO**, with **+3 at 16–32 inches**; its template or hit modes require the relevant attack roll. **FT2 +1SD applies to that shooting roll**, including the reactive turn. It **does not increase ARO Burst to 2** and does not add damage to a Direct Template Nanopulser attack.

**Strengths:** real visual-tech deterrence plus an impact template, useful even when the main assault is elsewhere. Albedo affects incoming LoF-based MSV/Marksmanship shots at the same time MSV2 bypasses the target's Mimetism. An opponent may be forced to change angles, use a non-visor gun, attack from outside LoF, or spend multiple Orders removing/avoiding the lane.

**Weaknesses:** 1.5 SWC competes with a TAG, Hospitaller/Justice heavy gun, linked Order Sergeant HRL, etc.; W1 means a single lost face-to-face roll can end the investment. It is not a 360º Total Reaction piece, and the attack-template benefit disappears when the attacker can avoid the chosen line.

**Default comparison:**
- **Order Sergeant FTO HRL 15/1.5:** better minimal-price Fireteam ARO; worse against Mimetism, MSV and Surprise.
- **Escort Sergeant Neurocinetics HRL + Auxbot 22/1.5:** independent **reactive B2 HRL** plus close Heavy Flamethrower bodyguard; lacks MSV2/Albedo and cannot join a Fireteam.
- **Sierra TR Thunderbolt 18/0:** independent 360º total-reaction, repairable REM; different weapon, enemy-tech interaction and table geometry.
- **Crosier MSV2 HMG 24/1:** stronger **active Burst/range** and potential FT5 in a Crosier Core; no Albedo and not an impact-template HRL.

**When not to bring:** SWC already committed, opposing list has little Mimetism/MSV/Marksmanship, your reactive lanes are too short/obstructed, or a cheaper defensive module already taxes enemy Orders adequately.

#### Profile 3: MULTI Rifle + Biometric + Drop Bears — anti-IMP niche (27/0)

**Best for:** a threatened local area with **IMP-1**, disguised attackers, or predictable short-range approaches.

- **Biometric Visor:** ignores IMP-1's **WIP -3** Discover penalty; on a successful Discover versus IMP-1, **cancels Impersonation outright**, not merely converting IMP-1 to IMP-2. The Biometric Visor's Surprise Attack protection partly **overlaps** the Friar's existing Combat Instinct.
- **Discover (+3):** WIP13 becomes **16 before other relevant modifiers**, synergizing strongly with Biometric.
- **Drop Bears:** can protect flanks/entry routes or add a short-range obstacle; they require positioning and are not an automatic turn-zero mine.
- **MULTI Rifle:** active **B3 AP/Shock** and an anti-materiel **DA B1** mode; valuable ammunition flexibility, but less reach than the Thunderbolt. The Nanopulser remains a close-range emergency template.

**What it costs:** **+3 points vs Thunderbolt**, but gives up its medium/long-range +3 band and the zero-SWC Thunderbolt's relatively low-commitment attack role. A **27pt W1 non-specialist** operating near dangerous IMP/CC attackers is at risk.

**Verdict:** Don't declare this profile unplayable: IMP-1 is an important matchup, and Discover 16 plus direct IMP removal has real value. But **default skip in blind lists**, unless the board/opponent makes the anti-Impersonation/Drop Bear package earn its points. Compare the same budget with a **Raveneye**, **Konstantinos Sensor/Disco**, **Pathfinder Sensor** or extra midfield specialist rather than assuming any of those is a direct drop-in replacement for Biometric.

#### Legal Fireteams and actual FT levels

**Pinned chart limitations:**
- **Military Orders Core/Haris/Duo:** allows **up to two Black Friars**; needs a required-choice **Crosier, Teutonic, or Santiago FTO** (or validated relevant equivalence).
- **Order Sergeants Haris/Duo:** allows **up to one Black Friar** and requires **at least one actual Order Sergeant FTO**.
- **Black Friars do not count as Crosiers or Order Sergeants.** An Order Sergeant + Friar Duo is **FT1**, *not* FT2; two Croisers + Friar form **FT2**, *not* FT3.
- There is only **one Haris** army-wide; building a defensive Haris excludes a second offensive Teutonic/Crusade/Order Sergeants Haris. No Black Friars in Crusade or Velox based on their dedicated rows.

N5.3 FT2 grants **BS Attack (+1 SD)**; FT3 grants **+3 Discover/+1 Dodge**; FT4 grants **+1 BS**; FT5 grants **Sixth Sense**. FT level measures qualifying **same-unit/counts-as** models, **not** total model count.

**The strongest case for a Friar in Fireteam is when it adds anti-tech coverage to a team you already wanted**, rather than purchasing a full team solely to activate an inexpensive 22–24pt shooter. **The Thunderbolt is the default 24pt MSV2 buy over the Crosier HMG**: save 1 SWC and gain PS6, Albedo, Combat Instinct, stronger ARM/BTS and wider Fireteam access, unless the Crosier's B4 and native FT-level contribution specifically matter. The MSV2/Albedo package also works **solo**, so a Fireteam is not mandatory.

##### Tactical combinations worth testing

1. **Cheap defensive Haris:** 2 Crosiers + HRL Friar. Place the Friar on a useful long lane with cover and keep Crosiers in positions where they do not expose themselves to the same attack. Excellent cost for 3 Regular Orders, but the single Haris slot is the opportunity cost.
2. **Second-wave Teutonic assault:** two inexpensive Teutonics + **Thunderbolt Friar**. Use Panzerfaust against armor, Friar against Mimetism/MSV, Teutonic Specialist for scoring. Don't force the W1 Friar to lead every approach or consume all your attack Orders.
3. **Defensive Core with commander:** three or four Crosier-equivalent bodies and HRL Friar. Gain FT4 if four qualifying Crosier-equivalent members exist; **never claim FT5** while the fifth member is a Black Friar. A Crosier MSV2 HMG Core instead is attractive when maximum Fireteam bonuses and active Burst matter more than Albedo.
4. **Order Sergeant utility Haris:** FTO Hacker + FTO HRL/Curator/Infirmarer + Thunderbolt Friar. The Friar supplies the anti-visual gun, while two Sergeant-equivalent bodies unlock FT2; if only one Sergeant-equivalent is included the link remains FT1. The purpose must justify surrendering the offensive Haris option.

#### Listbuilding guide — take, skip, deploy

**Take one Black Friar when:**
- Facing **Mimetism (-6)** defenders, MSV snipers, Marksmanship gunfighters or Smoke tricks (ordinary Smoke only), and you need an economical direct counter.
- Your **main attacker is a TAG/HMG** with a different target profile and you need a **0SWC backup**: favor Thunderbolt.
- You need a linkable **anti-visual HRL ARO** rather than an unmodified generic template launcher.
- You can make the opponent spend Orders **avoiding or removing** the Friar, without unnecessarily exposing it.

**Skip or deprioritize when:**
- You need **AP/DA anti-TAG** first, a fast objective runner, specialist buttons, a TinBot, or more cheap Regular Orders.
- Your threat is a normal high-BS B4–5 shooter **without MSV or Marksmanship**: Albedo contributes no defensive modifier.
- Your list is already rich in MSV2: a **Crosier MSV2 HMG**, other high-Burst anti-Mimetism solution and/or multiple premium ranged pieces cover the same need.
- SWC is scarce: HRL costs **1.5 SWC** even though it costs only 22 pts.
- An **Order Sergeants FT1 Friar Duo** was only added under a mistaken assumption that it boosts the Hacker's Pitcher; it does not.

**Quantity:** Usually **one** selected for a complementary role, or **zero** when the matchup/mission doesn't reward the counter-tech package. **Two** can be legal in a Military Orders Fireteam but is a deliberate skew with diminishing return and extra W1 non-specialist investment—not the default.

#### Practical ranking

| Role | First pick | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| General-purpose solo / secondary shooter | **Thunderbolt 24/0** | B3, 0SWC, long reach, MSV2 + Albedo |
| Defensive Core/Haris ARO | **HRL 22/1.5** | MSV2 + Albedo + Continuous Damage template, benefits from FT2 +1SD |
| Mobile 2 Teutonic Haris | **Thunderbolt 24/0** | Complements Panzerfaust and Specialist without consuming SWC |
| Counter MSV/Marksmanship gunfighters | **Thunderbolt for active; HRL for ARO** | Match actual range, mission and firing angle |
| Anti-IMP-1 / protected close approach | **MULTI Rifle/Biometric 27/0** | Discover (+3), Biometric cancellation, Drop Bears |
| Blind-list profile most likely to skip | **MULTI Rifle 27/0** | Specialist anti-IMP value does not routinely outweigh lost Thunderbolt reach |

**Bottom line:** The Black Friar is one of MO's better **cheap tech-counter pieces**, not an obligatory all-purpose shooter. **MSV2 + Albedo (-6) + Combat Instinct** rewards exploiting the opponent's visual tricks, while good Fireteam composition can supply **+1SD without pretending a mixed link reaches FT3/FT5**. Choose **Thunderbolt** for versatile active play, **HRL** for protective ARO, and **Biometric MULTI Rifle** only when its anti-IMP and Drop Bear jobs are specifically valuable.
