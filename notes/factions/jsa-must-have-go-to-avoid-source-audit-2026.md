# JSA / Ōban / Shindenbutai — Must-have, Go-to, and Avoid (N5.3, source-backed)

*Audit 23 October 2026. Three distinct rosters: [Vanilla JSA](../../sectorials/jsa/jsa.md), [Ōban](../../sectorials/jsa/oban.md), [Shindenbutai](../../sectorials/jsa/shindenbutai.md). Current pinned N5.3 Army data from Sept 2026 is authority for availability, exact profile costs, AVA and Fireteams. The following claims are **assessments grounded in directly reviewed YouTube and/or podcast MCP transcripts**, not statistical pick rates. An old interview's point cost or link assumption is NOT evidence of a current profile.*

## How to read the labels

- **Must-have / near-auto-include:** a creator expressly says "every list", "auto-include", "always", or several **actually played** builds repeatedly include it; when even one credible competing architecture omits the piece, call it "**near-must, not compulsory**" instead of inventing consensus.
- **Go-to:** source-reviewed as a repeatedly strong selection with a clear role that remains worthwhile even though an independent successful list can omit it. **Never imply all go-tos fit in one list.**
- **Never take / strong avoid:** commentator expressly rejects the **specific profile**, preferably for a direct substitute **in that same roster**, confirmed in current Army. **No faction has a defensible blanket "never take" list for every profile of an entire multi-option unit.** Where another interview disagrees, place it in **contested** rather than pretending an objective veto.
- **Specific source** is cited in the row (video/podcast, and approximate podcast minute when identifiable). These are opinions, not N5.3 legality. [Ōban transcript-reviewed tactica](oban.md), [Shindenbutai tactica](shindenbutai.md), and [Vanilla JSA event analysis](vanilla-jsa.md) contain deeper alternatives.
