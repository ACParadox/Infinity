### Shona Carano — Neoterran Capitaline Army (N5.3)

**Verdict: Strong specialist-role pick, not a universal must-field.** Shona is here primarily for **elite close combat**, a capability generally lacking in PanO and unusually affordable at **22 points / 0 SWC**. The main problem is delivering her into CC, not winning the melee once there. Prefer pairing her with **Richard Quinn's Disco Baller** or an Aquila FTO when an aggressive team needs Eclipse. Do not buy her merely as a close-range gun, Fireteam filler or static ARO.

#### Why take her: pure elite CC

- MA4 gives CC +3, opponent -3 in Face to Face rolls and +1 actual Burst in both turns. Against a target without other modifiers, Shona attacks at CC26 with B2, including in ARO. This is not the Fireteam BS Attack Special Die bonus.
- AP+EXP CCW forces three halved-ARM saves per ordinary successful hit (additional save for a critical). Two normal hits can force six saves, making her a serious threat to TAGs, HI and expensive conventional shooters. Immunities and enemy CC skills can change the matchup; Natural Born Warrior can negate Martial Arts.
- Additional eligible friendly participants in the same melee can add Burst under the multiple-trooper CC rules. A linked Shona must lead the relevant active CC Attack; avoid exposing other team members needlessly just to gain Burst.
- 6-2 MOV, Super-Jump, enhanced Dodge and Stealth help close distance or find alternative routes. NWI plus Shock Immunity provides two effective wounds against ordinary damage. Non-Hacker MI is not normally Hackable.
- Double active Nanopulser templates and the SMG give fallback attacks and close-range deterrence, but BS11 with no Mimetism is not a reason to select her over NCA's better shooters.

Her niche is distinct from cheap CSU perimeter defense, Blade-Ops order generation, Bolts' close shooting/mines, and Hannibal's shooting/Sensor. Maximus Agent (45/1 FTO) or TAG (80+ points) also offer formidable melee, but are far more expensive multi-role investments. If already using Maximus, evaluate whether additional dedicated CC is necessary.

#### Delivery is the limiting factor: Quinn Disco Baller

**Preferred enabler: Quinn MULTI Rifle / Disco Baller / FO (26/0).** NCA lacks the cheap Fennec Disco profile. Quinn adds WIP13 objective access, MSV1, close shooting and Fireteam composition as well as Eclipse. The two units cost 48/0 before adding a native anchor. Quinn's Chain of Command and MMR profiles do not carry Disco Baller; his AVA1 forces a choice.

##### CC inside Eclipse — legal and unaffected

CC Attack requires Silhouette contact with the enemy, not LoF. Eclipse blocks LoF even for MSV3 but does not prohibit CC or add a visibility MOD to CC rolls. Shona can move into Silhouette contact and declare CC Attack while both models are inside the Eclipse template.

Practical sequence:

1. Quinn deploys a Disco Ball covering the dangerous firing lane, approach or target. The targetless BS roll is a Normal Roll; do not expose Quinn to avoidable enemy Normal BS AROs.
2. The Eclipse template appears in the Conclusion step after a successful deployment. It cannot protect movement or shooting earlier in that same Order.
3. On a subsequent Order, advance Shona through the protected route and declare Move + CC Attack if she reaches legal Silhouette contact. Change Fireteam Leader as needed.
4. The target can still declare legal CC Attack or Dodge AROs. Stealth can prevent some ZoC-only AROs before contact, but does not suppress all AROs, particularly against eligible Sixth Sense/Combat Instinct defenders. Avoid exposed parts of her route, mines and other legal threats.

Eclipse does not neutralize CC specialists, all deployables, hacking or LoF-independent attacks. It is a temporary delivery tool, not invulnerability. Disco Baller is Disposable (2); a deployed Ball deactivates at the start of the States Phase and can be reactivated by a suitable Deactivator. Aquila FTO (49/0) is the premium alternative, supplying MSV3, MULTI Marksman Rifle, specialist access and its own Disco Baller; MSV3 itself cannot see through Eclipse.

Do not require Shona and Quinn to be in the same Fireteam: an independently deployed Eclipse zone can support Shona elsewhere. But linking them with a native anchor can make the approach more Order-efficient.

#### Weaknesses and when to skip

- No self-contained visibility control, infiltration, camouflage or Mimetism. Reaching melee through exposed lanes consumes Orders and risks losing her before she contributes.
- BS11 and short-range weapons do not compete with dedicated PanO attack pieces across open lanes.
- No scoring specialist role or personal extra Order; include teammates that exploit the space she clears.
- Frenzy triggers after directly inflicting a Wound or causing Dead State, granting Impetuous and Limited Cover in the States Phase. Plan post-attack positioning and do not assume she retains ordinary cover benefits.
- Skip in static gunline or TAG-heavy lists lacking a realistic close-assault plan, especially if Maximus or cheap layered defense already handles the anticipated threat.

**Recommended quantity: 0–1.** Must-own if exploring NCA's melee playstyle, not must-field. With Quinn Disco Baller and terrain that permits protected approaches she is an A-tier role specialist; without a practical delivery plan she is closer to B. The central reason to take her remains elite CC at modest cost, not her secondary shooting or Wildcard status.
