# Liang Kai — Svalarheima's Winter Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: B — situational independent melee attacker, not a default inclusion.** Excellent close combat, mobility and personal order economy for 21 points, but a warband without Smoke is difficult to deliver. Prefer Shona for a linked Eclipse-assisted melee attack or Beasthunter for cheap independent midfield disruption unless the table or mission specifically favors Liang Kai.

## Strengths

- **Elite melee:** CC26 before opposing modifiers, MA4 imposes -3 on opposing Face to Face rolls and grants +1 actual Burst in both turns. Normal CC is B2, and EXP CCW (PS=4) forces three ARM saves per ordinary successful hit, subject to target immunities. Particularly dangerous to conventional shooters; AP+EXP is preferable against high ARM.
- **Berserk:** 8-inch charge and CC Attack in one Long Skill if initial LoF exists and legal Silhouette contact is reachable. No printed Berserk (+3) modifier. It is not automatically an unopposed attack; do not assume the ordinary MA4 B2 applies to a Long Skill without checking Burst restrictions.
- **Order economy:** one pooled Regular Order and one optional personal Impetuous activation per turn. Forward Deployment 4 inches reduces the approach distance. Skip unsafe Impetuous activations rather than expose him for the sake of a free Order.
- **Mobility and defense:** Super-Jump, Stealth, Mimetism (-3), Dodge on PH16 and 3-inch Dodge movement on success. Excellent for finding protected approaches, repositioning and countercharging.
- **Secondary weapons:** Chain Rifle for direct-template trading, Light Shotgun at BS17 in its +6 range band before other modifiers, WIP17 Flash Pulse in its +3 band for opportunistic ARO. EXP CCW is Anti-materiel for applicable mission objectives.

## Main weakness — a warband without Smoke

Liang Kai has the tools to win melee, not the self-contained visibility control needed to reach it consistently. Forward Deployment is only 4 inches; Mimetism and improved Dodge do not substitute for Smoke or Eclipse. Limited Cover applies from deployment, and ARM1/BTS0 with NWI but no Shock Immunity is vulnerable. Twenty-one points is a meaningful investment for a warband intended to trade.

The Impetuous activation is valuable on a dense table but its movement restrictions can make it unusable on exposed lanes. His Regular Order preserves some value even if he remains a countercharge threat. Super-Jump provides alternative attack angles, not protection from AROs.

## External Eclipse support

- [Quinn](quinn.md) FO Disco — 26/0; preferred general-purpose carrier, MSV1 and scoring.
- [Vargar](vargar.md) FO Disco — 24/0; preferred mobile Alpha carrier, Super-Jump, Heavy Flamethrower and scoring.
- Bøyg Mk12 — 44/0; expensive heavy assault alternative if already selected.

Liang Kai can exploit their Eclipse without joining their Fireteam, but must spend separate activations to advance. Establish Eclipse before crossing the lane. Ordinary Move + CC Attack is legal through Eclipse once Silhouette contact is reached; Berserk requires initial LoF and cannot target an enemy completely hidden by Eclipse. Mines, legal Dodge/CC AROs and other LoF-independent threats remain relevant.

Do not purchase an expensive visibility-control package solely to deliver Liang Kai unless the mission justifies it. Prefer using a Disco carrier already serving the primary assault team.

Shona is usually preferable when building a dedicated melee Fireteam, for just one additional point. Liang Kai is better suited to independent forward pressure and starts with Impetuous rather than needing to trigger Frenzy.

Liang Kai and the 15-point Camouflaged Beasthunter cost **36/0** combined. They provide complementary independent assault and ambush threats alongside conventional Fireteams, but the Beasthunter is Irregular and the package does not itself supply Eclipse or scoring.

## Listbuilding conclusion

- **Consider:** dense tables, protected attack routes, independent countercharge, secondary assault alongside an existing Eclipse carrier, or a mission needing Anti-materiel melee.
- **Usually skip:** open tables, lists already containing sufficient close combat, or lists without a realistic approach route.
- **Recommended quantity: 0–1; B overall.** Strong melee and personal order economy do not overcome the fundamental delivery problem. The corrected Dodge modifiers improve his survivability but do not turn him into a self-sufficient Smoke warband.
