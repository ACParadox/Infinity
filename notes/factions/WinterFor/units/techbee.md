## WinterFor selection and use

- Cheap Specialist Operative/Flash Pulse/Deployable Repeater for missions and network placement; can complement Fusilier FO or RacerBots.
- TAGCom applies under its actual same-group conditions and does not boost Jotum BS; GizmoKit is useful but not a substitute for a full Engineer.
- Adhesive Launcher Rifle can discourage close advances; WIP12 and fragility limit independent aggression.
- Usually 0–1; particularly attractive when Jotum plus another scoring activation is needed.

##### Tech-Bee — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: strong 9-point utility / Order pick; near-default with a same-Combat-Group TAG, but not a universal must-have.** Even without a TAG, Tech-Bee is one of Kestrel's cheapest independent **Regular specialist Orders**, combining a last-resort objective scorer, Flash Pulse ARO, **B2 PARA Adhesive Launcher Rifle**, emergency **GizmoKit** recovery, **Deactivator**, and three **Deployable Repeaters**. Her repeater is especially appealing as an *extra* deployment in a **Coordinated Order** alongside mines or other equipment. The main limits are **MOV 10-10 cm (4-4")**, **WIP12**, **BS11**, **ARM0**, and having neither Fireteam access nor reliable direct-damage weaponry.

Source: [pinned Kestrel N5.3 roster](https://github.com/ACParadox/Infinity/blob/main/sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md#tech-bees). Kestrel has **only one Tech-Bee profile**.

###### Why buy her without a TAG?

- **One Regular Order for 9 points**: almost Fugazi/Mulebot pricing, but the model is an actual Specialist Operative.
- **Emergency scoring**: can operate mission objectives requiring an appropriate Specialist. WIP12 is unexceptional, and 4-4" movement means she is not a primary midfield runner; preserve her for reachable backfield objectives or a late opening.
- **Useful short-lane AROs**: Flash Pulse can Stun attackers, and Adhesive can threaten an expensive attacker without testing ARM/BTS. These are deterrents, not reasons to expose a 1W ARM0 cheerleader to a premium gun.
- **Backup repair**: GizmoKit can restore lost STR on allied TAGs/REMs or recover Unconscious STR targets; useful if a damaged REM is already nearby. This does **not** turn her into an Engineer.
- **Opportunity to place a Repeater**: defensive hacking coverage when a friendly hacker can actually exploit it, especially by piggybacking on a planned Coordinated Order.

She is **much more than a 9-point cheerleader**, but neither the specialist nor the utility will matter in every mission/list. A seven-point Fugazi is more efficient for a pure Order/Flash Pulse/repeater role.

###### Adhesive Launcher Rifle: respectable anti-elite deterrent, not a main gun

Tech-Bee shoots at **BS11** with **B2 active / B1 ARO** and **PARA (PH-6)** ammo. A successful hit followed by a failed target **PH-6** Saving Roll causes **Immobilized-A**; ARM/BTS are irrelevant. IMM-A restricts declarations to penalized Dodge until it is canceled (or an Engineer helps). PARAs do **not** affect targets with no PH Attribute.

| Range | MOD | Tech-Bee's modified BS before cover/other MODs |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0–8" | 0 | 11 |
| 8–16" | +3 | **14** |
| 16–24" | -3 | 8 |
| 24–32" | -3 | 8 |

**After an uncontested successful hit**, a PH10 target fails its PH-6 roll 80% of the time; PH12 fails 70%; PH15 fails 55%. These are **save-failure chances only**, not overall odds of winning the firefight. A high-ARM TAG or HI that pushes into 8–16" can be punished, but BS11, no Mimetism, W1 and ARM0 make an intentional duel against a serious attack piece unattractive.

**Best deployment:** a covered short lane or corner that protects a valuable support position, preferably alongside a stronger ARO. The Adhesive is a high-upside punishment tool, not an active-turn hunting plan.

###### Flash Pulse: decent secondary ARO

Flash Pulse is a **WIP-based B1 non-lethal shot**. Tech-Bee's WIP12 gives her target **15 from 8–24"**, before cover/visibility and other MODs. A hit followed by a failed BTS Saving Roll gives the target **Stunned**, disrupting subsequent attacks without dealing damage.

A **7pt Fugazi** is a better dedicated Flash ARO: **WIP13, Mimetism (-3), MOV 6-6", and an always-on Repeater**. Tech-Bee wins on **Specialist Operative, adhesive threat, GizmoKit and TAGCom** instead. Protect her Order rather than offering an obvious B1 duel to a powerful active gun.

###### GizmoKit and Deactivator: emergency support, not a replacement Engineer

**GizmoKit** works only on an **allied STR model**. In Silhouette contact it needs no attack roll; the **target** rolls PH. At range, Tech-Bee must first hit with GizmoKit using the weapon's short range bands (+3 up to 8", 0 at 8–16", -6 at 16–24"). A successful target PH roll removes a Wound; a failed roll **inflicts a Wound**, so attempts can finish an already badly damaged model. Against **Remote Presence**, one successful roll can cancel its Unconscious State even if it is at a deeper Unconscious level.

That makes her a plausible **secondary** rescuer of nearby Sierra, Pathfinder, Bulleteer, Peacemaker or TAG models. But she **cannot use Engineer** to clear states such as Isolated or Immobilized-B merely because she carries GizmoKit. A Machinist + Palbot or Firefly Engineer remains much better for planned TAG recovery and state clearing.

**Deactivator** can remove enemy placed mines, repeaters, turrets, WildParrots and other eligible deployables with a WIP roll (and cannot target a Camouflage Marker). This occasionally clears a crucial approach, but usually isn't worth exposing Tech-Bee just to use it.

###### Deployable Repeater: modest alone, **good in coordinated deployment**

Tech-Bee's **Deployable Repeater is Disposable (3)**. She places one using **Place Deployable**; it begins working at the **conclusion of the Order** and remains a static Repeater. It is **not** a built-in, always-on Repeater on Tech-Bee herself and **not** a FastPanda: because it lacks **Perimeter**, placement is in **Silhouette contact** with her or, after Move in that Order, with **any part of her movement route** (not anywhere in her 8" ZoC).

Once active, it projects a Repeater ZoC usable by friendly hackers **even in another Combat Group**. Defensive uses: add a hacking risk to a covered flank, put an enemy TAG/HI/REM route within a hacker's reach, or leave network coverage after Tech-Bee withdraws. Without a relevant friendly hacker or uncovered lane, placing it has little value. Enemy hackers can also use your extended network to attack your hackers, subject to applicable enemy-Repeater Firewall protection: do not blindly advance it.

###### Coordinated Order: Tech-Bee + other deployable users

**This is the attractive low-marginal-Order use.** N5.3 explicitly allows **different Deployable equipment/weapons and different placement points** within one Coordinated Order, as long as every participating trooper declares the **same sequence of Skills**.

Example, assuming all four are **Regular, in the same Combat Group, and not in Fireteams**:

| Participant | Common Skills | Actual equipment placed |
|---|---|---|
| **Tech-Bee** | Move + Place Deployable | **Deployable Repeater** |
| **Fennec Minelayer** | Move + Place Deployable | **Shock Mine** |
| **Firefly Engineer** | Move + Place Deployable | **AP Mine** |
| **Machinist Armed Turret profile** | Move + Place Deployable | **Armed Turret (Combi Rifle)** |

Cost: **1 Command Token + 1 Regular Order** from that Combat Group. Choose a Spearhead. **Each** participant moves along its own legal route and places **its own** deployable in a legal position, with the usual restrictions. The **Machinist's turret has Perimeter**, so its placement differs from the base-contact mine/repeater placements. The pieces appear at the **Order's conclusion**, so they cannot affect AROs during that same Order.

**Why it matters:** If you already wanted to place two mines or a turret, Tech-Bee can add a persistent Repeater covering **her current position or a point along her route** without paying a separate Regular Order. This is particularly useful as a **Group 2 Order sink** when several independent backfield troopers are in that group.

**Rules guardrails:**
- Same Combat Group and **same Training (Regular/Irregular)** for all participants. **Infiltrating Jackals are Irregular**, so cannot coordinate with Regular Tech-Bee.
- Everyone must declare **Move + Place Deployable** (or another identical sequence); each may use a different eligible deployable.
- **Current Fireteam members cannot coordinate.** This commonly rules out a linked Fennec/Firefly until they are no longer in their Fireteam.
- **Peripheral controllers and their Peripherals cannot participate** (so the synchronized Peacemaker/Auxbot is not a candidate).
- All normal placement/legality restrictions remain; each participant can place only its allowed deployable with that Place Deployable declaration.
- A solo repeater placement is usually **not worth spending a Command Token**; the point is to add it efficiently when you are **already coordinating** worthwhile deployable placements.

**Assessment:** **Situationally good**, not a reason to construct a full hacking strategy around Tech-Bee. Coordinated deployment increases her repeater's practical value materially.

###### TAGCom: primary reason she becomes near-default in TAG lists

**While Tech-Bee is on the table in a non-Null State**, TAGs in **her Combat Group only** benefit from:

- **Dodge (PH+3)**: a strong passive improvement to TAG Dodge rolls, relevant against templates and attacks where rolling Dodge beats shooting back.
- **GizmoKit (PH+1)**: increases the TAG's PH when resolving GizmoKit recovery, including repairs provided by other allied GizmoKit carriers. This **does not** improve Tech-Bee's own BS to hit with GizmoKit.

**Important Combat Group trap:** **Group 1 Tikbalang + Group 2 Tech-Bee receives no TAGCom bonus**. If her TAG is in Group 1, Tech-Bee usually belongs in Group 1 too, not Group 2 simply because she's a cheap cheerleader. The bonus applies to actual TAGs (Tikbalang, Squalos Mk-II, Maximus TAG); **Redeyes are VH, not TAGs**.

TAGCom is passive; Tech-Bee does **not** need to follow the TAG, spend Orders, establish LoF, or survive on the frontline to give the bonus. A safe backfield Tech-Bee is ideal.

###### Group 1 vs Group 2

| Situation | Placement |
|---|---|
| **A TAG is in Group 1** | **Group 1 strongly preferred** so TAGCom applies; protect Tech-Bee |
| **TAG is in Group 2** | **Group 2** if TAGCom desired there |
| **No TAG; Group 1 needs an inexpensive 10th Order** | **Group 1**; 9pt Regular Order + specialist backup |
| **No TAG; Group 1 full and Group 2 has 2–5 Orders** | **Group 2 is excellent** if spare Orders can occasionally score, repair, or place a coordinated repeater |
| **Dedicated coordinated mine/turret setup in Group 2** | **Group 2 is particularly attractive**, provided the other Regular participants are there and unlinked |
| **No use for extra orders, specialists already covered, no relevant hacker or REM support** | **Skip** or choose cheaper Fugazi/Mulebot |

Her repeater can serve **Group 1 hackers from Group 2**; the limitation on **TAGCom** should not be mistakenly applied to repeaters. Conversely, Group 2 Orders never become Group 1 Orders.

###### Comparison and opportunity cost

| Unit | Pts / SWC | Why take over Tech-Bee? | Tech-Bee's edge |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Fugazi Flashbot** | **7 / 0** | Cheapest effective Flash Pulse, Mimetism, 6-6" MOV, always-on Repeater | **Specialist, GizmoKit, Adhesive, TAGCom** |
| **Mulebot Baggage** | **8 / 0** | Baggage/reloading, more durable backfield body, passive Repeater | Specialist, more varied defensive utility and TAGCom |
| **Basic Fennec** | **10 / 0** | Combi Rifle, native FENNECS Fireteam seat | 1pt less, specialist, support gear |
| **Fennec FO/Disco** | **13 / 0** | FO/Sensor/Eclipse and Fireteam access | 4pt less; repair, Adhesive and TAGCom |
| **Pathfinder (base / Tactical Awareness)** | **14 / 17, 0 SWC** | MOV 6-4", FO/Sensor, permanent Repeater, better active specialist | Much cheaper Regular specialist/order; TAGCom |
| **Machinist (base)** | **15 / 0** | Full Engineer skill; D-Charges and stronger planned technical recovery | 6pt cheaper and an independent mission specialist |

**15-slot and list-construction discipline:** At 9pts she is a terrific way to fill an open slot with a useful Regular Order. She is not worth taking *solely* for the cheap Order if it forces out a critical attacker, mission tool, or a required Fireteam member. A lone late-game WIP12 scoring attempt should be an emergency plan, not the foundation of objective coverage.

###### Final selection rule

1. **TAG-centered list: A / near-default.** Prefer Tech-Bee in the **same Combat Group** as the TAG; Dodge PH+3 is strong enough to justify serious consideration even if she otherwise stays hidden.
2. **No TAG, REM support or objective scarcity: A- / strong.** Excellent cheap Regular specialist with two useful nonlethal reactive weapons and emergency GizmoKit support.
3. **No TAG, available coordinated deployables and some hacking: A- / good synergy.** An efficient Repeater placed *as part of a coordinated deployment* makes a niche part of her kit meaningfully useful.
4. **No TAG, no useful hacker, abundant specialists and only an Order wanted: optional.** A Fugazi or Mulebot is cheaper and may fit the actual job better.
5. **Group 2: very good when no same-group TAGCom dependency.** It keeps the Order cheap and permits spare-order specialist/utility jobs or a coordinated deployment with other Regular Group 2 troops.

**Bottom line:** **Tech-Bee is a strong 9-point pick, not an unconditional auto-include.** With a same-group TAG she approaches a default. Without one, she's still an unusually efficient combination of a Regular Order, last-resort specialist, Flash Pulse, Adhesive, emergency repair and an opportunistic repeater—especially if that repeater gets deployed alongside other equipment in a Coordinated Order.
