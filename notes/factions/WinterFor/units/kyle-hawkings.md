## WinterFor selection and use

- **BSG (+1B) 26/0:** default, BS14 with BS Attack (-3), W2, MA3 and Specialist Operative. Strong for clearing rooms and then scoring; better direct close-range fighting than 26pt Hospitaller BSG (which instead carries an E/Mitter and can anchor Beta).
- **MMR 32/0:** choose if the team needs 8–24in secondary shooting and Pulzar; otherwise the cheaper BSG makes a better complementary assault piece beside Karhu Feuerbach.
- **Fusilier Haris:** actual Fusilier FO12 + Hawkins BSG26 + Blade FTO Engineer23 = 61/0; all Fusilier-equivalent for composition, useful specialist and extra activation. Consider Quinn Disco if protected crossing is the priority rather than Engineer. Do not drag a static defensive Core forward just to exploit Hawkins.
- **Beta:** Karhu Feuerbach34 + Hawkins BSG26 + Hospitaller Firewall27 = 87/1.5, combining long gun, close-range specialist and durable Firewall support. A Karhu Engineer instead of the Hospitaller is another mission-oriented alternative. Beta needs an actual eligible anchor.
- **Independent:** workable, but no forward deployment, so advancing alone takes Orders. Use protected angles; Frenzy can compromise cover.
- Compared with Shona, Hawkins scores and shoots better; Shona is the dedicated elite CC finisher. Compared with ORC Firewall BSG28, Hawkins is a better attacker but lacks its Firewall and Alpha native access. Usually 0–1, near-default consideration for a close-assault Fusilier/Beta plan.

### Knight Hospitaller Kyle Hawkins — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A+ value for the 26pt Boarding Shotgun (+1B) Specialist Operative in an advancing Fireteam; A for the 32pt MULTI Marksman Rifle when the team genuinely needs a flexible 8–24in gun; B as an independent, non-forward-deployed mission runner.** **Default: 26pt Boarding Shotgun.** At the *same 26pt/0 SWC price* as the ordinary Hospitaller Boarding Shotgun, Hawkins is a dramatically superior close-range **Face-to-Face** attacker: **BS14, BS Attack (-3), BS Attack (+1B), CC23/MA3, Combat Instinct, and Specialist Operative**, while still counting as a **Knight Hospitaller** for appropriate Fireteam levels. The ordinary Hospitaller remains relevant as a **mandatory actual Hospitaller FTO** in Crusade, for **ARM4** and its **E/Mitter**; Hawkins cannot replace the native required member.

#### What makes the 26pt Boarding Shotgun exceptional

**Hawkins is not just another MO shotgun:** he combines offensive modifiers and scoring in a package that ordinary 26pt Hospitallers cannot match.

1. **BS14 + opponent -3 in Face-to-Face shooting.** **BS Attack (-3)** penalizes opponents' relevant Face-to-Face Rolls when Hawkins declares BS Attack, including against many normal gunfighters. This is **not Mimetism**: it does not supply a standing defensive -3 when he is not shooting, against unopposed attacks, or simply while moving/Dodging.
2. **Extra active-turn Burst.** **BS Attack (+1B)** increases the applicable weapon's Burst by 1 **in the Active Turn**: a normal B2 Boarding Shotgun makes **B3** rolled active attacks. **Do not apply this bonus to reactive BS Attack AROs**; the modifier is not a blanket passive B3 ARO. The ability is printed on the shotgun loadout as a BS Attack skill, not an extra +1 Success Die.
3. **Excellent close shotgun range.** When the target is within the Boarding Shotgun's favorable 0–8in band, the active B3 attack is made at a high effective BS, before environmental, cover and target modifiers, while the opponent must contend with BS Attack (-3). **AP ammunition** offers an unusually efficient threat to tougher targets; still do not claim a guaranteed TAG kill.
4. **FT2 multiplies reliability, not Burst.** In a legal Fireteam at **FT2 or higher**, relevant rolled BS Attacks gain **+1 Success Die**: Hawkins' active shotgun remains **B3** but rolls one additional die and discards one as required, **not B4**. This is distinct from the B3 due to his printed BS Attack (+1B). Non-rolled Direct Template modes do not become additional shots through +1SD.
5. **W2, BTS6, CC23, Martial Arts L3, DA CCW (PS5), Combat Instinct.** Hawkins remains a serious close-range threat if he needs to fight in melee or defend the objective. **Dodge (+1in)** helps positioning when dodging; **Stealth** helps on qualifying active approaches through hacking areas, but is not hacking immunity.
6. **Specialist Operative and 0 SWC.** The same model that trades shots at close range can push scenario buttons. This is the big role-compression advantage over the ordinary Hospitaller BSG and many similarly priced short-range assault units.

**Main limitation: getting there.** Hawkins has **no Forward Deployment, Infiltration, motorcycle, Mimetism, Eclipse, TinBot, Repeater, Hacking Device, Doctor, or E/Mitter** of his own. He excels at *winning* a short-range firefight, but is not automatically better than a faster or predeployed specialist at *reaching* the objective or establishing position before the enemy moves.

##### Practical reactive and defensive cautions

- **BS Attack (-3)** is conditional on a qualifying BS Attack Face-to-Face Roll; it does not protect Hawkins like always-on Mimetism when he is dodging or being shot unopposed.
- **BS Attack (+1B)** is **Active Turn only**. An ordinary B2 shotgun should not be represented as B3 in an ARO.
- **Frenzy** can become a serious defensive liability: after the qualifying casualty, Hawkins gains Impetuous / Limited Cover in the applicable States Phase. He can lose normal cover-based protection, even inside an elite Fireteam; do not treat the initial favorable duel as a guarantee of safety on the counterattack.
- **ARM3** is notably worse than the ordinary Hospitaller's ARM4; two Wounds and BTS6 do not make him immune to missiles, templates, hacking or strong AROs.
- **Close-range saturation:** when the army already has Joan, Mendoza, multiple short-range Teutonics, Montesas, Bulleteers or a Seraph's Auxbot threat, another short-range piece may be redundant despite Hawkins' excellent standalone efficiency.

**Selection rule:** if both would merely fill the same **26-point close-range fighting slot**, **Hawkins is the clearly stronger buy**. The ordinary Hospitaller BSG still earns its points when it is the **required actual Crusade anchor**, when its **E/Mitter** is a needed threat, or when Hawkins' AVA1 is already used. Do **not** claim that Hawkins replaces the ordinary Hospitaller FTO in constructing Crusade.

**Other Hospitaller profiles compete more by function than by head-to-head shooting:** **27pt TinBot** protects expensive hackable members; **30pt Doctor** recovers fallen Knights; **32pt HMG** provides long fire lanes. Hawkins doesn't replicate those services.

#### The 32pt MULTI Marksman Rifle: when to take it

**32/0 SWC · A situational.** The MMR version keeps all of Hawkins' *common* advantages: **BS Attack (-3)**, Specialist Operative, MA3, Combat Instinct and Hospitaller-equivalent FT access. It replaces the close-only shotgun emphasis with:

- A **MULTI Marksman Rifle** with useful midrange bands, normal **B3 active shooting**, and ammunition selection appropriate to the target.
- A **Pulzar** for close defensive/template applications.
- A **MULTI Pistol** instead of a Boarding Pistol.
- **Six additional points**, with **no BS Attack (+1B)**.

**Take MMR if** Hawkins needs to be a Fireteam's own **8–24in primary gunfighter**, particularly when other team members are TinBot/Doctor/support rather than a proper medium-range attacker. A linked MMR Hawkins can apply BS Attack (-3) and FT2 +1SD across a more practical range envelope than the BSG, which may reduce the number of risky Orders required to approach. It also preserves 0 SWC for a TAG, HMG, HRL or other weapon.

**Take BSG instead if** the Fireteam already includes **Joan Spitfire, Hospitaller HMG, De Fersen, Mendoza, Santiago Spitfire or Justice** covering longer bands. Paying six more points to overlap existing range coverage is often poor MO economics. Hawkins BSG is the better **second-stage assault/mission finisher**.

**Neither profile solves MO's general scarcity of long-range active guns.** The MMR is not a replacement for a TAG, high-grade HMG or dedicated long-range opener.

##### Who to partner with, and who to avoid

**Best general partner: Hospitaller TinBot 27.** It supplies a qualifying Crusade anchor, Firewall (-3) and a MULTI Rifle while Hawkins supplies the close B3 AP gun and objective scoring. For **53/0** this is a purposeful compact Duo, leaving room for a separate TAG or Fireteam.

**Best premium partner: De Fersen 42, plus an actual Hospitaller anchor.** De Fersen's WIP14 HD/Trinity has a mission distinct from Hawkins' BSG and appreciates TinBot support. The **95/0.5 Haris** has three Hospitaller-equivalent members (real FT3), hacking reach, specialist scoring and close-range anti-armor. Its main problem is **95 points concentrated on a hackable route**; it wants forward repeaters/target choices and a wider army with Orders.

**Hospitaller HMG 32:** a complementary first-stage gun for a Hawkins BSG. Choose it when the **Crusade itself** must open longer ranges, not when a Seraph/Tikbalang or other independent long gun already owns that job.

**Hospitaller Doctor 30:** an excellent *functional* partner when the team is heavy in expensive two-Wound bodies. Less useful if there is no recoverable nearby VITA casualty. As in the Hospitaller guide, a linked Doctor cannot also function as a Palbot controller.

**Joan v2 / Knight of Justice / Mendoza:** can form powerful teams, but judge **role overlap** first. Joan already supplies a fast AP Spitfire fighter; Justice adds premium durability/range/hacking or FO; Mendoza adds Mimetism(-6), grenades and TinBot. Hawkins' role should be **cheap specialist / close finisher** rather than purchasing another expensive melee/short-range specialist without a differentiated mission. **Mendoza does not count as Hospitaller** toward FT level, unlike Hawkins.

**Teutonics and generic Military Orders Fireteams:** legal with a valid native required-choice member, but a Teutonic Specialist costs only **19** and already handles durable scoring; adding Hawkins may be unnecessary short-range saturation and he does **not count as a Teutonic**. A defensive Crosier/Knight Commander Core is usually a bad investment for Hawkins' aggressive talents, even when Wildcard inclusion is legal.

**Konstantinos in Crusade caution:** the project notes an in-app Army report that Konstantinos FTO cannot join Crusade despite an unrestricted Wildcard row in the pinned source. Do not propose a Hawkins/Konstantinos Crusade combo until that discrepancy is resolved.

#### Final assessment

- **Boarding Shotgun option 2, 26/0:** **A+ Fireteam value / A overall.** Exceptional, unusually cheap **BS Attack(-3) + B3 active shotgun + objective specialist**. At the **same 26pt** cost, markedly better direct combatant than Hospitaller BSG. Particularly strong in FT2+ with the extra **+1 Success Die**.
- **MULTI MMR option 1, 32/0:** **A situational / B+ general.** Legitimate zero-SWC medium-range attacker and scorer; loses the BSG's +1 Burst for +6pts and often overlaps MO's existing midrange guns.
- **Independent Hawkins:** **B** because without forward deployment or extra Orders, getting him into his best range can be expensive and exposed.
- **Must-take?** **No across MO; near-default consideration for a Crusade that actually wants a short-range assault specialist.** Do not automatically add him to a list already saturated with that role.

**Bottom line:** Hawkins is not a general replacement for support profiles, but **he is a near-strict upgrade over a plain 26pt Hospitaller BSG as the close-range attacker and scoring piece**. Treat the actual Hospitaller FTO as the *Fireteam anchor/support purchase* and Hawkins as the *elite finishing specialist*. In MO's tight 300pt economy, that pairing is far more coherent than buying another expensive close-range Knight merely because the character is impressive.
