# Bøyg Soldiers — Svalarheima's Winter Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: C+ — niche pick / usually skip.** At 44 points the Bøyg costs as much as premium PanO heavy infantry without being an apex gunfighter, specialist, strong CC piece or order engine. Its best justification is a durable zero-SWC Mk12 secondary attacker with its own Disco Baller, saving a separate Eclipse carrier. The Missile Launcher is an interesting active-turn anti-armor niche, not the default reactive gun. Prefer Karhu for primary shooting and Quinn or Vargar for Eclipse in most lists. Collection: optional, one at most.

## What the Bøyg actually offers

- BS14, ARM5, BTS6 and two Wounds allow more sustained fighting than one-Wound Vargar or Karhu. ECM: Hacker (-3) mitigates applicable enemy hacking rolls; it remains Hackable.
- Albedo (-6) is a powerful conditional modifier against qualifying MSV/Marksmanship opponents, not a universal Mimetism substitute. Ordinary shooters do not suffer that Albedo penalty; the Bøyg has no MSV to counter opposing Mimetism.
- X-Visor converts -3 range MODs to 0 and -6 to -3; improves the Mk12's extended range, ML's poor bands and direct Disco Baller placement.
- BS Attack (+1B) improves active Burst, including its low-Burst launcher. Do not carry the bonus into ordinary ARO; native/Fireteam Special Dice are not additional actual Burst.
- Integrated Disco Baller can open an otherwise difficult crossing and enable the rest of the team. Eclipse is not a smoke-shooting combo and takes effect at Conclusion. Disposable (2) is a real limitation; budget ammunition and use legal Baggage resupply if appropriate.
- No Specialist, NCO, Tactical Awareness, deployment skill, exceptional CC, close-range template or enhanced movement. S5 is harder to hide and navigate than S2. MOV4-4 equals MOV6-2 on a double Move but is inferior for first Move + Shoot or other short-skill positioning.

**Mk12:** B4 PS5 at zero SWC is strong conventional midrange firepower. X-Visor gives reasonable engagement bands from 0–32 inches. A solo attack run can finish in Suppressive Fire (leaving any Fireteam), making the durable chassis useful for positional defense. However, Normal ammunition, absent general shooting MODs and no MSV limit performance against premium opponents. Do not confuse good stopping power against ordinary targets with AP performance against high armor.

**Missile Launcher:** B2 AP+EXP in active hit mode threatens up to six ordinary saves if both hits land; blast mode threatens clustered targets. X-Visor removes the usual -3 at 0–8 and beyond 40 inches in legal range. This is a genuine anti-armor and anti-MSV attack niche, but lower Burst than Karhu Feuerbach and only B1 in ordinary ARO. The KoJ ML (+1SD) is usually the superior general-purpose reactive choice.

### Hetkari — stronger general gunfighter

For four extra points and 1.5 SWC, the Red Fury Hetkari has the same BS14, ARM5, BTS6, W2 and B4, but Marksmanship and BS Attack (-3) provide a large relative advantage in conventional Face-to-Face rolls. With both models in cover and +3 range against a BS13 opponent, Bøyg rolls on 14 versus 13; Hetkari rolls on 17 versus 10, absent other MODs and exceptions (e.g. Warhorse). AP/Shock/DA ammunition, E/Marat and Combat Instinct also contribute. Hetkari is S2 and MOV6-2. Bøyg retains the advantages of zero SWC, PS5 against ordinary armor, better range bands with X-Visor, Eclipse and Albedo against eligible opponents. **Hetkari is the stronger general-purpose shooter.**

### Crux Knight — stronger overall assault package

For just two additional points and the same zero SWC, the Crux MMR FTO shares BS14, ARM5, BTS6, W2, Albedo(-6) and PS5 on its primary weapon. Crux rolls B3 +1SD (four dice, keep three), whereas Bøyg rolls B4 and may land four hits. Crux has AP ammunition, selectable DA mode and +1SD in reactive shooting; excellent CC22/MA2/Natural Born Warrior/Berserk, Specialist Operative, Drop Bears, D-Charges, Stealth, S2, MOV6-2 and Wildcard. Bøyg's meaningful advantages are X-Visor, true B4 and Eclipse. **Crux is far more complete at virtually identical cost.**

### Knight of Justice — stronger defined mission roles

The Bøyg Mk12 is arguably a better dedicated conventional active ranged gun than ordinary KoJ rifle/Spitfire options, and the Bøyg ML has B2 instead of B1 +1SD in active. However, the KoJ offers BTS9, WIP13, S2, MOV6-2, CC23/MA2, Stealth and Wildcard; its FO and HD/Pitcher are distinct valuable specialist roles. The KoJ ML's native +1SD works in ARO (B1 +1SD solo; B1 +2SD at FT2), unlike Bøyg +1B. Cheap Fusilier support can supply the composition bonus. Bøyg is better specifically when active AP+EXP or anti-MSV shooting plus Eclipse is needed. **Prefer KoJ for dedicated ARO or durable mission utility.**

### Aquila Guard — closest Disco comparison

Aquila MMR FTO costs 49/0, just five more points, with its own Disco Baller. BS15, MSV3, ECM: Hacker (-3), Specialist Operative, Deactivator, enhanced Jump, S2, MOV6-2 and Wildcard give it a genuinely premium capability and much broader mission utility. Its B3 PS7 MMR has AP/Shock/DA; Bøyg has B4 PS5 Normal, one extra ARM, Albedo and X-Visor. Aquila solves Mim(-6), marker and Smoke matchups as well as Eclipse delivery and objectives. **Aquila is generally more compelling at this price, though Bøyg has the superior raw Burst/stopping power against low armor.**

Swiss Guard, Mendoza, De Fersen and Agent Maximus likewise offer identifiable exceptional abilities (marker-state protection, strong Mimetism/CC, apex hybrid hacking, or Tactical Awareness plus immunity and specialization). Holy Sepulchre costs nine points more but provides a real order-efficient heavy attack engine. Bøyg is competent across several axes without comparable standout general-purpose value.

These are point-budget comparisons, not necessarily legal Fireteams together. Vargar FO Disco (24/0) is a mobile Albedo specialist with Heavy Flamethrower, Super-Jump and Deactivator. Quinn (26/0) is the preferred general-purpose Disco carrier with MSV1, Warhorse, Immunity (ARM), FO and Wildcard; Vargar is preferred for Alpha or mobile assault. Neither is merely an Eclipse tax. Adding a separate carrier provides a second Regular Order and independent positioning, in addition to mission capability. Integrated Eclipse on the Bøyg is worth something, but usually not enough to compensate.

Karhu Feuerbach 34/1.5 is the preferred primary gun: MSV1, Mim(-3), B3 AP+DA, NCO, Climbing Plus, AP Mines and non-hackable chassis. Nisse HMG 31/1.5 is the targeted anti-Mimetism/MSV2 choice. Bøyg's W2/ARM5 and zero-SWC Mk12 are meaningful if the rest of the list specifically needs a secondary durable gun rather than these cheaper, more efficient pieces.

The 100-point Hawkins/Doctor Haris is the clearest zero-SWC durability/assault niche, but consumes one-third of a 300-point list without additional personal Orders. The 98-point Gunnar/Karhu/Bøyg shape is supported by historical [report #012](../../../battle-reports/011-020-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md); that report referred to an HRL, which is not a current Bøyg profile. The current legal reconstruction uses the ML, not an asserted identical historical loadout. Prefer the smaller, more efficient Karhu Beta teams unless the Bøyg's specific anti-tech gun/Eclipse contribution is essential.

## Final selection

**Usually zero.** If selected, prefer Mk12 at 44/0 when the list already has a primary long-range gun, has scarce SWC and needs a durable independent midrange attacker with its own Eclipse. ML is conditional for active B2 AP+EXP and anti-MSV fights; take KoJ ML for a dedicated linked reactive position. No need to buy a separate Bøyg simply for Disco access. This preserves the broader WinterFor listbuilding guide's situational case for the Bøyg while reflecting the user's lower overall purchase/list priority.
