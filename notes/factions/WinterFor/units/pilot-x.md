## Why Pilot-X is a must-have

Three independently strong contributions in one package: a superb passive REM upgrade, a very good defensive Hacker, and access to two exceptional 8-point Regular utility REMs. Do not treat RemDriver as a niche tax or require a pre-existing REM-heavy list: buy one suitable REM specifically to exploit the buff if needed.

### RemDriver: fixed stats, not additive MODs

**BS=13, PH=13, BTS=6; no CC bonus.** Assign to one eligible already-deployed friendly REM during deployment. No activation or upkeep Orders and no stated range limit. The token is removed at the end of the Order in which Pilot-X becomes Null. No stacking multiple RemDriver tokens on one REM. Jotum is a TAG and is ineligible.

**Sierra Thunderbolt is the default:** 18/0, B3 Total Reaction, 360º Visor, now BS13/PH13/BTS6. A cheap but credible repeated ARO with improved Dodge and hacking resistance. Still ARM0 and vulnerable to elite attackers and vision control. HMG is an optional B4 upgrade for 5 points and 1 SWC. See [Sierra](sierra-trbot.md).

**Bulleteer Spitfire is the preferred active alternative:** 22/1, BS13, Mim(-6), PH13, BTS6; can Duo with a Karhu or eligible Wildcard (e.g. Engineer support), subject to the native chart. See [Bulleteer](bulleteer.md).

### Hacker: Zero Pain + BTS6

WIP13, BTS6 and Immunity (Shock) make Pilot-X a resilient backline specialist and Hacker for PanO. Zero Pain is B2 even in ARO and imposes -3 WIP on opposing Comms Attacks; a successful Face-to-Face defense cancels the attack. It is non-lethal and protects Pilot-X herself, not other REMs, and does not replace offensive hacking programs. Use the normal Hacking Device for Spotlight, Oblivion and Carbonite as appropriate. Keep her in Total Cover when possible; conventional attacks and going Null threaten both the hacker and her REM buff.

### RacerBots: exceptional 8-point bodies

Take both by default. Each adds a Regular Order, moves 8 inches on its first Move and 12 inches on a double Move, has S2 geometry and can Dodge 4 inches. Each carries three Deployable Repeaters, a disposable PARA Mine Dispenser and a Light Riotstopper. Mine Dispenser shoots on BS11 (14 in its +3 range band); Riotstopper threatens immobilization directly. Bangbomb (+4), ECM: Hacker (-3), 360º Visor and Remote Presence add utility.

- Project a persistent hacking network into separate lanes or around objective approaches; Repeaters remain after the carrier dies. Unlike a native Repeater, a carried Deployable Repeater requires Place Deployable and becomes active at the end of that Order.
- Use PARA Mines and Riotstopper to deny approaches, immobilize valuable enemies or force Orders spent on avoidance and recovery. They are primarily control tools, not lethal attackers.
- S2 and mobility help reach concealed positions, but No Cover means no Partial Cover benefits. ARM0, STR1, no Mimetism and hackability make direct firefights a poor use.
- Even when not advanced immediately, each supplies an inexpensive Regular Order. They are not mission specialists.

55/1 is the preferred starting module: four Regular Orders, a BS13 Total Reaction defender, hacking, persistent network projection and two fast disruption bodies. It is only 55 of 300 points. Additional Hackers are optional rather than prerequisites: Nøkken KHD 25/0 for counter-hacking, Locust HD/KHD for Pitcher and complementary hacking, or Knight of Justice HD 45/.5 for durable hacking. Clipper Guided is conditional on a deliberate Spotlight plan; Karhu Engineer/Machinist and Palbots can recover REMs. Avoid spending all the list's Orders on network setup at the expense of mission scoring.

## Agnes comparison and limits

Agnes RemDriver 16/0 is a cheaper linked specialist if all that is wanted is a pure REM buff; its existing pinned profile has +1 BS/+5 CC (not independently reverified against a new screenshot). Pilot-X offers the stronger fixed BS13/PH13/BTS6 buff, hacking, and access to the RacerBots for 5 additional points and 1 SWC before bots. Agnes CoC is a different role. Both RemDrivers can support different eligible REMs but cannot stack on the same one.

The 1 SWC, three list slots for the full team, and need to protect the driver matter. Pilot-X has no Fireteam access and should not be exposed unnecessarily. These limits do not outweigh the package's value in ordinary WinterFor lists.

## Final selection

- **Pilot-X: must-have (S-tier).**
- **Two RacerBots: near-automatic; take fewer only for a concrete list constraint.**
- **At least one suitable combat REM: strongly recommended; add one specifically for RemDriver if necessary.**
- Default to Sierra Thunderbolt for economical defense or Bulleteer Spitfire for an offensive plan. Additional hacking and engineering support depend on the mission and rest of the army.
