# Hannibal — Svalarheima's Winter Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: S-tier Vulkan 24/0 — near-must-have in an offensive WinterFor Alpha Fireteam, even if another close-range attacker is already present.** Order-efficient force multiplier first, secondary close-range attacker second.

## Why Vulkan is worth taking even with another close-range attacker

- **Two Orders for 24 points:** one Regular Order and one personal Tactical Awareness Order per turn while operational. The latter can activate the entire Fireteam with Hannibal as leader; it is not a freely transferable Regular Order. Use it for movement, Sensor or his own attacks. Nominally 12 points per generated Order.
- **Counterintelligence:** can preserve one additional first-turn Regular Order when the opponent uses the relevant strategic Command Token option. Army-wide utility, not a third recurring Order; works even outside a Fireteam.
- **Sensor / WIP14:** embedded anti-marker capability without having to move a separate Sensor unit with the team. Pathfinder still has independent scoring and repeater utility.
- **NWI:** additional resilience and a better chance of maintaining Fireteam composition; ARM2 and absence of printed Shock Immunity are caveats.
- **Nanopulser (+1B):** two direct templates in active turn, one in ARO. Excellent against high Mimetism, nearby enemies or low BTS; Special Dice do not add direct templates.
- **Vulkan Shotgun:** B2, AP and Continuous Damage, +6 inside 8 inches; excellent secondary close-range gun capable of threatening durable targets.
- **BS Attack (+1SD):** one intrinsic Special Die plus one from FT2 or higher. Benefits active attacks and AROs, including Flash Pulse. Special Dice improve reliability but are discarded rather than producing additional hits.
- **Flash Pulse:** uses WIP14 (not BS14), with favorable range at 8–24 inches; good non-lethal defensive coverage outside shotgun range. Breaker Pistol adds an alternative attack against low BTS.
- **Non-Hackable MI:** useful alongside hackable ORCs; not a specialist or CoC.

Even if another member already handles close-range attacks, the combination of extra activation, Counterintelligence, Sensor, NWI, templates and defensive shooting justifies the slot. Prefer Hannibal when planning an offensive Alpha rather than treating him as a redundant shotgun.

## Comparison with Blade-Ops

| Feature | Blade-Ops FTO BSG 20/0 | Hannibal Vulkan 24/0 |
|---|---|---|
| Order economy | Regular + TacAware | Regular + TacAware; Counterintelligence |

| Defense | Mimetism (-3), Stealth, Terrain (Total), W1 | NWI, no Mimetism |
| Weapons | Boarding Shotgun, D-Charges | Vulkan Shotgun, BS Attack (+1SD), Nanopulser (+1B), Flash Pulse, Breaker Pistol |
| Utility | Terrain and D-Charges | Sensor, Counterintelligence |
| Composition | Fusilier | WinterFor Alpha |

**Prefer Hannibal to ordinary Blade-Ops BSG for general offensive support where the Fireteam choice permits.** Four points buy much more utility, ARO reliability and resilience. Blade retains Mimetism, Stealth, Terrain and Fusilier composition; Hannibal cannot replace those composition benefits. The Blade-Ops Engineer (23/0) remains a distinct, strong alternative for mission scoring, repairs and linked TacAware in Fusiliers, not an inferior non-specialist.

Hawkins BSG (26/0) offers true W2, BS14, Specialist Operative and strong CC; ORC BSG Firewall (28/0) offers ARM4, W2 and Firewall; Valkyrie (28/0) supplies dedicated melee. These are complementary roles rather than reasons to exclude Hannibal.

## Final selection

**S-tier Vulkan 24/0; near-mandatory in offensive Alpha, not mandatory across the entire sectorial.** In Jotum, Karhu Beta, Fusilier or Pilot-X/REM-centric lists without a meaningful Alpha package, his utility remains strong but the opportunity cost rises. Counterintelligence works solo, but his TacAware gains particular value when moving a team. Strategic Deployment is optional for 3 points where terrain permits safe forward staging; MMR is secondary because WinterFor has other excellent ranged weapons and no extra SWC on Hannibal's upgraded profile.

### Hannibal — Neoterran Capitaline Army (N5.3)

**High priority: basic Vulkan Shotgun (23/0) or MULTI Marksman Rifle with Strategic Deployment (+1 SWC, 32/+1).** Prefer the basic Vulkan over the non-specialist Blade-Ops BSG for general offensive Neoterra support. #### Why Hannibal is excellent value

- **Tactical Awareness:** personal extra Order each turn while operational; when spent in a Fireteam, Hannibal becomes leader and activates the whole team. Ideal for advancing the team and using his own weapons or Sensor.
- **NWI + Shock Immunity:** effectively two combat wounds, with no Shock shortcut to Dead at the first lost Wound. ARM2 is still modest; unlike true W2, entering NWI is a state with its own recovery and survival rules.
- **BS Attack (+1SD):** improves Face-to-Face reliability in active and reactive turns; stacks with FT2's +1SD. Special Dice are rolled and discarded, not extra actual Burst or hits.
- **Sensor / WIP14:** valuable anti-marker utility; somewhat redundant if a Blocker or Pathfinder already supplies it. Flash Pulse on Vulkan profiles also uses WIP14.
- **Wildcard, counts as both Bolt and ORC:** preserves composition bonuses in either native Neoterra package, unusual flexibility for a 23-point non-specialist.
- **Close-range tools:** Nanopulser (+1B) gives two direct templates in the active turn; excellent against Mimetism/poor BTS and for trades backed by NWI. Breaker Pistol is a genuine alternative, not filler: +3 within 8 inches, B2, PS8, AP targeting BTS/2, useful against high-ARM/low-BTS targets and on the MMR's bad close range. Hannibal is not inherently Hackable merely for being MI.
- Limitations: no Mimetism, MSV, Marksmanship or specialist status; no mines; mediocre CC and ARM relative to heavy infantry. Prefer a better primary gun to open difficult lanes.

#### Vulkan Shotgun — 23/0 preferred

BS13 with +6 range within 8 inches; B2, PS6, AP and Continuous Damage. Three active dice solo or four at FT2+, retaining at most two hits. Continuous Damage can remove multiple Wounds from one hit if successive saves fail. Not necessarily the best pure Face-to-Face shotgun attacker, but excellent close-range threat plus order efficiency, Sensor, templates and resilience.

#### MMR +1 SWC — 32/+1 preferred when SWC matters

For only three points over the ordinary MMR (29/0), gain +1 SWC and Strategic Deployment. At 300 points this raises the normal allowance from 6 to 7 SWC, independently of the Lieutenant choice; it can stack with other legal positive-SWC profiles. Excellent when expensive Swiss, TAG, sniper or other weapons/hackers push the list over its allowance. If choosing between basic Vulkan and MMR SD, the actual difference is nine points; the additional range and SWC must both earn their place. NCA has numerous zero-SWC alternatives, so this is not universally mandatory.

The MMR is particularly effective rounding out an ORC HMG team or Bolt-based team. AP/Shock modes cover 8–24 inches at +3; DA mode gives a real deterrent in ARO, and Breaker Pistol/Nanopulser cover the MMR's negative range inside 8 inches. AP/Shock have Suppressive Fire capability (entering that state is a separate commitment; do not assume the linked shooting bonuses persist).

| Weapon / mode | Solo active dice (actual B + SD) | FT2+ active dice | FT2+ ARO dice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vulkan Shotgun | 3 (2+1) | 4 (2+2) | 3 (1+2) |
| MMR AP or Shock | 4 (3+1) | 5 (3+2) | 3 (1+2) |
| MMR DA | 2 (1+1) | 3 (1+2) | 3 (1+2) |
| Breaker Pistol | 3 (2+1) | 4 (2+2) | 3 (1+2) |
| Nanopulser (+1B) | 2 templates | 2 templates | 1 template |

Special Dice do not create additional saves by themselves; DA still imposes two saves per retained hit. The MMR's three-dice DA ARO is therefore one actual Burst plus two Special Dice. Direct templates make no shooting roll and receive no extra templates from SD. Do not expose Hannibal unnecessarily to apex active-turn attackers; his ARO is most useful covering approaches and complementary lanes.

#### Strategic Deployment — situational, not a must

Deploy Hannibal first as the initial Fireteam Leader; he and the team may deploy with FD (+4 inches). Scenario/tournament FD interaction can increase this under the printed rule. This can save movement and bring short-range weapons, Pitchers or specialists closer to useful positions. However, on many tables the available Total Cover and safe staging positions mean that the extra deployment distance provides little or no real advantage. Never move into an exposed position merely to use the skill.

For Vulkan, buy it only when three spare points are available and the planned team benefits. For MMR, the main attraction of the upgrade is the additional SWC; Strategic Deployment is a useful bonus.

**Bottom line:** default to basic Vulkan 23/0 instead of ordinary Blade-Ops BSG or ORC BSG for flexible non-specialist offensive support. Choose MMR SD 32/+1 if the army needs extra SWC and/or the team benefits from a secondary mid-range gun. NWI, Shock Immunity, Tactical Awareness, stacked Special Dice, double Nanopulser, Breaker Pistol and dual Bolt/ORC composition make Hannibal a priority Wildcard even when Strategic Deployment adds nothing.
