### Captain Uma Sørensen — Neoterran Capitaline Army (N5.3)

**Verdict: Generally mediocre / optional. Consider the 27/0 Camouflage profile for objective-heavy missions; usually skip the FTO and Lieutenant profiles.** Her value derives primarily from a real roster gap: she is NCA's only FWD8 specialist with reusable Camouflage. This is not a must-take or a premium gunfighter.

#### Why consider Camouflage Uma?

The 27-point profile is not especially efficient in absolute terms: FWD8 rather than Infiltration, Mimetism (-3) rather than (-6), one Wound, ordinary BS13 and WIP13. It is nevertheless a relatively conventional midfield skirmisher in a sectorial with very few alternatives.

- Deploys about eight inches beyond the DZ as a Camouflage marker and contributes her Regular Order if deployed normally in marker state.
- Can return to Camouflage after attacking, taking objectives or laying mines, when the rules permit. Unlike a Locust relying on Hidden Deployment, this protection is repeatable.
- B4 SMG is credible against softer targets at close range. The Silenced Pistol is B3 active with AP+Shock ammunition against halved BTS, providing an alternative to the SMG's normal ammunition.
- E/M Mine threatens heavy infantry, TAGs and other susceptible targets and can help defend an objective after capture. It is not Minelayer.
- Climbing Plus enables vertical shortcuts, rooftop access and alternative engagement angles; no Partial Cover while on a vertical surface.
- Combat Instinct ignores Surprise Attack MODs and negates enemy Stealth against her, but does not confer MSV, Sensor, Sixth Sense or melee proficiency.
- MI with no hacking device: not inherently Hackable. Shock Immunity improves recovery prospects, not the number of wounds.

Prefer using concealment and terrain to avoid unfavorable firefights rather than expecting Uma to carry the attack. She is a scoring and opportunistic attack piece. If remaining exposed after a run, Suppressive Fire with Mimetism and Partial Cover can impose a combined -9 on applicable opposing BS Attacks; this is an alternative to returning to Camouflage, not a combination of both states.

The three practical affordable choices for general mission scoring are Uma, Locust and Garuda. The Blade is expensive and mission-specific; Swiss is a different investment category. Hexa hackers have Hidden Deployment and Camo but no forward deployment. The Locust Camo (1 Use) Hacker is a literal FWD8 camouflaged specialist alternative, but cannot re-enter Camouflage after revealing; Uma is the only reusable FWD8 Camo specialist.

- **Uma:** early objective access and repeated concealment; does not have to withhold her Order to remain concealed.
- **Locust Specialist:** cheaper, stronger Mimetism and hidden scoring or mine coverage; normally withholds its Regular Order while Hidden until revealed. Can deploy openly instead if the Order matters more.
- **Locust Hacker/KHD:** prefer when hacking or repeater projection is the mission plan.
- **Garuda FO:** seven points cheaper, very mobile, attacks/scoring from an edge or jump landing, but contributes no pooled Order while off-table. No guaranteed early midfield marker.
- **Swiss KHD:** if the list already wants the premium heavy fighter and specialist resilience, Uma may be redundant.

#### FTO profiles: competent but crowded out

The 23/0 SMG option is the better FTO purchase: B4, E/M Mine, WIP13 specialist, Mimetism (-3), and X Visor to mitigate unfavorable range bands. The 25/0 Breaker Combi offers B3 AP against halved BTS and a better native 8–16" range band. X Visor improves negative range MODs by 3 (e.g. -3 to 0, -6 to -3), not positive range bands. Neither has Tactical Awareness, NWI, MSV or Marksmanship. Climbing Plus is less valuable when the rest of the Fireteam cannot follow.

#### Lieutenant

No extra Lieutenant Order, Strategos, Inspiring Leadership or additional SWC. The 25-point FTO Lieutenant risks command on a one-Wound active specialist. Prefer a cheap concealed Fusilier/Auxilia, a Bolt if a linked LT is desired, or an applicable Aquila/Maximus profile for actual command benefits. Take Uma LT only if already selecting the Breaker Combi and the list specifically benefits from the arrangement.

#### Final recommendation

**0–1 Uma; medium/low collection priority.** Take Camo Uma for objective-heavy missions that specifically reward forward deployment and recurring marker protection. Otherwise, prefer cheaper Locust/Garuda scoring alternatives or existing specialists. Usually skip both FTOs and the LT; Blade-Ops, Bolts and Hannibal offer stronger linked role compression.
