## Updated selection

- The FD profile saves one point and gains Surprise Attack (-3). The shotgun changes from B3 to B2+1SD: still three active dice, but only two retained hits; the selection die also helps its eligible reactive attack. It is not a straight damage upgrade.
- Both options now have Trench-Hammer and AP CCW PS5. A lower PS is more threatening, a higher PS less; apply the printed values rather than assuming both weapons improved.
- FD (+20), Decoy (2), Climbing Plus, Mim-3, CC23/MA2, WIP13 Specialist and TA compress deployment, movement, assault and scoring into a 28-point body. The initial Surprise Attack can stack with Mim-3 when applicable; it is not permanent. W1 and Frenzy remain limitations.
- FTO continues to provide a useful combat specialist/CC escort with personal TA in a mobile team, including the previously evaluated Karhu Beta Haris. TA must be spent with Gunnar as leader, not to let another member shoot.
- Prefer FD when an independent forward specialist fills a gap; FTO when the team needs his particular package. Collection: one model.

### Role and trade-offs

Gunnar compresses a competent gunfighter, WIP13 specialist, CC23/MA2 melee threat and extra personal Order into a non-Hackable infantry body. Mimetism (-3), Climbing Plus and flexible ammunition make him a useful escort rather than a pure melee missile. Berserk is a situational way to threaten difficult melee targets, not a default attack because it can expose his single Wound to a trade.

Tactical Awareness can activate his Fireteam with Gunnar as its leader, but cannot directly fund another member's shooting as leader. His main limitations are W1, lack of a marker state, no native visibility control, Frenzy and a 30-point price. Immunity (Shock) helps preserve the opportunity for recovery, not the number of hits he can withstand.

### Independent shotgun profile

FD8", Decoy (2), B3 Boarding Shotgun, Mimetism (-3), Climbing Plus and Tactical Awareness make this a useful independent forward assault specialist. It begins closer to the fight than the FTO and can attack from a separate angle. Decoy is not reusable Camouflage, however, and does not provide equivalent ongoing protection. The loss of Fireteam access for a mere one-point discount limits its general appeal. Use only when its deployment advantage directly supports the mission.

### Listbuilding conclusion

- **Strong pick:** Karhu offensive Beta Haris, especially when the team needs both melee protection and a second specialist.
- **Good pick:** offensive Fusilier Core when its movement, scoring and close assault plan justifies the premium.
- **Situational:** independent FD8 shotgun assault.
- **Skip:** defensive Core filler or pure CC attacker when cheaper dedicated alternatives meet the requirement.

Default to zero unless the list benefits from the full package. Gunnar's order economy, specialist status and composition flexibility are his real selling points; his single Wound and opportunity cost prevent him from being a universal selection. Proxy-test before purchasing, consistent with the existing WinterFor references.
