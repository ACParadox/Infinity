# Jotums — Svalarheima's Winter Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: S-tier / A+ to S-tier value. Must-own, not must-field.** WinterFor's only TAG is an excellent heavy assault, anti-armour and positional-control platform. At 82 points it costs only 13 more than a standard Tikbalang (69/1.5). The premium buys not just extraordinary durability but a **MULTI HMG** with B4 AP in the Active Turn and unlimited B1 EXP in ARO, plus a Panzerfaust. The lack of Mimetism, Climbing Plus and Fireteam access is the meaningful tradeoff. Usually take zero or one; two is a specialized matchup-dependent investment.

The Jotum costs only **13 points / 18.8% more** at the same SWC. The Tikbalang is better at winning conventional FtF through Mimetism, accessing elevated attack angles, and deploying mines. Its Fireteam access can also improve efficiency. The Jotum is much harder to damage, substantially more lethal into armour per successful HMG hit, and far more threatening in ordinary ARO. This is a reasonable trade, not an excessive durability tax.

## MULTI HMG: a major offensive upgrade

| Weapon / mode | Active B | ARO B | Ammo | Base PS | Effective PS with SR-1 |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| MULTI HMG AP | 4 | 1 | AP | 5 | 4 |
| MULTI HMG Shock | 4 | 1 | Shock | 5 | 4 |
| MULTI HMG anti-materiel | 1 | 1 | EXP | 5 | 4 |
| Panzerfaust | 1 | 1 | AP+EXP | 6 | 5 |
| Heavy Flamethrower | 1 | 1 | Normal; Continuous Damage | 6 | 5 |

SR-1 affects enemy Saving Rolls, not FtF target numbers; effective PS here is shorthand for the save calculation. HMG and Panzerfaust have a +3 range band at 16–32"; both suffer -3 at 32–48". Panzerfaust is Disposable (2). The flamethrower is a large direct template. The HMG AP and Shock modes support Suppressive Fire.

The main advantage is retaining **full B4 with AP** instead of paying for anti-armour performance through lower Burst. Use Shock against appropriate vulnerable infantry and EXP mainly for reactive fire or anti-materiel requirements.

Example: ordinary HMG hit against an ARM8 target in partial cover, with SR-1 on both attackers. Tikbalang HMG forces a save against ARM8+3, for 25% failure; Jotum AP mode halves base ARM to 4 before cover, for 45% failure. That is **80% more expected STR/W damage per successful ordinary hit**, before FtF odds. Mimetism still matters: this is not an 80% improvement per Order or a guarantee of winning the duel. Immunity (ARM), other special rules and cover can change the calculation.

The Jotum's lack of Mimetism or MSV means it is not automatically the best choice for clearing every opposing apex ARO. Its gun remains excellent; use Nisse MSV2, Karhu, Vargar Albedo/Disco or a Bulleteer when their specific modifiers solve a difficult engagement more efficiently.

## Durability and repair

Illustrative chance of losing one STR per ordinary successful PS5 hit, in partial cover and without extra save modifiers:

| Target ARM | Normal | AP |
|---|---:|---:|
| Jotum ARM10 | 10% | 35% |
| ARM8 TAG | 20% | 40% |
| Tikbalang / ARM6 TAG | 30% | 45% |

- ARM10 in cover is particularly oppressive for ordinary guns and direct templates where applicable; AP reduces the advantage substantially but does not eliminate it. Direct templates normally deny the cover ARM bonus.
- **Immunity (Critical)** removes additional saves caused by critical hits, not the critical hit itself, and does not neutralize AP/DA/EXP or protect against Comms Attacks.
- BTS9 is a substantial defensive improvement against applicable hacking/BTS attacks, but E/M, Oblivion, Carbonite, Total Control and other disabling tools remain relevant. ECM: Guided (-6) specifically counters Guided attacks, not ordinary hacking.
- Remote Presence provides two Unconscious levels; a successful Engineer roll or GizmoKit roll can restore the necessary STR to leave Unconscious, and Command Tokens can reroll failed Engineer WIP rolls. This creates an enormous tempo swing if the opponent spends many Orders downing the TAG only for it to return.
- CC specialists, Monofilament, PARA/immobilization, E/M and concentrated AP/EXP are real counters; CC18 and AP CCW (PS3) do not make the Jotum a dedicated melee fighter. S7 also makes some paths, concealment and repair positioning awkward.

## ARO and positional domination

The Jotum has a superior ordinary reactive gun package to a normal HMG TAG. B1 EXP MULTI HMG imposes three ARM saves per ordinary hit with no ammunition limit. Panzerfaust imposes three saves against half ARM at effective PS5, particularly threatening to high-ARM opponents. Choose ammo based on the target's actual protection and remaining disposable charges. The Heavy Flamethrower deters close infantry and can trade a potential STR for an enemy Wound. Cover and AP Suppressive Fire can also create excellent midfield area denial.

Do not leave it exposed to every opposing premium gun simply because it is tough. The most oppressive use is to eliminate genuine anti-TAG counters with whichever friendly attacker has the best matchup, then advance the Jotum to occupy space and force ordinary enemy weapons to waste Orders. Preserve retreat and Engineer routes. The opponent can sometimes bypass it and attack the order pool instead; screen vulnerable approaches.

## Lieutenant choice

The regular profile is preferred by default: 82/1.5 with Tactical Awareness, a protected Fusilier LT (10/0) and Karhu NCO convert the otherwise idle Lieutenant Order into a useful Karhu/Fireteam activation without risking command on the Jotum.

The 82/2 Lieutenant has both its own Tactical Awareness and ordinary Lieutenant Order, allowing two personal extra activations if used by Jotum. It does **not** have Lieutenant (+1 Order). Agnes CoC at 18/0 makes aggressive Jotum leadership much safer; Karhu NCO and the Jotum compete for the same ordinary LT Order. Pick the LT version for a deliberate Jotum-centric order plan, not merely because its point cost is unchanged.

## Opportunity cost and list selection

At 300 points, an 82-point Jotum is 27.3% of the list. A useful comparison is Karhu Feuerbach (34/1.5) + Karhu Engineer (25/0) + Vargar FO Disco (24/0) = **83/1.5**. That distributed alternative gives three Regular Orders, two specialists, NCO, mine/vision/engineering tools and legal Fireteam options, but cannot absorb incoming fire or occupy contested space like a Jotum. The Jotum contributes one Regular Order and one personal Tactical Awareness activation. These are alternative allocations, not mutually exclusive list concepts.

Two regular Jotums cost 164/3, about 55% of a 300-point list. This may overwhelm opponents who lack anti-TAG tools, but it doubles the same vulnerabilities and reduces mission reach. Usually one is enough.

**Preserve dissent:** existing [WinterFor listbuilding research](../svalarheima-listbuilding.md#tag-jotum-is-strong-but-the-ranking-genuinely-splits) records Swiss Miss S-tier, Lockie's positional-bully view, Loss of Lieutenant's high-B/contested-A assessment due to AP prevalence, and Shepherd's view that it is excellent but optional on specialist-heavy missions. The user's revised S-tier assessment explicitly weights the reasonable 13-point Tikbalang premium and the MULTI HMG upgrade in addition to extreme resilience; it does not erase those competing views.

**Default:** one Jotum when the mission rewards durable heavy assault, anti-armour fire or positional control. Support it with an Engineer and complementary attack/mission tools. Karhu and Pilot-X remain higher general-purpose listbuilding priorities; the Jotum is not mandatory in every WinterFor list, but is excellent value when its role matters.
