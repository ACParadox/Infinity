# Oktavia Grímsdóttir — Svalarheima's Winter Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: C tier overall — skip Missile Launcher by default; Heavy Rocket Launcher is a niche pick for a defensive Fusilier Core.** Normally 0 copies. Vargar, Nisse, Karhu and Knight of Justice offer stronger alternatives for most lists.

## Weapons and skills

**Important N5.3 distinction:** BS Attack (+1B) increases real Burst only in the Active Turn, not ARO. An eligible FT2+ adds +1SD in either turn, which is an extra selection die, not an extra hit.

| Weapon | Optimal range | Active B | ARO B | Ammunition |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| ML | 24–40" | 2 | 1 | AP+EXP hit or EXP circular template |
| HRL | 16–32" | 3 | 1 | Continuous Damage; circular template in blast mode |
| Contender | 8–24" | 3 | 1 | T2 |
| Heavy Pistol | 0–8" | 3 | 1 | Shock |

- ML threatens armor and multi-Wound targets through AP+EXP; its B2 active attack and BS12 are not attractive compared with stronger dedicated guns.
- HRL saves four points, gains Sixth Sense and offers a B3 active template-capable attack, but is less reliable against heavy armor.
- The B3 T2 Contender gives a meaningful secondary active weapon at medium range; one unsaved T2 hit causes two Wounds. Heavy Pistol covers close engagements.
- Albedo (-3) penalizes enemy MSV/Marksmanship LoF attacks; no protection against ordinary shooters. Combat Instinct ignores Surprise Attack MODs and negates Stealth against Oktavia.
- Sixth Sense on HRL allows retaliation against attacks from outside the normal LoF arc and removes the usual visibility penalty when shot through a Zero Visibility Zone; it does not permit unrestricted shooting through smoke or grant permanent 360-degree LoF.
- Dogged can force an additional enemy Order after a single nonlethal wound, but Oktavia dies at the end of that Player Turn once activated; Shock or sufficient damage can bypass it. She cannot be healed once Dogged is activated. Not equivalent to W2/NWI.
- Courage keeps her on a desired lane after surviving attacks. No Mimetism, low PH/ARM and BTS0 remain weaknesses. Neither profile supplies mission scoring or additional Orders.

A native Fusilier is mandatory. Counts-as Fusilier members can substitute where legal, changing costs. Oktavia prevents a five-member Fusilier Core from reaching FT5; HRL's innate Sixth Sense compensates for that loss only on Oktavia herself. In FT4, her effective BS is 13 (16 in the optimal range before opposing MODs). A small Haris preserves the Core slot but commits one of the two Haris allowances.

## Competition in WinterFor

| Alternative | Pts / SWC | Why prefer it |
|---|---:|---|

| Nisse MULTI Sniper | 29 / 1.5 | BS13, MSV2, Mimetism(-3), better general-purpose long-range gunfighter |
| Karhu Feuerbach (+1B) | 34 / 1.5 | MSV1, Mimetism(-3), NCO, Climbing Plus, AP+DA active gun |
| Bøyg ML | 44 / 1.5 | BS14, Albedo(-6), W2/ARM5, Disco Baller, Beta access |
| Knight of Justice ML (+1SD) | 46 / 1.5 | BS14, W2/ARM5/BTS9, innate +1SD, Wildcard |

**Vargar HRL** is the strongest direct value competitor: four points cheaper, superior Albedo and BS, mobility and FO. Oktavia's distinct advantages are the extra active Burst, Sixth Sense, Dogged, Combat Instinct and Fusilier link access. Prefer Vargar unless those specific reactive or composition benefits matter.

**Nisse sniper** costs only three points more than HRL and offers much broader shooting MODs, plus MSV2 for hunting Mimetism and exploiting enemy smoke in appropriate situations.

**Knight of Justice ML** is the premium defensive upgrade: sixteen points more than Oktavia ML, BS14 and true two-Wound durability. In FT2+, KoJ rolls B1+2SD in ARO versus Oktavia's B1+1SD; both remain one actual Burst.

## Final assessment

| Role | Rating |
|---|---|
| Dedicated ARO | B− |
| Anti-MSV defense | B+ |
| Active gunfighter | C+ |
| Survivability | C+ |
| Fireteam utility | B− |
| Mission utility | D |
| Overall value | C |

Select HRL only when a Fusilier-anchored defensive team specifically needs midrange template coverage and anti-ambush/anti-MSV resilience. Otherwise choose a cheap Fusilier/Sierra, Vargar for anti-tech and scoring, Nisse/Karhu for active gunfighting, or KoJ for premium missile defense. Oktavia is not mandatory and is generally a skip in this crowded roster.
