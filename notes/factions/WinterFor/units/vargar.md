# Vargar Maximum Security Team — Svalarheima's Winter Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A+ overall; excellent mobile Alpha assault/utility unit. Disco Baller FO is the best Vargar profile (S-tier for its role), but Quinn is the preferred general-purpose Disco carrier for only 2 points more. Vargar is preferred for Alpha composition, Super-Jump assault, anti-MSV angles and Heavy Flamethrower coverage. Neither is mandatory if another carrier already covers Eclipse.**

## Purpose and limitations

Vargar is a mobile enabler rather than a premium primary shooter. Albedo (-6) is a powerful defensive modifier specifically against MSV opponents, not general Mimetism. Super-Jump provides vertical access and alternative angles; MOV6-2 is particularly valuable for Move + Shoot or Move + objective interaction. Both 6-2 and 4-4 cover eight inches on a normal double Move. Terrain (Total) helps in applicable terrain. ARM3 and Shock Immunity are respectable, but one Wound, no universal defensive shooting modifier, no marker protection and no deployment skill make conventional frontal trading inefficient. Non-hackability does not prevent Spotlight or other attacks that do not require the target to be Hackable.

### Disco Baller FO — 24/0

Best profile: WIP13 specialist, projected Eclipse, large direct template with Continuous Damage, Flash Pulse, Deactivator, Albedo and Super-Jump. Eclipse enables other attackers, protects crossings, supports CC approach and allows objective access without winning every gunfight. Eclipse blocks MSV too; it is not a smoke-shooting combination. The screen is established at Conclusion, not retroactively against AROs declared earlier in the deployment Order. Limited ammunition can be replenished with eligible Baggage; Deactivator can reactivate an existing allied Disco Ball under normal rules. Keep the carrier available for scoring after enabling the attack.

This is a stronger specialized carrier than Quinn in a mobile Alpha, but Quinn's general combat skills and Wildcard make him a better default when no Alpha/mobility plan exists. Both are excellent for their cost.

### K1 Marksman Rifle / E/M Minelayer — 26/0

Useful no-SWC secondary gun into heavy armor: K1 ignores target ARM, Marksman Rifle offers medium-range coverage, Super-Jump opens alternative angles and E/M Minelayer taxes approaches by HI, TAGs and REMs. No specialist or Disco; do not buy as a replacement for either. Particularly attractive in a list that already has its primary gun and needs anti-armor redundancy or Alpha composition.

### Heavy Rocket Launcher FO — 22/1.5

Cheap long-range impact-template weapon with Continuous Damage, strong anti-MSV matchups through Albedo and alternative angles through Super-Jump. Light Shotgun protects close range; FO scores after the attack. A credible secondary gun/ARO and natural partner for the Disco FO. SWC is substantial for a one-Wound secondary shooter: prioritize Karhu Feuerbach for general premium ranged offense unless the Vargar assault plan or mission justifies HRL.

### Sensor / WildParrot — 27/0

Sensor and Triangulated Fire help against marker/modifier defenses; WildParrot projects E/M disruption and HFT covers close approaches. Good in a dedicated anti-midfield plan but no specialist. Pathfinder and Hannibal already offer Sensor; Karhu has E/M tools and Peacemaker/RacerBots provide complementary battlefield control (RacerBots' PARA mines are not E/M). With the preferred Pilot-X and RacerBot package, often redundant in the list's overall disruption budget. Not a default purchase.

### Spitfire — 26/1.5

Usually skip. Karhu Feuerbach (+1B) at 34/1.5 offers MSV1, Mimetism (-3), NCO, Climbing Plus and an AP Mine for eight additional points. Bulleteer Spitfire 22/1 with Mimetism (-6) is another economical attacker, particularly with Pilot-X assigning BS13/PH13/BTS6. Vargar Spitfire's case rests on specific Albedo/Super-Jump matchups and Alpha membership, not generic gunfighting.

## Disco Baller comparison: Vargar vs Quinn

| Attribute | Vargar FO | Quinn FO |
|---|---|---|
| Pts/SWC | 24/0 | 26/0 |
| MOV / PH | 6-2 / 11 | 4-4 / 10 |
| CC / BS / WIP | 15 / 13 / 13 | 13 / 13 / 13 |
| ARM / BTS / VITA | 3 / 3 / 1 | 2 / 3 / 1 |
| AVA | 5 | 1 |
| Rifle | Combi | MULTI (AP/Shock/DA modes) |
| Close-range weapon | Heavy Flamethrower (large direct template, Continuous Damage) | Boarding Pistol (+1B), B3 hit mode or B2 small direct-template mode in Active Turn |
| Shared utility | FO, Flash Pulse, Disco Baller | FO, Flash Pulse, Disco Baller |
| Additional utility | Deactivator | D-Charges |
| Visual skills | Albedo (-6) | MSV1 |
| Defensive skills | Immunity (Shock) | Immunity (ARM), Warhorse |
| Mobility skills | Super-Jump, Terrain (Total) | — |
| Fireteams | Alpha native, counts as Alpha | Wildcard max 1, counts as Fusilier |

**Gunfighting: Quinn.** MSV1 cancels Mimetism (-3) and reduces Mimetism (-6) to (-3); Warhorse ignores the enemy's BS Attack (-X) skill (not general MODs such as Mimetism, Surprise Attack or Cover). MULTI Rifle gives AP against armor and DA in its B1 mode; Boarding Pistol gives credible close-range firepower. Vargar is the specialist anti-MSV attacker, imposing Albedo (-6) where applicable. Neither is a premium all-purpose main gun.

**Durability: Quinn overall.** Against ordinary ARM-based Normal or Shock attacks Vargar has one extra ARM. Quinn's Immunity (ARM) also normalizes qualifying AP, DA and EXP ammunition and ignores applicable Continuous Damage: AP retains full ARM, DA/EXP reduce to one save per noncritical hit. Both can still take critical extra saves. Warhorse prevents Isolation and preserves Regular status in Loss of Lieutenant/Retreat; it is not blanket immunity to other states or BTS attacks. Neither has a second Wound.

**Mobility and approach: Vargar.** Greater first Move, Super-Jump and Terrain (Total) are particularly valuable when delivering a Disco screen or escorting Shona. The large HFT template covers corridors and multiple targets; Quinn's smaller template has B2 in Active Turn, B1 in ARO, not B2 ARO. FT2 Special Dice do not increase direct-template Burst.

**Fireteam flexibility: Quinn.** Can join Fusiliers, Nisses, Alpha, Beta and Bulleteers teams with an appropriate native anchor, preserving composition in Fusiliers. Vargar is limited to Alpha but preserves Alpha composition and can itself supply the native anchor. Quinn is not an Alpha-counting equivalent.

**Decision:** Quinn is the better general-purpose Disco carrier for just two points more. Vargar is the better mobile Alpha escort, anti-MSV specialist and source of large templates. Use the team's native composition and movement plan rather than treating one as strictly superior. A second carrier is useful for separate assault groups or independent lane coverage, not automatic.

Bøyg at 44 points is another Albedo/Disco option with two Wounds, ARM5, BS14 and a heavier weapon, but is Hackable and not a specialist; buy for its gun and resilience rather than Eclipse alone.

## Alpha Fireteams

WinterFor has one Core, two Harises and unlimited legal Duos. Alpha allows Core/Haris/Duo, requires at least one actual Vargar or WinterFor ORC (required-choice set), allows up to three Vargars, up to five ORCs and one Karhu, plus chart-legal Wildcards. Vargar, ORC, Hannibal, Valkyrie and Infirmarer share Alpha equivalence; Shona and Quinn do not. FT levels below use the pinned matching-composition convention.

| Composition | Pts/SWC | FT | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vargar Disco24 + Hannibal Vulkan24 | 48/0 | 2 | Cheap assault Duo; TacAware, Counterintelligence, Sensor, Eclipse and FO. Hannibal MOV4-4 limits first Move. |
| Vargar Disco24 + Valkyrie HSG28 | 52/0 | 2 | CC delivery, large template, specialist; Valkyrie has no Super-Jump. |
| Vargar Disco24 + HRL22 + Shona22 | 68/1.5 | 2 | All Super-Jump/MOV6-2; two specialists, long-range impact templates and elite CC. Report #182 supports this team shape. |
| Vargar Disco24 + K1 Minelayer26 + Hannibal Vulkan24 | 74/0 | 3 | Zero-SWC utility Haris, anti-armor, mines, Eclipse, TacAware and Sensor. |
| Vargar Disco24 + Karhu Feuerbach34 + Infirmarer BSG Doctor17 | 75/1.5 | 2 | Main gun, NCO, FO, Doctor, Eclipse; mixed vertical movement, Karhu is not Alpha-equivalent. |

The 68-point mobile Haris is the most distinctive Vargar package; the 48-point Hannibal Duo is economical and leaves both Harises free. Do not treat the movement of a Super-Jump leader as automatically granting Super-Jump to every member. FT2 gives +1 SD on eligible BS Attacks; it does not add an ordinary Burst die or enhance direct-template placement.

## Final list selection

- **Vargar Disco FO: high priority when building Alpha or needing mobile visibility control; otherwise compare Quinn first.**
- K1 Minelayer: good secondary anti-armor/control, conditional.
- HRL FO: valuable for a dedicated Vargar attack team; SWC competition matters.
- Sensor/WildParrot: niche when the list lacks equivalent disruption.
- Spitfire: generally skip in favor of Karhu or Bulleteer.
- Recommended quantity **0–2** normally: one Disco Vargar if the architecture favors it; a second Vargar for a defined additional profile or a distinct assault group. AVA5 does not imply five per Fireteam.

The preferred Pilot-X package supplies hacking, REM enhancement and persistent network/control; Karhu supplies premium ranged offense; Quinn or Vargar supplies Eclipse, scoring and close support. These functions are complementary. The existing WinterFor listbuilding guide emphasizes Vargar as the default mobile Alpha carrier, and the existing Quinn note calls him optional because of Vargar competition. This unit note refines the choice: Quinn wins the unrestricted general-purpose comparison, Vargar wins the mobile Alpha-specific comparison. Those other notes are retained unchanged.
