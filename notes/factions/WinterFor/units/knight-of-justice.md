### Missile Launcher

BS14, ARM5/BTS9, W2 and native +1SD are a distinctive combination. Solo ARO B1+1SD (roll two, keep one); FT2–3 B1+2SD (roll three, keep one); FT4–5 also adds +1 BS. EXP mode makes a lost Face-to-Face dangerous. Do not confuse Special Dice with additional retained hits. As a non-counting Wildcard, Justice needs four matching Fusilier-equivalent partners to reach FT4 in a five-member Core.

| Fusilier package | Pts/SWC | Level |
|---|---:|---|
| Justice ML + Fusilier LT + basic Fusilier | 66/1.5 | FT2 Haris or Core |
| Justice ML + Fusilier LT + two basic Fusiliers + Fusilier Paramedic | 88/1.5 | FT4 Core |

The 88pt Core gives the ML BS15 and two Special Dice, not Sixth Sense. It is a premium defense, not a must-have: Pilot-X's upgraded BS13 Sierra and Karhu Feuerbach offer cheaper distributed reactive coverage. Avoid investing 46 points into a lane the enemy can bypass.

Take it for the intersection of durable frontline HD, MMR shooter and mission specialist. Normal HD is not a KHD/Trinity substitute. ### Knight of Justice — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A− overall; strong but not mandatory.** The Knight of Justice (KoJ) is a durable, fast, BS14, ARM5/BTS9, two-Wound Wildcard that **counts as a Knight Hospitaller**. Its best reasons to appear in an MO list are **a robust forward Hacker/Pitcher**, **an FO MULTI Marksman Rifle specialist that raises Crusade Fireteam levels**, or a **specific premium Missile Launcher ARO plan**. As a pure Hacker it is less efficient than **De Fersen** (who has **Trinity (+1SD)**), and as a pure MMR gunfighter it competes badly with the **Crux Knight FTO** (native **BS Attack (+1SD)** and Albedo). Normally play **zero or one**, not two by default.

#### What the chassis actually buys

- **ARM5 / BTS9 / W2 / cover:** markedly harder to incapacitate than an ordinary rifleman or HI with BTS3–6. This does not make it immune to Oblivion, Isolation, powerful ammunition or enemy KHD/Repeater activity.
- **MOV 15–5 cm:** good first Short Movement Skill for moving into an MMR/Spitfire band, crossing a short exposure or scoring an objective. The longer initial step matters more than the total Move+Move.
- **BS14 / CC23 / MA2 / DA CCW:** respectable shooting and emergency close combat, but no blanket Mimetism/MSV/Marksmanship advantage. It wins by selecting engagements, retaining cover and working with a Fireteam.
- **No Frenzy or Impetuous:** reliable cover-based presence across turns, a real advantage over Frenzied Hospitallers and Impetuous/Limited-Cover Teutonics.
- **Number 2:** additional Fireteam leadership resilience. Unlike a pure defensive placeholder, this is on a forward-capable attacker.
- **Wildcard / counts as Hospitaller:** central reason to take it over similar profiles. This equivalence can raise **Crusade** Fireteam levels; it is **not** a generic Counts-as-Crosier/Teutonic in the Military Orders family.
- **D-Charges only on specific profiles:** Hacker, FO, FD Spitfire and FO LT; do not put them on the normal Spitfire, Missile Launcher or Spitfire LT.

**Core limitation:** 41–46 points buys a very strong model, but MO can purchase a **29-point Teutonic NCO Spitfire**, a **32-point Hospitaller HMG**, a **32-point Hawkins MMR**, **42-point De Fersen**, **46-point Crux FTO MMR**, or a much cheaper specialist. The KoJ must earn its premium through **combined** roles and Fireteam eligibility rather than generic stat efficiency alone.

#### Hacker + MMR + Pitcher (option 3, 45/0.5)

**Rating B+ overall; A− in a purpose-built forward cyber team.** This is a **gunfighting normal HD with self-contained repeater projection**, not a mini De Fersen. It can shoot with its MMR (PS6), project a Pitcher downfield, then threaten hackable enemies with the normal Hacking Device suite; BS14 and two Wounds help it survive the process. A nearby TinBot can further improve its defensive hacking position if the legal team shares the benefit.

##### Direct comparison: KoJ Hacker vs De Fersen HD

| Attribute / utility | **KoJ Hacker** | **De Fersen MULTI Rifle HD** |
|---|---|---|
| Points / SWC | **45 / 0.5** | **42 / 0.5** |
| BS / WIP | 14 / **13** | 14 / **14** |
| ARM / BTS / W | **5 / 9 / 2** | 4 / 6 / 2 |
| Main gun | **MULTI Marksman Rifle PS6** | MULTI Rifle |
| Pitcher | **Yes** | No |
| Anti-Hacker **Trinity** | **NO — no Trinity** | **Trinity (+1SD)** |
| Standard HD control | Oblivion, Carbonite, Spotlight | Oblivion, Carbonite, Spotlight |
| Close defense | Heavy Pistol, DA CCW, MA2 | **Light Flamethrower**, AP+Shock CCW, MA3 |
| Number 2 | **Yes** | No |
| Fireteam eligibility | Wildcard, counts Hospitaller | Same |

**Default choice: De Fersen.** He is **3 points cheaper**, **WIP14** rather than 13, and the **Trinity (+1SD)** upgrade makes him the far superior active and reactive *hacker-versus-hacker* choice. When MO needs a premium Hacker to defeat hostile hackers **and** disable HI/TAGs through Repeaters, De Fersen is the stronger role-compressed investment. KoJ's BTS9 is a defensive asset, not a replacement for a program it lacks.

**Choose KoJ instead when the Pitcher is on the model you actually plan to advance.** Its MMR prefers longer engagement distances than De Fersen's MULTI Rifle and the built-in Pitcher can use the same Orders/position to attack enemies, extend the Hacking Area and threaten heavy targets. It also has better raw ARM/BTS and Number 2.

**Do not buy KoJ as a 45-point disposable network relay.** An **18-point Order Sergeant FTO Hacker/Pitcher**, Trinitarian FO Deployable Repeater, Peacemaker/Pathfinder and other repeaters can build De Fersen's network more economically. That distinction underlies the existing podcast disagreement: **Ryan** likes the autonomous multifunction Justice; **Robert Shepherd** prefers **De Fersen + cheap projection** when the objective is merely extending the Hacking Area.

##### One, the other, or both?

- **One premium hacker, generic MO list:** **De Fersen**. Usually the best return on points and SWC.
- **One premium hacker, genuinely advancing Pitcher/gunfire Fireteam:** **KoJ can make sense**, if the army also has another plan to handle enemy Hackers and you value PS6 MMR/ARM5/BTS9.
- **Both:** Justified in a **deliberate cyber-pressure list**, where **KoJ actively projects repeaters and supplies a backup HD**, while **De Fersen performs the difficult WIP14/Trinity hacker duels and anti-heavy programs**. Not a default redundancy buy.
- **Both plus Order Sergeant Pitcher:** Usually too much duplicated projection; choose why each extra Pitcher matters.
- **Important**: A standard HD KoJ cannot replace a KHD or a De Fersen Trinity specialist when the problem is an enemy Hacker contest.

**Played proof of concept:** indexed **battle report #160** explicitly describes a **Teutonic TinBot + De Fersen + KoJ Hacker/Pitcher** Haris (106 pts / 1 SWC using pinned N5.3 loadouts). This is a documented use of **both**, not proof it is the optimal or common MO build. Teutonic is the required-choice Military Orders anchor; the two Wildcards count as **Hospitallers**, not Teutonics, so the three-body Haris is **FT2**, not FT3.

#### Forward Observer + MMR (option 4, 41/0)

**Rating B+ generally; A− when the list needs a durable FO and/or Hospitaller-equivalent team member.** It is a real **Forward Observer**, brings **Flash Pulse**, MMR (PS6) and **D-Charges**, and spends **zero SWC**. That makes it a defensive-capable midfield objective piece, FO-specific mission tool and potential Targeted setter.

However, it is not a cheap specialist, and MO has many cheaper mission solutions. The key comparison is not merely its stats but the **46-point Crux Knight FTO MMR**.

| BS / WIP / MOV | 14 / 13 / 15–5 | Same |

| Wildcard Counts-as | **Knight Hospitaller** | **No Counts-as-Hospitaller** |

**Crux wins the usual active gunfighter contest**: native +1SD improves its B3 MMR roll reliability, and **Albedo (-6)** creates favorable matchups against foes with MSV or Marksmanship. Drop Bears add objective-space and flank control. Albedo is **not general Mimetism** and does not penalize ordinary shooters without the relevant abilities.

**KoJ wins the narrower cases**:
1. **Five points saved** and **BTS9** against hostile hacking/technical attacks;
2. **PS6 MMR** rather than PS5, so it hits harder when the same ammunition gets through;
3. **Actual Forward Observer** instead of generic Specialist Operative, for FO-specific scoring, Flash Pulse and Targeted/Guided plans;
4. **Counts as Hospitaller in Crusade**, improving team level; **Number 2** also adds leadership resilience.

**The FT comparison is particularly meaningful:** **Hospitaller TinBot 27 + KoJ FO 41 = 68/0, Crusade FT2**, while **Hospitaller TinBot 27 + Crux FTO 46 = 73/0, Crusade FT1**. In the former Duo, KoJ gains FT2 +1SD; Crux already has native +1SD in the latter. In larger teams, count actual matching or Counts-as members rather than assuming all Wildcards raise Fireteam levels.

**Choose Crux FTO** when the goal is a **proactive MMR specialist who must win gunfights** and there is no special need for an FO or Hospitaller equivalence. **Choose KoJ FO** when the mission/Fireteam needs an actual FO, the tougher BTS9 body, PS6, or Crusade level contribution. **Consider Hawkins MMR 32/0** as a much cheaper Specialist Operative with less armour/BTS when FO-specific actions are unneeded.

**Not a must-take:** a 19pt Teutonic Specialist Operative, Montesa Paramedic or infiltrating Trinitarian may press objectives more efficiently. KoJ FO should be a **fighting and surviving** specialist, not a 41-point button tax.

#### Missile Launcher (+1SD) (option 1, 46/1.5): good or over-invested?

**Rating B+ in a well-supported linked defensive role; B overall.** This is a premium, visible long-range **BS14, ARM5/BTS9, two-Wound** Missile Launcher. **Native +1SD** is its important offensive/ARO discriminator; it is **not just a conventional B1 launcher**.

- **Solo ARO:** ML B1 + **one native special die** = roll two dice, keep one result.
- **FT2+ ARO:** native +1SD **cumulates** with the Fireteam L2 **+1SD** = roll three dice, retain one. **Not Burst 3, and never three retained hits.**
- **FT4+** may add the separate **+1 BS** where that Fireteam level is actually reached.
- Missile ammunition, template and anti-armour potential punish exposed expensive models and clustered targets.

**Why take it:** a table has a truly important long fire lane; the army wants a **hard-to-remove reactive anchor** and already has the team infrastructure; you can protect the ML from being isolated, hacked, bypassed or neutralized by non-line-of-fire options. Relative to an ordinary W1 ML, it survives mistakes and forces serious commitment.

**Why skip it:** **46 points + 1.5 SWC** is an unusually large budget for a gun that may take no useful AROs if the opponent avoids its arc. It has no Mimetism/MSV, no Hidden Deployment and remains a visible S2 model vulnerable to hacking/repeater pressure. In MO, a **Teutonic ML 28/1.5**, **Black Friar HRL 22/1.5**, **Order Sergeant FTO HRL 15/1.5**, or **Trinitarian MSR** can impose enough defensive cost while saving 18–31 points for Orders, scoring or a primary HMG. Note the Teutonic ML has its distinct **BS Attack (-3)** offensive/defensive modifier; don't treat the two units as same shooting rule with different BS.

**Budget perspective:** 46pts can instead purchase a **28pt Teutonic ML + 18pt Teutonic Panzerfaust**, a plausible two-body Military Orders defensive module with a second order/body; this is not equivalent resilience or native +1SD, but illustrates the opportunity cost.

##### Is the KoJ ML actually used in lists?

**Evidence is limited for current competitive MO, rather than evidence of an auto-include.** A [February 2025 MO list discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/InfinityTheGame/comments/1im2yaj) explicitly used **KoJ ML and Black Friar HRL** on ARO duty, but it predates the October 2025 MO profile refresh. The current repo's indexed **#160** MO hacker Haris and **#221** KoJ Spitfire LT mirror are **different KoJ profiles**. The repo's [cross-faction ARO ranking](../../MO/units/../../../list-building/aro-units-apex-and-value.md) rates the **linked KoJ ML especially highly in WinterFor**, which should not be represented as proof of frequent **MO** list inclusion.

**Conclusion:** a legitimate matchup/terrain-specific defensive star, **not enough observed post-refresh MO usage to call it a staple**. Start with cheaper distributed defense; bring the KoJ ML when a resilient main-lane anchor is itself part of the mission plan.

#### Fireteam legality, Counts-as and examples

**KoJ is Wildcard (max 2), counts as Knight Hospitaller.** Under the pinned chart:

1. **Crusade Duo/Haris/Core requires at least one actual Hospitaller FTO** (the KoJ, De Fersen, Joan and Hawkins **do not substitute for the minimum**). All qualifying Hospitaller-equivalent members count to the level.
2. **Military Orders Core/Haris/Duo requires a legal required-choice** — **Crosier / Teutonic / Santiago FTO**, as prescribed by the chart. Justice doesn't satisfy it by being a Wildcard. It does **not** count as Crosier or Teutonic for those level calculations.
3. **Order Sergeants** requires an actual Order Sergeant FTO; a Justice Wildcard does not replace that anchor.
4. Army still has **one Core and one Haris**, with Duos as separately permitted. A KoJ cannot turn a four-model Haris into a legal one, or import a non-FTO Crux variant into a link.

**Firewall note:** TinBot Firewall (-3) is relevant to a Hacker-led HI team. It does **not** stack by simply having an EVO Fairy Dust program or extra same-type TinBots; use the applicable MOD and Fireteam conditions. The **KoJ's own BTS9 does not grant Firewall**.

**Mixed-link opportunity cost:** A KoJ that cannot raise the Fireteam level may still be a good role carrier, but it can be the **41–46pt body preventing a cheaper team from reaching the next useful bonus**. Compare the whole composition, not just one knight's price.

#### Final use recommendation

- **Quantity:** Usually **zero or one KoJ**; two only for an intentional skew built around specialist/ARO roles with sufficient Orders.
- **Blind-list first choice:** **De Fersen over KoJ Hacker** when buying a premium hacker; **Crux FTO over KoJ FO** when buying a premium proactive MMR specialist.
- **Where Justice genuinely earns its slot:** The exact intersection of **ARM5/BTS9**, **no Frenzy**, **PS6 MMR or Pitcher/FO**, **Number 2**, **Wildcard** and **Hospitaller Counts-as**.
- **Profile priorities:** **Hacker/Pitcher** for forward cyber synergy; **FO MMR** for Crusade/FO-specific scoring; **FD Spitfire** if forward deployment or SWC savings matter; **ML** only as deliberate premium ARO; ordinary Spitfire and LTs usually behind alternatives.
- **Do not overstate:** The Justice is **not a second De Fersen**, since its Hacking Device **has no Trinity**; it is **not a Crux with the same active dice**; and the **ML is not a documented MO auto-include** simply because it is outstanding in a suitable linked WinterFor ARO package.

**Bottom line:** **A− overall, not mandatory.** KoJ succeeds in MO as a **specific role-compressing, Fireteam-compatible two-Wound specialist/attacker**, not as the default best Hacker, best MMR shooter or cheapest Missile Launcher.
