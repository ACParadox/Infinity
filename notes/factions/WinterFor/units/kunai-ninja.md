# Kunai Solutions Ninja — Svalarheima's Winter Force (N5.3)

**Verdict: B− overall / optional, 0–1.** The 26/0 AP + Shock Marksman Rifle (B+) is the preferred general-purpose profile. An interesting independent ambusher with reusable Camouflage, Hidden Deployment, Mimetism (-6), Surprise Attack and strong secondary close combat. Not a must-have: no advanced deployment, specialist role or Fireteam access. Prioritize Karhu, Pilot-X and Vargar first. Collection priority: medium.

## Profile selection

### AP + Shock Marksman Rifle — 26/0

Preferred general-purpose option. B3, PS7, +3 range from 8–24", combined AP and Shock effects, Suppressive Fire. Zero SWC is particularly attractive.

- Reusable Camouflage and Surprise Attack (-3) let the Kunai choose engagements and threaten both light infantry and armored targets.
- Mimetism (-6), cover and Suppressive Fire can impose a combined −12 on applicable opposing BS Attacks (within the state's range and other restrictions).
- BS12 and B3 are adequate rather than apex gunfighting; avoid MSV and unfavorable range bands. AP helps against armor but does not turn it into a premium anti-TAG gun.
- Excellent independent secondary attack axis, especially in lists already investing SWC in linked heavy weapons.

### MULTI Sniper Rifle — 28/1.5

A distinctive concealed long-range ARO. B2 in active turn, B1 in ordinary ARO, PS5, +3 range from 16–48", and DA/AP/Shock modes.

- Hidden Deployment allows the Ninja to wait for an important exposed target or create a crossfire the opponent could not see during deployment.
- DA forces two saves per hit; Mimetism (-6) and cover can impose −9 on conventional shooting.
- Can retreat and return to Camouflage on a subsequent active turn.
- BS12, low Burst, ARM1 and W1 are poor sustained-gunfighting characteristics; 1.5 SWC competes with stronger conventional WinterFor weapons.
- Surprise Attack is active-turn only and does not enhance the hidden reactive shot.

### T2 Thunderbolt + PARA Mine — 25/0.5

B2, PS6, +3 range from 16–32" and 0 from 32–48". T2 inflicts two Wounds on a failed ordinary save (the extra critical save inflicts only one Wound if failed).

- Threatens two-Wound HI, NWI and other multi-Wound troops with one failed save; best against targets without excessive ARM.
- Low Burst and lack of AP on this profile limit reliability against premium heavily armored opponents.
- PARA Mines provide non-lethal immobilization and defensive control after placement, but the Ninja is not a Minelayer.
- Useful as a concealed reactive ambusher and alternative to the more expensive sniper, especially when the table favors medium-range engagements.

## Why reusable Camouflage matters

The major differentiator versus Locust and Nøkken is sustained marker protection. A Kunai can move in Camouflage, attack with Surprise Attack, break enemy LoF and re-enter Camouflage with a legal Entire Order when the requirements are met. The next attack can benefit from Surprise Attack again.

Against a conventional opponent shooting back, Mimetism (-6), Partial Cover (-3) and Surprise Attack (-3) can combine to −12 before range and other modifiers, subject to the rules' MOD limits. Surprise Attack only affects qualifying opposing Face to Face rolls in the active turn; it is not an ARO bonus. MSV, Combat Instinct, Sensor and direct templates can undermine parts of this protection.

Hidden Deployment is optional:

- Deploy hidden for a surprise ARO or an entirely concealed reserve attack. The Regular Order is withheld from the pool while hidden and can be spent by the Ninja itself to reveal during its active turn.
- Deploy as a normal Camouflage marker if the pooled Regular Order and early movement are more valuable.
- Hidden Deployment cannot be regained after cancellation; Camouflage can be regained when eligible.

The main limitation is deployment position. Without Forward Deployment or Infiltration, the Kunai starts in its own deployment zone and requires orders to reach the midfield. Climbing Plus and Stealth provide alternative approaches but do not remove that cost.

## Close combat

CC21 with Martial Arts L3 gives effective CC24 before opposing modifiers, B1 +1 Special Die (not B2), and imposes −3 on the opposing Face to Face roll. Surprise Attack can impose a further −3 when attacking from a qualifying marker or hidden state. Dodge (+1") and Climbing Plus improve positioning.

The Shock CCW is a limitation against HI and TAGs: it lacks AP or DA damage. Close combat is a valuable secondary ability for opportunistic attacks, self-defense and dealing with vulnerable models, not the primary reason to take a Kunai. WinterFor already has dedicated melee specialists:

- [Shona Carano](shona-carano.md): dedicated melee threat with Fireteam access.
- [Gunnar](gunnar.md): strong melee plus specialist and Tactical Awareness utility.
- [Valkyrie](valkyrie.md): linked assault and melee role.
- Liang Kai: independent close assault and mobility.

### Kunai versus Locust MMR

Prefer the Kunai for sustained independent gunfighting at a similar price. The Locust starts farther forward, has strong Mimetism and its MULTI ammunition modes offer flexibility, but the MMR profile cannot return to Camouflage. The Kunai can repeat its surprise attack cycle and is much stronger in CC. Prefer the Locust when the forward starting position or a specialist/hacking-specific Locust loadout is the actual requirement.

### Kunai versus Nisse Sniper

The Nisse is the stronger conventional shooter and anti-Mimetism counter, especially linked. The Kunai is a hidden defensive threat. They can complement one another by covering distinct lanes, rather than directly replacing each other.

### Kunai versus Uma and Beasthunter

- Uma is preferable when reusable marker protection must also deliver objective scoring. Her FWD8 saves orders.
- Beasthunter is preferable when the job is inexpensive midfield defense, mines or a disposable marker threat rather than sustained ranged fighting.

**0–1 Kunai; prefer the 26/0 AP + Shock Marksman Rifle.** An optional independent attack vector that does not depend on Fireteam bonuses, Eclipse support or hacking infrastructure to exploit its core strengths. Take it when the list benefits from a concealed threat and repeatable marker attacks, not as a default objective runner. Prioritize Karhu, Pilot-X and Vargar for the core roster; add a Kunai when its distinct role is worth the orders and slot.
