## Why take Valkyrie?

### Elite melee countercharge

- MA3 gives +3 CC, opponent -3 in Face to Face rolls and +1 Special Die (not +1 actual Burst), including in ARO. Unmodified by other effects, she attacks at CC25 with B1 +1SD.
- Natural Born Warrior ignores negative MODs imposed by the opposing trooper in a CC Face to Face Roll when Valkyrie is targeted by CC Attack and declares CC Attack herself. This does not cancel the opponent's positive Martial Arts bonuses or actual Burst. It makes her especially useful against opposing elite melee specialists, including on her own turn if the target responds with CC Attack.
- Berserk (+3) allows a single Long Skill charge up to 8 inches, requiring LoF to the target when declared and legal Silhouette contact at the end of movement. CC28 before other MODs (22+3 MA3+3 Berserk); MA3's +1SD remains applicable. Berserk is not automatically a pair of unopposed Normal Rolls in N5.3. Do not confuse this with older editions.
- EXP CCW (PS=5) forces three saves per normal hit against an ordinary target; a critical adds a save. Strong against most targets, although Immunity can reduce this effect and Shona's AP+EXP is superior against high ARM.
- Dodge (+2in) gives 4-inch Dodge movement on success, useful for repositioning and reaching melee. Courage helps maintain position.

### Enhanced Immunity and NWI

ARM3/BTS3, NWI and Immunity (Enhanced) make Valkyrie more resilient than a typical one-VITA MI, particularly against special ammunition. AP loses its ARM-halving effect; DA/EXP normally become one normal save per hit; Shock does not bypass NWI; applicable Monofilament and Continuous Damage effects are ignored. Critical additional saves still apply, ordinary high-Burst damage remains dangerous, and applicable non-lethal/Stunned effects, Comms Attacks and combined ARM+BTS saving rolls are not all negated. She is not a two-VITA HI and NWI is not invulnerability.

See [Immunity](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Immunity.md), [Natural Born Warrior](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Natural_Born_Warrior.md), [Martial Arts](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Martial_Arts.md) and [Berserk](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Berserk.md).

### Shooting is secondary

Heavy Shotgun, Heavy Pistol and Grenades provide useful close-range options when melee is inaccessible. BS12 without Mimetism or a printed shooting MOD is not an elite ranged attacker. The MULTI Rifle option is rarely worth +5pts in a sectorial with many stronger mid-range guns. Keep the main ranged attacker responsible for clearing lanes.

- **Shona:** often the better pure offensive CC pick, particularly versus TAGs/HI, thanks to AP+EXP, MA4's actual B2, mobility and 6-point saving. Valkyrie buys stronger defensive CC, immunity, a Heavy Shotgun, Berserk (+3) and Alpha composition.
- **Liang Kai:** preferred independent assault if no Fireteam is needed; already starts forward and has a cheaper, more mobile profile.
- **Gunnar:** better order economy and general mission utility; valuable in Fusilier and Karhu teams, but less resilient in melee.
- **Hawkins:** stronger conventional close-range gunfighter and scoring specialist; cannot join Alpha. Choose when clearing and then pressing an objective is the main requirement.

### Disco Baller delivery

[Vargar FO Disco](../svalarheima-fireteams.md) supplies Eclipse to cross firing lanes, approach corners and reach CC against a vulnerable target. The Disco deployment must succeed and take effect before the protected movement Order. Eclipse blocks LoF even for MSV; ordinary Move + CC Attack does not require LoF once legal Silhouette contact is reached. Berserk, however, requires initial LoF and therefore cannot be declared against a target hidden by Eclipse. Use ordinary Move + CC Attack through the template, or Berserk from a position with LoF. Eclipse does not remove all CC/Dodge AROs, mines or other LoF-independent threats.

Valkyrie does not share Vargar Super-Jump, so routes that require it can split the team. If a linked Valkyrie declares Berserk as leader, members without Berserk Idle: check Fireteam coherency and plan for potential separation. Frenzy can also create Limited Cover/Impetuous drawbacks after she inflicts a wound; consult the Fireteam and Frenzy rules before using the extra activation.

## Limitations and final selection

No native forward deployment, marker state, Mimetism, Super-Jump, Climbing Plus or specialist skill; delivering her into melee costs Orders. Avoid paying for melee that will never be used, or asking BS12 to fight apex long-range shooters. Enhanced Immunity helps survive hits, not win shooting Face to Face rolls.

**0–1; prioritize the 28/0 Heavy Shotgun in an advancing Alpha team, especially with Vargar Disco.** Strong dedicated bodyguard and melee countercharge (S defensive CC / A offensive CC), A Alpha fit, B+ overall. Shona and Liang Kai are cheaper pure assault options, while Gunnar or Hawkins contribute broader mission utility. Skip the 33/0 MULTI Rifle unless the specific team genuinely needs its range band.
