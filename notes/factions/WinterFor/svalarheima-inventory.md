## Sectorial Inventory

### Lieutenant (LT), Chain of Command and Order economy
- **Fusilier LT 10/0:** cheap protected command; duplicate the ordinary Combi profile for an LT lookalike.
- **Hospitaller FTO LT 27/0** with MULTI Rifle/Firewall or **HMG LT 32/1**; **ORC LT 31/0** with Firewall or **HMG LT 36/1**: fighting command alternatives.
- **Knight of Justice LT:** Spitfire 42/1.5 or FO MULTI Marksman Rifle 41/0.
- **Uma FTO LT 25/0:** distinct from the non-FTO Camouflage option.
- **Jotum LT 82/2:** Tactical Awareness and standard LT Order, not LT(+1 Order).
- **Agnes CoC 18/0:** separate successor profile; **Hannibal 24–33/0** offers Counterintelligence, not CoC.
- **Karhu:** NCO, including Feuerbach 34/1.5; spends the LT Order without exposing the LT.
- **Tactical Awareness:** Jotum, Hannibal, Blade-Ops, Gunnar and the 17-point Pathfinder option; the latter cannot activate a Fireteam.

### Active gunfighters
- **Jotum 82/1.5:** MULTI HMG, ARM10/BTS9/3W; sole TAG family, no Fireteam.
- **Karhu Feuerbach(+1B) 34/1.5:** NCO, MSV1, Mimetism(-3), Climbing Plus.
- **Nisse HMG 31/1.5:** MSV2/Mimetism(-3) long gun.
- **ORC HMG 36/1.5 / Feuerbach 37/1.5:** two-Wound, Climbing Plus attack options.
- **Hospitaller FTO HMG 32/1.5; Knight of Justice Spitfire 42/1.5 or FO MMR 41/0:** durable combat options.
- **Bøyg Mk12 44/0:** two-Wound Albedo/Disco option with BS Attack(+1B).
- **Hawkins BSG(+1B) 26/0 or MMR 32/0:** close/medium assault specialists.
- **Hannibal MMR 30/0 or Strategic Deployment 33/0:** Tactical Awareness and support.
- **Bulleteer Spitfire 22/1 or Heavy Shotgun(+1B) 19/0:** Mimetism(-6) REM offense; Duo eligible.
- **Vargar Spitfire 26/1.5; Nøkken Spitfire 26/1.5:** mobile or forward-starting alternatives.

### ARO and deployment-zone defense
- **Knight of Justice ML(+1SD) 46/1.5:** premium linked missile.
- **Nisse MULTI Sniper 29/1.5:** MSV2/Mimetism(-3); Nisse link access.
- **Bøyg ML 44/1.5:** durable Albedo missile option.
- **Oktavia HRL 26/1.5 or ML 30/1.5:** Fusilier-team launchers.
- **Fusilier ML 15/1.5 or MULTI Sniper 16/1:** budget linked ARO.
- **Sierra Thunderbolt(+1B) 18/0 or HMG 23/1:** Total Reaction.
- **Fugazi 7/0 and Warcor 3/0:** cheap Flash Pulse coverage; Warcor is Irregular.
- **Beasthunter 9/0:** DZ Heavy Flamethrower, Panzerfaust and AP Mine; FTO label grants no link.
- **Machinist 20/0:** Armed Turret alternative to 17-point Engineer.
- **Kunai Ninja MSR 28/1.5:** Hidden Deployment ambush alternative.

### Midfield defense and forward scoring
- **Locust:** Hidden Deployment/Mimetism(-6); BSG Specialist 24/0, SMG/WildParrot 21/0, hacker alternatives below.
- **Nøkken FO BSG(+1B)/Panzerfaust 22/0; Shock Mine Minelayer 24/0.5:** FD(+20), Mimetism(-6), Bangbomb.
- **Uma non-FTO 27/0:** Camouflage, FD(+20), E/M Mine and Specialist Operative; FTO 23–25/0 is distinct.
- **Peacemaker 23–27:** FD(+20), native Repeater and selected Auxbot package; 24/0.5 Auxbot Minelayer, 23/0 BSG option has no Auxbot.
- **Beasthunter Camouflage 15–17/0:** FD(+20), templates and carried mines.
- **Vargar K1 MMR/ E/M Mine Minelayer 26/0:** Alpha midfield defense.
- **Gunnar non-FTO 29/0:** FD(+20), Decoy, BSG(+1B); not the 30-point linked FTO.
- **Kunai Ninja 26/0 AP Shock Marksman Rifle or 25/0.5 T2 Thunderbolt/PARA Mine:** independent Hidden alternatives.

### Hackers, Killer Hackers and network projection
- **Fusilier HD 15/0.5; Nisse HD 30/0.5; KoJ HD/Pitcher 45/0.5:** distinct budget, visor and durable Wildcard platforms.
- **Locust HD/Pitcher 29/0.5, KHD/Pitcher 27/0, HD one-use Camo 31/0.5:** distinct Hidden options with Firewall(-3); the last does not carry Pitcher.
- **Nøkken HD 27/0.5 or KHD 25/0:** forward cyber specialists.
- **Pilot-X 21/1 + one/two Mk-III RacerBots (29/1 or 37/1):** Zero Pain HD, RemDriver and fast Regular Repeater/mine delivery; same Combat Group and placement Orders.
- **Fusilier FO 12/0 and Tech-Bee 9/0:** carried Deployable Repeaters.
- **Fugazi, Pathfinder, Peacemaker, Bulleteer and appropriate Mulebot:** native Repeater coverage.
- **Mulebot EVO 14/0.5:** eligible supportware, not a normal Spotlight HD.

### Cheerleaders and force multipliers
- **Fusilier 10/0, Fugazi 7/0 (AVA3), Mulebot Baggage 8/0:** cheap Regular Orders.
- **Agnes Specialist 13/0 or RemDriver 16/0:** eligible REM buff, not Jotum.
- **Tech-Bee 9/0:** TAGCom, Specialist, Repeater and GizmoKit; not a full Engineer.
- **Pilot-X:** another RemDriver route; dependent Mk-III are Regular troops, not Servants.
- **Warcors and Beasthunters:** Irregular; **Palbot 3/0:** Peripheral with no independent Order.

### Close combat and vision control
- **Shona 22/0:** AP+EXP CCW, Wildcard; excellent melee escort.
- **Valkyrie HSG 28/0 or MULTI Rifle 33/0:** EXP, Natural Born Warrior and Berserk.
- **Liang Kai 21/0:** independent fast Regular/Impetuous CC attacker.
- **Hawkins 26–32/0; Gunnar 29–30/0; Hospitaller FTO 26–32:** melee-capable combat alternatives with distinct team access.
- **Vargar FO Disco 24/0; Quinn FO Disco 26/0; Bøyg 44/0–1.5:** projected Eclipse; distinguish Quinn's non-Disco MMR option.
- **Vargar/Bøyg/Oktavia:** Albedo; **Nisse MSV2**, **Karhu MSV1**, **Quinn MSV1** and **Nøkken MSV1 24/0** solve visual MODs differently.
- **Mulebot Baggage 8/0:** reload limited ammunition when legal.

### Doctors, Paramedics and repair
- **Infirmarer Doctor BSG 17/0 or Combi 20/0:** Wildcard, Alpha/Nisse counts-as.
- **Hospitaller FTO Doctor 30/0:** durable Beta option; **Trauma-Doc 14/0:** independent basic Doctor.
- **Fusilier Paramedic 12/0, Nisse Paramedic 28/0, Karhu Paramedic 24/0:** distinct linked recovery options.
- **Machinist WinterFor 17/0 or Turret 20/0:** Nisse-counting Wildcard Engineer.
- **Karhu Engineer 25/0; Blade FTO Engineer 23/0:** mobile linked Engineers.
- **Blade non-FTO Engineer 30/0:** FD(+20)/Decoy or Parachutist/Decoy, separate profiles.
- **Palbot 3/0:** Doctor/Engineer projection; no independent Regular Order.

### Specialists / objective play and DZ attack
- **Pathfinder FO/Sensor 14/0 or Tactical Awareness 17/0:** independent mobile scoring, no Fireteam.
- **Fusilier FO 12/0, Paramedic 12/0 or Commlink(+1) Specialist 12/0:** alternative cheap team profiles.
- **Vargar FO Disco 24/0 or FO HRL 22/1.5; Quinn FO 26/0:** mission/vision support.
- **Hawkins, KoJ FO 41/0, Uma, Locust, Nøkken, Agnes, Tech-Bee, Gunnar:** distinct specialist options; check selected profile.
- **Blade Parachutist Engineer 30/0:** airborne edge pressure, not Combat Jump; no FTO.
- **Liang Kai, Shona and forward Locust/Uma/Nøkken:** advance on enemy positions from their legal deployment, not Combat Jump.

### Anti-TAG, disabling and indirect fire
- **Karhu Feuerbach 34/1.5; ORC Feuerbach 37/1.5; Jotum MULTI HMG 82/1.5:** long-range anti-armor.
- **KoJ ML 46/1.5, Bøyg ML 44/1.5, Vargar K1 MMR 26/0:** additional armor answers.
- **Karhu Blitzen/E/Marat profiles, Hospitaller E/Mitter 26/0, Vargar E/M Mine, Uma E/M Mine, Blade E/Mitter 30/0:** disabling alternatives.
- **Nøkken FO/MSV1 Panzerfaust 22/24, Beasthunter Panzerfaust variants:** low-cost disposable anti-armor.
- **Clipper Guided ML 16/1.5:** needs actual Targeted delivery; 19/1.5 FTO-labelled ML(+1B) has no SWF Fireteam row.
- **Sierra and Mulebot Total Reaction 17/0:** independent reactive alternatives, not Guided.
