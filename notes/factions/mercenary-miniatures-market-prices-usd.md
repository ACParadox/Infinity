# Miniatures Market — Mercenary / miscellaneous category price reference (USD)

**Cumulative Miniatures Market price coverage after full-catalogue reconciliation (2026-10-30): 85 listings, USD $1100.85.** Original batch-specific counts/totals below remain unchanged for provenance. These are catalogue sums, not a buy list.


**Recorded / expanded:** 2026-10-30. **Source:** Two user-provided Miniatures Market Mercenary category product-text batches, attributed to [Miniatures Market — Infinity catalogue](https://miniatures.market/product-category/infinity/). **Currency: USD.** User-supplied price snapshot **(U)**, **not independently verified as live retailer stock or checkout pricing**. No product-specific source links/photos were supplied, so none are invented. Prices apply to the named listing or complete box, not each miniature, and exclude CAD conversion, taxes, postage, and duties.

**Availability:** Across two user-provided catalogue batches, this file now has **82 distinct product-title/price listings**: **81** explicitly labelled **“Add to cart”** and the original **Tanko Zensenbutai ($20.70)** with no visible availability label (**unconfirmed stock**). The second batch supplied **55 rows**, of which **9 exact name-and-price duplicates** of existing entries were skipped and **46 new listings/price variants** were added. **No entry in either batch was explicitly marked “Out of stock.”** Catalogue Add-to-cart text is not independently verified stock.

**One-each total for 82 listings: USD $1,058.35.** The **81 entries marked Add to cart total USD $1,037.65** (excluding the $20.70 Tankō). These are catalogue sums **not recommended purchase budgets**: character packs overlap, several items have alternative sculpts, and the category includes other factions.

**Scope:** This is the retailer's broad *Mercenary* category, **not a declaration that every product is a mercenary, legal in the same NA2 sectorial, or purchasable for JSA**. Example category mismatches: PanO Dronbots, Yu Jing Guijia, Tohaa Kiel-Saan, and JSA Domaru/Tankō. Check actual N5.3 [NA2 sectorial references](../../sectorials/na2/) and [JSA roster references](../../sectorials/jsa/).

## Key overlaps and purchase notes

- **Aftermath Characters Pack ($30.00)** names **Uhahu, Denma Connolly, Zellenkrieger K1llj0y, Zellenkrieger Raiza and external Zellenkrieger Ishinomori**. Four of those have separately priced singles: Denma **$6.20**, Ishinomori **$7.00**, Raiza **$6.20**, K1llj0y **$6.20**, totalling **$25.60** *without Uhahu*. See the [Nomads price reference](nomads-miniatures-market-prices-usd.md) for Uhahu singles; do not assume duplicates are required.
- **Druze Shock Teams ($27.00)** includes three Druze variants plus Arslan and Jethro by description. These should not be mistaken for five Druze troops. The description is truncated, so completeness is not independently confirmed.
- **Oktavia Grímsdóttir is listed twice** with differently styled titles and prices **$8.00 vs $18.30**. No SKU/photo comparison establishes equivalent sculpt, packaging, or why prices differ.
- **Warcors**: **Flash Pulse ($8.40)** and **Stun Pistol ($5.20)** are distinct sculpt/listing variants of a model playable in current Vanilla JSA, Ōban and Shindenbutai, not unique unit identities.
- **JSA relevance (original 36-row batch):** 11 entries in that batch are also recorded in [the JSA price reference](jsa-miniatures-market-prices-usd.md). **Sāchā / Monstruckers** are Shindenbutai, **Tankō / Yuan Yuan** Ōban only; **Domaru / Shinobu / Shikami / Karakuri** Vanilla and Ōban; **Mechazoid** Vanilla and Shindenbutai; Warcor across all three. The **Mechazoid Sokorentai** product's description incorrectly identifies a **GŬILÁNG Hacker**, so do not purchase without independently checking the listing.
- **Exclusive releases:** Shikami Limited Edition and Cube Jager OOP Limited Edition are edition-labelled by seller. Check physical versions rather than treating them as different army profiles.
- **No source confirms listed box contents for several lore-only entries** (Oktavia, Monstruckers, Karakuri, Guijia, etc.). Do not invent quantities; proxy choices require correct silhouettes and faction-appropriate recognizable models per [proxy guidelines](../miniature-proxy-guidelines.md).
