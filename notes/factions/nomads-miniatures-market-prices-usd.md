## Boxes, duplicate models and version cautions

- **New Corregidor half and Beyond Mazebreaker:** the **$21.65** three-figure Corregidor set and **$39.65 Beyond Operation Mazebreaker** share **Shifta (Thunderbolt), Coyote (MULTI Marksman Rifle), and BambaBot (Chain Rifle)**. The latter has six total models but the other three are cut off. Do not count both as unique Corregidor purchases.
- **Old versus new Riot Grrls:** retailer labels a newer **four-miniature Riot Grrls box ($27.50)**; the **$10.30** listing names **three Riot Grrls (Combi Rifle, Missile Launcher, Boarding Shotgun) plus TinBot**. The older $10.30 physical set has four pieces but only three Riot Grrl troopers. Do not assume they are the same sculpt or offer equivalent loadouts.
- **Nomads Army Pack / Booster / starters:** **New Nomads Army Pack ($36.85)** lists seven figures but details are truncated, including **Hellcat BSG, Intruder HMG, Grenzer Combi, and two Morlocks**. **Nomads Booster Pack Alpha ($17.00)** contains **Taskmaster HMG, Grenzer MULTI Sniper, Perseus**. The **Crimson Stone Nomads half ($31.80)** has seven other specified models. These can fill different needs but share some unit identities with older singles, Morlock boxes and Tunguska/Bakunin starters.
- **Bakunin and Tunguska overlap:** **Observance Action Pack ($47.50)** is described as revamped; it includes several Moiras, Reverend Healer and Custodier, though full contents are cut off. Compare older Bakunin Starter ($24.30), single Healer ($7.20), Reverend Moiras ($22.60), Custodiers ($6.80 each), and the **Cassandra Kusanagi Event Exclusive ($9.35)** vs regular ($9.10). **Tunguska starter ($24.30)** contains three Securitate, which may overlap the four-model Securitate box ($18.80) and other singles.
- **Mission-pack singles:** **Aftermath ($30.00)** contains Uhahu and four other named figures; **Uhahu Hacker ($6.20)** overlaps it, while **Uhahu Outrage ($6.90)** is a separate character-themed model. **Troubled Theft ($19.80)** includes **Agatha Wabara** and **Robin Hook**, both available singly ($7.50 and $7.00), plus **Konstantinos** (PanO). **Failsafe ($17.60)** includes **Liberty Cargo Pilot** (single $6.40) alongside PanO and Yu Jing units. **Xanadu Rush ($21.60)** contains **Jazz** (single $10.60) and **Lab-Tech** (single $8.40). **Bran do Castro ($7.60)** is already named in the **Zeros pack ($21.50)**.
- **Special releases and packaging:** **Illuminatrix Event Exclusive**, **Cassandra Kusanagi Event Exclusive**, **exclusive Wolfgang** and old **Outrage** minis need edition/roster validation; **Reinf. Lizard** has a Reinforcement label. **Hellcats two-model pack ($14.60)** explicitly comes **without bases and original box**. The **Go-Pods** listing truncates at “It comes without…”; check missing-parts warning before buying.
- **Troop vs accessories:** Pupniks, TinBots, CrazyKoalas, Fast Panda, and various remotes/mounted vs dismounted Zondnautica figures are physical miniatures/accessories but **not automatically independent troop orders**. This file **does not assume** Army roster validity solely from the retailer category tag (several products contain cross-faction named characters).
