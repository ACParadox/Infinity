## Sectorial Inventory

Quick role-based index for Kestrel list building. Entries identify **alternative profiles**, not simultaneous abilities on a single model. Costs, SWC, AVA and profile legality follow the [pinned N5.3 Kestrel roster](../../../sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md).

### Lieutenant (LT)

- **BCA Magistrate:** Strategos L1 Lieutenant (19 pts), Counterintelligence and FT Master (Jackals); a **separate** Magistrate CoC profile can insure the LT.
- **Fennec Fusilier:** cheap 10-point Lieutenant; field a **second, ordinary 10-point Combi Rifle Fennec with the identical visible loadout as an LT lookalike (a tactical decoy, not the Decoy skill)**. Pair with a **separate NCO** (e.g., Firefly, Sforza or the 66-point Squalos) to exploit the LT Order; neither Fennec has NCO.
- **Tikbalang:** Lieutenant (+1 Order), 73 pts; pair with a separate CoC model to protect succession.
- **Griffin:** HMG Lieutenant, 37 pts/**1 SWC** (0.5 SWC less than the 1.5-SWC non-LT HMG); separate CoC support recommended.
- **Hetkari:** Missile Launcher or MULTI Red Fury Lieutenant; separate CoC support recommended.
- **Maximus TAG:** 82-point AP HMG Lieutenant (0 SWC), Tactical Awareness and heavy CC threat; **only one Maximus** across the TAG and Agent profiles.

### Chain of Command (CoC)

- **BCA Magistrate:** 21 pts (Combi Rifle option; MULTI Marksman CoC is 25).
- **Firefly:** 23 pts.

### NCO

- **Firefly:** 19–25 pts across its profiles; NCO is a troop-wide skill.
- **Squalos Mk-II:** **66-point MULTI HMG profile only**; 55-point MMR and 62-point HMG/GL profiles do not have NCO.
- **Sforza:** 26-point solo or Albedo FTO profiles; 23-point FTO also has NCO.

### Tactical Awareness

- **Pathfinder FO/Sensor 17/0:** extra activation for objective movement or an eligible Fireteam; 14-point basic profile lacks Tactical Awareness.
- **Bipandra Doctor 25/0:** extra activation plus medical recovery in an eligible team; 23-point Doctor lacks Tactical Awareness.
- **Dr. Priya Harper:** 32-point FTO or 35-point Forward Deployment (+20) Specialist Operative; both have Tactical Awareness and grant +1 SWC.
- **Tikbalang:** HMG 69/1.5 or LT(+1 Order) 73/1.5; Tactical Awareness on both profiles, no NCO.
- **Squalos Mk-II:** MMR 55/1, HMG/GL 62/1.5 or MULTI HMG 66/1.5; all have Tactical Awareness, only the last has NCO.
- **Optimate Agent Maximus:** 45-point FTO or 48-point Minimus profile (including LT variants); Tactical Awareness on all profiles.
- **Maximus TAG:** 80–82 pts; Tactical Awareness on all profiles. Choose Agent or TAG, not both.

### Force Multipliers

- **BCA Magistrate:** Strategos L1 LT 19/0 (Combi Rifle) or 23/0 (MMR); Counterintelligence and FT Master (Jackals) on all profiles. Eligible linked Jackals become Regular at Order Count while FT Master conditions hold.
- **Tikbalang LT(+1 Order) 73/1.5:** two Lieutenant Orders for eligible NCO attackers.
- **Tech-Bee TAGCom 9/0:** Specialist Operative; grants Dodge (PH+3) and GizmoKit (PH+1) to eligible TAGs in the same Combat Group, not to Redeye Vehicles or REMs. Also carries a Deployable Repeater.
- **Dr. Priya Harper:** +1 SWC, Specialist Operative, Tactical Awareness; 32-point FTO Wildcard or 35-point Forward Deployment (+20) alternative, with Nanoscreen/NWI.
- **Mulebot EVO 14/0.5:** EVO Hacking Device and Baggage; Supportware option for REM or airborne plans. Distinct from the 8-point Repeater/Baggage cheerleader.
- **Crux FTO Strategic Deployment MMR 50/0:** optional forward deployment for eligible Fireteam.
- **Optimate Agent Maximus LT:** 45-point FTO or 48-point Minimus variant; grants +1 SWC. Choose Agent or TAG, not both.

### Warhorse

- **Richard Quinn:** 26 pts (both Forward Observer profiles).

### Long-Range Gunfighters

Primary 16–32in or longer attack lanes, including situational active shots from heavier ARO guns.

- **Griffin:** HMG 37/1.5 (LT 37/1); MSV1 and linked options. Feuerbach 38/1.5 is mainly ARO, with situational active anti-armour use.
- **Squalos Mk-II:** HMG/GL 62/1.5 or NCO MULTI HMG 66/1.5.
- **Tikbalang:** HMG, Mimetism (-3), Climbing Plus.
- **Knauf:** MULTI Sniper Rifle 31/1.5, MSV1.
- **Drummers:** Drumbot-1 MULTI HMG 40/1 or Drumbot-2 HRMC 42/1.5 packages; FO + Drumbot-1 + Drumbot-5 47/1 alternative.
- **Maximus TAG:** AP HMG 82/1.5 (LT 82/0) or AP Thunderbolt (+2B) 80/1; Tactical Awareness, high durability and strong CC.
- **Optimate Agent Maximus:** AP Thunderbolt (+2B), 45/1 FTO or 48/1 with Minimus; the latter cannot join a Fireteam. Choose Agent or TAG, not both.
- **Blink Team:** Hidden Deployment MULTI Sniper Rifle 32/1.5 or HRL 28/1.5; primarily ambush/ARO, situational active shooting.
- **Sierra TR HMG:** 23/1; secondary active long gun, primarily ARO.

### Mid-Short-Range Gunfighters

Primary 0–24in rifle, marksman rifle, Spitfire, Red Fury and close-assault attackers.

- **Bulleteer:** Spitfire 22/1 or Heavy Shotgun (+1B) 19/0; Mimetism (-6).
- **Redeye:** aerial Spitfire vehicle, strong flanking angles; Blitzen 37/1.5 or Continuous-Damage GL 38/1.5.
- **Crux Father-Knight:** FTO MULTI Marksman Rifle 46/0 or Spitfire 49/1.5; alternate FD MMR and Hidden AP SMG attack profiles.
- **Hetkari:** MULTI Red Fury 48/1.5; BS Attack (-3), Marksmanship.
- **Squalos Mk-II:** MULTI Marksman Rifle 55/1; shorter-range alternative to its HMG profiles.
- **Black A.I.R.:** K1 Marksman Rifle FTO FO 29/0.5; MSV2, Mimetism (-3).
- **Griffin:** MULTI Rifle 31/0; MSV1, durable midrange secondary gun.
- **Richard Quinn:** MULTI Marksman Rifle or MULTI Rifle FO 26/0; MSV1 and Warhorse.
- **Sforza:** Viral Rifle/Adhesive Launcher Rifle; solo or eligible FTO profiles.
- **Blink Team:** Shock Marksman Rifle 28/0; Hidden Deployment, Mimetism (-6).
- **Drummers:** Drumbot-4 Mk12/HRL 42/1 package; forward mixed-range attacker.
- **Banshee:** airborne shotgun/SMG and Pulzar assault options.
- **Peacemaker:** forward shotgun REM, with optional Auxbot fork.
- **Dart:** forward Camouflage AP SMG/Viral Tactical Bow; optional Sharvara package.
- **Dr. Priya Harper:** Plasma Carbine 32/+1 SWC FTO or 35/+1 SWC FD; specialist and close-range utility.

### ARO

- **Fennec Fusilier:** Thunderbolt (+1B), Shock Minelayer.
- **Blink Team:** Heavy Rocket Launcher or MULTI Sniper Rifle.
- **Sierra Dronbot TR Thunderbolt (+1B):** 18 pts/0 SWC; default economical B3 ARO, 360° Visor.
- **Sierra Dronbot TR HMG:** 23 pts/1 SWC; B4 ARO at 16–32in, 360° Visor. AVA2 shared across both Sierra options.
- **Mulebot TR Combi Rifle:** 17 pts/0 SWC; B3 ARO, native Repeater and Baggage. Niche short-lane defense; normally prefer the 18-point Sierra Thunderbolt, which has superior reach and a 360° Visor.
- **Black A.I.R.:** FTO Neurocinetics MULTI Sniper, MSV2.
- **Drummer:** Drumbot-3 Neurocinetics Feuerbach.
- **Sforza:** BS Attack (+1SD), Viral Rifle, Adhesive Launcher Rifle, Nanopulser.
- **Firefly:** Heavy Rocket Launcher.
- **Firefly:** Armed Turret Minelayer **or** Engineer support (different profiles).
- **Griffin:** Feuerbach.
- **Hetkari:** Missile Launcher.
- **Crux Father-Knight:** MULTI Marksman Rifle (particularly a legal FTO link).

### Midfield Defense

- **Jackals:** infiltrating Shock Minelayer for forward mine coverage; E/M attack and speculative-fire options indexed separately under Anti-TAG and Indirect Fire.
- **Beasthunter:** Forward Deployment Camouflage profile.
- **Peacemaker:** forward-deployed REM with Repeater and Auxbot options.
- **Scarecrow:** infiltrating Camouflage Minelayer.
- **Sāchā:** forward E/M Grenades and Flash Pulse.
- **Dart:** Camouflage, mines and optional Sharvara K9.
- **Yamabushi:** forward-close defence and disposable Camouflage.

### Deployment Zone (DZ) Defense

- **Fennec:** Thunderbolt (+1B) Shock Minelayer.
- **Fugazi:** Flash Pulse and Repeater.
- **Warcor / Sāchā:** low-cost Flash Pulse coverage.
- **Peacemaker:** forward defensive screen.
- **Firefly:** Armed Turret Minelayer.

### Hackers

- **Firefly:** Hacking Device.
- **Scarecrow:** Hacking Device.
- **Fennec Fusilier:** Hacking Device with FastPanda.

### Killer Hackers

- **Scarecrow:** Killer Hacking Device.
- **Banshee:** Killer Hacking Device; Combat Jump/Parachutist access.
- **Black A.I.R.:** **31 pts/0 SWC** Killer Hacking Device with **Parachutist (Deployment Zone)**; distinct from the FTO gunfighter and sniper profiles.

### Network Projection

- **Fennec Fusilier:** Hacker with FastPanda.
- **Pathfinder:** MOV 15-10, Tactical Awareness (17 pts), native Repeater.
- **Peacemaker:** Forward Deployment (+20), native Repeater.
- **Scarecrow:** Infiltration and Deployable Repeater (specific 26-point profile).
- **Tech-Bee:** Deployable Repeater.
- **Firefly:** Deployable Repeater (19-point profile).
- **Optimate Agent Maximus:** TinBot (Repeater) on the 45-point FTO profile, not the Minimus Peripheral profile.

### Cheerleaders / Order Generation

- **Magistrate + Jackal FTO:** 21 + 6 pts with the CoC Magistrate, or 17 + 6 with the basic Magistrate; FT Master can make linked Jackal Orders Regular at Order Count under its conditions.
- **Fennec Fusilier:** 10+ pts, AVA 5.
- **Fugazi:** 7 pts, AVA 1.
- **Tech-Bee:** 9 pts, AVA 1.
- **Mulebot:** 8-point Baggage option, AVA 2.
- **Pathfinder:** 17-point Tactical Awareness option, AVA 2 (Wildcard max 1 per legal Fireteam).

### Close Combat (CC)

- **Dart + Sharvara K9:** optional Peripheral package.
- **Optimate Agent Maximus:** strong CC/anti-heavy combat.
- **Maximus TAG:** CC24, Natural Born Warrior, AP+DA CCW and CC Attack (-6); a distinct, much more expensive close-assault chassis.
- **Yamabushi:** Martial Arts L3, DA CCW.
- **Crux Father-Knight:** Martial Arts L2, DA CCW.

### Vision Control

- **Fennec Forward Observer:** Sensor + Disco Baller.
- **Richard Quinn Forward Observer:** 26-point MULTI Rifle profile with Disco Baller (the MULTI Marksman Rifle FO profile has no Disco Baller).

### Doctor / Paramedics

- **Trauma-Doc:** 14-point Doctor.
- **Bipandra:** 23-point Doctor with MediKit (+1B), or **25-point Tactical Awareness** Doctor; Wildcard with Fennec/Kestrel counts-as.
- **Fennec Fusilier:** **12-point Paramedic** with MediKit; inexpensive medical coverage in a FENNECS Fireteam.
- **Firefly:** **25-point Paramedic** with MediKit, NCO and Albedo; KESTREL member / optional ARMORED support.
- **Yamabushi:** **16-point Paramedic FTO** with MediKit; despite the FTO label it has **no Kestrel Fireteam chart row**.

### Engineer / Repair Support

- **Machinist:** Engineer, with optional Deployable Cover or Armed Turret variants.
- **Firefly:** 23-point Engineer profile (distinct from its turret/CoC/Hacker profiles).
- **Palbot:** **3-point Peripheral (Servant)**, AVA 2; extends a Machinist's repair reach to TAGs/REMs while the Engineer remains protected. **No independent Order.**

### Deployment Zone (DZ) Attack

- **Redeye:** fast aerial angles into enemy backfield.
- **Black A.I.R.:** **31-point Killer Hacker with Parachutist (Deployment Zone)**; distinct from the FTO options.
- **TAGs:** Tikbalang / Squalos / Maximus for breakthrough; **Redeye is a Vehicle, not a TAG**.
- **Banshee:** Combat Jump assault or Parachutist entry.

### Specialists / Objective Play

- **Pathfinder:** Forward Observer + Tactical Awareness.
- **Drummer:** Forward Observer controller + Drumbot-5 Forward Observer.
- **Scarecrow:** Hacker, Killer Hacker or Forward Observer profiles; Infiltration + Camouflage.
- **Dart:** Specialist Operative, Forward Deployment (+20), Camouflage.
- **Fennec:** cheap Forward Observer / Sensor / Disco Baller utility or other specialist profiles.
- **Banshee:** Hacker or Killer Hacker; Combat Jump and Parachutist.
- **Crux Father-Knight:** elite Specialist Operative with combat capability.
- **Firefly:** Engineer, Hacker, CoC, **Paramedic**, or other utility profile as required.
- **Emily Handelman:** **22–24-point WIP14 Forward Observer**, Number 2 and Wildcard; counts as Fennec or ColCorp, with E/M/GL payloads detailed under Anti-TAG and Indirect Fire.
- **Tech-Bee TAGCom:** **9-point Specialist Operative** with Deployable Repeater and GizmoKit; TAGCom boosts eligible TAGs in the same Combat Group (see Force Multipliers).
- **Dr. Priya Harper:** **32-point FTO** or **35-point Forward Deployment (+20)** Specialist Operative; Tactical Awareness, NWI, Nanoscreen and **+1 SWC**.
- **Black A.I.R.:** **29-point K1 Marksman Rifle FTO Forward Observer**, or **31-point DZ Parachutist Killer Hacker**.

### Anti-TAG / E/M / Disabling

- **Minescorp Jackals:** **10-point Infiltration E/M Carbine**, **10-point Infiltration Blitzen**, or **11-point Infiltration E/M Grenade Launcher + Shock Minelayer**; these are distinct Irregular profiles. The **6-point Jackal FTO Panzerfaust** is a different, non-Infiltrating COLCORP option.
- **Emily Handelman:** **22-point E/M Grenade Launcher** or **24-point Grenade Launcher + E/M Grenade Launcher**, both 0 SWC; Regular FO/Number 2 Wildcard support.
- **Redeye:** **37-point Spitfire + Blitzen** aerial anti-heavy flank; the **38-point Continuous-Damage GL** profile is an alternative, not an additional weapon.
- **Firefly:** Panzerfaust and D-Charges on its applicable support profiles; some profiles also offer a deployable mine or turret. The HRL variant is a different loadout.
- **Crux Father-Knight:** **Drop Bears** on linked FTO profiles, or **CrazyKoala** on forward/Hidden Deployment profiles; D-Charges and strong close combat.
- **Optimate Agent Maximus / Maximus TAG:** E/M Carbine plus strong AP/CC follow-up; choose **Agent or TAG**, not both.
- **Hetkari MULTI Red Fury:** E/Marat as a short-range anti-heavy deterrent (distinct from the Missile Launcher profile).
- **Sforza:** Viral Rifle and **Adhesive Launcher Rifle**; a separate disabling approach from E/M.
- **Dart / Sāchā:** forward E/M Grenades; situational close-range disruption.

### Indirect Fire / Guided

- **Minescorp Jackal:** **11 pts/0.5 SWC**, Infiltration + E/M Grenade Launcher + Shock Minelayer; inexpensive Irregular Speculative Fire pressure.
- **Emily Handelman:** **22 pts/0 SWC** E/M Grenade Launcher, or **24 pts/0 SWC** Grenade Launcher + E/M Grenade Launcher; Regular, WIP14 Forward Observer and eligible Wildcard.
- **Redeye:** **38 pts/1.5 SWC** Spitfire + Continuous-Damage Grenade Launcher; mobile indirect-fire angle, distinct from the 37-point Blitzen option.
- **Squalos Mk-II:** **62 pts/1.5 SWC** HMG + Grenade Launcher; combines long-range direct shooting and speculative-fire access.
- **Clipper Dronbot:** **16 pts/1.5 SWC Guided Missile Launcher**; requires a reliable Targeted setup for Guided attacks. Its **19-point FTO Missile Launcher (+1B)** is a different, **non-Guided** profile with **no Kestrel Fireteam chart access**.
