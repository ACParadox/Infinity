## Rules, opportunity costs and source-derived strengths

| Constraint | Practical impact |
|---|---|
| **Slots** | One Core, **one Haris**, unlimited Duos; FENNECS can form **Core or Haris only**. Do not spend the single Haris on cheap generic DZ support. |
| **Required native members** | FENNECS: at least one actual Fennec; KESTREL: Griffin, Firefly or Hetkari; COLCORP: at least one Magistrate; ARMORED: Squalos or Tikbalang. Wildcards never waive an anchor. |
| **Counts-as** | Griffin counts as Fennec or Kestrel by family; Firefly as Kestrel; Bipandra/Quinn as Fennec/Kestrel; Emily as Fennec/ColCorp. Black A.I.R., Crux, Harper, Sforza, Pathfinder, Agent Maximus and Hetkari do **not** add a same-unit match. |
| **FT levels** | FT2 provides +1 Special Die (not extra Burst); FT3 improves Discover/Dodge; FT4 improves BS; FT5 provides Sixth Sense. A three-member team with only two matching members stays FT2. |
| **Profiles** | Black A.I.R., Crux, Harper and Sforza must take an FTO profile. Agent Maximus needs the **45pt FTO without Minimus**, because a Synchronized Peripheral's Controller cannot link. Only one Agent/TAG Maximus per Army. |
| **Movement** | Griffin/Hetkari/Crux MOV15-5; Pathfinder/Squalos/Tikbalang MOV15-10; most Fennecs/Fireflies/Quinn/Bipandra/Harper/Sforza MOV10-10. Match actual movement routes before linking. |
| **Orders** | Pathfinder Tactical Awareness17 provides a real extra restricted activation (not a second pooled Regular Order), even in FT1. Strategos Regular conversion and NCO spending of the same Lieutenant resource are alternative uses. |
| **Deployables** | Minelayer places **one** eligible mine or turret at deployment, never both for free. A carried E/M Mine without Minelayer costs the appropriate placement Orders. |
| **Positioning** | Dedicated long AROs and advancing HMGs often want different firing lanes. Prefer short-range protection, utility and mission specialists in the same team; split incompatible long AROs across the board. |

**From the Kestrel overview:** the 29-point **Magistrate17 + two Jackal FTO6 COLCORP Haris** is a distinctive three-Regular-Order engine through FT Master at **Order Count**, ranked [#5 Apex among cheerleader formations](../../list-building/cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md#apex--best-total-battlefield-impact-in-the-order-generation-role). The **13pt Fennec FO/Sensor/Disco** is a cheap Regular scorer and projected Eclipse tool; the **33pt Black A.I.R. Neuro Sniper** is a strong MSV2/Mimetism(-3) linked ARO; **29pt Black A.I.R. K1 FO** is a midrange anti-ARM specialist; **Fireflies** provide NCO/Albedo with distinct Engineer/CoC/Minelayer/HRL options; **17pt Pathfinder TacAware** adds FO/Sensor/Repeater and extra movement; **45pt Maximus Agent FTO** adds a linked anti-visor heavy shooter. These distinct tools and the sole Haris slot determine team value.

**Recommended Fireteam economy:** compact three-body FENNECS **Core** with one excellent gun and two *useful* Fennec-equivalent supports; one premium KESTREL **Haris** only when its FT2 and close/medical screen earn the Haris slot; economical **COLCORP Duo 23–27pts** to leave Haris available; multiple compact **Griffin + Firefly/Quinn/Bipandra or fast Pathfinder** Duos. A five-body FENNECS Core is an intentional FT5 choice, not the default.

## Detailed Fireteam compositions

### Particularly strong or unusual Fireteams

**N5.3 audited profiles and FT levels (19 November 2026):** [Open the exact 2–5-member Fireteam selections, option IDs, individual/total pts/SWC, FT levels, restrictions, and evidence notes](../../../sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md#recommended-fireteam-compositions--n53-audited). This pinned-roster-based table is the detailed reference; the examples below remain qualitative tactical commentary.

Start with **three-body FENNECS Core candidates** using **one** main shooter (Griffin HMG/Feuerbach, Black A.I.R. Neuro Sniper/K1 FO, or Crux FTO MMR) plus two Fennecs carrying **FO/Disco, Minelayer, Hacker or Paramedic** according to the mission. Prefer a **gunfighter + 17pt Tactical Awareness Pathfinder Duo even at FT1** when it provides more usable Orders and mission reach than a premium Haris. Choose **Magistrate + one Jackal FTO COLCORP Duo (23/0 or 25/0 Strategos LT)** when reserving the Haris slot, or **29/0 Magistrate + two Jackals Haris** when the cheap Order engine deserves that slot. The **four-Fennec + Griffin 87/1.5 FT5 Core** is a deliberate maximum-bonus alternative, not the default. Report-tested shapes are separately labelled from reconstructed N5.3 candidates.


- **COLCORP Haris — Magistrate + two 6-point Jackal FTO:** the signature **29/0 three-Regular-Order module**, or **31/0 with 19-point Strategos LT**. Converts Orders **only if FT Master and the eligible Fireteam are intact at Order Count**. Panzerfaust/LRL Jackals offer meaningful cheap reactive trade tools, and a separate **CoC Magistrate** can protect an aggressive LT. **If the sole Haris is already taken, use a COLCORP Duo instead:** 17-point Magistrate + one 6-point FTO Jackal = **23/0, FT2** (or Strategos LT 19 + Jackal 6 = **25/0, FT2**); both linked troopers can yield Regular Orders under the FT Master conditions. Do not describe a Haris reduced to two survivors as a new Duo; create an actual Duo. 
- **FENNECS Black A.I.R. K1 FO + two Fennecs:** focus on the **29pt/0.5SWC K1 specialist**, not double stacking two long-range AROs. **K1 FO + FO/Sensor/Disco + Minelayer = 56/0.5, FT2** as a compact Core/Haris; switch Minelayer to Paramedic or Hacker for role-specific support. [#158 AdeptiCon](../../battle-reports/151-160-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) supports the K1 profile and a flexible K1/Crux/Fennec group, **not this exact proposed trio**. **Positional compatibility:** a forward-moving Griffin HMG and a dedicated long-lane Black A.I.R. Neuro Sniper in the same team generally create conflicting movement goals; one wants new firing angles, the other wants to preserve a strong reactive position. A Fennec close-defense weapon that accompanies the Griffin is usually a better mixed-role complement. For a long ranged shell, use **one** Griffin HMG/Feuerbach and FO/Disco + chosen Fennec support; deploy a separate Black A.I.R. sniper on another lane.
- **KESTREL Haris / Duo distinction:** **Hetkari Missile Launcher** remains an independent long-range ARO or a deliberately defensive three-model **Haris** with close-protection Wildcards; do not spend a Duo slot on a stationary missile turret. For economical compact links, **Griffin + Bipandra TacAware (62/1.5 FT2)** adds a Doctor and extra team activation, while **Griffin + Firefly Engineer (60/1.5 FT2)** adds NCO/Albedo and technical support. Hetkari Duo proposals are excluded from the recommended candidates.
- **KESTREL Haris — Hetkari + Firefly + Bipandra or Quinn:** a premium gun with **NCO/Engineer/CoC/utility choices** on the Firefly and medical or FO/MSV/Disco support from the Wildcard. Check **two Kestrel-counting members** for the desired composition; potent but expensive and incompatible with choosing the Magistrate/Jackal Haris simultaneously.
- **ARMORED Duo — Squalos or Tikbalang + Pathfinder TacAware:** unusually practical **TAG + specialist + extra team activation** rather than paying for two TAGs and bunching their hacking exposure. **Squalos NCO** also makes sense with an **unconverted** Magistrate LT Order; decide conversion **at Order Count**. A Tikbalang LT's personal LT Orders must not be counted as automatic Duo Orders.

**Firefly HRL judgment:** A viable **budget reactive ARO**, especially with FT2 and a long, covered line. For 24/1.5, BS13, Albedo against relevant MSV/Marksmanship attacks, BS Attack (SR-1), Continuous Damage and HRL blast mode are meaningful. However, it has **W1/ARM2 and no Mimetism**; its low price is the appeal, not its staying power. Its HRL profile carries an AP Mine but **does not have Minelayer or a Panzerfaust**. Skip if your list already has a premium Griffin/Black A.I.R. sniper holding the same lane. [N5.3 Firefly profile](https://human-sphere.com/Firefly) · [HRL weapon chart](https://infinitythewiki.com/Weapons_Chart).

**Griffin MULTI Rifle judgment:** A **good 31pt/0-SWC closer/mid gun**, not an apex close-assault/CC unit. BS14/MSV1, W2/ARM4, AP/Shock options and Pulzar complement a long gun or Engineer. MULTI Rifle is +3 through 16in but -3 from 16–32in, so an HMG/Feuerbach remains the better true long attacker; the Pulzar provides a close-range template but does not grant specialist melee capability.

**Quinn verdict:** both **26/0** profiles are Forward Observers, MSV1, Warhorse and count as Kestrel/Fennec. **Default Disco MULTI Rifle** if the team needs Eclipse route-making, especially when moving a premium gun into a safe attack lane; **MMR** when already covered for Disco and a longer positive-range AP/Shock gun is useful. A second visor alone rarely justifies Quinn if his actual role overlaps Griffin.

**Team-selection discipline:** Do not consume the sole Haris for overlapping expensive guns or a specialist-only group when compact Duos or independent models achieve the objective. A second Firefly is normally worthwhile only when one is the HRL.

**Pathfinder and legality guardrail:** FT1 gunfighter + 17pt TacAware Pathfinder can be worth more than FT2 when it supplies a useful activation, fast FO/Sensor/Repeater and independent mission reach. Preferred anchors: Griffin HMG in KESTREL or Tikbalang/Squalos in ARMORED. **Crux + Pathfinder alone is not a legal KESTREL Duo** (both are Wildcards, with no required Griffin/Hetkari/Firefly) and FENNECS has no Duo. Neither is **Black A.I.R. + Pathfinder** a valid two-member FENNECS formation.

#### COLCORP — preserve a cheap Duo when another Haris is more useful

**Incremental-value test (20 Nov 2026):**

- **Emily vs Fennec:** E/M Grenade Launcher (Speculative Attack, circular template), WIP14 FO, X-Visor, Mimetism(-3), Dogged and Number 2 cannot be replicated by a basic Fennec. However, Griffin HMG + Fennec FO/Disco + Fennec Paramedic **62/1.5 FT3** or Minelayer **64/1.5 FT3** is **8–10 points cheaper and the same FT level**. Emily **72/1.5 FT3** is *only* worth the premium if her E/M projection (or WIP14/Number 2) has a defined job; she does not improve Griffin's +SD.
- **Sforza vs Griffin+Engineer:** Sforza provides **Viral versus BTS**, Adhesive Launcher Rifle PARA control, X-Visor, Dogged and a **native +1SD that stacks with team FT2's +1SD on Sforza's own BS Attacks** (two selection dice, not two added Burst). His NCO overlaps Firefly's NCO, and the trio is **83/1.5 FT2**, not FT3. The **60/1.5 FT2 Griffin HMG + Engineer Duo** is the usual best buy; adding Sforza only makes sense to buy a genuine alternate weapon/counter with the 23-point Haris premium.
- **Harper vs cheap support:** Harper FTO32 adds a **Plasma Carbine, Nanopulser, Viral Pistol(+1B), E/M Mine**, Specialist Operative, Nanoscreen/NWI, Tactical Awareness and **+1 SWC availability**. Her **Feuerbach38 + Fennec Minelayer14 + Harper32 = 84 / net 0.5 SWC FT2** defends close flanks and offsets SWC; **no FT3** because Harper lacks Fennec Counts-as. The **HMG37 + FO/Disco13 + Harper32 = 82/net0.5 FT2** alternative has a mobility mismatch (Harper MOV10-10, Griffin MOV15-5) and is rarely worth replacing a cheaper FT3 Fennec escort. Harper is **not a Doctor** and must spend Orders to deploy her E/M Mine.

#### Hetkari Missile Launcher — ARO plus genuine close-range screens

**Hetkari ML47/1.5 is a static long-range ARO**. Focus on the three-member defensive Harises below, with cheaper separate defensive pieces otherwise. Hetkari **does not count as Kestrel** for Fireteam Level: the Haris needs **two Kestrel-equivalent supports** (Griffin, Firefly, Quinn or Bipandra) to reach FT2. Prefer members that handle the ML's 0–16in approaches rather than another missile/sniper gun.

| Composition | Size / FT | Total pts/SWC | Role synergy and trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hetkari ML47 + Firefly Minelayer24 + Quinn Disco26** | KESTREL Haris **FT2** | **97/1.5** | **Preferred premium ARO:** Firefly+Quinn count Kestrel, earn the ML +1SD, with prelaid mine/turret, near-threat MULTI Rifle and Eclipse/FO. Uses sole Haris. |
| **Hetkari ML47 + Firefly Minelayer24 + Bipandra Doctor23** | KESTREL Haris **FT2** | **94/1.5** | One pre-placed mine **or** turret plus Doctor to recover Hetkari; less aggressive close defense than Griffin/Quinn, but can save the ARO gun. |
| **Hetkari ML47 + Griffin MULTI Rifle31 + Firefly Minelayer24** | KESTREL Haris **FT2** | **102/1.5** | Griffin+Firefly match, Pulzar/MULTI Rifle and mine cover true close threats; costs 102pts and concentrates Hackable models. Not default. |

**Minelayer rule check:** Firefly carries both AP Mine and Armed Turret, but its unnumbered Minelayer skill permits **one** deployable placed at deployment, not a free mine **and** a free turret. Choose the appropriate one, then place the other later using the normal Orders/equipment rules. [Current Minelayer rule](https://infinitythewiki.com/Minelayer).

**For a budget ML defense, use Hetkari solo plus separately positioned close-range screen/mines rather than a Hetkari Duo.** If investing the sole Haris slot, the **97pt Firefly Minelayer + Quinn Haris** is the leading linked candidate: FT2 (+1SD) plus short-range defense and specialist utility. These are proposed builds, not exact battle-report observations.


#### Crux Father-Knight — selective Harises and FENNECS links

**The recommended linked Crux remains the 46pt/0SWC MULTI Marksman FTO**, BS14, MOV15-5 (~6in first), ARM5/W2, Albedo, Specialist Operative, Drop Bears and **native +1SD**. Only its FTO option is a Wildcard; the Hidden Deployment profile is not. A purpose-built FENNECS Core/Haris or a sufficiently synergistic KESTREL Haris is preferable to forcing a low-benefit FT1 link. Crux + Pathfinder **alone is illegal** (no required KESTREL anchor), and FENNECS has no Duo.

| Composition | Formation | Total pts/SWC | Evaluation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Crux MMR46 + Griffin HMG37 + Firefly Engineer23** | KESTREL Haris **FT2** | **106/1.5** | Firefly+Griffin count Kestrel, giving Crux **+1SD on top of native +1SD**; Engineer protects against technical problems. Two expensive guns and sole Haris commitment. |
| **Crux MMR46 + Griffin HMG37 + Bipandra TA25** | KESTREL Haris **FT2** | **108/1.5** | Griffin+Bipandra match, +2SD total for Crux, Doctor/TacAware for recovery and a long gun; Bipandra slow 10-10. |
| **Crux MMR46 + Fennec FO/Disco13 + Bipandra Doctor23** | FENNECS Core/Haris **FT2** | **82/0** | **Preferred 0SWC specialist trio:** Fennec+Bipandra match, +2SD total for Crux, Eclipse/FO and Doctor. Member identities reported in [#005](../../battle-reports/001-010-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md); current profiles a reconstruction. TA Doctor25 makes **84/0**. |

**Pathfinder as a third member:** Crux46 + Griffin HMG37 + Pathfinder TA17 = **100/1.5 KESTREL Haris FT1** is chart-legal and all three have first MOV15, but no matching-unit FT2 and duplicate expensive attack/scoring roles. **Not recommended**; use a more purposeful FENNECS Crux Core/Haris or a separate Pathfinder gunfighter/TAG Duo instead.

#### Fast Pathfinder Tactical Awareness — MOV15 first-move gunfighter partners

**Pathfinder** is MOV15-10 (approximately 6in/4in), WIP13 FO/Sensor/Repeater/Deactivator, with a **17pt/0SWC Tactical Awareness profile**. The retained recommended anchors move at least **15cm / approximately 6in** on their first move (**Griffin MOV15-5**, **Tikbalang/Squalos MOV15-10**). These two-member teams are **FT1**, since Pathfinder is a non-counting Wildcard; the extra Tactical Order is not a second unrestricted pooled Regular Order. One Pathfinder per Fireteam, AVA2 overall. 

| Duo | First MOV each | Total pts/SWC | Reason to choose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Griffin HMG37 + Pathfinder TA17** | 15 + 15 | **54/1.5** | **Best long-range infantry partner:** HMG clears lanes, Pathfinder scores/projects network without a third body. |
| **Griffin Feuerbach38 + Pathfinder TA17** | 15 + 15 | **55/1.5** | Legal and fast, but reactive gun often wants to stay on its lane; use only when team advances to a final position, then separates. |
| **Squalos MMR55 + Pathfinder TA17** | 15 + 15 | **72/1** | Budget TAG, moving specialist, Repeater/Deactivator and an extra linked activation. |
| **Squalos HMG/GL62 + Pathfinder TA17** | 15 + 15 | **79/1.5** | Long-range TAG plus mission access; occasional indirect attack without second TAG. |
| **Squalos MULTI HMG NCO66 + Pathfinder TA17** | 15 + 15 | **83/1.5** | Anti-ARM long gun; NCO may use available LT Order while Pathfinder brings TacAware. |
| **Tikbalang HMG69 + Pathfinder TA17** | 15 + 15 | **86/1.5** | Mobile Mimetism/Climbing Plus TAG with matching-speed mission REM. |
| **Tikbalang LT73 + Pathfinder TA17** | 15 + 15 | **90/1.5** | LT has two personal LT Orders which **do not directly activate an intact Duo**; for solo aggression, a solo LT Tik may be superior. |

**Fast Duo recommendation order:** **Griffin HMG + Pathfinder (54/1.5)**, then **Squalos/Tikbalang + Pathfinder** according to a TAG-centered list. The **Griffin Feuerbach + Pathfinder** is a lower-priority repositioning option because a dedicated ARO gun often wants to remain in its lane.  Crux+Pathfinder and Agent Maximus+Pathfinder are not legal two-member Duos: neither provides the required KESTREL/ARMORED anchor, and FENNECS does not allow Duo.

**Squalos correction:** the [canonical Kestrel profile](../../../sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md#squalos-mk-ii) now correctly lists **BS Attack (SR-1)**, **not** native BS Attack (+1SD), following the [current N5.3 Squalos profile](https://human-sphere.com/Squalos_MK-II). Only the **same-unit FT2** bonus supplies +1SD to a two-Squalos Duo. At least **117 points for MMR+HMG/GL (39% of a 300pt list)** buys no specialist or repair and must maintain two S6/hackable TAGs in the same linked geometry; ordinarily buy one TAG plus a TA Pathfinder or specialist support. A double-Squalos skew can work for intentionally high-cost multi-lane denial, but **FT2 is not sufficient justification**.

**Firefly Engineer versus Pathfinder TA:** Firefly supplies NCO, Albedo, Deactivator and actual Engineer, but is MOV 10-10 versus Squalos 15-10, and no +SD is earned in their mixed Duo. It makes sense when the TAG intends a comparatively stationary hold-and-repair position or when the LT Order is otherwise available for NCO; a **mobile gun/scorer** favors Pathfinder TA. An independent Machinist + Palbot is often more economical repair support for a highly mobile TAG.

**Heavy-chassis rule:** Avoid expensive mixed double-TAG FT1 links; two Squalos FT2 are retained only as a thematic vanity option, never the default.

**Legal boundaries:** Kestrel allows **one Core, one Haris and unlimited Duos**. FENNECS requires an actual Fennec; KESTREL requires an eligible Griffin/Hetkari/Firefly; COLCORP requires a Magistrate; ARMORED requires a Tikbalang or Squalos. Wildcards **do not waive** those requirements; a printed **FTO** label without a matching chart entry does **not** confer a Fireteam (notably **Clipper FTO and Yamabushi FTO**). Consult the [canonical roster and chart](../../../sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md#fireteams).

### Specific character evaluations from the discussion

**Sforza (23pt FTO; 26pt Albedo FTO):** A legal situational Viral Rifle/Adhesive Launcher Rifle/Nanopulser response. **X-Visor** improves relevant ranged weapon range bands (especially Adhesive Launcher Rifle), **not** Nanopulser templates. His native +1SD makes his personal attacks/ARO more reliable; eligible FT2 +1SD stacks **on Sforza himself**, not as an extra bonus on Griffin. His **NCO** is useful with a Lieutenant Order, but **duplicates Firefly's NCO** in the Griffin+Engineer+Sforza Haris. The **26pt FTO** adds Albedo for +3: **Griffin HMG + Sforza Albedo = 63/1.5 FT1 Duo**, or **Griffin HMG + Firefly Engineer + Sforza Albedo = 86/1.5 FT2 Haris**. Take when Viral/BTS, PARA/Immobilization, anti-visor protection or an additional +SD ARO solves a real enemy; not by default.

**Emily (22pt E/M GL, 24pt GL+E/M GL):** Her high-value addition to a **Griffin HMG + Fennec FO/Disco** team is E/M **Speculative Attack**, WIP14 Forward Observer, X-Visor, Mimetism(-3), Dogged and Number 2 — not FT level, which is already **FT3** with a cheap third Fennec. Team **72/1.5** with 22pt Emily versus **62/1.5** with 12pt Fennec Paramedic or **64/1.5** with 14pt Minelayer. The **24pt version** additionally gains **Flash Pulse (+1B)**, ordinary Grenade Launcher and Combi Rifle (team **74/1.5**): a credible WIP14, Mimetism(-3) **secondary reactive Flash Pulse** option when the team can use it. No reason to pay for Emily purely as another Regular Fennec-sized Order.

**Dr. Harper (32pt FTO, +1 SWC):** Potentially good but **expensive defensive toolbox**, best behind **Griffin Feuerbach38 + Fennec Minelayer14** for **84 pts / net 0.5 SWC FT2**. Plasma Carbine, Nanopulser, E/M Mine, Viral Pistol (+1B), Nanoscreen/NWI, Specialist Operative and Tactical Awareness complement a dedicated long ARO. Her +1 SWC credit offsets most of Griffin's 1.5 SWC. She is **not a Doctor**, lacks Fennec Counts-as and cannot earn FT3 in this trio. HMG37 + Fennec FO/Disco13 + Harper32 costs **82/net0.5 FT2**, but is usually worse than cheaper Fennec FT3 support for a moving gun.

**Quinn:** Both 26pt Forward Observer options count as Fennec/Kestrel. Prefer the **MULTI Rifle + Disco** to escort an elite gun when projected Eclipse opens movement routes, or the **MULTI Marksman** if Disco is already available and an 8–24in gun adds meaningful shooting. MSV1 overlaps Griffin, so buy for FO/Disco or a specific medium-range job.

**Bipandra:** The 23pt Doctor or 25pt Tactical Awareness Doctor counts as Fennec/Kestrel; especially useful near a W2 expensive attacker. **Griffin HMG + Bipandra TA = 62/1.5 KESTREL FT2 Duo**, a strong shooter/Doctor/extra-activation package. Compare against a 12pt Fennec Paramedic in a FENNECS team when price matters.

**Agent Maximus:** Only **45pt FTO without Minimus** belongs in FENNECS; a 48pt Agent with Synchronized Minimus is not linkable. Strong AP Thunderbolt, Albedo(-6), NWI and own Tactical Awareness. It is **MOV15-5**, while its obligatory Fennec escort is MOV10-10. **Agent FTO + FO/Disco + Paramedic = 70/1 FT2** is the efficient starting point; **+Bipandra TA instead = 83/1 FT2** for Doctor and additional Tactical activation.
