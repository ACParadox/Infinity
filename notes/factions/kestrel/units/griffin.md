# Griffin Troops — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A+ overall; HMG S, Feuerbach A+, HMG Lieutenant A, MULTI Rifle B.** The Griffin HMG is the baseline against which Kestrel's other primary gunfighters should be judged. BS14/MSV1, two real Wounds, a Pulzar, and exceptionally economical Fireteam access make it a strong general-purpose attacker without consuming the points required by more specialized heavy troops. No particular Griffin or Black A.I.R. pairing is mandatory.

## Profiles

[Canonical Kestrel roster](../../../../sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md#griffin-troops), pinned Army revision 7.26246.158, unit 1810, profile group 1 (base profile and all four options checked against the pinned Army JSON).

**AVA 3 · Regular HI · MOV 15-5 (6–2in), CC16, BS14, PH12, WIP12, ARM4, BTS6, W2, S2.** Shared MSV1, Courage, Terrain (Desert), Jump. All profiles have Pulzar, Pistol and CCW in addition to their main weapon.

| Opt | Weapon / role | Pts / SWC | Rating |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 2 | HMG | 37/1.5 | **S** — default primary attacker |
| 3 | Feuerbach | 38/1.5 | **A+** — hybrid ARO and anti-armor attacker |
| 4 | HMG Lieutenant | 37/1 | **A** — aggressive command option |
| 1 | MULTI Rifle | 31/0 | **B** — durable short-range secondary attacker when SWC is tight |

The Pulzar is a large direct template, PS7 Normal ammunition against BTS, with Intuitive Attack. It is excellent insurance against infiltrators, warbands and opponents that are difficult to hit normally; it can trade even when the main gun's range is poor. The two-Wound chassis can accept some trades that would remove a single-Wound piece. Jump offers access to alternative firing positions, but is not Super-Jump. Weaknesses include Hackable HI status, modest CC, no specialist role, no Mimetism/Marksmanship and no inherent Tactical Awareness.

### HMG — default offensive choice

Use for repeated active-turn face-to-face engagements, clearing AROs, attacking multiple lightly/moderately armored targets, and opening routes for faster or specialist troops. B4 is substantially more consistent than B2; at FT2 it rolls five dice and keeps four. BS14 and MSV1 are a good foundation against Mimetism (-3). Suppressive Fire is available if independent, subject to its Fireteam restrictions.

Take this profile if the Griffin is expected to receive a large share of the combat group's Orders. It is also a good independent attacker, although a useful inexpensive partner often justifies FT2.

### Feuerbach — dual-purpose gun

Select when another trooper is the principal sustained attacker, when the list needs high-impact long-range ARO plus usable active-turn fire, or when anti-armor and Anti-materiel objectives matter. AP+DA B2 can inflict up to four ordinary Saving Rolls on successful hits; the HMG also produces up to four ordinary saves, but is better at winning face-to-face rolls. Feuerbach's ammo has greater effect per successful hit, especially against heavy ARM. Its 32–48in band is better than HMG's.

B1 EXP in ARO threatens three saves per ordinary hit. The Feuerbach does not have the Missile Launcher's circular impact template; its Blast Mode label is not itself a template trait. FT2 is useful but never makes B1 into two possible hits. Its active B2 becomes B2 +1SD at FT2.

The main reason to buy a Feuerbach instead of a Neurocinetics sniper is **genuine active/reactive flexibility plus a durable two-Wound body**. Do not expose it automatically just because it has a good reactive shot. Preserve it when the enemy has an overwhelming active shooter.

### HMG Lieutenant

Identical gun and cost to the ordinary HMG, with a 0.5 SWC saving and one Lieutenant Special Order. A competent aggressive LT, especially with an independently positioned Magistrate CoC and useful NCO. The Griffin is nevertheless Hackable and regularly exposed if used as the primary gun. A non-NCO Griffin spending its own LT Order cannot activate the Fireteam with that Order; leaving/breaking the relevant membership is the cost of personal use. NCO can instead spend the resource as permitted by the NCO and Fireteam rules. Don't select it solely for the SWC saving if the command plan is worse.

## Alternative gunfighters

### Griffin HMG versus Hetkari MULTI Red Fury

| Attribute | Griffin HMG | Hetkari Red Fury |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | 37/1.5 | 48/1.5 |
| BS / W / BTS | 14 / 2 / 6 | 14 / 2 / 6 |
| ARM / PH | 4 / 12 | 5 / 14 |
| Main shooting modifier | MSV1 | Marksmanship |
| Combat Instinct | — | Yes |
| Positive main-gun range | 16–32in | 8–24in |
| Main ammo / Burst | Normal B4 | AP/Shock B4 |
| Direct template | Pulzar | E/Marat |
| Fireteams | FENNECS or KESTREL; matching identity | KESTREL, no matching Kestrel identity |

Against a normal target in Partial Cover at optimal range, Griffin shoots on 14, Hetkari on 17 before other MODs. Against Mimetism (-3), Griffin cancels the -3; Hetkari ignores the Cover BS MOD instead. Hetkari's Marksmanship does not ignore the Saving Roll benefit of Cover. Both gain +1SD at FT2; Hetkari needs two matching escorts in a Haris, whereas Griffin can get it in an inexpensive Duo.

- **Griffin:** preferred general-purpose long-range attacker, budget efficiency and flexible support access. Better when the list needs cheap Eclipse, healing or technical support in the same team, or longer-range DZ-to-DZ engagement.
- **Hetkari Red Fury:** pay the 11pt premium for hard active-turn fights, AP ammo, ARM5, better PH, Combat Instinct and aggressive midfield pressure. E/Marat is particularly threatening to costly Hackable opponents. A solo Hetkari avoids paying for a near-100-point Haris unless the companions are needed.

Griffin HMG + Fennec FO/Disco + Paramedic is 62/1.5 FT3; Griffin + Firefly Engineer is 60/1.5 FT2. Hetkari Red Fury + Firefly Engineer + Quinn Disco is 97/1.5 FT2. Those are different support packages and not a pure like-for-like gun cost comparison.

### Feuerbach versus Hetkari Missile Launcher

- **Griffin Feuerbach (38/1.5):** better active/reactive generalist, B2 active AP+DA, MSV1, lower cost and efficient FT2.
- **Hetkari Missile Launcher (47/1.5):** premium anti-armor ARO, Marksmanship, ARM5, AP+EXP hit mode and circular EXP impact-template mode; especially threatening to clustered Fireteams. Positive band 24–40in, versus Feuerbach 16–32in. Better for a deliberate high-investment reactive position, but B1 makes it less suitable for repeated active gunfighting.

Prefer Feuerbach when expecting to use the gun offensively as well. Hetkari ML is not the default Hetkari profile.

### Griffin versus Agent Maximus

| Attribute | Griffin HMG | Agent Maximus FTO |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | 37/1.5 | 45/1 |
| BS | 14 | 13 |
| ARM / BTS | 4 / 6 | 3 / 3 |
| Durability | 2 genuine Wounds | W1 + NWI, Immunity (ARM) |
| Main gun | B4 HMG | B4 AP Thunderbolt (+2B) |
| Vision advantage | MSV1 | Albedo (-3) |
| Tactical Awareness | No | Yes |
| Specialist / CC | No / CC16 | Yes / CC22, NBW, CC Attack (-6) |
| Anti-HI hacking | Vulnerable | Not an HI/TAG/REM or Hacker |
| Close defense | Pulzar | E/M Carbine, Heavy Pistol, AP+DA CCW |
| Lieutenant SWC | 1 | +1 (bonus) |

Maximus's AP Thunderbolt is PS6, B4 and +3 at 16–32in, with 0 at 32–48in. It gives up one point of PS compared with HMG but adds AP and better long reach. HMG offers Suppressive Fire. Maximus's Albedo is useful against MSV, not general Mimetism. Griffin's BTS6 and genuine W2 have meaningful advantages despite Maximus's ARM Immunity.

- **Griffin:** choose for economical matching links, reliable MSV1 shooting, durability against BTS attacks and a direct-template threat. Especially attractive when Magistrate is already the preferred LT or when a KESTREL Duo is wanted.
- **Agent Maximus:** choose when the shooter must be a specialist, strong in CC, resistant to anti-heavy hacking, or provide additional Order economy. Particularly compelling as Lieutenant for the SWC bonus and with NCO support.

A Griffin generates one Regular Order. Maximus adds Tactical Awareness; Maximus LT also has a separate LT Special Order. Restricted Orders are not three freely transferable Regular Orders. Maximus FTO needs two Fennec-equivalents for FT2 in FENNECS, whereas Griffin itself counts as Fennec. The 48pt Minimus Agent cannot link, due to the synchronized Peripheral controller rule. See [Agent Maximus](maximus-agent.md).

### Griffin versus Black A.I.R.

- **Black A.I.R. Neurocinetics MULTI Sniper (33/1.5):** better dedicated long-lane conventional ARO, MSV2, Mimetism (-3), Combat Instinct, B2 reactive. ARM2/W1 makes it less forgiving of failed engagements; Neurocinetics limits active Burst to 1.
- **Griffin Feuerbach (38/1.5):** better durable hybrid that can still take meaningful active shots, and efficient matching Duo/Core options.
- **Black A.I.R. K1 MMR FO (29/0.5):** stronger specialist/low-SWC anti-ARM secondary attacker with MSV2/Mimetism and reduced exposure to anti-HI hacking. B3 K1, positive 8–24in. Better against Mimetism (-6); much less tolerant of template trades.
- **Griffin HMG:** B4, longer optimal bands, BS14 and real W2 make it preferred for repeated general active-turn work.
- **Griffin MULTI Rifle:** more durable, zero SWC and stronger close band than K1 MMR, but no FO and weaker general anti-armor/anti-Mimetism specialist utility.

Choose each piece for the actual remaining role. **Do not treat Griffin HMG plus independent Black A.I.R. sniper as a preferred/default package**; the user explicitly rejected that recommendation. The sniper remains a valid individual ARO choice where the army and table call for one. Do not routinely combine a stationary sniper with an advancing HMG in the same team either. See [Black A.I.R.](black-air.md).

## MULTI Rifle — situational, not bad

31/0, B3 AP/Shock, B1 DA Anti-materiel mode, PS7, +3 from 0–16in. At FT2 roll four dice and keep three in the B3 modes; BS17 before enemy MODs at optimal range. The Pulzar covers template trades, and independent Suppressive Fire offers midfield defense.

Take when:

- SWC is already spent on other long-range guns or TAGs.
- Terrain is dense and the model can reach 0–16in safely.
- A separate main attacker opens the route, leaving this Griffin to attack closer targets or hold objectives (not press specialist-only buttons).
- A durable, low-SWC secondary shooter with MSV1 and a direct template is specifically needed.

The 6pt upgrade to HMG makes a major difference to first-turn lane clearance. Do not buy MULTI Rifle just as a durable extra Fireteam body. Black A.I.R. K1 FO is cheaper in points and offers a specialist at 0.5 SWC; prefer that option when durability/zero SWC/short-range coverage are not the priorities. MULTI Rifle Griffin + Pathfinder TA remains omitted from the recommended shortlist per the existing roster filter. MULTI Rifle + Firefly Engineer is a legal FT2 zero-SWC closer-range option.

## Fireteams — Griffin's main listbuilding advantage

FENNECS Core/Haris requires at least one real Fennec and permits one Griffin, counting as Fennec. KESTREL Duo/Haris permits up to three Griffins and requires an eligible Griffin, Firefly or Hetkari native anchor; Griffin counts as Kestrel. One Core and one Haris per army; unlimited Duos. FENNECS cannot Duo. FT2 is BS Attack (+1SD), FT3 adds Discover/Dodge, FT4 adds BS and FT5 Sixth Sense; headcount alone does not determine level.

| Composition | Pts / SWC | Level | Rating / purpose |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| HMG + Fennec FO/Disco + Paramedic | 62/1.5 | FT3 Core/Haris | S — economical advancing attack and healing utility |
| HMG + Fennec FO/Disco + Minelayer | 64/1.5 | FT3 Core/Haris | S — attack plus pre-deployed mine and close protection |
| HMG + Quinn Disco FO | 63/1.5 | FT2 Duo | S — Eclipse/FO/short-range templates without consuming Core/Haris |
| HMG + Bipandra TA | 62/1.5 | FT2 Duo | A+ — Doctor and additional Tactical activation |
| HMG + Firefly Engineer | 60/1.5 | FT2 Duo | A+ — NCO, Albedo, engineering and equipment support |
| HMG + Firefly Repeater | 56/1.5 | FT2 Duo | A — cheapest listed matching partner, NCO and network utility |
| HMG + Pathfinder TA | 54/1.5 | FT1 Duo | A — FO, Sensor, Repeater, additional Tactical activation rather than +1SD |
| Feuerbach + Firefly Minelayer | 62/1.5 | FT2 Duo | A — long ARO plus turret/mine defense |
| Feuerbach + Bipandra Doctor | 61/1.5 | FT2 Duo | A — ARO plus recovery |
| HMG + Black A.I.R. K1 FO + Fennec FO/Disco | 79/2 | FT2 Core/Haris | A — conditional complementary ranges, anti-ARM and mission access |
| MULTI Rifle + Firefly Engineer | 54/0 | FT2 Duo | B — zero-SWC short-range package |

These are constructed alternatives, not all simultaneously fieldable nor uniformly report-confirmed. Firefly Engineer can clear eligible tech states but cannot heal Griffin's Wounds. A Doctor/Paramedic can provide Wound recovery under the applicable rules. A Tactical Awareness Order or NCO-converted Order activates the team through the owning leader; it is not an extra Griffin shooting Order. Firefly's NCO needs an available LT Order and cannot use one already converted to Regular by Strategos. Pathfinder supplies its own restricted Tactical Order, but does not boost the same-unit level. Prefer partners that bring real utility rather than bodies picked only to increase FT level.

A four-Fennec + Griffin Core can reach FT5 and is an option for maximum shooting/ARO bonuses rather than a default; the three-model Core preserves points, maneuverability and independent units.

Solo is fully viable when the Griffin has an independent lane, partner movement/coherency would constrain it, or support is already plentiful elsewhere. A useful FT2 Duo is often preferable, but an additional body is never free.

## Quantities and architecture

- **0:** valid when the list has a more appropriate main attacker, sufficient long-range reach and alternative team architecture.
- **1:** usual choice; HMG for primary shooting, Feuerbach for hybrid support.
- **2:** good when the two have distinct roles, independent firing angles and enough supporting Orders; not a default requirement.
- **3:** generally excessive in standard 300pt play unless a deliberate specialized architecture justifies it.

**Dual Griffin example:**

| Team | Role | Pts / SWC |
|---|---|---:|
| Griffin HMG + Quinn Disco FT2 Duo | Offensive, Eclipse and specialist | 63/1.5 |
| Griffin Feuerbach + Firefly Minelayer FT2 Duo | Hybrid/defensive, mine, turret, NCO | 62/1.5 |
| Total | Four Regular Orders, Core and Haris free | **125/3** |

A compact FENNECS Core with one Griffin and a separate KESTREL Duo with another is also viable. Rarely put two Griffins together in one team: independent lanes and complementary escorts usually offer more than duplicate guns clustered together. Two Griffins cannot both join the same FENNECS team; KESTREL allows it but legality is not a recommendation.

## Selection summary

| Need | Preferred option |
|---|---|
| Economical primary gun | Griffin HMG |
| Superior active face-to-face fighting | Hetkari Red Fury |
| Specialist attacker, Order economy or LT SWC bonus | Agent Maximus (LT if command plan supports it) |
| Dedicated visible long-range ARO | Black A.I.R. Neuro Sniper |
| Premium anti-armor missile ARO | Hetkari ML |
| Durable active/reactive hybrid | Griffin Feuerbach |
| MSV2 anti-armor specialist, low SWC | Black A.I.R. K1 FO |
| Zero-SWC durable close-range shooter | Griffin MULTI Rifle |

Start with Griffin HMG as the benchmark, then pay the premium for specialized capabilities only when those capabilities have an actual job. Feuerbach is the preferred Griffin alternative if the primary active shooter is already covered and the list needs a hybrid gun. The MULTI Rifle is a useful niche choice, not the normal third gun.
