**SKIP — C+ overall.** Niche experimental pick, not a standard inclusion. Ruby is a durable autonomous Specialist Operative and non-lethal anti-heavy disruption piece, but 19 points and an Irregular Order are a significant opportunity cost in Kestrel. Her baseline kit has no lethal ranged weapon; Booty can transform her, but list construction must work without a favorable roll.

## Weapons and disruption

Her native BS Attack (+1B) improves the active-turn Burst of her ranged attacks, including applicable Booty weapons. Ordinary reactive attacks remain Burst 1; Speculative Attack is capped at Burst 1.

| Weapon | Active Burst | Preferred range | Job |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Adhesive Launcher Rifle | 3 | 8–16" | Immobilized-A, anti-heavy disruption |
| E/M Grenades | 2 | 0–8" | Isolated and potentially Immobilized-B |
| Flash Pulse | 2 | 8–24" | Stunned; emergency denial |
| Stun Pistol | 3 | 0–8" | Close-range disruption |
| CCW | 1 | CC | Emergency lethal attack |

- **Adhesive Launcher Rifle:** B3 at BS15 in its +3 band before enemy modifiers. PARA forces PH-6 saves, bypassing ARM, BTS and wound count. Useful against costly TAGs, HI and low-PH elite pieces. Immobilized-A can be cleared by the target's Dodge or an Engineer; it is disruption, not permanent removal.
- **E/M Grenades:** PH12, B2 direct at PH15 within 8" before other applicable modifiers. A legal speculative throw within 8" is B1 at PH9. E/M forces two BTS/2 saves and can isolate enemies, plus immobilize eligible hardware. No lethal damage. The short range and lack of forward deployment limit initial threat.
- **Flash Pulse / Stun Pistol:** respectable non-lethal alternatives, but do not remove opposing Orders permanently. Stun does not cancel an otherwise successful attack in the same Order.
- **CC18 / CCW:** her only native lethal weapon; not a reason to seek melee against dedicated close-combat specialists.

## Durability and mobility

Two Wounds, ARM3, BTS6 and S2 for 19 points is a good chassis. Immunity (IMM-B) prevents the state from Carbonite or E/M, but does **not** prevent Isolated, other hacking effects, or conventional damage. She remains hackable HI.

Technorganic allows either a Doctor or Engineer (and appropriate kits) to recover her from Unconsciousness; it does not generally make Engineer a healing tool for a standing VITA trooper. Climbing Plus and 6" first movement help approach objectives along unconventional routes. She has ordinary 8" Move–Move total movement, no forward deployment and no native Mimetism. Do not expose her as a primary ARO gun.

The universal active-turn Burst bonus means that an acquired conventional firearm scales particularly well. Roll after deploying her; the result is not known when choosing her initial position.

**Valuation:** 10/20 excellent or very good, 4/20 good, 5/20 situational, 1/20 redundant. Weighting situational results at half gives **82.5% weighted usefulness**, not an empirical performance probability. Eight of twenty outcomes (40%) add a lethal ranged weapon, including grenades and the flamethrower. The HMG outcome is particularly attractive but only 5%. Never assume a favorable result when building the list.

| Sforza | 23–26 / 0 | Regular anti-elite gunfighter with lethal Viral Rifle, X-Visor Adhesive Launcher Rifle, NCO and appropriate FTO options |

Sāchā is the default autonomous budget specialist. Ruby's additional 13 points buy primarily a tougher objective-contesting body, repeatable B3 Adhesive Launcher and stronger Booty scaling; they do not buy better WIP or a pooled Regular Order. Jackals already cover inexpensive E/M denial particularly efficiently from Infiltration. Sforza has a better long-range Adhesive engagement envelope and a lethal baseline weapon.

## Narrow use case: autonomous Group 2 objective contester

Consider Ruby if a mission rewards surviving near objectives or occupying zones and a spare trooper slot and 19 points are available after essential roles are covered. Deploy along a protected approach, use personal Irregular Orders to advance or interact with objectives, and opportunistically disable expensive opposing pieces. Her own Order is not available to the main attack piece; additional activations require Orders from her own Combat Group or other legal Order sources.

- Use the durability to contest objectives that a fragile specialist is unlikely to survive.
- Use adhesive against an exposed heavy target when disabling it saves more Orders than trying to kill it conventionally.
- Exploit Climbing Plus for safe approach routes or whichever Booty result actually appears.
- Avoid sending her into unfavorable firefights, hacking networks, or buying her merely to fill a slot.

**Skip especially when the list already contains multiple autonomous Irregular units** (infiltrating Jackals, Sāchā, Warcor, Beasthunter) or needs additional pooled Orders. Ruby is not a mandatory specialist, a primary gunfighter, or a cheap cheerleader.
