# Knauf, Outlaw Sniper — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: Skip by default.** Knauf is a credible **independent active-turn sniper** with **B3**, Mimetism (-3), MSV1 and a one-time **Surprise Attack (-3)**, but Kestrel has stronger specialized alternatives at nearly identical cost. **Black A.I.R. Neurocinetics** is the preferred exposed ARO sniper; **Blink Team MULTI Sniper** provides the stronger concealed ambush/deployment package. Knauf is a narrow anti-Mimetism offensive alternative, not a standard roster priority.

## Current profile and rules-source correction

**31 points / 1.5 SWC · AVA 1 · Regular LI · no Kestrel Fireteam access.**

MOV 10-10, CC21, BS13, PH11, WIP13, ARM1, BTS0, VITA1, S2. **MSV1, BS Attack (+1B), BS Attack (Shock), Courage, Mimetism (-3), Stealth, Terrain (Total), Surprise Attack (-3), Decoy (1).** Weapons: **MULTI Sniper Rifle, AP Heavy Pistol, CC Weapon**.

**Important data discrepancy:** The [pinned Kestrel roster](../../../../sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md#knauf) currently omits **BS Attack (+1B)** and **BS Attack (Shock)**. The user-supplied Army profile (20 Nov 2026) explicitly has both; **this note uses the Army screenshot for those skills**. This note does not silently revise the pinned roster.

- **MULTI Sniper: B3 active / B1 ARO.** N5.3 BS Attack (+1B) increases Burst **only in the Active Turn**; it does *not* produce B2 ARO.
- **BS Attack (Shock)** adds Shock to his shooting, including **AP+Shock** or **DA+Shock** with the corresponding MULTI modes. It is relevant chiefly against VITA1 targets vulnerable to Shock; don't treat it as universal anti-heavy firepower.
- **Decoy (1)** creates deployment/target-identification uncertainty and helps set up his one-time **Surprise Attack (-3)**. It is **not** Hidden Deployment and does not reliably protect him once revealed.
- **No Fireteam access:** He cannot receive FENNECS FT2 +1SD or contribute to a linked gun/specialist package.

The Black A.I.R. Neuro has **MSV2**, **Combat Instinct** (resists Surprise Attack), two reactive shots, higher ARM/BTS and potential Fireteam +1SD. That is decisively preferable if the unit's job is **standing on a roof and defending a fire lane**. Its B1 active fire makes Knauf much better at proactive sniper attacks.

Blink has **Hidden Deployment and Mimetism (-6)**, which are usually better than Knauf's Decoy for protecting an ambush sniper and hiding its real location. However, Knauf's **extra active Burst and MSV1** make him more effective when *actively* attacking Mimetism (-3) enemies. He is not strictly inferior in all matchups; he is just harder to justify in a typical Kestrel list.

## Where Knauf can contribute

**Narrow niche — long-range active removal of Mimetism targets.** B3 BS13 with MSV1 is a workable sniper offense. When his **Surprise Attack (-3)** applies, the target can face an extra defensive penalty; Mimetism (-3) further taxes targets without the appropriate visor. The MULTI weapon's AP and DA options, with native Shock, provide flexibility. This is strongest when he can start from cover in a favorable 16–48-inch lane and knock out a valuable ARO model without needing the Fireteam or main assault package.

**Limited defensive flexibility.** After attacking he can remain a B1 sniper ARO, and Decoy might initially complicate enemy targeting, but **ARM1/BTS0/VITA1** means he is poorly suited to prolonged reactive exchanges. Do not take him primarily because of Decoy.

**Not a close-combat plan.** CC21 and AP Heavy Pistol are acceptable emergency fallbacks, not a reason to spend 31 points on a sniper.

## Opportunity cost within Kestrel

- **Black A.I.R. Neuro (33/1.5):** Spend **+2 points** for a far stronger dedicated ARO and Fireteam eligibility.
- **Blink MULTI Sniper (32/1.5):** Spend **+1 point** for Hidden Deployment, Camouflage and Mimetism (-6); usually the stronger surprise/defensive piece.
- **Griffin HMG (37/1.5):** Spend **+6 points** for BS14, B4, ARM4, two Wounds, MSV1 and FENNECS/KESTREL Fireteam access. Better general active attacker inside 32 inches, although Knauf has a much better 32–48-inch sniper band and an opening Surprise Attack.
- **Black A.I.R. K1 FO (29/0.5):** If the priority is midfield anti-ARM shooting *and* objectives, this Forward Observer has more mission utility and saves 1 SWC; it does not replace a sniper's 32–48-inch reach.

## Selection rule

**Take Knauf only if** the list already covers specialists, resilient active attack and reactive defense, **and** you specifically want an **unlinked B3 long-range MSV1 sniper** able to threaten Mimetism (-3) AROs with Surprise Attack and Shock-enhanced MULTI ammunition. He may also be considered when the Black A.I.R. and Blink slots are already committed elsewhere.

**Otherwise skip.** For ARO choose **Black A.I.R. Neuro**; for a hidden sniper ambush choose **Blink**; for general proactive firefights use **Griffin or another main attack piece**. Knauf's shooting is not bad, but his 31-point / 1.5-SWC price buys too little unique utility for Kestrel.
