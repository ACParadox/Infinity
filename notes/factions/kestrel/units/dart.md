# Dart, Optimate Huntress — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: Niche/Skip.** Dart is too expensive for her ordinary guns and midfield-specialist role in Kestrel. **The only potentially worthwhile experimental use is the 42-point / 0.5 SWC Dart (Minelayer) + Sharvara K-9 synchronized attack fork.** This is an unproven tactical experiment, **not** a default recommendation or a second primary gunfighter. Otherwise, take a cheaper Scarecrow, Peacemaker, Beasthunter, or Jackal according to the job.

## Why Dart is a skip in Kestrel

1. **Poor gunfighter return on 35–42 points.** BS13 with an AP Submachine Gun is threatening at short range but has neither the reach nor the Burst/range-band dominance expected from an expensive active attacker. The **Viral Tactical Bow (+1SD)** is a useful specialist attack/ARO against vulnerable biological targets, but remains **Burst 1** and is not a reliable gunfighting solution against strong AROs. An AP SMG is fine on a cheap midfield piece; it is not a compelling reason to buy Dart.
2. **A lot of the bill buys overlapping utility.** NWI + Shock Immunity makes Dart harder to remove, and MSV1, Climbing Plus, Camouflage, Decoy and Specialist Operative are individually good. But these do not automatically pay for themselves when her job is just to push one objective, deploy a mine or defend a short lane. Her **Mimetism (-3)** is weaker than a Scarecrow's -6.
3. **Midfield scoring and ambush are cheaper elsewhere.** A **Scarecrow FO at 25/0** has BS13/WIP13, **Infiltration**, **Hidden Deployment**, Mimetism (-6) and Specialist status. It starts farther forward and is better for low-risk late-game objectives. Scarecrow **BSG + AP Minelayer at 27/0.5** is much less expensive as an ambusher (but is **not** a specialist). Scarecrows have only one VITA and lack Dart's MSV/NWI, but cost **10–17 points less**.
4. **Other Kestrel purchases cover the narrow jobs better.** A **24/0.5 Peacemaker with Auxbot-3B** places a forward shotgun/Repeater, Heavy Flamethrower bot and Shock Mine. A **15/0 Beasthunter** offers a camouflaged HFT/Panzerfaust speed bump. An **11/0.5 infiltrating Jackal** brings E/M Grenade Launcher + Shock Minelayer. Those are better value if only those functions are needed.
5. **Competition for precious attack Orders.** A Kestrel list often already has a Griffin, Hetkari, Redeye or TAG for the expensive offensive role. A 42-point Dart package demands real Orders to close distance and justify its cost; sitting in Group 2 as an expensive passive order generator is unattractive.

Dart **does** have distinct benefits: MSV1 helps against enemy Mimetism, Climbing Plus can generate vertical angles, and NWI/Shock Immunity improve survival after taking a hit. But they do **not** reverse the Kestrel value comparison. **Do not promote the 35pt grenade or 36pt Minelayer profile as a separate recommended niche** merely because those tools work sometimes.

## Only experimental use: Dart + Sharvara synchronized attack fork (42 / 0.5)

The reason to test this version is **not** its AP SMG or a free mine. It is **two attack origins under a single Order**, with incompatible responses demanded of a nearby defender.

### Example: contesting a guarded corner

1. Deploy Dart's **Camouflage marker, Decoy, pre-placed Shock Mine**, and **Sharvara's Camouflage marker** as four initial markers, subject to the usual deployment and Minelayer placement rules. This creates confusion, not four independently order-generating troops.
2. Advance both models while maintaining **Peripheral (Synchronized) Coherency**. Dart seeks an 0–8" AP SMG angle; Sharvara approaches a related angle or corner where its **E/Marat** (for hackable/heavy targets) or **Heavy Riotstopper** (for an immobilization threat) can catch the defender.
3. Declare a legal synchronized attack: **Dart fires her AP SMG while Sharvara fires its appropriate weapon**. Under the normal synchronized-Peripheral ARO restrictions, a defender threatened by both generally must choose **which activated model to react against**. It may contest Dart's rolled attack and suffer Sharvara's direct template unopposed, or attempt to handle Sharvara and leave Dart's Burst-3 shots unopposed, subject to LoF, template positioning, and the actual declared ARO.
4. If advantageous, use Sharvara's **CC22, Natural Born Warrior, CC Attack (-6), DA CC Weapon** as a follow-up close-combat deterrent rather than trading it casually into an enemy template.

**The 'fork' is the entire experimental thesis.** It can manufacture an awkward defensive choice when *both attack origins are already in useful positions*. It is not two normal Order pools, does not make the AP SMG a good long-range gun, and does not let the models activate separately. An enemy able to screen, split the approach, kill the K9, or deploy expendable trade pieces can spoil the plan.

### What can go wrong

- **Only one pooled Regular Order:** Sharvara's activation is synchronized, not an extra independent activation or an extra unit slot's Order.
- **Coherency and movement mismatch:** Dart has Climbing Plus; Sharvara does **not**. Sharvara's S3 silhouette and different routes can make the promising double angle impossible in practice.
- **Fragile and hackable dog:** Sharvara is **ARM0/STR1/S3**, susceptible to hacking and templates; losing it leaves a **42-point Dart whose defining upgrade is gone**.
- **No significant stand-off firepower:** both need safe short-range delivery. Good enemy deployment, long ARO corridors and a marker screen increase the setup cost.
- **ARO vulnerability:** the fork is especially relevant **in Dart's active turn**. Sharvara does not make the pair invulnerable to enemy templates or coordinated threats, and an opponent may trade a cheap trooper for the K9.
- **Cost/opportunity:** 42 points also buys **Scarecrow AP Minelayer 27 + camouflaged Beasthunter 15** (two independent threats), or **Scarecrow FO 25 + infiltrating E/M GL Jackal 11**, leaving 6 points. These alternatives do not duplicate the synchronized fork, but offer substantially more efficient general battlefield coverage.

**Test only if:** the table has tight corners and workable K9 approach routes; you expect vulnerable high-value targets close enough for E/Marat/Heavy Riotstopper; your primary long-range attacker already exists; and you can allocate Orders to advance *both* halves of the pair. Measure success by **Orders consumed, enemy positioning constrained, immobilizations forced and objectives secured**, not just kills.

## Podcast / player evidence and how much weight to give it

- **[Tactical Awareness S3 Ep44 — Mazebreaker OSS update](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3E6Og0ydRErBokXHYOG6PG)**, around **1:24–1:26** (May 2026): explicitly explains Dart + dog at a corner, choosing **E/Marat or Riotstopper plus Dart's SMG** to produce the synchronized attack fork. Hosts also question whether **42pt** is worth it versus cheaper competitors, and highlight K9 fragility. **Useful tactical hypothesis, not repeated tournament proof.**
- **[Irregular Order #37 — Mazebreaker Army Update](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6AaLlwl5Nd3Uic0H3YzJnG)**, around **3:15** (May 2026): describes Sharvara as a cool but **very expensive** addition giving Dart a distinct role. More curiosity than demonstrated results.
- **[MetaChemistry #135 — Acontecimento](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2J6ByFT0PJGKB0DiSQ55AC)**, around **55:50–56:20** (pre-Sharvara): likes Dart but prefers **E/M Grenades to Minelayer** because E/M is distinctive *in Acontecimento*. This argument transfers poorly to **Kestrel**, where an infiltrating Jackal already offers E/M GL for 11pts.
- **[Loss of Lieutenant #168 — Operation Mazebreaker profiles](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4MxU8qsoJlbZkYCLt1fo6d)**, around **31–33 min** (May 2026): discusses Dart's high point cost versus other midfield specialists, with interest in stacking camouflage and Minelayer deception. This is **OSS context**, not direct Kestrel validation.

**Evidence limitation:** no convincing repeatable Kestrel battle-report track record was established for Dart + Sharvara. The fork above is **a plausible rules-based tactical experiment**, not an observed high-efficiency build. Other sectorials' praise for Dart should not be imported as a Kestrel roster recommendation.

## Decision

**Default: Skip Dart entirely.** Buy Scarecrow FO for hidden scoring, Peacemaker/Beasthunter/Scarecrow Minelayer for midfield denial, and Jackal E/M GL for low-cost anti-heavy pressure.

**Single exception worth testing:** **42/0.5 Dart (Minelayer) + Sharvara K-9** on a dense table, specifically to engineer **AP SMG + E/Marat/Heavy Riotstopper synchronized attack forks** and exploit the extra camouflage-marker uncertainty. If the fork cannot be set up consistently without spending several dangerous Orders, discard the experiment and return to the cheaper units.
