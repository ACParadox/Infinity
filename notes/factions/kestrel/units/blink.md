# Blink Team — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: Heavy Rocket Launcher + WildParrot is the clear preferred profile (strong situational pick, not mandatory); MULTI Sniper is niche; Shock Marksman Rifle is usually a skip.** Blink is an **independent Hidden Deployment ambush/ARO** purchase, not a Fireteam gunfighter. Its strength is controlling *when and where* the enemy discovers a threat, rather than maintaining a visible gunfight for several Orders.

## Pinned N5.3 profiles and shared capabilities

Source: [Kestrel Colonial Force authoritative roster](../../../../sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md#blink-team), Army revision `7.26246.158` (N5.3). **AVA 2; Regular LI; no Kestrel Fireteam access.** MOV 4–4", CC15, **BS13**, PH10, WIP12, ARM1, BTS3, VITA1, S2. Shared **Camouflage, Hidden Deployment, Mimetism (-6), Stealth, Surprise Attack (-3)**. None of the three profiles is a mission specialist, has Infiltration or has Forward Deployment.

| Opt | Loadout | Points | SWC | Assessment |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---|
| **3** | **Heavy Rocket Launcher, WildParrot, Assault Pistol, CCW; X-Visor** | **28** | **1.5** | **Preferred:** hidden continuous-damage template, unusually flexible HRL range, E/M area denial |
| **2** | **MULTI Sniper Rifle, Assault Pistol, CCW** | **32** | **1.5** | **Niche:** hidden 32–48" sniper ambush and AP/DA options, but overshadowed for exposed ARO |
| **1** | **Shock Marksman Rifle, Shock Mine, Assault Pistol, CCW** | **28** | **0** | **Usually skip:** credible B3 opening at 8–24", but insufficient deployment reach, utility and differentiation |

**Deployment distinction:** A Blink can remain in Hidden Deployment (no model or marker) or deploy as a Camouflage Marker. Its **Shock Mine is carried equipment, not a Minelayer mine**: the MMR profile does *not* put a free mine on the table during deployment.

## Heavy Rocket Launcher + WildParrot — why it wins

At **28/1.5**, the HRL Blink is an efficient **second-layer defensive trap** that punishes a committed push after the opponent has planned around the visible AROs. The HRL fires **B2 in the Active Turn / B1 in ARO**, with **Continuous Damage** and a **Circular Impact Template** in Blast mode (or a non-template Hit mode). A hidden impact-template ARO can threaten a Fireteam or multiple models crossing a closely packed lane; this is genuinely different from buying another conventional sniper.

### X-Visor makes the HRL unexpectedly flexible

The [weapon chart](../../../../rules/4_Ammunition%20and%20Weaponry/Weapon_Chart.md) gives the HRL `-3 / 0 / +3 / +3 / -3 / -3` across **0–8 / 8–16 / 16–24 / 24–32 / 32–40 / 40–48"**. [X-Visor](../../../../rules/5c_Equipment/X-Visor.md) improves its **negative *range* MODs** (`-3 → 0`), resulting in:

| Range | With X-Visor |
|---|---:|
| 0–8" | **0** |
| 8–16" | **0** |
| 16–24" | **+3** |
| 24–32" | **+3** |
| 32–40" | **0** |
| 40–48" | **0** |

The weapon still does not fire beyond its normal maximum and X-Visor **does not** remove Mimetism, cover, visibility or Surprise Attack penalties. The **B4 Assault Pistol** is the much stronger emergency close-range attack tool if the Blink survives to act. X-Visor also helps with unfavorable Discover range bands when relevant.

### WildParrot is not incidental

The [WildParrot](../../../../rules/4_Ammunition%20and%20Weaponry/WildParrot.md) is a **Disposable (1) deployable E/M Mine-like weapon** with the Perimeter placement trait; it is **not** a mobile Boost weapon and does not chase targets.

- **Placement:** Place the WildParrot **within the Blink's Zone of Control** (not necessarily in silhouette contact), with a legal path to the destination. It becomes active **at the conclusion of the Order**.
- **Reactive placement:** [Place Deployable](../../../../rules/5a_Common%20Skills/Place_Deployable.md) is an ARO if the Blink has **LoF to the active trooper**. Revealing from Hidden Deployment to place one is possible, but **the WildParrot cannot affect the same Order in which it was placed**. This is a trap for the *following* action, not an instantaneous anti-charge explosion.
- **Effect:** When subsequently triggered, the WildParrot makes **two E/M Saving Rolls against BTS/2**; a failed save can Isolate, and can also Immobilize-B eligible HI/REM/TAG/VH. This can cost a large attacker far more than a single Order.
- **Counterplay:** Once placed it is visible and static; the enemy may shoot it, dodge its trigger or find a different route. A Blink cannot project a WildParrot from its hidden written position without revealing/declaring an eligible skill.

**Tactical decision:** When a threat passes the hidden position, decide whether a **B1 continuous-damage HRL ambush now** is valuable enough, or whether **placing the E/M trap** sets up a worse next Order for a TAG/HI/REM. The latter may be strongest behind a corner or alongside another visible defensive piece.

### When the HRL is worth buying

- **Opponent likely to commit to a turn-one assault:** Hide to cover a plausible TAG/HI/REM or Fireteam entry route, ideally where the template or WildParrot threatens a meaningful follow-on decision.
- **Layered ARO architecture:** Pair a **visible Sierra TR, Black A.I.R. or other lane guard** with an undisclosed Blink at a different angle. After clearing the obvious defense, the opponent has not necessarily cleared the lane.
- **Fireteam clustering or expensive support pieces:** The circular HRL shot can potentially tag multiple models, preventing a straightforward “one gunfighter shoots down the defender” solution.
- **You can afford 1.5 SWC and 28 points without compromising the real attack piece and specialists.** One premium hidden ARO can be worthwhile; several redundant reactive guns often are not.

**Battle-report evidence:** [Kestrel list-building notes](../kestrel-listbuilding.md#aro-midfield-airborne-and-other-niche-choices) report **Daboarder strongly favoring Blink HRL** for hidden WildParrot-assisted DZ protection and using **visible Sierra TR + hidden Blink** as layered defense. This is practical tactical testimony, **not evidence that the HRL is mandatory or guarantees a kill**.

### Its limitations

It has **ARM1, one Wound and only B1 reactive HRL fire**. Once the ambush is exposed, a strong active attacker (particularly MSV2 or an appropriate template/trading piece) can remove it. It is an **ambush and Order-tax mechanism**, not a persistent turret. Do not reveal merely to take a low-value potshot; the hidden location and potential threat may be more valuable intact.

## MULTI Sniper — hidden ambush, not default sniper ARO

The **32/1.5 MULTI Sniper** buys **Mimetism (-6) and Hidden Deployment**, plus useful 16–48" sniper range bands and **AP / DA / Shock** ammunition options. It has **B2 active / B1 reactive** and can use Surprise Attack (-3) on an eligible **active-turn** attack when starting hidden or in marker state.

### Direct comparison: exposed Black A.I.R. Neurocinetics

| Trait | **Blink MULTI Sniper** | **Black A.I.R. FTO Neuro Sniper** |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | 32/1.5 | **33/1.5** |
| BS | 13 | 13 |
| MULTI Sniper active Burst | **B2** | B1 (Neurocinetics) |
| MULTI Sniper reactive Burst | B1 | **B2** |
| Mimetism | **-6** | -3 |
| Hidden Deployment | **Yes** | No |
| MSV | None | **MSV2** |
| Combat Instinct | No | **Yes** (ignores Surprise Attack MOD) |
| Armour | ARM1 | **ARM2** |
| Kestrel Fireteam | None | **FENNECS Core/Haris**, FTO |

**Black A.I.R. is usually better as a conventional exposed long-range ARO.** For one more point it gets reactive Burst 2, MSV2, Combat Instinct, ARM2 and a Fireteam option (including FT bonuses when the composition qualifies). It is an especially effective defensive choice against Mimetism-dependent attackers.

**Blink sniper is different, not numerically superior:** it cannot be preemptively targeted while hidden. Its niche is an unexpected **32–48" counter-ambush**, or one carefully chosen **B2 Surprise Attack** where the sniper's ammunition and reach matter. The HRL often provides a stronger *distinctive* hidden trap for **four fewer points**, with the template and WildParrot. Use the sniper only if the sniper-specific ammunition or 32–48" engagement is critical.

A Black A.I.R. sniper that is already deployed and visible can be avoided, attacked or neutralized; a hidden Blink changes what the opponent knows before committing to a lane. This information advantage is the real argument for choosing Blink.

## Shock Marksman Rifle — 0 SWC but usually poor value

At **28/0**, the MMR has **B3 active**, Shock ammunition, and a **+3 range band at 8–24"**. A BS13 attacker opening from Camouflage/Hidden Deployment can impose **Mimetism (-6)** and **Surprise Attack (-3)** against a suitable opponent, while the **B4 Assault Pistol** covers very short range. The **Shock Mine** helps protect a nearby route *after an Order is spent placing it*.

The problem is opportunity cost. Unlike a Scarecrow, the Blink **does not infiltrate**, so it normally starts around its own DZ, must spend Orders reaching a valuable midrange target, and cannot score as a specialist. It also lacks the HRL's long-range denial and anti-heavy E/M trap.

The 0-SWC argument is real **only** when the list has **28 spare points**, almost no SWC and a specifically useful hidden B3 defensive counterattack from its starting area. It should not be taken just because “BS13 + Mimetism (-6) is good”; that package is not enough to make it an efficient independent attacker.

## Better Kestrel alternatives by job

- **Black A.I.R. Neuro Sniper (33/1.5):** Preferred **persistent long-range reactive ARO**, especially against Mimetism/Smoke; no concealment but MSV2, Combat Instinct, Neurocinetics and FENNECS access.
- **Sierra TR:** Visible repeatable ARO gun and complementary bait/lane guard for a **hidden Blink HRL**. It is a different defence layer, not a one-for-one ambush replacement.
- **Fennec LRL (12/0) / FTO Jackals (6/0):** Cheaper defensive roadblocks and crossfire coverage when 28 points or 1.5 SWC are better used elsewhere. They do not replicate Hidden Deployment.
- **Scarecrow FO (25/0):** Hidden Deployment **and Infiltration**, with Forward Observer objective utility; generally a stronger purchase when the task is *midfield specialist / short-to-medium attack*, rather than defending an approach from your DZ.
- **Knauf MULTI Sniper (31/1.5):** B3 *active* sniper with MSV1 and Mimetism (-3), but visible and only B1 reactive; niche independent offensive long-range option. See [Knauf note](knauf.md).

## Order economy and concealment discipline

The [Hidden Deployment rules](../../../../rules/8_Game%20States%20and%20Glossary/Hidden_Deployment_State.md) impose a non-obvious cost: **while still Hidden, Blink's Regular Order is not added to its Combat Group's Order Pool**. It can instead use that withheld Order itself to reveal in the Active Turn. If it begins as a **Camouflage Marker** rather than fully Hidden, it contributes its normal Order but loses the invisible-deployment advantage.

**Surprise Attack (-3) is active-turn only.** A Blink **revealing to fire in ARO does not apply the Surprise Attack MOD**. The reactive ambush is powerful because the opponent committed movement/order/position without knowing the Blink existed there—not because it applies a -3 reactive surprise penalty.

Do not confuse the hidden/marker state with being immune to relevant discovery abilities: **Sensor** and other explicitly eligible effects can reveal Hidden Deployment in the appropriate conditions. Once Hidden Deployment is canceled, it cannot be regained (although Camouflage can be re-entered under its normal rules).

A hidden Blink that never meaningfully affects play may become **28–32 points and a withheld Order** spent on uncertainty alone. Place it where revealing, forcing a detour or preventing a dangerous advance would actually matter.

## Practical choice table

| List situation | Pick |
|---|---|
| Need undisclosed defensive ambush + close-area E/M | **Blink HRL 28/1.5** |
| Have visible Sierra/other ARO and want a surprise second layer | **Blink HRL** |
| Want a conventional anti-camo long-range ARO | **Black A.I.R. Neuro Sniper**, not Blink |
| Specifically need hidden AP/DA sniper at long range | **Blink MULTI Sniper 32/1.5**, niche |
| SWC is gone and want a B3 midrange camo counterattack | **Shock MMR 28/0**, only as an exception |
| Need a hidden midfield button-presser | **Scarecrow FO 25/0**, not Blink |
| Need inexpensive DZ defense / Fireteam filler | **Fennec LRL / FTO Jackal** |
| Already have adequate ARO and want to fund a stronger attack/specialist plan | **Skip Blink** |

**Final selection rule:** Evaluate **one HRL Blink** as a *situational, high-impact hidden defensive layer* when the board offers a meaningful surprise lane and the main list can afford 1.5 SWC. **Do not auto-include two.** The **MULTI Sniper** is a specialized concealed long-range alternative, not a better Black A.I.R. Neuro for routine ARO, and the **Shock Marksman Rifle** is generally an inefficient non-infiltrating, non-specialist 28-point gun.
