# Father Lucien Sforza — anti-tech / counter-elite toolbox (Kestrel N5.3)

**Verdict: Situationally strong counter-piece (B overall; B+ in a suitable match-up). Default to the 26pt/0 SWC Albedo (-6) FTO; use the 26pt Holoprojector solo only when forward deployment, Holoecho deception, and Surprise Attack are the actual plan.** The 23pt plain FTO is the budget option. Sforza is **not** a generic Firefly replacement, and is **not** a Specialist.

**Role definition:** “Anti-tech” here means an **anti-elite / countermeasure toolbox**, not a hacker or universal anti-machine weapon. His **Viral Rifle** punishes living targets with high VITA and low/moderate BTS; his **Adhesive Launcher Rifle** can immobilize TAGs and hard targets without penetrating ARM; **Albedo (-6)** counters MSV *and Marksmanship* attacks; his native **BS Attack (+1SD)** improves both shooting and ARO; **NCO** potentially spends otherwise unused Lieutenant Orders. His ability to handle very different defenses is what justifies the purchase.

### X-Visor is central, not incidental

Both Viral Rifle and Adhesive Launcher Rifle have these range bands in N5.3. **X-Visor changes a negative -3 range MOD to 0 and -6 to -3**, but gives no extra +3, cannot remove Cover/Mimetism, and does not extend weapon range.

| Distance | Normal range MOD | Sforza X-Visor |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0–8 in | 0 | 0 |
| 8–16 in | +3 | +3 |
| 16–32 in | -3 | **0** |
| 32–48 in | -6 | **-3** |

This matters especially for an **Adhesive rifle**: Sforza can threaten a TAG at 16–32 inches with no range penalty rather than having to rush inside 16 inches. It also gives Viral useful B3 counterfire at 16–32 inches; this is not equivalent to an HMG or MSV against difficult visual modifiers.

**Independent Suppressive Fire option:** The Viral Rifle has Suppressive Fire. X-Visor improves the suppressive -3 range band at 16–24 inches to 0, while native +1SD continues to help his rolled shots; Suppressive Fire gives B3 ARO and imposes -3 on relevant enemy Face-to-Face Rolls. It **cannot be maintained in a Fireteam**. This is available to an independently operated **Albedo FTO too**, not uniquely to the Holoprojector profile.

### Fireteam value — wants FT2 from *other* members

Sforza is a **Wildcard without Counts-as**: he contributes a body and weapons, **not a matching Fennec/Kestrel/ColCorp identity** for Fireteam Level. In a legal team with at least two matching members, however, FT2's **+1SD stacks with his own +1SD** on eligible BS Attacks/AROs:

- **Solo / FT1:** Viral **roll 4, keep 3**; Adhesive **roll 3, keep 2**; B1 ARO **roll 2, keep 1**.
- **FT2+:** Viral **roll 5, keep 3**; Adhesive **roll 4, keep 2**; B1 ARO **roll 3, keep 1**.

These are **not** 5 or 4 actual Burst; the surplus dice are discarded. FT2 benefits Sforza when two teammates match even if Sforza himself does not. In contrast, adding Sforza to a Griffin **Duo** leaves that KESTREL Duo **FT1**; a **Firefly Engineer** instead makes it FT2 and provides Engineer/Albedo/NCO for comparable points.

**Constructed, legally motivated examples** (not observations of exact played combinations):

| Team | Cost | FT | Judgment |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Sforza Albedo26 + Fennec FO/Disco13 + Fennec Paramedic12** (FENNECS Core) | **51/0** | **FT2** | Compact zero-SWC counter-gun/scoring package; Fennecs establish matching pair. A strong niche choice. |
| **Sforza Albedo26 + Griffin HMG37** (KESTREL Duo) | **63/1.5** | **FT1** | Rarely preferred over Griffin HMG37 + Firefly Engineer23 (**60/1.5 FT2**). |

Kestrel cannot legally form **Sforza + one Fennec** as a FENNECS Duo: FENNECS only forms Core/Haris, and a KESTREL Duo requires its native anchor. Always check the [Fireteam guide](../kestrel-fireteams.md) and [canonical chart](../../../sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md#fireteams).

## Which 26-point version: Albedo FTO or solo Holoprojector?

**Prefer Albedo FTO by default.** At equal cost:

1. It adds a persistent **-6 BS/Discover defense against MSV/Marksmanship**, directly supporting the gunfighter/counter-ARO role. This is far more generally useful in relevant matchups than a one-time Surprise Attack.
2. It preserves flexibility: **can deploy and work solo** or join any *legal* Wildcard Fireteam. It can enter Suppressive Fire when operating independently.
3. In an FT2-capable Core/Haris, **Albedo plus two +1SD effects** produces a very credible secondary active/ARO gun.

**Choose the Holoprojector solo when** you explicitly want **Forward Deployment (+10)**, Holoecho target/deployment confusion and a **Surprise Attack (-3)** opening into an eligible opponent. Its forward start may save Orders and bring the Viral/Adhesive pair into ideal range; Holoecho decoys can complicate enemy targeting, but expire/reveal by their normal rules. This profile has **no FTO access, no Albedo, and no HoloMask**. It is attractive on tables with few meaningful MSV/Marksmanship threats or where a forward ambush route matters more than passive protection. Also consider that decoys are not permanent Mimetism or immunity.

**23pt plain FTO:** choose only when the three saved points are necessary, or Albedo has negligible expected opposition; it gets the same native X-Visor, +1SD, Viral and Adhesive package.

## When to take / skip

**Take:** you already have the standard Kestrel gun/specialist infrastructure and need a **distinct Viral/anti-VITA + repeatable PARA anti-TAG weapon**, particularly with a useful 16–32 inch lane and opposing MSV/Marksmanship; or want an Albedo defensive gun in an FT2-capable inexpensive Core.

**Skip:** a specialist/Engineer is still missing; a Griffin, TAG or another primary gun needs Fireteam matching; there are few suitable VITA/low-BTS or valuable PARA targets; enemy hacking/long-range AROs dominate the planned approach; you cannot afford to protect an ARM2/W1 piece. **Firefly** is usually better as generic 19–25pt KESTREL support: native Kestrel Counts-as, Albedo, NCO and distinct Engineer/CoC/Hacker/Paramedic options. The counter-weapons, not NCO alone, justify Sforza.
