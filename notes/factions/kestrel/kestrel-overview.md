# Kestrel Colonial Force — Overview (Infinity N5.3)

**Kestrel guides:** **Overview** · [Sectorial Inventory](kestrel-inventory.md) · [Listbuilding](kestrel-listbuilding.md) · [Fireteams](kestrel-fireteams.md) · [References](kestrel-references.md).

## Overview

**What Kestrel does exceptionally well:** it pairs **PanO-quality shooting (BS14–15 elite HI/TAGs, strong AROs and linked visors)** with unusually efficient **command and Order generation**, then opens routes with **cheap projected Eclipse (Disco Baller)**, hidden threats, E/M disruption and fast specialists. Unlike an elite-gun-only PanO list, Kestrel can fund multiple independent attacks while paying relatively little for enabling pieces. Its signature sequence is **protect the command engine → create or deny a firing lane → activate the appropriate elite gun → score with a different cheap/surviving specialist**. It also has credible **TAG, aerial Vehicle, airborne and Hidden Deployment** alternatives.

**Fireteam compositions and observed-team legality are maintained in [kestrel-fireteams.md](kestrel-fireteams.md).** This overview retains faction-level strengths, role rankings and distinct unit capabilities, not full linked-team recommendations.

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **Disco/Eclipse, but not a normal Smoke army:** **Fennec 13/0** and **Richard Quinn FO/Disco 26/0** offer **projected Eclipse**, unusual for a PanO force. The pinned standard Kestrel roster does **not** show a native ordinary Smoke Grenade/Smoke GL or Eclipse Grenade carrier: use **Disco placement and its finite charges**, not a conventional **Smoke + MSV shoot-through-Smoke** plan. **Eclipse blocks MSV too**.
- **Command resilience + Order efficiency:** rare combination of **19pt Strategos L1 LT, Counterintelligence, FT Master**, **21pt Magistrate CoC**, and **23pt Firefly CoC**. **Firefly NCO** and **Squalos NCO** can instead consume an unconverted LT Order. **Converting with Strategos and spending the same LT Order via NCO are alternative uses**, not a double dip.
- **Visors and anti-tech:** **Black A.I.R. MSV2** (including the FTO Neuro sniper), **Griffin MSV1**, **Quinn MSV1**, **Banshee MSV1** and other specialist options answer Mimetism. **Firefly and Crux Albedo** create a different anti-visor matchup. Kestrel can attack strong defenses both by **better dice** and by **closing or bypassing a sightline**.
- **More than one heavy chassis style:** **Tikbalang, Squalos Mk-II and Maximus** provide distinct TAG plans; **Redeye is a Vehicle (VH), not a TAG**, with fast aerial Mimetism(-6)/Sensor access. **Pathfinder** offers economical mission support, while **Firefly Engineer or Machinist/Palbot** supplies actual repairs. Maximus trades efficiency for high durability/CC; these heavy assets invite hacking.
- **Strong hidden and backline disruption:** **Blink** Hidden Deployment AROs, **Scarecrow** Hidden Deployment/Infiltration hackers or FO, **Crux** hidden specialist assault, **Jackal** infiltration/E/M mines, plus **Banshee Combat Jump** and airborne Black A.I.R. KHD. This depth is unusual for an army often summarized as only PanO frontal shooting. **No ordinary Impersonation** is printed in the pinned roster.
- **Hacking limitations:** accessible **FastPanda Fennec, Firefly/Scarecrow hackers, Peacemaker/Pathfinder Repeaters** can build a local network, but this is not automatically a top-tier mass-Pitcher/Guided army. The **Clipper FTO** label does **not** make a linked Guided launcher legal in Kestrel. Enemy Repeater grids, E/M and White Noise/Albedo remain serious threats to expensive HI/TAG/visor investment.

**Practical default:** start from **Magistrate Strategos (or another deliberate command plan) + Fennec Disco/FO + a cheap E/M Jackal + one correctly ranged gun + a separate scoring/repair route**. Prioritize redundancy: a visible LT, a single hacked TAG, or one MSV-dependent primary gun should not be the entire game plan. The [dedicated Fireteam guide](kestrel-fireteams.md) now owns the one-Haris decision, legal profiles, compact combinations and observed examples.

## At a glance

- **Strength:** Kestrel combines top-quality shooters, cheap *useful* Orders, mines/hidden threats, Eclipse via Disco Baller, and competent specialists. **Why it works:** the shooting pieces rarely have to pay their own enabling/support costs.
- **Common spine, not compulsory:** **Fennec FO/Disco/Sensor (13)**; **Magistrate Strategos LT (19) + 6-point FTO Jackal(s)**; a main gun (Hetkari, Griffin, Tikbalang, Squalos, Agent Maximus or Crux); **11-point infiltrating E/M GL Minelayer Jackal(s)**; then mission-specific specialist/ARO/repair.
- **Main weaknesses:** aggressive enemy Pitchers/hacking grids into your HI/TAGs; a visible Magistrate LT; Albedo/White Noise versus over-reliance on MSV; off-table pieces reducing Turn-1 Orders; one expensive brawler that cannot score objectives. Build a second attack/scoring route.
- **Disagreement is substantial.** Shepherd likes a distributed-link, high-order all-rounder. The 2026 Irregular Order hosts favor very different units and plans—one prizes Black A.I.R. ARO and a defensive Hetkari ML; the other likes more aggressive pieces and unusual Fireteams. Daboarder (WIP12) emphasizes *scenario contingencies*, Redeye and cheap disruption instead of a fixed shopping list.
