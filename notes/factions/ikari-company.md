**Source of truth:** [Ikari Company roster, profiles, AVA, Fireteams and validation](../../sectorials/na2/ikari-company.md). Exact profile and Fireteam legality must be checked against the linked reference.

## Overview

**Identity:** Ikari fields **cheap Smoke and CC pressure, durable independent objective holders, and unusually varied mixed mercenary Fireteams**. The best construction keeps a second attack route after a cheap forward model trades.

### Particularly strong or unusual Fireteams

- **Wú Míng Haris/Core + Clipper FTO profile 5:** An **AP HMG**, durable HI and a linked **direct Missile Launcher (+1B)**. **Clipper FTO is not Guided**.
- **Keisotsu Haris/Core + Tankō:** Inexpensive Regular team supporting a **Tankō ML** for ranged ARO while Keisotsu specialists still score.
- **Tankō Haris + Daiyōkai:** More expensive mobile NCO/CC/fighting alternative. **Krakot Duo** adds a separate short-range pressure option.

[Current chart](../../sectorials/na2/ikari-company.md#canonical-fireteam-definitions): one Core, one Haris and multiple legal Duos, not arbitrary cross-NA2 joins.

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **Smoke:** **Yojimbo, Desperadoes, Yuan Yuan** and selected **Al Fasid** give credible normal Smoke; **Rui Shi and Brawler MSV2** can benefit.
- **Limited Disco:** **Wardriver, Arslan and Tankō** have profile-specific Disco Baller access, not unlimited Eclipse grenades.
- **CoC:** **Táowù 21/0** is optional succession cover.
- **TAGs:** **Scarface & Cordelia** and **Triphammer**; **no VH** (bikes are WB/LI).
- **Weakness:** Irregular heavy trading + HI/Tag investments can starve the real mission specialists of spendable Regular Orders. Karakuri cannot borrow JSA Fireteam access.

## Tactical research — Ikari Company (reviewed 2026-10-23)

**Use this after the verified roster/Fireteam reference above.** Tactical opinions come from transcript-reviewed analysis and games. **[P1]** is a September 2025 experienced-player review and generally more useful for *how Ikari plays* than the N5 launch reaction **[V1]** or N4 retrospective **[P2]**; nevertheless, exact N5.3 Army 7.26246.159 profiles and the canonical chart above always win. Do not port JSA/Yu Jing/NA2 Fireteam rights, pre-update point values or early-N5 Guided interactions into Ikari.

### At a glance — what works and why

| Plan / block | Buy it for | Failure mode and decision rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Cheap regular-order core + many attacks** | 9–12pt Keisotsu, an economical Wú Míng attacker, and multiple cheap fast threats mean another piece can take over after the first dies. Copper calls Ikari **wide rather than deep**: several good-enough gunfighters and attackers instead of one perfect solution. [P1] | Hard 15-trooper ceiling and too many Irregular warbands can choke Orders; identify two priority attackers and keep enough Regular Orders to run them. |
| **Wú Míng Core/Haris** | **AP HMG 33/1.5** fights armor; **HRL/E/M Carbine 23/1.5** deters lane crossing; **E/M GL 27/0 SWC** attacks threatening targets without a straight face-to-face. **TinBot Firewall (-6) 22/0** and Brawler Hacker can protect/use the formation. [P1, V1] | BTS3 and Hackable; full Core can become an expensive single point of failure. Shorter team if room/Orders matter more than extra Fireteam dice. |
| **Tankō + Daiyōkai Haris OR Keisotsu ARO Core** | **Tankō ML 26/1.5** threatens distant lanes, inexpensive Blitzen/Disco Baller profiles cover corners; Daiyōkai's NCO and 2W+NWI support mobile, mixed-range pressure. [V1, P1] | **Only one Tankō in a Keisotsu team**; old N4 “missile Tankō in cheap link gets huge bonuses” expectations are outdated. Limited Cover/Impetuous Tankō still need a careful ARO lane. |
| **Druze KHD + small Duo/Haris** | KHD **23/0 with Pitcher and D-Charges**, Warhorse, X-Visor: network projection, hacker hunting and button pushing. Pair with a Digger or Brawler to get helpful Fireteam roles while avoiding an expensive Druze-heavy group. [P1, V1] | KHD **does not itself provide ordinary Spotlight**; include HD/FO where needed. Ikari's Druze family is **Duo/Haris, not Core**. |
| **Warband/motorcycle pressure** | **Motorized Yojimbo 20/0**, Desperadoes 7–9, Yuan Yuan from 6 and Regular Krakots 14+ bring smoke, trades, flanking and close combat. Smoke can set up Brawler MSV2 or Rui Shi MSV2 shooting. [V1, P1] | The fast models are vulnerable when isolated; their Irregular/Impetuous Orders and S4 bike lanes demand a budget. Do not use them all because they are individually cheap. |
| **Objectives over tabling** | **Ninja FO/HD/KHD**, Karakuri FO, Pathfinder FO, Druze specialists, cheap Keisotsu FO and airborne Bashi specialists offer different approaches. [P1, V1] | **Weak midfield Camo presence** beyond the Ninja; several specialists must walk from the DZ. Reserve a protected objective-taker before spending all points on fights. |

**Default construction test:** Can the list (1) deploy safely going second, (2) clear a long ARO, (3) reach a midfield button, (4) threaten armor/hackers with AP/E/M/para or hacking, and (5) keep a useful second attacker after the first fails? **[P1]** recommends designing for going second and roughly **three Irregular models** as *his* preference—not a rule; adapt by mission and Orders.

### Strong unit choices; preserve disagreements

- **Wú Míng versus Daiyōkai:**
  - Copper [P1] prefers Wú Míng **33pt AP HMG** as the primary gun because it saves **17pt** against Daiyōkai HMG (50pt) *and* shoots AP, while the Wú Míng HRL and E/M GL offer cheap follow-ups.
  - **Opposing case:** Daiyōkai has **ARM5, NWI, NCO, BS Attack (SR-1)** and better independent fighting/CC options, and makes a **Tankō Haris**
  - it is not simply a worse AP HMG. Pay extra if the NCO order and Haris mobility matter. [V1, P1, P2]
- **Armand Le Muet MSV1 sniper (34/1.5) versus disposable AROs:**
  - Copper [P1] considers him unusually valuable: **Mimetism (-6)** plus MSV1, forward deployment, MULTI sniper, symbiont transmutation, backup pistols/CC make him both lane controller and active sweeper.
  - **Counterweight:** he costs ~3 Keisotsu and cannot replace specialist/fast-trade coverage
  - the cheaper **33pt Minelayer** profile trades MSV for an extra defensive mine. [P1]
- **Tankō:**
  - The **18–19pt Blitzen/Disco Baller** options are robust affordable close support, with hard CC threat
  - **26pt ML** is a major ARO commitment and may eat 1.5 SWC that could fund another gun. A Tankō is not automatically an advantaged gunfighter just because it joins cheap Keisotsu. [V1, P1]
- **Karakuri (31–38; AVA2, not Fireteam-linkable in Ikari):**
  - ARM3/BTS6/STR3 with **Immunity (Enhanced)**, FO and GizmoKit means an independent specialist can absorb danger while moving to an objective.
  - **Mk12** pushes direct midrange gunfights
  - **MULTI Rifle** broadens ammunition
  - **E/Mitter** offers technical suppression. Expensive relative to button-only alternatives, slow starting position, and cannot borrow JSA's different Karakuri Fireteam access. [P1, V1, P2]
- **Keisotsu (9–18):**
  - 9pt AP-rifle filler / 9pt LT costs **0.5 SWC**
  - 10pt combi LT also 0.5
  - 11pt FO adds a cheap mission Order. The **16pt ECM: Hacker (-6) + CrazyKoala** hacker is useful when Druze Pitchers project the hacking network. The **12pt Thunderbolt BS Attack (-3)** can complicate ARO dice from a Core, but does not become a premium active HMG. [V1]
- **Brawler hacker (16/0.5), MSV2 sniper (25/1.5), HRL (15/1.5):**
  - Wildcard utility across fireteams: native **Sixth Sense** hacker and TinBot-supported Wú Míng group can punish repeaters/Stealth
  - MSV2 shoots through smoke
  - HRL adds a cheap long-lane deterrent.
  - **Disagreement:** [V1] favors the new cheaper Keisotsu hacker and considers Brawler hacker less essential
  - [P1] values Sixth Sense and supporting the Wú Míng team. SWC/desired lane decide.
- **Yojimbo:**
  - [V1] rates the **motorized identity** highly for smoke launcher, CrazyKoala/Minelayer, Mimetism (-3), NWI, CC and mobility.
  - **Foot identity** may handle stairs/tight spaces but gives up the motorcycle's reach
  - V1 strongly favors motorized.
  - **Only one Yojimbo total**, across the two identities. Desperadoes compete with motorized bounty hunters for Irregular slots: Desperado 7pt smoke/chain rifle is usually more efficient for trade/smoke
  - **Motorized Bounty Hunter** brings alternate Booty/Red Fury/riotstopper utility, including Mechanized Duo eligibility. [V1, P1]
- **Bashi Bazouk T2/AP Boarding Shotgun (14) vs Cube Jäger:**
  - Bashi can appear from a flank to trade into a hardened target or Lieutenant
  - cheap Specialist Operative variants solve late objectives. Cube Jäger (15–18) is a Parachutist/Paramedic and different classifieds tool. Copper [P1] favors Bashi's kill potential
  - [V1] also prefers a Bashi specialist over Jäger for many missions, but Jäger wins where **Paramedic** or precise recovery is needed.
- **Arslan versus cheap Lieutenant:**
  - Copper [P1] dislikes **35pt Arslan** as an expensive short/midrange gun for what it buys
  - **9pt Keisotsu LT** or **15pt Brawler LT** hides more cheaply.
  - **Opposing case:** Arslan's WIP13, MSV1, NWI, Disco Baller and ability to fight are genuine upsides
  - is the list already equipped to protect a fighting LT?
  - **Táowù Chain of Command (21/0)** can insure, but has an opportunity cost. [V1, P1]
- **Al Fasid (44–50):**
  - ARM5/BTS6, Sixth Sense, Minelayer, Albedo and HMG+smoke/HRL make it a credible **standalone** anchor with area denial. Copper [P1] finds it squeezed out by cheaper **linkable** Wú Míng guns/HRL. [V1] is also skeptical of its inability to link despite the attractive profile. Worth reconsidering when independent fire support, layered smoke and no Fireteam order dependency are valuable.
  - **Warhorse is not synonymous with all hacking attacks being impossible**
  - apply actual N5 rules.
- **Krakots versus more Irregular warbands:**
  - Unlike Desperado/Yuan Yuan, Krakots are **Regular, Impetuous**, with Forward Deployment, Super-Jump and close combat
  - useful to preserve the Regular order pool and threaten midfield. Their direct fighting is relatively short-ranged
  - do not spend many Orders reaching an overprotected target. [V1, P1]

### Conflicting testimony and corrections to retain

1. **Faction strength:** [L1] December 2024 hosts initially considered post-N4 Fireteam restrictions a major Ikari weakness, revising their assessment slightly when accounting for cheap durable guns. [P1] September 2025 specialist argues those same cheap Wú Míng, Tankō and Irregular/Regular threats create **redundancy**, and no single piece must win the game. Treat placement/tier as dated opinion, not a settled win-rate fact.
2. **Daiyōkai/Al Fasid versus Wú Míng:** [V1] launch discussion values Daiyōkai HMG and [P2] N4 runs Daiyōkai/Tankō as primary guns. [P1] explicitly drops expensive Daiyōkai/Al Fasid from optimized lists because **AP Wú Míng 33** and cheaper HRL cover more jobs per point.
