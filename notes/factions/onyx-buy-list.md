## Collection verdict and most useful buys in order

**One owned Caskuda** is **exactly legal in Onyx** and should be counted, but its **Staldron is unconfirmed**. No dedicated Unidron, Nexus, Umbra, Fraacta, Maakrep, Noctifer or other Onyx force is recorded owned. **Old Combined Army six-starter contains 3 Unidrons + Fraacta + Maakrep + Umbra (6/6 Onyx)**, while newer generic Combined Army 10 Action Pack has **8/10 Onyx legal** (Dāturazi and Victor Messer not on Onyx roster). Onyx can use legal Unidron Core and layered drone/hacker teams; vanilla has different restrictions. The first valuable purchases are **Unidrons, Nexus, Umbra, cheap Ímetron/Ikadron, a support drone and Bit/KISS**.

## Proxy suggestions using already owned miniatures — strict policy

One physical mini can only represent **one** simultaneous combatant. Cross-army reuse is legal only by named roster; collection inventory is **not cumulative across buy lists**.

## Whole-unit skips and cross-sectorial purchase reuse

Unlike vanilla, **Onyx has Core** and true Unidron full teams. **No hard unit veto** but source-audited *Void as generic Base Operator* is lower-priority; rocket/KHD exceptions. A full Suryat shooter team needs a legal required-choice, not just generic Armored infantry. Already owned **Caskuda** is a better reason to delay Overdron/Xeodron/Sphinx than buy all. Never buy fake extra Unidrons to reach Total or imagine base-only accessory is another Caskuda.
