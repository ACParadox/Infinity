## Own versus buy: priorities

**Owned exactly:** Warcor x1 (Kestrel) and Yudbots x2 (Steel Phalanx). **Ioann Bann zero**, but a suitable owned **Caledonia McMurrough S6** dog-warrior is available as a provisional same-S proxy.
