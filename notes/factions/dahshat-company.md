# Dahshat Company — N5.3 tactical guide and collection analysis

## Overview

**Identity:** Dahshat excels at combining inexpensive forward traders, abundant Smoke, a linked MSV2 attacker, and accessible TAG/REM support. It wins through several independent routes rather than treating the Maghariba as mandatory.

### Particularly strong or unusual Fireteams

- **Zúyŏng Haris/Core with Rui Shi MSV2 Spitfire (23/1):** Tactical Awareness HMG opens long lanes while Rui Shi exploits Smoke and Mimetism. The chart allows **Rui Shi** in this family, not arbitrary REMs.
- **Brawler Duo/Haris/Core with Monstrucker:** Affordable Sixth Sense Hacker/HRL protection, Engineer recovery and legal wildcard utility such as a Digger. Strong support efficiency; modest raw firepower.
- **Ghulam Core/Haris with Fiddler FTO option 1:** Budget order/specialist shell. **Standalone Jackbot Fiddler variants do not link**.

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **Normal Smoke + MSV2:** **McMurrough, Kum, Saito and Ghulam NCO Smoke GL** support **Rui Shi/Brawler MSV2**.
- **Limited projected Eclipse:** **Odalisque Disco Baller** and a **Húláng** profile, not repeatable Tohaa-style Mirrorball.
- **TAGs:** **Maghariba** and cheaper **Triphammer**; **no VH-class Vehicles**.
- **Network/Guided:** **Valerya**, cheap Repeaters, **Shaytaniyah** and **Traktor Mul** support Targeted payoffs when the rest of the plan justifies them.
- **Missing Chain of Command:** The current standard roster has no CoC profile; cheap Lt security is more important.
- **Order pressure:** Irregular trades consume slots and cannot supply the Regular pool for a flagship Zúyŏng/TAG attack.

## Tactical research — Dahshat Company (reviewed 2026-10-23)

**This is tactical analysis, not a rules update.** Dedicated deep-dive episodes [W1/W2] and [R1] predate N5; [N1] is a transcript-reviewed N5 Dahshat battle report; [T1/H1/Y1] cover current shared TAG and REM mechanics. **The verified N5.3 Army 7.26246.159 tables above override every older claim about profiles, prices, skills, LT choices and Fireteams.** No long-form post-October-2025 Dahshat-only N5 faction focus was verified in the searched MCP catalogue, so the edition caveat matters.

### Winning concept: a good gun with numerous cheap second plans

| Plan / package | Why it wins Orders or objectives | Why it can fail |
|---|---|---|
| **8–9pt Libertos, 15–19pt Hunzakuts, 6pt Kum, 23pt McMurrough** | Multiple inexpensive Camo/smoke/close assault trades force enemy attention away from the primary gun. Dahshat can field these while still affording costly TAG/HI. [W1, R1, N1] | They are **Irregular**; the 15-trooper cap and limited Regular Orders can hurt more than points. Avoid paying for every excellent cheap attacker in one list. |
| **Zúyŏng + Rui Shi Core/Haris** | **33/1.5 Zúyŏng Tactical Awareness HMG**, **26/0 or 27/0 TinBot Firewall(-6)**, and **23/1 Rui Shi MSV2 Spitfire** generate reliable high-burst gunfighting, including through smoke. Rui Shi is legally joinable **only in the Zúyŏng family** here. [W1, R1, Y1] | Hackable HI and a **one-STR Rui Shi** put too many points/orders in one vulnerable formation. Consider a small Haris rather than automatically paying for a five-person Core. |
| **Ghulam LT/NCO/Valerya support** | **11/0 Ghulam LT**, **15/0 LT +1 Command Token**, **15/1 NCO smoke grenade launcher**, **16/0.5 Ghulam HD**, **15/0 Doctor(+3)** and **19/0.5 Valerya Wildcard HD/Pitcher** provide Regular Orders, smoke, protected missions and a projected hacking network. [W1, R1, N1] | These are mainly BS11 support troopers, not a premium frontline Core. Support the TAG, Rui Shi or midfield plan instead of pretending the Ghulam HMG replaces them. |
| **Brawler specialist defense** | **16/0.5 Sixth Sense hacker**, **25/1.5 MSV2 sniper**, **15/1.5 X-Visor HRL**, **14/0 Regular Digger** and **12–16pt Monstrucker Engineer** form complementary Brawler Duo/Haris/Core combinations. [W1, W2] | A WIP12 hacker/BS12 gun remains mediocre against dedicated opponents; Monstruckers are Irregular. Only one Core and one Haris total. |
| **Maghariba versus Triphammer** | **Maghariba 71–72/1.5, BS14/ARM8/TacAware** is a sustained ranged attack and lane-control center; **Triphammer 48/0** is a cheaper zero-SWC TAG with ancillary specialist. [T1, H1] | Maghariba is **Silhouette 8**, hard to conceal/manoeuvre on tight boards; Triphammer is lower-quality BS13/BTS3. Either costs troops and objectives. |

**Build-order check:** Have (1) main active shooter, (2) protected Lieutenant and useful Regular Orders, (3) an FO or infiltrating objective specialist, (4) long- and close-range armor answers, (5) smoke/MSV or hacking control if the list relies on it, and (6) a second attack lane after the primary piece is lost. [R1] argues **trooper slots are often scarcer than points**: do not add cheap troops merely because you can afford them.

### Individual unit value and opposing arguments

- **Libertos, AVA2:**
  - **8/0** chain-colt/SMG is a cheap forward trade
  - **9/0 Mimetism(-3)** costs one point more and makes the Camo/Forward Deployment (+20) nuisance harder to remove. **9/1 Mimetism(-3) Minelayer** trades precious SWC for a prepared board-defense layer. [W1/R1] favor Mimetism almost universally
  - **counter:** at the 15-slot cap, a Regular specialist or cheaper 8pt profile can be more valuable than a second disposable Irregular.
- **McMurrough (23/0) and Kum (6–11/0):**
  - Dog-Warrior's PH16, smoke, Super-Jump, Chain Rifle(+1B), Dogged and AP+DA CCW create a direct route to a valuable target and **smoke for the Rui Shi/Brawler MSV2**
  - bikes add fast smoke and cheap trades. [W1] calls him central to aggressive Dahshat.
  - **Counter:** speculative charges spend Orders exposing the piece before it reaches the high-value target. Motorized Bounty Hunters (8–16) compete for Irregular slots
  - **motorized 112** instead buys a **Regular Doctor**, mobility and objectives.
- **Hunzakut vs Zhēnchá:** **15/0 Hunzakut FO** or **19/0 FO + deployable repeater**, WIP14/Infiltration/Camo, buys cheap prepositioned objectives and hacking projection **but is Irregular**. **30–34pt Zhēnchá** buys a **Regular**, NWI, BTS6 Camo/Infiltration specialist and stronger late-turn durability, with FO, deployable repeater, minelayer or HD. If the mission needs the specialist alive in round 3, Zhēnchá justifies the cost. [W1/N1]
- **Critical hacking edition change:**
  - [R1]'s N4 statement **“no Trinity anywhere” is no longer true**. The current roster still contains **NO KHD profile**
  - however **Zhēnchá option 4 is a 34/0.5 Hacking Device with Trinity upgrade**. Ghulam, Brawler and Valerya have ordinary HD
  - Kameel can take EVO. An HD with Trinity is **not** a KHD, and a KHD is **not** needed to Spotlight. Flag the difference rather than preserving the old absolute claim. [N1]
- **Valerya (19/0.5):**
  - WIP14 HD, Pitcher, Total Control(+1B), Wildcard: useful Hacking/Spotlight/repeater network at low cost. [W1] calls her the lynchpin
  - [R1] calls Dahshat's hacking *thin*, because most hackers are lightly protected and there is no native KHD. Both views hold conditionally: plan a **specific** repeater + FO/Spotlight + attack rather than buying guided artillery on autopilot.
- **Saito (33/0), Húláng (31–38):**
  - Saito has Hidden Deployment, Infiltration, Mimetism(-6), smoke, T2 shotgun and EXP melee for assassination, **but no current specialist/FO skill**. Húláng brings Mimetism(-6), NWI, Specialist Operative, Monofilament and options for FD+20 mines or a cheaper Disco Baller. [W2] likes high-ceiling offense
  - **counter:** premium midfield tools compete with cheaper objective-taking Hunzakuts and resilient Zhēnchá.
  - **No Húláng may enter Dahshat Fireteams despite its FTO labels**.
- **Fiddler (29–43):**
  - **29/0 FTO Engineer with Super-Jump** can join **Ghulam Core/Haris only**, while **36/43 standalone Jackbot variants** are synchronized assault/repair packages but do **not** join Ghulam Fireteams. [R1] praises the Jackbots for breaking a position then exploiting it
  - **counter:** FTO at 29 can offer a protected 0-SWC specialist while keeping points for Libertos/REM backup. Peripherals are not independent Orders.
- **Brawlers + Diggers + Monstruckers:**
  - Sixth Sense HD and smoke-shooting **MSV2 MULTI sniper** give quality support
  - **14pt Regular Digger Wildcard counts as Brawler** and handles corner CC/templates. Monstruckers can repair TAGs/REMs and join Brawler family, but their own order is Irregular. [W1/W2] praise role coverage
  - **counter:** a maximal Brawler Core has weaker active shooting than a Zúyŏng/Rui Shi list.
- **Miranda / Odalisques / Sforza:**
  - **25–28pt Miranda Wildcard** has Mimetism(-6), Specialist Operative, Monofilament and counts as **Odalisque** in its family. **19–26pt Odalisques** offer NWI, Sixth Sense, combat-instinct tools, and 0-SWC Disco Baller/Paramedic/Viral Marksman profiles as Duo/Haris. **23/0 or 26/0 Sforza FTO Wildcard** brings NCO, Viral + adhesive, while **non-FTO option 1 (26/0)** is solo Holoprojector/FD/Surprise Attack. Useful tricks if their unique objectives beat an extra HI shooter
  - not mandatory filler. [W2/H1]
- **Ghulam LT, Sforza/NCO and Fireteams:**
  - Ghulam LT **11/0** or **15/0 +1 Command Token** and Brawler LT **15/0** are current choices.
  - **Ghulam smoke NCO 15/1** and **Sforza NCO** can exploit the LT Order. Unlike [W1/R1] N4 discussion, **the current Zúyŏng roster has NO Lieutenant loadout**. No standard Chain of Command
  - protect the LT and watch the 15-slot economy.
- **Al Fasid (44–50):**
  - ARM5/BTS6, Warhorse, Sixth Sense, Minelayer and HMG+Smoke GL or HRL make a **self-sufficient solo/Assault Duo anchor**.
  - **Counter:** its 1.5 SWC and inability to join Zúyŏng/Ghulam links can be an inefficient duplication of Maghariba or Zúyŏng HMG roles. [W2/H1]

### Keep disagreement and legality visible

1. **Discount swarm vs expensive flagship:** [R1] calls Dahshat the discount army where **troop slots, not points**, are scarce, and considers Maghariba slightly overpriced; [N1/T1/H1] show Maghariba is still a very good heavy anchor because the other pieces leave enough points. Both are conditional on mission/terrain.
2. **Linked Rui Shi vs independent Maghariba:** [W1/R1] prefer efficient **MSV2 Spitfire + smoke/EVO**, while [T1] praises **Maghariba mine dispenser/defense**. Do not rank them as interchangeable: one wins negative-mod gunfights efficiently, one absorbs shots and controls wide lines.
3. **Traktor vs Shaytaniyah Guided:** [W2] values **9pt Katyusha/1 SWC**; full Guided ML **16/1.5** offers a different weapon at more cost. The Shaytaniyah **19/1.5 +1B profile is NOT Guided**. Neither gains any Dahshat link.
4. **Hacking strength:** [W1] sees strong enough network through Valerya/Ghulam and Repeaters, while [R1] points out **no native KHD and weak anti-hacker resilience**. **N5 Zhēnchá HD now includes Trinity upgrade**, a meaningful improvement, while remaining **not a KHD**.
5. **Roster-only rules, do not inherit prior Fireteams:** **Zúyŏng Core/Haris legally includes Rui Shi** (not Fanous/Rafiq/Maghariba); **Ghulam Core/Haris legally includes Fiddler option 1 only**; Brawlers can include Monstruckers and **Zellenkrieger FTO profile 3 only (no Smoke)**. **Húláng FTO/FTO-2 and foot 112 FTO have no Fireteam access**. The Assault Duo chart has peculiar **required=true/min=0 for every row**; do not invent a universal required anchor or unsupported combos. Check authoritative chart.
6. **Current Lieutenant reality:** Older [W1/R1] recommend a Zúyŏng Lieutenant HMG. That profile **does not exist** in the pinned roster. Ghulam/Brawler provide current LTs, while Ghulam NCO and Sforza use LT Orders. Do not copy an N4 list.

## Battle-report-derived army and Fireteam tests (N5; checked against N5.3)

Dahshat is a **NA2 hybrid**, not a Haqqislam sectorial, but its Ghulam, Odalisque and Maghariba packages appear in the same [241-report archive](../battle-reports/n5-battle-reports-and-tournament-reports.md). The following are **historical observed formations and module trials**, not validated 300-point/SWC current Army exports. Use the [N5.3 Dahshat Fireteam chart](../../sectorials/na2/dahshat-company.md#canonical-fireteam-definitions): **one Core, one Haris, unlimited Duos** with explicit Ghulam/Brawler/Odalisque anchors and restricted Wildcards.

### What was actually fielded

- **Four-person Ghulam Core — Ghulam Lieutenant + Ghulam Doctor + Valerya Gromoz + Miranda Ashcroft.** [#117 Firefight vs Shindenbutai](../battle-reports/111-120-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) explicitly names the four linked bodies, using **Pitcher/E/M protection** while **Maghariba, Zhēnchá Hacker, Monstruckers, Libertos and two Uragan Traktor Muls** work independently. Current Ghulam min-one plus **Valerya/Miranda Wildcards** supports this four-member structure; do **not** turn the independent Uragans into an imaginary Fireteam. The report's **6–0** is from an early-N5 experimental list, not a claim that double Uragan is always optimal.
- **Brawler Hacker + Brawler MSV2 MULTI Sniper + Digger — Brawler link of at least three.** [#129 Tic-Tac-Toe](../battle-reports/121-130-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) explicitly activates a Brawler team, but translated narration cannot rule out additional members. As a **current trial Haris**, it has a legal Brawler anchor and Digger Wildcard. The same report's **Miranda + Sforza FTO + uncertain third troop** was called a separate Haris; current legality requires an **Odalisque anchor** if using that as an Odalisque Haris. Do **not** infer that an unknown Ghulam can join it or that the source verified its third profile.
- **Miranda Ashcroft + 2 Odalisques — three-person Odalisque Haris.** Explicit in [#183 Dire Foes Nocturne](../battle-reports/181-190-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md), with **Maghariba, McMurrough, Fiddler + two Jackbots and Brawler tower snipers** separate. The formation is current-chart plausible through **Odalisque minimum one + Miranda Wildcard (counts as Odalisque)**. The two Brawlers were nearby but **not confirmed as a Duo**, and Jackbots are peripherals, not Haris members.

### Practical list architecture and failure control

- **Network/attrition build (#117):** guarded **Valerya Pitcher/Ghulam Core**, **Maghariba + Engineer recovery**, **one or two reactive Uragan/REM threats**, and **Zhencha/Libertos objective approach**. Remember the attack relies on both specialists and safe repair lanes, not merely linked hacking or continuous Guided attempts.
- **Flexible mercenary scoring (#129):** Brawler ranged team with a **separate** Odalisque/Miranda harassment route, **McMurrough Smoke** and **Zhencha Hacker**. The **10–1 Military Orders** result exposed the danger of focusing on firefights while the enemy secured connected Tic-Tac-Toe objectives; protect an independent console runner.
- **Limited-insertion Nocturne (#183):** **Miranda/Odalisque Haris + Maghariba + McMurrough + Fiddler/Jackbots** threatened the center, yet **USARF won 6–5 by extracting the civilian**. A TAG-heavy attrition advantage cannot substitute for a mission courier. This custom narrative scenario is **not** a standard ITS result.

**Historical legality warning:** unlike old mixed chart examples, current **Ghulam links do not gain Miranda/Valerya by being native members**—they join via explicit Wildcard access. **Fiddler with Jackbots is a controller/peripheral package**, not an FTO link by default; only the **Fiddler FTO profile 1** is a native Dahshat Ghulam-team candidate. Current **Brawler links** may include Digger/Monstrucker under their own caps, but cannot import the old all-mercenary teams from other NA2 armies.
