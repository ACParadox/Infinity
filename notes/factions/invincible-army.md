## Overview

**What Invincible Army does especially well:** IA stacks **Tactical Awareness, LT-to-NCO Order use, multiple-Wound heavy infantry, strong long-range/ARO shooting, and protected HI Fireteams**. Its distinctive trade-off is that expensive Hackable pieces can dominate combat yet leave insufficient independent scoring and midfield protection if the list overconcentrates.

### Particularly strong or unusual Fireteams

- **Invincible Core/Haris:** an eligible **Zúyǒng or Shàng Jí** foundation, with **Hǎidào KHD/Pitcher** and a legal **Yān Huǒ FTO ML**, **Rui Shi**, or specialist Wildcard, creates a durable gun/hacking/scoring module. The Neurocinetics Yān Huǒ is *not* the linkable FTO; respect the current chart's required-choice flags.
- **Shock Duo:** selected **Mówáng/Gūijiă** options give independent armoured move-together pressure. The Army chart's unusual required-choice/minimum fields are not permission for arbitrary pairings.
- **Forward Duo:** **Húláng FTO-2 + optional Krit Kokram FTO-2** can combine forward attack and Engineer utility; **no Wildcards** can join this family.
- **Structural flexibility:** one Core, one Haris and unlimited legal Duos, rather than a compulsory all-HI five-member team.

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **Exceptional reactive fire:** **Yān Huǒ Neuro ML** works as an independent ARO; the **FTO ML** has a separate team-oriented role.
- **Disco/Eclipse route:** **Mówáng Disco Baller** profiles can block a dangerous line, but IA does not have a cheap **Shaolin-style conventional Smoke** warband engine.
- **HI hacking defence:** **TinBots, Hǎidào KHD/Pitcher, EVO Pangguling and Engineers** are consequential; a Firewall in one team does not cover solo HI elsewhere.
- **TAG/VH limits:** **Gūijiă AVA2** is the **only** standard IA TAG family; **no VH** appears. **Sù-Jiàn and Fēiquán are unavailable** in IA's main roster.
- **Main structural weakness:** high average troop costs and hacking exposure. Spend on real objectives and lane screens as well as elite guns.

### General plan and list-building priorities

**Thesis:** Invincible Army (IA) trades broad cheap board control for **durable multiple-Wound HI, active-turn Order concentration, and flexible specialist/attack Fireteams**. Its challenge is to **win missions after winning gunfights**, particularly when facing hacking/E/M, visual mods, and attacks around its expensive HI. The 2025 CanCon 2nd-/3rd-place IA players (Peter Henry and Jacob Ridley) attribute their success to board-control buffers and mission-specific defensive architecture, not just a giant Fireteam. Tactical Awareness shows both NCO-heavy 10-model lists and broader defensive lists.

1. **Build around the order engine.** **Dāoyīng LT(+1 Order)** comes in different profiles and fuels particular NCO recipients:
   - **23pt Boarding Shotgun:** inexpensive LT shell.
   - **28pt Hacker:** hacking and late objective access.
   - **34pt Sniper/Minelayer:** defensive command option.
   - **Mówáng, Tai Sheng, Krit, Bìxié or Hac Tao:** NCO candidates when the selected profile actually supports it.

   Keep Dāoyīng hidden until its own shooting, hacking or button-pushing matters; avoid buying more NCOs than the Orders use.
2. **Select the primary problem-solver:**
   - **Shàng Jí AP HMG + TinBot Firewall(-6):** protected team attack.
   - **Mówáng NCO:** durable independent advance.
   - **Yān Huǒ:** long-range offence or denial.
   - **Gūijiă TAG:** Super-Jump, heavy shooting and CC defence.

   Buying all four may leave too few Orders and objective specialists.
3. **Plan a second angle and actual scoring:**
   - **Zhēnchá:** camouflaged infiltrating specialist.
   - **Húláng:** forward assault.
   - **Liú Xīng:** airborne attack or objective work.
   - **Lù Duān / Rui Shi:** alternative REM attacking angles.
   - **Gūdǎn / Libertos:** inexpensive Camo, mines, Shock Mines and templates that protect an HI block.

   CanCon players specifically credited Gūdǎn forward defence with keeping the primary team effective.
4. **Allocate 10 vs 15 troopers deliberately:** Extra Tactical Awareness and LT→NCO activations allow compact IA builds to act like larger groups, but a low model count concentrates risk and coverage. One 5-man attack squad that cannot cross a hacking network is not efficient, regardless of its theoretical order count.
5. **Protect HI from counter-tech:**
   - **EVO Pangguling:** Fairy Dust where appropriate.
   - **Link TinBots:** Firewall for the protected team, not automatically for unrelated troops.
   - **Hǎidào KHD/Pitcher:** anti-hacker pressure and reach.
   - **Mines plus Engineer/servant:** local denial and usable recovery routes.
6. **Fireteam legality matters:**
   - **Limits:** Core 1, Haris 1, Duo source value 256 (unlimited).
   - **Invincible family:** Zúyǒng and Shàng Jí required=true/min=0 entries are ambiguous; check actual Army pairings.
   - **Shock Duo:** Mówáng and Gūijiă similarly show required=true/min=0; do not assume any arbitrary pair works.
   - **Forward Duo:** requires Húláng FTO-2, optionally Krit FTO-2; Wildcards are explicitly banned.
   - **Son-Bae/Pangguling:** only qualifying FTO profiles have Wildcard access.

### Disagreements worth retaining

- **Strong faction vs predictable HI:** **CanCon 2025 second/third place** demonstrates high-level results with purposeful Gūdǎn, mines, mobile objective play and defensive overlap. Swiss Miss sees broad viable N5 options. Shepherd's explicitly N4 skepticism about being outperformed by Vanilla/White Banner is *historical* and should not replace 2025 evidence.
- **Gūijiă vs smaller HI attackers:** Some like its ARM8, CC and MULTI HMG; others would trade its 77 pts for multiple Shàng Jí/Mówáng, a specialist and a separate angle. Main issue is **single-piece concentration**, not whether the TAG is useless.
- **Link concentration vs distributed NCO/Duos:** One large TacAware link can push a lane hard; smaller threats plus Zhēnchá/Gūdǎn score through different lanes and force less predictable decisions.
- **Yān Huǒ ARO bunker vs mobile scoring:** Multiple ML/HRMC are oppressive on open tables, but low mobility and expensive static pieces can lose objective missions to smoke, hacking, mine bypass and forward scoring.
- **Hac Tao hacker vs Zhēnchá hacker:** N5 podcast guests dispute the price premium: Hidden Deployment and durable BS14 armour are real, but infiltration gives earlier buttons and more affordable Trinity.
- **EVO/repair investment:** Valuable in Rui Shi, Liú Xīng, Gūijiă, firewall-heavy lists; not automatically worth its costs if the list only wants generic heavy infantry guns.
