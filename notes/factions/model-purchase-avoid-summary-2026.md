# Infinity N5.3 — Whole-model buying skips and low-priority purchases

**Updated 2026-10-30.** This document answers **“Can I skip buying this entire unit?”**, not “Which profile should I avoid in Army?”. It extracts the purchase-level decisions from all 11 current [must-have / go-to / avoid](./) faction audits.

## How to read these labels

- **Skip by default** — a named speaker/panel criticizes the *usual purpose* of the complete unit, not just a bad weapon. Buying it is low priority for ordinary 300-point lists **in the named sectorial**. Does **not** imply mathematical domination in every matchup.
- **Defer** — interesting but niche, expensive or disputed. Do not purchase before you have a list that actually uses the distinctive role.
- **Not a whole-unit avoid** — an unpopular **profile**, package or legacy build; the same troop has useful alternate loadouts. This is **not** a reason to skip its miniatures.
- **Hard never-buy / all-profile dominated across all sectorials:** **none verified** in the source transcripts and current N5.3 roster audits. One person's personal “never” is not a cross-faction theorem.

## Do **not** skip these whole units merely because of one avoided profile

| Profile-level warning | Why buying the troop/kit is still reasonable |
|---|---|
| **Ghulam / Fusilier / Moderator / Harbinger / Senku premium gun** | Cheap LT/Doctor/FO/Hacker/Fireteam utility often is the **reason to buy the unit**. |
| **Tohaa Chaksa Auxiliary Neurocinetics HMG, Sukeul ML, Sakiel Spitfire, Ectros NCO, Kamael Spitfire** | Different cheaper/utility/command profiles retain valuable independent roles. |
| **Nomads Corregidor Gecko Vulkan, Territorial Red Fury, Tomcat gun** | MMR/TacAware, higher-Burst or specialist/Engineer/Doctor/Paramedic alternatives can be worthwhile. |
| **Combined Umbra Legate LT, Yaogat Panzerfaust, Wraith hidden MMR, Gearhead plain Engineer** | Other options in the *same unit* are go-to choices in the detailed audits. |
| **JSA Genbutai BSG, Shinden Aïda Swanson non-FTO, old Shurayuki opinion** | Genbutai specialists/NCO, Aïda FTO or the *updated N5.3* Shurayuki link ability prevent full-model veto. |
| **ALEPH Naga sniper, Samekh FTO, Scylla FTO** | Naga has +1SD; **ordinary Samekh has Guided**; regular Scylla and legal configurations have separate jobs. |
