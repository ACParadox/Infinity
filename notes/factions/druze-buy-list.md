## What is owned and what to buy first

**Exact owned:** Armand x1, Warcor x1, four PanO Dronbot chassis, which are physically usable for named Druze Pathfinder/Clipper/Sierra/Fugazi as correctly equipped. No Druze, Hunzakut or Brawler recorded owned.

**P1** Buy Druze KHD/Pitcher first, plus Brawler HRL and Hunzakut FO; establish actual Targeted chain with Valerya/other standard HD rather than incorrectly linking Guided Clipper.

**P2** Take 1–2 cheap Yuan Yuan/Diggers and choose Scarface+Cordelia or Triphammer with full accessories; avoid buying multiple TAG systems before testing.

**Skip** second Armand/Warcor and duplicate Dronbots; ordinary Druze HMG as first main gun underperforms KHD utility; Anaconda is a conditional first-TAG deferral, not a never-take.

## Inventory-aware proxy options and cross-sectorial reuse

Under [the proxy policy](../miniature-proxy-guidelines.md), a proxy **must match exact S (base diameter plus height)**; prefer matching LI/MI/HI/SK/REM/TAG and suitable appearance. Don't proxy one unit using a model **whose own printed unit is also legal in this same sectorial**. A model can serve exactly one deployed body at a time. Robotic servants, motorbikes, creature companions, TAG Operators, transformations, and ancillary figures need their own suitable representation. Confirm tournament/event rules and explain distinct stand-ins to your opponent.

| Target family | Already-owned donor | Restriction |
