# Vanilla O-12 — N5.3 tactical analysis and collection decisions

## Overview

**What Vanilla O-12 does especially well:** unusually broad access to high-quality long-range guns, MSV, hacking, hidden midfield specialists, and multiple fast objective routes. More unusually, **this Vanilla army can field one Haris and two Duos**. It opens a lane with a premium attacker or visibility control, then scores with an independent specialist rather than committing everything to one expensive model. The drawback is a high points floor: too many 30–70pt tools can starve Orders, screens and late-game scoring.

### Particularly strong or unusual Fireteams

- **Gamma FT-II Haris — a legitimate Vanilla premium shooting package:**
  - **Gamma HMG:** primary shooter, maximum one Gamma.
  - **Vidocq FTO:** low-cost escort or Doctor profile.
  - **Ment CoC / Jackboot / Omega:** choose a legal third member for succession, technical support or a second attack role.
  - **Trade-off:** expensive and Hackable; this is a Haris, not a Core.
- **RoadBot + Oko FT-I Duo — an excellent mobile mission module:**
  - **RoadBot Paramedic 20:** fast climbing and transformed mobility.
  - **Oko FO 15 or TA 18:** Sensor, Repeater, scoring and possibly an independent Tactical Awareness activation.
- **Ioann Bann + Varangian FT-I Duo — uncommon close-assault/Smoke combination:**
  - **Ioann Bann 32:** durable Irregular/Impetuous dog-warrior attacker with Smoke.
  - **Varangian 12:** inexpensive Regular Smoke/trading partner.
  - **Rule caveat:** the Duo does not convert Ioann's Irregular Order into a pooled Regular one.
- **Other tailored FT-I/FT-II Duos:** eligible **Omega**, **Silverstar**, **Jackboot**, **PSI-Cop**, **Nimrod FTO** and **Sonya** combinations offer alternatives. Apply the [current member limits and FTO restrictions](../../sectorials/o-12/o-12.md#canonical-fireteam-definitions).

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **Unexpected Vanilla Fireteams:** **no Core, one Haris, up to two Duos**; do not import Starmada/Torchlight Wildcards.
- **Two TAG families and a separate Vehicle:**
  - **Zeta:** premium HMC, climbing, resilience, ancillary scorer.
  - **Wrecker:** cheaper Tactical Awareness, AP threat and ancillary scorer.
  - **Firebat:** aerial **Vehicle (VH)**, not a TAG.
- **Smoke, Eclipse and White Noise are distinct:**
  - **Varangian / Hippolyta / Ioann Bann:** conventional Smoke.
  - **Epsilon / Nimrod:** MSV2 guns exploiting normal Smoke.
  - **Bluecoat Disco 16:** limited-use *projected Eclipse* that blocks MSV as well.
  - **Cyberghost HD+ 21/0.5:** White Noise and Pitcher/Repeater network; its BS11 Pitcher is a fallback, not free reliable projection.
- **Excellent command insurance:** **Ment CoC 14** protects aggressive Lieutenants; **Alpha LT24** instead offers Strategos L1 and Counterintelligence. These are separate units.
- **Extensive midfield options, not infinite budget:** **Razor**, **Lynx**, **Nightshade**, **Gangbuster**, **Moonraker** and **Team Sirius** serve different hidden, adhesive and network roles. Focus on what the mission needs rather than buying all of them.

## Community tactics and transcript synthesis (2026-10-23)

**26 Oct 2026 Vanilla-only tactical update:** [Robert Shepherd's complete O-12 faction focus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Nl8BMJ8E5U) (full transcript reviewed) argues that O-12 has **very few capability gaps but severe points/opportunity-cost constraints**.

**Recommended starting infrastructure:**
- **Moonraker:** two 15pt/.5 SWC Minelayers providing overlapping Adhesive Launcher Rifle 8–16in approach AROs.

- **Ment CoC:** 14pt command insurance when taking an aggressive Lieutenant.
- **Kappa HD:** 13/.5 cheap hacking and objective support.
- **Raveneye LT / Alpha LT:** 12pt budget or 24pt command option, respectively.
- **Gamma HMG:** 50pt example of the deliberate premium gun purchase.

**Distinct strengths and mission packages:**
- **Ioann Bann (32)** and **Kyra Sharma (45):** close attack / mobile scoring options.
- **RoadBot (20) + Oko Tactical Awareness (18):** mobile objective work.
- **Epsilon HMG (34):** MSV shooting through conventional smoke.

**Source disagreements (specific profiles, not faction-wide rules):**
- **Gamma vs Zeta (70):** the presenter prefers Gamma as the default primary gun.
- **Razor:** FO (29) preferred over Monofilament Minelayer (31).
- **Sirius:** FO (21) preferred over Hacker (24).
- **Vidocq:** FTO (15) or Doctor (19) escort preferred over an automatic FTO Sniper (26).
- **Wrecker:** not the presenter's default Vanilla TAG choice.

### Play plan: why Vanilla O-12 works

- **Build a delivery plan, not a catalogue of expensive toys.** Select one primary attack package, a credible second angle, cheap midfield delay, and mission-specific specialists/network support. O-12 has strong but differently priced options:
  - **Gamma/Omega:** BS14 heavy-infantry shooting, at considerable point and Order cost.
  - **Zeta:** premium HMC TAG.
  - **Wrecker:** rockets and armor pressure.
  - **MSV, hacking, midfield control, fast specialists:** supporting solutions, not substitutes for a coherent plan.
  
  Gamma/Omega/TAG-heavy builds can leave too few Orders, screens or mission scorers. The June 2026 all-faction discussion rated Vanilla O-12 around **B tier** despite praising its toolkit.
- **Exploit Vanilla's actual 2 Duo + 1 Haris allowance.** A Haris improves mobility and potentially shooting/ARO output for eligible FT-II members:
  - **Gamma / Omega:** premium lead attackers.
  - **Jackboot / Silverstar:** eligible partners or alternative attack/support roles, subject to exact profiles.
  - **RoadBot + Oko:** one example of efficient coordinated movement where legal.
  
  Additional legal FT-I/II Duos can save movement Orders. A Duo is not a Core; all membership follows the [canonical Fireteam chart](../../sectorials/o-12/o-12.md).
- **Make gunfights fair *before* taking them.** Match the tool to the opponent:
  - **Cyberghost HD+ Pitcher / White Noise:** visibility and hacking lanes; White Noise helps non-MSV Gamma/TAG guns against hostile visors but can impair friendly MSV attacks.
  - **Repeater web:** extend hacker reach instead of accepting an unfavorable gun duel.
  - **Varangian / Hippolyta Smoke:** change movement and normal-visibility engagement routes.
  - **MSV pieces:** counter conventional Smoke and some visual MODs.
  - **Camouflage pressure:** distract and tax defenders.
  
  Do not expect a single high-BS gun to beat every Mimetism/MSV/template defense.
- **Win tempo with cheap pieces.** Layer cheap Orders, detection, defense and mission reach before spending on another premium upgrade:
  - **Moonrakers:** pre-placed midfield delay.
  - **Kyttä / Kappa / FuzzBot:** inexpensive Order and utility bodies.
  - **Peeler:** selective ARO coverage.
  - **Oko:** mobile mission runner and Sensor/Repeater support.
  
  These pieces force inefficient enemy Orders before the main striker activates. Premium specialist upgrades must justify their marginal cost for the actual mission.
- **Command structure is a strategic choice.** Pick the command mechanism that supports your intended attack:
  - **Alpha:** Strategos/Counterintelligence or Chain of Command, depending on profile.
  - **Cho / Ment:** succession and command-continuity alternatives.
  - **Sonya Lieutenant:** +1 SWC, not an interchangeable +1 Lt Order.
  - **NCO recipients:** spend available Lieutenant Orders only when their actual rules/profile permit.
  - **Zeta Lieutenant:** requires CoC planning, Engineer access, and protection from Immobilized/Isolated/possession risks; high ARM is not enough.

### TAGs, Vehicle, and REMs — practical choices

These are the *Vanilla* availability and costs in the current reference; Torchlight/Starmada commentary below is used for tactical insight **only**, not their AVA or link permissions.

| Platform | Why take it | Why skip it / support required |
|---|---|---|
| **Zeta TAG** — 70 Lt / 73 NCO, 2 SWC | HMC solves exposed targets with burst; BS14, ARM8, STR3, Climbing Plus, ECM Guided (-8), ancillary **Zetbot** make an intimidating mobile anchor. The Lt option combines well with deliberate Chain-of-Command insurance; NCO suits a different command plan. | Consumes ~quarter of 300 points, a lot of Orders, and can be hacked/isolated or forced into bad lines. Large silhouette cannot go Prone. **Strong disagreement** below: not every experienced player rates its cost-to-damage highly. |
| **Wrecker TAG** — 51 HRL / 55 Spitfire / 57 HMG, 1.5 SWC | TacAware means attack mobility without exhausting the main pool; the 51-point **HRL** combines BS Attack (+1B), AP, SR-1 and Continuous Damage/template reach with high BTS9 and STR3; **Zetbot** can handle an objective while the TAG fights. Often an especially efficient armor hunter. | BS13, little innate anti-Mimetism help, and must avoid hackers/AP or concentrated return fire. It is an alternative to, not automatic upgrade over, Gamma/Zeta. Budget a Lambda/Monstrucker/Tinker repair plan. |
| **Firebat (VH; not TAG/REM)** — 35–36, 0 SWC | Aerial + 20-5 movement, Super-Jump, Mimetism (-6), **NCO** and varied AP/E/M/adhesive/rocket arms offer unusual 0-SWC attack angles and a way to spend command resources; **Tech-Recovery** is relevant to recovery plans. | **No Cover**, ARM4/STR2, and mediocre return on ~35 points if caught in a firing lane. Reposition to a favorable short engagement rather than assume a Mimetism modifier makes it durable. Available in Vanilla and Torchlight, but no Starmada-style link privileges may be assumed. |
| **RoadBot REM** — 20–31 | Fast transformation and Climbing Plus let a **20/22-point Paramedic** reach objectives and flank; Panzerfaust/riotstopper/Drop Bears and optional SideBot add trading. A 27/31-point Red Fury is a more dedicated attacker. | The high-mobility form is less combat-accurate; a fast exposed bot can die before it repays its points. Usually compare it with a Lawkeeper bike *for the job*, not as a universal replacement. |
| **Oko Copperbot REM** — 15, or **18 TacAware** | Perhaps the most repeatably praised utility purchase: Sensor discovers hidden/camo threats, Forward Observer scores/targets, Repeater projects hackers, Climbing Plus reaches awkward buttons. Pay 3 extra for a mission-critical private activation; mobility plus TA can be worth more than another gun. | BS11 and no real armor: send it after a lane is cleared; TA is not a reason to run into an active ARO. |
| **Peeler Copperbot REM** — **19 Thunderbolt (+1B), 0 SWC**; 24 HMG, 1 SWC | Total Reaction + 360° + Climbing Plus creates a low-maintenance ARO lane. Thunderbolt's SWC-free 19-point entry is much easier to fit than the HMG and can tax an enemy's first turn. | BS11, ARM0; quality attackers still beat static TR, and open deployment invites easy trading. Pair with wider defense, not as its sole pillar. |
| **Kyttä / FuzzBot / Dikpala REMs** | **Kyttä 7**: mobile Mimetism (-3) Flash Pulse and Repeater. **FuzzBot 8/14/17**: Baggage+Repeater, EVO, or cheap TR combi respectively. **Dikpala 13 Repeater/15 Paramedic**: mobile mimetic objective/network pieces, plus inexpensive alternate guns. | Kyttä's BS8 is a deterrent, not attack. EVO needs a specific program/use. FuzzBot TR-combi is controversial versus Peeler. Dikpala's expensive weapon options should solve a matchup, not duplicate your heavy gun. |
| **Moonraker / StormBot / Millicent REMs** | **Moonraker 14/15**: FD(+20), one-use camo, shotgun/adhesive and mines delay a push; **StormBot HRL 31** is a compact 2-STR rocket/Reptr threat; **Millicent** is a guided threat that can punish *established* Targeted states through a proper Spotlight network. | Moonraker will not win sustained gun duels; StormBot spends 1.5 SWC and competes with Wrecker/Gamma; Guided is not free value without targets, legal Orders and protection. **FTO** wording on StormBot is not itself Vanilla Fireteam permission. |

**TAG/REM package checks:**
- **Zeta or Wrecker:** coordinate an Engineer, protection and a safe route for the **ZetBot Ancillary**. Neither TAG's ZetBot generates an independent Regular Order.
- **Cyberghost:** invest in Repeaters and **FuzzBot Baggage** only when Pitcher reloads support a real hacking plan.
- **Oko Tactical Awareness + Moonraker / Peeler:** mission tempo paired with early defensive tax.
- **RoadBot / Oko / Dikpala:** standalone REM roles, distinct from a TAG's Ancillary; the **Firebat** is a Vehicle, not a REM.
