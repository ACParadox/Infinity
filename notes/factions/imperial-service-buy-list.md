Authority for every roster entry, AVA, profile and Fireteam: [pinned Imperial Service N5.3 Army reference](../../sectorials/yu-jing/imperial-service.md), Army `7.26246.158` from September 2026, repository verified 2026-10-15. ## Executive decision: do not pay CAD $150 for an Action Pack without checking 401 Games

- **Imperial Service Action Pack** (ten metal models, `281348-1140`): **CAD $79.95 at [401 Games](https://store.401games.ca/products/infinity-yu-jing-imperial-service-action-pack), Toronto/Vaughan, Ontario**, on the *opened 2026-10-30 live product page*, with **10+ available online** displayed. **[Tistaminis](https://tistaminis.com/products/infinity-yu-jing-imperial-service-action-pack), Hamilton, Ontario, lists CAD $150**, matching your supplied reference. That's a **CAD $70.05 listed-price difference** for the same ten-model SKU before tax/shipping. **Earlier search indexing of 401 gave $99.95, but the actual opened page gave $79.95; favor the opened page and recheck checkout.** This is a strong first purchase if starting ISS; none of its ISS unit names has an exact currently owned dedicated model.
- **New Imperial Service Expansion Alpha** (`281352-1155`): **CAD $39.95 at [401 Games](https://store.401games.ca/products/infinity-yu-jing-imperial-service-expansion-pack-alpha), Toronto/Vaughan, ON**, 10 available online on live page (older index $49.95). It includes **Wú Míng AP HMG + Wú Míng Boarding Shotgun + Zhànyīng Hacker + a separate TinBot**. These are **three troop bodies plus one ancillary**, not four Wú Míng. Good complement to starter because it gives a practical Wú Míng fireteam core and hacking support.
- **Expansion Delta** (`281362-1206`): **CAD $60 at [Tistaminis](https://tistaminis.com/products/infinity-yu-jing-imperial-service-expansion-pack-delta), Hamilton, ON**, Tistaminis catalogue listed in stock. **Two Bào + one Kǎnrèn**: very specific visibility / anti-camo and midfield roles; recommended if deliberately building Hsien+Bào Zhian or Kǎnrèn pressure.
- **Expansion Gamma** (`281358-1178`): **CAD $49.95 at [401 Games](https://store.401games.ca/products/infinity-yu-jing-imperial-service-expansion-pack-gamma), Toronto/Vaughan, ON**, but **SOLD OUT** on opened product page (older index $54.95); **CAD $60 at [Tistaminis](https://tistaminis.com/collections/yu-jing), Hamilton, ON**, catalogue low stock. **Qiánfēng Hacker Parachutist + two Jìnwèi HI**. Worth buying for your favorite control/specialist architecture, not universally necessary.
- **Big missing decision for ISS:** **Kuang Shi + Celestial Guard Monitor/Smoke** and **Hsien+Bào**, **Wú Míng+Zhànyīng**, or **Sù-Jiàn / Lóngwáng** are **distinct alternative engines**. Do not buy all heroes, TAGs and three boxes simply to fill every AVA. **Adil** is a generally better everyday Crane-type first dedicated character than buying *another* generic Crane; the Action Pack already includes one Crane.
- **Strongest owned, exact, legal model reuse:** the **two total Yáoxiè Rui Shi / Lù Duān chassis** recorded in [Shindenbutai](../../inventory/shindenbutai.md) and **one Warcor** from [Kestrel](../../inventory/kestrel.md). **Standard Ninja owned zero**, though **one Kurayami Ninja** in Shindenbutai is a suitable declared proxy; keep Koga Ninja's separate class/role straight. All other suggested "OSS" reuse claims below are checked against real recorded figures rather than assumed ownership.

## Rare / weak / low-value purchases (whole units vs profiles)

1. **Extra generic Crane Rank**, especially just for CC/specialist combat: source-backed value criticism compared with **Adil**. **Action Pack includes one Crane already**; buy a separate old USD $7.90 one only for specific AP Spitfire/Hacker/LT or second Crane team role. **This is not “Crane is bad.”**
2. **Plain Celestial Guard Combi** as expensive pure filler: source-backed; preferentially use **WIP14 Hacker/Smoke GL/Monitor** options. Buying the new AP yields **three bodies** to represent useful variants even if standard sculpt has Combi.
3. **Second Bayara**: disputed/situational specialist-defense unit and S4 unusual creature; **one in AP** already, try that before dedicated second.
4. **Dragon Lord as dedicated Monofilament assassin**: poor use of 5pt irregular unit; actual strength is cheap mission/Flash Pulse/Nanopulser. **Preorder-only LE does not justify collector pricing to field one**. S2 LI proxy recommended and legal table usage depends on event.
5. **Four or eight Kuang Shi on autopilot**: not a universal minimum. Older Monitor+Smoke+cheap orders builds justify some; troop slots/your actual list decide quantity. **ISS AVA8 is maximum, not shopping target**. User price USD $19 for 4 conditional.
6. **Generic second/third Bào or Wu Míng gun** before a real combined list: action pack + Delta/Alpha already create a strong core; don't blindly fill AVA5 of either. Same with Hsien extra gun.
7. **Deva + Dikpala ALEPH Support**: useful if building a dedicated WIP15 MSV/hacking/Dikpala link, but **neither exact troop type owned**; substantial package purchase. No fictitious free Dakini from OSS.
8. **Optional high-cost architectures**: **Sun Tze Strategos, Sù-Jiàn, Lóngwáng S6 TAG, Fēiquán S7 VH** compete for budget and game points. Buy one center by role, not all. Fēiquán **is VH**, no heavy-infantry or S6 TAG stand-in.
9. **Niche characters**: Miranda, Táowù, Sforza, second Qíshì, extra Jīnyào are **proxy-first if not used in an actual roster**, not proof they are statistically bad. Sforza **must have the right Zhian FTO profile** for intended link.
10. **Do not buy non-ISS named units** solely from an “OSS/Yu Jing” price lead: **Dakini**, **Zhanshi**, **Hac Tao**, **Tiger Soldiers**, **Yuan Yuan**, **Haetae**, **Jujak** are **NOT on this pinned ISS roster** even though relevant in Vanilla/White Banner/IA. A different sectorial's buy list is not an ISS inventory.
