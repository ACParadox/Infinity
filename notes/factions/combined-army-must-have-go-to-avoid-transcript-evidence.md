# Combined Army — must-have, go-to and avoid audit (N5.3, 2026-10-23)

**Scope:** Vanilla Combined Army, Morat Aggression Forces (MAF), Shasvastii Expeditionary Force (SEF), Onyx Contact Force (OCF), Next Wave. The user-defined categories are deliberately demanding: **must-have** = an almost-every-list personal default reported by a speaker or genuinely indispensable faction engine; **go-to** = broadly good when assigned a sensible role; **never take / avoid** = profile-level displacement or explicit speaker rejection, **not** proof of mathematical domination. An empty hard-veto category is more accurate than inventing one.

**Factual authority:** the five [current repository sectorial reference files](../../sectorials/combined-army/) (all `status: current`, N5.3, Army `7.26246.158`, changed 2026-09-03), then [rules](../../rules/README.md). These establish **legality, costs and loadouts**, while videos/podcasts establish **opinions and observed results**. Earlier N4/2025 advice cannot override the current roster. [Official N5.3/ITS18 changes](https://infinityuniverse.com/en/news/infinity-rules-update-5-3) include updated Team-Ops/Spec-Ops and Sāchā availability. Normal-list recommendations do **not** silently assume optional Team-Ops.

**Evidence key:** **Direct** = attributable preference or rejection from a full transcript checked for this audit; **Corroborated** = source discussion + existing reviewed guide; **Contested** = sourced counter-opinion exists; **Edition-limited** = pre-N5.3 material must be interpreted using current reference. “Near-must” is still **not a mandatory unit**.

## 1. Vanilla Combined Army

**Most direct sources:** [Robert Shepherd, *N5 Combined Army faction focus*, April 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qoyylw0p0JM) (full YouTube MCP transcript rechecked); [Tactical Awareness, S3E15 Vanilla/CA Prime](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pTDaJJy86Sk), historical early-N5; [existing Vanilla research and opposing podcast summaries](./vanilla-combined-army.md). [Current Vanilla profiles](../../sectorials/combined-army/vanilla-combined-army.md).

**Near-must / actual speaker defaults**
- **Dāturazi 14/0 with Chain Rifle, Grenades, Smoke Grenades** — **Direct:** Shepherd says he always takes this particular Dāturazi option. Regular **Smoke + lethal close-range trade** gives a cheap enabling piece for Vector MSV2/Charontid MSV3; one of Vanilla's best order-to-utility buys. **Exception:** a list that attacks through White Noise, Albedo or a dedicated non-Smoke plan; don't call the trooper compulsory.
- **Bit & KISS 22/.5 (KISS with Pitcher, Bit KHD/Oblivion)** — **Direct:** Shepherd says always choose the profile with **KISS Pitcher**; the two projection points and Killer Hacking Device justify its role. **Exception:** he himself sometimes cuts Bit when Umbra Legate HD+ supplies the network. **Crucial current-profile distinction:** the **25/.5 alternative includes KISS (Repeater), not KISS (Pitcher)**; the distinction is about teamwide projection, not saying all other hacking is impossible.
- **Libertos 8/1 Minelayer** — **Direct:** Shepherd says he *almost* always includes one: cheap disposable Camouflage/Minelayer speed bump protecting critical Orders and costly threats. **Exception:** SWC-pressure lists, or lists where its Irregular Order causes an unacceptable active-order deficit.

**Go-to, not compulsory**
- **Vector plasma sniper + Unidrons / Nexus short team** — MSV2 + Smoke and a compact Fireteam impose bad opposing shooting odds while buying useful link bodies; Robert's notable 2026 middleweight archetype. Its main risk is exposed one-wound/Exrah fragility, Smoke denial and Fireteam opportunity cost.
- **Umbra Legate FTO HD+38/.5, Tactical Awareness** — Forward projected hacking/White Noise/K1, actual extra activation and specialist use; competing substitute for Bit, not a reason to stack every pitcher user.
- **Sheskiin 48–50** — fast gun-to-CC Protheion snowball; more reliable open fighting than many dedicated close-combat heroes. **Nourkias** offers more hacker/anti-TAG breadth but needs careful delivery; both are credible.
- **Charontid LT or Anathematic LT** — choose **Charontid MSV3 gun OR different HD+/Sepsitor option**, never pretend one loadout grants all capabilities. Anathematic is the premium defensive hacker with Camo. Not both a mandatory centerpiece and affordable filler.
- **Nexus LT/CoC, Ikadron, Noctifer ML/Spitfire, Speculo, Rasyat and Overdron** — reliable role-dependent command/backbone, hidden threat, Impersonation, flank or hard-gun solutions; choose according to mission and opponent rather than automatic stacking.

**Avoid / never take (profile-qualified)**
- **Umbra Legate LT Spitfire 37/1.5** — **Direct strong avoid, Shepherd:** explicitly says not to take this LT because the cost/SWC crosses into the territory of significantly more compelling command/attack choices. **Not** a veto on the excellent non-LT Legate HD+.
- **Non-LT Charontid gun variants** — **Direct speaker preference:** Shepherd says he would *probably never* take one because near-identical LT options cost the same and are a stronger command investment. **Exception:** another deliberate LT plan; no universal ban.
- **Bit & KISS 25/.5 when bought solely to project KISS Pitchers** — **Direct:** Shepherd calls the *wrong KISS version* a bad value for that mission. It is a distinct HD/Trinity + KISS Repeater profile; retain it only when those **specific** benefits are intended.
- **Not “never take”: Avatar or Sphinx.** Shepherd calls Avatar arguably among the game's strongest models but says he almost never takes it **because of list-wide opportunity cost**, not because it is weak.

## 2. Morat Aggression Forces

**Sources:** [Robert Shepherd, *N5 Morat focus*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QwcqdXMtDXI) (full transcript rechecked); [Tactical Awareness S3E13, Morats](https://open.spotify.com/episode/7HZuzXo3FDUr6Eqp08TzjR) (full podcast transcript rechecked); [existing Morat tactical review](./morat-aggression-force.md); [N5.3 roster](../../sectorials/combined-army/morat-aggression-forces.md).

**Near-must / central packages**
- **Reliable Smoke access, commonly Dāturazi** — cheap Regular smoke, CC/template trade, sets up **Yaogat MSV2** or safer transit. **Not uniquely mandatory:** Oznat smoke-launcher + legal Hungries infrastructure provides a different form of enabler; spending on both in every list is unnecessary.
- **Sogarat LT + optional NCO Sogarat Duo (one *command-architecture* default)** — **Direct:** Shepherd says this has substantially displaced his historic near-auto **Kornak** LT: durable Warhorse command, excellent AP/Feuerbach or AP HMG pressure, NCO spending otherwise restricted LT Orders. **Not** in lists already investing in Raicho/Bultrak, or needing cheaper Kornak Strategos instead.

**Go-to**
- **Yaogat 29/1.5 Spitfire or 32/1.5 MULTI sniper** — MSV2 through plentiful Smoke improves FtF odds versus costlier Suryat gunfighting; active push versus long-lane ARO.
- **Dartok FTO 18/.5** — linkable Pitcher hacker/Trinity, efficient local repeater/control utility; **not** an Anathematic-grade solo cyber bully.
- **Oznat FTO + Gakis/Pretas**, within the **actual pinned Fireteam chart** — converts applicable Hungries' order infrastructure through Fireteam Master; Smoke launcher and inexpensive bodies. Mixed Hungry species are **not forbidden** by the current chart.
- **Rindak Paramedic; Rasyat; Rodok Thunderbolt + Hacker + inexpensive Krakot; Zerat FO; Ikadron; Kaitok** — differentiated recovery/mobility/flank, multi-role assault, midfield scoring, cheap Baggage and hard short-range trade options. Take a subset to cover gaps rather than all of them.
- **Kornak LT** — still a sound Strategos/command option; Shepherd's Sogarat preference **does not** make Kornak obsolete.

**Avoid / source-qualified**
- **Yaogat Combi/Panzerfaust and Boarding Shotgun/Panzerfaust, as default shooters** — **Direct:** Shepherd explicitly says he does **not** take them; he instead uses Spitfire or MULTI sniper to maximize the reason Yaogats are picked: MSV2 gunfights. Specific cheap escort/short-range scenarios may still justify them.
- **Double Krakot + single Rodok or double Krakot-only Fireteam by default** — **Direct:** Shepherd discusses these and says he does *not* recommend them; the preferred mixed utility is **two Rodoks + one Krakot**, where the Rodoks provide the relevant Fireteam benefits.
- **A Suryat selected solely as your best odds-of-winning gunfight** — source comparison favors Yaogat+Smoke. **Not** a whole-unit veto: Suryat still has heavy resilience, TinBots, Tactical Awareness and appropriate team infrastructure.
- **Not “never take”: Raicho, Bultrak, Kornak.** Sogarat pressures their **opportunity cost**, not their legality or their unique jobs.

## 3. Shasvastii Expeditionary Force

**Sources:** [Robert Shepherd, *SEF faction focus*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mtqHMuHmjjo) (full transcript rechecked; older analysis); [Tactical Awareness S3E12 Shasvastii](https://open.spotify.com/episode/0oGd3tdSkCtTp2Yi99sRc5) (full podcast transcript rechecked); [MetaChemistry E139 — 2026 Battle by the Beach winner Brian/Nazeen](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6FErkreAsQoUKosH4WkdPW) (full podcast transcript rechecked; actual 2026 tournament evidence); [existing SEF guide](./shasvastii-expeditionary-force.md); [N5.3 roster](../../sectorials/combined-army/shasvastii.md).

**No defensible universal individual “must-have.”** The **2026 winner** describes at least two genuinely different solutions: a **face-down** build (2 Speculo, 3 Caliban, Shrouded, order bots, solo late-game Jayth) and a **face-up** mission/gun build (Sheskiin + Nox/Tensho specialists, Gwailo + Victor). Calling Speculo, Sheskiin, Gwailo or Noctifer mandatory across **both** would erase this direct evidence.

**Go-to**
- **Speculo Killer** — Impersonation/Mines makes opponent defend deployment and priority pieces; doubles exaggerate the threat, but cost Orders/specialists.
- **Caliban specialist / Engineer / MSV Spitfire and Shrouded FO/Minelayer/Dazer** — advanced Camo, objective scoring, Protheion or persistent area denial; Caliban stronger attacker, Shrouded cheaper workhorse.
- **Noctifer ML or Spitfire** — Hidden Deployment threatens ARO **or** opens a hidden attack route. Missile is not automatic; threatened shot can be prevented, while Spitfire offers a more proactive gun.
- **Sheskiin gunfighter** — rapid all-round assault, Protheion durability if kills are accessible; strong *when the mission wants face-up tempo*, not universal.
- **Mentor LT Counterintelligence/Decoy; Taigha; Ikadron / R-Drone / Q-Drone** — command shelter, rapid cheap trade and cheap defence. Irregular Taigha cannot simply replace Regular pooled activations.
- **Gwailo HRL MSV2 (39/1.5) + Victor Messer in eligible Shock arrangement** — late-2026 precedent for lane denial and objective-focused teamwork. **Important:** the **44pt Gwailo HMG does not have MSV2** in the current roster.
- **Jayth with disco balls, Dukash, Malignos** — mission-specific visibility bypass, flank attack and premium hidden specialist.

**Avoid / profile-qualified**
- **Cadmus KHD 22/0 instead of Boarding Shotgun 19/0 for a plain backline kill-drop** — **Direct:** Shepherd says not to buy the KHD for that specific job, which the cheaper BSG is designed for. **Not an absolute:** the extra 3 points add a Specialist/KHD for missions that need one.
- **Historical Cadmus Red Fury rejection is *not actionable***: the speaker also says not to buy the Red Fury, but **that profile is not present in the pinned 2026 roster**. Likewise an old, expensive hackable/armoured Sheskiin discussed on video is not a current listed loadout; don't import old “never take” claims.
- **Not “never take”: Gwailo, Jayth, Sphinx, Noctifer ML.** Shepherd was skeptical of some costs; Brian's 2026 event use strongly contradicts treating Gwailo/Jayth as traps. Sphinx 93pt/2SWC is a matchup and specialist/opportunity-cost question, not a proven bad unit.

## 4. Onyx Contact Force

**Sources:** [Irregular Order #56, *Onyx Contact Force Review*, 17 Oct 2026](https://open.spotify.com/episode/7b2QJ494pgPp6NY6WQ9QWF) (**full podcast transcript rechecked**; particularly ~85–120min Nexus/Unidrons, ~125–160min Morat/Exrah, ~165–210min TAGs and hidden pieces); [Robert Shepherd, *Onyx faction focus*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U238na0BXfs) (full YouTube transcript rechecked; older); [Tactical Awareness S3E14 Onyx](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u_e7wxbn1Zk); [existing Onyx guide](./onyx-contact-force.md); [N5.3 roster](../../sectorials/combined-army/onyx.md).

**No uncontested must-have.** In the **Oct 2026 Irregular Order review (~105–110min)** one host takes substantial **Unidron** infrastructure; the other openly says he generally *does not*. That means “must take Unidron Core” is a **playstyle judgment**, not faction consensus.

**Go-to**
- **Nexus LT/CoC/Engineer/Hacker + Unidron Plasma/K1/FO/Missile** — inexpensive resilient link infrastructure, real specialists, gun and Engineer recovery; cheap linked Unidrons repeatedly repairing via Remote Presence can consume enemy turns. **Contested** on quantity and emphasis.
- **Bit & KISS + other legal Repeaters** — threatens enemy hackers before TAG movement; compatible with cheap engineer and Umbra support. Distinguish its current Onyx options rather than importing Vanilla loadout wording.
- **Overdron (HRL 51 / Plasma Sniper 59 / HRMC 62, each 1.5 SWC)** — Albedo (-6) ranged MSV-counter gun, Remote Presence repair; a realistic turn-one ARO remover. HRL is the cheaper legitimate alternative, not simply “HRMC versus Plasma.”
- **Xeodron + Nourkias legal Duo/Haris contexts** — mobile Super-Jump gun + close-range/anti-armour combat or hacking; one of the sectorial's signature non-standstill attack packages.
- **Umbra Legate / Samaritan, Noctifer, Q-Drone, Ko Dali/Fraacta** — choose second-wave mobility, Hidden ARO, repeater/total-reaction defence, or off-axis strikes based on opponent and table. **Samaritan** can be outshone by Nourkias *on general utility*, but is not literally useless.

**Avoid / speaker-personal rather than categorical**
- **Void Operator as generic FTO filler instead of cheaper Base Operator or an actual premium Vector gun** — **Direct:** in *Irregular Order* (~146–148min), a host says he has **never picked Void over Base**; extra utility often fails to justify the premium in his lists. Another host points out its rocket/KHD utility. **Contested**, not a strict never.
- **Generic Suryat or Kurgat/Morat gun package without a special role** — 2026 hosts (~128min, ~157min) consider some imported Morats less efficient **in the Onyx ecosystem** than Nexus/Unidron/TAG/Ironwork choices. **Rodok**, especially a mobile Engineer, remains a defensible exception.
- **Kerr-Nau as an automatic inclusion** — 2026 reviewers compare its price/stat growth against newer Nexus; older Shepherd notes Kerr-Nau's KHD/plasma/Fireteam role. **No defensible unit ban.**
- **Not “never take”: Sphinx/Caskuda, Xeodron, ordinary Samaritan or Overdron.** All have real role/tradeoff discussions and speakers disagree over whether their specialist cost is warranted.

## 5. Next Wave

**Sources:** [Robert Shepherd, *N5 Next Wave faction focus*, Dec 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=52aFgWpv8es) (**full transcript rechecked**); [Tactical Awareness, E36 Next Wave](https://open.spotify.com/episode/1c9pgE6IkthlQ2mzQpH4Qe) (full transcript rechecked); [Loss of Lieutenant E172, *List Building 101: Next Wave*, Aug 2026](https://open.spotify.com/episode/5cVwqFnBxM9xYIskCHHuDt) (full transcript rechecked); [MetaChemistry E148 opposing Next Wave](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3X6yHHjjJNYmZVO8qltsUZ) and [existing guide](./next-wave.md); [N5.3 roster](../../sectorials/combined-army/next-wave.md).

**Near-must / genuine speaker defaults**
- **Pandora 24–29/.5** — **Direct strongest:** Shepherd expressly says he puts her in **every Next Wave list**, praises durable Zero Pain defensive hacking, specialist capability, and Fireteam Master. In an army with thin affordable hacking, multiple roles in one piece matter more than the raw weapon. **Not unconditional:** list/mission exceptions and the desired MediKit/CoC/Pitcher are **different actual loadouts**.
- **Gearhead Cyberplug 13/0 + Sartroid Puzzler 12–13/0 (total package ≥25)** — Shepherd calls the Puzzler a top disruption piece; **Loss of Lieutenant E172 (~17min)** independently argues it may be **Next Wave's best unit**. Hidden/forward E/Marat, PARA/Repeater denial stops TAGs or an aggressive Fireteam before it reaches scoring. **Not two Regular Orders:** Cyberplug controller, connected/autonomous modes and N5.3 Peripheral rules are real costs. It's near-core in disruption lists, not necessarily every rush/TAG list.

**Go-to**
- **Harbinger LT 13 + decoy / FO 13 / hacker/KHD** — indispensable *type of job* (affordable Regular orders, secure command, specialist glue); do not imply a fixed Harbinger loadout mandatory. Current roster allows other plans.
- **Tekdraken Blackheart, and Steeljaw when protecting a hero** — broad all-range threat and mine/E/Marat coverage; Fireteam Master converts **only qualifying** linked Irregular Orders. Don't count peripherals/Tekdrakens as free generic Regular Orders.
- **Stinger MSV2 or HMG based on lanes; Teucer MSV2; Ironside Albedo** — shooting modifiers and anti-MSV/smoke answers; pick complementary rangebands and avoid paying for three redundant premium guns.
- **Wraith-1 FTO 25/.5 + Harbinger Research / support link** — cheap (for this faction) linkable MSV/Mimetism midrange gun; Shepherd calls it standout for freeing 300-point list budget.
- **Sāchā, Contrabandolero, Cliff-Jumper, Skyhound VH, Achilles, Juggernaut TAG** — late scoring, objective deployment, AD flank, fast anti-ARO or centerpiece, depending on mission. **Sāchā AVA changed in N5.3**; no assumption that October 2025 cap still holds.
- **Drakios** — **contested go-to only when needing fast *nonhackable* specialist/Albedo and a particular Fireteam Master.** Shepherd openly dislikes him; **Loss of Lieutenant E172 (~23min)** expressly counters that Drakios has won useful roles and isn't what's wrong with the sectorial. Don't mislabel as universal veto.

**Avoid / explicit transcript negatives, current-profile checked**
- **Harbinger HMG 20/1 and Missile Launcher 18/1.5** — **Direct strongest:** Shepherd says **never** buy either; plain BS11 line-gunner and SWC cost compete badly with Stinger/Blackheart/Wraith/Teucer shooting while Harbinger LT/FO/Hacker provide more useful jobs. Profile-level advice, not condemnation of Harbingers.
- **Wraith-1 27/.5 Hidden Deployment MULTI Marksman** — **Direct:** Shepherd says **do not take** it; the **28/1.5 Hidden MULTI Sniper** better uses hidden surprise/range, while 25/.5 **FTO MMR** buys link value. **Exception:** strict SWC or a specific hidden-midrange corridor.
- **Gearhead plain Engineer 17/0 as a blind pick** — **Direct:** Shepherd says he never takes this profile; **13/0 Cyberplug** buys Puzzler access, while **18/0 Engineer FTO** can Wildcard and repair with a team. Choose Engineer only when its location/team eligibility beats the alternatives.
- **Next Wave Bit & KISS 25/.5 as a network-shooting cornerstone** — **Direct:** Shepherd calls its KISS Repeater version very weak versus the KISS Pitcher configuration found in other armies. **Current roster has ONLY the 25/.5 version in Next Wave**: this is an avoid *for long-range Pitcher work*, not a nonexistent alternate 22pt profile you can select here. Useful local hacking is still possible.
- **Not “never take”: Drakios** (direct conflicting Aug 2026 podcast), **Achilles** (high investment but legitimate), **Juggernaut** (TAG skew), or **Sāchā** (source AVA has changed).

## Confidence rules and conclusion

1. **Strict “never take” labels attach to exact current loadouts**, not to entire units. Use *speaker strongly avoids* rather than declaring a mathematical theorem from one opinion.
2. **Every faction has successful alternative list architectures.** At most a handful of troop choices are speaker-verified near-defaults; in Onyx and Shasvastii, **no individual unit has sufficiently strong all-build consensus**.
3. **Preserve opposing speakers.** Especially: Morat **Sogarat vs Kornak**, Shasvastii **Gwailo and Jayth skepticism vs 2026 winner**, Onyx **Unidron density**, Next Wave **Drakios**, Vanilla **Avatar effectiveness vs list cost**.
4. The roster is **N5.3, September 2026**, but most opinions are earlier; they are tactical heuristics, **not independently updated tournament win-rate data**. The Oct 2026 Onyx review and 2026 SEF tournament testimony receive more weight than older recordings where they conflict.

## Whole-unit purchasing audit — can I skip the miniature? (2026-10-30)

**Whole-unit** here means *every currently legal profile of that named troop in the specified army*, not one disliked loadout. **Defer** is a collection-building recommendation, not a competitive ban; evaluate the same miniature again when playing a different sectorial. **Hard whole-unit avoids: none established by these recordings.**

| Army | Entire model/unit to defer buying? | Why; what would change the answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Vanilla Combined** | **No source-supported whole-unit skip** | The Umbra Legate **LT** and non-LT Charontid criticisms leave useful alternative profiles; Bit & KISS's wrong network loadout does not disqualify its Pitcher configuration. Avatar is *strong but optional*, not bad. |
| **Morat** | **No** | Cheap Yaogat Panzerfaust profiles are disliked, but the MSV2 Spitfire/sniper are positively recommended. Suryat, Kornak and TAGs still have distinct uses. |
| **Shasvastii** | **No** | Cadmus KHD criticism applies to a backline-kill role; the BSG and KHD specialist jobs differ. 2026 results directly contradict blanket Gwailo/Jayth skips. |
| **Onyx** | **Defer Void Operator unless deliberately using its rocket/KHD utility — low confidence** | An [Irregular Order #56](https://open.spotify.com/episode/7b2QJ494pgPp6NY6WQ9QWF) host never preferred it over Base Operator; another defends its unique tools. A *personal* buying deferral, **not** all-profile dominance. |
| **Next Wave** | **No** | Harbinger heavy guns, Wraith hidden MMR, plain Gearhead Engineer and Bit & KISS as a *Pitcher substitute* are the poor choices. Other profiles/uses remain legitimate, so do **not** skip those entire model families. |

**Purchase rule:** consider skipping a full unit only when its *best* viable profile also loses its expected role in your chosen army. A bad SWC weapon or wrong Fireteam configuration does **not** justify avoiding an entire kit. See the [cross-faction buying shortlist](model-purchase-avoid-summary-2026.md).

## Worst-value, circumstantial and proxy-first miniature purchases by army (2026-10-30)

**Question:** For **Vanilla Combined Army, Morat Aggression Forces, Shasvastii Expeditionary Force, Onyx Contact Force and Next Wave**, which are the **weakest everyday picks**, most specialized rarely *needed* purchases, or **good units better represented by a proxy before buying an extra miniature**? An **AVA maximum is not a shopping target**. “Rare/circumstantial” below is a *qualitative estimate of how often a particular job arises in the reviewed list plans*, **not a measured tournament pick rate**. The current [N5.3 roster files](../../sectorials/combined-army/) (Army data 3 September 2026) take priority over earlier recordings for exact variants, base/silhouette, unit access and Fireteams.

**Decision labels:** **Defer/skip as a default dedicated miniature** = an explicit reviewer's negative judgment on that *usual role*, with exceptions explained. **Proxy first** = a specialized yet legitimate **inferred purchase priority**, *not* an invented direct quotation. **Avoid profile, NOT unit** = the named loadout/Fireteam plan is poor but other choices are strong. **Proxy the extra** = first copy or alternative sculpt may be a normal good purchase, but redundant physical duplicates need demonstrated simultaneous use. **Contested** = dated contrary opinions, successful tournament use or a distinct current-profile benefit make a universal “worst” label dishonest. **No verified complete-unit all-profile hard ban exists** in the present evidence.
