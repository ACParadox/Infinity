## Notable duplicate buys / old models / editions

- **Headquarters:** three $7.50 singles (**Hawkins**, **Curator**, **Military Order Grand Master**) total **$22.50**; the exact trio in **PanOceania Headquarters Pack** is **$21.45**, saving **$1.05**, before shipping.
- **Dire Foes vs singles:** **Konstantinos** standalone $7.00 repeats the character/loadout named in **Troubled Theft** $19.80; **Uma Sørensen FTO** standalone $6.40 also appears in **Failsafe** $17.60. **Richard Quinn** standalone $7.30 may overlap **Datacash** $19.80; the supplied Datacash excerpt does not enumerate its box.
- **Dronbots and Armbots:** **Dronbot Remotes Pack** $13.40 advertises two robots and **DRONBOT REMOTES (REM)** $18.50 explicitly names Sierra and Clipper; compare sculpt and parts before buying both. Generic **Armbots** $15.20 and explicit **Bulleteer 2-pack** $20.60 may also overlap. Support-bot/deployable figures should not be counted as independent orders.
- **TAG variants:** Two **Cutter** entries (**$29.70** and **$25.20**), **Tikbalang** plastic $16.40 vs **Toni Macayana** metal $18.80 vs **Stingray Uhlan/Tikbalang** $33.40, and older **Squalo** $29.70 vs **Reinf. Squalos Mk-II** $20.40 all require visual/version comparison. Do not silently equate different named releases or assume new vs old without SKU evidence.
- **Starter splits:** **PanO-only Beyond Kaldstrom** $16.20 and multi-faction **Beyond Kaldstrom Expansion** $32.40 are potential overlapping routes; likewise **PanO Operation Kaldstrøm** $32.40 is a **side/set description**, not automatically an entire two-player box. **PanOceania Beyond Icestorm** $15.80 gives a three-model expansion and **PanOceania Starter Pack** $35.00 is an older six-model Icestorm-era starter.
- **Other repeats:** **Knight Hospitaller HMG** $10.60 is already among the four loadouts in **Knights Hospitaller** $23.10. **Jeanne d’Arc Mobility Armor Exclusive** $11.50 and regular **Jeanne d’Arc MULTI Rifle** $11.00 are alternate character sculpts, not proof of two different unique characters. **Fusiliers** $29.80, exclusive Icestorm Fusiliers $20.30 (out of stock), and Fusiliers within Kestrel/Kaldstrøm starters may cover overlapping troop identities.
- **Explicit collector/limited labels:** Maximus Exclusive, Montesa Exclusive, Padre-Inquisidor Mendoza Exclusive, Jeanne d’Arc Exclusive, Nisse/Akali/ORC/Father-Knight Icestorm exclusives, Neoterra Bolt Cosplay Edition, and Penthesilea Special Edition. **“Exclusive” is an edition property, not current stock or roster legality.** Older **Father-Knight** releases retain their historical names without presuming current N5 replacements.
