## Skips and cross-sectorial model reuse

**Skip by profile, not whole unit**: Ghulam HMG/ML default link weapons are explicitly criticized, while Ghulam NCO smoke/doctor are excellent. An extra Ayyar or Sunduqbut is proxy-first unless running double-marker saturation. Avoid Rafiq FTO or Claire FTO purchases for **imaginary** Hassassin Fireteam slots: the canonical chart lacks both rows. Your existing McMurrough and Warcor are already suitable, no second copies.

**Proxy first** for low-play-frequency characters, extra expensive TAGs, optional aircraft and second attack pieces; do not buy all AVA models to complete the collection. Special/limited editions are collector options rather than required army rules. Cross-reference [Vanilla Haqq](vanilla-haqqislam-buy-list.md), [Hassassin Bahram](hassassin-bahram-buy-list.md), [Qapu Khalqi](qapu-khalqi-buy-list.md), [Ramah Taskforce](ramah-taskforce-buy-list.md), and [Ariadna buy lists](vanilla-ariadna-buy-list.md) before ordering shared minis. **No owned-inventory file was edited.**
