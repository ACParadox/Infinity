# Next Wave — N5.3 faction, Vehicle, TAG and REM guide

_Last researched: 2026-10-22. Latest main-branch source of truth for this guide is Infinity Army/N5.3, not older list examples. Research: full YouTube MCP transcripts of Robert Shepherd (Dec 2025), Tactical Awareness Next Wave (Oct 2025), Infinity the Dante first impressions; full podcast MCP transcripts of WIP12 E194/E195 (Oct–Nov 2025), MetaChemistry E130 tournament winner (Dec 2025), MetaChemistry E148 opposing Next Wave (Jul 2026), and Loss of Lieutenant E172 listbuilding (Aug 2026). **Rules and roster authority:** [Next Wave N5.3 roster, profile choices and Fireteam chart](../../sectorials/combined-army/next-wave.md) (Army **7.26246.158**, September 2026), plus [rules index](../../rules/README.md). Shared remotes and TAGs must independently appear on the Next Wave roster.

**Critical profile distinction:** **Patroclus has Smoke Grenades only on the unlinked Holoprojector Heavy Rocket Launcher profile (35/1).** His **FTO profiles, which can join Team Achilles/Shadow teams and provide Fireteam Master or CoC/MediKit, do not list Smoke Grenades in the pinned roster**. A linked Patroclus is **not** a reliable Smoke source; if Smoke is essential, plan a separately activated legal Smoke model and its Orders, or build around another visibility answer. **Skyhound is VH** and **Juggernaut TAG**, not interchangeable; see [Vehicle guide](../list-building/vehicles-vh.md) and [TAG guide](../list-building/tags.md).

## The one-paragraph answer

**Next Wave is a high-quality, low-slack Combined Army force built around small hero-led Fireteams, durable Tekdrakens, versatile guns and unusual anti-approach peripherals.** It has many outstanding 20–70-point profiles **but cannot afford all of them**: few disposable Regular Orders, competing expensive leaders, restricted Fireteam Master combinations, and limited cheap mission/hacking coverage force compromises. Build **one credible hammer, two complementary support/attack teams, a real scoring solution, and cheap Orders**. Winning is less about the biggest dinosaur and more about **making each model solve a different problem**.

**What makes Next Wave different from Vanilla Combined Army:** hero-specific link options, Fireteam Master converting applicable Tekdraken Irregular Orders within the linked team, Gearhead–Sartroid Cyberplug architecture, distinctive **Skyhound Vehicle** and **Juggernaut TAG**, and a mixed MSV/Albedo/BS(-3)/Mimetism gun roster. Shared Combined Army remotes do **not** confer Vanilla Fireteams or premium TAG availability.

### Listbuilding checklist, in order

1. **Orders/LT:** first budget affordable Harbingers and Ikadrons, secure the LT (Harbinger LT plus decoy is common) and count actual *assignable* Orders rather than just models and Tactical Awareness.
2. **One core threat:** a Patroclus/Pandora + Blackheart/Stinger package, **Achilles**, **Ironside**, or **Juggernaut**. The first is easiest to support; the larger packages demand sacrificed specialists/order count.
3. **Two different attack lanes:** long-range MSV/Albedo gun plus shorter-range Smoke, Tekdraken, Skyhound, hidden Puzzler or Cliff-Jumper flank. Do not buy three guns with identical rangebands and no solution to MSV/hacking.
4. **Mission:** Harbinger FO/hacker, Pandora, Sāchā, Contrabandolero, Wraith-1, Nexus, Drakios/Patroclus specialist or Staldron as available and legal.
5. **Defense:** Puzzler(s)/Cyberplug, Tekdraken mines, Ikadron corner coverage and optionally Q-Drone/M-Drone Sensor; if taking a big TAG or Skyhound, protect it against hacking and a direct first-turn shot.
6. **Check format:** Army AVA, Fireteam purity/Master, Cyberplug control state, 15-trooper counting, current ITS mission and **N5.3 change to Sāchā AVA**.

### Opposing views to keep in mind

- **Juggernaut: excellent TAG, poor fit?** Robert Shepherd's Dec 2025 view: genuinely excellent Guard, Climbing Plus, NCO/MULTI HMG or close-range Plasma chassis **but ~80 points break the economy**; he frequently prefers a 30-ish-point Skyhound. MetaChemistry's Jul 2026 opposing-faction view: an enemy still has to *plan around* Juggernaut and a Double-Juggernaut threat, though it is seen less than at release. **Use it when Guard + durable sustained attack actually matter; not just because the profile is efficient in isolation.**
- **Skyhound: auto-include or risky?** Aug 2026 Loss of Lieutenant strongly praises **Plasma + Mine Dispenser** to project dangerous angles and gain ground, while Robert cautions that a **S7 Vehicle cannot claim Cover**, making it surprisingly easy to lose to an MSV gun despite ARM4/STR2. Both: very strong on a vertical/covered table **and risky when deployment exposes it**.
- **Stinger vs Teucer:** Stinger offers more economical active-turn use and a flexible, linkable midrange weapon; Teucer offers a more specialized premium MSV sniper/ARO. Choose by table lanes, not assumed superiority.
- **Patroclus vs Drakios with Achilles:** Patroclus's **FTO link** offers speed, Fireteam Master and optional CoC/MediKit **but no Smoke**. The non-FTO Holoprojector HRL profile has Smoke but cannot join the Team Achilles/Shadow chart. Drakios supplies Albedo and a different anti-MSV/utility plan. Older theoretical lists can differ on Fireteam legality; current Army resolves it.
- **Puzzlers: broken vs merely good:** Robert considers them exceptional due to hidden E/Marat/control and the very low points outside the trooper count; the Dec 2025 tournament winner said their impact depends on actual lanes and pointed out **Surprise Attack does not automatically apply to templates**. Build placements that really force a choice.
- **Achilles or multiple ~30-point independent pieces:** the Dec 2025 satellite-winning Achilles approach proves the elite plan can work, while MetaChemistry E148 (Jul 2026) argues many builds prefer a spread of 30-point guns/specialists because an Achilles/Juggernaut list compromises the bottom end.
- **Fireteam-heavy vs independent:** Next Wave has multiple compact links and Fireteam Master synergy, but losing a Master may break the relevant team; compact Duos/Haris reduce the cost of a large clump and keep better angles. An Aug 2026 discussion specifically corrects the assumption that Fireteam Master universally converts irregular troops without the **required linked unit**.

## Vehicle, TAG, REM: don't mix the troop types

**Official current [Next Wave roster](https://infinityuniverse.com/en/factions/combined-army/next-wave):** **Juggernaut = TAG**; **Skyhound = Vehicle (VH)**; **Sartroid Puzzler/Ranter, Unidron, Q/M/T/E/R-Drones, Ikadron, Slave Drones = REM/support**. *Do not assume* Sphinx, Overdron, Xeodron, Cascuda, Morat Bultrak/Raicho or other Combined Army TAGs are in Next Wave simply because they appear in Vanilla/Onyx/Shasvastii.

| Platform | Role and **why** | Main failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Juggernaut TAG / Staldron** | High ARM/STR, **Guard** deters enemies trying to tie up a gun in CC; 8-2/Climbing Plus gives short-range reach; Tactical Awareness; option for MULTI HMG+NCO or higher-Burst Plasma. Staldron specialist offers a scoring route from a TAG investment. | Very costly, hackable, easily costs one specialist plus multiple Orders vs 2 cheaper attackers. Its cheap extra Staldron is not a replacement for a whole objective plan. Engineer/Techno-organic/repair interactions must be checked. |

Winning engagements through **angle + action fork** is the point. | **No Cover** and STR2: vulnerable to competent ranged AROs/MSV or aerial counters. A source calls it “effectively ARM1 once Cover is lost” as an *analogy*, not literal ARM. Hackable despite being remote-driven. |
| **Sartroid Puzzler (Cyberplug)** | Hidden E/Marat or PARA trap forces movement/hacking alternatives; one surprise ARO can end a premium attack run. | **M-Drone** | **Sensor** reveals Camo and deploys a cheap specialist/utility piece before expensive Ironside/Achilles fights; supports TAG/Skyhound approaches. | Sensor needs follow-through and Orders. |

### Combined Army cross-faction lens

- **Onyx (Oct 2026)**: Overdron/Xeodron, Nexus + Unidron and Q/E/M/R drones show **how much support tax premium robotics require**. **Do not copy their TAG availability or Fireteams** to Next Wave. Onyx is more machine-centric; Next Wave trades some pure REM focus for hero-led Fireteams, Cyberplug and Skyhound.
- **Shasvastii (Apr 2026 player case)**: hidden markers and cheap Repeater/counter-hacking can force a Next Wave flyer into hard tradeoffs. Consider how **Sensor and Blackheart mines** expose/protect angles. No automatic Speculo/Caliban in Next Wave.
- **Vanilla Combined**: cheap Q-Drone/Ikadron/M-Drone and Med-Tech logic translates; some Vanilla profiles and TAGs do not. Consult [official roster](https://infinityuniverse.com/en/factions/combined-army/next-wave) for legal Next Wave units before any Army export.
- **Other Vehicle factions (e.g. Kestrel/Haqqislam)**: lessons on Aerial Super-Jump, lack of Cover and rooftop sightlines **do transfer**; profiles, escape systems and specific weapon choices do not. A Skyhound is **not** a Redeye or Haytham.

## Three usable list approaches

1. **Balanced Pandora Research + Shadow:** cheap Harbinger LT/decoy and FO; Pandora + Stinger + Blackheart(s) as appropriate; Patroclus FTO + Steeljaw/Harbinger Shadow (**no Smoke on this linked Patroclus**); Gearhead + one/two Puzzlers; Ikadron(s) and an independent specialist. **Why:** good anti-MSV, disposable mine pressure, close templates, protected Orders and actual button pushers. **Robert's favored architecture** is close to this, with multiple Blackhearts, a Stinger, Patroclus, Pandora and two Puzzlers.
2. **Achilles attack + insurance:** Achilles and a compact Patroclus/Drakios duo/Haris (confirm exact linking); Pandora CoC if Achilles LT; Harbingers / Ikadron for Orders; inexpensive specialist plus Puzzler/Blackheart ARO. **Why:** few enemy pieces can solve an efficiently delivered Achilles, but it must have **CoC, scoring and support**. **Do not assume Smoke on the accompanying Patroclus FTO**; only the separate non-FTO Holoprojector HRL version carries it. **MetaChemistry's Dec 2025 event winner** successfully leaned on Achilles, Patroclus, Teucer and auxiliary specialists, while conceding early opponents did not fully know Next Wave.
3. **Ironside + Skyhound pressure:** Ironside AP HMG or anti-hacker build plus Pandora/Gearhead support, Skyhound, Blackheart mines and Puzzler defense; affordable Harbingers and scoring plan. **Why:** Ironside contests opposing **MSV and hacker protection** so Skyhound can exploit vertical angles. **Risk:** the flyer may still die in one bad FTF, so the mission cannot depend solely on it.

**Juggernaut variant:** replace one premium attacking package with Juggernaut and at least one defensive/Engineer/mission tool; test on mission layout before building a double-Juggernaut list. **Do not silently treat the Oct 2025 first-impression plans as proven 2026 tournament lists.**

## Counterplay and robustness checks

- **Opponent has MSV/long sniper?** Don't deploy Skyhound where one return shot can decide the game; remove it with Ironside/Drakios/Teucer or reframe the lane with **unlinked Patroclus Holoprojector HRL Smoke** where actually selected (**not FTO Smoke**).
- **Opponent has hacking/repeaters?** Kill/avoid the net before advancing Achilles, Ironside, Juggernaut, Skyhound or TAG-connected peripherals. Invest in a **real** KHD, Firewall or removal plan, not just “I have a hacker.”
- **Opponent has Sensor/mines/cheap trading units?** Protect Gearhead/hidden Puzzlers and use actual overwatch; tiny no-Order peripherals are valuable as a *delay*, not invulnerable assassination tools.
- **Your assault leader dies?** You need a Harbinger/Contrabandolero/Sāchā score path, an independent gun and remaining Regular Orders. If the link Master dies, check what actually breaks.
- **Mission scoring requires repeated pushes across table?** Spread a FO/hacker/extra specialist instead of adding one more expensive ARO. Near-full elite skew is not automatically better because it kills more units.
- **Baggage/Reload:** recheck current N5.3 Reload and Baggage wording, especially disposable equipment and old turret spam advice. In the December 2025 winner interview, tournament players disputed whether turrets were nerfed *too far*; rules are not solved by their opinions.

## Edition warnings and exact authority

- [Official October 15, 2025 N5.2 update](https://infinityuniverse.com/en/news/army-update-october-2025-part2) added Next Wave and changed multiple Nexus, Achilles, Pandora, Patroclus and shared Combined profiles. **Pre-release videos/podcasts are not rules references.**
- [Official Sept 1, 2026 N5.3 + ITS18](https://infinityuniverse.com/en/news/infinity-rules-update-5-3) revised Peripheral (Cyberplug), Aerial, Super-Jump, Fireteam integrity, Minelayer, Firewall, Hacking Device, Baggage/Reload and various rules; added **Blur Spec-ops and Next Wave Team-ops**, updated **Sāchā's Availability**. TEAM-ops extra Haris and Spec-ops options are event-format dependent; **not free standard ITS list upgrades**.
- N5.3 rules specify that **Aerial troops do not trigger Boost-property weapons** (not immunity to other direct attacks). Validate Vehicle placement and Cyberplug use with [current official rules Wiki](https://infinitythewiki.com/Category:N5.3_Update).
- In older podcasts, “Puzzlers” sometimes described as independent troopers. **Cyberplug peripherals cost points but are not automatically extra Regular Orders**. Connected/Autonomous modes have distinct limitations. Consult current Army/current Peripheral text.
- **Term normalization:** Tekdrakens are Blackhearts and Steeljaws (not “tech dragons”), Wraith-1, Sartroid Puzzlers/Ranters, Skyhounds (VH), Juggernauts (TAG). No extra Sphinx/Cascuda/Overdron/Xeodron is claimed legal in Next Wave.

## Vision control — role/value update (23 October 2026)

- **Strict profile split:** Patroclus **unlinked 35/1 Holoprojector HRL has Smoke**; **FTO link-capable Patroclus has no Smoke**. Do not construct an imagined linked Achilles + Patroclus Smoke engine. **Drakios/Ironside Albedo** punishes MSV directly but is not a shared blocking zone; Teucer MSV2 is the reverse matchup tool.
- **Disagreement:** N5 reviewers value Albedo's hard-ARO answer, but question the price of Drakios if the opponent has no MSV; if Smoke is only available by unlinked Patroclus, account for the independent Order cost. [Vision-control guide](../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).
