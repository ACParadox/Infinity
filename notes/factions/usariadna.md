# USAriadna Ranger Force (USARF) — N5.3 tactical guide

**Research checked:** 2026-10-22. **Purpose:** concise list-building choices, competing opinions, and *why*; not a substitute for the current [USAriadna reference](../../sectorials/ariadna/usariadna.md) and [rules](../../rules/README.md). Standard USARF Army **304**, N5.3, pinned Army **7.26246.158** (2026-09-03; reference verified 2026-10-15). Army metadata says *discontinued*, but standard roster/Fireteam data remains present and playable. **L'Équipe Argent Reinforcements use a different chart**, not the lists below.

**Evidence priority:** pinned local profiles/Fireteam chart, [official Oct 2025 Army update](https://infinityuniverse.com/en/news/army-update-october-2025-part2), then up-to-date media and tournament examples. The Oct 2025 update changed Minutemen, Blackjacks, Devil Dogs, Van Zant and USARF teams materially; **Jan–May 2025 reviews predate those buffs**. Tactical video timestamps approximate (automatic captions sometimes garble names).

## 30-second assessment

**USARF wins through multiple affordable attacks, fast smoke, a camouflaged midfield and distributed small Fireteams—not a single unbeatable gunfighter.** Compared with other Ariadna armies, its gunfighters are less dominant; the plan is to avoid an elite enemy's preferred engagements and win the mission while their Orders are tied up.

- **Best repeatable ingredients:** **Foxtrot FO 18 / Minelayer 18 (0.5 SWC)** to score and delay; **Devil Dog + K-9 23**, **Desperado 7** to force smoke/template/CC exchanges; **Minuteman FO/NCO 25**, **Blackjack 35** and **Unknown Ranger 42–45** to provide real shooting without making one centerpiece responsible for every lane.
- **Cheap Regulars matter:** **5-pt Traktor Mul Baggage** and **10-pt Grunts** preserve Orders and points for several 20–45pt combat pieces. But a full Grunt Core is a *choice*, not an automatic power unit.
- **Redundancy beats peak BS:** MSV1 Marauder HRL, an AP HMG/Spitfire, a second threat from another angle, and a flanking **Van Zant** make target priority harder than one pricey Core. Most infantry are one Wound; avoid donating them to a single strong ARO.
- **Main limitations:** thin hacking/network, limited anti-armor/long-range delivery compared with premium factions, scarce orders beyond native Regular Orders, Irregular pieces competing for 15 slots, and mission-specific gaps. **No standard USARF TAG or VH troop**: the large **Blackjack is HI, not TAG**. Only the **Traktor Mul** is a standard REM.

*Source split:* [Shepherd (May 2025)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mqNiL2ziIkU) calls USARF an unusually difficult challenge faction (~4–16m); [Tactical Awareness (Jan 2025)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h7cTjIztb98) agrees the roster repeats similar builds (~28–36m). **After the October rework**, [Swiss Miss (Oct 2025)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AboAGci9_9A) rates new Minutemen, Blackjacks, Devil Dogs and Unknown Ranger much more optimistically. Both can be true: **better individual tools**, but still less list/mission flexibility than many factions. [Oct 2025 community reactions](https://www.reddit.com/r/InfinityTheGame/comments/1o7dy5i) echo “meaningful improvements, not top tier.”

## Start with one of five architectures

| Architecture | Example legal nucleus (current 300-point-relevant costs) | Why / drawback |
|---|---|---|

Blackjack provides 2-Wound ARM5 and BS Attack (-3); Rosie and 112 are Wildcards counting as Grunts and can furnish a matching-pair composition benefit plus scoring/recovery. Defensive and active options without a 5-man death star. All three rely on legal Wildcard chart semantics. |
| **Minuteman + Unknown Ranger assault** | **Minuteman FO/NCO 25/0 + Unknown Ranger MSV1 Mk12 42/0** in a **USARF Duo**; alternative **Minuteman NCO AP HMG 34/1.5** | **67 pts, 0 SWC** baseline: a WIP12 FO with **BS +1SD**, T2/light-flamethrower utility and NCO supports the fast Tactical Awareness/MSV1 gunfighter. Spend Lt/NCO resources intentionally; no automatic assumption that the whole Fireteam gets a personal order. |
| **Ranger/bike objective run** | **Maverick FO 17/0 + Maverick AP Spitfire 26/1 + Motorized 112 19/0** in a **Mavericks Haris** | **62 pts / 1 SWC**: 20-10 movement, smoke, MSV1 gun and fast Doctor/specialists reach late objectives quickly. Opposing AROs and motorcycle footprints matter; the bikes aren't durable TAG substitutes. |
| **Marauder MSV ranged link** | **Marauder HRL/MSV1 23/1.5 + Marauder Paramedic 21/0** as a **USARF Duo**, or add an eligible USARF Wildcard for a Haris | HRL attacks clustered targets and MSV answers Mimetism; Paramedic preserves scoring. Forward deployment lets them start closer to good attack angles. Only 1 Wound each: avoid overpaying for a big static Core. |
| **Asymmetric assault / off-table threat** | **Van Zant 33–35/0** *or* Airborne Ranger FO **20/0**; two Foxtrots and 1–2 Desperadoes | Force enemies to screen their DZ and spend Orders unraveling midfield mines/decoys; hit the least-defended side. Van Zant's Tactical Awareness funds a raid, while the cheaper Airborne FO emphasizes mission access. Off-table presence reduces initial table control. |

**Construction default:** get **two distinct credible shooting ranges**, **two independent specialists**, **one lane of smoke or Disco-based visibility control**, **one genuine midfield barrier**, then budget for recovery and off-table threats. Do not buy every aggressive Irregular troop and leave no Order pool for an endgame specialist.

## Vehicles, TAGs and REMs — what USARF actually gets

**Standard USARF has no troop typed `TAG` and no troop typed `VH` (Vehicle).** **Blackjacks are 2-Wound, S5 Hackable HI** and *not* TAGs; **Desperado/Maverick/112 Motorized** are motorcycle troops, not VH units. **Traktor Mul is the only standard USAriadna REM** (AVA 2). Don't import Chernobog, Anaconda, Vystrel, Kuryer, or Dynamo just because another Ariadna force can field them.

### Traktor Mul — the shared Ariadna REM

| Profile | Buy it because | Avoid it when |
|---|---|---|
| **Baggage/Deactivator, 5 pts / 0 SWC** | A **5pt Regular Order** is a bargain in an army investing heavily in 20–45pt pieces. Baggage can service nearby reloadable equipment, including smoke/other disposable packages when conditions permit; Deactivator adds backup objective/area utility. | Even one S4 base demands safe DZ floor area. Do not equate cheapest points per Order with zero placement cost. |
| **Katyusha Guided, 9 pts / 1 SWC** | Low-price **Guided** option if Foxtrot/Maverick/Airborne Forward Observers are **already** part of the mission plan. Lets a successfully marked target be threatened from cover without a risky gunfight. | This is **BS10**, without a high-quality wide Spotlight network. If FO orders are costly or risky, 1 SWC is often better on an offensive shooter. Never build the entire list around a speculative indirect shot. |
| **Uragan Guided + Total Reaction, 14 pts / 1 SWC** | A cheap **reactive order tax**, with Guided as an opportunistic second role. Can stay in Group 2; Dozer recovery can make the opponent solve the same lane again. | Low BS/durability compared with elite TR REMs; easy to counter via superior FtF, templates or flankers. ### Motorcycles — the practical “vehicle” game

- **Fast mission package:** Maverick FO **17** + Maverick AP Spitfire **26/1** + 112 Motorized **19** in a legal Haris. The FO reaches objectives; Spitfire clears opposition; Doctor/healing/smoke supports the route. Two expensive bikes plus support should not be thrown down a straight enemy ARO lane.
- **Cheap smoke/disruption package:** 1–2 **7pt Desperadoes** alongside a Devil Dog or Van Zant. They force threats to react, place smoke before a stronger attacker crosses, and can cheaply trade where a 35pt shooter should not be risked.
- **Limitations:** big S4 footprints, enemy AROs, Irregular vs Regular distinction, and smoke visibility rules. **MSV1 opponents can still see through ordinary smoke under applicable conditions**; control line of sight, not merely whether smoke exists.

### Blackjack repair / pseudo-TAG warning

Treat the Blackjack as a **heavy-gun platform**, not an expensive TAG with remote repair logistics: it lacks TAG classification and its **Battle Ravaged** form becomes easier to hack/shoot after the first damaging transition. A Dozer can recover qualifying damage, but don't assume a servant bot or premium engineer package the roster **does not have**. Its best protection is often a good ARO angle, **BS Attack (-3)**, Fireteam composition, and other troops forcing the attacker to split Orders.

## Fireteam and command traps

- **Limits:** **1 Core, 1 Haris, unlimited Duos**. Native **Grunt Core/Haris** needs ≥1 actual Grunt (FD Grunt excluded). **USARF Core/Haris/Duo** uses a **Minuteman-or-Marauder required-choice foundation**. **Blackjack Haris/Duo** needs ≥1 Blackjack. **Mavericks Haris/Duo** needs ≥1 Maverick, up to 2 Mavericks and 1 Motorized 112. **USAMC Duo** = **Devil Dog FTO only**. Wildcards (112 FTO, Rosie, Unknown Ranger) can join an otherwise eligible formation, but do not erase a native requirement.
- **An FTO label doesn't authorize a Fireteam:** 9pt **Beasthunter FTO** has **no entry** in the USAriadna chart. The normal Devil Dog + K-9 is *not* USAMC FTO. The **12pt Forward Deployment Grunt** explicitly cannot join.
- **Lt choices:** **10pt Grunt LT** saves points and stays hidden; **Minuteman T2 FO LT 27/0** earns +1 Command Token and is a possible tougher command body, but risks command as a visible gun/specialist; **Marauder LT 19/0** is a midfield-risky alternative; **17pt Foxtrot camo LT costs 1 SWC** and creates concealment but not command security. **Rosie CoC 26/0** mitigates an active-LT plan. **Minuteman NCO** provides a sink for otherwise-unused Lt Orders; coordinate with Fireteam rules before assuming each is a team-wide activation.
- **Composition:** 112 FTO/Rosie count as **Grunts**; Unknown Ranger counts as **USARF**. Composition bonuses require the actual current chart/keyword combination: do not assert “all Wildcards count as everyone.” **Rosie + 112 + Blackjack** is a useful tested Haris shape, not a license to bring a solo Blackjack into any Fireteam regardless of constraints.
- **Off-table Orders:** Van Zant and 6th Airborne arrive later. Balance surprise with enough starting-on-table ARO/specialist pieces; losing two important Regular Orders before an airborne plan comes online is a real cost.
- **SWC exercise:** Blackjack AP HMG **1.5** + Marauder HRL **1.5** + Minuteman ML **1.5** + Maverick AP Spitfire **1** = **5.5 SWC**, leaving little for Foxtrot Minelayer/other guns. Choose *which* defensive and offensive weapon must really shoot rather than paying for every attractive loadout.

## Where the analysts disagree (short version)

1. **How strong is updated USARF?** Shepherd **May 2025** / TA **Jan 2025**: underpowered compared with opponents, limited redundancy, poor mission flexibility. Swiss Miss **Oct 2025**: recent upgrades give genuinely compelling Minuteman/Blackjack/Devil Dog choices. **Different dates** are a major part of the disagreement; don't copy pre-update ratings verbatim.
2. **Best main gun: Minuteman vs Blackjack vs Unknown Ranger?** **Swiss Miss:** Unknown Ranger near S; 2025+1SD Minutemen near top A; Blackjack solid A. **LoL Nov 2025**: Blackjack **low B/high C** due mobility, BTS collapse; Minuteman ML can be a more oppressive ARO. **Shepherd pre-update**: Blackjack usable if linked but not a premium gun; Unknown Ranger no longer N4's default superstar. **Practical rule:** Minuteman for special-die/NCO/FO utility, Blackjack for 2-Wound assault/ARO, Ranger for MSV+Tactical Awareness+scoring.
3. **Grunt brick vs distributed links:** Old USARF fans like HFT/line infantry stacking; Shepherd advocates **minimal** Grunt support and many small independent attacks. Grunts cost less; opponents also have easier tools to kill static W1 BS11 links. Choose based on table/mission, not nostalgia.
4. **Hardcase vs Beasthunter vs Foxtrot:** Hardcase is cheap decoy distraction but Irregular; Beasthunter presents a more serious AP mine/HFT/CC threat; Foxtrot is a Regular on-table scoring/mine asset. Which one matters depends on whether **Order pool, midfield defense, or disposable assassination** is missing.
5. **Devil Dog FTO vs K-9:** Solo 22pt FTO can form USAMC Duo and threaten with grenades; **23pt K-9 team** compresses Sensor/multiple bodies and better CC/mine clearing. **Shepherd** prefers K-9; **Swiss Miss** sees genuine upside to the new FTO Duo as a different list idea, not simply a cheaper replacement.
6. **Motorcycles:** Swiss Miss likes Mavericks' fast MSV/AP Spitfire, Shepherd warns motorcycle cover/positioning gives little error margin. Desperado smoke consistently useful but spamming Irregular bikes consumes regular-shooting/scoring slots.

## Vision control — role/value update (23 October 2026)

- **Mobility/value options:** **Desperado 7pt Smoke** is a cheap expendable route opener; **Maverick FO17/0 / AP Spitfire26/1** add very fast smoke and scoring/shooting; **Devil Dog+K9 23** uses Smoke to reach close-range pressure. USARF's standard MSV1 pieces are not equivalent to a ready-made MSV2 Smoke-shoot package.
- **Buy by destination:** Desperado for one inexpensive lane tax, Maverick when the same high-MOV model should also score, Devil Dog when protected close attack is the goal. Against MSV, normal Smoke may still expose the runner. [Vision tiers](../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).

### Hacker and KHD tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Wardriver HD14/1 SWC** adds ordinary anti-HI/TAG **Carbonite/Oblivion/Spotlight**, but no premium anti-Hacker KHD/HD+ toolkit or automatic long-range Repeater access. **Why:** USAriadna normally wins through efficient shooting, mines/Camo, and several nonhackable infantry threats. Use the Wardriver as deliberate *counter-tech* for an enemy TAG/HI bottleneck or hacking scenario, not because every army needs a Hacker. Keeping SWC for conventional guns is usually more reliable when there is no meaningful hackable target.

### ARO apex/value cross-reference (2026-10-23)

See [ARO units: N5.3 apex/value tiers, hidden-versus-visible and deployment/matchups](../list-building/aro-units-apex-and-value.md). The **current profile details here** control exact cost/SWC, Training and Fireteam eligibility; do not import another faction's link or specialist upgrades.

- **Marauder MSV1 HRL** — strong one-wound visor template; cover midfield approach, avoid plain shootout with premium active gun
- **Minuteman ML (+1SD)** — powerful linked long-lane reactive spike in USAriadna; one wound makes reliability/backup mandatory
- **Blackjack T2 Sniper** — 2W/ARM and BS Attack(-3) multi-role long-lane, but hacking and transmutation limitations matter
- **Grunt HFT / 0SWC sniper** — cheap S2 short-corner or modest rear gun; prioritize defending objectives and support
- **Traktor Mul support** — rare Ariadna cheap reload/guided/total-reaction choices; only buy a real Guided program if FO route is viable

**Choose by circumstance:** a hard long gun matters only when the opponent must cross its arc; cheaper mines, Camo markers, templates, Flash, DTWs and hacking force movement or risk without committing an entire attack plan. Budget a second layer and preserved counterattack, check pie-slicing, hidden Order cost, anti-visual bypass and repair routes.

### Airborne / enemy-DZ attack profile choices (2026-10-23)

**Van Zant33–35/0 (Parachutist(DZ), TA, Dogged)** is the premier reliable enemy-edge support/LT hunter: choose AP Rifle vs T2 BSG according to exposed soft targets versus armor. **6th Airborne Ranger19 SMG/20 BSG/20 FO** is cheaper *ordinary* Parachutist, **cannot directly enter enemy DZ** and is better for unguarded flank/late scoring. The extra 13–16pts to Van Zant buys direct DZ access and a personal extra Tactical Order, not an unconditional kill. Cover the edge with AROs if facing opposing AD.

### Warbands and inexpensive trading roles — N5.3

**Desperado Chain Rifle+Smoke GL7/0** is among the best **fast 20-10 Irregular/Impetuous** trades: force a gunner's Dodge or template exchange, lay lateral smoke for a forward Specialist or threaten opposing cheap Orders. **Boarding Shotgun9/0** only if actual quality shooting/short-range target exists. **No standard USARF Galwegian5, Morlock6 or Dāturazi14 import.** **Van Zant33–35/0** has backline DZ Parachutist + TA and plays a different premium attack vector; use 7pt Desperado as smoke/screen instead of risking Van Zant for one cheap kill. Protect the on-table LT/CoC; this sectorial's Fireteam chart remains authoritative.

### N5.3 midfield area denial / speed bumps (tactical synthesis)

**Foxtrot Boarding Shotgun + Shock Mine Minelayer 18/.5** is the inexpensive Regular Camo/Infiltration turn-zero obstacle. **Foxtrot FO 18/0** scores instead and does not deploy a mine at setup; Hardcase 12/0 is cheap Irregular Camo/Decoy and Beasthunter 15/0 provides close-range template and AP mine pressure, but neither duplicates Foxtrot Minelayer. Opposing concern: buying too many disposable midfield blockers can weaken USARF's counterattack and objective specialists.

### Doctor / Engineer / Recovery cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Warband/low-cost LI casualties** often aren't worth 3–4 Orders of Doctor travel unless they are the last scoring Specialist. Use a roster-legal **112-style Doctor/Engineer** only when it supports an expensive ARO, vehicle or objective specialist; **No-Cube targets** have no ordinary Cube-based token reroll. Consider **GizmoKit PH and Repair WIP** separately; the 2–3pt Servant is an Order-efficiency investment, not an extra independent activation. [Doctor/Engineer recovery odds and tier guide](../list-building/doctors-engineers-and-recovery-apex-value.md).

### N5.3 DZ defense / anti-alpha-strike tactical choices

**Warcor 360º3/0** is cheap Irregular protection versus Van Zant/Hellcat-style reverse attacks (also relevant in a mirror match); **Foxtrot BSG+Shock Mine Minelayer 18/.5** secures an infiltrated *outer* crossing route while **Foxtrot FO18/0** scores without a starting mine. **Hardcase** cheap Camo/Decoy and **Beasthunter HFT/Panzerfaust** force close corners, but neither means a free initial deployed mine without the printed Minelayer skill. **Counter:** defensive Irregular saturation cannot replace a Regular attack group, and static mine paths cannot guard every DZ-edge/rooftop Combat Jump entry.

[Whole-game DZ defense value/apex and anti-IMP matchups](../list-building/dz-defenders-alpha-strike-and-impersonation-apex-value.md).

### Close-combat assassins and counter-CC — N5.3 (2026-10-23)

**Devil Dog + K-9 Antipode23** is USARF's key *two-body close assault*: 15-10 Devil Dog CC23/PH16 with smoke/chain rifle and 15-15 Antipode can create contact pressure or multiple-body CC, if geometry and controller rules permit; it isn't a cheap lone MA5 specialist. **Van Zant33–35** uses Parachutist enemy-DZ entry and AP+T2 CC as a *secondary* finisher after the correct gun angle. **Desperado7** has fast 20-10 Smoke/chain rifle for cheap trade but S4 moto and Irregular orders make isolated assassination unreliable; Foxtrot Camo/Infiltration CC14 is not a duelist. [Close-combat apex/value, positioning and counter-CC](../list-building/dedicated-close-combat-assassins-apex-value.md).

### Anti-TAG / Anti-HI without hacking — N5.3 (2026-10-23)

**USARF** relies on **Blackjack/Grunt/Marauder rifle, rockets and specialist screens**, plus **Desperado7 Smoke motorcycle** to gain a usable side angle; **Van Zant33/35** can flank an armored target from DZ edge but AP+T2 CC only works if actually reaching contact. **Devil Dog23 + K-9** can tie up a TAG in Engaged, but template guard and S6 constraints matter. **Standard rifles alone** are poor against ARM8+ in cover: choose a roster-legal hard ARO/anti-armor gun and avoid treating a fast smoke carrier as a guaranteed kill. [Non-hacking armor-killer value rankings and tactical matchups](../list-building/anti-tag-anti-heavy-infantry-non-hacking-apex-value.md).

### N5.3 objective holding, zone domination & carried-objective choices

**5th Minuteman FO+NCO25/0** is **Non-Hackable** (not automatic hacker target), BS Attack(+1SD), Immunity(Shock, IMM-B), T2 Rifle+LFT and direct specialist eligibility: superb box-guard value for a covered area. **Marauder Paramedic21/0** ARM3/heavy flamer on a forward-deployed support role is a second cheaper carrier. **Unknown Ranger Specialist Operative+Tactical Awareness39/0** (Mimetism-3, W1+NWI, ARM4, immune to Shock) carries efficiently with dedicated Order and CC counter, compared with a more expensive heavy team. **Blackjack35/1–1.5** full W2/ARM5, battle-ravaged W2/ARM4, Transmutation, is **Hackable and not a Supplies Specialist**; useful *zone VP/support* gun and remains worth defending after transformation. **Opposing:** a 25pt Minuteman plus Marauder can cover two different scoring zones at lower concentration than one Blackjack+Unknown Ranger; prepare for templates rather than relying on Fireteam long ARO only.

[Cross-game objective holder and zone-domination apex/value tiers, scenario rules, and opposing transcript reviews](../list-building/objective-holding-zone-domination-and-carriers-apex-value.md). Exact profiles/Fireteams above are authoritative.

### Anti-Tech — enemy visors, Mimetism, markers and Impersonation (2026-10-23)

**Wardriver Disco Baller16/1** is the scarce low-cost **enemy-MSV/Marksmanship blind** in an otherwise smoke-heavy force; **regular 8pt Disco-bearing utility profile** (if its exact roster option is selected) can create another emergency screen. **Desperado Smoke, Foxtrot/Grunt cover and close templates** often solve an expensive Mimetism/visor ARO by **avoiding it** rather than beating it in a BS contest. Against Camo, use cheap Discover probes, deployment mines and protected routes; there is no justification to rank every Camo marker as a premium kill target. **Counter:** ordinary Smoke doesn't block MSV2, and Disco's two-use/placement+follow-up Orders can exceed the value of simply scoring elsewhere.

#### Classification and evidence standards

- **Must-have / near-must:** repeated first-choice inclusion across independently built example lists, or an explicit reviewer “always / most lists / auto-include” judgment. **Near-must is not universal:** a role can be indispensable without any one profile being mandatory.
- **Go-to:** repeatedly attractive price/role efficiency. Not all go-to units belong in the same 300-point list; missions, fire lanes, SWC, Orders and competing packages decide.
- **Never take / strong avoid:** a directly stated negative review of a **specific profile or construction**, ideally with a viable same-roster alternative. Use **conditional** or **contested** if the profile has a niche, there is dissent or the source is pre-rework. There is **no evidenced blanket ban on an entire Ariadna unit family**.
- **Evidence:** **explicit** = specific presenter says so; **repeat** = multiple independently constructed sample lists; **reasoned** = synthesis of recorded strengths/weaknesses plus current reference, **not** a claimed quotation.
- A recorded list choice alone does not prove “most players” use a unit. Transcripts may mangle Ariadna proper names, so check names and legality against the current repository. The standard lists here do **not** import `L'Équipe Argent` Reinforcements, which have separate charts.

#### Worst, uncommon and proxy-first purchases by sectorial (2026-10-30)

**Scope and confidence.** This answers a different question from the unit-role tables above: **“Which whole miniature can I skip buying, which uncommon specialist should I proxy until I actually use it, and which *bad profile* should I avoid without rejecting its whole unit?”** An **AVA limit is not a shopping target**. Video/podcast reviews are **opinion and illustrative lists, not tournament selection-frequency data**; “uncommon” means **not a normal first purchase in these reviewed builds**, not measured rare usage. The latest [N5.3 roster]() and its **Fireteam, silhouette, peripherals and availability** rules override old recordings.

**Buying labels:**
- **Skip by default; proxy if needed:** the **normal whole-model job** has explicit negative commentary or an unusually weak price/role comparison. The exception is recorded; **no Ariadna model is proven mathematically useless in every scenario**.
- **Proxy first:** a full unit with a credible specialty, but you should establish *a specific list and mission use* before buying its sculpt; this is an **inferred shopping recommendation**, not a presenter's alleged quotation.
- **Avoid profile only:** the unit has legitimate alternate profiles. **Never extrapolate to all its miniatures.**
