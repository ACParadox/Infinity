# USAriadna Ranger Force (USARF) — N5.3 tactical guide

## Overview

**What USAriadna does especially well:** cheap **motorcycle Smoke, regular midfield mines, non-Hackable mission fighters and several small legal Fireteams** force an opponent to solve many modest threats instead of one premium gun. The October 2025 Minuteman/Blackjack improvements matter; early-2025 tier opinions should not be treated as current profile verdicts.

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **No standard TAG and no VH-class Vehicle.** **Blackjack** is S5 Hackable HI, and the **Traktor Mul** is the sectorial's only standard REM.
- **Projected Eclipse on a very cheap carrier:** **Sāchā Disco Baller 8/0**, an Irregular specialist-style support piece, is unusual; **Wardriver Disco Baller 16/1** is another route. Both differ from ordinary Smoke and cost setup Orders.
- **Chain of Command exists:** **Rosie CoC 26/0** is a specific paid profile, so do not mistakenly describe USARF as having no CoC.
- **Vision and hacking gaps:** **Desperado/Maverick/Devil Dog** provide normal Smoke, **Marauder/Unknown Ranger** provide MSV1 options, but no native MSV2-based Smoke-shooting engine; hacking and repeaters remain thin.

## 30-second assessment

**USARF wins through multiple affordable attacks, fast smoke, a camouflaged midfield and distributed small Fireteams—not a single unbeatable gunfighter.** Compared with other Ariadna armies, its gunfighters are less dominant; the plan is to avoid an elite enemy's preferred engagements and win the mission while their Orders are tied up.

- **Repeatable list ingredients:** choose tools by job rather than presenting all profiles as one purchase:
  - **Foxtrot FO (18) / Minelayer (18/0.5):** mission specialist and midfield delay.
  - **Devil Dog + K-9 (23):** Smoke, templates and close combat.
  - **Desperado (7):** low-cost disposable pressure.
  - **Minuteman FO/NCO (25):** shooting and mission/Order utility.
  - **Blackjack (35):** durable-looking heavy gun and ARO pressure.
  - **Unknown Ranger (42–45):** independent elite shooting.
  - Preserve the scoring/Order plan rather than buying every attacker.
- **Cheap Regulars matter:** **5-pt Traktor Mul Baggage** and **10-pt Grunts** preserve Orders and points for several 20–45pt combat pieces. But a full Grunt Core is a *choice*, not an automatic power unit.
- **Redundancy beats peak BS:** MSV1 Marauder HRL, an AP HMG/Spitfire, a second threat from another angle, and a flanking **Van Zant** make target priority harder than one pricey Core. Most infantry are one Wound; avoid donating them to a single strong ARO.
- **Main limitations:** thin hacking/network, limited anti-armor/long-range delivery compared with premium factions, scarce orders beyond native Regular Orders, Irregular pieces competing for 15 slots, and mission-specific gaps. **No standard USARF TAG or VH troop**: the large **Blackjack is HI, not TAG**. Only the **Traktor Mul** is a standard REM.

*Source split:* [Shepherd (May 2025)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mqNiL2ziIkU) calls USARF an unusually difficult challenge faction (~4–16m); [Tactical Awareness (Jan 2025)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h7cTjIztb98) agrees the roster repeats similar builds (~28–36m). **After the October rework**, [Swiss Miss (Oct 2025)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AboAGci9_9A) rates new Minutemen, Blackjacks, Devil Dogs and Unknown Ranger much more optimistically. Both can be true: **better individual tools**, but still less list/mission flexibility than many factions. [Oct 2025 community reactions](https://www.reddit.com/r/InfinityTheGame/comments/1o7dy5i) echo “meaningful improvements, not top tier.”

**Smoke-delivery choices:**

- **Desperado (7pt):** expendable low-cost route opener and close-range tax.
- **Maverick FO (17/0) / AP Spitfire (26/1):** a fast carrier that can also score or shoot.
- **Devil Dog + K-9 (23pt):** use Smoke to approach for CC and template pressure.

They are alternatives with different destinations, not a mandatory three-piece package. USARF's MSV1 pieces are not an MSV2 Smoke-shoot package; conventional Smoke may fail against opposing MSV. See [vision-control tiers](../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).

**Apply four stress tests from the discussion:** (1) Can the list attack **heavy Camouflage**? (2) Does it have AP/T2 or other anti-armor/anti-materiel delivery on the *actual target*? (3) Are the 15 trooper slots generating usable **Regular Orders**, not merely isolated Irregular activity? (4) Is there an independent surviving Specialist and protected Lieutenant? The Minuteman buffs are pinned facts; claims that USARF is weak, fun or soon to be reworked are **opinion/speculation**.

## Vehicles, TAGs and REMs — what USARF actually gets

**Current standard USARF classifications:** no troop is typed TAG or VH (Vehicle).

- **Blackjack:** 2-Wound S5 Hackable HI; not a TAG.
- **Desperado:** motorcycle troop, not a VH.
- **Maverick:** motorcycle troop, not a VH.
- **112 Motorized:** motorcycle LI, not a VH.
- **Traktor Mul:** the only standard USARF REM (AVA2).

Do not import the following from other Ariadna rosters:

- **Chernobog**
- **Anaconda**
- **Vystrel**
- **Kuryer**
- **Dynamo**

Baggage can service nearby reloadable equipment, including smoke/other disposable packages when conditions permit; Deactivator adds backup objective/area utility. | Even one S4 base demands safe DZ floor area. ### Motorcycles — the practical “vehicle” game

- **Fast mission package:** Maverick FO **17** + Maverick AP Spitfire **26/1** + 112 Motorized **19** in a legal Haris. The FO reaches objectives; Spitfire clears opposition; Doctor/healing/smoke supports the route. Two expensive bikes plus support should not be thrown down a straight enemy ARO lane.
- **Cheap smoke/disruption package:** 1–2 **7pt Desperadoes** alongside a Devil Dog or Van Zant. They force threats to react, place smoke before a stronger attacker crosses, and can cheaply trade where a 35pt shooter should not be risked.
- **Limitations:** big S4 footprints, enemy AROs, Irregular vs Regular distinction, and smoke visibility rules. **MSV1 opponents can still see through ordinary smoke under applicable conditions**; control line of sight, not merely whether smoke exists.

### Blackjack repair / pseudo-TAG warning

Treat the Blackjack as a **heavy-gun platform**, not an expensive TAG with remote repair logistics: it lacks TAG classification and its **Battle Ravaged** form becomes easier to hack/shoot after the first damaging transition. A Dozer can recover qualifying damage, but don't assume a servant bot or premium engineer package the roster **does not have**. Its best protection is often a good ARO angle, **BS Attack (-3)**, Fireteam composition, and other troops forcing the attacker to split Orders.

## Fireteam and command traps

- **Fireteam limits:** 1 Core, 1 Haris and unlimited Duos, with different required-choice models:
  - **Grunt Core/Haris:** requires a native Grunt, excluding the Forward Deployment variant.
  - **USARF Core/Haris/Duo:** a Minuteman-or-Marauder foundation.
  - **Blackjack Haris/Duo:** requires a Blackjack.
  - **Mavericks Haris/Duo:** requires a Maverick and observes the actual maximum.
  - Check canonical profile/FTO and composition restrictions.
- **An FTO label alone never establishes membership:**
  - **Beasthunter FTO (9pt):** absent from the USARF chart.
  - **Devil Dog + K-9:** normal package is not the USAMC FTO.
  - **Forward Deployment Grunt (12pt):** explicitly cannot join.
- **Lieutenant choices:**
  - **Grunt LT (10):** cheap concealed command.
  - **Minuteman T2 FO LT (27/0):** +1 Command Token and a tougher-looking body, but exposing it risks command.
  - **Marauder LT (19/0):** alternative with midfield exposure risk.
  - **Foxtrot Camo LT (17/1):** hidden identity at an SWC premium.
  - Choose for command safety as well as shooting; a cheap LT is not necessarily protected.
- **Composition counting rules:**
  - **112 FTO / Rosie:** count as Grunts.
  - **Unknown Ranger:** counts as USARF.
  - **Rosie + 112 + Blackjack:** one tested Haris configuration, not general solo-Blackjack permission.
  - Wildcard status does not automatically grant every family keyword.
- **Off-table Orders:** Van Zant and 6th Airborne arrive later. Balance surprise with enough starting-on-table ARO/specialist pieces; losing two important Regular Orders before an airborne plan comes online is a real cost.
- **SWC exercise:** a heavy-weapon shopping list can spend nearly everything:
  - **Blackjack AP HMG:** 1.5 SWC
  - **Marauder HRL:** 1.5 SWC
  - **Minuteman ML:** 1.5 SWC
  - **Maverick AP Spitfire:** 1 SWC
  - **Total:** 5.5 SWC, leaving little for Foxtrot Minelayer or other guns. Select the actual firing roles instead of paying for every loadout.

## Where the analysts disagree (short version)

1. **How strong is updated USARF?** Shepherd **May 2025** / TA **Jan 2025**: underpowered compared with opponents, limited redundancy, poor mission flexibility. Swiss Miss **Oct 2025**: recent upgrades give genuinely compelling Minuteman/Blackjack/Devil Dog choices. **Different dates** are a major part of the disagreement; don't copy pre-update ratings verbatim.
2. **Main gun comparison (opinions from different dates):**
   - **Unknown Ranger:** Swiss Miss rated near S for its role.
   - **Minuteman:** Swiss Miss rated updated +1SD profiles highly; LoL valued the ML ARO.
   - **Blackjack:** Swiss Miss rated solid A, whereas LoL argued low B/high C due to mobility/BTS collapse.
   - Earlier Shepherd commentary predates those reworks; do not treat it as the same balance snapshot.
3. **Grunt brick vs distributed links:** Old USARF fans like HFT/line infantry stacking; Shepherd advocates **minimal** Grunt support and many small independent attacks. Grunts cost less; opponents also have easier tools to kill static W1 BS11 links. Choose based on table/mission, not nostalgia.
4. **Hardcase vs Beasthunter vs Foxtrot:** Hardcase is cheap decoy distraction but Irregular; Beasthunter presents a more serious AP mine/HFT/CC threat; Foxtrot is a Regular on-table scoring/mine asset. Which one matters depends on whether **Order pool, midfield defense, or disposable assassination** is missing.
5. **Devil Dog FTO vs K-9:** Solo 22pt FTO can form USAMC Duo and threaten with grenades; **23pt K-9 team** compresses Sensor/multiple bodies and better CC/mine clearing. **Shepherd** prefers K-9; **Swiss Miss** sees genuine upside to the new FTO Duo as a different list idea, not simply a cheaper replacement.
6. **Motorcycles:** Swiss Miss likes Mavericks' fast MSV/AP Spitfire, Shepherd warns motorcycle cover/positioning gives little error margin. Desperado smoke consistently useful but spamming Irregular bikes consumes regular-shooting/scoring slots.
