# Military Orders — N5.3 Sectorial Listbuilding and Tactical Guide

**Reference archive:** [Military Orders primary transcripts, cross-faction analysis, battle reports and purchasing audit](references/military-orders_refs.md). **Authoritative data:** [Military Orders N5.3 roster, profiles, AVA and Fireteam chart](../../sectorials/panoceania/military-orders.md). This guide uses the September 2026 Army snapshot documented there; historical video lists are tactical evidence, **not** present-day legality or points authority.

## Overview

**What Military Orders does exceptionally well:** field **durable, hard-fighting Knights** that can win shooting exchanges, threaten close combat, *and* push objectives; protect those Knights with **Santiago/Hospitaller/Teutonic TinBots** and an unusually credible **PanO hacking package**; and deliver them through a mixture of **modular Fireteams, Trinitarian midfield presence, fast Montesas, Combat Jump, and limited projected Eclipse**. Its best armies create a **long-range opening → safe midfield approach → specialist/objective finish** instead of asking one imposing Knight link to win every engagement.

**Its defining constraint is economic, not the quality of the Knights.** Cheap, transferable Regular Orders are relatively scarce (notably **Fugazi AVA 1**); many tempting 30–75-point attackers, AROs and specialist upgrades compete for the same 300 points. **Knight Commander LT(+1) + NCO**, **TAG Tactical Awareness**, and **compact links** can stretch the attacking budget, but none makes a thin pool of ordinary Orders disappear. This point is central to [Robert Shepherd's July 2026 review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JrMTA91V7wc), [Ryan's post-update guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xjs6rsspz8I), and [Sneaky Snake's post-update overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kRaambqovmw).

**Fast identification — apex/value:** The **Teutonic 19-point Specialist/TinBot** is a **Top 3 *cost-value candidate*** among durable, low-cost two-Wound specialists/Firewall providers; **De Fersen's 42-point HD + Trinity(+1SD)** is a **Top 5 hybrid-Hacker apex candidate (S apex / A value)**, while **9-point Tech-Bee TAGCom** and **23–24-point Peacemaker/Auxbot** are **Top 5 value candidates** in their respective support roles; the **Trinitarian FO/network package**, **Joan v2 Mobility fighting Lieutenant (A+ apex)**, **Knight Commander LT(+1)/NCO engine** and **15-point Order Sergeant FTO HRL** belong on **Top 10 shortlists** in their distinct jobs. These are editorial role comparisons, **not** overall-game rankings.

**Signature Fireteam combinations:** The **Teutonic + Santiago KHD/TinBot + Crux FTO** Military Orders Haris concentrates two-Wound scoring, Firewall and exceptional 0-SWC firepower; an **Order Sergeant FTO Pitcher + Black Friar** Duo/Haris creates an affordable network/anti-visor support wing; **Hospitaller HMG + Doctor + De Fersen or TinBot** creates a durable Crusade assault/recovery shell. All require the canonical Fireteam chart's host/minimum conditions; the main alternatives compete for the single Haris.

**Capability fingerprint:** **Yes:** **Seraph/Tikbalang TAGs**, potent **MSV2** (Black Friar and Crosier HMG), **Chain of Command** (Knight Commander), **NCO/Tactical Awareness**, **Combat Jump**, dangerous linked hackers, and **finite Disco Baller/Eclipse** (Konstantinos and Crusader). **Unusual gap:** no cheap standard Smoke-warband engine, **Fugazi only AVA 1**, no ordinary **VH/Redeye**, and **no TAG Fireteams**. MO can bypass sightlines with Eclipse or fight through enemy camouflage with MSV2, but cannot rely on a mass-Smoke/MSV strategy.

### Particularly strong or unusual Fireteams

- **Military Orders cheap-control Core/Duo:** **Crosier(s) + Knight Commander + Black Friar HRL/Thunderbolt or Crosier MSV2 HMG**, with optional **Order Sergeant FTO Pitcher** and an appropriate Wildcard. The **Military Orders** family must satisfy a required-choice member: **Crosier, Teutonic or Santiago FTO** (and applicable chart Counts-as). The goal is a protected command/order shell with a cheap ranged answer, not five premium Knights by default.
- **Military Orders combat Haris:** **Teutonic Specialist or TinBot 19 + Santiago FTO KHD/TinBot 31 + Crux FTO MULTI Marksman 46**, or substitute **Montesa FTO 20** when team composition/mission supports it. Real two-Wound threats plus short-range specialist/cyber defense; **very expensive**, and the only Haris slot must genuinely advance the mission.
- **Order Sergeants Haris/Duo:** **Order Sergeant FTO Hacker/Pitcher 18 + Black Friar HRL 22** and optionally **Infirmarer Doctor 17–20**, **Curator Engineer 17–20** or **Bulleteer Spitfire 22**. **At least one actual Order Sergeant FTO** is mandatory; the **Escort Sergeant + Auxbot is not FTO**. This is an economical support or independent anti-visual/repeater formation.
- **Crusade (Hospitaller) Duo/Haris/Core:** **Hospitaller FTO HMG 32 + Doctor 30 + TinBot 27**, with **De Fersen, Knight of Justice, Joan or Hawkins** as eligible Wildcards/counts-as or Crusade members when needed. **At least one actual Hospitaller FTO** is required. Durable shooting/recovery/hacking concentration, but a full team consumes much of the army and exposes several hackable models on one route.
- **Velox Duo:** **Motorized Montesa Paramedic 23 + Infirmarer Doctor 17/20**, or an eligible **Bulleteer / Joan v2** combination per the chart. Use coordinated speed and a second mission flank; this does **not** make foot Montesa a bike or grant TAGs any Fireteam membership.
- **High-speed solo gun + small support Fireteam:** **Seraph, Tikbalang or Holy Sepulchre** is **not itself Fireteam-eligible**. Pair the solo attack budget with a *separate* Crosier/Order Sergeant/Military Orders team that delivers a hacker, Doctor, cheap ARO or scorer.

**Hard limits:** **one Core, one Haris, unlimited Duos**; Wildcards never waive a native minimum/required-choice. **Clipper FTO(+1B) has no MO Fireteam row**. **Combat Jump Santiago, solo Crux Hidden variants, Pathfinder, Seraph and Tikbalang do not gain Fireteams** by borrowing another PanO sectorial's chart. [Canonical FT-MO-01 to FT-MO-04 and Wildcards](../../sectorials/panoceania/military-orders.md#fireteams).

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **Strong hacking despite a knight-heavy PanO identity:** **De Fersen's S-apex/A-value HD + Trinity(+1SD) hybrid** (normal **Oblivion/Carbonite/Spotlight** *and* upgraded anti-Hacker Trinity; **not** a KHD/HD+), **Santiago FTO KHD/TinBot**, **Trinitarian KHD**, **Order Sergeant FTO HD/Pitcher 18/0.5**, **Knight of Justice HD/Pitcher 45/0.5**, **Trinitarian turn-zero Repeater**, **Peacemaker/Pathfinder/Fugazi**. This can form an offensive Trinity and defensive anti-TAG shell. **Not** an unlimited cheap mass-Pitcher army; 42–45pt premium hackers compete directly with shooting and Orders.
- **Projected Eclipse, not ordinary smoke saturation:** **Konstantinos FTO Disco 23** is linked route-making; **Crusader Disco 23** is airborne independent access. The current roster has no cheap conventional Smoke warband battery. Eclipse blocks **friendly and enemy MSV** alike; budget the disposable charges and placement Order.
- **Unusually inexpensive two-Wound knight specialists:** **Teutonic Specialist/TinBot 19**, **Montesa FTO Paramedic 20**, **Santiago KHD 31**, **Hospitaller Doctor 30**, plus **Mendoza/Crux/Hawkins** in specialized roles. The catch is **Impetuous/Limited Cover**, Firewall needs, and choosing the correct actual specialist for objectives.
- **Sparsely populated low-cost Order floor:** **Fugazi7 AVA1, Mulebot8 AVA2, Crosier11+, Warcor3 Irregular**. **Knight Commander LT(+1)** is productive only with an NCO that can actually use the Orders. **Joan's Inspiring Leadership** converts only eligible **non-Religious** Irregular orders under the current rule; most Order knights are Religious, so do not count free mass conversion.
- **Diverse high-cost attackers, not unlimited support:** **Seraph/Tikbalang/Holy Sepulchre/linked Knights**, **Crux Hidden**, **Motorized Montesa**, **Mendoza**, **Santiago Combat Jump**, **Crusader**. MO has **no ordinary VH** (and no Redeye). Buying two 70+ point TAGs is possible but a specialist/repair/order-list commitment, not the default.
- **Independent marker utility is concentrated:** **Trinitarian AVA3**, **Dart AVA1**, **Konstantinos Infiltration** and **Crux Hidden** are meaningful, but there is no vast skirmisher bench. **Trinitarian's N5.3 profile lists Camouflage, not Hidden Deployment**; older report narration mentioning a “hidden” Trinitarian sniper must not confer a current Hidden Deployment skill.
- **Defensive breadth without obligate elite ARO:** **Raveneye E/M or Cybermine**, **Order Sergeant Escort Neuro HRL/Auxbot**, **linked FTO HRL**, **Black Friar Albedo/MSV2**, **Crosier E/M MSR Minelayer**, **Sierra TR**, **Trinitarian camo MSR**, **Curator turret/mine**, **Fugazi/Warcor Flash**. Select overlapping routes rather than purchasing every gun for one firing lane.

**Practical default:** **one competent long-range opener + 1–2 compact support/fireteam components + Trinitarian midfield scoring + a separate fast/airborne objective route + enough Regular Orders**, with the LT/NCO and hacker/repair choices made deliberately.

## At a glance

- **Strength:** durable specialist-shooters, strong CC deterrence, small-team flexibility, hard-to-displace linked hacking, cheap *for a two-Wound knight* battlefield utility, and asymmetric scoring.
- **Best pressure sequence:** clear a 24–32-inch lane with HMG/TAG/strong ARO answer; project Eclipse, Pitcher or a side-angle threat only as needed; advance Teutonic/Santiago/Montesa; preserve at least one separate FO/Doctor/hacker for Turns 2–3.
- **Typical failure:** expensive Knights all approach down one hackable corridor; off-table troops reduce the first-turn pool; insufficient independent specialists remain; an expensive defensive Core trades poorly or cannot cross the table.
- **Main design disagreement:** Shepherd prioritizes overcoming the bottom-end Order deficit; Ryan emphasizes **two-phase openings and modular Fireteams**; Sneaky Snake actively defends **true Knight-heavy competitive lists**; Tactical Awareness demonstrates **high-end cyber/Guided** and **two-TAG** extremes. All are conditional strategies, not universal templates.

| **Guided missile (deliberate variant)** | **Clipper Guided ML16/1.5** after a genuine Spotlight/FO/Repeater/Targeted plan | **Clipper FTO19/1.5 is not a legal MO Fireteam member and is not the Guided profile.** Extra infrastructure can be more expensive than taking a direct gun. |

[Tactical Awareness *Military Orders REDUX!* (Spotify episode)](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6xyHjc3R0N37bDHl0HiROO) provides directly reviewed podcast discussion of De Fersen's Trinity dice, protected hacker packages, the Knights' expanded hacking game, and very different sample lists. The hosts acknowledge the cost and vulnerability of concentrating cyber value in a single premium Knight. **Ryan likes the KoJ Pitcher/hacker as a robust multifunction Wildcard; Shepherd explicitly rejects it as a *generic network relay* versus the 18pt Sergeant plus better hacker.** Both make sense **only for the named role**.

## Reactive turn, DZ defense and midfield denial

**Choose threats that cost the opponent Orders even if they die.** Exposing three 40pt Knights to one long-range lane is often worse than layering a disposable defense over an objective route.

| Defender | Role and positioning | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| **Raveneye 12 E/M Mine or Cybermine** | FO/Minelayer homefield deterrent against HI/TAG/Impersonator entry corridors; E/Marat follow-up | W1/BS11; a mine alone doesn't reveal/guarantee stopping Impersonation. |
| **Escort Sergeant Neuro HRL+Auxbot 22/1.5** | ARO template + synchronized close-range Fork watches a DZ breach | Not FTO, W1, requires Peripheral coherency; avoid counting Auxbot as another Order. |
| **Order Sergeant FTO HRL15 / Black Friar HRL22** | Affordable linkable launcher; Friar adds MSV2/Albedo, FTO saves points | 1.5SWC and no automatic defensive win; exact team and terrain matter. |
| **Sierra TR Thunderbolt18/0 or HMG23/1** | 360º Total Reaction / roof and AD gate; Machinist/Curator repair if needed | Exposed one-Wound REM can be removed by specialist long-range attackers; 18pt has less reach than HMG. |
| **Trinitarian MSR25/1.5 Camo** | Hidden *identity/marker position* until revealed, independent asymmetric sniper lane; independent infiltrator presence | W1/BS12 and 1.5SWC; can be countered by Triangulated Fire, Sensors, mimetic TAGs or alternate routes; N5.3 roster **does not print Hidden Deployment**. |
| **Crosier MSR+E/M Minelayer19/1** | DZ long-lane ARO + prepaid E/M mine; can join a relevant Military Orders Fireteam | W1/BTS0, expensive SWC when pairings require HMG/Spitfire. |

The **July 2025 podcast predates the October 2025 MO overhaul**, so its matchup lessons are useful but not its historical roster claims.

## Played Fireteam constructions to compare (2026-11-03)

The [canonical Military Orders chart](../../sectorials/panoceania/military-orders.md#fireteams) supports **one Core, one Haris and multiple Duos**; a linked **Santiago must use an FTO profile**, not the independent Combat Jump loadout. These are **played modules**, not complete universal 300-point lists.

- **200-point balanced Core + Haris — explicitly fielded in [#221](../battle-reports/221-230-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md):** **three Crosiers + Knight Commander CoC + Knight of Justice Spitfire Lieutenant** as a **Core of five**, alongside **two Teutonic Knights (Panzerfaust specialists) + Black Friar** as a **Haris of three**. **Santiago KHD** and **Warcor** remained separate; if replicating this under current rules, do not count the independent Santiago in either link.
- **Same models, different allocation — #221 comparison:** the other Military Orders player used a **Knight of Justice + Santiago KHD Duo**, **two independent Teutonic Knights** and a **five-person Core** reconstructed as **three Crosiers + Commander + Black Friar**. The **second-player 6–4** win demonstrates *mission scoring and risk management*, not that a Duo is inherently stronger than the alternative Core. **Verify the Santiago's FTO KHD/TinBot option** before treating that Duo as current-legal.
- **Compact tournament hacker pressure — [#160 top table](../battle-reports/151-160-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md):** **De Fersen + Knight of Justice Hacker/Pitcher + Teutonic TinBot** formed an explicitly described **Haris of three**. The **Teutonic is the required-choice native member**; the other two are Wildcards. Pair it with independent **Trinitarian**, **Tactical Awareness REM** and a cheap objective specialist instead of buying a fourth premium Knight that cannot fit a Haris.
- **Elite Crusade or double-TAG alternative — [#009](../battle-reports/001-010-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) and [#181](../battle-reports/181-190-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md):** #009 explicitly described a **Hospitaller HMG + De Fersen + Hospitaller TinBot** three-body Crusade-style team, and #181's champion used **two independent Seraph TAGs** in four of five rounds. The anti-Morat alternative of #181 deployed a **five-Knight assault team** for *one* matchup. Keep **Seraph companions as Peripherals** and do not silently merge the anti-Morat Knights into the played double-Seraph army.

**Build decision:** for missions rewarding many distinct actions, start from the **Crosier/Commander/KoJ Core + cheap Teutonic/Black Friar Haris**, or instead the **small De Fersen cyber-Haris and independent scorers**. A five-Knight Core is a matchup-specific investment, not the standard cost of entry. The #221 mirror illustrates that retaining a scoring Knight alive can outweigh one more attack Order.

**Against dense hacking/Guided networks:** block or destroy Repeaters at the edge before moving grouped HI; use **Order Sergeant Pitchers + De Fersen**, **Santiago KHD Firewall**, **Trinitarian KHD**, **E/M or separate unhackable lane** as appropriate. A TAG with a Tech-Bee is **not immune to hack**, and a KHD does not solve every enemy TAG.

**Against MSV/Mimetism/defensive Fireteams:** start with **Crosier MSV2 HMG**, **Black Friar MSV2/Albedo** or a correct-range long gun; use **Disco Eclipse** for bypass rather than an even bigger frontal gamble. A Crux Albedo attack still needs a *favorable target and range*; don't mistake conditional MODs for guaranteed wins.

**Against TAG/HI without a cyber opening:** **Montesa FTO Blitzen20**, **Teutonic Panzerfaust18–19**, **Raveneye E/M12**, **Hospitaller E/Mitter26**, **Santiago E/M grenades**, **Holy Sepulchre AP HMG53** or **Seraph AP Spitfire72** give different cost/geometry options. E/M Immobilized/Isolated is control, **not** automatic destruction; keep a finisher and Engineer answer.

**Against first-turn DZ attacks and Impersonation:** place **Raveneye mine/Cybermine**, a different-angle **Fugazi/Sierra/Escort Auxbot**, and **Crosier/Black Friar ARO** to protect Commander, cheap Orders and Engineer. **Biometric Black Friar** is a specific profile, not an automatic Impersonator reveal; deployables and templates must obey state/trigger legality.

**Against objective-room holding / last-turn flips:** send **S2 foot Montesa, Trinitarian FO/KHD, Santiago/Hospitaller specialist** or a legal linked unit with finite Eclipse cover. Keep **Motorized Montesa** for open-lane far-flank switches, not a tight doorway. If the main attacker dies, a second mobile scorer **must already have enough Orders left**.

## Build and play checklist

1. **What wins the first 24–32-inch engagement?** Choose HMG/MULTI HMG/AP HMG, a correctly ranged Fireteam launcher, or a bypass route; don't put every main weapon in Spitfire/shotgun range.
2. **Where are ordinary Regular Orders generated and spent?** Count off-table losses and group assignments; add NCO/TacAware only where expected activations actually occur.
3. **What is the exact legal Fireteam?** Check **native minimum**, **required choice**, Counts-as, FTO restrictions, the one Haris, and whether Wildcards have an appropriate host.
4. **What prevents one hack from ending the game?** Pick Firewall/KHD, independent angle, actual Repeater-clearing tool, and Engineer/Palbot or Curator routes before committing multiple expensive HI/TAGs.
5. **Which two independent units score?** Identify the exact mission-eligible **FO/Hacker/Doctor/Engineer/Paramedic/Specialist**, one of them surviving if the primary gun or Knight team dies.
6. **What taxes the DZ assault?** Raveneye mine, Escort/Auxbot, cheap Flash/360º REM, camo specialist or dispersed defensive angle; do not spend every SWC point on an obvious gun castle.
7. **What happens on Turns 2–3?** Plan how **Montesa/Crusader/Trinitarian** crosses to the last objective, who holds the zone, and what happens if the expensive team is Broken or the LT is gone.
