## Overview

**Identity:** Druze concentrates unusually good **network projection, E/M threats, cheap mercenary trades and compact mixed Fireteams** into a force whose ordinary BS12 infantry are not elite gunfighters. Prefer making the enemy solve multiple small problems over investing in a single heroic link.

### Particularly strong or unusual Fireteams

- **Druze/Brawler mixed Duo/Haris/Core:** Combine **Druze KHD+Pitcher or E/M GL**, **Brawler Hacker/HRL/MSV2 sniper**, and optional support. The source demands **at least one Druze or Brawler required-source member**.
- **Clipper FTO Wildcard:** A legal linked **direct Missile Launcher (+1B)** option, **not the unlinked Guided missile profile**.
- **Fast Attack Duo:** **Bulleteer + Motorized Bounty Hunter** adds a fast small attack axis. **Mechanized Duo** uses its own **Anaconda/Scarface required-source** set.

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **Smoke:** **Yuan Yuan** and **Saito** among several cheap mercenary options; **Brawler MSV2** can exploit it.
- **Network and E/M:** **Druze Pitchers, Valerya, Hunzakut/Peacemaker Repeaters, E/M GL** provide a credible disruption game.
- **Limited Disco/Eclipse:** **Arslan Disco Baller** is not Tohaa's repeatable Mirrorball.
- **Chain of Command:** **Táowù 21/0** provides optional Lt insurance.
- **TAG choices:** **Anaconda, Scarface & Cordelia, Triphammer**; **no VH-class unit**. None merits an invented Top 3 TAG claim.
- **Weakness:** Expensive W1/BS12 link bodies, expensive TAG packages and network Orders compete with the basic Regular order pool.

## Tactical research — Druze Bayram Security (reviewed 2026-10-23)

**Evidence boundary:** This is a tactical interpretation of videos, podcasts, and reported games—not a replacement for the N5.3 Army profiles and canonical Fireteam chart above. Sources from 2022 (N4) and the December 2024 N5 launch can explain *why* a plan works, but their prices, hacking interactions, Fireteam bonuses, profile availability, and list codes are **not** authoritative in October 2026. The roster above remains verified to Army 7.26246.159 / N5.3.

### Executive decisions: choose the plan before the expensive pieces

| Plan | Starting package | Why / failure mode |
|---|---|---|
| **Network control + optional Guided** | Druze KHD with Pitcher; a second hacking source such as Valerya or Druze HD; Hunzakut FO/repeater; Fugazi/Peacemaker; **unlinked** Clipper Guided *if* investing in missiles | Multiple ways to establish a target or repeater let Druze fight without exposing expensive BS12 one-wound infantry. Do **not** spend orders laying repeaters and Spotlighting if you cannot exploit the marked target; bring direct guns and mission specialists too. [V1, V2, P1] |
| **Compact Fireteams + board denial** | Brawler HRL / MSV2 sniper with useful partner(s), Druze KHD or E/M GL, Diggers/Denma, cheap REMs | Brawler Sixth Sense and low price buy respectable reactive threats; Fireteam bonuses and templates punish opponents for advancing. Prefer a few useful Duos/Haris to an expensive Core built only to amplify one average-BS gun. [V1, V2] |
| **TAG-led direct attack** | Scarface & Cordelia **or** independent Triphammer/Anaconda; engineer support, a Pathfinder or Hunzakut specialist, cheap orders | Heavy armor/AP guns answer targets the light Druze/Bulleteer weapons struggle to remove. Scarface's entire **76–80 point** composite package competes with specialists and order generators; budget for his failure, not just his peak output. [V1, V2, V3, P1] |

**Key asymmetry:** The sectorial has impressive *tools* but relatively few premium face-to-face gunfighters: expensive, one-wound Druze/Brawlers with BS12 can trade sideways well, but do not reliably bully elite TAGs or hard AROs. Use smoke, mines, pitchers, E/M, hacking, AP weapons, and mission angles to create favorable fights rather than treating every linked shooter as a main attack piece. [V1, P1]

### Units and interactions worth building around

- **Druze KHD (23/0) + Pitcher:**
  - Good first hacker because it attacks enemy hackers and deploys the repeater network that enables the rest of the force
  - the HD (25/0.5) supplies non-killer hacking, so **KHD alone is not a Spotlight plan**. A Druze E/M grenade launcher (24/1) offers a different attack vector against high-value technological targets
  - spec-fire pressure costs SWC and Orders, so use it when direct attack is worse. [V1, P1]
- **Brawler HRL (15/1.5), hacker (16/0.5), MSV2 sniper (25/1.5):**
  - HRL + native Sixth Sense is a cost-efficient deterrent
  - the hacker broadens repeater-zone defence
  - MSV2 has an active smoke-shooting niche but becomes a costly reactive target without good range, cover and lane selection.
  - **Competing views:** V1's guest favors the cheap HRL
  - its host emphasizes the MSV2 MULTI sniper as an *active* smoke attacker, not a default exposed ARO model.
- **Valerya (19/0.5):**
  - WIP14 HD, Pitcher and Total Control (+1B) make her a lean specialist for hacking and anti-TAG duty.
  - **Counterview:** Druze HD/KHD or Brawler hacker can provide redundancy and other utility
  - Valerya is not an automatic include if the plan is neither hacking-heavy nor reliant on a Wildcard specialist. [V1, V2]
- **Hunzakut FO/repeater (19/0) or FO/D-Charges (15/0):**
  - Infiltrating Camo midfield mission presence, deployable repeater / FO access and mines make them disproportionately important
  - most of the rest of this sectorial starts farther from objectives. Their Irregular Orders mean they are not a substitute for the Regular order pool. [V1, P1]
- **Saito, Yuan Yuan, Denma, Diggers:**
  - Saito is a real independent close-attack/midfield threat
  - cheap Yuan Yuan bring smoke and flank pressure
  - Denma and Diggers threaten melee trades and can protect a team.
  - **Trade-off:** too many Irregular/Impetuous troops leave too few Regular Orders to operate the network, fireteams, or TAG. [V1, P1]
- **Lieutenant security:**
  - Cheap Brawler LT versus pricier Arslan LT is a real opportunity-cost decision. The Brawler is easier to hide
  - Arslan supplies a better equipped fighter and Disco Baller but becomes a tempting priority target. Táowù's Chain of Command option (21/0) is a separate insurance choice, not a requirement. [V1, P1]
- **Fiddler:**
  - Strong independent Engineer/mobility/JackBot tool
  - **FTO option 1 is not Fireteam legal in Druze** despite its name. Monstruckers are cheaper but Irregular Engineers
  - Cordelia belongs to the mandatory Scarface composite. Match engineer spend to what actually needs repairing.

### Disagreements to preserve rather than average away

1. **Guided as central win condition vs optional tool:** WIP12 [P1] (N4) and V1's launch analysis strongly emphasize saturation of Pitchers and missile pressure; some players reject that plan as expensive/uninteractive/fragile, and the later N5.3 chart specifically kills V1's **linked Guided Clipper** assumption. Keep unlinked Guided as one lane, not a universal core.
2. **Scarface investment:** V1's host and the Concilium list-builder like durable AP pressure; V1's veteran Druze guest and WIP12 guest question mandatory Cordelia's cost and how badly the rest of the list thins out. V3 agrees Scarface has useful NA2 role yet notes he's not a premier TAG-vs-TAG duelist.
3. **Fireteam size:** A larger Druze/Brawler team can improve a specific HRL/sniper's performance, but paying multiple medium-infantry models to buff a single BS12 one-wound attacker creates a brittle point concentration. V1 prefers flexible Duos/Haris for many lists; other build styles still legitimately use a larger core when mission/lanes justify it.
4. **Long-range defence:** Cheap Brawler Sixth Sense HRL versus pricier MSV2 MULTI sniper, and Sierra HMG versus 0-SWC Thunderbolt, are *mission and range* selections, not one profile objectively replacing the other.
5. **Old cross-sectorial advice:** PanO's REM-specific support characters and Qapu Khalqi's own TAG/Fireteam permissions **do not transfer** to Druze. Use [V4, V5] for movement, exposure, and target-priority lessons only.
