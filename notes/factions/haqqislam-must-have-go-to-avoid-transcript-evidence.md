# Haqqislam N5.3 — Must-have / Go-to / Avoid (YouTube + podcast evidence)

**Audit:** 23 October 2026. **Armies:** Vanilla Haqqislam, Hassassin Bahram, Qapu Khalqi and Ramah Taskforce. Roster, prices/SWC and Fireteam authority: [Vanilla](../../sectorials/haqqislam/haqqislam.md), [Hassassin](../../sectorials/haqqislam/hassassin-bahram.md), [Qapu](../../sectorials/haqqislam/qapu-khalqi.md), [Ramah](../../sectorials/haqqislam/ramah-taskforce.md), pinned N5.3 Army **7.26246.158** (September 2026). Videos/podcasts provide **specific players' preferences and explicit criticisms**, *not* measured inclusion rates. Current Army outranks spoken profile data, and **pre-October-2025 opinions are qualified if N5.2 changed the model**.

**Labels:** **Must/near-must** = an identified reviewer explicitly says nearly/every list or repeated independently defended list core, with exceptions noted. **Go-to** = reviewer establishes strong repeatable role but is neither always needed nor all simultaneously affordable. **Avoid/never** = presenter actually advises against the **specified profile or purpose**, with named competing opportunity cost; do not mistake disagreement or older footage for mathematical domination. **No supported veto** is the correct answer when none can be honestly defended.

## Vanilla Haqqislam

**Direct modern media:** [Robert Shepherd, *Vanilla Haqqislam faction focus*, November 23 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dmv4pfdTbc0) (MCP full transcript retrieved in this audit) and [Tactical Awareness Haqqislam Prime, October 13 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qlKwZjGWDDY) (transcript retrieved; **pre-October update**). [Current Vanilla strategy note](./vanilla-haqqislam.md) includes alternative builds but earlier research explicitly lacked full video transcripts—**this audit does not pretend those older source notes were presenter quotations**.

### Must-have / near-must
- **Saladin LT 39/0** — **high for a Shepherd-style army**. In the full post-update transcript Shepherd estimates Saladin around **95% of his builds**, then explicitly says **not an auto-include**. *Why*: post-October **Inspiring Leadership** makes cheap Ghazi, Daylami, Hunzakut and bikers contribute usable Regular Orders; Strategos L2, extra command resource and Coordinated Orders enable simultaneous advances, recovery of exposed warbands, smoke delivery and scoring. His vulnerable, expensive command profile is the price. **When to skip:** very few Irregular troops, unusually high EM/LT assassination exposure, a Hafza LT(+1)+Sekban NCO or other deliberate command alternative.
- **No other universal unit supported.** Saladin is a strategic choice, not proof Fiday, Ghazi or Mukhtar is mandatory.

### Go-to
| Profile | Why it repeatedly works | When to pass / source |
|---|---|---|
| **Ghazi Muttawi'ah Smoke/Jammer 5–8** | Extremely cheap screening and intuitive Jammer, Dogged, coordination with Saladin; makes even elite assault costly | Do not inflate the Irregular count without Saladin; Shepherd |
| **Daylami PF6 / Camo PF8** | Off-price ranged ARO and expendable minefield of threats; Saladin converts the Order contribution | Budget/reactive role, rather than all-out attack; Shepherd |
| **Mukhtar Red Fury MSV2 34/1** | Highly versatile speed, WIP14 specialist family, NWI/forward positioning and visor shooting | Other cheap gunfighters or TAG may own the active-turn budget; Shepherd + [existing Vanilla tactical note](./vanilla-haqqislam.md) |
| **Hunzakut FO15/19** | Starts at midfield objectives and projects threat/repeater for low points | Printed Irregular unless Saladin; Shepherd |
| **Fiday** | Impersonation attacks a support/LT/ARO weakness not always solvable by conventional guns | Requires favorable target and safe route; Shepherd |
| **Zeybek / Haytham, Janissary, Bokhtar, Maghariba, Shakush** | **Alternative attack engines**, not a shopping list; choose vehicle flying attack, hard gun, advanced mission or durability when required | Larger investment and different hacking/support risks; Shepherd |

### Never take / avoid
- **No defensible absolute unit veto from the post-N5.2 recordings.** Specifically, **do not mark Saladin alternatives, Asawira or other strong solo pieces as bad simply because the preferred Saladin engine exists**.
- **Strong, directly evidenced construction warning:** Shepherd warns **roughly 4–5+ Irregular purchases without conversion** can leave too few pooled Orders to execute a coherent active-turn plan. This is a **package failure**, not “never take Ghazi/Hunzakut.”
- **Saladin CoC insurance is not an automatic solution**: Shepherd explains that if Saladin is lost, CoC does *not* reproduce his Inspiring Leadership conversion, so protect him rather than overpaying for a successor assumed to fix the economy.
- **Edition check:** [October 2025 update](https://infinityuniverse.com/en/news/army-update-october-2025-part2) upgraded Saladin. The earlier Tactical Awareness Prime builds are **not** a valid counterexample to his later strength; they predate that addition.

## Hassassin Bahram

**Direct video sources:** [Robert Shepherd, *Hassassin Bahram faction focus*, April 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yHpAnRDw_E8) and [Tactical Awareness S3 Ep31 Hassassin Bahram](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=63MLOgyNONs), **both full MCP YouTube transcripts retrieved for this audit**. [Existing HB guide](./hassassin-bahram.md) has newer September 2026 loadouts and alternative sample list research; the Shepherd 2025 audio predates late-2025 balance updates, so **the video establishes his priorities, not the new ability/loadout legality**.

### Must-have / near-must
- **Ghulam NCO + Smoke GL15/1** — **Shepherd direct near-auto**: says he fields it in essentially every list. *Why*: baseline WIP14 Regular order, legal Ghulam Fireteam foundation, ranged linked Smoke access, NCO conversion of a normally unused lieutenant activation. **Exception:** an aggressively built list that deliberately omits Ghulam Fireteams / invests SWC elsewhere.
- **Barid KHD14/0 and HD16/.5** — **Shepherd direct personal default**: says typically one of each in every list. *Why*: WIP14, BTS3, two distinct anti-hacker/control jobs and cheap **Pitcher projection** inside Ghulam formations. Not proof of mandatory *two hackers* in a low-hackable enemy matchup.

### Go-to
| Profile | Why / matchup | Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| **Shujae E/M Minelayer(2)27/0** | N5.3 standout **Hidden Deployment, Mimetism(-6), FD+20, E/M traps, Flammenspeer and Specialist**; multiple independent ambush positions | Budget opening Order pool with hidden troopers; [HB guide](./hassassin-bahram.md), Shepherd's ambush doctrine |
| **Ghulam Doctor15 / Ghulam LT11** | Extremely cheap WIP14 basic support/command; linked recovery and fuel | Don't buy every Ghulam profile; Shepherd |
| **Asawira viral/AP/Doctor, ~32–38** | Reliable W2 BS14 close/midrange gun + durable scoring; Wildcard in varied short links | Competes with Fiday/Áyyār/Bokhtar for Orders; Shepherd, Tactical Awareness |
| **Daylami Camo PF8 / Ghazi5–8** | Reactive Order tax, smoke/Jammer/cheap templates, screen support | Printed Irregular not shared pooled Orders; Shepherd, Tactical Awareness |
| **Fiday / Al-Djabel** | Remove the specifically vulnerable enemy LT, specialist, support or key ARO | Not guaranteed necessary in every list; Shepherd, Tactical Awareness |
| **Áyyār38–40** | Exceptional expensive Hidden/Holoprojector/Specialist/Triangulated Fire solution to high negative shooting MODs | **Shepherd seldom takes two, sometimes only one** due to price; ambush specialist, not default filler |
| **Farzan CoC / Ragik / Bokhtar / Muyib** | LT protection, airborne attack, mobility or TA Fireteam module | Match plan and mission, not an obligatory fixed package |

### Never take / avoid
- **Ghulam HMG20/1 and Missile Launcher17/1.5** — Shepherd explicitly says he has **no interest in expensive Ghulam SWC guns**, preferring Ghulam **NCO/LT/Doctor utility** and genuine Asawira/Lasiq/Govad attack or ARO options. **Strong profile-level avoid as a general default**, not mathematical domination in every imaginable list.
- **Double Áyyār in an ordinary balanced list** — Shepherd explicitly rarely fields two and often zero because **~76–80pts** duplicates expensive ambush expertise; this is **avoid as default**, not strict veto: successful high-ambush builds exist. [HB guide](./hassassin-bahram.md).
- **Do not put all Shujae/Áyyār into Hidden Deployment by habit**: if unavailable to the starting order pool, the army can be stalled. **Do not claim Shujae + Áyyār universally mandatory**.
- **False “never take”: Sunduqbut**. One recording favors heavy marker defence while another list may skip it. The HB guide explicitly rejects declaring Sunduqbut universally poor. Some **early Tactical Awareness complaints about old hidden mine setups** are patch-sensitive.

## Qapu Khalqi

**Full MCP transcripts retrieved:** [Robert Shepherd QK, August 24 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rOy0x0BLuNU), [Tactical Awareness S3E30 QK, August 8 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n1vD-2YP0DM), **and [Tactical Awareness Ep35 N5.2 update, October 16 2025](https://open.spotify.com/episode/66UWrxKSTHcyWP3LJDM0H4)** (Spotify transcript, **~46:15–47:30**: speakers celebrate the improved Hafza LT, Odalisque Viral MMR/Marksmanship, Paramedic/BS+1B options; ~47:30–48:00 they remain somewhat skeptical of the newly buffed Al Fasid versus Azra'il). Repository also stores the [Shepherd QK transcript](../../rules/Youtube%20factions/Qapu%20Khalqi.txt). **The two original faction reviews PRECEDE the October 15, 2025 Haqqislam update; the third episode directly examines it.** See [Corvus Belli official update](https://infinityuniverse.com/en/news/army-update-october-2025-part2) and [current QK guide](./qapu-khalqi.md).

### Must-have / near-must
- **No recorded post-update every-list consensus.** Shepherd explicitly says even QK's newly strong **Iguana TAG** is not an automatic inclusion because 60+ points can preclude mission support, though it is his signature list centerpiece.
- **Hafza LT(+1 Order)19/0 + Sekban NCO** is an **excellent modern near-default architecture**, not a verified every-list quote: [Tactical Awareness Ep35, ~46:20](https://open.spotify.com/episode/66UWrxKSTHcyWP3LJDM0H4) specifically welcomes the upgraded Holomask Lieutenant, while recent N5.3 roster documents extra LT Order and Sekban universal NCO (e.g. 19pt BSG, 24pt Specialist, 27pt Red Fury); September-era [QK tactical analysis](./qapu-khalqi.md) recommends it. **Shepherd's August recording discussed older Hafza before the new LT(+1) profile existed**; DO NOT attribute endorsement of that precise package to him. An alternative Ghulam or Janissary Lt, heavy Link or 64–68pt Iguana branch remains credible.

### Never take / avoid (especially caution on dating)
- **Rouhani21 as a routine backline Doctor vs Ghulam Doctor15:** **Tactical Awareness explicitly says Ghulam Doctor outclasses the role by cost**. This is **contested, not a strict veto**: Rouhani's WIP15, BTS6, Wildcard status and improved survivability are real, and the hosts acknowledge that an army at the 15-unit cap can reasonably *upgrade* a cheap Doctor instead of adding a model. [Tactical Awareness QK](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n1vD-2YP0DM), [N5.3 roster](../../sectorials/haqqislam/qapu-khalqi.md).
- **Prepatch Odalisque / Kaplan / original Shakush condemnation is not an N5.3 never-take finding.** [Shepherd August 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rOy0x0BLuNU) called Odalisques/Kaplans expendable *before* official October update explicitly upgraded Odalisques dramatically. [Tactical Awareness August](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n1vD-2YP0DM) questions old Shakush versus Azra'il; N5.3 has changed capabilities and differing role/size. Current QK note highlights **Odalisque as high priority**: retain the disagreement's historical provenance, **reject old blanket veto**.
- **Modern profile-level absolute never:** **none securely established** from the dated English media and current roster. Buying full Druze or old-style huge links with no reason is an inefficient *package* warning, not a proven bad model.

## Ramah Taskforce

**Direct MCP transcript sources:** [Robert Shepherd Ramah focus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dvSrZU73hxA) (**post-October**), [burlesford Ramah review, August 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qfq4388HAzM) (**pre-update**), [Tactical Awareness Ramah episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p2omBWM-gr0) (review originally early October; episode republication later) and [current Ramah tactical/source chapter](../../sectorials/haqqislam/ramah-taskforce.md#community-tactics--n5-transcript-backed-research-2026-10-22). Original and current prices can differ; use pinned profiles.

### Must-have / near-must
- **Ghulam NCO + Smoke GL15/1** — **burlesford explicitly calls it an auto-include**: Ramah's long-range linked smoke delivery and LT-order NCO make its mobile shooters more reliable. Shepherd independently makes it a centerpiece of his small Ghulam/Zhayedan support duos. No actual Army obligation; omit when points/SWC or alternative delivery demands.
- **Fanous 7/0 (AVA3)** — **burlesford says he takes all three in the vast majority of lists**: three cheap Regular Orders, repeaters and Flash AROs create 15-body depth around expensive super-soldiers. Shepherd repeats that the high AVA is part of Ramah's competitive infrastructure, not necessarily an exact quota every mission.
- **None of the premium stars is verified universal.** Burlesford explicitly says **Tarik is *not* an auto-include** even though he is potent. Shepherd strongly recommends **Haytham Red Fury33/1.5**, but it chiefly shoots and is a large S7 No-Cover hackable vehicle, so he too does not establish universal tournament usage.

### Go-to
| Profile | Why | When it loses / sources |
|---|---|---|
| **Haytham Red Fury33/1.5** | MSV1, Mimetism(-6), Continuous Damage, advanced aerial angles; punishes enemy VHs | Must preserve reactive safety, No Cover; [Shepherd Ramah](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dvSrZU73hxA), [his Vehicle review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N6TYjMgU4ZM) |
| **Shihab Thunderbolt TR18/0 + Monstrucker SMG/Drop Bears12** | Cheap 360-degree reactive gun + repair/deployable screen; lower opportunity cost than sacrificing elite attack models | Vulnerable to favorable opponent FTf, requires repair reach; Shepherd says this is his preferred Group 2 style |
| **Zhayedan HMG31/1.5 or AP Sniper27/.5** | Marksmanship, Warhorse, Regeneration: better serious shooting than spending SWC on BS11 Ghulam | Still MI W1 and needs exposure control; Shepherd, [Ramah current guide](../../sectorials/haqqislam/ramah-taskforce.md) |
| **Khawarij MSV2 Mk12 NCO38/0 / Doctor30** | Super-Jump, unusual range geometry, CC and linked mission/recovery; order economy | 1W premium and higher points; burlesford / Tactical Awareness |
| **Tarik AP Spitfire49 / TacAware rifle46** | 2W, high reach/CC, BS Attack(-3), Super-Jump; striking second-turn shooter | Powerful, **not compulsory**, and LoL risk if LT; burlesford, Shepherd, TA |
| **Mukhtar Red Fury MSV2 and FO/Doctor team** | Cheap-ish forward specialist/gun counterpoint to Khawarij speed | **Direct dispute:** Shepherd praises efficient independent Mukhtar; burlesford now often prefers Khawarij. Neither is a never |
| **Maghariba / Shakush TAG, Namurr Specialist, Nahab** | Specific hard-gun, armored zone holding, long mission reach or airborne threat | Expense and weak defensive breadth when bought in combination; Shepherd/TA |

### Never take / avoid
- **Ghulam HMG20/1 and ML17/1.5** — **burlesford directly says he almost never takes the expensive Ghulam SWC guns**: they consume budget for BS11 guns while Khawarij, Zhayedan, Janissary or TAG alternatives do premium shooting much better. The Ghulam's cheap **NCO, Doctor, Hacker and LT** are the reason to buy them. As always, emergency budget/link cases exist.
- **Al Fasid HMG50/1.5, HRL44/1.5 as generic main guns:** *low-priority, **disputed**, not “never”*. Older tactical reviewers find them heavy for their impact against faster Khawarij/Tarik and Zhayedan. **The October 2025 update added Albedo and BS Attack skills**; an HRL/Minelayer/area-control matchup may now reward the full kit. Do not use an unpatched 2025 tier as a modern categorical veto.
- **Overstuffed Tarik+Khawarij+Mukhtar+TAG lists** starve Order count and perimeter defense. Shepherd calls Ramah demanding precisely because it must buy cheap reactive infrastructure around its exceptional but vulnerable attackers.
- **Do not blacklist Shakush or Mukhtar:** burlesford prefers some Janissary/Khawarij alternatives, while Shepherd sees viable niches. Their prices/roles differ.

## Worst-value, niche and proxy-first miniatures by army (2026-10-30)

**Buying question:** Which *complete units/miniatures* are the weakest, least broadly useful or most matchup-dependent **within each Haqqislam army**, and which should be **proxied rather than purchased**? This complements the preceding [whole-unit audit](#whole-unit-purchasing-audit--not-just-an-avoided-profile-2026-10-30) and **does not replace** its finding that no complete Haqqislam unit has a proven, unconditional all-profile ban. All judgments assume an ordinary **300-point N5.3** list, the [current September 2026 sectorial rosters](../../sectorials/haqqislam/) and current Fireteam charts. **“Rare” means not a normal first-choice purchase in the cited builds; published commentary is not representative pick-rate data.**

**How to read purchasing recommendations:** **Skip by default** = a comparatively strong, direct criticism of the *normal* full-unit role (but exceptions remain). **Proxy first** = an *inferred collection-priority judgment* for a legitimate but narrow/expensive unit, **not** a quote that it is bad. **Avoid profile only** = do **not** omit the rest of that unit's useful profiles. **One is enough** = AVA does not make duplicate sculpts compulsory. **Disputed** = the original media includes positive counterarguments or rules later improved the trooper; older negative remarks are never silently treated as post-N5.2 reviews.

**Proxy and purchase caveats:** Clearly disclose a proxy to the opponent; check current **Silhouette, base diameter, model count and team legalities** in Army. S2 infantry, larger HI, REMs, motorcycles, S7 aerial vehicles and TAGs are **not** automatically substitutes for one another. **Event proxy policy** depends on the current ITS season/organizer; an older season's text should not be cited as the present season without checking. For hobby collecting, aesthetic preference can independently justify a model even when its gameplay purchase priority is low.

**Short priority:** **Most direct default unit deferrals:** **Qapu Rouhani** if the job is only inexpensive Doctor coverage; **Ramah Al Fasid** if it is merely an expensive gun. **Strongest avoided *loadouts* rather than entire models:** **Hassassin/Ramah Ghulam HMG and Missile Launcher**, unsupported **Guided** missile REMs, and FTO variants with no legal link. **Best *proxy-first niche models*:** extra **Áyyār/Sunduqbut** (remember positive double-unit examples), **QK specialty mercenaries or alternative large TAG**, **Ramah Nahab/Hakim/Hortlak/extra armoured fighter**, and unused named character sculpts. **Never skip modern QK Odalisques because of a pre-buff recording.**
