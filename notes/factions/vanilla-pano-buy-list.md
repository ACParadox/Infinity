## Read this before ordering

- **FX for comparisons:** USD 1.00 = **CAD 1.3985**, Bank of Canada rate for **2026-10-29** (latest published when researched on Oct 30): [rate source](https://fincast.info/pairs/usd-cad). Values rounded to cents, **before shipping, FX-card spreads, GST/QST, duties, brokerage**.
- **Price evidence matters:** **V** means a linked retailer price was separately observed (with stock caveats); **U** means the price is drawn from the buyer's dated **USD** retailer catalogue Markdown, or a clearly marked older buyer lead—not an independently confirmed current offer. Missing product-specific URLs are not invented. All prices are per **listing/box**, not per miniature; CAD conversions are only estimates.
- [Miniatures Market (miniatures.market)](https://miniatures.market/) is **USD** and says it ships from several **non-EU warehouses** (precise origin/order unknown); its store may confirm stock only **after checkout**. [FAQ](https://miniatures.market/faq/) / [shipping](https://miniatures.market/ordering-and-shipping/). **It is not** the distinct US company [Miniature Market (miniaturemarket.com)](https://www.miniaturemarket.com/).
- [Ultimate Toy Zone](https://www.ultimatetoyzone.com/) individual product pages were not readable during review, but the repository now contains a [priced USD catalogue snapshot](ultimate-toy-zone-infinity-prices-usd.md); those prices are recorded as **U**, not confirmed checkout prices. Warehouse/shipping origin is unverified.
- Canadian fallback locations: [Tistaminis](https://tistaminis.com/) — **Hamilton, Ontario**; [401 Games](https://store.401games.ca/) — **Toronto/Vaughan, Ontario**; [Meeplemart](https://www.meeplemart.com/store/c/156-PanOceania-156.aspx) — **Toronto, Ontario**. These are store locations, **not** product stock at a specific branch.
- **Vanilla has no standard Fireteams.** FTO labels, Patsy's Fireteam Master, legacy Joan/Bolt/Helot links and old video recommendations **do not create a vanilla Fireteam**. AVA is a maximum, **not** a purchase target.
- Only one **Joan** (v1 or v2) and one **Maximus** (infantry Agent or TAG) are permitted by the roster's mutual-exclusion rules. One physical Joan is already owned.
- Base/silhouette, distinctness and organizer approval matter when proxying; a single owned model cannot appear twice in one list. **Cutter is S7**; your Tikbalang, Squalos Mk-II and Maximus are **S6**, so they are *not* visually/size-correct cutter replacements without a suitable S7 base/marker approved for casual testing.

## Skip / defer list (purchase-level, not “never field”)

1. **Skip duplicate already-owned REMs/support and starter armies**: four Dronbot chassis, two Armbots, two Mulebots/Palbots, engineer, doctor, Tech-Bee, Fennecs, Redeye, Joan, Tik, Squalos and Maximus already cover extensive army needs. Avoid filling AVA for its own sake.
2. **Skip additional Clipper, dedicated generic Fusiliers, extra Fennecs/Fireflies, second Redeye and third Karhu/Nisse** for vanilla collection efficiency; several are over AVA or redundant, and Clipper Guided is source-backed as a poor *default* without Targeted.
3. **Defer Cutter** despite its appeal: high-power S7 camo TAG is a **different role**, not strictly redundant, but three premium TAG chassis are already owned. It should be an intentional playstyle acquisition.
4. **Defer Aquila premium Strategos/Disco LT and Patsy** unless the particular NCO / visor / command plan fits. Separate HMG Aquila is still valid; don't mistake disputed premium **profiles** for a whole-unit ban.
5. **Defer Pilot-X + RacerBots, Nøkken, Dr. Priya Harper, infantry Optimate Agent Maximus, a second Drummer or duplicate premium Swiss/TAG** until actual repeated games justify dedicated models. These are proxy-first, **not factually unusable**.
6. **Do not buy Varuna Starter to field three Kamau in vanilla** or Reinforcements Beta to field Quinn/Shona as themselves. The models may still be cost-effective multi-use proxies if genuinely discounted.
7. **Do not buy a second Joan or Maximus to fill two roster lines**. The roster contains variant/character entries with a mutual-exclusion rule; the physical collection already includes one each of Joan and Maximus TAG.
