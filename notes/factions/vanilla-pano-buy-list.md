# Vanilla PanOceania — miniature buy list (N5.3, inventory-aware)

**Prepared:** 2026-10-30. **Army authority:** [current 51-entry vanilla PanO roster](../../sectorials/panoceania/panoceania.md), N5.3, Army 7.26246.158, verified 2026-10-15. **Buying rationale:** [vanilla tactics](vanilla-pano.md) and [PanO must-have/go-to/avoid purchasing audit](panoceania-must-have-go-to-avoid-transcript-evidence.md). **Physical ownership:** [Kestrel](../../inventory/kestrel.md), [Military Orders](../../inventory/military-orders.md), [other units](../../inventory/other-units.md), all dated 2026-10-30. This is a **buy list, not an additional owned inventory**: none of the suggested purchases is counted as owned.

## Read this before ordering

- **FX for comparisons:** USD 1.00 = **CAD 1.3985**, Bank of Canada rate for **2026-10-29** (latest published when researched on Oct 30): [rate source](https://fincast.info/pairs/usd-cad). Values rounded to cents, **before shipping, FX-card spreads, GST/QST, duties, brokerage**.
- **Price evidence matters:** **Verified** means an individually accessible vendor page/listing displayed the amount; **user lead** means the amount came from the buyer's research and **could not be independently confirmed on October 30**. Treat a user lead as a price to recheck, not a live quote. A number without confirmed currency has been **tentatively treated as USD** only when associated with the user's USD-shop research. Canadian stores state CAD explicitly.
- [Miniatures Market (miniatures.market)](https://miniatures.market/) is **USD** and says it ships from several **non-EU warehouses** (precise origin/order unknown); its store may confirm stock only **after checkout**. [FAQ](https://miniatures.market/faq/) / [shipping](https://miniatures.market/ordering-and-shipping/). **It is not** the distinct US company [Miniature Market (miniaturemarket.com)](https://www.miniaturemarket.com/).
- [Ultimate Toy Zone](https://www.ultimatetoyzone.com/) product pages were **not readable** during this review; its prices, currency, live stock and shipping origin are **not independently confirmed**. Its two Libertós URLs below remain usable buyer-provided leads, not verified listings.
- Canadian fallback locations: [Tistaminis](https://tistaminis.com/) — **Hamilton, Ontario**; [401 Games](https://store.401games.ca/) — **Toronto/Vaughan, Ontario**; [Meeplemart](https://www.meeplemart.com/store/c/156-PanOceania-156.aspx) — **Toronto, Ontario**. These are store locations, **not** product stock at a specific branch.
- **Vanilla has no standard Fireteams.** FTO labels, Patsy's Fireteam Master, legacy Joan/Bolt/Helot links and old video recommendations **do not create a vanilla Fireteam**. AVA is a maximum, **not** a purchase target.
- Only one **Joan** (v1 or v2) and one **Maximus** (infantry Agent or TAG) are permitted by the roster's mutual-exclusion rules. One physical Joan is already owned.
- Base/silhouette, distinctness and organizer approval matter when proxying; a single owned model cannot appear twice in one list. **Cutter is S7**; your Tikbalang, Squalos Mk-II and Maximus are **S6**, so they are *not* visually/size-correct cutter replacements without a suitable S7 base/marker approved for casual testing.
