# Shock Army of Acontecimento — N5.3 list-building and tactical research

**Research date:** 2026-10-19. **Purpose:** concise decisions with reasons and legitimate competing opinions. This is an **SAA** guide, not a Military Orders guide.

**Factual authority:** [current SAA roster / profiles / Fireteam chart](../../sectorials/panoceania/shock-army-of-acontecimento.md), Army `7.26246.158` (data changed 2026-09-03), N5.3. Podcast/video rankings are opinions; their old rules or costs do **not** override that reference. In particular, the Regular hacker **no longer has a grenade launcher** (it has Cybermine/FastPanda instead), and the SAA Regular Sapper sniper is **20 points/1 SWC**, not the older N4 configuration.

## In one minute

- **Identity:** unusually independent PanO combined arms: strong long-range BS guns, **WIP13–14 specialists**, cheap mines/Sapper defensive infrastructure, **Nagas/Dart**, AD Akalis, **Scylla hacking**, Eclipse from **Guarda's Auxbot**, and solid TAGs/REMs. More mission/position driven than simply rushing the opponent's DZ.
- **Best repeated construction:** inexpensive **Regulars** (LT, Sapper MSR and/or Minelayer/Sensor, hacker or paramedic) hold the home end; mobile **Bagh-Mari/ORC/Montesa or Bulleteer** link does work; **Pathfinder + TAG/REM**, **Naga/Dart**, Peacemaker and/or Akali give alternate scoring/attack routes.
- **Real weaknesses:** much of the faction depends on Mimetism, has modest BTS on cheaper pieces, and can struggle to finish close assaults after winning the long-range firefight. Obvious LTs, hackable TAGs/HI/REMs and limited high-quality non-TAG armor piercing are exploitable.
- **Do not confuse availability:** **no Vehicle-class (VH) troopers** in the pinned SAA roster. **Redeye is not an SAA option**, despite featuring in other PanO sectorial discussions. SAA **does** field **Tikbalang and Dragão TAGs** and several Dronbot/Armbot REMs.

## TAGs, Vehicles and REMs (cross-PanO research)

### TAGs — Dragão versus Tikbalang is a genuine disagreement

| TAG | What it solves | Who argues against it, and why |
|---|---|---|
| **Tikbalang 69/1.5; LT +1 Order 73/1.5** | BS15 HMG with Mimetism (-3), Climbing Plus, mine, TacAware and smaller S6 body: finds alternate attack angles and can retreat into safety. As solo **LT +1 Order** it can use its personal LT Orders for exceptional self-funding. **Historical Shepherd and TA** especially value practical mobility/deployment. | **Swiss Miss** questions whether a plain HMG earns 69–73 points versus tougher armored targets; **Dice Gods and TA** emphasize that a Dragão's HRMC breaks heavy armor more decisively. TAG LT risks Loss of Lieutenant if lost. **Personal LT Orders don't automatically move a Duo partner**; a two-model Fireteam may break if spent solo. |
| **Dragão 76/1.5** | BS15 **Hyper-Rapid Magnetic Cannon** is the roster's premium high-burst anti-armor answer. ARM8, continuous-damage immunity, TacAware and heavy-flamethrower extra burst defend the investment. **Dante, Swiss Miss and TA** emphasize why the actual gun matters when normal HMGs bounce off armor. | **TA hosts explicitly dissent**: only +7 points but bulky S7 with no Mimetism or climbing, harder to hide or flank, and can be outplayed by visibility MODs. **MetaChemistry** instead describes it as a real heavy long-range threat; choose it when the mission/opposition needs raw armor killing more than Tik's movement and MODs. |

**Repair/command package:** SAA **Machinist 17–20** is a Mimetism(-3) engineer and can join appropriate links; **Palbot 3** extends repair access. **Tech-Bee 9** supplies TAGCom **Dodge PH+3 / GizmoKit PH+1**, *not BS*. The TAG's own GizmoKit is not a replacement for a well-positioned engineer. Decide when paying for **Pathfinder TacAware 17** vs 14: the 3-point premium pays when the extra *team activation* actually saves orders.

**Vehicles:** SAA has **no VH entries**; don't copy **Redeye** (or other PanO flyers) into SAA lists. [Shepherd's PanO vehicle assessment](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N6TYjMgU4ZM) and [Dice Gods' aerial-vehicle discussion](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QscqX3hlUZU) are useful for *opponent threat assessment*, not SAA construction: flying side angles and Sensor can threaten exposed backfield pieces; flyers cannot rely on ordinary cover and can be vulnerable to hackers/long-range ARO. Both transcripts reviewed; no SAA vehicle was confirmed.

## Opposing advice worth preserving

1. **Sapper MSR:** **Shepherd (N4) / Dante / MetaChemistry** value near-terrain-independent, cheap Mimetism ARO; **Dice Gods Jan-2026** prefers putting the point/SWC elsewhere. Resolve from the table's DZ, mission and your other AROs.
2. **Regular firebase vs specialist assault:** **Shepherd's older formula** is mostly Regulars+Sapper with a mobile Bagh-Mari/engineer team; **Tactical Awareness's 2025 lists** instead explore mobile Montesa, mixed Bagh-Mari/Rao, double Guarda and even E/M-themed defense. Neither is a mandatory recipe.
3. **Dragão vs Tik:** **Swiss Miss / Dante** respect HRMC's decisive anti-armor punch; **TA** stresses the S7/no-Mimetism/no-Climbing opportunity cost against the smaller Tik. Opponent armor, terrain and anti-hacking plan decide.
4. **Bagh-Mari vs ORC vs Montesa:** **MetaChemistry** sees broad, useful Bagh-Mari as characteristic SAA opponents must prepare for; **TA** highlights their price per Wound; **Swiss Miss** finds many ORC purchases hard to justify against cheaper Montesa. These are different jobs (MSV/MOD control, durable gun, close defensive specialist).
5. **Dart and Naga spend:** **MetaChemistry/TA** see Dart and KHD Naga as legitimate stealth/objective threats; **Dante** dislikes several premium Naga guns; **Swiss Miss hosts** differ on Dart's opportunity cost. Avoid a compulsory expensive skirmisher package.
6. **Defensive vs aggressive list:** SAA can win by suppression **then scoring**, not always by running a TAG/Guard to the enemy DZ. **Shepherd's N4 assessment** remains a useful strategic concept, but its old roster/Fireteam numbers are non-authoritative in N5.3.

## Vision control — role/value update (23 October 2026)

- **Unique Eclipse route:** **Guarda de Assalto 40–44pts + synchronized Auxbot Eclipse launcher** is not a generic Smoke + visor package. It creates an MSV-proof crossing while the Guarda remains a BS15 active shooter; the synced delivery and base geometry cost Orders/positioning.
- **Value split:** choose Guarda when a real objective/gunfighter needs the crossing and the Auxbot can survive; otherwise cheaper Nagas/Dart/Peacemaker lanes and conventional shooting may do more per point. [Global comparison](../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).

### Mission specialist choices — N5.3 cross-game evaluation

An objective-heavy Acontecimento list needs **actual FO/Hacker/Engineer and Doctor** options on independent paths, plus a protected late closer rather than every point in Bagh-Mari/Regular ranged guns. **Pathfinder-like FO/Sensor REMs**, where present in this roster, give economical button and Camo-clearing utility, but compare them to any **infiltrating/Forward-Deploying Specialist** and the required Fireteam anchors. Do not import Vanilla PanO or Kestrel Wildcard permissions.

See [Mission Specialists / Button Pushers apex and value tiers](../list-building/mission-specialists-and-button-pushers-apex-value.md). This roster and its Fireteam chart are the final authority for exact options.

### Active-turn gunfighter cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Active-turn shooting choices:** **Bagh-Mari HMG30/1.5** supplies a BS13/MSV1/Mimetism(-3) **non-TAG** legal team gun, useful for Cover and Mimetism-heavy mid/long ranges. **Acontecimento Regular Spitfire16/1** is a very cheap second gun but lacks resilience for hard AROs. **Tikbalang HMG69/1.5** is the separate **TAG** purchase: BS15, Mimetism(-3), Climbing and TacAware, useful to make repeat strong shots at different angles. Buying a big TAG is worthwhile only when its unique geometry/durability solves a problem the two cheap guns cannot. [Separate TAG/non-TAG gunfighter tiers](../list-building/active-turn-gunfighters-apex-and-value.md).

### TAG tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Tikbalang** is the reliable mobile value assault: Climbing Plus, Mimetism, heavy gun and close defence. **Dragões** is a dedicated ranged anchor when the table rewards a heavy fire lane over mobility/marker safety. Compare both with the actual Moto-mecanizados Fireteam and Engineer cost, not chassis alone; do not use old N4 cost claims.

Cross-faction **apex vs points-value** comparisons, matchup table, and direct MCP-reviewed podcast/video sources: [N5.3 TAG field guide](../list-building/tags.md). Current profile and Fireteam legality above remain authoritative.

### Hacker and KHD tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**17/0.5 Acontecimento Regular HD/FastPanda** is inexpensive proactive network access. **Scylla 32/0.5 HD + Trinity and Devabot** combines an anti-heavy/anti-Hacker device with a companion Repeater/flamethrower; the EVO Scylla profile supplies *different* supportware, not ordinary HD attacks. **Naga 27/0 KHD or 29/0.5 HD + Deployable Repeater** trades cost for Camouflage/Infiltration, valuable if the mission requires a dangerous midfield console. **Choice:** Regular for general control, Naga for physical scoring, Scylla if synchronized threats and upgraded programs are actually useful; protect premium support from enemy Repeaters.

### N5.3 midfield area denial / speed bumps (tactical synthesis)

**13/0 Regular Sensor + Shock Mine Minelayer** is a cheap, 0-SWC **DZ** tripwire protecting against AD/Impersonation while supplying a Regular Order. **Naga 26/.5 Monofilament Minelayer** (or 25/.5 Shock) starts as a true midfield Camo/Infiltrator; **Peacemaker 24/.5 Auxbot-3B Minelayer** places FD mine/Repeater and close template at deployment. **Counterpoint:** the Regular is not a midfield skirmisher and a second mine may be redundant beside a Sapper sniper; Naga specialist versions are separate, and 27/0 active Peacemaker trades prepaid defense for attack.

### N5.3 DZ defense / anti-alpha-strike tactical choices

**Acontecimento Regular 13/0** (Shock Mine **Minelayer + Sensor + Triangulated Fire**) is the strongest low-SWC **deployment-zone** tripwire: a **Regular Order** and a prepaid mine guard the corner while Sensor can actively probe infiltrators. **Decision labels (not measured pick rates):**
