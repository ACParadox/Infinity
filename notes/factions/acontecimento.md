# Shock Army of Acontecimento — N5.3 list-building and tactical research

## Overview

**What Acontecimento does exceptionally well:** combine **premium PanO shooting** with **very cheap home-zone mines/Sensor, independent infiltrating specialists, and genuine flexible Fireteams**. Its unusual **Guarda de Assalto + synchronized Auxbot** brings **the only Eclipse Grenade Launcher in the game (N5.3)**: the *Auxbot* carries the launcher, projecting MSV-proof cover at range, while a mobile Tikbalang or Dragão can advance with support rather than consuming the entire list's plan.

### Whole-game standouts — apex versus value

**Sectorial tier / overall rank: B+ — #20 of 45**, from the [N5.3 faction and sectorial rankings](../sectorial-rankings-n5-3.md#overall-rankings) (last reviewed **13 November 2026**). SAA combines flexible PanO Fireteams, efficient mines/infiltrators, Tikbalang support and a unique Eclipse-launcher Auxbot; it has strong individual tools without an S-tier all-comers ceiling.

### Particularly strong or unusual Fireteams

- **Moto-mecanizados Duo:** a legal **Tikbalang or Dragão** anchor plus Wildcard **Pathfinder TacAware** combines long-range firepower with FO/Sensor/Repeater mission reach. The source required-choice set also includes motorized Montesa and Bulleteer; the exact combination matters.
- **Acontecimento Core/Haris:** a required-choice **Bagh-Mari or ORC** can travel with **SAA Machinist (counts as Bagh-Mari)**, eligible **Montesa FTO** or **Bulleteer**. Strong when the same squad contains both a gun and the specialist who exploits the cleared lane.
- **Forward Montesa Duo:** **two foot Montesas**, potentially **Forward Deployment (+20) Red Fury 32/1.5** plus **Paramedic 26/0**. This is a forward-scoring assault team, not the motorized model.
- **Regulars Core/Haris:** a cheap **Regular** anchor can add limited **Bagh-Mari, ORC, Machinist or Beasthunter FTO** muscle without spending five premium-unit prices.

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **Unique Eclipse Grenade Launcher:** the Guarda's synchronized **Auxbot** carries the **only Eclipse Grenade Launcher in the game (N5.3)**, allowing projected Eclipse that blocks even MSV sight. The weapon belongs to the Auxbot, not the Guarda, and is distinct from thrown Eclipse Grenades or Disco Ballers. It is not a faction-wide supply of cheap normal Smoke and has significant delivery cost.
- **Unusual PanO midfield mix:** **Naga** Camo/Infiltration profiles and **Dart** give independent objective and denial paths rather than dependence on a Fireteam escort.
- **Two TAG roles:** **Tikbalang** trades on Mimetism and climbing mobility; **Dragão** contributes an HRMC anti-armor gun. Neither is automatically strongest across all tables.
- **No aerial Vehicle (VH):** no **Redeye** in SAA; motorized Montesa is HI, not VH.
- **No cheap Disco Baller/Eclipse package:** the SAA roster has **no Disco Baller**. Its only projected Eclipse comes through the synchronized **Guarda de Assalto Auxbot**, requiring **40–44 points** for the full HI/peripheral package, unlike inexpensive Disco carriers in other PanO sectorials. The unique Eclipse Grenade Launcher is a stronger delivery method in some situations, but not a cheap disposable tool.
- **No MSV2:** **Bagh-Mari and Dart provide MSV1**, but SAA has **no MSV2** (or MSV3) unit to directly challenge high-Mimetism targets at that level; it must use Sensor, Triangulated Fire, positioning, templates, Eclipse, or favorable gunfights instead.
- **No ultra-cheap disposable defense comparable to Jackals or Helots:** **Regulars 10/0** and **Regular Sensor/Minelayer 13/0** are excellent *Regular* cheerleaders/home-zone defenders; **Beasthunter FTO 9/0** and **Peacemaker + Auxbot 23/0** add different defensive layers. SAA lacks **Kestrel's 6-point Jackal FTO** and **Varuna's 10-point Helot FTO** style of highly disposable ARO/speed-bump spam, and does not get NCA's **15-point Auxilia + Auxbot** combination.
- **Fewer singular, exceptionally high-ceiling threat profiles:** no equivalents to a **Swiss Guard** hidden heavy gun, **Cutter** hidden TAG, **Redeye** aerial Vehicle, **Kestrel Hetkari** heavy gunfighter, or **Crux Knight** specialized assault/ambush profile. This is a *breadth and ceiling* comparison, **not** an assertion that SAA has weak conventional attackers: **Tikbalang, Dragão, Guarda, Bagh-Mari, ORCs and Bulleteers** are legitimate high-performance guns. Some missing tools are also absent in other PanO sectorials.
- **Pitcher access is expensive and concentrated:** **Scylla 32/0.5** (or the **37/0.5 FTO + Charybdis FTO package**) is SAA's **only printed Pitcher carrier**, versus NCA **Blocker 15/0** or MO **Order Sergeant FTO HD 18/0.5**. The Regular FastPanda and SAA's native/deployable Repeaters help project hacking but are not equivalent to an inexpensive ranged Pitcher.
- **Command insurance exists:** **Agnes CoC** can protect a fighting LT; **Kirpal Singh CoC** depends on arrival timing if off-table.
- **Chart restrictions matter:** **Beasthunter FTO** has an SAA Fireteam row but **Clipper FTO does not**. [Canonical SAA chart](../../sectorials/panoceania/shock-army-of-acontecimento.md#canonical-fireteam-chart).

## In one minute

- **Identity:** unusually independent PanO combined arms: strong long-range BS guns, **WIP13–14 specialists**, cheap mines/Sapper defensive infrastructure, **Nagas/Dart**, AD Akalis, **Scylla hacking**, Eclipse from **Guarda's Auxbot**, and solid TAGs/REMs. More mission/position driven than simply rushing the opponent's DZ.
- **Best repeated construction:** inexpensive **Regulars** (LT, Sapper MSR and/or Minelayer/Sensor, hacker or paramedic) hold the home end; mobile **Bagh-Mari/ORC/Montesa or Bulleteer** link does work; **Pathfinder + TAG/REM**, **Naga/Dart**, Peacemaker and/or Akali give alternate scoring/attack routes.
- **Real weaknesses:** much of the faction depends on Mimetism, has modest BTS on cheaper pieces, and can struggle to finish close assaults after winning the long-range firefight. Obvious LTs, hackable TAGs/HI/REMs and limited high-quality non-TAG armor piercing are exploitable.
- **Do not confuse availability:** **no Vehicle-class (VH) troopers** in the pinned SAA roster. **Redeye is not an SAA option**, despite featuring in other PanO sectorial discussions. SAA **does** field **Tikbalang and Dragão TAGs** and several Dronbot/Armbot REMs.

**Vision-control choices:**

- **Guarda de Assalto (40–44 points) + synchronized Eclipse Auxbot:** creates an MSV-proof crossing while the Guarda remains a BS15 active shooter; delivery geometry, Orders and Auxbot survival matter.

## TAGs, Vehicles and REMs (cross-PanO research)

### TAGs — Dragão versus Tikbalang is a genuine disagreement

| TAG | What it solves | Who argues against it, and why |
|---|---|---|

**Repair, command and REM support:**

- **Machinist (17–20 points):** Mimetism (-3) engineer with access to appropriate Fireteams.
- **Palbot (3):** extends the engineer's repair reach.
- **Tech-Bee (9):** TAGCom **Dodge PH +3 / GizmoKit PH +1**, *not* a BS boost.
- **Pathfinder Tactical Awareness (17 vs 14):** the 3-point premium is worthwhile only when its extra activation saves real Orders.

The TAG's own GizmoKit is not a substitute for a well-positioned Engineer.

**Vehicles:** SAA has **no VH entries**; don't copy **Redeye** (or other PanO flyers) into SAA lists. [Shepherd's PanO vehicle assessment](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N6TYjMgU4ZM) and [Dice Gods' aerial-vehicle discussion](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QscqX3hlUZU) are useful for *opponent threat assessment*, not SAA construction: flying side angles and Sensor can threaten exposed backfield pieces; flyers cannot rely on ordinary cover and can be vulnerable to hackers/long-range ARO. Both transcripts reviewed; no SAA vehicle was confirmed.

## Battle-report-derived army lists and Fireteam ideas (2026-11-03)

These are **modules for a new list**, not complete 300-point Army exports. The underlying games used older N5/ITS packets; **the [current SAA Fireteam chart](../../sectorials/panoceania/shock-army-of-acontecimento.md#canonical-fireteam-chart) controls present-day eligibility**, especially foot Montesa FTO versus non-FTO.

### Three distinct constructions to test

- **Regular defensive Core (3–4):** the [#149 Last Launch list](../battle-reports/141-150-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) brought **two Regular Sapper MULTI Snipers**, a **Regular Paramedic** and **Agnes Chain of Command**. This is a **four-body Core candidate**, *not* a confirmed four-model starting link: the narrator proposed Agnes as fourth. It fits a mission where cheap DZ ARO, a real specialist and command insurance are more valuable than a fifth gun. The same game exposed the cost of repeatedly shooting a regenerating enemy when hacking was available.
- **Bagh-Mari mobile gun-and-repair Haris:** #149 included **Bagh-Mari HMG + Stephen Rao + SAA Machinist/Emitter Turret**. The host discussed a Duo or Haris but did **not** certify the deployed composition. As a **current-chart Haris option**, use a Bagh-Mari anchor in the *Acontecimento* family, Rao as its permitted Wildcard and Machinist as the chart's Bagh-Mari-counting member; keep the Regular Core separate. #116 additionally explicitly described a **Bagh-Mari Lieutenant + Machinist Duo**, providing a smaller version.
- **Foot Montesa medical attack:** the [#116 Outbreak report](../battle-reports/111-120-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) fielded **two foot Knights of Montesa** and explicitly discussed a **medical/scoring Duo**; the transcript does **not confirm that they stayed linked throughout the game**. They were backed by **Guarda de Assalto + Eclipse Auxbot**, **Dart**, **Akali Boarding Shotgun**, cheap **Fugazi** and **Agnes**. This is especially relevant to Doctor/Paramedic-scoring scenarios; the Auxbot is a **Peripheral**, not a third Duo member.
- **Five-body Montesa combined-arms *proposal*, not a played Core:** #116 discusses **ORC TinBot + 2 foot Montesa + Pathfinder + Scylla** (five). To build that composition under the pinned N5.3 chart, the Montesas **must use the specified FTO option**, Scylla **the FTO Wildcard profile**, and an ORC must anchor the *Acontecimento* family. The report does **not** prove that all five were activated together; the ordinary **two-foot-Montesa Duo** is a different formation.

**List-selection lesson:** pick **one defensive Regular Core and one Bagh-Mari/Montesa attack Haris or Duo**, then allocate points to the *independent* Akali/Dart/Guarda and medical/recovery infrastructure that the mission actually needs. Do not mistake two separate suggested configurations in #116 or #149 for simultaneous played Fireteams.

## Opposing advice worth preserving

1. **Sapper MSR:** **Shepherd (N4) / Dante / MetaChemistry** value near-terrain-independent, cheap Mimetism ARO; **Dice Gods Jan-2026** prefers putting the point/SWC elsewhere. Resolve from the table's DZ, mission and your other AROs.
2. **Regular firebase vs specialist assault:** **Shepherd's older formula** is mostly Regulars+Sapper with a mobile Bagh-Mari/engineer team; **Tactical Awareness's 2025 lists** instead explore mobile Montesa, mixed Bagh-Mari/Rao, double Guarda and even E/M-themed defense. Neither is a mandatory recipe.
3. **Dragão vs Tik:** **Swiss Miss / Dante** respect HRMC's decisive anti-armor punch; **TA** stresses the S7/no-Mimetism/no-Climbing opportunity cost against the smaller Tik. Opponent armor, terrain and anti-hacking plan decide.
4. **Bagh-Mari vs ORC vs Montesa:** **MetaChemistry** sees broad, useful Bagh-Mari as characteristic SAA opponents must prepare for; **TA** highlights their price per Wound; **Swiss Miss** finds many ORC purchases hard to justify against cheaper Montesa. These are different jobs (MSV/MOD control, durable gun, close defensive specialist).
5. **Dart and Naga spend:** **MetaChemistry/TA** see Dart and **Naga KHD 27/0** as legitimate stealth/objective threats. [**Swiss Miss**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hlmVXGHskS4&t=3420s) calls the **Naga Monofilament Minelayer** gimmicky as a default; [**Dante**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GKR_cd3oEFE&t=2370s) prefers Peacemaker Minelayer and Naga KHD/utility. Mono is **#13/A conditional apex** in cross-game denial, **not Top 10**; reserve it for a predicted TAG/HI forced crossing, not routine scoring or broad area control. Swiss Miss hosts also disagree about Dart's cost. Avoid a compulsory expensive skirmisher package.
6. **Defensive vs aggressive list:** SAA can win by suppression **then scoring**, not always by running a TAG/Guard to the enemy DZ. **Shepherd's N4 assessment** remains a useful strategic concept, but its old roster/Fireteam numbers are non-authoritative in N5.3.
