## Army-specific buying verdict

Ramah can reuse exactly **one Warcor and one Beasthunter**, but no official Ghulam, Khawarij, Mukhtar, Namurr, Zhayedan, Tarik or Janissary is recorded owned. Begin with Ghulam smoke/NCO and one versatile attacker, then remotes and specialists. The classic 10-model Haqq Action Pack provides **8/10 Ramah-legal models**: Fiday and Ragik are **not** Ramah. Ramah Expansion Alpha offers three real Ramah figures (Hunzakut, Hortlak and Al Fasid), but two of those are specialty pieces rather than automatically required.

One physical figure can represent one body in a particular list, and peripheral/turret/Jackbot/Nasmat/Scarface+Cordelia models need their own correctly sized representations. Event policy may limit proxies.

## Skips and cross-sectorial model reuse

The tactical source specifically criticizes **Ghulam HMG/ML** as default value versus specialist/Ghulam NCO; this is profile-only. **Al Fasid** pure second gun is contested, not forbidden—HRL/Minelayer and recent buffs offer a niche. Haytham is a **VH aircraft**, not a TAG, and must have credible S7 aircraft representation. Don't buy all TAG/aircraft choices before lists.

**Proxy first** for low-play-frequency characters, extra expensive TAGs, optional aircraft and second attack pieces; do not buy all AVA models to complete the collection. Special/limited editions are collector options rather than required army rules.
