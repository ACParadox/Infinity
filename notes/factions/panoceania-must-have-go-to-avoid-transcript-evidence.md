# PanOceania N5.3 — Must-have, go-to, and avoid picks (transcript-evidenced)

**Review:** 2026-10-23. **Authority:** [pinned PanO sectorial Army/Fireteam references](../../sectorials/panoceania/) (Army 7.26246.158, Sept 2026). The seven [tactical faction notes](./) and MO roster research were compared with the direct YouTube/Spotify MCP transcripts linked below. **Only attribute a recommendation to a presenter when they actually say it.** Older footage is opinion and does not overrule N5.3 prices, profiles, AVA or Fireteams.

**How to read:**
- **Must-have** = author **explicitly describes** near-universal pick, or multiple transcripts repeatedly favor it; label **single-reviewer** where unsupported as a consensus. When no unit meets this bar, say **none established**. These are NOT tournament inclusion statistics.
- **Go-to** = explicitly praised as good in broadly relevant roles, though architecture/missions choose the actual loadout.
- **Avoid / near-never** = direct presenter condemnation *of that profile/use*, not a blanket assertion the whole unit is unusable. **Disputed** marks another published view. “No-chart FTO” means **cannot use an implied link**, not that the weapon/model is forbidden.
- Costs are all-in **points/SWC** from pinned local roster when shown.

## 1. Vanilla PanO — no verified universal locked unit

- **Must? No single unit proven indispensable.** [WIP12 #177 KingButt](https://open.spotify.com/episode/5YoQVh0YvxS2DCtIIGyskl) favors active Joan/Cutter/Magistrate and Peacemaker/Pathfinder infrastructures, yet says Cutter is no longer his obligatory staple after leaving Varuna. [Dante + Tristan vanilla N5 reaction](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qp458fqYq_s) praise Fugazi and Peacemaker but do not establish one mandatory model.
- **Go-to:** **Fugazi7** (cheap Regular+Repeater+Flash), **Joan v2** when paying for the LT economy, **Magistrate CoC** when protecting an aggressive LT, **Peacemaker with Auxbot** for a forward mine/template/repeater gate; **Swiss/Tik/Cutter** remain alternative expensive win-condition shooters. **Why:** complementary Orders, deployment and asymmetric attack vectors, not three premium shooters asking for the same pooled Orders. [WIP12 #177](https://open.spotify.com/episode/5YoQVh0YvxS2DCtIIGyskl) · [Dante/Tristan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qp458fqYq_s).
- **Avoid (explicit opinion):** **Clipper Guided** without reliable Targeted: Dante/Tristan say they would not normally use missile bots in Vanilla because it is not naturally a Guided faction. **Counter:** a custom repeater/Spotlight strategy is possible; this is not a claim that Guided is illegal. [Dante/Tristan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qp458fqYq_s).
- **Critical correction:** WIP12 #177 discusses **Joan Haris** based on an older Army version. **Vanilla has NO standard Fireteam chart in pinned N5.3**, so do not use old Haris claims as a present-day justification. [Current roster](../../sectorials/panoceania/panoceania.md) · [vanilla tactical analysis](./vanilla-pano.md).

## 2. Kestrel Colonial Force

- **Must/near-must (not unanimous):** [Irregular Order #52 (September 2026)](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4RNxiaI1k5xXBVzVHxwnB9) speakers explicitly recommend **Magistrate LT and CoC with linked 6pt Jackals in their own lists**, to convert cheap Irregular Jackals into Regular Orders and insure the revealed Strategos LT. [Robert Shepherd Kestrel focus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8lyUk12Ed28) calls the **Magistrate + two Jackals =31pt** package extremely efficient but explicitly says **Tikbalang LT can displace Magistrate**. Thus *strongest repeated default*, not universal rule.
- **Go-to:** **Fennec FO/Disco/Sensor13** for cheap scoring+visibility+Camo counter; **Jackal E/M GL Minelayer11/.5** for cheap pre-deployed denial; **Tik LT** for self-funded solo gun; **Hetkari/Griffin** for core active/ARO piece; **Pathfinder TA17** as useful Duo partner; **Banshee** for cheap unexpected AD threat. [Shepherd](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8lyUk12Ed28) · [Irregular Order #52](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4RNxiaI1k5xXBVzVHxwnB9).
- **Avoid:** **Fennec Lieutenant10** is a profile Shepherd says players will "almost never take" because better command packages exist; **Drummer expensive W1 controllers** are described as costly vs the benefits from multi-body bots. [Shepherd](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8lyUk12Ed28). **Opposing:** [Irregular Order #52](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4RNxiaI1k5xXBVzVHxwnB9) reports meaningful Drummer/Sierra/hidden-ARO experiments; don't mark all Drummers as unplayable. The **FO + Drumbot21** is a different more economical profile. [Kestrel tactical note](./kestrel.md).

## 3. Military Orders

- **Must-have, presenter explicitly:** [Shepherd MO, July 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JrMTA91V7wc) says **Knight of Montesa FTO Paramedic20** is high value and he would put one in essentially every list: two Wounds, ARM4/BTS6, Mimetism, BSG, Blitzen, +1 Special Die, Specialist and legal Wildcard. Not the same as a motorcycle or FD foot profile. This is a **speaker-backed auto-include**, not observed universal tournament use.
- **Go-to:** **Trinitarian FO/KHD** for infiltrating mission work; **Teutonic Specialist19 or TinBot19** for cheap durable firefighting; **Santiago KHD/TinBot31** for a multifunction Fireteam specialist; **Knight Commander LT(+1)24 + NCO** for command economy; **De Fersen Hacker42/.5** when elite combat hacking matters. [Shepherd](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JrMTA91V7wc) · [Tactical Awareness Military Orders Redux](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RP6Uyyc447k) · [current roster](../../sectorials/panoceania/military-orders.md).
- **Never/avoid, explicit and strong:** Shepherd says **not to take Knight of Justice HD/Pitcher45/.5** when an 18pt linkable Order Sergeant supplies cheap Pitcher and De Fersen supplies the better offensive Hacker. **Counterargument:** the KoJ is W2 ARM5 BTS9 and can combine a combat-ready Wildcard with a Pitcher; this matters in a narrow mixed-role plan, but does not erase Shepherd's direct negative judgment. He also regards **Hospitaller HMG** as a less attractive solo gun than some competing options. [Shepherd](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JrMTA91V7wc).

## 4. Neoterran Capitaline Army

- **No single statistically confirmed must-have.** [Shepherd NCA Jan 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axa19k7qE1E) calls **Fugazi AVA3**, **Auxilia/Auxbots**, cheap **Tech-Bee**, and support pieces exceptionally efficient at supplying enough Orders for the powerful guns. This supports a *cheap infrastructure must-have role*, not necessarily a fixed individual unit. [WIP12 #201 with Trent](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3FT1No7zFHzpj7WC63hcCb) repeatedly builds around TAG+Auxilia/support.
- **Go-to:** **Auxilia15 / FO18** (cheap synchronized screen), **Blocker Specialist/Pitcher15** (Sensor, scoring and network); **Uhlan Feuerbach70 / aggressive80**, **Swiss** (hidden alternate ARO/attacker), **Squalos NCO + Pathfinder TA** (Order-efficient scoring duo), **Bulleteer** (cheap mod-stacking offense). [Shepherd](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axa19k7qE1E) · [WIP12 #201](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3FT1No7zFHzpj7WC63hcCb).
- **Avoid, explicitly:** [Shepherd](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axa19k7qE1E) says **Fusilier SWC guns (HMG/ML/sniper)** and usually **Fusilier specialists/Hacker** should not be taken—other NCA units handle those tasks better. Plain Fusilier **LT + decoy** remain correct. In particular, **Blocker Hacker19/.5** is preferred to Fusilier Hacker on capability, though **Blocker Specialist15** is the actual Pitcher loadout in September 2026; do not repeat obsolete claims that Hacker19 carries Pitcher.

## 7. Svalarheima's Winter Force (SWF)

- **Strongest near-auto from one presenter:** [Swiss Miss — WinterFor N5 tier list](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-pVR0OSVP4w) says they would **almost always take a Peacemaker with Auxbot**, emphasizing the forward Repeater, mine/flamethrower, nuisance ARO and route denial. **Fugazi7 AVA3** is also called S-tier, but **explicitly not always 3**. This is a documented reviewer preference, not a universal all-player rule.
- **Go-to:** **Karhu Feuerbach34/1.5** (NCO, MSV1+Mimetism for favorable face-to-face shooting), **Karhu Engineer25** (repair/specialist as team partner), **Vargar FO+Disco24 / FO HRL22** (Albedo and mission-compatible screen), **Blade FTO Engineer23** (TacAware and Fusilier-team utility), **Kyle Hawkins Specialist26–32**, **hidden Locust Specialist24**, **Jotum82** as optional TAG bull, not mandatory. [Shepherd WinterFor focus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oHeTzuV9WLY) · [Swiss Miss tier list](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-pVR0OSVP4w).
- **Avoid, explicit:** [Shepherd](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oHeTzuV9WLY) says **Fusilier HMG, ML and Sniper** are basically *never* the WinterFor purchase: buy Fusilier LT/decoy or occasional Paramedic instead and put serious guns on Karhu/other higher-quality troopers.
- **Low-priority/contested, not never:** [Shepherd](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oHeTzuV9WLY) finds **Bøyg44** difficult to justify against significantly cheaper Vargar Albedo+Disco, although he concedes its armor/extra wound and Beta-team potential; [Swiss Miss](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-pVR0OSVP4w) finds **unlinked Pathfinder TA17** C-tier, whereas Shepherd calls its Sensor/FO and personal Order useful despite no link access. **Neither is a guaranteed bad purchase.**

## Practical verdict

There are **only four direct source-backed near-universal assertions worth repeating as such**: **MO foot Montesa FTO20** (Shepherd), **Kestrel Magistrate+cheap Jackals** (Irregular Order speakers, with Tik LT exception), **Varuna Zulu KHD26** (Dante), and **SWF Peacemaker+Auxbot** (Swiss Miss). In **Vanilla, NCA and SAA**, the recording evidence supports **a highly efficient *role or package*** much more strongly than any individual compulsory profile.

The strongest no-default profile rejection examples backed by an actual recording are **MO KoJ Hacker45 (Shepherd)**, **NCA Fusilier SWC guns/Hacker (Shepherd)**, **SWF Fusilier premium guns (Shepherd)**, **Kestrel Fennec LT (Shepherd)** and **Varuna Clipper (Swiss Miss; low-tier, not literally unplayable)**. For SAA the negative evidence is **weak/conditional**. Apply each to the named loadout, not every profile sharing its unit name.

## Worst-value and rare/circumstantial picks — proxy-before-buy guide (2026-10-30)

**Question answered:** Which *entire miniatures* deserve no immediate purchase, which **specific profiles** should be avoided despite the unit having good uses, and which useful-but-rare units are best represented by an existing model? This expands — and does **not** overturn — the whole-unit purchasing audit above.

**Decision labels (not measured pick rates):**
- **Avoid profile** = sourced criticism of a particular loadout or application; **do not infer that the whole sculpt is bad**.
- **Proxy first** = *inference* from costly/redundant/narrow role, limited list demand or explicit published criticism. Not an assertion that a video presenter called every profile terrible.
- **One is enough** = a duplicate miniature is lower priority than its AVA suggests.
- **Specialist build** = deliberately purchase only for its unique tool or a list actually tested. **Strong / contested** identifies disagreement; never equate a rare pick with an unusable one.

The source judgments below are anchored in the [PanO faction transcript audit above](#1-vanilla-pano--no-verified-universal-locked-unit), [Kestrel](kestrel.md), [NCA](nca.md), [Varuna](varuna.md), [SAA](acontecimento.md), [WinterFor](svalarheima.md) and the [current MO roster research](../../sectorials/panoceania/military-orders.md). Source labels are **Direct** (recorded reviewer criticism), **Contested** (other reviewers disagree) or **Inference** (purchase-priority conclusion from those reviews and N5.3 roster). The evaluation is for ordinary **300-point N5.3** lists, not narrow custom missions.

**Proxy legality, silhouette and clarity:** A gaming proxy still represents a **real legal current Army entry and its actual profile**. A weapon variant usually needs **no second miniature**. Verify current *silhouette, base diameter, quantity and peripheral count* against the roster before substituting: a TAG, vehicle, bike, REM or S2 soldier is **not automatically physically interchangeable**. Clearly tell your opponent which sculpt represents which troop; avoid visually identical proxies for several different units where there could be confusion. For official events, check the current [ITS Season 18 rules/download](https://experience.corvusbelli.com/en/infinity/its) and the organizer; previous [ITS17 proxy provisions](https://downloads.corvusbelli.com/infinity/organized-play/its-rules-season-17-en-v1.0.2.pdf) allowed Corvus Belli stand-ins with the correct base and disclosure but did not allow other manufacturers. **Season 18's specific proxy paragraph was not independently retrievable for this audit**, so don't present an older season's wording as verified 2026 law.

**Final collection principle:** *(1)* buy common roles you actually use; *(2)* avoid the explicitly poor **profile** without blacklisting a useful troop; *(3)* **proxy** a named single-purpose unit or a second high-cost model for several games; *(4)* purchase its own miniature only if it repeatedly earns a distinct role or you want the sculpt for hobby reasons. **“Rare” and “never worth taking” are intentionally not synonyms.**
