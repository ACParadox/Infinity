# FRRM / Mérovingienne — List-Building and Tactical Guide (Infinity N5.3)

## Overview

**What FRRM does exceptionally well:** it combines **infiltrating one-use-Camo Métro roadblocks, non-Hackable Tactical Awareness Moblots, mixed-range MSV/Viral support and surprising off-axis attack**. The most successful architecture buys positioning, repeatable specialist work and multiple arrival angles rather than relying on superior baseline BS.

### Particularly useful Fireteams

- **Loup-Garou–Apache Haris — unusually dense cheap utility:**
  - **Loup-Garou Smoke GL 17/0** for ranged Smoke and Riotstopper.
  - **Loup-Garou Viral Marksman 24/0.5** for Marksmanship/Viral fire.
  - **Apache FTO 14/0** for a close-assault follow-through.
  - **55 points / 0.5 SWC** if the route and targets fit; the Smoke does not automatically defeat MSV.
- **Métros Core/Haris — low-cost specialist and gun carrier:**
  - **Native Métro** anchors the team.
  - **Up to two Moblots** can bring Tactical Awareness guns or an Engineer.
  - **Wardriver / Knauf FTO / 112 FTO** offer conditional network, sniper and medical alternatives in legal slots.
- **ETB Duo:** **Anaconda TAG + Moblot Engineer** (or eligible **112 Motorized/Kuryer**) is a legitimate recovery/armor flank. It is *conditional*, not a whole-game top TAG Fireteam; **Anaconda itself is rated C/C** in current cross-TAG comparisons. [TAG tiers](../list-building/tags.md).

### Battle-report audit — FRRM coverage gap and safe Fireteam tests

Across the [#001–#241 numbered N5 battle-report analyses](../battle-reports/n5-battle-reports-and-tournament-reports.md), **no directly verified modern FRRM full roster or deployed Fireteam** could be identified for this Ariadna audit. This **does not imply poor performance**; it means there is **no report-backed five-body Métro Core or Loup-Garou Haris** to cite. The older Merovingian media discussed below predates the N5.3 chart.

Three **current-chart test ideas**, **not observed tournament lists**, follow from the [pinned FRRM Fireteam rules](../../sectorials/ariadna/force-de-reponse-rapide-merovingienne.md#canonical-fireteam-chart):

- **Budget Métro Core:** three native Métros + eligible **Moblot gun + 112 FTO** (check exact specialist/loadout choices). This uses the legal Métros Core family with a veteran shooter and medical support; keep independent midfield specialists to operate buttons.
- **Loup-Garou Haris:** **Loup-Garou Smoke GL + Loup-Garou Viral Marksman + Apache FTO** (the 55-point candidate already discussed above). Test it as a ranged Smoke/viral/CC module beside Camo scorers.
- **ETB armor-recovery Duo:** **Anaconda TAG + Moblot Engineer** (or another permitted ETB support choice) as an **alternative** armored attack route, not an automatic best-choice TAG.

**Do not copy the [#210 *vanilla Ariadna* two-Loup-Garou + Briscard Haris](../battle-reports/201-210-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md#L323) into Merovingienne.** That formation is possible under **vanilla FT-02**, but the **FRRM Loup-Garou family only admits Loup-Garou and Apache FTO**; Briscards belong to a different **FRRM (Moblot/Briscard) Fireteam family**. A shared troop name is not a shared link chart. Until an actual FRRM N5.3 played Army export appears, mark these candidate teams **untested**.

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **Anaconda is a TAG**, but standard FRRM has **no VH-class Vehicle**. Having this one TAG does not by itself make the army exceptional.
- **No current Chain of Command** in the standard roster; protect a chosen Métro/Moblot/Briscard Lieutenant. **Briscards and Knauf** bring MSV1, not a native MSV2 Smoke-shooting package.
- **Normal Smoke plus projected Eclipse:** **Loup-Garou Smoke GL 17/0**, Duroc and Motorized 112 supply Smoke; **Wardriver Disco Baller 16/1** provides a separately paid Eclipse-style line-of-sight interruption. The hacking grid is limited, despite an optional Wardriver Pitcher.

## Quick read — how FRRM wins

**FRRM is a mobile, mixed-range toolbox army—not an elite stat-check army.** The best recurring pattern is:

**A practical FRRM architecture** contains different jobs, not every premium option:

- **Métro:** cheap Orders and midfield roadblocks.
- **Moblot Tactical Awareness:** one or two efficient guns/specialists.
- **Para-Commando / Mirage-5 / Anaconda:** one flank or angle-opening package.
- **Specialists:** enough survivors to score after the first attack.

- **Buy positioning, not fair gunfights.** Moblots bring Tactical Awareness on *every* loadout, Non-Hackable, WIP14, Shock immunity and Dodge (+1 Special Die). They can maneuver a Fireteam into a good AP Spitfire angle, or Dodge a bad encounter, rather than spending ordinary Orders trading into a stronger shooter. **But they still have only 1 Wound.** [Tactical Awareness S3E39 ~6–14m; WIP12 E197 ~33–41m; Loss of Lieutenant E157 ~84–90m]
- **Make Turn 1 expensive for the opponent.** The 12/13/14-point one-use-camouflaged infiltrating Métros and 19–20-point Chasseurs create a mine/template/unknown-threat midfield *crumple zone*. Their purpose is chiefly to absorb opponent Orders and protect the real attackers; do not expect cheap Métros to hold forever. [WIP12 E197 ~27–33m; Tactical Awareness ~28–31m]
- **Flank-entry choice:**
  - **Para-Commando FO (21/0):** WIP14/+1 Special Die, scoring and Targeted support for Guided Kuryer/Traktor.
  - **Mirage-5 (60/2):** larger rear assault:
    - **Margot:** BS14, Tactical Awareness and specialist utility.
    - **Duroc:** Smoke, templates and CC pressure.
  - These require a credible route and mission purpose. [WIP12; Tactical Awareness; LoL]
- **Solve visibility and range explicitly.** A **17-point Loup-Garou** has a *0-SWC* Smoke Grenade Launcher and Riotstopper; a linked **24/0.5 Viral Marksman** offers Marksmanship and X-Visor; **Vystrel** supplies a Sapper/Neurocinetics long-range ARO when the main force wants to fight inside Spitfire bands. [WIP12 ~22–26m; LoL ~71–74m, 107–111m]
- **Watch bottlenecks:** 6 SWC, fragile one-Wound pieces, no current Chain of Command and off-table Orders. Example weapon spend:
  - **Mirage-5:** 2 SWC.
  - **Moblot AP Spitfire:** 1.5 SWC.
  - **Anaconda:** 1.5 SWC.
  - **Total:** 5 SWC, leaving one for other weapons/hacking. Buy necessary packages first.

**Vision-control choices:**

- **Loup-Garou Smoke GL + Riotstopper (17/0):** economical Regular ranged Smoke for a legal small link, potentially with Apache FTO.
- **Duroc (Mirage-5):** independent Smoke for an airborne commitment.
- **112 Motorized:** mobile Doctor who also carries Smoke.

**Loup-Garou Viral Marksman (24/0.5)** is a different gun profile; Marksmanship does not confer MSV. Normal Smoke alone does not defeat MSV. See [vision-control choices](../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).

  - Wardriver (up to 1)

## Vehicles, TAGs and REM bots — precise roster and tradeoffs

**Standard FRRM has no VH-class Vehicle in its 22-unit roster.** Distinguish:

- **Anaconda:** TAG, not VH.
- **Carmen Johns:** motorcycle Warband.
- **112 Motorized:** motorcycle LI.
- **Kuryer:** REM.
- **Vystrel:** REM.
- **Traktor Mul:** REM.

A motorcycle is not the Vehicle troop type.

### Anaconda (TAG) — useful, not an automatic purchase

- **59 pts / 1.5 SWC; AVA 2; ETB Duo:** Tactical Awareness, ARM7/BTS6, Immunity (AP), ECM Guided (-6), Warhorse, Escape-System operator. The operator retains useful combat/scoring tools instead of leaving only a destroyed TAG. **AP Spitfire + Heavy Flamethrower + Thunderbolt** gives armor-piercing close–mid pressure, a short-range template and a long-range deterrent. **HMG + Light Shotgun + Panzerfaust** is a different range/tool mix.
- **Build for recovery and scoring:** pair a **Moblot Engineer** as its ETB buddy when expecting repairs, or a **112 Motorized** for fast Doctor + Smoke. The TAG is not cheap once its support and safe approach are considered.
- **Disagreement is strong and date-sensitive.** **WIP12 E197** (~53–56m) rates the reworked Anaconda good but not auto; **LoL E157** (~94–102m) has a pro-Anaconda side arguing 59pt/AP-Immunity/operator/TacAw resilience, and another side insisting **Mirage-5 is better at a nearly identical points cost** because the DZ strike gets more done. Older **LoL TAG Tier List #142 (Apr 2025)** put the **pre-return/rework Anaconda near bottom**; that historic verdict is **not** a verdict on the late-2025 N5.3 profile. A StarCo older reaction also dislikes its then-current version. Use newer FRRM reviews for this loadout.

### Vystrel (REM) — the long-range ARO fix

- **23/0.5** AP Sniper, **26/0.5** AP Sniper + Neurocinetics, **32/1.5** Portable Autocannon + Neurocinetics. Two Wounds, Sapper, Remote Presence and AP/long-range shooting make opponents spend Orders digging it out; the **26pt sniper** is the balanced default.
- **WIP12 (~74–76m):** one Neurocinetics sniper usually fits; autocannon is expensive in 1.5SWC slots already desired by Moblots/Mirage/TAG. **LoL (~107–111m):** Vystrel is important precisely because many other FRRM guns want close–mid range. Dozer repairs can repeat the Order-tax effect, but FRRM lacks the same cheap servant-repair network as some other Ariadna formations; keep repair position realistic.
- **Other-sectorial evidence:** [Tactical Awareness S3E09, Tartary Army Corps](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2VCGfZZhXpth6ahryGhPyw), and [WIP12 E203, TAK (2026)](https://open.spotify.com/episode/0fLvDDDa2baw7lxt3J4qo9) include shared long-range and artillery roles. **TAK Fireteams, AVA, support, and discounts do not transfer** to FRRM.

Makes deploying an otherwise costly 15-model force easier; reloads applicable disposable weapons when in the proper range/conditions. | S4 footprint and hiding locations are real costs; Baggage isn't a universal unlimited-ammo button. |
| **Traktor Guided Katyusha 9/1** | Low entry fee for occasional Targeted/Guided cleanup, especially after Para FO. | Low native BS10, not a credible straightforward shootout solution. |

| **Kuryer Uragan TR 18/1** | Reusable reactive saturation, Warhorse + mine threat and optional ETB Duo slot with an Anaconda. | Competes with cheaper TR Traktor and better-specialized long-range Vystrel; do not assume Kuryer is a global Wildcard. |

**Practical Guided sequence:** start with battlefield pressure from mines/camo and an FO/hacker already helping the mission → mark a vulnerable model with **Para-Commando WIP14/+1SD** or a linked Wardriver network → use **Guided** only when the target and remaining Order budget make it worthwhile. **TAK cross-check:** [WIP12 E203 ~46m](https://open.spotify.com/episode/0fLvDDDa2baw7lxt3J4qo9) explicitly cautions against betting a whole game on speculative firing from extreme range. This principle transfers; its army compositions do not.

**Guided and Speculative alternatives:**

- **Para-Commando FO (21/0) + Traktor Guided Katyusha (9/1):** low-cost Targeted/Guided follow-through when FO serves the mission.
- **Kuryer Katyusha (15/1):** six points more for independent Speculative Fire, Warhorse, mines and Baggage; still **ETB-only** for Fireteams.
- **Carmen & Bâtard (17/0):** close-range PH-based Speculative route with Sensor.

WIP12 E197 values Kuryer's wider utility but recognizes Traktor may suffice if Guided is all that is needed. See [indirect-fire alternatives](../list-building/indirect-fire-speculative-guided-apex-value.md).

### Motorcycles (not Vehicle-class units)

- **Carmen & Bâtard 17/0:** Irregular + Impetuous, fast Chain Rifle/Smoke Grenade Launcher, two-body threat and **Bâtard Sensor** to detect a camo-heavy opponent. Great independent flank/Smoke/Sensor tool, but does **not** add a flexible Regular Order and is not a specialist. **TA (~45–49m)** would often take two Regular Apaches first; **WIP12 (~50–52m)** values Sensor and smoke enough to include Carmen when needed.
- **112 Motorized 19/0:** 20-10 bike with Doctor, Smoke and Mimetism, and legal **ETB Duo** buddy. Use if high-speed healing/scoring or Anaconda escort serves the mission. **WIP12 (~81–83m)** prefers the cheap foot 112 or linked Paramedic in many lists; it's not a default order generator.

## Lieutenant, orders, deployment and common traps

- **Lieutenant options:**
  - **Métro LT (8/0):** cheapest on points, but must be concealed to survive.
  - **Moblot LT (27/0):** WIP14 and Tactical Awareness, yet still 1 Wound.
  - **Briscard FO LT (26/0):** adds +1 Command Token.
  - **Bruant FTO LT (32/1):** +1 Lieutenant Order, if its special Orders and exposure actually fit the list.
  - **No current Chain of Command:** preserve command safety and avoid accidentally breaking a needed Fireteam when spending the LT Order.
- **Order economy:** distinguish self-funded activity, off-table troops and support:
  - **Moblot Tactical Awareness:** self-funded active Orders.
  - **Margot / Duroc / Para-Commando:** off-table pieces, not on-table Turn-1 Orders.
  - **Traktor Mul Baggage (5pt):** cheap Regular support.
  - **Warcor (3pt):** Irregular observer, not interchangeable with Regular Orders.
- **SWC constraint:** prioritize a main gun job before spending on a Guided battery or two Warddrivers:
  - **Moblot AP Spitfire:** mobile anti-armor.
  - **Briscard HRL / Knauf / Vystrel autocannon:** long-range options.
  - **Anaconda:** TAG approach and operator scoring.
  - **Mirage-5:** rear assault.
  - **Example:** Mirage-5 (2) + Moblot AP Spitfire (1.5) + Anaconda (1.5) = **5 SWC**, leaving 1 SWC.
- **Damage/defense:** most premium FRRM linked infantry have only **one Wound**. Defensive value often comes from Dodge Special Die, Mimetism/Warhorse, obscured fire lanes, Smoke/Disco, mines and the enemy's Orders lost to reposition—not surviving sustained direct hits.
- **Avoid outdated N4 and initial-release Fireteam assumptions:**
  - **Apache FTO:** joins Loup-Garou; not a general Wildcard.
  - **Kuryer:** ETB-only, not global Wildcard.
  - **Knauf FTO:** belongs in Métros.
  - **Zouave Sapper:** not an FTO profile.
  - **Infiltrating Bruant:** not FTO.
  - **L'Équipe Argent Reinforcements:** uses a different Fireteam chart.
- **N5.3 first:** A 2024 Robert Shepherd MRRF battle report correctly illustrates old Chasseur screening and combined Fireteams, but mentions an earlier smoke deficit and other N4 assumptions that no longer apply. Current FRRM has Loups/Disco/Carmen smoke/visibility tools and reworked Moblots.
