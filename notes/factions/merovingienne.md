# FRRM / Mérovingienne — List-Building and Tactical Guide (Infinity N5.3)

**Research:** 2026-10-22. **Scope:** standard Force de Réponse Rapide Merovingienne (FRRM/MRRF), not L'Équipe Argent Reinforcements. This note records practical list patterns, alternatives, and *why* they work; it is not an Army-data replacement.

**Factual authority:** [current FRRM roster, loadouts, AVA, and canonical Fireteam chart](../../sectorials/ariadna/force-de-reponse-rapide-merovingienne.md), Army `7.26246.158` (2026-09-03), checked 2026-10-15; [N5.3 rules](../../rules/README.md). Community/video advice below is interpretation, not authority on profile legality. In particular, old N4 discussions and spring-2025 TAG ratings predate the returning-sectorial rework.

## Quick read — how FRRM wins

**FRRM is a mobile, mixed-range toolbox army—not an elite stat-check army.** The best recurring pattern is:

**Cheap Métro orders and midfield roadblocks + one or two Moblot Tactical Awareness guns/utility pieces + a complementary angle opener (Para-Commando, Mirage-5 or a TAG) + enough specialists to score after the attack.**

- **Buy positioning, not fair gunfights.** Moblots bring Tactical Awareness on *every* loadout, Non-Hackable, WIP14, Shock immunity and Dodge (+1 Special Die). They can maneuver a Fireteam into a good AP Spitfire angle, or Dodge a bad encounter, rather than spending ordinary Orders trading into a stronger shooter. **But they still have only 1 Wound.** [Tactical Awareness S3E39 ~6–14m; WIP12 E197 ~33–41m; Loss of Lieutenant E157 ~84–90m]
- **Make Turn 1 expensive for the opponent.** The 12/13/14-point one-use-camouflaged infiltrating Métros and 19–20-point Chasseurs create a mine/template/unknown-threat midfield *crumple zone*. Their purpose is chiefly to absorb opponent Orders and protect the real attackers; do not expect cheap Métros to hold forever. [WIP12 E197 ~27–33m; Tactical Awareness ~28–31m]
- **Attack from a different direction.** **Para-Commando FO (21/0 SWC)** arrives at a useful angle with WIP14 and BS Attack (+1SD), then scores or marks for a Guided Kuryer/Traktor. **Mirage-5 (60/2 SWC)** is the larger commitment, bringing Margot's BS14/Tactical Awareness/specialist status and Duroc's smoke/close assault to the enemy deployment zone. [WIP12 ~56–62m; Tactical Awareness ~30–34m; LoL ~74–78m]
- **Solve visibility and range explicitly.** A **17-point Loup-Garou** has a *0-SWC* Smoke Grenade Launcher and Riotstopper; a linked **24/0.5 Viral Marksman** offers Marksmanship and X-Visor; **Vystrel** supplies a Sapper/Neurocinetics long-range ARO when the main force wants to fight inside Spitfire bands. [WIP12 ~22–26m; LoL ~71–74m, 107–111m]
- **Watch the real bottlenecks:** **6 SWC, many 1-Wound pieces, no current Chain of Command, and off-table Orders.** Mirage-5 2 SWC + Moblot Spitfire 1.5 + Anaconda 1.5 leaves just 1 SWC for hacking/other guns. Decide which packages are *necessary* before adding nice-to-haves. [WIP12 ~97–101m; Tactical Awareness ~33–40m]

## Vehicles, TAGs and REM bots — precise roster and tradeoffs

**There is no standard FRRM troop with Army troop type `VH` (Vehicle) in the current 22-unit roster.** The **Anaconda is a TAG**; **Carmen Johns** (WB) and **112 Motorized** (LI) have motorcycles; **Kuryer, Vystrel and Traktor Mul are REMs**. Do not label motorcycles as a rules-equivalent Vehicle category.

### Anaconda (TAG) — useful, not an automatic purchase

- **59 pts / 1.5 SWC; AVA 2; ETB Duo:** Tactical Awareness, ARM7/BTS6, Immunity (AP), ECM Guided (-6), Warhorse, Escape-System operator. The operator retains useful combat/scoring tools instead of leaving only a destroyed TAG. **AP Spitfire + Heavy Flamethrower + Thunderbolt** gives armor-piercing close–mid pressure, a short-range template and a long-range deterrent. **HMG + Light Shotgun + Panzerfaust** is a different range/tool mix.
- **Build for recovery and scoring:** pair a **Moblot Engineer** as its ETB buddy when expecting repairs, or a **112 Motorized** for fast Doctor + Smoke. The TAG is not cheap once its support and safe approach are considered.
- **Disagreement is strong and date-sensitive.** **WIP12 E197** (~53–56m) rates the reworked Anaconda good but not auto; **LoL E157** (~94–102m) has a pro-Anaconda side arguing 59pt/AP-Immunity/operator/TacAw resilience, and another side insisting **Mirage-5 is better at a nearly identical points cost** because the DZ strike gets more done. Older **LoL TAG Tier List #142 (Apr 2025)** put the **pre-return/rework Anaconda near bottom**; that historic verdict is **not** a verdict on the late-2025 N5.3 profile. A StarCo older reaction also dislikes its then-current version. Use newer FRRM reviews for this loadout.

### Vystrel (REM) — the long-range ARO fix

- **23/0.5** AP Sniper, **26/0.5** AP Sniper + Neurocinetics, **32/1.5** Portable Autocannon + Neurocinetics. Two Wounds, Sapper, Remote Presence and AP/long-range shooting make opponents spend Orders digging it out; the **26pt sniper** is the balanced default.
- **WIP12 (~74–76m):** one Neurocinetics sniper usually fits; autocannon is expensive in 1.5SWC slots already desired by Moblots/Mirage/TAG. **LoL (~107–111m):** Vystrel is important precisely because many other FRRM guns want close–mid range. Dozer repairs can repeat the Order-tax effect, but FRRM lacks the same cheap servant-repair network as some other Ariadna formations; keep repair position realistic.
- **Other-sectorial evidence:** [Tactical Awareness S3E09, Tartary Army Corps](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2VCGfZZhXpth6ahryGhPyw), and [WIP12 E203, TAK (2026)](https://open.spotify.com/episode/0fLvDDDa2baw7lxt3J4qo9) include shared long-range and artillery roles. **TAK Fireteams, AVA, support, and discounts do not transfer** to FRRM.

### Kuryer vs Traktor Mul (REMs) — pay for effects, not just the gun

| Choice | Why | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| **Traktor Mul Deactivator 5/0** | Extremely cheap **Regular Order + Baggage**. Makes deploying an otherwise costly 15-model force easier; reloads applicable disposable weapons when in the proper range/conditions. | S4 footprint and hiding locations are real costs; Baggage isn't a universal unlimited-ammo button. |
| **Traktor Guided Katyusha 9/1** | Low entry fee for occasional Targeted/Guided cleanup, especially after Para FO. | Low native BS10, not a credible straightforward shootout solution. |
| **Traktor Uragan TR/Guided 14/1** | Adds Total Reaction if the list genuinely needs another ARO/threat. | Do not pay for a second long-range ARO when Vystrel already covers that role and SWC is needed elsewhere. |
| **Kuryer Katyusha 15/1** | Guided BS Attack (-3) pressures Dodge, while Baggage, Warhorse, Minelayer/Chest Mine improve passive survivability and area control. **WIP12 E197 + LoL E157 favor it if the +6pts fits.** | Same SWC as cheap Traktor Katyusha; a clean downgrade to Traktor often funds a vital Metro or weapon. |
| **Kuryer Uragan TR 18/1** | Reusable reactive saturation, Warhorse + mine threat and optional ETB Duo slot with an Anaconda. | Competes with cheaper TR Traktor and better-specialized long-range Vystrel; do not assume Kuryer is a global Wildcard. |

**Practical Guided sequence:** start with battlefield pressure from mines/camo and an FO/hacker already helping the mission → mark a vulnerable model with **Para-Commando WIP14/+1SD** or a linked Wardriver network → use **Guided** only when the target and remaining Order budget make it worthwhile. **TAK cross-check:** [WIP12 E203 ~46m](https://open.spotify.com/episode/0fLvDDDa2baw7lxt3J4qo9) explicitly cautions against betting a whole game on speculative firing from extreme range. This principle transfers; its army compositions do not.

### Motorcycles (not Vehicle-class units)

- **Carmen & Bâtard 17/0:** Irregular + Impetuous, fast Chain Rifle/Smoke Grenade Launcher, two-body threat and **Bâtard Sensor** to detect a camo-heavy opponent. Great independent flank/Smoke/Sensor tool, but does **not** add a flexible Regular Order and is not a specialist. **TA (~45–49m)** would often take two Regular Apaches first; **WIP12 (~50–52m)** values Sensor and smoke enough to include Carmen when needed.
- **112 Motorized 19/0:** 20-10 bike with Doctor, Smoke and Mimetism, and legal **ETB Duo** buddy. Use if high-speed healing/scoring or Anaconda escort serves the mission. **WIP12 (~81–83m)** prefers the cheap foot 112 or linked Paramedic in many lists; it's not a default order generator.

## Lieutenant, orders, deployment and common traps

- **LT:** **Métro 8/0** is the safe *points* default but not automatically secure; camouflage shell/decent concealment helps. **Moblot 27/0** is a tougher-looking WIP14 LT with Tactical Awareness but still only 1 Wound. **Briscard FO 26/0 (+1 Command Token)** changes your command-token budget. **Bruant FTO 32/1SWC (+1 Lieutenant Order)** is useful if deliberately spending those special Orders, but risks command collapse. **No current FRRM Chain of Command option.** Don't spend an LT Order in a way that unintentionally removes it from a needed Fireteam.
- **Orders:** **Moblot Tactical Awareness** is central, but do not double-count off-table Margot/Duroc/Para-Commandos as on-table Turn-1 Orders. A passive **5pt Traktor Baggage** can be a better third support piece than an isolated 3pt **Irregular Warcor** if flexible Orders matter.
- **SWC constraint:** prioritize **one** of Moblot AP Spitfire, long-range Briscard HRL/Knauf/Vystrel autocannon, Anaconda or Mirage-5 *before* adding a guided battery or both Warddrivers. For example, Mirage-5 (**2**) + AP Spitfire Moblot (**1.5**) + Anaconda (**1.5**) = **5 SWC**, with just 1 SWC remaining.
- **Damage/defense:** most premium FRRM linked infantry have only **one Wound**. Defensive value often comes from Dodge Special Die, Mimetism/Warhorse, obscured fire lanes, Smoke/Disco, mines and the enemy's Orders lost to reposition—not surviving sustained direct hits.
- **Avoid obsolete N4 / initial-release legality claims:** **Apache FTO joins Loup-Garou only**, and Apache is *not* a generic Wildcard; **Kuryer is ETB-only** not an army-wide Wildcard; **Knauf FTO belongs in Métros**; **Zouave Sapper and infiltrating Bruant are not FTO**. L'Équipe Argent **Reinforcements have a separate Fireteam chart**.
- **N5.3 first:** A 2024 Robert Shepherd MRRF battle report correctly illustrates old Chasseur screening and combined Fireteams, but mentions an earlier smoke deficit and other N4 assumptions that no longer apply. Current FRRM has Loups/Disco/Carmen smoke/visibility tools and reworked Moblots.

### Hacker and KHD tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Wardriver is FRRM's niche cyber counter:** the source includes **14pt/1 SWC HD+Zero Pain** for basic safe anti-heavy control and a **16pt/0.5 SWC Combi+Pitcher HD** variant. The *latter* is the more compelling network platform because it can project Repeater influence into midfield for fewer SWC; Zero Pain instead preserves an otherwise vulnerable hacker when enemy KHDs are present. **Why:** FRRM does not naturally field a dense premium Hacker/KHD suite, so its low-cost deployed specialists and conventional guns should remain the core of the plan. Use the Wardriver if the opponent brings TAG/HI or the mission rewards Spotlight; otherwise save its SWC.

#### Classification and evidence standards

- **Must-have / near-must:** repeated first-choice inclusion across independently built example lists, or an explicit reviewer “always / most lists / auto-include” judgment. **Near-must is not universal:** a role can be indispensable without any one profile being mandatory.
- **Go-to:** repeatedly attractive price/role efficiency. Not all go-to units belong in the same 300-point list; missions, fire lanes, SWC, Orders and competing packages decide.
- **Never take / strong avoid:** a directly stated negative review of a **specific profile or construction**, ideally with a viable same-roster alternative. Use **conditional** or **contested** if the profile has a niche, there is dissent or the source is pre-rework. There is **no evidenced blanket ban on an entire Ariadna unit family**.
- **Evidence:** **explicit** = specific presenter says so; **repeat** = multiple independently constructed sample lists; **reasoned** = synthesis of recorded strengths/weaknesses plus current reference, **not** a claimed quotation.
- A recorded list choice alone does not prove “most players” use a unit. Transcripts may mangle Ariadna proper names, so check names and legality against the current repository. The standard lists here do **not** import `L'Équipe Argent` Reinforcements, which have separate charts.
