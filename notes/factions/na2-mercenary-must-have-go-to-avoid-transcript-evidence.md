# NA2 mercenary sectorials — Must-have / Go-to / Avoid (N5.3)

**Audit date:** 25 October 2026. **Scope:** Druze Bayram Security, Ikari Company, StarCo Free Company of the Star, Dahshat Company, White Company. This companion to the other faction selection audits in this folder evaluates competitive **standard 300-point N5.3** lists. It does not import Reinforcement, optional Spec-Ops/Team-Ops, or a neighboring faction's Fireteam permissions.

**Authority order:** The five repository-native N5.3 sectorial references linked below are authoritative for available troops, particular profiles, AVA, points/SWC, and Fireteams (Army data 7.26246.159, 3 September 2026, verified 16 October). YouTube/podcast transcripts are evidence for **a speaker's opinion**, not a measured tournament inclusion rate or a guarantee of efficiency. Several sources predate N5.3; their strategic observations must be reconciled with the pinned roster. In particular, N5 fireteam changes invalidate some older linked Guided claims.

**Labels used here:**
- **Must / near-must:** An actual presenter's explicit almost-every-list recommendation, or a defensible near-universal list-building package. Near-must does **not** mean compulsory in every mission or list style.
- **Go-to:** Efficient, consistently defensible use of points, SWC, Order slots and a defined tactical job. The alternatives are often mutually exclusive.
- **Avoid / never take:** An **explicit reviewer rejection**, or a specific profile or construction displaced in its intended role by a better same-roster investment. Mark as **contested/conditional** when any credible niche or dissent exists. Do not invent categorical bans when only an older negative opinion is available.
- **Evidence:** **Direct** = explicit recommendation/rejection in a transcript; **Corroborated** = source rationale plus previously transcript-audited repository guide; **Contested** = meaningful opposing speaker's interpretation; **Edition-limited** = interpretation of pre-current Army data.

## 1. Druze Bayram Security

**Current profiles and Fireteams:** [Druze N5.3 sectorial reference](../../sectorials/non-aligned-armies/druze.md). **Primary direct media:** [D1: Infinity the Dante with experienced Druze player Luke Henry, Druze N5 Initial Reaction](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jmgABxVFU9U) (N5 launch, YouTube transcript reviewed); [D2: WIP12 E75, Druze Bayram Security, guest Leomaris](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4hppzgYaNybZeMexaNaykO) (2022/N4, podcast transcript reviewed); [D3: Concilium Watch, Army Builder Briefing Druze](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pJx--Qj7CQ4) (previously transcript-reviewed; edition-limited). [Existing Druze N5.3 tactical research](../../sectorials/non-aligned-armies/druze.md#tactical-research--druze-bayram-security-reviewed-2026-10-23) preserves source disagreement.

### Must / near-must

- **Druze Killer Hacker + Pitcher, 23/0 — Direct [D2], reinforced by [D1].** The WIP12 guest explicitly identifies the KHD as the single closest Druze auto-include; Luke Henry says the profile was in every one of his N4 lists and sees that continuing. **Why:** X-Visor lets Pitchers project into deep lanes, KHD removes enemy hackers and Warhorse helps survivability, while the trooper also performs specialist work. **Exception:** a deliberately non-network direct-gun/TAG build. **Important:** KHD does **not** supply ordinary Spotlight; partner an HD, FO or Valerya if using Guided.
- **Not a compulsory guided missile battery.** The cheap network often earns its place even when a Clipper is omitted. [D1,D2] advocate missile potential; the current **Guided Clipper is unlinked**, while the distinct FTO option is a direct Missile Launcher (+1B), **not Guided**. Never repeat early N5 linked-Guided-special-die claims.

### Go-to

| Unit/profile | Why it is good | Why not universal | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Brawler HRL, 15/1.5** | Sixth Sense, long-range area denial and cheap Fireteam ARO; very low point outlay | Uses SWC; MSV2 sniper trades to a different smoke-shooting job | D1 Direct |
| **Brawler Sixth Sense hacker, 16/0.5 / MSV2 MULTI sniper, 25/1.5** | Additional repeater defense, or active smoke-assisted visor shooting | WIP12/BS12 and one wound cannot replace premium attack models | D1,D2 |
| **Hunzakut FO 15/0 or 19/0 repeater** | Camo + Infiltration + FO, optional deployed network; solves weak starting midfield | Irregular Order; do not starve your regular gun/link pool | D1,D2 |
| **Valerya Gromoz, 19/0.5** | WIP14 HD, Pitcher, Total Control (+1B), Wildcard | Other ordinary hackers/FO may already cover the intended network | D1,D3 |
| **Yuan Yuan, Diggers/Denma** | Cheap smoke, close-assault or corner trades, protects medium-infantry links | Irregular/Impetuous pressure; not every model needed in one list | D1,D2 |
| **Bulleteer Spitfire or shotgun / Peacemaker Auxbot** | Mimetism(-6) speed shooting, or FD+20 repeater/templating denial | Large base, hacking exposure, and soft STR1; Bulleteer often needs EVO plan | D1,D3 |
| **Scarface + Cordelia** | AP weapon, ARM/STR and bundled Engineer for heavy armor matchups | **76–80 pts for the entire mandatory composite**, not the isolated TAG chassis; aggressive moves can leave Cordelia out of repair range | D1,D3; opposing opinions |

### Avoid / never-take candidates

- **Anaconda as a default TAG — Direct strong rejection [D1]; not a mathematical ban.** Both presenters call it poor head-to-head value relative to **Scarface + Cordelia**, and the surviving Operator is not sufficient to close that gap in their view. It can still fill a **59-point independent TAG/BTS or Mechanized Duo** requirement that Scarface's complete composite cannot fit. Mark **strong avoid as default**, not categorically illegal/useless.
- **Ordinary Druze HMG as primary investment — Direct/Corroborated [D1,D2].** Pay near 30 points and SWC for a BS12 one-wound gun that often needs further Fireteam investment; Scarface, linked Bulleteer, Armand or a specialist-heavy setup covers important objectives more efficiently. **Exception:** a compact existing Druze link where the HMG is a useful incremental gun, not an expensive list centerpiece.
- **Arslan LT as cost-neutral substitute for Brawler LT — Conditional [D1,D2].** Higher quality/command options come with conspicuous points and exposure. Can be worthwhile for a specific aggressive LT plan; not supported as a universal hard avoid.
- **Overbuilt 4–5 medium-infantry Core solely to improve a mediocre gun — source-supported construction warning [D1].** Compact Duos/Haris give less single-point-of-failure risk; larger defensive teams remain a legitimate scenario choice.

## 2. Ikari Company

**Current reference:** [Ikari N5.3](../../sectorials/na2/ikari-company.md). **Direct sources:** [I1: WIP12 E191, Ikari with Copper](https://open.spotify.com/episode/0XooAPvaG5RXEX9wUgsDvy) (September 2025, podcast transcript previously and selectively rechecked for this audit, particularly Arslan/expensive attackers); [I2: Infinity the Dante, Ikari N5 Initial Reaction](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6kZ2YeFDoNI) (N5 launch, YouTube transcript reviewed in current sectorial analysis); [I3: WIP12 E163, Cobraprime takes Ikari to IGL](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6sA5fiWOLSIILRw7DO06tv) (2024/N4, podcast transcript historically reviewed). [Current Ikari tactical research](../../sectorials/na2/ikari-company.md#tactical-research--ikari-company-reviewed-2026-10-23) includes the contrary TAG/Daiyōkai arguments.

### Must / near-must

- **No single current source establishes a literally compulsory individual troop.** The common **cheap Regular Keisotsu backbone (9–11 pts)** supports almost every wide Ikari design by giving Orders, a concealed inexpensive LT and economical FO. **Corroborated [I1,I2]**; the number and Fireteam configuration vary, so do not call a particular Keisotsu profile mandatory.
- **Wú Míng AP HMG, 33/1.5 — Copper's attack-piece default [I1].** A legal Wú Míng team provides inexpensive AP long-range shooting compared with **Daiyōkai HMG (50 pts)**, retaining room for smoke, anti-tech and specialists. **Exception:** Scarface-led, Tankō/Daiyōkai-NCO, or deliberately low-SWC lists. Thus this is a **near-must in inexpensive gunfighter Ikari**, not every legal Ikari list.

### Go-to

| Unit/profile | Why it is good | Opportunity cost / exception | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Motorized Yojimbo, 20/0** | Fast smoke, CrazyKoala/Minelayer, NWI and CC; helps MSV2 smoke-shooting | One of the Irregular fast attackers; foot Yojimbo alternative may solve tiny corridors | I1,I2 |
| **Yuan Yuan, 6+ / Desperado, 7–9** | Disposable aerial trade or immediate bike smoke/chain-rifle defense | Too many Irregular troopers choke Regular activations | I1,I2 |
| **Tankō ML (26/1.5), Blitzen/Disco Baller (18–19)** | Standoff ARO or cheap E/M close defense; Daiyōkai/Tankō Haris | ML eats SWC; old N4 Keisotsu bonus claims not current | I1,I2 |
| **Armand Le Muet, MSV1 MULTI sniper 34/1.5** | Mimetism(-6), forward deployment, survivable transmutation and versatile active/reactive lane threat | About three Keisotsu worth of list points | I1 |
| **Druze KHD, 23/0** | X-Visor Pitcher, Warhorse, D-Charges, hacker hunting | Not an ordinary HD; Ikari Druze family permits Duo/Haris, **not Core** | I1,I2 |
| **Karakuri FO 31–38 / Pathfinder 14–17** | Durable STR3 objective body vs very economical Sensor/FO/TacAware specialist | Karakuri **not linkable** in Ikari, unlike JSA; Pathfinder soft | I1,I2 |
| **Rui Shi MSV2 Spitfire, 23/1** | Efficient solo MSV2 smoke-shooting, optional EVO Marksmanship | **Not linkable** here; fragile/hackable | I2 |
| **Scarface + Cordelia / Triphammer** | Alternative armored AP striking or 0-SWC budget TAG plan | Compete with AP Wú Míng and required specialist slots; conditional [I1 vs I3] | I2,I3 |

### Avoid / never-take candidates

- **Security Chief Arslan as general-purpose 35pt shooter/LT — Direct strong avoid [I1].** Copper explicitly calls him a poor-value unit: midrange shooting and other tools do not justify paying so much when **9pt Keisotsu LT / 15pt Brawler LT** and **33pt Wú Míng AP HMG** cover primary purposes cheaper. **Exception:** specific MSV1/WIP13/Warhorse, NWI and Fireteam command plan. Do not mislabel all Druze Shock Teams as bad.
- **Al Fasid 44–50pt as generic main gun — Conditional, shared skepticism [I1,I2].** An independent premium solo anchor is often beaten in slot economy by *linkable* Wú Míng HMG + supporting pieces. **Counter:** ARM5/BTS6, Sixth Sense, Albedo, smoke/Minelayer may be needed where you want independent defense and no Fireteam exposure.
- **Daiyōkai HMG as a pure damage-per-point replacement for Wú Míng — Contested [I1 vs I2,I3].** Copper saves 17 points with AP Wú Míng; others value NCO/armor, a mobile Tankō Haris and close combat. It is **not** a general never-take.
- **Early video “linkable Guided Clipper” shortcut — invalid construction**, not a troop veto. Guided Clipper is unlinked; the link-eligible FTO is direct ML without Guided. Do not merge them.

## 3. StarCo Free Company of the Star

**Current reference:** [StarCo N5.3](../../sectorials/non-aligned-armies/starco-free-company-of-the-star.md). **Direct sources:** [S1: Infinity the Dante, StarCo N5 Initial Reaction](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qml2BQcHNv4) (6 October 2025, YouTube transcript reviewed); [S2: Loss of Lieutenant E158, Return of the Lost Sectorials Part 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EoQ3Ma7Hp7k) and [podcast](https://open.spotify.com/episode/1pQ6J6OGpzyu8QlxiFoo8D) (28 October 2025; StarCo discussion ~58–109min, podcast transcript checked). **Historical** [S3: Transmission Matrix E12, FoCo and StarCo Fiasco](https://open.spotify.com/episode/5T3lSmBsWknspl8S6FhkEU) has no retrieved podcast transcript and is **not attributed here**. [Existing StarCo tactical research](../../sectorials/non-aligned-armies/starco-free-company-of-the-star.md#tactical-research--starco-reviewed-2026-10-23) records corrected list legality and the major TAG disagreement.

### Must / near-must

- **Al Hawwa' Killer Hacker, 22/0 — Direct [S1].** The N5 reviewer says he would use it in every StarCo list because **WIP14 + Regular Infiltration/Camo** gives reliable anti-hacker defense and a midfield mission piece. **Exception:** plan already covers hacking defense through other units and is very tight on points; the **Bandit KHD** is only WIP12 and competes with an excellent Minelayer.
- **Irmandinho Engineer/smoke, 8/0 — Direct [S1].** Described explicitly as virtually automatic due to extraordinary low price, impetuous smoke plus repair for Reaktion/Anaconda/Tsyklon. **Exception:** only one Irregular slot left, or a list where regular smoke/other Engineer placement matters more.
- **Knauf FTO + Uhahu Outrage Duo — Strong recurring package [S1,S2], but not literally compulsory.** Knauf FTO **30/1.5**, MSV1 Mimetism(-3) precision rifle/sniper shooting; Uhahu **19/0.5**, WIP14 HD+, Pitcher and NWI. Together compress premium shooting and important hacking into a short legal Duo while retaining Core/Haris slots. **Do not** link Knauf's non-FTO solo profile. Outrage team source choices also permit other structures. Neither half must be bought in a specialized non-sniper/non-hacking build.

### Go-to

| Unit/profile | Why it is good | Tradeoff / opponent | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Brawler HD (16/0.5), HRL (15/1.5), Engineer (19/0)** | Sixth Sense anti-hacking in repeater coverage, cheap ARO, Armored TAG repair team | WIP12, BS12 one-wound; actual hacker quality lower than dedicated KHD | S1,S2 |
| **Digger (14) / CSU specialist (11–13)** | Efficient PMC Duo/Haris composition, corner defense, cheap score-capable team member | Digger close-range; CSU lacks Brawler's long-range or Engineer tools | S1,S2 |
| **Bandit Minelayer + deployable repeater, 25/0.5** | Rerollable Infiltration, Camo, hostile repeater/mine fork near enemy advance or DZ | Risky failed forward deployment; Irregular WIP12 model should act, not idle | S2 Direct |
| **Hellcat Paramedic, 23/0** | Combat Jump(+3), Super-Jump, MediKit, Deployable Repeater = sudden objectives and network angle | Starts off-board, must protect remaining board/control Orders | S1,S2 |
| **Mobile Brigada TinBot(-6) LT, 33/0 / HMG 38/1.5** | Viable protected command and mixed link with Uhahu; durable attacker | Hackable; large links cost points and order flexibility | S1,S2 |
| **Riot Grrl Spitfire/ML + Avicenna or Fiddler FTO** | Two-wound attackers plus MSV1, strong WIP15 Doctor(+3) or linked Engineer | Frenzy, SWC and link-slot opportunity costs | S1 and existing cross-Bakunin transcript analysis |
| **Jaguars / Señor Massacre / Raoul Spector** | Cheap Regular smoke and trades; Eclipse route past MSV; surprise arrival or FTO gunfighter | Different deployment/reach and irregularity; Raoul's AD profile cannot link | S1,S2 |
| **Al Hawwa' FO/KHD, Stempler FO, Reaktion Thunderbolt 19/0** | Reliable midfield objectives/Sensor; cheap 0-SWC long-lane TR deterrent | Regular specialist and fragile REMs still need Order support | S1,S2 |

### Avoid / never-take candidates

- **Alguacil premium SWC shooting as the default gun — Direct negative [S1].** The N5 speaker recommends avoiding Alguaciles except when they provide an actual cheap LT, necessary StarCo team foundation or Daktari link/decoy function; for serious shooting, pay for Knauf/Mobile Brigada/Riot Grrl or relevant Brawler. **Not a veto** on **10/0 Alguacil LT** or low-cost team glue. Note: the **current 10/0 Alguacil LT has no 0.5 SWC tax**, contrary to an early spoken claim.
- **Unplanned Anaconda / Triphammer TAG purchase — Direct [S1] is very negative; **contested** by later [S2].** S1 calls Anaconda among the worst TAGs and says not to take it, likewise dismissing Triphammer. However S2 re-evaluates the **Anaconda at 59/1.5**, arguing ARM7 with AP immunity, Warhorse, improved escape Operator and a **legal Anaconda + Brawler Engineer + Digger Armored Haris** creates a genuine defensive/zone-control anchor. Do **not** file Anaconda under unqualified never-take; mark **no-support standalone TAG** as an avoid, with the Haris as its counterexample. The 0-SWC Triphammer has a SWC-preservation niche but remains contested on raw gunfight quality.
- **Expensive Clockmaker purely duplicating Irmandinho's Engineer role — Conditional [S1].** Clockmaker at 18 has higher WIP15 and access to Zondbot reach, so is right for demanding repairs; poor use if no high-value repair targets. **Do not** label Clockmaker fundamentally bad.
- **Illegal purported Knauf FTO + Uhahu + Brawler LT Outrage Haris** — the [S2] hosts propose, then explicitly correct, this list: the Brawler LT is not eligible Outrage/Wildcard. Use a legal Duo or supported Haris with the correct members. This is a **rules correction**, not a Brawler LT veto.

## 4. Dahshat Company

**Current reference:** [Dahshat N5.3](../../sectorials/na2/dahshat-company.md). **Direct sources:** [H1: WIP12 E165, Dahshat Part 1](https://open.spotify.com/episode/5LyspedREmw1py5M8gLa1K) and [H2: E166 Part 2](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4rONwGAmjRXakeybrBVeBA) (July 2024, N4, both podcast transcripts previously reviewed; Part 1 Libertos recommendation directly rechecked); [H3: Robert Shepherd, N5 Dahshat vs PanO Acquisition battle report](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cQCB9XONJvU) (N5 single-game transcript); [H4: Robert Shepherd, N4 Dahshat Faction Focus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aj9eai4crps) (N4, historically transcript-reviewed). [Current Dahshat tactical research](../../sectorials/na2/dahshat-company.md#tactical-research--dahshat-company-reviewed-2026-10-23) corrects pre-N5 changes (Zhēnchá Trinity and absent Zúyŏng LT).

### Must / near-must

- **Libertos Mimetism(-3) 9/0 — Direct profile-level preference [H1], N5.3 roster corroboration.** WIP12 presenters emphatically recommend paying **+1 point** over an otherwise similar unmodified Libertos: Camo/Forward Deployment, Dogged and the extra negative-to-hit modifier can soak disproportionate opposing activations. **Exception:** hard 15-trooper ceiling/Regular Order needs or a scenario favoring different specialist/defensive budget. Do not claim both Libertos AVA2 are mandatory.
- **No singular compulsory heavy gun.** The [H1,H2] cheap-trader/Rui Shi philosophy competes legitimately with **Maghariba TAG**, and [H3] shows Maghariba/Valerya/Ghulam/Libertos supporting one another in an actual N5 game. These are alternative spend patterns.

### Go-to

| Unit/profile | Why it is good | Tradeoff | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hunzakut FO, 15/0 or 19/0 repeater** | WIP14 Camo/Infiltration, early objective and network projection | Irregular; durable Zhēnchá instead for hold-to-end missions | H1,H3 |
| **McMurrough, 23/0 / Kum 6–11** | Smoke, Super-Jump, powerful close anti-armor/chain-rifle trade or fast bike smoke | High Irregular count and speculative charges exhaust useful activations | H1,H2 |
| **Zúyŏng Tactical Awareness HMG 33/1.5 + Rui Shi MSV2 Spitfire 23/1** | Efficient legal linked heavy shooter and visor/smoke solution | Hackable team with 1 STR Rui Shi; not every list needs a full Core | H1,H4 |
| **Valerya Gromoz, 19/0.5** | WIP14 ordinary HD, Pitcher, Total Control (+1B), Wildcard | Dahshat hacking stays thin against dedicated KHD; no native KHD option | H1,H2,H3 |
| **Maghariba 71–72/1.5** | ARM8/STR3/BS14, TacAware, Baggage and anti-armor gun; HFT/mine dispenser especially strong | S8 hides/moves poorly on tight boards and occupies many points | H3 and N5 TAG reviews cited in current guide |
| **Zhēnchá 30–34 / Fiddler FTO or Jackbots** | Regular Camo Infiltration and durable scoring/HD Trinity vs protected Engineer or synchronized bodies | Costs more than Hunzakut, FTO cannot also be Jackbot profile | H2,H3 |
| **Brawler HRL / Sixth Sense HD + 14pt Digger** | Affordable ARO/repeater/CC support with flexible Fireteams | Worse direct primary offense than Zúyŏng/Rui Shi | H1,H2 |
| **Traktor Mul 5pt Baggage / 9pt Katyusha Guided** | Extremely efficient cheap Regular support, or unusual low-cost indirect threat | Guided needs actual Targeted and consumes SWC/setup; not automatic | H2,H3 |
| **Saito Togan / Húláng / Odalisque+Miranda** | Assassination, Monofilament close work, or resilient low-SWC zone-specialists | Mission-specific high points; Húláng FTO label does not grant a Dahshat Fireteam | H2 |

### Avoid / never-take candidates

- **No evidence-backed categorical current N5.3 never-take** from the retrieved recordings. The strongest warning is **building 15 models of excellent but too many Irregular bargains**, leaving the primary gun short of Regular Orders [H1,H4].
- **Al Fasid 44–50pt HMG/HRL as a redundant primary shooter — Conditional [H2,H4].** Solo cost and SWC often duplicate a linked Zúyŏng or Maghariba; he still offers a valid standalone ARM5/BTS6 smoke/Minelayer/Sixth Sense/Albedo utility package. Call it *role overlap*, not strictly inferior.
- **Ghulam HMG as premium primary offense — Conditional tactical downgrade [H1,H4].** BS11 Ghulam are strong because of cheap orders, LT, NCO Smoke GL, Doctor(+3) and Hackers; heavy spending on a plain HMG usually loses to Zúyŏng/Rui Shi or Maghariba shooting. The profile may still fit a specific economical link.
- **Pre-N5 claims “no Trinity anywhere” / “Zúyŏng Lieutenant” — invalid fact in N5.3.** Zhēnchá ordinary HD option **has Trinity upgrade**; there is **no current Dahshat Zúyŏng LT profile**. These are edition corrections, not negative rankings.

## 5. White Company

**Current reference:** [White Company N5.3](../../sectorials/na2/white-company.md). **Direct sources:** [W1: Robert Shepherd, Infinity N5 Faction Focus White Company](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y-iuVDPJ-Y4) (March 2025, full transcript reviewed); [W2: Infinity the Dante, Winning* a Tournament with WhiteCo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hiLOsN7c7zE) (May 2025, tournament report transcript reviewed); [W3: Infinity the Dante, White Company N5 Initial Reaction](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UwgmSkVnrpI) (Jan 2025, full transcript previously reviewed); [W4: WIP12 E160, White Company](https://open.spotify.com/episode/7zbcLwKEZXivvhDwutJba0) (June 2024/N4, transcript retrieved; historical only). [Current White Company tactical review](../../sectorials/na2/white-company.md#tactical-research--white-company-reviewed-2026-10-23) records anti-cases and tournament limitations.

### Must / near-must

- **No sourced universally compulsory individual unit.** White Company can meaningfully choose between K2 guns, Hawkwood/Fusilier defensive Core, ORC/Jujak heavy teams, direct TAGs, and Tiger/midfield-led mission plans. The sources endorse **packages**, not identical lists.
- **Hǎidào is the strongest near-default flexible family — Corroborated [W1,W2,W3].** Available as **24/0 Chain of Command**, **22/0 KHD+Pitcher**, **25/0 Engineer**, **29/1.5 MSV2 sniper** with NWI, Wildcard and strong team fit. **Do not pretend one Hǎidào profile simultaneously has all four jobs.** Whether any Hǎidào makes the final 15 depends on the chosen mission/link archetype.

| **Karhu Feuerbach 34/1.5 + Engineer 25/0 / Kaplan Doctor 24/0** | K2 firepower with MSV1/Mimetism, NCO/D-Charges or WIP14 Doctor healing | Overinvesting in 4–5 expensive K2 bodies crowds out midfield specialists | W1,W2,W3 |

### Avoid / never-take candidates

- **Fusilier SWC guns as the principal expensive damage dealer — Direct tactical warning [W1].** Shepherd recommends taking cheap Fusilier LT, FO, Hacker/decoy as a viable Hawkwood-Fusilier Core, **not** buying ordinary Fusilier HMG/ML upgrades to do premium gunfighting. *Fusilier as a cheap support backbone is excellent*, not an avoid.
- **Chaksa Longarms as a default main gun — Direct negative preference [W1,W3], conditional.** More compelling competing K2, Hawkwood, Nisse or ORC long-range tools often make their 24–29pt SWC shooting unattractive; their own legal independent Duo/Haris and specialized guns still fill particular lanes.

## Cross-sectorial ranking principles / edition checks

1. **A “must” is a reported list-building default, not tournament telemetry.** Most explicit Druze/StarCo/Dahshat endorsements are a named player's preferences; there is no verified representative inclusion-rate database here.
2. **Do not call every TAG “never take.”** Druze Anaconda has strongly negative direct testimony, whereas **StarCo Anaconda gets a persuasive later rehabilitation in a supported Haris**, and WhiteCo uses a different Kaplan Duo. It is the *same chassis with different team architecture*. The Triphammer's 0-SWC entry can also be right even if it loses raw shootout comparisons.
3. **Do not conflate FTO label and permission:** Druze Guided Clipper differs from Fireteam FTO direct ML; Ikari Guided Clipper similarly does not link; WhiteCo Clipper FTO has no Fireteam row at all; StarCo Stempler FTO likewise has no current StarCo chart row. Always use the exact current Fireteam chart and profile option.
4. **Irregular versus Regular economy is decisive:** Irregular smoke/assault excels individually in Ikari, StarCo and Dahshat, but 15 slots and too few pooled Orders can make even a roster of efficient units dysfunctional. The **Regular** cheap Keisotsu, Fusiliers, Fugazi/Fanous/Transductor and specialist options have systemic value.
5. **Mission scoring and delivery beat theoretical damage:** Druze/Dahshat Hunzakut, Ikari Karakuri/Pathfinder, StarCo Al Hawwa'/Hellcat and WhiteCo Guiláng/Tiger change whether a gun-heavy list can reach a console on time.
6. **No illegal legacy data:** Current N5.3 Army references override early N5/N4 transcripts: old StarCo Alguacil LT SWC tax, old N4 Dahshat Zúyŏng LT, imported Karakuri Fireteams, early linked-Guided Clipper assumptions and other-faction TAG/REM permissions are not reliable.
