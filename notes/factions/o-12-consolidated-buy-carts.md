# O-12 — consolidated Vanilla, Starmada and Torchlight Brigade buy carts (Infinity N5.3)

**Prepared 2026-10-30 from authoritative `main`.** All **three** current O-12 armies: [Vanilla O-12](vanilla-o-12-buy-list.md) (**49 current standard unit entries**), [Starmada](starmada-buy-list.md) (**44**), [Torchlight Brigade](torchlight-brigade-buy-list.md) (**38**). Rules legality follows pinned [Vanilla](../../sectorials/o-12/o-12.md), [Starmada](../../sectorials/o-12/starmada.md) and [Torchlight](../../sectorials/o-12/torchlight-brigade.md) N5.3 reference (Infinity Army `7.26246.159`, verified 2026-10-15); the existing *full unit-by-unit buy/proxy/skip audits* in the three individual files remain intact. This document is a **proposed shopping plan, not an owned-inventory update**.

**Actual ownership already applied:** **Warcor x1** and **two S1 Yudbots** are shared. **Starmada:** **Hector 1, Andromeda 1**, **Knights of Santiago 2** owned (but **Starmada AVA1** for Santiago, do not field two simultaneously). **Torchlight:** **Beasthunter 1** is owned. No full dedicated O-12 force, Moonrakers, Ment Agents, Betatroopers, Silverstar Rovers or Lambda infantry reported. The owned **Caledonia McMurrough S6** can test **Ioann Bann** when free and visually/size matched, not an exact owned O-12 character. Other people in other factions aren't automatically legal O-12 even if their model silhouette is useful for a proxy.

### Why the combined savings are greater than just counting repeated boxes

The ordinary union of the three standalone core baskets contains **14 different retail lines at $541.69 CAD**, compared with the independently bought **$881.96**, because the Reinforcements Alpha, Torchlight Beta, Raveneye, Tinker, Cyberghost and Varangian can be shared. But the **CodeOne 26-model Collection Pack already contains** the separately budgeted Starmada:
- O-12 Booster Beta **$12.99** (Katherine Cho / Hippolyta / Crusher);
- Lambda Support Pack **$25.31** (2 Lambda / 2 already-owned ancillary Yudbots);
- Betatrooper box **$17.62**, now confirmed as 3 soldiers plus TinBot (SKU282017-0916).

**Remove all three duplicate retail boxes** from the union: **$541.69 – $55.92 = $485.77 CAD**. There is **no need to buy an extra Hippolyta or separate Zeta** for Starmada/Vanilla once the 26-model pack is obtained. **This deduction is conditional on actually obtaining that Collection Pack** at the archived price; otherwise recalculate the cart based on genuinely available items.

## How the stated no-buy/proxy constraints are applied

- **Standard four robot profiles + baggage:** Use pooled **4 PanO Dronbots** (or suitable ALEPH Rebots) for the **Oko Sensor, Peeler Total Reaction, Kyttä flash, Millicent Guided** Copperbots, each with correct **S3 base/height and weapon/identification**. **Fuzzbots baggage/logistics** use **PanO Mulebots S4**. **Already-owned Yudbots S1** are Lambda servants. This is **one shared physical pool**, not 4 free Copperbots for each army simultaneously, and no new generic drone pack is budgeted.
- **Distinctive specialized robots and companions:** **StormBot (large 55mm base), Moonraker (25mm), RoadBot (transforming forms), Team Sirius bot, Tinker Zetbot, Wrecker Zetbot, Raptor Devabot, Šarko Sekudroid and Wildparrot** are NOT automatically interchangeable with basic remote/baggage models. Some are peripherals, some are regular troops; don't count every model in a multi-piece SKU as independent orders. Correct silhouette/height, similar appearance, different donor figure per simultaneously fielded body.
- **Minimum quantities that matter:** **3 Kappas** for many starting pools, additional Kappas from Starmada Action Pack can allow *some* distinct weapon/loadout sculpts (Starmada AVA5, so sixth cannot be fielded concurrently); **2 Moonrakers** for Torchlight/Vanilla AVA2, **3 Silverstar Rovers** in Torchlight starter (Torchlight AVA5, Vanilla AVA2), **2 Hellblazers** in same pack (Torchlight AVA3, Vanilla AVA2), **2 Kludgers** engineer/doctor, **2 Crushers** in Starmada starter plus one in Collection pack (Starmada AVA3), **2 Vidocq** in Reinforcements (Starmada/Vanilla AVA2, Torchlight AVA1), **3 Jackboots** in reinforcement set but no army here has AVA3. Don't buy an extra whole box solely to max AVA.
- **Named exact ownership and proxy conflict:** Hector, Andromeda, 2 Santiagos are **Starmada only** exact named models (one Santiago at a time); Beasthunter is **Torchlight only**. Shared Warcor and 2 Yudbots are already counted. **Owned Caledonia McMurrough S6** can portray Ioann Bann only if not concurrently deployed elsewhere and appropriate dog-warrior appearance. Don't use *named legal Starmada Hector* as a “random Bronze” on a list that also has Hector, or same-S alone to justify incorrect model scale.
- **Full-unit no-buy/proxy-first:** Collector **Zeta tournament LE**, Kappa paint-set exclusive, outdated two-player releases, repeat specific characters already supplied by box, independent purchase of alternate Jamie Handler and Rover, additional expensive Zeta/Wrecker simultaneously, **Firebat S7 VH** without matching vehicle model, uncommon Casanova/rare named Lieutenants, excess Silverstar Primes or extra Rover bodies, second Team Sirius/Sekudroid without tested synergy. Weak profile choices (e.g. BSG Varangian as generic smoke delivery, one Raveneye CoC where cheaper Ment CoC suffices) do **not** turn the whole unit into an illegal or universally unusable model.
- **Stock uncertainty:** One product with “low stock” today may disappear tomorrow. Review the actual seller photos/SKU for historic MM Betatroopers (its pasted description erroneously said **Knight of Santiago**), and for Starmada Alpha (2 troops+Wildparrot) and Torchlight Beta (StormBot+2 Moonrakers). No LE/event or scenic bases counted as unique roster units.
