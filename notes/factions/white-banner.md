## Overview

**What White Banner does especially well:** White Banner layers **exceptionally cheap forward E/M/Camo screens**, conventional Smoke, projected Eclipse/Disco, **Albedo against MSV**, and a high-utility mixed **HI/MI Fireteam** able to shoot, hack and score. It often wins by forcing the opponent to solve several different sightline and midfield problems before reaching the expensive team.

### Particularly strong or unusual Fireteams

- **White Banner mixed Core/Haris:** an eligible **Jujak Firewall(-6) + Hwarang Disco + Haetae HRL/Guided/TacAware + Dokkaebi KHD/Pitcher** shell can combine strong guns, hacking defence, Eclipse movement and specialist/Guided pressure. A **Shàng Jí, Jujak, Yě Māo or Yín-Shé** required-choice member satisfies the appropriate chart condition; **not all four** are mandatory.
- **Shaolin Haris:** **Liang Kai + two Shaolin** creates the 31/0 FT Master assault/Order package. The single Haris slot forces a decision against a mixed White Banner Haris.
- **Yě Māo / Yín-Shé team:** MSV1/Mimetism guns and **Albedo(-6)** provide a different anti-visor plan from expensive Jujak-heavy links.
- **Blue Wolf Duo:** **Blue Wolf + legal Shàng Jí** or another Blue Wolf pairs a TAG with an armoured companion; its own eligibility is separate from the mixed Core.

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **Smoke and Eclipse:** **Shaolin** has inexpensive *normal Smoke*; **Hwarang Disco Baller** projects Eclipse, which blocks MSV too and is not a smoke-shooting shortcut.
- **Anti-visor access, limited MSV2:** **Yín-Shé Albedo** and **Léi Gōng MSV1+Albedo** counter visor defenders. **Rui Shi MSV2** can shoot through normal Smoke but is fragile and **unlinked** here.
- **TAG/vehicle choices:** **Blue Wolf AVA2** and **Gūijiă AVA1** are TAG families, plus **Fēiquán VH AVA1**. The interesting difference is gun ranges and **Blue Wolf Duo**, not that two TAG families is intrinsically rare.
- **CoC:** **Léi Gōng Chain of Command** is a separate profile from its cheaper FTO attacker.
- **Fireteam exclusions:** **Son-Bae and Pangguling** FTO names do not make either eligible to join White Banner teams; one Core and one Haris remain hard limits.

## Tactical research — White Banner N5 (2026-10-22)

> **Interpretation, not replacement Army data.** The canonical N5.3 roster and Fireteam chart below (Army 7.26246.158, September 2026) take precedence over speaker recollections, older points and N4 examples. Opinions are identified by source. In particular, the current White Banner roster has **Blue Wolf TAG (AVA 2), Gūijiă TAG (AVA 1), Fēiquán vehicle/VH (AVA 1)**, and *neither Son-Bae nor Pangguling FTO can Fireteam in this sectorial* despite their FTO names.

### The 30-second list-building plan

**White Banner wins by layering low-cost defensive problems in front of a versatile, objective-capable attack force.** It combines cheap Gūdǎn and Lóng Yá denial, Shaolin smoke/CC, midfield Camo, high-value mixed Fireteams, and several independent specialists. Its constraints are **costly premium solo pieces, limited MSV2, sometimes weak ranged answers to Mimetism, and competing demands on its single Core and Haris slots**.

1. **Buy a deployable defensive floor:**
   - **Gūdǎn (usually 1–3):** one-use Camo, Forward Deployment, Zapper/HFT.
   - **Lóng Yá:** optional Panzerfaust, Flammenspeer or Minelayer.
   - **Hùndùn:** Hidden Deployment HRL.
   - **Chaīyì:** 7-point Flash Pulse/Repeater screen.
   - **Shaolin:** smoke and CC deterrence.

   Position mines/templates on enemy approach routes that matter.
2. **Choose one major team plan:**
   - **Jujak Firewall(-6) + Hwarang Disco + Haetae HRL Guided/TacAware + Dokkaebi Pitcher/Spotlight:** multi-role attack and hacking Core.
   - **Yě Māo + Yín-Shé:** MSV1/Mimetism and Albedo(-6)/K1 Sniper against visors.
   - **Zhanshi / Shàng Jí:** smaller team with a Doctor and more budget for independent attackers.

   These packages answer different terrain, opponents and missions.
3. **Reserve points for objectives outside the main team:**
   - **Guìláng:** midfield FO/hacker/Minelayer.
   - **Dàofěi:** two-Wound Infiltrating HI specialist or LT.
   - **Shaolin + Liang Kai:** close-quarters clearance.
   - **Kunai:** T2 Thunderbolt/Para Mine.
   - **Tiger Soldier:** Combat Jump threat.
   - **Léi Gōng:** fast flank and anti-MSV attack.
4. **Don't mistake maximum list size or maximum gun power for a plan:** a short burst of heavy attacks wins a lane, but missions need buttons/classifieds, dispersed board presence, an LT fallback and enough regular orders after casualties. WIP12's experienced tournament guest Axel specifically favors spreading abilities across inexpensive units over making one unanswerable death-star.
5. **Use smoke, Albedo and visors deliberately:**
   - **Shaolin:** conventional Smoke, blocking normal LoF.
   - **Hwarang:** Disco Baller for HI movement.
   - **Yín-Shé:** Albedo(-6) against MSV, not a universal shooting modifier.
   - **Rui Shi:** fragile, unlinked MSV2 gun for normal Smoke shooting.

   Against Mimetism(-6)/MSV AROs, seek alternative angles instead of forcing a fair BS13 FtF.
6. **Build legal teams:**
   - **Limits:** one Core, one Haris, unlimited Duos.
   - **Shàng Jí / Jujak / Yě Māo / Yín-Shé:** required-choice set, not four compulsory units; Jujak counts as Shàng Jí, Yín-Shé as Yě Māo, Hwarang as Jujak.
   - **Blue Wolf Duo:** one Wolf with another Wolf or Shàng Jí.
   - **Shaolin Duo/Haris:** Shaolin or Liang Kai anchor, potentially Jing Qo Wildcard where allowed.
   - **Tian Gǒu / Léi Gōng:** Wildcard eligibility on specified FTO profiles only.
   - **Pangguling / Son-Bae:** not linkable, despite FTO names.

### Disagreements to keep visible

- **Is the Jujak/Hwarang/Haetae/Dokkaebi Core mandatory?** It solves mobility, guns, hacking, defence and Guided exceptionally efficiently; *but* Shepherd's actual N5 battle report experiments with **Yě Māo+Yín-Shé** combinations and stresses avoiding homogeneous list construction. Use enemy Mimetism/MSV and mission needs to choose, not habit.
- **Blue Wolf versus Gūijiă:** Shepherd finds both useful-but-expensive; **Irregular Order 2026** sharply prefers Gūijiă (ARM8/MULTI HMG/Super-Jump/MA2 keeps it from entering enemy hacking/CC range) and calls Blue Wolf vulnerable at only Spitfire ranges. The counter-case is Blue Wolf's five-shot AP Spitfire and multi-weapon short-range fork, and unique Duo access.
- **Guìláng and Dàofěi costs:** **ITS Season 15 winner Axel** takes Guìláng LT/FO/Hacker for camo-based *mission board control*, despite knowing they're expensive; Shepherd finds Guìláng inefficient and Dàofěi 52pt too consuming except in Classified-heavy missions; BT Sherman strongly defends a mission-oriented Dàofěi LT/Specialist.
- **MSV versus Albedo plan:** Need direct MSV2 through smoke? Pay for **unlinked Rui Shi**. Expect opposing MSV AROs? Use **Yín-Shé Albedo(-6)** and/or Eclipse/Disco. Both do different jobs; neither universally supplants the other.
- **Hac Tao Hacker versus ordinary midfield piece:** 58pt Hidden Deployment Mimetism(-6) Trinity/ARM5 is hard to remove, but White Banner has **only that Hac Tao loadout**—no NCO HMG/ML—and cannot deploy it as cheaply or far forward as Guìláng/Dàofěi.
- **Lóng Yá and turrets:** Axel prizes cheap Lóng Yá and repeatedly fields them but questions spending multiple early orders on static turrets; other podcasters value layered AROs/recoverability. Avoid an architecture that collapses solely because deployment was lost.
