## Whole-unit purchase deferrals and shared model use

**No whole-unit hard veto.** Reviewers strongly discourage **Sonya as generic 28pt gun** (special Albedo/NCO/Silverstars Wildcard still useful), **Jamie Handler/Rover** without deliberate plan (different mutually exclusive versions), and **Firebat S7 VH** as ordinary early gun. **Strider Red Fury34** often inferior as generic mission piece to **FO29**. **Raveneye CoC18** needs more than succession to beat **Ment14**. **Silverstar Rovers** are not compulsory core bodies; **Silverstar Prime cannot join Silverstars Fireteam**. The existing Beasthunter1, Warcor1 and two Yudbots are already covered; don't repurchase merely to fill AVA.
