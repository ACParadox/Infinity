# O-12 N5.3 — Must-have / Go-to / Avoid (YouTube and podcast evidence)

**Audit:** 25 October 2026. **Armies:** [Vanilla O-12](../../sectorials/o-12/o-12.md), [Starmada](../../sectorials/o-12/starmada.md), [Torchlight Brigade](../../sectorials/o-12/torchlight-brigade.md). **Profile authority:** repository's `status: current` N5.3 sectorial references, Army `7.26246.159` (3 September 2026), including roster availability, SWC, Fireteam chart and FTO restrictions. **Interpretive authority:** actual YouTube/podcast discussions cited by each entry, not invented community consensus.

**Scope:** Ordinary 300-point list-building. **Must/near-must** = an explicit near-auto recommendation or a broadly repeated core *role/package*; these are not measured pick rates. **Go-to** = a dependable purchase for its role, rather than a compulsory inclusion. **Avoid** = a profile or standard use criticized by named speakers, or demonstrably poor opportunity cost relative to current alternatives; **not** an assertion of literal zero playable matchups. Where recording evidence is limited or contradictory, say so. Model legal Fireteams from the linked N5.3 charts, **not** historic recorded lists. `FTO` alone is not legal Fireteam access.

**New transcript audit (26 October 2026):** [Robert Shepherd, *Infinity N5 Faction Focus — O-12*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Nl8BMJ8E5U) (full ~82-minute YouTube transcript reviewed; supported by input from several O-12 players, including a recent Interplanetario competitor). This is **Vanilla O-12** commentary, not a new Army rules source or a Starmada/Torchlight tier list. See [detailed 2026 focus update](#2026-10-26-robert-shepherd-vanilla-o-12-faction-focus) below. This source explicitly **disagrees** with parts of earlier Tactical Awareness advice on Epsilon, Sirius and Vidocq; retain both opinions.

## 2026-10-26: Robert Shepherd Vanilla O-12 faction focus

**Source:** [*Infinity N5 Faction Focus — O-12*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Nl8BMJ8E5U) (full YouTube transcript inspected; timestamps below). Shepherd explicitly says his firsthand N5 Vanilla O-12 play is limited and consulted experienced players including an Interplanetario competitor. These are **his recommendations**, not a universal consensus or official Army errata. All **prices / legal team configurations** below are reconciled against the [September 2026 pinned Vanilla roster](../../sectorials/o-12/o-12.md); optional **Team-Ops** are **not** standard 300pt roster models.

### Faction-building thesis — availability is broad, budget is narrow (00:00–18:30)

- **Capability breadth:** ordinary Smoke+MSV shooting, White Noise, Disco Baller, HD/HD+/KHD, infiltration/Hidden Deployment, Combat Jump, CC, effective Vanilla **Duo/Haris**, and especially **high-mobility Specialists**. The usual bottleneck is not a missing special rule but needing to *pay for multiple components* of a plan. Special-case impersonation and enemy-DZ Parachutist were called out as exceptions, not proof that every other unusual tool is present.
- **A strong but expensive floor:** Moonrakers, Ment, Raveneye and Varangians are excellent *for 12–15 points* but cannot subsidize several elite attackers like 3–7pt orders in some other armies. Buy **one clear primary gun**, an objective path and sufficient protection before upgrading every support unit.
- **Reactive defence is a crumple zone:** two Moonraker markers/mines, potentially reinforced by Varangians and **Peeler Thunderbolt TR + Engineer** or a resilient RoadBot, but no automatic Swiss/Noctifer-style decisive lethal ARO anchor. Your own Turn 1 after going second must use surviving Orders decisively to score, retaliate or repair.
- **Nonlethal is most efficient in enemy turn:** Adhesive / Riotstopper stopping an attacker after they have committed Orders can nullify the plan; **an immobilized enemy is not a dead enemy** and may recover. In active turn, prefer actual killing or a specific objective-opening purpose over endless nonlethal shooting.

## Cross-faction disagreements and rules safeguards

1. **No evidence of a literal whole-unit universal “never take” in all three current rosters.** The clearest strong negative is **Torchlight ordinary Silverstar Rover-heavy construction**, followed by profile-specific **Torchlight Strider Red Fury**, **Starmada Casanova**, and **Starmada combat-role Tian Gǒu**.
2. **Vanilla O-12 is not Core-less-and-link-less:** current N5.3 chart permits **one Haris plus two Duos**. Gamma/Omega/Vidocq/Jackboot packages must use the actual FT-II required/allowed membership; avoid retrofitting an arbitrary triple.
3. **Starmada is not Vanilla:** it **does not** gain Team Sirius, Gamma, Razor, Moonraker or Wrecker just because those feature in a Vanilla discussion. Its current unique defenses/mission options make inexpensive Raveneye, Bronze, RoadBot, Šarko, Bluecoat and Parvati especially relevant.
4. **Torchlight Silverstar Prime is not a Fireteam source/member** despite the word *Silverstar*. The **Hellblazer** or **Silverstar Rover** supplies the required-source entry; Waverider and allowed Wildcards follow the chart. Older N4 Prime+Hippolyta+Hellblazer teams cannot be copied as legal N5.3 Fireteams.
5. **Only current FTO options actually listed on the chart link:** Torchlight Vidocq FTO 1/2/3, StormBot FTO 2/3, Jamie Rover FTO 1/3; Starmada Nyoka FTO 3/4, Tian Gǒu FTO 1/3/4/6, etc. The full repository chart is the authority, not these illustrative subsets.
6. **TAG disagreements are real.** [Loss of Lieutenant TAG review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dZo_gQWQYSY) strongly values **Zeta** and **Wrecker**. [TA Starmada](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1DKpabLoqyE) is skeptical about **Zeta specifically in Starmada**, where 70–73pts and no Tactical Awareness crowd out support. [Shepherd Torchlight](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PZKCQAupCMs) prefers **Prime for first reliable gun** but is positive on a Wrecker damaging armored targets.
7. **New N5.3 unit not graded without fresh recorded evidence:** **Ioann Bann** was introduced in September 2026. These key N5 discussions predate his arrival. Treat him as **ungraded**, not “never take” merely for absence from transcripts.
8. **N4 tournament evidence** (notably the 2024 Torchlight WIP12 winner) supports a *role architecture*—Moonrakers, camouflage, Prime, Hippolyta—but **does not verify N5.3 Fireteams or prove a current win-rate advantage**.

**Purpose:** Which models should you **not bother buying immediately**, which **genuinely criticized profiles** can simply be represented by an existing model, and which **excellent but specialized / alternate sculpts** should be proxy-tested rather than bought for inventory completeness? Cover all three current N5.3 O-12 armies: [Vanilla O-12](../../sectorials/o-12/o-12.md), [Starmada](../../sectorials/o-12/starmada.md) and [Torchlight Brigade](../../sectorials/o-12/torchlight-brigade.md). **No universally unusable whole unit** was demonstrated. *Rare* below means a **mission-dependent or non-default role in the reviewed lists**, not statistically measured play frequency.

The [September 2026 N5.3/Army 7.26246.159 profiles and canonical Fireteams](../../sectorials/o-12/) override old N4/N5 source lists. **FTO** on a profile does not by itself grant team access.

### 4. Across O-12 — even excellent units may be better off as proxies or shared models

**Ownership check:** no current authoritative O-12-specific inventory was found at the expected repository paths for **Vanilla, Starmada or Torchlight**. Consequently **no owned/available body count is inferred**. The recommendations distinguish **first needed model**, **alternate non-simultaneous weapon sculpt**, **additional actual trooper**, and **companion/peripheral body**. A cross-sectorial model can be *physically reused* for a **legal** unit in another list, but never magically grants the other army its excluded unit.

**Practical shopping rule:** (1) Buy/represent the **first** efficient **Ment, Raveneye, Moonraker where legal, Kappa HD, Varangian, Oko/cheap Copperbot, mission specialist** you actually want; (2) choose **one main ranged platform** (e.g. Vanilla Gamma/Epsilon, Starmada Bronze/Epsilon, Torchlight Prime/Wrecker); (3) **proxy** a *new specialized expensive hero, VH/TAG, optional extra gun, SideBot/peripheral package* for multiple games; (4) buy only after your lists repeatedly need it *simultaneously* and a lawful same-size model isn't available. On all proxy choices, confirm **current ITS/event policy, silhouette, base, distinct physical figure per simultaneously present model, team eligibility, exact selected profile and friendly-game opponent agreement**. Proxies change plastic, **not Army legality, equipment, Orders or Fireteam privileges**.

**Buying verdict by army:** **Vanilla:** few whole-model skips; proxy-first **Cuervo, unplanned Wrecker/Zeta/Firebat, special Lynx/Nightshade, duplicate premium guns**, and skip the **Sirius Hacker / Razor Mono / Vidocq Sniper** default profile. **Starmada:** **Casanova** is strongest full-model defer; proxy **Tian Gǒu combat variant, Firebat, Zeta, extra Bronze/Betatrooper or Nyoka**, keeping Raveneye/Bronze/Bluecoat/Šarko objective engine. **Torchlight:** strongest deferrals **excess Silverstar Rover, Sonya, Jamie, Firebat**, plus the **Strider Red Fury/Raveneye CoC** profile and illegal **Prime/Core or Beasthunter FTO** assumptions. None of these conclusions turns a valuable unit's alternate, distinct use into a blanket ban.
