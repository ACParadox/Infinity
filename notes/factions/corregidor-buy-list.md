## Collection-specific verdict, suggested shopping order and quantity

Your only **exact recorded Corregidor choices already owned** are **Warcor x1** and **McMurrough x1 (Caledonia)**, both legal in the pinned 40-entry roster. No Alguaciles, Morans, Jazz/Billie, Intruder, Gecko or Wildcat dedicated models are documented. The Corregidor Action Pack ten-figure set is **10/10 legal**, includes a useful mix of Alguaciles and specialized infantry/REM, and is a strong starting foundation. However, its **Sombra** is usable because the pinned Corregidor roster does list **Sombras**; do not confuse this with vanilla's missing Sombra. Corregidor has **Surface, Deepspace and Correctional** Fireteam contexts: don't assume the same list can mix their incompatible link packages.

## Proxy choices from owned models: precise silhouette, right visual archetype, no double counting

Apply [the repository's detailed proxy policy](../miniature-proxy-guidelines.md): **match actual profile's exact silhouette (base AND height), preferably the same troop class and similar armour/clothes, then weapon/role/appearance**. A model whose *named troop itself belongs in this force* is a poor proxy for another unit, even when free in today's list. A target being legal never makes its sculpt owned. Declare unusual proxies to opponents and use event/TO policy.
