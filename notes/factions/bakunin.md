**Reference archive:** [Bakunin scope, primary recordings, tactical cross-references, battle reports and purchasing audit](references/bakunin_refs.md).

**Direct video/podcast-supported unit priorities:** [Nomads four-roster must-have / go-to / avoid audit](references/bakunin_refs.md#must-have--go-to--avoid--source-backed-recommendations-2026). “Never take” opinions are profile- and role-scoped; current N5.3 Army data supersedes older clips.

## Overview

**What Bakunin does exceptionally well:** it creates several different problems at once—very cheap Smoke/Eclipse assault, concealed midfield trading, protected hacking and linked REM firepower. Its premium attackers are alternatives, not a requirement to build around a single TAG or five-model Core.

### Particularly strong or unusual Fireteams

- **Riot Grrl TinBot(-6) + Uhahu:** protected HD+/Pitcher on a durable MSV1 Fireteam; spend more points only when the team needs a real Riot Grrl attack gun.
- **Moderator + Clockmaker + Vostok FTO** (or **Tsyklon FTO**): a cheap required anchor, Engineer and recoverable attacking REM; verify the exact FTO profile because **Vostok FTO loses Repeater**.
- **Taskmaster + one/two Morlocks:** FT Master can convert linked Irregular warband Orders when the selected legal team qualifies; Morlocks outside the team remain Irregular.
- **Stigmata or Lizard + Orphan Engineer:** a **Duo-only Armored** family that joins a heavy chassis to repair, Repeater or scoring utility. This is not an unrestricted TAG Core.

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **Visibility:** **Morlock Smoke**, **Chimera Eclipse**, **Prowler Disco Baller** and **Uhahu/Custodier HD+ White Noise** solve different sight-line problems. **Riot Grrl MSV1** and **Orphan MSV2** supply counter-Smoke choices; ordinary Smoke is not Eclipse.
- **Heavy choices:** **Stigmata** is a hacker/TAG hybrid; **Lizard** is a conventional BS14 gun TAG; **Go-Pod** is a genuine aerial **VH**, not a TAG. Their roles and defenses differ.
- **Roster limits:** Bakunin cannot import **Gecko, Gator, Iguana or Szalamandra** merely because they are Nomad units. **Tsyklon** remains legal here despite its removal from normal Corregidor. The pinned roster has **no Lunokhod**.
- **Fireteam economy:** **one Core, one Haris, unlimited Duos**; a premium Observance/Riot/REM Haris excludes another Haris in the same list. The source [Fireteam chart](../../sectorials/nomads/bakunin.md#canonical-fireteam-chart) governs all combinations.

## One-minute verdict

**Bakunin wins by combining threats that demand different answers:**

- **Morlocks and Chimera/Pupniks:** disposable melee pressure, Smoke and Eclipse approaches.
- **Zeros and Initiated Observants:** midfield Camo, mines and repeater pressure.
- **Reverends/Moiras:** Mimetism(-6) shooting when the team structure suits them.
- **Riot Grrls:** direct MSV1 gunfighting and durable Fireteam positions.
- **Stigmata + Orphan:** resilient armored hacking and recovery, rather than a compulsory primary gun.
- **Lizard:** a more conventional direct-fire TAG alternative.
- **Vostok/Tsyklon and other supported REMs:** supplementary ranged attacks and network reach.

The opponent must solve close combat, Eclipse/Smoke, hacking, ARM, Mimetism, MSV and mines without enough answers for all of them.

**Main weakness:** excellent individual tools compete for **one Core, one Haris, and expensive SWC guns**, and some premium units have only **one wound**. Bakunin has versatile FTs but **not a free set of several Haris teams**. Plan which pair of missions a list serves instead of always buying whichever profile looks strongest.

**Three legitimate build engines:**

1. **Melee/control/area scoring:**
   - **Riot Grrl MSV1** with **Orphan MSV2** or **Rounder** for anti-Smoke/objective coverage.
   - **Sin-Eater** or **Reaktion** for an ARO lane.
   - **Cenobites, Chimera + Pupniks, and Morlocks** for close trades and Eclipse-enabled scoring.
   - **Uhahu** as a compact hacking package. The December 2025 tournament winner used this style without **Stigmata, Go-Pod or Guided**.
2. **Just-enough hacking + REM attack:**
   - **Moderator + Clockmaker + Vostok FTO** or **Tsyklon FTO** as the supported, repairable attack core.
   - **Uhahu + Riot Grrl TinBot(-6)** or **Custodier** for hacking and protection.
   - **Zeros and Initiated Observants** for mines/repeaters; **Stempler** for independent Sensor/FO.
   - An Engineer recovers the attack REM after its early shooting while the specialist network guards approaches.
3. **Armored denial:**
   - **Stigmata + Orphan Engineer/Repeater** or **Lizard + Orphan/Marspider** in a legal Armored team.
   - **Morlocks** and deployables for pressure; one **separate reliable active gun** for hard AROs.
   - The TAG survives, projects hacking or direct threat, and draws responses while other pieces score. **Stigmata is a durable hacker/ARO problem, not automatically the main long-range gun.**

**Visibility-control choices (use the one the list needs):**

- **Morlock 6/0 Smoke:** inexpensive but Irregular/Impetuous.
- **Chimera + Pupniks 19/0 Eclipse:** high-ceiling assault and objective delivery.
- **Uhahu HD+ 19/.5** or a legal **Reverend Custodier HD+:** White Noise versus visors/Marksmanship.
- **Prowler Disco Baller 34/0:** an infiltrating scoring and alternative-angle tool.
- **Orphan MSV2 / Rounder:** stop ordinary-Smoke objective theft; **Cenobites** retain threat when White Noise/Albedo shuts down visor AROs. Eclipse blocks both.

The last contrast is supported by the MetaChemistry #132 winner's list; compare [whole-game vision-control roles](../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).

## Vehicle — Go-Pod: fast ceiling, high failure risk

**Go-Pod is Bakunin's AVA1 VH; choose a legal profile:**

- **33pt/0SWC MULTI Rifle + E/Mitter** for lower SWC commitment.
- **32pt/1.5SWC Heavy Rocket Launcher + AP SMG (BS Attack SR-1)** for the alternate attack job.

Both use 20–5 move, Aerial, S7, ARM2, Mimetism(-3), No Cover, Super-Jump, Tech-Recovery and a Go-Bot Peripheral. Neither has a legal Fireteam row.

- **Pro-vehicle:** amazing airborne flanking angles, ability to skip roadblocks with flight/super-jump, backline penetration once hackers/ARO pieces are neutralized; threatens missions requiring **rapid zone change**. In late 2025 a Korean opponent told MetaChemistry’s Bakunin winner Go-Pod was nearly auto-include locally.
- **Opposing practice:** that winner excluded Go-Pod **partly because he lacked a model**, but *also* because his missions rewarded **static MSV-backed zone control and repeated scoring**, while the Go-Pod tends to be exposed after one spectacular attack run. This is **not conclusive evidence Go-Pod is bad**.
- **Stronger skeptical account:** Robert Shepherd’s **Tunguska** review (~60–69min) says the nominally strong Go-Pod often **loses orders in early ARO fights**, is extremely hard to conceal (**S7/no cover**), **dies quickly once hit at ARM2**, and cannot safely enter hostile **Repeater/KHD** zones. It wants other units to **clear heavy AROs and enemy hackers first**. As a later-turn finisher it can be exceptional; as the *list’s sole primary assault gun* it is unreliable.
- **Actionable build test:** for any Go-Pod list, write down **(1) first-turn ARO clearer, (2) hacker-killer/network cleanser, (3) landing-safe parking spot, (4) objective or target achievable with the fast run, (5) recovery plan**. If you cannot answer all five, redirect 32–33 points toward a scoring/screen package. **Avoid assuming the expensive HRL is a better investment than 0SWC MULTI Rifle**; it directly competes with a TAG or Riot Missile.

## Disagreements to test, not erase

1. **Is Bakunin mainly a hacking sectorial?** Robert/Dante: flexible defensive Mimetism, Pitchers, Firewall and TAG-hacker make meaningful network play. **MetaChemistry winner**: explicitly used **just Uhahu with Riot Firewall(-6)** and no Guided/TAG-hacker; melee and ARO area control were sufficient for his particular five-round wins. *Rule*: hacking is a **tool**, not a required strategic identity.
2. **Stigmata S-tier or A-tier?** Loss of Lieutenant podcast **both**: high tier for uniquely resilient hacker that fits well; lower tier due to BS13/3-dice active shooting, ARM6 and some weapon limitations. Robert: an **opponent-problem/ARO and opportunity attacker**; do not expect it to solo dismantle a prepared enemy. Buy for denial and repaired board presence.
3. **Stigmata or Lizard?** Stigmata defensive hacker vs **Lizard BS14 MULTI HMG direct attacker**. Robert likes spec-firing E/M grenades at null-deployed opponents; a risk-averse player will prefer **HFT multi-HMG** or another gun for predictable kills. Repeater is not uniquely valuable when Bakunin already has Pitchers/FO repeaters.
4. **Go-Pod mandatory?** Some players see its threat range and locally deem it auto-include. December 2025 winner rejects it for mission fit, table vulnerability and model availability. Robert Tunguska review argues its **high ceiling is paid for by a very low floor** and heavy support tax. Use for a specifically planned late-game angle, not as default.
5. **Riot Grrls vs Reverends:** Dante’s N5 reaction favors Riot Grrls: durable 2W and Fireteam bonuses no longer rescue 1W BS12 Mimetism gun as much. Robert counters: **Moiras + NCO + Mimetism(-6) + linked Custodier/Healer** still supply unique vision and hacking tools. Mission, opponents' MSV/White Noise and objective distance decide.
6. **Sin-Eater or Reaktion?** Dante expects both to gain from weaker linked N5 AROs; Robert calls Sin-Eater a **niche reactive luxury**, often unable to contribute when no favorable lane. Reaktion has maneuver/climbing, but also loses ARO to a serious active weapon. Use only if it protects a lane opponent **must** cross.
7. **Orphan MSV2 versus usual Engineer Orphan:** default TAG guides take the 17/21pt Engineer. December 2025 winner convincingly used **25pt MSV2 MMR** to defend objectives against smoke and Camo. Distinct roles; do not mislabel MSV2 shooter as Engineer.
8. **Vostok or Tsyklon?** Robert prefers Vostok for **Mimetism(-6) reliable attack** and full-stack repair in Moderator+Clockmaker. Tsyklon provides **Pitcher, Repeater, Feuerbach and 360º**, giving stronger network construction/ARO when hacking is your focus. Vostok FTO **loses Repeater**, unlike Tsyklon FTO.
9. **Morlock spam versus Regular pool:** 6pt Morlocks are excellent trades; bringing too many **outside Taskmaster Ft** lowers usable Regular Orders. **Taskmaster FT Master** changes the calculus for a legal link; losing that FT can also change your remaining order resources. Calculate both states.
10. **Prowlers / specialized midfield gear:** Dante likes Hidden Deployment Spitfire/Disco Baller as underestimated; critics note 30–36pts is 1W and a Zeros+Morlocks shell delivers more separate threats. Match to mission pressure and opponent anti-Camo tools.
11. **Fireteam configuration:** Moderator+Clockmaker+Vostok/Tsyklon is efficient. Riot/Observance attack Haris can be better. You only have **one Haris**; strongest *individual* combo may lose to a broader list's opportunity cost.
12. **Zoe alone versus FTO pairing:** not a player-preference debate; **an obsolete-availability error** in an older transcript. **Current Bakunin 42/45pt Zoe+Π-Well FTO only**, not 23pt Zoe alone. Current chart, not speaker confidence, wins.

## Practical two-list audit

- [ ] Decide whether **both lists** emphasize **zone/room and close combat**, **MIDFIELD hacking/REMs**, or a **TAG plus support**; missions justify switching.
- [ ] Before taking a Haris, **allocate your sole Haris** (Moderator+Clockmaker+Vostok vs Riot/Observance/Taskmaster). **Armored can be Duo**, separate from the Core/Haris cap.
- [ ] Include **at least one strong active ranged gun** with reliable hit probability (Riot Spitfire/Kusanagi/linked Vostok/Lizard): Stigmata+Go-Pod+Taskmaster HRL by themselves can all fail sustained high-Burst ARO breaking.
- [ ] Include anti-smoke/MSV for **zone defense** (**Riot MSV1, 25pt Orphan MSV2, Rounder MSV1**, depending on mission); **White Noise/Albedo** counter-plan also needed.
- [ ] Keep an **objective specialist** alive for Round 3 (Zero FO, Orphan Engineer, Stempler FO, Bran, Riot specialist, Meteor, appropriate Reverend); winning melee alone does not automatically score objectives.
- [ ] Count **Regular / Irregular / Impetuous / Tactical Awareness / Lieutenant / NCO** separately. **Morlock and Überfall** are not extra standard Regular Orders by default.
- [ ] If taking **Stigmata**, pair an Engineer and consider firewall/KHD backup; if taking **Lizard**, clear hostile hacking lanes; if taking **Go-Pod**, clear enemy hackers **and** serious long-range AROs first.
- [ ] Calculate **SWC before finishing**: 1.5 Lizard or Stigmata; 1.5 Penitent AP Spitfire; 1.5 Riot Missile/Spitfire; 1.5 Vertigo; 1.0 Tsyklon. A cheap well-positioned Moderator can matter more than the third premium gun.
- [ ] Verify **legal chosen unit profiles**, not just unit names: Vostok FTO only links; Tsyklon FTO links; Fiddler FTO loses JackBots; standalone Zoe not on this roster; Stempler does not link; correct VH is Go-Pod.
- [ ] Against a tough opposing TAG/HI or hidden gun, test the answer from **E/M, close combat, White Noise, continuous AP, Spotlight, and Engineer repair** rather than expecting ballistic brute force alone.

**Suggested starting philosophies (concepts, not Army-validated 300-point exports):**

- **List A — mission/area control:**
  - **Riot Grrls + Uhahu/TinBot** for linked protection.
  - **Orphan MSV2 or Rounder**, with **Sin-Eater or 0SWC Reaktion** to deny key lanes.
  - **Chimera/Pupniks, Morlocks**, and **Zeros/specialists** for close trades and scoring.
- **List B — armored/network:**
  - **Stigmata + Orphan Engineer** or **Lizard + Marspider** in a legal Armored Duo.
  - **Moderator + Clockmaker + Vostok/Tsyklon** if the single Haris slot and budget permit.
  - **KHD/Repeater backstop, Zeros** and a finishing specialist.

Build the actual lists after choosing missions, table density and current Army legality.
