## Scope, sources, and what changed

**Authoritative profiles:** [Bakunin N5.3 roster and Fireteam chart](../../sectorials/nomads/bakunin.md), Army `7.26246.158` (source 3 September 2026, verified 15 October 2026), **48 standard units**; this guide provides interpretations rather than overwriting points, weapons, legality, or [rules](../../rules/README.md). [Vanilla Nomads](vanilla-nomads.md) and [Corregidor](corregidor.md) are complementary comparisons, **not legal ways to mix their rosters or Fireteams**. Sources from N4/early N5 are explicitly dated. **Cross-sectorial rule of thumb:** Other Nomad guides explain how a **Stigmata/Lizard/Go-Pod/Vostok/Tsyklon** behaves tactically; they **do not confer** e.g. Corregidor Gecko/Evader Haris or Tunguska Interventors to Bakunin.

## One-minute verdict

**Bakunin wins through multiple mutually supporting problems:** cheap disposable **Morlock/Chimera melee**, midfield Camo/Para mines, **Mimetism(-6) Rev gunfighting**, **Riot Grrl/MSV1 guns**, **Stigmata/Orphan resilient hacking** *or* **Lizard direct gun**, and **Fireteam-supported REMs**. The opponent needs answers to **close combat, Eclipse/smoke, hacking, high ARM, Mimetism, MSV and mines**; they usually cannot answer all simultaneously.

**Main weakness:** excellent individual tools compete for **one Core, one Haris, and expensive SWC guns**, and some premium units have only **one wound**. Bakunin has versatile FTs but **not a free set of several Haris teams**. Plan which pair of missions a list serves instead of always buying whichever profile looks strongest.

**Three legitimate build engines:**
1. **Melee/control/area scoring:** Riot Grrl MSV1 ARO + **Orphan MSV2** / Rounder + Sin-Eater or Reaktion; **Cenobite / Chimera/Pupniks / Morlocks** push threats, Eclipse carries objectives. A December 2025 Bakunin tournament winner played roughly this with only **Uhahu** as the core hacking package; **no Stigmata, Go-Pod or Guided**.
2. **Just-enough hacking + REM attack:** Moderator/Clockmaker + **Vostok FTO** or **Tsyklon FTO**, **Uhahu + Riot Grrl TinBot(-6)** or a Custodier; **Zeros and Initiated Observants** seed mines/repeaters, **Stempler** does independent Sensor/FO. Good because a supported attack REM shoots, then its Engineer can recover it, while your specialist network protects approaches.
3. **Armored denial:** **Stigmata + Orphan Engineer/Repeater** Armored Duo, or **Lizard + Orphan/Marspider** as legal armored team. Add Morlocks, dense deployables and one **separate reliable active gun**. TAG survives, projects threat/repeater or punishes enemy formations while rest of list scores. The Stigmata is a durable **hacker/ARO problem**, **not necessarily your primary long-range gun**.

## Vehicle — Go-Pod: fast ceiling, high failure risk

**Bakunin AVA1 VH: 33pts/0SWC MULTI Rifle+E/Mitter**, or **32pts/1.5SWC Heavy Rocket Launcher+AP SMG (BS Attack SR-1)**; 20-5 move, **Aerial, S7, ARM2, Mimetism(-3), No Cover, Super-Jump**, Tech-Recovery, **Go-Bot Peripheral**. No legal Fireteam row.

- **Pro-vehicle:** amazing airborne flanking angles, ability to skip roadblocks with flight/super-jump, backline penetration once hackers/ARO pieces are neutralized; threatens missions requiring **rapid zone change**. In late 2025 a Korean opponent told MetaChemistry’s Bakunin winner Go-Pod was nearly auto-include locally.
- **Opposing practice:** that winner excluded Go-Pod **partly because he lacked a model**, but *also* because his missions rewarded **static MSV-backed zone control and repeated scoring**, while the Go-Pod tends to be exposed after one spectacular attack run. This is **not conclusive evidence Go-Pod is bad**.
- **Stronger skeptical account:** Robert Shepherd’s **Tunguska** review (~60–69min) says the nominally strong Go-Pod often **loses orders in early ARO fights**, is extremely hard to conceal (**S7/no cover**), **dies quickly once hit at ARM2**, and cannot safely enter hostile **Repeater/KHD** zones. It wants other units to **clear heavy AROs and enemy hackers first**. As a later-turn finisher it can be exceptional; as the *list’s sole primary assault gun* it is unreliable.
- **Actionable build test:** for any Go-Pod list, write down **(1) first-turn ARO clearer, (2) hacker-killer/network cleanser, (3) landing-safe parking spot, (4) objective or target achievable with the fast run, (5) recovery plan**. If you cannot answer all five, redirect 32–33 points toward a scoring/screen package. **Avoid assuming the expensive HRL is a better investment than 0SWC MULTI Rifle**; it directly competes with a TAG or Riot Missile.

## Disagreements to test, not erase

1. **Is Bakunin mainly a hacking sectorial?** Robert/Dante: flexible defensive Mimetism, Pitchers, Firewall and TAG-hacker make meaningful network play. **MetaChemistry winner**: explicitly used **just Uhahu with Riot Firewall(-6)** and no Guided/TAG-hacker; melee and ARO area control were sufficient for his particular five-round wins. *Rule*: hacking is a **tool**, not a required strategic identity.
2. **Stigmata S-tier or A-tier?** Loss of Lieutenant podcast **both**: high tier for uniquely resilient hacker that fits well; lower tier due to BS13/3-dice active shooting, ARM6 and some weapon limitations. Robert: an **opponent-problem/ARO and opportunity attacker**; do not expect it to solo dismantle a prepared enemy. Buy for denial and repaired board presence.
3. **Stigmata or Lizard?** Stigmata defensive hacker vs **Lizard BS14 MULTI HMG direct attacker**. Robert likes spec-firing E/M grenades at null-deployed opponents; a risk-averse player will prefer **HFT multi-HMG** or another gun for predictable kills. Repeater is not uniquely valuable when Bakunin already has Pitchers/FO repeaters.
4. **Go-Pod mandatory?** Some players see its threat range and locally deem it auto-include. December 2025 winner rejects it for mission fit, table vulnerability and model availability. Robert Tunguska review argues its **high ceiling is paid for by a very low floor** and heavy support tax. Use for a specifically planned late-game angle, not as default.

## Practical two-list audit

- [ ] Decide whether **both lists** emphasize **zone/room and close combat**, **MIDFIELD hacking/REMs**, or a **TAG plus support**; missions justify switching.
- [ ] Before taking a Haris, **allocate your sole Haris** (Moderator+Clockmaker+Vostok vs Riot/Observance/Taskmaster). **Armored can be Duo**, separate from the Core/Haris cap.
- [ ] Include **at least one strong active ranged gun** with reliable hit probability (Riot Spitfire/Kusanagi/linked Vostok/Lizard): Stigmata+Go-Pod+Taskmaster HRL by themselves can all fail sustained high-Burst ARO breaking.
- [ ] Include anti-smoke/MSV for **zone defense** (**Riot MSV1, 25pt Orphan MSV2, Rounder MSV1**, depending on mission); **White Noise/Albedo** counter-plan also needed.
- [ ] Keep an **objective specialist** alive for Round 3 (Zero FO, Orphan Engineer, Stempler FO, Bran, Riot specialist, Meteor, appropriate Reverend); winning melee alone does not automatically score objectives.
- [ ] Count **Regular / Irregular / Impetuous / Tactical Awareness / Lieutenant / NCO** separately. **Morlock and Überfall** are not extra standard Regular Orders by default.
- [ ] If taking **Stigmata**, pair an Engineer and consider firewall/KHD backup; if taking **Lizard**, clear hostile hacking lanes; if taking **Go-Pod**, clear enemy hackers **and** serious long-range AROs first.
