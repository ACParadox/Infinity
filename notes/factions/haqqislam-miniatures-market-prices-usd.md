## Duplicate, pack-selection and edition considerations

- **Hassassin Expansion Gamma ($20.00)** lists **two Fiday, Hussein Al Djabel and an Ayyar**. The **Hassassin Áyyār Viral Pistols ($9.20)** is a separate listing; the same unit identity does not prove identical sculpt. Other Fiday and Djabel sculpts may overlap.
- **Dire Foes: Obsidian Head ($19.80)** versus three **$7.04** singles: **Rouhani, Trisha, Underworld Fixer**. Singles sum to **$21.12**, so the listed pack saves **$1.32** before shipping, if physically the same miniatures. Trisha is not a native Haqqislam troop.
- **Beyond Red Veil Haqqislam ($13.40)** gives **Tarik, Fiday and Ragik**, all included in the **Beyond Red Veil Expansion Pack ($27.30)** listing alongside Yu Jing figures. **Operation: Red Veil ($54.70)** and the **Haqq side-only Operation: Red Veil ($27.30)** are another full/split-box comparison. Do not pay twice for the same faction half unless duplicates are intended.
- **Two Haqqislam support-pack prices:** **$21.60** for the listing describing Nasmat remotes and Ghulam Doctor/Najjarun Engineer resculpts, versus **$18.80** for “Haqqislam Support Pack new” with explicitly **Ghulam Doctor, Najjarun Engineer and two Nasmat**. Packaging/sculpt equivalence is **not established** from the excerpts.
- **Individual vs grouped weapons:** Azra’il **Feuerbach $12.70 vs $10.30** are similarly named listings, without sufficient information to call one a resculpt. Azra’il AP HMG is different. Govads HMG **$8.40** vs Govads box **$26.40**, Kaplan Spitfire **$6.80** vs four-Kaplan box **$22.70**, Tuareg Sniper **$6.80** vs the Haqq Starter **$24.30**, and Naffatun single **$12.10** vs Naffatun pack **$26.80** need separate model counts and sculpt checks.
- **Cross-faction merchandise in the category:** the Hùndùn and Haidào are **Yu Jing**, Bìxié is a Yu Jing named character, Trisha is **ALEPH**, while Scarface/Cordelia and other mercenary or fiction-character models have conditional or separate roster access. Store-category inclusion **does not establish Haqqislam N5.3 legality**.
- **Edition and completeness:** Bìxié is expressly **exclusive**, Haytham is **unassembled resin**, and several boxes have truncated or lore-only descriptions. Do not invent figure counts or current sculpt dates. Items marked “Add to cart” may still be unavailable at checkout.
