# Hassassin Bahram — List-Building and Tactical Note

**Reference archive:** [Scope, primary recordings, N5.3 cross-references, battle reports and purchasing audit](references/hassassin-bahram_refs.md). **Roster and Fireteams:** [Hassassin Bahram N5.3 reference](../../sectorials/haqqislam/hassassin-bahram.md).

## Overview

**What Hassassin Bahram does exceptionally well:** make the opponent spend Orders *discovering, clearing and second-guessing* threats. The army layers **Hidden Deployment, E/M mines, Decoy, Impersonation, Jammers and cheap Pitchers**, then attacks the openings with selected gunfighters rather than one overgrown Fireteam.

### Particularly strong or unusual Fireteams

- **Ghulam Core/Haris — cheap Pitcher and support shell:**
  - **Ghulam:** required base, Orders, Doctors and inexpensive FO.
  - **Barid:** low-cost specialist, Pitcher or anti-hacker.
  - **Muyib/Govad:** exact Smoke, E/M, engineering or gun jobs.
  - **Asawira Wildcard:** fighting/Doctor upgrade, not obligatory.
- **Bahram Haris/Core — mission-dependent combat module:**
  - **Lasiq:** Viral ARO or midrange control.
  - **Muyib/Govad:** Tactical Awareness, Smoke and utility choices.
  - **Bokhtar FTO:** only **options 1, 2, 5 and 6** may take this Bahram team row.
- **Shakush Duo:** **Shakush** plus selected **Avicenna, Sayiq or Muyib** support.

The current chart permits **one Core, one Haris, unlimited Duos**. A Fiday, Shujae or Áyyār is not a Fireteam member simply because it cooperates with one. See [canonical Bahram teams](../../sectorials/haqqislam/hassassin-bahram.md#fireteams).

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **Concealed/Impersonation saturation:** **Shujae, Áyyār, Nadhir, Sunduqbut, Fiday and Al-Djabel** force different Discover and response decisions.
- **Very cheap board friction:** **Ghazi Jammer/E/Marat**, **Daylami Panzerfaust**, **Shujae E/M mines** and **Barid Pitchers** attack different weaknesses; too many Irregular defenders weaken the active Order budget.
- **Eclipse/Disco:** **Asawira Boarding Shotgun Disco**, **Áyyār Disco** and **Claire Doctor Disco** are selected profiles, not universal Smoke.
- **Smoke and visors:** Smoke-equipped **Ghazi, Ghulam NCO Smoke GL, Muyib Smoke GL** plus **Yara MSV2**; 5pt E/Marat and 6pt Jammer Ghazi have **no Smoke** in this roster.
- **Command:** unusually useful **Farzan CoC** combines backup LT and infiltration.
- **Gap:** **Shakush TAG** is present, but **Maghariba, Zeybek/Haytham and any standard VH** are absent. Haqq parent-faction access does not carry over.

## Core identity — layered uncertainty, disruption, then exploitation

**Modern HB is primarily an asymmetric ambush/control army, not a conventional linked-gun sectorial.** It can field a formidable Fireteam or an aggressive HI, but its signature advantage is to make the opponent waste Orders deciding *what a piece is*, *whether to Discover*, and *which dangerous ARO to address first*.

A typical sequence:

1. **Deploy layered threats:** hidden Shujae/Nadhir, Daylami markers, Jammer or E/Marat Muttawi'ah, overlapping Flammenspeer/E/M mine lanes, and a concealed LT shell.
2. **Force unattractive decisions:** Discover versus Move/Dodge/Reset versus attacking the visible roadblock. A cheap or unseen piece can punish every option.
3. **Preserve the attack budget:** the threat of the ambush often extracts Orders even without dealing damage.
4. **Exploit the result:** Áyyār, Asawira, Bokhtar, Fiday/Al-Djabel, Ragik, McMurrough, or a compact linked Lasiq package attacks weakened lanes or fragile infrastructure.
5. **Score:** HB can carry substantial specialist density; do not spend all its Orders killing when the mission needs buttons or zones.

The strongest conceptual lesson from the Attack on Atlanta 2025 winner: **build something that can survive going second**. That winner lost the initiative roll in all five games, and the reported *main list* used two Áyyār, two Shujae, two Barids, cheap disruption and a Guided option, **without a Fiday in that main list**. This is an example, not proof that there is one solved build.

## Shujae — first defensive anchor to price in

**Current standout:** option 3, **27pts: SMG + Flammenspeer + E/M Mine + Minelayer (2)**, Hidden Deployment, Mimetism (-6), Forward Deployment (+20), Specialist Operative. AVA 2.

- Build an ambush where the opponent already needs to manage a Daylami, smoke route, Mutt Jammer, or exposed objective.
- The enemy's choice between cautious Discover/Move and an aggressive attack becomes much less clean once a **hidden Flammenspeer** can reveal.
- The E/M mines matter even if they never hit: they distort the safest path and make heavily armoured/hackable opponents spend Orders.
- **Double Shujae** is a major repeating N5 pattern; Shujae 2 can cover a different axis or threaten the opponent's fallback after the first has been exposed.
- After the traps trigger, a surviving Shujae remains a WIP14 specialist. Preserve its ability to complete the mission, not just trade it as an ARO.
- Other Shujae profiles are legitimate: 27pt boarding shotgun + Shock Mine Minelayer (2), 30pt Viral Rifle, or 29pt AP Rifle + D-Charges. Select for attack/missions rather than automatically duplicating the E/M version.

**Trap:** deploying both Shujae in Hidden Deployment can mean accepting fewer usable early Orders until they reveal. Budget that deliberately.

## Áyyār — the modern flexible problem-solver

**Current standout:** option 1, **40pts: AP Rifle, E/Mitter, Viral Pistol (+1B), BS Attack (+1B)**. AVA 2; Hidden Deployment, Holoprojector, Surprise Attack (-6), Triangulated Fire, Specialist Operative, 2 Wounds.

- **Triangulated Fire** is a Long Skill that ignores ordinary range, Cover and Mimetism MODs on the shooter's Roll; Burst modifiers still apply. It still requires LoF and range within the weapon's maximum range.
- Use it when the opponent's defence is mostly **negative shooting MODs**, not reflexively against every target. Check effective Burst, the ARO, and kill probability.
- Viral pistol (+1B) is especially threatening at short range; AP Rifle + E/Mitter gives an answer to other target categories.
- Hidden Deployment and Surprise Attack create an opening where the opponent cannot simply pre-position every defence against it.
- Alternative options are *real*: 39pt Thunderbolt (AP) + Zapper; 38pt Breaker/Panzerfaust/Drop Bears; 39pt AP Marksman Rifle/Drop Bears; 38pt Breaker/Panzerfaust/**Disco Baller**, with Forward Deployment (+10) on the latter three.
- **Double Áyyār** gives separate angles, redundancy and more credible hidden threats; it is supported by the Atlanta-winning *main list* and other sample lists.

**Trap:** two 40pt specialists plus two Shujae are a substantial investment. That can be justified, but still provide Order generators, a way to solve hard AROs, and your mission's scoring needs. Triangulated Fire is not a magic answer to the enemy's saves.

## Barids and the hacking shell

- **Barid KHD:** 14pts, Pitcher, Trinity (-3), WIP14. Most natural low-cost hacking first pass.
- **Barid HD:** 16pts/0.5 SWC, Pitcher. Adds conventional hacking/control and Spotlight/immobilization options.
- Both are native to the **Ghulam Core/Haris family** (up to two Barids), not the separate Bahram-family row.
- A Ghulam/Barid home Core can deploy Pitchers more efficiently and support ARO removal. **Do not assume a lone hacker must be the main active attacker.**
- **Guided branch:** Barid/Repeater → Spotlight → **Shaytaniyah Guided ML (16pts / 1.5 SWC)**. Strong against pieces that cannot easily leave/clear the network. The Guided bot is optional, not a tax on including Barids.
- **Leila Sharif:** 22pt KHD with Firewall (-3), Carbonite upgrade, Shock Marksman Rifle, E/M Mine; legal Wildcard. Helps protect/control an important mixed team.
- **Govad HD/KHD:** Warhorse + Combat Instinct; useful when you expect isolation attacks or Stealth intruders to stress the network. Can join Ghulam or Bahram families.
- Other network pieces: **Rafiq** Sensor/FO/Repeater, **Kameel** support/repeater, deployable repeaters from Ghulam, and the 19pt **Rafiq FTO** Red Fury as a *solo* utility/attack option (FTO label does **not** grant HB Fireteam access).
- Buy at least one real mission attacker alongside this network. Hacking pressure itself does not score objectives automatically.

## Cheap roadblocks — Daylami and Ghazi are not interchangeable

### Daylami

The standard disruptive marker option is **8pt Camo (1 Use) + Infiltration + Panzerfaust** (option 6), not a full permanent Camouflage lifecycle. AVA 5. The bare 6pt Panzerfaust is also useful.

- Cheap camo/Panzerfaust presence creates positional pressure against TAGs/HI and warbands.
- An opponent who must Discover first has often spent an Order for no positional progress.
- Once the Daylami is revealed, plan for it to become expendable; its value was frequently earned through diverted enemy Orders.
- Intermingle with Sunduqbut threats when appropriate; a marker/decoy shell rewards ambiguity.

### Ghazi Muttawi'ah

**Visibility choices by actual profile:**
- **5pt E/Marat Ghazi:** no Smoke.
- **6pt Jammer Ghazi:** no Smoke.
- **7–8pt shotgun Ghazi:** combined Smoke profiles.
- **Ghulam NCO Smoke GL 15/1 / Muyib Smoke GL:** projected corridors and linked utility.
- **Disco-equipped profile:** Eclipse-like answer to MSV lanes. Compare Smoke/Eclipse by the tactical job. See [vision-control analysis](../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).

Current options range **5–8pts**, AVA 4, Irregular + Impetuous, Dogged. Profile-specific distinctions matter:

- 5pt **Chain Rifle + Smoke**: cheap self-funding movement/smoke.
- 5pt **Chain Rifle + E/Marat**: short-range E/M denial.
- 6pt **Chain Rifle + Jammer**: order-tax/communications threat, **no smoke on this profile**.
- 7pt boarding shotgun/E/Marat/Smoke or 8pt boarding shotgun/Jammer/Smoke: pay for a combined package.
- Place them where their *own* Irregular and any safe Impetuous Orders can accomplish something. You may decline an unsafe Impetuous activation; do not run a Jammer into gratuitous death.
- Do not mistake a poor Regular Order economy for a lot of activations: Impetuous/Irregular models may self-fund themselves, **but they cannot directly fuel Áyyār/Asawira/Bokhtar**.

**Normal starting heuristic:** about 1–3 Irregular blockers in a balanced list. **Deliberate exception:** a dedicated defensive-saturation list can take far more, but it must accept weaker active-turn flexibility. The GMG/Owen list below illustrates that exception.

## Fireteam philosophy — use the team for a specific job

Current source chart: **Core 1, Haris 1, Duo unlimited.** Valid families:

1. **Ghulam — Core/Haris**; requires at least one native Ghulam, and can include up to two Barids, two Muyibs, one Govad, Avicenna or Shakush pilot Sayiq where permitted.
2. **Hassassin Bahram — Core/Haris/Duo**; Bahram-member required choice comes from **Govad / Muyib / Lasiq / Bokhtar FTO** (Bokhtar FTO **options 1, 2, 5, 6** only).
3. **Shakush — Duo**; Shakush native, with Avicenna, Sayiq or Muyib access.
4. **Wildcards:** Asawira; Yara Haddad; Leila Sharif; **Yasbir FTO option 1 only**. Yasbir FTO and Leila count as Ghulam/Bahram per current chart.

The canonical row at sectorials/haqqislam/hassassin-bahram.md decides legality; *not* an appealing profile name.

### Go-to FT 1 — home Ghulam Core

**Ghulam LT (+1 Command Token) + Ghulam + Barid KHD + Barid HD** (four members; can adapt with an NCO/Doctor/FO).

- Cheap Orders, natural LT hiding, Pitchers and hacking.
- Change a Ghulam to the **15pt/1 SWC NCO Grenade Launcher + Smoke Grenade Launcher** when you want to spend the LT Order through an NCO-led team.
- NCO transforms the Special LT Order into a Tactical Order; **if the NCO is the Team Leader, that Tactical Order may activate the entire Fireteam**. Do **not** activate the LT directly with its personal LT Order and assume the team remains together.
- All profiles still need their assigned role: buying both Barids and a big Core is not automatically optimal.

### Go-to FT 2 — compact Ghulam/Barid/Asawira Haris

**Ghulam + Barid + Asawira (usually AP Spitfire or Doctor)**.

- A compact assault/gun team with a hacker and useful WIP14 specialist infrastructure.
- **Asawira is a Wildcard**, not a native Ghulam/Bahram row member.
- Prefer this over a five-model brick when the points saved fund another hidden threat or objective specialist.

### Go-to FT 3 — Lasiq Bahram team

**Lasiq Viral Marksman + Lasiq FO + optional Asawira** (Bahram family) or **Lasiq + Govad hacker** Duo.

- Lasiq options: 25pt/1 SWC **Viral Sniper**, 25pt/0.5 SWC **Viral Marksman with BS Attack (AP)**, 22pt **Viral Rifle FO**. X-Visor, Marksmanship, Mimetism (-3), Climbing Plus.
- This is the **conventional ranged killing alternative** to investing every premium point in hidden attackers.
- Govad MSV1 + Warhorse/Combat Instinct can add a different kind of protection to a team; choose by table/matchup.
- **Leila + Lasiq/Muyib/Govad** can provide a wildcard hacker whose offensive gun isn't the team's only purpose.

### Go-to FT 4 — Muyib Tactical Awareness module

**Muyib HRL + another Bahram native (e.g. Lasiq or Govad)**, with optional Leila/Asawira, or **Muyib + Ghulam** in Ghulam-family Core/Haris.

- **22pt HRL / Tactical Awareness / X-Visor**, or **32pt Spitfire / Tactical Awareness**.
- Tactical Awareness allows the **TA user as Fireteam Leader to activate the whole team** with its Tactical Order.
- Other useful Muyibs: smoke Grenade Launcher, Viral Mine Minelayer, Doctor (+3), Forward Observer.
- Do not buy a costly long team solely to obtain one Tactical Order; compare the cost with running independent pieces.

### Go-to FT 5 — Bahram control Duos

Examples to consider:

- **Lasiq + Govad hacker:** covered shooting plus durable network control.
- **Leila + Muyib HRL TA:** compact hacking / defence / TA mobility.
- **Bokhtar FTO + another Bahram native or Wildcard:** mobile premium TA package, but stay within Bokhtar FTO restriction.
- **Shakush + Muyib** or **Shakush + Avicenna**: a *different Shakush-specific Duo family*, not a Bahram-family mix.

**These names/FTO suffixes do not independently confer Bahram Fireteam access:**
- **Áyyār**
- **Shujae**
- **Fiday**
- **Ragik**
- **Daylami**
- **Muttawi'ah**
- **Rafiq FTO**
- **Claire Lazhari FTO**

A coordinated battlefield combination is not automatically a Fireteam.

## Lieutenant and succession packages

### 1. Ghulam LT (+1 Command Token) — safest baseline

Current option 19 is **15pts, +1 Command Token**. Easy to conceal among Ghulams. Pair with NCO smoke/grenade launcher if that is valuable to the team; normal Ghulam LT is cheaper at 11pts.

- Home Ghulam Core is a convenient shell; keep LT away from obvious blast/assassination paths.
- Farzan CoC is *optional* in an otherwise well-protected, non-aggressive LT plan.
- Spending the personal LT Order on the LT is a separate question from NCO use; don't accidentally break your Fireteam.

### 2. Barid LT — economy and camouflage of identity

Current **12pt Barid FO LT** or **16pt/1 SWC HD LT**. Useful when you want another apparent Barid, but the actual hacking LT carries risk if exposed to KHD/hacking counterplay. Do not automatically make the LT the frontline Pitcher operator.

### 3. Bokhtar LT + Farzan CoC — aggressive TA plan

Current **46pt Red Fury LT FTO** or **45pt Viral Rifle Paramedic LT FTO**.

- Bokhtar has **Tactical Awareness** and 15–5 MOV, MSV1, Mimetism (-3), Super-Jump (Jet Propulsion).
- A personal LT Order on a fighting LT can be productive, but understand Fireteam activation and possible breakage if choosing to spend it personally; TA can activate a team when Bokhtar is its leader.
- **Farzan CoC** stays separately concealed, not in the same vulnerable pocket as the LT.

### 4. Asawira LT + Farzan CoC — durable frontliner

**33pt AP Rifle LT** or **38pt AP Spitfire LT**; W2/ARM4/BTS9, Regeneration, potent CC. A high-impact choice if your plan already budgets an Asawira. Protect with CoC and avoid exposing the LT merely for a marginal gunfight.

### 5. Yara Haddad LT — MSV2 utility branch

**24pt LT** or **27pt LT + Strategic Deployment**; good if your FT/visibility plan values MSV2 and her AP Marksman Rifle. Compare with non-LT Yara and a safer Ghulam.

### 6. Muyib LT — a deliberate shell-game alternative

Current 21pt smoke-GL LT. May be attractive inside a Muyib-oriented plan, but is not inherently safer than a cheap Ghulam LT.

### Farzan CoC placement rule

Current **24pt Farzan BSG + Shock Mine + CoC**, Camouflage/Infiltration. AVA 3 overall. Treat it as a **separate protected succession asset** that can also score/contest midfield. **Never place LT and CoC together in a Fireteam or obvious single-template kill zone simply for convenience**: that can lose both to one attack.

## Main attacking branches

### Asawira — independent or linked conventional bully

- AP Spitfire 38pts/1.5 SWC; Doctor (+3) AP Rifle 37pts; other useful Disco Baller/BSG, Viral Rifle and AP Rifle options.
- W2, ARM4/BTS9, BS14, CC23, Regen, Dodge (+5). Asawira is a **true top-tier direct fighter**, not obsolete because Áyyār is popular.
- Use when you need to **break a visible lane** or anchor a compact Haris. Doctor version can save significant investment.
- Watch Frenzy/exposure and don't let a 35–38pt piece get isolated from realistic recovery.

### Bokhtar — fast visible gunner/mission runner

- Red Fury FTO (46pts/1.5 SWC); Viral Rifle Paramedic FTO (45pts); Parachutist alternatives are **not FTO**.
- Built-in TA, Super-Jump (Jet Propulsion), MSV1 and Mimetism. Good when the job requires lateral movement, difficult elevation or rapid scoring, not just assassin-style deployment surprises.
- Bokhtar LT + CoC is an explicit list concept; ordinary non-LT Bokhtar is equally useful if safer LT construction wins out.

### Fiday and Hussein Al-Djabel — assassination with *target discipline*

- Fiday 23–26pts; Impersonation, smoke and high CC, AVA 2. Hold as **reserve** when counterdeployment matters.
- Ideal victim may be **LT, key sniper, hacker, specialist, medic/engineer, or cheerleader**, not the opponent's strongest TAG.
- Al-Djabel is 33pts, has MA4, Viral CCW and Climbing Plus: more committed elite assassination branch.
- An Impersonation model's value includes forcing awkward deployment, bodyguard investment and moving targets away from useful lanes.
- **Double Fiday** is a dedicated assassination theme; don't blindly purchase it on top of every other premium option.

### McMurrough — cheap independent violence / smoke

- Current 23pts; Irregular/Impetuous, 15–10 MOV, Super-Jump, Dogged, Chain Rifle (+1B), Smoke, strong AP+DA CC.
- Useful for forcing responses around corners, killing blockers, clearing a path and finishing something revealed by the network.
- A very common pick in player discussions, but **not obligatory**: one can choose better Regular Orders or another specialist in a list with a different plan.
- Impetuous movement is not mandatory suicide. Think carefully about smoke placement and the path of his own Irregular Order.

### Ragik — off-table exploitation

- 25pt **Hacking Device / BSG / D-Charges** is a real airborne specialist; 23pt BSG (+1B) and 29pt Spitfire provide other angles.
- A Ragik threatens models and objectives beyond the static Barid network. Plan an insertion lane and enough usable Orders before buying it.
- Dogged is a means of finishing an attack; avoid losing a specialist that the mission needs later.

## Defence and deception packages

### Shujae + Daylami + Jammer Mutt

- Put one *obvious* bad ARO in the lane, one cheap marker where the opponent must respect it, and a **hidden Shujae** to punish committing to the wrong solution.
- Important: an ARO trap is only good if it covers a **real route/objective**, not empty terrain the enemy can ignore.
- Reveal Shujae for a meaningful shot or denial effect, not just because a target entered the general area.
- If placing Jammer and Smoke capability on the same Mutt, use the profile that **actually has both**; the 6pt Jammer option does not.

### Shujae + Nadhir

- Nadhir brings Hidden Deployment, Mimetism (-6), Forward Observer and alternatives such as Flammenspeer, Panzerfaust, AP Marksman or Thunderbolt.
- Useful as a second surprise layer on a different angle or an independent mission specialist.
- **Do not over-stack hidden bodies** unless you consciously accept the initial Regular Order shortfall.

### Sunduqbut + Daylami marker shell

- Sunduqbut (AVA2) is **not Hidden Deployment**: it has **Camouflage (1 Use), Decoy (1), Forward Deployment (+20)** and Remote Presence.
- Variants: 42pt Viral Rifle Neurocinetics (+1B); 47pt Feuerbach Neurocinetics; 43pt Viral Rifle (+1B) + Mines/Minelayer; AP Spitfire options.
- Use when you want to *hold an area* with an ambiguous marker and a substantial reactive/direct-fire threat. Combine with Daylami markers to make enemy deployment/Discover choices less certain.
- **Double Sunduqbut is a real specialist defensive archetype**, not the generic balanced-list default. It is expensive and replaces other active-turn options.
- Pay special attention to silhouette, approach geometry, mine coverage and whether it can be bypassed; a stationary bunker is wasted if the battle moves away.

### Shihab / Lasiq defensive range package

- Shihab HMG Total Reaction (23pts/1 SWC) or **Thunderbolt (+1B) Total Reaction (18pts/0 SWC)** gives predictable defensive coverage.
- Lasiq Viral Sniper adds more specialist infantry firepower. MSV/Marksmanship roles are not interchangeable; plan the actual ARO matchups.
- Avoid a single expensive visible ARO turret as the entire defence. It can be isolated/removed while the hidden layers are what demand difficult choices.

## Less-used and sleeper choices

### Govad hacker / engineer

- HD (25pts/0.5 SWC), KHD (23pts) with Warhorse + Combat Instinct; a good partner for a Lasiq or Muyib Bahram team.
- **Govad Engineer (25pts)** has AP Mine, D-Charges, GizmoKit; an unusual repair/specialist candidate in the correct legal team.
- Govad HMG / AP Sniper / ML can substitute for less specialised ranged packages when MSV1/Combat Instinct matter.

### Leila Sharif / Yara Haddad

- Leila brings versatile short-range hacking, E/M Mine and Wildcard link utility.
- Yara's MSV2 + AP Marksman can pair well with **planned smoke**; check Zero-Visibility shooting, deployment and useful ranges, not just the advertised synergy.
- Yara **Strategic Deployment** costs extra; use it if the actual deployment plan benefits.

### Muyib smoke GL, minelayer and Doctor

- Not every Muyib needs to be the TA HRL. **Smoke GL**, **Viral Mine Minelayer**, **Doctor (+3)** and FO profiles can fix structural weaknesses.
- Especially relevant to resilient second-turn teams and objectives.

### Shakush TAG

- Current **HMG (Continuous Damage) 58pts** or **AP Spitfire 56pts**, each with TA and **Jandab ancillary specialist**.
- A legitimate more visible **armoured tempo** archetype, with a Shakush-family Duo (Muyib, Avicenna or Sayiq possible), but it competes with Bahram's exceptional cheap ambush toolkit.
- Do not dismiss it solely because the internet's most common lists are hidden-infantry builds. Equally do not assume the TAG is secretly mandatory.
- A **Shakush + Muyib** team is an interesting way to add another tool to the TAG plan; ensure team legality and positioning are deliberate.

### Rafiq, Fanous, Kameel, Najjarun/Nasmat, Avicenna

- **Rafiq FO/Repeater/Sensor/Triangulated Fire (13pts)** is a useful active specialist and marker-clearance piece.
- **Fanous** cheap Regular Flash Pulse/Repeater support; especially valuable when the rest of HB includes several Irregular troopers.
- **Kameel** adds support and Repeater infrastructure; build it for a role, not only as a cheap filler.
- **Najjarun Engineer + Nasmat** can protect your TAG/REM investment and fix damaging states; **Avicenna** is a major Doctor alternative with Ghulam/Shakush team access.
- Avoid treating a double-hidden, double-impersonator, multiple-warband list as needing no recovery plan.

### Yasbir and Al-Djabel as alternate tricks

- **Yasbir FTO** is a 25pt Wildcard Holomask/Viral Rifle/Drop Bears option which counts as Ghulam/Bahram; *other Yasbir options do not link*. The Parachutist/Decoy variant is an independent attack plan.
- **Al-Djabel** trades up into a named elite impersonator if your mission strongly rewards high-certainty target deletion. Compare its cost with a cheaper Fiday and the quality of the target.

## Practical list archetypes to test

### A. Balanced “standard Bahram”

- **1 Áyyār, 1–2 Shujae, Barid KHD**, plus Ghulam LT/Orders and one other Barid/Ghulam specialist.
- 1–2 Daylami/Mutt roadblocks.
- One real attack specialist: **Asawira, Bokhtar, McMurrough or Fiday**.
- Optional compact home Core / Ghulam Haris; cheap Fanous/Rafiq where needed.
- **Farzan CoC if LT exposure justifies it**, not automatically if a safe Ghulam LT remains buried.

**Objective:** learn to run hidden defence and active attack without order starvation.

### B. Double-hidden “going-second ambush”

- **2 Shujae + 1–2 Áyyār**, plus 1–2 cheap roadblocks.
- Barid hacking, cheap support Orders; independent exploitation gun; enough mission specialists.
- Deliberately deploy each surprise unit into a *different decision point*, not one blast/mine cluster.

**Objective:** punish an enemy attack regardless of the winning deployment/initiative roll.

### C. Conventional Asawira / Lasiq hybrid

- **Asawira Spitfire or Doctor**, Lasiq viral gunner and a compact legal Haris/Duo.
- One Áyyār / Shujae or a pair of hidden defenders; Barid and Ghulam support.
- Stronger visible F2F shooting, with enough hidden friction to protect it.

**Objective:** trade away some deception density for reliable direct damage.

### D. Network / Guided control

**Indirection choices:**
- **Shaytaniyah Guided 16/1.5:** payoff when Barid HD + Pitcher and FO/Repeater access actually apply.
- **Daylami GL 10/.5:** economical Speculative harassment, not a complete offensive engine.
- **Muyib Rifle GL + Smoke GL 21/1:** projected pressure in a legal team.
- **Fiday / Asawira / other direct attackers:** may remove a critical enemy with fewer setup Orders.

See [whole-game indirect-fire decisions](../list-building/indirect-fire-speculative-guided-apex-value.md).

- **Barid HD + KHD; Repeater geometry; Shaytaniyah Guided**.
- 1–2 Shujae E/M mines; Mutt Jammers or E/Marat; selective Áyyār or Ragik.
- Objective specialist and late-turn killer.

**Objective:** make enemy movement and Reset/Discover decisions miserable, then score. Don't rely on missile kills as the only winning condition.

### E. Defensive “Sunduqbut wall”

- **1–2 Sunduqbut**, Daylami marker shell, Shujae/Nadhir hidden AROs, selective Mutt roadblocks.
- At least one affordable attack/counterattack and sufficient Regular Orders to win after the wall is attacked.

**Objective:** defend a constrained table or a mission with a specific contested room/sector. Owen's list is the extreme version, not a universal 300pt template.

### F. Aggressive LT / mobility

- **Bokhtar LT + Farzan CoC**; complement with Áyyār/Shujae, Ghulam support and another fast/flanking weapon.
- Choose Bokhtar FTO or Parachutist options according to the **actual** FT/attack plan; Parachutist is not FTO.
- An Asawira LT is an alternative if you need reliable brute-force shooting rather than speed and elevation.

**Objective:** spend meaningful command resources on an attack, with separate succession insurance.

### G. Impersonation strike

- **Fiday or Al-Djabel (possibly two Fidays)** to threaten LT/support/ARO shell before the main army advances.
- Other specialist assault route, Shujae defence and a sensible Regular Order budget.
- Reserve the impersonator when seeing the opponent's actual deployment changes the target significantly.

**Objective:** win on positional pressure and surgically remove the piece that enables the enemy's plan.

## Repeated mistakes and disagreements

1. **“Always take a Fiday / McMurrough / Asawira.”** All are excellent, but real lists win without one or more. First buy the list's function, not the famous names.
2. **“Every sectorial needs a big Core.”** Not HB. A four-model home Core, small Haris, a utility Duo or *no Fireteam* can make sense.
3. **“Daylami and Mutts are cheap, so fill the rest with them.”** Cheap does not equal a Regular Order; group placement and Turn 2/3 economy matter. Dedicated defensive lists are the exception.
4. **“The hidden units are free Orders.”** Hidden Deployment may reduce usable early Orders; do not put your whole plan off table.
5. **“Triangulated Fire always beats the enemy gun.”** It ignores MODs to the Roll, not armour/saves, and it still needs effective weapon, range and Burst.
6. **“Sunduqbut is bad / mandatory.”** It is a strong role-specific defensive investment. The Double Sunduqbut GMG list demonstrates a style, not a consensus auto-include.
7. **“Pick the most expensive target for the Fiday.”** Removing one key hacker, Doctor, LT, ARO, or two cheerleaders can be worth far more than dying against a heavily guarded TAG.
8. **“LT + CoC together is safer.”** A clustered LT and CoC can die in the same attack. Keep the Farzan separate, concealed and relevant to later scoring.
9. **“FTO means the REM links.”** Rafiq FTO and Claire Lazhari FTO lack a Bahram Fireteam chart row; don't infer access.
10. **“Old published 2025 prices/profiles still apply.”** The current Army-derived repository reference prevails over older videos/list screenshots.

## Build-order checklist

1. **Mission and terrain:** where does the opponent have to move, what scores, what are the exposed long lanes and restricted S2/S3/S6 routes?
2. **Reactive trap:** 1–2 Shujae, cheap Daylami/Mutt, or a different intentional defence.
3. **Active attacker:** select Áyyār for MOD-resistance/hidden attack, Asawira for direct fighting, Bokhtar for speed/height, Fiday for target removal, McMurrough for smoke/close trading, or Ragik for an airborne angle.
4. **Hacking and marker clearance:** Barid(s), repeater path, a Sensor/Rafiq plan where useful. Only buy Shaytaniyah if Guided is actually in the strategy.
5. **Command:** safe Ghulam LT + NCO where useful, or fighting LT + **separately protected Farzan CoC**.
6. **Fireteam:** choose the job and only then its legal family. Need Pitcher efficiency? Ghulam. Viral/direct ARO? Bahram Lasiq. TAG mobility? Shakush Duo.
7. **Order audit:** count **Regular usable Orders**, Irregular self-funding, Impetuous risks, Hidden Deployment bodies, and TA/NCO activations; check the crucial first two turns.
8. **Mission redundancy:** enough surviving specialists to score late if the first ambush and first attacker both die.
9. **Adversary plan:** enemy primary gun, Repeater net, LT/CoC shell, DZ strike, recovery and reserve. Deploy to make their first turn expensive.
10. **Endgame:** which piece can capture/hold the key objective on Turn 3 after most of the killing is done?

## Short version

**Most repeated HB foundation:** **Shujae Minelayer + Barid KHD + efficient Ghulam command/orders**, frequently with **Áyyār**; Farzan CoC when a fighting LT merits protection. The attack slot is a choice among **Asawira, Bokhtar, Fiday/Al-Djabel, McMurrough, Ragik, Lasiq**. Cheap Daylami/Mutt roadblocks and an optional Fireteam reinforce the plan.

**Three legitimate different ways to win:** (1) double-hidden ambush/control; (2) compact Ghulam/Barid + conventional Asawira/Lasiq fighting; (3) heavy Sunduqbut/Daylami defensive saturation. **Don't force them all into one list.**

### Community tactics — transcript-backed N5 research (2026-10-22)

> **Evidence status:** The roster, profiles, equipment, points, AVA, and Fireteam chart **above** remain the N5.3 / Army `7.26246.158` authority. Everything below is *community tactical assessment*, not official rules or a change to the reference data. #### One-minute read

- **Win asymmetrically:** Bahram is strong at attacking an opponent's *weak* piece from an unexpected angle, not at outshooting the enemy's best gunfighter. Create bad choices with Impersonation, hidden threats, mines, jammers, smoke and cheap AROs.
- **Three viable list identities:** (1) *Ambush/denial:* Shujae, Áyyār, Nadhir, Daylami, Sunduqbut; (2) *Professional Fireteam:* Ghulam + Barid smoke/pitchers, Asawira, Muyib/Govad/Lasiq; (3) *Hybrid:* some hidden threats **plus** a mobile gunfighter or Shakush. Building both tournament lists with differing offensive/defensive emphasis is more useful than treating one archetype as compulsory.
- **Hidden Deployment is not free:** A hidden unit generates **no Order while hidden**, and the Shujae/Áyyār/Nadhir lack Camouflage to re-conceal safely after revealing. Predict routes, do not reveal merely because a target appears, and budget active-turn Orders based on *visible* Regular troops.
- **Protect Order economy:** Cheap Daylami and Ghazi are excellent, but their Irregular/Impetuous Orders do not replace a workable Regular pool. Link some utility, keep specialists, and ensure pricey models get enough Orders to earn their cost.

#### Real disagreements — retain both sides

- **Shakush value:** *Positive:* Robert Shepherd's later Vanilla review finds the cheap BS14 Continuous Damage HMG TAG efficient; Jandab/Sayiq give objective and support coverage in Bahram. *Negative:* Tactical Awareness's N5 HB panel jokes that a Shakush slot is often better spent on **several more troops** or exceptional Asawira/Áyyār/Bokhtar/Sunduqbut, while Musterkrux (April 2026) finds TAG Duos mostly inefficient. **Resolution:** pick Shakush for a specific gun/lane/mission need, not as a Bahram auto-include; avoid transplanting Vanilla Hafza-Duo praise into Bahram.
- **Defense saturation vs usable Orders:** Tactical Awareness's Owen showcases an extreme defense-heavy low-Regular-Order tournament-winning list; Robert Shepherd/Musterkrux caution that Hidden Deployment plus Irregulars can starve your active turn. **Both valid:** saturation can deny a specific opponent, but count actual usable Orders *before* an ambush reveals.
- **Lasiq vs Sunduqbut vs Govad:** Lasiq advocates favor linked Mimetism/Marksmanship/Viral at range; Sunduqbut advocates prefer Decoy/Camo/Neurocinetics resilience and threat ambiguity; Govad advocates value MSV1/Combat Instinct/Warhorse in a link. Pick based on expected range, cover and opponent tricks rather than a universal ARO champion.
- **Avicenna vs Asawira Doctor:** Some hosts regard a 28-point dedicated Doctor as poor value beside an Asawira Doctor that can also fight; she still offers a fast WIP15 Doctor (+3), NWI and unusual survival package where that matters. No universal verdict.
- **Nadhir vs Shujae/Áyyār:** Cheap 20-point hidden FO has a real scoring/Flammenspeer niche; higher-budget hidden options have scarier kill/reveal potential. Mixing types helps force mistaken enemy assumptions.
- **Accessibility:** DDDDan describes one of the best defensive factions; Counterintelligence calls the defenses easy to *access* but hard to *pilot well* on offense. Hidden Deployment/Impersonation timing and which enemy piece to scalp create the learning curve.
