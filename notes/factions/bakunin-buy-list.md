## Proxy choices from owned models: precise silhouette, right visual archetype, no double counting

Apply [the repository's detailed proxy policy](../miniature-proxy-guidelines.md): **match actual profile's exact silhouette (base AND height), preferably the same troop class and similar armour/clothes, then weapon/role/appearance**. A model whose *named troop itself belongs in this force* is a poor proxy for another unit, even when free in today's list. A target being legal never makes its sculpt owned. Declare unusual proxies to opponents and use event/TO policy.

## Whole-unit skips, profiled bad buys, shared purchases across armies

The most direct *unit-family purchase deferral* is **generic Zellenkrieger** when Morlocks provide the same main smoke/trade job cheaper. Do **not** turn that into a ban on **Zellen FTO** or **Extreme Zellenkrieger**, distinct useful special profiles. **Moderator Spitfire** is poor SWC gun by reviewer versus Riot/Vostok, but basic Moderator, Pitcher/FO remain valuable. Some successful Bakunin lists use no TAG at all; don't assume Stigmata and Go-Pod are required. Morlock/Chimera/Pupnik/Jackbot/ TinBot counts are not independent orders.

**Shared purchase planning:** Compare the other three [Vanilla](vanilla-nomads-buy-list.md), [Corregidor](corregidor-buy-list.md), [Bakunin](bakunin-buy-list.md), and [Tunguska](tunguska-buy-list.md) files; an owned model is **one physical miniature**, even if reused legally in several armies. The [Ariadna Caledonia buy list](caledonia-buy-list.md) has an *unowned* Denma Connolly lead, while its **McMurrough is really owned**; don't confuse those. The [PanO NCA](nca-buy-list.md), [Steel Phalanx collection](../../inventory/steel-phalanx.md), and [JSA proxy guidelines](../miniature-proxy-guidelines.md) provide alternative borrowed human/REM figures, but buying a model for Nomads does **not** silently update the inventory.
