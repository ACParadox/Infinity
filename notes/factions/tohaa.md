## One-minute assessment

- **Identity:** Multiple **Triads/Haris**, two Duos maximum, no Core; pair **Mirrorball/Eraser/Endgame** with Symbiont-equipped Wound reserves and Kaeltar **SymbioMates** to make the opponent fight unfavorable battles. The strength is **control of *which* fights occur**, not winning every straight BS contest.
- **Premier attack:** **Sukeul HMG (33/1.5)** with Mimetism -3, BS13 and Symbiont durability is the most natural conventional active gun. **K1 sniper/FO** provide *different* anti-armor or scoring jobs. Alternatives: Ectros HMG NCO, Gorgos AP HMG, Neema AP Spitfire, Draal AP Marksman, Kosuil K1.
- **Premier control:** **Kriigel (21/.5)** for Eraser + Mirrorball in a compatible link; **Taqeul WIP14 (35–36/0)** for Mirrorball + Endgame and excellent LT/CoC options. Endgame/Eraser pressure **W**, not STR. A robot/TAG cannot be controlled by assuming biological Pheroware works on it.
- **Premier resilience:** **Kaeltar** 15/0 (one Mate), 21/.5 (CoC + one Mate), 28/.5 (CoC + two Mates). The Mate spends a key order absorbing/softening a save; it is **limited, triggered equipment**, not always-on ARM9.
- **Competitive problem:** 2–3 attractive 20–40pt pieces plus a Mate/CoC support shell burn points and slots fast; no Netrod/Flash REM + Baggage order swarm, narrow normal hacking, and a handful of merely BS12–13 long-range guns. Aggressive offense + protected ARO + hidden midfield can crowd out cheap orders. **Decide the mission plan before building three expensive fireteams.**
- **Ranking disagreement:** The 2025 Tactical Awareness group found Tohaa versatile and locally tournament-winning; Shepherd/Jordan's **summer 2026 top-of-C** conclusion argues other factions' N5 multiwound, Eclipse and heavy-gun upgrades erode that advantage. Both can hold: Tohaa remains **dangerous at executing *specific* denial plans** while its *generic all-comers power* is less automatic.
- **N5.3 update important:** the summer review was expecting the Varangian Dog-Warrior; current Tohaa **does include Ioann Bann (32/0)**. Reassess smoke/CC trading with this actual profile, without pretending a single addition proves a new tier.

### Practical legal formations and opportunity cost

- **Triad:** choose any *legal* up-to-three members from [FT-01](../../sectorials/tohaa/tohaa.md#ft-01--triad-fireteams). Example roles: **Sukeul HMG + Makaul + Kamael FO/Paramedic** (gun / vision + CC / scoring), or **Taqeul + Kriigel + Kaeltar** (Pheroware / Mate / command), or **Gao-Rael MSV2 + Makaul + Kumotail** (visual answer / CC screen / recovery). This is a role sketch, **not three mandatory links**. Account for chart limits per model.
- **Reex Escorts FT-02:** **at least one Reex**; up to two Reex, one Kriigel and one Kiel-Saan in any *legal 2–3 body team*. Cheapest path to a linked control piece. Kriigel “counts as Reex” here but **does not remove Reex minimum**.
- **Chaksa FT-03:** **at least one Chaksa Longarm**; up to three Longarms, at most two **Chaksa Aux FTO**, at most one Kiel-Saan, inside permitted 2–3 model team. Not a generic way to make every 10pt Chaksa an FTO without the required Longarm.
- **No Core; unlimited Haris/Triads; maximum TWO Duos.** Pair count and the **full 15-trooper / combat-group order budget** constrain the list more than “everything links with everything” shorthand. No official generic Wildcards are declared; do not import Spiral Corps.
- **Two useful axes rather than a single death ball:** (1) one serious gun plus Mirrorball/Pheroware; (2) one objective tool plus cheap roadblocks/specialist recovery. A third large Haris is optional **only if its distinct job and order source are clear**.

## TAG / Vehicles / REMs — actual 2026 faction options

**Do not import other factions' unit categories:** [the authoritative 40-entry roster](../../sectorials/tohaa/tohaa.md#standard-roster-index) contains **one TAG (Gorgos)**, **no VH-class aerial/ground vehicles**, and **no standard REM-category combat/repeater bots**. Tohaa has an **8–16pt Motorized Bounty Hunter** on a motorcycle (LI: fast Irregular troop, **not VH**) and organic/support units which fulfill *some* common REM roles. **Triphammer** mentioned in older Tohaa/TAG discussions is **not** on this current normal roster; an old-era mercenary or Spiral Corps selection does not override current Army data.

**Gorgos disagreement:** *Tactical Awareness E37* argues 63–70pts gets a remarkably durable, BS14 AP-HMG TAG with a nasty E/M/Pulzar peripheral; at least one host likes it and discusses list use. *Loss of Lieutenant E142* is significantly more reserved (rough C-tier placement; several discussants had not played into it); Gorgos is valuable as **Tohaa's** AP weapon yet not automatically a top cross-faction TAG. **Use it when your list cannot otherwise crack high-ARM/STR lane holders and can keep a scoring plan**. Skip when Sukeul K1/FO + Kosuil/Draal + mission workers already solve the problem more efficiently.

**REM and hacking implications:** Do **not** plan EVO/Assisted Fire/RemDriver/Flash-bot/Guided-ML/Repeater-bot engines by analogy with ALEPH or PanO; current Tohaa lacks their corresponding standard troop network. It *does* have **Kamael Hacking Device (17/.5), Wardriver (14/1 or 16/1 with Disco Baller), Aelis KHD**, and **Deployable Repeater** on Kamael/Sukeul FO. These provide *situational* network play, not broad, free repeaters. **Pheroware is biological area-control, not standard hacking**, and cannot substitute for anti-TAG Oblivion or a dense Spotlight network.

## Four practical plans

**A. Mission-first flexible:** LT **Taqeul(+1 Order)** or cheaper **Kamael/Sakiel**, one **Kaeltar CoC/Mate** if LT exposure warrants it, **Sukeul HMG**, **one Kriigel**, a **Kamael specialist/Clipsos FO or Draal**, plus **Kaauri/Chaksa 10pt** lane screens. Fill remaining orders only after selecting actual two-triad/one-duo uses. Prefer a **reserve specialist** to a third redundant sniper.

**B. Pheroware holdfast:** Taqeul + Kriigel in different legal Triads or two denial packages; Kaauri Mirrorball where spare SWC; cheap bodies/mine threats around midfield; direct BS gun waits until enemy has committed to an approach. **Endgame/Eraser do not stop STR models**—budget K1/AP/E/M for them and make placement before relying on “all approaches covered.”

**C. Anti-TAG/AP pressure:** **Gorgos (63 or 67)** as primary attacker *or* **Sukeul K1 sniper/FO + Kosuil K1 + Neema AP Spitfire**, with Makaul/Mirrorball vision and an Engineer/Doctor for other valuable troopers. Don't buy both an expensive Gorgos + multiple long-range/HI fireteams unless enough orders and scoring specialists remain.

**D. Impersonation/mission assassination:** **Jaan Staar OR Kiiutan** (never both), cheaper Greif as alternative; force a reaction on a key sniper/LT/engineer/hacker; then deliver Sukeul/Kriigel, and save a Clipsos/Draal/Kamael to **score** after the assassination. An early kill with no objective/order benefit is a poor trade.

**End-of-list checklist:** 1) Which **two scoring units** can reach objectives without the main gun? 2) How do I beat **one ARM/BTS-heavy TAG/STR target** after realizing Pheroware does not affect it? 3) Which **cheap lane screens** stop AD/impersonators and guided/fire templates? 4) What is my **turn-two plan if the Sukeul/Gorgos is dead**? 5) Are the 2 Duos/Haris actually legal in the [current Army chart](../../sectorials/tohaa/tohaa.md#canonical-fireteam-definitions), and which combat group will spend their orders?

## Historical/edition boundaries and validation

- The dedicated October 2025 tactical show, the summer 2026 tier panel, and the Spanish July 2026 interviews **predate Army source 7.26246.159**. Their strategic reasoning is useful, not their profile tables or precise “auto include” rankings.
- **Spiral Corps** was a separate former NA2 sectorial with distinct link/AVA/mercenary options; its discontinuation affects the models' relative value and nostalgia but does not resurrect its Fireteams. The current Tohaa roster retains Reex, Kiel-Saan, Draal and related units **only under the Tohaa chart**.
- Gorgos is the only normal-roster TAG, and the faction has **no VH** and **no standard REMs**. Drone-like biological pieces and a motorcycle should not be mislabeled to meet a “TAG/REM/VH” checklist.
- This is tactical **analysis**; for every points/SWC, required min, profile exception and special skill, defer to [the pinned September 2026 Tohaa reference](../../sectorials/tohaa/tohaa.md) and [N5.3 rules](../../rules/README.md).

## Vision control — role/value update (23 October 2026)

- **Repeatable Eclipse:** **Kriigel 21/.5 Mirrorball** (no-LoF/WIP/ZoC) or **Taqeul 35–36/0** if the command/Pheroware secondary role pays; **Kaauri 14/.5** and **Igao 26/.5** diversify the approach. **Makaul 14/0** is still the cheaper **Eclipse grenades + HFT + Triad/CC** choice.
- **Opposing plans:** favor Kriigel for multiple precise repeated corridor closures; favor Makaul when a single advance also needs an HFT/CC deterrent. Mirrorball/Disco are not White Noise and normal Smoke is not guaranteed vs MSV. [Apex/value comparison and videos](../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).

## Toolbox / jack-of-all-trades assessment (2026-10-23)

**Draal32/0 Dazer or33/0 Viral Mine** is a highly effective forward Specialist/screen/denial shooter (FD20, Mimetism -6, 2W). **Kriigel21/.5** handles repeatable Mirrorball+Eraser but is not the same generalist gun; **Taqeul35–36** adds command. Opposing evaluation: multiple Triad toolbox specialists provide mission resiliency, but too many midline 30pt bodies can dilute the **Sukeul HMG** attack budget.

#### 4. Concrete list-building implications

1. **First buy the essential capabilities:** one protected long gun (**Sukeul HMG**), one Pheroware/visibility plan (**Kriigel** or **Taqeul**), one SymbioMate/CoC plan (**Kaeltar**), a couple of actual objectives specialists, and one cheap rear screen (**Kaauri / Chaksa 10**). The **Delegate** is almost free but Irregular.
2. **Choose one attack architecture.** Traditional **Sukeul + Makaul/Ioann Bann + specialist** Triad advances with visibility control; newer **Samsa + Kiiutan/Greif + Ioann Bann** uses already-forward bodies for short, order-efficient strikes. **RS26** illustrates both.
3. **Do not neglect STR threats.** Eraser and Endgame are biological/Vitality threats; take real **K1/AP/E/M**, especially Sukeul K1, Kosuil K1 or Gorgos AP HMG. Use actual hacking only if bringing **Kamael FO/Repeater + Hacker** or other legal network sources. **MC23** opponent-side caution aligns with this.
4. **Two distinct scoring paths beat a third luxury ARO.** Hidden **Clipsos**, a linkable **Kosuil/Kamael FO**, or an AD **Gao-Tarsos Paramedic** must reach and complete missions after the main gun has fought.
5. **Current fireteam legality controls.** No Core; unlimited Triad/Haris and two Duos. Reex team requires Reex; Chaksa team requires Longarm. **Ioann Bann** has current Triad compatibility; don't import former Spiral fireteam charts. Jaan Staar and Kiiutan **share AVA1** and cannot appear together.

#### Disagreements and edition boundaries

- **Source-backed ranking is not statistical truth.** RS26 is unusually explicit about “take” and “avoid” but is one player's experience. TA25 and MX26 are valuable balancing perspectives. **RS-tier26's top-of-C faction verdict** does not mean every Tohaa profile is weak.
- **Ectros NCO:** [existing guide](tohaa.md) allows the LT-order conversion angle; **RS26 calls its premium a trap**. Keep both viewpoints: cost and lack of Mimetism tip the default ranking to avoid.
- **Kiel-Saan:** RS26 is decisively negative; MX26 has a defender. Treat as deliberate off-meta assault investment, **not** literally impossible to win with.
- **Makaul:** formerly a near-auto Triad warband; modern **Mirrorball** and **Ioann Bann** reduce the need to buy one in *every* list. Still excellent for reusable Eclipse + HFT + serious CC.
- **Gorgos:** TA25/LoL25 debate capability relative to price; RS26 finds it hard to fit. It is the **only current TAG**, not directly dominated by Sukeul's much lighter chassis.
- **N4 sources are strategic context only**; Sept 2026 Army roster controls availability, current costs, legal teams and exact profiles. **No conventional REMs/VH units** appear in current base Tohaa roster, and the Motorcycle Bounty Hunter is not a VH.

**Bottom line:** Near-default **Kaeltar + Sukeul + 5pt Specialist Delegate**; fill core go-to roles using **Kriigel/Taqeul, Ioann Bann/Makaul, Kaauri/10pt Chaksa, Taagma/Kosuil**, then select independent striker or hidden scorer packages to match the mission. Avoid **Ectros NCO, Kiel-Saan, 24pt Neurocinetics Chaksa**, and overpriced secondary Spitfires as general-purpose default purchases; keep disputed niches explicitly conditional.

**Suggested collection order absent inventory and mission details:**

1. **First-model priorities (not a fixed army list):** **Kaeltar15**, one **Sukeul HMG33 or K1 FO29**, **Delegate5 Specialist**, a real visibility plan (**Kriigel21, Taqeul35 or Makaul14**), and cheap **Kaauri11 / Chaksa FTO10** where useful. Add the scoring specialist your selected missions require; note the Delegate is **Irregular**. [RS26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=93L3POjJKkU) and [TA25](https://open.spotify.com/episode/5zhPiR44EzbQ4GFKkICRvG).
2. **Buy one legitimate distinct game plan next:** hidden **Clipsos FO**, **Draal** Dazer/area defense, **Samsa** independent infiltration, **Kiiutan/Greif** impersonation, **Taagma** anti-smoke sniper, or **Gorgos** supported TAG. Don't buy all of them solely to cover their cards.
3. **Proxy-tested specialist investments:** **Kiel-Saan/Reex/Longarm**, **Aelis**, **Kumotail**, **Neema**, **Rasail + Chaksa**, **Kerail + beasts**, **Ectros NCO or other alternative gun**, **Gao-Rael Spitfire**, **Armand**, expensive third/fourth Sukeul, extra Makauls/Chaksas/snipers.
4. **Buy the models your lists repeatedly need simultaneously**, with all required peripheral bodies and correct silhouettes—not every AVA gap or alternate weapon profile. One clearly declared proxy model can test **several mutually exclusive options in different games**, but cannot be several troops at once.

**Proxy/event caveat:** All temporary substitutions must be **clearly identified**, correctly **based and silhouetted**, with complete physical body count for deployed peripherals; get opponent approval in friendly games and check the **current ITS/event organizer's** proxy rules for tournaments. A proxy does not change **unit access, shared AVA, Symbiont/Transmutation state, link eligibility, Irregular orders or the rules printed on the selected profile**. Ancient Spiral Fireteams, unrelated combined-army biotech and older edition profiles are not restored by swapping miniatures.

**Bottom line:** **Whole-unit deferrals** — Kiel-Saan first, then *niche-with-caveats* Aelis/Kumotail/Neema/Rasail/Kerail/Gorgos/Longarms; **strong profile-only avoids** — Ectros NCO HMG, Sakiel/Kamael/Gao-Rael Spitfires as generic shooters, Chaksa Neuro HMG, expensive Sukeul ML when K1 sniper suffices, Kaeltar 28pt two-Mate premium, Greif non-IMP infiltration and non-specialist Delegate in missions requiring a Specialist. **Proxy-first even when the unit is good:** alternative Sukeul/Kaeltar/Makaul sculpts, a second Kiiutan/Jaan alternative (never both in one list), extra high-end snipers, specialist characters, multiple physical peripheral packages and the lone Gorgos TAG. **Do not avoid the basic Tohaa triad engine or cheap objective/defensive pieces because another profile of the same troop was reviewed negatively.**
