## Purchasing verdict

**No dedicated Yu Jing Vanilla collection is recorded** in your inventory. The strongest **existing actual cross-army savings** are the **two Yáoxiè Rui Shi / Lù Duān REM chassis** already in [Shindenbutai](../../inventory/shindenbutai.md), the shared **one Warcor** and **one Beasthunter**. There are also plausible but non-identical **PanO Dronbot/Palbot/Mulebot** and **ALEPH Yudbot** proxy pools. Do **not** double count any shared chassis or assume four new Yu Jing remotes.

**Recommendation order for a Yu Jing army you will actually play:**

1. **A Dāoyīng Lieutenant / Hǎidào utility / Zhēnchá specialist group:** your **Invincible Army Expansion Pack (3) for conditional CAD $22.38** is a strong deal if the $16 overseas quote really is USD and available. Three units are Vanilla legal, despite the sectorial label; Dāoyīng's **LT(+1 Order)** and Hǎidào **KHD/Pitcher** are particularly relevant. The physical **Dāoyīng Hacker is NOT the same printed weapon as an LT Multi Sniper**, but can represent another loadout of the same unit subject to opponent/TO policy. [401 Games CAD $36.95](https://store.401games.ca/products/infinity-yu-jing-invincible-army-action-expansion-pack) is a currently identifiable Canadian alternative.
2. **Mówáng (S5 HI) or one complementary S5 Yān Huǒ:** $12/$9 leads respectively, **CAD $16.78/$12.59** conditionally. The **Mówáng NCO + Hǎidào Duo** is a source-supported Vanilla attack/specialist architecture. **Yān Huǒ HRMC** adds genuinely different anti-armor firepower. Don't buy two bulky HI by reflex before deciding which role is missing.
3. **Sù-Jiàn two-form pack:** **CAD $27.97** conditional from the $20 lead. Two separate **S3 high-mobility and S5 combat** figures are necessary to represent both forms correctly; excellent distinct kit, difficult to proxy with ordinary humans/robots. Confirm original versus newer **resculpt** before buying. Canadian [Tistaminis CAD $55.00](https://tistaminis.com/products/infinity-yu-jing-su-jian-immediate-action-unit-new) is a comparator.
4. **Support Pack (4)**: conditional **CAD $16.78** (doctor, engineer, two Yáozăo servants) is attractive for sustain-heavy heavy infantry/TAG rosters. You already own **2 ALEPH Yudbots** and **2 PanO Palbots** as possible S1 servant proxies if suitable and not used elsewhere; do not buy kit merely to reach Yáozăo AVA3. [Tistaminis CAD $44](https://tistaminis.com/collections/corvus-belli-infinity-miniatures/products/infinity-essentials-yu-jing-yu-jing-support-pack), currently shown sold out.
5. **Tiger Soldier AD or a few Shaolin/Gūdǎn:** Tiger is valid but not universally required; buy/test **one** for flanking missions. Current Yu Jing reviewers favor **Gūdǎn cheap defensive traps** and **Shaolin smoke/CC** even though they were absent from your research shortlist. Test these before buying many niche named characters.
6. **Best larger starter:** **2025 Essentials Yu Jing Action Pack**, SKU **281346-1127**, **10/10 models Vanilla legal**, Canadian [401 Games CAD $99.95](https://store.401games.ca/products/infinity-yu-jing-essentials-action-pack). Its Zúyŏng, Dāoyīng, Hǎidào, Zhēnchá, Húláng, Liú Xīng and Zhanshi supply an entire legal physical army base. **This is NOT your $27 older White Banner-based 10-model box**.
7. **Delay** Léi Gōng, generic solo Shàng Jí, costly Hac Tao Hacker, extra Yān Huǒ/Hùndùn sculpts, expensive TAGs/Vehicle, Táowù, Yuan Yuan, Jujak and Dragon Lady **as Vanilla-specific buys**. The last four are **not Vanilla troop selections** in the pinned roster (Dragon Lady is HVT). Other sectorial use and collector value are separately valid reasons.

**Most consequential listing correction:** There are **two different “Yu Jing Action Pack” groups and two different “Booster Pack Alpha” eras**. Order by **SKU + full contents + photo**, not just seller title. Current N5 roster legality is not inferred from a box label.

## Skips, old-profile traps and near-never purchase candidates

- **Whole models that are not currently fieldable in Vanilla Yu Jing:** **Táowù (ISS)**, **Jujak/So-Ra Kwon (White Banner/reinforcement)**, **Yě Māo and Jing Qo (White Banner)**, **Yuan Yuan (NA2/other eligible rosters)**, **Dragon Lady (HVT)**. **Do not buy these solely for Vanilla.** Their appearance in a box with “Yu Jing” on the front is not evidence of ordinary Vanilla legality.
- **Generic solo Shàng Jí when not forming a useful Duo** is a disputed value compared with Mówáng, Hac Tao or Yān Huǒ / Haetae. Buying all 3 soldiers in a box can be value for **IA**, but **Vanilla Shàng Jí AVA2**.
- **Hac Tao Hacker solely as a standard objective button pusher** often wastes points compared with Zhēnchá/Hǎidào/Gŭiláng. **Hac Tao NCO gunfighter remains a strong unit**, not an avoid.
- **Dàofěi generic expensive forward attacker** overlaps cheaper camo specialist jobs; contested rather than useless. Proxy before dedicated sculpt if no specific list.
- **Four Kuang Shi out of habit** can crowd the 15-trooper slot economy; one/two can remain valuable. Shepherd versus Tactical Awareness explicitly disagree. Don't purchase to AVA4 for its own sake.
- **Extra one-shot expensive centerpieces** (Gūijiă, Blue Wolf, Lóngwáng, Fēiquán Vehicle, Hac Tao, Sù-Jiàn, Mówáng, Yān Huǒ) should be **alternatives**, not an accumulating mandatory shopping cart. Fēiquán **VH** requires suitable flying/vehicle geometry; do not call an owned TAG a correct VH proxy by default.
- **Older outdated weapons**: original Mówáng **MULTI Rifle** description, old Taowu Pulzar wording, historical Shàng Jí gun profiles and older starter prints may not directly match 2026 N5.3 legal loadouts. Same-unit model can represent other current legal weapons with correct declaration, but no rules carry over from N3/N4.
- **Redundant box buys**: 2025 Essentials Action Pack plus IA Expansion adds second **same named** Dāoyīng/Hǎidào/Zhēnchá bodies (different loadout sculpture). Useful if you specifically want alternate configurations, not eight completely new unit types. White Banner Expansion + old Booster Alpha duplicates **Hac Tao Hacker**. Jujak box contributes **zero Vanilla troops**.
- **Model edition premium**: 2025 Essentials pack is a separate product assembly, not automatically a complete brand-new sculpt line; **newer Sù-Jiàn genuinely described as resculpt**, **Léi Gōng new SKU described as repackage**, and **Dragon Lady / Yuan Yuan actually have identifiable LE *alternates***. Don't pay collector prices for ordinary commercial blisters.
