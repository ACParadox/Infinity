## Skips and cross-sectorial model reuse

**Not a universal unit veto:** source-audited weakest generic purchase is **Rouhani purely as a backline Doctor** compared with cheaper Ghulam Doctor; Wildcard/healing reliability is meaningful exception. Full Druze, Kaplan, Janissary teams can be powerful if planned, but don't buy five solely because AVA allows. **Proxy first** for low-play-frequency characters, extra expensive TAGs, optional aircraft and second attack pieces; do not buy all AVA models to complete the collection.
