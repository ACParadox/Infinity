## Army-specific buying verdict

Qapu is flagged **discontinued in Army metadata** but has a **current, complete N5.3 roster and Fireteams** on `main`. Your collection has one **Warcor**, but no exact Ghulam, Hafza, Sekban, Odalisque, Al Hawwa, Djanbazan or Azra'il figures. The generic ten-model Haqqislam Action Pack contains only **three Qapu-legal Ghulams**. Prefer a **vintage six-model Qapu Starter** if reasonably available, or the **six-model Haqq Reinforcements Alpha**, whose listed names are all standard Qapu legal.

One physical figure can represent one body in a particular list, and peripheral/turret/Jackbot/Nasmat/Scarface+Cordelia models need their own correctly sized representations. Event policy may limit proxies.

## Skips and cross-sectorial model reuse

**Not a universal unit veto:** source-audited weakest generic purchase is **Rouhani purely as a backline Doctor** compared with cheaper Ghulam Doctor; Wildcard/healing reliability is meaningful exception. Full Druze, Kaplan, Janissary teams can be powerful if planned, but don't buy five solely because AVA allows. Fiddler/Claire `FTO` suffixes do not grant Qapu Fireteam access; Korsan/Rafiq are profile-specific chart entries. Qapu discontinued metadata does not mean its pinned roster is absent.

**Proxy first** for low-play-frequency characters, extra expensive TAGs, optional aircraft and second attack pieces; do not buy all AVA models to complete the collection. Special/limited editions are collector options rather than required army rules.
