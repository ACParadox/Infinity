## Proxy, model-count and roster-legality controls

- **Normal robots:** Use 4 owned PanO **Dronbots S3** for the usual **Reaktion, Vertigo, Transductor, Stempler** roles, 2 owned PanO **Mulebots S4** for the Salyut logistics/baggage category, and **PanO Palbots / ALEPH Yudbots S1** for servant Zondbots. You cannot multiply the available physical pool by listing it under every faction or type. Distinct specialist REMs or unusual model sizes require separately credible donors.
- **Known physical bodies and distinctive teams:** Jazz+Billie requires 1 LI hacker and one suitable separate Billie robot. Zoe+Π-Well requires an engineer and a Pi-Well REM. Überfallkommando needs **Chimera + 3 distinct S5 Pupniks**; the owned **4 S5 Ariadna dog proxy pool** is a single physical shared pool (not four for every team). Puppetactica **Master + up to 3 PuppetBots**, Zondnautica **bike, foot, ZondMate**, Corregidor **Coyote + BambaDroid**, and **Go-Pod + Go-Bot** are multi-piece models, not multi-trooper order bundles.
- **Human proxies:** Kestrel Fennecs/Crosiers for unused **S2 LI** Alguaciles/Moderators/Securitate/Interventor; Kestrel Fireflies/Black A.I.R. for properly sized **MI**; TAK Scout / Caledonia SAS / OSS Naga for clearly distinguished S2 **SK** camo infiltrators; Steel Phalanx Myrmidons S2 WB for additional **Morlocks**. Use different figures for units actually present at the same time, and check actual S of any unusual HI or REM. **Owned McMurrough is Corregidor legal but NOT current Vanilla, Bakunin or Tunguska**; do not buy it again.
- **Big models and no false-silhouette proxy:** No known owned Nomad-styled **Szalamandra, Gator, Gecko, Iguana, Stigmata, Lizard or Anaconda**. Existing PanO TAGs or alien Caskuda do **not** automatically qualify as visually/size-matched Nomads TAGs. **Go-Pod is an S7 Vehicle (VH)** rather than a regular mecha, and S5 Pupniks cannot be represented by S6 Cameronians without a legitimate rebase/appropriate model.
- **Box distinctions:** Older Corregidor 10-figure Action Pack is a *Crimson Stone 7 + Beyond Crimson Stone 3* repack and was **CAD99.95 sold out** at 401 Games. 2025 Essentials 10 is a different 10, chiefly useful Vanilla and Tunguska; 2026 Essentials **Army Pack 7** is another box (only **2/7 Tunguska** legal in pinned roster), overlapping Essentials in **two Morlocks, Grenzer, Sin-Eater and Mary** (5 physical bodies across four unit identities). Do not order all three because each says Nomads Action/Army Pack. **Bakunin Expansion Beta** and **older Riot Grrls + TinBot** are not new Bakunin Observance sets.
- **Fireteam access:** Corregidor **Surface, Deepspace, Correctional** charts are separate contexts; select the exact FTO profiles that are chart-legal, rather than treating all generic "Fireteam" product labels as interchangeable. Bakunin Observance/Riot and Tunguska Securitate/Hollow similarly require actual profile restrictions.
- **Uncertain or discontinued:** Confirm actual product numbers, old vs new generation, missing bases (e.g. MM **Hellcats without bases**), limited editions, stock, troop count on Tomcats, Jaguars and Cheerkillers, and the proper game mode before ordering; MM/UTZ quotes are archived U, not live inventory.
