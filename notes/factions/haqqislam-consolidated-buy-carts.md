# Haqqislam — consolidated collection-completion buy carts (N5.3)

**Prepared 2026-10-30.** All four ordinary Haqqislam armies in the current repository: [Vanilla Haqqislam](vanilla-haqqislam-buy-list.md), [Hassassin Bahram](hassassin-bahram-buy-list.md), [Ramah Taskforce](ramah-taskforce-buy-list.md), [Qapu Khalqi](qapu-khalqi-buy-list.md). Current roster/Fireteam authority: [Vanilla](../../sectorials/haqqislam/haqqislam.md) **56** standard units, [Hassassin](../../sectorials/haqqislam/hassassin-bahram.md) **33**, [Ramah](../../sectorials/haqqislam/ramah-taskforce.md) **37**, [Qapu](../../sectorials/haqqislam/qapu-khalqi.md) **38**; N5.3 Army `7.26246.158` verified 2026-10-15. The per-army files preserve the existing **full roster-by-roster** unit audits; this file is a purchasing synthesis, **not** an addition to existing ownership.

**Buying principle:** At least one useful real sculpt per troop type when reasonably priced, with justified extras for regular list constructions (**3 Ghulam, 3 Daylami, 2–3 Muyib, 2 Govad, 2 Barid, 2 Sekban, 2 Odalisque**), not the unit's full AVA or one of every weapon. Rare heroes / premium or OOP sculpts / unusual motorized or large models are proxy-first or deferred. Everyday four S3 robot roles, baggage S4 and S1 servants may use existing ordinary **non-Combined-looking** PanO/ALEPH robots matching the *target* silhouette and base, not four of every chassis type.

**Prices:** Historical, user-captured [Miniatures Market Haqqislam USD](haqqislam-miniatures-market-prices-usd.md) and [Ultimate Toy Zone USD](ultimate-toy-zone-infinity-prices-usd.md) are **U — unverified live stock, checkout prices and ship-from countries**. USD→CAD at **1.3985** (2026-10-29); round individual product amounts to cents. Canadian **V** price records come from 401 Games (Toronto/Vaughan ON), Tistaminis (Hamilton ON) or current indexed Canadian product comparisons, **not checkout commitments**. The quoted CAD total is **before GST/QST, shipping, credit-card FX spreads, customs/duties and brokerage**; it is not one confirmed orderable cart.

## Owned robot pool, proxy and whole-unit skips

- **Owned exact Haqq-standard named models:** Warcor **1** shared from Kestrel, Knauf **1** in Vanilla, McMurrough **1** owned Caledonia and Hassassin-legal. The currently stored collections do **not** report a separately owned Haqq Ghulam, Hassassin, Ramah or Qapu model. These buy plans do not mark any proposed models as owned.
- **Normal Haqq robots:** Use existing **4 PanO Dronbots S3 total** for S3 **Fanous (Flash Pulse), Rafiq (Sensor/FO), Shihab (TR gun), Shaytaniyah (ML)** when silhouette/height and obvious weapon designation meet venue rules. **2 PanO Mulebots S4** can proxy Kameel baggage/EVO; **2 PanO Palbots + 2 ALEPH Yudbots S1** for Nasmat servant roles. Pools are **shared**, not four Dronbots per Haqq REM identity, and should never simultaneously represent more physical robots than exist.
- **Human silhouette donors:** **Fennecs/Crosiers S2 LI** for Ghulam/Daylami/Hafza/Odalisques as appropriate; **TAK Scout / Caledonian SAS / OSS Naga S2 SK** for Fiday/Farzan/Al Hawwa/Hunzakut; **Kestrel Firefly/Black A.I.R. S2 MI** for Mukhtar/Zhayedan/Govad and other MI; **Griffin/Hetkari/MO armoured models S2 HI** for Asawira/Janissary/Bokhtar (only where specific target S actually matches). A **Ghazi Muttawi'ah is Hassassin legal only** in pinned N5.3; do not use a Ghazi model as a Vanilla-legal unit type without declaring a separate *legal* proxy role.
- **Important physical constraints:** Asawira, Bokhtar, Sunduqbut and Azra'il may have distinct S/base silhouettes for different profiles; **do not assume all HI are S2**. Proper S7 aircraft (Haytham/Zeybek) require Vehicle appearance; normal PanO TAGs may have incompatible silhouette and faction design. Old Maghariba metal miniature is not the same sculpt as newer resculpt. Fiddler with Jackbots needs actual separately representable Jackbots. Peripheral servants and multiple-body unit states must not be counted as multiple soldier Orders.
- **Intentional skips:** Never buy all named characters, old OOP Qapu starter at collector premium, discontinued LE models, extra ordinary baggage REMs, separate remotes for four named robot types, duplicate Dire Foes pack with unrelated miniatures/HVT, all three competing Haqq TAGs, all specialist aircraft, full AVA Ghulam, every Hassassin Fiday variant, extra Shujae/Farzan heroes unless tests show repeated use. For Qapu, **Hassassin 2025 Action Pack has no legal current Qapu soldier types**; Qapu is discontinued in metadata but current N5.3 roster and Fireteams remain pinned.
- **Edition and complete-package checks:** Older Operation Red Veil Haqq-only seven and Beyond Red Veil Haqq-only three are **split seller packages**, not necessarily in stock nor the full two-faction sets. Modern Hassassin Action is a Blackwind-era repack, **not guaranteed new sculpt generation**. The dated Miniatures Market **Gamma describes four including two Fiday**, whereas Human Sphere and 401 confirm exactly **three** (Fiday, Ayyar, Hussein); never price it as four troopers. Rare Qapu N3 starter can be proxied with existing and new Qapu Reinforcement Alpha **six** rather than bought at inflated collector price.
