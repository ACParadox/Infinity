## Overview

**What Vanilla Yu Jing does especially well:** Vanilla combines **independent elite HI, broad Camo/Hidden Deployment, inexpensive screening, efficient LT/NCO Orders, multiple TAG and VH roles and functional REM specialists** without forcing a single Fireteam architecture. Its advantage is buying very different threats from the Yu Jing catalogue to work on separate flanks, rather than expecting a five-model Core to solve everything.

### Particularly strong or unusual Fireteams

- **FT-02 Haris — Zúyǒng + Zúyǒng + Dokkaebi:** a smaller HI/Spotlight/Pitcher team without a five-model Core. Use the exact vanilla FT-02 profiles, not White Banner bonuses.
- **FT-01 Duo — Mówáng + Hǎidào:** mobile NCO/Disco pressure beside a selectable KHD/Pitcher or Engineer support piece; Hǎidào does **not** carry every version's equipment together.
- **FT-01 Duo — Sù-Jiàn / Gūijiă / Blue Wolf with an eligible partner:** useful for moving heavy guns and specialists together; only combinations permitted by the chart count. **Lóngwáng and Fēiquán are not linkable in Vanilla.**
- **Distinctive restriction:** **no Core**, only **one Haris and two Duos**; **Johnny Kao** is the only main-force global Wildcard. Vanilla is not an unlimited-Duo sectorial.

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **Broad heavy platforms:** **Gūijiă TAG AVA2, Blue Wolf TAG AVA1, Lóngwáng TAG AVA1** and **Fēiquán VH AVA1** span different roles. More TAG families do not imply universal TAG superiority.
- **Separate Remote Presence HI:** **Sù-Jiàn** is powerful mobile HI/repair utility, **not** a fourth TAG.
- **Smoke, MSV and Disco:** **Shaolin normal Smoke** enables **Rui Shi/Hsien** visor shooting; selected **Mówáng Disco Baller** provides a different projected Eclipse lane denial. Keep the equipment and Orders separate.
- **Succession:** a legal **Pheasant CoC** profile can protect aggressive Lieutenant play; **Sun Tze** offers a distinct expensive command architecture.
- **Main limitation:** no sectorial Core shooting bonus and substantial hacking risk across independent HI/TAGs. Do not copy White Banner, ISS or IA Fireteam legality into Vanilla.

### Identity, priorities and list-building patterns

**Practical identity:** Vanilla Yu Jing has **excellent distributed heavy infantry, cheap defence, good enough midfield/hacking support, and LT/NCO/Tactical Awareness order efficiency**. Compared with a sectorial it exchanges big Core links for freedom to combine specialized units that never share a roster elsewhere. The main risks are concentrating 200+ points in hackable bodies, lacking a non-hackable route through enemy repeaters, and paying for troops that overlap roles.

1. **Start with mission, then LT/order engine:**
   - **Dāoyīng LT(+1 Order):** Camo command with NCO beneficiaries such as Mówáng, Krit or Hac Tao; Hacker LT can score late but is exposed near KHD/Pitchers.
   - **Sun Tze/Strategos:** separate Regular-order/deployment/Counterintelligence plan; expensive and may compete with conventional LT→NCO use.
2. **Buy two different attack angles:**
   - **Mówáng NCO Disco + Yān Huǒ HRMC:** mobile attack plus long-range armour answer.
   - **Hac Tao Hidden HMG/ML + Sù-Jiàn:** surprise/ranged attack plus fast close assault.
   - **Shàng Jí TacAware + Hǎidào/Dokkaebi:** Order concentration with specialist/Pitcher/KHD network.

   Red Fury alone does not reliably solve a hard TAG.
3. **Choose specialist and network reach:**
   - **Zhēnchá:** Camo Infiltration with KHD/FO/Minelayer.
   - **Guìláng:** midfield Infiltration and Repeater access.
   - **Dàofěi:** durable Infiltrating attack/specialist.
   - **Hǎidào 22pt KHD + Pitcher:** network and anti-hacker role.
   - **Dokkaebi 22pt KHD/Spotlight/Pitcher:** hacking and mission support.
   - **Wèibīng:** FO/Sensor/Repeater.
   - **Liú Xīng / Tiger Soldier:** airborne attack or scoring.

   A Guided plan requires Spotlight delivery, not just the missile platform.
4. **Insure against an alpha strike:**
   - **Gūdǎn (1–2):** forward one-use Camo/Zapper/HFT.
   - **Shaolin (5–6pt):** Smoke.
   - **Libertos:** Camo screen.
   - **Lóng Yá:** Panzerfaust/Minelayer trap.
   - **Chaīyì:** Flash Pulse and cheap Order.
   - **Hùsòng:** Total Reaction ARO.
   - **Kuang Shi:** situational cheap Regular Orders, not a compulsory N4-style tax, with Impetuous trade-offs.

   Block actual approach routes with templates/mines.
5. **Allocate 15 slots and extra Orders purposefully:**
   - **Long-range gun:** primary armour answer.
   - **Mobile attacker:** independent close/midrange coverage.
   - **Specialists:** two or three objective routes.
   - **Command:** protected LT and deliberate NCO plan.
   - **Screen + recovery:** cheap area denial and anti-hacking/Engineer support.

   Tactical Awareness provides personal activations, not interchangeable Regular Orders; each profile needs a useful task.
6. **Current Fireteam restrictions are important:**
   - **Limits:** zero Core, one Haris, two Duos maximum.
   - **FT-01:** Duo family; **FT-02:** Duo/Haris family, including eligible two Zúyǒng + Dokkaebi Haris.
   - **Mówáng + Hǎidào:** FT-01 Duo for move economy and better angles.
   - **Gūijiă / Blue Wolf / Sù-Jiàn / Shàng Jí:** only exact FT-01 options.
   - **Lóngwáng / Fēiquán:** no Vanilla Fireteam eligibility.
   - **Johnny Kao:** only source-defined main Wildcard.
   - **Húláng vanilla FTO IDs 3/4 / Krit Kokram FTO IDs 2/3:** exact IDs only; other FTO-2 options do not satisfy vanilla constraints.
7. **Do not copy sectorial-specific teams:**
   - **White Banner:** Haetae/Dokkaebi/Jujak large Core.
   - **Imperial Service:** Celestial Guard teams.
   - **Invincible Army:** high-AVA Shàng Jí/Yān Huǒ team access.

   Vanilla instead mixes independent Haetae fire support, ISS TAG/vehicle options and IA infiltration/HI in a single roster.

### Actual disagreements (avoid flattening them)

1. **TAGs vs HI:** Shepherd: Lóngwáng, Gūijiă and Blue Wolf are viable but usually less efficient than independent **Hac Tao / Mówáng / Sù-Jiàn / Haetae** spread around the board. Tactical Awareness: a *triple-armoured* Sun Tze/Blue Wolf/Lóngwáng/Fēiquán shell creates complementary threats, reserve deployment trickery and hacking/E/M lockdown. Take TAGs to exploit specific table/mission matchups; not because Vanilla is automatically a TAG army.
2. **Blue Wolf vs Gūijiă:** Loss of Lieutenant rates Blue Wolf's extra-Burst AP Spitfire and disposable fork strongly; newer Aug 2026 Irregular Order prefers Gūijiă's ARM8 and long MULTI HMG, which lets it spend fewer Orders in enemy hacking radius. Both views account for opponent counter-tech, but weight *attacking volume vs safe threat range* differently.
3. **Yān Huǒ vs Haetae vs Hac Tao:** HRMC for volume/high ARM insurance; Haetae TacAware to save points while preserving a big gun and occasional Guided; Hac Tao ML/HMG for hidden surprise and modifier stacking. Do not confuse the independently useful Haetae with **White Banner's linked** Haetae Guided.
4. **Linked Haris vs solo problem solvers:** Shepherd considers a two-Zúyǒng+Dokkaebi Haris a niche *Spotlight/Pitcher Special Die* method but prioritizes solo Hǎidào/Mówáng plus other independent attack angles. Tactical Awareness favors its small link/TacAware economies. In Vanilla, a link trades *flexible independent orders* for small amounts of activation economy/bonuses—not huge 5-model Core output.
5. **Automatic Kuang Shi spam?** Shepherd says no: cheap Camo/HI costs improved enough that 4 Kuang Shi waste precious 15 troop slots in many lists. Tactical Awareness still uses 4 in TAG/Sun Tze budget builds to preserve premium firepower and smoke. Context, not a universal rule.
6. **Premium infowar vs cheap network:** **Hǎidào KHD** versus **Dokkaebi KHD/HD** versus **Hac Tao hacker**. Cheap linked/unlinked pitchers generally enable more board area than paying 58 points for hidden high-armour Trinity, but the latter offers different missions, gunfighting and Hidden Deployment.
7. **Lóng Yá decline vs value:** Shepherd believes current N5 firepower makes BS11 cheap attack remotes easy to remove; White Banner tournament player Axel and Irregular Order still rate Camo, AP mines and Panzerfaust as excellent cheap order drains. Table lanes determine the winner.
