## Proxy selection under collection-wide guidelines

[Proxy rules](../miniature-proxy-guidelines.md): **same target silhouette including base and height**, similar troop class/armour/look, and avoid sculpts of units that **can themselves be selected in this army**. Use each physical model once per simultaneous fielded body, and record correct separate pets, robots, bikers and forms. Borrow **Kestrel Fennecs S2 LI** for line infantry, **TAK Scouts or OSS Naga S2 SK** for skirmishers where they are not legal named troops in the target, **TAK Veteran Kazak or Kestrel Griffin/Hetkari S2 HI** for appropriately armoured HI, **Steel Phalanx Myrmidons S2 WB** for human warbands if appearance is acceptable, and **TAK Dynamo S4 motorcycles** for otherwise missing S4 motorbikes. Confirm physical appearance, weapon and profile-specific Fireteam eligibility. No owned S6/S7 humanoid TAG is assumed to be a faithful proxy for an alien/special vehicle merely due to S.

**Non-additive model pools:** two TAK Traktor Mul/Kuryer chassis **total**; four shared **S5 dog** proxies (two USAriadna Devil Dog sculpts *rebased to S5* and two Caledonia S5 dogs); separate **two S6 TAK Dog-Warriors**, one Caledonia S6 Cameronian and one S6 McMurrough. S5 dog bodies **cannot** represent S6 Dog-Warriors/Devil Dogs/Cameronians under the repository rules; an Antipode pack needs **three S5 dogs and a handler**, plus matching models for any other simultaneously fielded canine. The one owned **112 motorcycle** does not become a second **112 on foot**. An expensive TAG, airborne vehicle or dog team with no faithful proxy is a **buy/borrow actual sculpt or skip** decision, not a free stand-in.

## Whole-unit skips and multi-sectorial purchase discipline

The [Ariadna audit](ariadna-must-have-go-to-avoid-source-audit-2026.md) shows no defensible blanket ban for all units in any army. Weak/rare/conditional means **proxy before buying**, not necessarily never field. Do not buy to AVA maximum, or buy many sniper/large attack pieces that compete for the same deployment roles without a specific list. Purchase once for [Vanilla Ariadna](vanilla-ariadna-buy-list.md), [TAK](tak-buy-list.md), [Kosmoflot](kosmoflot-buy-list.md), [Caledonia](caledonia-buy-list.md), [USAriadna](usariadna-buy-list.md), and [Merovingienne](merovingienne-buy-list.md). An exact miniature used by multiple sectorials remains one physical item, not one per force. **No inventory files modified.**
