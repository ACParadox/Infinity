# Kestrel Colonial Force — Listbuilding Guide (Infinity N5.3)

**Purpose:** practical choices, the reason each works, and disagreements worth testing. **As of 2026-10-19.** This guide consolidates Kestrel-specific video/podcast research instead of retaining repeated transcripts and historical discussion.

**Rules authority:** [Kestrel roster and Fireteam chart](../../sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md), Army revision **7.26246.158**; local N5.3 rules/FAQ supersede older videos. Costs below refer to that pinned roster, not necessarily the version a presenter recorded against. Advice attributed to a presenter is an *opinion*, not a proven tournament usage rate.

## At a glance

- **Strength:** Kestrel combines top-quality shooters, cheap *useful* Orders, mines/hidden threats, Eclipse via Disco Baller, and competent specialists. **Why it works:** the shooting pieces rarely have to pay their own enabling/support costs.
- **Common spine, not compulsory:** **Fennec FO/Disco/Sensor (13)**; **Magistrate Strategos LT (19) + 6-point FTO Jackal(s)**; a main gun (Hetkari, Griffin, Tikbalang, Squalos, Agent Maximus or Crux); **11-point infiltrating E/M GL Minelayer Jackal(s)**; then mission-specific specialist/ARO/repair.
- **Main weaknesses:** aggressive enemy Pitchers/hacking grids into your HI/TAGs; a visible Magistrate LT; Albedo/White Noise versus over-reliance on MSV; off-table pieces reducing Turn-1 Orders; one expensive brawler that cannot score objectives. Build a second attack/scoring route.
- **Disagreement is substantial.** Shepherd likes a distributed-link, high-order all-rounder. The 2026 Irregular Order hosts favor very different units and plans—one prizes Black A.I.R. ARO and a defensive Hetkari ML; the other likes more aggressive pieces and unusual Fireteams. Daboarder (WIP12) emphasizes *scenario contingencies*, Redeye and cheap disruption instead of a fixed shopping list.

## Start with one of five architectures

| Plan | Core construction | Why / cost of choosing it |
|---|---|---|
| **Balanced, distributed (Shepherd)** | Griffin + 3 Fennecs **Core**; Hetkari + TacAware Pathfinder **KESTREL Duo**; Griffin + TacAware Bipandra **KESTREL Duo**; Magistrate + 2 Jackals **COLCORP Haris** | Multiple independent strong guns, specialists, special Orders and cheap Regulars; uses the one Haris slot on the order engine. Costs troop slots and requires careful positioning |
| **Heavy Fennec Core (Irregular Order alternative)** | Fennec base + Black A.I.R. sniper for ARO **or** Fennec + Agent Maximus + Bipandra for an aggressive Core; add small independent attacks | Stacks specialist utility and strong bonuses on one formation. A clustered Core is more vulnerable to loss of the critical gun/link and can be slow to reposition |
| **Hetkari Haris (defensive or offensive)** | Hetkari + Firefly + Bipandra/Quinn, ensuring **two Kestrel-counting members** for full composition; ML for strong ARO, MULTI Red Fury for aggressive shooting | Medical/engineering cover and exceptional FtF quality. Near 100 points and occupies the sole Haris; Pathfinder Duo usually costs substantially less |
| **TAG assault** | Solo **Tikbalang LT +1 Order**, or **Squalos Mk-II** (GL or NCO MULTI HMG), plus cheap command, hacking/repair countermeasures and scoring pieces | Heavy shooting and mobility with a concentrated Order budget. Tik LT risks LoL; Squalos is more economical but lacks Tik's Mimetism/Climbing Plus |
| **Mobile/asymmetric (Daboarder; experimental podcasters)** | Magistrate/Jackals support **Redeye (sometimes two)**, Peacemaker mine/Repeater, Banshee/Scarecrow/Crux surprise, or a Drummer battery | Exploits side/rear angles and forces the opponent to divert Orders. Less resilient if expensive mobile units are countered before attacking; mission access still needs specialists |

**Fireteam limits:** **1 Core, 1 Haris, unlimited Duos**. FENNECS Core/Haris needs ≥1 Fennec; KESTREL Duo/Haris needs ≥1 Griffin, Hetkari or Firefly; ARMORED Duo needs a Tikbalang or Squalos; COLCORP Duo/Haris needs a Magistrate. Wildcards never waive those minima. *FTO* on a profile alone does **not** grant link access; e.g., the current Kestrel chart does not list Clipper or Yamabushi FTO.

## Command and cheap Orders

| Unit/package | Why it works | Competing interpretation / when to pass |
|---|---|---|
| **Magistrate Strategos LT (19) + Jackal FTO (6)** | Strategos L1 and Counterintelligence preserve order tempo; **FT Master (Jackals)** turns linked Jackals **Regular at Order Count**. Duo = 25 points/two Regular troopers; with two Jackals Haris = **31 points/three Regular troopers**, plus cheap Panzerfaust/LRL defence | A named, visible LT and FT Master are a vulnerability: after the link/master is lost, next-turn Jackal order generation worsens. The two Magistrate profiles (LT + separate **21-point CoC**), ideally in **different protected positions**, are even safer but consume points and AVA 2 |
| **Infiltrating Jackal E/M GL Minelayer (11/0.5)** | Places a mine *before spending an Order*; threatens speculative E/M into expensive targets; opponent wastes Orders hunting it or risks a crippling hit. **Shepherd advocates at least two** and likes one in each Group | Printed **Irregular** and not a COLCORP FTO. A list short on group slots, SWC or specialists may take only one. Irregular Order is a free local shot *only if the Jackal is safe/able to attack* |
| **Jackal FTO Panzerfaust vs Light Rocket Launcher (6 each)** | **Panzerfaust** offers longer useful lanes and a punishing hit/multiple saves; **LRL** covers closer approaches with a reusable continuous-damage template and is useful against clustered attacks | **Irregular Order Sep-2026 hosts disagree on ideal ARO** but both lean Panzerfaust for open lanes, LRL for cross-lane or nearer defence. Cheap Jackals still die easily if offered fair FtF |
| **Tikbalang LT (+1 Order, 73)** | Regular + its own Tactical Awareness + **two Lieutenant Orders** means **four self-funded Orders** when used solo; high BS/Mimetism/Climbing Plus convert them into actual kills. **Shepherd likes this aggressively** | Irregular Order agrees the TAG is strong but prefers other threats in some lists. If it dies, loss of command can be worse than the gun loss; take independent CoC when worthwhile |
| **Magistrate Strategos vs NCO** | Strategos converts LT Order into a Regular Order, often more valuable for a **Redeye/Banshee**; leaving it unconverted allows a **Squalos NCO or Firefly/Sforza NCO** to use it, potentially **across Combat Groups** | Daboarder deliberately switches modes by round: early NCO Squalos for suppression, later Strategos for a fast scoring piece. **Choose at Order Count, not halfway through the turn** |

**TAG Duo order trap:** A **Tik LT + Pathfinder TacAware Duo** looks like six potential activations from two Regular Orders + two TacAware Orders + two LT Orders. **Only the first four are naturally Duo activations**; the Tik's personal LT Orders do *not* automatically activate its partner and may split the Fireteam. Compare **solo Tik LT**, **non-LT Tik + Pathfinder** or **Squalos NCO + Pathfinder** before counting “six Duo Orders.” The 17-point TacAware Pathfinder is worth upgrading from 14 mainly when its extra team activation gets used.

## Vehicles, TAGs and REMs — cross-PanO transcript findings (19 Oct 2026)

**Read this as choices, not a tier list.** All costs/Fireteams use [Kestrel N5.3](../../sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md); outside-sectorial links are **not** automatically legal in Kestrel.

### TAG decision: what problem is the big gun solving?

| Pick | Why it works | Why choose something else? |
|---|---|---|
| **Tikbalang 69/1.5; LT +1 Order 73/1.5** | **Fast self-funded attacker**: BS15, Mimetism (-3), Climbing Plus, HFT and Shock Mine. A solo **LT** can spend its Regular, TacAware and **two personal LT Orders** on aggressive repositioning/attacks. **Loss of Lieutenant TAG review** values the mine as self-defence against skirmishers; Shepherd favors solo Tik LT | Losing your attacker *also* loses the LT. Keep CoC elsewhere or use **non-LT Tik + Pathfinder TacAware Duo**; solo LT Orders are **not** automatically Duo Orders. Against tough armor Squalos MULTI HMG has AP/EXP |
| **Squalos Mk-II MMR 55/1, HMG+GL 62/1.5, NCO MULTI HMG 66/1.5** | **Best value big gun**, BS15 +1 Special Die, TacAware, S6 silhouette, 3 STR. **NCO MULTI** handles armor and spends an unconverted LT Order; **GL** fires indirectly against unreachable/hidden defenders; **MMR** saves points/SWC | **Shepherd prefers GL** when Strategos already supplies a Regular LT conversion; **Loss of Lieutenant** argues over the value of MULTI versus Tik's climbing/Mimetism/mine. Choose which solves the actual matchup, not the biggest weapon |
| **Maximus TAG 80–82; 0–1.5 SWC** | **Durable CC/assassination counter:** Immunity (ARM), Escape System, AP guns, E/M Carbine, CC skill and Tactical Awareness. **Daboarder** likes it against ambushes and anti-TAG alpha strikes; Tech-Bee TAGCom improves Dodge/GizmoKit recovery | **Loss of Lieutenant #142 ranks it poorly:** premium cost and no normal TAG pilot/objective specialist. **Important correction:** its April 2025 “lacks AP” criticism is **outdated**: current Maximus has **AP HMG / AP Thunderbolt**, AP+DA CCW. Durability and resilience, not raw economy, justify it |
| **Two TAGs or ARMORED Duo** | Two independent armored attack vectors can overload target priority; a cheap Squalos MMR reduces second-TAG cost | **Irregular Order #52** argues TAG+TAG **Duo** bunches up vulnerabilities without a meaningful bonus. Usually **TAG + Pathfinder TacAware (17)** is better: a Sensor/FO specialist and a usable extra team activation. ARMORED requires Tik or Squalos |

**TAG command and repair:** **Magistrate Strategos LT** enables Regular-order flexibility, whereas **Squalos NCO** wants the LT Order *unconverted*; decide at **Order Count**. **Firefly Engineer (23)**, **Machinist (15) + Palbot**, and **Tech-Bee (9)** each have different jobs. **TAGCom is Dodge (PH+3) / GizmoKit (PH+1), not +1 BS**. Leave an actual repair route to the likely end-of-turn position; a Paramedic cannot repair STR.

### Redeye vehicle — speed versus SWC

**Current options:** **Blitzen 37/1.5** or **Continuous-Damage Grenade Launcher 38/1.5**. BS14, Sensor, Mimetism (-6), ARM6/BTS6, 2 STR, **S7, No Cover**, Super-Jump Jet Propulsion, Tech-recovery. Independent VH, **not a TAG or Fireteam member**.

- **Strongly pro-Redeye — Dice Gods' flyer video, Irregular Order #52:** fast vertical/side-angle attacks can bypass front AROs, discover camo with Sensor and hunt exposed backfield support. **AVA2** permits two-Redeye skews; a well-chosen line can hit and return behind total cover before the opponent acts.
- **Blitzen case — Daboarder (WIP12 #180):** threatens other aerials, TAGs and HI **reactively**, allowing the Redeye to park at an awkward angle and make the enemy spend Orders detouring. He rarely felt the need to spend Orders spec-firing GL, so prefers saving one point.
- **GL case — Shepherd's aerial analysis, NCA #201:** fast BS14 access to unexpected angles and **speculative Continuous-Damage grenades** punishes hiding or targets the main gun cannot see. NCA presenter sees it as an *occasional high-value shot*, not the reason to field every Redeye.
- **Against Redeye — Shepherd:** **1.5 SWC on a B4 Spitfire** replaces a premium HMG/Feuerbach able to clear tough defenders. **S7/No Cover**, MSV answers to Mimetism, and hacking make careless exposure costly. Use it **after** the heavy gun opens a safe lane rather than as the only primary attacker.

**Sequence:** deploy fully concealed → clear long-range/MSV/hacking threats → jump for an attack or Sensor angle → **return to total cover** when future turns matter. **Tech-recovery via GizmoKit** can address disabling states, but requires real support access; **Redeye cannot touch/pick up objectives**. The core disagreement is **whether a flank exploit for 37–38 points is worth the SWC it removes from stronger frontal guns**.

**Practical packages:**
- **Aggressive NCO TAG:** *Squalos MULTI HMG NCO + Pathfinder TacAware*; **Magistrate LT** can leave LT Order for NCO or convert it for an attack in the other Combat Group. Borrowed **strategy**, not an NCA Fireteam rule.
- **Solo LT gun:** *Tikbalang LT + separate Magistrate CoC + repair coverage*. Four personal activations on Tik when safe; keep succession insurance away from the attacker.
- **Redeye exploitation:** *Hetkari/Griffin/Squalos primary long gun + Redeye + Peacemaker/Jackal mines*. Let the heavy gun clear a route, then use Redeye's vertical mobility and reserve/counter-deployment.
- **Layered ARO:** *Sierra (visible) + Blink (hidden) + cheap mine/Jackal*, and optional Palbot. Enemy must budget for a second threat after spending Orders removing the first.
- **REM gun experiment:** *Bulleteer HSG escort or independent trade piece*. **Counterview:** default to Pathfinder when missing specialist/Sensor rather than another gun.

### Fennec / Pathfinder — which utility actually adds a new role?

- **FO/Sensor/Disco Fennec** is excellent *when Sensor and vision control are missing*: one cheap Regular Order scores, detects markers and helps an attack piece cross defended angles.
- **Thunderbolt (+1B) / Minelayer Fennec** is the higher-marginal-value second choice **when a Pathfinder already supplies FO/Sensor**. Why: an extra deployment mine and 0-SWC ranged contingency add a different layer rather than duplicating Sensor. A Disco Fennec is still justified if repeated Camo encounters or its vision-control effect are part of the plan.
- **Hacker/FastPanda Fennec** is a *network tool*, not a universal Hacker requirement. Why: WIP12 struggles in hostile hacking grids, but forward Repeater projection can make another friendly Hacker or protection plan matter. Ask where the FastPanda will go and who will exploit it.
- **14-point Pathfinder versus 17-point Tactical Awareness Pathfinder:** both do FO, Sensor, Repeater, Triangulated Fire and Deactivator work. Buy TacAware when it funds actual development, retreat or Fireteam movement; in a group that needs its **own unlinked Specialist** but has no useful partner, the cheaper version may suffice.
- **Partner route check before any Duo:** a fast TAG + Pathfinder often wants to advance toward the same lane; a static backline gun + Pathfinder may not. The first arrangement compresses useful movement; the second can strand either the gun or the Specialist. Kestrel Wildcards do not waive the native **ARMORED/KESTREL required-choice** conditions.

### Squalos / Tikbalang choice after accounting for the Order engine

| Actual need | Favor | Why / caution |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile independent killer with climbing, Mimetism and mine | **Tikbalang**, often non-LT if a Pathfinder Duo must persist | Mobility and concealment improve attack and retreat geometry. A **Tik LT** spending its own LT Order **leaves its Fireteam**, so do not count those LT Orders as intact Duo activations |
| Spend unused LT Orders while an Armored Duo remains intact | **Squalos Mk-II NCO MULTI HMG + TacAware Pathfinder** | NCO routes the command resource to the linked attacker without the same *personal LT Order* break; must have a valid LT/NCO economy |
| Long-range gun with indirect answer to hidden support | **Squalos HMG + grenade launcher** | Occasional speculative or obstructed-target attacks are an option, not a license for speculative-fire spam |
| Cheapest durable TAG body for midfield/support rather than an apex shooter | **Squalos MULTI Marksman Rifle** | Saves points/SWC for Specialist/ARO/secondary threat; weaker long-range active problem-solving must already be covered |

**Sequencing check:** use both partners' eligible Tactical Awareness activations while their linked movement matters, then commit whichever piece must break away. An NCO in another independent team (such as an eligible Firefly or Sforza) can instead convert the Tik LT's additional Orders, preserving a Tik/Pathfinder Duo until it is no longer required. Separate **Chain of Command** physically from the aggressive Lieutenant.

### Drummer / Maximus / Dart — peripheral and specialist opportunity cost

- **21-point FO Drummer + Drumbot-5:** low-cost synchronized scoring and a second physical body. Useful when you need mission reach without buying a premium gun, **not** because two bodies universally grant two objective interaction rolls; the scenario and Peripheral rules govern those attempts.
- **42-point HRMC Drummer**, **40-point Neurocinetics Feuerbach Drummer**, and **47-point FO + combat bot + Drumbot-5** are fundamentally different roles. HRMC maximizes one active gun package; Neurocinetics purchases an ARO; the 47-point controller packages buy a bigger combined-arms toolkit. The controller is a vulnerable W1 commitment, so take the expensive one only when its extra bodies/weapons actually solve different engagements.
- **Agent Maximus + Minimus** versus **FTO Agent Maximus:** the **48-point option includes the synchronized Peripheral**, adding nearby E/M/close-range angles, whereas **45-point FTO replaces Minimus with TinBot (Repeater)**. Do not treat the peripherals and team integration as the same benefit. The Kestrel Fireteam chart lists **MAXIMUS AGENT without an FTO-only restriction**; still check the **current Peripheral/Fireteam interaction** before assuming simultaneous linked and synchronized activations.
- **Dart with Sharvara K9 (42/0.5)** is for active midfield hunting/attack forks; **Dart with Minelayer (36/0.5)** is cheaper when concealment, prepaid mine coverage and late scoring are the real jobs. Paying for K9 and leaving it idle wastes the premium; neither option is a Kestrel Fireteam member.

### Less obvious independent specialists and NCO choices

- **Quinn:** the **26-point MULTI Marksman FO** is a compact MSV1 ranged Specialist; the equally priced **MULTI Rifle/Disco Baller FO** is worth it when an actual vision-control route is part of the plan. **Quinn has no Chain of Command option** in the pinned Kestrel roster.
- **Emily Handelman:** the **22-point E/M GL FO** creates indirect control, while **24-point combined GL/E/M GL** buys a broader template/indirect toolbox. She has **Number 2, not Chain of Command** in current Kestrel. An infiltrating **11-point Jackal E/M GL Minelayer** can cover the cheaper disruptive angle; Emily is justified by her **Regular status, FO, WIP14, Number 2 and Wildcard access**, not by magically superior speculative-fire odds.
- **Sforza FTO:** NCO lets a separate group or team spend the LT resource when a Tik LT would otherwise fracture a Duo. Take him when the Viral/Adhesive/X-Visor package adds a real engagement type, not as another generic gun.
- **Knauf:** unlinked MSV1/Mimetism(-3) MULTI Sniper with no Fireteam dependency. Better when long-range visor fire is missing, worse if Hetkari/Black A.I.R./Griffin already fulfills the same role for the same Order budget.
- **Bipandra TacAware Doctor:** worth 25 points when the active link can regularly use her additional activation and valuable HI models are actually recoverable. A 12-point Fennec Paramedic is cheaper insurance when the team is static.
- **Firefly NCO versus CoC:** a profile offering NCO and repair/hacking/defence can be useful to a TAG-LT plan, but **CoC's survival role favors deploying the backup away from the Lieutenant's attack path**. The best NCO location and best CoC location may be different.

**Fast marginal-cost question:** after buying a primary attacker, ask whether the next 3–20 points purchase (a) another useful Order and scorer, (b) a genuinely different attack mode, (c) protected recovery or succession, or (d) a duplicate ability already supplied by Pathfinder/Fennec/Fireteam. Favor the first three unless the mission or expected opponent explicitly rewards redundancy.

## Build and play checklist

1. **Choose the mission and fallback initiative:** if the mission favors going second, can the list still score if forced to go first and deploy first? **Daboarder** deliberately builds for that adverse outcome.
2. **Buy the command plan:** Magistrate/Jackal shell, alternative LT, or exposed Tik/Hetkari plus *physically separate* CoC if needed. If relying on FT Master, protect the Magistrate for next turn's Order Count.
3. **Pick one principal attacker and a genuinely different backup:** Hetkari Red Fury + short-range Banshee; Tikbalang + E/M Jackal/mines; Redeye + Squalos; Griffin Feuerbach + Crux/hidden unit. Another similar HMG is often less valuable.
4. **Ensure objective access:** cheap Fennec FO/Disco, Pathfinder, infiltrating Scarecrow, AD Banshee, Doctor/Engineer/classified coverage as demanded by the packet. Account for **off-table units** before counting opening-turn Orders.
5. **Decide defensive architecture:** cheap mines and Panzerfaust crossfires first; add Black A.I.R., Sierra or Blink only if they cover *different* attack approaches. Use reserve deployments to put a disruptive piece opposite the enemy plan.
6. **Account for anti-hacking and recovery:** don't expose every HI/REM/TAG to one Turn-1 Pitcher. Put a deactivator/engineer/KHD/disposable close-assault route where it can actually answer an advancing repeater or hacker.
7. **Sequence your activations:** clear key ARO → pressure critical enemy piece → exploit gap with specialist/Redeye/Banshee → **spend a TacAware partner's Order to retreat the TAG/link**. Preserve a score-on-Turn-3 piece rather than winning unnecessary firefights.
8. **Audit SWC, silhouette and exact Fireteam composition in the live Army app.** Older YouTube and podcasts include pre-N5.3 unit prices, Fennec LT Decoy claims and turret/reload assumptions that do not survive the current reference.

**Proxy legality, silhouette and clarity:** A gaming proxy still represents a **real legal current Army entry and its actual profile**. A weapon variant usually needs **no second miniature**. Verify current *silhouette, base diameter, quantity and peripheral count* against the roster before substituting: a TAG, vehicle, bike, REM or S2 soldier is **not automatically physically interchangeable**. Clearly tell your opponent which sculpt represents which troop; avoid visually identical proxies for several different units where there could be confusion. For official events, check the current [ITS Season 18 rules/download](https://experience.corvusbelli.com/en/infinity/its) and the organizer; previous [ITS17 proxy provisions](https://downloads.corvusbelli.com/infinity/organized-play/its-rules-season-17-en-v1.0.2.pdf) allowed Corvus Belli stand-ins with the correct base and disclosure but did not allow other manufacturers. **Season 18's specific proxy paragraph was not independently retrievable for this audit**, so don't present an older season's wording as verified 2026 law.

**Final collection principle:** *(1)* buy common roles you actually use; *(2)* avoid the explicitly poor **profile** without blacklisting a useful troop; *(3)* **proxy** a named single-purpose unit or a second high-cost model for several games; *(4)* purchase its own miniature only if it repeatedly earns a distinct role or you want the sculpt for hobby reasons. **“Rare” and “never worth taking” are intentionally not synonyms.**
