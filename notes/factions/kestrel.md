# Kestrel Colonial Force — Listbuilding Guide (Infinity N5.3)

**Purpose:** practical choices, the reason each works, and disagreements worth testing. **As of 2026-10-19.** This guide consolidates Kestrel-specific video/podcast research instead of retaining repeated transcripts and historical discussion.

**Rules authority:** [Kestrel roster and Fireteam chart](../../sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md), Army revision **7.26246.158**; local N5.3 rules/FAQ supersede older videos. Costs below refer to that pinned roster, not necessarily the version a presenter recorded against. Advice attributed to a presenter is an *opinion*, not a proven tournament usage rate.

## At a glance

- **Strength:** Kestrel combines top-quality shooters, cheap *useful* Orders, mines/hidden threats, Eclipse via Disco Baller, and competent specialists. **Why it works:** the shooting pieces rarely have to pay their own enabling/support costs.
- **Common spine, not compulsory:** **Fennec FO/Disco/Sensor (13)**; **Magistrate Strategos LT (19) + 6-point FTO Jackal(s)**; a main gun (Hetkari, Griffin, Tikbalang, Squalos, Agent Maximus or Crux); **11-point infiltrating E/M GL Minelayer Jackal(s)**; then mission-specific specialist/ARO/repair.
- **Main weaknesses:** aggressive enemy Pitchers/hacking grids into your HI/TAGs; a visible Magistrate LT; Albedo/White Noise versus over-reliance on MSV; off-table pieces reducing Turn-1 Orders; one expensive brawler that cannot score objectives. Build a second attack/scoring route.
- **Disagreement is substantial.** Shepherd likes a distributed-link, high-order all-rounder. The 2026 Irregular Order hosts favor very different units and plans—one prizes Black A.I.R. ARO and a defensive Hetkari ML; the other likes more aggressive pieces and unusual Fireteams. Daboarder (WIP12) emphasizes *scenario contingencies*, Redeye and cheap disruption instead of a fixed shopping list.

## Start with one of five architectures

| Plan | Core construction | Why / cost of choosing it |
|---|---|---|
| **Balanced, distributed (Shepherd)** | Griffin + 3 Fennecs **Core**; Hetkari + TacAware Pathfinder **KESTREL Duo**; Griffin + TacAware Bipandra **KESTREL Duo**; Magistrate + 2 Jackals **COLCORP Haris** | Multiple independent strong guns, specialists, special Orders and cheap Regulars; uses the one Haris slot on the order engine. Costs troop slots and requires careful positioning |
| **Heavy Fennec Core (Irregular Order alternative)** | Fennec base + Black A.I.R. sniper for ARO **or** Fennec + Agent Maximus + Bipandra for an aggressive Core; add small independent attacks | Stacks specialist utility and strong bonuses on one formation. A clustered Core is more vulnerable to loss of the critical gun/link and can be slow to reposition |
| **Hetkari Haris (defensive or offensive)** | Hetkari + Firefly + Bipandra/Quinn, ensuring **two Kestrel-counting members** for full composition; ML for strong ARO, MULTI Red Fury for aggressive shooting | Medical/engineering cover and exceptional FtF quality. Near 100 points and occupies the sole Haris; Pathfinder Duo usually costs substantially less |
| **TAG assault** | Solo **Tikbalang LT +1 Order**, or **Squalos Mk-II** (GL or NCO MULTI HMG), plus cheap command, hacking/repair countermeasures and scoring pieces | Heavy shooting and mobility with a concentrated Order budget. Tik LT risks LoL; Squalos is more economical but lacks Tik's Mimetism/Climbing Plus |
| **Mobile/asymmetric (Daboarder; experimental podcasters)** | Magistrate/Jackals support **Redeye (sometimes two)**, Peacemaker mine/Repeater, Banshee/Scarecrow/Crux surprise, or a Drummer battery | Exploits side/rear angles and forces the opponent to divert Orders. Less resilient if expensive mobile units are countered before attacking; mission access still needs specialists |

**Fireteam limits:** **1 Core, 1 Haris, unlimited Duos**. FENNECS Core/Haris needs ≥1 Fennec; KESTREL Duo/Haris needs ≥1 Griffin, Hetkari or Firefly; ARMORED Duo needs a Tikbalang or Squalos; COLCORP Duo/Haris needs a Magistrate. Wildcards never waive those minima. *FTO* on a profile alone does **not** grant link access; e.g., the current Kestrel chart does not list Clipper or Yamabushi FTO.

## Command and cheap Orders

| Unit/package | Why it works | Competing interpretation / when to pass |
|---|---|---|
| **Magistrate Strategos LT (19) + Jackal FTO (6)** | Strategos L1 and Counterintelligence preserve order tempo; **FT Master (Jackals)** turns linked Jackals **Regular at Order Count**. Duo = 25 points/two Regular troopers; with two Jackals Haris = **31 points/three Regular troopers**, plus cheap Panzerfaust/LRL defence | A named, visible LT and FT Master are a vulnerability: after the link/master is lost, next-turn Jackal order generation worsens. The two Magistrate profiles (LT + separate **21-point CoC**), ideally in **different protected positions**, are even safer but consume points and AVA 2 |
| **Infiltrating Jackal E/M GL Minelayer (11/0.5)** | Places a mine *before spending an Order*; threatens speculative E/M into expensive targets; opponent wastes Orders hunting it or risks a crippling hit. **Shepherd advocates at least two** and likes one in each Group | Printed **Irregular** and not a COLCORP FTO. A list short on group slots, SWC or specialists may take only one. Irregular Order is a free local shot *only if the Jackal is safe/able to attack* |
| **Jackal FTO Panzerfaust vs Light Rocket Launcher (6 each)** | **Panzerfaust** offers longer useful lanes and a punishing hit/multiple saves; **LRL** covers closer approaches with a reusable continuous-damage template and is useful against clustered attacks | **Irregular Order Sep-2026 hosts disagree on ideal ARO** but both lean Panzerfaust for open lanes, LRL for cross-lane or nearer defence. Cheap Jackals still die easily if offered fair FtF |
| **Tikbalang LT (+1 Order, 73)** | Regular + its own Tactical Awareness + **two Lieutenant Orders** means **four self-funded Orders** when used solo; high BS/Mimetism/Climbing Plus convert them into actual kills. **Shepherd likes this aggressively** | Irregular Order agrees the TAG is strong but prefers other threats in some lists. If it dies, loss of command can be worse than the gun loss; take independent CoC when worthwhile |
| **Magistrate Strategos vs NCO** | Strategos converts LT Order into a Regular Order, often more valuable for a **Redeye/Banshee**; leaving it unconverted allows a **Squalos NCO or Firefly/Sforza NCO** to use it, potentially **across Combat Groups** | Daboarder deliberately switches modes by round: early NCO Squalos for suppression, later Strategos for a fast scoring piece. **Choose at Order Count, not halfway through the turn** |

**TAG Duo order trap:** A **Tik LT + Pathfinder TacAware Duo** looks like six potential activations from two Regular Orders + two TacAware Orders + two LT Orders. **Only the first four are naturally Duo activations**; the Tik's personal LT Orders do *not* automatically activate its partner and may split the Fireteam. Compare **solo Tik LT**, **non-LT Tik + Pathfinder** or **Squalos NCO + Pathfinder** before counting “six Duo Orders.” The 17-point TacAware Pathfinder is worth upgrading from 14 mainly when its extra team activation gets used.

### Which TAG? Four sharply different jobs

| TAG / pinned profile | Choose it because… | Alternative opinion / condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Tikbalang HMG + HFT, Shock Mine (69); LT +1 Order (73), 1.5 SWC** | **Best solo mobile killer/assassin:** BS15/Mimetism (-3), Climbing Plus, 15-10 MOV and short-range HFT + Shock Mine against warbands. **LT generates two personal LT Orders plus TacAware**, so it can run its own offensive turn at low Regular-order cost. **Loss of Lieutenant #142** rates mine counterplay and 3D mobility highly; Shepherd likes the solo LT | **Squalos outguns armour** with selectable AP/EXP MULTI HMG, costs less, and avoids risking LT. A non-LT Tik + **Pathfinder 17** Duo preserves group flexibility; **Tik LT + Pathfinder** may not remain linked when Tik spends its personal LT Orders. Keep **CoC outside** an attacking TAG team |
| **Squalos Mk-II MMR (55/1), HMG+GL (62/1.5), NCO MULTI HMG (66/1.5)** | **Value/power and narrower S6 footprint:** BS15 +1 Special Die, TacAware, Remote Presence and 3 STR. **NCO MULTI HMG** is excellent when you deliberately retain a Strategos LT Order; **HMG+GL** adds speculative attacks; **55-point MMR** provides a low-cost second armored gun. WIP12 #201 NCA presenter likes **Squalos NCO + TacAware Pathfinder** as a fast repeatable attack-plus-Sensor package | **Shepherd** usually selects HMG+GL when Strategos already converts LT Orders, valuing *another type of attack*. **Loss of Lieutenant** participants differ over how often AP/EXP merits the NCO premium versus cheap HMG, and some rate Tik higher because Mimetism/mine/Climbing Plus cover more battlefield problems |
| **Maximus TAG (80–82; 0–1.5 SWC)** | **Anti-assassination/attrition:** 4 W total including Escape System, Immunity (ARM), strong CC defence, TacAware, **AP HMG or AP Thunderbolt**, E/M Carbine and AP+DA CCW. **Daboarder** selects it into projected hacking, CC assassins and objective-destruction missions. **Tech-Bee TAGCom** improves Dodge and self/nearby GizmoKit odds; support recovery is a real tactical package | **Loss of Lieutenant #142** ranks it low as expensive, less mission-capable and costly for the shooting you get; **Irregular Order #52** disagrees internally, with one host much more enthusiastic about TAG Maximus than the other. **Important historical correction:** the April 2025 tier-list criticism that it **“has no AP shooting” is no longer true** in pinned N5.3 (AP HMG, AP Thunderbolt). Its *high cost* and *absence of a routine TAG pilot/specialist* remain genuine objections |
| **Two TAGs / ARMORED Duo** | Two separate high-BS threats can demand different answers; Squalos MMR can keep the second chassis relatively affordable. A **single TAG + Pathfinder TacAware (17)** is generally the more economical Duo for moving specialist/Sensor and a retreat activation | **Irregular Order #52** finds a TAG+TAG Duo awkward: two expensive vulnerable targets travel together, compositions often earn little and the formation risks exposure to the same counter. Prefer **separate TAGs** when each needs its own attack lane; ARMORED requires a Tikbalang or Squalos and **does not become a mixed KESTREL Haris** |

**TAG support rules of thumb:** **Firefly Engineer (23)** can accompany an ARMORED Duo, while **Machinist (15) + Palbot** repairs without chaining the Engineer to the exposed gun. **Tech-Bee (9)** is not “+1 BS”: **TAGCom is Dodge (PH+3) and GizmoKit (PH+1)**. Build for **where the damaged TAG will end its turn**, not only whether a repair model exists in the roster. A **Fennec Paramedic/Doctor cannot perform TAG repair**.

### Redeye VH: exceptionally mobile, but the list must exploit it

**Current:** **Blitzen 37/1.5** or **Continuous-Damage GL 38/1.5**; BS14, Sensor, **Mimetism (-6)**, ARM6/BTS6, **2 STR, S7, 20-5 MOV, Super-Jump (Jet Propulsion), Tech-recovery, No Cover**. This is **not** a TAG: no ARMORED link, pilot or base-contact scoring; it is an independent *angle creator/denial threat*.

- **For it — Dice Gods (Oct 2025) and a 2026 Irregular Order host:** can move **11 inches in a single aerial short movement** with Jet Propulsion, rise above rooftop parapets and attack prone or hidden-backfield targets. Fast enough to strike an exposed LT or bypass a front ARO then *return behind total cover*. 37–38 points for BS14/Mimetism (-6)/ARM6/2 STR is unusually dense; Kestrel AVA2 allows a double-Redeye skew.
- **For it, with a different plan — Daboarder (WIP12 #180) and NCA #201:** keep a **Blitzen** available for anti-TAG/anti-vehicle ARO. Park at a lateral angle where the opponent must either reset/dodge or spend Orders routing around the flyer. **NCA 2026** highlights **Sensor** on the updated Redeye and the repositioning advantage; a GL sometimes punishes a defended TAG from an unexpected arc, but the player describes GL as a luxury or “swing” choice.
- **Against it — Shepherd's Oct-2025 vehicle ranking:** strong profile, yet **1.5 SWC for a B4 Spitfire** competes with Kestrel's necessary HMG/Feuerbach/HARIS gun that actually opens tough defensive lanes. S7 and **No Cover** make it hard to park safely; the Mimetism can be answered by MSV, and hacking can strand a flyer. **Use after your primary gun has cleared a route**, not instead of that gun.
- **Loadout disagreement:** Shepherd prefers **38-point GL** because it adds a solution to **Null Deployment / hidden defenders** without direct LOF. **Daboarder prefers 37-point Blitzen** because he rarely wants to divert active Orders from mobile Spitfire attacks into speculative grenades; the Blitzen discourages opposing flyers or armour during their turn. **Dice Gods likes both** and argues even two Redeyes can justify a list. Which to pick depends on **whether you need extra unseen-target reach or a reactive E/M anti-air/anti-TAG answer**.
- **Play sequence:** hide S7 completely → clear missile/MSV/hacking obstacles with other troops → move/jump to a side/rear strike or Sensor a camo cluster → attack a weak enemy support segment → **withdraw to real total cover** if next turn matters. Do not mistake **ARM6 without Cover** for TAG-like immunity, nor assume it can touch objectives. **Tech-recovery** means a **GizmoKit attack can clear relevant states**; getting a support model into range of a flying S7 model requires planning.

**Best Redeye viewing:** [Shepherd's ranked aerial analysis](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N6TYjMgU4ZM) for **opportunity costs/terrain**, [Dice Gods flyer problem](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QscqX3hlUZU) for the **double-Redeye/alpha-strike case**, [WIP12 #180](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3JXkarf51Os0UUopYrM7IW) for **Blitzen vs GL and reactive parking**, and [WIP12 #201 NCA](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3FT1No7zFHzpj7WC63hcCb) for a **later cross-sectorial use case (Redeye + NCO Squalos)**. The 2025 videos precede N5.3; verify current movement/vehicle rules before following precise examples.

**Hardware combinations to try, in order of purpose:**

1. **All-rounder / mission TAG:** **Squalos NCO MULTI HMG (66) + Pathfinder TacAware (17)**, backed by Magistrate Strategos. Leave LT Order for NCO during the Squalos attack turn; convert it via Strategos instead when the **other group** needs an extra Regular activation. *NCA #201* reports this as a strong package, and **Kestrel's ARMORED Duo and Pathfinder Wildcard permit the analogous pairing**.
2. **Safer mobile gun:** **Non-LT Tik (69) + Pathfinder TacAware (17)**, **Firefly Engineer** elsewhere or in a role-appropriate support position. *Why:* climbing BS15 gun and self-defence plus Sensor specialist and a team retreat Order; avoids betting the succession plan on the TAG.
3. **LT alpha:** **Solo Tik LT (73)** + **Magistrate CoC (21) in protected separate location** + cheap FO/Engineer. *Why:* four potentially self-funded Tik activations without miscounting personal LT Orders as link activation.
4. **Air raid:** **Redeye Blitzen or GL (37–38)** + a **real primary long-range gun** (Hetkari, Griffin Feuerbach, Squalos) + **Peacemaker mine/Repeater**. *Why:* other unit clears a dangerous lane; Redeye then uses vertical mobility to punish the backfield. Add a reachable GizmoKit/recovery plan.
5. **Reactive REM net:** **Sierra Thunderbolt 18/0 or HMG 23/1** + hidden **Blink HRL**, a cheap mine/Jackal and a **Machinist/Palbot**. *Why:* visible ARO consumes early enemy Orders; hidden ARO delays later; recovery forces another clearance attempt.
6. **Attack-REM experiment:** **Bulleteer HSG 19/0** alone or as a **Tik/Squalos ARMORED Duo alternative to Pathfinder**. *Why:* overlapping angles and close-range template/shotgun pressure. **Counterview:** a Pathfinder's Specialist/Sensor/TacAware usually yields more scenario value and a less wasteful spare Order.

**Selection filter:** each REM must earn its slot as **(a) an Order generator, (b) an immediate obstacle, (c) a specialist/network projector, (d) a repair platform, or (e) a real second attacker**. Kestrel can already buy cheap linked Regular Jackals and exceptional gunfighters. “Every standard PanO REM is automatic” is therefore a weaker hypothesis than filling uncovered jobs.

| Decision | Take A because… | Take B because… |

| **CoC shell** | **Two Magistrates, LT + CoC in separate Duos:** redundant command and cheap Jackal Orders (Irregular Order) | **One Magistrate:** spending another 21 + escort points delays an extra attacker/objective unit (Shepherd); a Firefly CoC can accompany a different team |
| **Reactive slots** | **Black A.I.R. / Blink:** high-impact visible or hidden shot (Irregular Order; Daboarder for Blink) | **Griffin/Jackals/mines:** cheaper, durable or more distributed ways to consume opponent Orders (Shepherd) |
| **Redeye/Drummer vs heavy shooter** | **Redeye or Drummer:** mobility, unique angle, peripheral attacks and opponent-distraction (Daboarder/Irregular Order) | **Hetkari/Tik/Squalos:** simpler superior firepower and fewer W1/peripheral vulnerabilities (Shepherd) |

These are **purpose-specific disagreements, not a popularity vote**. The fact that multiple reviewers favor a model does not make it obligatory across scenarios.

## Build and play checklist

1. **Choose the mission and fallback initiative:** if the mission favors going second, can the list still score if forced to go first and deploy first? **Daboarder** deliberately builds for that adverse outcome.
2. **Buy the command plan:** Magistrate/Jackal shell, alternative LT, or exposed Tik/Hetkari plus *physically separate* CoC if needed. If relying on FT Master, protect the Magistrate for next turn's Order Count.
3. **Pick one principal attacker and a genuinely different backup:** Hetkari Red Fury + short-range Banshee; Tikbalang + E/M Jackal/mines; Redeye + Squalos; Griffin Feuerbach + Crux/hidden unit. Another similar HMG is often less valuable.
4. **Ensure objective access:** cheap Fennec FO/Disco, Pathfinder, infiltrating Scarecrow, AD Banshee, Doctor/Engineer/classified coverage as demanded by the packet. Account for **off-table units** before counting opening-turn Orders.
5. **Decide defensive architecture:** cheap mines and Panzerfaust crossfires first; add Black A.I.R., Sierra or Blink only if they cover *different* attack approaches. Use reserve deployments to put a disruptive piece opposite the enemy plan.
6. **Account for anti-hacking and recovery:** don't expose every HI/REM/TAG to one Turn-1 Pitcher. Put a deactivator/engineer/KHD/disposable close-assault route where it can actually answer an advancing repeater or hacker.
7. **Sequence your activations:** clear key ARO → pressure critical enemy piece → exploit gap with specialist/Redeye/Banshee → **spend a TacAware partner's Order to retreat the TAG/link**. Preserve a score-on-Turn-3 piece rather than winning unnecessary firefights.
8. **Audit SWC, silhouette and exact Fireteam composition in the live Army app.** Older YouTube and podcasts include pre-N5.3 unit prices, Fennec LT Decoy claims and turret/reload assumptions that do not survive the current reference.
