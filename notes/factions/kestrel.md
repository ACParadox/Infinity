# Kestrel Colonial Force — Listbuilding Guide (Infinity N5.3)

**Purpose:** practical choices, the reason each works, and disagreements worth testing. **As of 2026-10-19.** This guide consolidates Kestrel-specific video/podcast research instead of retaining repeated transcripts and historical discussion.

**Rules authority:** [Kestrel roster and Fireteam chart](../../sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md), Army revision **7.26246.158**; local N5.3 rules/FAQ supersede older videos. Costs below refer to that pinned roster, not necessarily the version a presenter recorded against. Advice attributed to a presenter is an *opinion*, not a proven tournament usage rate.

## At a glance

- **Strength:** Kestrel combines top-quality shooters, cheap *useful* Orders, mines/hidden threats, Eclipse via Disco Baller, and competent specialists. **Why it works:** the shooting pieces rarely have to pay their own enabling/support costs.
- **Common spine, not compulsory:** **Fennec FO/Disco/Sensor (13)**; **Magistrate Strategos LT (19) + 6-point FTO Jackal(s)**; a main gun (Hetkari, Griffin, Tikbalang, Squalos, Agent Maximus or Crux); **11-point infiltrating E/M GL Minelayer Jackal(s)**; then mission-specific specialist/ARO/repair.
- **Main weaknesses:** aggressive enemy Pitchers/hacking grids into your HI/TAGs; a visible Magistrate LT; Albedo/White Noise versus over-reliance on MSV; off-table pieces reducing Turn-1 Orders; one expensive brawler that cannot score objectives. Build a second attack/scoring route.
- **Disagreement is substantial.** Shepherd likes a distributed-link, high-order all-rounder. The 2026 Irregular Order hosts favor very different units and plans—one prizes Black A.I.R. ARO and a defensive Hetkari ML; the other likes more aggressive pieces and unusual Fireteams. Daboarder (WIP12) emphasizes *scenario contingencies*, Redeye and cheap disruption instead of a fixed shopping list.

## Start with one of five architectures

| Plan | Core construction | Why / cost of choosing it |
|---|---|---|
| **Balanced, distributed (Shepherd)** | Griffin + 3 Fennecs **Core**; Hetkari + TacAware Pathfinder **KESTREL Duo**; Griffin + TacAware Bipandra **KESTREL Duo**; Magistrate + 2 Jackals **COLCORP Haris** | Multiple independent strong guns, specialists, special Orders and cheap Regulars; uses the one Haris slot on the order engine. Costs troop slots and requires careful positioning |
| **Heavy Fennec Core (Irregular Order alternative)** | Fennec base + Black A.I.R. sniper for ARO **or** Fennec + Agent Maximus + Bipandra for an aggressive Core; add small independent attacks | Stacks specialist utility and strong bonuses on one formation. A clustered Core is more vulnerable to loss of the critical gun/link and can be slow to reposition |
| **Hetkari Haris (defensive or offensive)** | Hetkari + Firefly + Bipandra/Quinn, ensuring **two Kestrel-counting members** for full composition; ML for strong ARO, MULTI Red Fury for aggressive shooting | Medical/engineering cover and exceptional FtF quality. Near 100 points and occupies the sole Haris; Pathfinder Duo usually costs substantially less |
| **TAG assault** | Solo **Tikbalang LT +1 Order**, or **Squalos Mk-II** (GL or NCO MULTI HMG), plus cheap command, hacking/repair countermeasures and scoring pieces | Heavy shooting and mobility with a concentrated Order budget. Tik LT risks LoL; Squalos is more economical but lacks Tik's Mimetism/Climbing Plus |
| **Mobile/asymmetric (Daboarder; experimental podcasters)** | Magistrate/Jackals support **Redeye (sometimes two)**, Peacemaker mine/Repeater, Banshee/Scarecrow/Crux surprise, or a Drummer battery | Exploits side/rear angles and forces the opponent to divert Orders. Less resilient if expensive mobile units are countered before attacking; mission access still needs specialists |

**Fireteam limits:** **1 Core, 1 Haris, unlimited Duos**. FENNECS Core/Haris needs ≥1 Fennec; KESTREL Duo/Haris needs ≥1 Griffin, Hetkari or Firefly; ARMORED Duo needs a Tikbalang or Squalos; COLCORP Duo/Haris needs a Magistrate. Wildcards never waive those minima. *FTO* on a profile alone does **not** grant link access; e.g., the current Kestrel chart does not list Clipper or Yamabushi FTO.

## Command and cheap Orders

| Unit/package | Why it works | Competing interpretation / when to pass |
|---|---|---|
| **Magistrate Strategos LT (19) + Jackal FTO (6)** | Strategos L1 and Counterintelligence preserve order tempo; **FT Master (Jackals)** turns linked Jackals **Regular at Order Count**. Duo = 25 points/two Regular troopers; with two Jackals Haris = **31 points/three Regular troopers**, plus cheap Panzerfaust/LRL defence | A named, visible LT and FT Master are a vulnerability: after the link/master is lost, next-turn Jackal order generation worsens. The two Magistrate profiles (LT + separate **21-point CoC**), ideally in **different protected positions**, are even safer but consume points and AVA 2 |
| **Infiltrating Jackal E/M GL Minelayer (11/0.5)** | Places a mine *before spending an Order*; threatens speculative E/M into expensive targets; opponent wastes Orders hunting it or risks a crippling hit. **Shepherd advocates at least two** and likes one in each Group | Printed **Irregular** and not a COLCORP FTO. A list short on group slots, SWC or specialists may take only one. Irregular Order is a free local shot *only if the Jackal is safe/able to attack* |
| **Jackal FTO Panzerfaust vs Light Rocket Launcher (6 each)** | **Panzerfaust** offers longer useful lanes and a punishing hit/multiple saves; **LRL** covers closer approaches with a reusable continuous-damage template and is useful against clustered attacks | **Irregular Order Sep-2026 hosts disagree on ideal ARO** but both lean Panzerfaust for open lanes, LRL for cross-lane or nearer defence. Cheap Jackals still die easily if offered fair FtF |
| **Tikbalang LT (+1 Order, 73)** | Regular + its own Tactical Awareness + **two Lieutenant Orders** means **four self-funded Orders** when used solo; high BS/Mimetism/Climbing Plus convert them into actual kills. **Shepherd likes this aggressively** | Irregular Order agrees the TAG is strong but prefers other threats in some lists. If it dies, loss of command can be worse than the gun loss; take independent CoC when worthwhile |
| **Magistrate Strategos vs NCO** | Strategos converts LT Order into a Regular Order, often more valuable for a **Redeye/Banshee**; leaving it unconverted allows a **Squalos NCO or Firefly/Sforza NCO** to use it, potentially **across Combat Groups** | Daboarder deliberately switches modes by round: early NCO Squalos for suppression, later Strategos for a fast scoring piece. **Choose at Order Count, not halfway through the turn** |

**TAG Duo order trap:** A **Tik LT + Pathfinder TacAware Duo** looks like six potential activations from two Regular Orders + two TacAware Orders + two LT Orders. **Only the first four are naturally Duo activations**; the Tik's personal LT Orders do *not* automatically activate its partner and may split the Fireteam. Compare **solo Tik LT**, **non-LT Tik + Pathfinder** or **Squalos NCO + Pathfinder** before counting “six Duo Orders.” The 17-point TacAware Pathfinder is worth upgrading from 14 mainly when its extra team activation gets used.

## Key Fireteam and gunfighter choices

| Choice | Why pick it | Why another player might not |
|---|---|---|
| **Fennec FO/Sensor/Disco Baller (13)** | Cheap mission scoring, Camouflage detection and Eclipse-style visibility control on the same Regular Core body. **Strongest cross-source consensus** | Not a combat hacker or a primary damage gun; a static Core may not be the best place for a mobile FO |
| **Fennec Hacker/FastPanda/E/Mitter (16/0.5)** | A cheap defensive hacker **plus its own network projection**; one trooper/slot covers several jobs. Shepherd and Daboarder like this cheap utility | **One Irregular Order Sep-2026 host calls it a trap:** WIP12/BTS3 is a weak gunfight in serious hacking, and adding it to the Core exposes the entire team to hacking risk. Instead take **Firefly WIP13/BTS6**, **Black A.I.R. Combat Instinct Hacker** (specialized anti-Stealth net) or **no hacker** if your plan is to neutralize repeaters by other means |
| **Fennec Thunderbolt (+1B), Triangulated Fire, Minelayer (14)** | Starts with a DZ mine and remains a **0-SWC backup gun**; Triangulated Fire gives an alternate attack into severe MODs and a non-MSV backup if Albedo/White Noise troubles your primary shooter | A full Core already led by an excellent Griffin/Black A.I.R. may need a **Paramedic/FO** more than another gun. Even admirers call it backup, not the reason to buy the Core |
| **Griffin HMG (37) / Feuerbach (38)** | BS14/MSV1 on W2 ARM4 with Courage; **counts as Fennec or Kestrel** in the respective team. HMG favors attack, Feuerbach credible ARO and heavier targets. Shepherd and Dice Gods feature Griffin extensively | **A 2026 Irregular Order host actively dislikes Griffin**: 1.5 SWC competes with far stronger FtF Hetkari or the Black A.I.R. sniper. The other host sees the merit but less excitement. Don't buy it when the list already has excellent primary shooting |
| **Hetkari MULTI Red Fury (48)** | BS14 with Marksmanship and BS Attack (-3), ARM5/BTS6/W2 and 15-5 MOV: lethal, efficient active-turn engagements. **Shepherd's top expensive infantry shooter**; one 2026 podcast host reports extremely strong assaults | Other Irregular Order host prefers **47-point Hetkari Missile Launcher** for hard-to-remove reactive control. Choose ML if survival/order denial is the primary task; Red Fury for Turn-1/Turn-2 attack. The same speakers flag vulnerability to E/M/isolating effects |
| **Crux FTO MULTI Marksman (46/0)** | BS14, +1 Special Die even in ARO, Albedo, ARM5/BTS6/W2, specialist, Drop Bears and credible CC: **high-quality gun and button-pusher without SWC**. Shepherd's efficient pick | **Irregular Order argues for Spitfire FTO (49/1.5) only when the higher active Burst is worth SWC**; another host prefers 47–48-point Hidden Deployment Crux/Armed Turret for surprise Berserk/objective attacks. Without an FTO link, the hidden version loses team bonuses but gains unpredictability |
| **Agent Maximus (FENNECS) / Maximus TAG** | Agent is an aggressive FENNECS gun and Tactical Awareness option; with Bipandra, can form a strong forward Core. **Maximus TAG** offers Immunity (ARM), strong CC, many effective wounds and Dodge/repair synergy—Daboarder likes it into assassination and heavy hacking threats | 2026 podcasters find TAG Maximus a very expensive competing investment against Hetkari or Tik/Squalos; one prefers Agent strongly. TAG lacks the easy built-in pilot/scoring access of some TAGs—bring separate objective specialists |
| **Squalos Mk-II HMG+GL (62) / NCO MULTI HMG (66) / MMR (55)** | BS15, +1 Special Die, TacAware, cheap for a TAG. **Shepherd prefers GL** for flexible/speculative attack when a Strategos LT already supplies its order; **2026 Irregular Order host prefers NCO MULTI HMG** for AP/EXP plus order access. MMR is a low-cost specialist-free gun support / second Squalos in thematic dual-TAG plans | No Tik mobility/Mimetism; a single Squalos isn't a substitute for specialist or engineer. NCO LT Order competes with Strategos conversion *unless intentionally left unconverted* |

**Most productive small links:** **Griffin + Bipandra TacAware** (gun + Doctor + team Order); **Hetkari + Pathfinder TacAware** (gun + Sensor/Deactivator/FO + team Order, *impure*); **Squalos + Pathfinder TacAware** (TAG + mobile specialist + retreat Order); **Magistrate + FTO Jackal** (LT/CoC protection + cheap Regular). **All are context-dependent:** do not spend the one Haris on a third elite body unless it solves a real mission problem.

## ARO, midfield, airborne and other niche choices

| Profile | Practical case for taking it | Opposite view / limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Black A.I.R. Neurocinetics MULTI Sniper (33/1.5)** | MSV2 + Mimetism (-3) + reactive special-die fire is a severe lane tax; strongly favored by some 2026 Irregular Order builds | **Shepherd calls it situational:** W1, expensive, vulnerable to a single successful attack. Griffin Feuerbach / cheap mines may consume more enemy Orders at lower opportunity cost |
| **Blink HRL (28/1.5)** | Hidden, Mimetism (-6) shock ARO or surprise attack; **Daboarder is strongly positive** because the Hidden WildParrot can **interrupt a DZ assault**, and HRL threatens TR REMs. Combining a visible Sierra with an unrevealed Blink can strand a late-turn attack without remaining Orders | **Both Irregular Order hosts are skeptical for general use**, and Shepherd calls it an occasional meta/mission piece: one shot, fragile once revealed and often hard to fit. **Dice Gods keeps it** in starter-based lists. Try periodically to force respect, not automatically |
| **Sierra TR** | Persistent ARO from long lanes; Engineer support can revive it. **Daboarder combines a visible Sierra with hidden Blink** so killing the former does not clear the defence entirely | In short/blocked lanes or metas that easily remove TR REMs, a mine + Panzerfaust picket can cost fewer points and less support |
| **Yamabushi camo Chain Rifle/Panzerfaust (16)** | Regular Order, Dogged/MA3, DZ picket with a Panzerfaust across entry lanes, or cheap finishing CC. Both newer Irregular Order hosts strongly endorse one or two; Shepherd likes one | **No Surprise Attack on current profile**: Camo does not itself grant it. Long runs to enemy DZ are costly; don’t treat it as automatic attacking missile |
| **Scarecrow (especially KHD/FO)** | Hidden/advanced **BS13 Mimetism (-6)** specialist; can press a late button or counter an enemy Hacker after opponents commit. Irregular Order hosts agree it can fight after scoring, not just be a disposable WIP bot | One host says this is costly for a **pure** button-pusher, whose cheaper 18-point analogue could free 8–9 points elsewhere. Take its combat/survival capability only if you plan to exploit it |
| **Banshee LSG/Pulzar or KHD (19–23)** | AD/parachutist + Super-Jump + MSV1 and templates; cheap way to remove skirmishers, assassinate hackers, reach elevated objectives or score classifieds. **Shepherd and Daboarder rate it highly; Daboarder has fielded two** | One 2026 host calls it an average drop troop relative to Tiger Soldiers, missing Mimetism. Don't leave **both** Banshees off table if the first turn cannot afford the lost Orders; one may begin on-table. Black A.I.R. Parachutist KHD (31) is premium DZ insertion when elite/classified requirements justify cost |
| **Peacemaker mine/Repeater + Auxbot** | **Daboarder's favorite reserve/disruptor**: one slot projects a mine, direct templates, Repeater and an attack threat in the midfield, forcing the opponent to spend Orders before reaching your big gun | Less attractive if you don't intend to exploit hacking network or already have sufficient Jackal/Fennec mines. **Bulleteer** is a fast Mimetism (-6) shotgun or Spitfire alternative, but Pathfinder usually better accompanies a TAG |
| **Redeye Blitzen (37/1.5) vs GL (38/1.5)** | **Daboarder prefers Blitzen**: hard to attack with another aerial, excellent mobile E/M ARO, forces enemy big targets to dodge rather than advance; flies into a scoring zone and forces the enemy to divert many Orders. **Irregular Order Sep-2026 rates Redeye a top flyer and considers two** | **Shepherd is less convinced** because its gun/points compete with Squalos. GL gives speculative-fire reach for +1 point, but Daboarder found he almost never wanted to spend Orders on those grenades. Big **Drumbot HRMC + controller** can threaten with multiple attacks against different ARO choices, but the controller is W1 and expensive. **Daboarder** repeatedly cut even cheap Drummers to fund other tools. | **Firefly Engineer / CoC / Hacker / HRL / Turret** | **Engineer (23)** repairs TAG/REM, **CoC (23)** insures an active LT outside its endangered team, **Hacker (24/0.5)** is WIP13/BTS6 with Stealth/Albedo, **HRL (24/1.5)** is a gun using Albedo, **Minelayer/Turret (24)** deploys a free threat; all have NCO | **Major disagreement:** Shepherd thinks turret nerfs make it a secondary option; Irregular Order still likes multiple Firefly turrets because even one-use obstacles consume enemy Orders; Daboarder prefers flexibility of selecting mine versus turret in reserve. **Old reloadable-turret spam discussions are obsolete**. | **Bipandra TacAware (25)** | WIP13 Doctor reroll with MediKit (+1B), team Order and **Fennec/Kestrel counts-as**. | **Emily (24 GL/E/M GL), Sforza FTO, Quinn (26), Harper FTO, Knauf/Dart** | **Emily** adds regular FO/Number 2 and Speculative E/M/normal GL from a Fireteam; **Sforza FTO** gives NCO, +1SD, X-Visor Viral and sometimes Albedo; **Quinn** provides MSV1, Disco and counts-as; **Harper** offers SWC support and resilient specialist utility; **Knauf** is an unlinked long-range shooter; **Dart** advanced attack/mission option | **Irregular Order favors Sforza over Quinn/Harper** because the linked viral rifle has a distinct lethal role; Emily loses a cost comparison to 11-point E/M Jackals *unless* you want Regular linked FO/Number 2. Another host and Daboarder like Emily for spare-order spec-fire. Harper and Dart are often squeezed out by cheaper specialists or superior guns. Knauf is a discretionary solo shooting slot, not a Fireteam member |

**Support notes:** **Fugazi AVA1 (7)** is excellent only if a Flash Pulse/Repeater/Regular Order is worth the model slot—do not auto-include over a role-complete Fennec or linked Jackal. **Sāchā (6 Irregular)** is a situational FD specialist/E/M grenade filler when permitted by current ITS. **Mulebot Baggage** matters when reloading supported disposables or reinforcing an active REM/hacking plan, not by default. **Tech-Bee TAGCom** currently boosts **Dodge (PH+3)** and **GizmoKit (PH+1)**—**not BS+1**. With TAG Maximus or Tikbalang, evaluate repair architecture (Tech-Bee, Machinist, Palbot) rather than casually assuming a Doctor/Paramedic can repair a TAG.

## Opposing opinions — shortest useful version

| Decision | Take A because… | Take B because… |
|---|---|---|
| **Active vs reactive Hetkari** | **Red Fury:** extreme FtF superiority makes an offensive turn count (Shepherd; one 2026 host) | **Missile:** ARM5/BTS6/W2 and strong modifiers make a costly ARO sink (other 2026 host) |
| **Griffin vs another gun** | **Griffin:** durable MSV1 attacker **and** Core-enabling piece; Feuerbach doubles as ARO (Shepherd/Dice Gods) | **Hetkari / Black A.I.R. / Maximus:** more decisive specialized shooting for incremental points/SWC (Irregular Order dissent) |
| **Hacking investment** | **Fennec hacker/Black A.I.R./Firefly:** Repeater coverage, anti-Stealth, missions and electronic deterrence | **Avoid relying on weak hacking:** superior enemy network can disable your own HI/TAG while a WIP12 hacker contributes little (Irregular Order/Dante) |
| **CoC shell** | **Two Magistrates, LT + CoC in separate Duos:** redundant command and cheap Jackal Orders (Irregular Order) | **One Magistrate:** spending another 21 + escort points delays an extra attacker/objective unit (Shepherd); a Firefly CoC can accompany a different team |
| **Reactive slots** | **Black A.I.R. / Blink:** high-impact visible or hidden shot (Irregular Order; Daboarder for Blink) | **Griffin/Jackals/mines:** cheaper, durable or more distributed ways to consume opponent Orders (Shepherd) |
| **Redeye/Drummer vs heavy shooter** | **Redeye or Drummer:** mobility, unique angle, peripheral attacks and opponent-distraction (Daboarder/Irregular Order) | **Hetkari/Tik/Squalos:** simpler superior firepower and fewer W1/peripheral vulnerabilities (Shepherd) |
| **Mission specialists** | **Banshee or Scarecrow:** late unexpected objective access after attack routes open | **Fennec FO + Pathfinder + Bipandra:** reliable deployed order access/recovery and no reliance on AD timing |
| **Jackal FTO LRL vs Panzerfaust** | **LRL:** reusable close-lane template/continuous damage | **Panzerfaust:** range and multiple saves into the important attacker; favored as default by both 2026 hosts |

These are **purpose-specific disagreements, not a popularity vote**. The fact that multiple reviewers favor a model does not make it obligatory across scenarios.

## Build and play checklist

1. **Choose the mission and fallback initiative:** if the mission favors going second, can the list still score if forced to go first and deploy first? **Daboarder** deliberately builds for that adverse outcome.
2. **Buy the command plan:** Magistrate/Jackal shell, alternative LT, or exposed Tik/Hetkari plus *physically separate* CoC if needed. If relying on FT Master, protect the Magistrate for next turn's Order Count.
3. **Pick one principal attacker and a genuinely different backup:** Hetkari Red Fury + short-range Banshee; Tikbalang + E/M Jackal/mines; Redeye + Squalos; Griffin Feuerbach + Crux/hidden unit. Another similar HMG is often less valuable.
4. **Ensure objective access:** cheap Fennec FO/Disco, Pathfinder, infiltrating Scarecrow, AD Banshee, Doctor/Engineer/classified coverage as demanded by the packet. Account for **off-table units** before counting opening-turn Orders.
5. **Decide defensive architecture:** cheap mines and Panzerfaust crossfires first; add Black A.I.R., Sierra or Blink only if they cover *different* attack approaches. Use reserve deployments to put a disruptive piece opposite the enemy plan.
6. **Account for anti-hacking and recovery:** don't expose every HI/REM/TAG to one Turn-1 Pitcher. Put a deactivator/engineer/KHD/disposable close-assault route where it can actually answer an advancing repeater or hacker.
7. **Sequence your activations:** clear key ARO → pressure critical enemy piece → exploit gap with specialist/Redeye/Banshee → **spend a TacAware partner's Order to retreat the TAG/link**. Preserve a score-on-Turn-3 piece rather than winning unnecessary firefights.
8. **Audit SWC, silhouette and exact Fireteam composition in the live Army app.** Older YouTube and podcasts include pre-N5.3 unit prices, Fennec LT Decoy claims and turret/reload assumptions that do not survive the current reference.
