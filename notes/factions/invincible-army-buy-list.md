## Low-value purchases / avoid buying first (source-backed, not unsupported bans)

- **Qiang Gāo as plain expensive primary gun:** Swiss Miss placed him in **C tier** compared with generic Shàng Jí utility/linked power. Useful anti-E/M/IMM-B/immunity matchup survives; **proxy instead of buying first**. Not categorically awful.
- **Zhanshi Yīshēng Doctor and base Mech-Engineer:** user's **skip** respected. Swiss Miss specifically questions the doctor as routine support versus embedded linked Paramedics. Still useful in recovery-specific missions; a borrowed/declared **S2 LI donor + existing S1 Palbot/Yudbot** is lower-cost test.
- **Tai Sheng without explicit CoC/NCO/FO purpose:** costly character in a 15-trooper limited roster. Since **older Action Pack and Zúyǒng character box include her**, do not buy a *third* copy for compulsory painting. Krit/KHD/paramedic can cover other utility; not strict replacement.
- **Chung-Hee Jeong and Johnny Kao as redundant premium engineering:** specialized bonuses and turrets can matter, but **Krit** plus a generic engineer can satisfy normal repairs. Price leads for named engineers are not mandates, and **turret model not presumed included**.
- **Hac Tao Hacker just to press ordinary mission buttons:** choose cheaper **Zhēnchá/Hǎidào** if concealed durability is unnecessary; **NCO gun Hac Tao** remains a credible premium plan. The old Booster Alpha and White Banner Expansion have **only one IA-legal model** each.
- **Duplicate S5 attacker / duplicate TAG / too many static Yān Huǒ**: Mówáng and Yān Huǒ provide genuinely different active-turn and ARO roles, but first copies are usually enough. Guijia at 77pt changes the entire list. Avoid buying toward full **Yān Huǒ AVA4, Mówáng AVA3, Gūijiă AVA2, Shàng Jí AVA5** without actual planned simultaneous models.
- **Second/third Liú Xīng and EVO just for one landing roll**: combat jump utility can be good, but source-backed opportunity cost is high. You have an initial Liú Xīng in either modern starter; try one before needing more or EVO chassis.
- **Léi Gōng** as another generic shooter: nuanced. Albedo, MSV and Super-Jump are distinctive matchup tools, but regular Shàng Jí linked shooting often solves more situations for the points. **Proxy first**.
- **Sāchā 6pt filler / other rare utility**: **no justified universal “never take” finding**. New exclusive ITS sculpt does not mean bad unit. Its tournament utility can be worthwhile, use S2 LI proxy first.
- **Missing Gūdǎn is a more serious collection gap than one rare character**: repeat recommendations in Swiss Miss, CanCon and Loss E169 identify cheap Zapper/HFT one-use Camo/defensive unit as a major order-protection engine. [Swiss Miss IA tier list](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SvycUWMYKr4) · [CanCon podium](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rJJ4PamI-hE) · [Loss E169](https://open.spotify.com/episode/7fhSbt1XOU69pXVhLnZEmu).
- **No universal whole-unit “never take”** claim: the IA video closes with “no real duds anymore.” All “skip” above means **not worth buying a dedicated model for this collection/plan**, not a declaration that current Army excludes it.
