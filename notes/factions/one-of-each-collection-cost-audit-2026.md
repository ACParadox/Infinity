# One-of-each unit collection costing — source-gap audit (2026-10-31)

**Status: PARTIAL / not authoritative full-cost totals.** This audit does not replace the existing core-purchase totals or imply that any unpriced miniatures are free. The latest current N5.3 sectorial references and recorded physical inventories continue to control legality and ownership.

## Target accounting

- For each current standard roster, obtain **one physical dedicated miniature for each distinct unit identity** absent from the recorded inventory, rather than filling its AVA or buying several weapon variants. A named unit in another legal sectorial owned already is covered by that same physical model; a *proxy of another named unit* is not automatically an owned dedicated miniature.
- Do not charge for the four conventional generic REM roles (Flash/repeater, Forward Observer/Sensor, Total Reaction, Guided Missile) or standard baggage/servant bodies outside **Combined Army**. Continue to account for specialized REMs separately, including Vostok, Tsyklon, Meteor and other unusual troops. For Combined Army, include basic REMs unless exact ones are recorded owned.
- Exclude **whole-unit Avoid purchases** when specifically directed, rather than inferring an entire legal unit is worthless because one weapon option, tactic or use case was criticized. This audit provisionally leaves conditional/contested avoids open and flags them, rather than hiding their costs.
- Prefer recorded Miniatures Market and Ultimate Toy Zone USD product prices, converted to CAD at the saved benchmark **1 USD = 1.3985 CAD**. When unavailable, report separately any explicit Canadian retailer fallback. Product box contents must be verified for overlap and one box is charged only once. Archived price != verified current stock or checkout total.
- If **any required unique unit has no defensible product price**, the full one-of-each completion cost is **unknown**. Report the *priced subset* only, never present it as a full cost. Taxes, shipping and FX card spreads are excluded.
