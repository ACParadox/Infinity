# Neoterran Capitaline Army — List-Building and Tactical Note

**Reference archive:** [Primary source recordings, N5.3 tactical cross-references, battle reports and purchasing audit](references/nca_refs.md).

## Overview

**What Neoterra does exceptionally well:** field **some of the game's strongest concealed first-shot guns** while keeping cheap, useful scoring infrastructure behind them. The Swiss Guard, camouflaged Uhlan and economical Squalos pose different problems; **Bolts, Blockers, Hexas, Auxilia and mobile REMs** prevent the entire plan from collapsing if one attacker loses its first exchange. NCA excels at *layered, recoverable threats*, not just BS15 spam.

### Particularly strong or unusual Fireteams

- **Ultima Ratio Duo:** **Squalos Mk-II** with Wildcard **Pathfinder TacAware** joins actual shooting to FO/Sensor/Repeater scoring and a personal activation. Native choices also include the **Maximus TAG**. This is **not** a legal Uhlan Duo.
- **Neoterra Core/Haris:** **Bolt or ORC** required-choice gun base, with **Machinist NCA** (counts as Bolt), **Blockers**, and legal Wildcards such as **Black Friar, Quinn or Hannibal**. Excellent when linked Bolt ARO and repair/hacking help are both needed.
- **Fusilier Core/Haris:** a cheap required **Fusilier** can support **Blocker** network defense and limited ORC/CSU firepower rather than tying the whole budget into elite infantry.
- **CSU Duo:** **CSU + Deva** or eligible **Blocker** makes an economical independent scoring/hacker module.

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **Swiss Hidden Deployment and Uhlan Camo are independent apexes**, not a Fireteam; build distinct routes so a Sensor or a strong ARO cannot answer every threat.
- **Aerial Redeye VH access:** rare among PanO sectorials; fast vertical/off-angle attack, but **No Cover, S7, hacking and 1.5 SWC** are serious limitations.
- **MSV3 plus projected Eclipse:** **Aquila Guard Disco** or **Quinn Disco** gives high-end vision-control access, but at much greater cost than a Kestrel Fennec.
- **Hacking and CoC infrastructure:** **Blocker**, **Hexa** and **Locust** cover different cyber positions; **Quinn** or **Bolt CoC** protects an exposed fighting LT.
- **Auxilia FO + Auxbot** is inexpensive synchronized objective defense; the two bodies do not create two Regular Orders.
- **Profile-specific legal limits:** Aquila/Blade/Uma Wildcards have FTO restrictions; **Clipper FTO and Uhlan are not Fireteam-eligible** under NCA's chart. [Canonical NCA chart](../../sectorials/panoceania/neoterran-capitaline-army.md#fireteams).

### The 30-second listbuilding rule

**Start with a problem-solving chain, not an impressive Core:** (1) one premium gun to suppress the opponent; (2) a *different* approach if it fails (hidden attack, flank, Sensor, hacking or Redeye); (3) 1–2 Auxilia, cheap Fugazi, and a real objective plan; (4) recovery within reach; (5) optional small Fireteams if they improve the chain. NCA has inexpensive Regulars but its premium guns eat SWC and points. *Reason:* without an alternative opening, NCA can be shooting-locked when the first one or two engagements go wrong (Shepherd). Trent instead almost always starts with **a TAG + Auxilia + support**, a stronger emphasis on TAG-led construction.

## Core identity — specialist pieces and effective activations

The strongest repeated modern NCA theme is:

> **Use cheap structural/control pieces to enable a small number of extremely specialized premium threats, then exploit Tactical Awareness, Hidden Deployment, Repeaters, Sensor and flexible small Fireteams to get more useful activations than the raw Regular-Order count suggests.**

Current NCA has:

- **1 Core**;
- **1 Haris**;
- **unlimited Duos**;
- a very broad Wildcard pool;
- many Tactical Awareness profiles;
- several premium Hidden/Camouflage attackers;
- unusually dense Repeater/Sensor infrastructure for PanO.

A modern NCA list often looks more like:

> **cheap LT / support shell + 2–3 premium threats with different attack vectors + network/control layer + recovery + mission pieces**

than:

> **five-model Fusilier Core + one Swiss Guard + generic REMs.**

The old five-Fusilier structure remains legal and usable, but it is no longer the automatic center of the army.

## Major current list-building principle — threat diversity over redundant weapon roles

The current 2026 community discussion repeatedly recommends a premium package such as:

> **Uhlan + Redeye + Swiss Guard**

This works because those pieces fail differently:

- **Uhlan:** Camouflage TAG, long-range anti-armour hunter, durable chassis, Tactical Awareness.
- **Swiss Guard:** Hidden Deployment, Mimetism (-6), premium BS15 asymmetric attacker/ARO.
- **Redeye:** extreme speed, Mimetism (-6), BS14 exploitation piece.

The useful principle is:

> **Do not merely buy three expensive guns. Buy expensive pieces that attack from different information states, movement vectors and engagement bands.**

If the opponent solves one layer, the others should still function.

## Architecture A — premium combined-arms NCA

A broad, reliable structure is:

- one premium long-range piece, usually **Uhlan / Swiss / Squalos / Aquila**;
- one asymmetric or exploitation attacker, often **Swiss / Locust / Redeye / Garuda / Bulleteer**;
- Blocker / Pathfinder / Auxilia / Fugazi network infrastructure;
- an Engineer or Doctor appropriate to the premium pieces;
- cheap command shell.

A common strong starting idea is:

> **Uhlan + Swiss + Blocker Specialist + Pathfinder + Auxilia/Fugazi + cheap LT**

This gives conventional dominance, Hidden Deployment, Sensor/Pitcher utility, mission scoring, and enough cheap bodies to support the premium pieces.

### Uhlan — current premium darling

The current 70-point Feuerbach Uhlan is one of the most repeatedly praised current NCA profiles.

It brings:

- BS15;
- Camouflage;
- Mimetism (-3);
- Surprise Attack;
- Tactical Awareness;
- Feuerbach;
- Panzerfaust;
- Blitzen;
- BS Attack (+1B).

The community role is best described as:

> **Camouflage hunter-killer TAG, not static gun-platform TAG.**

It can remain a marker until the right engagement, avoid being forced into reactive duty, and use Surprise Attack on the critical attack.

#### Double Uhlan is proven, not merely theoretical

The September 2026 Satellite-winning NCA list used:

- **2× Feuerbach Uhlan**;
- Squalos Mk-II NCO;
- Blade FTO Engineer + Palbot;
- Pathfinder Tactical Awareness;
- Fugazi;
- Tech-Bee;
- Fusilier LT;
- Shona;
- Warcor.

That makes **two Uhlans** a legitimate skew architecture rather than novelty duplication.

The tactical reason is simple: each Uhlan is independently capable, self-funded by Tactical Awareness, and can pressure a separate lane.

### Swiss Guard — still one of the defining NCA pieces

Current useful profiles include:

- HMG;
- Missile Launcher (+1SD);
- Hacker with MULTI Rifle/X-Visor;
- cheap Forward Deployment AP-SMG profile;
- Forward Deployment KHD.

The important value is not only BS15 shooting.

> **The opponent does not know which Swiss profile is present, where it is, or when it will reveal.**

That means Swiss can function as:

- active-turn assassin;
- hidden reactive threat;
- protected hacker/KHD;
- late-game mission piece;
- cheap-ish forward premium body on the AP-SMG profile.

A Swiss does not need to kill its points to justify itself if Hidden Deployment forces the opponent to respect angles and network threats that may not actually exist.

## Architecture B — Tactical Awareness / TAG compression

The 2026 Satellite-winning list provides strong evidence for a distinct NCA archetype:

> **Low raw Order count, high effective activation count.**

Its key pieces were:

- 2× Uhlan — each Tactical Awareness;
- Squalos Mk-II NCO — Tactical Awareness + NCO;
- Pathfinder Tactical Awareness;
- cheap Fusilier LT;
- Blade Engineer + Palbot support.

The list had only **9 Regular Orders**, but **5 Tactical Awareness Orders** before considering NCO interaction with the Lieutenant Order.

The important lesson is not “three TAGs are good.” It is:

> **NCA can compress a large amount of active-turn work into expensive pieces that partially fund themselves.**

This allows low-headcount lists to remain functional without paying for a conventional 14–15 Regular Order pool.

### Fusilier LT → Squalos NCO

This is one of NCA's cleanest command/economy packages.

Current Squalos Mk-II NCO profile brings:

- MULTI HMG;
- Tactical Awareness;
- NCO;
- BS15 TAG chassis.

A 10-point Fusilier LT therefore contributes a Lieutenant Order that can become useful attack fuel instead of remaining stranded in the DZ.

This package is particularly attractive in TAG-heavy lists where the Fusilier LT is otherwise just cheap command infrastructure.

### Pathfinder Tactical Awareness

The 17-point Tactical Awareness Pathfinder is one of NCA's strongest quiet utility pieces.

It brings:

- Forward Observer;
- Sensor;
- Repeater;
- Triangulated Fire;
- mobility;
- Wildcard access;
- its own Tactical Awareness Order.

That means it often self-funds the movement needed to:

- score;
- Sensor a marker;
- advance the Repeater network;
- reposition into a useful Fireteam role.

The Satellite-winning triple-TAG list used exactly this profile.

## Blocker — core structural unit

Current Blocker chassis provides:

- Sensor;
- Discover reroll;
- X-Visor;
- BS Attack (+1B);
- adhesive threat;
- Pulzar;
- multiple Fireteam families.

The current profile distinction is critical.

### 15-point Specialist Blocker

Current role:

- Specialist Operative;
- **Pitcher**;
- Sensor;
- Discover support;
- cheap Fireteam glue.

This is arguably the default structural profile.

It does not need to win fights. Its job is to build the table state that lets the premium pieces and hackers work.

### 19-point Hacker Blocker

Current role:

- Hacking Device;
- **Firewall (-3)**;
- Sensor/control chassis;
- no Pitcher.

Take it when the list specifically wants another real Hacker and the Firewall matters.

A useful current rule of thumb is:

> **Start with the 15-point Specialist. Add/upgrade to the Hacker because the hacking plan needs it, not because old tacticas said the Hacker also launches Pitchers.**

## NCA hacking — excellent geometry, not necessarily elite individual hackers

Current community discussion contains a useful disagreement:

- some players call NCA's hackers themselves merely average;
- others call NCA one of PanO's best hacking factions because the network is so dense.

The clean reconciliation is:

> **NCA's hacking quality is decent; its hacking geometry is excellent.**

Network/projection pieces include:

- Blocker Specialist Pitcher;
- Fugazi Repeaters;
- Pathfinder Repeaters;
- Bulleteer Repeaters;
- Peacemaker Repeaters;
- Auxilia Deployable Repeater profile;
- Tech-Bee Deployable Repeater;
- Agent Maximus TinBot Repeater;
- Locust Hacker/KHD Pitchers.

Actual hackers include:

- Blocker Hacker;
- Bolt HD/KHD;
- Deva HD/KHD;
- Locust HD/KHD;
- Hexa HD/KHD;
- Swiss HD/KHD;
- Fusilier Hacker.

The goal is usually not to out-hack Nomads directly.

The goal is:

> **Make enemy HI/TAG movement through the midfield expensive and uncertain while your BS14–15 pieces dominate ordinary gunfights.**

### Visible network + hidden hacker

A particularly strong architecture is:

> **Blocker/Fugazi/Pathfinder/Auxilia network + hidden Hexa KHD or Swiss/Locust hacker.**

The opponent can see the Repeaters but may not know where the actual counter-hacker is.

That creates information pressure: trying to attack the network with their own hacker may reveal into a hidden KHD they could not account for.

Compare complete indirect-fire packages, not just the launcher:

- **Clipper Guided (16/1.5):** requires real Spotlight setup, SWC and appropriate linked hacking or FO/Repeater support (Fusilier, Fugazi, Pathfinder or Peacemaker when legal).
- **Clipper FTO +1B Missile:** different *non-Guided* role where available.
- **Squalos Mk-II HMG + GL (62/1.5):** an active shooter with optional Speculative attacks.
- **Swiss Guard / Aquila:** conventional gun and command alternatives that avoid the full setup, save and Reset sequence.

[Whole-game apex/value, situational decisions, reviewed disagreements](../list-building/indirect-fire-speculative-guided-apex-value.md).

## Locust — substantially improved current role

Current Locust brings:

- Hidden Deployment;
- Mimetism (-6);
- Forward Deployment (+20);
- several cheap specialized loadouts.

Important profiles include:

- cheap SMG + D-Charges + WildParrot;
- Hacker with Breaker Combi + Pitcher + Firewall;
- KHD with Breaker Combi + Pitcher + Firewall.

The Hacker/KHD versions let NCA create a hacking entry point already near midfield:

> **Hidden Deployment → reveal → Pitcher from advanced position → hack through the new network.**

This reduces the Order cost of moving a conventional hacker from the DZ.

The cheap WildParrot profile is also a good sleeper pick: inexpensive Hidden Deployment disruption in an army where the opponent must already respect Swiss/Hexa-style hidden threats.

## Hexa — hidden mission and counter-hacking piece

Hexa retains:

- Hidden Deployment;
- Mimetism (-6);
- Counterintelligence;
- HD/KHD and gunfighter options.

The 2026 Mainfinity list paired three different tools:

- **Hexa KHD:** hidden counter-hacking.
- **Blocker Pitcher:** network projection.
- **Locust Hacker:** another hacking route.

The useful lesson is:

> **Hidden Deployment specialists do not need to be assassins.**

A Hexa can remain hidden until:

- an enemy hacker reveals;
- a console becomes reachable;
- the opponent commits into the Repeater net;
- late-game scoring requires a protected specialist.

This makes Hidden Deployment part of NCA's mission resilience, not only its attack plan.

## Fireteam philosophy — small purpose-built teams

Current NCA has unlimited Duos and an unusually flexible Wildcard pool.

Therefore:

> **Use the smallest team that gives the needed movement/equipment synergy. Do not build to five members by reflex.**

The main Fireteam families are:

- Fusiliers;
- Neoterra (Bolt/ORC);
- Ultima Ratio;
- CSU;
- Wildcards.

### Fusilier teams

At least one real Fusilier is required.

Useful current small packages include:

#### Fusilier + Blocker + Bulleteer

Roles:

- Fusilier = cheap mandatory anchor;
- Blocker = Pitcher/Sensor/specialist/control;
- Bulleteer = actual attack piece.

This is often a more efficient use of the Haris slot than filling a conventional five-Fusilier Core.

#### Fusilier + Blade Engineer + Bulleteer

This packages:

- linked REM attacker;
- in-team Engineer;
- Tactical Awareness from Blade;
- cheap Fusilier structural body.

It is particularly attractive in REM-heavy lists.

#### Fusilier + Blocker + Aquila FTO

This provides:

- BS15 MSV3 shooting;
- Sensor/Discover support;
- Pitcher/network projection;
- specialist utility.

Use when the Aquila is a central anti-marker/mission piece.

Current Bulleteer profiles:

## Redeye — exploitation piece, not default opener

Current Redeye provides:

- extreme 20-5 movement;
- BS14;
- Mimetism (-6);
- Sensor;
- Tech-Recovery;
- Spitfire;
- grenade-launcher variant.

The Mainfinity report is useful because the Redeye did not dominate the game. It was held until late, then attempted to exploit an opening and failed.

The correct role is therefore better framed as:

> **Use Redeye after another piece has opened the board.**

Redeye's speed can exploit routes opened by:

- **Swiss Guard**;
- **Uhlan**;
- **Squalos Mk-II**.

Those heavy guns may open lanes they cannot exploit efficiently themselves.

A useful sequencing idea is:

> **Swiss/Uhlan/Squalos opens the position → Redeye crosses the table and attacks the exposed secondary layer.**

## Garuda — classic exploitation/airborne follow-up

Current Garuda options provide cheap airborne pressure after the board has been disrupted.

Typical roles:

- Boarding Shotgun/Chain-colt profile = cheap enemy-DZ attacker;
- Forward Observer = airborne mission piece;
- Spitfire = higher-investment flanking gun.

Garuda is most valuable after a premium NCA gun has forced the opponent into awkward facing/deployment or removed long-range AROs.

## Aquila — more than “BS15 MSV3 shooter”

Current FTO Aquila profiles bring:

- BS15;
- MSV3;
- MULTI Marksman Rifle;
- Disco Baller;
- Specialist Operative;
- Wildcard access.

The Strategos LT version adds command value and an SWC rebate.

The Mainfinity tournament report demonstrates an important use: the Aquila LT advanced late using the **Disco Baller** to reach the remaining antenna.

That means the Aquila's value is not merely winning FtFs.

It can deliberately **decline the gunfight**, block visibility, and complete the mission.

### Aquila Strategos LT

Use when the list wants:

- active command piece;
- deployment/Strategos benefit;
- MSV3 anti-marker gun;
- specialist;
- Disco Ball mission access;
- +1 SWC rebate on the Strategos profile.

It is a genuine build-around LT rather than a passive command model.

**Not consensus/default:** Robert Shepherd's 2026 transcript argues that the Aquila Disco Baller LT often costs too much compared with a protected Fusilier/Auxilia LT, with or without a separate decoy. Take the Aquila when the plan really uses its specialist, MSV3, Strategos, Disco Baller and Fireteam access—not simply because it has several premium abilities.

**Vision-control choices are expensive in NCA:**

- **Aquila MSV3 / Disco Baller:** a premium specialist and protected-crossing solution. Gravitypool considers the FTO Disco spearhead plausible; Shepherd questions its price unless the mission demands it. A Mainfinity match illustrates its unusual late-antenna utility.
- **Richard Quinn Disco:** another scoring/crossing route with a real cost.
- **Fennec (13-point Disco in Kestrel):** **not** automatically an NCA option merely because both are PanO; do not import Kestrel's cheap Eclipse plan.

## Recovery architecture matters more than it looks

The Mainfinity battle report illustrates recovery from losses of two separate gunfighters:

- **Bolt Sniper:** returned to the fight after recovery.
- **Black Friar:** likewise returned after recovery.

That means recovery is not merely a safety net.

> **If an opponent spends several Orders removing a premium ARO piece and it stands back up, the recovery model has effectively increased the opponent's total Orders-to-solve-the-threat.**

Prioritize support according to the list:

- **Blade FTO Engineer + Palbot** for mechanical-heavy lists;
- Machinist for Bolt/ORC/TAG/REM packages;
- Trauma-Doc + Palbot for expensive organic shooters;

## Battle-report-backed compact Fireteams and army modules (2026-11-03)

These are additions to the existing Uhlan/Swiss/TAG archetypes, not replacements for them. Both linked examples use a **Bolt anchor** in the [current NCA Neoterra family](../../sectorials/panoceania/neoterran-capitaline-army.md#fireteams); **Aquila must be an allowed FTO loadout** and Wildcards do not eliminate the required ORC/Bolt choice.

- **Hannibal forward attack Haris — [#044](../battle-reports/041-050-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md):** **Hannibal (Strategic Deployment) + Shona Carano + Bolt Killer Hacker** was expressly identified as a three-member link. It combines forward positioning, credible CC deterrence and a Hacker without purchasing three expensive guns. The reporter also mentions a *second* three-body group, but **does not identify all three members**; do **not** certify its composition or claim NCA ran two Harises.
- **Aquila anti-marker mission Haris — [#227](../battle-reports/221-230-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md):** **Aquila Guard FTO + Bolt MSV1 Sniper + Shona Carano**, **three-body team explicitly activated together**. Complementary pieces in that actual **7–1 Evacuation** list included **Uhlan Feuerbach, Tech-Bee, Fugazi, Hexa KHD, Blocker Hacker, Quinn CoC, CSU and support REMs**. The mission was won by **surviving specialist/civilian deliveries and board control**, not a presumed five-Bolt Core.
- **Two alternatives for the one Haris slot:** **Hannibal/Bolt/Shona** prioritizes early aggressive positioning; **Aquila/Bolt/Shona** prioritizes MSV3 precision and reactive containment while **Uhlan** supplies the second attack axis. They **cannot be fielded as separate Harises simultaneously** in current NCA, and **Shona is unique**. Replace her with another chart-legal support option if a different list requires both offensive and defensive teams.
- **Full-list takeaway from #227:** an **Uhlan + compact Bolt-centered Haris + cheap mission Specialists + actual engineering/hacking defence** can outperform a low-body-count high-Order opponent by keeping multiple scoring routes alive. This complements, rather than contradicts, the existing **double-Uhlan/triple-TAG** skew; one played **7–1** is not a general matchup rating.

**Legality trap:** current **Fusiliers** teams still require an actual Fusilier, and a named **Aquila FTO** in a Bolt-based Neoterra team does **not** make it a Fusilier Core. Check **Aquila FTO options 5/7/8** and profile-specific Hacker/Firewall coverage in the authoritative reference before buying the models.

## Final list-building heuristic

A strong NCA list should normally answer these questions before filling the remaining points:

1. **What opens the table?** — Uhlan, Swiss, Squalos, Aquila, Bolt package.
2. **What attacks from a different axis?** — Hidden Swiss/Locust, Redeye, Garuda, Bulleteer.
3. **How do I detect/pressure marker states?** — Blocker, Pathfinder, Hannibal, Aquila, Deva.
4. **How do I pressure hackable targets?** — Repeaters/Pitchers plus one or more appropriate hackers.
5. **Who scores after the expensive pieces fight?** — Pathfinder, Auxilia, Blocker, Blade, hidden specialist.
6. **Can I recover my expensive pieces?** — Blade/Machinist/Trauma-Doc + Palbot as appropriate.
7. **Does my command plan generate usable Orders?** — cheap LT, Squalos NCO, TacAware density, or aggressive Aquila/Maximus LT.
8. **Am I paying for a Fireteam because it solves a problem, or merely because I can fill it?**

The main modern NCA insight is:

> **Do not judge the list only by raw Regular Orders or raw BS. Judge how many useful activations the army can produce, how many different attack vectors the opponent must respect, and how cheaply the support shell can create favorable engagements for the premium pieces.**
