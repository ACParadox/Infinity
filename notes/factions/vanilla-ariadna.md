# Vanilla Ariadna — Listbuilding Guide (Infinity N5.3)

**Purpose:** practical choices, why they work, and competing opinions. **Research checked 2026-10-19.** Video and podcast transcripts are used as tactical evidence, **not** authority for unit costs, Army legality or tournament usage. Current profiles/Fireteams come from the [N5.3 Vanilla Ariadna reference](../../sectorials/ariadna/vanilla-ariadna.md) (Army **7.26246.158**, 2026-09-03 data; verified 2026-10-15). Older 2025 opinions can predate roster/rules changes.

## At a glance

- **Identity:** many *independent* camouflaged threats, exceptionally punishing AP/EXP/portable autocannon fire, cheap mines/Smoke, Speculative Fire from Dog-Warriors, and dangerous deployment-zone parachutists. **Why it works:** the opponent cannot efficiently identify/clear every threat, and losing one attacker rarely disables the others. [Shepherd 2026; Loss of Lieutenant #164]
- **Strong repeatable engine:** **Dynamo AP Spitfire (25/1 SWC) + Paramedic (16/0.5) Duo** = **41 points / 1.5 SWC**. The fast attacker chooses favorable angles; the specialist scores; both lay Shock mines and provide Smoke. A **5-point Traktor Mul Baggage** keeps Mine Dispensers and disposable weapons supplied. [Shepherd; Tactical Awareness; CNN]
- **Hard defensive architecture:** **TankHunter autocannon/ML camouflage + Strelok Decoys parked in the DZ + Vystrel Neurocinetics/Sapper**. **Why:** camouflage makes shooting positions ambiguous and expensive to discover; Vystrel threatens an obvious lane. Do not reveal a TankHunter simply because an inconsequential target passed. [Shepherd; Loss of Lieutenant #164]
- **Command fork:** **Voronin Strategos LT (22) + William Wallace Chain of Command (38)** creates strong command resilience and an independent endgame attacker; **9-point Rokot LT** frees ~13+ points when premium command is not useful. Wallace is **not a vanilla LT** on the pinned Army roster. [Shepherd; current reference]
- **Structural weakness:** weak native hacking/anti-hacking, mostly **MSV1 rather than MSV2**, and sometimes awkward mission scoring under attrition. Ariadna's many *nonhackable* attacks can make enemy hacking investment inefficient, **but the Chernobog and REMs remain hackable**. [Shepherd; LoL #164]
- **Important counterargument:** the 2025 Tactical Awareness team preferred Kosmoflot/TAK for stronger integrated Fireteams, while Shepherd's 2026 tournament-winning vanilla build obtained unique value from the wider roster, the Kibervolk and the **threat** of Mirage-5/Van Zant even when absent. **Conclusion:** vanilla rewards independent, redundant plans; a sectorial may suit players who want a stronger linked gun. [Tactical Awareness S3E11; Shepherd 2026]

**Against hacking:** Ariadna's best nonhackable attackers can refuse the electronic fight. This does **not** protect a Chernobog, Vystrel or Traktor (Hackable), so support your TAG/REM with safe paths, screen the approach with mines/smoke, and prioritize enemies that project Repeaters. **Wardriver** exists (**14/1 Hacking Device**, or **16/1 with Disco Baller**) but does not transform Ariadna into a high-end hacking faction. [Shepherd; LoL #141]

## Confirmed disagreements / source-age corrections

1. **Vanilla vs sectorial:** *Tactical Awareness S3E11* (Feb 2025) calls vanilla an inferior leftovers list versus TAK/Kosmoflot due missing strong Core and fewer unique new tools. *Shepherd/LoL #164* (Feb 2026) argues vanilla wins through wider selection, Kibervolk, portable-autocannon/Decoy defense and rear-entry threat—even without fielding the rear-entry pieces. Both can be true for different list architectures.
2. **Are Haris/Duos worth the orders?** *CNN Counterintelligence* and some Tactical Awareness speakers would largely take only the **Dynamo Duo**; other Tactical Awareness plans take FT-02 Briscard/Loup/Bruant/Moblot links for viral/MSV/specialist synergy. Evaluate each against 3 independent troopers and the one Haris slot.
3. **Chernobog rating:** *LoL #142* splits **higher B** vs **C** for the 63-point TAG: cheap AP/ARM8 Tactical Awareness vs the abrupt Transmutation drop and PH9 Dodge/self-Gizmo. The **Kosmoflot Tactical Awareness** hosts see a genuinely strong gun but also debate paying 7 points for the rocket profile. Current Chernobog **63/70**, not old “65” quotes.
4. **Camo gun as killer vs deterrent:** Shepherd chose Ariadna largely for huge autocannon damage in practice, but reports those TankHunters **mostly delayed and threatened, rather than delivered kills**, in the actual five games. Include the order-tax effect in unit valuation.
5. **Old list legality and mistakes:** 2025 episode comments on vanished units and incomplete N5 rosters may be outdated; **Kibervolk does not have Climbing Plus** (Shepherd's own correction); Vystrel is **AVA1 in vanilla**, and the vanilla Chernobog is **AVA1/FT-01 max1** even though Kosmoflot can construct different teams. The pinned chart, not a remembered 2025 podcast layout, determines legality.

## Vision control — role/value update (23 October 2026)

- **Budget movement first:** **Dynamo AP Spitfire + Paramedic Duo 41/1.5** can provide fast Smoke, firepower and scoring across separate angles; Dog-Warrior/Polaris and Irmandinho offer other Smoke/close threats. **Mul Baggage** improves Disposable reload plans when positioning allows.
- **Not the same as MSV smoke-shooting:** Ariadna often uses Smoke to move nonhackable attackers, not to enable an elite MSV2 sniper. Against MSV defenders use flanks, mines, alternative gun types and scoring pressure. [Apex/value vision guide](../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).

## Indirect fire — Speculative and Guided (2026-10-23)

**Value:** Traktor Katyusha **9/1** is a cheap Guided payoff *only* when a Chasseur/Strelok/SAS/other FO already marks targets. **Dog-Warrior PH-based Speculative**, Carmen+Bâtard grenades and Kuryer where available work **without** target-state setup. Against dispersed nonhackable lists, an expensive Hacking→Guided plan can do very little (Shepherd's 2026 CanCon account); count Range/Setup/Reset/Orders.

[Global apex/value tiers, when-to-use decisions, and MCP-reviewed opposing views](../list-building/indirect-fire-speculative-guided-apex-value.md).

## Force multipliers — N5.3 apex/value update (2026-10-23)

**Traktor Mul Baggage5/0** is exceptional cheap Regular Order + reload where legal; don't confuse its artillery profiles with free Baggage cost. **Voronin Counterintelligence LT** is solid early-turn protection, but **current TAK Voronin has NO Strategos**—army variants differ. **Spetsnaz/Strelok/Scout Decoy** creates hidden target ambiguity; false Decoys don't make extra AROs or Orders. Ariadna rarely gains from EVO unless a legitimate rostered device and meaningful support targets exist.

**Voronin LT 22/0 Strategos L1/Counterintelligence/Sensor** is extremely affordable flexible command; **Wallace LT 38/0 Inspiring Leadership** is better when several genuinely **Irregular** attackers become Regular. **Wallace CoC 38/0** is a *different non-LT fighter* and does **not** regularize the army until succession. **TankHunter CoC 25/0 Minelayer** offers protected camouflaged succession, while **Vassily CoC 39** adds a gun/midfield role at higher cost. ### Mission specialist / button-pusher choices (N5.3)

**Strelok FO17/0** with Camo/FD+20/Decoy(1) is among the cheapest **midfield objective openers**; **Dynamo/other biker specialists** are faster lateral closers but more exposed. **TankHunter CoC25/0** combines concealed succession, Minelayer and Specialist access; **Vassily LT+FO35** is an alternate Camo/Decoy(2) command scoring asset but do not risk a vital LT merely for a console. Pair Doctor/paramedic/Engineer from legal roster for actual medical/tech Classifieds.

### Airborne / enemy-DZ attack profile choices (2026-10-23)

**Van Zant AP Rifle33/0 or T2 BSG35/0** is reliable *enemy-DZ Parachutist(DZ)* with **TA, Dogged and MA3**, excellent for 2+ exposed Order/support kills; **Mirage-5 Margot+Duroc60/2** is a compound **two-body** DZ pressure package (1 Regular +1 Irregular + Margot TA + Impetuous), not two generic Regular Orders. **Strelok/Chasseur camo** gives safer midfield deployment when edge screens are heavy. **Wallace CoC/LT** are different on-table choices; do not assign them direct airborne attack. ### Active-turn gunfighter cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Active-turn shooting choices:** **Spetsnaz Camo HMG36/1.5** combines Marksmanship/Mimetism(-3)/Surprise for a cheap **non-TAG** anti-Cover first shot; it has only 1W and lacks AP for top armor. **Veteran Kazak AP HMG45/1.5** instead buys robust NWI/Warhorse and **nonhackable AP** sustained attacks. **Chernobog** is a separate **TAG** multi-STR/TacAware AP breakthrough for the bigger investment, while **Van Zant/Mirage** are alternative rear-attack delivery pieces avoiding frontal FtF rather than extra HMGs. ### TAG tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Chernobog (63 HFT / 70 HRL)** is a rare true high-ARM AP HMG chassis in Ariadna, useful when durable conventional ranged attack is missing. Its Transmutation/damage progression means judge the *remaining useful role* after a failed armor save; cheap camo specialists/warbands still need points and Orders. Ariadna cannot simply assume another faction's Repeater/Firewall support infrastructure.

For the two-tier **apex/points-value** comparison, TAG counterplay, matchup matrix and direct transcript sources: [N5.3 TAG field guide](../list-building/tags.md). Treat current profiles, points/SWC and canonical Fireteam data above as authoritative.

### Hacker and KHD tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Only a thin standard cyber package:** the **Wardriver HD14/1 SWC** (a 16pt option brings Disco Baller) provides Carbonite/Oblivion/Spotlight, but **WIP13, few Repeaters and 1 SWC** make it a *situational counter-tech* rather than an Interventor-style net. There is **no equivalent broad native KHD network** in the pinned Vanilla roster. **Why:** Ariadna's largely non-Hackable attack pieces and cheap Camo/warbands often deny enemy hacking by simply not being legal HD targets; spend SWC on a true ranged ARO/attacker first unless the mission or enemy TAG/HI makes a Wardriver's cyber effect worth more.

See the [cross-game apex/value Hacker/KHD tier guide](../list-building/hackers-and-killer-hackers.md). **The sectorial's current profiles, AVA and canonical Fireteam chart above remain authoritative; device upgrades are profile-specific.**


### Cheerleader / Order-generation tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

Cross-faction [apex and cost-value Order-generator rankings](../list-building/cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md), circumstance guide and transcript disagreements. **This file remains authoritative for profiles, AVA, Training and Fireteam legality.**

- Line Kazak — S2 baseline Regular Order with table-defense/decoy benefits; its cheapest loadout beats passive expensive guns.
- Warcor — Irregular Flash Pulse ARO distraction, NOT a passive transferable Regular Order.
- Baggage — prioritize a legal 8pt basic bot if actually on roster, remembering its large base.

#### Classification and evidence standards

- **Must-have / near-must:** repeated first-choice inclusion across independently built example lists, or an explicit reviewer “always / most lists / auto-include” judgment. **Near-must is not universal:** a role can be indispensable without any one profile being mandatory.
- **Go-to:** repeatedly attractive price/role efficiency. Not all go-to units belong in the same 300-point list; missions, fire lanes, SWC, Orders and competing packages decide.
- **Never take / strong avoid:** a directly stated negative review of a **specific profile or construction**, ideally with a viable same-roster alternative. Use **conditional** or **contested** if the profile has a niche, there is dissent or the source is pre-rework. There is **no evidenced blanket ban on an entire Ariadna unit family**.
- **Evidence:** **explicit** = specific presenter says so; **repeat** = multiple independently constructed sample lists; **reasoned** = synthesis of recorded strengths/weaknesses plus current reference, **not** a claimed quotation.
- A recorded list choice alone does not prove “most players” use a unit. Transcripts may mangle Ariadna proper names, so check names and legality against the current repository. The standard lists here do **not** import `L'Équipe Argent` Reinforcements, which have separate charts.

**Tournament proxy policy:** Always match **current model footprint/base and Silhouette**, reveal every declared proxy to your opponent, and check the **current-season ITS and organizer rules** before bringing conversions or another faction's miniature. It is not safe to treat older season-specific event proxy wording as current 2026 policy. **A list's compound units must have all required pieces**; proxies do not create extra model counts, and an unavailable unit or illegal Fireteam remains illegal even if a physical model fits.

**Buy-order conclusion:** If already collecting TAK/Caledonia, the highest payoff normally comes from **new capabilities and missing mission roles**, not duplicating an existing TankHunter, Vystrel, dog warrior, artillery chassis or front-line HMG merely to reach AVA. **Most defensible default purchasing skips:** **USARF Wild Bill**; **FRRM infiltrating Bruant** (especially Bruant + Zouave NCO); and **TAK Pavel as an unplanned generic skirmisher**, although your Pavel is already owned. **Ratnik is contested**: you own one, so there is no reason to buy a second without a tested Mobility build. **Other good proxy-first niche models:** **Kosmoflot Wallace when Irregular conversion is unnecessary, second Chernobog or Polaris**; **Caledonia McMurrough if existing Cameronians suffice**; **FRRM Anaconda, Wolfgang or Knauf** until their particular legal list recurs. These are **not** blanket unit bans.
