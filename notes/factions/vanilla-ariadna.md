# Vanilla Ariadna — Listbuilding Guide (Infinity N5.3)

## Overview

**What Vanilla Ariadna does exceptionally well:** it assembles an unusually broad **non-Hackable Camo, mine/Decoy, hard-ammunition and independent-attack toolbox** without relying on a large Fireteam. Camouflaged guns force Discover decisions while fast bikes, warbands and enemy-DZ entrants attack different routes. Its flexibility, rather than a universally dominant link, is the main advantage.

### Whole-game standouts — apex and points value

The **Top 3 / Top 5 / Top 10** labels below mean **qualitative candidates for the stated role and price band**, not official overall unit rankings or measured win rates.

- **Top 3 candidate — cheapest Regular Baggage support:** **Traktor Mul Baggage 5/0** is S+ value: an exceptionally cheap Regular Order with conditional disposable-ammunition reloads. Its Guided/Total Reaction variants are *different paid profiles*. [Support tiers](../list-building/force-multipliers-special-abilities-apex-value.md).
- **Top 5 candidate — infantry anti-TAG gun (apex/value):** **TankHunter Portable Autocannon (+1B) 36/1.5** delivers exceptional armor-killing bursts from Camo; the +1B *Vanilla* profile should not be silently substituted for TAK's plain autocannon. [Non-hacking anti-heavy tiers](../list-building/anti-tag-anti-heavy-infantry-non-hacking-apex-value.md).
- **Top 5 candidate — medium-cost active-turn HMG value:** **Kazak Spetsnaz Camo HMG 36/1.5**, rated S, combines Marksmanship, Mimetism and Surprise Attack to challenge targets in Cover. One Wound and extreme ARM remain limitations. [Gunfighter tiers](../list-building/active-turn-gunfighters-apex-and-value.md).
- **Top 10 candidate — budget midfield specialist:** **Strelok FO 17/0**, rated S, brings Camo, FD(+20), Decoy and Forward Observation without paying premium-soldier prices. [Specialist tiers](../list-building/mission-specialists-and-button-pushers-apex-value.md).
- **Top 10 candidate — multi-body Sensor/CC scouting:** **Antipode Assault Pack 26/0** is S+/S in its role: a Handler plus three Antipodes with Camo/Sensor and a Tactical Awareness activation. **One Spearhead** attacks on an Order; these are not three freely acting Regular troops. [Multi-body tiers](../list-building/peripheral-synchronized-multibody-apex-value.md).
- **Other standout, not a forced global rank:** **Voronin Strategos LT 22/0** is A+ command value; **Wallace CoC 38/0** provides succession and a dangerous independent attacker. They offer an unusually meaningful command choice. [Command tiers](../list-building/command-units-apex-and-value.md).

- **Top 5 candidate — self-funded enemy-DZ raider:** **Van Zant AP Rifle 33/0** earns **S airborne points value**, with Deployment Zone Parachutist, Tactical Awareness and Dogged; **T2 Boarding Shotgun 35/0** targets different victims. A prepared edge can still strand him. [Airborne rankings](../list-building/airborne-and-enemy-dz-attackers-apex-value.md).
- **Top 10 candidate — two-vector DZ assault (apex):** **Mirage-5, Margot + Duroc 60/2** is **S/A airborne apex/value**: Margot's BS14 specialist gun and Duroc's Smoke/close threat can split the defender's response. Both models come in the full 60-point package; their Orders are not interchangeable. [Airborne rankings](../list-building/airborne-and-enemy-dz-attackers-apex-value.md).
- **Top 5 candidate — cheapest active Engineer/Smoke/trader:** **Irmandinho Engineer + Chain Rifle + Smoke 8/0** is **S+ cheap-warband value**, and can repair or score technical objectives after screening. Its Order is Irregular until another legal effect changes that. [Cheap-warband rankings](../list-building/warbands-and-cheap-trading-units-apex-value.md).
- **Top 10 candidate — forward Speculative Fire without Targeted:** **Dog-Warrior grenade profile (21/0)** earns **S in the role** because PH-based Speculative attacks punish models hidden from ordinary direct guns; the same attacker also supplies Smoke and close violence. Approach/Order cost is the limit. [Indirect-fire rankings](../list-building/indirect-fire-speculative-guided-apex-value.md).
- **Other A+/S cross-game value omissions:**
  - **Chasseur Shock Minelayer 20/0.5:** **A+** concealed midfield corner defence with Sixth Sense and a Light Flamethrower; the **FO** profile is a separate choice. [Midfield rankings](../list-building/midfield-area-denial-and-speed-bumps-apex-value.md).
  - **Veteran Kazak AP HMG 45/1.5:** **A+** durable non-Hackable active gun, offering AP and NWI when Spetsnaz's one-Wound attack is too fragile. [Gunfighter rankings](../list-building/active-turn-gunfighters-apex-and-value.md).
  - **Strelok K-9 25/0:** **A+** synchronized Sensor/Camo pressure, but the **Strelok FO 17/0** does not also include the K-9. [Multi-body rankings](../list-building/peripheral-synchronized-multibody-apex-value.md).
  - **Cateran T2 Sniper 21/1 or AP Sniper 21/0.5:** **S value in cheap ARO roles**, with Camo and dangerous ammunition but Irregular, one-Wound fragility. [ARO rankings](../list-building/aro-units-apex-and-value.md).
  - **Polaris Alpha, Masha + Bearpode 32/0:** **A** multi-body independent-threat value, with **two Regular Orders** for two actual troopers rather than a single Synchronized Order. [Multi-body rankings](../list-building/peripheral-synchronized-multibody-apex-value.md).

### Particularly useful Fireteams

- **Dynamo Duo — the clearest signature:** 
  - **Dynamo AP Spitfire 25/1**
  - **Dynamo Paramedic 16/0.5**
  - **41 points / 1.5 SWC** buys fast Smoke, shooting, mine delivery and objective coverage in a compact package.
- **FT-02 viral/visor Duo or Haris — situational specialist alternative:**
  - **Briscard MSV1** for visual-mod interactions.
  - **Loup-Garou Viral** for BTS-focused attacks.
  - **Frontovik / eligible Moblot / Bruant FTO** to cover specific guns or tasks within the chart.
  - More expensive and less universally compelling than the Dynamo package; **no Core**, only **one Haris and two Duos** under the pinned vanilla chart.

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **True TAG access:** **Chernobog** supplies an AP-HMG heavy gun; it is **B apex / A value**, not a top-tier all-game TAG. Other Ariadna armies cannot automatically copy its roster access. [TAG comparison](../list-building/tags.md).
- **Chain of Command:** **Wallace, Vassily and selected TankHunter/other profiles** supply meaningful protection; check exact profiles, as Wallace is *not* a Vanilla LT.
- **Vision:** plenty of normal **Smoke**, plus a **Wardriver Disco Baller 16/1** for projected Eclipse-like sight denial; Disco requires successful placement and is not a free Eclipse Grenade. Native **MSV2 is notably absent** in the pinned standard roster.
- **Limits:** thin hacking and few Repeaters; many infantry attacks are non-Hackable, but **Chernobog and REMs are Hackable**. The lack of a Core is the main Fireteam drawback.

## At a glance

- **Identity:** many *independent* camouflaged threats, exceptionally punishing AP/EXP/portable autocannon fire, cheap mines/Smoke, Speculative Fire from Dog-Warriors, and dangerous deployment-zone parachutists. **Why it works:** the opponent cannot efficiently identify/clear every threat, and losing one attacker rarely disables the others. [Shepherd 2026; Loss of Lieutenant #164]
- **Strong repeatable engine:** **Dynamo AP Spitfire (25/1 SWC) + Paramedic (16/0.5) Duo** = **41 points / 1.5 SWC**. The fast attacker chooses favorable angles; the specialist scores; both lay Shock mines and provide Smoke. A **5-point Traktor Mul Baggage** keeps Mine Dispensers and disposable weapons supplied. [Shepherd; Tactical Awareness; CNN]
- **Hard defensive architecture:** the defensive puzzle is built from complementary threats:
  - **TankHunter autocannon/ML:** camouflaged heavy ARO; reveal for a worthwhile target.
  - **Strelok Decoys:** create ambiguous deployment-zone markers and waste Discover Orders.
  - **Vystrel Neurocinetics/Sapper:** visibly threatens the lane while the markers remain uncertain.
  - **Why:** discovery costs Orders and poor attacks; do not reveal the TankHunter just because an irrelevant target passed. [Shepherd; Loss of Lieutenant #164]
- **Command fork:** select an Order/command package with a purpose:
  - **Voronin Strategos LT (22) + William Wallace Chain of Command (38):** resilient command and an independent late-game attacker.
  - **Rokot LT (9):** frees roughly 13+ points when premium command does not justify its cost.
  - **Legality:** Wallace is **not** a vanilla LT under the pinned roster. [Shepherd; current reference]
- **Structural weakness:** weak native hacking/anti-hacking, mostly **MSV1 rather than MSV2**, and sometimes awkward mission scoring under attrition. Ariadna's many *nonhackable* attacks can make enemy hacking investment inefficient, **but the Chernobog and REMs remain hackable**. [Shepherd; LoL #164]
- **Important counterargument:** the 2025 Tactical Awareness team preferred Kosmoflot/TAK for stronger integrated Fireteams, while Shepherd's 2026 tournament-winning vanilla build obtained unique value from the wider roster, the Kibervolk and the **threat** of Mirage-5/Van Zant even when absent. **Conclusion:** vanilla rewards independent, redundant plans; a sectorial may suit players who want a stronger linked gun. [Tactical Awareness S3E11; Shepherd 2026]

**Vision-control choices:**

- **Dynamo AP Spitfire + Paramedic Duo (41/1.5):** fast Smoke, shooting, mines and objective reach.
- **Dog-Warrior / Polaris:** independent close-pressure alternatives that exploit Smoke.
- **Irmandinho:** a separate low-cost Smoke and trading option when it fits.
- **Traktor Mul Baggage:** refresh eligible disposable weapons and Mine Dispensers when positioned within the rules.
- **Against MSV:** use flanks, mines, different guns and scoring; normal Smoke is for crossing lanes, not a substitute for an MSV2 gun. See [vision-control roles](../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).

## Choose a list architecture, not a fixed shopping list

| Plan | Key pieces | Why / when not to pick it |
|---|---|---|
| **Camouflage / area-denial (Shepherd, CanCon 2026)** | Voronin, 2 TankHunters, 2 Streloks, Scout E/M Minelayer, Vystrel Neurocinetics, Dynamo Duo, Traktor Baggage, Dog-Warrior or Wallace; Kibervolk Patrol | Four or more dangerous-looking DZ camo positions via Strelok Decoys make ARO discovery costly. Mine Dispensers re-establish the midfield every turn; hard guns punish exposed troops. The primary guns can fail and opponents with Sensor / template-clearing tools may deconstruct the puzzle |
| **Mobility / mission first** | Dynamo Paramedic + AP Spitfire Duo; Chasseur/SAS/Strelok specialist, mobile 112 Doctor or Polaris as needed; cheap command and Baggage | Buttons, mines and Smoke from mobile, mostly nonhackable troops; less resilient if bike approach routes are exposed or specialists die |
| **Independent attack / reactive mix** | TankHunter autocannon, Vystrel sniper/autocannon, Strelok/Chasseur, Mirage-5 or Dog-Warrior, cheap Rokot LT | Preserves orders and options across multiple attack directions. ARO guns and aggressive parachutists compete for SWC, points and Combat Group order allocation |
| **Premium Chernobog TAG** | Chernobog (63 or 70), Dozer/Dozer KVP repair, optional Mekhamobilya TAGCom, screening camo and specialists | BS14/AP HMG/ARM8 + Tactical Awareness brings a tough direct assault unavailable to a pure camo plan. Large S7 silhouette, vulnerable hacking, weak Dodge/self-repair, and degraded Transmutation profile may be a poorer investment than several independent guns |
| **Viral / MSV small Haris (specialist alternative)** | Briscard (MSV1), Loup-Garou viral, Moblot or Bruant **FTO** / Veteran Kazak as FT-02 allows | Supplies BTS-targeting viral and MSV1 in a faction concentrated on AP vs ARM. **CNN 2025** finds these expensive links mediocre next to a solo-gun plan; **Tactical Awareness** tests/likes their specialized fire lanes. Decide based on actual mission/opponent, not a compulsory Haris |

**Against hacking:** independent nonhackable attackers can decline electronic fights, but the following still need safe routes and screens against Repeaters:

- **Chernobog:** Hackable TAG.
- **Vystrel:** Hackable REM.
- **Traktor Mul:** Hackable REM.
- **Wardriver (14/1 HD or 16/1 with Disco Baller):** limited ordinary anti-HI hacking and visibility utility, *not* a high-end hacking network.

Prioritize enemy Repeaters, mine the approach and protect recovery options. [Shepherd; LoL #141]

**Indirect-fire and support choices:**

- **Traktor Mul Katyusha (9/1):** cheap Guided payoff, but only after creating a valid Targeted state.
- **Forward Observers for Guided:** use an eligible profile and budget Targeted setup:
  - **Chasseur FO**
  - **Strelok FO**
  - **SAS FO**
- **Dog-Warrior:** PH-based Speculative Fire without a Targeted prerequisite.
- **Carmen & Bâtard:** alternative grenade-based Speculative attacks where roster-legal.
- **Kuryer:** conditional alternative *only in rosters where available*; do not import other Ariadna profiles.
- **Traktor Mul Baggage (5/0):** cheap Regular Order and conditional disposable-ammo reloading, distinct from artillery profiles.
- **Voronin LT:** Counterintelligence and Strategos in vanilla; current **TAK Voronin does not have Strategos**.
- **Camo/Decoy uncertainty:** choose only profiles that actually carry the relevant rules:
  - **Spetsnaz**
  - **Strelok**
  - **Scout**
  - False Decoys do not create extra Orders or AROs.
- **EVO:** no automatic value without a rostered device and real support targets.

The CanCon account warns costly hacking-to-Guided plans can do little against dispersed nonhackable lists. Check Targeted setup, range, Reset and Order costs. ## Confirmed disagreements / source-age corrections

1. **Vanilla vs sectorial:** *Tactical Awareness S3E11* (Feb 2025) calls vanilla an inferior leftovers list versus TAK/Kosmoflot due missing strong Core and fewer unique new tools. *Shepherd/LoL #164* (Feb 2026) argues vanilla wins through wider selection, Kibervolk, portable-autocannon/Decoy defense and rear-entry threat—even without fielding the rear-entry pieces. Both can be true for different list architectures.
2. **Haris/Duos versus independent pieces:** CNN and some Tactical Awareness contributors mostly favor **Dynamo Duo**; other legal FT-02 experiments include:
   - **Briscard:** visor utility.
   - **Loup-Garou:** Viral fire.
   - **Bruant FTO:** special-weapon/specialist member.
   - **Moblot:** a mixed-link alternative.
   - Compare each formation with three independent pieces and the single Haris limit.
3. **Chernobog rating:** *LoL #142* splits **higher B** vs **C** for the 63-point TAG: cheap AP/ARM8 Tactical Awareness vs the abrupt Transmutation drop and PH9 Dodge/self-Gizmo. The **Kosmoflot Tactical Awareness** hosts see a genuinely strong gun but also debate paying 7 points for the rocket profile. Current Chernobog **63/70**, not old “65” quotes.
4. **Camo gun as killer vs deterrent:** Shepherd chose Ariadna largely for huge autocannon damage in practice, but reports those TankHunters **mostly delayed and threatened, rather than delivered kills**, in the actual five games. Include the order-tax effect in unit valuation.
5. **Older list and rules corrections:**
   - **Kibervolk:** no Climbing Plus (Shepherd correction).
   - **Vystrel:** AVA1 in vanilla.
   - **Chernobog:** AVA1 and FT-01 maximum one, even when Kosmoflot permits a different structure.
   - Use the current Army reference rather than 2025 video legality.
