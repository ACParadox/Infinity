# Vanilla Ariadna — Listbuilding Guide (Infinity N5.3)

**Reference archive:** [Transcripts, cross-references, battle reports and purchasing audit](references/vanilla-ariadna_refs.md).

**Purpose:** practical choices, why they work, and competing opinions. **Research checked 2026-10-19.** Video and podcast transcripts are used as tactical evidence, **not** authority for unit costs, Army legality or tournament usage. Current profiles/Fireteams come from the [N5.3 Vanilla Ariadna reference](../../sectorials/ariadna/vanilla-ariadna.md) (Army **7.26246.158**, 2026-09-03 data; verified 2026-10-15). Older 2025 opinions can predate roster/rules changes.

## At a glance

- **Identity:** many *independent* camouflaged threats, exceptionally punishing AP/EXP/portable autocannon fire, cheap mines/Smoke, Speculative Fire from Dog-Warriors, and dangerous deployment-zone parachutists. **Why it works:** the opponent cannot efficiently identify/clear every threat, and losing one attacker rarely disables the others. [Shepherd 2026; Loss of Lieutenant #164]
- **Strong repeatable engine:** **Dynamo AP Spitfire (25/1 SWC) + Paramedic (16/0.5) Duo** = **41 points / 1.5 SWC**. The fast attacker chooses favorable angles; the specialist scores; both lay Shock mines and provide Smoke. A **5-point Traktor Mul Baggage** keeps Mine Dispensers and disposable weapons supplied. [Shepherd; Tactical Awareness; CNN]
- **Hard defensive architecture:** the defensive puzzle is built from complementary threats:
  - **TankHunter autocannon/ML:** camouflaged heavy ARO; reveal for a worthwhile target.
  - **Strelok Decoys:** create ambiguous deployment-zone markers and waste Discover Orders.
  - **Vystrel Neurocinetics/Sapper:** visibly threatens the lane while the markers remain uncertain.
  - **Why:** discovery costs Orders and poor attacks; do not reveal the TankHunter just because an irrelevant target passed. [Shepherd; Loss of Lieutenant #164]
- **Command fork:** select an Order/command package with a purpose:
  - **Voronin Strategos LT (22) + William Wallace Chain of Command (38):** resilient command and an independent late-game attacker.
  - **Rokot LT (9):** frees roughly 13+ points when premium command does not justify its cost.
  - **Legality:** Wallace is **not** a vanilla LT under the pinned roster. [Shepherd; current reference]
- **Structural weakness:** weak native hacking/anti-hacking, mostly **MSV1 rather than MSV2**, and sometimes awkward mission scoring under attrition. Ariadna's many *nonhackable* attacks can make enemy hacking investment inefficient, **but the Chernobog and REMs remain hackable**. [Shepherd; LoL #164]
- **Important counterargument:** the 2025 Tactical Awareness team preferred Kosmoflot/TAK for stronger integrated Fireteams, while Shepherd's 2026 tournament-winning vanilla build obtained unique value from the wider roster, the Kibervolk and the **threat** of Mirage-5/Van Zant even when absent. **Conclusion:** vanilla rewards independent, redundant plans; a sectorial may suit players who want a stronger linked gun. [Tactical Awareness S3E11; Shepherd 2026]

**Vision-control choices:**

- **Dynamo AP Spitfire + Paramedic Duo (41/1.5):** fast Smoke, shooting, mines and objective reach.
- **Dog-Warrior / Polaris:** independent close-pressure alternatives that exploit Smoke.
- **Irmandinho:** a separate low-cost Smoke and trading option when it fits.
- **Traktor Mul Baggage:** refresh eligible disposable weapons and Mine Dispensers when positioned within the rules.
- **Against MSV:** use flanks, mines, different guns and scoring; normal Smoke is for crossing lanes, not a substitute for an MSV2 gun. See [vision-control roles](../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).

## Choose a list architecture, not a fixed shopping list

| Plan | Key pieces | Why / when not to pick it |
|---|---|---|
| **Camouflage / area-denial (Shepherd, CanCon 2026)** | Voronin, 2 TankHunters, 2 Streloks, Scout E/M Minelayer, Vystrel Neurocinetics, Dynamo Duo, Traktor Baggage, Dog-Warrior or Wallace; Kibervolk Patrol | Four or more dangerous-looking DZ camo positions via Strelok Decoys make ARO discovery costly. Mine Dispensers re-establish the midfield every turn; hard guns punish exposed troops. The primary guns can fail and opponents with Sensor / template-clearing tools may deconstruct the puzzle |
| **Mobility / mission first** | Dynamo Paramedic + AP Spitfire Duo; Chasseur/SAS/Strelok specialist, mobile 112 Doctor or Polaris as needed; cheap command and Baggage | Buttons, mines and Smoke from mobile, mostly nonhackable troops; less resilient if bike approach routes are exposed or specialists die |
| **Independent attack / reactive mix** | TankHunter autocannon, Vystrel sniper/autocannon, Strelok/Chasseur, Mirage-5 or Dog-Warrior, cheap Rokot LT | Preserves orders and options across multiple attack directions. ARO guns and aggressive parachutists compete for SWC, points and Combat Group order allocation |
| **Premium Chernobog TAG** | Chernobog (63 or 70), Dozer/Dozer KVP repair, optional Mekhamobilya TAGCom, screening camo and specialists | BS14/AP HMG/ARM8 + Tactical Awareness brings a tough direct assault unavailable to a pure camo plan. Large S7 silhouette, vulnerable hacking, weak Dodge/self-repair, and degraded Transmutation profile may be a poorer investment than several independent guns |
| **Viral / MSV small Haris (specialist alternative)** | Briscard (MSV1), Loup-Garou viral, Moblot or Bruant **FTO** / Veteran Kazak as FT-02 allows | Supplies BTS-targeting viral and MSV1 in a faction concentrated on AP vs ARM. **CNN 2025** finds these expensive links mediocre next to a solo-gun plan; **Tactical Awareness** tests/likes their specialized fire lanes. Decide based on actual mission/opponent, not a compulsory Haris |

**Legal Fireteam limits:** 0 Core, up to 1 Haris and 2 Duos; no vanilla Wildcards.

- **FT-01 (Duo only):**
  - Dynamo (max 2)
  - Dog-Warrior (max 1)
  - Ratnik (max 1)
  - Chernobog (max 1)
- **FT-02 (Duo/Haris):**
  - Frontovik
  - Briscard
  - Loup-Garou
  - Moblot (max 1)
  - Veteran Kazak (max 1)
  - Apache FTO / Barsuk FTO / Bruant FTO where allowed

Non-FTO forward Apaches and infiltrating Bruant cannot join. Check the canonical chart for required choices and restrictions.

## Key pieces — why and the competing view

| Pick or combination | Why use it? | Trade-off / competing interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **Dynamo AP Spitfire + Paramedic Duo (41/1.5)** | Very fast Burst fire, linked Smoke, dual Shock Mine Dispensers and a specialist. Maneuver for favorable angles; reload with a 5-point Mul. Strongest repeated cross-source recommendation. [Shepherd; LoL #164; Tactical Awareness; CNN] | **CNN** regards this as almost the *only* compelling vanilla link; other Tactical Awareness presenters consider double Paramedic support bikes or a wider utility Haris. The AP Spitfire is expensive and **Limited Cover** punishes careless exposure |
| **2 TankHunters + Strelok Decoys** | Camo/Decoy makes autocannon/ML AROs hard to distinguish from harmless markers; TankHunter 36/1.5 portable autocannon (+1B) hits armor exceptionally hard. **Reveal** when a genuinely high-value target must cross exposed space, not for every passing model. [Shepherd; LoL #164] | TankHunters underperformed in Shepherd's actual tournament games versus practice; concentrated points/SWC may be better spent on mobile guns. ARO ability is as much *delay/uncertainty* as actual kills |
| **Vystrel Neurocinetics** | **26/0.5 AP Sniper + LRL** or **32/1.5 portable autocannon + LRL**; Sapper, BS Attack (SR-1), Shock, Neurocinetics, 2 STR. Controls long lanes, especially on open tables. [Shepherd; Tactical Awareness TAK episode] | Powerful *reactive* burst, less effective active-turn gun. At AVA1 in vanilla it is not the two-Vystrel defense some TAK/early hosts discuss. Low armor/BTS; needs cover and good deployment |
| **Kibervolk Patrol (Dozer KVP + Kibervolk)** | Compact *Regular* engineer plus Sensor, fast melee/close defense, and optional CrazyKoala/Chest Mine pressure. A second form of board control that doesn't need a Core. [Shepherd; LoL #164] | Pair must share a Combat Group; not two freely independent unit selections. **Rules correction:** Kibervolk does **not** have Climbing Plus—Shepherd admits using it incorrectly at CanCon; do not copy that play |
| **Voronin LT + Wallace CoC** | Voronin's Strategos/Counterintelligence supports order tempo and resists Command Token damage; Wallace's Chain of Command protects LT loss while his EXP CC attack, T2 gun and movement threaten priority objectives and late assassinations. [Shepherd; LoL #164] | 60 points for command/assault can be too much in a budget/mission-first army. If Voronin dies, CoC maintains command but you still lose his other buffs. Wallace **is not** an LT option in the current vanilla reference |
| **Dog-Warrior (sometimes two) / Polaris Bearpode** | Smoke, speed, durable close pressure; crucially, **Dog-Warrior PH-based Speculative Fire** reaches enemies concealed behind walls and clears scoring lanes. [Shepherd; LoL #164] | 2025 Tactical Awareness argues N5 CC/Berserk changes mean you should not assume an automatic melee win versus dedicated MA specialists. The Polaris Alpha team gives **two Regular orders** at 32 points, but Masha+Bear are a compulsory paired package |
| **Mirage-5 vs Van Zant / independent parachutists** | Margot+Duroc threaten a rear-angle assault, templates and specialist tasks; simply having DZ-entry options in vanilla forces opponents to defend their rear. [Tactical Awareness; Shepherd] | Tactical Awareness prefers **Mirage-5** over **Van Zant** on gun/spec/companion efficiency; mono-hero options are less flexible than distributed midfield pieces. Shepherd **did not field either** in the winning lists; the *threat* influenced opponent deployments |
| **Apaches (solo Forward Deployment/Berserk vs FTO link)** | Non-FTO units offer forward tempo, CC disruption, burst/close-range templates; FTO versions can occupy FT-02, letting a cheap assault troop accompany Briscards/Loups. [Tactical Awareness] | One host deliberately leaves Apaches solo to keep Berserk/forward deployment; another likes FTO combos. Do **not** assign Forward Deployment/Berserk rules from non-FTO options to their FTO counterparts |
| **Loup-Garou viral + Briscard MSV1** | Viral attacks **BTS** instead of ARM and Briscard solves some smoke/Mimetism challenges in a low-MSV roster. A valid FT-02 Haris/Duo route. [Tactical Awareness] | Costs can displace numerous camo/skirmisher tools. **CNN** is skeptical of link value; the 2025 group favors finding *some* viral threat where anti-BTS weapons are scarce |
| **Specialists: Chasseur FO, Strelok FO, SAS, Scout; cheap Rokot infiltrator** | Mines, decoys, infiltration and objectives start pressure near the scoring zone; **FO** also supports Guided artillery. Chasseur has Sixth Sense, Strelok provides Decoy, SAS supplies dangerous close-range options. | Some options only AVA1; too few independent objective specialists creates a hidden single point of failure; target acquisition for Guided still costs Orders |
| **Veteran Kazak / Moblot / Ratnik HI** | Hardier conventional attackers; Veteran Kazak AP HMG and specialist options can advance as solo shooter or appropriate FT-02 member. **Ratnik is heavy infantry, not a TAG.** [Irregular Order #10; current roster] | Expensive vs multiple light AROs/specialists or a Chernobog; Irregular Order disputes whether Ratnik is worth a premium slot against the Veteran Kazak/Moblot |

The pinned 51-unit vanilla roster does not contain a separate conventional **VH troop-class** option |

**Against hacking:** independent nonhackable attackers can decline electronic fights, but the following still need safe routes and screens against Repeaters:

- **Chernobog:** Hackable TAG.
- **Vystrel:** Hackable REM.
- **Traktor Mul:** Hackable REM.
- **Wardriver (14/1 HD or 16/1 with Disco Baller):** limited ordinary anti-HI hacking and visibility utility, *not* a high-end hacking network.

Prioritize enemy Repeaters, mine the approach and protect recovery options. [Shepherd; LoL #141]

**Indirect-fire and support choices:**

- **Traktor Mul Katyusha (9/1):** cheap Guided payoff, but only after creating a valid Targeted state.
- **Chasseur FO / Strelok FO / SAS FO:** potential mission FOs where the actual roster/profile permits them; do not assume Targeted is free.
- **Dog-Warrior:** PH-based Speculative Fire without a Targeted prerequisite.
- **Carmen & Bâtard:** alternative grenade-based Speculative attacks where roster-legal.
- **Kuryer:** conditional alternative *only in rosters where available*; do not import other Ariadna profiles.
- **Traktor Mul Baggage (5/0):** cheap Regular Order and conditional disposable-ammo reloading, distinct from artillery profiles.
- **Voronin LT:** Counterintelligence and Strategos in vanilla; current **TAK Voronin does not have Strategos**.
- **Spetsnaz / Strelok / Scout:** Camouflage/Decoy ambiguity only on eligible profiles; Decoys create neither Orders nor extra AROs.
- **EVO:** no automatic value without a rostered device and real support targets.

The CanCon account warns costly hacking-to-Guided plans can do little against dispersed nonhackable lists. Check Targeted setup, range, Reset and Order costs. See [indirect-fire tiers](../list-building/indirect-fire-speculative-guided-apex-value.md) and [force-multiplier comparisons](../list-building/force-multipliers-special-abilities-apex-value.md).

## Confirmed disagreements / source-age corrections

1. **Vanilla vs sectorial:** *Tactical Awareness S3E11* (Feb 2025) calls vanilla an inferior leftovers list versus TAK/Kosmoflot due missing strong Core and fewer unique new tools. *Shepherd/LoL #164* (Feb 2026) argues vanilla wins through wider selection, Kibervolk, portable-autocannon/Decoy defense and rear-entry threat—even without fielding the rear-entry pieces. Both can be true for different list architectures.
2. **Are Haris/Duos worth the orders?** *CNN Counterintelligence* and some Tactical Awareness speakers would largely take only the **Dynamo Duo**; other Tactical Awareness plans take FT-02 Briscard/Loup/Bruant/Moblot links for viral/MSV/specialist synergy. Evaluate each against 3 independent troopers and the one Haris slot.
3. **Chernobog rating:** *LoL #142* splits **higher B** vs **C** for the 63-point TAG: cheap AP/ARM8 Tactical Awareness vs the abrupt Transmutation drop and PH9 Dodge/self-Gizmo. The **Kosmoflot Tactical Awareness** hosts see a genuinely strong gun but also debate paying 7 points for the rocket profile. Current Chernobog **63/70**, not old “65” quotes.
4. **Camo gun as killer vs deterrent:** Shepherd chose Ariadna largely for huge autocannon damage in practice, but reports those TankHunters **mostly delayed and threatened, rather than delivered kills**, in the actual five games. Include the order-tax effect in unit valuation.
5. **Old list legality and mistakes:** 2025 episode comments on vanished units and incomplete N5 rosters may be outdated; **Kibervolk does not have Climbing Plus** (Shepherd's own correction); Vystrel is **AVA1 in vanilla**, and the vanilla Chernobog is **AVA1/FT-01 max1** even though Kosmoflot can construct different teams. The pinned chart, not a remembered 2025 podcast layout, determines legality.
