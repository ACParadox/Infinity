## Lieutenant and Order economy
- **Fusilier LT 10/0:** inexpensive protected command; identical basic profile as a lookalike.
- **Kamau LT 20/0:** Combi/E/M Carbine; **Zulu-Cobra LT 23/0:** Camouflage, Sensor/Jammer.
- **ORC LT HMG 35/1 or MULTI Rifle/Firewall 30/0:** fighting command; **Uma FTO LT 25/0:** mobile specialist command.
- **Cutter LT 95/2.5:** expensive command concentration.
- **NCO:** Patsy 34/0, Cutter 97/2, Squalos MULTI HMG 66/1.5.
- **Tactical Awareness:** Squalos, Kamau Paramedic 25/0, Bipandra 25/0, Pathfinder 17/0.
- **Regular Order infrastructure:** Fusilier 10/0, Fugazi 7/0, Mulebot 8/0, Tech-Bee 9/0. Warcor 3/0 and Helots are Irregular; Patsy FT Master conditionally converts linked Helots at the next Order Count.
- No Chain of Command in the pinned roster; Quinn does not supply it.

## Fireteam roles
- **Varuna:** Fusilier or eligible Helot FTO anchor; Kamau, ORC, Bipandra and eligible Wildcards.
- **Undertow:** ORC or Kamau anchor; Montesa FTO and Machinist, with eligible Wildcards.
- **Cavalry Duo:** Squalos or motorized Montesa anchor; Kamau/Bulleteer or eligible Wildcard.
- **Forward Montesa Duo:** exactly two foot Knights of Montesa.
- **Wildcards:** Pathfinder, Clipper FTO, Quinn, Uma FTO, Patsy; restrictions and counts-as in the [Fireteam guide](varuna-fireteams.md).
