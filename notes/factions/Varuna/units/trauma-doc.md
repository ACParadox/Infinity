### Trauma-Doc — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Summary:** The Trauma-Doc is a **basic Doctor intended primarily for use with Palbots**. It is not especially good, but **14 points alone or 17 with one Palbot** is cheaper when you genuinely need to save points for the rest of the army. Otherwise, **Bipandra with Tactical Awareness** or a **Paramedic** is likely superior. Its niche is independent, economical backfield recovery rather than accompanying an assault Fireteam.

#### Profile and cost

| Kestrel option | Points / SWC | Relevant abilities |
|---|---:|---|
| Trauma-Doc | **14 / 0** | WIP 12; Doctor, MediKit; MOV 10-10; ARM 1, BTS 0, W 1; Combi Rifle; **no Fireteam access** |
| Trauma-Doc + Palbot | **17 / 0** | Remote Doctor coverage via one **3-point Peripheral (Servant)**, MOV 15-10, Mimetism (-3) |
| Trauma-Doc + two Palbots | **20 / 0** | Two potential recovery routes, although only one Servant may activate at a time |

Trauma-Doc is **AVA 1**; Palbots are **AVA 2** in Kestrel. Each Palbot needs a legal controller, contributes no independent Regular Order, and shares the army's Palbot availability.

#### When to take a Trauma-Doc

1. **Points are genuinely tight.** A 17-point Doctor-plus-Palbot package preserves 8 points compared with the 25-point Tactical Awareness Bipandra, while retaining an independent Regular Order and an actual Doctor skill. Do not spend those saved points without a specific list-building purpose.
2. **Recovering expensive backfield ARO infantry.** Hide the Trauma-Doc and position a Palbot to reach a **Black A.I.R. Neurocinetics sniper, Griffin Feuerbach, or Hetkari Missile Launcher** that falls Unconscious. This is the clearest use case: the attacking Fireteam can operate elsewhere while a cheap support package covers the static defense.
3. **A Doctor is required in a different part of the table.** A backfield Doctor can handle Doctor-specific mission requirements or medical recovery, while a cheap Paramedic stays with a mobile FENNECS team. Unlike a Paramedic's MediKit, Doctor healing uses the Doctor's WIP instead of the patient's PH.
4. **Bipandra is assigned to another role.** The Trauma-Doc can serve as a *second*, separate recovery option rather than pulling Bipandra away from the main Fireteam.

**Practical setup:** Place the Trauma-Doc safely behind cover. Deploy the Palbot near likely casualty locations and move it into silhouette contact with a fallen friendly trooper. The **Trauma-Doc rolls its own WIP 12** to use Doctor remotely. There is **no maximum operating distance** between a Servant and its controller, but each Doctor use targets only one trooper per Order. If you buy two Palbots, they cannot both activate at once.

#### Comparison with the better alternatives

| | Trauma-Doc | Bipandra — Tactical Awareness | Fennec Paramedic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Points | **14**, or **17 with Palbot** | **25** | **12** |
| Medical ability | Doctor WIP 12, MediKit | Doctor **(ReRoll -3)** WIP 13, MediKit **(+1B)** | Paramedic, MediKit |
| Contact Doctor success | **60%** | **82.5%** (WIP 13 then WIP 10) | Not a Doctor; MediKit uses target PH |
| Fireteam | None | **Wildcard**, counts as Fennec/Kestrel | **FENNECS** |
| Order utility | Regular Order; Palbot can extend recovery reach | **Tactical Awareness** can add a Fireteam activation when used legally | Regular Order and inexpensive linked support |
| Durability | ARM 1 / BTS 0; W 1 | ARM 2 / BTS 3; Shock immunity, Dodge (+1”) | Light infantry |
| Best reason | **Budget remote backfield healing** | **High-quality mobile Fireteam Doctor** | **Cheap medical support inside FENNECS** |

**Why Bipandra is usually better:** Tactical Awareness, Fireteam eligibility, superior WIP and a built-in Doctor reroll make the 25-point profile a much stronger overall package. MediKit (+1B) provides an additional shot when firing healing darts, but does not make her Doctor roll Burst 2 or restore multiple Wounds with one use. Her Nanopulser and durability also improve short-range survivability.

**Why the Paramedic is often better:** The **12-point Fennec Paramedic** accompanies a FENNECS team and keeps its medical equipment with the moving models rather than buying a separate backfield Doctor. The trade-off is a MediKit recovery based on the patient's PH and the absence of the Doctor skill.

**Important Palbot trade-off:** Bipandra could also control a Palbot, but **a controller with a Peripheral (Servant) cannot be part of any Fireteam**. Thus Trauma-Doc + Palbot fills a different role from **linked Tactical Awareness Bipandra**; remote healing is not exclusive to the Trauma-Doc.

#### Reliability, rules and limitations

- **Doctor success:** WIP 12 passes on 12 or less: **60%**. Failing the Doctor roll ordinarily sends the Unconscious patient to Dead, unless an applicable reroll changes the result.
- **Command Token reroll:** A failed Doctor roll on a target **with a Cube** can be rerolled using a Command Token. One extra WIP 12 attempt gives **84%** total success, at the cost of that resource. Do not assume the reroll is available on every patient.
- **Bipandra's built-in reroll:** WIP 13 followed on failure by WIP 10 gives **82.5%** success before Command Tokens.
- **MediKit:** Recovery uses the **target's PH**; ranged use first requires a BS hit. A failed PH roll can kill the patient. A Paramedic's healing therefore is not interchangeable with a Doctor WIP roll.
- **Palbot restriction:** A Servant uses its controller's Doctor skill and WIP from a remote location. It does not create another Order, and the controller cannot join a Fireteam while controlling Servants.
- **Not a TAG/REM repair plan:** Doctor is for appropriate troopers with VITA; STR-based TAGs/REMs ordinarily need an Engineer/GizmoKit, making a **Machinist** the proper support choice for them.

#### Recommendation

**Use Trauma-Doc + Palbot (17/0)** when an infantry-heavy defensive list benefits from cheap remote recovery and the points saved have a concrete use. A second Palbot may justify **20/0** if the table has two distinct recovery areas, but that is niche.

**Otherwise prefer Tactical Awareness Bipandra (25/0)** for a mobile FENNECS/KESTREL Fireteam, or a **Fennec Paramedic (12/0)** for low-cost healing within FENNECS. The Trauma-Doc should be a deliberate budget-and-positioning choice, not the default Kestrel medic.
