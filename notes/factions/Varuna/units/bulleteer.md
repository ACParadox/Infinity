### Bulleteer Armbots — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A− for both the 22pt/1SWC Spitfire and 19pt/0SWC Heavy Shotgun (+1B); neither is a must-take.** Their value is **not** that MO lacks Spitfires or shotguns—it plainly does not. The distinctive package is **Mimetism (−6) + MOV 6–4in + Repeater + an inexpensive Regular gunfighter**, with the option of an actual **FT2 two-Bulleteer Velox Duo**. The Spitfire is the better *general-purpose secondary attack piece*; the Heavy Shotgun is the better *zero-SWC close-range trader*. A well-supported two-REM Duo can be A-level for a deliberate offensive plan, but the models are expendable, non-specialist, hackable, and compete for the Orders already wanted by MO's Knights and TAGs.

#### Pinned roster and profile ranking

**Unit ID 35 · AVA 2 · Regular REM · MOV 15–10cm (6–4in) · CC13 · BS12 · PH10 · WIP13 · ARM1 · BTS3 · STR1 · S4.** Both profiles have **Mimetism (−6), Repeater, Remote Presence, Courage** and **PARA CC Weapon (−3)**. Neither is a specialist, starts forward, has NCO or Tactical Awareness, or can enter a generic MO/Crusade Fireteam.

| Option | Weapon | Pts / SWC | Rating | Choose when |
|---:|---|---:|---|---|
| **2** | **Spitfire** | **22 / 1** | **A−** | Cheap B4 attack gun for the 8–24in band; strong independent flanker, Suppressive Fire candidate, or FT2 Velox leader |
| **1** | **Heavy Shotgun (+1B)** | **19 / 0** | **A−** | Zero-SWC B3 AP close-range gun that can challenge a target through Mimetism (−6); complement to a longer-ranged shooter or Spitfire partner |

**Accuracy correction:** The pinned MO roster *now* properly lists **Heavy Shotgun (+1B)**. The old MO entry omitted the printed bonus; the pinned Kestrel roster and published N5 profiles already contained it. The Heavy Shotgun is normally **B2, PS5, AP**; this Bulleteer's weapon becomes **B3 during its active attacks**, not B2. Do not confuse its printed **+1 Burst** with a Fireteam **+1 Success Die**. The +1B does not turn its ARO into reactive B2.

##### What the Spitfire actually contributes

- **22 points is a cheap price for a credible Mimetism (−6) B4 active gun.** In a favorable 8–24in rangeband the Bulleteer is BS15 before other modifiers; it forces a typical shooter with neither MSV nor alternative attack mode to suffer a **−6 Mimetism MOD**. In Partial Cover that can total **−9** to the opponent's BS while the Bulleteer suffers the normal −3 cover penalty unless Marksmanship is active.
- **Independent gunfighting.** A Bulleteer does not need to pull a 35–50pt Knight team out of position just to challenge one medium-range lane. It can trade into an ARO piece, then be repaired if practical.
- **Cheap second wave.** It follows a Tikbalang/Seraph/Sepulchre HMG opener onto a secondary route, rather than pretending a Spitfire replaces the list's serious long gun.
- **Suppressive Fire:** particularly unpleasant when the enemy must face Mimetism (−6), cover, and the Suppressive Fire MOD together. Use only with a viable lane and protection against template, visor, or hacking counterplay. Entering Suppressive Fire removes it from a Fireteam; joining one cancels its Suppressive Fire state.

**Limits:** no AP on the Spitfire, no MSV or innate Marksmanship, no two-Wound durability, no specialist role, and no self-funded extra Order. High-ARM targets may be better handled by a TAG, AP shotgun, Blitzen, hacking, or a dedicated anti-armor Knight. A B4 Spitfire is not a substitute for the AP HMG Sepulchre or MULTI HMG Seraph on long lanes.

##### Why the Heavy Shotgun is better than MO's shotgun saturation suggests

- **B3 AP within 8in** with the shotgun's close-range +6 MOD and **Mimetism (−6)** is a real offensive niche. It is a cheap *gunfighter* rather than simply one more template/short-range unit.
- **Zero SWC** is valuable when a TAG/HMG, Black Friar launcher, Guided Clipper, or expensive active weapon has already consumed the SWC budget.
- **The 6–4in MOV helps obtain the close-range attack angle**, but it starts in the deployment zone—not in the midfield as a Peacemaker does.
- **It is not an upgraded Boarding Shotgun.** The Heavy Shotgun uses **PS5** instead of the Boarding Shotgun's **PS6**; the Bulleteer's advantages are **Burst 3, Mimetism (−6), price, and Repeater**, not better per-hit power.

**Limits:** exposing an S4, ARM1, one-STR REM to reach 0–8in invites templates, mines, AROs and hacking. MO has excellent short-range specialists already: the **23pt motorized Montesa Paramedic** trades pure shooting efficiency for two Wounds, a specialist objective role and free Impetuous movement; the **17pt Infirmarer Doctor** brings healing and linked Boarding Shotgun; a **26pt Hawkins BSG (+1B)** offers two Wounds and better BS/CC. This is the most redundant rangeband in MO. A shotgun Bulleteer is an optional attack tool, not default filler.

#### Fireteam access and exact MO packages

**MO chart restrictions:**
- **Velox Duo:** any two eligible Velox members from **Bulleteers (max 2), motorized Montesas (max 2), Joan v2 (max 1), Infirmarer (max 1)**. Two Bulleteers are the **same Unit**, so they reach **FT2**. Mixed Bulleteer + Montesa/Joan/Infirmarer Duos are **FT1** (no same-unit/counts-as pair).
- **Order Sergeants Duo or Haris:** **at least one actual Order Sergeant FTO** is required; **max one Bulleteer**, and the Bulleteer **does not count as an Order Sergeant**. A Curator or Infirmarer **does** count as an Order Sergeant *in this row*, allowing FT2 with an FTO Sergeant.
- **No Military Orders Core or Crusade membership**, no Kestrel-style ARMORED Tikbalang + Bulleteer Duo in MO. An eligible Wildcard does not let a team ignore its native minimum or chart row.
- **FT2 = BS Attack (+1 Success Die), not +1 Burst.** It does not create one more damaging attack; roll the extra die and retain the permitted number of results. Only the designated Fireteam Leader fires in a normal active linked BS declaration. **One Core, one Haris, unlimited Duos** army-wide.

##### Does a double-Bulleteer Velox actually pay?

**Sometimes.** For 41/1, the Spitfire and shotgun share **one team Order for movement**, cover each other's preferred rangebands and, with both still in the Fireteam, receive **+1SD** when leading attacks. This is a genuinely different option from a costly Knight-led Haris: it uses a **Duo**, leaving the only Haris free.

But the second body cannot also shoot in the same active Fireteam Order, the Duo must stay coherent, and both are **S4, hackable and single-Structure**. The shared approach may expose both to the same hacker, template or limited route. Compare the same 41-point investment in **one gun plus an independent specialist/order generator** rather than automatically taking two REMs.

**Order Sergeants Haris:** Hacker18 + Curator17 + Spitfire22 is the best *functional* mixed construction; there are three different jobs in one group and the Curator can repair the REM **when position and legal engineering access permit**. It is not a free upgrade over the outstanding **Hacker + Curator FT2 Duo (35/0.5)**: adding the Bulleteer costs 22pts/1SWC and consumes your Haris. A Curator/Doctor in a Fireteam cannot simultaneously use a Palbot Servant.

#### Repeater and EVO synergies: real, but not free

The Bulleteer is an **active/mobile Repeater**. It can extend **De Fersen's** Hacking Area as it advances, giving him and other legal Hackers access to enemy Hackable targets. That helps **Carbonite/Oblivion/Spotlight** plans and, if a separate Guided Clipper is deliberately included, contributes to a network for targeting. The Bulleteer has **no Hacking Device of its own** and its Repeater is not a deployable/persistent marker left behind after it moves away. An advanced Repeater also presents a surface enemy hackers may exploit; plan KHD/Firewall coverage and do not push it blindly into enemy hacking control.

The **14pt / 0.5SWC EVO Mulebot** can make the REM much stronger:

| EVO program | Result | In-MO tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| **Assisted Fire** | Chosen Bulleteer gains **Marksmanship**, negating the normal Partial Cover BS penalty | Good for Spitfire (or HSG) attacking cover; requires an Order/program and keeps EVO committed |
| **Enhanced Reaction** | Selected REM gets improved reactive fire (B2) | Niche defensive use; a single-STR Bulleteer still loses to templates or favorable enemy counters |
| **Fairy Dust — HI** | **Firewall (−3)** to qualifying HI across the army | **Often stronger priority in an HI-heavy MO roster** with several exposed Knights |
| **Fairy Dust — REM** | Firewall (−3) for qualifying REMs | Useful only if the REM/hacking threat density warrants it |

**Supportware exclusivity matters:** each Hacker sustains **only one Supportware at a time**. A *single* EVO cannot sustain Bulleteer Assisted Fire and army-wide HI Fairy Dust concurrently. A REM targeted by a new Supportware also loses its prior target-specific Supportware. **Fairy Dust Firewall (−3) does not stack with TinBot Firewall (−3)** into −6; select/use the applicable protection under the Firewall rules. Do not assume an EVO is automatically efficient solely because a Bulleteer is in the list: MO may prefer HI Fairy Dust, support for Combat Jump, or simply the 8pt Baggage Mulebot plus a better attacker.

**Bulleteer vs Peacemaker is the important REM decision:** take the Bulleteer to **win face-to-face gunfights** with Mimetism (−6); take Peacemaker to **start the hacking/template/minelayer threat in the midfield without using Orders to get there**. Both carry Repeaters. When the plan is De Fersen network projection and stopping a rush, the Peacemaker may solve more problems; when the plan is an economic second attack, the Bulleteer wins.

#### Failure modes and when to skip

- **Fragile, hackable S4 chassis:** ARM1/STR1, vulnerable to guided hacking threats, templates, mines, and physically awkward terrain. Remote Presence helps with later Engineer recovery; it does not keep the REM fighting after a disabling hit.
- **No MSV, no templates-as-defense against templates, no specialist:** a visor shooter can erase some/all Mimetism advantage; direct templates avoid the favorable Face-to-Face BS contest. It cannot solve mission objectives for your Trinitarians/Montesas.
- **Attack-Order contention:** 22pts does not buy another Lieutenant/NCO/Tactical Awareness attack. If Seraph/Tikbalang plus Knight Haris are already competing for Orders, the Bulleteer may spend the entire game as a moderately expensive cheerleader.
- **Range overlap:** the HSG competes with many effective BSG/close-range Knights; Spitfire competes with Santiago/Teutonic/Justice/Montesa and TAG AP Spitfire. Neither fixes MO's long-range non-TAG premium gun deficit.
- **Support opportunity cost:** if EVO is chiefly needed to protect Knights via Fairy Dust, do not evaluate Bulleteer as though Assisted Fire is automatically active.

#### Practical MO selection

1. **Need a cheap independent medium-range attacker after your HMG/TAG opens the table?** Choose **Spitfire 22/1**, usually solo. Try it in Suppressive Fire at the end of an attack turn if protected from its counters.
2. **Need AP at 0 SWC, a short-range gun able to exploit −6 Mimetism, or a cheaper second Velox body?** Choose **Heavy Shotgun (+1B) 19/0**.
3. **Want a genuine +1SD gun without using your Haris?** Test **Spitfire + HSG Velox Duo at 41/1**. This is the best distinctive Bulleteer-specific MO package, but *not* automatically better than one REM plus another independent scorer.
4. **Want an economical cyber-support package?** First compare **Order Sergeant FTO Hacker18 + Curator17 FT2 Duo (35/0.5)**; only expand to **57/1.5 with Bulleteer** if the aggressive Haris and extra attack Orders are genuinely intended.
5. **Building an HI-heavy Joan/De Fersen/Knights list?** Prioritize **Firewall, KHD protection, specialists and Orders** before paying for an EVO-enhanced Bulleteer. Fairy Dust on HI can trump Assisted Fire on one REM.
6. **Already using a strong TAG plus offensive Knight team, or need objectives/turn-zero area denial?** **Skip** the Bulleteer for a Montesa/Trinitarian/Pathfinder or Peacemaker as the mission demands.

**Bottom line: good optional MO value, not compulsory.** The Spitfire is an unusually affordable Mimetism (−6) gun, and the HSG (+1B) is much more threatening than a generic shotgun chassis for 19 points. Yet MO's exceptional close/midrange Knights make them less necessary than in an army that lacks those alternatives. Field one when it serves a distinct second-wave / FT2 / hacking-network role—not merely because the weapon profile looks efficient.
