# Kamau — Varuna

**A tier — priority unit for mixed Undertow Fireteams.** The MSV2 Sniper and Tactical Awareness Paramedic are the standout profiles; the FastPanda Hacker and MSV1 HRL are strong alternatives.

**Source:** [Pinned N5.3 roster](../../../../sectorials/panoceania/varuna-immediate-reaction-division.md#kamau-amphibious-intervention-teams) · [Listbuilding](../varuna-listbuilding.md) · [Fireteams](../varuna-fireteams.md) · [References](../varuna-references.md). Tactical ratings updated 2026-11-24.

## Chassis

AVA 5 · Regular LI · MOV 6-2 (15-5 cm), CC15, BS13, PH11, WIP13, ARM1, BTS6, W1, S2. Mimetism (-3), Immunity (Shock), Stealth, Terrain (Aquatic).

- BS13 + Mimetism (-3) is a solid gunfighting foundation without the inherent hacking vulnerability of HI (the Hacker is itself hackable).
- BTS6 is exceptional for inexpensive infantry and particularly useful on the Hacker.
- Fast first Move and Shock Immunity support mobile teams and recovery.
- ARM1/W1 means even excellent Face-to-Face odds do not make the Kamau a durable apex attacker. Direct templates, close combat and failed saves remain dangerous.
- No native NCO, advanced deployment or extra durability.

Kamau remain fundamental to Varuna, but current Varuna should not be understood as "build the army around a Kamau Core." Their value comes from excellent components inside mixed teams.

## Profile analysis

### MSV2 MULTI Sniper — 29/1.5 — A+

Signature long-range roadblock and secondary active attacker. BS13, Mimetism (-3), MSV2 and DA/AP ammunition are an excellent combination for a sub-30-point infantry sniper.

- Cancels Mimetism and ordinary Visibility Zone modifiers, including Smoke; Eclipse and White Noise remain counters.
- FT2 gives +1 SD: B1 ARO rolls two dice and discards one, not B2 or two potential hits.
- Useful actively against Mimetism (-6) targets as well as defensively; unlike Helots, it is not restricted by Neurocinetics.
- FT4 adds +1 BS, but do not buy an entire expensive Core merely to buff this W1 gun.

Use when there is a lane worth contesting, Smoke/visual-modifier attackers are a concern, or the list needs a reactive piece the opponent must solve. Do not deploy it simply because Varuna is supposed to have one; hide it on poor tables or when the opponent can readily remove it. Keep sufficient independent active pieces.

Compared to a Croc Men MSR (29/1.5), the Kamau trades Hidden Deployment, Infiltration, Mimetism (-6) and Surprise Attack for MSV2 and Fireteam access. Compared to the Helot MSR (17/1.5), it costs more but is Regular and a legitimate active gunfighter.

### Tactical Awareness Paramedic — 25/0 — A+

Best general-purpose Kamau for an active Undertow team. For five points above the basic profile, gain specialist capability, MediKit and an additional Tactical Order. The order can move the whole Fireteam when the Kamau is leader, but is not a freely transferable Regular Order or an extra HMG attack led by a different member.

The Combi Rifle remains useful at normal midfield ranges and the E/M Carbine can threaten expensive HI/TAGs. FT2 also improves rolled MediKit shots.

| Alternative | Pts | Distinction |
|---|---:|---|
| Fusilier Paramedic | 12 | Cheap backline specialist |
| Montesa foot FTO Paramedic | 20 | W2, CC, Blitzen, shotgun, native +1 SD; Limited Cover |
| Kamau TA Paramedic | 25 | Extra order, Combi/E/M, 6-2, normal Cover |
| Quinn Disco Baller FO | 26 | Visibility control, MSV1, specialist |

Montesa is a stronger close-range brawler and more durable, but Kamau generates an additional order and provides conventional midrange firepower. They complement one another.

### Hacker with FastPanda — 22/0.5 — A

Strong mobile hacking support. Compared to Fusilier Hacker (15/0.5), seven points buy WIP13 instead of 12, BTS6 instead of 0, Mimetism (-3), Shock Immunity, 6-2 movement and FastPanda, at the cost of a Combi Rifle replaced by SMG.

FastPanda extends the repeater network without exposing the Hacker at the intended repeater position. Pair with ORC Firewall (-3) for a protected frontline Hacker, or with the Zulu-Cobra KHD/Pitcher (26/0) for complementary hacking and network coverage. Useful for TAG protection and midfield denial.

Fusilier Hacker remains preferable if all that is needed is cheap backline hacking. No native KHD, and BTS6 does not make the Kamau immune to hacking.

### MSV1 Heavy Rocket Launcher — 23/1.5 — A

Six points cheaper than the MSV2 sniper, but the same SWC. MSV1 cancels Mimetism (-3), reduces Mimetism (-6) to -3, and can see through ordinary Smoke at -6 (Sixth Sense can negate that penalty when targeted by a BS Attack through the zone). MSV2 remains superior for visibility control.

Impact template and Continuous Damage threaten clustered opponents and Fireteams; the SMG offers a useful close-range option. Better fit for a mobile team than a dedicated static sniper when the table supports its ranges. Do not confuse the HRL's damage with a dedicated AP/EXP anti-armor weapon.

Clipper FTO Missile Launcher (+1B) is 19/1.5 and offers stronger anti-armor ammunition and native extra Burst, but lacks Mimetism/MSV and is a separate composition member. Prefer HRL for visual-modifier targets and template pressure; prefer MSV2 sniper for premium long-range visibility control.

### HMG — 26/1 — B+

Efficient conventional linked active gunfighter.

| Alternative | Pts/SWC | Distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Fusilier HMG | 18/1 | BS12, cheap |
| Kamau HMG | 26/1 | BS13, Mimetism (-3), W1 |
| ORC HMG | 35/1.5 | BS14, ARM4, W2, Warhorse, hackable |
| Bulleteer Spitfire | 22/1 | BS12, Mimetism (-6), REM, shorter range |
| Zulu-Cobra Spitfire | 27/1 | BS13, Mimetism (-3), Camo/Surprise/FD |

Kamau can outperform ORC in Face-to-Face odds against non-MSV targets under otherwise similar conditions, but is much less resilient if hit. No MSV, AP, NCO, TA or special deployment. Good when Undertow needs inexpensive sustained long-range Burst; not an apex replacement for Cutter. Bulleteer and Zulu-Cobra offer different attack routes.

### FO / Sensor / Triangulated Fire — 23/0 — B

Useful mobile infantry toolbox: MULTI Rifle with AP mode, Flash Pulse, specialist, Sensor and Triangulated Fire. Worth considering against marker-heavy opponents, if Zulu Sensor is absent or overloaded, or if the Fireteam wants its own Camo-solving tool.

Varuna has unusually strong alternatives:

| Sensor carrier | Pts | Distinction |
|---|---:|---|
| Helot FTO SMG/LRL | 10 | Cheap Neurocinetics defense, Irregular |
| Pathfinder TA | 17 | Extra order, Repeater, REM mobility |
| Zulu-Cobra Sensor | 23 | Camo, FD4", Jammer, Triangulated Fire |
| Kamau FO/Sensor | 23 | MULTI Rifle, FT equivalence, BTS6 |

Zulu-Cobra is the better independent midfield controller; Pathfinder is more order-efficient. Kamau is attractive when native infantry composition and the active team's own Sensor capability matter.

### Basic Combi/E/M Carbine — 20/0 — C

Not bad, but overshadowed by upgrades: +2 points for Hacker/FastPanda (different gun), +5 for TA Paramedic with identical guns. As Undertow filler, 17-point Machinist and 20-point Montesa FTO offer more distinct roles. Take only when points are constrained and a non-specialist E/M carrier or identical Lieutenant decoy is specifically needed.

### Lieutenant — 20/0 — Skip by default

No extra Lieutenant Order or SWC rebate. Two Fusiliers (LT + decoy) cost the same and provide two Regular Orders. Particularly efficient with Cutter NCO or Patsy NCO. Zulu-Cobra LT (23/0) provides marker protection, Jammer and Sensor; ORC HMG LT (35/1) saves 0.5 SWC versus regular ORC HMG, but exposes the Lieutenant if used aggressively. Kamau LT is niche if Undertow composition and E/M defense specifically justify it.

## Fireteam access and recommended packages

- **Varuna Core/Haris/Duo:** up to three Kamau; requires Fusilier or Helot FTO. Kamau do not count as Fusiliers.
- **Undertow Core/Haris/Duo:** up to five Kamau; requires Kamau or Varuna ORC. Kamau, ORC and foot Montesa FTO share Undertow equivalence; Varuna Machinist counts as Kamau, and Quinn/Uma FTO can also count as Kamau via Wildcard rules. Patsy counts as Undertow.
- **Cavalry Duo:** max one Kamau, requires Squalos Mk-II or motorized Montesa. Mixed Kamau/TAG or Kamau/bike is normally FT1; consider the mobility mismatch and whether a Pathfinder or independent specialist works better.

| Package | Members | FT level | Pts/SWC |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Defensive/support Duo | MSV2 Sniper 29/1.5 + Machinist Engineer 17/0 | 2 | 46/1.5 |
| Economical active Haris | HMG 26/1 + TA Paramedic 25/0 + Montesa foot FTO Paramedic 20/0 | 3 | 71/1 |
| Hacking/visibility Haris | HRL 23/1.5 + Hacker 22/0.5 + ORC BSG Firewall 28/0 | 3 | 73/2 |
| Specialist support Haris | TA Paramedic 25/0 + Hacker 22/0.5 + Montesa foot FTO Paramedic 20/0 | 3 | 67/0.5 |

All four are Undertow alternatives, not simultaneous Haris choices. The defensive Duo grants the sniper +1 SD while the Machinist supports TAGs/REMs; the Engineer cannot heal the organic Kamau. Separate them if repair positioning demands it. The active Haris trades some redundancy in Paramedics for mobility, a tactical order and Montesa close-range power. The hacking Haris shares the ORC's Firewall while maintaining a visor-equipped gun and durable close-range piece. The specialist Haris is particularly suited to an independent Cutter apex attacker and lacks a dedicated long-range active gun.

FT2 grants +1 SD (not +1 Burst); FT3 adds Discover/Dodge bonuses; FT4 gives +1 BS and FT5 Sixth Sense. Prefer the smallest useful team that reaches FT2+ rather than forcing a full Core.

## Final assessment

Typically select two or three Kamau as needed rather than an entire five-member Kamau Core. MSV2 Sniper for premium ARO and visibility control; TA Paramedic for active order efficiency; FastPanda Hacker for the hacking network; HRL for affordable MSV1 and template pressure. HMG is a good conventional secondary attacker, Sensor FO is situational, and basic/LT are low priority.

In Cutter-centric lists, prioritize TA Paramedic and Hacker when they complement the TAG rather than competing with it for attack orders. Preserve independent midfield pieces, cheap Orders and support capacity.
