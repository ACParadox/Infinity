## Original Varuna tactical note — retained

### Varuna ORCs — Varuna

**Source:** Existing Varuna tactical analysis, reorganized without a new profile audit. [Pinned roster](../../../../sectorials/panoceania/varuna-immediate-reaction-division.md) · [Listbuilding](../varuna-listbuilding.md) · [Fireteams](../varuna-fireteams.md) · [References](../varuna-references.md).

Do not import generic ORC prejudice into Varuna.

Current Varuna ORCs are repeatedly praised because the sectorial-specific combination of **Stealth + Warhorse** makes them far more functional as forward heavy infantry.

Their role is different from Kamau:

- **Kamau** = precision, Mimetism / MSV / specialist utility;
- **ORC** = durable active body that can actually lead a team through danger.

Use an ORC when:

- you want a Fireteam that advances rather than remains static;
- the team needs a durable point model;
- you need a conventional active gun rather than another reactive sniper;
- hacking / ZoC movement would otherwise make a heavy team awkward and Stealth materially improves the route.

The key listbuilding lesson is:

> **A mixed ORC / Kamau / Montesa package is often more coherent than stacking several expensive Kamau gunfighters.**

### ORC Troops — Neoterran Capitaline Army (N5.3)

**Optional, not a default inclusion. Prioritize the 27/0 Boarding Shotgun (+1B) Firewall and the 34/1.5 HMG specifically for dedicated Neoterra Duo/Haris combinations with Hannibal or Agent Maximus. For a generic gun added to a Fusilier/Bolt Fireteam, prefer the 24/0 Black Friar Thunderbolt (+1B).** Collection: one Firewall ORC and one HMG ORC if planning the dedicated assault modules; otherwise one is sufficient.

**Default to the Friar** when adding a secondary gun to an existing Fusilier or Bolt team. Saves 10 points and 1.5 SWC, counters Mimetism and can penalize qualifying MSV/Marksmanship opponents with Albedo. Combat Instinct adds Surprise Attack/Stealth protection. Albedo is not general Mimetism. The ORC remains better in many straightforward engagements against unmodified opponents due to B4, BS14, more damaging PS5 and greater durability.

Neither counts as Fusilier or Bolt; the Friar is a Wildcard without Counts-as. Thus swapping an ORC HMG for the Friar in a Fusilier/Bolt FT need not reduce composition level if the matching majority is unchanged. The ORC is different when it is the native Neoterra anchor and other members count as ORCs.

Other competition: Swiss HMG 56/1.5 (Camo, Hidden Deployment, Mimetism -6); Aquila HMG 52/1.5 (MSV3); Squalos NCO MULTI HMG 66/1.5 (durability, TacAware and NCO); Bolt Spitfire 25/1.5 (Marksmanship and Bolt composition); Bulleteer Spitfire 22/1 (Mimetism -6); Blade BSG 20/0 (Mimetism, SR-1, Tactical Awareness); Hannibal Vulkan BSG 23/0 (NWI, Sensor, native +1SD, TacAware). Swiss and Aquila HMG cannot link in NCA. ORC HMG is the budget durable linked long gun, not the default premium primary attacker.

#### Firewall — the distinctive support profile

BSG (+1B) Firewall 27/0 has B3 in active turn, AP shotgun ammunition, two VITA and a shared Firewall (-3). MULTI Rifle Firewall 29/0 trades the shotgun focus for conventional mid-range and selectable ammunition.

The TinBot protects eligible Fireteam members against applicable Comms Attacks: attacker suffers -3 WIP and the protected model gets +3 to relevant Saving Rolls. Firewalls do not stack. The Blocker HD's personal Firewall protects only the Blocker; Agent Maximus FTO's TinBot (Repeater) is not a Firewall. Maximus and Hannibal are not inherently Hackable, so an ORC's Firewall in a Duo with either primarily protects the ORC against normal anti-HI hacking; its shared value grows with additional eligible hackers, REMs or TAGs. A mobile Repeater projects the network but does not itself make Maximus Hackable.

Particularly valuable with a Bolt HD/KHD, Aquila, Bulleteer, Squalos or Maximus TAG. Use a Blade Engineer to clear applicable states, not to heal VITA wounds.

#### Fireteam legality and bonuses

- Fusiliers Core/Haris: up to 2 ORCs; at least one actual Fusilier required.
- Neoterra Core/Haris/Duo: native ORC or Bolt required-choice; up to 5 ORCs. Hannibal counts as ORC/Bolt, Agent Maximus counts as ORC, Blade FTO counts as Fusilier/Bolt. Black Friar and Blocker do not count as ORCs.
- Ultima Ratio Duo: up to 1 ORC; native Squalos or Maximus TAG required-choice. Agent Maximus does not replace the TAG anchor.
- One Core, one Haris, unlimited Duos. A third member makes a Haris or three-member Core; 4–5 members require a Core.
- Agent Maximus 45/1 FTO or 47/+1 SWC FTO LT is linkable. The 48/1 Minimus Controller cannot join under general Peripheral rules. Agent and TAG Maximus are mutually exclusive.
- FT2 gives +1 Special Die (roll one extra, discard one), not +1 Burst. This stacks with Hannibal's native +1SD. FT3 adds +3 Discover and +1 Dodge; FT4 +1 BS; FT5 Sixth Sense. Bonuses apply to the team, including nonmatching members.
- Tactical Awareness activates the whole team with its owner as leader; cannot use Hannibal's Tactical Order to shoot with the ORC as leader. Use it for movement/repositioning or Hannibal's own attacks.

##### ORC HMG + Hannibal Vulkan Shotgun — 57/1.5

The strongest reason to retain the HMG model. BS14 B4 HMG with +1SD handles long lanes; Hannibal handles close ranges with B2 AP/Continuous Damage Vulkan Shotgun, native +1SD plus FT2 +1SD (roll four dice, discard two for eligible B2 attack), Nanopulser (+1B), Sensor and Tactical Awareness. Four effective wounds combined (ORC W2; Hannibal W1+NWI, Shock Immunity). Move both together with Hannibal's Tactical Order before switching to the ORC for long-range fighting using a pooled order.

The 26pt Hannibal Strategic Deployment option adds three points and lets an eligible team deploy further forward, reducing movement costs. Neither is a Specialist; add one in a Haris or use other mission pieces. No shared Firewall in this HMG Duo. Unlike adding the HMG to a Fusilier/Bolt Core, this Duo achieves FT2 specifically through ORC equivalence and preserves both Core and Haris slots.

##### ORC Firewall BSG + Agent Maximus — 72/1

Maximus is the main gunfighter (B4 AP Thunderbolt + FT2 SD), brings Specialist Operative, CC deterrence, NWI, Immunity (ARM), Albedo (-3), Tactical Awareness and Repeater. ORC is a two-Wound close-range escort and native FT anchor. Maximus is not Hackable, so do not overvalue the Firewall for him personally. Consider cheaper supporting anchors if the ORC's durability and composition are unnecessary. LT version saves two SWC relative to non-LT for two additional points; consider Chain of Command and NCO (e.g. Squalos) elsewhere.

#### Other profiles and use cases

- **HMG LT:** saves 0.5 SWC versus ordinary HMG. No extra LT order or command multiplier. Direct use of the LT Special Order breaks ordinary Fireteam membership; NCO elsewhere can exploit the order. Quinn/Bolt CoC helps mitigate exposed command.
- **Feuerbach:** B2 AP+DA burst mode versus armor; B1 EXP blast mode for reactive or specialized engagements. Lower Burst than HMG makes it less attractive for ordinary active duels. Bolt ML/MSV1 sniper generally cheaper dedicated AROs.
- **Basic rifles/shotguns:** pay one point for Firewall unless every point matters or existing coverage makes it redundant. Hannibal and Blade offer more efficient general close-range attacking; Bolts add mines and Marksmanship.
- **Hacker:** WIP12/BTS3 at 33/0.5 is inefficient. Prefer Blocker HD 19/0.5 (WIP13, personal Firewall), Bolt HD 24/0.5 (BTS6, E/M Mine) or Deva HD 25/0.5 (WIP15). Two VITA does not justify the premium and added anti-HI hacking exposure.

##### Ultima Ratio alternative

Squalos NCO MULTI HMG 66 + ORC Firewall BSG 27 = 93/1.5 FT1; durable TAG protection and close escort. Pathfinder TacAware 17 is usually the better general-purpose partner (83/1.5 total) for extra activation, FO, Sensor and Repeater. Choose ORC for actual Firewall need. A Maximus TAG can similarly take an ORC; this is different from the ORC-equivalent Agent Maximus Duo.

#### Bottom line

Do not automatically insert ORCs into Fusilier or Bolt teams. Prefer Black Friar Thunderbolt as generic secondary gun and Blade/CSU/Blocker for efficient utility. Retain the ORC HMG for the **57-point Hannibal Duo** (or expanded Haris), and the Firewall BSG for dedicated ORC-equivalent teams, vulnerable hacker/HI/REM/TAG protection or a robust close-range anchor. Other profiles are lower priority.
