# Echo-Bravo — Varuna Immediate Reaction Division (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: Situational / B+ overall, not a priority purchase.** Echo-Bravo provides an independent airborne flank attack or late-game specialist route that complements Varuna's frontal defensive shell. The **27/0 Paramedic/WildParrot** is the preferred general-purpose profile; the **26/0 Boarding Shotgun (+1B)/Light Rocket Launcher** is the preferred dedicated attacker. Prioritize Zulu-Cobras, Croc Men and Montesas for routine battlefield roles before adding Echo-Bravo for its distinct deployment vector.

## Why the chassis is interesting

- **Parachutist:** reliable flank entry without a PH Roll. It can bypass frontal ARO lanes, but is restricted to legal table edges outside the enemy Deployment Zone.
- **Decoy (1):** the combination with airborne deployment creates uncertainty over the real position. Both placements must respect applicable deployment conditions and Coherency, and Decoy replicas are static and targetable as models, not protected Camouflage markers.
- **Surprise Attack (-3):** improves an eligible first active-turn Face to Face engagement when the Echo-Bravo begins the Order in Decoy Marker form. Merely entering from a table edge does not automatically grant Surprise Attack. The penalty applies to opposing Face to Face Rolls, not unopposed attacks.
- **BS13:** good ordinary shooting, especially against exposed targets in favourable range.
- **Limits:** Decoy cannot be regenerated after reveal; no ongoing visual MOD or extra Wound. ARM1 and WIP12 are weak for a 25–27-point trooper. Echo-Bravo relies on choosing engagements rather than surviving them.

## Profile analysis

### Boarding Shotgun (+1B) + Light Rocket Launcher — 26/0

**A− — preferred dedicated attacker.**

- B3 Boarding Shotgun in active turn offers strong close-range direct fire.
- Light Rocket Launcher adds medium-range coverage and Impact Template attacks, especially valuable against clustered enemies or Fireteams whose positioning was designed to defend against frontal attacks.
- Surprise Attack can improve the initial eligible engagement.
- No SWC cost, attractive alongside Varuna's long-range guns.
- Relatively expensive for a single-Wound non-Specialist. It needs a worthwhile attack route rather than merely adding another gun.

### Paramedic + WildParrot — 27/0

**A− — preferred general-purpose profile.**

- Only one point more than the BSG/LRL option, gaining a mission Specialist, MediKit and a distinctive E/M delivery tool instead of the dedicated attack weapons.
- Combi Rifle and Light Shotgun give ordinary shooting options for opportunistic flank attacks.
- The [WildParrot](../../../../rules/4_Ammunition%20and%20Weaponry/WildParrot.md) is a Disposable (1) static E/M mine placed wholly within ZoC using the appropriate Skill. It becomes active after placement; it neither pursues enemies nor instantly attacks on the placement Order.
- E/M forces two Saving Rolls against BTS/2 and may Isolate and, for eligible heavy troop types, Immobilize-B. The alternative delivery angle can threaten a valuable TAG/HI/REM without relying on a frontal gunfight.
- Can still score if there is no profitable shooting target. MediKit offers incidental recovery utility.
- WIP12 makes it a weaker routine objective runner than Varuna's WIP13 alternatives. Its value is unusual access, not specialist quality.

### Red Fury — 25/1

**B — situational.** B4 active-turn fire and useful medium-range bands support a sustained attack against lightly armoured targets. Costs 1 SWC, has no Specialist fallback and lacks Mimetism or MSV. Prefer the zero-SWC BSG/LRL for flexibility unless the intended attack route specifically rewards sustained Burst.

### Hacking Device + Panzerfaust — 26/0.5

**B− — niche.** Can introduce an unexpected Hacking Area for Spotlight, Carbonite, Oblivion or other eligible programs, with a disposable anti-armour weapon as an additional threat. WIP12/BTS3 is mediocre for a Hacker. Choose it for airborne HD access or Hacker-specific objectives, not ordinary network defence.

## Deployment and Order economy

- Normal deployment is legal, provides a pooled Regular Order from the first eligible Order Count and can be preferable when safe side-entry routes are absent.
- Off-table Echo-Bravo does not generate a pooled Order. Its own Order pays for Parachutist entry; all subsequent actions that turn need available group Orders.
- Parachutist is a Long Skill. No simultaneous shooting, hacking or scoring on entry, no Partial Cover during the entry Order, no Prone placement, and no contact with objectives or enemy/neutral models.
- Decoy is available on airborne placement subject to the special Coherency check and normal legal-placement restrictions. An eligible attack from Decoy Marker form cancels the state; there is no repeatable Camouflage loop.
- Delaying entry until Turn 2–3 can exploit enemy repositioning or protect a late scorer, but sacrifices earlier pooled Orders. Budget enough follow-up Orders to exploit the arrival.

## Role in Varuna and final assessment

Varuna's Kamau, Helot and TAG pressure encourages opponents to focus on frontal lanes. Echo-Bravo adds a qualitatively different threat direction, attacking exposed support or scoring from the side after the main force shapes the board. Even an unrevealed airborne threat can influence enemy deployment, though it costs Orders while off-table.

Use it when the mission rewards side objectives, an alternative attack route, or late scoring, and when the combat group has sufficient Orders to exploit entry. Do not force it into every list or replace core midfield tools with a more expensive airborne model.
