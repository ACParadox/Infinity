## Why the FTO is exceptional

- Only 20 points for a Regular Order, two Wounds, ARM4/BTS6, Mimetism (−3), Paramedic, AP Boarding Shotgun and CC23/MA2/DA. It escorts a long-range shooter, protects corners, contests objectives and offers close-combat deterrence.
- Blitzen provides disposable E/M threat against HI/TAG/REMs. Native +1SD stacks with FT2's +1SD: a normal B1 rolled ARO rolls three dice, retains one. This is not Burst 3, three hits or three expenditures. Direct templates do not benefit from SD.
- Limited Cover removes the normal enemy BS penalty from Partial Cover while retaining applicable Saving Roll bonuses. Hackable HI, no visor, no marker protection, ordinary 4–4 movement. Avoid unsupported long lanes and hostile Repeater networks.
- Unlike MO, this FTO counts as Undertow and can increase matching FT level alongside Kamau or Varuna ORCs. It can also operate alone using optional Impetuous activation. FTO has no FD.

## Forward profiles

- **26/0 Paramedic:** FD (+20 cm/8 in) gives immediate S2 forward positioning, valuable on dense maps and when going second; both FTO and FD are Impetuous, so the free activation does not erase FD's advantage. Pay six points and lose Blitzen/Undertow only when starting position is important.
- **32/1.5 Red Fury:** B4 with native SD; good forward short/midrange shooting, but Shock is less useful against heavy armor. No specialist.
- **35/1.5 Spitfire + Drop Bears:** three extra points buy a different range profile and deployable area denial. Drop Bears are carried, not pre-deployed via Minelayer. Expensive for a limited-cover non-specialist.

A mixed FD/non-FD Duo loses much of the FD advantage when deploying in Coherency. Prefer Undertow for FTO unless deliberately choosing an independent two-knight team. At 58–61 points, consider whether separate marker specialists provide better coverage.

## Impetuous and team flexibility

Linked members forgo individual Impetuous activation if remaining linked. An Impetuous activation makes the member leave; a leader departure cancels the team. A non-leader may leave a surviving Haris/Core and rejoin in Coherency during the States Phase under the Fireteam Integrity rules; a cancelled Duo cannot automatically rejoin. Prefer an independent Montesa when its route differs from the team. The optional activation must obey its movement priorities and limited declarations; do not assume unrestricted free objective movement.

## Selection

Start with one 20/0 FTO, consider two for an assault Undertow Haris/Core. Select the 26/0 FD specialist for immediate visible S2 presence, otherwise use Varuna's excellent marker specialists or a 23/0 motorized Paramedic. Only buy the forward gun options for a specific attack plan.
