# Captain Uma Sørensen — Varuna (N5.3)

**Verdict: Optional / low priority (0–1).** The 23-point FTO SMG is the best linked option; the 27-point Camo profile is situational. Varuna has stronger competing midfield specialists and linked support pieces than NCA.

## Camouflage SMG — 27/0

A competent independent specialist with FWD8, repeatable Camouflage, B4 SMG, Surprise Attack, Mimetism (-3), E/M Mine, ARM3 and Climbing Plus. Combat Instinct ignores Surprise Attack MODs and negates enemy Stealth against Uma. She is not inherently Hackable and contributes a Regular Order while deployed in marker state.

Use terrain and marker protection for early scoring and opportunistic close-range fights; re-Camouflage when permitted. E/M Mines threaten susceptible heavy targets and help protect objectives, but she does not have Minelayer. Climbing Plus creates unusual routes, though she cannot claim Partial Cover while on a vertical surface. One Wound and ordinary BS/WIP13 mean she is not an apex attacker.

All four have reusable Camouflage. The Croc can start much farther forward and conceal its presence with Hidden Deployment (normally withholding its Order while hidden). Zulu FO is cheaper with better native midrange and Flash Pulse; Zulu KHD offers network projection and cyber utility. Uma offers closer-range shooting, E/M and vertical mobility. Prefer Zulus first and Croc for hidden/deeper scoring; select Uma when her exact tools or deployment geometry matter.

## FTO SMG — 23/0

Loses Camouflage, Surprise Attack and Forward Deployment; gains X Visor and Wildcard access. B4 SMG, B3 Silenced Pistol, E/M Mine, Mimetism (-3), Combat Instinct and ARM3 provide a credible close-range, non-hackable specialist. X Visor retains the native 0 MOD at 8–16 inches and improves the SMG's 16–24-inch band from -3 to 0 and 24–32-inch band from -6 to -3. The 25-point Breaker Combi is an alternative for native 8–16-inch +3 and BTS-targeting ammunition.

Not all alternatives share the same Fireteam access or composition equivalence. Kamau TA is particularly attractive for two extra points; its Tactical Order can move the Fireteam while Kamau leads, not transfer freely to the main gun. Montesa FTO is cheaper and more durable in wounds; Quinn gives unique Eclipse access. Uma remains attractive if the list specifically needs a non-hackable close gun and E/M Mine. Climbing Plus is less useful when constrained by teammates.

Constructed alternatives, not simultaneous teams. Uma counts as Kamau alongside a native Kamau anchor or as Fusilier alongside Fusiliers; she does not remove native required-choice restrictions. FT2 adds +1 SD, not +1 Burst. An Undertow Engineer supports REMs/TAGs, not healing organic infantry. The HMG Duo is probably Uma's most coherent offensive use, but the TA Paramedic often offers superior order economy.

## Lieutenant — 25/0

Skip by default. No additional Lieutenant Order, Strategos or SWC rebate; Varuna has no Chain of Command. A one-Wound linked active specialist is a risky LT. Prefer a protected Fusilier LT plus identical decoy (20 points for both) or Zulu LT 23/0 with Camo, Sensor and Jammer. Use NCO where it independently makes sense, such as Cutter NCO, rather than buying an aggressive LT merely to spend its Order.

## Final recommendation

- Camo SMG: situational independent scoring, especially on vertical terrain or for E/M control.
- FTO SMG: best linked Uma, but generally behind Kamau TA, Montesa and Quinn in role compression.
- FTO Breaker Combi: low priority.
- Lieutenant: skip.

0–1, low collection/listbuilding priority. Uma is less distinctive in Varuna than NCA or WinterFor. Prioritize Zulu-Cobras, Croc Men, Kamau and Montesa before her unless the non-hackable close-range E/M specialist niche is specifically required.
