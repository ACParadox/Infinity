# Croc Men — Varuna

**Verdict: A — strongly recommended independent midfield specialist/ambush piece.** Default: Forward Observer (30/0). Generally take one alongside Zulu-Cobras rather than replacing them.

**Authority:** [Pinned N5.3 roster](../../../../sectorials/panoceania/varuna-immediate-reaction-division.md) · [Listbuilding](../varuna-listbuilding.md) · [Fireteams](../varuna-fireteams.md) · [References](../varuna-references.md).

## Base profile

**AVA 4 · SK · Regular · No Fireteam access.** MOV 10-10 (4-4"), CC14, BS12, PH12, WIP13, ARM1, BTS0, W1, S2.

Common skills/equipment: X-Visor, Camouflage, Hidden Deployment, Infiltration, Mimetism (-6), Surprise Attack (-3), Stealth, Terrain (Total).

Excellent deployment flexibility and visual modifiers compensate for modest BS12. ARM1/BTS0/W1, no Shock Immunity, and vulnerability to MSV, Sensor and direct templates make it physically fragile. Infiltration reaches the centreline without a roll on a normal table (mission restrictions apply). No Fireteam bonuses.

All profiles also carry Silenced Pistol and CC Weapon. The Hacker, FO and Minelayer are distinct loadouts; do not combine their equipment.

### Forward Observer — 30/0

**Default choice.** Infiltration saves movement Orders; Hidden Deployment protects a scoring piece and conceals an advanced threat. WIP13 FO, Flash Pulse, mines and D-Charges give broad mission utility. Deployable Repeater adds a forward hacking node without exposing the primary Hacker. It still requires deployment by the Croc Man; it is not a native Repeater or Minelayer.

X-Visor changes negative range MODs of −3 to 0 and −6 to −3, including applicable Suppressive Fire and Discover ranges. Combi Rifle is consequently versatile. In cover and Suppressive Fire, ordinary opposing BS attacks can face −12 (Mimetism −6, cover −3, suppression −3) before other modifiers/counters; the Croc Man is no longer hidden or camouflaged while suppressing.

### Minelayer — 26/0.5

Deploy a Shock Mine and retain an unseen Croc Man where legal, creating a visible marker and an unknown second threat. Boarding Shotgun is suited to tight midfield geometry. Excellent when the opponent's first turn must be slowed, but 26 points/0.5 SWC for a non-specialist is significant alongside cheap Helots and Zulu-Cobra Jammer/Sensor. Prefer FO in general objective missions.

### Hacker — 33/0.5

Hidden Infiltration can threaten an unsuspecting HI, TAG or other legal hacking target from midfield. A standard HD is valuable where a KHD cannot perform the relevant program. WIP13/BTS0 and lack of Firewall or special hacking bonuses limit hacking duels; Mimetism does not protect from hacking. No Deployable Repeater or Pitcher on this option.

Often prefer the 30/0 FO plus a separate Kamau HD/FastPanda (22/0.5) when the list can afford the two pieces; they split scoring, hacking and network projection, rather than representing a cheaper direct replacement. Zulu KHD/Pitcher is another complementary option but not equivalent to an HD.

### MULTI Sniper Rifle — 29/1.5

Use for an unexpected secondary lane or active ambush, not as a replacement for the obvious primary linked Kamau ARO. Infiltration and Hidden Deployment can establish unconventional firing angles; X-Visor broadens useful ranges. Mimetism −6 and active Surprise Attack −3 can stack with cover against eligible opponents, but Surprise Attack is not a reactive bonus.

| Attribute | Croc sniper | Kamau MSV2 sniper |
|---|---|---|
| Pts/SWC | 29/1.5 | 29/1.5 |
| BS / BTS | 12 / 0 | 13 / 6 |
| Visual mods | Mim−6 | Mim−3, MSV2 |
| Deployment | Infiltration, Hidden, Camo | Normal |

Kamau is the better conventional reactive gun; Croc is the better surprise angle. Avoid overpaying for redundant sniper coverage.

### Boarding Shotgun — 25/0

Usually skip. Minelayer adds turn-zero utility for 1 point plus 0.5 SWC; FO adds specialist, repeater and versatile rifle for 5 points with no SWC increase. Basic shotgun remains a niche option when both points and SWC are tight.

## Comparison: Croc FO vs Zulu FO vs Uma Camo

| Attribute | Croc FO | Zulu FO | Uma Camo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pts/SWC | 30/0 | 24/0 | 27/0 |
| BS / WIP | 12 / 13 | 13 / 13 | 13 / 13 |
| ARM / BTS | 1 / 0 | 1 / 3 | 3 / 3 |
| Deployment | Infiltration | FD(+10 cm / 4") | FD(+20 cm / 8") |
| Hidden Deployment | Yes | No | No |
| Camouflage / Mimetism | Yes / −6 | Yes / −3 | Yes / −3 |
| Specialist | FO | FO | Specialist Operative |
| Weapon | Combi, X-Visor | Breaker Combi | SMG (+1B) |
| Mine | Shock | None | E/M |
| Network | Deployable Repeater | None | None |
| Other | D-Charges | — | Climbing Plus, Shock Immunity, Combat Instinct |

On a standard 48-inch table with 12-inch deployment zones and no mission restrictions, Croc gains 8 inches of guaranteed deployment over Zulu FO. The 6-point premium also buys Hidden Deployment, stronger Mimetism, mines, D-Charges, X-Visor and repeater, while losing BS1 and BTS3. Very reasonable for a dedicated objective specialist.

Zulu is more economical and offers distinctive alternatives: Jammer/Sensor/Triangulated Fire (23/0), KHD/Pitcher (26/0), and MULTI MMR/E/M Minelayer (26/0.5). Uma is better suited to close-range attacks, vertical mobility and E/M denial. Croc supplies hidden presence and delayed reveal, not the same control toolkit.

## Hidden Deployment and Orders

A trooper remaining in Hidden Deployment at Order Count does not contribute a pooled Regular Order. Its own Order can activate it to reveal; the state cannot be regained. Ordinary Camouflage is reusable under its normal requirements. Start as a Camo marker when pooled Order efficiency matters more than concealment; especially relevant with a 95–97-point Cutter.

Do not confuse the tactical value of Hidden Deployment with extra Orders. The opponent can infer that something is absent from the courtesy information without knowing its identity or position.

## Recommended midfield package

| Unit | Pts/SWC |
|---|---:|
| Croc Man FO | 30/0 |
| Zulu-Cobra Jammer/Sensor | 23/0 |
| Zulu-Cobra KHD/Pitcher | 26/0 |
| **Total** | **79/0** |

Three Regular troopers (not necessarily three pooled Orders if Croc is hidden), two specialists, three Camo-capable units, hidden scoring, repeater/Pitcher coverage, mines, Sensor and Jammer. Add an HD where broader hacking programs are required; KHD is not an HD substitute.

## Final decision

- Midfield scoring / ambush and information denial: S.
- Midfield defence / network projection / independent active gunfighting / value: A.
- Conventional reactive gunfighting: B.
- Physical durability: C.

Aim for one Croc Man in most Varuna lists, potentially two for objective-heavy missions; FO first, Minelayer or Sniper for a specific defensive geometry, Hacker when a hidden HD is integral to the plan. AVA4 is flexibility rather than a target.
