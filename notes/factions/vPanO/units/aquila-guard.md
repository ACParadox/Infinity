### HMG: real niche, not the default gun

MSV3 adds automatic Discover against CAMO, direct active-turn attacks against CAMO markers (apply the marker's Mimetism MOD), and Surprise Attack protection under the applicable LoF/CC conditions. MSV2 already removes the Mimetism of revealed targets and ordinary visibility modifiers. Choose Aquila specifically when marker clearance, BS15 and two Wounds justify the price, particularly against marker-heavy armies such as TAK.

| Competing gun | Pts / SWC | Why choose it instead |
|---|---:|---|
| Nisse HMG | 31 / 1.5 | MSV2, Mim(-3), unhackable MI; saves 21 pts |
| Griffin HMG | 37 / 1.5 | BS14, MSV1, 2W; cheaper durable long gun |
| Karhu Feuerbach (+1B) | 34 / 1.5 | MSV1, Mim(-3), Climbing Plus, NCO, B3 AP+DA; saves 18 pts |
| Swiss Guard HMG | 56 / 1.5 | BS15, Hidden Deployment, Camo, Mim(-6), ARM5/2W; better generic apex for 4 pts more |

Aquila lacks Mimetism, AP on its HMG, NCO and Tactical Awareness; remains hackable HI. White Noise, Albedo and Eclipse limit its visor. Sensor from Fennec/Pathfinder offers a cheaper complementary marker-clearance route. Do not use the HMG as a routine ARO investment.

### FTO MMR / Disco / command

The FTO can form a legal mixed Family I FT1 Duo, e.g. Blade FTO BSG20/0 (69/0 with ordinary specialist or 72/+1 with Strategos). Shared movement and Eclipse help mission access, but no automatic +1SD; do not import NCA's Haris/FT2 bonuses. The HMG is not eligible. Eclipse blocks friendly MSV3 too.

Black A.I.R. K1 MMR FO29/0.5 + Fennec FO/Sensor/Disco13/0 = 42/0.5: two Regular Orders, two specialists, MSV2/Mim(-3), Sensor and distributed vision control. Both are unhackable as ordinary targets of anti-HI/REM/TAG programs. Aquila retains higher BS, MSV3, personal durability and zero SWC on opt 5, but the distributed package is generally better value.

Strategos LT52/+1 compresses active command, specialist scoring, Eclipse, MSV3 and a real SWC rebate; choose only when those roles will actually be used. Magistrate Strategos LT19/0 gives cheap protected command and Counterintelligence. Prefer Joan v2 or double Fusilier + Karhu as generic starts. Strategos converts the LT Order to Regular rather than creating an additional Order; NCO cannot use that converted Order as a separate LT Order. Consider protected CoC if the Aquila LT is intended to fight. Do not buy succession insurance merely to justify an already expensive LT.

### Aquila Guard — Neoterran Capitaline Army (N5.3)

**Verdict: Optional build-around unit, not a must-have. Prefer the 52-point/+1 SWC Strategos FTO Lieutenant. The Aquila offers exceptional role compression in a Fireteam, but costs apex-gunfighter points for a Burst 3 MULTI Marksman Rifle rather than an apex heavy weapon.** Treat it primarily as a durable midfield Fireteam leader and mission enabler, with a separate long-range attack piece. Normally take 0–1.

Reviewed 23 November 2026. Rankings are tactical judgments.

#### Why the FTO package is attractive — and why it is not mandatory

A single model brings BS15, MSV3, AP/Shock MULTI Marksman Rifle, Eclipse delivery, specialist eligibility, two Wounds, ARM4/BTS6, ECM: Hacker (-3), and a flexible Wildcard slot. At FT2 it gains BS Attack (+1SD), improving both conventional shooting and rolled Disco Baller attacks. The Strategos Lieutenant adds flexible order economy, reserve deployment and +1 SWC.

This is unusually good role compression, particularly under the 15-trooper cap. But the weapon remains an MMR: B3, strongest at 8–24 inches, with AP/Shock active modes and a DA mode at B1. +1SD rolls one additional die and discards one; it is **not** an extra hit or actual Burst 4. At favorable range the Aquila is a strong gunfighter, not a substitute for a B4 HMG opening long lanes. It has no Mimetism and is still a hackable HI vulnerable to direct templates, E/M and close assault. Do not expose a 52-point Lieutenant merely to trade shots.

Had the FTO carried a MULTI HMG or AP Spitfire, it would be more compelling as a primary attack piece. Its actual strength is doing several different jobs in the same activation plan.

#### MSV3 and anti-marker work

- MSV2 and MSV3 both negate revealed targets' Mimetism and ordinary visibility MODs and allow shooting through ordinary Smoke. Neither sees through Eclipse; White Noise, Reflective and Albedo remain relevant counters.
- MSV3 automatically succeeds at Discover against eligible Camouflaged State targets. It may instead attack a CAMO marker in LoF in the Active Turn without prior Discover, but must apply the marker's Mimetism MOD. Normal state cancellation clauses still apply.
- MSV3 ignores Surprise Attack MODs when it has LoF, and ignores them for CC Attacks even without LoF.
- Automatic Discover can remove a marker's protection for subsequent attacks by other models; direct shooting can save a declaration or Order when the Mimetism penalty is acceptable. MSV3 is not an automatic anti-Impersonation solution.

MSV3 is particularly valuable against marker-heavy armies such as TAK or vanilla Ariadna. Against already revealed mimetic enemies, a Black Friar or Deva MSV2 often solves the same visual problem much more cheaply. Sensor-equipped Blockers, Pathfinders and Hannibal provide complementary marker clearance. Do not pay 49–52 points solely for MSV3.

#### Disco Baller and mission play

The FTO can deploy Eclipse at BS18 in the +3 range band (8–16 inches), before other modifiers, and benefits from FT2 +1SD on eligible rolled attacks. This makes it a particularly reliable tool for moving an entire team past an unfavorable ARO, crossing a lane, or reaching a console without killing the defender. Eclipse also blocks the Aquila's own MSV3; this is not a Smoke-shooting combination. Do not expect a Disco Baller attack to protect against an enemy shot by winning a conventional Face-to-Face Roll; deploy from safety where possible. Manage Disposable uses and consider Deactivator interactions with deployed equipment under the applicable rules.

A 26/0 Quinn MULTI Rifle/Disco Baller FO is the much cheaper alternative if Eclipse and a specialist are the only requirements. The Aquila justifies the extra 23 points through durability, BS15, MSV3 and (on opt 8) command value.

Use the main long-range gun to clear the table, then advance the Aquila's team for midrange fights, marker removal and scoring. The existing NCA listbuilding notes cite a Mainfinity tournament example in which an Aquila LT used its Disco Baller late to reach an antenna. This demonstrates mission utility rather than general superiority as a gunfighter.

#### Strategos Lieutenant

For 3 points over the otherwise identical FTO Lieutenant:

- +1 SWC: standard 300-point list can spend up to 7 SWC, subject to normal list rules.
- Strategos L1: may keep two reserve Troopers instead of one during deployment.
- Converts the Special Lieutenant Order into a Regular Order in the Lieutenant's Combat Group, available to the whole group including Fireteams. It does not create a second LT Order or add an Order on top of the existing one.
- **NCO interaction:** the converted Lieutenant Order is no longer available for NCO, so do not pay for Squalos NCO expecting an extra activation beyond Strategos. Tactical Awareness remains separate.

| Command package | Pts / SWC | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Fusilier LT + identical basic Fusilier | 20 / 0 | Cheap protected command |
| Auxilia LT + identical basic Auxilia | 30 / 0 | Command plus meaningful DZ defence |
| Aquila Strategos LT | 52 / +1 | Active mission leader, deployment and flexible Orders |
| Aquila Strategos LT + Quinn CoC | 76 / +1 | Aggressive command with succession insurance |
| Aquila Strategos LT + Bolt CoC | 78 / +1 | Succession insurance and Bolt Fireteam anchor |

CoC is worth considering if the Aquila will fight actively, but the resulting 76–78-point investment is substantial. Keep the CoC carrier protected rather than exposing both officers together. CoC prevents Loss of Lieutenant when its conditions are met; it does not transfer Strategos to the successor. The regular FTO LT normally saves too little to forgo Strategos and the SWC rebate.

#### Fireteam selection

Aquila FTO is a Wildcard in all NCA families but does not count as Fusilier, Bolt, ORC, CSU or TAG. A three-member team can reach FT2 by including two matching support members; a five-member team with a single Aquila and four matching members can reach FT4, not FT5. Fusilier family has Core/Haris only (no Duo); Neoterra requires an actual Bolt or ORC, CSU Duo an actual CSU, Ultima Ratio Duo a Squalos or Maximus TAG.

| Members (using standard 49/0 FTO) | Family | Pts / SWC | FT |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Aquila + CSU Specialist (11) | CSU Duo | 60/0 | 1 |
| Aquila + Fusilier basic (10) + Blocker Pitcher SO (15) | Fusiliers Haris | 74/0 | 2 |
| Aquila + Fusilier basic (10) + Blade-Ops FTO BSG (20) | Fusiliers Haris | 79/0 | 2 |
| Aquila + Bolt KHD (22) + Machinist (17) | Neoterra Haris | 88/0 | 2 |
| Aquila + 2 basic Fusiliers (20) + Blocker Pitcher SO (15) + Blade-Ops FTO BSG (20) | Fusiliers Core | 104/0 | 4 |
| Aquila + Bolt MSV1 MSR (26/1.5) + Shona (22) | Neoterra Haris | 97/1.5 | 1 |

For Strategos, add 3 points and gain +1 SWC. The final example is a documented three-unit combination in battle report #227, with modern profile choices reconstructed; it is FT1, not FT3.

**Preferred compact package:** Aquila Strategos LT 52/+1 + Blade-Ops FTO BSG 20/0 + Fusilier Paramedic 12/0 = **84 points / +1 SWC, FT2 Haris**. BS15 +1SD MMR and Eclipse, TacAware from Blade-Ops, close-range shotgun and D-Charges, plus Paramedic recovery. The converted LT Order and Blade Tactical Order improve usable activations; the latter is restricted to its bearer under TacAware rules. Alternative: basic Fusilier + Blocker Pitcher with the Strategos Aquila = **77/+1, FT2 Haris**, trading the Blade's TacAware/close-range tools for network projection.

A Bolt KHD + Machinist pairing provides counter-hacking and engineering, but no Doctor/Paramedic for the Aquila's lost Wounds. A Bolt Paramedic can fill that recovery role instead. Blocker Hacker Firewall or a suitable ORC TinBot is worth considering if the team will operate inside enemy repeater coverage. Avoid inflating the team purely for FT4: FT2 is already the important shooting and Eclipse reliability breakpoint.

#### Alternatives and opportunity cost

| Alternative | Pts / SWC | Prefer when |
|---|---:|---|
| Black Friar Thunderbolt (+1B) | 24/0 | Economical MSV2, Albedo and 16–32 inch counter-gun |
| Deva MSV2 Spitfire | 29/1 | MSV2 with higher active Burst |
| Bolt MSV1 MULTI Sniper | 26/1.5 | Marksmanship, long-range linked ARO |
| Quinn Disco FO | 26/0 | Cheap Eclipse specialist |
| Black Friar Thunderbolt + Quinn Disco FO | 50/0 | Split MSV2 and Eclipse across two bodies and two Regular Orders |
| Black Friar Thunderbolt + ORC MULTI Rifle LT | 52/0 | Separate counter-gun and durable conventional LT |
| ORC HMG | 34/1.5 | Much cheaper linked long-range attack gun |
| Swiss Guard HMG | 56/1.5 | Independent apex attack, Mimetism (-6), Hidden Deployment, ARM5 |
| Squalos MULTI Marksman Rifle | 55/1 | Durable TAG, Tactical Awareness, speed and BS15 MMR |

The Aquila HMG is an excellent independent anti-marker shooter, especially into camouflage, Smoke or opposing visors that negate the Swiss Guard's Mimetism. The Swiss HMG generally makes the better universal attacker: Mimetism (-6), Hidden Deployment, ARM5 and BS15 for only four additional points. The cheaper ORC HMG also has FT access and B4. Take the Aquila HMG for its specific MSV3 matchup advantage, not because it is automatically NCA's best gun.

Existing NCA transcript notes distinguish Gravitypool's interest in the FTO Disco spearhead from Robert Shepherd's concern about its cost compared with cheap protected Fusilier/Auxilia command. The local conclusion is consistent with that trade-off, rather than a claim of consensus.

**Bottom line:** The Strategos FTO is a premium midfield mission/command piece. It is worth testing when the list uses MSV3, BS15 +1SD, Eclipse, specialist work and the command/SWC package together. Otherwise take cheaper specialists or a true heavy gun. The MMR is the principal limitation; the Aquila is not a mandatory inclusion.
