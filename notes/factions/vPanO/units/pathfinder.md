### Pathfinder Dronbot — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A-tier · Strong / Go-to, not Must-have.** The **17-point Tactical Awareness Pathfinder** is an unusually efficient mobile mission specialist, Sensor platform, Repeater and flexible Fireteam support model. Its best feature is not its Combi Rifle: **17 points buys one Regular Order plus one personal Tactical Awareness Order**, and its useful non-combat jobs make that extra Order very likely to find a use in a normal game.

**Profile selection:** **Never take the 14-point non-Tactical Awareness version in ordinary Kestrel list building.** Saving three points is not worth losing a recurring useful activation. All recommendations below use **Pathfinder Tactical Awareness (17 pts / 0 SWC)**.

#### Pinned N5.3 profile

**Kestrel Pathfinder Dronbot:** AVA 2, REM, Regular, MOV **15-10 cm (6-4")**, CC 13, BS 11, PH 10, **WIP 13**, ARM 0, BTS 3, STR 1, **S3**. Remote Presence, Courage, Deactivator, Repeater, Forward Observer, Sensor and Triangulated Fire. Carries **Combi Rifle, Flash Pulse, PARA CCW (-3)**.

| Selected profile | Points / SWC | Fireteam access |
|---|---:|---|
| **Pathfinder Tactical Awareness** | **17 / 0** | **Wildcard max 1 per Fireteam**; does **not** Count-as Fennec, Kestrel or another native family |

The profile is **not** a gunfighter, Engineer or dedicated ARO piece. It is a fast, somewhat fragile **specialist / Sensor / hacking-network / Fireteam-mobility package**.

#### The strongest argument: 17 points for effectively two Orders

- **Regular Order:** contributes one Order to its Combat Group's pool.
- **Tactical Awareness Order:** an additional, personal Tactical Order available while eligible. It can move the Pathfinder, run Sensor, perform a Forward Observer attack, move to a console or reposition.
- **Linked Tactical Order:** when Pathfinder belongs to a legal Fireteam, spending its Tactical Order makes **Pathfinder the Fireteam Leader** and activates the team. The team can move or reposition together, but a premium gunfighter **cannot lead and shoot using Pathfinder's Tactical Order**.
- **Likely to be spent:** with **6-4" MOV**, objectives, Sensor, FO and Repeater positioning, the extra activation is unlikely to be worthless. Even if it is **only a move Order**, it still saves a pooled Order.

**Pure points/Orders evaluation is excellent:** **17 points** for a Regular Order contributor **and** a potentially productive personal Order. It is not literally two interchangeable Regular Orders: the Tactical Order cannot be freely assigned to another trooper, and a Fireteam activation uses Pathfinder as Leader.

#### When to run it independently versus in a Fireteam

| Role / context | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Independent specialist** | **Excellent solo** | Runs buttons, reaches objectives and uses the personal Order without dragging a gunfighter around |
| **Camouflage / Hidden Deployment hunter** | **Excellent solo** | Quickly brings Sensor into ZoC and reveals threats ahead of other attackers |
| **Supporting a Fireteam** | **Strong** | Fast specialist/Sensor, extra **repositioning Order**, useful **second Flash Pulse ARO**, and a **moving Repeater** |
| **Squalos Mk-II Duo** | **Strong** | Combines TAG firepower with specialist and Sensor access; especially appealing with the Grenade Launcher |
| **Tikbalang Duo** | **Good, conditional** | Useful mission and Sensor support, but the Tik's **Climbing Plus** often favors separate positioning |
| **Pure static ARO team** | **Situational** | Flash Pulse and Repeater still matter, but the movement Order is less attractive |

**Fireteam legality and bonuses:** Kestrel's current chart gives Pathfinder **Wildcard max 1**, but it is **not a Counts-as member**. It does not itself increase the same-family Fireteam Level. A **Squalos + Pathfinder** or **Tikbalang + Pathfinder** **ARMORED Duo is FT1**, so no FT2 +1 Special Die. A **KESTREL Duo** needs a native required-choice Griffin/Firefly/Hetkari; **FENNECS** permits Core/Haris, not a two-model Duo, and still requires a native Fennec. A Wildcard never overrides those anchors. For a 2–3-model team, its main selling points are the **extra activation and support tools**, not a shooting bonus.

##### Squalos Mk-II

**Best TAG partner, but not compulsory.** A **Squalos HMG + Grenade Launcher (62/1.5) + Pathfinder TA (17/0)** gives a **79-point / 1.5 SWC FT1 ARMORED Duo**. Pathfinder adds:
- **FO / Targeted** support for the Squalos Grenade Launcher;
- Sensor to uncover Camouflaged and Hidden threats;
- a faster objective specialist that does not require the TAG to dismount;
- Repeater coverage and another potentially useful team movement activation.

Other current combinations: **Squalos MULTI HMG NCO66 + Pathfinder17 = 83/1.5**; **Squalos MMR55 + Pathfinder17 = 72/1**. Both are **FT1**, not better TAG gunfighters by virtue of the Duo. The **Squalos + Pathfinder** formation was observed in **battle report #219**, although the exact modern loadouts in that game were not fully established.

##### Tikbalang

**Good but more optional, and often better independent.** **Tikbalang HMG69 + Pathfinder17 = 86/1.5 FT1 Duo**. Sensor, FO and the specialist are genuinely useful, but the Tikbalang's Climbing Plus, Mimetism and movement freedom can make linking it to an S3, non-climbing REM restrictive. If the TAG is going to climb and hunt independently, let Pathfinder handle Sensor and objectives elsewhere instead of tying their positions together.

#### Sensor or Forward Observer: which matters more?

**Both are core reasons to take Pathfinder, for different jobs.**

1. **Sensor is the most distinctive tactical utility.** Sensor makes a **Normal WIP +6 Roll** within ZoC, without needing LoF, to discover **all eligible Camouflaged or Hidden Deployment enemies in the area simultaneously**. On WIP13 that is **19 (95%)**, before any exceptional circumstance. It also grants +6 to Discover Camouflage Markers and impedes re-camouflaging within Sensor ZoC. Pathfinder's mobility makes positioning for a sweep realistic. It is a valuable screen ahead of TAGs or close-assault troops.
2. **Forward Observer is why it is an all-purpose mission specialist.** WIP13 objective play, **B2 FO attack in the active turn**, and Targeted designation are broadly useful. Targeted improves subsequent eligible attacks and supports Guided/indirect-fire plans.
3. **The combined package is the real appeal.** Pathfinder can reveal threats, designate them, extend a hacking network and complete objectives while spending an extra personal Order.

**Competition matters:** Kestrel also has the **13-point Fennec FO/Sensor/Disco** (WIP12, S2), which is a cheap native-Fennec specialist with Disco utility. Pathfinder's main edge is **faster movement + Tactical Awareness + its Repeater**, not exclusive access to Sensor or FO.

#### Flash Pulse: an underrated secondary Fireteam ARO

**Flash Pulse is valuable chiefly as an ARO, especially when it provides a second simultaneous threat to an attacker approaching a Fireteam.** It is a **WIP-based, B1, Non-Lethal BS Weapon** that can apply **Stunned** after a successful attack and failed BTS Saving Roll. At 8–24", its +3 range MOD gives Pathfinder **WIP16**, before target or visibility MODs.

**Example:** a Fireteam gunfighter and Pathfinder both have a valid ARO against an enemy moving into a lane. Both declare **BS Attack** as their Fireteam ARO; the gunfighter uses its gun while Pathfinder uses **Flash Pulse**. Each resolves its own attack and can threaten the active model. Their weapons can differ because the **Skill declared is the same**.

This makes an enemy attack run risk a **Stunned** result in addition to the gunfighter's damage. Stunned only matters **after the current Order resolves**; it does not retrospectively cancel the attack in progress. A Flash Pulse is also useful for guarding an approach while Pathfinder remains partially protected.

**Do not overexpose it:** **ARM0 / STR1 / S3** means Pathfinder can die to a strong gunfighter. A second ARO is a benefit, not a reason to sacrifice a valuable objective specialist.

#### Repeater: especially good attached to an advancing Fireteam

Pathfinder's **Repeater moves the friendly Hacking Area forward** with the team, assuming the list has a Hacker able to use the relevant programs. That creates a second kind of defensive pressure on an approaching enemy:

| Approaching target | Potential support from friendly Hackers using Pathfinder's Repeater |
|---|---|
| **Hackable HI or TAG assault piece** | Carbonite, Oblivion or other available anti-Hackable programs to impede its advance |
| **Enemy Hacker** | Appropriate counter-hacking program, e.g. Trinity where available |
| **Non-Hackable close-assault trooper** | **Spotlight** can still put it into **Targeted**, even though it is not Hackable |

**Spotlight is not an immediate stopper:** it can mark a non-Hackable enemy for later attack, but cannot by itself Immobilize it. Its N5.3 application also involves WIP, saving rolls and the normal ARO conditions; it is **not automatic Targeted**.

**Caveat:** Pathfinder is itself **Hackable**, and a forward Repeater creates interaction opportunities for the opponent as well. Repeater coverage is **potential protection only if an appropriate friendly Hacker and usable Hacking Area are actually present**. When the Fireteam stays ahead of the hacking network, the Repeater can be much more valuable than an otherwise separate static support REM.

#### Triangulated Fire: Forward Observer > Combi Rifle > Flash Pulse

**Triangulated Fire is a Long Skill, not an ARO.** It requires LoF, removes attack-roll MODs from range, cover, Mimetism and similar sources, while retaining Burst-affecting MODs and the weapon's maximum range. **It does not let Pathfinder Move + shoot**; when Pathfinder leads a Fireteam, other members normally Idle for the non-Movement Skill.

| Weapon / mode | Normal active B | Triangulated value |
|---|---:|---|
| **Forward Observer** | **B2, WIP13** | **Best use:** attempt Targeted at **flat WIP13** against a target otherwise protected by stacked negative MODs |
| **Combi Rifle** | **B3, BS11** | Emergency damage option at flat BS11 when ordinary fire would be at an even worse number |
| **Flash Pulse** | **B1, WIP13** | Rarely useful in own active turn; mainly an ARO weapon rather than something to spend a Long Skill on |

**Example:** FO against a Mimetism (-6) target in Partial Cover at a neutral FO range would normally test **WIP4** (13 - 6 - 3). Triangulated FO instead tests **WIP13 with B2**, while the target may still ARO. This makes **Triangulated FO** a real deliberate option for enabling Guided attacks or Squalos Grenade Launcher follow-up, **provided Pathfinder already has LoF and a survivable firing position**.

Triangulated **Combi Rifle** is a last resort: BS11 without helpful positive MODs and ARM0 defenses do not make Pathfinder an effective gunfighter. **Flash Pulse** is B1 and usually better kept for reactive defense. Nor does Triangulated Fire make smoke transparent: **LoF is still required**.

#### Objective access and S3 limitations

Pathfinder is a **valid Forward Observer specialist**; being **S3** does **not** stop it from interacting with appropriate objectives by itself. Its **40 mm base** compared with S2 infantry's usual **25 mm** makes cramped passages, narrow gaps and protected console approaches more difficult.

It **can go Prone** in N5.3 (ordinary REMs are not among the blanket prohibited troop types). Prone changes its silhouette height, but **not the 40 mm base**, and movement while Prone is reduced. Its 6-4" starting movement mitigates but does not erase the terrain-access issue.

Use S2 infantry for objectives hidden in tight corridors; use Pathfinder where mobility, Sensor and its additional Order allow it to take a practical route.

#### Must-have or not?

**Overall: A-tier · Strong / Go-to, not Must-have.** The **17-point Tactical Awareness** profile is routinely desirable on raw Order value, even before the specialist utility is counted.

| Use | Rating | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| **Independent Pathfinder TA** | **A** | Fast self-sufficient specialist and Sensor platform with extra Order |
| **Squalos GL + Pathfinder TA Duo** | **A** | Best complementary TAG/specialist and Targeted support |
| **Tikbalang + Pathfinder TA Duo** | **B+** | Good support, but can constrain a climbing TAG |
| **Generic useful Fireteam + Pathfinder TA** | **A-/B+** | Depends on repositioning needs, Flash Pulse coverage and Hacker network |
| **Triangulated FO specialist use** | **Situationally strong** | Good against heavy attack MODs from an established LoF |
| **Direct shooting as its main role** | **Poor** | Do not buy it for Combi Rifle firefights |

**Practical list-building principle:** buy Pathfinder TA for **17-point double-Order economy plus FO, Sensor, Repeater and Flash Pulse**. Usually deploy it independently if it has different objectives than the main attacker. Link it when **free team repositioning, a second Flash Pulse ARO and mobile Repeater coverage** have genuine value. **Neither Squalos nor Tikbalang requires one**, and it is not a compulsory pick in every Kestrel list.
