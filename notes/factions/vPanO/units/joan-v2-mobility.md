### Joan of Arc v2 (Mobility Armor) — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A for the basic 45pt Lieutenant; A− for the 49pt Lieutenant (+1 Command Token); A− conditional for the 45pt Chain of Command in a Knight Commander Lieutenant (+1 Order) + NCO list; B+ conditional for the 51pt Strategic Deployment Lieutenant; B− / usually skip for the 51pt CoC + Strategic Deployment.** Joan v2 is a **BS15, 6–2-moving, AP-Spitfire second-wave attacker** with genuine melee threat, Tactical Awareness and Lieutenant-dependent Inspiring Leadership. She is **not mandatory** in today's MO: Knight Commander LT (+1 Order) + NCO, De Fersen, dedicated specialists and other elite shooters provide competing use cases.

**Important names:** The +1 on option 5 is **one extra Command Token**, **not Lieutenant (+1 Order)**. V2 has **no Lieutenant (+1 Order)** profile. Each v2 Lieutenant gives one ordinary Special Lieutenant Order, plus Joan's **separate** Tactical Awareness Order; her own Regular Order joins her Combat Group's pool. CoC versions do **not** receive an LT Order until Joan actually becomes Lieutenant.

#### Core value: fast, credible AP-Spitfire attacker

- **BS15 + AP Spitfire B4** is a legitimate active-turn gun, particularly at **8–24 inches**; AP addresses hard targets better than a plain Spitfire, while the **X-Visor** reduces the difficulty of unfavorable bands. Neither X-Visor nor high BS is a substitute for Mimetism/MSV or correct engagement selection.
- **6–2 Move** is especially useful for **Move + Shoot**, crossing short gaps, getting into a firing position or exiting it. Total Move+Move distance is the same as v1's 4–4; v2 gets a superior first Short Skill.
- **2 Wounds + NWI**, BTS6, **Pulzar (+1B)**, **MA3/EXP CCW** and Stealth give her unusually strong short-range resilience, deterrence and assault flexibility. But **ARM3 without a shooting MOD** means she should not absorb repeated unfavorable face-to-face attacks like a Mimetism TAG.
- **Tactical Awareness** is real Order efficiency on an expensive combat piece. If Joan is a legal Fireteam Leader, her Tactical Order can activate that Fireteam; a solo Joan spends it on herself.
- **Frenzy** triggers after she directly inflicts a qualifying Wound/kill. Impetuous and Limited Cover make subsequent exposed active-turn fights riskier; plan a protected end position. Do not assume she remains a full-cover gunfighter after attacking.

**Preferred job:** Your longer-range HMG/TAG/NCO shooter removes ARO roadblocks, then Joan attacks the surviving medium-range defenses, defends a newly opened corridor, threatens CC or advances a legal Fireteam. A 45pt **non-specialist** should not consume the specialist/scorer budget required to win the mission.

#### The two sensible command architectures

The key comparison is **not** Joan CoC with an arbitrary active Hospitaller Lieutenant. In a dedicated MO list, the strongest reason to choose Joan CoC is to **keep Knight Commander LT (+1 Order)** and feed its **two Special Lieutenant Orders** to a separate **NCO attacker**.

| Command architecture | Command package | Pts / SWC | What it buys |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **A — Fighting Joan Lieutenant** | **Joan v2 LT 45/1 + Knight Commander CoC 23/0** | **68 / 1** | Inspiring Leadership from Turn 1, Joan's own LT Order + Tactical Awareness, Commander protected in the backfield as succession insurance |
| **B — NCO economy with Joan backup** | **Knight Commander LT(+1 Order) 24/0 + Joan v2 CoC 45/1.5** | **69 / 1.5** | Two LT Orders **for a separate NCO**, safer backfield actual LT, Joan as second-wave independent attacker and CoC successor |

**Only 1 point and 0.5 SWC separate these packages before purchasing any NCO**. Compare them using the **same planned NCO**, which makes the trade particularly clear:

- **Package A:** Joan's one LT Order can activate her directly, and an NCO (if included) could alternatively use that **single** LT Order. Inspiring Leadership works immediately; the protected CoC Commander avoids Loss of Lieutenant if Joan falls.
- **Package B:** The Knight Commander generates **two LT Orders each turn**. A **Seraph, Holy Sepulchre NCO AP HMG, or Teutonic Knight NCO Spitfire** can use **both** as converted Tactical Orders. **MO Tikbalang has no NCO and cannot use these LT Orders.** **Joan herself is not NCO**; she still has Tactical Awareness and may use normal pooled Orders. Inspiring Leadership does **not** turn on yet.
- **If Commander LT dies / becomes Isolated and Joan succeeds via CoC:** Joan becomes the **ordinary** Lieutenant. Her Inspiring Leadership LT effects switch on (assuming their normal conditions), but she **does not inherit** Lieutenant (+1 Order); the NCO plan drops from two LT Orders to one in later turns. CoC is checked at its specified Loss-of-Lieutenant timing: Joan must remain deployed and not Null/Isolated.
- **If Joan CoC dies before the Commander:** the NCO engine can continue while the Commander survives, but the army has **lost its succession protection**. Sending Joan to make a suicidal trade is not free.

**Best NCO partner:** The **Knight of the Holy Sepulchre NCO AP HMG (53pt)** is attractive as a longer-ranged independent attacker, freeing Joan to solve closer 8–24-inch fights; Seraph is the stronger NCO TAG-centered version (Tikbalang is an independent Tactical Awareness gunfighter, **not NCO**), while a Teutonic NCO can extend the Lieutenant Orders into **Fireteam activations if he is its legal Leader**. Do not assume NCO requires being in the Lieutenant's Combat Group: the pinned rule explicitly allows different Groups.

**Why not Joan CoC + Hospitaller HMG Lieutenant as the default?** Although legally an alternative command structure, it **does not supply the two LT Orders** that make Knight Commander LT (+1) so attractive. In an NCO-centered list it sacrifices the principal point of taking CoC Joan. Only compare that Hospitaller pairing as a specific *non-NCO*, SWC-constrained experiment, not the recommended architecture.

##### When to favor each

**Joan LT 45 + Commander CoC:** you want Joan's **Inspiring Leadership active immediately**, the free restricted Coordinated Order, Irregular-to-Regular conversion for eligible units (e.g., Warcor), and an LT Order that can be spent on Joan; you are deliberately protecting your aggressive LT with a backfield successor.

**Commander LT(+1) + Joan CoC + NCO:** you own a primary NCO attacker expected to spend **two** LT Orders almost every turn, want your Lieutenant to remain hidden in a defensive/support Core, and can afford Joan as a second substantial attacker. **This is the strongest conditional argument for Joan CoC**, not passive 45pt backup insurance.

**When neither package is sensible:** points and SWC cannot support a strong NCO **and** Joan while retaining required specialists, ARO defense, hacker protection and enough Orders. Do not buy Joan CoC simply because the architecture looks efficient on paper.

#### Lieutenant (+1 Command Token) — 49 / 1: useful, not mandatory

This is **+4 points over 45pt LT** for **one extra Command Token for the battle**. It is **not** +1 LT Order, not another Tactical Awareness Order, and not automatically one free activation per Turn.

**Buy when** the army is Command Token intensive: changing/reforming multiple Fireteams, Coordinated Orders that *do not* use Joan's special Inspiring Leadership permission, Combat Group management or crucial eligible support re-rolls. That can be worth four points in a complex MO team.

**Usually default to 45** when the army already has a stable Fireteam plan and Joan's Lieutenant-enabled **special Coordinated Order** reduces your Command Token consumption. Importantly, **Inspiring Leadership's Coordinated Order must include Joan, who cannot participate while a Fireteam member**; do not treat the ability as a free group move for a linked Crusade team.

#### Strategic Deployment: +6pts is a Fireteam-wide purchase

Per [the pinned Strategic Deployment rule](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Strategic_Deployment.md), Joan must be **deployed first and as the Team Leader of her Fireteam**. She and its members can then deploy as if they had **Forward Deployment (+4 inches)**. Where a scenario separately grants FD (+4 inches), the rule may grant **FD (+8 inches)** under its stated conditions. **It is not a general solo Forward Deployment skill** to add to an independently deployed Joan.

It is a **deployment-phase positioning tool**, not an extra Order. Its six-point value is larger when it shifts **three or more useful, legal Fireteam members** together, or moves a specialist directly toward a mission-relevant approach. It is weaker when bought only for two bodies, and particularly weak if no legal Fireteam will deploy with Joan.

**Value hierarchy:**

1. **Lieutenant + Strategic Deployment 51/1, planned Crusade Haris/Core — conditional B+/A−:** strongest application. A meaningful forward start for Joan plus a Doctor, TinBot or other eligible assault members may spare a valuable early Fireteam Move, shorten the AP-Spitfire approach and pressure mission midfield.
2. **Lieutenant + Strategic Deployment 51/1, Velox Duo — conditional B:** possible with an eligible **Infirmarer, Bulleteer or motorized Montesa** according to the current Velox chart. Moving only Joan and one partner 4 inches often fails to save a full activation; the point premium is then hard to recoup.
3. **CoC + Strategic Deployment 51/1.5 — B− / mostly skip:** the same six-point premium, but **a forward attacker is also your CoC succession insurance** behind Knight Commander LT(+1). It is possible to plan a forward CoC Crusade team, but that means explicitly accepting the added risk to succession and paying **51pts/1.5SWC** before her support.
4. **Solo Joan with Strategic Deployment — skip:** no Fireteam package to capitalize on the deployment rule; pay 45 for CoC/LT instead.

**Illustrative exact modules (without other list members):**

| Legal Fireteam module | Base | With Joan Strategic Deployment | Assessment |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Velox Duo:** Joan LT + Infirmarer Doctor (17/0) | **62pts / 1SWC** | **68pts / 1SWC** | 6pts advances two bodies; convenient but frequently not essential |
| **Crusade Haris:** Joan LT + actual Hospitaller FTO TinBot (27/0) + Hospitaller FTO Doctor (30/0) | **102pts / 1SWC** | **108pts / 1SWC** | Better payoff: 6pts advances three expensive attack/support models |
| **Crusade Haris:** Joan **CoC** + same two Hospitallers | **102pts / 1.5SWC** | **108pts / 1.5SWC** | Same movement benefit, but more risk to backup command and 0.5 extra SWC |

**Crusade requires one actual Knight Hospitaller FTO**—Joan's “counts as Hospitaller” does *not* waive the native minimum. **Velox is Duo only**, and **v2** alone (not v1) has that eligibility. A **Hospitaller Doctor is not a Velox member** merely because an Infirmarer Doctor is.

**Counterarguments to Strategic:** six points can be a meaningful cheap Order piece or list upgrade; mission terrain may make 4 inches irrelevant; advancing a 100pt team exposes it earlier to mines, hacking, short-range templates and enemy alpha strikes. **Ryan's 2026 MO analysis** favors simply opening routes with a long-range HMG, while **Sneaky Snake's post-update analysis** is more receptive to Strategic Deployment when starting closer helps avoid deployment-zone lockout. Neither treats it as mandatory.

#### Fireteam role and safety

- **Solo v2:** Strong personal Tactical Awareness use and ability, as Lieutenant, to exploit the free restricted Coordinated Order. Still a hackable, expensive attacking LT; avoid pointless open-lane trades. A solo CoC Joan can work as a second-wave AP Spitfire while your LT(+1)/NCO attack happens elsewhere.
- **Crusade Duo/Haris/Core:** counts as Knight Hospitaller; must have an **actual Hospitaller FTO**; another Knight's TinBot/Doctor helps her operate. The Fireteam can gain tactical activation efficiency if Joan is Leader and spends Tactical Awareness, but the full assault group is an expensive and vulnerable concentration.
- **Velox Duo:** v2 can join with an eligible **Infirmarer**, **motorized Montesa**, or **Bulleteer** under the chart. Useful for a flank or paired objective route, but not automatically better than Joan's solo mobility. A Fireteam containing Joan can gain **+2 inches of Coherency Check ZoC** from Inspiring Leadership if all members have Regular Training *printed*, irrespective of whether she is LT.
- **Protected command:** Joan LT + Commander CoC in a separate defensive Military Orders team is more coherent than exposing both to the same attack. Conversely, Commander LT(+1) should stay back while Joan CoC and the NCO tackle different roles.

#### Why v2 is usually preferred to v1

**V1 LT 47pts/+1 SWC** gives ARM5, PH15, MOV 4–4 and a **MULTI Rifle with BS Attack (SR−1)**. **V2 LT 45pts/1SWC** gives ARM3, PH14, MOV 6–2, Terrain (Total), **AP Spitfire B4**, and Velox access. Both offer Inspiring Leadership, Tactical Awareness, Number 2 and an ordinary Lieutenant Order.

At 300 points (standard base **6 SWC**), **v1 LT allows 7 SWC remaining**, while **v2 LT leaves 5 SWC** after paying 1 for its AP Spitfire. That is a **real two-SWC budget swing**, but it only justifies v1 if it enables better other units. V2 is much more persuasive if Joan must **actively spend Orders shooting**. See [detailed Joan v1 analysis](../../MO/units/joan-v1.md).

#### Decision guide

- **Default Joan:** **LT 45/1 — A.** Give her a clear assault mission; add **Knight Commander CoC 23/0** if losing an attacking Lieutenant is an unacceptable risk.
- **LT 49/1 (+1 CT) — A−.** Choose when the extra Command Token has a real purpose; do not misread it as Lieutenant (+1 Order).
- **Joan CoC 45/1.5 — B+, rising to A− in the preferred CoC architecture:** **Knight Commander LT(+1 Order) 24/0 + a strong *separate* NCO + Joan CoC**. This is the main exception to defaulting to Joan Lieutenant.
- **LT Strategic 51/1 — situational B+.** Buy for a planned aggressive **Crusade Fireteam**, not merely to move Joan 4 inches forward.
- **CoC Strategic 51/1.5 — B−, usually skip.** Six additional points actively push forward a model that is simultaneously your command succession insurance; only justify it with an entire team/scenario plan.
- **Skip Joan altogether** when the TAG/NCO + Commander engine and essential specialists already exhaust the points/SWC. MO has enough alternatives that neither version is mandatory.

**Bottom line:** Do not frame CoC Joan as a costly way to guard a random fighting Lieutenant. Its strongest niche is a **24pt Knight Commander LT (+1 Order) + meaningful NCO** army, where Joan supplies a **second AP-Spitfire attacker, Tactical Awareness and CoC insurance**. Strategic Deployment is **an optional six-point team-positioning package**, and the **CoC Strategic** premium is the least compelling purchase of the five v2 options.
