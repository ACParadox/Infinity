### Nøkken — Svalarheima's Winter Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: B tier — situational pick, not a priority.** No Fireteam access or native deployment marker state. The 22/0 Forward Observer is the main reason to take one: a prepositioned independent close-range attacker, specialist and Regular Order. Usually prefer a Locust for protected scoring or forward hacking, or other dedicated WinterFor alternatives. Do not build around AVA4.

#### What is its purpose?

The Nøkken is a forward-deployed gunfighter with optional specialist utility, not a conventional marker-state skirmisher. Its main advantage is active-turn tempo: it begins up to 8" ahead of the deployment zone and can attack from an independent flank without moving an expensive Fireteam. It can deploy in total cover and contribute its Regular Order immediately.

The FO profile is the most compelling because B3 Boarding Shotgun, Mimetism (-6), Panzerfaust, Flash Pulse and WIP13 scoring are compressed into 22 points without SWC. The Panzerfaust gives a limited anti-heavy option rather than requiring the Nøkken to fight armor with a shotgun. The MSV1 option gives better matchups against mimetic enemies but loses the specialist role.

#### Why it is not a priority in WinterFor

- No native Camouflage or Hidden Deployment: the opponent knows its position and can target it directly when exposed. Mimetism is much better when choosing engagements in the active turn than when an opponent chooses the counter.
- One Wound, modest BS12, PH10 and CC13: templates, MSV and melee can defeat it efficiently. Bangbomb improves qualifying Dodge rolls to PH14 before other modifiers, but does not apply against template attacks.
- No Fireteam bonuses or shared activation economy, particularly relevant in a sectorial with two Haris allowances.
- Forward Deployment is not Infiltration: a standard 12" deployment zone still leaves it 4" short of the centerline on a 48" table.
- High AVA does not solve the lack of role differentiation. Additional copies duplicate the same vulnerabilities.

Locust Hidden Deployment is optional; while hidden its Order is not in the shared pool. The Nøkken FO has a genuine advantage in immediately available Orders, B3 shotgun and Panzerfaust, but a Locust can also deploy openly when order economy matters. The Locust's concealed deployment is usually more valuable when a mission depends on keeping a forward specialist alive. Uma's reusable Camouflage is better for sustained marker-state movement.

#### Listbuilding conclusion

Default to zero. Consider one FO if a list specifically needs a cheap forward shooter/scorer with an immediately available pooled Order and zero SWC. A second may be justified for distinct flanks or objective redundancy, but diversify rather than exploiting AVA4. The other profiles generally compete poorly with dedicated WinterFor alternatives.

**Consistency note:** the overview recognizes the FO/KHD as high raw toolbox value; this is compatible with lower sectorial selection priority because of competition. The older references document mentions Nøkken Camouflage and Deployable Repeaters, which are not supported by the pinned roster. Do not use those claims for listbuilding; the KHD's potential Cybermask program is distinct from native Camouflage. This unit note does not change the broader rankings or references.
