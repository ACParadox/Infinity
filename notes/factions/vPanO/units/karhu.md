## Vanilla selection and use

**Best NCO outlet for double Fusilier command.** All Karhu profiles have NCO, including Engineer25 and Paramedic24. A 20/0 Fusilier LT + identical decoy and Feuerbach34 + Engineer25 FT2 Duo cost 79/1.5; four pooled Regular Orders plus one NCO activation. The analogous Magistrate19 package is 78/1.5, three pooled + one NCO activation, saving a troop slot but losing one recurring Order. Both Karhu can lead and spend the same LT resource (only once); choose the Feuerbach for attacks, Engineer for movement/repair/scoring. Paramedic Duo58/1.5 and MULTI Rifle Duo56/1.5 also work. Prefer the double Fusilier command if committing to a Karhu Duo; Joan v2 can instead use her LT Order personally or share it with NCO when useful. Tik LT(+1) gives two LT Orders, not one per NCO.

**Verdict: S+ value / must-have, alongside Bolts.** Feuerbach (+1B)34/1.5 combines MSV1, Mim-3, Climbing Plus, NCO and B3 active AP+DA (B3+1SD in FT2; B1+1SD reactive). Excellent long-lane anti-armour, defensive threat and vertical geometry; W1 and lower retained Burst than HMG remain limitations. Prefer Feuerbach + Engineer59/1.5 FT2 Duo or Feuerbach + Paramedic58/1.5 FT2 Duo. Both follow similar climbing routes; Engineer repairs eligible STR assets, Paramedic treats Wounds, both contribute NCO access. Engineer has Blitzen/D-Charges, not AP Mines. NCO models compete for actual available LT Orders; they do not each generate one. Cheap MULTI Rifle profiles remain useful if a long gun is already present. Prefer Karhu/Bolt compact matching Duos to the more expensive Nisse Haris by default; choose Nisse specifically for MSV2/HMG volume.

### Karhu Special Group — Svalarheima's Winter Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: MUST-HAVE / S-tier value.** One of WinterFor's best all-rounders, with very few drawbacks for its price. Take at least one by default; the Feuerbach (+1B) is the premier gun profile and the Engineer is an excellent complementary specialist. The Nisse and Vargar can be better for deliberately selecting particular matchups, but Karhu is the outstanding general-purpose choice.

#### Why Karhu is a must-have

- **Shooting:** BS13 with MSV1 and Mimetism (-3) gives favourable modifiers into ordinary and Mim(-3) opponents; MSV1 reduces Mim(-6) to -3. Unlike a conventional HI gunfighter, Karhu combines offensive and defensive MODs with low cost.
- **NCO:** makes the protected 10-point Fusilier Lieutenant's otherwise unused Special Lieutenant Order available to the attacker. When Karhu is Team Leader, the converted Tactical Order activates its entire Fireteam. Multiple NCOs share the available Lieutenant Order(s); they do not generate extra ones.
- **Movement:** 6-inch first Move, S2 and Climbing Plus open elevated and unusual attack angles; Move + BS Attack on vertical surfaces is possible, but no Partial Cover applies while on a vertical surface. Fireteam partners without equivalent movement can constrain the route.
- **Mine placement:** the Feuerbach and other AP Mine profiles can Move 6 inches + Place Deployable in one Order, placing a mine in Silhouette contact with any legal point along the route on a supported nonvertical surface. This adds persistent board control to an attack run or retreat; the mine is active after Order conclusion, not during placement. AP Mines threaten armoured enemies as well as light infantry. The Engineer does not have AP Mines.
- **Non-hackable MI:** can advance through repeater networks without being vulnerable to the ordinary anti-HI/REM/TAG hacking attacks such as Oblivion and Carbonite. Spotlight and other applicable attacks remain possible; non-hackable is not blanket immunity to hacking or E/M.
- **Flexibility:** useful as a solo attacker, offensive or reactive Fireteam member, mobile specialist, repair escort or secondary gun. No requirement to commit to a particular Fireteam architecture.

#### Feuerbach (+1B) — 34/1.5

Excellent anti-armour gun and credible long-range ARO piece. Its +3 range band is 16–32 inches, with 0 MOD at 32–48 and 48–96 inches in the pinned chart. Both modes use PS6 and are Anti-materiel.

| Mode | Active Burst with +1B | Active FT2+ dice | ARO FT2+ dice | Ammunition |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Burst | 3 | 4, keep 3 | 2, keep 1 | AP+DA: two saves against halved ARM per hit |
| Blast | 2 | 3, keep 2 | 2, keep 1 | EXP: three ARM saves per hit |

The weapon's +1B applies in the Active Turn; do not carry it over to ordinary ARO. FT2 grants +1SD in both turns, not extra actual Burst. Blast mode in ARO is particularly punishing against an enemy that loses the Face-to-Face Roll. AP+DA can instead be preferable against high ARM. Use the active gun to clear lanes, advance or seed mines, then use the same piece for selective reactive coverage. Avoid exposing a valuable single-wound attacker to the opponent's strongest dedicated ARO-clearing gun unnecessarily.

#### Specialist profiles

- **Engineer 25/0:** outstanding all-purpose specialist with MULTI Rifle, long-range Blitzen E/M, D-Charges and Deactivator. WIP12 is modest, but NCO, MSV1, Mimetism and Climbing Plus are unusually good for an Engineer. Repairs Jotum or combat REMs, including the Pilot-X-supported Bulleteer/Sierra and RacerBots, when positioned to do so. A strong alternative to a dedicated Machinist; can accompany the attacker instead of relying only on a backline support piece.
- **Paramedic 24/0:** comparable combat utility, retains AP Mines, can score and attempt medical recovery of wounded infantry. Particularly good with the one-wound Feuerbach; MediKit success is not guaranteed and depends on the target's PH.
- **E/Marat 22/0:** large direct E/M template gives a close-range threat to valuable enemies, but lacks specialist scoring and is less reliable as a general pick.
- **Basic Blitzen 22/0:** useful if points are extremely tight, otherwise the specialist premium is small.

#### Fireteams

Karhu is not a general Wildcard. WinterFor allows one Core, two Harises, unlimited legal Duos.

| Family | Types | Karhu limit / restriction |
|---|---|---|
| Fusiliers | Core, Haris | Max 1; at least one native Fusilier |
| WinterFor Alpha | Core, Haris, Duo | Max 1; ORC WinterFor or Varg required-choice anchor |
| WinterFor Beta | Haris, Duo | Max 3; Karhu is a required-choice anchor |
| Bulleteers | Duo | Max 1; at least one native Bulleteer |

Preferred packages:

| Package | Pts / SWC | Level | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Karhu Feuerbach + Karhu Engineer | 59 / 1.5 | FT2 Beta Duo | Compact long-range attack/ARO, specialist, +1SD, shared climbing and NCO |
| Karhu Feuerbach + Karhu Paramedic | 58 / 1.5 | FT2 Beta Duo | Alternative medical recovery and more mine coverage |
| Karhu Feuerbach + Karhu Engineer + Gunnar FTO | 89 / 1.5 | FT3 Beta Haris | +1SD, +3 Discover, +1 Dodge; CC and specialist backup, Gunnar Tactical Awareness and Karhu NCO |
| Bulleteer Spitfire + Karhu Engineer | 47 / 1 | FT1 Bulleteers Duo | Pilot-X RemDriver offensive REM plus mobile engineer and NCO; shared activation but no +1SD |

Gunnar FTO counts as Karhu and shares Climbing Plus and Mimetism; three Regular Orders plus Gunnar's Tactical Awareness and access to the Lieutenant Order make the 89-point Haris efficient when all roles are useful. The 59-point Duo is often sufficient and does not consume a Haris slot. A mixed Fusilier or Alpha team may still gain composition bonuses from other members, but Karhu does not count as Fusilier or WinterFor Alpha.

#### Alternatives and matchup selection

- **Nisse HMG 31/1.5:** MSV2, Mimetism (-3), BS13, ARM3, W1. Better when deliberately hunting Mim(-6) and shooting through ordinary Smoke; B4 HMG versus B3 AP+DA Feuerbach. Less versatile movement and order economy, but a natural complementary gun. Nisse MSR 29/1.5 is a dedicated long-lane ARO alternative.
- **Vargar:** BS13, Albedo (-6), Super-Jump, ARM3/BTS3, Immunity (Shock); FO HRL 22/1.5 or FO Disco Baller 24/0 offer specialist utility, targeted anti-MSV matchups and projected Eclipse. Excellent when choosing fights against MSV or avoiding unfavourable sightlines, whereas Karhu is the stronger general-purpose conventional gunfighter. Albedo is conditional, not universal Mimetism.
- **ORC WinterFor Feuerbach 37/1.5:** BS14, ARM4, W2 and Climbing Plus; better at absorbing hits but loses Karhu's shooting MODs, MSV1, NCO, AP Mines and active Burst bonus, while becoming hackable. Prefer Karhu by default.
- **Bulleteer Spitfire 22/1:** cheaper Mim(-6) attack axis, BS13 with Pilot-X; excellent complementary choice, but hackable and lacks Karhu's anti-armour Feuerbach, MSV and specialist versatility.
- **Jotum 82/1.5:** vastly more durable and threatening in some matchups; Karhu offers more distributed capabilities for far fewer points and remains useful alongside a TAG.

#### Limits and final selection

One wound, ARM2, vulnerability to direct templates/CC, modest WIP12 and lack of marker state are real limitations. MSV1 is not MSV2 and enemy MSV can counter the Karhu's Mimetism; avoid unfavourable engagements rather than treating it as an apex tank. These drawbacks are minor relative to the price and combination of roles.

**Default:** at least one Karhu, commonly Feuerbach plus Engineer or Paramedic. The Feuerbach is not merely a primary attack piece: it also provides meaningful ARO, NCO activation, AP Mine deployment while moving, and freedom from ordinary anti-HI hacking. Even lists built around other shooters should seriously consider the specialist Karhu. Choose Nisse/Vargar when their specific vision or anti-MSV matchup advantage is the priority, not as a general reason to omit Karhu.
