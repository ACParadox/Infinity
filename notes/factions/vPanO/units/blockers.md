### Blockers — Neoterran Capitaline Army (N5.3)

**Near auto-include: Pitcher Specialist 15/0. Strongly consider AVA 2 as two Pitchers when running Deva HD + KHD. Hacker 19/0.5 is situational.** Prioritize at least one Pitcher in most NCA lists with useful hacking coverage. With dedicated backline hackers, the second Pitcher is usually more valuable than the Blocker HD. Do not build a five-member Core solely to give the Hacker Sixth Sense.

**Sources:** [Pinned NCA roster and Fireteam chart](../../../../sectorials/panoceania/neoterran-capitaline-army.md#blockers), [NCA Fireteams](../../NCA/nca-fireteams.md), [NCA listbuilding](../../NCA/nca-listbuilding.md), [network projection rankings](../../../list-building/network-projection-repeaters-pitchers-apex-value.md). Ratings are tactical judgments.

#### Pitcher — why it is exceptional

BS12 + X-Visor + native BS Attack (+1B) on a 15-point, zero-SWC Regular Specialist is exceptional network projection. The roster's wider role rankings identify it as a leading points-value Pitcher. The N5.2-updated Pitcher range bands remain applicable in N5.3.

| Direct Pitcher range | Normal range MOD | With X-Visor | Solo BS | FT4+ BS |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0–16 in | 0 | 0 | 12 | 13 |
| >16–24 in | -3 | 0 | 12 | 13 |
| >24–48 in | -6 | -3 | 9 | 10 |

Native B2 in the Active Turn if both Disposable (2) uses remain. FT2 adds a Special Die (roll an extra die, discard one), not a third deployed Repeater or extra ammunition consumption. A single B2 declaration expends both uses; consider using B1 when preserving a second shot matters. The FT Special Die does not apply to Long Skills such as Speculative Attack.

- **Sensor / Discover reroll / Biometric Visor:** anti-marker and anti-Impersonation utility; Sensor is a separate skill with its own rules.
- **Adhesive Launcher Rifle:** nonlethal immobilization pressure, particularly against armor-heavy targets; increased Burst and X-Visor support ranged control.
- **Pulzar / MULTI Pistol:** close-range template and lethal ammunition alternatives.
- **Flash Pulse / PARA CCW (-6):** inexpensive reactive deterrence.
- **Specialist Operative:** push objectives after establishing network coverage.

Weaknesses: one wound, ARM1, no conventional lethal medium-range rifle, only two Pitcher uses. Clear threatening AROs with a proper gunfighter; do not treat the Blocker as a primary attack piece. It can often shoot from the DZ, but forward movement opens safer angles, deeper coverage and mission opportunities.

Rules: [Pitcher](https://infinitythewiki.com/Pitcher), [X-Visor](https://infinitythewiki.com/X-Visor), [Fireteam Bonuses](https://infinitythewiki.com/Fireteam_Bonuses).

#### Two Pitchers — preferred with dedicated Deva hackers

The [Deva HD and KHD](../../NCA/units/deva.md) can both operate independently from total cover in the DZ through friendly Repeaters. They do not need to accompany the Blockers. A standard HD is the priority to exploit the network against HI/TAG/REM and Spotlight targets; the KHD adds Trinity against enemy Hackers.

| Package | Pts / SWC | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Deva HD 25 + Pitcher 15 | 40 / 0.5 | Minimum preferred hacking package |
| Deva HD 25 + Deva KHD 20 + Pitcher 15 | 60 / 0.5 | Dedicated cyber package |
| Deva HD 25 + Bolt KHD 22 + Pitcher 15 | 62 / 0.5 | Alternative with a linked combat specialist |
| **Deva HD 25 + Deva KHD 20 + two Pitchers 15 each** | **75 / 0.5** | **Preferred strong network package** |

The last package costs 25% of a 300-point army, supplies four Regular Orders, four specialists, two Sensors and four total Pitcher ammunition uses. The second Blocker allows opposite-flank coverage, replacement of destroyed Repeaters, redundancy if one Blocker dies, and independent attack axes. Both retain Adhesive Launcher, Pulzar, Sensor and scoring utility after expending ammunition. Four uses are not four guaranteed deployed Repeaters.

With both Devas, prefer two Pitcher profiles over one Pitcher plus one Blocker HD unless a specific Firewall or Fireteam requirement justifies the latter. Existing mobile/deployable Repeaters and mission requirements can still make one Pitcher sufficient.

**Preferred deployment:** split the Pitchers between a CSU Specialist Duo (26/0, FT2) and the main offensive Core/Haris, or field two separate CSU Specialist + Blocker Pitcher Duos (52/0 total, each FT2). This preserves Core/Haris slots in the latter case, gives each Pitcher +1 SD, and permits separate attack axes. A solo Blocker is also viable. Do not cluster both unnecessarily.

#### Hacker — useful, not mandatory

The four-point premium over the Pitcher buys an HD with personal Firewall (-3), but loses the Pitcher. The Hacker wants to stay protected if its main job is reactive network defense, leaving much of its expensive shooting/anti-marker toolbox unused. It can instead accompany an offensive team if its scoring, Sensor and close defense will matter.

| Standard HD | Pts / SWC | WIP / BTS | Reason to select |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Fusilier | 15 / 0.5 | 12 / 0 | Bare-bones cheap backfield HD; native Fusilier anchor |
| Blocker | 19 / 0.5 | 13 / 3 | Personal Firewall, toolbox, Fusilier composition |
| Bolt | 24 / 0.5 | 12 / 6 | E/M Mine, Marksmanship, Bolt composition |
| Deva | 25 / 0.5 | 15 / 3 | WIP15, NWI, Immunity (Isolated) |
| Locust | 29 / 0.5 | 13 / 3 | Hidden Deployment, FD+20, Pitcher and personal Firewall |
| Hexa | 30 / 0.5 | 13 / 3 | Hidden Deployment, Camouflage, Counterintelligence |

**Firewall distinction:** the Blocker HD Firewall applies to Comms Attacks against the Blocker itself while its HD remains enabled. It imposes -3 WIP on the attacker and +3 to the Blocker's Saving Rolls. It does not protect teammates. An ORC BSG TinBot 27/0 or MULTI Rifle TinBot 29/0 grants its Firewall to the Fireteam while eligible. Firewalls do not stack. If the team already has such a TinBot, the Blocker's own Firewall is less of a differentiator (but useful if separated). If a cheaper HD, higher-WIP Deva or same-family Bolt fits the list better, skip the Blocker Hacker.

Pair the network with a KHD such as Deva 20/0 or Bolt 22/0 if enemy Hackers need direct Trinity pressure. A KHD alone is not a substitute for standard HD programs against enemy HI/TAGs/REMs.

Rules: [Firewall](https://infinitythewiki.com/Firewall), [TinBot](https://infinitythewiki.com/TinBot), [Hacking Area](https://infinitythewiki.com/Hacking_Area).

#### Fireteams — compact and purpose-built

- Fusiliers: Core/Haris only; at least one native Fusilier; Blockers count as Fusiliers, max 2.
- Neoterra: Core/Haris/Duo; native Bolt or ORC required-choice; Blockers max 2, **do not count as Bolts or ORCs**. Blade FTO counts as Bolt, as do applicable Quinn/Uma/Hannibal and Machinist NCA.
- CSU: Duo only; at least one native CSU; Blocker counts as CSU.

For the preferred 63-point team, Bolt and Blade count together: FT2 for all three, three Regular Orders plus one Tactical Awareness Order usable when Blade is the legal Fireteam Leader. The Bolt clears lanes, Blade adds repair/scoring and efficient movement, and the Blocker establishes coverage. The same roles can be distributed into smaller teams if the mission requires.

##### Should the two Blockers stay together?

Not necessarily. The Pitcher often wants to move forward; a defensive HD wants to stay hidden and can operate through friendly Repeaters from anywhere. There is no need to maintain proximity to the Pitcher. Both in an offensive link is reasonable when the Hacker will actually use its toolbox, or both in a static link if safe DZ Pitcher angles suffice. Otherwise split the two, or simply omit the Blocker HD in favor of a different Hacker. A solo Pitcher is entirely viable because B2 and X-Visor are native; FT2 is valuable, not essential.

##### Sixth Sense and FT5: desirable bonus, not a listbuilding goal

Sixth Sense cancels Stealth, allowing otherwise legal reactive hacking through the network against Stealth movement. This is useful for a defensive HD, especially with broad network coverage and against Stealth-heavy opponents. It does not improve WIP or hacking Burst and it disappears when the matching composition drops below FT5.

NCA can build an economical FT5 Fusilier Core (e.g. Fusilier ML 15/1.5 + both Blockers 34/0.5 + CSU Specialist 11 + CSU Breaker Rifle 10 = 70/2). This is a legal option if all five serve the overall plan, but do not buy the extra members purely for Sixth Sense. Large teams are harder to hide and maneuver and can prevent the Pitcher from pursuing its natural forward role. Blockers do not advance Bolt composition, so a five-body mixed Neoterra Core is not automatically FT5. Favor compact useful teams, TacAware and independent attack/scoring pieces over maximizing Fireteam level by default.

Rules: [Sixth Sense](https://infinitythewiki.com/Sixth_Sense), [Fireteam Bonuses](https://infinitythewiki.com/Fireteam_Bonuses).

#### Listbuilding takeaway

1. Start with one Pitcher if a standard HD or KHD network is relevant.
2. Prefer a compact Neoterra team with Bolt + Blade FTO/Engineer + Pitcher, or a cheap CSU Duo; solo is fine.
3. Select the Hacker independently based on WIP, personal protection, cost and Fireteam role. Take the Blocker HD when its Firewall or multifunctional support is worth the premium, not merely because it pairs with the Pitcher.
4. Strongly consider a second Pitcher instead of the HD when Deva HD + KHD or equivalent dedicated hackers cover the network. Split their attack axes where useful. Native mobile Repeaters (Pathfinder, Bulleteer, Peacemaker, Fugazi) can reduce the need for redundancy.
5. Keep FT5 Sixth Sense as an optional bonus if the rest of the team is already worth fielding. Do not force five bodies.
