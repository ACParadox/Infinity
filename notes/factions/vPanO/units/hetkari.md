### Hetkari Shooters — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A+ overall.** MULTI Red Fury **A+ (default solo assault piece)**; Red Fury Lieutenant **A**; Missile Launcher **B+ (durable explosive ARO niche)**; Missile Launcher Lieutenant **C−**. The Hetkari is an exceptional conventional midrange gunfighter that can transition into powerful Suppressive Fire. The Crux Knight is a better all-purpose specialist and often a better listbuilding purchase, but is not a direct substitute for pure gunfighting.

Reviewed 21 November 2026. Ratings are tactical judgments, not measured win rates.

Both weapon packages can join KESTREL Duo/Haris, maximum one Hetkari per team. Hetkari does **not** have the Kestrel Counts-as identity, despite being a native required-choice anchor.

#### Why the shooting platform is exceptional

- **Marksmanship:** ignores the negative BS MOD from Partial Cover/Nanoscreen, not the opponent's Saving Roll bonus. Does not negate Mimetism.
- **BS Attack (-3):** imposes a -3 MOD on an opponent's face-to-face roll when the Hetkari declares BS Attack; Warhorse ignores this MOD. Unlike Marksmanship, this also makes the opponent less likely to win.
- **Combat Instinct:** ignores Surprise Attack MODs; Stealth does not suppress otherwise legal AROs against Hetkari. Does not grant MSV, 360° LoF or unrestricted out-of-LoF shooting.
- **ARM5/BTS6/W2 and PH14:** tolerates some failed exchanges, dodges well and has a favorable PH for MediKit recovery. Not immune to E/M, Isolation, hacking or failed Guts Rolls.

Against a conventional BS13 enemy in cover and mutual +3 range, with Hetkari also in cover, Hetkari shoots on 17 and enemy on 10, absent other MODs. This is a six-point relative improvement over a conventional BS14 shooter without these skills (14 versus 13). Mimetism (-6), poor range, Albedo and White Noise can still cause serious problems; Marksmanship users are affected by anti-visor technology. BS Attack (-3) does not protect against unopposed attacks.

#### MULTI Red Fury — default

B4 AP or Shock, PS7, plus B1 DA Anti-materiel mode. Suppressive Fire available in AP/Shock modes. E/Marat is PS7 E/M, two BTS/2 saves, large Direct Template, Non-Lethal. Its ability to threaten Isolation/IMM-B as applicable is valuable against heavy troops and clustered teams, including trades that bypass ordinary shooting MODs. It does not kill directly in this mode.

| Range (inches) | MULTI Red Fury | AP SMG | Missile Launcher |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0–8 | 0 | +3 | -3 |
| 8–16 | +3 | 0 | 0 |
| 16–24 | +3 | -3 | 0 |
| 24–32 | -3 | -6 | +3 |
| 32–40 | -3 | — | +3 |
| 40–48 | -6 | — | -3 |
| 48–96 | — | — | -3 |

The Red Fury has a better 32–40in band and more ammunition options than a conventional Spitfire, at the cost of one point of stopping power (PS7 versus PS6). It is not an HMG replacement on every table: move into 8–24in rather than taking repeated suboptimal long shots.

##### Solo — preferred

The native shooting modifiers already provide exceptional performance without buying a Fireteam. Independent movement lets Hetkari move through narrow routes, attack from different angles, and finish in a useful defensive position without dragging fragile support models along.

**Suppressive Fire is a major reason to prefer solo.** After the attack run, choose a defensible position and enter Suppressive Fire using the MULTI Red Fury. Within 24in an enemy shooting back against a covered Hetkari can suffer -9 total (cover -3, BS Attack -3, Suppressive Fire -3), before other MODs and exceptions. B3 reactive, PS7, with the selected AP or Shock mode retained; Marksmanship remains active. Suppression range is 0–16in at 0, 16–24in at -3. Do not claim that the weapon retains its normal +3 range in this State.

Suppressive Fire is a Long Skill, not a Short Skill. Entering it removes Hetkari from a Fireteam. A failed Guts Roll, hacking states, entering Engaged, choosing other AROs or attacks from beyond the suppression range can defeat the position. Keep a separate mine/template/repeater screen, protect flanks and provide retreat options. Avoid exposing the HI to unopposed E/M or hacking in pursuit of a perfect shooting lane.

##### Haris — A−, expensive but legitimate

One Core, one Haris and unlimited Duos. KESTREL has Duo/Haris, not Core. Hetkari needs two Kestrel-equivalent supports to achieve FT2 in a Haris; Fennecs cannot join KESTREL. FT2 adds one Special Die, not one potential hit. A three-body Hetkari Haris can reach FT2 but not FT3.

| KESTREL Haris | Pts / SWC | FT | Use |
|---|---:|---|---|
| RF + Quinn Disco26 + Bipandra TA25 | 99/1.5 | 2 | Preferred forward package: FO/Eclipse, Doctor, independent Tactical Order and close defense |
| RF + Firefly Engineer23 + Quinn Disco26 | 97/1.5 | 2 | Technical recovery, NCO, FO/Eclipse and close defense |
| RF + Firefly Engineer23 + Bipandra TA25 | 96/1.5 | 2 | Doctor and Engineer, TA and NCO; no Disco |
| RF + Firefly Minelayer24 + Quinn Disco26 | 98/1.5 | 2 | Deployable protection and Eclipse |
| RF + Firefly Repeater19 + Bipandra TA25 | 92/1.5 | 2 | Economical option with repeater projection and medical support |

- **Quinn Disco + Bipandra TA:** best when mission scoring, Eclipse and sustaining the HI are actual needs. Quinn has FO, MSV1 and Boarding Pistol (+1B); Bipandra can recover the Unconscious Hetkari and generate a separate restricted Tactical Order. This does not let Hetkari fire its own gun on Bipandra's Tactical Order.
- **Firefly Engineer:** preferable when enemy hacking/E/M is a major concern; can recover appropriate states rather than Wounds. NCO must lead to use the LT resource for a team activation. Hetkari cannot shoot as team leader on Firefly's NCO Order.
- **Firefly Minelayer:** predeploys one eligible mine or turret, not both.

At 92–99 points, these teams consume around one-third of a 300pt army and the sole Haris. Only take one when the supports will advance and contribute. Do not buy two expensive bodies purely for +1SD. A cheaper FENNECS scoring team or COLCORP order engine may be more valuable. A solo Hetkari can benefit from independently positioned support and cheap Fennec Disco without linking. Avoid recommending Hetkari Duos as ordinary builds, consistent with the current roster's recommendation filter; the historical Hetkari + Pathfinder Duo is evidence of a played structure, not a current recommendation.

#### Missile Launcher — real niche, not default

47/1.5, B1 PS6. EXP circular Impact Template or AP+EXP single-target hit mode; both threaten three Saving Rolls per ordinary hit. +3 at 24–40in. The B3 AP SMG covers close approaches and provides a usable active-turn and Suppressive Fire alternative.

| ARO | Pts / SWC | Reactive gun | ARM/W | Main differentiator |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Hetkari ML | 47/1.5 | B1 EXP / AP+EXP | 5/2 | Marksmanship, BS Attack (-3), impact template, durability |
| Black A.I.R. Neuro Sniper | 33/1.5 | B2 MULTI Sniper | 2/1 | MSV2, Mimetism (-3), cheaper conventional ARO |
| Griffin Feuerbach | 38/1.5 | B1 EXP | 4/2 | MSV1, stronger active/reactive flexibility |
| Blink HRL | 28/1.5 | B1 HRL | See unit guide | Hidden Deployment, Mimetism (-6), ambush |

Take the ML when a meaningful long firing lane constrains movement, expensive HI/TAGs or clustered teams are expected, and another model can handle repeated active-turn engagements. The ML's single successful hit can punish a clustered Fireteam, but the lower Burst provides fewer chances to survive an enemy's high-Burst attack. ARM5/W2 makes it harder to remove, not invulnerable. Its modifiers also matter against an attacker that ignores ordinary Cover; Combat Instinct frustrates surprise-based removal.

- **Black A.I.R. Sniper + Fennec Minelayer, 47/1.5:** same investment with two Regular Orders, prelaid mine and better anti-Mimetism coverage. Often preferable in a general-purpose list.
- **Griffin Feuerbach:** better when the same slot needs to shoot meaningfully in both turns.
- **Blink HRL:** cheaper hidden reactive threat, useful for layered defense rather than a visible roadblock.

Prefer solo ML with independently placed close screens. A premium defensive Haris is possible:

| KESTREL Haris | Pts / SWC | FT |
|---|---:|---:|
| ML + Firefly Minelayer + Bipandra Doctor | 94/1.5 | 2 |
| ML + Firefly Minelayer + Quinn Disco | 97/1.5 | 2 |
| ML + Griffin MULTI Rifle + Firefly Minelayer | 102/1.5 | 2 |

B1 +1SD improves odds of winning without increasing potential hits. The points and Haris opportunity cost are normally excessive for a static position. Use meaningful lanes, cover and overlapping defense instead of exposing the missile to every enemy gun.

#### Hetkari versus Crux Knight

| Attribute | Hetkari RF | Crux FTO MMR | Crux FTO Spitfire |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pts / SWC | 48/1.5 | **46/0** | 49/1.5 |
| BS / PH / ARM / BTS / W | 14/14/5/6/2 | Same | Same |
| Active weapon | B4 AP/Shock, B1 DA | B3 AP/Shock, B1 DA | B4 Normal |
| Native shooting | Marksmanship, BS Attack (-3) | +1SD, Albedo | +1SD, Albedo |
| CC | 17 | 22, MA2, Berserk | 22, MA2, Berserk |
| WIP / specialist | 12, no | 13, Specialist Operative | 13, Specialist Operative |
| Utility | Combat Instinct, E/Marat | Stealth, Drop Bears, D-Charges, DA CCW PS5 | Same |
| Team access | KESTREL native, no matching label | FTO Wildcard | FTO Wildcard |

In the same ordinary BS13 versus BS14 example above, solo Crux shoots on 14 with native +1SD against enemy on 13. Hetkari shoots on 17 versus 10. Against an MSV or Marksmanship opponent, Crux's Albedo changes the calculation; against Warhorse, Hetkari loses its enemy BS Attack MOD. Crux can be substantially better than this conventional shooting example in the correct matchup.

Crux FTO's native +1SD stacks with FT2's +1SD. MULTI MMR becomes B3 +2SD, Spitfire B4 +2SD; discard two dice rather than increasing the number of potential hits. Cheap Fennec support is a decisive listbuilding advantage.

| Offensive team | Pts / SWC | FT |
|---|---:|---:|
| Crux MMR + Fennec FO/Disco + Paramedic | 71/0 | 2 |
| Crux Spitfire + Fennec FO/Disco + Paramedic | 74/1.5 | 2 |
| Crux MMR + Fennec FO/Disco + Bipandra TA | 84/0 | 2 |
| Hetkari RF + Firefly Engineer + Quinn Disco | 97/1.5 | 2 |

Crux teams may use FENNECS Core or Haris; Hetkari uses KESTREL Haris. The Crux MMR saves 26 points and 1.5 SWC in the first versus last package, though support contents differ.

Choose **Crux** when:
- A durable objective specialist, Classified coverage, demolition or high-end CC is important.
- Albedo counters the expected MSV/Marksmanship roadblock.
- The army already has a strong pure attacker, needs cheaper linked support, or has limited SWC.
- Forward Deployment or Hidden Deployment changes the attack geometry. The 47/0 Hidden Deployment AP SMG/CrazyKoala Crux is a separate ambush role, not a substitute for the Hetkari Red Fury. Only Crux's FTO profiles may link.

Choose **Hetkari** when the army needs sustained conventional active-turn shooting, a self-contained assault piece that can end in Suppressive Fire, or an aggressive solo LT. No need to pay for the Crux's CC and mission tools if those jobs are already covered.

#### Other alternatives and list fit

- **[Griffin HMG](../../kestrel/units/griffin.md), 37/1.5:** default economical long-range attacker, MSV1, Pulzar, matching identity for cheap Fennec Core or KESTREL Duo. Better at 24–32in; choose Hetkari for superior conventional midrange exchanges, AP/Shock and suppression control.
- **[Agent Maximus](../../kestrel/units/maximus-agent.md):** excellent linked anti-visor gunfighter and specialist utility when its special orders and anti-visor tools matter more than Hetkari's universal conventional shooting.
- **[Black A.I.R. K1 FO](../../kestrel/units/black-air.md), 29/0.5:** useful inexpensive anti-ARM, Mimetism/MSV2, specialist and non-HI alternative; not a sustained B4 heavy assault piece.
- **[Squalos](../../kestrel/units/squalos.md) / [Tikbalang](../../kestrel/units/Tikbalang.md):** TAGs bring heavier weapons and different reach, but Hetkari's S2 silhouette can go Prone, exploit narrow routes and avoid Total Control, though ordinary HI hacking remains dangerous.

One Hetkari is normally enough. Two consume 95–96 points and 3 SWC; only consider when the two distinct weapon roles genuinely fit the remaining army. A primary shooter plus a different backup threat generally offers more coverage.

#### Lieutenant

Red Fury Lieutenant costs exactly the same as regular RF. Best solo, where the extra Lieutenant Order can directly fund a productive attack or personal repositioning. In a Fireteam, spending the personal LT Order without NCO does not activate the link and removes the Lieutenant from membership; a separate NCO can spend the LT resource as permitted by the rules, but it does not generate another Order.

**Hetkari RF LT48 + Magistrate CoC21 + Jackal FTO6 = 75/1.5.** Keep the Magistrate's COLCORP Duo physically separate, protecting command succession and providing inexpensive DZ defense; the Jackal is Regular only under FT Master conditions at Order Count. See [Magistrate](../../kestrel/units/magistrate.md) and [Jackals](../../kestrel/units/jackals.md). This does not make the exposed Lieutenant immune to Loss of Lieutenant risks, and the cheaper Strategos Magistrate LT is a better default when distributing Orders is more valuable.

Avoid the ML Lieutenant in most armies: a static ARO exposes the command model while providing fewer attractive uses for its personal Order.

#### Final recommendation

Default to **solo MULTI Red Fury**; consider its Lieutenant variant with a protected command plan. Build the army with a separate scoring and technical/hacking network. Use a Haris only if the accompanying Doctor/Engineer/Disco tools have a genuine common route and purpose. The Missile Launcher is a credible durable explosive ARO for suitable terrain and target mix, but not a default over the cheaper Black A.I.R. Sniper or flexible Griffin Feuerbach. Prefer Crux when objectives, CC, Albedo, deployment tools and SWC efficiency matter more than maximizing pure midrange shooting.
