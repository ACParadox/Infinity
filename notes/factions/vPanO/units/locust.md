### Locust, Clandestine Action Team — Neoterran Capitaline Army (N5.3)

**Generally skip (C+).** The KHD/Pitcher (27/0) is the best practical reason to take a Locust, but prefer a Deva KHD + Blocker Pitcher (35/0) for only eight additional points. Use the Locust when genuinely short of points or constrained by the 15-trooper limit, or when its independent forward deployment and concealed attack axis serve a specific plan. Collection priority: optional, low.

#### Why the basic package is not compelling

- Forward Deployment (+8") is not Infiltration: on a standard 48" table with 12" deployment zones, it starts up to 4" short of the centerline.
- Hidden Deployment is optional. While hidden, its Regular Order is withheld from the shared pool; it may spend that Order itself to reveal in the active turn. Deploy openly in total cover when the shared Order is more valuable than concealment.
- Hidden Deployment cannot be regained. Most profiles lack native Camouflage, and the 31-point HD only has Camouflage (1 Use), not a repeatable Camo loop. The KHD can use Cybermask subject to its rules, but this is not reusable Camouflage.
- Surprise Attack (-3) is useful when revealing directly into a legal active-turn attack. Revealing to place a Pitcher first normally forfeits this benefit for a subsequent hacking Order unless a qualifying marker state is regained.
- Mimetism (-6) provides strong protection against ordinary shooting but does not protect against hacking or direct templates. ARM1, one wound and no Shock Immunity make an exposed Locust fragile.
- Bangbomb improves eligible Dodge rolls but not against template attacks. BS12 is modest for sustained solo gunfighting.

#### KHD + Pitcher — 27/0: budget role compression

The strongest Locust profile combines a specialist, a Pitcher and an offensive KHD in one slot. Its personal Firewall (-3) penalizes enemy Comms Attacks and adds +3 to relevant Saving Rolls. It can exploit an existing friendly Repeater to reveal directly into a Surprise Attack with Trinity against a legal enemy Hacker. Alternatively, use its advanced position to establish a second network axis, then hack through it with the Locust or a separate HD.

However, the preferred alternative remains **Deva KHD + Blocker Pitcher**.

**Eight points is a very small premium** for the extra Regular Order, extra specialist, WIP15, superior Pitcher and additional toolbox. The Deva can remain in total cover while the Blocker advances. Prefer these two troopers unless squeezing points or slots.

##### Pitcher comparison

Direct fire, no other modifiers, at least one successful Repeater placement; the linked Blocker assumes FT2 Special Die and no higher-level BS bonus.

| Range | Locust B1 | Blocker B2 | Blocker B2 +1 SD |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0–16" | 60% | 84% | 93.6% |
| >16–24" | 45% | 84% | 93.6% |
| >24–48" | 30% | 69.8% | 83.4% |

B2 spends both Disposable (2) ammunition uses; the Special Die does not consume another use or create a third Repeater. The Blocker can instead shoot B1 to preserve ammunition. The Locust's forward starting position can shorten a shot by up to 8" compared with a DZ operator, and Mimetism can help against conventional opposing fire where applicable; neither is a direct bonus to the Pitcher roll.

The 67-point package is an interesting compromise when unable to afford or fit the full 75-point package. The Locust can cover the opposite flank while a Blocker travels with a Fireteam. Nevertheless the **75-point package is preferred when affordable**. A KHD is not a substitute for a standard HD against ordinary hackable HI/TAG/REM.

#### Other profiles

- **HD + Pitcher (29/0.5):** a forward independent hacker that can set up its own network and reveal for a legal hacking ARO. Usually prefer the Deva HD (25/0.5) through an established network or the Blocker HD (19/0.5) when Firewall matters. A 29-point Locust does not necessarily replace all network infrastructure.
- **SMG + WildParrot (21/0):** distinctive E/M disruption. The WildParrot is Disposable (1), placed entirely within ZoC using the relevant deployable rules, and functions as a visible E/M Mine after placement. It forces two BTS/2 saves when triggered; a failed save isolates and also immobilizes applicable heavy targets. Can be placed around corners without LoF to the placement point, subject to placement requirements. No Minelayer or specialist status. Requires an activation rather than furnishing a prepaid screen; useful for a deliberate anti-heavy-unit plan, not a default purchase.
- **Boarding Shotgun Specialist (24/0):** WIP13 scoring, Grenades and Drop Bears; hidden late-game reserve. Prefer Uma's 27/0 forward profile in most cases: reusable Camo, FD +8", BS13, ARM3, Shock Immunity, SMG (+1B), E/M Mine, specialist. Locust retains full concealment until revealed and stronger Mimetism (-6), but lacks sustained marker protection.
- **HD + Camouflage (1 Use) (31/0.5):** can reveal into a marker under eligible declarations, useful for crossing dangerous spaces, but loses the Pitcher and costs more. Generally skip.
- **MMR (27/0):** first-shot ambush potential, but BS12 and one wound make it a poor sustained gunfighter. Bulleteer, Black Friar and Quinn offer stronger or more versatile conventional shooting/Fireteam roles.

#### Listbuilding conclusion

Default to zero Locusts. One KHD is an acceptable emergency points/slot compression choice; the WildParrot is the most distinctive niche alternative. Do not force in a Locust simply because NCA has few advanced-deployment specialists. Prefer the two separate Deva/Blocker pieces for eight extra points, and prioritize those units and Uma over Locust for collection and listbuilding.
