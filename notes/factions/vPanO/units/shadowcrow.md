## Weapons and roles

- **Assault Pistol — A+:** B4 +1SD in active turn, PS6, +3 at 0–8", 0 at 8–16", -6 at 16–24". Five rolled dice, up to four retained hits. BS16 in good range before other modifiers, with Mimetism (-3) against opponents lacking applicable vision counters.
- **E/Mitter — A:** B2 +1SD, PS7, +3 at 16–32", 0 at 8–16" and 32–48", -3 at 0–8". Three rolled dice, up to two retained hits. Each successful hit forces two saves against halved BTS. Isolation and, for eligible heavy targets, Immobilized-B can neutralize valuable pieces without inflicting wounds. Non-Lethal: do not confuse disabling a target with killing it.
- **Drop Bears:** deployable mine coverage to threaten pursuit, protect an objective or constrain enemy movement. Useful after the initial attack.
- **D-Charges:** anti-materiel and eligible Classified objectives; CC17 without dedicated melee skills is not a primary melee plan.

The pistol covers close range and the E/Mitter covers medium-long range; both lack a positive range modifier at 8–16". No direct template weapon or MSV. Avoid prolonged fights with premium entrenched gunfighters and Mimetism (-6) targets.

## Mobility, durability and mission use

- **Mobility — S:** MOV 15-5 (6–2") and Jet Propulsion allow unusual lateral and vertical approaches. Super-Jump is not an automatic safe attack: check LoF, AROs and cover restrictions for each Order.
- **Durability — A:** ARM3, BTS6, Mimetism (-3), NWI and Shock Immunity provide effectively two-wound resilience against ordinary damage. NWI does not make the unit immune to E/M, hacking or other state effects.
- **Specialist — A:** WIP13 Specialist Operative with independent objective access, D-Charges and mine deployment. It is not a Hacker, Engineer or Forward Observer.
- **Deployment flexibility:** Parachutist provides deterministic legal edge entry outside the enemy DZ; Combat Jump (PH=13) gives a 65% unassisted landing chance, including eligible enemy-DZ locations. Failure sends the unit to the friendly DZ edge. Neither entry allows an immediate simultaneous attack; arrival is a Long Skill and may provoke AROs. Prefer safe landing locations. The model's own Order can fund arrival but contributes no pooled Order while off-table.
- **Limitations:** 31-point investment, Hackable HI without Firewall or KHD, no personal Tactical Awareness Order, no marker state and no guaranteed enemy-DZ edge insertion. BTS6 and Stealth mitigate some threats but do not eliminate hacking risk. Deploy normally when immediate pooled Order or mission presence matters more than airborne surprise.

- **Versus Black A.I.R. KHD:** same price, different purpose. Black A.I.R. is better for reliable enemy-DZ assassination, anti-Hacker operations and vision control. Shadowcrow is better for durability, mobility, close-range conventional assault, ranged E/M disruption and objective/area control. Combat Jump is not equivalent to guaranteed Parachutist (DZ). AVA1 Black A.I.R. may instead be allocated to its premium Neurocinetics sniper, making Shadowcrow a useful complementary choice.
- **Versus Banshee KHD:** eight points more buys BS13, Mimetism, NWI, Shock Immunity, improved ARM/BTS, +1SD, Drop Bears and E/Mitter. It sacrifices MSV1, KHD and direct templates. Banshee remains the default when the budget or hacking role dominates.
- **Versus Blade-Ops Parachutist Engineer:** generally prefer Shadowcrow for one point more when a generic assault/scoring role is intended. Blade-Ops retains the distinct Engineer, Tactical Awareness and Decoy/Surprise Attack niche, especially in repair-dependent missions.

## Listbuilding selection

Choose Shadowcrow when the list already has a strong frontal attack piece but needs an independent lateral attack, durable late-game scorer or anti-TAG/HI disruption tool. A Karhu Feuerbach package and/or Swiss Guard HMG can provide the frontal precision; Helots or other inexpensive defenders protect the home position while Shadowcrow threatens an alternate axis.

Do not buy it solely as a second airborne specialist when Banshee KHD or Black A.I.R. already fills the required mission role. In low-order lists, account for the delayed pooled Order from off-table deployment and the Orders needed after arrival.

**Selection priority:** Banshee KHD > Black A.I.R. KHD > Shadowcrow for general airborne value; elevate Shadowcrow when durability, anti-heavy E/M or generic scoring is specifically required. Prefer Shadowcrow over airborne Blade-Ops unless Engineer access or Tactical Awareness is essential.

**Collection recommendation:** one model; AVA1 and a single vanilla option.
