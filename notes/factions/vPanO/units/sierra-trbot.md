### Sierra Dronbot (TR Bot) — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A-tier points value / B-tier standalone reactive gunfighting.** The **18-point / 0 SWC Thunderbolt (+1B)** is the default pick. The **23-point / 1 SWC HMG** is a conditional upgrade, not an automatic use of five spare points and one spare SWC. Sierra is particularly appealing as a Group 2 defensive contributor or as a repairable secondary ARO in a list *already* paying for an Engineer and TAG support.

**Role:** Independent, unlinked, long-range **Order tax / area-denial ARO**, not an elite anti-Mimetism duelist, anti-TAG specialist, or primary active-turn attacker. Its strongest contribution is making an enemy spend movement, positioning, attacks, or follow-up Orders to clear a lane that matters to the mission.

#### Pinned N5.3 profiles

The [pinned Kestrel roster](../../../../sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md#sierra-dronbot) lists **AVA 2**, **Regular**, REM, **MOV 15–10 cm (6–4")**, **BS 11**, **PH 10**, **WIP 13**, **ARM 0**, **BTS 3**, **STR 1**, **S3**; **Remote Presence, Courage, Total Reaction, 360º Visor**, and **PARA CCW (-3)**. **Neither profile can join a Kestrel Fireteam.**

| Profile | Points | SWC | Native/ARO B | Weapon PS | Best use |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **Thunderbolt (+1B)** | **18** | **0** | **3** | 6 | Default, economical standing ARO |
| **HMG** | **23** | **1** | **4** | 5 | Important 16–32" lane where the fourth reactive die matters |

**Rules precision:** Total Reaction allows the **full weapon Burst, including the Thunderbolt's +1B, in ARO**. Thus this is *B3 vs B4*, not B2 vs B4. The HMG's lower PS is marginally more damaging against the same ARM, but **neither is AP**. This is the ordinary HMG, not an AP/MULTI HMG.

| Range | Thunderbolt | HMG |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0–8" | -3 | -3 |
| 8–16" | 0 | 0 |
| 16–24" | +3 | +3 |
| 24–32" | +3 | +3 |
| 32–40" | **0** | **-3** |
| 40–48" | **0** | **-3** |

The Thunderbolt has **better accuracy beyond 32"**, while HMG has the fourth die and lower PS at shared ranges. HMG also has the **Suppressive Fire** trait, but paying an Order to enter Suppressive Fire replaces rather than adds to its native Total Reaction functionality; it is *not* the ordinary reason to upgrade. Both weapons are poor at close engagement distances.

#### The 5-point / 1 SWC HMG decision

**Default: keep Thunderbolt.** The HMG costs **five more points (28% of the 18-point profile)** and one SWC. Its fourth die improves each opposed shooting exchange and its lower PS improves lethality when it lands a hit, especially in the important **16–32"** band. But **BS11, ARM0, no Mimetism, no MSV and hackability do not improve**. It remains vulnerable to elite active guns, favorable range/mimetism stacking, hacking and bypass.

**Buy the HMG if most of the following are true:**
- Sierra holds an **unavoidable or mission-critical 16–32" lane**, forcing repeated relevant FtF rolls rather than merely inviting an enemy to go around.
- You specifically want an **upgraded standing ARO anchor**, not an expendable 18-point speed bump.
- The list has **one spare SWC and five points with no higher-value use**, after buying scoring tools, mobility, command protection and close defense.
- A protected Engineer can plausibly recover Sierra, so keeping it dangerous over multiple enemy turns has value.
- Sierra might occasionally take an active-turn shot in a favorable position, although BS11 still makes it a backup attacker.

**Do not upgrade just because one SWC is unused.** Unspent SWC is not itself a reason to buy a worse-value profile; the opportunity cost is the **five points and one SWC together**. If the five points buy meaningful specialist, defense or repair capability elsewhere, usually leave Sierra at 18/0.

**Examples of competing Kestrel upgrades** (actual availability and profile legality depend on the rest of the list):

| Alternative use of points | Increment | Usual comparison |
|---|---:|---|
| Add a Palbot to an existing Machinist | **+3 / 0 SWC** | Often better if it creates a safer, practical repair route for a TAG or Sierra |
| Fennec basic Combi 10 → FO/Sensor/Disco 13 | **+3 / 0** | Often better: specialist, Sensor and Eclipse projection |
| Fennec basic Combi 10 → Thunderbolt Shock Minelayer 14 | **+4 / 0** | Often better for layered DZ defense; mine begins deployed |
| Firefly 19-point Panzerfaust/Repeater profile → 23-point Engineer | **+4 / 0** | Often better when the army lacks repair coverage, but changes the profile's other equipment |
| Jackal FTO 6/0 → infiltrating E/M GL Shock Minelayer 11/0.5 | **+5 / +0.5** | Strong different-role option; loses FTO/FT Master conversion possibilities and remains Irregular |
| Sierra Thunderbolt 18/0 → Sierra HMG 23/1 | **+5 / +1** | Prefer only for meaningful repeated gunfights on the right lane |

**Ranking:** Thunderbolt **A value**; HMG **B/B+ upgrade value depending on table**, even though the HMG is the better gunfighter on its intended lane. Neither is a compulsory pick.

| Fennec Missile Launcher | **15/1.5** | Impact template and Fireteam support | Sierra takes far less SWC and makes repeated B3 attacks |
| Mulebot Total Reaction Combi | **17/0** | ARM2, Baggage, Repeater and short-lane coverage | Sierra's 16–32" bands and higher damage threat |
| Firefly Heavy Rocket Launcher | **24/1.5** | BS13, Continuous Damage template, KESTREL/ARMORED eligibility | More economical 0 SWC standalone sustained ARO |
| Blink Hidden HRL | **28/1.5** | Hidden timing, Mimetism(-6), decisive surprise coverage | Sierra begins as an overt Regular Order and is repairable |
| Black A.I.R. FTO Neuro MULTI Sniper | **33/1.5** | Better BS, MSV2, Mimetism(-3), sniper reach, legal FENNECS support | Sierra is 15pts cheaper, 0 SWC, unlinked, and can be repaired |
| Hetkari Missile Launcher | **47/1.5** | Durable ARM5/2W, BS14 and heavy template ARO | Much cheaper distributed defense without tying up a premium HI |

**Do not assume two expensive AROs stacked in the same position are stronger.** Separate angles and cover different enemy routes; an active piece that wants to push forward often clashes with the position a static ARO needs.

The **Black A.I.R. Neuro Sniper** is the substantially stronger choice into Mimetism-heavy/smoke-supported attackers. **Jackals, mines, templates, E/M and Panzerfausts** are more cost-effective where the mission asks for a close obstacle or anti-heavy threat rather than repeatable long-range normal ammunition.

#### Existing TAG + Engineer: Sierra becomes more attractive

The most convincing *list-building* reason to take Sierra is **shared support infrastructure**. If a TAG already justifies a Machinist, Firefly Engineer, or well-positioned repair system, an extra 18-point **repairable** ARO can exploit that investment without another SWC purchase.

**Remote Presence** supplies **two levels of Unconscious** for a STR-based trooper. A successful Engineer WIP or GizmoKit PH roll recovers the REM from either level; Command Tokens may reroll failed Engineer WIP rolls on Remote Presence troopers. It **can still be killed outright** by a sufficient further hit, and a downed silhouette may be exposed to an enemy finishing attack. Avoid treating the repair cycle as guaranteed.

| Package | Points/SWC | Assessment |
|---|---:|---|
| Sierra Thunderbolt alone | **18/0** | Efficient disposable ARO |
| Sierra + Machinist 15 | **33/0** | Less efficient if Machinist was bought *solely* for Sierra |
| Sierra + an Engineer already paid for a TAG | **18/0 incremental** | **Very attractive**, if repair positions are practical |
| Two Thunderbolt Sierras + existing Engineer | **36/0 incremental** | Possible layered defense (AVA2), but costs two trooper slots and should cover different lanes |
| Sierra + Machinist 15 + Palbot 3 | **36/0** | Two Regular Orders from Sierra and Machinist; Palbot adds reach, not an Order |

##### Machinist near Sierra; Palbot near TAG?

**Often yes.** A practical layout is:

- Keep the **Machinist hidden in a protected rear position near the Sierra's recovery route**, not beside it where a blast or flank attack could kill both.
- Send a **3-point Palbot** forward along a safe route to support the TAG. The Servant does not generate an Order and is controlled by its Engineer.
- Alternatively, if available and worth the points, use **two Palbots**: one for the stationary REM and one for the distant TAG, while the Engineer remains sheltered.
- Plan **actual Silhouette-contact access** for repair, including terrain, fire lanes and the likelihood of the Unconscious model being finished off.
- An Engineer can repair troopers in **other Combat Groups**; repair is not constrained to the Engineer's own group. But only Orders available to the Engineer's group can activate it.
- If the selected **Firefly Engineer is actively in a Fireteam** with the TAG, do not assume it provides a free long-distance Servant repair network; check Fireteam/Peripheral restrictions and the separate rear repair route.

**Main limitation:** If the Engineer's only safe approach serves the TAG and Sierra is isolated on a rooftop, the alleged repair synergy may be fictional. Positioning is more important than simply listing both profiles.

#### EVO Mulebot / Assisted Fire: timing is the problem

**EVO Mulebot = 14 points / 0.5 SWC.** **Assisted Fire** is a **Long Skill** that costs an Order and gives the chosen REM **Marksmanship** (ignores enemy Partial Cover/Nanoscreen negative BS MODs when declaring BS Attack). The REM must be in the Hacker's Hacking Area when the program starts; once active its range covers the table. Marksmanship also makes the recipient vulnerable to **White Noise, Reflective and Albedo** interactions.

**Crucial turn-order objection:**

- **If Kestrel goes first:** an EVO can buff Sierra before the opponent's first attack turn, but that uses a valuable active Order for a defensive investment, not a scoring/attack activation.
- **If Kestrel goes second:** there is **no opening active-turn setup opportunity**. Sierra must first survive the opponent's full opening turn unbuffed. It may be dead or no longer cover a relevant lane when Assisted Fire becomes available.
- In later turns, Marksmanship can be good on exposed covered targets, but must produce **enough repeat useful ARO exchanges** to repay the EVO's 14pts/0.5SWC plus the setup Order.
- Assisted Fire is **not a free permanent squad-wide buff**. A Hacker sustains only one Supportware at a time, and a target receives only one; cancellation conditions matter.
- **Enhanced Reaction is redundant** on Sierra: native Total Reaction already provides B3/B4 in ARO, better than Enhanced Reaction's B2.
- Fairy Dust/Controlled Jump are potentially useful **army-wide reasons** to own an EVO, not reasons to automatically attach one to Sierra.

| EVO context | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| EVO purchased **only** for Sierra Thunderbolt | **Generally skip** |
| EVO purchased **only** for HMG Sierra | **Usually skip**, even when going first |
| EVO already in list for several REMs / Combat Jump / TAG or other mission support | **Potential bonus**: buff Sierra if circumstances make it better than other uses |
| Going second and expecting a brutal opening alpha strike | **Do not assume Assisted Fire protects Sierra on Turn 1** |

The **EVO is justified by a multi-unit plan**, not by simply making an 18-point Sierra more accurate. If an EVO is already present, buffing a mobile Bulleteer or other REM on a decisive active-turn attack can be more rewarding than buffing a passive Sierra.

#### Group 2 and Order economy

**Sierra is a good Group 2 occupant** because its normal value arrives in the opponent's turn without spending Group 1 attack Orders. Its native **Regular Order** contributes to **Group 2**, even if the model rarely attacks actively.

Good Group 2 examples:

| Group 2 members | Total pts/SWC | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Sierra Thunderbolt | 18/0 | Reactive long-lane guard plus one Regular Order |
| Sierra + Machinist | 33/0 | Defend, then spend Group 2 Orders on local repair if safe |
| Sierra + Machinist + Palbot | 36/0 | Rear ARO and forward repair coverage |
| Sierra + EVO | 32/0.5 | Two Orders in the group; EVO *may* spend one on supportware rather than passing it to an attacker |

**Caveat:** One Sierra isolated in a tiny Group 2 can accumulate an **unspent active Order**, particularly if it is well deployed and there is no repair/specialist action to take. Group 2 efficiency improves when other members can meaningfully use those Orders. Do not place a necessary forward Engineer into Group 2 if that group lacks the Orders needed for recovery movement.

Group choice does not make Sierra stronger in ARO. It primarily protects Group 1's attack economy.

#### Deployment: how to get value out of it

1. **Cover a meaningful mission route**, not the widest empty skyline. Make an attacker spend Orders to remove Sierra before crossing, reaching an objective, or exposing a valuable teammate.
2. **Seek Partial Cover and an accessible repair route**; ARM0 needs the defensive benefit. Avoid leaving the Unconscious model open to an effortless finishing shot.
3. **Use the 360º Visor to reduce flank-entry blind spots**, but never rely on it as protection against poor range, Mimetism, hacking, Smoke/Eclipse or direct templates.
4. **Make enemies choose different answers:** pair Sierra with a mine, Jackal anti-heavy launcher, Flash Pulse or close template covering a separate approach. Avoid presenting multiple ARO guns to the same favorable enemy slice.
5. **Prefer Thunderbolt's 32–48" 0 MOD** when the table really features exceptionally long sight lines; HMG loses accuracy at these distances.
6. **Be willing not to ARO shoot** when preserving the model or avoiding a needless confrontation is more important than an unfavorable exchange (subject to legitimate ARO choices).
7. **Use repair opportunistically**, not automatically: recovering a Sierra is good only when the Engineer/Palbot can reach it without losing more value than the revived gun contributes.

#### When to avoid taking Sierra at all

- **Dense/short-range tables** without credible 16–32" lanes: mines, direct templates, close guards or forward pieces often cover objectives better.
- **Mimetism(-6), smoke/MSV, Eclipse, hacking or template-heavy enemy plans** that consistently negate a BS11 normal gun and expose its weak ARM/BTS. Sierra's 360º Visor does **not** grant MSV.
- **SWC/point pressure:** the list needs another specialist, Chain of Command, command protection, Engineer reach, E/M answer, or attack activation more than a static ARO.
- **Already defensive-heavy lists:** Black A.I.R. Neuro, Hetkari ML, Griffin Feuerbach, Blink and Fennec/Jackal defenses can provide more than enough passive protection.
- **Needing true anti-TAG stopping power:** ordinary N ammunition is not the answer to strong armor; prefer Panzerfaust, E/M, anti-armor hacking, or an appropriate premium gun.
- **Irrelevant or easily bypassed firing lanes:** high reactive Burst cannot justify shooting at nothing.
- **A plan that depends on EVO buffing it before the opponent's first turn when you are going second**: the timeline does not work.

#### Final priority and decision rule

| Scenario | Choice |
|---|---|
| General-purpose cheap reactive defense | **Sierra Thunderbolt 18/0** |
| Already own an Engineer/Palbot for Squalos, Tikbalang or another important REM | **Increase Sierra's priority** |
| Side Combat Group with useful repair/support Orders | **Sierra is a strong Group 2 pick** |
| Spare 1 SWC and exactly 5 more points | **Still default Thunderbolt**; compare every other five-point upgrade first |
| Critical, unavoidable 16–32" lane; no better marginal upgrade | **HMG 23/1 can justify itself** |
| EVO solely to buff one Sierra | **Do not buy by default** |
| EVO already exists for multiple REM/airborne uses | **Assisted Fire is situational upside**, especially after you can actually activate it |
| Need anti-Mimetism elite ARO or anti-heavy ammunition | **Choose a different defensive tool** |

**Short version:** **18/0 Thunderbolt first; 23/1 HMG only with a specific lane and otherwise unused resources.** Engineer/Palbot synergy and Group 2 placement are stronger reasons to select Sierra than spending 5 points/1 SWC on the HMG or buying an EVO just to activate Assisted Fire.
