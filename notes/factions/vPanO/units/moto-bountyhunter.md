# Motorized Bounty Hunters — Vanilla PanOceania (Infinity N5.3)

**Assessment: A+ overall value; S with Joan Lieutenant.** Extremely cheap, fast and expendable close-range trading or control piece. The 8–9pt options are the reason to take the unit; usually skip the Red Fury.

## Vanilla command economy — 24 November 2026

**Joan v2 economy:** The 8/0 Boarding Shotgun + Heavy Riotstopper profile is a useful cheap trading piece that also contributes a converted Regular Order under Inspiring Leadership while eligible. Together with two Helots and Warcor it forms the 31/0 four-Irregular package. Expect to spend or sacrifice the bike in active play, not preserve it solely to generate Orders. This is another reason Joan becomes easier to commit later in the game.

## Profile selection

- **Boarding Shotgun + Heavy Riotstopper (8/0):** excellent disposable control piece. Large direct template with PARA (PH-6), threatening Immobilized-A against valuable targets. The Boarding Shotgun supplies a separate B2 AP close-range attack. Prefer for defensive control or anti-heavy disruption; the Riotstopper does not cause wounds.
- **SMG + Chain-colt (+1B) (9/0):** preferred aggressive trader. B2 direct templates in the Active Turn threaten clustered troops and support pieces; the SMG provides a conventional B3 close-range alternative. The pinned N5 weapon chart lists the ordinary SMG as Normal ammunition, not the AP/Shock modes from older editions.
- **Red Fury (16/0.5):** fast secondary ranged attacker, but BS12, W1, Limited Cover and lack of specialist status make the additional points and SWC difficult to justify in PanO. Usually skip.

## Mobility, durability and Booty

- Up to 14" movement per Move + Move activation; Impetuous provides an optional free activation with mandatory movement priorities and restricted skill combinations if used.
- Unhackable LI: Motorcycle is not Vehicle (VH), and the unit does not have a printed hackable characteristic.
- Limited Cover removes the enemy's -3 BS modifier from Partial Cover but retains its Saving Roll benefit when applicable. Mimetism (-3) and Dodge (+2") provide useful defensive tools.
- Booty (ReRoll) adds equipment to the existing loadout; possible outcomes include Mimetism (-6), +4 ARM, +2 ARM, MSV1, HMG, MULTI Rifle, Panzerfaust, Light Flamethrower and Monofilament CCW. Adapt to the result rather than building a plan around it. The model is placed before rolling; do not assume it can be repositioned afterward.
- No Smoke, marker state, Forward Deployment or specialist role. S4, inability to go Prone or Climb, and other motorcycle movement restrictions limit routing and concealment.

## Joan Lieutenant synergy

Inspiring Leadership makes eligible order-generating troopers Regular while Joan is Lieutenant, on the table and not in a Null State. The Bounty Hunter retains Impetuous, so it can contribute a pooled Regular Order and still use its optional personal Impetuous activation.

| Unit | Pts | SWC | Orders with Joan active as LT |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Motorized Bounty Hunter SMG | 9 | 0 | 1 Regular + optional Impetuous activation |
| Warcor | 3 | 0 | 1 Regular |
| Helot FTO SMG/LRL | 10 | 0 | 1 Regular |
| **Total** | **22** | **0** | **3 Regular + optional Impetuous activation** |

This is an excellent infrastructure package with mobile trading, cheap Flash Pulse disruption and a defensive LRL. The FTO Helot is independent; no Fireteam bonuses. The 12pt one-use Camo LRL is an alternative (24pt total). Joan and any other command support are additional costs. Do not assume regularization continues if Joan is no longer an active Lieutenant.

Without Joan, the Bounty Hunter provides only its personal Irregular Order and optional Impetuous activation, making it a conditional inclusion when the list already has sufficient pooled Regular Orders.

The Bounty Hunter is not a replacement for network projection or forward-deployed board control. It supplies a distinct, inexpensive unhackable threat.

## Practical usage

Deploy on a protected flank with a route toward vulnerable infantry or enemy support. Use the optional Impetuous activation when its movement priorities permit a safe approach; skip it when the route would expose the model to a strong ARO. Preserve the model until a worthwhile trading opportunity arises rather than automatically sacrificing it on turn one.

Avoid conventional duels against dedicated ARO pieces. Use total LoF blocking terrain, favorable range, direct templates or a separate attacker to open routes. Fennec Disco Baller can help with vision blocking, but investing several pooled Orders merely to deliver an 8pt trader defeats much of its efficiency.

The unit can also stay back as a cheap template threat to protect a flank until a later-turn opening. It can contest space but is not a button-pusher.
