32/+1 FTO is an independent specialist; 35/+1 adds forward deployment. No shared activations; buy for SWC gain, personal TA, Nanoscreen and unusual weapons, not link support. Fennec FO13 and Pathfinder TA17 cheaper for ordinary scoring.

## Original kestrel analysis — retained reference copy

The original is retained for transferable mechanics and reasoning. Sectorial ratings, profiles, AVA and linked packages are historical source-context only. The vanilla profile and notes above take precedence. Links retain original destinations.

### Dr. Priya Harper — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: B-/Niche; skip by default, but credible when +1 SWC, guaranteed Tactical Awareness, and Plasma/Viral/E/M coverage are all useful.** Harper is **not** a Doctor despite her title. She is a WIP13 Specialist Operative with a Regular Order, a personal Tactical Awareness Order, Nanoscreen, NWI, X-Visor and Booty. The 32-point FTO can support a Fireteam; the 35-point Forward Deployment (+20 cm / +8") profile is solo only. Do not buy her merely because she is a relatively durable specialist: the 13-point Fennec FO/Disco and 17-point Tactical Awareness Pathfinder are much more efficient for ordinary objective work.

**Bottom-line list-building rule:** When building an attacking team from scratch, prefer one genuine premium gunfighter/specialist (Crux Knight FTO MMR or Agent Maximus FTO) plus cheap Fennecs, rather than paying for **Griffin HMG + Harper** as two overlapping expensive combat bodies. Harper's best niche is an *existing* Griffin team that can use her distinct close-range ammunition and +1 SWC, or an independent second-group specialist that actually exploits both personal Orders. Booty is meaningful upside, not the plan.

#### Pinned N5.3 profiles

**ID 1814 · AVA 1 · LI · Regular · MOV 10–10 cm (4–4") · CC17 · BS12 · PH10 · WIP13 · ARM1 · BTS3 · W1 · S2.**

Shared skills/equipment: **Nanoscreen, X-Visor, Booty, Dodge, Immunity (Shock), Specialist Operative, No Wound Incapacitation (NWI), No Cover and Tactical Awareness.**

| Option | Pts | SWC | Weapons | Fireteam |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| **1: Harper FTO** | **32** | **+1** | Plasma Carbine, Nanopulser, E/M Mine, Viral Pistol (+1B), CCW | **Wildcard max 1** |
| **2: Harper Forward Deployment** | **35** | **+1** | Same package; **Forward Deployment (+20 cm / +8")** | **None** |

**+1 SWC is an army-building credit**, not a spent SWC. It is most valuable in a list that would actually use that capacity. Neither profile has Doctor, Engineer, Forward Observer, Sensor, Minelayer or a built-in long-range heavy anti-armour gun.

#### Real order economy: good, but conditional

- One **Regular Order** enters the Combat Group pool, plus one **personal Tactical Awareness Order** while eligible. A crude 32 / 2 = **16 points per available activation** is reasonable as a *potential* efficiency metric, not equivalent to two pooled Regular Orders.
- When Harper FTO belongs to a legal Fireteam, her Tactical Order **can activate the whole Fireteam with Harper as Fireteam Leader**. Other members can move or perform permitted shared Movement/Reset skills, but **the Griffin cannot lead and shoot on Harper's Tactical Order**.
- She can use that activation to move the team, shoot with her own weapon, place her carried E/M Mine, score an objective (where the mission permits), or reposition. A team that needs to advance already gets shared movement for its pooled Orders, so she can *ride along for free* on a Griffin-led attack.
- The 35-point solo Forward Deployment profile begins closer to the objectives and can spend her personal Tactical Order immediately; this helps only if her independent task/positioning actually matters. A solo model still competes for other Regular Orders if the personal activation is insufficient.
- Her NWI state and opponent attack choices matter: losing Harper loses both the specialist and the Tactical Order.

**Practical distinction:** A linked Harper does not gain two fully assignable Orders for the Griffin's HMG. She supplies one pooled Regular Order and *one Harper-led team activation*. This distinction is central to whether 32 points are justified.

#### Weapons, range bands and X-Visor

X-Visor improves **negative range MODs**: -3 becomes 0, and -6 becomes -3. It does not change positive range bands, Cover, Mimetism, the Speculative Attack penalty, a weapon's maximum range, or non-range modifiers. It also helps ranged Discover.

| Weapon | 0–8" | 8–16" | 16–24" | 24–32" | 32–40" | What it adds |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **Griffin HMG** | -3 | 0 | **+3** | **+3** | -3 | B4/BS14 primary 16–32" shooter |
| **Harper Plasma Carbine + X-Visor** | **+3** | **+3** | **0** | **0** | **-3** | B2 BS12 Plasma, with Hit and circular Blast modes |
| **Harper Viral Pistol (+1B) + X-Visor** | **+3** | **0** | **-3** | — | — | **B3 active** Viral at very close range |
| **Firefly SMG** | **+3** | 0 | -3 | -6 | — | Efficient close anti-infantry, not a long gun |
| **Firefly Panzerfaust** | -3 | 0 | **+3** | **+3** | -3 | B1, Disposable (2), **AP+EXP** for hard targets |
| **Maximus AP Thunderbolt (+2B)** | -3 | 0 | **+3** | **+3** | 0 | B4 active, reliable anti-armour primary weapon |

Harper's most complementary band with a Griffin HMG is **0–16"**. At 16–32", the HMG already covers long engagements more efficiently in ordinary gunfights. The Plasma Carbine contributes **different ammunition and circular-template threat**, not generally higher head-to-head winning odds: Harper is BS12/B2 against a BS14/B4 Griffin. Plasma forces distinct **ARM and BTS Saving Rolls** per hit; its Blast Mode can punish clustered models. Her Viral Pistol is an important close-range alternative against suitable infantry. The Nanopulser adds a direct template but overlaps some of the Griffin's native Pulzar defensive role.

**Anti-tech distinction:** Harper has a carried **E/M Mine**: useful against HI, TAG, REM and VH approaching the team (potential Isolated and Immobilized-B effects), but **not** a ranged reusable E/M attack and **not** pre-deployed without Minelayer. She must spend an Order placing it. **Firefly Engineer** has better general technical support: Panzerfaust to threaten armour directly, AP Mine, D-Charges, NCO and an actual Engineer to recover disabled Griffin states. **Maximus's E/M Carbine** provides repeatable direct anti-tech pressure. Harper's Plasma and Viral weapons are primarily damage/anti-infantry alternatives, not equivalent substitutes for hacking or repair.

#### Survivability: better than the 1W profile suggests, not a heavy-infantry substitute

- **Nanoscreen** imposes **-3** on qualifying incoming BS Attack Rolls and **+3** on Harper's Saving Rolls against BS Attacks, even when not physically in cover. No Cover prevents stacking ordinary terrain Cover on top. Nanoscreen does **not** protect against CC or BS Attacks with the Comms Attack Trait; automatic-hit templates still bypass the to-hit penalty, though applicable Saving Roll protection remains.
- **NWI + Immunity (Shock)** allows her to keep fighting after a Wound under the usual NWI rules. This gives her substantially more staying power than a cheap one-Wound Fennec, but not the genuine ARM5/BTS6/two-Wound toughness of a Crux Knight.
- **Mimetism (-6) from Booty** can compound the Nanoscreen -3 into **-9 against ordinary LoF-dependent BS Attacks** when both apply; counterplay includes visors, direct templates, non-LoF attacks and CC.
- **ARM improvements from Booty** combine with the Nanoscreen Saving Roll modifier where applicable; +4 ARM makes her ARM5 before Nanoscreen's separate +3 BS-Attack Saving Roll bonus.
- She is a **S2 infantry specialist**, easier to hide or send through tight routes than an S3 Pathfinder, but she lacks Camo/Hidden Deployment and has no reliable first-turn surprise mechanism.

#### Booty: ranked specifically for Harper

Booty is rolled **after deploying Harper in Model form**. The outcome is added, not a replacement. You **cannot** guarantee any weapon, stat improvement or deployment adjustment based on the die result. Each face of the D20 is 5%; two-face results are 10%.

| D20 | Result | Chance | Harper-specific assessment |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **19** | Mimetism (-6) | 5% | **Excellent:** stacks with Nanoscreen for unusually hard-to-hit shooting target; biggest defensive spike |
| **18** | +4 ARM (non-TAG) | 5% | **Excellent:** ARM5 plus Nanoscreen BS-Attack Saving Roll protection, better objective staying power |
| **15** | MOV 8–4" | 5% | **Excellent solo:** massive mobility and Tactical Awareness reach; reduced relative value if linked to slower companions and Coherency constraints |
| **20** | HMG (non-TAG) | 5% | **Excellent situational offense:** B4 long-range gun, X-Visor softens negative bands, FT2 adds +1SD; still only BS12 |
| **9** | MSV1 | 5% | **Excellent when needed:** counters Mimetism and complements X-Visor; somewhat redundant if Griffin MSV1 is handling those fights |
| **11** | Adhesive Launcher Rifle | 5% | **Strong:** B2 active PARA/PH-6 control against big targets; X-Visor helps its poorer ranges |
| **12** | +2 ARM (non-TAG) | 5% | **Strong:** ARM3 plus Nanoscreen, a useful all-game durability increase |
| **13** | Panzerfaust | 5% | **Strong:** adds AP+EXP Disposable (2) anti-heavy fire at 16–32"; X-Visor makes negative bands less punishing |
| **16** | MULTI Rifle (non-TAG) | 5% | **Strong:** B3 AP/Shock versatility and Suppressive Fire, with X-Visor range smoothing; Plasma still offers different saves |
| **17** | MULTI Sniper Rifle | 5% | **Strong, board-dependent:** real long-range AP/DA attacks, especially good with FT2 +1SD; may not suit a forward-moving team |
| **1–2** | +1 ARM | 10% | Modest, but always-on and not wasted on an NWI specialist |
| **3–4** | Light Flamethrower | 10% | Situational: Continuous Damage direct template, but Harper already has a Nanopulser |
| **5–6** | Grenades | 10% | Situational: indirect Speculative Attack option, but PH10 and Order cost limit reliability |
| **7–8** | DA CC Weapon | 10% | Weak: CC17, no dedicated CC modifiers; don't turn her into a melee assault piece |
| **10** | EXP CC Weapon | 5% | Niche threat on a weak basic CC profile |
| **14** | Monofilament CC Weapon | 5% | High potential payoff if an opening appears; unreliable as a plan on CC17 |

**Practical weighted view:** Five **excellent** single-face outcomes = **25%**; five additional **strong** single-face outcomes = **25%**; the remaining ten faces (**50%**) are modest, situational or weak. Thus **50% of rolls land in the two most useful editorial categories**, but that is *not* a 50% guarantee of a game-changing result. In particular, MSV1 may overlap Griffin, MOV 8–4" may be bottlenecked by a linked team, and sniper/HMG results may not match the deployment position. Do not pay 32 points assuming one of the best results.

#### FTO versus Forward Deployment (+8")

**32pt FTO — preferred if choosing Harper for an attacking team.** It is 3 points cheaper, has Wildcard access to a legal Core/Haris/Duo, and her Tactical Order can move the whole Fireteam with Harper leading. If a Griffin is already moving down her intended route, Harper's movement comes with those Orders; paying to start her 8" forward would not necessarily save army-wide Orders.

**35pt FD — conditional independent specialist.** It starts up to 8" farther ahead, allowing her personal Tactical Order to be spent near an objective, a protected corner or an E/M Mine position. This is appealing in Group 2 **only when** her independent mission is real. Starting in forward Total Cover matters because she is not Camo/Hidden; do not expose her just to maximize the head start. Its 3-point premium does not permit joining a Fireteam.

**Movement nuance:** Griffin is MOV 6–2", Harper 4–4". On a Move + Move Order their total maximum path lengths can match at 8", but their *first Short Skill* movement differs; Coherency, terrain and firing-point choices can constrain the Griffin's preferred route. Don't automatically assume either a full free 8" head start or a catastrophic linked-speed mismatch.

#### Fireteam legality and real comparisons

Kestrel's **FENNECS** teams are **Core or Haris, not Duo** and require **at least one actual Fennec**. Griffin **Counts as Fennec**, and Harper FTO can join as **Wildcard**, but **Harper does not Count as Fennec or Kestrel**. Consequently, Griffin + Fennec + Harper is a **three-model FT2**, not FT3: Griffin and Fennec provide the two matching units. **FT2 adds +1 Special Die, not +1 Burst**, to eligible BS Attacks.

| Legal team | Points | SWC | FT | Practical distinction |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| **Griffin HMG37 + Fennec FO/Disco13 + Harper FTO32** | **82** | **net 0.5** | **FT2** | Griffin shoots B4 +1SD; Harper Plasma B2 +1SD when leading; TA team activation, E/M Mine, Plasma/Viral, two specialists |
| Griffin HMG37 + Fennec FO/Disco13 + Pathfinder TA17 | **67** | 1.5 | FT2 | **15 points less**, same TA movement option, faster WIP13 FO/Sensor/Repeater/Flash Pulse; much less dangerous in a fight |
| Griffin HMG37 + Fennec FO/Disco13 + Fennec Paramedic12 | **62** | 1.5 | **FT3** | **20 points less**, better Discover/Dodge team level, simple and efficient medical insurance |
| Griffin HMG37 + Fennec FO/Disco13 + Fennec Minelayer14 | **64** | 1.5 | **FT3** | 18 points less; starts with a Shock Mine, cheap strong native link |
| Griffin HMG37 + Fennec FO/Disco13 + Bipandra TA Doctor25 | **75** | 1.5 | **FT3** | **7 points less** than Harper; actual Doctor, TA and counts-as Fennec; no Plasma/+SWC |
| Crux Knight FTO MULTI MMR46 + Disco13 + Paramedic12 | **71** | **0** | FT2 | All-in-one BS14/ARM5/2W attacking specialist; native +1SD stacks with FT2, no Tactical Awareness |
| Agent Maximus FTO45 + Disco13 + Paramedic12 | **70** | **1 non-LT / +1 LT** | FT2 | All-in-one B4 AP Thunderbolt and E/M Carbine specialist with TA; stronger primary shooter |

**Firefly comparison must respect team types:** Firefly **cannot** join the FENNECS Griffin + Fennec Disco team. A **Griffin HMG37 + Firefly Engineer23 KESTREL Duo** is **60pts / 1.5SWC / FT2** (with NCO/Engineer/Panzerfaust but no linked Disco), or add an independent Disco Fennec13 to bring the army allocation to **73 points, three pooled Regular Orders**. Another KESTREL Haris may be possible but spends the single Haris slot.

- **Why take Harper with Griffin?** Her **+1 SWC** relieves a tight gun-heavy list; Plasma/Viral punishes short-range threats and clusters; E/M Mine threatens approaching HI/TAGs; her guaranteed TA provides a useful team movement/Harper attack activation; she can take a second specialist role. Those benefits must matter together.
- **Why usually not?** The same team can take a 13pt Fennec Disco plus 12pt Paramedic and finish **20 points cheaper with FT3**; or gain **the same TA activation plus FO/Sensor/Repeater** using a 17pt Pathfinder. Griffin already consumes most desirable attack Orders, so a BS12/B2 Plasma secondary gun is frequently underused.
- **Firefly Engineer as anti-tech:** generally better at preserving a Hackable Griffin, with repair, Panzerfaust and NCO. Harper has a *mine* as her only guaranteed E/M disruption; she's not replacing an Engineer or a Hacker.

#### Premium alternatives: Crux Knight and Optimate Agent Maximus

| Question | **Harper FTO 32 / +1 SWC** | **Crux FTO MMR 46 / 0** | **Agent Maximus FTO 45 / 1 or +1 LT** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core role | Complementary close-range specialist | **Primary attack specialist** | **Primary AP/anti-tech attack specialist** |
| Best offense | BS12 B2 Plasma; B3 active Viral Pistol at close range | **BS14 B3 MULTI MMR, native +1SD**, strong 8–24" | **BS13 B4 AP Thunderbolt**, strong 16–32", E/M Carbine |
| Survival | Nanoscreen, NWI, Shock Immunity; Booty variance | **ARM5 BTS6 and 2W**, but Hackable | ARM3 BTS3, NWI, CC defenses, **not Hackable** |
| Extra Order | **Tactical Awareness** | None | **Tactical Awareness**; LT choice can add useful NCO order elsewhere |
| Specialist | WIP13 Specialist Operative | WIP13 Specialist Operative | WIP13 Specialist Operative |
| Fireteam | **Wildcard** | **FTO Wildcard** | **FENNECS FTO** only |
| Distinct angle | Plasma blast/Viral and +1 SWC | Superior independent gunfighter, AP and Drop Bears | AP Thunderbolt, reusable E/M Carbine, LT SWC/NCO synergy |

**The main opportunity-cost argument:** When the goal is *one strong attacking specialist with cheap escorts*, the **70–71pt Maximus/Crux + two Fennecs** structures are often better than **82pt Griffin + Harper + Disco**. This consolidates shooting and objective duty in a model that is genuinely good at both, while leaving more points for other threats. Harper can still be valuable as an independent second attack vector, but she is much less valuable if her Plasma never fires.

**Important qualifications:** Crux's best MMR Fireteam option is **46/0** (the 54pt FD MMR cannot join). Maximus's **45pt no-Minimus FTO** is the link-legal Agent; the 48pt synchronized-Minimus profile cannot join under Peripheral restrictions. The **45pt FTO Lieutenant** grants +1 SWC and a Lieutenant Order, but personally spending that LT Order can break the link; an external NCO or linked Sforza NCO can use it without Maximus personally detaching, subject to NCO leadership rules. These are distinct from Harper's +1 SWC with **no Lieutenant exposure**.

#### When to use / skip

**Consider 32pt FTO Harper when:**
- You **need the +1 SWC** to enable other important guns, and do **not** want to buy an aggressive Maximus Lieutenant.
- You already have a Griffin + Fennec Disco attack team, need a strong close-range Plasma/Viral complement, and can use a **Harper-led TA activation** frequently.
- The mission rewards an advancing durable S2 specialist and you want separate roles for the Griffin and Harper.
- You are willing to accept the **20-point premium over Griffin + Disco + Fennec Paramedic**, and the loss of FT3.

**Consider 35pt FD solo when:**
- She has a **different forward objective or flank** than the main fireteam, ideally with useful Group 2 personal-order activity.
- +8" deployment meaningfully saves **her own** early movement Orders, and the +1 SWC matters.
- You want a self-contained near-midfield close combatant/specialist, **not** simply an objective button-pusher.

**Skip Harper when:**
- You only need objectives: take **Fennec FO/Disco13** or **Pathfinder TA17** (same WIP13 and extra Tactical Order for the Pathfinder).
- You want to protect a Griffin from technical disablement: a **23pt Firefly Engineer**, separate as legal, serves that plan better.
- You need a **primary reliable gunfighter**: pay for **Crux Knight** or **Maximus** rather than attaching a pricey BS12/B2 second gun to an existing primary.
- The +1 SWC will remain unused, Booty is your justification, or you lack Orders to capitalize on her short-range fire.

**Final assessment: B-/Niche, not must-have.** Two available activations for 32 points are *respectable*, especially when one can move a Fireteam; Booty has roughly **50% of faces classified here as excellent/strong potential improvements**. Nonetheless, she is neither a top-value objective specialist nor a consistent primary gunfighter. Keep her on the shortlist for **SWC-constrained, short-range-complementary, or independent two-Order builds**; default to cheap Fennec/Pathfinder support, or buy **Crux/Maximus** to consolidate gunfighting and scoring.
