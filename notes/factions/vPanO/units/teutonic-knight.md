## Vanilla selection and use

**Vanilla NCO Spitfire29/1.5: B-tier secondary attacker**, especially if using cheap double Fusilier or Tik LT(+1). Karhu Feuerbach or Squalos/Cutter NCO are generally stronger default outlets; Limited Cover and no Mimetism/MSV constrain solo gunfights. LT18/0 is C-tier versus double Fusilier20 or Magistrate19: visible forward command and no extra LT Orders. Do not import the MO Knight Commander or sectorial links.

AVA2 vs MO3, same six options. 19/0 SMG Specialist/Panzerfaust is a strong cheap standalone two-Wound close specialist, 18/0 LSG/Panzerfaust budget attack, 29/1.5 NCO Spitfire conditional. TinBot19 protects itself rather than a link; Impetuous is useful solo; Religious status matters for Inspiring Leadership.

**Preferred vanilla profile — Option 5, Specialist Operative (19 pts / 0 SWC):** Cheap solo secondary attack piece and specialist. Use optional Impetuous movement to advance when safe, the SMG and DA CCW for close-range pressure, and the Panzerfaust for opportunistic anti-armor shots or AROs. Two Wounds and enhanced Dodge make it a useful independent mission runner and follow-up attacker after the primary gunfighter opens a route. No Fireteam or Lieutenant-order investment required. This profile appeared in the [Borderbash 2025 winning list](https://infinitytheuniverse.com/army/list/ZQpwYW5vY2VhbmlhEVNhaW50IG9mIE1pbWl0aXNtgSwCAQEACQAqAQQAAITGAQMAAITGAQQAAAkBAgAAEwEBAAATAQEAAB4BBQAAhw8BBAAAhwwBAwACAQAGAIXNAQUAAIXNAQMAADIBAQAAhh0BAgAAhwsBBwAAhwsBCQA%3D), alongside Joan v2, Swiss Guard HMG and a Karhu Duo. ### Teutonic Knights — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A for the 19-point TinBot as a cheap Military Orders Fireteam anchor; A for the 29-point NCO Spitfire with a worthwhile Lieutenant-order plan; B+/A− for the 19-point Specialist Operative depending on mission and whether FT equivalence matters; B solo / B− linked for the 18-point basic Panzerfaust; C+ for the Missile Launcher; C for the Lieutenant.** **Teutonics are a budget/activation-economy alternative, not a straight upgrade over Hospitallers.** In an elite-character link, **Hospitaller FTOs are usually stronger** because they retain full cover until Frenzy triggers, have better BS/ARM/BTS and Stealth, and count toward the same Fireteam level as Joan, De Fersen, Hawkins and Knights of Justice. Do **not** force a Teutonic into a link merely because it is inexpensive.

#### 1. Teutonic versus Hospitaller: why use Teutonics in a Fireteam at all?

| Property | Teutonic | Hospitaller | Practical significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cheapest useful linked TinBot | **19** | **27** | **Teutonic saves 8 pts** |
| Cheapest non-TinBot | 18 (Light Shotgun) | 26 (BSG + E/Mitter) | **Teutonic saves 8 pts** |
| Movement | **6–2** | 4–4 | Teutonic gets a better initial advance but shorter second MOV |
| BS / ARM / BTS | 13 / 3 / 3 | **14 / 4 / 6** | Hospitaller is much better gunfighting/hacking insurance |
| Defense | Impetuous + **Limited Cover immediately**; enhanced Dodge | **Full cover until Frenzy is gained**; Stealth | Hospitaller has stronger initial reactive/active protection and can traverse hacking threats stealthily |
| Team family | **Military Orders** | **Crusade** | They solve **different** chart requirements |
| Same-unit level with De Fersen, Justice, Hawkins, Joan | **No** | **Yes** | Hospitaller often buys a higher FT level with fewer bodies |
| Distinct utility | **NCO, native Panzerfaust + TinBot, cheap generic Specialist, optional solo Impetuous activation** | **Doctor, E/Mitter, HMG, MULTI Rifle and better defenses** | Choose on the specific job |

**The central trade:** Pay **8 points more for the Hospitaller TinBot** when the Fireteam contains valuable Hospitaller-equivalent Wildcards. It is normally a more durable and better-armed carrier and also raises the FT level. Conversely, use the **Teutonic TinBot** when the team does not benefit much from Hospitaller equivalence, eight points materially improve the rest of the list, or the *Military Orders* family is required for its members.

##### Equivalence changes the arithmetic

**Example A — De Fersen escort, no other members:**

- **Teutonic TinBot 19 + De Fersen MULTI Rifle Hacker 42 = 61/0.5, Military Orders Duo, FT1.** They are different units for equivalence; De Fersen does *not* become a Teutonic.
- **Hospitaller FTO TinBot 27 + De Fersen 42 = 69/0.5, Crusade Duo, FT2.** Both count as Hospitallers; De Fersen gains the **BS Attack (+1 Success Die)** team benefit. The Hospitaller itself is also a more capable protector.

**Recommendation:** For **just De Fersen + TinBot**, spend the extra 8 points on the Hospitaller whenever possible. This is **not** a compelling reason to field a Teutonic link.

**Example B — De Fersen + Knight of Justice Hacker/Pitcher + TinBot:**

- **Teutonic TinBot 19 + De Fersen 42/0.5 + Justice Hacker 45/0.5 = 106/1, Military Orders Haris, FT2**, because De Fersen and Justice count as Hospitallers.
- **Hospitaller TinBot 27 + the same characters = 114/1, Crusade Haris, FT3.** The +8 pays for a better escort and **+3 Discover/+1 Dodge**, but **both already have FT2's +1SD**.

Here the Teutonic is a **defensible eight-point economy choice**: the actual attack bonus is already online from the two Hospitaller-equivalent characters. The Hospitaller still offers better attributes/cover, so choose based on budget, mission and expected exposure rather than assuming cheaper automatically wins. The Teutonic variant has also appeared as a played tournament Fireteam in [report #160](../../../battle-reports/151-160-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md).

**Critical limit:** A **Hospitaller FTO is mandatory to create a Crusade**. A Wildcard that *counts as* a Hospitaller does not replace that minimum. Similarly, a Military Orders team requires its own qualifying choice (Crosier, Teutonic or Santiago FTO per the source chart).

#### 2. What does a good Teutonic team actually look like?

A Teutonic does not become a superior shooter merely because it joins a Fireteam. Its best use is **economical level-building and supplying a capability that other members need**. Keep these options separate rather than regarding them as mandatory stackable modules.

| Military Orders team | Pts / SWC | FT level | Why take it / why not |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **TinBot 19 + NCO Spitfire 29** Duo | **48 / 1.5** | **FT2** | Compact Firewall + NCO attack; leader can use Commander LT Orders to activate the Duo. Not ideal if long-range primary shooting is already covered |
| **TinBot 19 + NCO 29 + Infirmarer Doctor 17** Haris | **65 / 1.5** | **FT2** | Includes a *real Doctor* and two native Teutonic contributors. Infirmarer does **not** count as a third Teutonic |
| **TinBot 19 + NCO 29 + Curator Engineer 17** Haris | **65 / 1.5** | **FT2** | Same economical assault shell with a *real Engineer* to remove IMM states/repair allied tech |
| **TinBot 19 + Specialist 19 + NCO 29** Haris | **67 / 1.5** | **FT3** | Three matching Teutonics, two Panzerfausts, TinBot, NCO and scoring backup; pays for native level, not Doctor/Engineer/Hacker |
| **TinBot 19 + Specialist 19** Duo | **38 / 0** | **FT2** | Extremely cheap two-Wound/Panzerfaust link and specialist; lacks a dedicated attack gun or medical/engineering support |
| **TinBot 19 + De Fersen 42 + Justice Hacker 45** Haris | **106 / 1** | **FT2** | Tournament-observed protected hacking shell; see Hospitaller comparison above |

In a three-person team containing **two Teutonics and one Infirmarer/Curator**, the team is **FT2, not FT3**. A non-equivalent Wildcard adds abilities and a body, not the next equivalence bonus. Likewise, a **Teutonic + one non-equivalent Wildcard Duo is FT1**, which can be much less attractive than a two-Hospitaller-equivalent Crusade Duo.

**Best practical reason for the NCO Haris:** Put **Knight Commander Lieutenant (+1 Order)** in a *separate backfield support Core*, and let the Teutonic NCO lead the forward Haris. The NCO converts the Commander's **two Special Lieutenant Orders** into activations for itself or its active Fireteam, while the Commander remains protected. This is the most distinctive linked Teutonic role that a normal Hospitaller cannot replicate. The Teutonic's **X-Visor** makes its Spitfire more flexible outside optimal range; it does **not** compensate for no Mimetism, MSV or AP ammunition.

**When not to build this team:** Another NCO (for example, a TAG or Sepulchre) already claims both LT Orders; you need a more reliable long-range primary gun; the mission favors independently deployed midfield specialists; or the link merely duplicates a better Crusade setup. In those cases a cheap **Crosier/Order Sergeant shell** plus independent heavy hitters may offer more value.

#### 3. Impetuous is mainly a *solo* advantage, not an advantage of linking

**Impetuous is optional**: once per own Turn's Impetuous Phase, the Teutonic may activate without spending a pool Order, but the activation has **restricted Skills and mandatory movement priorities**. It is **not a fully flexible extra Regular Order**. For example, it cannot use that activation to operate an objective console; moving in the required direction can expose a 2W but Limited Cover Knight to serious AROs/hacking. **Declining** the token is normal.

**Linked trade-off:** Activating a linked non-Leader Teutonic in the Impetuous Phase **removes it from its team**. Activating the **Fireteam Leader** with Impetuous **cancels the Fireteam**. A Duo that loses one member cancels because only one remains. Therefore an intact Teutonic Duo/Haris usually **forgoes the free Impetuous activations** in exchange for coordinated Orders and FT benefits.

**Haris/Core hybrid:** A non-Leader Teutonic may leave on its Impetuous activation while **at least two other members remain** in the original Fireteam. If the existing team survives and the departing model is once again in Coherency with its Leader, it can **automatically rejoin at the end of the next States Phase**, without a Command Token. It does not receive that team's active-turn bonuses on the turn it left. This is a situational option, **not a default reason to link Impetuous models**.

**“Angry deployment”:** You may instead deploy Teutonics unlinked, use safe Impetuous advances, then spend a **Command Token to create** their planned Fireteam later. Podcast discussions note the tactic but also question whether giving up a Command Token—and possibly early linked AROs—is worth the movement. Use only on a table with good safe approaches.

**Solo roles:** The **18-point basic** is a cheap 2W Panzerfaust threat; the **19-point Specialist** is a slightly more useful roaming objective option, though WIP13 generic Specialist Operative lacks the tools of an Engineer/Doctor/Hacker; the **29-point NCO Spitfire** is a high-tempo independent attacker if there are LT Orders to spend. A solo Teutonic can benefit from its free Impetuous activation without sacrificing any Fireteam.

#### 4. Detailed profile decisions

##### Option 2 — Light Shotgun/Panzerfaust + TinBot: 19/0 — A linked

**Default linked Teutonic.** Pay the extra **one point** over option 1 unless another suitable Firewall already covers the team or the list literally cannot afford it. The TinBot is especially valuable around hackable HI and a **De Fersen or Justice Hacker**. It is a protective utility purchase; a Light Shotgun is not an efficient primary long-range attack weapon. The **Panzerfaust** gives credible ranged anti-armor/ARO threat and means this is not an entirely passive support body.

**Do not take it solely because it is cheaper than a Hospitaller TinBot.** The Hospitaller's 8-point premium also buys BS14, MULTI Rifle, better ARM/BTS/Stealth, stronger initial cover and superior FT equivalence with many high-value characters. Additional TinBots are not a way to stack the same Firewall (−3) benefit.

##### Option 6 — Spitfire, X-Visor, NCO: 29/1.5 — A with LT-order economy

Most interesting **linked or independent attack** profile. NCO converts otherwise awkward Special LT Orders into useful actions, and can **activate its Fireteam as Leader** with that Order. Good at clearing light AROs, reaching corners and providing the attack piece a cheap Teutonic Duo/Haris lacks. Still BS13, Limited Cover and no anti-Mimetism tech. **Don't buy if NCO is redundant** because a TAG, Sepulchre or other centerpiece already uses the Commander’s LT Orders.

**Versus Hospitaller HMG 32/1.5:** Hospitaller is BS14, better protected, better long-range; Teutonic offers NCO, faster initial movement, X-Visor, and solo Impetuous potential. Pick on the *activation plan*, not raw gun quality.

##### Option 5 — SMG/Panzerfaust, Specialist Operative: 19/0 — B+/A− contextually

Good value **when you want a second/third matching Teutonic** to advance FT level or a disposable-ish two-Wound objective runner. As an independent mission piece, the optional Impetuous advance helps cross ground before spending a normal Order to interact. But its **Specialist Operative is generic**, not Doctor/Engineer/Paramedic/Hacker: no MediKit, GizmoKit, D-Charges by implication, or hacking programs. Prefer a **17-point Infirmarer Doctor or Curator Engineer** in a mixed Fireteam if those functions are the list's actual gap, accepting that neither boosts Teutonic equivalence. A separate Trinitarian often scores more reliably through midfield concealment.

##### Option 1 — basic Light Shotgun/Panzerfaust: 18/0 — B solo; B− linked

A credible cheap solo Panzerfaust/warband-style HI with good Dodge. In a link, **usually skip** because option 2 adds useful Firewall for **one point** with identical weapons, stats and team equivalence. A rare second basic Teutonic is justified when you already have a TinBot, need another **matching** body for a higher FT level, and only have 18 points left.

##### Option 3 — Missile Launcher/Zapper: 28/1.5 — C+

The most difficult sell. **Impetuous is nearly irrelevant on a stationary ARO piece** and would disrupt a Fireteam if used. More importantly, **Limited Cover from deployment, no Mimetism, no MSV**, and only BS13 make a **28-point/1.5 SWC long-range defender** a fragile investment when opponents can choose their attack vectors. Its close Zapper is useful insurance, not a substitute for defensive shooting modifiers.

A linked **BS Attack (+1SD)** helps the ML's relevant BS rolls, and post-update discussion identifies **BS Attack (−3)** on the ML, making it somewhat better than the bare pinned row suggests. Still **not a default**: prefer the 18–19pt Panzerfaust Teutonics for economical ARO threat, and allocate serious ranged ARO spending to more suitable platforms. Consider the ML only for a deliberate layered ARO/anti-clumping plan and with the current Army profile confirmed.

##### Option 7 — Lieutenant: 18/0 — C

**Normally skip.** The **Knight Commander Lieutenant (+1 Order) 24/0** offers a safer backfield Lieutenant, Holomask, Crosier-equivalent support level and an additional LT Order for NCO. The Teutonic LT is a visible **Impetuous, Limited Cover front-line Knight**; exposing it wastes the safety expected from the Lieutenant. Only use it in a tight-point plan with no NCO economy requirement and acceptable Loss-of-Lieutenant risk. Do not conflate cheapness with good command reliability.

#### 6. Practical listbuilding rules

1. **Default linked Teutonic is 19pt TinBot, never automatically 18pt basic.** If there is already a Firewall provider, reconsider before paying for duplicate TinBot coverage.
2. **Don't mistake cheaper for better Fireteam equivalence.** In a **De Fersen/Joan/Hawkins/Justice** team, start by pricing a **Hospitaller FTO**. It often produces the same attack bonus with fewer members or a higher FT level.
3. **Pay for NCO only with a productive Commander LT(+1) or other credible Special-LT-order plan.** An unused 29pt Spitfire is merely a mediocrely protected BS13 gun.
4. **Use real specialists when the role matters.** Infirmarer Doctor, Curator Engineer, Order Sergeant Hacker/Pitcher or an independent Trinitarian may solve the mission better than a Teutonic Specialist. However, recognize that non-Teutonics **don't raise Teutonic FT level**.
5. **Use independent Teutonics when you actually intend to spend Impetuous activations.** Linked troops usually surrender that benefit; a Haris non-Leader can sometimes leave/rejoin, while a Duo generally cannot.
6. **Skip ML and Lieutenant in ordinary lists.** Both have narrow use cases and strong alternatives within MO.
7. **Don't fill AVA 3 out of habit.** Typical use is **one TinBot anchor**, sometimes with **one NCO**. Add a third matching Teutonic **only if the FT-level increase is worth more than the alternative utility trooper**.

**Bottom line:** The **Teutonic's point in an MO Fireteam is to buy the correct team functionality for dramatically less**—a **19pt native, two-Wound TinBot/Panzerfaust carrier**, potentially with a **29pt NCO leader using the Knight Commander's two LT Orders**. If your desired team is mostly expensive Hospitaller-equivalent Wildcards and does not use NCO, the **Hospitaller generally wins** despite the extra 8 points. This is not a unit that needs to be linked—or included—in every MO list.
