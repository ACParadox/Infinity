## Vanilla selection and use

**Revised command priority:** S Strategos LT19/0, S standalone CoC21/0. Vanilla has no Jackals; the Kestrel linked Order engine does not transfer. With a strong dedicated NCO (especially Karhu Feuerbach/Engineer Duo), prefer Fusilier LT10 + identical decoy10: two pooled Regular Orders plus one LT Order for NCO vs Magistrate's one pooled + one LT Order. The Magistrate saves one trooper slot and one point, offers Counterintelligence, extra reserve flexibility and WIP13 Specialist Operative, and can convert the LT Order to a Regular Order when no NCO activation is wanted. Conversion and NCO use are mutually exclusive. AVA1 prevents using both Magistrate command profiles. User preference: Combi CoC21 is the default independent insurance for exposed Joan/Tik/Maximus; Bolt CoC26 in a defensive FT2 sniper Duo (52/1.5) is the other preferred option; five points over standalone sniper + Magistrate buys +1SD, close defense and Warhorse at the cost of Counterintelligence and specialist. MMR CoC25 is situational; Firefly CoC is niche, not a default alternative. Keep passive CoC out of danger.

19/0 Strategos LT or 21/0 CoC are key command options. AVA1 means cannot buy both Magistrate versions; no Jackals on vanilla roster, FT Master not a live engine. Strategos conversion and NCO spending same LT Order are alternatives. CoC for Joan/Tik LT, Strategos for independent Swiss/TAG.

### BCA Magistrate — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A+/S-tier command-and-order package, not an automatic include.** The **19pt Strategos L1 Lieutenant** and **21pt Chain of Command (CoC)** Combi profiles are the primary selections. The **17pt basic Magistrate is a credible A-tier budget choice when paired with a 6pt Jackal FTO**, even if the list intentionally omits CoC. A **COLCORP Duo** is the normal way to use the Magistrate without consuming Kestrel's sole Haris; the second Jackal is efficient if that Haris slot is genuinely free. The **MULTI Marksman Rifle (MMR) costs +4pts** and is worthwhile only when the Magistrate will meaningfully contest 8–24" lanes or objectives.

**Why it matters:** FT Master (Jackals), Counterintelligence, WIP13 Specialist Operative, inexpensive CoC or Strategos, and cheap template/launcher-equipped Jackal escorts compress command, order economy, scoring and deployment-zone defense into a small package. The linked Jackal's Regular Order is **conditional**, unlike a Fennec's independent Regular Order.

**Authoritative references:** [Kestrel's pinned N5.3 roster, profiles and Fireteam chart](../../../../sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md#bca-magistrates), [Kestrel Fireteam guide](../../kestrel/kestrel-fireteams.md), [Kestrel listbuilding guide](../../kestrel/kestrel-listbuilding.md), [cheap-Order comparison](../../../list-building/cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md). #### All six profiles

**Shared chassis:** AVA **2**, Regular LI; MOV 10–10 cm (4–4"), CC15, BS12, PH10, **WIP13**, ARM1, BTS3, W1, S2. All have **Biometric Visor, Counterintelligence, FT Master (Jackals), Immunity (Shock), Specialist Operative**, Nanopulser, Heavy Pistol, Stun Pistol (+1B), and CCW.

| Opt | Points | SWC | Main weapon | Extra | Recommendation |
|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | **17** | 0 | Combi Rifle | — | **A in a Jackal Duo**: budget command/order engine if no CoC or LT role needed |
| 2 | **21** | 0 | MULTI Marksman Rifle | — | **Niche/skip**: +4 weapon but no command upgrade |
| 3 | **21** | 0 | Combi Rifle | **Chain of Command** | **Default non-LT**: protected successor, economical Jackal support |
| 4 | **25** | 0 | MULTI Marksman Rifle | **Chain of Command** | Situational: CoC specialist actively covering midrange |
| 5 | **19** | 0 | Combi Rifle | **Lieutenant, Strategos L1** | **Default LT**: excellent cheap command and Order engine |
| 6 | **23** | 0 | MULTI Marksman Rifle | **Lieutenant, Strategos L1** | Situational: defensible firing lane, but usually don't risk LT |

**The upgrade economics:** Basic → CoC is **+4pts** and basic → Strategos Lieutenant is **+2pts**, with no SWC or loss of other abilities. Thus, when either command skill is useful, the basic profile is hard to justify. However, **do not call the 17pt basic profile a universal skip**: Magistrate17 + Jackal6 is a distinct, efficient package.

**CoC and specialist status:** The CoC Magistrate is a specialist, but all Magistrates are **already** Specialist Operatives. Chain of Command itself also counts as a Specialist skill for applicable ITS objectives unless marked **(Non Specialist)**; Kestrel's **23pt Firefly CoC** is therefore also a WIP13 Specialist, not a non-specialist.

#### Fireteam rules that determine value

- **COLCORP** permits **Duo or Haris**, requires at least **one Magistrate**, allows **1–2 Magistrates and up to 2 Jackal FTO**, and can add eligible Wildcards. Magistrate and Jackal FTO **count as ColCorp**, so Magistrate + Jackal is **FT2**, and Magistrate + two Jackals is **FT3**.
- Only the **6pt FTO Jackals** (SMG/Panzerfaust or Light Rocket Launcher) can join COLCORP. The 10/11pt *Infiltrating* Jackals **cannot** enter this link or benefit from its FT Master.
- A legal **COLCORP Duo** does **not** occupy the only Haris slot. A three-member Haris **does**. Kestrel has **one Core, one Haris, unlimited Duos**; FENNECS has **Core/Haris, not Duo**.
- **FT Master (Jackals)** converts eligible linked Jackals' Irregular Orders into **one pooled Regular Order per Jackal at Order Count**, provided the Master and the relevant Fireteam meet the conditions then. It does **not** grant an additional Order on top of their Irregular Order. Losing the Master/team affects **future** Order Counts, not the already-built pool retroactively.
- **FT2 = +1 Special Die**, not +1 Burst. It assists eligible shooting rolls (including a launcher/hit-mode shot), **not automatic Direct Template attacks**. FT3 improves Discover and Dodge; it is **not** the +1 BS bonus.
- A Magistrate also joins **FENNECS Core/Haris** (max 1), but in that formation **Jackals cannot accompany it**; this sacrifices the cheap FT Master Jackal conversion and its specific COLCORP value. An actual Fennec is mandatory in a FENNECS team.

Rules references: [FT Master](https://infinitythewiki.com/FT_Master), [Fireteam rules](https://infinitythewiki.com/Fireteams:_Basic_Rules), [bonuses](https://infinitythewiki.com/Fireteam_Bonuses), [canonical Kestrel chart](../../../../sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md#fireteams).

#### The Jackal: 6pts is more than an Order

Two COLCORP-eligible Jackals, both **6pts / 0 SWC**, with **Booty**, ARM0, BTS0, W1:

| Jackal FTO | Weapons | Best deployment-zone role |
|---|---|---|
| **Light Rocket Launcher** | LRL; **Boarding Pistol** | Reusable ranged/template lane threat plus very-close Direct Template deterrent; default if expecting clustered/airborne entry |
| **SMG + Panzerfaust** | SMG, Panzerfaust; **Boarding Pistol** | Short-range shooting and a disposable hard-target ARO; preferable against heavy units |

**Boarding Pistol:** both have its **Small Teardrop Direct Template mode**. This makes a 6pt Jackal a realistic *trade-up threat* in close defense: it can place an automatic template hit in an ARO against a more expensive troop, even if that Jackal loses its life in the exchange. It still requires the target to fail a normal damage save; it **does not reliably kill multiwound HI/TAGs**, and an opponent can Dodge. The LRL's longer-range area denial and the Boarding Pistol's close template are complementary. The Magistrate's **Nanopulser** supplies a second close-range direct template.

**Positioning:** keep the Magistrate relatively concealed; expose the Jackal only where the threat costs the opponent Orders or forces unfavorable decisions. Losing a six-point Jackal is acceptable; losing your CoC/LT + FT Master at once is much worse. The Duo members must remain coherent, so they cannot defend opposite corners independently.

#### The essential COLCORP packages

All values **0 SWC**. Pooled Orders assume the indicated link is intact **at Order Count**, all troopers are on-table/eligible, and (when indicated) Strategos converts the Lieutenant order.

| Composition | Points | Pooled Regular Orders | Formation | Assessment |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| **Basic Magistrate17 + Jackal6** | **23** | **2** | Duo FT2 | **A-tier budget**: Counterintelligence, specialist, defense; no CoC |
| **LT Magistrate19 + Jackal6** | **25** | **3** | Duo FT2 | **Top budget LT**: Strategos converts the LT order; Haris remains free |
| **CoC Magistrate21 + Jackal6** | **27** | **2** | Duo FT2 | **Default CoC module** with two Orders and defensive cover |
| Basic Magistrate17 + 2 Jackals6 | **29** | **3** | Haris FT3 | Very efficient cheerleading if Haris can be spared |
| LT Magistrate19 + 2 Jackals6 | **31** | **4** | Haris FT3 | Excellent command/Order engine; consumes Haris |
| CoC Magistrate21 + 2 Jackals6 | **33** | **3** | Haris FT3 | Excellent CoC/Order engine; consumes Haris |

**The first Jackal is usually worth its six points once a Magistrate is in the list.** It creates another pooled Regular Order and brings a template plus LRL or Panzerfaust. The **second** Jackal is also efficient *in points*, but has the large hidden cost of the **only Haris slot**. Therefore, default to **one Jackal Duo** and upgrade to a Haris only when no better attacking/scoring Haris is planned.

**Strategos/NCO resource contention:** The LT Magistrate's **Strategos L1** may convert its Lieutenant Special Order into a pooled Regular Order at Order Count, and provides its reserve-deployment benefit. If you convert the LT Order, a **Firefly/Sforza/Squalos NCO cannot also spend that same Special Order**. Choose conversion versus NCO access; never count it twice.

#### Is the basic 17pt Magistrate good without CoC?

**Yes, conditionally.** Magistrate17 + LRL Jackal6 = **23pts/0 SWC** for two pooled Regular Orders, WIP13 Specialist Operative, Counterintelligence, Biometric Visor, Nanopulser, Boarding Pistol trade threat, LRL ARO and FT2. This is valuable when:

- You **intentionally skip CoC** and another troop is LT;
- The list wants cheap Orders **plus** a backfield emergency scorer and anti-DZ-intrusion deterrent;
- You have a separate plan for Core/Haris and want to **reserve Haris**;
- The Magistrate can remain protected throughout Order Count.

**Not an automatic buy:** 23pts buys two bodies but only two **conditional** Orders, while independently Regular cheerleaders can be spread across the DZ and cannot lose Order conversion merely because a Fireteam breaks.

#### Closest competitors: two Fennecs, not only cheap REMs

These pairs have **two independent pooled Regular Orders**, can deploy separately, and may join a larger **FENNECS Core/Haris** later. **Two Fennecs alone do not form a legal FENNECS Duo**.

| Package | Cost | Independent Orders | Actual value |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Magistrate basic17 + LRL Jackal6 Duo** | **23 / 0** | **No: 2 conditional** | Counterintelligence, WIP13 specialist, two close templates, LRL, FT2 |
| **Fennec Paramedic12 + Fennec Thunderbolt Minelayer14** | **26 / 0** | **2** | Recovery, a **predeployed Shock Mine**, Thunderbolt (+1B), Triangulated Fire |
| **Fennec FO/Sensor/Disco13 + Fennec Thunderbolt Minelayer14** | **27 / 0** | **2** | **Sensor, Eclipse via Disco Baller, Flash Pulse**, mine, ranged ARO, specialist |
| **Magistrate CoC21 + LRL Jackal6 Duo** | **27 / 0** | **No: 2 conditional** | As 23pt package, **plus CoC**; same price as FO + Minelayer pair |
| **Fugazi7 + basic Mulebot8** | **15 / 0** | **2** | Cheapest independent REM Order/support mix; no comparable armed/specialist package |

**Deployment-zone defense trade:** The **Fennec Minelayer's Shock Mine is placed at deployment** and the Fennecs can cover unrelated lanes. The **Magistrate/Jackal** protects a more compact pocket with disposable template trades and the distinctive Counterintelligence/FT2/FT Master economy. Fennec FO/Disco + Minelayer is usually the **better general-purpose utility pair** if CoC is unwanted and four extra points are affordable. Prefer the **Paramedic** variant when specific recovery plans outweigh Sensor/Eclipse.

**Don't confuse the 14pt Fennec with a basic cheerleader:** its Thunderbolt (+1B), Triangulated Fire and Minelayer make it a significant defensive body for zero SWC. A Fennec FO/Disco + Minelayer is also useful as the support half of a **Griffin + two Fennecs Core**, which can achieve FT3. Conversely, COLCORP stays a separate Duo and does not burden that Core.

#### Firefly CoC comparison: the opportunity-cost argument

| Two-model comparison | Cost | Regular Orders | Key trade |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Magistrate CoC21 + Jackal6 Duo** | **27/0** | 2, conditional | Counterintelligence + FT2, defensive templates, WIP13 Specialist |
| **Firefly CoC23 + basic Fennec10** (independent) | **33/0** | 2, native | 6pts more; independent Orders, **NCO**, better fighting, WIP13 CoC Specialist |

A Firefly CoC is not bad: BS13, ARM2/BTS6, **Albedo, NCO, BS Attack (SR-1)**, SMG, Panzerfaust, AP Mine, KESTREL/ARMORED access. It is just **competing against excellent non-CoC Firefly profiles that keep NCO**:

| Firefly | Cost | Why its unique role often matters more |
|---|---:|---|
| SMG/Panzerfaust + Deployable Repeater | **19/0** | Cheapest NCO and network projection |
| **CoC** | **23/0** | Independent CoC backup and WIP13 Specialist |
| **Engineer** | **23/0** | GizmoKit/Deactivator, repair support and NCO at same price |
| **Minelayer with Armed Turret (MMR)** | **24/0** | Predeployed **one mine or turret**, layered defense and NCO |
| **Hacker** | **24/0.5** | Cyber capability, Specialist and NCO |
| Paramedic | **25/0** | Recovery and NCO |
| HRL | **24/1.5** | Long-range template fire + NCO |

**Preferred division of labor when slots/points permit:** Magistrate CoC + one Jackal for protected command and cheap Orders; **Firefly Engineer or Minelayer** in the combat-oriented team, still using the aggressive LT's Special Order through **NCO**. If taking another 27pt support module pushes out an important gunfighter or specialist, **Firefly CoC** compresses roles into one trooper instead and can be more efficient.

#### Is the MMR worth +4pts?

**Usually no on a hidden LT or passive CoC/FT Master.** The Combi Rifle is better inside 8", and the Magistrate already has its Nanopulser for a no-roll close template. The **MULTI Marksman Rifle** is significantly better for **8–24" engagement**, especially **16–24"** where its good range is six effective BS target points better than the Combi's corresponding poor range. AP/Shock/DA modes add real target flexibility, particularly versus armor and susceptible living targets.

Pay +4 when a **CoC Magistrate with a COLCORP Duo** is actually expected to occupy a midfield/scoring lane and fire with FT2's extra selection die, or when a defensive 8–24" lane genuinely needs an AP-capable gun. Do not pay just because the MMR is a bargain in isolation; **BS12, ARM1, W1 and a crucial command role make an intentional duel unattractive**. The **21 basic MMR** is especially hard to justify versus **21 CoC Combi**, while **23 LT MMR** pays for a weapon that encourages exposing the Lieutenant.

**Default:** 19 LT Combi or 21 CoC Combi; 17 basic Combi when CoC deliberately omitted. The 25 CoC MMR is the primary *situational* MMR pick.

#### Experimental double-Magistrate order engine

**Legal under current AVAs and Fireteam limits**, but **not verified as an exact played list** in the reviewed battle-report archive.

| Team | Members | Points | Pooled Regular Orders |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| **COLCORP Haris FT3** | **Magistrate LT/Strategos19 + two Jackal FTO6** | **31** | **4** (3 models + Strategos LT conversion) |
| **COLCORP Duo FT2** | **Magistrate CoC21 + one Jackal FTO6** | **27** | **2** |
| **Combined** | **2 Magistrates + 3 Jackals; 5 trooper slots** | **58/0** | **6** |

**Upside:** SIX pooled Orders for 58pts; Strategos Lieutenant, separate Chain of Command, two WIP13 Specialists, Counterintelligence redundancy, three Boarding Pistol template defenders, two Nanopulsers, LRL/Panzerfaust coverage, and an entirely free **Core**. Separate Haris and Duo can defend different DZ pockets. This is a legitimate **order-engine skew**, not a universal efficiency win.

**Costs and hazards:**
1. Five of fifteen trooper slots and nearly 20% of a 300pt list committed to support/defense, not a premium mobile attacker.
2. Consumes the **sole Haris**; cannot also field a KESTREL or FENNECS Haris.
3. **Magistrate LT is a high-value failure point**: if killed, CoC prevents Loss of Lieutenant, but the LT Haris loses its Master/FT conversion at future Order Counts, and Strategos conversion is no longer available from that fallen LT.
4. Two Counterintelligence providers create **redundancy**, not a stronger stacked effect.
5. The conditional Jackal Orders can disappear on subsequent turns with Fireteam loss; independent Fennecs/Mulebots/Fugazi are not linked to this failure mode.

**Alternative: two COLCORP Duos:** **LT19 + Jackal6** and **CoC21 + Jackal6** = **52pts/0, four troopers, five pooled Orders** (including Strategos conversion) and **Haris stays free**. Often the better balance when you need a premium fighting/scoring Haris. A full double-Magistrate build should be tested against this alternative, not assumed superior solely for its sixth Order.

#### Matchup / listbuilding recommendations

- **Aggressive LT (Agent Maximus, Tikbalang):** Usually start with **CoC Magistrate21 + Jackal6**. Protect CoC in the DZ, let the LT exploit its own attacks/orders; preserve your Fireflies for Engineer, Minelayer or other NCO roles. But CoC is still a choice, not mandatory when points/slots and mission utility favor independent Fennecs.
- **Ordinary protected LT / distributed gunfighters:** **Strategos LT19 + Jackal6** is exceptional. It frees the premium gunfighter from command risk; decide Strategos conversion versus NCO use of the Lieutenant Order.
- **Skip CoC intentionally:** **Basic Magistrate17 + LRL Jackal6** is a strong pure cheerleader/defense/backup-specialist candidate; compare **FO/Disco Fennec13 + Minelayer Fennec14** at only +4pts for better independent Orders, Sensor/Eclipse/mine and dispersed DZ coverage.
- **Haris free and need Orders:** Add the second 6pt Jackal; the **LT 31/4** or **CoC 33/3** package is strong if the opponent cannot cheaply eliminate the FT Master.
- **Haris reserved for combat:** keep a **COLCORP Duo**. Do not sacrifice a meaningful assault/specialist Haris just for a marginal cheap Order.
- **Very constrained 15-model list:** consider a lone **Firefly CoC** already working as part of a combat team rather than forcing the two-slot Magistrate/Jackal module.
- **Don't double-count an LT order:** Strategos conversion and NCO use are alternate destinations of the *same* LT resource, not two Orders.

#### Observed use versus constructed theory

**Supported historical usages (not the exact double-Magistrate build):**

- [Battle report #111](../../../battle-reports/111-120-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md): **Magistrate Strategos LT + two Jackals** was explicitly identified as a played **Haris**. Jackal weapons were not established.
- [Battle report #175, CanCon Kestrel winner Hector](../../../battle-reports/171-180-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md): **Day 1 Magistrate Strategos LT + one Jackal Duo**; **Day 2 Magistrate CoC + two Jackals**, in a different list alongside a **Tikbalang LT**. The Day 2 report includes a **two-Haris historical legality error** in another formation; do not copy the whole list as legal N5.3 without correcting it.
- [Observed Fireteam register](../../../list-building/Fireteams/OBSERVED-COMPOSITIONS.md) records the confirmed **LT Magistrate + two Jackals Haris** and other played examples. 

**Evidence boundary:** A simultaneous **LT Magistrate + two Jackals Haris and CoC Magistrate + one Jackal Duo** is **legal constructed theorycraft**, but no exact competitive-list occurrence was verified in the reviewed archive. The separate component packages have real battle-report precedent; that is not evidence the combined five-model shell has won an event.

#### Final selection hierarchy

1. **Strategos LT Combi 19**, paired with a **6pt FTO Jackal** where possible: top cheap LT and order engine.
2. **CoC Combi 21**, paired with a **6pt FTO Jackal**: top cost-effective command insurance for an aggressive LT.
3. **Basic Combi 17 + Jackal6**: very good if explicitly skipping CoC and already using a different Lieutenant, but compare Fennec utility.
4. **MMR upgrades**: pay +4 only for a credible 8–24" fighting/specialist lane; do not expose the command anchor needlessly.
5. **Second Jackal**: efficient *only if Haris isn't more valuable elsewhere*.
6. **Double Magistrates**: promising order-heavy build (**52pts two Duos** vs **58pts Haris+Duo**), worthy of testing but not a default for every Kestrel list.

**Bottom line:** The Magistrate is not automatically mandatory, but **CoC Magistrate + 6pt Jackal** is one of Kestrel's most efficient ways to buy succession protection *without wasting the CoC trooper as a passive tax*. And **basic Magistrate + Jackal** remains a legitimate cheap, defensive two-Order package when CoC is intentionally omitted.
