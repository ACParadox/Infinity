## Vanilla selection and use

**A-tier role-specific gunfighter/ARO.** K1 MMR FO29/0.5 gives MSV2, Mim-3, specialist and anti-armour; MSV2 Neurocinetics sniper33/1.5 is strong dedicated independent ARO, not sustained active gun. HD26/0.5 and DZ parachutist KHD31/0 situational. Bolt MSV1 MSR FT2 is often preferred dual-use long gun; Nisse HMG/MSR counters Mim-6 more directly. AVA1.

### ARO comparison after the corrected chart

**S-tier dedicated independent visible ARO; A-tier overall role-specific choice.** Against an enemy in cover at +3 range, Black A.I.R. rolls B2 on BS13 against no Mimetism, Mim-3 or Mim-6; the FT2 Bolt MSR rolls B1+1SD on 16/16/13 respectively. Black A.I.R. imposes Mim-3 on opponents without MSV, retains both winning dice (up to two DA hits), sees through ordinary Smoke without the MSV1 penalty, and ignores Surprise Attack through Combat Instinct. The Bolt has superior accuracy when the enemy has cover and its own MSV, Warhorse against BS Attack (-X), Shock Immunity and BTS6, and B2+1SD active. With a suitable partner already wanted, the Bolt is generally the better dual-purpose purchase; Black A.I.R. remains the better dedicated standalone sniper and costs no link slot. Do not call it strictly better against all attackers. Bolt BSG Duo45/1.5, KHD Duo48/1.5, CoC Duo52/1.5 versus Black A.I.R.33/1.5; compare marginal gun cost and entire package separately. Nisse + two Helot FTOs is another MSV2 defensive option. No Fireteam bonus for Black A.I.R. in the visible chart.

### Black A.I.R. — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A+ overall.** The **Neurocinetics MULTI Sniper is S-tier as a premium conventional ARO**, but not an automatic inclusion. The **K1 FO and DZ Parachutist KHD are A-tier** and fulfill distinct offensive/mission roles. The **standard Hacker is B-tier**, principally for anti-Stealth hacking. One sniper and one of the other two strong profiles is a compelling package; all three can coexist without much role redundancy.

The first three options can join FENNECS Core/Haris (maximum two Black A.I.R. per team); the DZ KHD cannot link. FTO profiles can also operate independently.

| Opt | Loadout | Points / SWC | Rating | Typical count |
|---:|---|---:|---|---:|

All carry Pistol and CCW. The common strength is the combination of MSV2, Mimetism and Combat Instinct, not sustained durability. At 26–33 points for ARM2/VITA1, a failed engagement can remove a large investment.

#### Neurocinetics MULTI Sniper — near-default premium ARO

**B2 reactive / B1 active** MULTI Sniper, BS13, MSV2, Mimetism (-3), Combat Instinct and Nanopulser. DA threatens two Saving Rolls per hit; AP is useful against high ARM, while Shock has its own target-specific application. Positive sniper bands span 16–48 inches. MSV2 negates Mimetism and enables LoF through ordinary Smoke subject to the relevant visibility rules; Combat Instinct ignores Surprise Attack and negates Stealth against the user. Neither provides universal protection against White Noise, Eclipse, templates or attacks from outside LoF.

This is Kestrel's first premium conventional sniper ARO to consider, particularly against camouflage/Mimetism-heavy forces or smoke-assisted attacks. It is not mandatory: one wound and ARM2 remain vulnerable to an apex active-turn gunfighter. Deploy to cover a meaningful approach, preferably with cover and overlapping close-range defenses, rather than exposing it to every enemy gun. Consider a narrower lane or reserve deployment if going second. Its task is to consume Orders, constrain movement and threaten expensive failures, not to survive an entire enemy turn.

##### Solo versus linked

**Solo is fully viable.** It has native B2 ARO without needing any other model, and can occupy a separate lane from the main attack team. FT2 is a significant upgrade, but the Haris/Core slot and support costs must earn their place independently.

Black A.I.R. does not count as Fennec. Two matching Fennecs (or eligible Fennec-equivalent members with an actual Fennec anchor) yield FT2 in a three-body team. FT2 grants **B2 +1SD**, rolling three dice and discarding one after the rolls, not B3 or three potential hits. FT3 adds Discover/Dodge bonuses; FT4 adds +1 BS attack MOD. One Black A.I.R. plus four matching Fennecs reaches FT4, not FT5. FENNECS has no Duo; Kestrel has one Haris and one Core.

| Suggested team | Points / SWC | Level |
|---|---:|---|
| Sniper + Fennec Minelayer + Paramedic | **59 / 1.5** | FT2 |
| Sniper + Fennec Minelayer + FO/Sensor/Disco | **60 / 1.5** | FT2 |
| Sniper + Fennec Minelayer + Hacker | **63 / 2** | FT2 |
| Sniper + two Fennec Minelayers | **61 / 1.5** | FT2 |

The Minelayer + Paramedic is a practical dedicated ARO shell: prepaid mine, close-defense Thunderbolt, three Regular Orders and a chance to recover an Unconscious sniper. MediKit recovery is not guaranteed. FO/Disco adds Sensor, scoring and Eclipse projection when positioned to use it; Hacker adds network denial; two Minelayers favor distinct DZ approaches.

**Do not spend the sole Haris purely to add +1SD.** Use the defensive Core/Haris when its supporting Fennecs already belong in the army and the competing team slots are not more valuable. Prefer solo if using an offensive FENNECS Core and a KESTREL assault Haris or COLCORP order engine. Avoid routinely putting a stationary sniper in a forward-moving Griffin HMG team: the two usually want different positions.

##### Alternatives and complementary defenses

- **Griffin Feuerbach:** More durable, ARM4/two wounds, useful active-turn shooting; preferable when the list needs a dual-purpose gun rather than a dedicated ARO.
- **Blink HRL (28/1.5):** Hidden Deployment, Mimetism (-6), continuous-damage impact template and WildParrot. Excellent complementary second layer behind a visible sniper, not an interchangeable visible ARO.
- **Blink MULTI Sniper (32/1.5):** Concealed ambush and long-range reach, but B1 reactive and no MSV2. Black A.I.R. is usually better for conventional exposed ARO.
- **Sierra TR:** B4 ARO and Remote Presence; useful when different lanes or a cheaper visible roadblock justify it.
- **Firefly HRL (24/1.5):** Saves nine points, offers Albedo, NCO and impact-template ammunition; useful linked budget ARO, but no Mimetism and inferior general anti-Mimetism coverage.
- **Fennec LRL (12/0):** Cheap roadblock when premium ARO investment would compromise main attacks or objectives.

Two Neuro snipers cost **66/3** and are usually excessive. Distributed cheap defenses may tax more Orders than a second expensive single-wound ARO.

Report evidence: [ITC 2025 reports #002–004](../../battle-reports/001-010-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md) document a four-member Black A.I.R. sniper/Fennec shell. The [listbuilding guide](../../kestrel/kestrel-listbuilding.md) records strong support from some Irregular Order builds but a more situational assessment from Shepherd, who emphasizes fragility and opportunity cost.

#### K1 Marksman Rifle FO — efficient secondary attack and scoring

**29/0.5, B3, PS7, K1 (ARM=0), Anti-materiel, Suppressive Fire.** Range modifiers: -3 at 0–8, +3 at 8–24, -3 at 24–40, -6 at 40–48 inches. Direct template Nanopulser covers close-range emergencies. MSV2 and Mimetism (-3) make many face-to-face exchanges favorable, and Combat Instinct protects against ambush MODs.

K1 treats native ARM as zero, irrespective of how heavily armored the target is; Partial Cover still modifies Saving Rolls where applicable. This makes it especially attractive against TAGs and HI, but PS7 and a single save per normal hit mean it is not automatically more lethal than every AP/DA/EXP weapon. Use it to solve particular armored or Mimetism-dependent opponents rather than assuming it is a universal apex gun. Its zero ARM interaction also makes it useful against relevant Anti-materiel objectives.

**FO and Flash Pulse are real added value.** A useful gun can double as a late-turn button-presser, rather than requiring a separate specialist to follow behind. Good as a solo secondary attack or FT2 leader (B3 +1SD, not B4).

| Alternative | Relative advantage |
|---|---|
| Griffin HMG | BS14, B4, longer optimal reach, ARM4 and two wounds; better repeated general attacks |
| Crux Knight | Resilient specialist and CC capability |
| Hetkari | Premium active-turn assault piece |
| Agent Maximus | Higher Burst and much greater resilience |
| Black A.I.R. K1 | MSV2, Mimetism, anti-ARM, FO and only 0.5 SWC |

The K1 is especially valuable when the main attack is vulnerable to hacking or when the list has little SWC left. It is not itself HI/TAG/REM, nor a Hacker, so ordinary anti-heavy hacking does not directly stop it. Targeted/Spotlight and other applicable threats remain relevant.

The K1 was used against a Cameronian after Sensor support.

#### Standard Hacker — Combat Instinct niche

**26/0.5, WIP13/BTS3, Hacking Device, SMG, Nanopulser and D-Charges.** The primary reason for paying the premium is **Combat Instinct**, which means Stealth cannot suppress this Hacker's otherwise legal AROs inside its Hacking Area, including through friendly Repeaters. An eligible hackable HI/TAG/REM using movement-only Skills can therefore trigger hacking AROs that a normal Hacker might miss. It also ignores Surprise Attack MODs in Comms Attacks. It does not acquire new programs, extra Hacking Area or unrestricted AROs merely from Combat Instinct.

| Hacker | Points / SWC | WIP / BTS | Distinctive value |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Fennec HD | **16 / 0.5** | 12 / 3 | Cheapest HD, FastPanda network projection, E/Mitter |
| Firefly HD | **24 / 0.5** | 13 / 6 | NCO, BTS6, broader linked utility |
| Black A.I.R. HD | **26 / 0.5** | 13 / 3 | Combat Instinct, MSV2, Mimetism, close-range template |

Fennec is the default standard Hacker to activate Kestrel's Repeaters: it is ten points cheaper and brings its own FastPanda. Firefly is preferable when NCO and BTS6 are important. Much of Black A.I.R.'s premium is wasted if it stays prone in the DZ with an SMG, and FT2's +1SD applies to BS Attacks, **not hacking programs**.

Take Black A.I.R. HD when anti-Stealth hacking is a genuine defensive requirement, the existing Repeater coverage is extensive, a forward-moving close-range anti-camo Hacker is needed, or Elite/D-Charge Classified coverage is important. Protect it from superior enemy KHDs. It is not the default choice merely because it has WIP13 or MSV2.

#### DZ Parachutist KHD — premium backline attack

**31/0, SMG, Flash Pulse (+1B), D-Charges, Killer Hacking Device, Parachutist (Deployment Zone), no Fireteam.** The key advantage is deterministic access to a legal table-edge position inside the enemy DZ, without a PH Roll. It is not free placement anywhere in the DZ and does not guarantee a safe entry. Entry is a Long Skill; cannot enter Prone, in contact with enemy/neutral models or objectives, or benefit from Partial Cover during the arrival Order. Enemy AROs still apply. Choose blind spots.

Potential jobs: attack the enemy Lieutenant or cheerleaders from behind, eliminate vulnerable Hackers with Trinity, kill TAG support specialists, remove backline camouflage targets with MSV2, and reach enemy-side HVTs, scenery and Classified locations. Cybermask (KHD) is another route to a protected approach after reaching a position in which it can be legally activated. Do not assume the KHD can Immobilize or Isolate a TAG with standard HD programs. The **Flash Pulse (+1B) is an active-turn Burst modifier**, not an automatic B2 ARO.

Unlike other Black A.I.R. profiles this option has **no Nanopulser in the pinned loadout**. Do not treat it as a template-trading piece. It must select favorable SMG or hacking encounters; enemy templates, mines and well-screened rear arcs can be costly. MSV2/Mimetism are good tools for winning a selective fight, not permission to charge through a defensive screen.

##### One or two?

| Package | Points / SWC | Judgment |
|---|---:|---|
| One Black A.I.R. DZ KHD | **31/0** | Normal choice |
| Two Black A.I.R. DZ KHD | **62/0** | Experimental, opposing flank pressure |
| Black A.I.R. + Banshee KHD | **54/0** | Flexible dual insertion and cyber options |
| Black A.I.R. + Banshee LSG/WildParrot | **50/0** | Preferred mixed double-airborne package for distinct attack tools |
| Two Banshee KHD | **46/0** | Cheaper dual airborne specialist option |

One is usually enough to force backline screening. Two Black A.I.R. threaten opposite edges but duplicate a 31-point single-wound investment. Prefer one Black A.I.R. plus a 19-point Banshee LSG when seeking two attack vectors with different tools: the Black A.I.R. supplies no-roll deep insertion, the Banshee brings templates and WildParrot.

**Order economy is critical:** while off-table, each parachutist withholds its Regular Order from the pool. Its own Order can deploy it, but subsequent attack/scoring Orders come from available legal resources. Do not place two airborne pieces in an undersupplied group; a second threat with no Orders is not a meaningful threat. Deploying on-table normally or holding until turn two/three may be better than forcing an early raid. Against a screened DZ, use a legal midfield edge or preserve the specialist for scoring rather than insisting on assassination.

#### Combining Black A.I.R. profiles

| Package | Points / SWC | Judgment |
|---|---:|---|
| Sniper alone | 33/1.5 | **S** — premium ARO |
| Sniper + K1 | 62/2 | **S** — complementary defense and midfield attack |
| Sniper + DZ KHD | 64/1.5 | **A+** — defense plus backline threat |
| K1 + DZ KHD | 60/0.5 | **A** — low-SWC offense and mission play |
| Sniper + K1 + DZ KHD | 93/2 | **A** — diverse but expensive |
| Sniper + standard HD | 59/2 | **B** — only with a clear anti-Stealth requirement |
| Two DZ KHD | 62/0 | **B** — experimental |
| Two Snipers | 66/3 | **C** — usually excessive |

The sniper and K1 can share a legal FENNECS Core with two Fennecs at FT2 (89/2 with FO/Disco and Minelayer); however, their desired positions differ, so often keep one independent. A single Black A.I.R. profile already fills its own distinct role; the AVA3 limit is not a reason to fill all three slots. Three distinct profiles at 93/2 are reasonable only if the remaining 207 points cover a robust primary attacker, sufficient usable Orders, command security and screening.

**Final selection rule:** Start with one Neuro sniper when premium visible ARO is warranted. Link it at FT2 if useful supporting Fennecs and available slots justify it, otherwise run it solo. Strongly consider the K1 as an inexpensive-SWC secondary gun/specialist, particularly against armor and Mimetism. Take one DZ KHD when terrain and mission reward enemy backline access; prefer a complementary Banshee over a duplicate. Default to Fennec HD for network activation, or Firefly HD for BTS/NCO, unless Combat Instinct specifically merits the Black A.I.R. Hacker.
