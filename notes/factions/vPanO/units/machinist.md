### Machinist — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Summary:** The Machinist is Kestrel's **economical, independent Engineer**, primarily used to repair TAGs and REMs through **Palbots**. For a list centered on a Tikbalang, Squalos Mk-II or Maximus TAG, **Machinist + one Palbot (18 points)** is close to a default support package unless another Engineer already has a realistic recovery route. The **18-point Armed Turret Machinist** is a worthwhile optional upgrade when **Group 2 has spare Orders** and three points remain; it is not an automatic replacement for buying a Palbot.

#### Profiles and costs

**Kestrel Machinist:** AVA 1, Regular LI, MOV 10-10, BS 12, **WIP 12**, ARM 1, BTS 3, W 1; Engineer, GizmoKit, Deactivator. All variants carry Combi Rifle, D-Charges, Pistol and CCW and **have no Fireteam access**.

| Profile / package | Points / SWC | Main reason |
|---|---:|---|
| **Machinist (base)** | **15 / 0** | Cheapest straightforward Engineer and Regular Order |
| **Machinist + Deployable Cover** | **16 / 0** | Situational protection/positioning tool; not the default repair choice |
| **Machinist + Armed Turret (Combi Rifle)** | **18 / 0** | Add a disposable defensive ARO piece, preferably with a spare Group 2 Order |
| **Base Machinist + one Palbot** | **18 / 0** | **Preferred economical TAG/REM repair package** |
| **Armed Turret Machinist + one Palbot** | **21 / 0** | TAG recovery plus optional board control when points and Orders permit |
| **Base Machinist + two Palbots** | **21 / 0** | Two separate repair routes; only one Servant can be activated at a time |

**Palbot:** 3 points, AVA 2 across Kestrel, MOV 15-10, Mimetism (-3), Peripheral (Servant). It creates **no independent Order**. The Machinist's Order activates the chosen Servant, and the Machinist's WIP is used for its remote Engineer action. A Palbot is not a second Engineer and is not a separate pool of Orders.

#### When is the Machinist worthwhile?

1. **Protecting a valuable TAG.** Keep the Machinist hidden and place a Palbot near the TAG's likely end-of-turn fallback location. This provides a recovery path without making the Engineer follow a large, fast TAG into danger. This is particularly attractive for Tikbalang, Squalos Mk-II or Maximus TAG builds.
2. **Recovering an important REM.** A Sierra Thunderbolt/HMG, Pathfinder or another strategically useful STR-based REM can justify a nearby Palbot, especially if its return restores an ARO lane or mission capability.
3. **Supporting a TAG in Group 1 from Group 2.** The Machinist may spend **its own Combat Group's Orders** to activate the Palbot and repair a TAG in the other group; the TAG does not need to share the Machinist's group. This preserves Group 1's attack budget.
4. **Keeping the Engineer safe.** The Machinist should ordinarily be a protected support trooper, not a frontline gunfighter. The Palbot's mobility and remote access are generally worth its three points.
5. **When no practical repair can happen.** If the TAG is deliberately traded far into enemy territory, its expected end position is inaccessible, or the list has more immediate mission/attack needs, the Engineer package is **not strictly mandatory**. Points and Orders spent on recovery only pay off when a recovery route exists.

#### Is Machinist + Palbot a must with a TAG?

**Strong default, not an absolute rule.** A centerpiece TAG is a large investment, and a pre-positioned Servant often gives the cheapest credible way to get it operating again after an Unconscious result or to handle eligible Engineer-cancelable states.

| TAG configuration | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Solo Tikbalang** | **Strongly recommend** Machinist + Palbot |
| **Solo Squalos Mk-II** | **Strongly recommend** Machinist + Palbot |
| **Solo Maximus TAG** | **Strongly recommend** Machinist + Palbot |
| **Squalos/Tikbalang + Firefly Engineer in a legal ARMORED Fireteam** | Machinist usually **optional/redundant** if Firefly can realistically reach the TAG |
| **TAG with a separate nearby Engineer and reliable recovery route** | Second Engineer generally not needed |
| **TAG committed to a deep, risky one-way attack** | Consider omitting recovery if the expected repair travel Orders make it unrealistic |

**Fireteam caveat:** The ARMORED Fireteam requires its specified **Tikbalang or Squalos** anchor. **Maximus TAG + Firefly alone is not a legal ARMORED Duo** merely because Maximus is a TAG. Confirm the exact current chart before using any mixed TAG team.

**Why the independent Machinist may still beat a linked Firefly:** A Firefly Engineer offers higher WIP and valuable NCO/Albedo/combat utility, but its **MOV 10-10** can constrain a faster **MOV 15-10** TAG, and a linked Firefly must reach the damaged TAG itself. The Machinist stays safe while a Palbot approaches from a different route. Conversely, when the Firefly is already adjacent to the TAG for a useful Fireteam plan, paying for a redundant Engineer often wastes points.

#### Machinist versus Firefly Engineer

| | **Machinist** | **Firefly Engineer** |
|---|---|---|
| Points / SWC | **15 / 0** | **23 / 0** |
| WIP / first-roll Engineer success | **12 / 60%** | **13 / 65%** |
| Mobility | MOV 10-10; Palbot MOV 15-10 | MOV 10-10 |
| Typical role | **Backfield recovery controller** | Mobile Engineer, NCO and support combatant |
| Fireteam access | **None** | Eligible KESTREL/ARMORED configurations |
| Palbot trade-off | Excellent reason to buy one | Controlling a Servant rules out Fireteam membership |
| Best choice | Independent TAG/REM support at low cost | When the Fireteam, NCO and extra utility are being used |

The Firefly's 8-point premium is **not** justified by the WIP improvement alone. Choose it for the **whole package**: legal team participation, Albedo, NCO, Panzerfaust and other utility. Firefly Engineer and other Firefly specialist roles are different profiles.

#### Armed Turret upgrade: particularly useful in Group 2

The **Armed Turret (Combi Rifle)** costs **+3 points** over the base Machinist and requires a **Place Deployable Short Skill** during play. It is **not a free deployment-time turret**. Its value changes materially if the Machinist sits in **Group 2 with a Regular Order you otherwise would not use**.

**Turret characteristics:** BS 10, **Total Reaction**, **360º Visor**, ARM 2/BTS 3/STR 1, stationary, **Disposable (1), Non-Reloadable**. Its **Perimeter** trait allows placement wholly within the Machinist's Zone of Control instead of in Silhouette contact, subject to normal placement restrictions. It attacks enemy **Models**, not Camouflage Markers.

**Good uses:**
- Spend an otherwise underused **Group 2 Order** to cover a flank, objective route or rear approach.
- Add an independent obstacle near a TAG's retreat/repair position so an opponent has to account for another ARO.
- Buy the upgrade when the list genuinely has **three spare points** and a convincing placement.

**Reasons to skip:**
- Your Group 2 Orders are already needed for Palbot travel, repairs, mission actions or other support.
- No useful turret lane exists, or it can be removed cheaply at range.
- Those three points are needed for the **first Palbot**; in a TAG-centered list, **reach and recovery normally win**.
- You are going second and need protection in the **opponent's first turn**: the turret has not been placed yet.

**Practical value assessment:** Base Machinist **A**; base Machinist + Palbot **A+ for TAG support**; Armed Turret Machinist **B+ when a spare Group 2 Order and good lane are available**; Armed Turret + Palbot **A as a flexible 21-point support package**. These are list-building judgments, not observed win-rate measurements.

#### Palbots, Engineer rolls and Command Tokens

- **First-roll WIP 12 repair:** **60%** success.
- **Remote Presence target with an eligible Command Token reroll:** **84%** chance of at least one success over two WIP 12 attempts. The reroll spends a Command Token and is **not automatic for every target**.
- **A successful Engineer repair on a Remote Presence TAG:** can return it from either Unconscious level under the applicable N5.3 rules. **Dead** is not recoverable.
- **Two Palbots:** cover two locations, but do not give two activations on one Order. Assign the Servants to their legal Controller.
- **A Controller with a Peripheral (Servant)** cannot join a Fireteam or participate in a Coordinated Order. The Machinist has no native Fireteam option anyway, but **Palbot-equipped Machinists cannot join Coordinated Orders to place turrets**.
- **Coordinating deployable placement** can be efficient for eligible troopers without Peripherals: one Regular Order plus a Command Token can cover multiple distinct placements. Do **not** assume this applies to a Machinist with a Palbot.
- **Unconscious TAG accessibility matters more than nominal odds:** a 60% repair chance from a Palbot one Order away is often more valuable than a higher-WIP Engineer stranded across the table.

#### Recommended Kestrel setups

**Default TAG support:** **15-point Machinist + 3-point Palbot = 18 points**, generally in **Group 2** when Orders and roster structure allow. Hide the Engineer and position the Palbot near the likely TAG fallback route.

**Optional upgrade if the list has room:** **18-point Armed Turret Machinist + 3-point Palbot = 21 points**, with the turret deployed using a spare Group 2 Order when a useful lane is available. Do not count on the turret in the first Reactive Turn before placement.

**TAG already has Firefly Engineer in a legal Fireteam:** often **skip the Machinist**, unless you need genuinely separate backline REM coverage or expect Firefly to die/be stranded away from the TAG.

**Bottom line:** Buy **repair reach first**, then consider the **+3-point Armed Turret** as an opportunistic Group 2 Order sink. The Machinist is close to an auto-include for TAG-centered Kestrel **when there is no other credible Engineer plan**, not a universal tax on every TAG list.
