## Original kestrel analysis — retained reference copy

The original is retained for transferable mechanics and reasoning. Sectorial ratings, profiles, AVA and linked packages are historical source-context only. The vanilla profile and notes above take precedence. Links retain original destinations.

### Bipandra — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: Strong / Go-to, but not Must-have.** **A-tier overall, A+ as a mobile elite-infantry Fireteam support piece** (editorial, role-dependent). The **25-point Tactical Awareness Doctor** is worth serious consideration when she can travel with, reposition alongside, and potentially revive a valuable attacker such as **Griffin, Hetkari or Crux Knight**, or **Optimate Agent Maximus** (who is **MI**, not HI). She is much less attractive as a passive, independent 25-point medic. Her value is the **combination of a good Doctor, a useful extra Fireteam activation, and Fennec/Kestrel counts-as membership**—not medical ability alone.

#### Current Kestrel profile

**AVA 1 · LI · Regular · W 1 · MOV 10-10 · CC 17 · BS 12 · PH 11 · WIP 13 · ARM 2 · BTS 3 · S 2**

| Profile | Pts / SWC | Equipment and skills | Fireteam |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Bipandra Tactical Awareness** | **25 / 0** | Combi Rifle, **Nanopulser**, Pistol, CCW; **Doctor (ReRoll -3)**, **MediKit (+1B)**, Tactical Awareness, Dodge (+1"), Immunity (Shock) | **Wildcard max 1**; **Counts as Fennec or Kestrel** |
| Other published profile | 23 / 0 | Same base skills, **Nanopulser (+1B)**, **without Tactical Awareness** | Same Wildcard / Counts-as |

**Preferred default: 25-point Tactical Awareness.** The two-point upgrade is usually worthwhile when Bipandra is in an active Fireteam. Her **25-point profile has an ordinary Nanopulser**, not Nanopulser (+1B); **MediKit (+1B) belongs to both profiles**. Neither has more than one Wound or Doctor (2W).

#### Why she is good

1. **Reliable recovery of expensive infantry.** Doctor (ReRoll -3) is **WIP 13 on the first attempt** (65%), then **WIP 10 on a failed first attempt** (50%); overall **82.5%** successful contact recovery before any eligible Command Token reroll. Recovering a 37–50-point, two-Wound attack piece can justify the support cost in a way recovering an expendable 10-point trooper usually cannot. She removes **one** Wound when the Doctor roll succeeds, not two.
2. **A Tactical Awareness activation for an accompanying Fireteam.** Her Tactical Order can activate a legal team **with Bipandra designated as Fireteam Leader**. This is excellent for a move/reposition, retreat, or Bipandra-led support action without consuming a pooled Regular Order. It **does not let Griffin, Hetkari, Crux or Maximus act as Fireteam Leader and shoot with their premium weapon** on her Tactical Order.
3. **Fireteam efficiency.** As a Wildcard that **Counts as Fennec/Kestrel**, she provides medical utility *and* a same-family member for relevant Fireteam composition levels. This is a genuine advantage over a separate, backfield Trauma-Doc. Counts-as bonuses depend on the **entire exact legal team**, not merely adding a Wildcard.
4. **Useful close-defense package.** ARM 2, BTS 3, Immunity (Shock), Dodge (+1"), CC 17 and a Nanopulser make her less helpless than an ordinary basic Doctor. She is **still a one-Wound LI support trooper**, not a frontline assault fighter.
5. **Ranged medical option.** MediKit (+1B) fires **two shots** in the Active Turn, raising the chance of getting at least one hit or several hits on an Unconscious patient. Each successful hit prompts a **PH roll by the patient**; if at least one PH roll passes, only **one Wound** is recovered. The +1B does **not** improve Doctor WIP rolls, contact MediKit use or the amount healed.

#### Best time to pay 25 points

**Preferred scenario:** A **mobile, valuable VITA infantry attacker** expects to fight over successive turns, may go Unconscious somewhere Bipandra can safely reach, and benefits from a separate **Tactical Awareness repositioning Order**. Keep her close enough to the same cover network without putting the Doctor in the gunfighter's line of fire.

| Supported attacker | Recommended relationship | Why it pays | Main trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Griffin HMG (HI)** | **Griffin HMG37 + Bipandra TA25: KESTREL Duo, 62pts/1.5 SWC, FT2** | Best compact mobile gun + Doctor combination; efficient same-family Duo and one extra team-repositioning activation | Bipandra's MOV 10-10 versus Griffin's 15-5; protect the W1 medic |
| **Hetkari MULTI Red Fury (HI)** | **Hetkari48 + Firefly Engineer23 + Bipandra TA25: KESTREL Haris, 96pts/1.5 SWC, FT2** | Premium active gun, engineering/utility and reliable nearby Doctor support for an expensive 2W fighter | Very expensive and uses the only Haris; Hetkari does **not** itself count as Kestrel for the composition bonus |
| **Crux Knight FTO MULTI Marksman (HI)** | **Crux46 + Fennec FO/Disco13 + Bipandra TA25: FENNECS Core/Haris, 84pts/0 SWC, FT2** | High-quality gun and specialist, Disco/Eclipse utility, Doctor and Tactical Awareness without SWC expenditure | Need the third member; a **FENNECS Duo is not a legal team type**; 84 points is a substantial commitment |
| **Optimate Agent Maximus (MI)** | **Agent Maximus FTO45 + Fennec FO/Disco13 + Bipandra TA25: FENNECS Core/Haris, 83pts/1 SWC, FT2** | Supports a mobile NWI attack specialist while providing another Tactical Awareness activation and Doctor access when a legal recovery opportunity exists | **Agent Maximus is MI**, not HI; his Tactical Order likewise requires **him** to lead if used for the team; distinct from the Maximus TAG |

**Other use:** **Griffin Feuerbach + Bipandra** can protect a defensively positioned long-range HI. This is less compelling for Tactical Awareness when the team intends to remain stationary; an extra movement activation is not automatically valuable.

**Fireteam constraints:** Verify the current FENNECS/KESTREL chart and selected FTO options before copying the combinations. Kestrel has **one Haris**; taking the expensive Hetkari or Crux Haris can displace the valuable Magistrate + Jackal order-economy Haris. The proposed teams above are **legal constructed examples**, not claims those exact loadout combinations were used in a recorded event.

#### Tactical Awareness: important limitation

Bipandra's **Tactical Order is personal, not a Regular Order added to the pool**. It activates the Fireteam only **when Bipandra is its designated Team Leader for that Order**. A Griffin HMG or Hetkari Red Fury can move along as a member, but **cannot use Bipandra's Order as its own extra active-turn attack**.

That makes Bipandra particularly good at:
- **Repositioning an attacking team** after its primary gunfighter has opened a lane.
- **Advancing the team toward objectives** while preserving a pooled Order.
- **Taking Bipandra's own medical action** when a patient is legally within reach.
- **Retreating or re-forming a safer position** without paying a pooled Order.

It is less impressive when the team never moves, the medic cannot reach a patient, or the attacker needs its *own* Tactical Awareness or additional pooled Orders to make further attacks.

#### MediKit (+1B) versus Doctor

| Method | Resolution | Practical benefit |
|---|---|---|
| **Doctor (ReRoll -3), contact** | Bipandra WIP13; failed initial roll rerolled at WIP10 | **82.5%** recovery before any qualifying Command Token repeat; usually the strongest option if contact is safe |
| **MediKit (+1B), ranged** | Up to **two BS attacks** in the Active Turn; each hit causes a patient PH roll | Can rescue at range without placing Bipandra directly next to the casualty; multiple hits improve the chance of at least one passing PH roll |
| **MediKit, contact** | **One** patient PH roll; no BS hit needed | An option if the patient's PH makes it worthwhile, but **+1B does not improve contact use** |

**One successful MediKit Order recovers at most one Wound**, even if both shots hit and both PH rolls pass. Doctor likewise normally restores one Wound on a successful use; Bipandra does **not** have Doctor (2W). Failed recovery may kill the Unconscious patient. A qualified Cube target may also allow a Command Token repeat of a failed Doctor roll, separately from her built-in reroll.

**Do not confuse medicine with repairs:** **Maximus TAG**, Squalos and other STR-based troops usually need an **Engineer/GizmoKit**, not Bipandra's Doctor. **Optimate Agent Maximus** is the mobile MI option discussed above.

#### When to pass on her

- **No expensive recoverable infantry within reach.** In a TAG/REM/VH-heavy list, she may have almost nothing relevant to heal.
- **The Fireteam is a stationary ARO bunker.** Tactical Awareness may add little, and buying a 25-point support member can crowd out a gun or a useful mission specialist.
- **Too many points tied up in one group.** A premium Haris with a powerful gunfighter can leave the rest of the list short on Orders, midfield pieces, or independent objective play.
- **The plan only needs cheap linked medical insurance.** The **12-point Fennec Paramedic** may do enough for a FENNECS team. The **14-point Trauma-Doc + 3-point Palbot** is more appropriate for a separate, hidden backfield recovery route.
- **You want a Palbot support network from Bipandra.** A controller with **Peripheral (Servant)** cannot also be a Fireteam member, forfeiting much of her unique value as a linked Tactical Awareness Doctor.

**Practical decision:** Would the list make regular use of **her own Tactical Awareness Order**, her **Counts-as membership**, and a **reachable high-value VITA casualty**? If at least two of these benefits matter, Bipandra is a strong buy. If she is merely 25 points of passive insurance, she is usually replaceable.

#### Evidence of actual Kestrel play

- **Report #005 — Kestrel versus Combined:** **Fennec + Crux Knight FTO + Bipandra** is an explicitly described **three-member Haris**. The battle report describes the group helping maintain mission access after a difficult Redeye opening. This supports the *combination of units*, not an assertion about the exact present-day 25-point profile.
- **Report #175 — CanCon 2025 winner, Day 1:** **Griffin Feuerbach + Bipandra Tactical Awareness + three Fennecs** in a five-model defensive Core. This is direct tournament usage. The **Day 2 list had a reported illegal simultaneous double-Haris configuration**, so do not use that separate configuration as precedent.
- **Report #158 — AdeptiCon 2026:** An alternate Maximus-oriented Kestrel list includes **Bipandra Doctor** with a Black A.I.R./Fennec specialist team. Exact membership and loadouts are not fully enumerated.
- **Irregular Order #52, September 2026 (approx. 1:12–1:17):** Reviewers disagree strongly: one reports near-automatic inclusion, while the other rarely takes her unless her Doctor and tactical activation are truly useful. This is **opinion evidence**, not a measured pick rate.

**Usage conclusion:** Bipandra has credible competitive use in multiple observed Kestrel builds, including a tournament winner. That establishes she is **played and effective in real lists**, but does **not** establish a reliable N5.3 pick percentage, that she personally performed a successful revive in each event, or that she belongs in every list.

#### Final recommendation

**Overall: A · Mobile high-value Fireteam: A+ · Standalone / idle Doctor: B.**

**Strong / Go-to, not Must-have.** Prefer **Bipandra Tactical Awareness (25/0)** when escorting an expensive, moving HI such as **Griffin, Hetkari or Crux Knight**, or the **Optimate Agent Maximus MI profile**. Her medical reliability, same-family Fireteam eligibility and useful extra activation can justify the investment together. For cheap link support or remote backfield healing, consider the Fennec Paramedic or Trauma-Doc + Palbot instead.
