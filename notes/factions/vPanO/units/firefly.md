## Original kestrel analysis — retained reference copy

The original is retained for transferable mechanics and reasoning. Sectorial ratings, profiles, AVA and linked packages are historical source-context only. The vanilla profile and notes above take precedence. Links retain original destinations.

### Fireflies — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Overall: A — excellent KESTREL Duo enablers, not universal auto-includes.** Engineer is the default offensive support; Minelayer is the default defensive/territorial support. The 19pt Repeater profile is an underrated budget FT2 partner. The main reason to buy a Firefly rather than inexpensive independent support is the combination of NCO, Albedo, equipment and Kestrel Counts-as.

#### Profiles

**AVA 5 · Regular MI · MOV 10-10 · CC17 · BS13 · PH11 · WIP13 · ARM2 · BTS6 · W1 · S2.** All profiles have Albedo, BS Attack (SR-1), Immunity (Shock), Stealth, NCO and Terrain (Total).

| Opt | Profile | Pts/SWC | Rating | Equipment / purpose |
|---:|---|---:|---|---|
| 4 | Engineer | 23/0 | A+ | SMG, Panzerfaust, D-Charges, AP Mine; Engineer, Deactivator, GizmoKit |
| 5 | Minelayer | 24/0 | A+ | SMG, Panzerfaust, D-Charges, AP Mine, Armed Turret (Marksman Rifle); Minelayer |
| 2 | Repeater | 19/0 | A− linked / C solo | SMG, Panzerfaust, D-Charges, Deployable Repeater; no mine |
| 7 | Hacker | 24/0.5 | B+ | SMG, Panzerfaust, D-Charges, AP Mine, Hacking Device |
| 6 | Paramedic | 25/0 | B− | Combi Rifle, Panzerfaust, D-Charges, AP Mine, MediKit |
| 1 | HRL | 24/1.5 | B− | Heavy Rocket Launcher, D-Charges, AP Mine, Assault Pistol; no Panzerfaust or Minelayer |
| 3 | Chain of Command | 23/0 | C+ | SMG, Panzerfaust, D-Charges, AP Mine, CoC |

All can join KESTREL Duos/Haris (Counts as Kestrel, max 3), or an ARMORED Duo as the optional Firefly (max 1; a Tikbalang/Squalos anchor is required). They cannot join FENNECS. Ratings are tactical judgments, not measured win rates.

The SMG/Panzerfaust profiles are useful support combatants: AP/Shock SMG ammunition for near threats, disposable long-range anti-armor threat, SR-1 on applicable BS Attacks, and D-Charges for eligible objectives. Albedo penalizes relevant MSV/Marksmanship attacks against the Firefly, not attacks against its teammates. Against ordinary non-MSV gunfighters Fireflies have no Mimetism and only one Wound. No native direct-template weapon on these Firefly profiles.

#### HRL versus Black A.I.R. sniper

| Characteristic | Firefly HRL | Black A.I.R. Neurocinetics MULTI Sniper |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | 24/1.5 | 33/1.5 |
| BS / ARM / W | 13 / 2 / 1 | 13 / 2 / 1 |
| BTS | 6 | 3 |
| Reactive Burst | 1 | 2 |
| Defensive modifiers | Albedo vs MSV/Marksmanship | Mimetism (-3), MSV2 |
| Threat | Impact template, Continuous Damage, SR-1 | Long-range MULTI ammunition, anti-Mimetism/Smoke |
| Active turn | Normal HRL Burst | Neurocinetics restricts active Burst |
| Fireteam | KESTREL Duo/Haris | FENNECS Core/Haris |

**Default primary visible ARO: Black A.I.R.** Its reactive B2, MSV2 and Mimetism generally outweigh the nine-point premium. The HRL has a distinct role as a cheaper, dangerous template ARO or anti-MSV counter, especially with FT2 without spending the Core or Haris slot. Its impact template punishes tightly spaced Fireteams; use hit opportunities rather than assuming it will survive an enemy attack turn.

**Observed architecture:** Firefly HRL + Firefly Engineer Duo (47/1.5 FT2; two Regular Orders, shared access to NCO, Engineer) was fielded by the AdeptiCon 2026 winner in [report #158](../../../battle-reports/151-160-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md). Modern prices are reconstructed. The Engineer does not heal the HRL Firefly's Wound.

Other options:
- **Griffin Feuerbach:** durable visible ARO with credible active-turn anti-armor capability.
- **Blink HRL:** surprise template/Hidden Deployment ambush; different role from a visible ARO.
- **Firefly HRL:** secondary lane, budget FT2 ARO, template against grouped enemies, or if Haris/Core are already committed.

Skip HRL when Black A.I.R. and/or Griffin Feuerbach already cover the required long lanes. Avoid pairing HRL and Feuerbach in the same Duo by default: overlapping guns, 3 SWC, awkward active/reactive positioning.

#### Repeater — cheapest NCO/FT2 partner

**19/0, four points cheaper than Engineer and five cheaper than Minelayer.** Retains Panzerfaust, SMG, D-Charges and native skills but loses the AP Mine and has no Specialist skill. The Deployable Repeater is Disposable (3), requires normal placement, and becomes usable after the placement Order resolves.

- Griffin HMG + Repeater Firefly = **56/1.5 FT2**; Feuerbach version = 57/1.5 FT2.
- Advance alongside the Griffin, leaving repeaters behind to extend a friendly Hacker's coverage over objectives, retreat routes and approaches. Unlike a mobile Repeater, the deployed token remains after the Firefly moves away.
- NCO can fund team movement and repeater placement, provided the Firefly leads and a Special Lieutenant Order remains available.
- Can also reinforce the DZ's hacking screen, but does not have Minelayer or a predeployment repeater.
- Do not pay for it solely to deploy repeaters if no friendly Hacker will exploit the network. Enemy hackers can also interact with your repeater network under the usual Firewall rules.

Compare Tech-Bee (very cheap independent repeater placement), Fennec Hacker16 (FastPanda plus Hacker), Pathfinder TA17 (mobile Repeater, Sensor, FO, genuine extra activation), and forward-deployed Peacemaker. The Firefly's reason to exist is the combination of **budget FT2, NCO, credible backup weapons and persistent network extension**, not superior raw repeater coverage. Even with weak repeater demand it can be a points-saving FT2 partner, but compare against other list architectures.

#### Engineer versus Minelayer

##### Engineer — default for active Griffin HMG

WIP13 specialist (65% first-roll unmodified success), Deactivator, GizmoKit, AP Mine and normal Firefly combat skills. D-Charges also cover eligible mission tasks.

- Recover a Griffin from eligible hacking-induced states (Isolated or Immobilized), rather than losing the main attack piece for the game. Engineer does **not** heal a Griffin's Wounds.
- Repair eligible STR units (TAGs, REMs) when realistically accessible.
- Handle objectives and enemy deployables; lay AP Mines at objectives or retreat positions.
- NCO can fund engineering, specialist actions, mine placement or team movement.
- For independent backfield repair, [Machinist + Palbot](../../kestrel/units/machinist.md) at 18/0 is often more efficient. A Firefly controlling a Palbot cannot remain linked; linked engineers need to reach their patient themselves.

##### Minelayer — default for defensive Griffin Feuerbach and territorial control

For one point more than Engineer, exchange technical specialist skills for a Marksman Rifle Armed Turret and Minelayer. The turret has BS10, Total Reaction, 360° Visor, ARM2, BTS3, STR1 and Perimeter placement. It is stationary, expendable, and reacts to enemy Models, not Markers; it does not generate Orders or occupy a trooper slot.

**Minelayer places ONE eligible deployable at deployment: AP Mine OR turret, never both for free.** Deploy the other later through normal placement. The turret's Perimeter trait allows placement wholly within ZoC subject to normal restrictions.

- Use the Griffin on the primary long firing lane, the turret on a separate approach and the mine to protect a blind corner, objective or retreat route.
- Keep the Firefly safe and avoid offering clustered template targets. The turret only needs to tax the opponent's Orders to justify itself.
- Offensive use: advance with HMG, then establish a turret/mine near a captured objective or crossing. A turret placed in the DZ is stationary when the Fireteam advances.
- Choose mine at deployment when the approach is better suited to a template or likely camouflaged intrusion; choose turret for a useful protected independent firing angle.

**Selection:** Engineer if the Griffin needs mobile hacking recovery, a specialist, or tech support. Minelayer if specialists/engineering are already covered and the list benefits more from extra first-turn defense and board control. The Minelayer is not a specialist.

#### Paramedic — inferior to Bipandra for medical work

| Partner/support | Cost | FT2 Griffin Duo? | Recovery / order utility |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Fennec Paramedic | 12 | No (FENNECS Core/Haris only) | Cheap MediKit |
| Trauma-Doc + Palbot | 17 | No | Independent WIP12 Doctor with remote reach |
| Bipandra Doctor | 23 | Yes | WIP13 Doctor (ReRoll -3), Nanopulser (+1B) |
| Bipandra Tactical Awareness | 25 | Yes | Doctor, Nanopulser, separate Tactical Order |
| Firefly Paramedic | 25 | Yes | MediKit, NCO, AP Mine, Panzerfaust, Combi Rifle |

Griffin PH12 gives a 60% MediKit recovery chance after a successful delivery (ranged MediKit also needs a hit). Bipandra's WIP13 plus native ReRoll at -3 reaches 82.5% before any other eligible reroll, and her TA profile generates its own Tactical Order regardless of the Lieutenant's plan.

Choose Firefly Paramedic only when NCO, Albedo, BTS6, mine/Panzerfaust or Bipandra's availability matter more than reliability and direct-template defense. Otherwise prefer Bipandra TA at exactly the same price, or cheap Fennec Paramedic in a FENNECS Fireteam. See [Bipandra](../../kestrel/units/bipandra.md) and [Fennecs](../../kestrel/units/fennecs.md).

#### Hacker — better platform, not automatic upgrade

| | Fennec | Firefly | Black A.I.R. FTO |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Pts/SWC | 16/0.5 | 24/0.5 | 26/0.5 |
| WIP | 12 | 13 | 13 |
| BTS | 3 | 6 | 3 |
| ARM | 0 | 2 | 2 |
| Network tool | FastPanda | — | — |
| NCO | No | Yes | No |
| Other weapons | SMG, E/Mitter | SMG, Panzerfaust, AP Mine, D-Charges | SMG, Nanopulser, D-Charges |
| Combat Instinct | No | No | Yes |
| KESTREL Duo | No | Yes | No |

**Fennec:** default economical backfield standard Hacker to exploit friendly Repeaters, provide reactive hacking pressure and Spotlight when useful; FastPanda extends network. Keep it safe from stronger enemy hackers.

**Firefly:** stronger mobile combat Hacker (WIP13/BTS6) and more appropriate when directly supporting a Griffin Duo, scoring or actively contesting enemy hackers/TAGs/HI. NCO funds its own hacking and team movement. It does not bring its own repeater projection. Beware concentrating two valuable Hackable targets in one Fireteam.

**Black A.I.R.:** distinct anti-Stealth role from Combat Instinct; useful in a defensive or specialist FENNECS formation.

The 8pt upgrade over Fennec buys real survivability and broader combat/FT utility, not just +1 WIP. The [Kestrel reference and fireteam notes](../../kestrel/kestrel-fireteams.md) document competitive criticism of the Fennec Hacker's WIP12/BTS3 platform. Nonetheless, for a modest defensive hacking network prefer Fennec Hacker plus a Firefly Engineer in their preferred roles over upgrading automatically.

#### Chain of Command — niche

23/0, a functional specialist but normally prefer the inexpensive [Magistrate CoC + Jackal](../../kestrel/units/magistrate.md) for independent protected command redundancy. Avoid exposing a critical CoC with an aggressive Griffin or using it as the primary active NCO just because it has the skill. Consider Firefly CoC with a defensive Duo or when the list cannot accommodate the Magistrate package and the Firefly's link/NCO/weapon utility is needed.

#### Best Griffin Duos

All below are legal KESTREL **FT2** Duos (+1 Special Die, not +1 Burst), with two Regular Orders, no Core/Haris commitment. Assumes Griffin HMG37/1.5 or Feuerbach38/1.5, not the HMG Lieutenant discount.

| Firefly partner | Griffin HMG pts/SWC | Rating | Griffin Feuerbach pts/SWC | Rating |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| Engineer | 60/1.5 | A+ | 61/1.5 | A |
| Minelayer | 61/1.5 | A | 62/1.5 | A+ |
| Repeater | 56/1.5 | A− | 57/1.5 | B+ |
| Hacker | 61/2 | A− | 62/2 | B+ |
| Paramedic | 62/1.5 | B | 63/1.5 | B |
| CoC | 60/1.5 | C+ | 61/1.5 | B− |
| HRL | 61/3 | C | 62/3 | C− |

**HMG + Engineer:** preferred all-purpose attack Duo. HMG/MSV1 primary gun, specialist, technical recovery, Panzerfaust, mine, Pulzar (Griffin's direct template). Griffin MOV15-5 and Firefly MOV10-10 each total 20cm/8in, but their first/second movement allocation differs. Avoid exposing the slower first-move Firefly unnecessarily.

**Feuerbach + Minelayer:** preferred defensive Duo. Long-range durable ARO, expendable turret, mine and Panzerfaust cover distinct approaches. Can later secure midfield. Feuerbach + Engineer is also a proven architecture in [report #002](../../../battle-reports/001-010-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md).

**NCO limitation:** Firefly must lead to spend the Lieutenant Order as a Fireteam activation. Griffin cannot be the active HMG/Feuerbach shooter on that Order. Use it for movement, applicable engineering/hacking/objective actions, deployables or Firefly shooting. Strategos conversion of the Lieutenant Order to Regular means that same resource cannot be spent by NCO. Multiple NCOs do not generate extra Orders. Griffin HMG Lieutenant costs **37/1** (same points, 0.5 SWC saving). Consider command risk separately.

Firefly Engineer is balanced but not universal: prefer Quinn when Eclipse is central, Bipandra for Wound recovery, Pathfinder for inexpensive mobile mission utility, and Repeater Firefly when points and FT2 are the priority. A KESTREL Haris (e.g. Griffin HMG + Firefly Engineer + Quinn Disco, 86/1.5 FT3; or Bipandra TA instead, 85/1.5 FT3) adds valuable support but costs the sole Haris. Often a Duo is enough. FENNECS compact Core with Griffin + cheap Disco/Paramedic Fennecs remains an economical alternative.

#### Quantities and list architecture

- **0:** reasonable for FENNECS-centered lists or other support architectures.
- **1:** usual choice for a Griffin Duo or a premium KESTREL Haris.
- **2:** worthwhile when each supports a distinct Griffin, or HRL + Engineer Duo is deliberate.
- **3+:** expensive and redundant unless every role is justified; multiple NCOs share the same Lieutenant Order.

Example: Griffin HMG + Engineer and Griffin Feuerbach + Minelayer = **122/3**, four Regular Orders, two FT2 Duos, Core and Haris both free. Attractive distributed architecture, but check whether the remaining points/SWC cover hacking, Eclipse, specialists and independent attackers. Not a mandatory foundation.

For ARMORED Duos, Firefly Engineer is legal with Squalos/Tikbalang but only FT1 (no same-unit bonus). Its lower movement and need for close repair may constrain the TAG; independent Machinist/Palbot or TA Pathfinder may be better. Maximus TAG + Firefly alone is not a legal ARMORED Duo.
