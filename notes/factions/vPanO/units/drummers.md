### Drummers (Mobile Support Section) — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: 21/0 dual Forward Observer A-tier; 47/1 MULTI HMG + Drumbot-5 A-tier; 47/1 Feuerbach + Drumbot-5 B+; 42/1.5 HRMC B+; other profiles niche.** The Drummer's defining advantage is **two or three separately positioned models for one trooper slot**. It is effectively a small Synchronized attack/utility group: multiple weapon origins and specialists activated with one Order, while leaving precious list slots free for Warcor, Sāchā, an infiltrating Jackal, Fugazi, etc. It is **not** a true Fireteam and does **not** generate two or three Regular Orders.

**Role:** Independent gunfighter, mission scorer and/or layered ARO defense outside Kestrel's Fireteam economy. The cheap dual-FO version is the most efficient mission pick; the 47-point MULTI HMG/HFT is the most versatile expensive pick. Do not automatically replace a good Core/Haris with a Drummer or automatically buy a 40-point weapon-only version.

#### Defining advantage: miniature Fireteam for one trooper slot

[Current N5.3 Peripheral rules](https://infinitythewiki.com/Peripheral) explicitly count a Controller and **all** its Peripherals as **one trooper** when creating an Army List and determining Combat Group size. This compresses several **physical models** into **one of the 15 trooper slots** in a standard 300-point list, not three separate army entries.

| Purchase | Physical models on table | Trooper slots | Regular Orders produced | Fireteam usage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **21-point Drummer + Drumbot-5** | **2** | **1** | **1** | None |
| **47-point Drummer + two Drumbots** | **3** | **1** | **1** | None |
| Three-member FENNECS Haris/Core | 3 | 3 | 3 | Uses Haris or Core |
| Five-member FENNECS Core | 5 | 5 | 5 | Uses Core |

This is an **especially valuable list-building feature when nearing 15 troopers**. A 47-point Drummer occupies one entry while putting three separately positioned shooting/ARO/specialist origins on the board. Relative to purchasing three independent models, it **preserves two slots** that can be spent on a **3-point Warcor**, **Sāchā**, a **10–11-point infiltrating Jackal**, a **7-point Fugazi**, or another useful entry. The freed slots still require enough remaining points and must respect AVA and group limits; the Drummer does not create extra Orders.

**Why this is not a real Fireteam:**
- One Order activates **controller and Synchronized Drumbots together**; they declare **the same Short Skills** (or Long Skill), but may choose **different targets**. A model unable to fulfil a declared Skill Idles.
- All models keep their own eligible **reactive AROs**, but their **ARO Skill declarations must match** (e.g., all declare BS Attack, with individually legal weapons/targets). They cannot freely choose independent skills such as one Dodging and another shooting.
- Every Drumbot must maintain **8" coherency to the controller**, checked at the required times. The human's 4–4" MOV and need for a safe position limit exploitation of Drumbots' 6–4" MOV.
- A controller in **Null or Isolated** State disconnects the Drumbots at the end of the Order; an Isolated Drumbot disconnects itself. This creates a **single vulnerable point of failure** despite having multiple bodies.
- Synchronized Peripherals and their Controller **cannot join a Fireteam or Coordinated Order**. No FT2/FT3/FT4/FT5 bonuses, no independently activated peripherals, no extra order generation.

**Deployment principle:** Keep the fragile ARM1/VITA1 human out of easy LoF while the Mimetism (-3) Drumbots cover relevant lanes and corners. Avoid clustering the three models into one enemy template or leaving the controller exposed just to gain a marginal firing angle.

#### The 21/0 dual Forward Observer: most efficient mission choice

**The default Drummer to evaluate whenever the mission rewards specialist interaction.** For 21/0, it brings:
- **Two specialist models** (controller WIP12, Drumbot-5 WIP11), potentially contesting objectives from different positions.
- **Two Flash Pulses**, 360° Visors, and a BS13 human **Marksman Rifle** for competent supplementary fire.
- **Heavy Flamethrower** on the Mimetism (-3) Drumbot for close-range roadblocking, direct-template threat and defensive trading.
- **Two separate physical models for one troop slot**, still yielding one Regular Order.

**Dual-roll objective interaction:** If the mission allows both to attempt the *same* objective in the same activation, both meet positioning/skill requirements, and either successful roll suffices, the combined chance before MODs is **1 - (1 - 12/20) × (1 - 11/20) = 82%**. This compares with **60%** for WIP12 alone or **55%** for WIP11 alone. **Do not assume every mission permits simultaneous duplicate attempts**: read the specific console/objective requirements; any model not meeting them Idles for that Skill. The double specialist also helps score **different objectives** across sequential Orders, provided coherency is maintained.

The *Heavy Flamethrower + Flash Pulse* is not incidental: it gives the specialist pair meaningful defensive presence even if the controller is hidden and no more button-pressing is required.

**Compared with:**
- **Fennec FO 13/0**: 8 points cheaper, **Sensor + Disco Baller**, can join FENNECS Fireteam, more economical Regular Order; lacks synchronized extra FO/HFT.
- **Pathfinder FO 14/0**: faster independent REM with **Sensor, Repeater, Deactivator**, can be a Wildcard; no second FO/HFT.
- **Scarecrow FO 25/0**: infiltration, Hidden Deployment, camouflage/Mimetism (-6) for initial midfield access; very different activation and deployment plan.

**Take it:** objective-heavy missions, lists with enough Orders already, need an inexpensive HFT deterrent while keeping one slot for two models, or the main attack plan is a TAG/Fireteam rather than the Drummer.

**Skip it:** specialists already saturate the list, need Disco Baller/Sensor/Repeater much more, have too few Regular Orders, or the mandatory 8" sync bubble makes objective access impractical.

#### MULTI HMG versus HRMC: pure firepower vs package efficiency

| Feature | 40/1 MULTI HMG | 42/1.5 HRMC | 47/1 MULTI HMG + FO/HFT |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heavy-gun Drumbot | Drumbot-1 | Drumbot-2 | Drumbot-1 |
| Standard anti-armour burst | **B4 AP** | **B5 AP** | **B4 AP** |
| Other useful mode | B4 Shock; B1 EXP | B5 Shock; B1 DA | B4 Shock; B1 EXP |
| Beyond 32" | HMG negative range MOD | **HRMC retains 0 MOD through 48"** | HMG negative range MOD |
| Human FO + Flash Pulse | No | No | **Yes** |
| FO/HFT Drumbot-5 | No | No | **Yes** |
| Models / slots | 2/1 | 2/1 | **3/1** |

**HRMC wins as a dedicated active-turn heavy attacker.** B5 AP instead of B4 AP is a genuine advantage against heavily armoured targets, as is its forgiving 32–48" range band. The HRMC costs **only +2 points** over the basic MULTI HMG, but **+0.5 SWC**; if you specifically need one high-output long-range gun and already have specialists and close defense covered, HRMC makes sense.

**The 47-point MULTI HMG + Drumbot-5 is usually the stronger army-building purchase.** Compared with HRMC it costs **+5 points but saves 0.5 SWC**, adds a *third* physical model for the same slot, two FOs, two Flash Pulses and a HFT. Compared with basic 40/1 MULTI HMG it costs **+7 points, no extra SWC** for a major increase in mission and defensive utility.

**When the basic 40/1 MULTI HMG survives consideration:** exact seven-point gap funds another important order/specialist, the mission provides no realistic use for the dual FO, or a third synchronized model creates unwelcome deployment/coherency congestion. Otherwise the 47/1 is preferable.

**EVO interaction:** [Assisted Fire / Marksmanship on the attack REM](../../kestrel/units/mulebot-baggagebot.md#assisted-fire-best-for-active-gunfighting-rems) can help the HRMC or MULTI HMG Drumbot shoot covered targets; the Drummer controller is **LI**, not the REM recipient. It costs an Order and the correct target/range conditions are still necessary. This is a reason to consider EVO **if it already has multiple applications**, not to buy EVO automatically for one Drummer. Verify any disputed Peripheral-targeting interaction using current Army/rule interpretation.

#### Feuerbach vs Black A.I.R. Neurocinetics sniper

| Attribute | 40/1 Drummer Feuerbach | 47/1 Feuerbach + FO/HFT | Black A.I.R. Neuro MULTI Sniper 33/1.5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary ARO | **Drumbot-3 Feuerbach (+1B), Neurocinetics** | Same | **MULTI Sniper Rifle, Neurocinetics** |
| Reactive attack | B3 AP+DA or B2 EXP (Feuerbach modes) | Same | B2 AP / Shock (MULTI sniper modes) |
| Gun model traits | BS13, Mimetism (-3), 360° | Same | BS13, Mimetism (-3), **MSV2** |
| Human controller vulnerability | Yes | Yes | No Synchronized controller |
| Mission specialist / HFT | No | **Dual FO + HFT** | Neither on sniper profile |
| Fireteam access | No | No | **FENNECS Core/Haris FTO** |
| Trooper slots / models | 1 / 2 | **1 / 3** | 1 / 1 |

**Feuerbach advantages:** high-impact **AP+DA or EXP** punishment for TAGs/HI, Mimetism (-3), 360° ARO coverage, distributed physical positions, and only 1 SWC. **With Drumbot-5** it becomes an actual defensive-scoring hybrid: anti-armour lane plus HFT close cover and two Forward Observers.

**Black A.I.R. advantages:** **MSV2** handles Mimetism and visibility threats that Kestrel otherwise struggles with; it is cheaper, has no fragile controller to protect, and can join FENNECS Fireteams. It remains a better **dedicated long-range anti-Mimetism ARO pick** in many lists.

**Recommendation:** If all you need is one efficient long-range defensive gun, usually **Black A.I.R. sniper**; if a 47-point independent slot-compressed **anti-armour ARO + objective/HFT package** is useful, choose **Feuerbach + Drumbot-5**. The **40-point Feuerbach-only** profile is harder to justify against Black A.I.R.; it is a niche SWC-saving heavy ARO choice. The Feuerbach Drumbot is not an anti-camo substitute for MSV2.

**Important practical distinction:** On the Drummer's synchronized AROs, the controller and bot must declare the same ARO **skill**, though their attacks can use different weapons and targets. The HFT cannot independently flame while its controller elects to Dodge.

#### Remaining profiles: when, if ever?

**28/1.5 MULTI Sniper + Drumbot-5 (B):** Long-range human sniper with **BS Attack (-3)**, still dual FO and HFT. It is only **+7 points over 21/0** for the sniper but costs **1.5 SWC**, the same as Black A.I.R. Neuro sniper. Take when a specialist pair needs a long-range gun and the controller can remain reasonably protected; do not expect it to replace the MSV2/Mimetism(-3) defensive Black A.I.R. sniper. A sniper human controller exposed to LoF is a serious vulnerability.

**42/1 Mk12 (+1B) + Heavy Rocket Launcher, FD (+4") (B-/niche):** Both human and bot start 4" forward. The B4 Mk12 and HRL support an aggressive mid-range attack / anti-cluster plan; unlike the FO/HFT options, this profile lacks specialist objectives and a reactive HFT roadblock. Use for a deliberately forward attack profile with short approach geometry, not as an all-purpose Drummer.

**40/1 Feuerbach-only (B-/niche):** Strong specialist anti-armour ARO but missing the HFT/FO utility. It is **+7 points over Black A.I.R.**, though it saves 0.5 SWC and gains the synchronized two-model geometry. Choose only with a specific anti-heavy ARO plan.

**40/1 MULTI HMG-only (C+/usually skip):** Still a credible BS13 Mimetism(-3) REM heavy gun, but the **47/1** profile adds two objective-capable FOs, HFT, Flash Pulses and a third physical model at unchanged SWC. The cheaper profile is a points-trim option, not the default.

#### Drummer versus a Fireteam — **legal** Kestrel comparisons

**Fireteam legality is important:** Per the [pinned Kestrel Fireteam chart](../../../../sectorials/panoceania/kestrel-colonial-force.md#fireteams), **FENNECS support Core or Haris, not Duo**. Do **not** propose a Griffin + one or two Fennecs as a Duo: a three-model Griffin + two Fennecs is instead a **3-member FENNECS Core or Haris**. The Griffin counts as Fennec for matching FT levels, but at least one *actual* Fennec is required. A 3-model Core may be preferable to preserving the sole Haris slot; **KESTREL** has legal Duo/Haris combinations of its own.

**Concrete example:** 
- **47/1 Drummer with MULTI HMG + Drumbot-5:** one Regular Order; three physical models; **one** troop slot, dual FO/HFT.
- **60/1.5 Griffin HMG 37 + Fennec FO/Disco 13 + basic Fennec 10, as legal FENNECS Core or Haris:** **three Regular Orders**, three troop slots, BS14 Griffin HMG with FT2 +1SD and FT3 Discover/Dodge bonus. Griffin is **ARM4, 2 Wounds, MSV1**, significantly more durable as the primary gun.
- **64/1.5 Griffin HMG 37 + FO/Disco 13 + Fennec Minelayer 14:** three slots/Orders; adds a prepaid defensive mine; still a legal **3-model Core/Haris**.
- **80/1.5 Griffin HMG 37 + Fennec FO/Disco 13 + three basic Fennecs 10, as a 5-model FENNECS Core:** five troop slots and **five Regular Orders**, FT5 including +1 BS, +1SD and Sixth Sense (with eligible counts-as matches). Expensive in points and slots but stronger face-to-face odds than the solo Drummer.

| Decision factor | **47-point Drummer (3 bodies/1 slot)** | **60-point Griffin + 2 Fennecs (3 bodies/3 slots)** |
|---|---|---|
| Up-front cost | **13 points and 0.5 SWC cheaper** | +13 points/+0.5 SWC |
| Regular Order economy | **1** | **3** |
| Slot economy, especially at 15 | **Exceptional** | Occupies three list entries |
| Attack quality / durability | Good BS13 MULTI HMG, but REM STR1 and vulnerable controller | **BS14 +1SD HMG, MSV1, ARM4, W2** |
| Mission scoring | **Two FOs** | One FO + Disco/Sensor |
| Short-range defensive template | **Separate HFT model** | Griffin Pulzar |
| Movement/activation | Several simultaneous attack origins, constrained to same skill and coherency | Fireteam leader/shooting rules; team coherency |
| Fireteam opportunity cost | **No Core/Haris consumed** | Uses Core or **sole Haris** |

**Correct strategic conclusion:** The Fireteam generally has **better raw fighting resilience and more Regular Orders**. The Drummer has **better one-slot model density, cheaper mixed-role capabilities, and doesn't consume Fireteam allowances**. You are not just comparing HMG stats; you are deciding whether three independent Regular Order generators and Fireteam bonuses are worth two extra trooper slots, 13 points and 0.5 SWC.

**Especially strong Drummer use case:** Your list already contains a TAG or a strong FENNECS Core / KESTREL Haris (or a Magistrate/Jackal Haris), and you're at **13–15 troopers**. A **47/1 Drummer** adds a three-model support/attack/scoring presence using one slot, leaving room for a **Warcor, Sāchā, infiltrating Jackal or Fugazi** that would be squeezed out by adding several separate models. This is a **major defining advantage**, not a marginal bonus. With ample available trooper slots and too few Orders, building the Core/Haris or adding regular cheap troopers first can be better.

**Additional list-building caveat:** Drumbots must be deployed and maneuvered safely. Multiple S2 models around a controller need cover and coherency, and a human VITA1 controller falling to a single attack can disable the entire package. They do *not* replace the independent board coverage, ability to split into groups, or Order supply of three separate troopers.

| Army / mission situation | Drummer recommendation |
|---|---|
| Specialist-intensive objectives / scarce scoring attempts | **21/0 dual FO** |
| At or near 15-trooper cap; still need several bodies in one slot | **47/1 MULTI HMG+HFT**, or Feuerbach+HFT if defense is priority |
| Need extra independent active-turn firepower without consuming Core/Haris | **47/1 MULTI HMG+HFT** |
| Need best B5 AP volume and have sufficient specialists | **42/1.5 HRMC** |
| Need anti-armour ARO *and* button pressing / close-defense template | **47/1 Feuerbach+HFT** |
| Need one MSV2 anti-Mimetism ARO | **Black A.I.R. Neuro sniper**, not Feuerbach Drummer |

#### Practical play and sources

- **Use different angles, not a clump:** the controller should remain hard to shoot while Drumbots cover orthogonal approach lanes without leaving 8" coherency. A three-model Drummer can demand several distinct decisions from an opponent, but all share one vulnerable controller.
- **Match common Skills deliberately:** moving and then shooting separate enemies in the same Order is legal with Synchronized Peripherals if each can perform the declared Skills; trying to have one Dodge while another Shoots is not. In the reactive turn they may each produce eligible AROs, but must choose the **same ARO Skill**.
- **Do not assume the bot can rush 6–4" ahead while the 4–4" human stays in the DZ**. It must remain coherent.
- **Specialist interaction is scenario-specific:** simultaneous double FO attempts rely on the exact mission and both models' eligibility, not a universal promise of 82% on every console.
- **The anti-armour Drumbots are hackable REMs**, while the human LI is not automatically a hackable REM. Enemy hacking can stop a gun bot even if the controller survives.
- **Evidence is limited:** one contemporary [Corvus Belli community report](https://forum.corvusbelli.com/threads/what-about-kfc-black-air-and-hetkari.43386/) mentions an HRMC Drummer buffed by Marksmanship being quickly lost to a critical hit—an illustration of fragility, **not** statistical evidence that HRMC is bad. Rankings here are tactical judgments from the pinned profiles/rules, not a quantified tournament pick-rate study.
