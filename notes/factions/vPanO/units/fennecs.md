### FO / Sensor / Disco Baller — 13/0

**Default purchase.** Three points above a basic line infantry model buys a Regular Order with mission specialization, Flash Pulse, Sensor and two initial disposable Eclipse uses. Eclipse bypasses ordinary LoF even against MSV, but does not stop hacking or retroactively cancel AROs during the placement Order. Place it from a safe position to cross lanes, isolate overlapping AROs or reach objectives. Baggage can replenish uses. Sensor can reveal eligible markers within ZoC on WIP18 (90% Normal Roll).

| Alternative | Pts / SWC | Distinct value |
|---|---:|---|
| Fennec FO | 13 / 0 | Eclipse, Sensor |
| Fusilier FO | 12 / 0 | Deployable Repeater |
| Pathfinder FO | 14 / 0 | Mobile Sensor, Repeater, Deactivator |
| Pathfinder FO TA | 17 / 0 | Extra personal activation |
| Blocker SO | 15 / 0 | Pitcher, Sensor, adhesive and direct-template utility |

The Fennec offers unique Eclipse utility among these alternatives, not a replacement for every other network or Sensor piece. Unlike Kestrel, the shooter and the supported attacker must move separately. Solo B1 direct-fire placement at 8–16 inches is 75% (BS15 after range MOD), with no Fireteam Special Die. Double Shot can increase reliability at the cost of ammunition. Consider a second Fennec for opposite-flank coverage or four initial Eclipse uses; avoid automatically duplicating Sensor if coverage is already ample.

### Standard Hacker / FastPanda — 16/0.5

**Excellent economical network support.** Keep it in Total Cover, often Group 2, using allied Repeaters across combat groups. FastPanda can extend local coverage via a deployable Repeater placed within ZoC; it does not require a BS Roll. E/Mitter provides a separate LoF-based anti-tech threat (B2 active), not a hacking program.

| Hacker | Pts / SWC | Distinct value |
|---|---:|---|
| Fusilier | 15 / 0.5 | Cheapest standard HD |
| Fennec | 16 / 0.5 | BTS3, FastPanda, E/Mitter |
| Blocker | 19 / 0.5 | WIP13, Firewall −3, Sensor |
| Firefly | 24 / 0.5 | NCO, BTS6, tougher chassis |
| Scarecrow | 29 / 0.5 | Hidden Deployment, Infiltration, midfield specialist |

The Fennec generally supersedes the Fusilier Hacker for one extra point. The Blocker is the principal backfield alternative when hacking resilience matters more than FastPanda projection (the Blocker HD profile does not have the SO profile's Pitcher). Firefly and Scarecrow justify premiums through mobility, order economy or forward positioning. Take a Fennec HD only if the network and matchup justify it; a KHD alone does not provide standard HD control programs.

### Thunderbolt (+1B) / Minelayer — 14/0

**Defensive order generator.** A predeployed Shock Mine adds protection without spending Orders. The normal-ammunition PS6 Thunderbolt is B3 active, B1 reactive, with range MODs −3 at 0–8 inches, 0 at 8–16, +3 at 16–32 and 0 at 32–48. Triangulated Fire is a situational active-turn Long Skill against extreme shooting MODs, not a reason to expose a fragile BS12 ARM0 model as a primary gun. It does not negate Cover's saving-roll benefit.

| Alternative | Pts / SWC | Defensive role |
|---|---:|---|
| Fugazi | 7 / 0 | Flash Pulse, Repeater |
| RacerBot Mk-II | 9 / 0 | PARA Mine Dispenser, Heavy Riotstopper |
| Helot Camo LRL | 12 / 0 | Concealed Neurocinetics ARO, Irregular unless converted |
| Fennec Minelayer | 14 / 0 | Predeployed Shock Mine, secondary gun |

Joan makes Helots particularly competitive by converting their Orders while Inspiring Leadership applies. Neither a Helot ARO nor a RacerBot's carried equipment directly replaces a deployment-time Shock Mine. Choose the Fennec when screening a Lieutenant, Hacker, or uncovered entry lane matters.

### Lieutenant and basic Combi — 10/0 each

A budget LT is more interesting in vanilla than Kestrel because independent NCO options (e.g. Karhu) can spend its Lieutenant Order. Two identical Combi Fennecs cost 20/0 and supply two Regular Orders plus the LT Order; this conceals identity but is **not the Decoy skill**. An identical Fusilier pair costs the same (ARM1/BTS0 vs ARM0/BTS3). Magistrate Strategos LT (19/0) offers Counterintelligence, specialist utility and conversion of the LT Order; adding a Fugazi costs 26/0 for two Regular troopers. A basic Fennec otherwise competes poorly with cheaper Fugazi, Mulebot and RacerBots or the upgraded Fennec profiles.

### Lower-priority profiles

- **HMG / Missile Launcher:** Skip; fragile BS12 shooting without Fireteam bonuses is inferior to dedicated independent attackers or ARO pieces.
- **SMG/LRL:** usually skip; Helot offers Neurocinetics and optional Camouflage, with an Irregular-order tradeoff outside Joan lists.
- **Commlink SO:** usually skip; one more point buys the FO/Sensor/Disco package unless Commlink is specifically required.
- **Paramedic:** situational cheap mission specialist. Without shared movement, Trauma-Doc + Palbot or Bipandra is generally preferable for dedicated recovery; patient PH and accessibility still matter.
- **Basic Combi:** budget filler or identical-LT concealment only.

## Original kestrel analysis — retained reference copy

The original is retained for transferable mechanics and reasoning. Sectorial ratings, profiles, AVA and linked packages are historical source-context only. The vanilla profile and notes above take precedence. Links retain original destinations.

### Fennec Fusiliers — Kestrel Colonial Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A+ overall; S-tier utility on the FO/Disco and Hacker profiles.** Start with **one 13pt FO/Sensor/Disco Baller and one 16pt/0.5SWC standard Hacker** in most lists with a useful Repeater network. Add a **14pt Minelayer** for deployment-zone protection. Taking **3–5 upgraded Fennecs** is entirely reasonable, particularly with both an offensive Core and a defensive Haris. The limiting factor is often the 15-trooper cap and Haris opportunity cost, rather than points. The basic Combi and basic Lieutenant are both **C-tier**.

#### FO / Sensor / Disco Baller — 13 points

**Default purchase.** The three-point upgrade from a basic Fennec buys a specialist, two initial Disposable Eclipse uses, Sensor and Flash Pulse, while retaining the Regular Order and same-family Fireteam contribution.

- **Disco Baller:** Targetless BS weapon, Disposable (2), Double Shot, Speculative Attack. On a successful shot it places an Eclipse zone at the Conclusion of the Order. Eclipse blocks ordinary LoF even for MSV-equipped troops. It does not protect against hacking, and does not retroactively cancel AROs against the shooter during the placement Order. Use safe firing positions, not a face-to-face duel with a sniper.
- **FO:** Cheap mission specialist and access to Forward Observer/Targeted; Flash Pulse adds a useful nonlethal ARO.
- **Sensor:** WIP12 +6 = **18 (90%)** for the Normal Roll to reveal eligible Camouflage/Hidden Deployment within ZoC, without LoF. Also prevents enemy Camouflage re-entry within ZoC and strengthens ordinary Discover against Camouflage. It does not mass-reveal Impersonators.
- **Baggage:** A Mulebot can replenish expended Disco Baller uses. A deployed ball can be reactivated by an eligible allied Deactivator user (not by the Fennec merely because it carries a Disco Baller). Eclipse deactivates at the start of the States Phase, but the device can remain on the table.

##### Direct-fire Eclipse reliability

For a single direct-fire B1 placement, no additional MODs, and an applicable FT2 Special Die:

| Range | Range MOD | BS12 solo | FT2 +1SD |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0–8" | 0 | 60% | 84% |
| 8–16" | +3 | 75% | 93.75% |
| 16–24" | −3 | 45% | 69.75% |
| 24–32" | −6 | 30% | 51% |

The extra Special Die improves the roll without spending extra ammunition. It does not apply to Long Skills such as Speculative Attack. Fennecs can also exploit Double Shot when appropriate, but ammunition is finite.

##### Why two Disco Ballers in the same offensive Fireteam are good

Two cost **26 points / 0 SWC**, just one point more than Disco + Paramedic. Four initial Eclipse uses are not excessive for an aggressive team that may need to cross multiple lanes, isolate overlapping AROs, bypass a bad gunfight, score objectives and retreat. Both models bring FO/Sensor/Flash Pulse; the second is not simply a spare grenade launcher. If one dies or must split off, the other can retain the team's Eclipse capability.

Only the active Team Leader performs the relevant attack or specialist action. Two linked Disco Ballers do not both shoot in the same ordinary Fireteam Order.

**Prefer two Disco Ballers** when flexible movement is the main priority, when another medical package exists, or when the primary attacker is fragile or NWI. Prefer **Disco + Paramedic** when a valuable recoverable infantry gunfighter is likely to need medical support. If two independently positioned Eclipse sources matter more than moving both together, distribute them between teams or Combat Groups.

##### What a Disco Baller does in a defensive ARO team

Its main contribution is **army-wide Active Turn utility, not persistent defensive Eclipse**.

- A defensive Haris may cover the opposite flank from the offensive Core, placing a second Eclipse source in a different useful part of the map.
- A Group 2 Fennec may spend Group 2 Orders opening a crossing for Group 1 attackers, avoiding expenditure of their attack Orders.
- Eclipse can block a lane to permit medical recovery of the sniper, safe relocation/redeployment of the defensive team, or late-game objective access.
- The Fennec can leave the team later and act as an independent FO if mission needs outweigh the lost composition bonus.

These benefits depend on the Fennec having useful range and LoF to the desired placement point (or a viable Speculative Attack). A deeply tucked-back Fennec on an irrelevant flank is not automatically useful merely because it has Eclipse. **Do not take two Disco Ballers in a dedicated stationary ARO Haris by default.** Minelayers and a Hacker provide more genuine reactive value.

#### Thunderbolt (+1B) Minelayer — 14 points

**A+ for just four points above basic.** One prepaid Shock Mine, a credible long-range secondary gun and Triangulated Fire are exceptional zero-SWC upgrades. Prefer one in a balanced list and consider two when deployment-zone coverage is insufficient.

- **Minelayer:** Deploy one eligible Shock Mine legally within ZoC at deployment, spending one of its uses but no Orders. Cover rear approaches, airborne entry areas, command models, stationary snipers or the Hacker. Do not expose the Fennec itself merely to try to trade with an apex attacker. Other mines can be placed later, including in midfield if the Fennec advances with a Core.
- **Thunderbolt (+1B):** Standard Thunderbolt PS6, normal ammunition; B3 in the Active Turn, ordinarily B1 in ARO before any applicable Special Die. Range MODs: −3 at 0–8", 0 at 8–16", +3 at 16–32", 0 at 32–48". Stronger ranged stopping power and reach than the Combi Rifle (PS7), but poorer at close range. It is not the AP Thunderbolt.
- **Triangulated Fire:** Active Turn Long Skill, requires LoF; ignores BS Roll MODs such as Mimetism, range and Cover, but does not remove Cover's saving-roll bonus, allow fire through Eclipse, or increase maximum range. At 16–32" against Mimetism (−6) in Cover, normal shooting is BS6 whereas Triangulated Fire is BS12, before any applicable exceptions. No FT2 Special Die for this Long Skill.

Two Minelayers can cover separate deployment-zone approaches and remain useful without moving. Their value decreases if the army already has sufficient Jackal, Peacemaker, Drummer or other screening coverage. A Fennec's deployment-zone mine complements an infiltrating Jackal's midfield mine; they are not identical roles.

#### Standard Hacker / FastPanda — 16 points, 0.5 SWC

**S-tier list-building priority in normal Repeater-rich Kestrel lists.** Take at least one standard Hacking Device in most such lists. The Hacker makes the Repeater network an actual offensive and reactive threat: Spotlight, Carbonite, Oblivion and Total Control where legal. A KHD or EVO alone cannot replace these programs. Repeaters themselves are not switched off without a Hacker; rather, their useful standard-program threat is absent.

##### Other Hacker options

| Hacker | Pts / SWC | Why pay more? |
|---|---:|---|
| **Fennec** | **16 / 0.5** | Cheapest standard HD, FastPanda, E/Mitter |
| Firefly | 24 / 0.5 | NCO, BTS6, tougher chassis and mines |
| Banshee | 25 / 0.5 | Airborne offensive delivery |
| Black A.I.R. FTO | 26 / 0.5 | MSV2, Mimetism, template weapon |
| Scarecrow | 29 / 0.5 | Concealed forward deployment and midfield specialist role |

The alternatives may be better at particular jobs, but are not dramatically better merely as standard Hackers. The Fennec is the best pure economical access purchase. Firefly is particularly attractive if a stronger hacking chassis is needed. A KHD Banshee or Scarecrow can complement, not necessarily replace, the Fennec.

##### FastPanda and E/Mitter

FastPanda is a one-use Deployable Repeater placed completely within the user's ZoC by Place Deployable, without a BS Roll. Its own ZoC extends hacking coverage another 8", giving roughly 16" of projection from the Fennec in a favorable direction. It can be placed through suitable terrain geometry without exposing the Fennec to an unnecessary gunfight. The network becomes relevant after legal deployment; follow up with hacking in subsequent Orders.

The E/Mitter supplies a separate anti-tech threat requiring LoF rather than a Repeater: B2 in the Active Turn, E/M (two saves against halved BTS), +3 at 16–32", and 0 at 32–48". The SMG supplies short-range shooting. A linked Hacker can benefit from FT2 +1SD for the E/Mitter, **not from that BS Attack bonus for hacking programs**.

##### Positioning: backfield by default, advancing when justified

| Deployment | Benefits | Risks / costs |
|---|---|---|
| Independent backfield Hacker (often Group 2) | Safest order generator, uses distributed allied Repeaters, local FastPanda defense, does not consume FT slot | Needs independent Orders to advance; restricted local Panda deployment |
| Defensive Haris | Combines a useful static hacking role with FT composition and E/Mitter SD; can protect a sniper position | Do not cluster all three models where a template or enemy push can remove them |
| Offensive Core | Moves alongside the main attacker, projects FastPanda forward, accesses objectives | Risks exposing the only Hacker to enemy KHDs, templates and gunfire |

**Preserve the only standard Hacker whenever possible.** It can advance later to place the Panda, score or use the E/Mitter. Keep it in Total Cover, and recognize that an enemy Repeater or remote hacking attack can still threaten it even when physically protected. Hacking Area is not restricted to the Hacker's Combat Group; Group 2 can provide coverage to Group 1's Repeaters. A second standard Hacker is situational, not a default requirement.

#### Paramedic — 12 points

**A−; good linked support, not a mandatory medical purchase.** Paying two points above basic buys another mission specialist and possible recovery of expensive VITA infantry. Its value is highest when it already travels with an attacker and can safely reach or shoot a fallen model. Normal MediKit use requires an allied VITA model in Unconscious State; it is not generic healing of wounded, still-active or NWI models. It is not a repair tool for normal STR casualties.

| Medical package | Cost | Contact recovery | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Fennec Paramedic | 12 | Patient PH (60% for PH12) | Inexpensive linked specialist |
| Trauma-Doc | 14 | WIP12 (60%) | Independent Doctor |
| Trauma-Doc + Palbot | 17 | WIP12 (60%) | Independent remote recovery |
| Bipandra | 23 | 82.5% with Doctor (ReRoll −3) | Linked Doctor, short-range template |
| Bipandra Tactical Awareness | 25 | 82.5% with Doctor (ReRoll −3) | Linked Doctor and extra personal/team movement activation |

Doctor success above excludes any applicable Command Token rerolls. A ranged MediKit must first hit, then the patient rolls PH. FT2 +1SD can improve the hit, not the patient's PH. A failed patient PH roll can kill the casualty.

- **Griffin or Crux Knight:** Disco + Paramedic is attractive because a two-Wound premium gunfighter can be worth recovering. Compare against the extra Eclipse use and specialist redundancy of two Disco Ballers.
- **Black A.I.R. sniper:** A nearby Paramedic can recover it with little movement; a Trauma-Doc with Palbot may offer safer reach and cover several separate ARO positions.
- **Maximus Agent:** NWI reduces ordinary medical recovery opportunities; prioritize two Disco Ballers or other utility.
- **Bipandra:** Pay the premium only when reliable Doctor recovery, Counts-as composition, close defense and/or Tactical Awareness justify it. Her Tactical Order requires her to lead the team if used as a Fireteam activation.

Do not buy a standalone Fennec Paramedic by default. Skip medical support entirely if the list is dominated by STR units, cheap expendable infantry, or attackers with poor recovery prospects. See [Trauma-Doc](../../kestrel/units/trauma-doc.md) and [Bipandra](../../kestrel/units/bipandra.md).

#### Basic Combi and Lieutenant — both C-tier

The opportunity cost of selecting a 10pt basic Fennec is substantial: +2 buys a Paramedic, +3 buys FO/Sensor/Disco, +4 buys Minelayer/Thunderbolt. For pure Orders, consider the 7pt Fugazi, 8pt Mulebot or a Magistrate/Jackal package first.

**Basic Combi:** Use only as a final points-saving measure, inexpensive same-family FT filler when no utility upgrade fits, or a genuine identical-LT decoy.

**Basic Lieutenant:** Functional but weak relative to Kestrel alternatives. Its main selling points are minimum price, possible concealed identity among identical Combi Fennecs and a personal LT Order that an eligible NCO can spend. Do not assume Decoy skill on this pinned profile. Buying another plain Fennec to hide it sacrifices a better upgrade.

| Command package | Cost | Regular Orders | Comparison |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Fennec LT + basic Fennec | 20 / 0 | 2 | Inexpensive, possible identity concealment |
| Magistrate Strategos LT + Jackal FTO | 25 / 0 | 2 if FT Master conditions met at Order Count | Strategos, Counterintelligence, specialist, economical launcher/template defense |

For five points more, the Magistrate package is usually worth considering. Aggressive LT options can be appropriate when their personal Orders are genuinely usable and the command risk is acceptable (or protected by CoC). See [Magistrate](../../kestrel/units/magistrate.md) and [Jackals](../../kestrel/units/jackals.md).

#### Defensive Haris: Black A.I.R. Neurocinetics Sniper

The 33pt/1.5SWC Black A.I.R. MULTI Sniper plus two Fennecs reaches **FT2**; the Sniper does not count as Fennec. The Special Die is valuable in its reactive Burst 2 shooting, but the Fennecs should also justify their own cost.

|---|---:|---|---|

#### Dual three-trooper Fireteam architecture

**Preferred example:**

| Offensive FENNECS Core (Group 1) | Defensive FENNECS Haris (Group 2) |
|---|---|
| Griffin HMG 37/1.5 | Black A.I.R. Sniper 33/1.5 |
| Fennec FO/Disco 13 | Fennec Minelayer 14 |
| Fennec FO/Disco 13 or Paramedic 12 | Fennec Hacker 16/0.5 |

Double-Disco version: **126pts / 3.5SWC**, six Regular Orders, FT3 Core + FT2 Haris, four actual Fennecs; one more Fennec remains available. Paramedic version: **125pts / 3.5SWC**. In a 300pt/6SWC list this leaves 174–175 points, 2.5SWC and nine trooper slots.

An alternative is an offensive Core with Griffin + Disco + Paramedic (62/1.5), defensive Haris with Sniper + Minelayer + Disco (60/1.5), and separate Fennec Hacker (16/0.5): **138/3.5, seven Regular Orders, all five Fennecs**. This buys completely independent Hacker positioning and opposite-flank Eclipse.

**Haris opportunity cost:** Kestrel only gets one Haris. A defensive Fennec Haris excludes a premium Hetkari Haris or the inexpensive Magistrate + two Jackal COLCORP Haris. A Magistrate/Jackal Duo remains available regardless of the Haris choice.

- **Defensive FENNECS Haris:** Choose when the linked Black A.I.R. sniper's reactive reliability and combined defensive support are a central part of the plan.
- **Hetkari Haris:** Choose instead when you want a concentrated premium gunfighting formation.
- **COLCORP Haris:** Choose instead when the cheap third Regular Order and launcher defense matter more than linking a sniper. Magistrate17 + two Jackal FTO6 = 29/0, with conditional Regular conversion at Order Count.
- **Solo Black A.I.R. sniper:** Keep the sniper, Minelayer and Hacker/Disco independently deployed if the Haris is more valuable elsewhere. This sacrifices the sniper's linked Special Die but retains the Fennecs' individual utility.

An enlarged Fennec Core is optional: Griffin HMG + Disco + Minelayer + Paramedic = **76/1.5 FT4**; adding another Disco makes **89/1.5 FT5**. The extra bonuses may be valuable, but three-body teams are easier to conceal, move and protect against templates.

#### Quantities and practical packages

| Package | Composition | Pts / SWC |
|---|---|---:|
| Minimal | FO/Disco + Hacker | 29 / 0.5 |
| Balanced default | FO/Disco + Hacker + Minelayer | 43 / 0.5 |
| Offensive | 2 FO/Disco + Hacker + Minelayer | 56 / 0.5 |
| Infantry assault | FO/Disco + Paramedic + Hacker + Minelayer | 55 / 0.5 |
| Full utility | 2 FO/Disco + Paramedic + Hacker + Minelayer | 68 / 0.5 |
| Maximum defensive utility | 2 FO/Disco + 2 Minelayers + Hacker | 70 / 0.5 |

- **0–1:** Only for an alternative architecture (TAG/REM-heavy, distributed Duos, alternate standard Hacker or Eclipse sources). One Fennec can be independently deployed; no obligation to link it.
- **2–3:** Efficient baseline for one compact Fennec Core and/or independent Hacker.
- **4–5:** Excellent for dual Fireteams, distributed Eclipse/defense or an intentionally large Core, provided the troop cap allows it. Do not sacrifice needed independent attack pieces just to reach AVA5.

**Bottom line:** Offensive Fennecs should facilitate movement and mission play; defensive Fennecs should ideally generate value without moving. A solo backfield Hacker, a linked Minelayer, and one or two mobile Disco Ballers form an especially attractive foundation. Avoid basic filler or a basic Lieutenant unless the final list genuinely needs the savings.
