### Patsy Garnett — Varuna

**B- overall — Skip by default.** Patsy is a competent durable close-range specialist and Varuna's only non-TAG NCO, but the 34-point investment is difficult to justify compared with Kamau, Montesa and independent Helots. FT Master (Helots) is her unique niche, not a reason to build around her automatically.

**Sources:** [Pinned N5.3 roster](../../../../sectorials/panoceania/varuna-immediate-reaction-division.md#patsy-garnett) · [Fireteams](../../Varuna/varuna-fireteams.md) · [Listbuilding](../../Varuna/varuna-listbuilding.md) · [Kamau](../../Varuna/units/kamau.md) · [Helots](../../Varuna/units/helots.md). Tactical assessment agreed 2026-11-24.

| Option | Pts | SWC | Equipment and skills | Priority |

Wildcard (max 1), counts as Undertow and Helot. She does not replace the native required-choice member of a team.

#### What she brings

- BS14, ARM4/W2, PH14 and Warhorse make a resilient specialist and close-range counterattacker.
- AP SMG, E/Mitter, Grenades and D-Charges give useful anti-armor, indirect and mission utility. The AP SMG is primarily a short-range weapon; she lacks Mimetism, MSV, or a true long-range attack weapon.
- NCO can spend an otherwise unused Lieutenant Order on her own activation or her Fireteam when she leads. It does not create an extra Order.
- Hackable HI, BTS3, and no inherent visual or gunfighting modifiers limit her value as an independent attacker.
- FT Master is unique and useful only when the list and deployment can exploit it consistently.

#### Patsy + Helots: unique but not a default

| Package | Pts/SWC | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Patsy + two FTO SMG/LRL Helots | 54/0 | FT3, three Regular Orders conditionally |
| Patsy + FTO LRL + FTO Shock MMR | 55/0 | FT3, complementary defensive ranges |
| Patsy + FTO LRL + FTO Red Fury | 57/1 | FT3, higher reactive Burst |

Only FTO Helot options 1, 3 and 4 qualify. A valid Varuna Core or Haris with Patsy and two Helots reaches FT3; FT2 provides +1 Special Die (not additional Burst). FT Master converts qualifying linked Helots at the next Order Count while the valid Fireteam exists. Independent Camouflage Helots are not converted. Use a three-member Core rather than a second Haris if another Haris is present.

The tactical tension is fundamental: Helots are inexpensive Neurocinetics defensive turrets, while Patsy wants to advance, spend the Lieutenant Order and score or fight at close range. Keeping her back wastes much of her 34-point chassis; advancing the team risks exposing the fragile Helots and abandoning useful defensive angles. Separating them sacrifices the conditional conversion. An opponent killing the link members can also reduce future Order income.

Prefer independent 12/0 Camouflage (1 Use) LRL Helots for marker protection and freedom to cover separate approaches. A Montesa FTO (20) plus two Camo LRL Helots (24) costs 44/0; adding a 10-point Fusilier brings the package to the same 54/0. This trades the linked package's three conditional Regular Orders for two Regular and two Irregular Orders across four models, plus better independent defensive positioning. The lost pooled Order and NCO access are genuine trade-offs, not automatic reasons to buy Patsy.

| Orders | Regular + access to LT Order via NCO | Regular + additional Tactical Awareness Order |
| Main weapons | AP SMG, E/Mitter | Combi Rifle, E/M Carbine |
| Other advantages | Warhorse, Grenades, D-Charges, FT Master | Shock Immunity, MediKit, non-HI chassis |
| Undertow equivalence | Yes | Yes |

**Kamau TA Paramedic is the better general-purpose linked component.** For nine fewer points it supplies a genuinely additional Tactical Order, Mimetism, useful conventional midfield range, E/M threat, specialist and recovery capability. Its Tactical Order can move the whole team when Kamau leads, but cannot be spent as a freely transferable Order. Patsy is substantially tougher and brings different classified and close-assault tools, so the two profiles are not identical. Do not overvalue NCO when inexpensive Tactical Awareness is already available.

The foot Montesa FTO Paramedic (20/0) is another preferred alternative for a durable W2 close-range specialist: Mimetism (-3), Blitzen, close combat and Limited Cover. ORC Boarding Shotgun (+1B)/Firewall (28/0) is a cheaper direct close-range HI gunfighter if specialist capability is not needed.

An offensive Undertow Haris such as Kamau HMG (26/1), Patsy (34/0), Kamau TA Paramedic (25/0) totals 85/1 and reaches FT3. It can use both special Order sources, but costs too much for a team without an apex attacker by default.

#### Lieutenant architecture without Patsy

The pinned roster has **no Chain of Command**, and Patsy is the only non-TAG NCO. Do not buy her solely to avoid wasting a Lieutenant Order.

| Architecture | Pts/SWC | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Fusilier LT + basic Fusilier decoy | 20/0 | Default inexpensive command shell; protect both and accept the unused LT Order if necessary |
| Zulu-Cobra LT | 23/0 | Camouflage, Jammer, Sensor and forward deployment; conceal and protect, not Hidden Deployment; avoid unnecessary exposure |
| Fusilier LT + decoy + Squalos NCO | 86/1.5 | NCO on a real primary attacker; Squalos also has Tactical Awareness |
| Fusilier LT + decoy + Cutter NCO | 117/2 | NCO on an apex Camo TAG; no Tactical Awareness on Cutter |

Pathfinder TA (17/0, AVA2), Kamau TA Paramedic (25/0) and Bipandra TA (25/0) offer extra activations without relying on the Lieutenant Order. An NCO TAG is a better recipient of that Order if the TAG is already wanted. Multiple NCOs compete for the same limited Lieutenant Order.

#### Verdict

- **Skip by default.** Do not include Patsy just because she is the only infantry NCO.
- Prefer Kamau TA Paramedic for ordinary Undertow support, Montesa FTO for durable specialist assault, and independent Camo Helots for reactive defense.
- Consider Patsy only if a deliberate FT Master Helot package or a particular mobile NCO/FO Fireteam has a clear mission-specific purpose.

The existing Varuna overview and Fireteam guide describe the Helot combination as a signature or particularly strong architecture. Its legality and unusual Order conversion remain valid; this unit analysis explicitly downgrades its default listbuilding priority on opportunity-cost and deployment grounds.
