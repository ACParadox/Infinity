### Fusiliers — Neoterran Capitaline Army (N5.3)

**Situational — take for Fireteam glue or a cheap Lieutenant and decoy.** Usually 0–2 Fusiliers; a third is reasonable if using both a command shell and a Fusilier Fireteam. Do not build around them merely because they are the sectorial's basic infantry. Compared with Kestrel's Fennecs, they offer much less independent utility.

#### Why they are weaker than Fennecs

The basic, LT and Paramedic profiles cost exactly the same in the two units. The difference is in the high-value utility upgrades.

| Role | Fusilier | Fennec |
|---|---|---|
| FO | 12 pts: Deployable Repeater | 13 pts: Sensor, Disco Baller |
| Hacker | 15/0.5: Combi Rifle | 16/0.5: FastPanda, E/Mitter, SMG |
| Deployment defense | No comparable dedicated mine profile | 14/0: Thunderbolt (+1B), Minelayer |
| Heavy weapons | HMG, ML, MSR | Thunderbolt (+1B) |

Fennecs provide Eclipse movement, anti-marker support, mine coverage and efficient repeater projection. Fusiliers mostly provide cheap orders and modest specialist upgrades. NCA obtains much of its analogous utility from other units.

- **CSU 10/0:** Same price as basic Fusilier; Sixth Sense, MetaChemistry, Breaker Rifle and Nanopulser (+1B). Better general close defense; counts as Fusilier in its Fireteam.
- **CSU Specialist 11/0:** Cheap scoring, close-range weapons and same-family composition; preferable if no repeater or medical requirement.
- **Blocker Specialist 15/0:** WIP13, Sensor, Pitcher, Pulzar, Biometric Visor and Discover (ReRoll). Excellent utility; counts as Fusilier in the Fusilier team.
- **Pathfinder 14/0 (17/0 with Tactical Awareness):** Mobile FO/Sensor/Repeater/Deactivator; Wildcard but not Fusilier-counting.
- **Auxilia FO 15/0:** Independent specialist with Deployable Repeater and Auxbot; useful for distributed mission play, not Fusilier Fireteam membership.
- **Fugazi 7/0 and Mulebot 8/0:** Cheaper independent Regular Orders and Repeater coverage; neither fills a native Fusilier requirement.
- **Tech-Bee 9/0:** Independent specialist with TAGCom, Deployable Repeater and repair utility.

#### Lieutenant and decoy

**Fusilier LT 10/0 + basic Fusilier 10/0 = 20/0, two Regular Orders plus the Lieutenant Order.** The second Combi profile can conceal the LT's identity; this is not the Decoy Special Skill. Deploy both safely, avoid revealing the LT unnecessarily and consider whether the second Fusilier's concealment benefit is worth a troop slot.

The cheap LT is particularly attractive with a **Squalos Mk-II NCO** that can put the personal Lieutenant Order into useful attack activations. Otherwise, compare the inexpensive command shell against premium LT options that genuinely contribute to the list's plan.

The LT does not need to join a Fireteam. Avoid dragging an otherwise safe commander into an offensive link solely to satisfy its native minimum.

#### Fireteam glue

The canonical Fusiliers Core/Haris requires at least one actual Fusilier. CSU and Blockers count as Fusiliers but do not waive this native minimum. Blade FTO, Quinn and Uma FTO also count as Fusiliers via their Wildcard entries. No Fusilier-family Duo is available.

##### FO / Deployable Repeater — 12 points

Default native anchor if the team wants a mobile specialist or network expansion. The two-point premium over basic buys FO, Flash Pulse and a Deployable Repeater.

The Repeater is especially valuable when it covers a new approach, objective or hacking target. It can be redundant when a Blocker Pitcher or mobile REM already provides sufficient coverage. A Deployable Repeater is placed within the user's Zone of Control, rather than launched at range like a Pitcher.

##### Paramedic — 12 points

Prefer when the Fireteam contains valuable recoverable infantry and already has adequate scoring and network projection. A successful MediKit application to a PH14 ORC or Aquila gives a 70% recovery roll; ranged application requires a successful hit first. A failed patient roll can kill the casualty. It does not repair ordinary STR casualties or heal active wounded troopers.

For TAG/REM support, prioritize a linked Blade FTO Engineer or another appropriate Engineer instead.

##### Basic — 10 points

Use as a true LT decoy or when saving two points matters. In an active team the FO or Paramedic usually earns its upgrade; in a stationary defensive team it is reasonable to use basic bodies if their utility upgrades would never be used.

#### Hacker — usually skip

| Standard HD | Pts / SWC | WIP | BTS | Advantages |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Fusilier | 15/0.5 | 12 | 0 | Cheapest standard HD |
| Blocker | 19/0.5 | 13 | 3 | Firewall (-3), Sensor, Pulzar |
| Bolt | 24/0.5 | 12 | 6 | E/M Mine, BSG (+1B), Marksmanship |
| Deva | 25/0.5 | 15 | 3 | NWI, Immunity (Isolated) |

The four-point upgrade to Blocker is generally worthwhile. A Fusilier Hacker can still supply Spotlight, Carbonite, Oblivion and other applicable standard programs from safe physical cover through an allied network; WIP12 and BTS0 are liabilities in contested hacking. Do not confuse a KHD with a replacement for a standard HD.

#### Heavy weapons — generally skip

- **Missile Launcher 15/1.5:** Best niche of the heavy weapons; expendable long-range ARO at a low points cost. Compare Bolt ML 24/1.5 with Marksmanship and a better chassis.
- **MULTI Sniper 16/1:** Budget ARO, but Bolt MSV1 MSR 26/1.5 offers markedly better quality. Sierra TR HMG 23/1 is another independent defensive option, with different range and ammo characteristics.
- **HMG 18/1:** Cheap long-range Active Turn gun, but lacks the advantages of dedicated NCA attackers. Bulleteer Spitfire 22/1 supplies Mimetism (-6), mobility and a Repeater for four more points, at shorter effective range.

Use a heavy-weapon Fusilier only when inexpensive secondary shooting specifically solves a list problem. Do not make it the default attack or defensive anchor.

The Blade/Bulleteer package is particularly attractive if the team already needs a mobile Engineer. The Paramedic/ORC package is an analogous VITA-based module. Quinn's Disco Baller restores some of the movement-enabling utility available much more cheaply in Kestrel. A pure five-Fusilier Core is rarely compelling given the many inexpensive Counts-as alternatives; even the utility-heavy FT5 example is optional rather than the default NCA architecture.

#### Quantities and collection

- **0:** Entirely viable with another LT and Neoterra/CSU/Ultima Ratio Fireteams or independent attackers.
- **1:** Cheap LT or native Fireteam anchor (FO/Paramedic).
- **2:** LT + basic decoy, LT + separate utility anchor, or two utility Fusiliers in a larger Core.
- **3:** LT + decoy + separate native anchor; occasionally useful.
- **4–5:** Only for a deliberately specialized inexpensive Core; generally unnecessary.

For collection purposes, prioritize Combi Rifle (LT/basic), a second identical-profile model if using a decoy, and FO/Paramedic representations. Heavy-weapon variants and the Hacker are low priority. **The primary reasons to take Fusiliers remain Fireteam glue and cheap command infrastructure.**
