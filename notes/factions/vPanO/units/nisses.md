## Vanilla selection and use

**Verdict: A+ overall; excellent MSV2 gun, but Bolt and Karhu Duos are the first-choice efficient links.** HMG31/1.5 is preferred active anti-Mimetism/Smoke gun (B4); MSR29/1.5 is an alternate long-range ARO. Mim-3, MSV2 and HMG volume are distinctive, but W1/BTS0 and no NCO limit sustained exposure. Nisse can receive +1SD from a Haris of two matching partners, not a same-Nisse Duo.

| Package | Pts/SWC | Level |
|---|---:|---|
| Nisse HMG + 2 Blade FTO BSG | 71/1.5 | FT2 Haris |
| Nisse HMG + Blade FTO BSG + Blade FTO Engineer | 74/1.5 | FT2 Haris |
| Nisse HMG + 2 Bolt KHD | 75/1.5 | FT2 Haris |

Preferred Haris when MSV2 and Blade utility are wanted: HMG + BSG + Engineer. Three Regular Orders while operational, two Blade Tactical Orders, all Mim-3, close protection, AP SMG and D-Charges/Engineer utility. Each TA Order activates the Fireteam only with its own Blade as leader; it is not a free Nisse HMG attack. The Haris consumes the only Haris slot, has three W1 bodies and costs 15–26 more than preferred Bolt/Karhu Duos. Solo HMG remains strong when no Haris is justified. Use Disco Baller/Eclipse to cross lanes, not to shoot through Eclipse.

### ARO Haris — A+ with purposeful partners

MSR29/1.5 solo B1 or FT2 B1+1SD; MSV2/Mim-3 lets it fight ordinary Smoke and Mimetism. One Nisse max per Family II team, so any mixed Duo is FT1. In a Haris two matching other members grant +1SD to all three, including Nisse. Defensive packages: Nisse MSR + two Helot FTO LRL49/1.5, + LRL and Shock MMR50/1.5, + two MMR51/1.5 (Joan regularization elsewhere supports these); or Nisse MSR + Blade FTO BSG20 + Engineer23 =72/1.5, or + Bolt BSG19 + KHD22 =70/1.5. Helots are cheap high-Burst defenses; maintain legal training/coherency and do not automatically tether an attacking Nisse HMG to static Helots. Black A.I.R.33/1.5 remains preferable as a dedicated solo sniper with B2; use Nisse MSR Haris when its partners already justify the sole Haris slot and active B2+1SD matters.

### Nisses — Svalarheima's Winter Force (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A tier; HMG A+, MULTI Sniper A.** Excellent anti-Mimetism and anti-Smoke long-range gunfighters, particularly in inexpensive FT2 Duos with a 17pt Infirmarer. Not mandatory: Karhu offers superior anti-armor, NCO and Fireteam flexibility. Usually consider 0–1 Nisse; two only for genuinely distinct attack and defense roles.

Reviewed 24 November 2026. Ratings and proposed combinations are tactical judgments, not measured win rates.

#### Profiles

**ID 3 · AVA 5 · MI · Regular** — MOV 15–5 cm, CC14, BS13, PH12, WIP13, ARM3, BTS0, W1, S2. Common: MSV2, Mimetism (-3), Terrain (Total), Climb 7.5.

| Opt | Pts | SWC | Loadout / differences | Rating |
|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| 2 | 31 | 1.5 | HMG, Boarding Pistol, CCW | A+ — primary attacker |
| 3 | 29 | 1.5 | MULTI Sniper Rifle, Boarding Pistol, CCW | A — anti-vision ARO |
| 5 | 30 | 0.5 | Combi Rifle, Light Shotgun, Boarding Pistol, CCW; Hacking Device | C — usually skip |
| 6 | 28 | 0 | Combi Rifle, Light Shotgun, Boarding Pistol, CCW; MediKit, Paramedic | B — situational team support |

- **HMG:** MSV2 ignores Mimetism (-6) and normal Smoke visibility penalties, while Mimetism (-3) imposes a defensive MOD on non-MSV opponents. B4 at long range; FT2 rolls five dice and retains four. The overview ranks this profile #6 apex and #4 points value among non-TAG long-range attackers. Good at removing revealed mimetic infantry. ARM3 helps, but W1 discourages prolonged attrition.
- **MULTI Sniper:** efficient long-range reactive threat, particularly against opponents relying on ordinary Smoke. FT2 rolls two dice and retains one in ARO, not B2. DA ammunition threatens multiple saves. Can also attack in the active turn, but HMG is the default sustained attacker. The overview cites #15 in ARO apex/value.
- **Hacker:** 30 points is expensive for WIP13/BTS0 hacking. MSV2 and Mimetism do not modify hacking rolls, and the Hacker itself is Hackable. Prefer [Pilot-X](../../WinterFor/units/pilot-x.md) for hacking, REMDriver and RacerBot utility or a cheaper dedicated Hacker.
- **Paramedic:** useful if another MSV2 short-range fighter and MediKit are specifically needed, but the 17pt Infirmarer provides a proper WIP13 Doctor, close defense and the same Nisse counts-as for 11 fewer points.

#### Nisse HMG versus Karhu Feuerbach

| Attribute | Nisse HMG | Karhu Feuerbach (+1B) |
|---|---|---|
| Pts / SWC | 31 / 1.5 | 34 / 1.5 |
| BS / active Burst | 13 / 4 | 13 / 3 |
| Mimetism / MSV | -3 / 2 | -3 / 1 |
| ARM / BTS / W | 3 / 0 / 1 | 2 / 3 / 1 |
| Long-range ammunition | Normal | AP+DA |
| Order economy | No NCO | NCO uses existing Lieutenant Order |
| Vertical movement | Climb 7.5 | Climbing Plus |
| Additional tools | Boarding Pistol | AP Mine, NCO, Courage |
| FT access | Nisses, Beta max 1 | Fusiliers, Alpha, Beta, Bulleteers |

At favorable +3 range against Mimetism (-6) in cover (-3), with no other modifiers, the Nisse shoots on BS13 and Karhu on BS10. Nisse also has an additional actual Burst die. MSV2 can shoot through ordinary Smoke without its visibility penalty; MSV1 normally suffers -6 through Smoke.

The Nisse is better suited to hunting mimetic infantry and the Karhu to HI/TAG targets. Karhu is arguably the stronger general toolbox thanks to anti-armor ammunition, NCO, mines and mobility. They complement one another; neither replaces the other's strongest role. NCO does not generate an additional Order.

#### Fireteam access and recommended packages

Native **Nisses Core/Haris/Duo** requires at least one actual Nisse (max 5); **WinterFor Beta Haris/Duo** allows max 1 Nisse alongside a qualifying required-choice Karhu, Hospitaller FTO or Bøyg. Nisse does not count as Karhu or Hospitaller. No native Fusilier or Alpha access.

Nisse-counting Wildcards: Infirmarer (max 2), Machinist WinterFor (max 1), Uma FTO (max 1; options 2/3/4 only). Other legal Wildcards do not automatically count as Nisses. WinterFor allows one Core, two Harises and unlimited Duos.

|---|---:|---|---|

| Nisse MSR + Infirmarer BSG | 46 / 1.5 | FT2 Duo | Preferred medical ARO module |
| Nisse HMG + Machinist | 48 / 1.5 | FT2 Duo | Attack with Engineer, especially alongside REM/TAG support |
| Nisse MSR + Machinist | 46 / 1.5 | FT2 Duo | Reactive gun plus repair utility for nearby equipment/REM/TAG; Engineer cannot heal Nisse wounds |
| Nisse HMG + Uma FTO SMG | 54 / 1.5 | FT2 Duo | Close-range SMG, E/M Mine and specialist |
| Nisse HMG + Infirmarer BSG + Machinist | 65 / 1.5 | FT3 Haris | Long gun with Doctor and Engineer |
| Nisse HMG + Nisse MSR + Infirmarer BSG | 77 / 3 | FT3 Haris | Two long guns and Doctor; high SWC |
| Nisse HMG + two Infirmarers BSG + Machinist | 82 / 1.5 | FT4 Core | BS14 HMG, +1SD, three specialists; redundant medical support |

**Best offensive Duo: Nisse HMG 31 + [Infirmarer BSG](../../WinterFor/units/infirmarers.md) 17 = 48/1.5.** The Infirmarer contributes BS13 shotgun, Boarding Pistol, CC21/MA1, WIP13 Doctor and the FT2 bonus. It can recover the Nisse if it survives in Unconscious State, and protects the team's weak range bands. Keep the Doctor safe and in coherency; recovery still costs Orders and requires a legal target and positioning. FT2 affects rolled shooting, not the Doctor's contact WIP roll.

The same logic applies to the Sniper Duo at 46/1.5, though the Doctor should normally stay protected while the Sniper watches the lane. Neither Duo consumes a Haris or Core slot. The existing repository also recommends Machinist and Uma combinations; Infirmarer variants are proposed alternatives, not replacements for those historical or mission-specific recommendations.

FT3 adds +3 Discover and +1 Dodge, FT4 adds +1 BS. FT5 adds Sixth Sense but requires five matching members and is expensive. The economical Duo is normally preferable to buying additional bodies just for FT levels.

#### Nisse MULTI Sniper versus Knight of Justice ML

| Attribute | Nisse MSR | Knight of Justice ML |
|---|---|---|
| Pts / SWC | 29 / 1.5 | 46 / 1.5 |
| BS | 13 | 14 |
| MSV / Mimetism | 2 / -3 | None / None |
| ARM / BTS / W | 3 / 0 / 1 | 5 / 9 / 2 |

| Native +1SD | No | Yes |
| Hackable | No | Yes |

The [Knight of Justice](../../WinterFor/units/knight-of-justice.md) is the more durable apex ARO piece; its native +1SD stacks with a legal FT2 bonus. The Nisse is cheaper and covers normal Smoke and Mimetism matchups much better. Choose the sniper for efficient anti-vision lane control rather than expecting it to survive repeated engagements. The HMG, sniper and paramedic are not Hackable merely for being MI; the HD profile is Hackable.

#### Weaknesses and listbuilding conclusion

- W1: losing an engagement can immediately stop the attack. A Doctor cannot recover a Dead trooper.
- White Noise and Albedo penalize MSV-dependent pieces. Eclipse blocks friendly MSV too; Disco Ballers are movement/approach tools, not ordinary Smoke for the Nisse to shoot through.
- WinterFor lacks readily available conventional Smoke support, limiting offensive MSV2 exploitation.
- No NCO or Tactical Awareness; relies on shared pooled Orders.
- Long-range profiles lack substantial close-range and CC capabilities beyond the Boarding Pistol. Pair with a specialist that covers those bands.
- MSV2 is not MSV3: Camouflage markers still need to be revealed before direct shooting; Surprise Attack is not automatically negated.

**Selection:** HMG highly recommended, MULTI Sniper recommended if anti-vision defense is needed, Paramedic situational, Hacker generally skip. A single Nisse HMG/Infirmarer Duo is a particularly strong starting module, leaving the two Harises and Core available. Choose Karhu first if armor hunting, NCO or climbing angles are the priority. No need to exploit AVA5 or buy a large Nisse Core by default.
