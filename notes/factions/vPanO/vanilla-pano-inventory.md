## Role index

### Command, succession and Orders

- **Joan v2:** Inspiring Leadership and Tactical Awareness; active Lieutenant.
- **BCA Magistrate:** Strategos LT 19/0 or separate CoC 21/0; AVA1 prevents taking both.
- **Tikbalang:** Lieutenant (+1 Order) 73/1.5; personal Orders, not team activations.
- **Zulu-Cobra, Aquila Guard, Cutter and Fusilier:** alternative [Lieutenant architectures](vanilla-pano-listbuilding.md#lieutenant-architectures).
- **Karhu:** NCO, with distinct Feuerbach and utility loadouts.
- **Squalos Mk-II:** NCO MULTI HMG 66/1.5.
- **Fugazi:** cheap Regular Order, Repeater and Flash Pulse.
- **Pathfinder:** 17/0 FO/Sensor/Repeater Tactical Awareness or separate 14-point non-TA option.

### Long-range attackers and TAGs

- **Swiss Guard:** Hidden Deployment HMG 56/1.5, separate ML reactive profile.
- **Cutter:** concealed MULTI HMG 97/2.
- **Tikbalang:** mobile Mimetism(-3) HMG 69/1.5.
- **Squalos Mk-II:** MMR 55/1, HMG 62/1.5, or NCO MULTI HMG 66/1.5.
- **Aquila Guard:** MSV3 HMG 52/1.5.
- **Karhu:** Feuerbach (+1B) 34/1.5.
- **Nisse:** MSV2/Mimetism(-3) HMG 31/1.5.
- **Griffin:** HMG 37/1.5, without Kestrel link bonuses.

### Secondary attackers and mobility

- **Bulleteer:** Mimetism(-6) Spitfire 22/1.
- **Redeye:** aerial Mimetism(-6) Spitfire, Sensor, No Cover; distinct Blitzen and Continuous-Damage GL options.
- **Hetkari:** MULTI Red Fury 48/1.5, independent.
- **Joan v2:** AP Spitfire LT 45/1.
- **Black A.I.R.:** K1 MMR FO 29/0.5, distinct from Neurocinetics sniper.
- **Banshee:** LSG/Pulzar 19/0 or KHD 23/0; airborne attack.

### Midfield, hackers and network projection

- **Zulu-Cobra:** separate Sensor/Jammer, KHD/Pitcher, Spitfire and MMR/Minelayer options.
- **Fennec:** FO/Sensor/Disco 13/0, Thunderbolt/Minelayer 14/0 or Hacker/FastPanda alternative.
- **Peacemaker:** forward Repeater and Auxbot template pressure.
- **Blocker:** Sensor/Pitcher/Adhesive Specialist 15/0.
- **Locust:** hacker and midfield utility.
- **Pilot-X:** 21/1 with one or two dependent Mk-III RacerBots at 8/0 each; independent Regular Orders, not Fireteam activations.
- **Pathfinder:** fast Sensor, FO and Repeater support.

### Reactive defense and mission support

- **Black A.I.R.:** Neurocinetics MULTI Sniper FTO 33/1.5, unlinked.
- **Swiss Guard:** hidden ML reactive option.
- **Helot:** independent defensive profiles; no Joan Haris.
- **Fennec:** Thunderbolt (+1B) Shock Minelayer 14/0.
- **Teutonic Knight:** SMG Specialist 19/0, two Wounds, Impetuous/Limited Cover.
- **Bipandra:** Doctor and Tactical Awareness support.
- **Karhu Engineer and Blade Engineer:** independent recovery choices.
- **Tech-Bee:** 9/0 Specialist/TAGCom; not an Engineer.
- **Drummer:** synchronized FO and gun profile alternatives; check separate loadouts.
