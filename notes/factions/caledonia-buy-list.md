## Sectorial buying skips and lower-priority profiles

The [Ariadna audit](ariadna-must-have-go-to-avoid-source-audit-2026.md) finds **no well-supported blanket “never field this whole Caledonian unit”**. These are **purchase deferrals**, not unsupported claims that a unit is fundamentally bad:

1. **Full Wulver Core / 3–5 additional Wulvers:** your single Wulver, Cadin and existing Grey/Mormaer firepower cover core attack jobs. Only buy further Wulvers if building a specifically tested Wulver Fireteam.
2. **Third Mormaer, fourth/fifth Highlander Grey:** you already own 2 Mormaers and 3 Greys; choose the gun and link architecture rather than fill unit AVA. A heavy Mormaer/Grey plan and a premium Wulver plan compete for points and Orders.
3. **Third/fourth Cateran:** niche extra long-ARO coverage after owning 1–2; table-dependent and potentially too many passive shooters.
4. **Third/fourth SAS:** two allow the important FO + Minelayer/assault roles; extra bodies only for a marker-skew plan.
5. **A second McMurrough or three dedicated Cameronians:** one McMurrough is the legal max; one real Cameronian + two owned S6 TAK Dog-Warriors can stand in for all three.
6. **Additional Armand, Isobel, Uxía, Cadin, Wardriver, Warcor, Beasthunter or support REMs:** already owned the relevant unique body/pool. No need to rebuy a full boxed set to obtain them. Isobel is **not** the old assumed N3/N4 Chain of Command safety-net profile in current CHA N5.3.
7. **Guided Traktor/Katyusha/Uragan-specific extra sculpt:** two physical Traktor/Kuryer chassis are already shared from TAK; buy only if the list specifically needs different simultaneous build/representation. Guided artillery needs an actual targeting plan.
8. **Expensive foot 112/Support Pack only for one miniature:** try the owned Kazak Doktor proxy; the **2026 four-model Essentials support box** duplicates Dozer/Kazak Doktor ownership and includes an Elektronik not legal in main Caledonia.

Do **not** reject these units as unplayable. Current N5.3 Caledonia uses distinct list architectures: **Wallace + Irregular-price economy**, or **conventional Mormaer/Grey Regular backbone**, plus **Uxía/SAS midfield**, cheap smoke/pressure and appropriate specialist coverage. ## Cross-sectorial reuse and spending discipline

- **Kosmoflot** current roster also allows **Wallace, Denma, Scots Guards, SAS, Caterans**, so the proposed purchases can serve *both* armies. Specifically, **Scots Guard FTO restriction also applies to its Kosmoflot Fireteam**. Avoid double purchasing figures merely because separate buy lists mention them.
- **TAK** supplies a real **Kazak Doktor for foot 112**, **Scouts for SK/camo proxies**, **Frontovik for Scot Guard MI**, **2 S6 Dog-Warriors for extra Cameronians**, as well as the shared Traktor chassis, Dozer, motorized 112, Wardriver, Warcor, and Beasthunter. These are already owned, not new Caledonia purchases.
- **Steel Phalanx** has **4 Myrmidons + one Myrmidon Officer**, possible S2 warband donors, but deliberately non-Scottish aesthetics. Do not count an Army-legal Caledonian donor as a preferred distinct-identity proxy.
- **Ariadna Action Pack** is more attractive if building Kosmoflot, because the other seven figures are useful outside Caledonia; **Beyond Crimson Stone Ariadna three-model split** is the cleaner Caledonia purchase.
- **2026 limited reissue sets** may appeal to collectors, but a repack release year is not evidence of newer sculpt geometry or better gameplay. Only the **Characters** box is specifically an exclusive-variant purchase.
