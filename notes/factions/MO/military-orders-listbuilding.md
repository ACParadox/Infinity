## Start with one of five architectures

| Plan | Core construction (illustrative, not a full 300pt export) | Why choose it / main opportunity cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Balanced independent scoring** | **Holy Sepulchre NCO AP HMG 53** or **Tikbalang68**; small Military Orders **Crosier/Knight Commander or Teutonic** team; **Trinitarian FO**, **Montesa FTO or motorized Paramedic**, affordable support | A strong first-stage long gun, multiple mission routes and retained points. Not the most extreme hacking or armor list. |
| **Knight pressure and Firewall** | **Teutonic TinBot/Specialist + Santiago FTO KHD** in a **Military Orders** link; **Crux FTO / Montesa FTO / De Fersen** where paid-for roles match; independent Trinitarian and affordable first-stage gun | Battles from midrange to CC with durable specialists. Very easy to overpay for redundant linked guns; plan long-range access and Order count first. |
| **Hacker + network mission control** | **De Fersen HD 42 + cheap FTO Order Sergeant Pitcher18 + Santiago TinBot31** in legal team structure; **Trinitarian DR Minelayer23/1**, **Peacemaker/Pathfinder**; mission/scoring backup | Punishes enemy Hackable approaches and threatens Spotlight/Trinity. Risk of one expensive Hacker dying to KHD, Repeaters being cleared, or too little ranged fire. **Guided Clipper** is optional, never automatic. |
| **NCO TAG spearhead** | **Seraph MULTI HMG74 / AP Spitfire72** or **Tikbalang68** + **Knight Commander LT(+1)24** + **Tech-Bee9** + actual **Machinist/Palbot or Curator repair** + cheap scorer/ARO shell | Makes the Special LT/TacAware/TAGCom package earn its points through repeated safe attacks. Loss of NCO/Commander, hostile hackers, or TAG exposure collapses effective value; no MO TAG Duo. |
| **Asymmetric pressure / twin heavy skew** | **Crux Hidden or Santiago Combat Jump** plus a complementary opener and Trinitarian; *advanced experiment*: **two Seraphs with elite hacking and repair** | Threatens a second axis or overloads opposing answers. The [2025 tournament champion used double Seraph in 4/5 rounds](../../battle-reports/181-190-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md#181--autumn-assault-2025-military-orders-champion-and-tournament-meta), **but switched away for a specific anti-TAG Morat matchup**. The skew is evidence-backed, not a default recommendation. |

## Command and cheap Orders

| Decision | Actual mechanics and value | Avoid this mistake |
|---|---|---|
| **Teutonic LT 18/0** | Cheapest durable LT and actual assault/short-range presence; saves 6 points versus Commander LT(+1). | **Impetuous** plus an identifiable aggressive unit is an LT risk. |
| **Knight Commander LT 20/0 vs LT(+1)24/0** | Holomask-protected MI/Crosier equivalence; +1 option provides **two** Special LT Orders to an NCO. | The +1 variant is not two general Regulars. Without NCO, 4pt premium does not buy flexible Orders. |
| **Knight Commander CoC 23/0** | Separate protected successor for aggressive LT (Joan/Hospitaller/Teutonic/Knight of Justice) plans. | It is not an LT simultaneously; keep CoC physically away from the threatened front. |
| **Joan LT or Joan v2 LT** | Fighting LT, Tactical Awareness and Inspiring Leadership; some variants add +1 Command Token or Strategic Deployment. | Conversion is **not general MO army-wide Regularisation of Religious troopers**. Losing Joan threatens both attacker and command. |
| **NCO: Sepulchre / Seraph / Tik / Teutonic Spitfire** | Converts appropriate Special LT Order into personal Tactical activation; a **Fireteam leader** can use relevant Tactical Order to activate its legal team. | Cannot convert an Order twice; losing the NCO can strand an otherwise expensive Commander package. |
| **7pt Fugazi; 8pt Mulebot; 11pt Crosier; 12pt Raveneye** | Cheap regular infrastructure with distinct Flash/Repeater, Baggage, link/utility or mine/FO defensive jobs. | **Warcor is Irregular**; Palbot/Auxbot do not give independent transferable Orders; off-table AD/Hidden options affect early practical pool. |

**Plan group placement explicitly.** A TAG in one group does not receive Tech-Bee TAGCom from another; an NCO's personal Tactical Orders do not become regular pool fuel. The [N5.3 Order rules](https://infinitythewiki.com/Tactical_Order) control the activation and timing details.

[Tactical Awareness *Military Orders REDUX!* (Spotify episode)](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6xyHjc3R0N37bDHl0HiROO) provides directly reviewed podcast discussion of De Fersen's Trinity dice, protected hacker packages, the Knights' expanded hacking game, and very different sample lists. ## Reactive turn, DZ defense and midfield denial

**Choose threats that cost the opponent Orders even if they die.** Exposing three 40pt Knights to one long-range lane is often worse than layering a disposable defense over an objective route.

| Defender | Role and positioning | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| **Raveneye 12 E/M Mine or Cybermine** | FO/Minelayer homefield deterrent against HI/TAG/Impersonator entry corridors; E/Marat follow-up | W1/BS11; a mine alone doesn't reveal/guarantee stopping Impersonation. |
| **Escort Sergeant Neuro HRL+Auxbot 22/1.5** | ARO template + synchronized close-range Fork watches a DZ breach | Not FTO, W1, requires Peripheral coherency; avoid counting Auxbot as another Order. |
| **Order Sergeant FTO HRL15 / Black Friar HRL22** | Affordable linkable launcher; Friar adds MSV2/Albedo, FTO saves points | 1.5SWC and no automatic defensive win; exact team and terrain matter. |
| **Sierra TR Thunderbolt18/0 or HMG23/1** | 360º Total Reaction / roof and AD gate; Machinist/Curator repair if needed | Exposed one-Wound REM can be removed by specialist long-range attackers; 18pt has less reach than HMG. |
| **Trinitarian MSR25/1.5 Camo** | Hidden *identity/marker position* until revealed, independent asymmetric sniper lane; independent infiltrator presence | W1/BS12 and 1.5SWC; can be countered by Triangulated Fire, Sensors, mimetic TAGs or alternate routes; N5.3 roster **does not print Hidden Deployment**. |
| **Crosier MSR+E/M Minelayer19/1** | DZ long-lane ARO + prepaid E/M mine; can join a relevant Military Orders Fireteam | W1/BTS0, expensive SWC when pairings require HMG/Spitfire. |
| **Curator AP Mine17 / Armed Turret20** | Actual Engineer/Deactivator and independent placed threat; extends repair planning | A Curator in a mobile team cannot also solve every Servant/Fireteam activation issue. |
| **Flash Pulse cheap cover** | **Fugazi7 Regular, Warcor3 Irregular** anchor secondary corners and AD paths | Flash is a tax, not hard long-range stopping power. |

**Trinitarian caution from [WIP12 E188 (Sneaky Snake)](https://open.spotify.com/episode/7mVONdav4Fn1GIlb6OYZ8T):** he reports excellent double-sniper defensive performance but also an opponent exploiting **Holoprojector + Triangulated Fire** to remove those snipers. The **July 2025 podcast predates the October 2025 MO overhaul**, so its matchup lessons are useful but not its historical roster claims.

**Mission matching matters more than a powerful statline.** For consoles/classifieds identify *who is an FO, Hacker, Doctor, Engineer, Paramedic or generic Specialist* and whether the mission accepts that exact role. [Can'tCon26 #020](../../battle-reports/011-020-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md#020--cantcon26-military-orders-three-game-tournament) contains an actual mistake where the available Trinitarian FO could not satisfy a Hacker-specific objective.

## Build and play checklist

1. **What wins the first 24–32-inch engagement?** Choose HMG/MULTI HMG/AP HMG, a correctly ranged Fireteam launcher, or a bypass route; don't put every main weapon in Spitfire/shotgun range.
2. **Where are ordinary Regular Orders generated and spent?** Count off-table losses and group assignments; add NCO/TacAware only where expected activations actually occur.
3. **What is the exact legal Fireteam?** Check **native minimum**, **required choice**, Counts-as, FTO restrictions, the one Haris, and whether Wildcards have an appropriate host.
4. **What prevents one hack from ending the game?** Pick Firewall/KHD, independent angle, actual Repeater-clearing tool, and Engineer/Palbot or Curator routes before committing multiple expensive HI/TAGs.
5. **Which two independent units score?** Identify the exact mission-eligible **FO/Hacker/Doctor/Engineer/Paramedic/Specialist**, one of them surviving if the primary gun or Knight team dies.
6. **What taxes the DZ assault?** Raveneye mine, Escort/Auxbot, cheap Flash/360º REM, camo specialist or dispersed defensive angle; do not spend every SWC point on an obvious gun castle.
7. **What happens on Turns 2–3?** Plan how **Montesa/Crusader/Trinitarian** crosses to the last objective, who holds the zone, and what happens if the expensive team is Broken or the LT is gone.
