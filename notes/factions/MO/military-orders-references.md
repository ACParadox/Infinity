**Against dense hacking/Guided networks:** block or destroy Repeaters at the edge before moving grouped HI; use **Order Sergeant Pitchers + De Fersen**, **Santiago KHD Firewall**, **Trinitarian KHD**, **E/M or separate unhackable lane** as appropriate. A TAG with a Tech-Bee is **not immune to hack**, and a KHD does not solve every enemy TAG.

**Against MSV/Mimetism/defensive Fireteams:** start with **Crosier MSV2 HMG**, **Black Friar MSV2/Albedo** or a correct-range long gun; use **Disco Eclipse** for bypass rather than an even bigger frontal gamble. A Crux Albedo attack still needs a *favorable target and range*; don't mistake conditional MODs for guaranteed wins.

**Against TAG/HI without a cyber opening:** **Montesa FTO Blitzen20**, **Teutonic Panzerfaust18–19**, **Raveneye E/M12**, **Hospitaller E/Mitter26**, **Santiago E/M grenades**, **Holy Sepulchre AP HMG53** or **Seraph AP Spitfire72** give different cost/geometry options. E/M Immobilized/Isolated is control, **not** automatic destruction; keep a finisher and Engineer answer.

**Against first-turn DZ attacks and Impersonation:** place **Raveneye mine/Cybermine**, a different-angle **Fugazi/Sierra/Escort Auxbot**, and **Crosier/Black Friar ARO** to protect Commander, cheap Orders and Engineer. **Biometric Black Friar** is a specific profile, not an automatic Impersonator reveal; deployables and templates must obey state/trigger legality.

**Against objective-room holding / last-turn flips:** send **S2 foot Montesa, Trinitarian FO/KHD, Santiago/Hospitaller specialist** or a legal linked unit with finite Eclipse cover. Keep **Motorized Montesa** for open-lane far-flank switches, not a tight doorway. If the main attacker dies, a second mobile scorer **must already have enough Orders left**.

## Competing source opinions

| Question | Position A | Position B | Working test |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Is there a near-auto-include?** | **Shepherd (2026): Montesa foot FTO Paramedic20**, exceptional for the price | Other recordings emphasize building from mission and link shape rather than declaring a compulsory model | Start with it in a legal team; cut only if its specialist/Blitzen/2W role is redundant. |
| **Is MO a TAG or Knights faction?** | **Sneaky Snake / WIP12:** elite linked Knights and Trinitarian sniper defense are viable competitively | **Shepherd/TA:** Seraph/Tik and even **double Seraph** are powerful specialized attacks, but pay a severe order/support cost | Compare three attack plans against the **same mission packet**, budget and opposing hacking tools. |
| **Knight Commander LT(+1) versus cheap Teutonic LT** | **Ryan:** versatile command insurance/active NCO options | **Shepherd:** 24pt passive LT can be a trap when a 18pt Teutonic is enough | Only pay +1 for expected repeated NCO Tactical use and protected command position. |
| **KoJ Hacker/Pitcher45 versus De Fersen + Sergeant18** | **Ryan / Tactical Awareness:** KoJ is robust independent **BS14 Pitcher/W2/BTS9** gun-and-hacker | **Shepherd:** inefficient *pure Pitcher purchase* when the 18pt linkable Sergeant projects De Fersen's superior Trinity | If KoJ will also shoot/score and exploit a link, test; otherwise buy cheap relay + De Fersen. |
| **Crosier cheap gun versus elite Knights** | **Ryan:** MSV2 HMG/MSR and modular Duo/Haris give useful staged shooting | **Shepherd:** BS12 Crosier is a contingency tool, not the main gun one wants in all matchups | Include as targeted anti-Mim/backup ARO, not because a cheap HMG is always a premium attack substitute. |
| **Seraph AP Spitfire/Auxbot versus MULTI HMG** | Dense maps and armor make AP/template fork compelling; TAG podcasters like the close tools | **Shepherd/Sneaky:** HMG range makes Super-Jump long-angle shots safer and more reusable | Choose by **opponent ARM and available 24–32-inch landing/firing geometry**, not aesthetics. |
| **Hospitaller HMG/Doctor Crusade versus compact links** | **Ryan/Sneaky:** durable long-range shooting and in-team Doctor can drive a Knight attack | **Shepherd:** multiple pricey Hospitallers often squeeze the Orders and independent scoring | Ask whether Doctor/Firewall + gun will move together over two turns; if not, split the package. |

**Swiss Miss dissent (Brookhurst 2026, ~33–35m):** A PanO player in [this later tournament discussion](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WOvaahZlyQE) struggles to build a Military Orders list they consider versatile and would rather play NCA, Varuna or perhaps WinterFor; another remembers MO performing well in particular games. This is **subjective frustration with points, Orders and roles**, not a new legality ruling or general tournament result. It reinforces the existing checklist: Knights/TAGs need cheap Orders, Lieutenant protection, independent specialists and credible hacking/long-range plans. There is **no new MO example list** in this recording to supersede the stronger verified analyses above.
