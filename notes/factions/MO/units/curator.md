# Military Order Curator — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A for an actual Engineer/repair need; A- as a linked Order Sergeants utility model; not a universal must-take.** The **17pt AP Mine Curator** is the baseline. The **18pt Deployable Cover** variant is a small, situational upgrade; the **20pt Marksman Rifle Armed Turret** is worth considering when Orders and a useful firing lane exist. Compared with [Kestrel's Machinist](../../kestrel/units/machinist.md), Curator pays 2 more points for Religious Troop, Immunity (Shock), AP Mine and, most importantly, **eligible Fireteams**. But a **Palbot-equipped Curator cannot join a Fireteam**, so choose between a linked local Engineer and a protected remote TAG-repair controller.

## Pinned profile and ranking

**Military Order Curator: ID 1587 · AVA 1 · Regular LI · MOV 10-10 cm · CC15 · BS12 · PH10 · WIP12 · ARM1 · BTS3 · W1 · S2.** All variants have **Engineer, Immunity (Shock), Religious Troop, Deactivator, GizmoKit**, plus **Combi Rifle, D-Charges, AP Mine, Pistol and CCW**. No built-in Minelayer.

| Opt | Loadout | Pts / SWC | Rating | Selection |
|---:|---|---:|---|---|
| **1** | **AP Mine / basic** | **17 / 0** | **A linked with useful repair route; A as solo TAG mechanic** | **Default.** Engineer, D-Charges and mine, with Fireteam eligibility |
| **2** | AP Mine + **Deployable Cover** | **18 / 0** | **A- conditional** | Pay +1 if a defensive Fireteam or protected repair station can actually use the cover |
| **3** | AP Mine + **Armed Turret (Marksman Rifle)** | **20 / 0** | **B+ conditional** | Pay +3 for a placed independent ARO piece when the list can spare its placement Order |

**Independent Palbot packages (no Fireteam):** base Curator + one Palbot **20/0**, Cover + Palbot **21/0**, Turret + Palbot **23/0**, base + two Palbots **23/0**. A Palbot costs **3pts**, contributes **no additional Regular Order**, and is controlled by the Curator for remote Engineer actions. Two Servants provide alternative routes, **not two simultaneous repairs**.

**The Curator does not have Specialist Operative printed in its profile.** Engineer can fulfill Engineer-specific scenario objectives or Classified requirements when applicable, but do not count it as an unrestricted console-pusher in every mission.

## The decisive difference from Kestrel Machinist: Fireteams

The same **WIP12 = 60%** Engineer roll applies to both. The Curator is not a better pure repair die; it is a more flexible **on-table support package**.

| Consideration | Kestrel Machinist | Military Order Curator |
|---|---|---|
| Basic cost | 15pts | **17pts** |
| WIP / first Engineer roll | 12 / 60% | **12 / 60%** |
| Base weapons | Combi, D-Charges | Combi, D-Charges, **AP Mine** |
| Other defensive skills | — | **Religious, Immunity (Shock)** |
| Armed Turret at +3pts | Combi Rifle turret (18pts total) | **Marksman Rifle turret (20pts total)** |
| Fireteams | **None** | **Military Orders / Order Sergeants**, correct chart entries only |
| Palbot controller | Yes, independently | Yes, **but forfeits Fireteam participation** |

### Exactly where the Curator links

1. **Military Orders Core/Haris/Duo:** one Curator is legal, but **does not count as Crosier**. This family needs a valid chart required-choice member (Crosier, Teutonic or Santiago FTO, using applicable chart equivalences). Do not treat the Curator as a Crosier-equivalent for FT level.
2. **Order Sergeants Haris/Duo:** one Curator is legal and **counts as an Order Sergeant** for FT level. **At least one actual Order Sergeant FTO** is mandatory for this Fireteam family; Curator alone is not an anchor.
3. **No Crusade or Velox native Curator row:** Curator is not a Wildcard. Do not import another sectorial's engineer or FT permissions.

**FT2 is BS Attack (+1 Success Die), NOT +1 Burst.** Two equivalent members (e.g. **Order Sergeant FTO Hacker/Pitcher + Curator**) get this benefit on eligible BS attacks, including a directly fired Pitcher; an additional die is rolled, then discarded. It does not change the Pitcher's ammunition consumption or apply to a Long Skill. **FT3** gives +3 Discover/+1 Dodge; **FT4** +1 BS; **FT5** Sixth Sense, following the actual qualifying/equivalent model count.

The linked Curator saves independent **movement Orders** when its entire team is already advancing to the place requiring repair, technical intervention or mine placement. But to perform its own Engineer or place a deployable on the active turn, it must be the **Fireteam Leader** for that Order; it does not repair or mine while another leader independently attacks.

**Important Core trap:** the Curator is an eligible extra body, **not** an Order Sergeant/Crosier-equivalent in a Military Orders Core. Replacing a fourth/fifth Crosier-equivalent with Curator may lower the Fireteam level and forfeit important bonuses. Add only if its actual Engineer job warrants the change.

## Best linked arrangements

| Exact composition | Pts / SWC | Formation and level | Why take it |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **FTO Hacker/Pitcher 18 + Curator 17** | **35 / 0.5** | **Order Sergeants Duo, FT2** | **Preferred linked support choice**: direct-fire Pitcher +1SD, Hacking Device, actual Engineer, AP Mine; keeps Haris and Core free |
| **FTO HRL 15 + Curator 17** | **32 / 1.5** | **Order Sergeants Duo, FT2** | Inexpensive reactive launcher +1SD, plus a real Engineer and mine; less attractive if no recoverable targets |
| **FTO HRL 15 + FTO Hacker 18 + Curator 17** | **50 / 2** | **Order Sergeants Haris, FT3** | HRL defence, Pitcher and Hacker, Engineer/mine; all three count as Order Sergeants |
| **FTO Hacker 18 + Infirmarer BSG Doctor 17 + Curator 17** | **52 / 0.5** | **Order Sergeants Haris, FT3** | Technician, Doctor and network; specialist/support concentration but minimal premium gunfighting |
| **FTO Hacker 18 + Bulleteer Spitfire 22 + Curator 17** | **57 / 1.5** | **Order Sergeants Haris, FT2** | Mobile Mimetism(-6) attacker plus a close Engineer; **Bulleteer is not an Order Sergeant equivalent** |

**Best practical MO option: FTO Hacker/Pitcher + Curator Duo (35/0.5).** The 18pt Hacker already wants a same-unit/counts-as partner to gain FT2's more reliable Pitcher shot. Compared with the **13pt basic FTO** partner, Curator costs **+4 points** and adds an actual Engineer, AP Mine, Deactivator and D-Charges. This is excellent role compression **if** TAG/REM recovery, engineering mission actions or mine placement is relevant. The **17pt Infirmarer** is the same-price alternate partner when a **Doctor** for valuable infantry is more useful.

**Defensive HRL Duo (32/1.5)** is similarly economical, but the 1.5 SWC cost competes with MO's HMGs, TAGs and other attack weapons.

**Haris caution:** MO gets only **one Haris**; the 50/2 Engineer/Hacker/HRL team is not automatically more valuable than a **Teutonic/Santiago/Crux** pressure Haris. Build the team for a real mission/route/repair task, not to bundle tools merely because they fit.

## Linked Curator versus Palbot: mutually exclusive plans

**N5.3 Peripheral rule: a Servant's Controller cannot join any Fireteam or Coordinated Order.** The Curator and Palbot are counted as one trooper/Order source and always belong to the same Combat Group; the remote Engineer roll uses the **Curator's WIP**, not the Palbot's printed WIP. The Servant can travel far from its Controller and activate via the Controller's Orders, including when the TAG is in a different Combat Group.

### A. Solo Seraph or Tikbalang: favor independent Curator + Palbot

- **20pts total** for base Curator 17 + Palbot 3 is a reasonable default for a valuable, recoverable TAG. Hide the Curator and preposition the Palbot behind safe retreat/recovery geometry.
- **Seraph Super-Jump and Tikbalang Climbing Plus** often carry the TAG far from a walking infantry team; a Palbot's **MOV15-10** and ability to approach from elsewhere is substantially more useful than theoretical link movement.
- Curator can sit in **Group 2** while the TAG spends Group 1 attack Orders; this is often a better order-economy division than dedicating a high-value attacking team to engineering.
- For Remote Presence targets, eligible **Command Token reroll** converts the independent **60% first WIP12 success** to **84% across two attempts**. This is not a blanket reroll for any target. A failed wound repair can still cause damage; survival and reachable geometry matter.
- The **Tech-Bee at 9pts** is complementary and an excellent cheap Order, especially for TAGCom, but **GizmoKit is not Engineer** and does not fully replace Curator's engineering role. The two may be taken together.

**Not an absolute tax:** skip the independent repair package when the TAG is intended to trade deep into impossible-to-reach territory, the scenario rewards immediate scoring more, or an existing effective recovery route already exists.

### B. Order Sergeants + Bulleteer or advancing specialist team: favor linked Curator

A Curator actually travelling with a **Bulleteer** and/or an Order Sergeant Hacker is in position to attempt a repair without needing a separate Servant route. It can also deal with eligible tech states, apply Deactivator and place AP Mines on the team route. **A disabled or Unconscious Bulleteer still needs legal repair access and does not automatically rejoin a Fireteam merely because it is repaired.**

The advantage is **movement efficiency and role compression**, not higher Engineer success. The downsides are **ARM1/W1 fragility, MOV10-10** alongside a faster Bulleteer, and losing the safe Palbot option.

### C. Linked Curator supporting a nearby independent TAG

Possible if a dedicated **Crosier/Order Sergeant fireteam** naturally operates near the TAG's fallback position. But **the TAG is not in that Fireteam** (MO has no TAG Fireteam); it can Super-Jump/climb away while the Curator remains with the foot team. Be skeptical of claims that linked Curator is automatic TAG insurance.

## AP Mine, Deployable Cover and Armed Turret

### AP Mine: useful but not predeployed

All Curator variants retain **AP Mine**. Unlike a Raveneye Minelayer, Curator **does not pre-place one at deployment**; it must spend a legal Order placing it. On an advancing team, this can screen a flank, protect a specialist/objective or leave an obstacle on a return route. The mine is attractive precisely because the Curator **also** fulfills an Engineer role, rather than being a standalone 17pt minelayer purchase.

**For turn-zero DZ denial, compare Raveneye 12pt E/M Mine/Cybermine**, whose Minelayer deployment is much more immediate and economical. Curator brings an entirely different repair/linked utility package.

### Deployable Cover (18pts): reasonable +1 when there is a placement plan

The +1pt profile preserves all base equipment and adds **Deployable Cover**, subject to its own deployment/placement rules. It can create protected positions for the Curator, Hacker or launcher, or establish more selective shooting/cover geometry. Its actual worth is limited by needing an Order and finding useful positioning; natural terrain often solves the same problem for free.

**Prefer 18pt Cover** over 17pt basic if the linked team is explicitly holding a known vulnerable position or mobile placement offers a tangible objective benefit. Otherwise save the point.

### Armed Turret Marksman Rifle (20pts): optional +3, especially Group 2

The +3pt turret is **not deployed for free**: Curator uses **Place Deployable** during play. The turret is a separate stationary defensive threat, not a Fireteam member or another Regular Order. The Marksman Rifle changes its engagement bands versus Kestrel Machinist's **Combi Rifle turret**. Under the Armed Turret rules it uses **BS10, Total Reaction and 360º Visor**, has **ARM2/BTS3/STR1**, is **Disposable (1)/Non-Reloadable**, and may be positioned via its **Perimeter** rule subject to legal placement. The turret cannot attack a Camouflage Marker as if it were an exposed Model.

**Good:** spend an underused **Group 2** Order to cover a TAG fallback location, lateral DZ approach, or objective route. **Bad:** choosing turret over the first **3pt Palbot**, or expecting protection in the opponent's first turn when you went second and have not placed it. If joined to a Fireteam, Curator must take the leader role for its own placement Order, so this is not a free turret alongside another leader's attack.

**Default priority:** for TAG lists buy **repair reach (Palbot)** before turret; for linked lists choose **17pt base or 18pt Cover** unless spare Orders and turret geometry justify 20pts.

## When to include, and when to skip

| MO list/mission | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Seraph / Tikbalang centered, no other Engineer** | **Strongly consider 17pt Curator + Palbot (20pts)**; don't expect the Fireteam to keep pace |
| **Order Sergeant FTO Hacker/Pitcher Duo is planned** | **Curator 17 as an A-level partner** if engineering/mines are relevant; Infirmarer is same-price doctor alternative |
| **Bulleteer Spitfire in Order Sergeants Haris** | Curator is interesting close repair insurance; team speed/FT2/sole-Haris opportunity cost remain |
| **Defensive Order Sergeant HRL Duo** | Curator 17 or Cover 18 is viable, but only if there's a realistic second job |
| **Already optimized FT5 Crosier/Commander Core** | **Usually skip inside Core** if it reduces Counts-as equivalence or FT5 |
| **No TAG/REM investment and engineering scenario irrelevant** | **Usually skip**; Tech-Bee/Raveneye/cheap Orders and other specialists may do more |
| **Group 2 with surplus Orders and defended flank** | Optional **20pt turret** plus Palbot as affordable extra layered defence |

## Final recommendation

**Curator is not an automatic include in all Military Orders lists, but it is an efficient and often correct Engineer choice.** Pick between two distinct plans:

1. **TAG insurance:** **base Curator 17 + Palbot 3 = 20pts**, independent and protected. This is the most convincing default when the Seraph/Tikbalang is a major investment with a realistic recovery route.
2. **Fireteam utility:** **base Curator 17**, especially in **Order Sergeant FTO Hacker 18 + Curator 17 = 35pts/0.5 SWC, FT2**. This gives a meaningful second role to a high-value support Duo without spending Core or Haris.

**Avoid** a supposed linked Curator + Palbot combination; buying the turret before basic repair reach; confusing the Curator's Counts-as **Order Sergeant** with a general **Counts-as Crosier**; and treating Engineer as universal Specialist Operative. Curator's edge over the Kestrel Machinist is **Fireteam access and utility**, not raw repair odds.
