# Infirmarers of Saint Lazarus — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A for the 17pt Boarding Shotgun Doctor in an advancing Fireteam; A+ as the second member of a true FT2 Order Sergeant Hacker/Pitcher Duo; A− independently with a Palbot. The 20pt Combi Rifle is usually a skip (C).** The Infirmarer is valuable not simply because it heals: **BS13 + Boarding Shotgun + Boarding Pistol** contributes practical close-range fighting and protection to a linked team while remaining an inexpensive WIP13 Doctor. Generally take **zero or one**, not an automatic second or third.

## Pinned profiles and ranking

**Infirmarers of Saint Lazarus: ID 1487 · AVA 3 · Regular MI · MOV 15–5 cm · CC21 · BS13 · PH11 · WIP13 · ARM2 · BTS3 · W1 · S2.** Both options have **Martial Arts L1, Doctor, Dodge (+2.5 cm), Stealth, Religious Troop, Terrain (Total), MediKit**, **Boarding Pistol** and **Shock CCW**.

| Opt | Loadout | Pts / SWC | Rating | Selection |
|---:|---|---:|---|---|
| **2** | **Boarding Shotgun**, Boarding Pistol, Shock CCW | **17 / 0** | **A linked; A+ Hacker Duo** | **Default**: proper Doctor plus credible BS13 close-range threat for a very small premium over a basic doctor |
| **1** | **Combi Rifle**, Boarding Pistol, Shock CCW | **20 / 0** | **C** | Generally skip: pays +3pts for more conventional midrange shooting at the expense of the shotgun's more distinct complementary role |

**Important:** 15–5 cm movement allows a good initial advance but is not a constant 15 cm for every Move declaration. ARM2/BTS3 and Dodge (+2.5 cm) help, but it remains a **one-Wound, non-camouflaged model**. Neither profile has a printed Doctor reroll, Doctor (2W), Immunity (Shock), or Specialist Operative. Its Doctor skill itself satisfies requirements that specifically recognize Doctors; consult the mission for other specialist eligibility.

## Why the 17pt Boarding Shotgun is the default

The core insight is **role compression**. A cheap doctor sometimes does nothing until a casualty occurs; this Infirmarer can:

- **Cover the approach of a valuable Fireteam.** BS13 with a Boarding Shotgun is a serious short-range answer to corner guards, enemies trying to close on a Hacker or a longer-range main gun, and models that cannot be efficiently handled by the team's primary shooter.
- **Use its Boarding Pistol as a second close-quarters option.** It is not helpless once a target comes inside the comfortable engagement envelope of the rest of the team. Consult the pinned weapon chart for the precise mode, range, and ammunition interactions rather than treating all short weapons as interchangeable.
- **Heal nearby two-Wound Knights** if they enter the applicable Unconscious state and are reachable. This is a real potential swing in MO, which frequently pays 25–50+ points for individual infantry models.
- **Advance with the Fireteam for shared movement**, avoiding separate positioning Orders just to get a Doctor to where the action is happening. When performing the Doctor action in the active turn, the Infirmarer must be the Fireteam Leader for the Order; it does not heal automatically while the gunfighter is attacking.

**FT2 matters for shooting, not Doctor WIP.** FT Level 2 grants **BS Attack (+1 Success Die)**: roll an additional die and discard one, **not** +1 Burst. The Boarding Shotgun's normal rolled BS Attack benefits; so does a directly fired MediKit when applicable. A Direct Template attack requiring no Roll does not benefit. A contact **Doctor WIP13 roll remains 65%**, regardless of Fireteam level.

The **20pt Combi Rifle** makes sense only if the team's spare gun truly needs a longer conventional rifle engagement range and the +3pt premium is acceptable. For most advancing MO Fireteams, the different range band and close-range utility of the 17pt shotgun is more valuable than a redundant Combi.

## Exact Fireteam eligibility — do not confuse membership with counts-as

The pinned Army chart provides three native avenues:

1. **Military Orders Core/Haris/Duo** — **max 1 Infirmarer**. Requires an eligible chart required-choice trooper (Crosier, Teutonic or Santiago FTO, using the chart's conditions). **Infirmarer does not count as Crosier or Teutonic** for FT level; adding one can lower levels in a carefully optimized Core.
2. **Order Sergeants Haris/Duo** — **max 1 Infirmarer**, **counts as Order Sergeant** in this row, so it can contribute to FT2/FT3. **At least one actual FTO Order Sergeant is mandatory**; an Infirmarer cannot anchor this Fireteam by itself.
3. **Velox Duo** — **max 1 Infirmarer** and legal beside an appropriate Motorized Montesa, Bulleteer or Joan v2. **Does not count as those units**; a mixed two-model Velox Duo is FT1 unless some other exact equivalence applies.

**Not eligible for Crusade.** The 30pt Hospitaller FTO Doctor, not the Infirmarer, fills the actual linked Doctor role in a Crusade/Hospitaller Fireteam. The Infirmarer is also **not a universal Wildcard**.

### Why the Hacker + Infirmarer Duo is exceptionally efficient

The **18pt FTO Hacker/Pitcher** wants an actual same-unit/counts-as partner for FT2 so its direct-fire Pitcher has a better chance of placing a Repeater. The **17pt Infirmarer counts as Order Sergeant** in that exact chart row, so the combination achieves FT2 without buying an otherwise passive filler model.

For **35pts/0.5 SWC**: two Regular Orders, standard Hacking Device, Pitcher with a +1SD normal BS Attack, WIP13 Doctor/MediKit, BS13 Boarding Shotgun and close-in protection. It is a purposeful support Duo, not a two-model replacement for a primary attack Fireteam. The Hacker's Repeater position and the Doctor's useful patient/engagement position must still overlap enough to justify linking them.

**Compare the same-cost [Curator](curator.md) partner (17pt):** choose **Infirmarer** for elite **VITA infantry** and a stronger close-range gun; choose **Curator** for **STR TAG/REM repairs**, technical scenario jobs, D-Charges and AP Mine. Neither is universally better.

### Other Fireteam trade-offs

- **HRL Duo:** good defensively, especially if the Doctor might pick up nearby fallen infantry, but a static HRL and forward Doctor can pull the Duo in opposite directions. The HRL gains FT2 +1SD in reactive shooting; it is not +1 Burst.
- **Combat Core:** Infirmarer is valuable when it genuinely travels to heal a two-Wound fighter or needs its BSG. It is a **non-equivalent body** in the MO chart, unlike Crosier, Knight Commander and eligible FTO Order Sergeants that contribute Crosier equivalence. Do not forfeit FT4/FT5 just to add a theoretical doctor.
- **Velox:** possible with Joan v2, the Montesa bike and Bulleteers, but not a free +1SD Duo or a reason to slow a faster independent attack piece. Doctor does **not** repair the Bulleteer (STR); an Engineer such as Curator does.

## Compared with Trauma-Doc (Kestrel) and other medical choices

The [Kestrel Trauma-Doc](../../kestrel/units/trauma-doc.md) is a useful **cross-sectorial benchmark**, **not a legal MO list option**.

| Factor | Infirmarer | Kestrel Trauma-Doc |
|---|---|---|
| Cheapest Doctor | **17pts** | **14pts** |
| With one 3pt Palbot | **20pts** | **17pts** |
| Contact Doctor success | **WIP13: 65%** | **WIP12: 60%** |
| If eligible for one Command Token Cube reroll | **87.75%** | **84%** |
| Movement / shooting | **MOV15–5; BS13** | MOV10–10; BS12 |
| Defense | **ARM2/BTS3, Dodge (+2.5 cm), Martial Arts L1, Stealth** | ARM1/BTS0 |
| Cheapest weapon | **Boarding Shotgun, Boarding Pistol** | Combi Rifle |
| Fireteam role | **MO / Order Sergeants / Velox** | **Cannot join Kestrel Fireteams** |
| Best job | **Linked forward infantry support + close-range counterplay** | **Cheap independent backfield healing via Palbot** |

**Doctor success is only a +5 percentage-point improvement**, not the main reason for the extra 3pts. The larger benefit is a higher-quality standalone model and **actual Fireteam eligibility**. For a protected backfield medic with Palbot, paying extra for BSG/BS13 often accomplishes little; in a moving MO Fireteam, those abilities are useful throughout the game.

### Infirmarer versus other MO medical options

- **30pt Hospitaller FTO Doctor:** substantially more durable two-Wound combat member, and the correct **Crusade** medic. Prefer it when already buying a Hospitaller team; avoid paying 13 extra points if an inexpensive support Duo or independent doctor does the job.
- **13pt Crosier Paramedic:** cheaper contributing model to a Crosier-equivalent Core. Its MediKit is not equivalent to a Doctor WIP13 recovery roll; it may be preferable if keeping full FT level and conserving points matter more.
- **20pt foot Montesa FTO Paramedic:** durable two-Wound Knight with Blitzen and a legal Wildcard slot, but a Paramedic rather than Doctor.
- **17pt Curator Engineer:** repairs STR and tech states, not ordinary VITA Doctor work; preferred with Seraph/Tikbalang/Bulleteer investment. Distinct but complementary support.

## Linked Doctor versus Palbot controller

A **Palbot costs 3pts**, is a Peripheral (Servant), provides **no independent Regular Order**, and uses the controller Doctor's WIP when in Silhouette contact with the patient. There is no maximum operating distance between a Servant and its controller, but only one patient can be Doctor-targeted per Order. Two Palbots offer alternate routes, not simultaneous healing.

**Crucial restriction:** A Doctor controlling a Peripheral (Servant) **cannot be part of a Fireteam**. Choose one of these plans:

- **Linked Infirmarer BSG 17:** moves along with the infantry Fireteam and personally heals accessible casualties; no Palbot.
- **Independent Infirmarer BSG + Palbot = 20pts:** hides behind cover, sends the Palbot toward a vulnerable infantry casualty/ARO perch; can also buy a second Palbot for **23pts** if distinct recovery routes justify it.
- **No Doctor:** sensible when most investment is STR REMs/TAGs, eligible recoverable infantry is scarce, or the medical Orders are unlikely to pay off.

### Doctor mechanical cautions

- Contact **Doctor WIP13 succeeds 65%**; an ordinary failed Doctor attempt on an Unconscious patient causes Dead. With a Cube and an eligible Command Token reroll, two WIP13 chances succeed **87.75%** in total, but cost the resource.
- Doctor cancels an eligible Unconscious state and normally removes **one Wound**, **not two**. It can also help cancel certain medical states such as Stunned when the conditions are met.
- The target needs **VITA** for ordinary Doctor healing; **STR** targets ordinarily need an Engineer/GizmoKit, subject to exceptions such as Technorganic. The Infirmarer cannot serve as a TAG engineer.
- Being nearby is not enough: an Unconscious model must be reachable in **Silhouette contact** (directly or by an eligible Servant), and the tactical situation may make rescue unsafe or too expensive.
- **Do not assume all casualties are recoverable.** Shock or other relevant ammunition/states may remove a vulnerable model directly instead of leaving it Unconscious; a doctor cannot revive Dead.
- An Infirmarer in a Fireteam must become **Fireteam Leader** to perform its Doctor/medical action on activation; healing does not happen for free as the other trooper shoots.

## When to take and when to skip

| MO context | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Order Sergeant FTO Hacker/Pitcher Duo available** | **Excellent (A+)** 17pt BSG partner if forward medical reach and close-range protection are useful |
| **Advancing Knight/Core Fireteam, real recoverable infantry** | **Strong (A)**, but only when the FT-level loss/slot is acceptable |
| **Defensive HRL Duo and reachable ARO recovery** | **Good (A−)**, though the Doctor and HRL may want different positioning |
| **Protected independent backfield Doctor** | **17pt BSG + Palbot 3 (A−)**; good but pays more than cross-sectorial Trauma-Doc benchmark |
| **Crusade/Hospitaller team** | **Skip the Infirmarer in that FT**; take actual FTO Hospitaller Doctor if appropriate |
| **TAG/REM-dominated, few VITA patients** | **Usually skip**; Engineer/Palbot has a more relevant mission |
| **Need to preserve high-level Crosier-equivalent Core** | **Usually skip as a Core replacement** if it lowers FT4/FT5 |
| **20pt Combi Rifle** | **Usually skip (C)**: +3 points for less differentiated support shooting |
| **Second or third Infirmarer** | **Usually skip (C)**: repeated Doctor capacity often yields diminishing returns |

## Bottom line

**Default 17pt Boarding Shotgun.** It is one of MO's better examples of a support purchase that can fight: **BS13 short-range pressure + WIP13 Doctor + Fireteam access**. The most compelling package is **Order Sergeant FTO Hacker/Pitcher 18 + Infirmarer 17 = 35pts/0.5 SWC, FT2**, leaving Core/Haris slots for harder hitters. Use an independent **Palbot controller** instead when safe, separated, remote recovery is the real priority. Do not force the Infirmarer into a full Core if doing so drops an important Fireteam level.
