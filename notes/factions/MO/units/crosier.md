# Coadjutor Crosiers — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: optional 0–2 cheap support pieces in most MO lists; not a mandatory line-troop Core.** Crosiers provide **Regular Orders, inexpensive Blitzen defensive insurance, cheap Paramedic/Commlink specialists, and the native Crosier Fireteam equivalence** needed for certain Military Orders Duo/Core constructions. **Start from zero Crosiers**, then add only the profiles that solve an actual budget, scoring, deployment or Fireteam problem. The **24pt/0SWC Black Friar Thunderbolt (+1B)** is generally a **better MSV2 buy** than the **24pt/1SWC Crosier HMG**; a **Sierra Total Reaction HMG or Thunderbolt** is generally a better stand-alone long-range ARO investment than paying 19pt/1SWC for a Crosier MULTI Sniper Minelayer.

## 1. Pinned roster and profile ranking

**Coadjutor Crosiers — ID 1560 · AVA Total · Regular LI · MOV 10–10 cm (4–4") · CC15 · BS12 · PH10 · WIP12 · ARM1 · BTS0 · W1 · S2 · Religious Troop.** No native Mimetism, Sixth Sense, MSV, TinBot or protection other than equipment printed on a particular option; **only the HMG option has MSV2**. All seven options are eligible for the **Military Orders Core/Haris/Duo** family, with the chart's Crosier cap **5 in that formation**.

**Typical quantity:** **0–2** Crosiers, often **one Blitzen and/or Paramedic**. Increase to 3–5 only if constructing a *deliberate* light-infantry defensive/command Core and the Fireteam bonuses merit the points and Core slot.

**What makes them less attractive than line infantry elsewhere?** Their **11-point starting price** has neither cheap-specialist premium combat attributes nor strong default defenses. Other cheap MO order infrastructure includes **Fugazi 7/0 (AVA1), Mulebot 8/0 (AVA2), Tech-Bee 9/0 (AVA1), Raveneye 12/0 (AVA1)**. Those are not all interchangeable (Flash/Repeater, Baggage, TAGCom, mines/FO), but if all you need is a transferable Regular Order, they make an 11pt Crosier's opportunity cost clear.

## 2. Are Crosiers mandatory for an MO Core? **No**

The canonical **Military Orders** Fireteam family has three *required-choice* types: **Crosier, Teutonic Knight, or Santiago FTO**. A native Crosier **does not have to be present** if the formation satisfies this condition through a valid Teutonic or Santiago FTO. The sectorial also has **Crusade** Fireteams built around Hospitaller FTOs, **Order Sergeants Duo/Haris** requiring a genuine FTO Sergeant, and **Velox Duo** with its own requirements. **No standard MO list is required to include a Crosier, Core, or any Fireteam**.

**What Crosiers actually contribute to a Military Orders formation:**
- **One cheap required-choice anchor** for a Duo/Haris/Core that otherwise lacks a Teutonic or Santiago FTO.
- **Native Crosier equivalence** for FT levels. **Order Sergeant FTO** and **Knight Commander** count as Crosiers *in this specific formation*. They can cheaply create **FT2**, **FT4**, or an all-Crosier-equivalent **FT5**.
- **Low-cost specialist slots:** Paramedic 13 and Commlink Specialist Operative 13, cheaper than premium midfield Knights while remaining able to activate with the link.
- **An inexpensive protected body for a command Fireteam** containing **Knight Commander LT(+1)24** (fueling a *separate* Seraph, Teutonic NCO or Sepulchre) or **Knight Commander CoC23** (behind an aggressive Joan LT).

**But the trade-off is severe:** Crosiers are **BS12, ARM1, BTS0, W1** and easily removed. Paying 60–100 points for a static Crosier-based Core can deny those points to **Teutonic or Santiago combat teams, Hospitallers, forward scoring, hackers and a second attack plan**. MO's strong alternative Fireteams mean **Crosier Core is a choice, not the faction's essential backbone**.

**Fireteam level precision:** N5.3 **FT2 = BS Attack (+1 Success Die)**, not +1 Burst. **FT3 = +3 Discover / +1 Dodge; FT4 = +1 BS; FT5 = Sixth Sense.** Actual FT level tracks matching unit/Counts-as equivalence, *not* the number of physical bodies. A Black Friar can legally join the Military Orders family but **does not count as Crosier**, so adding a Friar may fail to advance the team's FT level.

## 3. Profile decisions

### Combi Rifle 11/0 — mostly a cheerleader and native FT anchor

**Best use:** one safe Regular Order plus the cheapest *actual* Crosier body when the Fireteam chart requires one or a formation needs another equivalent member for FT2/FT4/FT5. An 11pt Crosier also helps reach the 10-trooper Combat Group limit when the army's scarcer cheap REMs/support troops are already in use.

**Why skip:** the 12pt **Blitzen** usually provides substantially more defensive threat for **one point**. If no Fireteam/required-choice advantage exists, **Fugazi 7, Mulebot 8, Tech-Bee 9, Raveneye 12** or a different mission specialist can be more efficient.

### Combi + Blitzen 12/0 — best minimalist defense

For +1 point over basic, the Blitzen gives a **Disposable (2) E/M ranged ARO**. It can threaten TAG/HI approaches, especially at its favorable **16–32" range**, without using SWC or requiring Fireteam activation. It is a *low-cost deterrent*, not a reliable standalone TAG stopper: BS12, W1 and no defensive MOD mean an elite attacker can remove it.

**Why this often earns a slot:** unlike an exposed 11pt Combi cheerleader, it can occasionally force an expensive Hackable assault piece to Dodge, reroute, or risk an E/M state. **Don't spend Orders repositioning it** as though it were a premium attacker; hide it until a useful lane matters.

**Alternative:** **Raveneye 12/0** already has predeployed E/M Mine/Cybermine plus FO/other utility. Prefer Raveneye for *persistent deployed denial and scoring* where its chosen profile and AVA apply; prefer Crosier Blitzen when the cheap **Fireteam equivalence + directly targeted ranged E/M** is the goal.

### Paramedic 13/0 — best low-cost scoring/link utility

Two points over basic buys the **Paramedic Skill and MediKit**. It remains a cheap Regular Order and can perform objectives when the mission permits that specialist type. As a Fireteam member it can accompany the team to a terminal and provide conditional recovery.

**Limitations:** The Crosier's **WIP12** and **PH10** are modest; MediKit success relies on the affected target's appropriate PH roll, and failures have real consequences. It **does not replace** an Infirmarer Doctor. More importantly, a DZ-based Core doesn't magically become a good midfield-scoring team merely by containing a Paramedic: consider **Trinitarian FO/KHD** and **Montesa Paramedic** for actual forward missions.

### Commlink (+1) Specialist Operative 13/0 — not an extra ordinary Order

The Commlink version is an ordinary **Specialist Operative**, so it remains capable of appropriate specialist mission actions in standard play. **Commlink (+1)** is a **Reinforcements-Extra-specific** rule; it does **not** award extra Command Tokens, Lieutenant Orders or another Regular Order in a normal game. In ordinary matched play it competes directly with the **same-cost 13pt Paramedic**, which offers its own MediKit upside. Use Commlink when playing **Reinforcements** or if its exact scenario role creates an advantage; don't silently include Reinforcements benefits in regular MO list evaluations.

### MSV2 HMG 24/1 — capable, but Black Friar TB generally wins the budget contest

At the same **24pt** price, the **Black Friar Thunderbolt (+1B)** has **0 SWC**, **BS13 instead of 12**, **PS6 instead of 5**, **ARM2/BTS3 instead of ARM1/BTS0**, **Albedo (-6)** versus eligible MSV/Marksmanship enemies, **Combat Instinct**, a **Nanopulser**, and **Military Orders plus Order Sergeants Fireteam access**. Both have MSV2, +3 at **16–32"**, and B1 in ordinary ARO. Friar's regular Thunderbolt is **Normal ammo (not AP)**, like the Crosier's ordinary HMG. The Thunderbolt also has **0 at 32–48"**, versus the HMG's **-3**.

**Crosier's two actual edges:** **B4 instead of B3 in the Active Turn**, and **native Crosier FT-level contribution**. The distinction is material, particularly if the objective is to clear ordinary non-MSV shooters by maximizing separate hits or to build a specific **FT2 HMG + Hacker Duo** or **FT5 Core**. FT2 HMG = **roll 5 dice, discard 1, allocate at most B4 hits**, *not* real B5. Friar FT2 = **B3 +1SD**, not B4.

**Default verdict:** **Black Friar Thunderbolt A+/first look** for a 24pt MSV2 gun. **Crosier HMG A only when B4 and FT2/FT5 geometry are intentional**, otherwise B+. This is not a claim that B3 PS6 mathematically always wins more Face-to-Face duels than B4 PS5; opponent/cover/range matter.

### MULTI Sniper + E/M Minelayer 19/1 — a weak default ARO buy

The 19/1 Crosier sniper does at least **two things**: threatens a long fire lane with MULTI Sniper modes and places **one E/M Mine at deployment** (subject to Minelayer legality). The mine costs **no active Order** and can shield a flank, ladder or backfield access. In a **Crosier + Knight Commander FT2 Duo**, the sniper also gains one Success Die for eligible AROs.

But for **23pts / 1 SWC**, the independent **Sierra Total Reaction HMG** gives **B4 in ARO, 360° Visor, Remote Presence and Engineer repairability** without needing a linked partner. Sierra's default **18pt / 0 SWC Thunderbolt (+1B)** is itself a strong independent **B3 ARO with PS6**. In straightforward long-lane defense, either Sierra can be a better deal than building around a one-Wound, BS12, B1 sniper.

**When the sniper still has a niche:** You expressly need **E/M Mine Minelayer + MULTI Sniper from one slot**, have a **Knight Commander** already whose link can grant FT2 without purchasing a new unrelated member, or want **AP/DA sniper ammunition** against long-range targets. Its E/M Mine is a distinct defensive obstacle, and the Sierra has neither an E/M Mine nor MSR ammunition flexibility.

**Default:** **B−, generally skip** unless a legal FT2 defense and mine placement are central to the plan. Do not elevate it to a top ARO by describing the prelaid mine as though it were guaranteed to stop a TAG.

### Spitfire + X-Visor 18/1 — little reason to take

**BS12/B4** and an **X-Visor** reducing unfavorable range penalties are usable, but MO has many stronger close-/midrange attackers, including **Teutonic NCO Spitfire29/1.5**, Knight or TAG options, and cheap **Bulleteer Spitfire22/1 with Mimetism(-6)**. Crosier Spitfire does **not** provide Mimetism, MSV or meaningful durability. At **only six more points and the same 1 SWC**, the Crosier MSV2 HMG provides a stronger differentiated long-range/anti-Mimetism task; the zero-SWC Black Friar Thunderbolt is an even clearer rival.

**Default:** skip; take only if an unusual native FT-level requirement or very tight points budget specifically favors this loadout.

| **Crosier MSV2 HMG24 + Order Sergeant FTO Hacker/Pitcher18**, Military Orders Duo | **42/1.5** | **FT2** | The strongest special-case HMG argument: B4+1SD MSV2 and a useful Pitcher/Hacker. The Hacker must lead an Order to actually fire a Pitcher. |
| **Crosier MSR Minelayer19 + Knight Commander CoC23**, Military Orders Duo | **42/1** | **FT2** | One prelaid E/M Mine + B1(+1SD) sniper ARO; still often less efficient as pure ARO than a standalone Sierra. |
| **Crosier basic11 + Crosier Paramedic13 + Commander LT(+1)24 + Order Sergeant Hacker18 + FTO HRL15**, Military Orders Core | **81/2** | **FT5** | Five Crosier-equivalents; command + projected hacking + specialists + HRL. Costs Core slot/2 SWC and leaves little heavy combat power in team. |
| **Crosier MSV2 HMG24 + three basic Crosiers33 + Knight Commander CoC23**, Military Orders Core | **80/1** | **FT5** | HMG BS13/B4+1SD plus Sixth Sense; large light-infantry investment to earn that gun bonus. |
| **Black Friar TB24 + three basic Crosiers33 + Knight Commander CoC23**, Military Orders Core | **80/0** | **FT4** | Friar BS14/B3+1SD/PS6, Albedo, saves SWC; Friar itself is **not** a Crosier and **does not grant FT5**. |

**Bonus warning:** Black Friar + Order Sergeant Hacker is a legal **Order Sergeants Duo** at **FT1**, not FT2, because Black Friar is neither an Order Sergeant nor a Counts-as. Conversely, a Crosier + Order Sergeant FTO forms an **FT2 Military Orders Duo** when selected in that family's row. A Duo requires a legal native choice; generic Wildcards and Counts-as equivalence do not automatically erase Fireteam minimum/required-choice rules.

**Alternative no-Crosier links are real:** Teutonic(s) and Santiago FTO can satisfy the **Military Orders** required-choice condition, so a **Teutonic Panzerfaust18 + Teutonic Specialist19 + Black Friar Thunderbolt24** is a **61/0 SWC Military Orders Haris, FT2**. Its job is anti-tech shooting plus sturdy Panzerfaust and scoring presence, and **no Crosier is required**. Alternatively, the **Order Sergeant FTO Hacker18 + Curator17** is a **35/0.5 SWC FT2 Order Sergeants Duo** and similarly needs no Crosier. They are different unit/job architectures, not a universal answer to every mission.

## 5. Competitive role/opportunity cost in MO

| Question | If yes | If no |
|---|---|---|
| Do I need a cheap **native Crosier** to build a specific legal Duo/Core or reach FT2/FT5? | **11pt basic**, **12pt Blitzen** or **13pt Paramedic** are efficient | A Teutonic/Santiago-anchored Military Orders Fireteam or a Sergeant Duo may be better |
| Do I have a safe **1pt upgrade** for a DZ cheerleader? | **12pt Blitzen** is usually preferable to 11pt Combi | If a cheap mine/Flash/Repeater/Order is the only job, compare 7–12pt support units |
| Do I require an additional cheap **mission specialist in a moving link**? | **13pt Paramedic** or **13pt SO**, subject to mission and whether it can reach the objective | A hidden/infiltrating Trinitarian or Montesa may be a more realistic scorer |
| Do I need **MSV2 gunfire**? | Start with **Friar TB24/0**; choose **Crosier HMG24/1** for B4 / high-level Crosier FT bonuses | Avoid buying redundant MSV2 gun just to fill a link |
| Do I need an **independent long-lane ARO**? | Prefer **Sierra TB18/0 (B3)** or **Sierra HMG23/1 (B4)**; use mines/deployables separately | MSR Minelayer19/1 only when its mine + sniper + FT synergy is the *actual* reason |
| Is the main Core already occupied by powerful Knights/Hospitallers? | **No need for Crosier Core**; can still buy 0–1 independent cheap Order/Blitzen | Consider tiny Duo or deliberate command-support Core only if it fits better |

**Primary evidence/opinions (not win-rate proof):**
- [Robert Shepherd MO faction focus (~10–14m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JrMTA91V7wc&t=630s): Crosiers' cost squeezes MO's cheap Regular infrastructure; HMG is a *contingency* visor gun, sniper/minelayer and Blitzen are modest upside, not list-defining purchases.
- [Sneaky Snake MO review (~95–98m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kRaambqovmw&t=5700s): examples of HMG + Sergeant Hacker Duo and MSR + Commander Duo; also cautions that large cheap-light-infantry links can displace MO's premium Knights. These describe *permitted tactical approaches*, not proof of superiority.
- [Ryan MO post-update guide (~12–14m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xjs6rsspz8I&t=720s): praises MSV2 HMG as a viable backup opener, the +1pt Blitzen, and the E/M Mine Minelayer, while conceding their BS12 limitations.

**Final ranking within MO:**
1. **0–2 total Crosiers unless deliberate Core.** **12/0 Blitzen**, **13/0 Paramedic**, then **11/0 basic** as needed for legal FT level, Specialist coverage or cheap Regular Order.
2. **24/1 MSV2 HMG** is an **optional FT2/FT5-specific gun**, not the default visor choice over Black Friar Thunderbolt.
3. **19/1 MULTI Sniper Minelayer** is a **niche mine/linked sniper**, not a preferred default ARO over Sierra.
4. **13/0 Commlink Specialist** is mainly differentiated under **Reinforcements** (though Specialist Operative remains functional otherwise); **18/1 Spitfire** is a general skip.

**Bottom line:** **Crosiers are useful only when they earn their slot as low-cost Regular Orders, 0SWC E/M threats, specialists, or native Fireteam equivalents. They are not compulsory MO line troops.** Do not automatically trade an effective Knight/Teutonic/Order Sergeant Fireteam for a weak light-infantry Core merely to field them.
