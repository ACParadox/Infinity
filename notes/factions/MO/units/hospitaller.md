# Knights Hospitaller — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A as a Crusade Fireteam enabler and premium-character support; A− overall.** The best reasons to buy a Hospitaller are **the required actual FTO anchor for Crusade** and **durable, BS14, two-Wound Fireteam glue** alongside Joan, De Fersen, Hawkins or Knights of Justice. **TinBot Firewall (-3) 27** is the first pick for a hacker-led Crusade, **Boarding Shotgun + E/Mitter 26** is the economical combat anchor, and **Doctor 30** rises substantially in value when escorting multiple expensive Knights. **HMG 32/1.5** is an efficient *linked* ranged option, but rarely the best isolated MO attacker. Usually take **one or two Hospitallers in an intended Crusade**, not five by default; **zero is perfectly normal without Crusade**.

## The core case: Hospitaller as Crusade Fireteam glue

**Crusade Core, Haris and Duo each require at least one actual Knight Hospitaller FTO.** A Hospitaller-equivalent Wildcard, even Joan or De Fersen, **does not replace this native minimum**. A solo non-FTO Hospitaller cannot satisfy it either.

The practical benefit of paying 26–30 points is not just one extra durable Order: the FTO **legally anchors a Fireteam that gives higher-level bonuses to more expensive characters**. Counts-as matters:

| Crusade-compatible participant | Counts as Hospitaller for FT level? | Tactical implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Hospitaller FTO** | **Yes** | Required actual anchor; 2W BS14 support/combat |
| **Joan v1 or v2** | **Yes** | Premium offensive/command character; only one Joan total |
| **De Fersen** | **Yes** | WIP14 Hacking Device / Trinity (+1SD), wants Firewall |
| **Hawkins** | **Yes** | Actual Specialist Operative and very efficient close fighter |
| **Knight of Justice** | **Yes** | Durable gun, FO or Hacker/Pitcher depending on profile |
| **Mendoza FTO** | **No** | Eligible Wildcard with own TinBot and Number 2, but lowers the level in a mixed team |
| **Santiago FTO** | **No** | Can join Crusade, but does not contribute Hospitaller equivalence |

As a result, **Hospitaller FTO + De Fersen = FT2**; **Hospitaller FTO + Joan + De Fersen = FT3**; **Hospitaller FTO + Hawkins + Justice = FT3**. In contrast, **Hospitaller FTO + Mendoza FTO = FT1**, and adding one equivalent such as Joan makes that three-body team **FT2**, not FT3.

**Fireteam bonuses:** FT1 activates as a team; FT2 gives **BS Attack (+1 Success Die)** (roll one extra die then discard one, **not** +1 Burst); FT3 adds **+3 Discover / +1 Dodge**; FT4 adds **+1 BS**; FT5 grants **Sixth Sense**. Check **equivalent member count**, not simply team size. A Mendoza-containing team may be good on its own merits without attaining FT3.

**Alternative team architecture:** A Teutonic TinBot is 19 points in the *Military Orders* Fireteam family, but cannot anchor a *Crusade*. Paying 27 for the Hospitaller is buying both improved stats/durability and access to a different team architecture. If the character package works well as a Military Orders Haris with a cheap required-choice unit, compare the whole team costs before assuming Crusade is mandatory.

## Profile 2: MULTI Rifle + TinBot Firewall (-3) — the hacker escort

**27 / 0 SWC · A+ when it protects De Fersen or a Justice Hacker.**

- **Firewall (-3)** gives protective hacking modifiers to eligible members of the TinBot bearer’s Fireteam; useful for a cluster of expensive, hackable Knights and particularly valuable for a forward **De Fersen** or **Justice Hacker/Pitcher**.
- **BS14 MULTI Rifle, ARM4/BTS6, W2** are not dead-weight filler. The bearer can fight at normal rifle distances, provide a second competent shooter and survive mistakes that would remove a cheap line troop.
- **No extra SWC**, so the team can preserve allowance for a TAG, Seraph, Sepulchre, MSV2/HRL or independent HMG.
- **Not automatic in a non-Hacker team.** If enemy hacking exposure is low or the team has no hacker to duel enemy hackers, the BSG's attack utility or the Doctor's recovery may be worth more.

**Redundancy check:** **Mendoza FTO (40/0)** already brings a **TinBot Firewall (-3)** and **Number 2**. Likewise, a relevant **Santiago FTO KHD/TinBot** can cover Firewall needs. Do **not** pay for a Hospitaller TinBot solely to stack a second -3 Firewall; choose BSG/Doctor unless the exact team composition demands otherwise.

## Profile 4: Boarding Shotgun + E/Mitter — efficient fighting glue

**26 / 0 SWC · A.** The **lowest-cost FTO Hospitaller** is arguably the most flexible one when the premium characters already have their own impressive weapons.

- **Boarding Shotgun** gives BS14 close-range pressure, including AP mode where appropriate, to complement midrange MULTI Rifles/Marksman Rifles and longer-ranged Spitfires or HMGs.
- **E/Mitter** extends the model's influence against vulnerable HI, TAGs and REMs: ranged E/M threat is strategically different from just another shotgun. It also avoids spending SWC on a second heavy gun.
- **Two Wounds, ARM4/BTS6, Stealth and DA CCW** make it a real second-wave assault piece, not just an ordinary regular-order filler.
- In **FT2+**, rolled BS Attacks benefit from **+1SD**. This **does not** increase weapon Burst or enhance a Direct Template roll that is not made.
- The drawback is **no objective-specialist skill**. It provides the required anchor and guns, but a companion character or independent infiltrator must handle scoring.

**Especially attractive with:** Joan as the higher-quality assault centerpiece; De Fersen when another TinBot exists; Hawkins when you want an additional E/M ranged tool; Mendoza FTO, which already carries Firewall; or a Justice FO/Spitfire if no second ranged HMG is needed.

**Versus Hawkins BSG 26:** Hawkins brings BS Attack (+1B) on his own BSG profile plus Specialist Operative and Combat Instinct. Hawkins is often the more impressive independent shotgun specialist; **Hospitaller BSG is still essential if this specific team needs an actual Crusade-required Hospitaller FTO**.

## Profile 7: Doctor + MULTI Rifle — underrated with expensive characters

**30 / 0 SWC · A in Joan/Hawkins/Justice-heavy Crusade.** This is one of the unit's strongest examples of role compression:

- Advances with the same Fireteam as two-Wound fighters it may need to recover.
- Provides **WIP13 Doctor, MediKit** and a BS14 MULTI Rifle on a comparatively durable two-Wound platform.
- A successful recovery of Joan, Justice, Hawkins or another elite Knight can recover far more combat value than rescuing a cheap infantry model.
- Counts as Hospitaller, helping the character-heavy team reach **FT2/FT3**.

**What not to overstate:** A standard contact Doctor roll at WIP13 is **65%** before eligible rerolls, the same basic WIP as an Infirmarer. A Doctor action requires Orders, positioning and a recoverable casualty. A model in Dead state cannot simply be recovered. The Doctor's additional durability **does not grant a better WIP roll**.

**Versus 17-point Infirmarer BSG:** Infirmarer is cheaper, has valuable BS13 close-range fighting and can join other MO team families, **but cannot join Crusade**. If using an actual Crusade, the Hospitaller Doctor is the relevant *linked* medical choice. A **Palbot controller cannot remain in a Fireteam**: choose personal linked support *or* a separate Palbot-based recovery model, not both on the same Hospitaller.

**When to skip:** one offensive character in a cheap Duo, a list with few recoverable VITA pieces, or when paying an additional 30 points removes mission scoring, tech defense or an important independent attacker.

## Profile 3: HMG — efficient *linked* long-range opener, not mandatory

**32 / 1.5 SWC · A− in Crusade, B independently.** At only 32pts for BS14, ARM4, BTS6 and W2, the HMG is good at engaging ordinary defenders and clearing long lanes. It is a credible active-turn Fireteam leader, especially with FT2 +1SD.

**But the user-facing objection is correct:** no persistent **Mimetism, MSV, AP HMG, Surprise Attack or equivalent visual/anti-ARM advantage**. It struggles to generate an especially favorable face-to-face roll against Mimetism (-6), strong reactive shooters or heavily armored TAGs. Frenzy also penalizes cover later in the game after wounding a target.

Choose the HMG when **the Crusade itself** is responsible for opening the long-range route. If a **Tikbalang, Seraph, Sepulchre, linked Justice gun or another effective first-stage shooter** already does that job, prefer BSG/TinBot/Doctor support instead. Compare **Crosier MSV2 HMG 24/1** as a lower-cost, differently specialized anti-Mimetism option, not a durability equivalent.

**A full HMG + Doctor + TinBot Haris costs 89/1.5:** sturdy and autonomous, but it still lacks the unique qualities and objective access of the character-focused options below.

## Stealth, Frenzy, Lt and other operational cautions

### Stealth on hackable HI is valuable, not immunity

Hospitallers have **Stealth**. During their Active Turn, appropriate movement/Idle declarations can avoid ordinary Zone of Control and Hacking Area AROs from enemies without LoF. This is useful when pushing a hackable Crusade through enemy Repeater coverage.

**Limits:** firing, hacking, Doctor actions or interacting with objectives can grant normal AROs; enemy **Sixth Sense/Combat Instinct**, Targeted and some equipment/deployable interactions can defeat the stealth approach. Mixed team members can also generate ARO complications. Do not treat a Stealth Knight as having automatic protection whenever a teammate is activated.

### Frenzy and Limited Cover

After a Hospitaller directly inflicts a qualifying Wound or kill, **Frenzy activates in the States Phase**: the model gains **Impetuous and Limited Cover** for the rest of the game. This is part of the price discount on a tough, elite troop, but makes an exposed HMG or long-lane ARO less reliable after it has done damage.

Being in a Fireteam does **not erase the printed Frenzy trait**. Do not assume that linked HMG can rely indefinitely on the same defensive cover modifiers. If the trooper actually uses an Impetuous activation, consider the consequences for Fireteam integrity and positioning.

### Lieutenant variants: generally skip

The **27/0 TinBot LT** and **32/1 HMG LT** can be selected for an intentionally aggressive Lieutenant plan, but both compete with **Knight Commander LT (+1 Order) 24/0** protected in the backfield and with **Knight Commander CoC 23/0** as insurance for Joan/Justice/Hospitaller fighting-LT strategies.

Knight Commander LT (+1) is best when **NCO** truly uses the second Special Lieutenant Order; otherwise the premium is not free value. A Hospitaller HMG LT saves **0.5 SWC** versus the 32/1.5 ordinary HMG, but you place command responsibility on a model you normally want exposed to enemy fire. Spending an ordinary Lieutenant Special Order on a linked Lieutenant requires checking **Fireteam integrity/leader rules**, not assuming a cost-free linked bonus attack.

**Verdict:** **C+ TinBot LT**, **C HMG LT**; neither is a default MO command piece. If Joan is the fighting LT, keep Knight Commander **CoC in a separate defensive team**, not alongside the primary assault package.

### Doctor and Fireteam positioning

A linked Doctor needs to reach the patient and usually be the activated Fireteam Leader for a Doctor action. A remote **Palbot** route is a distinct independent-Doctor configuration, incompatible with having that controller Doctor in the Fireteam. More generally, a medic that trails a forward HMG by too many Orders may not provide real value; plan where the casualties will fall.

## Decision guide and what to skip

| Listbuilding situation | Selection |
|---|---|
| No Crusade Duo/Haris/Core intended | **Usually take zero Hospitallers** |
| Cheapest legal Crusade anchor for characters | **BSG + E/Mitter 26** |
| Crusade with De Fersen or Justice Hacker | **TinBot 27**, barring existing Firewall |
| Crusade with Mendoza FTO | **BSG 26 or Doctor 30**; Mendoza already brings Firewall |
| Crusade with Joan/Hawkins/Justice and plausible recovery targets | **Doctor 30** |
| Need the Fireteam itself to open long ARO lanes | **HMG 32/1.5** |
| Already have effective TAG/Sepulchre/other long gun | **Skip Hosp HMG**; use support loadout |
| Solo flank/scorer | **Skip 32pt Forward Deployment BSG by default**; compare actual mission specialists and other flank threats |
| Want a premium fighting LT | Consider the Lieutenant variants **only** with a full command-risk plan |
| Considering 4–5 elite Hospitaller/character bodies | Ask whether missing Orders, specialists, redundancy or a second attack axis are more important |

**Bottom line:** Hospitallers are not MO must-takes, but they are **the premier actual Crusade anchors**. The smartest use is often **one 26pt BSG, 27pt TinBot, or 30pt Doctor accompanying one or two superior characters**. Take the HMG only when the formation itself needs a long-range opener; take Lieutenant profiles rarely; do not mistake Mendoza or Santiago FTO for Hospitaller-equivalent FT-level contributors.
