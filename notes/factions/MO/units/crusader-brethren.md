# Crusader Brethren — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A situational utility piece; not a must-take.** The strongest Crusaders are the two **23pt/0SWC** loadouts: **Disco Baller** for independent airborne Eclipse projection and **Forward Observer** for late-entry mission scoring. They provide a different attack vector from an advancing Knight Fireteam, but **ARM3/W1, no Mimetism, no Fireteam, and an off-table Order opportunity cost** make them poor generic line fighters. Usually consider **0–1 Crusader**; a second needs a specific asymmetric/scenario plan.

**Profile separation matters:** the Disco Baller is **not a Specialist** and has **no Flash Pulse**; the FO is a Specialist but **does not carry Disco Baller**; the MULTI/LFT and Spitfire versions have **neither FO nor Disco Baller/D-Charges**. Do not merge the loadouts.

## How the airborne package actually works

**Parachutist is normally the conservative default.** Choose a useful table-edge entry outside the **enemy deployment zone** and avoid a potentially disastrous Combat Jump roll. It has no PH check, but the landing surface must border the table edge; entering does not bypass AROs. Ordinary **Parachutist does not grant Deployment Zone** and cannot just walk onto the enemy's DZ edge through a prohibited entry point.

**Combat Jump is the higher-ceiling tool, not guaranteed deep-strike access.** It can choose a legal horizontal landing surface anywhere on the table, except the restrictions on enclosed scenery, contacts and objectives. A basic **PH11 landing roll succeeds 55%** without modifiers; friendly **Controlled Jump +3** makes it **PH14 = 70%**, before any opposing modifiers. Failure places the Crusader in its own DZ along the table edge. Reactive troops get their ARO opportunity following landing resolution. A landing model gets **no Partial Cover in the entry Order**. Plan a genuinely clear landing spot, not just the most devastating possible position.

**Off-table Order accounting:**
- An AD Crusader **does not contribute its Regular Order to the pool** during the Tactical Phase while off-table. This is a real cost to an already order-sensitive MO list.
- It **can enter with its own Order** via Parachutist or Combat Jump. It is not forced to spend a pooled Regular Order merely to arrive; further actions after entry require available Orders.
- Keeping the model off-table for two turns leaves its pooled-order contribution unavailable for those turns. If immediate order efficiency is more important than reserve threat, consider deploying normally, if permitted by the selected deployment plan.
- Put the Crusader in a Combat Group that can fund useful **post-arrival** actions. An airborne model that enters and then cannot shoot, place Eclipse or score is a poor use of its 23–31 points.

**After landing:** Super-Jump (Jet Propulsion) lets it change direction during its jump and potentially use **Jump + BS Attack** to exploit angles over terrain. The entire flight trajectory is declared for AROs and a jumping trooper receives **no Partial Cover along that trajectory**. Combat Instinct mitigates Surprise Attack/Stealth interactions but **does not grant 360º LoF, Mimetism or immunity to AROs**.

**CC is a deterrent or opportunistic finish.** CC21 + MA2 + E/M CCW is meaningful if an exposed Hackable target is already accessible, but PH11, one Wound and a one-model delivery problem do not make Crusaders the preferred TAG or elite-CC hunter.

## Option 3: Boarding Shotgun + Disco Baller — 23/0 (A)

This is the most distinctive Crusader. For the same cost as Konstantinos FTO, an **independent** trooper can arrive on a different axis and launch Eclipse where the opponent did not expect it.

**Good uses:**
1. Establish an Eclipse crossing route for another MO attacker or mission specialist after the Crusader enters from a flank.
2. Deny an exposed long ARO lane for a subsequent movement/attack Order; Eclipse blocks **MSV** as well as ordinary vision, including friendly MSV.
3. Force the opponent to deal with a flanking Boarding Shotgun while the heavy Knights/TAG press the opposite side.
4. Place D-Charges for a relevant objective/classified when the mission specifically calls for that equipment.

**Precise Disco rules:**
- **B1, Disposable (2), Double Shot, Targetless, Speculative Attack.** Normal direct fire places a Disco Ball after a successful **Normal BS Roll**, and the Eclipse template appears in the **Conclusion** step of that Order. The Eclipse **cannot protect the Crusader from AROs during the placement Order** or its earlier arrival Order.
- Direct-fire range MODs: **0 at 0–8 inches; +3 at 8–16; −3 at 16–24; −6 at 24–32**. The Crusader has **BS13**; it does **not** receive the Fireteam's +1 Success Die that Konstantinos can gain in a valid FT2.
- **Double Shot** allows both disposable shots to be committed in one attack when desirable, or they can be conserved for later. Don't casually spend both charges against an insignificant position.
- A deployed Disco Ball remains after its Eclipse template **deactivates at the start of the States Phase**. An allied trooper **with Deactivator** can try to reactivate an eligible ball using **Activate Disco Ball** (WIP+3, Deactivator range MODs, allied ball in LoF or ZoC). **The Crusader itself has no Deactivator** on this option.
- Speculative Attack can project around a corner but is a **Long Skill** with the applicable −6 MOD; it is not a free way to place guaranteed Eclipse.

**Important limitation:** this loadout is **not an FO or Specialist Operative**. Once the route is cleared, another mission-eligible model must push the required buttons. This creates some dependence on the rest of the list, but is also why pairing the Disco Crusader with an already-planned Trinitarian or moving Knight specialist is sensible.

**When it makes the list:** you want Eclipse **not tied to a specific Fireteam movement path**, or Konstantinos FTO would be inefficient in a defensive/static link.

## Option 6: Boarding Shotgun + Forward Observer — 23/0 (A)

**Best for:** reserve scoring, far-flank or backfield objectives, and missions/classifieds that reward an FO or D-Charges. It is the most straightforward way to justify the Crusader on a mission-first basis.

- **WIP13 Forward Observer** is a real specialist for applicable mission objectives. The model also has **D-Charges**, and its **Flash Pulse** adds a nonlethal reactive choice.
- **Boarding Shotgun** can threaten soft defenders and make the objective route safer without needing to outshoot the opponent's main HMG/linked ARO.
- **Parachutist** can exploit an undefended side route, while **Combat Jump** can reach a harder position if the mission demands it and the landing risk is acceptable.
- **Super-Jump** helps with elevated objective approaches and nonstandard post-entry movement.

**Main competition: Trinitarian FO 22/0** (or a specialized 23pt option). The Trinitarian offers infiltration, Camouflage and Mimetism(-6), and if deployed normally is contributing a pooled Order from Turn 1. It will generally be the **first independent midfield scorer**. The Crusader adds **a reserve entry axis**, not better marker-state survivability. Take it when the mission geography justifies that second route.

**FO versus Disco at equal price:** take **FO** if no second unit can realistically activate the desired objective after entry; take **Disco** when the objective specialists are already present and the bottleneck is **crossing enemy LoF**.

## Option 4: MULTI Rifle + Light Flamethrower (+1B) — 28/0 (B+ situational)

The most compelling **aggressive kill-profile**. The MULTI Rifle gives more ammunition flexibility than a Boarding Shotgun and the **Light Flamethrower (+1B)** is a **Burst-2 Direct Template threat** during active shooting. That can punish grouped cheap troops, weak backline defenses or an unlucky placement near an AD route.

**Why it works:** unlike the Spitfire, it does not spend SWC; the templates can challenge enemies whose BS modifiers would otherwise disadvantage a BS13 gun. The +1B matters for laying multiple eligible flame templates in one activation.

**Why it is not the default:** **+5pt over the utility profiles**, still W1/ARM3 without Mimetism, and lacks FO/D-Charges/Disco. It needs a genuine close-range attack opportunity and sufficient follow-up Orders; losing it after one trade is painful. Use when a backline clearance plan is more important than scoring or Eclipse.

## Option 5: Spitfire — 31/1.5 (C; usually skip)

**BS13, B4 Spitfire from an unexpected angle** can work, but this buys a comparatively expensive single-Wound shooter without Mimetism, MSV, defensive modifiers, mission utility or Fireteam bonuses.

At **+8pts / +1.5 SWC** relative to Disco/FO, it competes with core MO shooting investments: **Hospitaller FTO HMG, Teutonic NCO Spitfire, Joan v2, Holy Sepulchre, TAGs**, or inexpensive linked weapons and the support to run them. Its ranged attack is strongest only after flank geometry has already solved the hard opposition.

**Exception:** a deliberate airborne side-lane gun plan with an unusually favorable map and SWC to spare. Otherwise prefer the **23pt shotgun** for flank attacks and put the SWC into an attacker that can fight repeatedly.

## What Crusader does not do — Fireteam and order-economy caution

- **No MO Fireteam membership.** Unlike Hospitallers, Santiago FTO, Black Friars or Konstantinos FTO, there is **no Crusader entry** in MO's Core/Haris/Duo charts. Do not treat the name *Crusader* as eligibility for the separate **Crusade Fireteam**: Crusade is a **Hospitaller-based** formation requiring an actual Hospitaller FTO.
- **An independent one-Wound attacker is not a replacement for an armored fighting Knight.** Avoid repeated exposed face-to-face rolls against elite BS/ARO models.
- **It does not generate a free extra pool Order** while hidden off-table. The personal entry Order prevents paying a pooled Order solely for entry, but the opportunity cost of having no pooled contribution until arrival is still substantial.
- **No built-in EVO or landing bonus.** Buying EVO solely for one 23pt Crusader is hard to justify unless the list also benefits from the other programs/REM support.
- **AVA3 is permission, not a recommendation.** Buying two or three Crusaders creates a large missing initial pool and may strand too many points off-table.

## Practical deployment and sequencing

1. **Before deployment:** identify the objective, rear/side weakness or Eclipse lane the Crusader is supposed to exploit. Name the **post-entry action**, not merely the drop location. Ensure its Combat Group can fund it.
2. **Early game:** let the long-range TAG/HMG or a legal offensive Fireteam remove lane control that would also threaten the air entry; preserve the option of a safe Parachutist flank if Combat Jump is unnecessary.
3. **At entry:** prefer a landing where the Crusader **is not exposed to several AROs**, remembering **no Partial Cover in the entry Order**. Combat Jump is an option, not an obligation.
4. **Follow-up:** use the **FO** to score, **Disco** to project Eclipse for the *next* Order, or **MULTI/LFT** to attack clustered rear defenders. Use Jet Propulsion for favorable angles, not indiscriminate exposed jumps.
5. **End state:** hide the W1 model, guard an objective, or accept an intentional favorable trade. Don't leave 23–31 points visible to an elite shooter merely because the flank assault succeeded.

## Final listbuilding recommendation

**Default: 0–1, usually either 23pt profile.** No Crusader is mandatory in a generic MO list.

- **23pt Disco (A):** best unusual utility; take when Eclipse from an independent late-entry position solves a specific movement problem and you already have mission scorers.
- **23pt FO (A):** best mission insurance; take when a reserve specialist can flip a flank or satisfy FO/D-Charge objectives.
- **28pt MULTI/LFT (B+ situational):** select for deliberate backline close-range clearance.
- **31pt Spitfire (C):** usually skip; too much SWC for what an unsupported W1 gunfighter offers.

**Build decision:** first fund a stage-one ranged opener, enough on-table Regular Orders, hacking/repair as required, and at least one reliable midfield scorer. The Crusader then becomes a purposeful **second axis**, rather than an automatic tax on a Knight list.
