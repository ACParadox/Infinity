# Joan of Arc v1 (standard armour) — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: B− generally; situational B+ as a SWC-enabling Lieutenant.** The 47pt **Lieutenant (+1 SWC)** is the only v1 profile worth actively considering by default. Joan v1 is not a better commander than v2: **both have Inspiring Leadership, one normal Special Lieutenant Order, Tactical Awareness and Number 2**. V1 trades v2's fast AP Spitfire assault for **ARM5, a MULTI Rifle with BS Attack (SR−1), and a major SWC budget swing**. The most defensible use is a protected, occasionally fighting Lieutenant in a list whose *other* attackers justify the extra SWC. She is **not a must-take** and is usually worse than v2 when asked to carry offensive Orders.

## V1 versus Joan v2: the real trade

| Feature | Standard Joan v1 LT | Mobility Joan v2 LT | Operational effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base LT | **47 pts / +1 SWC** | **45 pts / 1 SWC** | V1 costs two more points but leaves **two additional SWC for other units** |
| MOV | **10–10 cm (4–4 in)** | **15–5 cm (6–2 in)** | Same full Move+Move total; v2 has a superior first Move for Move+Shoot, exposure and positioning |
| ARM / PH | **ARM5 / PH15** | **ARM3 / PH14** | V1 better withstands conventional Saves and certain PH tests |
| BS / WIP / BTS / W | 15 / 15 / 6 / 2+NWI | Identical | Neither wins on base accuracy or order/command stats |
| Gun | **MULTI Rifle**, BS Attack (SR−1), X-Visor | **AP Spitfire**, X-Visor | V2's extra Burst and better 8–24 in attack bands are decisive for active gunfighting |
| Other weaponry | Pulzar (+1B), EXP CCW | Same | Both credible short-range and CC deterrents |
| Order tools | Inspiring Leadership, Tactical Awareness, Number 2, ordinary LT Order | Same | **No intrinsic command advantage for v1** |
| Fireteam access | **Crusade only** | **Crusade + Velox Duo** | V2 is more flexible |
| Extra mobility skill | — | Terrain (Total) | Another situational v2 movement advantage |

**SWC accounting, with standard 300-point/6-SWC allowance:** v1 Lieutenant grants **7 available SWC** and spends none on her own MULTI Rifle; v2 Lieutenant spends **1 SWC**, leaving **5** for the rest of the roster. **Net v1 advantage = 2 SWC**. V2's 1-SWC AP Spitfire is itself **0.5 SWC below the usual 1.5-SWC Spitfire price**, but that discount is **already included** in the direct 7-vs-5 calculation; do not subtract it again or confuse it with v1's allowance bonus.

**Do not overvalue theoretical SWC:** If the rest of the list requires 4–5 SWC, v1's extra capacity may not enable anything. It matters when it genuinely fits another desired high-SWC ARO, HMG, launcher, TAG or hacker package without sacrificing a critical scorer. Build the **specific** 300-point alternative and identify the actual newly affordable unit/weapon; v1's weaker 47pt shooter otherwise pays for a bonus that goes unused.

## Why the v1 MULTI Rifle is the bottleneck

- **BS15 + X-Visor + MULTI Rifle + BS Attack (SR−1)** is respectable for a secondary attacker. SR−1 penalizes the target's relevant Saving Rolls; it is not an additional shooting die or a shooting-face-to-face MOD.
- **AP and DA mode flexibility** is useful against armored targets or in selected AROs, but it does not make a B3 rifle behave like a B4 high-impact assault gun. Choose the actual ammunition mode and associated Burst rather than assuming every benefit applies together.
- V2's **AP Spitfire B4** and +3 band across the principal 8–24-inch attack corridor let her clear enemies much more efficiently, especially in the crucial 16–24-inch zone where the MULTI Rifle lacks the equivalent strong band. The X-Visor improves poor range bands but does not create a rifle +3 band.
- At 0–8 inches, v1 retains the rifle's positive short-range accuracy, plus a **Pulzar (+1B)** direct-template deterrent and strong EXP-CCW threat. That is a useful *defensive/counterattack* package, not a reason to march her across the table.
- **ARM5 versus ARM3** is meaningful on a two-Wound-plus-NWI Lieutenant, particularly while protecting a central objective or crossing the final short lane. Against AP/E/M/hacking, the margin shrinks or is irrelevant; neither version has Mimetism to improve face-to-face odds. Both have **Frenzy**, so directly wounding an enemy eventually adds Impetuous and Limited Cover, weakening exposed active-turn cover-based defense.

**Practical offensive test:** If Joan is expected to personally spend 4–6 attacks in a turn, select **v2**. If a separate HMG/TAG takes the hard first-stage fights and Joan mostly defends, helps a Crusade team advance and picks off close-range opportunities, **v1 can be adequate**—but question whether a cheaper LT and independent mission units would be better.

## Command and Order economy: good, but the same as v2

When she is a valid active Lieutenant, **Inspiring Leadership** can convert eligible Irregular Order providers (notably the **3pt Warcor**) into Regular training, supplies the rule's Courage/Retreat! benefits, and permits **a Coordinated Order including Joan using only her Special Lieutenant Order without a Command Token or additional Orders**. Use the exact [pinned Inspiring Leadership rule](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Inspiring_Leadership.md), including its Religious Troop caveat, rather than projecting a huge Irregular-order conversion onto an army already full of Regular Knights.

- **Tactical Awareness** funds one of Joan's activations, independent of her ordinary Lieutenant Order. Her own Regular Order enters her Combat Group's pool as normal. **Neither Joan has LT (+1 Order).**
- **Coordinated Order vs Fireteam:** Fireteam members **cannot** participate in Coordinated Orders. A linked Joan cannot simply use Inspiring Leadership to coordinate that linked Fireteam; if that is the core plan, keep Joan independent or deliberately change her Fireteam situation.
- **Fireteam coherency:** Inspiring Leadership extends the Team Leader's Zone of Control by **+2 inches for Coherency Checks** while Joan is in a Fireteam whose members all have **Regular Training printed**. That benefit does not require Joan to be the Lieutenant.
- **Chain of Command Joan:** If a non-Lieutenant Joan becomes the Lieutenant through CoC, her Lieutenant-dependent Inspiring Leadership effects start then. But 47 points for that eventuality competes with **Knight Commander CoC at 23/0** and a better existing attacker.

**Alternative command engine:** [Knight Commander LT (+1 Order) 24/0](knight-commander.md) plus a real **NCO Seraph/Holy Sepulchre/Teutonic Spitfire** (**not** Tikbalang, which has TacAware but no NCO) can convert two LT Orders into productive offensive activations while keeping the Lieutenant hidden in a defensive support team. This is often a more efficient use of MO points than buying v1 as a mostly passive commander.

## Solo or Crusade Fireteam?

**Solo v1 LT — situational B:** The strongest reason to keep her solo is retaining access to the **Lieutenant-order Coordinated Order** with Joan as a participant. Give her a protected position, use Tactical Awareness for safe movement/close reactions, and allow the *real* heavy gun to open lines. A solo v1 has no native midfield deployment skill and must not be treated as a long-range ARO platform.

**Crusade Duo/Haris — situational B / B+:** Joan counts as a Hospitaller for equivalence, but the pinned **Crusade** chart requires **at least one actual Knight Hospitaller FTO**. Her count-as does **not** waive that native minimum. **V1 cannot join Velox** and isn't a Wildcard for ordinary Military Orders or Order Sergeant links. If Joan is the Fireteam Leader, her **Tactical Awareness Order can activate the legal Fireteam**; the tradeoff is no Inspiring Leadership Coordinated Order involving a linked Joan.

| Legal module | Cost / SWC | Best reason | Major cost |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Joan v1 LT 47/+1 + Hospitaller FTO TinBot 27/0** | **74 pts / net +1 SWC** | Compact armored Duo with Firewall (-3) for team protection, and shooting/CC deterrence | Still 74pts in two non-specialist bodies; v1 is the main weapon |
| **Joan v1 LT 47/+1 + Hospitaller FTO Doctor 30/0** | **77 pts / net +1 SWC** | Resilience, healing and a real Doctor on the route | Joan's rifle cannot clear every long lane; both are expensive |
| **Joan v1 LT 47/+1 + Hospitaller HMG FTO 32/1.5 + Doctor FTO 30/0** | **109 pts / net 0.5 SWC spent** | Stage-one HMG, Doctor recovery, Joan as durable stage-two fallback; rifle no longer the only gun | Huge point concentration and no TinBot in this exact trio |
| **Joan v2 LT 45/1 + same Hospitaller HMG/Doctor** | **107 pts / 2.5 SWC** | Much more credible second active gun; direct comparison with the v1 trio | Extra SWC consumption and lower ARM |

All rows are **alternative modules**, not a single army list. For the 109pt Haris, a different protective layout may be preferable if hostile hacking is prevalent. Do not claim Firewall is present without its actual TinBot profile.

**Strategic Deployment (v1 LT 53/+1):** the extra six points are a **Fireteam deployment package**, granting Forward Deployment (+4 inches) to the qualifying Crusade team under its leader/first-deployed conditions; not a generic +4-inch solo deployment mode. Better when a carefully planned forward Crusade team genuinely saves early Orders, worse when the expensive LT simply becomes easier to attack.

**Deployment caution:** Joan is both **command and expensive two-Wound asset**; she is a high-value hacking, E/M and assassination target. Keep a CoC Commander separate if the list expects significant fighting-LT exposure, and avoid concentrating all objective specialists inside her team.

## When to include, when to skip

**Consider v1 47/+1 only if most conditions are met:**

1. The full **300pt list actually needs more than five other SWC**, ideally to obtain a specific meaningful additional heavy gun/ARO package.
2. The army already has a reliable **long-range primary attacker**, allowing Joan to operate as defensive command, second-wave pressure or a Crusade fallback.
3. The list can survive when Joan's Tactical Awareness is not profitably used every turn, and there is a deliberate **Lieutenant risk** plan.
4. The overall points still leave **independent mission specialists, regular Orders and cyber protection**; SWC allowance cannot replace those.
5. The desired Joan setup is a valid **solo or Crusade** arrangement; don't invent v1 Velox access.

**Skip v1 when:** the army isn't SWC-constrained; you need Joan to clear targets at 8–24 inches; you want a fast solo gunfighter, a Velox Duo or a command piece that can remain passive for far fewer than 47pts; or the list already has a better LT(+1)/NCO engine. **Do not take the 47pt CoC because it sounds like a versatile specialist**—it explicitly is not.

## Final tier and listbuilding decision

- **V1 standard LT 47/+1 — B− normally; B+ with demonstrable SWC conversion.** Not auto-include, not completely unplayable.
- **V1 +1 Command Token 51/+1 — B−** unless the extra token directly enables a specified plan.
- **V1 Strategic Deployment 53/+1 — C+** unless the entire legal Crusade team genuinely exploits deployment.
- **V1 CoC 47/0 — C+; Strategic CoC 53/0 — C.** Usually skip.
- **V2 LT 45/1 — A** for a real second-stage AP-Spitfire fighting Lieutenant, often with protected [Knight Commander CoC](knight-commander.md).
- **Knight Commander LT(+1 Order) 24/0 + productive NCO — A+ command-economy alternative** when Joan's command/fighting bundle is unnecessary.

**Bottom line:** The +1 SWC is v1's *one powerful differentiator*—a real **two-SWC advantage relative to v2 LT**, despite v2's already-discounted 1-SWC AP Spitfire. It can be worth building around, but **v2 converts Orders into combat impact much better**, and modern MO has excellent cheaper command and mission alternatives. Test v1 against a concrete complete list rather than rating the bonus in isolation.
