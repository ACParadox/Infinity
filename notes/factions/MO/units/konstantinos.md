# Indigo Brother Konstantinos — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A / situational A+ for the 23pt FTO inside an advancing Fireteam; B for the 26pt independent Hidden Deployment profile. Not an automatic inclusion.** His standout function is **delivering Disco Baller Eclipse while a Core or Haris already needs to advance**, with Sensor, Specialist Operative and a credible short-range MSV1 gun bundled into the same 23-point Regular trooper. **Do not add him to a static Crosier/Knight Commander command Core just to gain a Disco Baller**: a team that intends to remain in the deployment zone will rarely exploit what makes him worth his points.

## Core tactical proposition: carry Eclipse with an advancing Fireteam

Konstantinos is not purchased for Fireteam bonus arithmetic alone. **Take the attacker/mission Fireteam you already want to move forward, then assess whether adding Konstantinos supplies a missing route, counter-marker tool and specialist without buying a separate escort.**

1. **Disco Baller:** create an Eclipse crossing point to avoid an unfavorable long-range ARO or to move onto an objective. Most useful when the linked fighters must traverse open or contested lanes.
2. **Sensor:** a WIP+6 Normal Roll can reveal enemy Camouflaged or Hidden Deployment pieces within his Zone of Control **without LoF**; it also strengthens Discover against Camo Markers. Useful against a midfield mine/camo screen encountered along the team's actual route. Sensor is not blanket immunity to mines or hidden threats.
3. **Specialist Operative:** performs qualifying mission objectives after the team reaches them. A specialist already moving with the assault package saves a separately dispatched scoring activation.
4. **Short-range backup shooting:** MSV1, BS12, Mimetism (-3), a MULTI Rifle and Boarding Pistol provide a reasonable secondary option against Mimetism-equipped opposition and nearby threats. He is **not** a dedicated main gunfighter: his range, one Wound and BTS0 are limiting.

**Key decision:** Will the team advance, spend Orders in the midfield, and realistically use at least two of these functions? If yes, 23/0 is attractive; otherwise another cheaper Order or better gun/support specialist may be preferable.

### Disco Baller mechanics that affect listbuilding

- **Direct fire** is a Targetless **Normal BS Attack** at a chosen legal location: no enemy needs to be targeted. Its range bands are **0 at 0–8in, +3 at 8–16in, -3 at 16–24in, -6 at 24–32in**. The weapon has **B1, Disposable (2), Double Shot, Speculative Attack**.
- In an **FT2+**, eligible direct-fire BS Attacks gain **+1 Success Die**, not +1 Burst or an extra Disposable use. At 8–16in, BS12+3 gives **75%** success solo; +1SD gives **93.75%** for a Normal Roll (no other MODs). This is a useful reliability bonus, not the primary reason to hire him.
- The **Eclipse template is placed in the Conclusion step** of the Order that deploys the Ball. It cannot retroactively protect the team from AROs generated during that same shooting Order. Set up the shot from a safe angle or position and then cross on a later Order.
- **Speculative Attack** can place a Ball without LoF, but it is a **Long Skill** with **-6**, retains range MODs, and **cannot receive FT2 +1SD**; it also cannot be combined with Move in that Order. Prefer straightforward direct placement when possible.
- At the **start of the States Phase**, the Eclipse template goes away. The Ball itself remains unless destroyed. Konstantinos's **Deactivator** allows him to use the short **Activate Disco Ball** Skill on an allied, previously deployed Ball in LoF or ZoC, with **WIP+3 and applicable Deactivator range MODs**, creating a new Eclipse template on success. This means two Disposable shots need not mean only two useful Eclipse turns.
- **Eclipse blocks friendly as well as enemy MSV.** Do not use it as though the linked MSV shooter can see through it.

### Why the defensive Crosier/Commander Core is a poor use

A mostly stationary **Crosier MSV2 HMG + basic Crosiers + Knight Commander** Core is an Order/command/ARO shell, not an advancing assault link. Konstantinos might technically maintain useful Crosier-equivalent Fireteam levels, but **his 23pt cost is largely wasted if nobody leaves the DZ to exploit Eclipse, Sensor or objectives**. Do not confuse a neat FT5 arithmetic result with a good field role. Keep that Core lean and spend Konstantinos's points on an advancing Haris, or leave him out.

Similarly, a static **Order Sergeant HRL + Hacker + Konstantinos** Haris (56pts/2SWC, FT3) is technically attractive for link bonuses, but the HRL wants a defensive lane while Konstantinos wants to advance. Only take this shape if the team is deliberately transitioning from defense into the midfield; otherwise use a purpose-built attacking gun or a smaller support Duo.

## When is the 26pt Hidden Deployment / Infiltration profile worth taking?

The 26pt option is a **different job**: pre-positioned secret threat rather than an Eclipse escort.

- **Best case:** keep a midfield objective/angle secretly covered, then reveal with an **AP SMG or Flammenspeer** to surprise a key target, counter a light unit, or score a late objective. Sensor + MSV1 can help punish marker-heavy opposition.
- **Hidden Deployment is optional:** deploying him visibly sacrifices secrecy but means a normal deployed Regular trooper can contribute to the Order pool from the beginning.
- **Order cost:** while truly Hidden, his Order does **not** enter the initial Regular pool; it is reserved for him to reveal and act. A Hidden piece not used until Turn 2 or 3 has a real early-game opportunity cost.
- **After reveal:** no Camouflage state is printed, so he cannot simply re-camouflage. He remains a one-Wound ARM2/BTS0 trooper. The strong ambush is not sustained battlefield concealment.
- **Why not default:** the **22–23pt Trinitarian FO** also has **Hidden Deployment**, Infiltration, Camouflage, Mimetism(-6), D-Charges and Shock Mine, with Forward Observer scoring. Konstantinos is not buying unique Hidden deployment; his differentiators are **Sensor/MSV1, AP SMG, Flammenspeer and the specific ambush geometry**, at a 3–4pt premium and without the Trinitarian's recurring Camouflage/FO toolkit.

**Assessment:** **B**, mission/matchup dependent. Worth testing for a genuine planned hidden scoring or assassination fork. **Do not choose it merely because Hidden Deployment sounds powerful**, or confuse it with the FTO's Disco Baller role.

## Listbuilding decision and rating

- **A / A+ — FTO inside an advancing Core or Haris** that has a real gunfighter, intends to cross unsafe lanes, and can subsequently exploit Sensor and Specialist Operative. A+ is conditional on route geometry and mission demand.
- **A- — offensive support Duo** when the same movement route also genuinely benefits a Hacker/Pitcher partner.
- **C — FTO inside a stationary defensive Crosier/Commander or HRL shell**; legal FT arithmetic is not sufficient justification.
- **B — 26pt Hidden Deployment infiltrator** for a deliberate Sensor/MSV1 + Flammenspeer surprise/scoring plan; usually behind a **cheaper Hidden Deployment Trinitarian FO** in general midfield efficiency.
- **Not a must:** take **one FTO** when it unlocks a valuable advancing link; otherwise skip. AVA1 means the two different Konstantinos profiles compete for the same roster slot.

**Bottom line:** Konstantinos FTO is a **movement and mission-support upgrade for an existing offensive Fireteam**. He makes the team better at reaching its fight, uncovering midfield markers and finishing an objective; he is not an invitation to convert a static Core into an expensive pseudo-assault group. The 26pt Hidden option is a viable but less generally useful independent surprise piece.
