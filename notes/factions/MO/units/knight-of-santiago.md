# Knights of Santiago — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: B+ overall; A− for both KHD roles.** The **31/0 FTO MULTI Rifle + Killer Hacking Device + TinBot Firewall (-3)** is a credible mobile specialist and cyber-defense package, especially in a mixed Fireteam with a real long-range gun or cheap full HD/Pitcher. The **38/0 Combat Jump KHD + Boarding Shotgun (+1B)** is a distinctive, high-risk independent assault piece that opens an attack vector few other MO Knights offer. The **36/0 non-KHD drop trooper** is usable but normally worth upgrading for two points. The **33/1.5 standard Spitfire** is usually a skip given the faction's numerous superior or cheaper medium-range attackers. Neither Santiago role is mandatory; normally field **zero or one**, sometimes two for deliberately separate roles.

**Do not merge options:** Combat Jump options 3/4 have **no Parachutist, no TinBot, no FTO** and explicitly cannot join a Fireteam. Only 1/2/5/6 are FTO. ## Option 2: FTO KHD + TinBot — 31/0

The best reason to include a conventional Santiago is **role compression**: BS14 MULTI Rifle, two Wounds, ARM4, Stealth, Specialist Operative, active anti-Hacker Trinity and a Firewall protecting eligible members of his legal Fireteam. 360° Visor also prevents the usual rear-arc bypass when LoF exists. Unlike MSV or Mimetism, it does not improve the face-to-face shooting MODs.

### Firewall and hacking comparisons

| Unit | Pts / SWC | ARM / BTS | Firewall | Hacking role |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Teutonic TinBot | 19/0 | 3/3 | -3 | Cheap protection; no hacking, Limited Cover |
| Hospitaller TinBot | 27/0 | 4/6 | -3 | Crusade anchor/equivalent; no hacking |
| **Santiago KHD** | **31/0** | **4/3** | **-3** | Trinity, Cybermask, specialist |
| Mendoza FTO | 40/0 | 4/6 | -3 | Premium combat/support; no hacking |
| De Fersen MULTI | 42/0.5 | 4/6 | — | WIP14 full HD + Trinity (+1SD) |

For **+4pts over Hospitaller TinBot**, Santiago gains KHD, Specialist Operative, 360° Visor, faster first MOV, enhanced Dodge movement, D-Charges and Boarding Pistol. He loses 3 BTS, MA L2 (has L1 instead), PH14 (has PH13), and Hospitaller equivalence/Crusade anchor eligibility. CC23 rather than CC22 does not automatically make Santiago the better melee fighter.

Firewall (-3) applies the appropriate enemy WIP penalty and +3 to eligible defending Comms Saves under N5.3. It is valuable but does not make BTS3 equal to a naturally BTS6 trooper in every situation. Avoid paying for redundant TinBots or assuming Firewall stacks with EVO Fairy Dust. See [Hacking Device](../../../../rules/5c_Equipment/Hacking_Device.md).

**Against Hackers:** KHD grants standard Trinity, not De Fersen's +1SD upgrade. Santiago cannot use Spotlight, Oblivion, Carbonite or Total Control; a KHD is not a full HD. FT2 improves rolled BS Attacks, not hacking. A KHD alone cannot exploit a Guided Clipper by applying Spotlight.

**With De Fersen?** Not required merely to protect him: Teutonic19 or Hospitaller27 can do that more cheaply, and De Fersen is already the stronger cyber duelist. But two independently positioned Hackers can exert greater reactive pressure via shared Repeaters, and tournament evidence shows both in the same army. Santiago is especially interesting with the **18/0.5 Order Sergeant FTO HD/Pitcher** if not investing in De Fersen. Do not assume an expensive pair of Hackers replaces network coverage; Trinitarian Deployable Repeater Minelayer, Pitcher and mobile REM Repeaters remain relevant.

**Cybermask:** a no-roll Long Skill requiring being outside enemy LoF, granting IMP-2. Particularly useful for independent repositioning through ordinary ARO lanes and protecting the model from direct attacks until revealed. It is not immunity to Discover, and declaring it may generate eligible AROs. The Marker State is incompatible with Fireteam membership; after Frenzy activates, the resulting Impetuous skill prevents re-entering such Marker States. Use before killing when a protected approach matters. See [Cybermask](../../../../rules/3_Combat/Cybermask.md) and [Frenzy](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Frenzy.md).

## Options 3/4: heavy airborne assault — 38/36 points

**The most differentiated Santiago role.** Unlike another conventional short-to-medium-range Knight, this model can enter directly on a different axis, bypassing the initial approach through enemy AROs.

- **BS14 BSG (+1B):** three rolled AP shotgun dice at BS20 in the +6 range band before negative MODs. Excellent when landing geometry brings it close to vulnerable defenders; no Fireteam bonuses.
- **ARM4/W2:** can survive exchanges that would remove W1 airborne troops, but no Mimetism or special BS opponent MOD. Hackable HI with BTS3 and no native Firewall.
- **Super-Jump (Jet Propulsion):** bend the trajectory and, when the permitted distance allows, jump to establish LoF, attack and return behind Total Cover in one Order. Also allows roof-to-roof/obstacle routes. No Partial Cover while jumping and all eligible AROs along the declared path matter.
- **360° Visor, Shock Mine, Flash Pulse, Boarding Pistol:** useful when operating alone in the opposing half, guarding multiple directions or creating a mine-and-shotgun dilemma. A mine takes an Order to deploy; do not assume ordinary Shock Mine automatically deploys during Combat Jump.
- **Specialist Operative, D-Charges, DA CCW and KHD on option 3:** provides independent scoring, relevant classifieds, and anti-Hacker capability. KHD adds Cybermask, enabling a protected approach before revealing.

**Take 38/0 rather than 36/0 by default.** Both are already Hackable HI. For two points the KHD adds anti-Hacker and marker-state options. It also makes Santiago a valid enemy Trinity target, so it is not pure upside against strong KHDs. The non-KHD is sensible when points are exceptionally tight or avoiding Trinity matters. Neither has a TinBot.

### Landing reliability and cost

| Situation | PH target | Chance of success |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unmodified Combat Jump | 13 | 65% |
| Friendly EVO Controlled Jump | 16 | 80% |
| Enemy EVO Controlled Jump | 10 | 50% |
| Both players have Controlled Jump | 13 | 65% |

- **No Parachutist fallback.** Santiago must roll to deploy through Combat Jump; Crusader can choose a guaranteed legal side entry instead. Santiago can also deploy normally at the start if the reserve plan is undesirable.
- The entry uses his own Order, but he contributes no pooled Order while off-table. Entry is a Long Skill, not a simultaneous shoot or objective action. No Partial Cover on arrival; no placement in contact with enemy/neutral models, markers, tokens or scenario objectives, or within prohibited scenery.
- A failed landing under current N5.3 places him at the edge of his own DZ as a Model, not dead and not scattered. This costs the intended attack angle and may require multiple approach Orders to recover the investment.
- **EVO Mulebot14/0.5** supplies Controlled Jump; it is only +6pts/0.5SWC relative to Baggage8/0 if already taking that model. The jump package is 52pts/0.5SWC outright (with KHD). Don't buy EVO only for one Santiago by default: a 15 percentage point improvement is useful, but it spends a setup Order, can telegraph AD, competes with Fairy Dust/other supportware and still leaves 20% failure. More attractive if the EVO already has meaningful TAG/HI/REM/other AD uses. Enemy EVO can react with Controlled Jump anywhere on the table.
- Reserve enough pooled Orders after landing for attack, scoring and safe end position. A small independent combat group with 3–4 cheap Regular orders is a reasonable shell. Landing late with no Orders remaining wastes much of the profile's value.

Rules: [Combat Jump](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Combat_Jump.md) · [Parachutist](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Parachutist.md) · [Controlled Jump](../../../../rules/3_Combat/Controlled_Jump.md) · [Super-Jump](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Super-Jump.md) · [360° Visor](../../../../rules/5c_Equipment/360_Visor.md).

### Santiago versus Crusader Brethren

| | Crusader FO | Santiago KHD |
|---|---|---|
| Cost / SWC | 23/0 | 38/0 |
| BS / PH | 13 / 11 | 14 / 13 |
| ARM / BTS / W | 3 / 3 / 1 | 4 / 3 / 2 |
| Shotgun | B2 | B3 |
| Combat Jump unmodified | 55% | 65% |
| Guaranteed Parachutist side entry | Yes | No |
| Super-Jump Jet Propulsion | Yes | Yes |
| Specialist | FO | SO + Hacker |
| Other equipment | Flash Pulse, D-Charges, E/M CCW | Flash Pulse, Shock Mine, D-Charges, Boarding Pistol, DA CCW, 360° Visor |
| Hackable / Frenzy | No / no | Yes / yes |

**Crusader** is the economical flank specialist or independent Eclipse provider (separate Disco profile, also 23/0). The guaranteed flank entry is important. **Santiago** is a primary/secondary assault piece intended to spend multiple Orders, remove defenders and stay relevant afterward. The 15-point premium buys a substantially stronger fighting platform, not better guaranteed arrival or better low-cost mission scoring. Take one according to mission and table, not both by reflex.

## Options 1/5: Spitfire + E/M Grenades

The non-LT **33/1.5** profile is usable but seldom the best purchase. MO already offers Bulleteer Spitfire22/1 (Mimetism -6), Teutonic NCO Spitfire29/1.5 (X Visor), Joan v2, De Fersen Spitfire46/1.5, Justice and other elite shooters. Santiago's BS14, SO and E/M Grenades are real benefits but no persistent visual MOD, MSV or special shooting modifier. E/M Grenades can disrupt HI/TAG/REM and isolate other eligible troops, but PH13 is not extraordinary. Best as a follow-up gun in an intentional specialist team after long-range AROs are removed.

**Lieutenant Spitfire33/0:** saves a full **1.5 SWC** over the ordinary option. This can matter in a deliberately SWC-tight active-LT list with Knight Commander CoC23 or scenario rules that remove Loss of Lieutenant. It is not the default over Commander LT(+1)24 and an effective NCO. Santiago cannot spend an ordinary LT Order to activate a Fireteam merely by leading it.

**Lieutenant KHD31/0:** no price/SWC discount versus non-LT, but Robert Shepherd points out the useful independent option of spending his own LT Order on Cybermask instead of the Regular pool. This is most attractive in no-LoL scenarios or with protected CoC and a genuine solo movement/scoring plan. It remains a niche option rather than a reason to expose the army's command structure routinely.

## Fireteams and FT level

| Formation | Types | Santiago maximum | Requirement |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Military Orders | Core, Haris, Duo | 2 | Actual Crosier, Teutonic or Santiago FTO required-choice; Santiago can anchor |
| Order Sergeants | Haris, Duo | 1 | At least one actual Order Sergeant FTO |
| Crusade | Core, Haris, Duo | 2 | At least one actual Hospitaller FTO |
| Velox | — | 0 | Santiago is not a Wildcard |

**Santiago does not count as Crosier, Order Sergeant or Hospitaller.** His inclusion in a larger team is not automatically an extra FT level. Two Santiagos give FT2, but a mixed trio needs at least two same-unit/Counts-as members for FT2. FT2 is **BS Attack (+1SD)** (roll one extra, discard one), not +1B and not an improvement to Hacking. Higher FT levels require same-unit equivalence. Combat Jump options cannot join any Fireteam.

## YouTube/podcast and actual play evidence

### Robert Shepherd — MO faction focus (July 2026)

[Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JrMTA91V7wc). Direct transcript review: describes Santiago as slightly bloated and a difficult but worthwhile specialty purchase; generally discourages the normal Spitfire. Prefers experimenting with the Lieutenant versions over standard solo foot profiles, particularly KHD using LT Order on Cybermask in suitable missions. Not a universal beginner recommendation.

He compares the drop Santiago with the **30pt Bearcat KHD BSG (+1B)**, an exceptional airborne unit. Santiago has +1 BS, +1 ARM, legitimate CC, Shock Mine and Stealth advantages, but costs 6–8 more points and lacks guaranteed Parachutist entry. Calls the airborne model highly threatening, personally finding the mandatory landing roll too risky for 36–38 points. An EVO is an option, but he cautions that it telegraphs the drop and may otherwise be redundant. This is the strongest skeptical view: **power after arrival is not the issue; delivery reliability is**.

### Tactical Awareness — Military Orders REDUX (November 2025)

[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RP6Uyyc447k) · [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6xyHjc3R0N37bDHl0HiROO). Direct transcript review: one host proposes an airborne KHD in group two supported by **Fugazi, Tech-Bee, Sacha and Mulebot**, four pooled Regular Orders after the jump, while the first group includes De Fersen Spitfire LT and Hospitaller TinBot. Praises the B3 shotgun, broad classified utility, Cybermask, Super-Jump and mine/shotgun pressure. The two hackers are **independent attack vectors**, not redundant members of one expensive Haris. The speaker loosely calls Santiago a parachutist; **the pinned profile has Combat Jump only**. Treat the list as a proposed historical concept, not a current exact list validation; verify current availability/legality of every battery unit before copying it.

### Ryan — Military Orders guide

[Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xjs6rsspz8I). Direct transcript review: calls Santiago his favorite unit and reports successfully using the drop trooper as an alternative opener. Recommends removing long-range AROs first with the HMG, then advancing medium-range assault/scoring elements such as Santiago Duos. ### New Year Knockout 2026 — observed tournament lists

[Three-round tournament battle reports](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aGZ--rtGqXA) · [Repository report #009](../../../battle-reports/001-010-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md#009--new-year-knockout-2026--military-orders-three-round-invitational). Player placed **third of six** and included Santiago FTO KHD in both submitted lists. Explicitly describes the **Santiago KHD + Crosier MSV2 HMG + Crosier Paramedic Haris** as a main or backup assault team depending on mission/opponent; in the alternative list, **Santiago KHD + Hospitaller BSG/E/Mitter Duo** as a disruptive independent team. **Synthesis:** strongest practical evidence is for the linked KHD, including actual tournament use. Airborne KHD gets strong expert endorsement as a distinctive offensive option, but mixed opinion on whether its high landing variance justifies the cost. The available examples are not a usage-frequency or win-rate dataset and do not establish a universal must-take.

## Listbuilding and collection recommendation

- **First test:** 38/0 Combat Jump KHD in a separate group with enough cheap Regular Orders; strongest differentiation from MO's many similar assault Knights. Use only when a plausible landing plan and useful fallback exist.
- **Second test:** 68/1 Santiago/Crosier HMG/Paramedic Haris, particularly if the army needs an affordable MSV2 long gun and additional hacker/scoring coverage.
- **Third test:** 66/0.5 Santiago/Sergeant HD/Infirmarer Haris as a cost-effective full HD + KHD package without De Fersen.
- **Skip ordinarily:** standard Spitfire, double KHD, and generic KHD LT unless their specific utility is needed. LT Spitfire is conditional on SWC savings and command plan.
- **No automatic inclusion:** if De Fersen already handles hacking and a cheaper TinBot protects him, or if a Crusader can handle independent airborne utility more reliably, spend Santiago's points elsewhere.

[Current inventory](../../../../inventory/military-orders.md) lists **two owned Santiago models**. Both main roles are covered already; do not buy a third solely to reach AVA3. A third only makes sense after repeated successful use of a two-Santiago FTO formation plus a separate airborne profile.
