# Tikbalang of Montesa — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: strong but optional solo long-range TAG (A− as a standalone ranged gunfighter; conditional B+/A− in MO list-building).** Choose it for **BS15, Mimetism (-3), Climbing Plus, ARM6/STR3, a Heavy Flamethrower and Tactical Awareness**. Do **not** build a Knight Commander LT (+1 Order) engine around it: **the MO Tikbalang does NOT have NCO**. The Seraph has that skill; the MO Tikbalang does not. Its normal HMG also lacks AP ammunition, and, unlike the Kestrel version, it lacks Shock Mines.

## 1. Legal profile — exactly one

| Profile | Points | SWC | AVA / Fireteam |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Tikbalang of Montesa** (only MO option) | **68** | **1.5** | **AVA 1; no MO Fireteam** |

**Attributes:** MOV 6–4 in / 15–10 cm, CC17, BS15, PH15, WIP13, ARM6, BTS6, STR3, S6.

**Equipment:** HMG, Heavy Flamethrower, Crabbot, **DA CCW (PS=5)**.

**Special Skills:** ECM: Guided (-6), **BS Attack (SR-1)**, **CC Attack (-3)**, Climbing Plus, Courage, **Dodge (PH=11)**, **GizmoKit (PH=13)**, Mimetism (-3), Remote Presence, Tactical Awareness, **Terrain (Total)**.

**Crucial absences:** **no NCO**, no Shock Mines, no AP/MULTI HMG, no Lieutenant (+1) profile, no Fireteam membership. Do not add abilities from the generic PanO/Kestrel Tikbalang or from the MO Seraph.

The Kestrel analysis's **preplaced mine + HMG fork**, close-assault area denial, and Tik/Pathfinder Duo do **not** transfer to MO. MO gets a small points saving and its own CC package, but loses those defensive/utility conveniences. Both versions have one Tactical Awareness Order and **neither** profile may consume LT Orders via NCO; the Kestrel *Lieutenant* variant has its own different personal LT-Order plan.

## 3. Main jobs and practical play

1. **Conventional ARO and infantry removal (best):** BS15 HMG plus a persistent Mimetism (-3) penalty is excellent for repeatedly winning favorable Face-to-Face shooting. Use the 16–32 in bands and flank targets out of their cover/strong weapon range. This is the main reason to buy it.
2. **Vertical flanking and retreat:** Climbing Plus allows wall routes and attacks while climbing. Exploit diagonals, reverse angles, and safe exit positions; do not leave S6 exposed on a wall with no cover.
3. **Independent tempo:** Tactical Awareness funds **one personal activation** each eligible active turn. It is not NCO and cannot consume a Commander's extra LT Orders.
4. **Close approach deterrence:** Heavy Flamethrower punishes risky close entries and provides a template option, but without Shock Mines it cannot leave behind persistent corridor control or build a preplaced mine/HMG fork.
5. **Durable attacker, not invulnerable anchor:** ARM6, BTS6, 3 STR and Remote Presence can absorb punishment, but the TAG remains exposed to hacking, E/M and focused anti-TAG attacks. Its CC Attack (-3) and DA CCW do not make it a specialist melee piece.

**Main weakness:** The **ordinary HMG does not reduce ARM**. BS15 and Mim(-3) make hits easier; they do not solve repeated saving rolls for ARM7–9 TAGs in cover. Bring AP/E/M/hacking or attack other objectives rather than insist on the frontal TAG duel.

## 4. Order economy: Knight Commander trap

**Knight Commander LT (+1 Order) 24 + Tikbalang 68 = 92 points / 1.5 SWC** is **not an NCO engine**. The Commander produces two LT Special Orders, but the Tikbalang **cannot spend them**. Its Tactical Awareness Order still functions independently.

- **If the Tik is the only premium attacker and no other NCO is planned:** consider the **20-point ordinary Knight Commander Lieutenant**, or another legal protected LT, rather than pay 4 points for an unused extra Special LT Order.
- **If a Seraph, Sepulchre AP HMG, or Teutonic Spitfire NCO is also present:** the 24-point Commander can still make sense, but those **other models**, not the Tikbalang, are the recipients.
- There is **no Tikbalang LT (+1)** in MO, so the Kestrel personal Lieutenant strategy is unavailable here.

This substantially reduces the Tikbalang's value as an **MO command-centered centerpiece**, not its raw merit as a sustained gunfighter.

## 5. Direct competition in MO

| Option | Cost | Key edge | Choose when |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Tikbalang HMG** | **68 / 1.5** | **Persistent Mim(-3), Climbing Plus**, BS15, TacAware; smaller S6 | You primarily want repeated infantry/ARO gunfights, angle selection, and a mobile independent heavy gun |
| **Seraph MULTI HMG** | **74 / 1.5** | **MULTI/AP firepower, ARM7, Super-Jump, NCO + TacAware**; S7 and no Mim | You want a main TAG to penetrate heavy armor, move vertically with jumping attacks, and use Knight Commander LT Orders |
| **Seraph AP Spitfire + Auxbot** | **72 / 1.5** | AP at shorter ranges, synchronized template threat, NCO + TacAware | Dense table / midrange assault and close-range forks matter more than HMG reach |
| **Holy Sepulchre AP HMG NCO** | **53 / 1** | **15 pts and 0.5 SWC cheaper, AP HMG, Holoprojector, NCO**, BTS9 | You want reliable AP reach or Commander synergy while preserving a larger specialist/Fireteam budget |
| **Hospitaller HMG FTO** | **32 / 1.5** | Much cheaper, legal linked offensive gun | A suitable Crusade team is already planned and your TAG budget would displace essential mission pieces |

**Most important choice:** The Seraph MULTI HMG is **only 6 points more**. That buys a lot: AP-capable HMG, +1 ARM and NCO, but trades away Mim(-3), the smaller silhouette, and Climbing Plus. If the army already uses Knight Commander LT (+1), the Seraph's NCO makes its incremental value even more substantial. If shots against ordinary troops and repeated FtF defense matter more, the Tik remains competitive.

**Sepulchre alternative:** Its AP HMG and NCO are very relevant to MO's shortage of other sustained AP long-range guns. Tik is the better persistent visual-modifier gunfighter and mobile TAG; Sepulchre is often the better *whole-list* choice once the 15-point saving and LT-Order synergies are included.

## 6. Support package and complementary pieces

- **Tech-Bee 9** in the **same Combat Group**: TAGCom improves the Tik's printed Dodge PH11 to PH14 and GizmoKit PH13 to PH14 while the relevant support remains active. Useful but **not an Engineer**.
- **Curator Engineer 17 + Palbot 3**: practical repair/status recovery. The Palbot must have a physically legal route to a TAG that climbed to its position.
- **Trinitarian FO 22** (or another appropriate specialist): independent midfield scoring while the TAG opens lines; do not spend 68 points trying to do every mission role with one gun.
- **Teutonic Panzerfaust 18**, **Montesa Blitzen**, **Seraph AP**, **Sepulchre AP HMG**, or planned **De Fersen hacking**: separate answers to heavy armor and Hackable units.
- **Mulebot EVO/Fairy Dust** is situational against enemy hackers; not free, does not generally negate E/M weapons, and requires a real hacking/support rationale.

**Illustrative support core:** Tikbalang **68** + ordinary Knight Commander LT **20** + Tech-Bee **9** + Curator Engineer **17** + Palbot **3** = **117 points / 1.5 SWC**, leaving **183 points** for scoring, Fireteams, defenses and a serious anti-armor complement in a 300-point list. This is not a complete Army export. A cheaper/more useful LT may replace the Commander depending on the rest of the list.

## 7. Practical verdict

**Priority: optional; 0–1, not a must-take.** The MO Tikbalang remains a capable solo **first-stage HMG opener**, especially on a table with exploitable vertical routes and infantry-heavy long-range fights. It is **not** the obvious default TAG for an NCO/LT-order architecture: **Seraph or Sepulchre fill that plan more efficiently**. Nor does it bring the Kestrel model's Shock Mine denial or Pathfinder Duo. It earns its place specifically when **Mimetism (-3) + Climbing Plus** is worth the 68-point commitment over AP and NCO.
