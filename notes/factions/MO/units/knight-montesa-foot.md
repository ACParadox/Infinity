# Knights of Montesa (Foot) — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: S / A+ value for the 20pt FTO Paramedic used independently or as a flexible *leave-and-rejoin* Fireteam escort; A− for the 26pt Forward Deployment Paramedic when the mission rewards an immediate S2 midfield position; B for the 35pt Spitfire; C for the 32pt Red Fury.** **Default: 20pt FTO**. A foot Montesa is not a mandatory Fireteam purchase merely because its only link-eligible profile is labeled FTO. Because the Montesa has **Impetuous** but **no same-unit / Counts-as equivalence**, its presence may add a body and weapons without increasing the team's **FT level**; remaining independent often preserves more activation and positioning flexibility.

## Why the 20pt FTO is the standout even when solo

- **Cost efficiency:** a Regular, ARM4/BTS6, two-Wound, Mimetism (−3) S2 specialist for **20/0**. The Boarding Shotgun allows an effective short-range active-turn attack or nearby defensive trade. Martial Arts L2 / DA CCW supply an emergency CC answer, not a reason to charge every target.
- **Blitzen:** **Disposable (2)**, useful E/M control against hackable HI/TAG/REMs. The Blitzen is an important differentiator from the 26pt FD Paramedic. It threatens Isolation/IMM outcomes, **not automatic kills**. It is still finite ammunition and its best shooting bands matter.
- **Mimetism (−3) and Limited Cover:** still difficult to hit compared with an unmodified model, but **Limited Cover does not confer the usual −3 BS MOD from Partial Cover**; don't rate it like a conventional two-Wound knight benefiting from cover penalties. Avoid exposing it to high-burst guns just because it has ARM4.
- **Independent Impetuous activation:** when a useful, *legal and safe* forward path exists, it can move or attack in the Impetuous Phase **without consuming a pooled Regular Order**. It still contributes its own normal Regular Order to the pool. The free activation is **optional**, not a compulsory suicide run.

**Default battlefield job:** a **solo escort to an independently activated advancing Fireteam**, or a second flanking specialist/Blitzen threat. It can advance on a nearby but separate route, protect a corner against a hackable heavy attacker, and score after the primary shooter opens a lane. It does **not** need to be linked to earn its 20 points.

## Impetuous in a Fireteam: legal, but not a free Fireteam Order

**Impetuous does not prohibit Fireteam membership.** The restriction is that **using the Impetuous activation causes the trooper to leave its Fireteam immediately**. If the **Fireteam Leader** uses an Impetuous activation, the **entire Fireteam is cancelled**. If a **non-Leader** Montesa leaves a three-or-more-member Fireteam, the survivors can continue **provided at least two remain**. A departing member does not automatically keep the old Fireteam bonus.

Important movement constraints when using that activation:

- The Impetuous Phase is **before the regular Orders Phase**.
- Allowed Impetuous declarations are limited (for example Move + Move, Move + BS Attack, Move + Dodge, Move + CC Attack, Move + Idle, Jump, Climb, etc. as specified by the N5.3 skill). **You cannot use it to push an objective, throw a Blitzen while stationary using an arbitrary unsupported combination, or freely wander sideways**.
- Impetuous movement generally must use **full MOV**, first seeking enemy Silhouette contact when possible, otherwise advancing toward the enemy Deployment Zone according to the rule's priorities. It cannot finish **Prone**. This can expose the Montesa to mines, AROs or hackers; **decline the activation** when the path is poor.
- Fireteam bonuses are based on **FT level (same Unit or valid Counts-as)**, **not simply the number of bodies**. Montesa FTO has **no Counts-as entry**. A Teutonic + Teutonic + Montesa Haris is a three-body Haris with **FT2**, not FT3. A Teutonic + Montesa Duo is **FT1**, not FT2.

### Hybrid: leave for the free Order, then rejoin

**Example: Teutonic Specialist 19 + Teutonic TinBot 19 + Montesa FTO 20 = 58pts / 0 SWC, Military Orders Haris, FT2.**

1. **Deployment:** create the legal Haris. Make **one of the Teutonics** Fireteam Leader, **not the Montesa**.
2. **Impetuous Phase:** if valuable, activate Montesa alone. It **immediately leaves** the Haris and carries out its Impetuous activation under the movement/declaration restrictions.
3. **Orders Phase:** the two Teutonics remain a **two-member Haris** (it **does not change type to Duo**) with **FT2** from their matching identity. The Montesa can act separately using ordinary Orders; it does not participate in team Orders this turn while outside the Fireteam.
4. **States Phase:** if the Montesa **previously belonged to that still-existing Fireteam** and is back in **Coherency with its current Leader**, it may **automatically rejoin** at the end of the States Phase, **without a Command Token**. It then has the appropriate linked status for the enemy turn.

**Do not over-generalize:** this is **not** arbitrary mid-turn entry to any nearby Fireteam. The Montesa has to have *left that team*, the team has to **survive**, and the return occurs at the **States Phase**, not immediately after moving back into Coherency. A **two-model Duo** that loses the Montesa is cancelled when reduced to one model and **cannot use this automatic-rejoin trick**. The hybrid is mainly a **Haris/Core** tactic.

**Opportunity-cost judgment:** this is a neat option, **not a reason to link by default**. Moving the Fireteam and solo Montesa over converging paths may cost Orders or expose the specialist. If the Montesa will be off on its own task anyway, just leave it **independent from deployment**.

Each example must meet its formation's **actual host/minimum/required-choice** conditions. The **Montesa's Wildcard status never supplies those native requirements by itself**. An FT2-linked Montesa benefits from **+1 Success Die on applicable BS Attacks**, including appropriate Blitzen AROs, *only because other troops created FT2*. A third non-equivalent body gives **no extra FT level**.

**Link it when:** the chosen team actually needs a durable, short-range scoring/Blitzen body on its own route; getting that body across the table with Fireteam Orders saves more than its lost independent flexibility; or you intentionally plan the States-Phase leave/rejoin tactic.

**Don't link it when:** the team stays back while Montesa must score forward; it provides no needed FT level or distinctive team role; or the free Impetuous activation is consistently worth more than coordinated movement. Don't pay an extra Fireteam slot just because the option says *FTO*.

## Is Forward Deployment (+8in) worth six points?

**Correct comparison: both the 20pt FTO and the 26pt FD Paramedic are Impetuous.** The 20pt model's free Impetuous activation **does not erase** the FD model's advantage, because the **26pt model gets that same optional activation**. The FD model starts up to **8 inches farther forward before Turn 1**, without having to cross that distance in an Order or expose itself to an Impetuous movement path.

What six points **buy**: guaranteed *deployment positioning* (subject to legal deployment locations), often meaningful first-turn midfield access for an S2 two-Wound specialist. What six points **lose** versus the FTO: **Blitzen**, linking capability and budget efficiency.

- **Pay the +6** when a midfield console/objective-room approach, central defensive position, dense terrain or mission timing makes an **S2 model already 8in up** substantially stronger, especially if an S4 motorcycle can't safely fit.
- **Do not default to +6** when a separate Trinitarian/Peacemaker already covers the midfield, the game plan values the Blitzen roadblock more, or the Montesa's purpose is a later turn objective swing.
- **Watch turn order:** deployment before Turn 1 may matter *a lot* if going second, whereas an Impetuous move doesn't occur until your own Impetuous Phase. Still, FD is **visible**, hackable HI presence and can itself be pressured before activating.

**Practical rating:** 26pt FD Paramedic **A− on the right mission, B otherwise**. The **20pt FTO is the more broadly attractive buy** and, importantly, can already be played solo; a Fireteam is optional.

## Other MO alternatives / opportunity costs

- **Motorized Montesa Paramedic 23 / 0:** separate unit, **MOV 20–15 cm (8–6in)** and S4. Faster for **repeated flanking/scoring movement**, three points cheaper than FD foot specialist. Prefer the bike on open routes or distant Turn-3 switches; prefer foot S2 for tight doors, roofs/vertical access, dense terrain and objective-room geometry. The bike does **not** have the foot FTO's Blitzen.
- **Teutonic Specialist Operative 19 / 0:** two-Wound, Impetuous, faster first MOV and a **Panzerfaust**, plus **native Military Orders Fireteam identity**, making it much better when the Fireteam **level** is the actual purchase. The Montesa trades for **ARM4/BTS6, Mimetism (−3), Boarding Shotgun/Blitzen** and Paramedic rather than generic Specialist Operative.
- **Trinitarian FO 22 / 0 (or other current pinned options):** infiltrating Camouflage / Mimetism (−6) specialist; prefer when **concealed forward presence** matters more than a visible two-Wound HI with E/M weaponry.
- **Infirmarer / Hospitaller Doctor:** actual Doctors with different role/cost; a **Paramedic is not a Doctor** and cannot replace a Doctor's broader treatment rules.
- **FD Spitfire / Red Fury:** the Spitfire's stronger gun and Drop Bears can support an independent midfield attack plan, but **35/1.5** buys a lot of MO alternative shooting. **Red Fury 32/1.5** saves only three points and forfeits Drop Bears; it usually loses the comparison. Both **cannot join Fireteams**.

## Selection rule

1. **Start with FTO Paramedic 20 / 0, independent**: one of the strongest value pieces in MO when you want an extra scoring body and E/M short/medium defensive control.
2. **If you already have a moving Haris/Core and need its weapons/specialist in formation**, add FTO **only when the synchronized movement earns its keep**; remember it usually **doesn't increase FT level**.
3. **If you want both free Impetuous value and late reactive-team membership**, test **leave in Impetuous Phase → operate solo → return to Coherency → automatically rejoin during States Phase**, with an existing 3+ model team and non-Montesa leader.
4. **Switch to FD Paramedic 26 / 0** only when its **eight inches of deployment advantage** is actually worth six points **and losing Blitzen/linking**.
5. **Spitfire** for a specific forward attack/mine plan; **usually skip Red Fury**.

**Overall: FTO is a premium solo utility buy that *can* become a Fireteam asset—not a Fireteam filler you should force into every list.**
