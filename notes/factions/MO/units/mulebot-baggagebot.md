# Mulebot (Baggage / EVO / Total Reaction) — Military Orders (N5.3)

**Verdict: basic 8/0 S for cheap Order/Baggage; EVO 14/0.5 A+ in a heavily HI MO list; 17/0 Total Reaction Combi usually skip.** AVA2 total, no Fireteam access, identical profiles to Kestrel. MOV10-10, BS11, WIP13, ARM2, BTS3, STR1, S4, Baggage, Remote Presence, Courage.

| Profile | Pts/SWC | Equipment | Decision |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Basic | **8/0** | Baggage, Repeater, Deactivator | **Default low-cost Regular Order** |
| EVO | **14/0.5** | EVO Hacking Device, Baggage; **not** native Repeater or Deactivator | **A+ with many HI; A otherwise** |
| TR Combi | **17/0** | Repeater, Combi Rifle, Total Reaction, Baggage | C, normally take Sierra Thunderbolt 18/0 |

## Basic 8-point Mulebot: what actually makes it worthwhile

At **8 points / 0 SWC**, a protected Order is already a credible purchase. The bonuses are:

1. **Baggage / Reload:** friendly non-Null troopers inside the Mulebot's **8" Zone of Control** can replenish their expended **Disposable** ammunition/equipment through the **Reload Short Skill / ARO**; in the **States Phase**, *each* eligible allied trooper in ZoC is replenished **automatically, without an Order**. The Mulebot must remain functional. Equipment already deployed remains deployed, and **Non-Reloadable** items are excluded.
2. **Deactivator:** WIP-based way to address revealed mines and other eligible deployables without sacrificing a valuable fighter. Crucially, it is also used to **Activate Disco Ball**.
3. **Repeater:** passive 8" extension of friendly Hackers' hacking coverage **across Combat Groups**, but its utility depends on having a useful Hacker/program; it can also present enemy counter-hacking risks if advanced unwisely.
4. **ARM 2 / S4:** somewhat sturdier than a Fugazi, but a substantially bigger, slower model with neither Mimetism nor Flash Pulse.
5. **Mission value:** Baggage can matter in missions whose scoring rules credit it in Zone of Operations control; **check the actual mission**, rather than assuming bonus points apply universally.

**Do not spend active Orders moving this REM forward solely to make its Repeater relevant**, unless an objective, a clearly valuable hack, or a reload/deactivation target makes the route efficient. It is generally a rear/near-midfield utility unit, not an attack platform.

## Baggage-specific MO opportunities

- **Order Sergeant FTO HD/Pitcher (18pt):** replenish spent Pitcher ammunition if the operator is actually within the Mulebot's **8-inch ZoC** during the States Phase.
- **Konstantinos FTO Disco Baller:** recover eligible expended uses. The **basic** Mulebot's Deactivator can also reactivate eligible existing Disco Balls; the EVO profile lacks Deactivator.
- **Teutonic Panzerfausts, Montesa Blitzens, Crosier mines, Trinitarian Deployable Repeaters:** possible replenishment if legally close. The already-deployed devices stay deployed; a slow rear S4 bot cannot reach every forward soldier.
- Kestrel's **13pt Fennec Disco Baller** is not a MO choice. MO benefits more from **Pitcher and numerous HI**.

## EVO Fairy Dust is a major MO advantage

**One Long Skill, no Roll; table-wide Supportware.** Choose **HI, REM, TAG or VH**. **Every eligible friendly model of that troop type** benefits from **Firewall (-3)** against Comms Attacks: enemy Hacker **-3 WIP**, target **+3 Saving Roll**. This is **all models of the chosen type on the table**, not a single target or Combat Group.

**MO with many Knights:** Selecting **HI** can protect a large number of exposed Teutonics, Santiagos, Montesas, Knights of Justice, Hospitallers and other eligible HI **including dispersed independent models and different Fireteams**. That is a major force multiplier for the incremental **+6pts/0.5SWC** over basic Baggage. You don't need a TAG or a Bulleteer to justify the EVO.

**Does not stack with TinBot Firewall(-3).** Troopers can use **only one Firewall at a time**. A Knight protected by a TinBot (-3) and Fairy Dust (-3) does **not** impose -6 WIP, nor receive +6 SR: use one Firewall. **The gain is protecting every other HI without TinBot coverage.** Fairy Dust on HI does not protect a Seraph/Tikbalang TAG, a Bulleteer REM or a non-HI trooper; pick that type instead if you need it.

**Timing/limitations:** This is not automatic protection before your first Active Turn. Going second leaves your Knights unbuffed for the enemy opening turn. Each EVO Hacker sustains just **one** Supportware program; switching to Assisted Fire cancels Fairy Dust, and a trooper cannot benefit from two simultaneous Supportware programs. The effect ends if the sustaining Hacker is Isolated/Null. Details: [Fairy Dust](https://infinitythewiki.com/Fairy_Dust), [Firewall](https://infinitythewiki.com/Firewall), [TinBot](https://infinitythewiki.com/TinBot).

### Other EVO use cases

- **Assisted Fire:** Marksmanship on the 22pt Spitfire Bulleteer (or selected active Peacemaker), very strong when that REM actually attacks.
- **Controlled Jump:** helps MO's Combat Jump Crusader Brethren or Santiago, especially if more than one jump is planned.
- **Enhanced Reaction:** less compelling for Sierra, which already has Total Reaction.

**EVO priority in MO:** **A+ with substantial uncovered HI** and real opposing hacking risk. **A conditional** for Assisted Fire/Combat Jump plans. Only B/B+ if most HI already enjoy TinBot protection and there is no REM or AD plan.

## How many?

**Fugazi 7 + one basic Mulebot 8 = 15pts for two native Regular Orders**, often a baseline. The second Mulebot can be another cheap Order if both S4 bases can hide and trooper slots permit. One EVO remains just one pooled Regular Order; **Fairy Dust's selected Troop Type can be in either Combat Group**. Keep it protected from flank/AD attack.

## TR Combi Mulebot (17 / 0): skip / niche

The profile is **BS 11, Total Reaction, Burst 3 Combi Rifle in ARO, ARM 2, Baggage, Repeater, S4**. It is **not** a ranged dedicated ARO comparable to a long-lane Sierra.

| Comparison | TR Mulebot | Sierra Thunderbolt |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cost | **17/0** | **18/0** |
| ARO Burst | 3 | 3 |
| BS | 11 | 11 |
| Weapon | Combi Rifle | Thunderbolt (+1B) |
| Native 360º Visor | No | **Yes** |
| ARM | **2** | 0 |
| Baggage / Repeater | **Yes / Yes** | No / No |

For **+1 point**, Sierra has much more convincing medium/long-range denial, **360º Visor**, and stronger gun pressure. The TR Mulebot only has a case when a **short interior corridor** specifically rewards the Combi's better close range, while **Baggage + a Repeater** also need to be present there. Even then you risk all that utility as soon as you expose the S4 body.

**Do not buy TR because you have free SWC:** it costs **nine points more than a basic Mulebot** for mediocre close-range ARO. If the intention is long-range defense, buy Sierra; if the intention is Baggage or a cheap Order, buy basic. **Rating: C-/D, generally skip.**

**Final:** prioritize **Fairy Dust** if an MO list actually contains many vulnerable HI, not to improve an existing TinBot (-3) into a fictional (-6).
