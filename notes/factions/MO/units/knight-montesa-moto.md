# Motorized Knights of Montesa — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: S value for the 23pt Boarding Shotgun Paramedic; B / specialized for the 29pt Red Fury; C+ for the 26pt MULTI Rifle; C for the 21pt plain Boarding Shotgun.** The motorized Paramedic is the default **independent, extremely mobile two-Wound specialist**. **Velox Duo is optional** rather than the normal reason to buy it: with Impetuous, a motorcycle gives up valuable free solo movement to stay linked. The best exception is **two Motorized Montesas** gaining actual **FT2**; mixed Velox Duos with Joan v2, Bulleteer or Infirmarer are **FT1** and primarily buy shared movement (with one important Joan Tactical Awareness interaction).

## Roster and profile ranking

**Unit ID 1209 · AVA 2 · Regular HI · MOV 20–15 cm (8–6in) · CC23 · BS13 · PH13 · WIP13 · ARM4 · BTS6 · W2 · S4.** Every profile has **Motorcycle, Martial Arts L2, Mimetism (−3), Impetuous, Limited Cover, Immunity (Shock), Dodge (+2.5cm / +1in), Religious Troop** and a **DA CCW**. These bikes are **not** the S2 foot Montesa unit. All four bikes are eligible for a **Velox Duo**, maximum two Motorized Montesa members per Velox.

| Pinned option | Profile | Pts / SWC | Rating | Selection |
|---:|---|---:|---|---|
| **4** | **Boarding Shotgun, MediKit, Paramedic** | **23 / 0** | **S** | **Default:** full close-range combat kit, two Wounds, fast scoring and emergency healing for only 2pts more than the nonspecialist |
| 3 | **Red Fury** | **29 / 1** | **B; B+ with FT2 plan** | Dedicated mobile B4 Shock gun, better sustained favorable range and shooting volume than BSG; costs 6pts and 1 SWC more than Paramedic |
| 2 | **MULTI Rifle** | **26 / 0** | **C+** | More midrange B3 flexibility; niche when a 0-SWC non-specialist shooter is specifically wanted; BSG already supplies AP on a fast, cheaper specialist |
| 1 | **Basic Boarding Shotgun** | **21 / 0** | **C** | Almost always spend **+2pts** for Paramedic; only take if those two points literally cannot be found or specialist capacity adds nothing |

### Why Paramedic beats the other cheap profiles

The 23pt Paramedic is **not** a weaker gunfighter just because it costs less than the MULTI Rifle. A **Boarding Shotgun** has a very strong **0–8in +6 range band**, **AP ammunition** and close-range threat; the motorcycle's **8–6in MOV** gives it much better access to favorable BSG engagements than a normal foot troop. A MULTI Rifle buys an additional active Burst die and easier 8–16in engagements, but **does not uniquely provide AP**. These are real advantages, not usually worth losing the specialist and paying **3pts more** for a fast short-range mission piece.

For **2pts** over the basic BSG, Paramedic adds **MediKit and mission-specialist eligibility** with no reduction in shooting or mobility. Even without an actual heal, touching a late console / interacting with a mission objective can decide a game. Paramedic **is not a Doctor** and does not replace the broader utility of an actual Doctor.

The **29pt / 1SWC Red Fury** is a more meaningful alternate role. Its B4 and 8–24in favorable range support repeated active shooting and mobile flank attacks that the BSG doesn't perform as safely. But Shock lacks the BSG's AP anti-armor edge, and MO has many other Spitfire/HMG/elite shooting options. Prefer it when the list deliberately needs **a second independent medium-range attacker** or a **two-bike FT2 Red Fury shooting leader**; not merely as an upgrade to the Paramedic.

The **21pt basic BSG** is a poor default discount: **2pts saved** sacrifices scenario interaction and the MediKit without changing the close-range threat.

## Solo bike: why its free activations are so valuable

**Impetuous gives each independent bike one optional free activation in its Impetuous Phase**. If terrain and legal movement priorities permit **Move + Move**, an 8–6in bike can travel up to **14in without spending an ordinary pooled Order**. It also contributes its own normal **Regular Order**. This is the central economic reason to take the motorized unit: **fast repositioning while conserving the army's regular Orders for the main attack and objectives**.

This is **not unrestricted free movement**: Impetuous has a limited set of Skill combinations; movement must follow its enemy-contact / enemy-DZ priority constraints, use complete applicable MOV where legal, and cannot finish Prone. You may simply **not use** the free activation if it would cross multiple AROs, a mine trap or a hacking network.

**Practical solo jobs:**
- **Late-game objective switch:** cross to a far console/zone after the main fire lanes are cleared.
- **Second approach vector:** use Impetuous movement along a protected route, then spend Regular Orders to make the final BSG attack or specialist interaction.
- **Enemy DZ incursion:** threaten light units and orders from a flank with BSG, CC23/MA2/DA CCW, and considerable movement. Do not rush into defended open lanes just because speed is available.
- **Two independent Paramedics (46/0):** separate scoring routes and up to **two** useful Impetuous activations per own turn, rather than one cluster. This is often a better mission package than a fixed pair.

**Durability has limits:** ARM4, BTS6, W2, Immunity (Shock) and Mimetism(−3) are excellent at this price, but **Limited Cover eliminates the normal Partial Cover −3 enemy BS MOD**, although the usual cover Saving Roll bonus can still apply. **S4 geometry**, a motorcycle's movement restrictions and Hackable HI status make exposure to open fire lanes, Repeaters and vertical/crowded tables dangerous. Its Mimetism is not equivalent to a full normal-cover shooting defense. Motorcycle is an Equipment/rules distinction; never presume the bike can navigate stairs, ladders or rooftops as freely as S2 infantry.

## Velox Duo: good sometimes, but independent is the baseline

Pinned MO chart allows **Velox Duo**, drawing from **Motorized Montesas (max 2), Bulleteers (max 2), Joan of Arc v2 (max 1), Infirmarer (max 1)**. All are members of the formation's required-choice set. **Same Unit / Counts-as**, not mere duo size, establishes **FT2**. Thus **two bikes** can reach FT2; **bike + Joan, Bulleteer or Infirmarer** is **FT1**.

| Duo composition | Total pts / SWC | FT level | Assessment |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Motorized Red Fury29 + Motorized Paramedic23** | **52 / 1** | **FT2** | **Best offensive Duo (B+)**: B4 Red Fury with +1SD for linked leader, plus accompanying scorer/BSG |
| **Two Motorized Paramedics23 + 23** | **46 / 0** | **FT2** | **B**: two close-range specialists with joint movement, linked +1SD; independent often better for separate objectives |
| **Joan v2 LT AP Spitfire45 + Paramedic23** | **68 / 1** | **FT1** | **B− situational**: Joan's **Tactical Awareness Order** can move both when she leads; no FT2 and mismatched mobility |
| **Bulleteer Spitfire22 + Paramedic23** | **45 / 1** | **FT1** | **C+**: forward Spitfire + BSG scorer; weaker than letting both use different routes |
| **Bulleteer Heavy Shotgun19 + Paramedic23** | **42 / 0** | **FT1** | **C**: two short-range pieces with limited additional synergy; Bulleteer brings Repeater/Mim(−6) but no FT2 |
| **Infirmarer BSG Doctor17 + Paramedic23** | **40 / 0** | **FT1** | **C / niche**: actual Doctor/recovery package; substantial S2/S4 and MOV mismatch |

For Joan **CoC45**, the pair is **68 / 1.5**; different Joan option costs/SWC must be checked. The table uses her **LT45/1** profile. Avoid inventing FT2 for a mixed duo, or confusing Fireteam **+1 Success Die** with **+1 Burst**. Only the **Fireteam Leader** attacks in a normal active linked shooting declaration; the partner moves/Idles according to the team order and earns reactive FT benefits when applicable.

### The meaningful FT2: Red Fury + Paramedic

Best reason to link: the Red Fury becomes a **B4 +1SD** attack piece, while the Paramedic uses those same attacking Orders to cross the table. This is worthwhile when both want the **same route** and the Red Fury expects several productive attacks. The Paramedic can subsequently lead for short-range BSG scoring. They still do **not** both fire simultaneously in one Fireteam Order.

**Why not auto-link:** two Impetuous bikes operating solo can each potentially take their **own free 14in Move + Move activation** in a turn. A linked Duo must **forgo using those free activations if it wants to remain intact**, instead paying Regular Orders for movement. FT2 shooting accuracy and shared Regular-order movement must compensate. A six-activation *opportunity* over three turns for two survivors is not a guarantee that all six activations have legal/safe/valuable routes.

### Joan v2 + bike: Tactical Awareness is the special case

**Joan v2 has Tactical Awareness.** If she spends her **Tactical Order** while designated Fireteam Leader, it can **activate the entire Velox Duo**, allowing the bike to move while Joan attacks or repositions—without drawing a pooled Regular Order. This is the best mechanical argument for this **FT1** pairing.

**Limitations:** Joan moves **6–2in** versus the bike's **8–6in**, making Coherency/terrain awkward. Joan is often better off independently conducting her own aggression while the motorcycle travels a different flank using its free Impetuous activation. And **the Lieutenant spending her own Lieutenant Special Order leaves the Fireteam** under Fireteam Integrity; in a two-model Duo, that cancels the team. **Tactical Awareness does not have that restriction.** Joan's Inspiring Leadership and LT/CoC profile choice are separate issues: the bike itself is already Regular and does not receive a second free Impetuous Order from Joan.

### Bulleteer + bike: useful weapons, little FT payoff

The **22pt Spitfire Bulleteer** has Mimetism(−6), Repeater and a medium-range attack, while the Montesa brings BSG AP and scoring. However, both are S4 and hackable, move **6–4in vs 8–6in**, only reach **FT1** and can become exposed together. The Bulleteer gains **no +1SD** merely by linking. Usually better to let the cheap Bulleteer open a shooting angle while the bike uses a separate safe lane and Impetuous movement. The **19pt Heavy Shotgun Bulleteer** gives a cheaper 0SWC Duo but overlaps short ranges and rarely adds enough to justify the link.

### Is it worth creating the Duo later?

**Yes, situationally:** start two bikes independently, exploit their Impetuous movement in the opening phase, and **spend a Command Token during a legal creation window** to form a Duo once both are where a **decisive FT2 push** is useful. This converts an independent mobility plan into a concentrated attack plan; creation is **not free**.

Important **Duo-specific restriction:** Impetuous activation of a linked bike causes it to leave immediately. If the Duo's leader is the activated bike, the whole Duo cancels; if the non-Leader leaves, the resulting **single member cancels** the Duo anyway. Unlike a surviving three-member Haris reduced to two, a **cancelled Duo cannot automatically restore itself in the States Phase**. To establish it again later generally requires **another Command Token**. Rejoining at the end of the next States Phase works only for a **surviving** Fireteam and former members in Coherency, not for recreating a cancelled Duo.

## Motorized vs foot Montesa: do not substitute one for the other

| Attribute | Foot Montesa | Motorized Montesa |
|---|---|---|
| Profile identity / AVA | Separate **foot AVA1** | Separate **bike AVA2** |
| Movement | **4–4in** | **8–6in** |
| Silhouette / terrain | **S2**, better at tight/vertical approaches | **S4 motorcycle**, larger and more constrained |
| ARM / BTS / W | **4 / 6 / 2** | **4 / 6 / 2** |
| Mimetism / Impetuous / Limited Cover | **−3 / yes / yes** | **−3 / yes / yes** |
| Special resilience | **BS Attack (SR−1)**; some profiles FD+8in | **Immunity (Shock), Dodge (+1in)** |
| Best specialist | **FTO BSG + Blitzen + Paramedic 20/0** | **BSG + Paramedic 23/0** |
| Other specialist | **FD +8in BSG Paramedic 26/0** | — |
| Fireteam | Only **FTO foot** Wildcard; no Counts-as FT level | Any bike profile in **Velox Duo** |
| Main role | Cheap S2 E/M/scoring roadblock or specific midfield deployment | **Repeated table-wide mobility**, flank scoring and fast close attack |

**The key comparisons:**

- **Foot FTO 20 vs bike Paramedic 23:** the bike buys **much faster repeat movement for +3pts**; foot keeps **Blitzen**, smaller S2 geometry, **BS Attack (SR−1)** and FTO Wildcard option. Both are Impetuous and score as Paramedics. The foot does **not** need to link to justify its points.
- **Foot FD Paramedic 26 vs bike Paramedic 23:** FD starts **8in forward before turn 1** and remains S2; the bike is cheaper by 3pts and faster on every useful later movement. **Both** retain their own Impetuous activations: don't argue that one gets a free Order and the other doesn't.
- **Foot Spitfire35/1.5 or Red Fury32/1.5 vs bike Red Fury29/1:** the bike is an attractive **fast flanker** at less points/SWC; foot Spitfire has Drop Bears, SR−1 and forward deployment, but costs more and operates as S2. Role and terrain decide, not pure weapon name.
- **Can take both foot and motorized**: separate unit entries. For a zero-SWC, two-route scoring package, **foot FTO20 solo + motorized Paramedic23 solo = 43pts / 0 SWC** gives Blitzen/E/M area control plus exceptionally fast objective reach without committing any Fireteam slot.

## Listbuilding selection rule

1. **Default motorized pick: Paramedic BSG 23 / 0, solo.** Buy one when you need a rapid late scorer or dangerous flanking specialist.
2. **A second independent Paramedic** can be excellent in dispersed scoring missions; verify safe bike routes and what else needs those 23 points before taking both.
3. **Red Fury 29 / 1** only for a deliberate mobile ranged attacker or strong **Red Fury + Paramedic FT2** plan.
4. **Usually skip MULTI Rifle 26 / 0.** It improves midrange B3 shooting, but the cheaper Paramedic BSG already supplies AP and actual objective work.
5. **Almost always skip basic BSG 21 / 0** unless exactly two points decide list legality.
6. **Velox is an option, not the default:** link **two bikes** for meaningful FT2 shooting/shared Orders, or consider **Joan TA-led FT1** for a specific jointly moving mission push. Bulleteer/Infirmarer mixes are usually worse than independent operation.
7. **Consider forming Velox later** if two independent bikes converge for a pivotal attack; budget a Command Token, and remember a broken Duo cannot automatically rejoin.
8. **Choose foot FTO 20** when Blitzen/E/M, S2 routes or durable cheap specialist defense outweigh the repeated-speed advantage.

**Overall: Motorized Montesa Paramedic is an exceptional cheap independent specialist. Velox is situational tactical flexibility—not a standing obligation to surrender its Impetuous movement.**
