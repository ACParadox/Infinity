# Dart, Optimate Huntress — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: Skip / Niche (C overall).** Dart is **not a default MO pick or must-have**. At **35–42 points**, her short-range BS13/AP SMG, BS1 Viral Tactical Bow, and W1+NWI do not deliver the combat return MO receives from cheaper Trinitarians, Hawkins, Montesas, or slightly dearer premium knights. **Niche exceptions:** 35/0 E/M Grenades for an independent non-hackable camouflaged MSV1 hunter with an anti-heavy trick; **42/0.5 Sharvara K-9** as an experimental synchronized close-range attack fork on a dense table. Neither is established competitive value; the 35pt Shock Mine and 36pt Minelayer are generally **skip**.

### Sharvara K-9, only with option 4

**MOV 15–10 cm (6–4 in), CC22, BS11, PH12, WIP12, ARM0, BTS3, STR1, S3.** Camouflage, CC Attack (−6), Courage, Dodge (+3), Dodge (+2"), Forward Deployment (+8"), Mimetism (−3), Natural Born Warrior, Peripheral (Synchronized), Stealth. **Weapons:** Flash Pulse; **Heavy Riotstopper**; **E/Marat**; **DA CC Weapon (PS=7)**; **PARA CC Weapon**. ## Why MO mostly skips her

1. **Bad premium-attack return.** Dart is BS13 with a short-range **AP SMG**, only **Mimetism (−3)**, modest ARM1, and W1+NWI/Immunity (Shock). NWI helps, but it is not the same as being a durable ARM4 two-Wound knight. The Viral Tactical Bow is a B1 niche attack/ARO and **(+1SD) does not make it a competitive high-Burst primary gun**. Climbing Plus, Decoy, MSV1 and Surprise Attack are useful, but do not automatically justify 35–42 points.
2. **Her main scoring role is already covered better.** **Trinitarian FO 22/0**, **FO Submachine Gun (PS6) 23/0**, and **KHD 24/0** bring **Infiltration, Hidden Deployment, Camouflage and Mimetism (−6)** for 11–20 fewer points. The **23/1 FO/Deployable Repeater Minelayer** further supports MO's hacking plan at deployment. Dart's Specialist Operative is generic: it is **not** a Forward Observer, Hacker, Doctor or Engineer for objectives demanding those skills.
3. **MO has much stronger close-range combatants.** **Hawkins Boarding Shotgun (+1B) / BS Attack (−3) / Specialist Operative at 26/0** is a devastating and much cheaper close-range shooter with two Wounds and better CC. **Mendoza 40/0** adds BS14, Mimetism (−6), ARM4/W2, Continuous Damage and serious CC; his solo Heavy Shotgun starts Forward Deployment (+8"). The extra 5 points over 35pt Dart is often worth far more than Dart's equipment bundle.
4. **Near-price premium alternatives offer an army plan, not just a tool kit.** **De Fersen 42/0.5** is a two-Wound BS14/WIP14 Hacking Device with **Trinity (+1SD)**, link eligibility and useful weapon choices. **Joan v2 Lieutenant 45/1** brings a BS15 AP Spitfire, two Wounds + NWI, Tactical Awareness, excellent combat skills and command effects. **Crux FTO MULTI Marksman 46/0** is a BS14 ARM5/W2 specialist with Albedo (−6), +1SD and Fireteam access. The specific roles differ, but Dart is buying too little direct impact for nearly comparable points.
5. **MO can cheaply buy the narrow pieces instead.** **Peacemaker + Auxbot 23/0** offers a forward Repeater and template pressure; **foot Montesa Forward Deployment Paramedic 26/0** offers a tough S2 mission piece; **motorized Montesa Paramedic 23/0** is a fast independent scorer. Cheap supporting soldiers are particularly valuable in MO because Knights/TAGs consume Orders and points.
6. **No Fireteam, LT-order or Tactical Awareness leverage.** Dart does not improve a Core/Haris level, cannot profit from link Burst/BS, has no NCO, and does not self-fund extra activity. **42pts parked defensively** wastes an elite attacking budget; **42pts actively advancing** can drain Orders from the actual MO spearhead.

| Purchase | Pts / SWC | Consequence |
|---|---:|---|
| Dart + Sharvara | **42 / 0.5** | One synchronized specialist/attack package and **one Regular Order** |
| **Two Trinitarian FOs** | **44 / 0** | **Two independent infiltrating Hidden Deployment/Mimetism (−6) specialists; two pooled Orders only while deployed on the table rather than Hidden** |
| **Trinitarian FO + Teutonic specialist** | **41 / 0** | Two independent Regular Orders; infiltration/scoring plus a two-Wound Panzerfaust-bearing HI specialist |
| **Hawkins BSG specialist + Raveneye** | **38 / 0** (26+12) | Exceptional linked short-range attacker plus independent cheap order/defence |
| **De Fersen HD** | **42 / 0.5** | Two-Wound gunfighter/hacker and potential linked cyber/assault engine |

These purchases are **not tactically identical**. The point is that **Dart must perform her specific tricks reliably enough to justify giving up broader capability and sometimes an extra Regular Order**. Most MO lists have no such need.

## The few genuine reasons to field Dart

### 35/0 — E/M Grenades and anti-camouflage hunting

Unlike Kestrel, **MO does not have an 11-point infiltrating Jackal E/M Grenade Launcher**. This makes Dart's E/M Grenades more distinctive *within MO*, but **distinctive is not automatically efficient**: MO also has Blitzens, strong hackers/Repeaters, CC solutions and long-range AP guns.

- **MSV1 + BS13 + Camo Surprise Attack (−3)** can hunt Mimetism-bearing midfield opponents or force awkward AROs. MSV1 ignores Mimetism (−3) and reduces Mimetism (−6) to −3; it does **not** grant an automatic Discover or solve every Camo-marker interaction.
- **AP SMG** is serviceable inside 8" after a favorable approach, especially with surprise. It is not the primary answer to a developed ARO network.
- **E/M Grenades** can threaten tough/hackable enemies behind corners. At 0–8", **PH13 +3 range −6 Speculative = 10**, before other applicable modifiers; beyond that good range band reliability deteriorates. B1 speculative lobs can consume precious Orders. E/M is an immobilization/isolation play, **not a substitute for reliable long-range anti-TAG damage**.
- **Non-hackable Regular specialist with Climbing Plus and W1+NWI** can approach by alternate routes and sometimes finish objectives after other pieces engage. But MO's cheaper Trinitarians already do most basic independent scoring more efficiently.

**Use only if** a mission/opponent/table strongly rewards the **combination** of MSV1 skirmisher hunting, independent objective ability, vertical approach and E/M, **and** the primary MO gunfighter plus order infrastructure are already funded. Do not include her just because the profile has many useful keywords.

### 42/0.5 — Sharvara synchronized attack fork (experiment, not default)

The meaningful reason for the K-9 is **two attack origins in a single Order**. Under legal Peripheral (Synchronized) attack declarations, Dart can attack with her B3 AP SMG while Sharvara attacks with **E/Marat** (E/M direct template) or **Heavy Riotstopper** (PARA direct template). If the target is exposed to both, its ARO choice can favor one threat and leave the other unopposed, subject to actual LoF/template/coherency and reactive-turn rules.

The option also sets up a **Camo/Decoy/Minelayer deception package**: Dart marker, Decoy, predeployed Shock Mine, and Sharvara marker can create multiple apparent threats under legal deployment conditions. Sharvara's **CC22, Natural Born Warrior, CC Attack (−6), DA CCW** adds a dangerous follow-up.

**Why this stays experimental:**

- **42pts / 0.5SWC** is near De Fersen and Joan money, but the pair is still short-ranged, fragile to screening and needs activation/setup.
- **Sharvara ARM0/STR1/S3** can be removed by inexpensive templates, hacking or good counterpositioning. The dog is not a durable second assault knight.
- **Coherency and unequal routes** matter: Dart climbs, Sharvara does not.
- One Peripheral package **does not create a second Order** or become two independent objective runners.
- The best attack fork needs **both origins already in threatening positions**; solving that geometry can be more costly than attacking with a primary MO gunfighter.
- The K-9 profile retains Shock Mine/Minelayer but **forfeits the E/M Grenades profile**. Its E/M effect must come through the K-9's E/Marat.

**Only test when** a dense table supports close synchronized angles, there is an opposing high-value target vulnerable to E/Marat/Riotstopper, and a separate long-range attacker can first open the route. Track **setup Orders, useful attack forks actually created, K-9 survival and mission points**, not spectacular theoretical outcomes.

### 35 Shock Mine / 36 Minelayer — not enough

**Skip both.** Dart's Decoy plus a placed Shock Mine can complicate deployment, but her **35–36pt** cost is too high when MO has Trinitarian mines/Minelayer and a forward Peacemaker with Auxbot. A **36/0.5 Dart without Sharvara** offers no compelling incremental purpose over the 35/0 E/M version.

## Who to pair her with? (Not a Fireteam)

Dart **cannot join any MO Fireteam**. Pair her strategically with an **independent long-range opener**, not by proposing a legal link that does not exist:

- **Holy Sepulchre AP HMG NCO / Tikbalang / Seraph:** clears long firelanes and threats before Dart crosses to short range. Watch the total elite-point burden.
- **De Fersen or an MO hacker/repeater module:** Dart can attack hostile marker/hacker screens that threaten the cyber/HI formation. But if De Fersen is already present, ask whether 35–42pts would be better spent on **a Trinitarian and more orders**.
- **Crosier/Commander or compact support Fireteam:** cheap Orders and defensive coverage while Dart works an alternate lane. She contributes **no Fireteam level or bonus**.

Avoid paying for Dart *plus* Mendoza/Hawkins/Crux/Joan merely to accumulate premium 0–16" attackers. MO already has abundant close-range combat; **range extension, orders, cyber support and actual scoring** usually matter more.

## Community evidence: do not overstate usage

The [Kestrel analysis](../../kestrel/units/dart.md#podcast--player-evidence-and-how-much-weight-to-give-it) records:
- **Tactical Awareness S3 Ep44** (May 2026, ~1:24–1:26): describes the Dart + Sharvara **SMG versus E/Marat/Riotstopper ARO fork**, while questioning its **42pt value** and the dog's vulnerability.
- **Irregular Order #37** (May 2026, ~3:15): characterizes Sharvara as an expensive, interesting novelty.
- **MetaChemistry #135** (~55:50–56:20, pre-Sharvara): favors E/M Grenades over Minelayer in Acontecimento because the capability was distinctive there. **Do not transplant that preference into a claim that Dart is efficient in MO.**
- **Loss of Lieutenant #168** (May 2026, ~31–33 min): discusses marker/Minelayer deception, primarily in OSS context.

None establishes recurring **MO tournament use** or a measured win-rate advantage. These are tactical hypotheses and cross-sectorial impressions, not proof of an MO auto-include.

## Final build decision

**Default: Skip Dart in Military Orders.** Choose **Trinitarian FO/KHD** for infiltrating specialist work; **Hawkins BSG** for efficient close assault plus scoring; **Mendoza** for a premium durable close-range gunfighter; **De Fersen** for fighting and hacking; **Crux FTO or Joan v2** when an elite gunfighter is worth the list investment; or **Peacemaker/Montesa** for cheaper forward utility.

**Niche 1:** **35/0 E/M Grenades** only when the exact anti-camo/MSV1 + independent skirmisher + E/M combination is mission-relevant. **Niche 2:** **42/0.5 Sharvara** as a deliberate synchronized attack experiment on tightly obstructed terrain. **Skip** the two mine-only profiles. **No Dart profile is mandatory**, and the unusual skills alone do not reverse her poor opportunity cost.
