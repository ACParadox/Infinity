# Knights of the Holy Sepulchre — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: AP HMG NCO 53/1 SWC — A− overall, situational A in a well-supported non-TAG long-range plan; MSV1 MULTI Marksman Rifle (+1B) 53/0 — B+ situational.** The **AP HMG is the default recommendation** because it fills a scarce MO job: a genuinely threatening, repeatable **16–32-inch AP gun** on an independent, Warhorse-protected HI that does not consume Core/Haris slots. The MMR is individually good against Mimetism at 8–24 inches but overlaps MO's many existing Spitfire, Red Fury and MMR attackers. **Neither is mandatory.** In particular, **Knight Commander LT (+1 Order) is a good synergy, not an automatic A+ combination**: the Seraph, Tikbalang or a legal Teutonic NCO Fireteam may extract more value from those Orders.

**Evidence standard:** The profile and Army legality below are sourced from the pinned N5.3 roster; rules claims link to current N5.3 wiki; video/podcast opinions are attributed, not treated as measured win rates. A verified **2025/26 IGL list entry** confirms competitive use of the AP HMG + Commander combination, **not** that it won the game or was decisive. Reviewed **23 November 2026**.

## 1. Exact N5.3 profiles and priority

**ID 29 · AVA 2 · Regular HI · MOV 15–5 cm (6–2 inches) · CC22 · BS14 · PH13 · WIP13 · ARM5 · BTS9 · W2 · S5.**

**Common skills:** Holoprojector, Surprise Attack (−3), Natural Born Warrior, Stealth, Religious Troop, **Warhorse**, BS Attack (SR−1). Common sidearms: Heavy Pistol and **Shock CC Weapon (PS=6)**. Both are **independent** and **cannot join any Military Orders Fireteam**.

| Pinned option | Profile, distinguishing equipment | Pts / SWC | Rating | Use |
|---:|---|---:|---|---|
| **4** | **AP Heavy Machine Gun; NCO** | **53 / 1** | **A−; situational A** | Default long-range anti-armor opener, especially if a standard LT Order can be spent through NCO |
| 1 | MULTI Marksman Rifle **(+1B); MSV1** | 53 / 0 | **B+ situational** | Independent anti-Mimetism 8–24-inch fighter; SWC-free option when the list already has long-range coverage |

**Critical profile correction:** The MMR profile has **MSV1, not Deployable Repeater**; the AP HMG has **NCO, not MSV1**. Do not merge their equipment or grant either profile Tactical Awareness, Specialist Operative, Fireteam access or a third Wound. The +1B is on the MMR weapon, not an ARO-wide +1B skill.

**Cost context:** 53 points is 17.7% of a 300-point list, before supporting Orders, Doctor/medikit coverage and scoring pieces. AVA 2 is permission to select two, not a recommendation to do so.

## 2. Why the AP HMG matters more in MO than the MMR

MO has many effective medium-/close-range threats: Joan v2 AP Spitfire, Knights of Justice Spitfire/MMR, Crux FTO Spitfire/MMR, Santiago Spitfire, De Fersen Spitfire, Teutonic Spitfire, Bulleteer Spitfire, Montesa Red Fury/Spitfire and multiple shotguns. **What it lacks outside TAGs is a deep bench of high-quality, long-range active guns**.

| Non-TAG alternative | Profile | Differentiation versus Sepulchre AP HMG |
|---|---|---|
| **Hospitaller FTO HMG** | **32/1.5**, BS14, W2 | Excellent **linked** economical long gun; ordinary HMG (no AP); Crusade investment for Fireteam bonuses, weaker as stand-alone shooter |
| **Crosier MSV2 HMG** | **24/1**, BS12, W1 | Superior against Mimetism/visual threats, but cheaper and much less durable/accurate baseline |
| **Sierra Total Reaction HMG** | **23/1**, BS11, W1 | Primarily ARO; not a premium independent active attacker |
| **Knight of Justice Missile Launcher (+1SD)** | **46/1.5**, BS14, W2 | Premium defensive anchor; launcher is not sustained B4 HMG fire |
| **Trinitarian MULTI Sniper** | **25/1.5**, BS12, Camo/Mim−6 | Long-range asymmetric piece, mainly reactive; limited active Burst versus HMG |
| **Holy Sepulchre AP HMG** | **53/1**, BS14, W2, NCO | Rare **AP HMG + SR−1 + Holoprojector + Warhorse** in one independent model |

**Role fit:** Use the HMG as stage-one lane clearance against heavy AROs and armored opponents. Have the *other* MO Knights or specialists exploit the cleared area. A second 53-point midfield attacker can instead duplicate jobs that cheaper models already perform.

**Not an absolute shooting apex:** BS14 is good, but the Sepulchre has **no permanent Mimetism, MSV, Marksmanship or Fireteam bonus**. Against Mim−6 or an unfavorable defensive gun, its one conditional Surprise Attack is not an adequate long-run answer. For elite long-range dueling, a Tikbalang often has better sustained FtF odds.

## 3. AP HMG versus MSV1 MMR (+1B): actual weapon comparison

All entries below use the **same BS14 and BS Attack (SR−1)** bearer. Lower **PS is better** for the attacker in N5 because it reduces the defender's chance of passing the Saving Roll. A PS5 AP HMG penetrates better than a PS7 AP MMR for identical successful hits.

| Attribute | **AP HMG NCO** | **MSV1 MULTI MMR (+1B)** |
|---|---|---|
| Points/SWC | 53/1 | 53/0 |
| Range role | **16–32 inches** | **8–24 inches** |
| AP active Burst | **4** | **4** (base B3 + weapon +1B) |
| AP Power of Survival | **PS5** (better) | PS7 |
| AP armor handling | ARM/2, one Save per ordinary hit | ARM/2, one Save per ordinary hit |
| Extra ammo | — | Shock B4, DA anti-materiel B2, with different target/use cases |
| Vs Mimetism | Ordinary BS MODs; Surprise Attack can help one opening exchange | **MSV1 cancels Mim−3; reduces Mim−6 to Mim−3** |
| Order advantage | **NCO** can spend Special LT Orders | No NCO |
| Range/shotgun role overlap | Minimal | **High** within MO |

| Distance | MMR range MOD | HMG range MOD | Practical reading |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 0–8\" | −3 | −3 | Both poor; HMG stronger PS on same AP hit |
| 8–16\" | +3 | 0 | MMR has a real FtF/range advantage; weapon PS still favors HMG |
| 16–24\" | +3 | +3 | **Against a non-Mimetism armored target the AP HMG wins**, since B/BS/AP are equal but PS5 is better than PS7 |
| 24–32\" | −3 | +3 | **HMG overwhelmingly better** in intended use |
| 32–40\" | −3 | −3 | Similar range MODs, but neither is ideal; PS favors HMG, MSV may change the matchup |

**Example — ARM8 TAG in Cover, without Mimetism, at 20 inches:** Both shoot AP B4 with the same BS target. After AP halves ARM, Cover grants its Saving Roll bonus and the knight applies SR−1. The defender's effective Save target is **11 versus HMG PS5** and **13 versus MMR PS7** (under the N5 Saving Roll convention, **the defender fails more often against HMG**). **Do not call the MMR superior at 16–24 inches solely because it has MSV1:** against a target without Mimetism, that visor does nothing.

**Where MMR can outperform:** 8–16 inches due to its +3 vs the HMG's 0, or against Mim−3/Mim−6 targets where MSV1 changes Face-to-Face rolls. Shock/DA modes and saving 1 SWC may matter. **But even when good, it answers a problem for which MO already has many alternatives.** The MMR's anti-materiel B2 DA mode should not be confused with simultaneously firing B4 AP+DA.

## 4. Holoprojector is partly a defensive investment, not just an offensive −3

Holoprojector is especially relevant **before the Sepulchre's first activation and after ending a turn hidden**. It has meaningful anti-alpha and counterattack value even if its shooting is only used intermittently.

**Current [N5.3 Holoecho State](https://infinitythewiki.com/Holoecho_State):**

- **At deployment:** The player can place **three Holoecho positions** — one real bearer and **two decoys** — under the deployment/coherency restrictions. The real position is recorded secretly. This can impede turn-one attackers trying to immediately remove the 53-point gun.
- **As reactive defense:** The opponent may need to choose which Holoecho to attack/Discover. Decoys count for LoF/AROs and can activate enemy Mines/perimeter gear. A successful hit on a decoy removes it rather than wounding the real knight. This can cost the enemy Orders or complicate an alpha strike; it is **not two extra Wounds**.
- **At the States Phase:** If the real bearer is **outside enemy Marker/Trooper LoF**, it may automatically regenerate up to two decoys **for free**, placing them in Silhouette contact. This refresh can protect against next-turn counterattacks. The starting three positions may spread under deployment/coherency rules; **later refresh places new echoes in Silhouette contact**, a more constrained geometry.
- **During its active turn:** It may instead spend a **Long Skill** to restore Holoecho State when outside enemy LoF; not normally worth doing just to gain one more surprise shot.
- **Cancellation:** Shooting, declaring skills requiring Rolls, being forced to make a Saving Roll, being Discovered, etc., can cancel the real bearer's state; if all decoys are Discovered, it also collapses. Cancellation affects the entire Order. The defender may **delay ARO** and then react if the actual bearer reveals itself. Respect these limits when planning surprise shots.
- **Not permanent Mimetism:** Once the echoes are gone, the Sepulchre is a conventional BS14, ARM5, two-Wound HI without an enduring FtF MOD. A template, a correct target selection or patient Discover can defeat the deception.

**Recommended activation cycle:** Begin in Holoecho State; use an AP HMG Surprise Attack for a favorable opening exchange; **keep shooting if there are worthwhile targets**; spend needed movement to finish outside enemy LoF (often using Prone if terrain permits); re-enter Holoecho State in States Phase without expending another Order. The critical condition is **a safe end position**, not exactly one attack per turn.

**Counterplay caveats:** Opponents can force multiple saves, hit correct echoes, attack from angles that expose the real model, attack all eligible models with appropriate templates, or simply avoid its engagement lane. Holoecho is valuable defensive uncertainty and tempo, **not** an invulnerable defense or an automatic roadblock.

## 5. Warhorse, BTS9 and Stealth: anti-tech advantages

[Warhorse](https://infinitythewiki.com/Warhorse) means the Sepulchre **cannot become Isolated**, continues operating with Regular status under Loss of Lieutenant and Retreat!, and ignores enemy **BS Attack (−X)** MODs. With **BTS9** and Stealth, it is unusually resistant to some popular HI/TAG denial strategies.

**Precise limit:** Warhorse **does not** grant blanket immunity to hackers: IMM-B/Targeted and damaging hacking or other effects can still matter under their normal rules. A Guided/Spotlight strategy remains a real concern. The Sepulchre may be healed by an appropriate Doctor rather than repaired as a Remote Presence TAG. Its 2W, ARM5 still represents a materially smaller durability budget than a TAG's STR3 and better ARM.

## 6. NCO and Lieutenant investment — good synergy, not an A+ guarantee

[NCO](https://infinitythewiki.com/NCO) lets the bearer turn available **Special Lieutenant Orders into its own Tactical Orders**. The NCO and the LT need not be in the same Combat Group. However, these are **not regular pooled Orders**, and the Sepulchre has **no Tactical Awareness** of its own.

| Command setup | Total Sepulchre + LT cost | Implication |
|---|---:|---|
| **Sepulchre53 + Knight Commander LT(+1)24** | **77 pts** | **Two Special LT Orders** available to NCO each active turn; a strong *option* if both are expected to be useful |
| Sepulchre53 + Knight Commander basic LT20 | 73 pts | One Special LT Order, less command investment |
| Sepulchre53 + Teutonic LT18 | 71 pts | Cheapest nominal package; obvious/imprudent Impetuous-LT exposure considerations |
| Sepulchre53 + a different LT | Varies | One LT Order via NCO may be sufficient, especially if Sepulchre plays a secondary/Group-2 opener |

**Opportunity cost of Knight Commander:** Its 24 points include its normal Regular Order, protected command role, Holomask and potential defensive Fireteam equivalence — it is not merely a 24-point tax for two shooting Orders. But compared with the 18-point Teutonic LT, the Commander costs **six more points**, for a safer command design and **one extra Special LT Order**. Those Orders can become stranded if the Sepulchre has no favorable fight.

**Competing NCO recipients in MO:**

| NCO recipient | Why it might use two LT Orders better |
|---|---|
| **Tikbalang HMG68/1.5** | BS15, permanent Mim−3, Climbing Plus, STR3, **Tactical Awareness**; can sustain more favorable shots/movement |
| **Seraph MULTI HMG74/1.5 or AP Spitfire72/1.5** | BS15, Super-Jump, STR3, **Tactical Awareness**, high mobility and often a larger Order appetite |
| **Teutonic NCO Spitfire29/1.5** | Very cheap NCO; when leader of a legal Fireteam its converted Tactical Order can move/activate the whole team, though solo shooting is relatively fragile |
| **Holy Sepulchre AP HMG53/1** | Cheapest genuinely premium standalone long AP gun here; may not want multiple Orders after one good Surprise Attack |

**Verdict:** The **Sepulchre itself remains A−** whether or not the Commander is selected; a protected Commander may elevate the whole plan to **situational A**, but **not automatic A+**. Judge by actual turns in which you could profitably spend **both** LT Orders, not theoretical Orders generated.

## 7. Mini-TAG comparison: Tikbalang and Seraph

| Characteristic | **Sepulchre AP HMG** | **Tikbalang Montesa HMG** | **Seraph MULTI HMG** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Points / SWC | **53/1** | 68/1.5 | 74/1.5 |
| BS | 14 | **15** | **15** |
| ARM / BTS | 5 / **9** | 6 / 6 | **7** / 6 |
| W / STR | 2 | 3 | 3 |
| MOV cm | 15–5 | 15–10 | 15–10 |
| S | 5 | 6 | 7 |
| Native NCO | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Tactical Awareness | No | **Yes** | **Yes** |
| Special advantages | **AP HMG, Holoprojector, Warhorse, Prone** | **Mim−3, Climbing Plus, HFT** | **Super-Jump, MULTI HMG, Pulzar(+1B)** |
| Attacker drawbacks | Conditional Surprise, 2W, no self-Order | Ordinary HMG lacks AP on its own; hackable | Large S7, expensive, no permanent Mim |

**Sepulchre vs Tikbalang (15-point gap):** Sepulchre's **AP** is valuable into ARM, and Holoecho/Prone/Warhorse alter defensive play. Tikbalang has **more consistent FtF defense, extra STR, mobility and Tactical Awareness**. Choose Sepulchre when 15 points or 0.5 SWC buys actual independent scoring, hacking or order support and long AP is needed; choose Tik when repeated ranged engagements and mobility are the core plan.

**Sepulchre vs Seraph (21-point gap):** A Seraph MULTI HMG already has **B4 PS5 AP mode**, as well as Shock/anti-materiel modes. The Sepulchre has **no anti-armor weapon superiority** here. It wins on budget, smaller footprint, Holoecho protection and Warhorse, but not raw firepower, mobility or repeat-order durability. The Seraph AP Spitfire+Auxbot is a **different shorter-range role**, not a direct HMG equivalent.

**Do not treat Holoechoes as three wounds or Tactical Awareness:** the Sepulchre is *mini-TAG-like in firepower and price*, not literally equivalent to a full TAG.

## 8. Real player opinions and demonstrated usage

### Robert Shepherd — [July 2026 Military Orders faction focus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JrMTA91V7wc)

**Skeptical, prefers AP HMG over MMR when taking the unit.** He acknowledges its long-range contribution but warns **BS14 and conditional Surprise Attack do not equal sustained top-tier shooting**. Recommends considering a **Group-2 secondary gunfighter** that takes perhaps one favorable surprise attack per turn, retreats and refreshes holograms. Would generally rather add the ~15 points for a **Tikbalang** as a reliable main gun. He challenges the assumption that investing in Knight Commander LT(+1) is always justified for this unit, versus supporting a higher-ceiling TAG.

**Interpretation:** Sound warning against over-rating the Sepulchre as a solo all-game primary attacker. The **one-shot/turn use is a *viable employment pattern*, not a mandatory restriction**: if a genuine opportunity opens, keep shooting.

### Sneaky Snake — [post-October-2025 Military Orders overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kRaambqovmw)

**More favorable:** Calls attention to its strong BTS/Warhorse/defensive skill package, the rare **AP HMG + SR−1**, and its *mini-TAG* character. Notes its good **NCO + Knight Commander LT(+1)** synergy. Also considers the newly **MSV1 MMR** more interesting than its previous Chain-of-Command iteration, but does not establish that it is a generally better choice than the AP HMG.

**Interpretation:** Supports the **role uniqueness and versatility** case. Favorable commentary is **not proof of tournament auto-include status**.

## 9. How to field the AP HMG

**Recommended default:** **One** Sepulchre AP HMG, deployed independently with a protected withdrawal plan, accompanied by cheaper midfield scoring and/or a separate Knight team. Choose whether it is **(a) main long gun** with a moderate Order allocation or **(b) Group-2 secondary opener** supported by a standard Lieutenant Order. It is never a Fireteam member.

**Operational checklist:**

1. **Before buying:** Does the list already have reliable 24–32-inch premium fire (Tikbalang, Seraph, effective linked HMG)? If yes, is the AP niche worth another 53 points?
2. **Deployment:** Use Holoecho geometry to complicate enemy first-turn access, especially enemy DZ attackers; keep real model capable of going Prone or withdrawing. Do not cluster all three where one template neutralizes the trick.
3. **First attack:** Favor visible, un-Mimetized heavy defenders or a target for which Surprise Attack materially changes the FtF. Compare your own and opponent's range bands.
4. **During turn:** **Do not impose a one-shot limit**. Continue shooting valuable targets where AP HMG B4 is the correct tool; spending an entire turn on an unfavorable Mim−6 defender may be a mistake.
5. **End turn:** Try to finish **outside enemy LoF** so Holoecho returns automatically in the States Phase and protects against the counterstrike; use Prone/terrain.
6. **Order allocation:** Commander LT(+1) is worthwhile **only if** two LT Orders are frequently actionable; otherwise ordinary LT + NCO, cheap independent scorers, or a better NCO recipient may provide better utility.
7. **Mission backup:** The Sepulchre is **not a Specialist**. Keep Trinitarian, Montesa, Doctor/Hacker or another button pusher available even if the HMG dies.

**When to take:** You want **an independent AP HMG without a TAG**, you can exploit 16–32-inch lanes, you value Holoprojector protection against initial/return strikes, or the 15–21 points saved versus a TAG purchase a meaningful mission capability.

**When to skip:** You already have a TAG long gun; the mission rewards several cheap objective activations; tables/terrain prevent favorable HMG lanes; you face too many Mim−6/MSV/linked shooting contests; you cannot protect 53 points with Orders and deployment geometry; or you would rather have a Tikbalang/Seraph for repeat gunfighting.

**When to take MMR instead:** You have no need for a second long-range gun or NCO, desperately want **0 SWC**, and need a durable **MSV1, B4 AP/Shock** fighter for dense 8–24-inch angles. This is a **specific counter/role choice**, not the generic alternative to the AP HMG.

## Final judgement

**AP HMG NCO: A−; sometimes A in the right MO build. Not A+, not a must-take.** It is a **scarce and useful role-filler**: real 16–32-inch AP fire, Holoecho counterattack/alpha-strike friction, Warhorse anti-Isolation and NCO Order access. But **BS14 without permanent defensive shooting MODs and only two Wounds** means it cannot demand or safely win every direct exchange.

**MSV1 MMR (+1B): B+ and situational.** It has a legitimate anti-Mimetism medium-range use, particularly with 0 SWC, but competes with a large number of MO midrange guns. Crucially, **against an ordinary non-Mimetism TAG at 16–24 inches, the AP HMG is better** because both have AP B4 and identical shooting MODs while **HMG PS5 is better than MMR PS7**.

**Listbuilding decision:** Start by asking **which range and attacker role the list is missing**. Select **Sepulchre AP HMG** when the missing role is non-TAG long AP pressure; **Tikbalang** when sustained gunfighter consistency, mobility and a Tactical Awareness Order are worth 15 additional points; **Seraph** when mobility, projected routes and sustained premium TAG output justify 21 extra points.
