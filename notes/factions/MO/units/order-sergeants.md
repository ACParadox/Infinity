# Order Sergeants — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A+ for the FTO Hacker/Pitcher in a true FT2 Duo; A− for the linked FTO HRL and standalone Neurocinetics HRL + Auxbot.** These are efficient MO enablers, **not mandatory inclusions**. The strongest argument for Order Sergeants is not an entire Haris: an **18pt Hacker/Pitcher plus a 15–17pt same-unit/counts-as partner** creates an inexpensive Duo with **BS Attack (+1SD)** on the Pitcher and an independently valuable second role. This leaves the Core and single Haris slot free for Knights.

## Roster and ranking

**ID 24 · AVA 4 · Regular LI · MOV 10–10 cm · CC18 · BS12 · PH10 · WIP13 · ARM1 · BTS3 · W1 · S2 · Religious Troop.** Six standard MO profiles are available:

| Opt | Profile | Pts / SWC | Rating | Default selection |
|---:|---|---:|---|---|
| 5 | **FTO Hacking Device + Pitcher**, Combi, Nanopulser | **18 / 0.5** | **A+ linked FT2; A solo** | **Best overall**: economical network projection, standard Hacker and Fireteam enabler; generally 1 |
| 3 | **FTO Heavy Rocket Launcher**, Assault Pistol | **15 / 1.5** | **A− in FT2; B solo** | Excellent cheap ranged/template ARO, particularly beside Hacker; usually 0–1 |
| 15 | **Escort HRL + Neurocinetics + Auxbot**, Assault Pistol | **22 / 1.5** | **A−** | Independent Burst 2 reactive HRL with short-range peripheral screen; usually 0–1 |
| 16 | **Escort Hacker + Auxbot**, Combi, Nanopulser | **22 / 0.5** | **B** | Local defensive Hacker + template escort; no Pitcher, cannot link |
| 14 | **Escort Combi + Auxbot**, Nanopulser | **17 / 0** | **B** | Defensive/trade piece if Auxbot geometry matters; otherwise compare Raveneye/Peacemaker |
| 1 | **FTO basic Combi + Nanopulser** | **13 / 0** | **C+** | Cheap FT2 partner or filler, not the default passive Order purchase |

**Terminology is decisive:** FTO Order Sergeants are eligible for **Military Orders** or **Order Sergeants** teams. All three **Escort** profiles have a synchronized Auxbot but **no Fireteam access**. A Peripheral does not generate a second Regular Order or satisfy another Fireteam body.

## Best pick — FTO Hacking Device + Pitcher (18 / 0.5)

A remarkably efficient purchase in an army with real premium hacking and limited inexpensive Regular Orders:

- **Standard Hacking Device, WIP13, BTS3:** provides ordinary Hacking Device programs, including Spotlight, Oblivion and Carbonite against legal targets. Its Hacking Device is not a KHD or a replacement for De Fersen's anti-Hacker Trinity (+1SD).
- **Pitcher, Disposable (2):** projects a Deployable Repeater to extend the Hacking Area of every allied Hacker able to use that network. This is often the cheapest way to deliver **De Fersen's** high-quality programs toward the midfield without buying the **45pt Knight of Justice HD/Pitcher** just for network reach.
- **Fireteam role:** one actual FTO Sergeant qualifies as the required member of an Order Sergeants Duo/Haris. Two qualified same-unit or Counts-as models produce FT2, with +1SD on the Pitcher. This is the primary reason to take him **linked** rather than solo.
- **Backup tools:** a specialist Hacker, an ordinary pooled Regular Order, Combi Rifle and defensive Nanopulser. He is still **ARM1/W1**, not a disposable front-line gunfighter.

**Plan:** place the Sergeant where the Pitcher can reach a useful Repeater spot **from cover or without exposing him**, and let De Fersen, Santiago/Trinitarian KHD, or the Sergeant himself exploit targets in the resulting Hacking Area. A forward Repeater is not free: a successful launch consumes an Order, the ammunition is finite, and the Repeater can be removed or exploited by enemy hackers. Trinitarian Deployable Repeater/Minelayer, Peacemaker, Pathfinder, Fugazi and Tech-Bee are complementary nodes.

### Why an FT2 Duo is the key breakpoint

The N5.3 **FT Level 2** bonus is **BS Attack (+1 Success Die)**, not +1 Burst. With the **Burst 1** Pitcher, roll two dice and select one valid result; **only one Disposable use is consumed**, including when the first shot fails. The extra Success Die applies to eligible normal BS Attacks, **not to the Long Skill Speculative Attack**. A friendly model in the wrong Fireteam composition does not automatically grant FT2.

**Pinned N5.3 Pitcher range chart:** **0 MOD through 16 inches; −3 at 16–24; −6 at 24–32.** (Older comments suggesting a +3 band are not applicable to this pinned chart.)

| Direct-fire Pitcher distance | BS target | Solo success | FT2 +1SD success |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0–16 inches | 12 | 60% | **84%** |
| 16–24 inches | 9 | 45% | **69.75%** |
| 24–32 inches | 6 | 30% | **51%** |

Probabilities assume an unobstructed eligible **Normal Roll**, no other MODs, and successful placement if the roll passes. The +1SD probabilities are `1 − (1 − p)²`; they do not apply to Speculative Attack. These percentages quantify why **a 31–35pt FT2 Duo** can be worth its cost even without spending the Haris slot.

### Compared with other MO hacking options

| Option | Pts / SWC | When it is preferable |
|---|---:|---|
| **Sergeant FTO HD/Pitcher** | **18 / 0.5** | Cheapest linkable **hacking + ranged network projection** |
| Escort Sergeant Hacker/Auxbot | 22 / 0.5 | Defensive corner/short-range screening when Pitcher/FT bonuses are not required |
| Trinitarian KHD | 24 / 0 | Infiltrating Camouflage cyber assassin / specialist; cannot replace ordinary HD programs |
| Santiago FTO KHD/TinBot | 31 / 0 | Two-Wound team protection/Firewall and anti-Hacker threat; **not** ordinary HD or Pitcher |
| De Fersen HD + Trinity (+1SD) | 42 / 0.5 | Elite WIP14, two-Wound **main Hacker**; especially benefits from a cheap projected Repeater |
| Knight of Justice HD/Pitcher | 45 / 0.5 | Expensive, durable independent fighting Hacker/Wildcard; do **not** purchase solely to duplicate cheap Pitcher delivery |

**Default:** take one Sergeant FTO Hacker when the list intends to exploit Repeaters or when a cheap support Duo genuinely adds two useful functions. It is excellent but **not mandatory** in a list with little hacking demand, other repeaters already reaching objectives, or an SWC/Order budget under pressure.

## The best Hacker/Pitcher Duos

These use the dedicated **Order Sergeants Duo**, which requires **at least one actual FTO Order Sergeant**. Both Curator and Infirmarer **count as Order Sergeant in this particular chart row** (not universally). Thus they give **FT2** even though neither is actually an Order Sergeant model. No Core or Haris allowance is consumed.

| Partner of 18/0.5 Hacker | Total pts / SWC | FT level | Evaluation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **15/1.5 FTO HRL** | **33 / 2** | **FT2** | **A**: cheapest strong ARO + reliable direct-fire Pitcher; HRL also gets +1SD in ARO. High SWC cost. |
| **17/0 Curator Engineer, AP Mine** | **35 / 0.5** | **FT2** | **A+ for tech support**: network projection, actual Engineer/GizmoKit, D-Charges, mine and repair for nearby REM/TAG; WIP12. |
| **17/0 Infirmarer Doctor, Boarding Shotgun** | **35 / 0.5** | **FT2** | **A+ for infantry support**: reliable Pitcher, WIP13 Doctor/MediKit and stronger close-range counterplay; especially useful near valuable Knights. |
| **13/0 FTO basic Combi** | **31 / 0.5** | **FT2** | **B**: most economical access to +1SD, with a second Regular Order but little additional specialization. |
| 22/1.5 Black Friar HRL | **40 / 2** | **FT1** | **B− for Pitcher delivery**: MSV2/Albedo HRL is excellent in its own right, but Friar is **not** an Order Sergeant equivalent, so **no FT2 bonus** in this Duo. |

**The practical decision is what the other member does while the Hacker pitches:**

- **FTO HRL:** static DZ/safe-lane defence. The HRL attacks in reactive turn at standard Burst 1 **plus one Success Die** if FT2 remains active; this is **not** Neurocinetics Burst 2. ARO and Pitcher positions may diverge, so place the team to satisfy both.
- **Curator:** best with a TAG, REM or other serious repair target nearby. Option 2 with Deployable Cover is **18pts** (Duo **36/0.5**); armed-turret option is **20pts** (Duo **38/0.5**), not identical to the basic 17pt Engineer.
- **Infirmarer:** best beside Knights needing real Doctor recovery. Its Boarding Shotgun/Boarding Pistol supplies a credible close defence for a vulnerable Hacker. It should not be taken solely because it is a 17pt second body: ask whether there is a realistically recoverable patient.
- **Basic FTO:** fine when low points and zero extra SWC are the main constraint, but compare the +4pt Curator or Infirmarer upgrade with a mostly passive generic partner.

**Servant restriction:** A Curator or Infirmarer **in a Fireteam cannot simultaneously operate with a Palbot Peripheral (Servant)**. Do not write a misleading “linked Curator/Doctor with Palbot” package. When remote repair/healing is the actual plan, compare an independent support model with Palbot instead.

### Alternative: FTO HRL + another FTO Sergeant

The **HRL + 13pt Combi** Duo is **28pts/1.5 SWC, FT2**, giving the inexpensive launcher a reactive +1SD without the Hacker. This is a reasonable pure defensive option if the list already has its standard Hacker and network solved. But the extra 5pt Hacker upgrade usually adds enough utility to justify the 33pt **Hacker + HRL** combination when SWC allows.

## Standalone Escort Sergeant + Auxbot profiles

Escorts trade link eligibility and positional flexibility for a synchronized **Auxbot**. Controller and peripheral follow the applicable Synchronized/Peripheral declaration and coherency rules; **the Auxbot does not inherit Neurocinetics or a Fireteam bonus**.

### Neurocinetics HRL + Auxbot — 22 / 1.5 (A−)

**Strongest standalone Escort choice** when a safe long-lane ARO and a separate close-range template trap are useful:

- Neurocinetics grants the HRL's **full native Burst 2 in the Reactive Turn against one target**, at BS12 before range/cover/mimetism, but restricts the weapon to **B1 in the Active Turn**. Neurocinetics changes how native Burst is allocated; it does not itself add Burst.
- The Heavy Rocket Launcher punishes grouped attackers with its template and Continuous Damage potential. Even a cheaper defender can force an attacker to take an unpleasant engagement.
- The accompanying Auxbot's **Heavy Flamethrower** covers a nearby corner or approach, not the HRL's whole long fire lane. The Peripheral is another angle and another vulnerability, not a free separate Order or a linked model.
- Being **ARM1/W1 and lacking native Mimetism**, the Sergeant can lose straight shooting contests to elite active-turn guns and remains vulnerable to surprises, templates and close assault.

**Comparison:** versus **FTO HRL 15/1.5**, the Escort spends 7pts for **native reactive B2 plus Auxbot**, but loses FT eligibility. Versus **FTO HRL + Hacker Duo 33/2**, it saves 11pts and 0.5SWC but does not include a separate Hacker/Regular Order or reliable Pitcher. Versus **Black Friar HRL 22/1.5**, it prefers raw reactive Burst and template screening over **MSV2 + Albedo (−6)**.

**When to skip:** insufficient SWC, a protected Black Friar or linked HRL already doing the job, several other DZ deterrents, or a board where the opponent can avoid the lane entirely.

### Escort Hacker + Auxbot — 22 / 0.5 (B)

A real WIP13 Hacking Device with short-range Nanopulser and template-bearing bodyguard, but **no Pitcher and no link access**. Choose it for **local defensive hacking** in a critical corner, not for projected network offense. The controller cannot declare a Hacking Program while its synchronized Auxbot independently declares a Heavy Flamethrower BS Attack in the same Order/ARO; relevant Peripheral declarations must be synchronized.

**Default alternative:** the 18pt FTO Hacker is 4pts cheaper, has Pitcher, and enables a much better linked support package.

### Escort Combi + Auxbot — 17 / 0 (B)

A Regular Order with both Nanopulser and synchronized Heavy Flamethrower pressure. The 6pt premium over an 11pt Crosier can be justified if the flame angle stops a threatening attack route. Still compare **Raveneye 12pt predeployed mine/FO**, **Peacemaker 23–27pt forward Repeater/Auxbot**, and a Curator's mines/turret. It is not inherently a more efficient Order generator than the cheap Crosier or Tech-Bee.

## Good Haris and Core packages

An **Order Sergeants Haris** can be useful, but consumes MO's sole Haris allowance that might otherwise support **Teutonic/Santiago/Crux** or Hospitaller pressure. Do not spend that slot only because three models happen to fit. Unlike the dedicated Order Sergeants family, a **Military Orders Core** can contain FTO Sergeants, who **count as Crosiers for Fireteam level** in that formation; ensure the chart's required-choice condition is met.

| Team | Total | FT level | Reason to choose / limitation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Hacker18 + FTO HRL15 + Konstantinos FTO23**; Order Sergeants Haris | **56pts / 2 SWC** | **FT3** | Both FTO Sergeants and Konstantinos (Counts-as Order Sergeant) achieve FT3: projected hacking, ranged ARO and Disco Baller/mission utility. Different preferred positions and high SWC are real costs. |
| **Hacker18 + Bulleteer Spitfire22 + Curator17**; Order Sergeants Haris | **57pts / 1.5 SWC** | **FT2** | Mimetism (−6) Spitfire attacker, Pitcher/hacking and Engineer repair. Bulleteer does **not** count as Order Sergeant; thus FT2 from Hacker + Curator, not FT3. |
| **Crosier11 + Crosier Paramedic13 + Knight Commander LT(+1)24 + Hacker18 + FTO HRL15**; Military Orders Core | **81pts / 2 SWC** | **FT5** | Crosiers, Commander and FTO Sergeants count as Crosiers for Level calculation; defensive/command Core with full bonuses, hacker and HRL. Sacrifices some flexibility and commits the main Core slot. |

Other worthwhile compact arrangements: a **Hacker + Infirmarer or Curator Duo** beside an *independent* Seraph/Tikbalang, or **Hacker + HRL Duo** behind an offensive Knight Haris. A **Black Friar + Hacker** remains legal as an Order Sergeants Duo, but it is only **FT1**, so do not buy it on the false premise of a boosted Pitcher.

## Buying and listbuilding guidance

1. **Default first look:** **one FTO Hacker/Pitcher in a 31–35pt FT2 Duo**. Most interesting partners are **Curator** when the list has TAGs/REMs needing repair, **Infirmarer** when Knights need medical recovery, or **FTO HRL** when a defensive lane is missing and 2 SWC is affordable.
2. **Then assess ARO saturation:** the **22pt Neurocinetics HRL/Auxbot** is attractive when you need an independent defender; it is not automatically superior to the cheap linked HRL or 22pt Black Friar.
3. **Rarely take two separate Hackers only for redundancy:** choose a second one if list geometry and vulnerable premium Hackers justify it; otherwise spend on midfield Trinitarians, a long-range opener, defence, or Orders.
4. **Do not spam basic FTOs for the Order economy:** 13pt filler is useful when a specific link needs it, but the **11pt Crosier**, Tech-Bee, Fugazi or Mulebot generally buy a cheaper pooled Order or different utility.
5. **Preserve the Haris for high-impact combat** unless an Order Sergeant Haris genuinely needs synchronized movement to an objective and delivers several useful actions. A FT2 Duo already gets the key Pitcher bonus without consuming a contested formation slot.

**Bottom line:** The **18pt FTO Hacker/Pitcher + FT2 Duo** is the Order Sergeants' standout value proposition in MO. **Curator and Infirmarer both count as Order Sergeants in that Duo** and make a low-cost, multifunction package (without Palbots). Choose the **FTO HRL** for a cheap linked ARO, or the **Neurocinetics HRL Escort** for autonomous reactive Burst 2 plus an Auxbot. None is a blanket must-take; the right profile is the one that complements an otherwise complete MO list.
