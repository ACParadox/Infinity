# Clipper Dronbot — Military Orders (N5.3)

**Verdict: 16pt/1.5SWC Guided Clipper A-/B+ conditional in a serious pre-existing MO hacking build; C without network. The 19pt/1.5SWC non-Guided FTO(+1B) is C/skip.** Unlike Kestrel, MO can build De Fersen + linked Pitcher + turn-zero Trinitarian Repeater infrastructure that is independently worthwhile.

**AVA1, Regular REM:** MOV15-10, BS12, ARM0/BTS3/STR1, S3, Remote Presence/Courage. Neither profile joins MO Fireteams, despite FTO label.

| Profile | Pts/SWC | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| **Missile Launcher, Guided** | **16/1.5** | Attack eligible **Targeted** opponents with Guided (not (+1B)) |
| **FTO Missile Launcher (+1B)** | **19/1.5** | B2 direct missile, **not Guided**, no MO FT |

## Why MO is a plausible Guided faction

1. **De Fersen Hacking Device 42/0.5** is an apex hybrid Hacker with **Trinity(+1SD)** and standard HD programs including Spotlight, Carbonite and Oblivion. Useful with or without Clipper.
2. **Order Sergeant FTO HD/Pitcher 18/0.5** in a legal **FT2 Order Sergeants Duo** (e.g. plus **Infirmarer/Curator 17**) gets **+1 Success Die for eligible direct-fire Pitcher shots**, facilitating projection.
3. **Trinitarian FO + Deployable Repeater + Minelayer 23/1** establishes a legitimate **turn-zero** network node. Other Trinitarian FO/KHD profiles are *not* the same combined package.
4. **Peacemaker** Forward Deployment/native Repeater, **Fugazi**, **Pathfinder TA/FO/Sensor**, and **Mulebot basic Repeater** can complement those nodes if independently worthwhile.

That combination creates a genuine chance to Target enemies through **Spotlight** or **FO**, then threaten protected enemy models via Guided while the Knights exploit the opening. It is better than buying a network just to justify an otherwise weak missilebot.

## Targeting and Order limits

## What Guided still does, and why the setup matters

- **Potential upside:** attack a Targeted defender behind Total Cover without normal LoF; pressure static protected troops; punish clustered defenders with the legal Guided Blast/template mode.
- **Important restrictions:** requires a legal **Targeted** enemy, valid range and trajectory; attacks are subject to the N5 Guided rules, the target's defensive response, and the active-turn limit on Guided attacks. It is not an unrestricted table-wide auto-hit or a guarantee that a Targeted model dies.
- **Frequent practical result:** the army has no appropriate Targeted enemy, or getting one consumes too many valuable Orders. A dead or idle 16-point/1.5-SWC REM contributes little beyond its Regular Order.

The *threat* of Guided might force an opponent to spread out or address Repeaters, but in MO that is an incidental benefit, not a sound reason to buy the piece.

**Spotlight and FO are not guaranteed**: resolution and the opposing ARO/Saving Rolls matter. Hostile hackers can use your own Repeaters; Targeted enemies may Reset. Guided attacks have their normal active-turn limitations. MO also has costly Knights, main-range guns and limited pooled Orders. Keep the **1.5 SWC** opportunity cost in mind: do not sacrifice your only serious long-range active gun solely for Clipper.

## Why the 19pt FTO is still poor

The FTO (+1B) **is not Guided, nor legal in any MO Fireteam**. An unlinked, ARM0 BS12 B2 direct missilebot is rarely better than **Sierra Thunderbolt 18/0**, **Order Sergeant FTO HRL 15/1.5** (linked) or **Black Friar HRL 22/1.5** (visual-tech counter). No FTO bonus can be claimed without a legal FT row.

**Use Guided only when** De Fersen/Pitcher/Trinitarian infrastructure and target-setting Orders already exist, and an enemy behind Total Cover is otherwise difficult to dislodge. Otherwise **skip both Clipper profiles**. The MO uplift from Kestrel's C rating is a role-specific, unproven but coherent tactic—not an asserted win-rate fact.
