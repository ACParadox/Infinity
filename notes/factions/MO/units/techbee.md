# Tech-Bee — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: S-tier points/value in MO; near-auto-include as a 9-point Regular cheerleader with substantial utility, even without a TAG. With a same-Combat-Group Tikbalang or Seraph, she becomes an especially strong default.** Her primary selling point is *not* TAGCom alone: she costs **less than a basic 11-point Crosier** while bringing a pooled Regular Order, Specialist Operative, Flash Pulse, Adhesive Launcher Rifle, GizmoKit, Deactivator and three Deployable Repeaters. Take the cheap order infrastructure together with Fugazi/Mulebot/Warcor where slots and points allow; these units are complementary, **not mutually exclusive substitutions**.

**Source and scope:** This adapts the detailed [Kestrel Tech-Bee analysis](../../kestrel/units/techbee.md) to MO's sparse cheap Order floor, actual Fireteam options and TAGs. All profile details follow the [pinned MO N5.3 roster](../../../../sectorials/panoceania/military-orders.md#tech-bees). Ratings are tactical recommendations, not tournament win-rate measurements.

## Pinned MO profile

**Tech-Bees:** AVA **1**, **Regular LI**, **9 points / 0 SWC**, **MOV 10-10 cm (4-4")**, CC12, **BS11**, PH10, **WIP12**, ARM0, BTS3, W1, S2; **Specialist Operative, TAGCom [Dodge (PH+3), GizmoKit (PH+1)], Deactivator, GizmoKit**.

| Profile | Pts / SWC | Weapons/equipment | Fireteam |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Tech-Bee (only option)** | **9 / 0** | Adhesive Launcher Rifle, Flash Pulse, Deployable Repeater (Disposable 3), Pistol, PARA CCW (-3); GizmoKit, Deactivator | **None** |

She has no native Fireteam access and cannot contribute to Fireteam levels. TAGCom is passive and does not require proximity or Orders, but it only benefits eligible TAGs **in her own Combat Group** while she is on the table and non-Null.

## 1. The real MO value: a 9-point Regular Order that is also a specialist

Military Orders has expensive Knights, TAGs and comparatively few very cheap Regular troopers. An independently useful 9-point cheerleader is valuable *before* assuming any TAG synergy.

| Cheap MO option | Pts / SWC | Order | What it brings |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Warcor 360º** | **3 / 0** | Irregular normally; can be converted by Joan LT's Inspiring Leadership when eligible | WIP13 Flash Pulse ARO and wide rear/flank lookout; no Specialist |
| **Fugazi** | **7 / 0** | Regular | WIP13 Flash Pulse, Mimetism (-3), 6-6" MOV, always-on Repeater |
| **Basic Mulebot** | **8 / 0** | Regular | Baggage, Repeater, Deactivator; S4 and no Flash Pulse |
| **Tech-Bee** | **9 / 0** | **Regular** | **Specialist, Flash Pulse, Adhesive, GizmoKit, Deactivator, Deployable Repeater, TAGCom** |
| **Basic Crosier** | **11 / 0** | Regular | Combi Rifle, Religious Troop, **Fireteam membership** |
| **Raveneye** | **12 / 0** | Regular | FO, Flash, E/Marat, Minelayer with E/M Mine or Cybermine |
| **Crosier Specialist/Commlink** | **13 / 0** | Regular | Specialist Operative, Commlink (+1), **Fireteam membership** |

**Default comparison: Tech-Bee over an unlinked basic Crosier.** For **two fewer points**, Tech-Bee has a real specialist role and a striking amount of tech/defensive utility. Basic Crosier's advantages (Combi Rifle, ARM1, Religious Troop and ability to join a relevant Fireteam) rarely justify the premium when the troop's job is simply to generate an Order while staying protected.

**Important exception:** a basic Crosier can be worth far more than its printed price when it is a needed **Military Orders Fireteam member** or unlocks a useful **Fireteam level**. Tech-Bee cannot fill that seat. A second or third Crosier can therefore be right in a particular Core, even while Tech-Bee is the stronger **independent** cheap Order purchase.

**Warcor is not the alternative to Tech-Bee.** You can select **both**, plus Fugazi and Mulebots. Joan LT makes Warcor's personal Irregular Order potentially Regular via Inspiring Leadership; this is an excellent *additional* reason to take Warcor, not a reason to omit the already-Regular Tech-Bee. Without Joan, the 360º Warcor still earns consideration as a 3pt DZ sentry rather than a pooled-order source. See [MO Warcor analysis](warcor.md).

### The cheap foundation

| Component | Points | Native Regular Orders |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fugazi (AVA1) | 7 | 1 |
| Mulebot (basic) | 8 | 1 |
| Mulebot (basic) | 8 | 1 |
| **Tech-Bee** | **9** | **1** |
| **Total** | **32** | **4** |

**32 points / 0 SWC buys four Regular Orders**, a Specialist, multiple Repeaters, two Baggage bodies, Flash Pulse, GizmoKit and Deactivators. Add a 3-point 360º Warcor as an independent rear defender for **35 points / five troopers**; it normally adds an **Irregular** Order, but Joan LT can change that under Inspiring Leadership.

This is a **starting menu, not four mandatory purchases**. Two S4 Mulebots can congest a TAG-heavy deployment zone; Combat Group/trooper caps, protection, mission needs and actual useful Order consumers still matter. Fugazi/Tech-Bee are especially appealing first pickups; the second Mulebot is the easiest part of the package to make conditional.

## 2. TAGCom: turns an already-good pick into a near-automatic one

A non-Null Tech-Bee on the table gives friendly **TAGs in her Combat Group**:

- **Dodge (PH+3)**, a passive boost against attacks/templates where Dodge is preferable to shooting or accepting a hit.
- **GizmoKit (PH+1)**, improving the recipient TAG's PH when resolving a GizmoKit repair, whether Tech-Bee or another GizmoKit carrier supplies it.

| MO TAG | Printed Dodge target | With TAGCom Dodge (+3) | Normal-roll Dodge chance before other MODs |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Tikbalang (68/1.5)** | 11 | **14** | **55% → 70%** |
| **Seraph (72–74/1.5)** | 12 | **15** | **60% → 75%** |

These are individual unopposed Dodge roll odds, **not** probabilities of winning a Face-to-Face roll. The TAGs have printed Dodge overrides (Tikbalang PH=11 and Seraph PH=12), so TAGCom's +3 applies to those targets, **not** to their base PH15/PH16. Against ordinary -3 mine/template Dodge modifiers, TAGCom can cancel that penalty. Other modifiers and enemy successes still apply. Both TAGs have printed GizmoKit (PH=13), improved to 14 by TAGCom. Sources: [pinned MO roster](../../../../sectorials/panoceania/military-orders.md), [Seraph N5 profile](https://human-sphere.com/Seraph).

**Seraph:** PH15 Dodge with TAGCom is useful for a big S7 model facing dense-board threats and templates. It helps both the MULTI HMG's repositioning and the AP Spitfire/Auxbot's closer-range play. TAGCom does not address the Seraph's vulnerability to a good hacking attack or improve offensive shooting.

**Tikbalang:** PH14 Dodge with TAGCom complements Mimetism (-3), Climbing Plus and an aggressive HMG. Because Tikbalang is only 68 points but still critical to the plan, a passive 9pt support model offers excellent insurance.

**Double-Seraph experiment:** if both TAGs share Tech-Bee's Combat Group, one Tech-Bee buffs **both**. The underlying double-TAG list is still extremely expensive; this is not an argument that two Seraphs are automatically optimal.

**Essential positioning:** Group 1 TAG + Group 2 Tech-Bee receives **no TAGCom**. Put Tech-Bee in the TAG's group whenever you are buying her for that bonus; hide her behind terrain and preserve her. No need to follow the TAG into danger.

TAGCom applies to **TAGs**, not a Sierra, Pathfinder, Peacemaker, Bulleteer or other non-TAG REM.

## 3. Adhesive Launcher Rifle: credible cheap deterrence

Tech-Bee's **Adhesive Launcher Rifle** is **Burst 2 in the Active Turn / Burst 1 in ARO**, with **PARA (PH-6)** rather than ordinary damage. Successful hits demand a PH-6 Saving Roll; failure produces Immobilized-A, irrespective of the target's ARM/BTS. It is not a conventional lethal gun.

| Range | MOD | BS target before other MODs |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0–8" | 0 | 11 |
| **8–16"** | **+3** | **14** |
| 16–24" | -3 | 8 |
| 24–32" | -3 | 8 |

An uncontested PARA hit against PH10 produces an **80%** failure chance on the target's PH-6 roll; PH12 gives **70%**, PH15 gives **55%**. These percentages **exclude hit probability and Face-to-Face interaction**. PARA is not effective against targets lacking a PH Attribute.

**MO positioning:** put Tech-Bee in cover on a **short-to-mid DZ entrance, flank or TAG/Engineer retreat route**. An attacker attempting to clear your rear may need to risk being Immobilized by a nine-point trooper. This is an *upside* to preserving a cheap Order, not justification to expose an ARM0 W1 support model to a heavy gun.

## 4. Flash Pulse and defensive overlap

Flash Pulse rolls on **WIP12**, not BS11, reaching **WIP15 in its +3 range band (8–24")** before other MODs. A hit followed by a failed BTS Saving Roll causes **Stunned**, preventing Attacks and penalizing other rolls while the state lasts; the state does not invalidate the triggering Order retroactively.

- **Fugazi** is a superior dedicated Flash Pulse ARO: **WIP13**, Mimetism (-3), faster movement and an **always-on Repeater**.
- **Warcor 360º** is a superior *3pt rear-arc lookout* for Parachutists/Combat Jump approaches, though normally Irregular.
- **Tech-Bee** is the superior **9pt specialist/support-order toolbox** with a meaningful Adhesive threat.

These are three distinct cheap defensive angles. **Taking Warcor or Fugazi does not reduce the value of Tech-Bee's Regular Order or specialist capability.** Deploy them in *different* lanes rather than exposing all three to the same long-range attacker.

## 5. GizmoKit: valuable emergency repairs, not a Curator replacement

GizmoKit can recover allied **STR-based** TAGs/REMs. In Silhouette contact, the recipient makes its PH roll without an initial shooting roll; at range, Tech-Bee must first hit with the GizmoKit and then the **target** rolls PH. A failure **inflicts a Wound** instead of repairing one, so damaged targets can be lost to an attempted fix. Remote Presence recovery follows the N5.3 rules for Unconscious STR targets.

Useful targets in MO include **Tikbalang, Seraph, Sierra, Pathfinder, Fugazi, Peacemaker, Bulleteer and Mulebot** if accessible. Sometimes the nine-point specialist can get an important REM working again without dedicating a full Engineer activation route.

**Crucial MO distinction:** The standard PanOceanian **Machinist is not on the pinned MO roster**. MO's real Engineer is the **Military Order Curator (17–20 points)**, optionally using a **Palbot (3 points)** when not keeping the Curator in a Fireteam. A genuine Engineer can handle Engineer-cancelable states such as **Isolated** and **Immobilized-B**; Tech-Bee's GizmoKit does **not** confer the Engineer Skill.

For a centerpiece TAG, a Curator + Palbot remains the proper *planned* recovery option if a safe repair route exists. Tech-Bee is **additive**, both as a cheap Order and as backup recovery. Do not replace essential Engineer coverage with the Tech-Bee merely because she has GizmoKit.

**Fireteam trade-off:** MO Curator can join Military Orders or Order Sergeants Fireteams, but controlling a **Peripheral (Servant) Palbot** prevents Fireteam membership. Decide between linked, forward Curator support and an independent Curator/Palbot recovery route; do not assume both simultaneously.

## 6. Deployable Repeater and Deactivator

Tech-Bee has **Deployable Repeater (Disposable 3)**. This is **not a carried always-on Repeater or FastPanda**. The repeater must be placed with **Place Deployable**, in Silhouette contact (or a legal point on her Move route), and begins functioning at the **conclusion of that Order**. Afterward it projects its Repeater area for relevant allied Hackers, including Hackers in a different Combat Group.

MO has real ways to capitalize on it: **De Fersen's HD/Trinity, Order Sergeant FTO HD/Pitcher, Santiago FTO KHD, Knight of Justice HD and Trinitarian KHD**. The repeater is worthwhile when its final **location** exposes a consequential target or defends a lane; placing it casually in an already-covered DZ often adds little.

A forward enemy Hacker can also exploit the network, so avoid leaving an unnecessarily dangerous reciprocal hacking route.

### Coordinated Orders: useful extra when already placing deployables

The Kestrel analysis identifies an efficient N5.3 interaction: **eligible independent Regular troops in the same Combat Group** can use **Move + Place Deployable** together in a Coordinated Order, each placing different legal equipment. MO examples:

| Participant | Deployable |
|---|---|
| **Tech-Bee** | Deployable Repeater |
| **Raveneye** | E/M Mine or Cybermine, depending on profile |
| **Independent Curator** | AP Mine or Armed Turret, depending on profile |
| **Other eligible unlinked Regular deployable user** | Its own eligible deployable |

The coordinated action costs **one Regular Order plus one Command Token**, rather than separate Orders for every participant. Every participant must follow the same Skill sequence and their individual placement rules. **Current Fireteam members cannot coordinate**, and **a Curator controlling a Palbot cannot participate** due to the Peripheral rules. The deployed pieces become active only after the Order concludes. This is an **opportunistic bonus**, not a reason to spend a Command Token just to place one repeater.

**Deactivator** can remove eligible revealed enemy deployables or reactivate an existing allied Disco Ball when the relevant rules and positioning permit. In MO, the latter can occasionally interact with **Konstantinos FTO** or a **Crusader Disco Baller**. Do not assume Tech-Bee can efficiently follow a fast forward Fireteam for repeated Disco activation: she moves 4-4" and is fragile.

## 7. Group placement, listbuilding and real reasons to skip

| Situation | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Seraph/Tikbalang in Group 1** | **Put Tech-Bee in Group 1** for TAGCom; hide and preserve her |
| **No TAG; Group 1 needs cheap Regular Orders** | **Strong default Group 1 purchase** at 9pts |
| **Group 1 already full; Group 2 has useful activations** | **Excellent Group 2 support specialist**, especially to deploy a repeater, clear a deployable or rescue a REM |
| **Joan LT plus Warcor** | **Take both if useful.** Inspiring Leadership can improve Warcor's Order economy; Tech-Bee remains an independent Regular Order |
| **Crosier required to reach a meaningful Fireteam level** | **Take the Crosier for that role**; it is not a pure cheerleader comparison |
| **15-trooper cap or scarce safe deployment positions** | Spend the slot on the most essential mission/attack function; cheap Orders are not free |
| **No useful extra Order consumer or no remaining list budget** | Tech-Bee becomes optional, though her 9pt price keeps that threshold high |

**Do not mistake the 32pt four-Regular foundation for an automatically legal single combat group** when combined with the rest of an elite army; group placement, total troopers and desired Orders for the main attacker still govern.

For an MO list with no TAG, the normal priority is **Fugazi + Tech-Bee**, **one Mulebot** when the Order/Baggage is useful, a **second Mulebot** when deployment space and actual need justify it, and **Warcor separately** as a cheap rear ARO (particularly attractive with Joan). **Raveneye** is also a high-quality 12pt defensive specialist, but it is not an either/or replacement for Tech-Bee.

## Final rating

| Role | MO rating | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Cheap pooled Regular Order** | **S** | Only 9pts; undercuts 11pt unlinked Crosier |
| **Utility per point** | **S** | Specialist + Flash + Adhesive + GizmoKit + Deactivator + Repeater |
| **Specialist / objective backup** | **A** | Genuine Specialist Operative but WIP12, 4-4" and fragile |
| **Short-lane defensive deterrence** | **B+** | Adhesive/Flash can punish valuable attackers; not reliable primary ARO |
| **Hacking-network extension** | **B / conditional** | Needs deployment Orders, legal geometry and useful allied Hackers |
| **Emergency STR recovery** | **B+ / conditional** | Useful backup, not Engineer state-clearing or guaranteed rescue |
| **Same-group TAG support** | **S** | Passive PH+3 Dodge and PH+1 GizmoKit |
| **Fireteam contribution** | **None** | Cannot unlock/boost an MO Fireteam level |
| **Overall MO listbuilding** | **S value; near-auto-include** | Good even with no TAG; especially strong with Tikbalang/Seraph |

**Selection rule:** **Default to taking the AVA1 Tech-Bee in MO.** She is one of the sectorial's best non-Fireteam cheerleaders, and her specialist/technical capabilities make her more useful than an unlinked basic Crosier for two fewer points. Choose basic Crosiers to make a legal/useful **Fireteam**, not merely to buy another protected Regular Order when Tech-Bee is still available. Include **Fugazi, Mulebots and Warcor alongside her** where they earn their own points and slots. A TAG makes an already excellent purchase even better.
