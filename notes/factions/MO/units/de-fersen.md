# Father-Officer Gabriele De Fersen — Military Orders (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A+ as a protected, network-enabled combat Hacker; A as a linked dual-role Spitfire gunfighter; not a universal must-take.** De Fersen is MO's standout **WIP14 full Hacking Device + Trinity (+1 Success Die)** hybrid: he combines ordinary HD control (Oblivion/Carbonite/Spotlight and applicable anti-TAG programs) with unusually threatening **anti-Hacker Trinity in both active and reactive turns**. At **42/0.5** he is already a real BS14, two-Wound ARM4/BTS6 Knight. The **46/1.5 Spitfire** is a serious alternative—not an automatic mistake—when the same model must also provide sustained midrange active-turn shooting. Either profile benefits from a TinBot-protected legal Fireteam and a deliberate Repeater network; neither is mandatory in a list that doesn't exploit hacking.

## Why he is exceptional: full HD control plus anti-Hacker killing

- **Against enemy Hackers:** Trinity **(+1SD)** is the reason De Fersen is not simply a durable ordinary HD. The normal Trinity attack receives an extra **Success Die**, including in ARO. For standard B3 active/B1 reactive Trinity this means **roll four and retain up to three in active, roll two and retain one in reactive**, subject to the actual program and MODs. It does **not** add an extra Burst hit and is **not** a Fireteam shooting bonus.
- **Against other Hackable threats:** the full HD retains **Oblivion/Carbonite** to impair Hackable HI/TAG/REM targets and **Spotlight** to mark appropriate targets; **Total Control** can threaten TAGs under its own conditions. The model is useful even when there is no opposing Hacker to Trinity.
- **Survivability:** WIP14 + BTS6 + ARM4 + two Wounds is a substantial improvement over cheap one-Wound support Hackers. **TinBot Firewall (-3)** on a legitimate teammate makes the cyber duel much less appealing for the opponent, and his upgrade matters on the reactive turn. This is deterrence, **not immunity**: a dedicated KHD attacking from a safe Repeater can still beat or cripple him.
- **Physical threat:** BS14 MULTI Rifle or Spitfire, MA3, CC22, AP+Shock CCW and 15–5 cm MOV mean the same hacker can help force a midfield approach. **Do not value him only as a passive DZ Hacker**, but do not voluntarily send a 42–46pt specialist into a needless melee.
- **Mission utility:** Hacker is a valid specialist type for objectives that accept Hackers or generic Specialists; **he is not printed as Specialist Operative** and cannot be assumed to satisfy an FO/Doctor/Engineer-only objective.

**Critical distinction:** FT2's **BS Attack (+1SD)** improves his *eligible BS Attacks*, **not hacking programs**. His Trinity bonus is printed on his own profile and operates whether or not he has FT2; the value of linking him is protection, movement efficiency, and better rolled shooting.

### High-value use pattern

Use a **Turn-0/early network** (Trinitarian FO + Deployable Repeater/Minelayer, or Peacemaker), **projected Pitcher** (18pt FTO Order Sergeant, or 45pt KoJ only if its other combat features are needed), or **mobile native Repeater** (Pathfinder/Fugazi), while De Fersen stays relatively safe. Force an enemy Hacker to accept Trinity, disable exposed HI/TAG/REMs with ordinary HD programs, or use Spotlight with a **real follow-up**, optionally Guided Clipper. Reposition De Fersen physically only when that creates an advantage or delivers a mission action; his own Fireteam is not a substitute for network coverage.

**Avoid excess infrastructure:** a 42pt Hacker does not automatically justify *both* expensive premium Pitcher carrier and Clipper, plus multiple redundant hackers, if the mission/opponent does not reward those tools. A 23pt Trinitarian Deployable Repeater/Minelayer costs **1 SWC**; the 18pt Sergeant Pitcher costs **0.5 SWC**; Guided Clipper 16pt costs **1.5 SWC**. Price the **whole** package, including actual Orders spent placing/reloading Pitchers. The Clipper **FTO +1B** option is a different, *non-Guided* profile and **cannot link in MO**.

**Firewall and Fairy Dust:** a same-team TinBot Firewall (-3) is the best local protection. EVO **Fairy Dust** is a valid alternative/broad protective layer when the army contains numerous HI, especially beyond that one Fireteam, but **do not stack Fairy Dust's protection with Firewall (-3) as though both modifiers add together**; compare the applicable defensive effects under current rules.

## MULTI Rifle/LFT versus Spitfire — the real decision

| Decision dimension | **42/0.5 MULTI Rifle + LFT** | **46/1.5 Spitfire** |
|---|---|---|
| Primary role | Elite Hacker plus *secondary* gun/close defense | Elite Hacker plus credible *primary or secondary* active-turn midrange gun |
| Shooting | Versatile MULTI Rifle ammunition; less sustained active Burst | B4 Spitfire at BS14; FT2 rolls **five Success Dice, retains up to four**, not five effective hits |
| Defensive options | **Light Flamethrower** offers Direct Template/fork and protection against nearby threats | **Loses the Light Flamethrower**; relies more on gun and positioning |
| SWC economy | **0.5** preserves 1 SWC for HMG/ML/other packages | **1.5**, competes directly with PanO's many other midrange/long-range guns |
| Best army shell | Separate Hospitaller HMG, Seraph/Tik, Joan, Sepulchre or other dedicated opener | Small link with no other strong midrange attacker; De Fersen intentionally spends multiple Orders shooting |
| Weakness | May not offer enough repeat shooting if the rest of the list lacks guns | No special visual MOD/MSV or AP Spitfire; still no answer to long HMG/sniper lanes by itself |

**Practical preference: not dogmatic.** Earlier default preference for the MULTI Rifle is defensible in a *hacker-led* MO list, but the current commentary does **not** support dismissing Spitfire as a poor profile: **Robert Shepherd explicitly prefers the Spitfire** for only +4pts once the army is already investing in De Fersen. Its **+1 SWC**, loss of the flamethrower and redundancy with existing Spitfires are nevertheless real constraints.

**Select Spitfire** when (a) he is the intended active-turn gunfighter for 8–24in engagements, (b) his Fireteam can deliver FT2 shooting, and (c) you can afford the SWC without losing a more needed long-range opener/anti-Mimetism piece. **Select MULTI Rifle** when he is the dedicated cyber centerpiece behind another HMG/TAG/Joan/KoJ, the LFT's area denial matters, or SWC is tight. The Spitfire is a **role decision**, not an unconditional upgrade for four points.

## Legal Fireteam use: anchor requirements and FT levels

De Fersen is **Wildcard (AVA1, max one); counts as Knight Hospitaller** for same-unit/Counts-as Fireteam level. Wildcard eligibility does **not** waive a native Fireteam requirement:

- **Crusade Duo/Haris/Core:** needs **at least one actual Hospitaller FTO**. De Fersen alone with Hawkins/Joan/Justice cannot found it merely because all count as Hospitallers. With a Hospitaller FTO, De Fersen contributes toward **FT2**, and with Hawkins/Joan/Justice can form **FT3**.
- **Military Orders Duo/Haris/Core:** needs an actual required-choice **Crosier, Teutonic or Santiago FTO** under the pinned chart. With a **Teutonic TinBot19 + De Fersen42**, only De Fersen counts as Hospitaller, therefore this is **FT1**, not FT2. Add a **KoJ/Hawkins** (also counts Hospitaller) to reach **FT2**.
- **Order Sergeants Duo/Haris:** needs **at least one actual Order Sergeant FTO**. FTO Sergeant + De Fersen is a legal support concept but remains **FT1**, since De Fersen counts as Hospitaller, *not* Order Sergeant. A second actual/equivalent Sergeant can supply FT2.
- **Velox:** the pinned Wildcard row looks broad, but the Velox required-choice and specialized movement role still apply; do **not** assume he grants the team an extra bike-level movement rate.
- **No blanket FT5** from five mixed Knights: count the valid same-unit/Counts-as members. FT2 adds BS Attack (+1SD), FT3 adds Discover/Dodge bonuses, FT4 +1 BS and FT5 Sixth Sense, per the pinned MO chart.

## Lieutenant: viable but not the default economy plan

Both LT loadouts cost exactly their regular variant; taking the LT role buys **no extra LT Order and no additional SWC**. The risk is high because De Fersen wants to be both an **active Hacker** and possibly an advancing shooter, making him a natural target for enemy KHD/Oblivion/Isolation.

- **Usual alternative:** **Knight Commander Lieutenant (+1 Order)24** in a protected defensive team plus a relevant **NCO** (Seraph, Tik, Holy Sepulchre AP HMG, or Teutonic Spitfire). This is better command/Order economy when you intend to use the NCO.
- **Viable aggressive plan:** De Fersen LT (same 42/.5 or 46/1.5) plus **Knight Commander Chain of Command23** separately hidden/protected. A concrete played list used **De Fersen MULTI Rifle LT + Hospitaller HMG/TinBot**, with Commander CoC elsewhere. Your whole command insurance costs 23 extra points, and the CoC should not share the attack route.
- **When LT can make sense:** a chosen active-LT battle plan with separate CoC and no strong reason to buy the 24pt LT(+1) NCO engine. **Do not take LT solely because it has no price premium.**

## When he earns inclusion, and when to skip

| List / matchup | Take? | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Multiple enemy Hackable attackers/Hackers expected; Trinitarian Repeater/Pitcher support present | **A+** | Hybrid Trinity and ordinary HD threats both become active; Firewall makes counterattack expensive |
| Compact protected Crusade Duo already wants to advance | **A+** | Strong model/Fireteam fit; MULTI for hacking support or Spitfire if expected to shoot |
| HMG-led Crusade and hacking mission objectives | **A+ MULTI** | HMG clears first lanes; 42/.5 controls hacking and has LFT short-range defense |
| Solo with meaningful Repeater coverage | **A− / A** | Retains full HD+Trinity threat but forgoes TinBot and FT2 gun reliability |
| Solo with no network and no plan to bring him forward | **B** | Paying 42pts for remote passive hacking that may never see relevant targets |
| TAG/NCO spearhead needs Orders, specialists and cheap support; little cyber value expected | **Skip is sensible** | 42–46pt elite sidecar can remove more necessary scorer/order infrastructure than it adds |
| Already bought KoJ45 HD/Pitcher, Santiago KHD/TinBot and other cyber defense | **Reassess** | More cybersecurity is sometimes useful, but mission and enemy match may not justify overlapping premium costs |
| Opponent mostly unhackable and mission has limited Hacker scoring | **Usually skip** | Ordinary HD still can Spotlight applicable targets, but limited high-value cyber interactions make points hard to recoup |

**Recommended default for MO:** **one** De Fersen **42/0.5 MULTI Rifle/LFT** when building a serious protected cyber network (e.g., **Hospitaller TinBot27 + De Fersen42 = 69/0.5 FT2**), with cheap Pitcher/Trinitarian/Pathfinder support. **Upgrade deliberately to 46/1.5 Spitfire** when De Fersen must also be a primary **8–24in active shooter**; the Shepherd recommendation and a Tactical Awareness Spitfire LT example make this a real A-tier alternative, not a profile to dismiss.

**Bottom line:** **not mandatory**; De Fersen is an elite hybrid Hacker whose premium is worth paying only when his **full HD, +1SD Trinity, durability and linked combat utility** all have a real job. Choose **MULTI for a hacker-first role, Spitfire for an intentional hacker-plus-gunfighter role**; give either a legal Firewall escort and inexpensive network reach instead of treating him as an isolated expensive trooper.
