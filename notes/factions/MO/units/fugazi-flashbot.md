# Fugazi Dronbot — Military Orders (N5.3)

**Verdict: S, near-default 7-point/0SWC Regular Order + Flash Pulse + Repeater.** MO Fugazi has precisely the same AVA1, profile and **no Fireteam access** as Kestrel; MO's pricey Knights make cheap pooled Orders even more valuable.

**Profile:** REM, MOV15-15, WIP13, ARM0/BTS3/STR1, S3, Remote Presence, Courage, Mimetism(-3), Flash Pulse and Repeater. It is neither a specialist nor a hard gun.

## Why it is a near-default cheerleader

- **7 points buys one pooled Regular Order** in its own Combat Group, so the value does not depend on spending that Order on Fugazi.
- **Flash Pulse ARO** is an additional nonlethal threat at useful distances, especially against an attacker that would otherwise face only one dangerous ARO.
- **Mimetism (-3)** makes a conventional BS Attack against it less reliable unless the attacker has appropriate counters. This helps it survive, but ARM0 and a single Wound make it fragile.
- **Repeater** contributes to the entire army's hacking network even while Fugazi is parked defensively.
- **6-6" mobility** lets it change Repeater coverage, reposition after a flank opens, or make a last-turn sacrifice without buying a dedicated mobile hacking piece.

**Do not confuse cost-efficiency with compulsory inclusion.** It still consumes a Combat Group slot and needs a reasonably safe S3 deployment space. Taking its Order only matters when the relevant group can use another Order productively.

## Flash Pulse: legitimate secondary deployment-zone defence

Flash Pulse uses **WIP13** rather than Fugazi's **BS8**. Its **B1** is not impressive in an active-turn duel, but it makes a cheap reactive check against attackers.

| Range | Flash Pulse MOD | Fugazi target number before other MODs |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0–8" | 0 | 13 |
| 8–16" | +3 | 16 |
| 16–24" | +3 | 16 |
| 24–32" | -3 | 10 |

On a successful Flash Pulse hit followed by a failed **BTS** Saving Roll, the target enters **Stunned State**. Stunned prevents declarations of Attacks and imposes **-3 on other Rolls** (except Saving Rolls), potentially disrupting an expensive attack run. **It does not retroactively cancel the Order that caused it.** The state also **automatically cancels at the start of the States Phase of the Player Turn in which it was caused**; Doctor/Engineer can remove it earlier under the appropriate rules. Do **not** describe it as a permanent disable.

**Best deployment:** protect the Order first, then choose a partially concealed lane covering a side approach, DZ entrance, or path to a valuable ARO/command piece. A Fugazi watching a different angle from a Sierra, linked sniper or other meaningful ARO is more useful than exposing it to the same long HMG lane. It can force a hostile attacker to consider a BTS roll in addition to the primary firefight.

**Weaknesses:** one B1 Flash Pulse ARO generally loses many direct fights against effective multi-Burst active guns; it does not protect blind corners without LoF and is no substitute for a template guard, mine, or serious long-range ARO. Avoid handing the opponent a free 7-point kill and shrinking your future Order pool merely for a speculative long-distance Flash Pulse.

## MO's network use

MO's **De Fersen HD + Trinity(+1SD)**, **FTO Order Sergeant HD/Pitcher**, **Knight of Justice HD**, **Trinitarian pre-positioned Deployable Repeaters** and **Peacemakers** support meaningful hacking. Fugazi is an economical extra defensive/mobile Repeater. It doesn't automatically mark enemies, and opposing Hackers can exploit your network as well. Avoid pushing it several Orders forward unless that unlocks a specific hack.

**Compare:** Fugazi 7 vs **Mulebot Baggage 8**, **Tech-Bee 9**, **Crosier 11** or **Raveneye 12**. All can provide one Regular Order except the additional Palbot, which gives none. Crosier is the required kind of Fireteam member; Fugazi cannot substitute. Tech-Bee has TAGCom/specialist; Raveneye predeployed mine/FO.

**Default:** place the one available Fugazi safely in the Combat Group whose attack/mission pool most needs another Order, cover an incidental Flash Pulse/AD lane, keep Repeater positioning deliberate. In MO an active Hacking Device can make its 7pt Repeater particularly valuable.
