# Bulleteer Armbots — Military Orders (N5.3)

**Verdict: Spitfire 22pt/1SWC A+; Heavy Shotgun (+1B) 19pt/0SWC A.** Fast Mimetism(-6) attack REM, with compelling **Velox** and **Order Sergeant** Fireteam options absent from its Kestrel ARMORED role. No dedicated Bulleteer unit note exists in the Kestrel/units source directory, so this MO note uses the pinned rosters instead.

**Stats:** AVA2, REM Regular, MOV15-10, BS12, WIP13, ARM1/BTS3/STR1, S4, Repeater, Remote Presence, Courage, Mimetism(-6).

| Profile | Pts/SWC | Best reason |
|---|---:|---|
| **Heavy Shotgun (+1B)** | **19/0** | Close corner assault and zero-SWC gun |
| **Spitfire** | **22/1** | Good sustained medium-range Mimetism(-6) fire |

**Profile correction:** The MO pinned HSG option had omitted **(+1B)**; corrected to match the already-correct Kestrel roster and [published N5 Bulleteer profile](https://human-sphere.com/Bulleteer). It is a printed weapon bonus, not an extra FT bonus.

**Rules:** Velox has max2 Bulleteers. Order Sergeants Duo/Haris has **max1 Bulleteer** and **requires an actual Order Sergeant FTO**; Curator/Infirmarer count as Order Sergeants in this formation. **Bulleteer does not.** FT2 grants **+1 Success Die** on eligible BS attacks, **not +1 Burst**. MO has no Kestrel ARMORED Tikbalang+Bulleteer Duo, and no TAG Fireteam.

## Use and counters

**Spitfire:** cheaper than a Knight gunfighter, Mimetism(-6) and good for medium-range exploitation after a TAG/HMG clears long lanes. **HSG(+1B):** a potent short-range, 0SWC backup; pairing with Spitfire creates complementary range coverage in the same Velox FT2. The two REMs are still vulnerable to templates, hacking, S4 path/cover geometry and one-Structure fragility.

**EVO Assisted Fire** adds Marksmanship to a selected Bulleteer, but **the same EVO Hacker cannot simultaneously sustain Fairy Dust** for all HI. In HI-heavy MO with many exposed Knights, Fairy Dust can be more valuable. A linked **Curator** can repair a nearby Bulleteer, but cannot also have a Palbot.

**Skip** when main TAG + expensive Knight link already monopolizes attack Orders, you need a specialist more than another gun, or a turn-zero mine/Repeater HFT defense would be better achieved by a **Peacemaker**.
