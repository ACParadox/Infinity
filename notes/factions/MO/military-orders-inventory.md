## Sectorial Inventory

### Lieutenant (LT)
- **Teutonic LT 18/0:** cheap durable commander; Impetuous exposure is the risk.
- **Knight Commander LT 20/0 or LT(+1 Order) 24/0:** Holomask; +1 is worthwhile when NCO will spend both Special Orders.
- **Joan of Arc / Joan v2 LT:** powerful fighting/Inspiring Leadership alternatives; v2 AP Spitfire LT 45/1, with distinct command-upgrade loadouts.

### Chain of Command (CoC)
- **Knight Commander CoC 23/0:** protected successor for a fighting LT; not the same profile as Commander LT(+1).
- **Joan CoC alternative:** expensive distinct choice; cannot simultaneously use the LT version's abilities.

### NCO and Force Multipliers
- **Holy Sepulchre AP HMG 53/1:** independent NCO long-range attack.
- **Seraph TAG 72–74/1.5:** NCO and Tactical Awareness; MULTI HMG or AP Spitfire+Auxbot are different packages.
- **Tikbalang HMG 68/1.5:** **Tactical Awareness only (no NCO)**, Mimetism(-3), Climbing Plus; cannot spend Knight Commander LT Orders.
- **Teutonic NCO Spitfire 29/1.5:** premium over the low-price utility profiles.
- **Tech-Bee TAGCom 9/0:** supports a same-Combat-Group TAG; not an Engineer or extra pooled Order.

### Active Gunfighters
- **Heavy solo:** Holy Sepulchre AP HMG 53/1, Tikbalang HMG 68/1.5, Seraph MULTI HMG 74/1.5.
- **Shorter-range TAG:** Seraph AP Spitfire+Auxbot 72/1.5.
- **Knight of Justice:** Spitfire 42/1.5, ML(+1SD) 46/1.5, FO MMR 41/0.
- **Crux FTO:** MMR(+1SD) 46/0 or Spitfire 49/1.5; neither is the Hidden profile.
- **Hospitaller FTO HMG 32/1.5:** linked two-Wound long gun.
- **Crosier MSV2 HMG 24/1; Black Friar Thunderbolt(+1B) 24/0:** economical target-specific visor answers.

### ARO
- **Knight of Justice ML(+1SD) 46/1.5:** elite two-Wound missile anchor.
- **Order Sergeant FTO HRL 15/1.5; Black Friar HRL 22/1.5:** cheap linkable gun versus MSV2/Albedo option.
- **Escort Sergeant Neuro HRL+Auxbot 22/1.5:** independent Peripheral defense, not FTO/linkable.
- **Trinitarian camo/Hidden Deployment MSR 25/1.5:** optional genuinely Hidden sniper ambush; hiding withholds its Regular Order until reveal (Army screenshot checked 23 Nov 2026).
- **Crosier E/M Minelayer MSR 19/1; Sierra TR Thunderbolt 18/0 or HMG 23/1:** defensive alternative lanes.

### Midfield Defense and Forward Scoring
- **Trinitarian FO 22/0, KHD 24/0, FO/Deployable Repeater Minelayer 23/1:** separate Camouflage + Hidden Deployment + Infiltration tools; only the last preplaces a Repeater. A hidden Minelayer can still lay its Repeater at deployment, subject to the usual restrictions.
- **Peacemaker+Auxbot 23–27:** FD(+20) native Repeater and a close-template fork; distinguish no-Auxbot profile.
- **Foot Montesa FD(+20) Paramedic 26/0:** independent S2 scorer, not the FTO.
- **Dart, Mendoza, Konstantinos Hidden Deployment + Infiltration (26/0), Hidden Crux:** higher-cost alternate pressure than the **22–24pt Trinitarians, which also have Hidden Deployment and Infiltration**. Konstantinos's 23/0 FTO Disco profile is separate and has no Hidden Deployment.

### Deployment-Zone Defense
- **Raveneye E/M Mine or Cybermine 12/0:** regular FO/Minelayer, Flash Pulse and E/Marat.
- **Escort Sergeant HRL/Auxbot 22/1.5:** template approach denial.
- **Curator Engineer AP Mine 17 or Armed Turret 20:** deployable defenses plus repairs.
- **Fugazi Flash 7 (Regular); Warcor Flash 3 (Irregular):** cheap corners, not interchangeable Order economy.

### Hackers / Killer Hackers
- **De Fersen HD/Trinity(+1SD) 42/0.5:** robust WIP14 hybrid controller and cyber duelist; 46/1.5 Spitfire is separate.
- **Knight of Justice HD/Pitcher 45/0.5:** durable gun+hacker Wildcard, costly as a pure relay.
- **Order Sergeant FTO HD/Pitcher 18/0.5:** cheap legal link projection.
- **Santiago FTO KHD/TinBot Firewall(-3) 31/0:** protected linked cyber specialist.
- **Trinitarian KHD 24/0; Santiago Combat Jump KHD 38/0:** independent midfield and off-table options; the latter cannot link.

### Network and Indirect Fire
- **Order Sergeant FTO Pitcher, KoJ Pitcher:** distinct relay prices and roles.
- **Trinitarian FO/DR Minelayer 23/1:** a Repeater already placed at deployment.
- **Peacemaker, Pathfinder FO/Sensor/Repeater+TacAware 17/0, Fugazi:** forward, mobile and DZ networks.
- **Mulebot Baggage 8:** disposable reload support.
- **Clipper Guided ML 16/1.5:** optional independent Guided strategy; **Clipper FTO 19/1.5 is neither Guided nor legal in an MO Fireteam**.

### Vision Control
- **Konstantinos FTO Disco 23; Crusader Disco 23:** finite linked or independent airborne Eclipse.
- **Black Friar MSV2/Albedo(-6) Thunderbolt 24/0; Crosier MSV2 HMG 24/1:** anti-visibility guns.
- **No cheap normal Smoke battery:** Eclipse also blocks friendly MSV and is consumable.

### Cheap Orders / Cheerleaders
- **Crosier from 11; Fugazi 7 AVA1; Mulebot 8 AVA2; Raveneye 12:** Regular infrastructure with different support functions.
- **Warcor 3:** Irregular, not a transferable Regular Order.
- **Auxbot and Palbot:** Peripherals, not additional pooled Orders.

### Doctors, Paramedics, Engineers
- **Infirmarer Doctor 17–20; Hospitaller Doctor 30:** compact support versus durable in-team recovery.
- **Montesa FTO Paramedic 20:** linked specialist/Blitzen, without FD.
- **Montesa foot FD(+20) Paramedic 26; Motorized Paramedic 23:** independent forward S2 versus fast S4 specialist.
- **Curator Engineer 17–20; Machinist/Palbot:** real repairs; Tech-Bee GizmoKit/TAGCom is not Engineer.

### DZ Attack / CC
- **Santiago Combat Jump 36 non-Hacker / 38 KHD:** two-Wound off-table fork, no Fireteams.
- **Crusader FO/Disco 23, MULTI Rifle 28:** cheaper off-table objective or Eclipse pressure.
- **Crux Hidden+FD(+20) AP SMG 47:** Albedo/CC surprise, not enemy-DZ Infiltration.
- **Motorized Montesa BSG 21:** fast two-Wound close breach.
- **Teutonic Knights and Joan:** premium CC and assault rather than disposable warband substitutes.

### Anti-TAG / E/M
- **Montesa FTO Blitzen 20; Teutonic Panzerfaust 18–19:** low-cost roadblocks.
- **Raveneye E/M Mine/E/Marat 12; Hospitaller E/Mitter 26; Santiago E/M grenades:** distinct disabling geometry.
- **Holy Sepulchre AP HMG 53; Seraph AP Spitfire 72:** direct AP guns when hacking is unsafe.
