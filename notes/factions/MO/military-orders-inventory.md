## Sectorial Inventory

### Lieutenant (LT)

- **Teutonic LT 18/0:** cheap two-Wound commander; Limited Cover and Impetuous exposure are risks.
- **Knight Commander LT 20/0 or LT(+1 Order) 24/0:** Holomask; +1 is worthwhile with a productive NCO.
- **Joan v1 MULTI Rifle LT 47/+1 SWC:** protected fighting commander; grants an extra SWC. Command Token and Strategic Deployment upgrades available.
- **Joan v2 AP Spitfire LT 45/1:** mobile fighting commander; Command Token and Strategic Deployment upgrades available. Only one Joan across versions.
- **De Fersen LT 42/0.5 (MULTI Rifle) or 46/1.5 (Spitfire):** fighting hacker LT; protect succession.
- **Santiago FTO LT 33/0 (Spitfire) or 31/0 (KHD MULTI Rifle):** zero-SWC fighting LT options; exposure risk.
- **Hospitaller FTO LT 27/0 (TinBot MULTI Rifle) or 32/1 (HMG):** durable linked LT; HMG saves 0.5 SWC over non-LT.
- **Knight of Justice LT 41/0 (FO MMR) or 42/1.5 (Spitfire):** expensive fighting LT, normally prefer safer command.

### Chain of Command (CoC)

- **Knight Commander CoC 23/0:** Holomask successor for a fighting LT; not the LT(+1) profile.
- **Joan v1 CoC 47/0 or Strategic Deployment 53/0:** expensive, Non-Specialist; generally skip.
- **Joan v2 CoC 45/1.5 or Strategic Deployment 51/1.5:** independent attack plus succession insurance; Non-Specialist.

### NCO, Tactical Awareness and Force Multipliers

- **Holy Sepulchre AP HMG 53/1:** independent NCO long-range attack.
- **Seraph TAG 72–74/1.5:** NCO and Tactical Awareness on both alternative loadouts.
- **Tikbalang HMG 68/1.5:** Tactical Awareness only (no NCO); Mimetism(-3), Climbing Plus; normally prefer Seraph.
- **Teutonic NCO Spitfire 29/1.5:** useful with Commander LT(+1).
- **Joan v1:** Tactical Awareness, Number 2, Inspiring Leadership when eligible as LT; LT grants +1 SWC.
- **Joan v2:** Tactical Awareness, Number 2, Inspiring Leadership when eligible as LT.
- **Pathfinder FO/Sensor 17/0:** Tactical Awareness, independent extra activation.
- **Tech-Bee TAGCom 9/0:** same-Combat-Group TAG Dodge (PH+3) and GizmoKit (PH+1); not an Engineer or extra pooled Order.
- **Mulebot EVO 14/0.5:** Fairy Dust for HI-heavy lists; REM/airborne Supportware as appropriate. Not the native Repeater profile.
- **Crux FTO Strategic Deployment MMR 50/0:** optional forward deployment for eligible Fireteam.

### Long-Range Gunfighters

Primary 16–32in or longer lane openers; low-Burst ARO weapons are indexed here only when usable for deliberate active shots.

- **Seraph MULTI HMG 74/1.5:** preferred main TAG gun; AP/Shock, EXP single-shot mode, NCO and jumping angles.
- **Holy Sepulchre AP HMG 53/1:** AP, NCO, Holoprojector, Warhorse.
- **Tikbalang HMG 68/1.5:** Mimetism(-3), climbing and smaller S6; skip normally in favor of Seraph.
- **Hospitaller FTO HMG 32/1.5:** linked two-Wound long gun; LT alternative 32/1.
- **Crosier MSV2 HMG 24/1:** economical linked anti-Mimetism gun.
- **Black Friar Thunderbolt(+1B) 24/0:** zero-SWC MSV2/Albedo long-lane answer.
- **Knight of Justice ML(+1SD) 46/1.5:** primarily ARO; situational active anti-armour shot.
- **Sierra TR HMG 23/1:** secondary active long gun, not a primary attack investment.

### Mid-Short-Range Gunfighters

Primary rifle, marksman rifle, Spitfire, Red Fury, shotgun and SMG attack pieces (typically 0–24in). Availability is not a recommendation; niche choices retain their unit-guide verdicts.

- **Seraph AP Spitfire+Auxbot 72/1.5:** dense-board AP assault and Heavy Flamethrower forks.
- **Joan v2 AP Spitfire 45/1 LT or 45/1.5 CoC:** mobile BS15 AP attacker.
- **Joan v1 MULTI Rifle 47/+1 SWC LT or 47/0 CoC:** durable secondary gun; primarily command/SWC value.
- **Crux FTO MMR(+1SD) 46/0 or Spitfire 49/1.5:** preferred MMR; Spitfire usually skip. FD MMR 54/0 is costly.
- **Kyle Hawkins BSG(+1B) 26/0 or MMR 32/0:** BS Attack(-3), two-Wound specialist; preferred cheap close assault.
- **Mendoza FTO MULTI Rifle 40/0:** Mimetism(-6), Continuous Damage and Firewall(-3).
- **Mendoza FD Heavy Shotgun 40/0 or MULTI Rifle 45/0:** independent premium hunter; latter generally skip.
- **Knight of Justice FO MMR 41/0 or HD MMR 45/0.5:** durable scoring or hacking attacker.
- **Knight of Justice Spitfire 42/1.5 or FD(+10) Spitfire 45/1:** conventional midrange alternative.
- **Holy Sepulchre MSV1 MMR(+1B) 53/0:** alternative to AP HMG, shorter range and no NCO.
- **De Fersen MULTI Rifle 42/0.5 or Spitfire 46/1.5:** hybrid cyber and shooting attack.
- **Teutonic NCO Spitfire 29/1.5:** X Visor, extra LT-order efficiency.
- **Bulleteer Spitfire 22/1 or Heavy Shotgun(+1B) 19/0:** fast Mimetism(-6), native Repeater, economical non-specialist secondary gun.
- **Crosier X Visor Spitfire 18/1:** cheap linked midrange gun.
- **Santiago FTO Spitfire 33/1.5 or KHD MULTI Rifle 31/0:** prefer KHD utility; Spitfire normally skip.
- **Hospitaller FTO MULTI Rifle/TinBot 27/0 or BSG/E/Mitter 26/0:** durable linked secondary gun, especially for native Crusade anchor.
- **Foot Montesa FD Red Fury 32/1.5 or Spitfire 35/1.5:** forward assault; Limited Cover.
- **Motorized Montesa Red Fury 29/1, MULTI Rifle 26/0 or BSG 21/0:** fast flanker, Limited Cover.
- **Peacemaker BSG(+1B) 23/0 without Auxbot or 27/0 with Auxbot:** forward attack, template fork with Auxbot.
- **Black Friar MULTI Rifle/Drop Bears 27/0:** MSV2, Albedo and Biometric Visor utility.
- **Konstantinos FTO MULTI Rifle 23/0:** MSV1/Mimetism(-3), specialist and Disco; Hidden AP SMG 26/0 for forward attacks.
- **Crusader Brethren Spitfire 31/1.5 or MULTI Rifle 28/0:** airborne surprise attack angles.
- **Dart AP SMG/Viral Tactical Bow 35–42:** niche; generally skip in MO.

### ARO

- **Sierra TR Thunderbolt(+1B) 18/0:** default independent 360° B3 ARO; AVA2 shared with HMG.
