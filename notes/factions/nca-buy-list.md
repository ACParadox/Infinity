## Safe skips and nonautomatic purchases

1. **Definitely skip buying Garuda:** already own one via OSS. Also skip new **Warcor, Tech-Bee, Palbot, Mulebot, basic Machinist, Trauma-Doc, Black Friar, and extra general Dronbot/Armbot bodies** until a specific list exceeds the available physical chassis.
2. **Skip a new Tikbalang for NCA itself**: Tik is **not** on NCA's standard roster; the historic two-TAG box helps only for its Uhlan and possibly for extra PanO-sectorial collection use.
3. **Avoid blindly buying outdated generic PanO starter/booster boxes:** Nisse, Kamau, Akali, Father-Knight, Teutonic and Crux are **not on current NCA's standard roster**. Those sculpts are only proxies in NCA.
4. **Defer a second/third Aquila, second Uhlan, third Hexa, second Swiss, second Redeye, second Squalos, many Bolts and multiple ORCs** until actual simultaneously fielded lists need them. AVA is a ceiling, not an instruction to complete it.
5. **No dedicated Blade-Ops or Blockers** per buyer preference. **This is explicitly a model-purchase decision, not a claim that the 15-point Blocker specialist or 23-point FTO Blade Engineer are poor gameplay picks.** Proxy when testing those strong roles.
6. **Defer Aïda, infantry Agent Maximus, premium Deva hacker/Devabot, and Uma** until your preferred mission/Fireteam asks for them. Quinn/Shona/Hannibal are optional, not automatic "must-have" purchases despite attractive discounted offers.
7. **Clipper Guided missile plan** is unnecessary unless your list has consistent Targeted/Spotlight/Pitcher setup; no NCA FTO Clipper Fireteam bonus in pinned chart. Existing Dronbot pool can represent occasional Guided.
8. **No need to buy a complete alternate NCA army**: you already have the high-cost TAG/REM/support end of a PanO list. Missing cheap **Auxilia**, **Bolts**, **Swiss**, **Hexa** fill the faction-identity gap best.
