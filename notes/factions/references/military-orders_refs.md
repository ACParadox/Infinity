### N5.3 mission specialists — apex/value decisions (2026-10-23)

**Knight Commander LT(+1)24** is *command economy*, **not a generic mission Specialist unless its actual printed profile/scenario qualifies**. **De Fersen HD+Trinity(+1SD)42/.5** is a high-end *linked fighting Hacker and button-pusher* when both roles matter; a cheap separate Hacker/Doctor may score more safely. **Joan Chain of Command (Non Specialist) is not automatically a button-pusher**. Check actual linked Doctor/Paramedic and mission category before paying for a heroic assault shell.

### Airborne / enemy-DZ attack profile choices (2026-10-23)

**Crux Knight hidden FD+20 47/0** is a true high-investment **forward** strike/mission model—2W/ARM5/Albedo, CrazyKoala and DA CC—but **does not Infiltrate into enemy DZ**. **Motorized Montesa21–29** moves 20-15 with 2W/CC tools to attack through a flank once the route is cleared, not an off-table ambush. **Tikbalang of Montesa68** is fast NCO/TacAware TAG that exploits opened long lanes. Avoid treating **Knight Commander/Joan LT** as a DZ infiltration profile; if an aggressive commander is exposed, compare actual legal CoC successor and LoL risk.

### Active-turn gunfighter cross-reference (2026-10-23)

### N5.3 cheap warband, template and CC trade decisions

Military Orders has **few true 5–10pt Smoke/Chain warbands**. **Motorized Montesa BSG21/0 or Paramedic23/0** is a fast **20-15, 2W, MA2, Impetuous, Mimetism(-3)** aggressive *durable specialist/trade*, but too valuable for a one-for-one 5pt warband exchange. **Foot Montesa Paramedic FD+20 26/0** or **FTO20/0 Blitzen** plays infiltration-like position/scoring and anti-HI E/M threat, yet are **different options**; not a cheap Chain Rifle substitute. Plan actual safe approach with Banshee/Crux/other legal Brethren models, not an invented Galwegian. Commander/CoC risk belongs to selected LT option.

### TAG tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Tikbalang of Montesa** is the more self-protecting mobile attacker (Mimetism, Climbing Plus, HFT; NCO for unused LT Orders), while **Seraph** brings AP Spitfire or MULTI HMG plus Pulzar, EXP CCW and Super-Jump to force closer angles. **Disagreement:** Tik is generally safer against opposing shooters; Seraph's AP and stronger close tools matter when armored targets/vertical approaches dominate. MO's tight order/support budget can make either expensive without a second scoring route.

### Hacker and KHD tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Apex cyber shell:** **De Fersen HD + Trinity(+1SD), WIP14, 42/0.5**, a 2W combat Knight; **Knight of Justice HD 45/0.5** supplies a **BS14 Pitcher** and durable W2/BTS9 network entry. **Cheap alternative:** **Order Sergeant FTO HD/Pitcher 18/0.5** projects network without spending Knight money; **Trinitarian Camouflage/Infiltration KHD 24/0** is an independently scoring anti-Hacker. **Knight Santiago FTO KHD + TinBot 31/0** protects a legal team and can shoot; the 38pt Combat Jump KHD is **not** the FTO option. **Disagreement:** Tactical Awareness MO REDUX praises De Fersen's +SD Trinity/linked Firewall power but concedes even cheap enemy KHD can dice him. Buy premium Knights when the same model will fight and score, not for WIP alone.

### Cheerleader / Order-generation tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

The N5.3 [cheerleader/order-generator apex and value guide](../../list-building/cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md) contrasts Orders-per-point, passive utility, matchup dependence, and opposing transcript opinions. **Use this roster for legal AVA, exact profile costs, Training and Fireteam restrictions.**

- 7pt Fugazi — Flash Pulse/Mimetism/Repeater Regular bot: usually prime passive fuel, with S3 footprint and AVA caveats.
- 8pt Mulebot — Baggage/Repeater Regular support; reload and a protected DZ slot increase its value.
- Warcor — a 3pt Irregular Flash ARO speedbump, not a transferable pool Order without actual conversion.
- Peacemaker — pricier Regular REM/peripheral defensive coverage and network projection; not an economical pure cheerleader.
- Pathfinder — more points than 7pt Flashbot but real Sensor/FO/scoring utility; an upgrade only if the mission/network uses it.

**Decision:** first buy enough protected *transferable* Regular Orders for the actual Combat Group attacker/scoring plan, then pay for genuine role compression. Irregular/Impetuous, LT, Tactical Awareness and Peripheral bodies are different resources. Account for a packed S3/S4 deployment zone and mandatory Fireteam members.


### ARO apex/value cross-reference (2026-10-23)

See [Best ARO Units — N5.3 apex and cost-value tiers](../../list-building/aro-units-apex-and-value.md) for gunfighter vs hidden-trap vs cheap Order-tax comparisons, when each wins, transcript disagreements and counterplay. **The roster, prices/AVA and canonical Fireteam chart above remain authoritative**; a shared unit's permissions do not transfer between armies.

- **Knight of Justice ML** — durable +SD lane anchor; better when real mission approaches must pass its arc
- **Sierra Thunderbolt TR** — 18pt/0SWC multi-arc Regular ARO where profile legal; add Engineer/recovery if its lane matters repeatedly
- **Warcor Flash Pulse** — 3pt Irregular zero-SWC Order-tax ARO; never count as a generic Regular Order
- **Fugazi Flash/Repeater** — 7pt Regular cheap secondary ARO and hacking net; not a main gun
- **Peacemaker/Auxbot screen** — template and multi-body route denial; protect arrival corners rather than expose controller

**Deployment choice:** put the disposable/tax ARO on the approach, preserve a separate hidden/mission-denial gate where legal, and avoid leaving an expensive active-turn gun as your only exposed defence. Test pie-slicing, Smoke/Eclipse/White Noise, hacking, AD entry, recovery access and specialist survival before paying more SWC for a second sniper.

### Doctor / Engineer / Recovery cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Recovery:** **Hospitaler Doctor** in a legal Crusade/Knight team gains *movement compression* beside expensive VITA Knights; a cheaper **Infirmarer of St Lazarus** may cover a small mobile team instead. **Machinist + Palbot** remains the low-cost **Seraph/REM** repair plan. A Controller carrying an active Servant cannot simply retain normal Fireteam membership; pick team-delivered Doctor **or** independent remote healing architecture. [Doctor/Engineer apex, points-value, recovery odds and transcript disputes](../../list-building/doctors-engineers-and-recovery-apex-value.md).

### N5.3 DZ defense / anti-alpha-strike tactical choices

**Raveneye 12/0** (AVA1) is a **Regular** FO+Deactivator+Minelayer: select the E/M Mine or Cybermine profile to protect vulnerable Crosier/Specialist routes before any Orders. **Order Sergeant ESCORT + Auxbot 17/0** adds a reactive two-body close-template fork while supplying one Regular Order; the Escort option isn't the Fireteam-eligible FTO Order Sergeant. **Crosier 19/1 MULTI Sniper+E/M Minelayer** adds a second home obstacle but consumes SWC. **Warcor 360º 3/0** watches rear/side edge (Irregular); **Sierra Thunderbolt 18/0 TR+360º** watches roof/AD and is repairable. **Knight of Santiago 31/0 KHD/TinBot/360º** protects a legal Fireteam and counterattacks but is too expensive to die for a basic cheerleader. **Why:** one Raveneye plus a cheap angle sentry usually protects more *real* Order generators than another exposed expensive Knight; against Fiday/Speculo add a mine/reveal-square template, not a Koala-only solution.

### Tactical cross-reference — vision control (23 October 2026)

**Brother Konstantinos FTO23/0 + Disco Baller + Deactivator** is MO's more reusable *linked/Wildcard* Eclipse lane opener; **Crusader Brethren23/0 Disco** is an independent arrival/attack route, **not** a free Konstantinos substitute or a legal link without its own chart permission. MO lacks mass cheap Smoke, so evaluate the **Orders to place and exploit** each Ball. Versus MSV, Disco/Eclipse can cross where normal Smoke would fail; conversely it prevents friendly MSV shooting through the screen. **Value decision:** choose Konstantinos when a melee/scorer Fireteam benefits from protected approach, Crusader when independent arrival geometry is the solution. [Full cross-game apex/value tiers, dissent and transcripts](../../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).

### Close-combat assassins and counter-CC — N5.3 (2026-10-23)

**Teutonic Knight18/0** (MA2/CC22, 2W, shotgun/Panzerfaust) is a very efficient **covered close-assault fighter**, but Impetuous can expose him. **Motorized Montesa21/0** brings 20-15 speed and MA2/CC23, yet S4 bike geometry fails at narrow doors. **Mendoza40/0 FD+20/Mimetism−6** closes midfield more safely; **Joan47+** (MA3/EXP/NWI) and **Kyle Hawkins32** are expensive multifunction frontline fighters, not 29pt hidden assassination substitutes. **Seraph** is a **TAG** with strong CC23/EXP but should be priced in its separate TAG category; don't rank against 18pt Teutonic. [CC apex/value and reach-versus-lethality comparisons](../../list-building/dedicated-close-combat-assassins-apex-value.md).

### N5.3 network projection — Pitchers, Repeaters, TinBots and disposable delivery

**Order Sergeant FTO hacker+Pitcher18/.5** places ranged network from a **legal team** and delivers anti-heavy HD, while **Trinitarian FO+Deployable Repeater Minelayer23/1** begins a forward network *already placed at Turn 0* with Camo/Infiltration; **Trinitarian FO22/0** is a different scoring profile. **Fugazi7/0**, **Pathfinder FO/Sensor/Repeater**, **Peacemaker FD native Repeater23–27** are budget/dual-purpose physical carriers. **Counter:** Knight of Justice HD/Pitcher45/.5 is a very pricey gun + hacker, not best network value; Order Sergeant/Trinitarian choices leave more points for Knights. No arbitrary TinBot Firewall implies Repeater.

### Battle-report effectiveness and combinations (qualitative, 2026-11-01)

**Observed patterns:** **Trinitarian FO/mines/repeater** establishes a scoring/denial front while **Knight Commander Chain of Command, Hospitaller Doctor/Engineer support and a secondary long-range gun** prevent one Knight's loss from collapsing the mission (#009, #017, #020). #221's mirror highlights the same value of retaining mission specialists and cover after the first heavy attack.

**High-ceiling alternative:** #181's winning **double Seraph TAG + Defersen / Knight of Justice Hacker + repair** package used speed, different attack lanes and hacking defense to beat exposed defenses. It was selected for four of five games, **not** every matchup: the player switched away from double Seraph into Morat anti-TAG/MSV/hacking threats. Buying a second premium attack piece at the expense of specialists/CoC can erase these advantages. See [#181](../../battle-reports/181-190-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md).

**Role test:** Always reserve independent FO/Doctor/Engineer/anti-materiel scoring routes after the primary Knight/TAG advance; do not infer present-day Fireteam membership from historical videos.

**Original audit title:** PanOceania N5.3 — Must-have, go-to, and avoid picks (transcript-evidenced)

**Review:** 2026-10-23. **Authority:** [pinned PanO sectorial Army/Fireteam references]() (Army 7.26246.158, Sept 2026). The seven [tactical faction notes]() and MO roster research were compared with the direct YouTube/Spotify MCP transcripts linked below. **Only attribute a recommendation to a presenter when they actually say it.** Older footage is opinion and does not overrule N5.3 prices, profiles, AVA or Fireteams.

**How to read:**
- **Must-have** = author **explicitly describes** near-universal pick, or multiple transcripts repeatedly favor it; label **single-reviewer** where unsupported as a consensus. When no unit meets this bar, say **none established**. These are NOT tournament inclusion statistics.
- **Go-to** = explicitly praised as good in broadly relevant roles, though architecture/missions choose the actual loadout.
- **Avoid / near-never** = direct presenter condemnation *of that profile/use*, not a blanket assertion the whole unit is unusable. **Disputed** marks another published view. “No-chart FTO” means **cannot use an implied link**, not that the weapon/model is forbidden.
- Costs are all-in **points/SWC** from pinned local roster when shown.

There are **only four direct source-backed near-universal assertions worth repeating as such**: **MO foot Montesa FTO20** (Shepherd), **Kestrel Magistrate+cheap Jackals** (Irregular Order speakers, with Tik LT exception), **Varuna Zulu KHD26** (Dante), and **SWF Peacemaker+Auxbot** (Swiss Miss). #### Worst-value and rare/circumstantial picks — proxy-before-buy guide (2026-10-30)

**Question answered:** Which *entire miniatures* deserve no immediate purchase, which **specific profiles** should be avoided despite the unit having good uses, and which useful-but-rare units are best represented by an existing model? This expands — and does **not** overturn — the whole-unit purchasing audit above.

**Decision labels (not measured pick rates):**
- **Avoid profile** = sourced criticism of a particular loadout or application; **do not infer that the whole sculpt is bad**.
- **Proxy first** = *inference* from costly/redundant/narrow role, limited list demand or explicit published criticism. Not an assertion that a video presenter called every profile terrible.
- **One is enough** = a duplicate miniature is lower priority than its AVA suggests.
- **Specialist build** = deliberately purchase only for its unique tool or a list actually tested. **Strong / contested** identifies disagreement; never equate a rare pick with an unusable one.

Source labels are **Direct** (recorded reviewer criticism), **Contested** (other reviewers disagree) or **Inference** (purchase-priority conclusion from those reviews and N5.3 roster). **Proxy legality, silhouette and clarity:** A gaming proxy still represents a **real legal current Army entry and its actual profile**. A weapon variant usually needs **no second miniature**. Verify current *silhouette, base diameter, quantity and peripheral count* against the roster before substituting: a TAG, vehicle, bike, REM or S2 soldier is **not automatically physically interchangeable**. Clearly tell your opponent which sculpt represents which troop; avoid visually identical proxies for several different units where there could be confusion. For official events, check the current [ITS Season 18 rules/download](https://experience.corvusbelli.com/en/infinity/its) and the organizer; previous [ITS17 proxy provisions](https://downloads.corvusbelli.com/infinity/organized-play/its-rules-season-17-en-v1.0.2.pdf) allowed Corvus Belli stand-ins with the correct base and disclosure but did not allow other manufacturers. **Season 18's specific proxy paragraph was not independently retrievable for this audit**, so don't present an older season's wording as verified 2026 law.

**Final collection principle:** *(1)* buy common roles you actually use; *(2)* avoid the explicitly poor **profile** without blacklisting a useful troop; *(3)* **proxy** a named single-purpose unit or a second high-cost model for several games; *(4)* purchase its own miniature only if it repeatedly earns a distinct role or you want the sculpt for hobby reasons. **“Rare” and “never worth taking” are intentionally not synonyms.**
