## N5.3 tactical cross-references, battle reports and purchasing audit

### Command-unit apex/value cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Wallace LT 38/0** makes genuinely Irregular **Irmandinhos** and other eligible models Regular while adding a free special Coordinated Order; **Varangians and Denma are already Regular**, so are not extra conversions. **Wallace CoC 38/0** offers durable smoke/CC succession as a non-LT choice; **Zenit-7 CoC 30/0.5** is a different role. **Kosmosoldat LT(+1) 48/1** may spend its own Orders but pinned **Scots Guard FTO does NOT have NCO**. Count actual available pooled Orders rather than a fictional LT→Scots Guard engine.

See [cross-game Lieutenant, Chain of Command and Order-package tiers](../../list-building/command-units-apex-and-value.md). **Use this roster/Fireteam chart above for exact legality.**

### Mission specialist / button-pusher choices (N5.3)

Mission plan should preserve a **dedicated specialist among Irmandinho/Kibervolk Patrol/Rokot/Dynamo/Zenit** legal profiles rather than assuming Wallace attack pieces automatically push buttons. **Kibervolk Patrol includes one bundled Dozer KVP**; Engineer support is not two independently optional purchases. **Wallace LT** can regularize eligible *Irregular* models, but Varangians/Denma were already Regular. **Zenit CoC30/.5** is succession/scoring when mission recognizes CoC, not an all-purpose FO/Doctor. Late scoring still needs an intact Order route.

[Cross-game apex and value specialist tier guide](../../list-building/mission-specialists-and-button-pushers-apex-value.md). The unit profiles/Fireteams above control exact legality.

### Airborne / enemy-DZ attack profile choices (2026-10-23)

**Mirage-5 Margot+Duroc60/2** is the high-ceiling DZ-intrusion package: **both Parachutist(DZ)**, Margot BS14 AP Rifle/GL and personal TA/Specialist, Duroc 2W chain(+1B), Smoke/Super-Jump. The compound supplies **one Regular, one Irregular, one Tactical Awareness and one Impetuous token**, not two freely pooled Orders. It earns 60pts only if both bodies have jobs and opponent lacks covered side/rear edges. **Wallace LT38 Inspiring** supports eligible Irregulars from *on-table*, not an airborne LT. **Wallace CoC** is separate option; original pinned Scots Guard FTO has no NCO.

[Cross-game N5.3 airborne and DZ-attack apex/value tiers](../../list-building/airborne-and-enemy-dz-attackers-apex-value.md). **Pinned profiles and the canonical Fireteam chart above govern legality.**

### Active-turn gunfighter cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Active-turn shooting choices:** **Chernobog AP HMG** is the heavy **TAG** 3STR/TacAware anti-armor anchor with a greater direct-fire budget than most infantry HMGs; damage-state changes reduce its staying power. **Mirage-5 60/2** instead buys side/back attacks by landing path and Smoke rather than winning a frontal HMG FtF; both pieces pay heavily in Orders if their entry/target geometry is poor. Prefer cheaper specialist and supporting gun redundancy if a massive 1-turn strike prevents later objectives. [Separate TAG/non-TAG gunfighter tier guide](../../list-building/active-turn-gunfighters-apex-and-value.md).

### Warbands and inexpensive trading roles — N5.3

**Varangian Chain+Smoke10/0** is a standout **Regular+Impetuous** cheap CC23/Berserk/Smoke/Dogged order/body (unlike 5pt Irregular Galwegian). **Irmandinho8/0** is cheap Irregular Smoke+Engineer with D-Charges for mission, while **Denma12/0** adds 15-5/Berserk/AP+DA/MetaChemistry; use the 4pt premium to cross an important gap. **Ioann Bann32/0** is heavy dog-warrior with Viral bow+Smoke, not a throwaway. **Wallace LT38 Inspiring Leadership** can regularize eligible Irmandinhos, but Varangians and Denma are **already Regular**; **Wallace CoC38** is another legal loadout. FT-05 permits listed Varangians/Denma/Ioann with current limits only.

### TAG tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Chernobog** adds AP HMG/HRL or HFT reach to Kosmoflot and has a local Duo option. Prefer the **63-point HFT** when saving for scoring troops and personal defence; **70-point HRL+1B** to contest hard/clustered lanes. Compare the entire legal FT-04 Duo against buying a different second gun/specialist, and keep screens against E/M/close attacks.

For the two-tier **apex/points-value** comparison, TAG counterplay, matchup matrix and direct transcript sources: [N5.3 TAG field guide](../../list-building/tags.md). Treat current profiles, points/SWC and canonical Fireteam data above as authoritative.

### Hacker and KHD tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Wardriver HD 14pts/1 SWC** supplies basic Carbonite/Oblivion/Spotlight and a Specialist in a faction otherwise built on Camo, warbands and potent direct fire. **Why:** without a thick Repeater network, it will generally cover a small area and can be a worse use of SWC than a dedicated attacker. Bring it only to punish a predictable hackable TAG/HI lane or meet a hacking-specific scenario; otherwise nonhackable pressure/impetuous movement and mission specialists do the work. Kosmoflot's current main roster does **not** inherit Nomad KHD hybrids just because units are shared elsewhere.

See the [cross-game apex/value Hacker/KHD tier guide](../../list-building/hackers-and-killer-hackers.md). **The sectorial's current profiles, AVA and canonical Fireteam chart above remain authoritative; device upgrades are profile-specific.**

### Close-combat assassins and counter-CC — N5.3 (2026-10-23)

**Varangian Guard10/0 Regular Smoke+CC23/Berserk** anchors strong *cheap forward-threat* play: important value is Regular Order and denial of enemy fire while stronger assault pieces advance. **Mirage-5 Duroc+Margot** is the premium two-body flank assault: **Duroc** pressures from unexpected DZ/edge rather than winning a Ninja-style Hidden first strike; check composite points/orders, not unit-alone price. **Ioann Bann** provides a tougher dog-warrior close fighter when the route is legal. **Uxía** and legal infiltration targets offer better starting proximity but inferior raw MA5 ceiling. ### Anti-TAG / Anti-HI without hacking — N5.3 (2026-10-23)

**Varangian10 Regular Smoke** (not panzer anti-TAG gun), **Duroc/Mirage-5** Smoke/flank, and **Uxía infiltration** create delivery; choose a current rostered **Frontovik Panzerfaust**, **Beasthunter Panzerfaust** or **Kazak/Traktor hard-ARO** for ranged wounds instead. **Mirage-5** is a two-model attack investment, not a 6pt direct can-opener; kill support/Engineer after landing an attack. ### Anti-Tech — enemy visors, Mimetism, markers and Impersonation (2026-10-23)

**Kibervolk** has rare **Sensor + Biometric Visor**: Sensor clears eligible Camo/Hidden *within ZoC*, Biometric improves legal Discover versus IMP-1; neither is automatic universal detection. **Wardriver Disco Baller16/1** is a true anti-enemy-MSV/Marksmanship **Eclipse** crossing, while **Varangian Smoke10/0** efficiently masks ordinary fire but not MSV2. **Decision:** if the opponent deploys marker/IMP attackers, use Kibervolk to guard their approach; if an elite visor blocks an objective, spend Disco/setup Orders or find a covered fast flank. Avoid paying both packages when ordinary Smoke and mission route suffice.

#### Classification and evidence standards

- **Must-have / near-must:** repeated first-choice inclusion across independently built example lists, or an explicit reviewer “always / most lists / auto-include” judgment. **Near-must is not universal:** a role can be indispensable without any one profile being mandatory.
- **Go-to:** repeatedly attractive price/role efficiency. Not all go-to units belong in the same 300-point list; missions, fire lanes, SWC, Orders and competing packages decide.
- **Never take / strong avoid:** a directly stated negative review of a **specific profile or construction**, ideally with a viable same-roster alternative. Use **conditional** or **contested** if the profile has a niche, there is dissent or the source is pre-rework. There is **no evidenced blanket ban on an entire Ariadna unit family**.
- **Evidence:** **explicit** = specific presenter says so; **repeat** = multiple independently constructed sample lists; **reasoned** = synthesis of recorded strengths/weaknesses plus current reference, **not** a claimed quotation.
- A recorded list choice alone does not prove “most players” use a unit. Transcripts may mangle Ariadna proper names, so check names and legality against the current repository. The standard lists here do **not** import `L'Équipe Argent` Reinforcements, which have separate charts.

#### Worst, uncommon and proxy-first purchases by sectorial (2026-10-30)

**Scope and confidence.** This answers a different question from the unit-role tables above: **“Which whole miniature can I skip buying, which uncommon specialist should I proxy until I actually use it, and which *bad profile* should I avoid without rejecting its whole unit?”** An **AVA limit is not a shopping target**. Video/podcast reviews are **opinion and illustrative lists, not tournament selection-frequency data**; “uncommon” means **not a normal first purchase in these reviewed builds**, not measured rare usage. The latest [N5.3 roster]() and its **Fireteam, silhouette, peripherals and availability** rules override old recordings.

**Buying labels:**
- **Skip by default; proxy if needed:** the **normal whole-model job** has explicit negative commentary or an unusually weak price/role comparison. The exception is recorded; **no Ariadna model is proven mathematically useless in every scenario**.
- **Proxy first:** a full unit with a credible specialty, but you should establish *a specific list and mission use* before buying its sculpt; this is an **inferred shopping recommendation**, not a presenter's alleged quotation.
- **Avoid profile only:** the unit has legitimate alternate profiles. **Never extrapolate to all its miniatures.**
- **One enough / proxy the extra:** owning/fielding multiple is an elective skew rather than a default; distinguish genuine duplicate bodies from alternate loadout sculpts. **Contested** flags genuine source disagreement or tournament examples.

**Tournament proxy policy:** Always match **current model footprint/base and Silhouette**, reveal every declared proxy to your opponent, and check the **current-season ITS and organizer rules** before bringing conversions or another faction's miniature. It is not safe to treat older season-specific event proxy wording as current 2026 policy. **A list's compound units must have all required pieces**; proxies do not create extra model counts, and an unavailable unit or illegal Fireteam remains illegal even if a physical model fits.

**Buy-order conclusion:** If already collecting TAK/Caledonia, the highest payoff normally comes from **new capabilities and missing mission roles**, not duplicating an existing TankHunter, Vystrel, dog warrior, artillery chassis or front-line HMG merely to reach AVA. **Most defensible default purchasing skips:** **USARF Wild Bill**; **FRRM infiltrating Bruant** (especially Bruant + Zouave NCO); and **TAK Pavel as an unplanned generic skirmisher**, although your Pavel is already owned. **Ratnik is contested**: you own one, so there is no reason to buy a second without a tested Mobility build. **Other good proxy-first niche models:** **Kosmoflot Wallace when Irregular conversion is unnecessary, second Chernobog or Polaris**; **Caledonia McMurrough if existing Cameronians suffice**; **FRRM Anaconda, Wolfgang or Knauf** until their particular legal list recurs. These are **not** blanket unit bans.
