### Command-unit apex/value cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**BCA Magistrate LT Strategos L1 19/0** is an exceptional low-price general Order+Counterintelligence engine; **Magistrate CoC 21/0** is a different profile for a Tikbalang LT(+1) or other exposed leader. **Tik LT personal Orders can break an Armored Duo**; an eligible NCO Team Leader converts LT Orders into linked Tactical activations. CoC must survive away from the LT's attack footprint.

### Mission specialists — N5.3 apex and value cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Mission benchmark:** **Pathfinder FO/Sensor/Repeater14 or TacAware17** is excellent cheap objective/repeater work and a roster-legal Wildcard in limited contexts. **BCA Magistrate base Specialist Operative17 / Strategos LT19 / CoC21** give alternative commander-and-scorer roles, **Bipandra Doctor23/TA25** gives recovery and extra personal movement. None of their extra personal/Tactical Orders becomes an unrestricted pool Order; a Palbot does not press buttons independently.

### Airborne / enemy-DZ attack profile choices (2026-10-23)

**Banshee 19/0 LSG+Pulzar, 23/0 KHD** is a cheap **Combat Jump or normal edge-Parachutist** threat: D-Charges, MSV1, Super-Jump and high Classified coverage, but no **Parachutist(DZ)**. **Black A.I.R. KHD31/0** does have **Parachutist(DZ)**, unlike its link-only FTO variants; stronger backline cyber assassination but costs 12 more than Banshee LSG. **Hidden FD+20 Crux Knight47/0** breaches from near-midfield, not direct DZ deployment. **Tikbalang LT73/1.5** is a *ground-breaching* TAG, not a dropper, and **Magistrate CoC21** protects its active LT; do not merge mutually exclusive profiles.

### Active-turn gunfighter cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Active-turn shooting choices:** **Griffin HMG37/1.5** is the cheaper MSV1, longer-range Fireteam gun; **Hetkari MULTI Red Fury48/1.5** pays +11 for Marksmanship against Cover at shorter ranges. **Squalos Mk-II55/1** is an economical midrange **TAG**, while **Tikbalang69/1.5** is the mobile Climbing/Mimetism **TAG**. **Irregular Order #52 disagreement:** prefer Griffin for cost/long HMG band and Mimetism, Hetkari for harder Covered defenders. ### N5.3 cheap warband, template and CC trade decisions

**Minescorp Jackal FTO Panzerfaust+SMG6/0 or Light Rocket Launcher6/0** is Kestrel's premier **cheap long-range disposable trade/Order scaffold**: Irregular alone, but proper **ColCorp legal team** may change efficiency. **Infiltrating 10pt E/M Carbine+BSG or Blitzen10pt**, or **E/M GL Minelayer11/.5** are separate **non-FTO** profiles, great for threatening Hackable TAG/HI from an unexpected approach; **NOT 6pt linked Jackals with E/M GL**. Kestrel lacks generic Galwegian/Morlock/Smoke infrastructure. **Magistrate LT19 Strategos** versus **CoC21** separate safety options when expensive Tik/Banshee attackers trade.

### TAG tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Default decision:** solo **Tikbalang LT (+1 Order)** for an aggressive high-order mobile gun *when the LT can be protected*; **Squalos NCO MULTI HMG** into armor or **HMG+GL** for inaccessible opponents; **Maximus TAG** only for CC/Immunity matchups. A **Tik LT + Pathfinder Duo does not gain two extra Duo activations** from the Tik's personal LT Orders. Compare the full partner cost and retain mission specialists. [Existing deeper tactical review](../kestrel.md).

### Vehicle (VH) tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Redeye AVA2:** BS14, ARM6, Mimetism(-6), Sensor and 20–5 Aerial movement. Choose **37/1.5 Blitzen** for mobile E/M ARO; **38/1.5 Continuous Damage GL** for speculative/high-angle attacks. **Irregular Order #52 (Sep 2026)** proposes **double Redeye** with a Magistrate/Jackal/Fennec shell; **Shepherd** argues paying 3 SWC for two B4 Spitfires leaves too few elite ARO-clearing guns. Plan two safe S7 hiding points, Engineer/GizmoKit rescue and ground scorers; Redeyes cannot directly touch objectives.

### Hacker and KHD tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Best rate:** **Fennec HD + FastPanda 16/0.5** cheaply moves the network forward, but WIP12 requires a backup. **Black A.I.R. FTO HD 26/0.5** adds **Combat Instinct** and MSV2/Mimetism(-3): with a real Repeater net it can deny Stealth and defend the Fennec link. **Scarecrow Camo KHD 27/0 vs HD 29/0.5** trades points for midfield mission safety; **Banshee KHD 23/0 vs HD 25/0.5** is an AD scoring/late hacker attack, not a Turn-0 network member. **Why:** don't confuse defending a wide network (Black A.I.R.) with cheaply projecting it (Fennec) or crossing the table (Banshee/Scarecrow). Check FTO chart before claiming a TinBot/bonus.

### Cheerleader / Order-generation tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

The N5.3 [cheerleader/order-generator apex and value guide](../../list-building/cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md) contrasts Orders-per-point, passive utility, matchup dependence, and opposing transcript opinions. **Use this roster for legal AVA, exact profile costs, Training and Fireteam restrictions.**

- 7pt Fugazi — Flash Pulse/Mimetism/Repeater Regular bot: usually prime passive fuel, with S3 footprint and AVA caveats.
- 8pt Mulebot — Baggage/Repeater Regular support; reload and a protected DZ slot increase its value.
- Fusilier/Fennec — about 10pt small-base Regular line material where legal; costs more than a Flashbot but helps meet link, LT, defensive and scoring requirements.
- Warcor — a 3pt Irregular Flash ARO speedbump, not a transferable pool Order without actual conversion.
- Peacemaker — pricier Regular REM/peripheral defensive coverage and network projection; not an economical pure cheerleader.

**Decision:** first buy enough protected *transferable* Regular Orders for the actual Combat Group attacker/scoring plan, then pay for genuine role compression. Irregular/Impetuous, LT, Tactical Awareness and Peripheral bodies are different resources. Account for a packed S3/S4 deployment zone and mandatory Fireteam members.


### ARO apex/value cross-reference (2026-10-23)

See [Best ARO Units — N5.3 apex and cost-value tiers](../../list-building/aro-units-apex-and-value.md) for gunfighter vs hidden-trap vs cheap Order-tax comparisons, when each wins, transcript disagreements and counterplay. **The roster, prices/AVA and canonical Fireteam chart above remain authoritative**; a shared unit's permissions do not transfer between armies.

- **Hetkari ML** — BS14/ARM5 Combat Instinct hard defence; buy for a forced route, not as a passive expensive Order
- **Black A.I.R. sniper** — MSV2, Mimetism and Neurocinetics threatens camo/smoke approaches; W1 makes the 1.5SWC investment risky
- **Blink hidden HRL** — unseen second gate behind a Sierra or cheap Jackal; arrival geometry matters more than broad firing arcs
- **Jackal FTO Panzerfaust/LRL** — disposable 6pt ARO, long-lane anti-armor vs close-corner template; Irregular unless genuinely converted
- **Sierra Thunderbolt TR** — 18pt/0SWC multi-arc Regular ARO where profile legal; add Engineer/recovery if its lane matters repeatedly

**Deployment choice:** put the disposable/tax ARO on the approach, preserve a separate hidden/mission-denial gate where legal, and avoid leaving an expensive active-turn gun as your only exposed defence. Test pie-slicing, Smoke/Eclipse/White Noise, hacking, AD entry, recovery access and specialist survival before paying more SWC for a second sniper.

### Doctor / Engineer / Recovery cross-reference (2026-10-23)

Favor prepositioned Palbot for the Tik/Squalos unless the Doctor already earns team movement. ### N5.3 midfield area denial / speed bumps (tactical synthesis)

**11/.5 infiltrating Jackal** brings a **Shock Mine Minelayer plus E/M Grenade Launcher** before spending any Orders; it is **Irregular** and not COLCORP FTO. Critically, the mine is **Shock, not E/M**. Two cover independent approaches when SWC allows. **Fennec Thunderbolt+Shock Minelayer 14/0** is 0-SWC *DZ* security, not Infiltration; **Peacemaker 24/.5 with Auxbot-3B** adds FD, mine, template and Repeater, but needs S4/base space. **Counterview:** 6pt FTO Jackal supports legal COLCORP Fireteams differently; 27pt +1B Peacemaker may win more actively than the passive mine version.

### N5.3 DZ defense / anti-alpha-strike tactical choices

**Fennec 14/0 Thunderbolt(+1B) + Shock Mine Minelayer** gives a prepaid **DZ mine, 0-SWC backup gun and legal Fennec team member**; **Fennec FO+Sensor+Disco13/0** is a distinct mission/marker alternative. **Sierra TR/360º Thunderbolt18/0** watches airborne or rear-edge arrival; **Drummer FO+Drumbot-5 21/0** provides 360º **controller + synchronized bot** and a useful Forward Observer. **Jackal LRL/Panzerfaust FTO6/0 is Irregular**, not an automatic shared Order. **Disagreement (Shepherd/Irregular Order):** Fennec is exceptional cheap static insurance, whereas Drummer/Peacemaker packages merit their price only if they contest a second angle or score; keep premium attackers safe until own turn.

### Close-combat assassins and counter-CC — N5.3 (2026-10-23)

**Crux Father-Knight47/0 hidden/FD(+20), MA2, Berserk, 2W** is Kestrel's *real* costly close-assault assassin: stealthy breach and Specialist work rather than Shinobu's Monofilament/MA5. **Jackals6–9** are cheap mines/rocket/assault scaffolding, **not** melee assassins (CC14). **Banshee19** is airborne shotgun/template flanking (CC15), likewise not high-CC. ### N5.3 rare abilities & unusual combinations — tactical synthesis

**Drummer FO+Drumbot-5 21/0** is a genuinely rare **double-Forward-Observer synchronized package**: both the controller **and Drumbot-5** have FO; bot also has 360º, Mimetism(-3), **HFT+Flash Pulse**. Both can declare FO at separate LoF-legal targets on the *same Order*, each rolling its own WIP; only **one pooled Regular Order** and must maintain Coherency. **Proxy legality, silhouette and clarity:** A gaming proxy still represents a **real legal current Army entry and its actual profile**. A weapon variant usually needs **no second miniature**. Verify current *silhouette, base diameter, quantity and peripheral count* against the roster before substituting: a TAG, vehicle, bike, REM or S2 soldier is **not automatically physically interchangeable**. Clearly tell your opponent which sculpt represents which troop; avoid visually identical proxies for several different units where there could be confusion. For official events, check the current [ITS Season 18 rules/download](https://experience.corvusbelli.com/en/infinity/its) and the organizer; previous [ITS17 proxy provisions](https://downloads.corvusbelli.com/infinity/organized-play/its-rules-season-17-en-v1.0.2.pdf) allowed Corvus Belli stand-ins with the correct base and disclosure but did not allow other manufacturers. **Season 18's specific proxy paragraph was not independently retrievable for this audit**, so don't present an older season's wording as verified 2026 law.

**Final collection principle:** *(1)* buy common roles you actually use; *(2)* avoid the explicitly poor **profile** without blacklisting a useful troop; *(3)* **proxy** a named single-purpose unit or a second high-cost model for several games; *(4)* purchase its own miniature only if it repeatedly earns a distinct role or you want the sculpt for hobby reasons. **“Rare” and “never worth taking” are intentionally not synonyms.**
