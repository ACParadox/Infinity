## N5.3 tactical cross-references, battle reports and purchasing audit

### TAG tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Triphammer** fits the low-cost assault/second-gun role; **Anaconda** is the costlier, generally lower-ceiling AP Spitfire/Thunderbolt with an operator follow-up. Compare against StarCo's existing guns and objective pieces—an expensive chassis with no new attack angle is a trap. Use the local Armored Fireteam chart, not another NA2 company's options.

For the two-tier **apex/points-value** comparison, TAG counterplay, matchup matrix and direct transcript sources: [N5.3 TAG field guide](../../list-building/tags.md). Treat the linked current profiles, points/SWC and canonical Fireteam data as authoritative.

### Hacker and KHD tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Uhahu HD+ 19/0.5 WIP14 with Pitcher, NWI** is one of the best cheap network/White Noise engines in NA2; **Zero KHD22/0** and **Bandit KHD26/0** add field-camouflaged Trinity/Cybermask and objectives. **Brawler HD16/0.5 / Alguacil HD15/0.5** buy inexpensive normal Carbonite/Oblivion; **Mobile Brigada HD36/0.5 with TinBot Firewall(-6)** gives a sturdier legal-link anchor when heavy infantry must cross repeater space. **Why:** StarCo can have **Uhahu + cheap KHD** for a complete low-cost hacker stack while freeing points for core attack pieces. A pricey linked Brigade HD is useful only if its gun/Fireteam and firewall actually get Orders and protect the rest.

See the [cross-game apex/value Hacker/KHD tier guide](../../list-building/hackers-and-killer-hackers.md). **the linked authoritative sectorial profiles, AVA and Fireteam chart remain authoritative; device upgrades are profile-specific.**


### Cheerleader / Order-generation tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

The N5.3 [cheerleader/order-generator apex and value guide](../../list-building/cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md) contrasts Orders-per-point, passive utility, matchup dependence, and opposing transcript opinions. **Use this roster for legal AVA, exact profile costs, Training and Fireteam restrictions.**

- Warcor — a 3pt Irregular Flash ARO speedbump, not a transferable pool Order without actual conversion.
- 10pt Alguacil — standard S2 Regular Order and link/decoy body; SWC weapon upgrades need a specific purpose.
- 7pt Transductor — passive Repeater/Flash Regular Order; consider base congestion and AVA.
- 8pt Salyut — Baggage/Repeater Regular support; useful reload infrastructure.

**Decision:** first buy enough protected *transferable* Regular Orders for the actual Combat Group attacker/scoring plan, then pay for genuine role compression. Irregular/Impetuous, LT, Tactical Awareness and Peripheral bodies are different resources. Account for a packed S3/S4 deployment zone and mandatory Fireteam members.


### ARO apex/value cross-reference (2026-10-23)

See [Best ARO Units in N5.3 — apex, cost-value, Hidden Deployment, reactive traps, opposed podcast views](../../list-building/aro-units-apex-and-value.md). **Only the legal roster above** establishes options, exact costs/AVA, upgrades and Fireteam permissions in this army.

- **Reaktion 19pt Thunderbolt TR** — cheap Regular defence and later recovery, but single STR can be defeated by a strong shooter
- **Knauf sniper** — long-range anti-armor/visor opportunity without consuming a full Fireteam; protect against better active dice and flanks
- **Cateran camo T2/AP sniper** — 21pt Irregular long-range trade up, valuable despite not making generic pool Orders
- **Warcor 3pt Flash** — token defensive tax/late game stun, Irregular so not a generic Order

**List test:** a strong ARO consumes enemy Orders by forcing Smoke, rerouting, poor range, discoveries or risky trades. A robust list normally prefers *serial, different* defenders—a cheap visible or marker/template screen, a covered second gate, and a preserved attack/scoring unit—rather than two adjacent visible snipers. Plan for opponent pie-slicing, AD, anti-camo, hacking and recovery; evaluate whether extra SWC is better spent on mission attackers.

##### Avoid / never-take candidates

- **Alguacil premium SWC shooting as the default gun — Direct negative [S1].** The N5 speaker recommends avoiding Alguaciles except when they provide an actual cheap LT, necessary StarCo team foundation or Daktari link/decoy function; for serious shooting, pay for Knauf/Mobile Brigada/Riot Grrl or relevant Brawler. **Not a veto** on **10/0 Alguacil LT** or low-cost team glue. Note: the **current 10/0 Alguacil LT has no 0.5 SWC tax**, contrary to an early spoken claim.
- **Unplanned Anaconda / Triphammer TAG purchase — Direct [S1] is very negative; **contested** by later [S2].** S1 calls Anaconda among the worst TAGs and says not to take it, likewise dismissing Triphammer. However S2 re-evaluates the **Anaconda at 59/1.5**, arguing ARM7 with AP immunity, Warhorse, improved escape Operator and a **legal Anaconda + Brawler Engineer + Digger Armored Haris** creates a genuine defensive/zone-control anchor. Do **not** file Anaconda under unqualified never-take; mark **no-support standalone TAG** as an avoid, with the Haris as its counterexample. The 0-SWC Triphammer has a SWC-preservation niche but remains contested on raw gunfight quality.
- **Expensive Clockmaker purely duplicating Irmandinho's Engineer role — Conditional [S1].** Clockmaker at 18 has higher WIP15 and access to Zondbot reach, so is right for demanding repairs; poor use if no high-value repair targets. **Do not** label Clockmaker fundamentally bad.
- **Illegal purported Knauf FTO + Uhahu + Brawler LT Outrage Haris** — the [S2] hosts propose, then explicitly correct, this list: the Brawler LT is not eligible Outrage/Wildcard. Use a legal Duo or supported Haris with the correct members. This is a **rules correction**, not a Brawler LT veto.

#### Cross-sectorial ranking principles / edition checks

1. **A “must” is a reported list-building default, not tournament telemetry.** Most explicit Druze/StarCo/Dahshat endorsements are a named player's preferences; there is no verified representative inclusion-rate database here.
2. **Do not call every TAG “never take.”** Druze Anaconda has strongly negative direct testimony, whereas **StarCo Anaconda gets a persuasive later rehabilitation in a supported Haris**, and WhiteCo uses a different Kaplan Duo. It is the *same chassis with different team architecture*. The Triphammer's 0-SWC entry can also be right even if it loses raw shootout comparisons.
3. **Do not conflate FTO label and permission:** Druze Guided Clipper differs from Fireteam FTO direct ML; Ikari Guided Clipper similarly does not link; WhiteCo Clipper FTO has no Fireteam row at all; StarCo Stempler FTO likewise has no current StarCo chart row. Always use the exact current Fireteam chart and profile option.
4. **Irregular versus Regular economy is decisive:** Irregular smoke/assault excels individually in Ikari, StarCo and Dahshat, but 15 slots and too few pooled Orders can make even a roster of efficient units dysfunctional. The **Regular** cheap Keisotsu, Fusiliers, Fugazi/Fanous/Transductor and specialist options have systemic value.
5. **Mission scoring and delivery beat theoretical damage:** Druze/Dahshat Hunzakut, Ikari Karakuri/Pathfinder, StarCo Al Hawwa'/Hellcat and WhiteCo Guiláng/Tiger change whether a gun-heavy list can reach a console on time.
6. **No illegal legacy data:** Current N5.3 Army references override early N5/N4 transcripts: old StarCo Alguacil LT SWC tax, old N4 Dahshat Zúyŏng LT, imported Karakuri Fireteams, early linked-Guided Clipper assumptions and other-faction TAG/REM permissions are not reliable.

#### Worst-value, rare-use and proxy-first mercenary miniature purchases (2026-10-30)

**Scope and evidence.** This is a **miniature-buying audit**, not a re-ranking of every profile. It covers each current NA2 army separately: [Druze Bayram Security](../../../sectorials/na2/druze.md), [Ikari Company](../../../sectorials/na2/ikari-company.md), [StarCo](../../../sectorials/na2/starco-free-company-of-the-star.md), [Dahshat Company](../../../sectorials/na2/dahshat-company.md), and [White Company](../../../sectorials/na2/white-company.md). References are the **pinned N5.3, September 2026 Army 7.26246.159 rosters and Fireteam charts**. **Direct negative** means the speaker actually criticized that use in the earlier [transcript-backed source register](#source-coverage-and-limitations). **Proxy first**, **conditional whole-model deferral**, and **extra-copy defer** are *reasoned buying suggestions*, not claims the media said those literal words. **Rare** means unusual in reviewed all-comers constructions, *not a measured tournament inclusion rate*. When the existing podcast reviews predate current Army, the **current printed profiles** govern. No all-profile universally unplayable unit is established.

**Four distinctions matter:** a **weak gun profile** can belong to a very good unit; a **conditional whole-model deferral** is not a zero-win-rate label; a **rare specialized peripheral/character** can deserve a proxy even when genuinely excellent; and **AVA / alternate weapon sculpts** alone never create a shopping need. Also **one bought model can be reused across different legal sectorials**, not fielded twice simultaneously. No current complete inventory of the five mercenary armies was found at the expected inventory paths; the cross-army proxy examples below **do not assume ownership** of NA2-exclusive models.
