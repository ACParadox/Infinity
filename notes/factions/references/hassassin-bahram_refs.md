## N5.3 tactical cross-references, battle reports and purchasing audit

### Command-unit apex/value cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Farzan CoC 24/0** is among the strongest multi-role backups: Camouflage/infiltration/mine-and-mission value protects an **active Bokhtar or other expensive LT** while still scoring. A cheap Ghulam commander is viable when exposure is low; don't pay for CoC only to throw the Farzan away as first assault. **Saladin's Vanilla Haqq list-wide Inspiring engine is not automatically available to Hassassins.**

See [cross-faction Lieutenant, CoC and Order package tiers](../../list-building/command-units-apex-and-value.md). **Current roster and canonical Fireteam chart above remain authoritative.**

### Mission specialists — N5.3 apex and value cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Mission benchmark:** **Farzan FO22/0** is Camouflaged Infiltration, D-Charges/Flash Pulse; **Farzan CoC24/0** buys succession for +2pts but loses the FO-only option. **Ghulam FO12 / Doctor(+3)15** supply cheap WIP14 mission categories. **Asawira Doctor(+3)35–37** is a frontline 2W fighting medic **only if** its gun, recovery and button role share a route; otherwise Ghulam15 provides better marginal utility. Do not expose sole CoC early.

[Cross-game specialist and button-pusher tiers](../../list-building/mission-specialists-and-button-pushers-apex-value.md). **Current profile options and Fireteam chart in this file remain authoritative.**

### Airborne / enemy-DZ attack profile choices (2026-10-23)

**Fiday BSG23/0, Smoke+DA CC** is S-value pre-deployed Impersonation with near-DZ threat; **Hussein Al-Djabel33/0** raises CC/Viral ceiling but can trade worse into soft 10pt Orders. **Ragik BSG(+1B)23/0 vs HD25/.5** chooses kill vs Classified/Spotlight utility after risky Combat Jump or **normal** table-edge Parachutist. **Bokhtar Parachutist BSG+Paramedic49/0 / Red Fury52/1.5** attacks from side edge with 2W; **Bokhtar LT FTO45/46 has no Parachutist**. **Farzan CoC24** backs the aggressive on-table LT; keep it separated from the strike route.

### Active-turn gunfighter cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Active-turn shooting choices:** **Asawira AP Spitfire38/1.5** is the sturdy BS14/2W/BTS9 **non-TAG** armor-killer at midrange; **Boarding Shotgun(+1B)32/0** saves SWC for a second long gun and dominates objective-space corners. **Fiday** may force/clear the defender's screening so the Asawira spends the next Orders shooting relevant targets instead of walking into a prepared ARO. **Ragik** arrives at new angles but is not a long-lane heavy-gun substitute. ### Warbands and cheap high-upside trading — N5.3

**Ghazi 5pt Chain+Smoke**, **5pt Chain+E/Marat NO SMOKE**, **6pt Chain+Jammer NO SMOKE**, 7/8pt shotgun+Smoke variants are distinct profiles: **E/Marat disables Hackable**, Jammer causes communications/Isolated control where not Warhorse, normal Chain threatens single-W infantry. **Dogged** helps complete a cheap trade but 1W doesn't imply free kills. **Daylami 6pt Panzerfaust /8pt Camo+Panzerfaust** is the cheap *ranged* anti-TAG deterrent. **Fiday BSG23** trades/deploys differently: Impersonation/T0, not Irregular warband. **Farzan CoC24** should survive if active **Bokhtar LT45–46** is the attacking commander.

### TAG tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Shakush (56–58)** is particularly useful *here* because Hassassin often needs a durable, reliable ranged attack/long-gun anchor. The HMG Continuous Damage threatens repeated failed saves; AP Spitfire is for armored targets at shorter bands. **Disagreement:** the 2025 TAG podcast ranks Shakush modestly against other TAGs, but Haqq players argue its **sectorial role scarcity** makes it much better than a global head-to-head comparison suggests.

For the two-tier **apex/points-value** comparison, TAG counterplay, matchup matrix and direct transcript sources: [N5.3 TAG field guide](../../list-building/tags.md). Treat current profiles, points/SWC and canonical Fireteam data above as authoritative.

### Hacker and KHD tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Barid KHD+Pitcher 14/0 versus HD+Pitcher 16/0.5**, both WIP14, is a premier infowar return for points. **Leila Sharif 22/0 KHD+Carbonite+Firewall(-3) and Combat Instinct** combines KHD Trinity, anti-HI control and stealth-proof reactive network defense in a permitted team. **Hassassin Govad KHD 23/0 or HD 25/0.5** supports a fighting link and WIP14, while **Ghulam HD16/0.5** is the cheap second ordinary HD. **Ragik WIP15 HD25/0.5** provides Combat Jump mission reach but cannot defend the board in Turn 0. **Why:** Barid is a low-cost Pitcher/specialist; Leila is a resilient hybrid network guard. Buying extra standard HDs without Hunzakut/Repeater reach is not added cyber control.

See the [cross-game apex/value Hacker/KHD tier guide](../../list-building/hackers-and-killer-hackers.md). **The sectorial's current profiles, AVA and canonical Fireteam chart above remain authoritative; device upgrades are profile-specific.**


### Cheerleader / Order-generation tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

Cross-faction [apex and cost-value Order-generator rankings](../../list-building/cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md), circumstance guide and transcript disagreements. **This file remains authoritative for profiles, AVA, Training and Fireteam legality.**

- 7pt Fanous — Regular Flash/Repeater REM with Mimetism; excellent cheap network but limited S3 concealment.
- 8pt Kameel — Baggage/Repeater/Deactivator and Regular Order, particularly valuable with disposable launchers.
- 11pt Ghulam — S2 protected Regular Order; FO/Doctor/deployable repeater profiles can earn their premium.
- Barid FO/Pitcher — combines Regular Order, forward objective access and a mobile network projection threat.
- Ghazi Muttawi'ah — normally Irregular smoke/Jammer/E-M cheap defender; only count as pool Order with current legal conversion.

**When to pick:** Prefer transferable, safe Regular Orders when the Combat Group needs fuel; choose the higher-priced option only when its smoke, template, weapon, repeater, Fireteam function or scoring ability has a real job. Do not count Irregular/Impetuous/Tactical/Lieutenant Orders or Peripheral bodies as interchangeable Regular pool Orders; account for DZ footprint and link/enabler package costs.


### ARO apex/value cross-reference (2026-10-23)

The [N5.3 cross-faction ARO apex/value and matchup study](../../list-building/aro-units-apex-and-value.md) compares hidden missile traps, Neurocinetics/TR, cheap templates/mines, DZ guards and conflicting creator opinions. Consult the **roster and Fireteam chart above** for legally available profiles, SWC, Training and AVA.

- **Sunduqbut Neurocinetics Feuerbach** — 47pt/1.5SWC durable hidden/ambush-capable long-lane anchor with serious armor punch; expensive if easily bypassed
- **Nadhir Flammenspeer/Panzerfaust** — cheap-to-medium concealed route trap; choose timing, not first possible shot
- **Daylami Panzerfaust** — 6pt/0SWC Irregular disposable defense, or 8pt Camo(1 use)/Infiltration; outstanding trading value, not reliable continuous lock
- **Ghazi Chain Rifle/E-M/Jammer** — ~5–6pt Irregular close-area screen, punishing TAG, Assassin and hacker routes; no general shared Regular Order
- **Warcor Flash** — 3pt Irregular ARO tax/late-game Stun attempt, not a Regular Order

**Practical deployment:** put cheap disposable/Flash/DTW or mine pressure on the approach; retain a separate unseen or recoverable threat for the decisive square; avoid exposing a premium active attacker and check opponent Smoke, MSV, hacking, AD and pie-slicing. No automatic order from an off-table Hidden model; Irregular ARO units do not supply generic Regular Orders.

### N5.3 midfield area denial / speed bumps (tactical synthesis)

**Apex pre-deployed control:** **Shujae SMG/Flammenspeer E/M Mine Minelayer (2) 27/0**: Hidden + FD(+20) + specialist and two mines, strongest at Hackable chokepoints but no Order while Hidden. **Farzan Viral Minelayer 21/.5** supplies a *different* camouflage/mine threat; using both discourages enemy mine-type identification. **Counterpoint:** Sensor/Deactivator and cheap scouts can defeat predictable clusters; Shujae mine spacing can betray the hidden launch position. Do not mistake the 24/0 Farzan CoC for its minelayer.

### Doctor / Engineer / Recovery cross-reference (2026-10-23)

Where current roster permits **Ghulam Doctor(+3)**, it is the economical medical core for high-value VITA/Asawira; a **Nasmat3** staged behind the likely ARO position can reduce retrieval to 1–2 Orders. Repair **STR** separately through an eligible Engineer, not a Doctor. A Fiday or Ragik committed behind enemy lines usually leaves the recovery radius and should be evaluated as a trading unit, not as a guarantee of a medical return. [Doctor/Engineer recovery odds and tier guide](../../list-building/doctors-engineers-and-recovery-apex-value.md).

### N5.3 DZ defense / anti-alpha-strike tactical choices

**Daylami LSG+Panzerfaust 6/0** or bare **LSG 5/0** is disposable Irregular gun/corner friction protecting 11pt Ghulam and the Barid/Doctor; **Ghazi Chain+E/Marat 5/0** or **Jammer 6/0** deters a different close rush (no Smoke on these E/Marat/Jammer loadouts). Neither gives pooled Regular Orders. **Shujae E/M Minelayer(2) 27/0** is a premium hidden *outer* approach deterrent, not a substitute for a guard on the DZ exit square. **Counter:** Fiday/Speculo in an IMP marker does not trigger Boost, so a Koala-only defense is insufficient; screen routes with mines/DTWs and spread vital staff. Don't buy too many Irregular blockers for a list already low on Regular Orders.

[Whole-game DZ-defender apex/value tiers and media disagreements](../../list-building/dz-defenders-alpha-strike-and-impersonation-apex-value.md).

### Close-combat assassins and counter-CC — N5.3 (2026-10-23)

**Fiday BSG23/0** is the cheap **Impersonation + Smoke + DA CCW** attacker against DZ support; **Hussein Al-Djabel33/0** pays ten extra for **MA4, Viral CCW, Climbing Plus and WIP15**. **Asawira BSG32/0** is a 2W/Berserk(+3)/MA2 hard corner fighter **after** a legal shooting/Smoke route; don't sacrifice it solely to initiate CC. **Fiday is not Monofilament**: choose targets appropriate to DA/Viral, deny mine/Guard screens and avoid forcing a head-on contest with NBW. ### N5.3 network projection — Pitchers, Repeaters, TinBots and disposable delivery

**Hassassin Barid12/0 FO+Pitcher** is an unusually cheap Regular Fireteam projector without SWC; **Barid14/0 KHD+Pitcher** supplies anti-Hacker Trinity; **16/.5 HD/Pitcher** adds anti-heavy programs. Don't combine those *separate* profiles. **Rafiq13/0 Sensor+FO+native Repeater** is a strong MOV15-10 scoring kamikaze node, **Fanous7/0** and **Kameel8/0 Baggage+Repeater** form low-cost Regular scaffolding. Ghulam13/0 Deployable Repeater normally requires an Order to place. **Counter:** Barid BS11 outside team/range is unreliable at 24″+, and many non-Hackable attackers require Ghazi/Daylami physical guard instead of more cyber.

### Anti-TAG / Anti-HI without hacking — N5.3 (2026-10-23)

**Ghazi E/Marat5 (NO Smoke on Bahram's 5pt option)** is the extreme points-value TAG-disabler *if* the template route exists; 7pt E/Marat+Smoke is a different option. **Daylami Panzerfaust6 / Camouflaged+Infiltration8**, **Ghulam12 Panzerfaust**, **Nadhir23 Hidden Panzerfaust** cover ranged ambush. **Sunduqbut47 Feuerbach+Neurocinetics** is hard defensive ARO, **Muyib23 FO/Panzerfaust** brings mission duty; don't import Vanilla-only Azra'il without roster check. **Choose by route:** Ghazi if a TAG must enter template range; concealed Daylami/Nadhir/Shujae ambush lanes if it can remain far away. Cheap Irregular traps still require **Regular Orders** to exploit a successful hit. ### N5.3 objective holding, zone domination & carried-objective choices

**Asawira Doctor+3 35/0** = **W2 ARM4 BTS9 Regeneration + real Specialist**, ideal difficult-to-kill Supply Box keeper inside source-legal Fireteam; **32/0 Boarding Shotgun+Disco** and **33/0 AP rifle** are **not Specialist just from Asawira name**. **Shakush56–58/1.5** 3STR/ARM6/Tactical Awareness can swing a quadrant and screen a box, but only its **Jandab Ancillary Specialist** has specific ancillary mission permissions—**TAG itself cannot pick up normal Supplies**. **Farzan CoC24/0** and Shujae Hidden/E/M mine protect pickup squares and secure secondary zones; CoC eligible Specialist where scenario accepts it. **Opposing:** two Asawira holding one room may fall to coordinated AP/CC E/M, whereas one Doctor Asawira plus cheap specialist/markers and Jammer screens split risks.

### Anti-Tech — enemy visors, Mimetism, markers and Impersonation (2026-10-23)

**Rafiq FO/Sensor/Triangulated Fire13/0** is the cheapest clear Camo/Hidden search and mission utility instead of chasing every marker with expensive Asawira. **Govad MSV1** reduces ordinary Mimetism/visibility penalties but **does not fully erase Mimetism(-6)**; Bahram's own **Shujae/Nadhir Hidden/Daylami markers** tax opponent Discover efforts. **Áyyār Triangulated Fire** can fight *revealed* Mimetism in a chosen LoF without standard attack MODs, but costs a Long Skill; no direct automatic Sensor of IMP. **Choice:** cheap Rafiq first to clear a marker-heavy midfield; linked Govad/Asawira shooting when opposing guns must actually be removed. Do not equate Impersonation defense with generic Sensor.

### N5.3 peripheral / synchronized / multi-body apex-value update

**Shakush TAG56–58/1.5** includes a **Jandab Ancillary Specialist** in normal source profile, *not a second Order*, deploy via **Place Deployable** only when job exists; after deployed active Ancillary, Fireteam Controller restrictions apply even to source-legal **Shakush Duo**. **Doctor(+3) Asawira35–37/0 + Nasmat3pt** extends excellent WIP14+3 care to physically distant allies; **only one patient per Order**, Nasmat unarmed / not a new scoring Specialist. **Disposable traps:** Shujae Hidden E/M Mine and Ghazi warbands are actual independent unit threats; E/M/Mines are equipment, not the same as **Pupnik** physical attack. **Counter:** rushing 3STR Shakush without Engineer and assuming Jandab free extraction leaves it vulnerable to Cybernetics and expensive loss; better to buy 3pt Servant and preserve actual Specialist until later.

##### Must-have / near-must
- **Ghulam NCO + Smoke GL15/1** — **Shepherd direct near-auto**: says he fields it in essentially every list. *Why*: baseline WIP14 Regular order, legal Ghulam Fireteam foundation, ranged linked Smoke access, NCO conversion of a normally unused lieutenant activation. **Exception:** an aggressively built list that deliberately omits Ghulam Fireteams / invests SWC elsewhere.
- **Barid KHD14/0 and HD16/.5** — **Shepherd direct personal default**: says typically one of each in every list. *Why*: WIP14, BTS3, two distinct anti-hacker/control jobs and cheap **Pitcher projection** inside Ghulam formations. Not proof of mandatory *two hackers* in a low-hackable enemy matchup.

##### Never take / avoid
- **Ghulam HMG20/1 and Missile Launcher17/1.5** — Shepherd explicitly says he has **no interest in expensive Ghulam SWC guns**, preferring Ghulam **NCO/LT/Doctor utility** and genuine Asawira/Lasiq/Govad attack or ARO options. **Strong profile-level avoid as a general default**, not mathematical domination in every imaginable list.
- **Double Áyyār in an ordinary balanced list** — Shepherd explicitly rarely fields two and often zero because **~76–80pts** duplicates expensive ambush expertise; this is **avoid as default**, not strict veto: successful high-ambush builds exist. [HB guide](../hassassin-bahram.md).
- **Do not put all Shujae/Áyyār into Hidden Deployment by habit**: if unavailable to the starting order pool, the army can be stalled. **Do not claim Shujae + Áyyār universally mandatory**.
- **False “never take”: Sunduqbut**. One recording favors heavy marker defence while another list may skip it. The HB guide explicitly rejects declaring Sunduqbut universally poor. Some **early Tactical Awareness complaints about old hidden mine setups** are patch-sensitive.
