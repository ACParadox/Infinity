## Scope

**Scope:** Ordinary 300-point list-building. **Must/near-must** = an explicit near-auto recommendation or a broadly repeated core *role/package*; these are not measured pick rates. **Go-to** = a dependable purchase for its role, rather than a compulsory inclusion. **Avoid** = a profile or standard use criticized by named speakers, or demonstrably poor opportunity cost relative to current alternatives; **not** an assertion of literal zero playable matchups. Where recording evidence is limited or contradictory, say so. Model legal Fireteams from the linked N5.3 charts, **not** historic recorded lists. `FTO` alone is not legal Fireteam access.

## N5.3 tactical cross-references, battle reports and purchasing audit

### Command-unit apex/value cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Raveneye LT(+1) 17/0** is a premier low-cost Holomask, FO, Minelayer two-Order commander when **Strider/Vidoc or another legal NCO** can spend those Orders; **Raveneye CoC 18/0** is a different legal profile that supplies hidden succession. **Jamie Arantes LT(+1) 49/0** is for actually spending command Orders on the fighter, but adds substantial risk. **Ment CoC** is another backup option. Keep successor away from active LT attack and do not count personal Tactical Awareness as global Orders.

See [cross-game Lieutenant, Chain of Command and Order-package tiers](../../list-building/command-units-apex-and-value.md). **Use the linked authoritative roster and Fireteam chart in the linked authoritative reference for exact legality.**

### Mission specialist / button-pusher selections (N5.3)

**Strider SO27/0 or FO29/0** is Camo/FD+20/NWI/NCO: a self-delivering specialist who can spend **Raveneye LT(+1) Orders**. **Raveneye FO base12, CoC18, LT17** are different mission/command options; CoC18 is cheap protected succession and scorer. **Vidoc FTO Doctor19/0 + NCO** moves with allowed Fireteams and heals while scoring; **Ment CoC** can fill a cheaper alternate backup. Choose distributed routes over one big attacking team on far-apart console missions.

[Cross-faction apex and points-value guide](../../list-building/mission-specialists-and-button-pushers-apex-value.md). Use **the linked authoritative roster and Fireteam chart** for exact availability.

### N5.3 airborne / backline-strike archetypes and trades (2026-10-23)

**Jamie Rover Parachutist/Decoy 53/0** is a premium surprise edge assault/mission threat where this roster permits it; **ordinary Parachutist cannot enter enemy DZ**, and 53pt deserves multiple critical targets or late score. **Nightshade Hidden FD+20** is safer than a rolled Combat Jump into defended roofs but may cost time reaching enemy DZ. **Strider Camo FD+20 NCO** can breach after Raveneye LT(+1) supplies personal NCO Orders. **Raveneye CoC18 vs LT17** are separate defensive command profiles, **not a combat-jumping CoC**.

[Full N5.3 apex/value and delivery-probability tier guide](../../list-building/airborne-and-enemy-dz-attackers-apex-value.md). Current roster/profile restrictions and canonical Fireteam chart in the linked authoritative reference govern legal choices.

### Active-turn gunfighter cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Active-turn shooting choices:** **Firebat Thunderbolt/LRL36/0** gives a zero-SWC Mimetism(-6), +1SD 20-5 **non-TAG aerial gun** that creates fast side LoF; **S7/No Cover** makes hostile Repeaters and missiles a serious risk. **Silverstar HMG37/1.5 Tactical Awareness** is the more conventional **non-TAG** 2W Fireteam long gun when the flyer cannot approach safely. Combine the chosen attack with a scoring specialist moving under the same legal Fireteam activations rather than paying for too many redundant guns. [Gunfighter value with separate TAG table](../../list-building/active-turn-gunfighters-apex-and-value.md).

### TAG tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Wrecker** is unusually high **value**: **51-point HRL+1B** punishes bunching and hard targets, while Spitfire/HMG versions switch to more conventional direct attacks. TacAware helps fund aggression without reducing your specialists. It is not an HRMC/Zeta replacement—use TLB's other threats to open the lane and a Wrecker to exploit and hold it. In the 2025 TAG transcript, hosts highlight the lethality of the HRL's attack modes.

For the two-tier **apex/points-value** comparison, TAG counterplay, matchup matrix and direct transcript sources: [N5.3 TAG field guide](../../list-building/tags.md). Treat current profiles, points/SWC and canonical Fireteam data in the linked authoritative reference as authoritative.

### Vehicle (VH) tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Firebat AVA1:** **36/0 AP SMG+Thunderbolt+LRL** adds 0-SWC short-to-long coverage, or **35/0 MULTI Rifle/Riotstopper/Akrylat/Blitzen** provides nonlethal attacks. **NCO** makes an available Raveneye LT(+1 Order) valuable for aerial repositioning or defensive Suppression; Mimetism(-6)/+1 Special Die make active engagements reliable against weak units. It is **not** Wrecker TAG, and at BS13 cannot replace the primary heavy-gun/ground-scoring architecture. Older Shepherd analysis rates its value and Suppression option higher than pure raw power.

See [VH apex/value tier list and matchup guide](../../list-building/vehicles-vh.md). **Current profiles, AVA and canonical Fireteam chart in the linked authoritative reference remain authoritative.**

### Hacker and KHD tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Waverider HD + Oblivion(+1B) 22/0.5 or KHD 20/0**, both with Pitcher, combines **15–5 MOV, Mimetism(-3), BTS6** and legal **Silverstars** access: the HD attacks HI/TAG, the KHD counters hacker networks. **Kappa HD13/0.5** provides a very cheap second control specialist; **Ment FTO HD20/0.5** and **PSI-Cop HD24/0.5 vs KHD23/0** provide team/mission alternatives. **Nightshade KHD34/0** pays for Hidden Deployment and close-range physical kill potential rather than pure hacking power. **Why:** a Waverider can project an accurate Pitcher while moving with a relevant team, but **TinBot/team legality** must follow the canonical Silverstars row. Upgrade **Oblivion(+1B)** matters in active anti-HI/TAG turns, unlike a plain KHD.

See the [cross-game apex/value Hacker/KHD tier guide](../../list-building/hackers-and-killer-hackers.md). **The sectorial's current profiles, AVA and canonical Fireteam chart in the linked authoritative reference remain authoritative; device upgrades are profile-specific.**


### Cheerleader / Order-generation tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

The N5.3 [cheerleader/order-generator apex and value guide](../../list-building/cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md) contrasts Orders-per-point, passive utility, matchup dependence, and opposing transcript opinions. **Use this roster for legal AVA, exact profile costs, Training and Fireteam restrictions.**

- 7pt Kyttä — cheap mobile Flash/Repeater/Mimetism Regular Order; check local AVA and deployment safety.
- 8pt FuzzBot — Baggage/Repeater plus Regular Order, excellent if the S4 footprint can be concealed.
- 12pt Kappa — S2 baseline Regular Order, with modest Specialist/Commlink upgrades; compare their actual mission utility against cheaper REMs.
- Waverider — fast forward network/multi-role piece rather than pure passive Order filler; invest for actual hacking and scoring payoff.
- Warcor — a 3pt Irregular Flash ARO speedbump, not a transferable pool Order without actual conversion.

**Decision:** first buy enough protected *transferable* Regular Orders for the actual Combat Group attacker/scoring plan, then pay for genuine role compression. Irregular/Impetuous, LT, Tactical Awareness and Peripheral bodies are different resources. Account for a packed S3/S4 deployment zone and mandatory Fireteam members.

**Raveneye FO+Minelayer 12/0** starts an **E/M Mine or Cybermine** at the DZ/route while remaining an Order-generating Forward Observer/Deactivator. **Moonraker15/.5 Shock Minelayer** is a cheap FD(+20) outer-layer REM/adhesive speed bump, not a home-DZ 360º sentry; **Warcor 360º3/0** is the disposable edge Flash. **Peeler/other reactive guns** should cover the *next* square after the initial mine. **Counter:** do not conflate **Raveneye 12pt FO** with its 17pt LT(+1) or 18pt CoC profiles; overload of static mines can leave elite Torchlight action teams short on active Orders or scoring.

**Torchlight** benefits primarily from **Yudbot Servants (3/0 each)** extending local Doctor/Engineer from rear to two separate damaged Silverstar/Prime or TAG casualty fronts, **not** from Sirius/Raptor imported from Vanilla/Starmada (they are absent in pinned standard roster). **Firebat support/Ancillary** must follow its current vehicle/TAG-specific subordinate deployment when present; do not assume two Orders or synchronized gun without an actual listed bot. **Silverstar Fireteams are multiple independent troopers activated together by link rules, not Peripheral Synchronized:** don't count shared movement as extra independent body for 1/10 slot. **Counter:** if not running expensive recoverable armor, a cheap Regular guard beats 3pt+ Servant that never heals; if running Prime/TAG, wide repair can save several Orders.

#### Cross-faction disagreements and rules safeguards

1. **No evidence of a literal whole-unit universal “never take” in all three current rosters.** The clearest strong negative is **Torchlight ordinary Silverstar Rover-heavy construction**, followed by profile-specific **Torchlight Strider Red Fury**, **Starmada Casanova**, and **Starmada combat-role Tian Gǒu**.

The **Hellblazer** or **Silverstar Rover** supplies the required-source entry; Waverider and allowed Wildcards follow the chart. Older N4 Prime+Hippolyta+Hellblazer teams cannot be copied as legal N5.3 Fireteams.
5. **Only current FTO options actually listed on the chart link:** Torchlight Vidocq FTO 1/2/3, StormBot FTO 2/3, Jamie Rover FTO 1/3; Starmada Nyoka FTO 3/4, Tian Gǒu FTO 1/3/4/6, etc. The full repository chart is the authority, not these illustrative subsets.

8. **N4 tournament evidence** (notably the 2024 Torchlight WIP12 winner) supports a *role architecture*—Moonrakers, camouflage, Prime, Hippolyta—but **does not verify N5.3 Fireteams or prove a current win-rate advantage**.

| **Torchlight — ordinary Silverstar Rovers** | **Do not categorically skip the whole unit** | Shepherd often fields **zero**, but a distinct TinBot, specialist, TacAware gun or Fireteam role can justify one. His critique is *overbuying Rovers as a default Core*, not that every Rover profile has no job. |

**Do not convert** the Strider Red Fury, Raveneye CoC or Sirius Hacker **profile** avoid into a whole-unit buying ban. Opposing opinions on Zeta, Wrecker and Go-Pod-like flyers remain material. [Cross-faction shortlist](../buy-lists/model-purchase-avoid-summary-2026.md).

#### Worst-value, rarely necessary and proxy-first miniature purchases by O-12 army (2026-10-30)

**Purpose:** Which models should you **not bother buying immediately**, which **genuinely criticized profiles** can simply be represented by an existing model, and which **excellent but specialized / alternate sculpts** should be proxy-tested rather than bought for inventory completeness? Cover all three current N5.3 O-12 armies: [Vanilla O-12](../../../sectorials/o-12/o-12.md), [Starmada](../../../sectorials/o-12/starmada.md) and [Torchlight Brigade](../../../sectorials/o-12/torchlight-brigade.md). **No universally unusable whole unit** was demonstrated. *Rare* below means a **mission-dependent or non-default role in the reviewed lists**, not statistically measured play frequency.

**Evidence and decision labels:** **Direct negative** = a named speaker criticizes that specific unit, profile or default use in previously checked [recordings](#recordingsource-register-and-quality-notes); **Contested** preserves a counter-review or successful alternative role. **Defer model / proxy first** is an *inferred buying priority*, **not** an invented reviewer quote. **Avoid the profile, not the unit** applies when the troop's other options are genuinely good. **Extra-copy proxy** means buy the first model for a useful core role but do not blindly purchase multiple sculpt/weapon variants or fill AVA. The [September 2026 N5.3/Army 7.26246.159 profiles and canonical Fireteams]() override old N4/N5 source lists. **FTO** on a profile does not by itself grant team access.

##### 4. Across O-12 — even excellent units may be better off as proxies or shared models

**Ownership check:** no current authoritative O-12-specific inventory was found at the expected repository paths for **Vanilla, Starmada or Torchlight**. Consequently **no owned/available body count is inferred**. The recommendations distinguish **first needed model**, **alternate non-simultaneous weapon sculpt**, **additional actual trooper**, and **companion/peripheral body**. A cross-sectorial model can be *physically reused* for a **legal** unit in another list, but never magically grants the other army its excluded unit.

**Practical shopping rule:** (1) Buy/represent the **first** efficient **Ment, Raveneye, Moonraker where legal, Kappa HD, Varangian, Oko/cheap Copperbot, mission specialist** you actually want; (2) choose **one main ranged platform** (e.g. Vanilla Gamma/Epsilon, Starmada Bronze/Epsilon, Torchlight Prime/Wrecker); (3) **proxy** a *new specialized expensive hero, VH/TAG, optional extra gun, SideBot/peripheral package* for multiple games; (4) buy only after your lists repeatedly need it *simultaneously* and a lawful same-size model isn't available. On all proxy choices, confirm **current ITS/event policy, silhouette, base, distinct physical figure per simultaneously present model, team eligibility, exact selected profile and friendly-game opponent agreement**. Proxies change plastic, **not Army legality, equipment, Orders or Fireteam privileges**.

**Buying verdict by army:** **Vanilla:** few whole-model skips; proxy-first **Cuervo, unplanned Wrecker/Zeta/Firebat, special Lynx/Nightshade, duplicate premium guns**, and skip the **Sirius Hacker / Razor Mono / Vidocq Sniper** default profile. **Starmada:** **Casanova** is strongest full-model defer; proxy **Tian Gǒu combat variant, Firebat, Zeta, extra Bronze/Betatrooper or Nyoka**, keeping Raveneye/Bronze/Bluecoat/Šarko objective engine. **Torchlight:** strongest deferrals **excess Silverstar Rover, Sonya, Jamie, Firebat**, plus the **Strider Red Fury/Raveneye CoC** profile and illegal **Prime/Core or Beasthunter FTO** assumptions. None of these conclusions turns a valuable unit's alternate, distinct use into a blanket ban.
