## N5.3 tactical cross-references, battle reports and purchasing audit

### Hacker and KHD tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Wardriver HD14/1** with Zero Pain is a low-points but relatively **high-SWC** anti-heavy controller/mission specialist. A responsive **KHD hunter** is not part of the usual native TAC network, so avoid trying to beat Interventors/Combined at their own repeater war. **Disagreement in existing TAC analysis:** WIP12/Shepherd value its Disco Baller and tech-side mission utility, while Swiss Miss warns the SWC can crowd strong attack guns. **Decision:** pay for Wardriver only when a TAG/HI-heavy opponent, relevant classified or a real vision-control route creates an Order-saving task that a cheaper Forward Observer cannot do.

See the [cross-game apex/value Hacker/KHD tier guide](../../list-building/hackers-and-killer-hackers.md). **The sectorial's current profiles, AVA and canonical Fireteam chart above remain authoritative; device upgrades are profile-specific.**

### ARO apex/value cross-reference (2026-10-23)

See [ARO units: N5.3 apex/value tiers, hidden-versus-visible and deployment/matchups](../../list-building/aro-units-apex-and-value.md). The **current profile details here** control exact cost/SWC, Training and Fireteam eligibility; do not import another faction's link or specialist upgrades.

- **Tankhunter Camo ML/Panzerfaust/Autocannon** — hidden-information threat and serious anti-armor; Minelayer closes approach, 1W/expensive gun remains fragile
- **Traktor Mul support** — rare Ariadna cheap reload/guided/total-reaction choices; only buy a real Guided program if FO route is viable
- **3pt Warcor Flash** — Irregular very-cheap ARO tax, not a transferable pool Order

**Choose by circumstance:** a hard long gun matters only when the opponent must cross its arc; cheaper mines, Camo markers, templates, Flash, DTWs and hacking force movement or risk without committing an entire attack plan. Budget a second layer and preserved counterattack, check pie-slicing, hidden Order cost, anti-visual bypass and repair routes.

### N5.3 airborne / enemy-DZ profile and value choices (2026-10-23)

**Kazak Spetsnaz Parachutist BSG27/0 or AP SMG27/0** creates a reliable side-edge attack with Decoy(1), Mimetism(-3), Marksmanship and D-Charges: target support/Order generators from an unexpected range band rather than frontally challenging ARO. The **Camo HMG36/1.5 and AP sniper32/0.5** are different non-airborne profiles; no free merger of Camo+HMG and Parachutist. Unlike **Van Zant**, the Spetsnaz uses **ordinary Parachutist and cannot enter directly in enemy DZ**. Preserve a cheap forward specialist and exit Order for scoring.

[Cross-game N5.3 airborne/rear-strike apex-value and landing-risk tiers](../../list-building/airborne-and-enemy-dz-attackers-apex-value.md). **Use this roster and canonical Fireteam chart for exact legality.**

### Warbands and inexpensive trading roles — N5.3

**Irmandinho Engineer+Chain+Smoke8/0** is the premier utility trader: has a relevant objective/repair function even if no target gets within template range. **Dog-Warrior21/0** uses 15-10/Super-Jump/3W Transmutation/PH Grenade Speculative Fire for **multiple Orders of attack**, not pure sacrificial efficiency. **Carmen+Bâtard17/0** provides bike Smoke+Sensor synchronized counter to enemy Camo. **Ioann Bann32** gives another high-end large-base close-in threat; expensive compared with Irmandinho. Compare **Dynamo** mobility vs tech/midfield mission instead of stuffing every Irregular WB into a single Group 2.

[Cross-game warband/cheap-trader apex and value tiers, targets and command packages](../../list-building/warbands-and-cheap-trading-units-apex-value.md). Profile-specific sectorial legality/Fireteams above control all selections.

### Doctor / Engineer / Recovery cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Irmandinho Engineer8** is exceptional cheap Smoke/trading plus occasional forward technical rescue, but usually **cannot safely replace Dozer13 + Remontnik2** staged for STR gun/vehicle recovery. **112 Doctor11**, **Motorized112 19** and **Kazak Doktor12** are cheap WIP13 VITA alternatives; motorized 20-10 MOV covers wider forward zones but risks open crossing. On No-Cube targets Doctor failure is a **35% chance to lose patient**, with no Cube token bailout. [Doctor/Engineer recovery odds and tier guide](../../list-building/doctors-engineers-and-recovery-apex-value.md).

### Close-combat assassins and counter-CC — N5.3 (2026-10-23)

**Dog-Warrior21** (15-10, Super-Jump, Berserk, Smoke, 3W in current roster) and **Ioann Bann** are the meaningful *multi-Order close assault* choices; **Irmandinho8** instead gives Smoke and an occasional AP-CC technical trade at minimal cost, but **Irregular/Impetuous** exposure is real. **Spetsnaz CC21/MA2** is still mainly a camouflaged *gunfighter*, not a 29pt Monofilament assassination piece; **TankHunter/Strelok** are ranged/Camo control, not high-CC. Counter incoming enemy CC with mine/camo screen, long-range guns, and templates rather than a Veteran Kazak CC17 duel. [Close-combat apex/value, positioning and counter-CC](../../list-building/dedicated-close-combat-assassins-apex-value.md).

### Anti-TAG / Anti-HI without hacking — N5.3 (2026-10-23)

**Beasthunter9/15 Panzerfaust/HFT** and **Dozer14 Blitzen** are low-cost deterrents; **Dynamo14 Panzerfaust+Smoke GL** may reach a side-angle with Impetuous/motorcycle risk. **TankHunter33 AP HMG** is repeatable B4 AP gun; **TankHunter36 Portable Autocannon** is AP+EXP B2 **without assuming Vanilla Ariadna's +1B**. **Scout E/M Minelayer28** traps short approach, **Veteran Kazak AP HMG45** adds high survivability. Prioritize contact safety over pure PS math. [Non-hacking armor-killer value rankings and tactical matchups](../../list-building/anti-tag-anti-heavy-infantry-non-hacking-apex-value.md).

### Tactical cross-reference — indirect fire (2026-10-23)

**Kuryer Katyusha15/1** is one of the game's apex **independent Speculative Fire** choices: +3 Range band to 24″, B1 Long Skill, Warhorse/Baggage/Minelayer/Guided. **Traktor Katyusha Guided9/1** wins cheapest conditional payoff if Chasseur/Strelok/Dynamo/other FO already creates Targeted. **Kuryer Uragan18/1** is the Total Reaction defensive variant; **don't** claim its benefits simultaneously with Katyusha. Shepherd favors several safe Kuryer speculative shots over expensive multi-Order Spotlight+Guided setup in many *midfield* situations; WIP12 likes Kuryer flexibility but six saved points on Traktor can fund actual objectives. [Indirect Fire apex/value and media disagreements](../../list-building/indirect-fire-speculative-guided-apex-value.md).

### N5.3 objective holding, zone domination & carried-objective choices

**Veteran Kazak FO36/0** (Mimetism(-3), W1+NWI, ARM4, Warhorse, Sixth Sense, Immunity IMM-B/Shock, T2 Rifle/HFT) is a sturdy multipurpose terminal carrier; **Paramedic38/0** is separate. **Frontovik Paramedic23/0** Warhorse+Mimetism(-3) is the budget linked holder, **Strelok FO17/0** Camo/Decoy/FD+20 excellent retrieval but not invisibility while carrying. **Strelok K-9 Specialist Operative26/0** + synchronized K-9 Antipode makes two physical threats with *one shared Order*, not two independent orders. **Ratnik HRL41/1.5 / AP Spitfire46/1.5** has full armor W2/ARM6, Transmutation battle-ravaged (still physically active) and TA: high zone VP gun, **not an ordinary Supplies specialist**. **Counter:** exposed Ratnik draws anti-armor/guns; two Streloks plus one Veteran FO can preserve several quadrant options for similar points.

### Anti-Tech — enemy visors, Mimetism, markers and Impersonation (2026-10-23)

**K-9 Specialist26 with synchronized Sensor Antipode** or a legal **Antipode Assault Pack** gets Sensor to enemy Camo/Hidden markers; the *15-15 movement and scouting route* matters more than the keyword alone. **Voronin WIP14 Sensor** guards a local LT/command area, not the entire midfield. **Wardriver Disco Baller16/1** is scarce **Eclipse** against hostile MSV/Marksmanship; ordinary Smoke/cheap Irmandinho cannot blind MSV2. **Competing TAK judgments:** Antipode Sensor pressure is worth a dedicated approach when multiple markers actually block the mission; simply **ignore a low-value marker** if Discovering it would consume scoring Orders.

### N5.3 peripheral / synchronized / multi-body apex-value update

**Antipode pack26/0** = Handler+3 fast Camouflaged/Sensor/AP melee Control Peripherals, **Regular+Tactical Awareness** from handler; only **one Spearhead** performs target-requiring Attack with controller per Order, other Antipodes can Move; no triple independent CC. **Strelok K-9 Specialist Operative26/0** = STRELOK SMG/Camo/Decoy/FD+20 + synchronized **K-9 Antipode with Sensor/Climbing+**, which can approach separate angles within Controller Coherency; **17/0 FO** has **no dog**. **Dozer/Irmandinho Engineer + 2pt Remontnikbot Servant** remote recovery can position on separate flanks without Engineer walking; one target per Order. **Opposing:** 26pt Antipode pack is stronger high-speed Sensor/CC strike engine, 26pt Strelok+K-9 is stronger **actual specialist plus one dog** carrying mission when alive; `Control` Spearhead ≠ `Synchronized` two full attack targets.

#### Classification and evidence standards

- **Must-have / near-must:** repeated first-choice inclusion across independently built example lists, or an explicit reviewer “always / most lists / auto-include” judgment. **Near-must is not universal:** a role can be indispensable without any one profile being mandatory.
- **Go-to:** repeatedly attractive price/role efficiency. Not all go-to units belong in the same 300-point list; missions, fire lanes, SWC, Orders and competing packages decide.
- **Never take / strong avoid:** a directly stated negative review of a **specific profile or construction**, ideally with a viable same-roster alternative. Use **conditional** or **contested** if the profile has a niche, there is dissent or the source is pre-rework. There is **no evidenced blanket ban on an entire Ariadna unit family**.
- **Evidence:** **explicit** = specific presenter says so; **repeat** = multiple independently constructed sample lists; **reasoned** = synthesis of recorded strengths/weaknesses plus current reference, **not** a claimed quotation.
- A recorded list choice alone does not prove “most players” use a unit. Transcripts may mangle Ariadna proper names, so check names and legality against the current repository. The standard lists here do **not** import `L'Équipe Argent` Reinforcements, which have separate charts.

#### Worst, uncommon and proxy-first purchases by sectorial (2026-10-30)

**Scope and confidence.** This answers a different question from the unit-role tables above: **“Which whole miniature can I skip buying, which uncommon specialist should I proxy until I actually use it, and which *bad profile* should I avoid without rejecting its whole unit?”** An **AVA limit is not a shopping target**. The latest [N5.3 roster]() and its **Fireteam, silhouette, peripherals and availability** rules override old recordings.

**Buying labels:**
- **Skip by default; proxy if needed:** the **normal whole-model job** has explicit negative commentary or an unusually weak price/role comparison. The exception is recorded; **no Ariadna model is proven mathematically useless in every scenario**.
- **Proxy first:** a full unit with a credible specialty, but you should establish *a specific list and mission use* before buying its sculpt; this is an **inferred shopping recommendation**, not a presenter's alleged quotation.
- **Avoid profile only:** the unit has legitimate alternate profiles. **Never extrapolate to all its miniatures.**
- **One enough / proxy the extra:** owning/fielding multiple is an elective skew rather than a default; distinguish genuine duplicate bodies from alternate loadout sculpts. **Contested** flags genuine source disagreement or tournament examples.

**Tournament proxy policy:** Always match **current model footprint/base and Silhouette**, reveal every declared proxy to your opponent, and check the **current-season ITS and organizer rules** before bringing conversions or another faction's miniature.
