### Command-unit apex/value cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Domaru LT(+1) 23/0** is a cheap route to extra personal Order pressure; **Kempeitai CoC 17/0 BSG / 20/0 Combi** is excellent budget insurance for aggressive Ōban commanders. A Kempeitai successor should guard **different ingress** from the LT's main assault. These are roster-legal Ōban options; don't infer that every other JSA sectorial has identical access.

See [cross-game Lieutenant, CoC and Order-economy tiers](../../list-building/command-units-apex-and-value.md). **Refer to the linked pinned roster and Fireteam chart for profile legality.**

### Mission specialist / button-pusher selections (N5.3)

The most important choice is **how many independent turn-3 scoring routes survive the attack**, not the number of Samurai combat models. **Kempeitai CoC17/0** supplies inexpensive Specialist/command insurance for Domaru/Hatamoto-style attacking LT plans when roster-legal. **Ninja/Kurayami hidden specialists** shorten contested-console routes; **Aragoto KHD** is fast but more vulnerable. Do not import Vanilla JSA Shikami or other profile unless this Ōban roster lists them.

[Cross-faction apex and points-value guide](../../list-building/mission-specialists-and-button-pushers-apex-value.md). Use **this roster and Fireteam chart** for exact availability.

### N5.3 airborne / backline-strike archetypes and trades (2026-10-23)

**Oniwaban29/0 and Shinobu44/.5** are near-deployment **Hidden/Infiltration Monofilament** enemies and force early opponent screening; the enemy-half roll is optional, not an automatic DZ start. **Oniwaban LT30/1 / Shinobu LT44/.5** must be on-table as Model/Marker at the LoL check—not sitting Hidden. **Kempeitai CoC17** protects a risky on-table LT but is not an airborne assassin. Check this **Ōban roster** for current **AD biker/other airborne** options; don't import Vanilla JSA Teishin by name unless listed.

### Active-turn gunfighter cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Active-turn shooting choices:** **O-Yoroi MULTI HMG78/1.5** is a full **TAG** BS14/3STR/TacAware long-range firepower anchor with CrazyKoala/CC close protection; strong on open tables but poor value if the huge base must navigate tight hacker-controlled corridors. **Shikami42/0 or MULTI Rifle47/0** is a **non-TAG** Mimetism(-6), Climbing/Super-Jump/Eclipse mobile specialist that can win on **movement and scoring**, not raw HMG volume. The strongest melee trooper often opens the route **before** choosing whether the TAG's gun needs to fire. Preserve the Kobaruto/Engineer/support and enough mission specialists, rather than allowing one TAG to absorb all activation budget; use its long-range threats to clear lanes for the infiltration units. ### Hacker and KHD tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Kyoshō KHD20/0 versus HD20/0.5**, **Keisotsu HD16/0.5** and **Ninja KHD26/0 (Hidden)/HD33/0.5** give Oban both inexpensive backline hacking and expensive *infiltrating* mission specialists. **Why:** Kyoshō KHD supplies Trinity/Cybermask and remains a scoring troop; choose HD if the main opponent threat is hackable TAG/HI instead. Ninja's higher cost is a **deployment/hidden scoring premium**, not superior bare hacking dice. ### Cheerleader / Order-generation tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

See the N5.3 [cross-game apex/value rankings and transcript-backed competing assessments](../../list-building/cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md). The linked current roster and official Fireteam definitions remain authoritative; unit access/cost may differ from vanilla.

- Keisotsu — inexpensive S2 Regular bodies, line-FT material and possible utility upgrades; optimize the cheapest legal role rather than premium heavy guns.
- Servant REM — does not generate a new Order, even when helping an Engineer cover more ground.
- Yuriko/Engineer — buy for recovery and mission support, not cheap generic Order generation.
- Karakuri — durable multiple-role attacker/specialist, not a minimal-cost backline cheerleader; compare total Order demand.
- 3pt Warcor — very useful Irregular Flash ARO/cheap screen, NOT an ordinary transferable Order.

**Selection rule:** Count deployed, transferable Regular Orders per Combat Group, not total models or restricted bonus Orders; choose the lowest-cost protected Regular profile that *also* contributes a relevant Repeater, Baggage, Fireteam, template, ARO or Specialist job. Check Group slots and DZ hiding space before buying a third large-base REM or replacing a needed scoring piece.

### N5.3 midfield area denial / speed bumps (tactical synthesis)

**Reactive deception, not universal infiltrator:** **Ryūken-9 HRL 23/1.5** begins with Minelayer Shock Mine, Decoy(1), one-use Camo and Mimetism(-6). Hold a long lane so enemy must Discover or detour. **Ryūken 22/.5 SMG** is the *separate* FD(+20) midfield denial option; cheaper SWC but poor long-distance reach. **Counterview:** its 1W/1.5SWC HRL may be bypassed by templates/Sensor or cost too much for objectives; consider durable Karakuri and other Camo instead. **O-Yoroi** Koala/Minelayer is a **TAG** and priced as a premium gun/armour package, not a cheap speed bump.

**Shinobu44/.5** (Hidden, Infiltration PH19, CC25/MA5, Monofilament) has the highest precision-assassin ceiling; **Oniwaban SMG29/0** is the value substitute (CC24/MA4, Mono/E/M). **Saito33/0** trades Mono for Smoke, T2 shotgun and EXP; **Musashi21 Irregular or 25 FTO Regular** wins pure CC25/MA5/EXP at cheap price but has to **walk**; **Shikami42** trades one-hit kill for Eclipse, Super-Jump, Climbing, 2W and scoring. **Yojimbo bike20 vs foot19** are defensive smoke/Koala counter-CC, not another Hidden killer. #### How to read the labels

- **Must-have / near-auto-include:** a creator expressly says "every list", "auto-include", "always", or several **actually played** builds repeatedly include it; when even one credible competing architecture omits the piece, call it "**near-must, not compulsory**" instead of inventing consensus.
- **Go-to:** source-reviewed as a repeatedly strong selection with a clear role that remains worthwhile even though an independent successful list can omit it. **Never imply all go-tos fit in one list.**
- **Never take / strong avoid:** commentator expressly rejects the **specific profile**, preferably for a direct substitute **in that same roster**, confirmed in current Army. **No faction has a defensible blanket "never take" list for every profile of an entire multi-option unit.** Where another interview disagrees, place it in **contested** rather than pretending an objective veto.
- **Specific source** is cited in the row (video/podcast, and approximate podcast minute when identifiable). These are opinions, not N5.3 legality. #### Worst-value, circumstantial and proxy-first miniature purchases by JSA army (2026-10-30)

**Scope:** Ōban, Shindenbutai, and Vanilla JSA are **three distinct current armies**, each with separate legal profiles and Fireteam charts. This section distinguishes **a genuinely criticized profile**, **a seldom-needed specialist whose dedicated miniature can be deferred**, **a strong unit worth proxying before buying duplicates**, and **a multi-body/size/legality trap**. The [pinned Ōban](../../../sectorials/jsa/oban.md), [Shinden](../../../sectorials/jsa/shindenbutai.md), and [Vanilla JSA](../../../sectorials/jsa/jsa.md) references (N5.3, Army 7.26246.159, September 2026) control exact rules. Actual direct negative evidence is from previously checked [YouTube/podcast transcripts](#highest-value-primary-recordings-mcp-transcripts-directly-retrieved-in-this-audit); **buy/proxy judgment is a reasoned inference unless called a direct reviewer verdict**. *Rare/circumstantial* means an unusual role in reviewed standard list architectures, **not measured event usage**. **No fully evidenced universal, whole-unit never-take exists.**

**Buying labels:** **Avoid profile only** = wrong gun/task for the cost, not a ban on the unit. **Defer whole dedicated model / proxy first** = a specialized valid choice whose role should be demonstrated before purchasing. **Proxy extra copy** = one model is good, but buying to AVA/owning every weapon sculpt is unnecessary. **Contested** = source disagreement or current roster changes defeat absolute rankings. A proxy must still match required silhouette/base and represent every actual separately deployed figure; check current event rules.
