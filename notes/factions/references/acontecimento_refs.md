### Mission specialist choices — N5.3 cross-game evaluation

An objective-heavy Acontecimento list needs **actual FO/Hacker/Engineer and Doctor** options on independent paths, plus a protected late closer rather than every point in Bagh-Mari/Regular ranged guns. **Pathfinder-like FO/Sensor REMs**, where present in this roster, give economical button and Camo-clearing utility, but compare them to any **infiltrating/Forward-Deploying Specialist** and the required Fireteam anchors. Do not import Vanilla PanO or Kestrel Wildcard permissions.

See [Mission Specialists / Button Pushers apex and value tiers](../../list-building/mission-specialists-and-button-pushers-apex-value.md). This roster and its Fireteam chart are the final authority for exact options.

### Active-turn gunfighter cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Active-turn shooting choices:** **Bagh-Mari HMG30/1.5** supplies a BS13/MSV1/Mimetism(-3) **non-TAG** legal team gun, useful for Cover and Mimetism-heavy mid/long ranges. **Acontecimento Regular Spitfire16/1** is a very cheap second gun but lacks resilience for hard AROs. **Tikbalang HMG69/1.5** is the separate **TAG** purchase: BS15, Mimetism(-3), Climbing and TacAware, useful to make repeat strong shots at different angles. Buying a big TAG is worthwhile only when its unique geometry/durability solves a problem the two cheap guns cannot. [Separate TAG/non-TAG gunfighter tiers](../../list-building/active-turn-gunfighters-apex-and-value.md).

### TAG tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Tikbalang** is the reliable mobile value assault: Climbing Plus, Mimetism, heavy gun and close defence. **Dragões** is a dedicated ranged anchor when the table rewards a heavy fire lane over mobility/marker safety. Compare both with the actual Moto-mecanizados Fireteam and Engineer cost, not chassis alone; do not use old N4 cost claims.

Cross-faction **apex vs points-value** comparisons, matchup table, and direct MCP-reviewed podcast/video sources: [N5.3 TAG field guide](../../list-building/tags.md). Current profile and Fireteam legality above remain authoritative.

### Hacker and KHD tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**17/0.5 Acontecimento Regular HD/FastPanda** is inexpensive proactive network access. **Scylla 32/0.5 HD + Trinity and Devabot** combines an anti-heavy/anti-Hacker device with a companion Repeater/flamethrower; the EVO Scylla profile supplies *different* supportware, not ordinary HD attacks. **Naga 27/0 KHD or 29/0.5 HD + Deployable Repeater** trades cost for Camouflage/Infiltration, valuable if the mission requires a dangerous midfield console. **Choice:** Regular for general control, Naga for physical scoring, Scylla if synchronized threats and upgraded programs are actually useful; protect premium support from enemy Repeaters.

See the [cross-game apex/value Hacker/KHD tier guide](../../list-building/hackers-and-killer-hackers.md). **The sectorial's current profiles, AVA and canonical Fireteam chart above remain authoritative; device upgrades are profile-specific.**

- 8pt Mulebot — Baggage/Re­peater Regular Order, excellent if support reloads are useful and S4 bases fit.

### ARO apex/value cross-reference (2026-10-23)

See [Best ARO Units — N5.3 apex and cost-value tiers](../../list-building/aro-units-apex-and-value.md) for gunfighter vs hidden-trap vs cheap Order-tax comparisons, when each wins, transcript disagreements and counterplay. **The roster, prices/AVA and canonical Fireteam chart above remain authoritative**; a shared unit's permissions do not transfer between armies.

- **Sierra Thunderbolt TR** — 18pt/0SWC multi-arc Regular ARO where profile legal; add Engineer/recovery if its lane matters repeatedly
- **Warcor Flash Pulse** — 3pt Irregular zero-SWC Order-tax ARO; never count as a generic Regular Order
- **Fugazi Flash/Repeater** — 7pt Regular cheap secondary ARO and hacking net; not a main gun

### Doctor / Engineer / Recovery cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Recovery:** **Machinist/Palbot** supports **Tikbalang/REM** and can restore either Unconscious level of Remote Presence on one successful WIP roll; **Trauma-Doc/Palbot** is the alternative for VITA infantry/ARO pieces. If an Acontecimento team already includes a Paramedic, compare target PH and safe contact rather than automatically paying for a second dedicated Doctor. ### N5.3 midfield area denial / speed bumps (tactical synthesis)

**13/0 Regular Sensor + Shock Mine Minelayer** is a cheap, 0-SWC **DZ** tripwire protecting against AD/Impersonation while supplying a Regular Order. **Peacemaker 24/.5 Auxbot-3B Minelayer** instead places an FD mine/Repeater and close template at deployment; **Naga 25/.5 Shock Minelayer** is a distinct infiltrating Boarding Shotgun option. **Naga 26/.5 Monofilament Minelayer** offers Camo/Infiltration and a potentially decisive **conditional anti-TAG/HI choke ambush**, but no Specialist skill and **not a broad Top 10 area-denial pick**. [**Swiss Miss (~57:00)**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hlmVXGHskS4&t=3420s) finds Mono entertaining but does not favor it over general Naga loadouts; [**Dante (~39:30)**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GKR_cd3oEFE&t=2370s) prefers Peacemaker as Minelayer and Naga KHD. **Naga KHD 27/0 or FO 25/0** is generally more useful for scoring and mission flexibility; reserve Mono for a premium enemy forced through a narrow lane. The Regular is DZ rather than midfield defense, and 27/0 active Peacemaker trades prepaid defense for attack.

[Cross-game apex/value, matchups, and transcript disagreements](../../list-building/midfield-area-denial-and-speed-bumps-apex-value.md). Tactical evaluation, not a modification to Army profiles or Fireteam legality.

### N5.3 DZ defense / anti-alpha-strike tactical choices

**Acontecimento Regular 13/0** (Shock Mine **Minelayer + Sensor + Triangulated Fire**) is the strongest low-SWC **deployment-zone** tripwire: a **Regular Order** and a prepaid mine guard the corner while Sensor can actively probe infiltrators. Pair with **Sierra Thunderbolt TR+360º 18/0**, if purchased, for a second answer to enemy DZ-edge/airborne attack. **Peacemaker 24/.5 + Auxbot-3B Minelayer** instead provides forward mine/Repeater/close template but may be better in midfield. **Opposing views (Swiss Miss / MetaChemistry):** utility 12pt FO and 20pt Sapper sniper compete for team slots; don't build a passive castle when offense and specialists win the scenario.

[DZ Defenders — whole-game apex/value, matchups, and MCP transcript debate](../../list-building/dz-defenders-alpha-strike-and-impersonation-apex-value.md). Tactical commentary; the pinned profile/Fireteam tables above are authoritative.

### Close-combat assassins and counter-CC — N5.3 (2026-10-23)

**Acontecimento's Dart35/0** has Camouflage/FD(+20), NWI, Climbing and **AP SMG/Viral bow/E/M grenades**, but **CC16**: excellent *ranged ambush*, not a dedicated MA4 melee assassin. **Naga** similarly uses Camo/Infiltration and mines, not intrinsic melee lethality. **Bagh-Mari** and **Dragoons TAG** gunfire control approach lanes; avoid direct CC against an Oniwaban/Speculo. Use visible cheap screens/mines to stop opponents reaching an expensive gunline. [CC apex/value and reach-versus-lethality comparisons](../../list-building/dedicated-close-combat-assassins-apex-value.md).

### N5.3 network projection — Pitchers, Repeaters, TinBots and disposable delivery

**Scylla+Charybdis32/.5** bundles an HD/Trinity/Pitcher and synchronized Repeater+template bot, while **Regular FO+Deployable Repeater12/0** or **HD+FastPanda17/.5** are inexpensive *place-then-project* tools (no free T0 repeater if not Minelayer). **Fugazi7/0** and **Pathfinder FO/Sensor/native Repeater** are Regular alternatives; **Peacemaker FD(+20) native Repeater** offers an immediate projection point and template/defensive fork. **Opponent view (MetaChemistry):** cheap utility Regulars and guns may do more than a fixed hacking castle, especially when enemy can't be hacked effectively.

### N5.3 objective holding, zone domination & carried-objective choices

**SAA** can put a **Peacemaker FD+20 Repeater+Auxbot23/0** plus **Regular FO+Deployable Repeater12/0** into an objective approach cheaply, with hard **Tikbalang** where currently roster-legal for quadrant VP. **Scylla HD/Trinity+Charybdis32/.5** is a 2-body hacking/DTW defender that protects an eligible nearby holder (Charybdis cannot inherit Scylla's Specialist qualification), and **Regular FO+Sensor/Minelayer** options should be checked per chosen profile. **Opposing MetaChemistry SAA:** many utility Regulars win by being in two quadrants while elite gun clears one, but a Minelayer+smoke/protection choke can pay for itself against early rush; don't treat a carried box as a marker.

### Anti-Tech — enemy visors, Mimetism, markers and Impersonation (2026-10-23)

**Regular13/0 Shock Minelayer + Sensor + Triangulated Fire** is exceptional cheap *regular-Order* anti-Camo/Hidden DZ insurance: pre-placed mine plus WIP+6 ZoC search; it isn't an Infiltrator. **Pathfinder14/0 Sensor/FO** is the better **mobile** search if the valuable Camo is beyond the home zone. **MSV** attack is an alternative when the target has already revealed but still imposes Mimetism; a long-skill Triangulated Fire gun ignores those roll penalties without needing a visor. **Opposing SAA reviews:** some value the 13pt guard's many jobs over another Sapper ARO, others need direct long-range firepower. Both are valid depending on how likely the enemy markers enter Sensor reach.

### N5.3 peripheral / synchronized / multi-body apex-value update

**Peacemaker23/0 HSG+Auxbot /24/.5 Minelayer+Auxbot /27/0 BSG+Auxbot** is the sectorial's true synchronized fork: FD+20/Repeater/Mim(-3), HFT bot forces enemy to choose which legal active model receives ARO. **23/0 BSG(+1B)** is an alternative with **no Auxbot**. **Scylla/Charybdis** are separate hacker/support-body architecture and must use their exact roster relationship, not automatically multiple independent Orders; when choosing Scylla Hacking Device with Trinity upgrade, count her minions/other combat group entries as printed. **Doctor/Engineer+3pt Palbot** covers distant repair at 1 target per Order, not multiple free activations. **Counter:** opponents can template Peacemaker and Auxbot together or Isolate REM; 2 Regular independent cheap troops may preserve more Orders on open tables.

#### Practical verdict

There are **only four direct source-backed near-universal assertions worth repeating as such**: **MO foot Montesa FTO20** (Shepherd), **Kestrel Magistrate+cheap Jackals** (Irregular Order speakers, with Tik LT exception), **Varuna Zulu KHD26** (Dante), and **SWF Peacemaker+Auxbot** (Swiss Miss). In **Vanilla, NCA and SAA**, the recording evidence supports **a highly efficient *role or package*** much more strongly than any individual compulsory profile.

The strongest no-default profile rejection examples backed by an actual recording are **MO KoJ Hacker45 (Shepherd)**, **NCA Fusilier SWC guns/Hacker (Shepherd)**, **SWF Fusilier premium guns (Shepherd)**, **Kestrel Fennec LT (Shepherd)** and **Varuna Clipper (Swiss Miss; low-tier, not literally unplayable)**. For SAA the negative evidence is **weak/conditional**. Apply each to the named loadout, not every profile sharing its unit name.

#### Worst-value and rare/circumstantial picks — proxy-before-buy guide (2026-10-30)

**Question answered:** Which *entire miniatures* deserve no immediate purchase, which **specific profiles** should be avoided despite the unit having good uses, and which useful-but-rare units are best represented by an existing model? This expands — and does **not** overturn — the whole-unit purchasing audit above.

**Decision labels (not measured pick rates):**
- **Avoid profile** = sourced criticism of a particular loadout or application; **do not infer that the whole sculpt is bad**.
- **Proxy first** = *inference* from costly/redundant/narrow role, limited list demand or explicit published criticism. Not an assertion that a video presenter called every profile terrible.
- **One is enough** = a duplicate miniature is lower priority than its AVA suggests.
- **Specialist build** = deliberately purchase only for its unique tool or a list actually tested. **Strong / contested** identifies disagreement; never equate a rare pick with an unusable one.

The source judgments below are anchored in the [PanO faction transcript audit above](../vanilla-pano.md#1-vanilla-pano--no-verified-universal-locked-unit), [Kestrel](../kestrel.md), [NCA](../nca.md), [Varuna](../varuna.md), [SAA](../acontecimento.md), [WinterFor](../svalarheima.md) and the [current MO roster research](../../../sectorials/panoceania/military-orders.md). Source labels are **Direct** (recorded reviewer criticism), **Contested** (other reviewers disagree) or **Inference** (purchase-priority conclusion from those reviews and N5.3 roster). The evaluation is for ordinary **300-point N5.3** lists, not narrow custom missions.
