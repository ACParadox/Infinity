## N5.3 tactical cross-references, battle reports and purchasing audit

### Mission specialist / button-pusher selections (N5.3)

**Dokkaebi KHD+Spotlight22/0** is excellent anti-Hacker + physical scoring + Pitcher/Spotlight; **HD Carbonite23/.5** is a better anti-HI/TAG program route but a *different profile*. **Gŭiláng camouflaged infiltrating FO/Hacker**, and cheap **Zhanshi/basic FO** complement it on different objective flanks. A premium linked gun must not be the only button access after the midfield infiltration is removed. This sectorial's team chart controls eligibility.

[Cross-faction apex and points-value guide](../../list-building/mission-specialists-and-button-pushers-apex-value.md). Use **this roster and Fireteam chart** for exact availability.

### N5.3 airborne / backline-strike archetypes and trades (2026-10-23)

**Tiger Soldier BSG20/0** is an efficient BS13/Mimetism(-3) **Combat Jump or ordinary Parachutist** attack; **Spitfire29/1.5** is justified only when multiple backline targets exist, with **HD29/.5 / Paramedic26** for role-based objectives. Don't claim legal direct **Parachutist(DZ)** on ordinary Tiger. A Camo **Guilang/Daofei** approach is often lower variance if the enemy's landing spots are all defended; **Dāoyīng LT+1** supplies command resources but isn't an airborne Lieutenant by being a backline commander.

[Full N5.3 apex/value and delivery-probability tier guide](../../list-building/airborne-and-enemy-dz-attackers-apex-value.md). Current roster/profile restrictions and canonical Fireteam chart above govern legal choices.

### Active-turn gunfighter cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Active-turn shooting choices:** **Tiger Soldier Boarding Shotgun20/0 or Spitfire29/1.5** supplies Mimetism(-3) airborne **non-TAG** side attacks; 29pt Spitfire only earns its SWC when several targets are reachable from its landing geometry. **Jujak Spitfire31/1.5** is a BS13/2W team midrange gun with Heavy Flamethrower to deter close revenge; **Missile Launcher29/1.5** is mainly a longer reactive/impact threat. Use the cheaper team attack when a safe advance and mission Specialist matters more than surprise deployment. [Gunfighter apex/value and matchup guidance](../../list-building/active-turn-gunfighters-apex-and-value.md).

### N5.3 cheap warband, template and CC trade decisions

**Shaolin Monk Chain Rifle+Smoke5/0 Shock CCW or 6/0 DA CCW** is elite **points value** for PH13/CC23/MA3 and vision control. **5pt does not have DA CC**, 6pt does; both religious/impetuous and **not automatically regularized** by any Inspiring Leadership ability. **Shaolin Fireteam Duo/Haris** has a **required-choice set** involving Shaolin/Liang Kai, not unlimited link access. **Tiger Soldier20 BSG** is separate rear entry, **Guilang/Daofei** skirmishers are premium concealed tools, and long-range attacker must still get a safe smoke route.

[Whole-game cheap-trading apex/value, specific targets and Lt/CoC notes](../../list-building/warbands-and-cheap-trading-units-apex-value.md). Current unit loadouts and canonical Fireteam chart remain authoritative.

### TAG tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Blue Wolf** supports direct attack/CC deterrence and legal White Banner Fireteams, while **Gūijiă** is the more straightforward sustained heavy gun. Pick Blue Wolf when movement/pressure/close angles matter, Gūijiă for conventional ranged trading. Check the actual Fireteam-required member/profile; neither TAG automatically gains the other's access.

Across all factions, compare **apex power** separately from **points value**, counterplay and mission fit: [N5.3 TAG guide](../../list-building/tags.md). The current unit profiles and canonical Fireteam chart above take precedence over tactical opinions.

### Vehicle (VH) tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Fēiquán AVA1:** **28/0 Pitcher/E/M/Sensor** creates high-angle hacking access; **27/0 X-Visor MULTI Rifle** buys modest ranged damage instead. **Irregular Order #47 (Aug 2026)** argues that White Banner already has useful ground Repeaters, making Pitcher less essential here than in **Imperial Service**, where it opens the network. Use only if the hacker payoff or Sensor matters; both versions are very vulnerable at S7/ARM3/No Cover.

See [VH apex/value tier list and matchup guide](../../list-building/vehicles-vh.md). **This sectorial's current profiles, AVA and canonical Fireteam chart above remain authoritative.**

### Hacker and KHD tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Dokkaebi KHD + Spotlight/Pitcher 22/0** is a standout inexpensive **Trinity + scoring/Targeted** hybrid; **HD + Carbonite 23/0.5** is better if the main problem is *non-Hacker* HI/TAG. **Guìláng Infiltration/Camo HD 31/0.5** and Forward/Minelayer Repeater variants give the network **Turn-0 reach**, while **Tian Gǒu FTO KHD 22/0** offers protected specialist/hacker deception; **Yě Māo KHD25/0 or HD27/0.5** combines mobility and attack. **Irregular Order #47 (Aug 2026)** calls the Dokkaebi KHD near-auto-include because it still has Spotlight when no enemy Hacker is nearby; **counterpoint:** Spotlight now requires a saving roll and an HD/Oblivion shell may better stop many opposing TAGs. **Hac Tao ~58pt HD+Trinity** is a deliberately costly ambush plan.

See the [cross-game apex/value Hacker/KHD tier guide](../../list-building/hackers-and-killer-hackers.md). **The sectorial's current profiles, AVA and canonical Fireteam chart above remain authoritative; device upgrades are profile-specific.**


### Cheerleader / Order-generation tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

See [Cheerleaders and Order Generators — N5.3 apex, points-value and matchup guide](../../list-building/cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md) for complete comparative tables and verified transcript disagreements. The **current roster above controls** legality, AVA, Training and Fireteams.

- 7pt Chaīyì — Flash/Repeater Regular REM when AVA permits; S3 footprint and fragility limit repeats.
- Zhanshi — Regular S2 line with Fireteam/decoy utility; compare actual 7pt Flashbot and specialist upgrades before choosing expensive passive profiles.
- Warcor — 3pt Irregular Flash Pulse distraction, not a transferable Regular Order.

**Order audit:** add up only deployed eligible **Regular** Orders in each Combat Group; special LT/Tactical, Irregular and Peripheral activations are not transferable pool Orders. Buy the cheapest protected body that supplies the *second function the list truly needs*; consider large-base DZ crowding, expensive mandatory Fireteam slots, and whether another scoring specialist beats one more cheap Order.


### ARO apex/value cross-reference (2026-10-23)

See [Best ARO Units in N5.3 — apex, cost-value, Hidden Deployment, reactive traps, opposed podcast views](../../list-building/aro-units-apex-and-value.md). **Only the legal roster above** establishes options, exact costs/AVA, upgrades and Fireteam permissions in this army.

- **Hùsòng TR** — 18pt/0SWC Thunderbolt (+1B) all-arc guard (where profile legal); ideally recoverable, not a sole ARO plan
- **Son-Bae FTO ML** — launcher for a *source-chart-authorized* team, not the same as 16pt Guided missile profile
- **Chaīyì 7pt Flash/Repeater** — cheap Regular anti-approach tax and network, not premium long-lane gun
- **Ninja hidden/camo** — anti-impersonation or corridor trap if positioned near scoring; may be more valuable unrevealed for own active turn
- **Hùsòng** — expensive single-STR HMG vs zero-SWC Thunderbolt profile when roster grants both; choose actual range

**List test:** a strong ARO consumes enemy Orders by forcing Smoke, rerouting, poor range, discoveries or risky trades. A robust list normally prefers *serial, different* defenders—a cheap visible or marker/template screen, a covered second gate, and a preserved attack/scoring unit—rather than two adjacent visible snipers. Plan for opponent pie-slicing, AD, anti-camo, hacking and recovery; evaluate whether extra SWC is better spent on mission attackers.

**Mech-Engineer14 WIP13 + Yáozăo3** is the low-cost **Blue Wolf/Guijia/REM** recovery line; ordinary WIP13 contact success is **65%**, **87.75%** if eligible Remote Presence Command Token used on failure. **Chung-Hee Jeong31** buys Engineer(ReRoll), Repeater and turret/linked utility; **Johnny Kao36** buys high-mobility, survivability and Engineer ReRoll as a different tier. **Zhanshi Yīshēng14** treats VITA instead. ### N5.3 DZ defense / anti-alpha-strike tactical choices

**Warcor 360º3/0** is low-cost Irregular rear-edge Flash. **Hùsòng Total Reaction Thunderbolt18/0** covers airborne/vertical avenues while generating a Regular Order and permitting Engineer repairs; **Chaīyì7/0 Flash+Repeater** is another Regular support body, not a hard gun. **Lóng Yá Camo+FD/AP Mine Minelayer/Panzerfaust(+1B)17/.5** (actual option) controls a critical *outer* approach; **Gūdǎn one-use Camo/Zapper or HFT** gives a separate short-range template/ambush job. **Counter:** cheap physical sentries stop non-Hackable raiders better than hacking alone, but a single 360º bot or Camo mine cannot see all safe Combat Jump rooftops; split valuables and cover next movement squares.

### Tactical cross-reference — vision control (23 October 2026)

**Hwarang20/0 Disco + Deactivator** is a strong HI Fireteam Eclipse enabler; **Shaolin5–6/0 ordinary Smoke** is the efficient loose screen/CC trade; **Rui Shi23/1 MSV2** can instead attack *through normal Smoke*, and **Yín-Shé Albedo** targets an opposing MSV gun directly without protecting allies. **Opposing approaches:** the Jujak/Hwarang/Haetae/Dokkaebi combo gains safe scoring/approach, but costs multiple model slots and Orders; cheaper Shaolin + a single attacker may be better on open/simple missions. **No** amount of White Noise/Albedo should be mistaken for Eclipse's universal LoF block. ### Close-combat assassins and counter-CC — N5.3 (2026-10-23)

**Shaolin Warrior Monk5/0 CC23/MA3/Smoke** is an extremely cheap *approach enabler and disposable counter-charge*, but Impetuous/Irregular limits pooled Orders. **Jing Qo32/0** is true **CC23 MA3, Berserk, NWI, Climbing Plus** medium-cost close fighter who also contributes shooting and legal Shaolin-team mobility; can overpower ordinary defenders, but **not** an Oniwaban's Hidden/Monofilament alpha strike. **Kunai28** is a Hidden **sniper MA3**, no Infiltration or premium Mono kill: don't misrank it as an assassin. ### N5.3 rare abilities & unusual combinations — tactical synthesis

**Léi Gōng FTO29/0** combines **Albedo(-6)+MSV1+Super-Jump+NWI**, making a late-game anti-visor/midfield cleaner (Parachutist32/0 or CoC34/0 are separate options). **Yín-Shé22/0** offers much cheaper Albedo(-6)+E/M/Panzerfaust, **26/0 K1 MMR** general hunting or **27/.5 K1 sniper Marksmanship**, but **Yín-Shé has NO MSV**. Pair Yín-Shé Albedo with friendly Shaolin Smoke to punish enemy MSV protecting Smoke lanes. **Opposing Shepherd vs TA:** one sees Albedo as niche except against enemy MSV, other advocates multiple Albedo bodies as a factional skew; avoid paying for six if opponent has none. **Bìxié MSV1+FO+NCO+Super-Jump+Climbing+** is rare mobile *specialist/gun/CC* role compression, not Albedo.

**Evidence method:** Rankings synthesize named-player opinions from the directly retrieved YouTube and podcast transcripts listed below, plus the sectorial files' earlier documented transcript audits. Earlier N5 reviews are especially useful for tactical reasoning; the 2026 N5.3 references take priority for present-day rule/profile specifics.

**Labels**
- **Must / near-must:** presenter calls it near-automatic, or independent sources repeatedly promote the same reliable core. All choices here are *near*-must/defaults unless explicitly described as compulsory in a particular list archetype.
- **Go-to:** reliable, well-explained role choice; the list only needs to include it when that role is required.
- **Avoid / “never take”:** direct reviewer criticism of a particular *profile or job*, with the better-value competitor and source identified. Mark **contested** wherever another credible speaker advocates it. **No confirmed whole-unit universal never-take verdict** emerges for these four armies.
- **Evidence:** “explicit” means a creator gives a clear personal positive/negative evaluation; “repeated” means multiple presented lists/voices support the choice; “synthesis” means a conclusion drawn from those views and the current roster, not a direct spoken quote.

#### Disagreements that must remain visible

1. **Guìláng and Dàofěi:** tournament-proven scenario deployment (Axel/BT Sherman) versus Shepherd's higher priority on cheap Gūdǎn and ordinary attack efficiency. **Do not label either “never take.”**
2. **Gūijiă versus Blue Wolf:** close-range five-shot AP attack fork versus the safety of an ARM8 long-range MULTI HMG and stronger melee deterrence. **Irregular Order August 2026 favors Gūijiă**; other reviews like Blue Wolf's output.
3. **Lóng Yá:** defensive area-denial return per point (Axel/Irregular Order) versus BS11 exposure to newer N5 gunfighters (Shepherd).
4. **Full Kuang Shi support in Vanilla:** efficient discount Orders to buy a TAG-heavy plan (Tactical Awareness) versus taking scarce slots from independent cheap troopers (Shepherd).
5. **IA profile “duds”:** Swiss Miss's negative C-tier judgments for specific expensive roles do **not** contradict that reviewer's explicit end statement that IA has no whole-unit dead picks.
6. **ISS generic Crane vs Adil:** the strongest direct everyday-role cost criticism; still inspect Crane's *specific* hacker/LT/gun/Fireteam profile before calling one strictly dominated.

#### Worst-value, rare/circumstantial and proxy-first models by army (2026-10-30)

**Purpose:** distinguish (1) an actually **weak profile**, (2) a **rarely justified or mission-dependent whole unit**, and (3) a **good unit that is still better proxied before purchasing** because it overlaps with already-covered roles or requires a specialized list. This is a **collection-buying guide**, not a statistical rarity survey: the cited recordings and battle reports do **not** establish actual inclusion percentages. Recommendations refer to the **September 2026 N5.3 standard rosters**, not optional Spec-Ops/Team-Ops or Reinforcements. In particular, a poor **White Banner Shàng Jí** purchasing case does **not** invalidate **Invincible Army Shàng Jí**.

**Labels and evidence standard:**
- **Skip purchase; proxy first** = strongest *shopping* recommendation, usually based on an explicit negative reviewer assessment; a niche or dissent may still exist. It is **not** “unplayable.”
- **Proxy/test before buying** = potentially excellent in an intentional build, but an uncommon *requirement* for generic lists or an expensive single-role/duplicate kit. This is a **reasoned purchasing inference**, **not a claimed presenter quote about frequency**.
- **Avoid profile only** = there are **other valuable profiles in the same unit**. **Do not blacklist the model.**
- **One is enough / proxy the second** = a quantity recommendation, **not** an AVA or legality change. **Contested** flags other reviewers, tournament use or different valid roles.
