## Scope

**Scope:** Ordinary 300-point list-building. **Must/near-must** = an explicit near-auto recommendation or a broadly repeated core *role/package*; these are not measured pick rates. **Go-to** = a dependable purchase for its role, rather than a compulsory inclusion. **Avoid** = a profile or standard use criticized by named speakers, or demonstrably poor opportunity cost relative to current alternatives; **not** an assertion of literal zero playable matchups. Where recording evidence is limited or contradictory, say so. Model legal Fireteams from the linked N5.3 charts, **not** historic recorded lists. `FTO` alone is not legal Fireteam access.

## N5.3 tactical cross-references, battle reports and purchasing audit

### Mission specialist choices — N5.3 cross-game evaluation

Use **roster-legal Kappa/Razor/Nightshade/KHD/Hacker and Doctor/Engineer** tools according to current Starmada access, not automatic Vanilla O-12 availability. **Fast Specialist or legitimate O-12 AD** can take a far objective while a separate **medical/technical tool** covers Classifieds. **CoC (Non Specialist)** never qualifies for generic consoles solely from CoC; premium TAG/HI may carry/hold a zone but is not necessarily a button pusher.

See [Mission Specialists / Button Pushers apex and value tiers](../../list-building/mission-specialists-and-button-pushers-apex-value.md). This roster and its Fireteam chart are the final authority for exact options.

### N5.3 airborne / backline-strike archetypes and trades (2026-10-23)

**Knight of Santiago KHD Combat Jump38/0 or non-hacker Combat Jump36/0** is powerful late backline reach, but **cannot simultaneously be in a Fireteam**—FTO options are different. **Crushers Combat Jump27/0 vs FD+20 25/0** choose direct landing risk versus stable early midfield pressure. **Raptor BS14 HI + Devabot** is a fast *on-table* anti-DZ breaker, not Combat Jump. **Parachutist/FO** choices in current roster remain profile-specific; preserve a separate late objective specialist and safe LT/CoC rather than betting all scoring on a 36–38pt landing.

[Full N5.3 apex/value and delivery-probability tier guide](../../list-building/airborne-and-enemy-dz-attackers-apex-value.md). Current roster/profile restrictions and canonical Fireteam chart in the linked authoritative reference govern legal choices.

### Active-turn gunfighter cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Active-turn shooting choices:** **Zeta HRMC73/2** is a **TAG** BS14/AP high-Burst/ARM8/3STR climbing long-gun; it shines against durable defenders and tall terrain but is 2SWC and needs hacker protection. **Starmada's legal Fireteam gun + specialist** can instead compress movement and score while leaving points for a separate flank threat; a 73pt visible TAG is not automatically best in button-heavy missions. **Oko FO/Sensor15/0** supports target identification and scoring but is not a competing main gun. [Gunfighter apex/value, dedicated TAG tier](../../list-building/active-turn-gunfighters-apex-and-value.md).

### N5.3 cheap warband, template and CC trade decisions

**Varangian Guard 12/0 SMG+Chain+Smoke or 13/0 BSG** is a **Regular+Impetuous** low-cost vision/corner screen with CC23/Berserk+Dogged; **AVA2** here, not Vanilla O-12 AVA4. **Ioann Bann32/0** offers multi-Wound dog-warrior Chain(+1B)/Viral bow/T2 CC threat at almost 3x points, useful for repeated smoke/grenade pressure—not a one-for-one soft target trade. **Current Varangian Duo** can include **Ioann Bann as Varangian** only with at least one Varangian; no general FT permission. Preserve the actual LT and mission specialist after close run.

### TAG tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Zeta** is the S7 HRMC centerpiece for high-powered ranged engagements and vertical routes (Climbing Plus); it needs dedicated hacker-screening and mission scorers, not just another gun. Compare NCO/lieutenant choices *only if this sectorial's current profile actually offers them*. When missions require many distributed specialists, reserve enough points for the excellent Starmada objective network.

For the two-tier **apex/points-value** comparison, TAG counterplay, matchup matrix and direct transcript sources: [N5.3 TAG field guide](../../list-building/tags.md). Treat current profiles, points/SWC and canonical Fireteam data in the linked authoritative reference as authoritative.

### Vehicle (VH) tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Firebat AVA1:** **36/0 AP SMG/Thunderbolt/LRL** provides 0-SWC opportunistic ranged flanks; **35/0 MULTI Rifle/Riotstopper/Akrylat/Blitzen** controls dangerous enemy troopers. **NCO** converts usable LT Orders into attack/retreat/Suppression and **+1 Special Die/Mimetism(-6)** improve FtF quality, but BS13/S7/No Cover still require hard AROs cleared first. This is a **VH**, unlike Starmada's **Zeta TAG** and RoadBot REM; no Fireteam permission.

### Hacker and KHD tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Cyberghost HD+ 21/0.5** projects White Noise/Oblivion via Pitcher, whereas **KHD18/0** protects the same network with Trinity; **Kappa HD13/0.5** supplies cheap general HD. **Knight Santiago FTO KHD+TinBot Firewall(-3) 31/0** is a resilient **Bronzes** team counterhacker; the **Combat Jump KHD38/0** is *not* the linked FTO profile. **PSI-Cop KHD23/0** adds mission/Disco Baller flexibility; **Raptor KHD41/0 + Devabot** buys a strong attack/peripheral fork, not cheap backline cyber coverage. **Why:** White Noise helps Zeta/Bronze clear MSV, and TinBot/KHD helps resist enemy Trinities. ### N5.3 DZ defense / anti-alpha-strike tactical choices

**Bluecoat 13/0 Biometric/Heavy Riotstopper/Adhesive** remains an excellent anti-Impersonation cheap Regular guard, with **AVA4 in Starmada** versus **AVA2 in vanilla O-12**. **Raveneye 12/0 FO+Deactivator+Minelayer** supplies E/M or Cybermine turn-zero tripwire; **Sekudroid 18–19/0** provides 360º, STR2, Mimetism(-3), Flash(+1B) and Adhesive/Akrylat for rear/roof arrivals. **Raptor 32/0 + 360º Devabot HFT** is a protected counterattack package, not a disposable sentry. **Disagreement (TA Starmada):** distributed Bluecoat placements can be excellent against attackers but consume points if no likely breach square; prioritize one useful entry funnel and preserve mission pressure.

### Tactical cross-reference — vision control (23 October 2026)

**Bluecoat16/0 Disco**, **PSI-Cop KHD23/0 Disco** and **Regular Varangian12/0 Smoke** are different defensive and offensive tools: Disco/Eclipse stops MSV too, ordinary Smoke can pair with MSV shooting, and a KHD does not supply White Noise. **Cyberghost HD+21/.5 + Pitcher/Repeaters** adds anti-MSV/Marksmanship White Noise; **Hippolyta37/0 Smoke** buys a fast durable attacker rather than a cheap one-use screen. **Choice:** protected last-turn scoring corridor favors Bluecoat; significant repeater net and visor gun favor Cyberghost; multiple ordinary crossing points favor Varangians. ### Close-combat assassins and counter-CC — N5.3 (2026-10-23)

**Andromeda30/0** is top *midfield anti-elite Guard* piece: Infiltration(PH15), MA3, NWI, D-Charges and **CC attack in LoF+ZoC** avoid actual base contact. **Varangian Guard12–13** is the low-cost Smoke+CC23 Berserk enabler; **Ioann Bann** provides resilient transmuting close pressure at higher cost. **Bluecoat16/0 NBW + Sixth Sense + Biometric** is unusually effective *counter-CC/anti-Impersonation* screening near important troops, but needs actual CC ARO to ignore imposed penalties. Opposing plan: shoot the assassin or template its entry instead of sending an expensive Bronze directly into MA5. ### N5.3 network projection — Pitchers, Repeaters, TinBots and disposable delivery

**Saladin CoC33/0 or Lieutenant34/0** with **Minelayer(2) and Deployable Repeater** can pre-place **two nodes at deployment**, unusually strong *Turn-0* network if already purchasing his command/Decoy/Counterintelligence package. LT gains Strategos L1/+1 Command Token; **CoC does not**. **Cyberghost HD+/Pitcher21/.5** supplies ranged White Noise/Oblivion, **Kyttä7/0 native Repeater/Flash** and **Oko FO/Sensor/native Repeater15/0** are cheaper Regular route builders, **FuzzBot8/0 Baggage/Repeater** reloads eligible disposables. **Counter (TA Starmada):** extra defensive/command investment competes with mobile Betatroopers and actual scoring; a 33pt Saladin is not a value pick *solely* to place Repeaters.

#### Cross-faction disagreements and rules safeguards

1. **No evidence of a literal whole-unit universal “never take” in all three current rosters.** The clearest strong negative is **Torchlight ordinary Silverstar Rover-heavy construction**, followed by profile-specific **Torchlight Strider Red Fury**, **Starmada Casanova**, and **Starmada combat-role Tian Gǒu**.
2. **Vanilla O-12 is not Core-less-and-link-less:** current N5.3 chart permits **one Haris plus two Duos**. Gamma/Omega/Vidocq/Jackboot packages must use the actual FT-II required/allowed membership; avoid retrofitting an arbitrary triple.
3. **Starmada is not Vanilla:** it **does not** gain Team Sirius, Gamma, Razor, Moonraker or Wrecker just because those feature in a Vanilla discussion. Its current unique defenses/mission options make inexpensive Raveneye, Bronze, RoadBot, Šarko, Bluecoat and Parvati especially relevant.
4. **Torchlight Silverstar Prime is not a Fireteam source/member** despite the word *Silverstar*. The **Hellblazer** or **Silverstar Rover** supplies the required-source entry; Waverider and allowed Wildcards follow the chart. Older N4 Prime+Hippolyta+Hellblazer teams cannot be copied as legal N5.3 Fireteams.
5. **Only current FTO options actually listed on the chart link:** Torchlight Vidocq FTO 1/2/3, StormBot FTO 2/3, Jamie Rover FTO 1/3; Starmada Nyoka FTO 3/4, Tian Gǒu FTO 1/3/4/6, etc. The full repository chart is the authority, not these illustrative subsets.
6. **TAG disagreements are real.** [Loss of Lieutenant TAG review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dZo_gQWQYSY) strongly values **Zeta** and **Wrecker**. [TA Starmada](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1DKpabLoqyE) is skeptical about **Zeta specifically in Starmada**, where 70–73pts and no Tactical Awareness crowd out support. [Shepherd Torchlight](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PZKCQAupCMs) prefers **Prime for first reliable gun** but is positive on a Wrecker damaging armored targets.
7. **New N5.3 unit not graded without fresh recorded evidence:** **Ioann Bann** was introduced in September 2026. These key N5 discussions predate his arrival. Treat him as **ungraded**, not “never take” merely for absence from transcripts.
8. **N4 tournament evidence** (notably the 2024 Torchlight WIP12 winner) supports a *role architecture*—Moonrakers, camouflage, Prime, Hippolyta—but **does not verify N5.3 Fireteams or prove a current win-rate advantage**.

#### Worst-value, rarely necessary and proxy-first miniature purchases by O-12 army (2026-10-30)

**Purpose:** Which models should you **not bother buying immediately**, which **genuinely criticized profiles** can simply be represented by an existing model, and which **excellent but specialized / alternate sculpts** should be proxy-tested rather than bought for inventory completeness? Cover all three current N5.3 O-12 armies: [Vanilla O-12](../../../sectorials/o-12/o-12.md), [Starmada](../../../sectorials/o-12/starmada.md) and [Torchlight Brigade](../../../sectorials/o-12/torchlight-brigade.md). **No universally unusable whole unit** was demonstrated. *Rare* below means a **mission-dependent or non-default role in the reviewed lists**, not statistically measured play frequency.

**Evidence and decision labels:** **Direct negative** = a named speaker criticizes that specific unit, profile or default use in previously checked [recordings](#recordingsource-register-and-quality-notes); **Contested** preserves a counter-review or successful alternative role. **Defer model / proxy first** is an *inferred buying priority*, **not** an invented reviewer quote. **Avoid the profile, not the unit** applies when the troop's other options are genuinely good. **Extra-copy proxy** means buy the first model for a useful core role but do not blindly purchase multiple sculpt/weapon variants or fill AVA. The [September 2026 N5.3/Army 7.26246.159 profiles and canonical Fireteams]() override old N4/N5 source lists. **FTO** on a profile does not by itself grant team access.

**Practical shopping rule:** (1) Buy/represent the **first** efficient **Ment, Raveneye, Moonraker where legal, Kappa HD, Varangian, Oko/cheap Copperbot, mission specialist** you actually want; (2) choose **one main ranged platform** (e.g. Vanilla Gamma/Epsilon, Starmada Bronze/Epsilon, Torchlight Prime/Wrecker); (3) **proxy** a *new specialized expensive hero, VH/TAG, optional extra gun, SideBot/peripheral package* for multiple games; (4) buy only after your lists repeatedly need it *simultaneously* and a lawful same-size model isn't available. On all proxy choices, confirm **current ITS/event policy, silhouette, base, distinct physical figure per simultaneously present model, team eligibility, exact selected profile and friendly-game opponent agreement**. Proxies change plastic, **not Army legality, equipment, Orders or Fireteam privileges**.
