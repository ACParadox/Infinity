## Scope

**Scope:** opinionated *Vanilla O-12* list-building advice, not Army rules. Profiles, prices, AVA and Fireteam eligibility above are authoritative (N5.3); the linked discussions span N4–N5 and sometimes describe older costs, unit availability or rules. Re-check the profile table in the linked authoritative reference before copying an old list. Do not import Starmada/Torchlight Fireteams into Vanilla.

**Scope:** Ordinary 300-point list-building. **Must/near-must** = an explicit near-auto recommendation or a broadly repeated core *role/package*; these are not measured pick rates. **Go-to** = a dependable purchase for its role, rather than a compulsory inclusion. **Avoid** = a profile or standard use criticized by named speakers, or demonstrably poor opportunity cost relative to current alternatives; **not** an assertion of literal zero playable matchups. Where recording evidence is limited or contradictory, say so. Model legal Fireteams from the linked N5.3 charts, **not** historic recorded lists. `FTO` alone is not legal Fireteam access.

## N5.3 tactical cross-references, battle reports and purchasing audit

### Command-unit apex/value cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Alpha LT 24/0** supplies **Strategos L1/Counterintelligence** and +1 Command Token instead of needing to fight; **Alpha CoC 24/0** is a *different* command-backup model. **Katherine Cho CoC 20/0** offers cheaper insurance, while **Ment CoC** adds a different specialist/Number 2 role. Choose a fighting Gamma/Zeta/other LT only when its extra Orders are actually exploitable; Strategos converts the LT Order to pooled Regular, while NCO converts to an NCO-only Tactical activation.

See [cross-game Lieutenant, Chain of Command and Order-package tiers](../../list-building/command-units-apex-and-value.md). **Use the linked authoritative roster and Fireteam chart in the linked authoritative reference for exact legality.**

### Mission specialist / button-pusher selections (N5.3)

**Razor FO29/0 vs HD+Trinity34/.5**: both Hidden/Infiltrating Mimetism(-6), but role-specific Classified access differs. **Nightshade KHD34/0** is a high-end Hidden CC/anti-Hacker scorer. **Kappa cheap HD/FO**, **Raveneye/Ment CoC where roster-legal**, and other independent specialists provide redundancy; **Alpha LT Strategos** supplies pooled Orders but isn't automatically a console-qualified Specialist just by being Lieutenant. Preserve late hidden scorer.

[Cross-faction apex and points-value guide](../../list-building/mission-specialists-and-button-pushers-apex-value.md). Use **the linked authoritative roster and Fireteam chart** for exact availability.

### N5.3 airborne / backline-strike archetypes and trades (2026-10-23)

**Delta BSG+Panzerfaust19/0** delivers cheap Combat Jump; **Delta FO22/0 / Doctor22/0 / KHD24** provide late Classified/score and strike alternatives. **Crusher BSG+Panzerfaust27/0** has **Combat Jump**, whereas **SMG25/0** has **FD+20**: mutually exclusive positioning! **Razor Hidden/Infiltration**, **Nightshade hidden forward threat**, and **Raptor NCO/Devabot** are alternative *on-table* breach tools; all have different geometry. Protect Alpha/Raveneye command against enemy AD, and don't count an ordinary LT as Specialist.

[Full N5.3 apex/value and delivery-probability tier guide](../../list-building/airborne-and-enemy-dz-attackers-apex-value.md). Current roster/profile restrictions and canonical Fireteam chart in the linked authoritative reference govern legal choices.

### Active-turn gunfighter cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Active-turn shooting choices:** **Gamma HMG50/1.5** is the strong raw **non-TAG** BS14 +1Burst/ARM6 long gun; **Epsilon HMG34/1.5** is a cheaper MSV2/Mimetism(-3)/Climbing counter to visual defense. **Zeta HRMC73/2** is the separate premium **TAG** AP high-volume climbing long-range attack; **Wrecker HMG57/1.5 or Spitfire55/1.5** is the points-value TAG with Tactical Awareness. Choose one of these by the opponent's armor, Mimetism and vertical terrain, not only nominal Burst. [Separate TAG/non-TAG apex and value tiers](../../list-building/active-turn-gunfighters-apex-and-value.md).

### N5.3 cheap warband, template and CC trade decisions

**Varangian Guard SMG+Chain Rifle+Smoke12/0 or BSG13/0** is one of N5.3's best **Regular+Impetuous** 2-role screens: CC23/Berserk(+3)/Dogged, PH14 Smoke and a real pooled Order if alive; higher value to O-12 than 5pt Irregular Galwegian when supporting 40pt specialists. **Ioann Bann32** (if actually on roster) is far higher-cost multi-Wound dog-warrior, not a 12pt cheap template. **Alpha Strategos LT**, **Raveneye/Ment CoC** are protected command insurance, not smoke-bearing cheap warriors.

[Whole-game cheap-trading apex/value, specific targets and Lt/CoC notes](../../list-building/warbands-and-cheap-trading-units-apex-value.md). Current unit loadouts and canonical Fireteam chart remain authoritative.

### TAG tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Zeta (70 LT / 73 NCO)** has a premium HRMC, Climbing Plus and unusually strong Guided ECM, with an excellent absolute and **points-value** case. **Wrecker (51 HRL / 55 Spitfire / 57 HMG)** is the second-plan bargain: TacAware, S6 and heavy templates leave far more room for specialists/ARO. Pick Zeta for long-lane apex attacks; Wrecker where templates and redundant objective pressure win.

For the two-tier **apex/points-value** comparison, TAG counterplay, matchup matrix and direct transcript sources: [N5.3 TAG field guide](../../list-building/tags.md). Treat current profiles, points/SWC and canonical Fireteam data in the linked authoritative reference as authoritative.

### Vehicle (VH) tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Firebat AVA1:** **36/0 AP SMG+Thunderbolt+LRL** covers varied range bands at **0 SWC**; **35/0 MULTI Rifle/Riotstopper/Akrylat/Blitzen** favors control. **NCO +1 Special Die + Mimetism(-6)** allows efficient attack then Suppression when an LT Order is available. **Shepherd (2025)** ranks it only #6/8 including Garuda REM, mainly due to BS13/modest gun Burst, but calls #4–6 close. Not a TAG/REM and cannot directly operate objectives while Aerial.

See [VH apex/value tier list and matchup guide](../../list-building/vehicles-vh.md). **Current profiles, AVA and canonical Fireteam chart in the linked authoritative reference remain authoritative.**

### Hacker and KHD tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

**Cyberghost HD+ 21/0.5 / KHD 18/0**, both WIP14/BTS6 **with Pitcher**, is the efficient pair: White Noise/Oblivion for heavies, Trinity for enemy hackers. **Kappa HD13/0.5** is a budget scoring/Spotlight Order, **Gangbuster KHD22/0 + MadTraps/Minelayer** a midfield trap and hacker hunter. **Razor Hidden/Infiltration HD+Trinity34/0.5** buys premium surprise; **Nightshade Hidden CC KHD34/0** is primarily a physical mission/assassination tool, not a backfield Repeater controller. **PSI-Cop KHD23/0 with Disco Baller** contributes active vision control, while **Team Sirius HD24/0.5** adds a peripheral Repeater fork. **Counterpoint:** expensive hidden KHD doesn't necessarily replace one 21pt Cyberghost plus cheap Kyttä/Oko network.

See the [cross-game apex/value Hacker/KHD tier guide](../../list-building/hackers-and-killer-hackers.md). **The sectorial's current profiles, AVA and canonical Fireteam chart in the linked authoritative reference remain authoritative; device upgrades are profile-specific.**


### Cheerleader / Order-generation tactical cross-reference (2026-10-23)

The N5.3 [cheerleader/order-generator apex and value guide](../../list-building/cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md) contrasts Orders-per-point, passive utility, matchup dependence, and opposing transcript opinions. **Use this roster for legal AVA, exact profile costs, Training and Fireteam restrictions.**

- 7pt Kyttä — cheap mobile Flash/Repeater/Mimetism Regular Order; check local AVA and deployment safety.
- 8pt FuzzBot — Baggage/Repeater plus Regular Order, excellent if the S4 footprint can be concealed.
- 12pt Kappa — S2 baseline Regular Order, with modest Specialist/Commlink upgrades; compare their actual mission utility against cheaper REMs.
- Warcor — a 3pt Irregular Flash ARO speedbump, not a transferable pool Order without actual conversion.

**Decision:** first buy enough protected *transferable* Regular Orders for the actual Combat Group attacker/scoring plan, then pay for genuine role compression. Irregular/Impetuous, LT, Tactical Awareness and Peripheral bodies are different resources. Account for a packed S3/S4 deployment zone and mandatory Fireteam members.


### ARO apex/value cross-reference (2026-10-23)

[Best ARO units across N5.3 — absolute apex vs points-value](../../list-building/aro-units-apex-and-value.md) compares hidden reactive fire, TR/Neurocinetics, Flash/DTW/expendable defenders and why competing creators disagree. The **linked authoritative roster** is authoritative for actual costs, Training, AVA and Fireteam construction.

- **StormBot HRL** — two-structure mobile shooter with durable reactive potential; FTO/non-FTO costs and legal link matter
- **Bluecoat Riotstopper/Adhesive** — ~13pt close defence/board-control unit with Sixth Sense; guard objectives and AD entry, not 32-inch gun lanes
- **FuzzBot TR** — 17pt/0SWC Regular cheap Repeater/Combi deterrent; compare stronger but dearer StormBot
- **Kyttä Flashbot** — 7pt Repeater/Flash secondary ARO and cheap Order; insufficient alone against assault gun
- **Warcor 3pt Flash** — disposable Irregular Stun/Orders tax, not a generic Regular cheerleader

**Best circumstance:** long exposed lanes justify a real sniper/missile/linked gun; dense tables, DZ landings and mission rooms favor E/M, mine, Shotgun, Riotstopper and hidden traps. Cover the destination/approach, layer serial threats rather than two pie-sliceable visible guns, and preserve scoring units for the active turn.

### N5.3 DZ defense / anti-alpha-strike tactical choices

**Bluecoat 13/0 Adhesive Launcher Rifle + Heavy Riotstopper(+1B)** is a cheap Regular **Biometric, Sixth Sense, Discover(+6), Natural Born Warrior** defense against Fiday/Speculo reveal/CC. **Raveneye 12/0 FO+Deactivator+Minelayer** adds a turn-zero **E/M Mine or Cybermine** and E/Marat/Flash; choose its actual mine type by enemy. A **Bluecoat + Raveneye** protects Impersonation approaches better than a boost-only MadTrap. **Counter:** neither offers 360º rear-edge coverage; backstop with an appropriate TR/Flash guard and separate the cheerleaders.

### Tactical cross-reference — vision control (23 October 2026)

**Varangian 12/0 Regular Smoke** or **Hippolyta37/0** safely delivers troops against *ordinary* gun AROs; the former is excellent full-list value because it also generates a Regular Order. **Bluecoat16/0 Disco** and **PSI-Cop23/0 KHD + Disco** project an **Eclipse** screen through MSV lanes (do not mistake KHD for HD+ White Noise). **Cyberghost HD+21/.5** can deploy selective **White Noise** in its Hacking Area/Pitcher network against MSV or Marksmanship, but not non-MSV guns. **Counterpoint:** if that specific visual threat is absent, investing in a large Repeater engine can cost more Orders than a simple Smoke/Disco crossing. [Vision-control apex/value guide](../../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).

### Close-combat assassins and counter-CC — N5.3 (2026-10-23)

**Hippolyta37/0** (MA3/CC24 EXP, 2W, Mimetism−6, 15-5 Super-Jump, Smoke and Berserk) is the actual premium *mobile close-assault* choice. **Varangian12–13/0 Regular+Impetuous** is better cheap Smoke/Berserk/Dogged **CC23** trading support. **Alpha24/0 Guard+MA2** is a *defensive* LoF+ZoC melee counter with Biometric, not an attacker meant to traverse midfield; Guard doesn't pin at range. **O-12 Remotes** are ranged/mission pieces, not duelists; screen valuable Zeta/Wrecker from an enemy Mono infiltrator. [Cross-faction CC assassin apex/value and anti-CC](../../list-building/dedicated-close-combat-assassins-apex-value.md).

### N5.3 network projection — Pitchers, Repeaters, TinBots and disposable delivery

**Team Sirius FO+SiriusBot21/0** begins with **FD(+20) + a synchronized native Repeater**, an exceptional *Turn-0 forward network* with FO and a close template fork; **BSG Sirius20/0** is cheaper but drops FO. SiriusBot generates **no second Regular Order**. **Cyberghost HD+/Pitcher21/.5** supplies White Noise/Oblivion and projectiles, **Kyttä7/0 native Repeater/Flash**, **Oko FO+Sensor/native Repeater15/0**, **Tinker native Repeater/support** give useful regular-order physical delivery. **Kappa FO+Deployable Repeater14/0** requires an activation to place. **Counter:** most O-12 KHD cannot shut down non-Hacker HI/TAG alone; network pays best when a protected HD/HD+ and a viable payoff are present.

[Whole-game apex/value network guide, current profiles, and opposing media views](../../list-building/network-projection-repeaters-pitchers-apex-value.md).

### N5.3 rare abilities & unusual combinations — tactical synthesis

**Team Sirius FO+SiriusBot21/0** is rare **Forward Deployment(+20) + FO scoring controller + synchronized native-Repeater bot** for a single purchase/Regular Order; the **SiriusBot is NOT FO**. **Sirius HD24/.5** is another route: live hacker + bot Repeater, but not FO. **Tinker Repeater/GizmoKit support** and **Oko Sensor+FO+Repeater15/0** often provide stronger standalone multi-role specialist utility when no second synchronized threat is needed. **Opposing:** an ordinary Kappa FO+Repeater14/0 plus Kyttä7/0 costs same as Sirius21 but gives **two normal Regular Orders/bodies**, at expense of turn-zero FD bot positional fork.

### Tactical cross-reference — indirect fire (2026-10-23)

**Vanilla O-12:** **Millicent Guided16/1.5** is a conditional payoff for **Cyberghost HD+ Pitcher21/.5, Kyttä/Oko/SiriusBot Repeaters or a real FO**. ### N5.3 objective holding, zone domination & carried-objective choices

**Omega FO37/0** is a top specialist defender: **Mimetism(-6)+W2/ARM4**, MULTI Rifle, Heavy Riotstopper, suitable to hold box in Total Cover and punish a CC/short entry; **Silverstar Specialist27/0**, W2/ARM3/BTS6/Light Riotstopper provides cheaper carrier. **Team Sirius FO21/0 + Repeater SiriusBot** creates an FD(+20) two-body *approach screen*, but SiriusBot cannot pick box (FO only on controller). **Raptor FO39/0+HFT Devabot**, W2 NCO+360º, is ideal threatened corner carrier with two reactive angles. **Zeta TAG73/2** or **Gamma HMG50/1.5** flips zone by sheer VP/armor, but neither automatically Specialist. **Counter:** if target zone can be secured behind terrain, 21pt Sirius or 27pt Silverstar gives more distributed independent scorers than one 73pt TAG; cover last-turn Silhouette contact square.

### Anti-Tech — enemy visors, Mimetism, markers and Impersonation (2026-10-23)

**Bluecoat Adhesive+Heavy Riotstopper13/0** is elite *anti-IMP* budget value: Biometric+Sixth Sense+Discover(+6), Regular, close-route deterrence; **Oko FO/Sensor15/0 or TacAware18/0** is for **Camouflage/Hidden ZoC** sweeps, not IMP detection. **Cyberghost HD+21/.5** generates **White Noise** against elite enemy MSV/Marksmanship if a Repeater reaches the actual blocking lane; **Epsilon MSV2 HMG34/1.5** or **Nimrod MSV2** is for winning a real Mimetism firefight. **Opposing options:** MSV shooters can protect against ordinary Smoke but get countered by White Noise; spread guards and keep a non-MSV lane. #### 2026-10-26: Robert Shepherd Vanilla O-12 faction focus

**Source:** [*Infinity N5 Faction Focus — O-12*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Nl8BMJ8E5U) (full YouTube transcript inspected; timestamps below). Shepherd explicitly says his firsthand N5 Vanilla O-12 play is limited and consulted experienced players including an Interplanetario competitor. These are **his recommendations**, not a universal consensus or official Army errata. All **prices / legal team configurations** below are reconciled against the [September 2026 pinned Vanilla roster](../../../sectorials/o-12/o-12.md); optional **Team-Ops** are **not** standard 300pt roster models.

##### Faction-building thesis — availability is broad, budget is narrow (00:00–18:30)

- **Capability breadth:** ordinary Smoke+MSV shooting, White Noise, Disco Baller, HD/HD+/KHD, infiltration/Hidden Deployment, Combat Jump, CC, effective Vanilla **Duo/Haris**, and especially **high-mobility Specialists**. The usual bottleneck is not a missing special rule but needing to *pay for multiple components* of a plan. Special-case impersonation and enemy-DZ Parachutist were called out as exceptions, not proof that every other unusual tool is present.
- **A strong but expensive floor:** Moonrakers, Ment, Raveneye and Varangians are excellent *for 12–15 points* but cannot subsidize several elite attackers like 3–7pt orders in some other armies. Buy **one clear primary gun**, an objective path and sufficient protection before upgrading every support unit.
- **Reactive defence is a crumple zone:** two Moonraker markers/mines, potentially reinforced by Varangians and **Peeler Thunderbolt TR + Engineer** or a resilient RoadBot, but no automatic Swiss/Noctifer-style decisive lethal ARO anchor. Your own Turn 1 after going second must use surviving Orders decisively to score, retaliate or repair.
- **Nonlethal is most efficient in enemy turn:** Adhesive / Riotstopper stopping an attacker after they have committed Orders can nullify the plan; **an immobilized enemy is not a dead enemy** and may recover. In active turn, prefer actual killing or a specific objective-opening purpose over endless nonlethal shooting.
