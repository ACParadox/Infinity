# Starmada — N5.3 tactical analysis and collection decisions

## Overview

**What Starmada does especially well:** layered nonlethal defense, command/protection tools, climbing and repairable HI/REM teams, and **mission-focused Core/Haris/Duo flexibility**. Its signature is a *Security* screen holding objectives while an independent *Bronze*, *Betatrooper* or *Road* module attacks or scores. The limitation is points: a heavy Core, Zeta and premium midfield units cannot all be bought without losing Orders and backup scorers.

### Particularly strong or unusual Fireteams

- **Security Haris/Core — adjustable defense-to-assault architecture:**
  - **Kappa or Bluecoat:** at least one is required as the Security source.
  - **Raveneye:** preplaced E/M mine and FO support.
  - **Sekudroid:** one inexpensive repairable adhesive member.
  - **Bronze / Ment / Jackboot / Vidocq FTO:** selected heavy gun, command insurance, technology or range/visor role.
  - **Trade-off:** cheap security screens can support an expensive push; avoid turning every slot into premium HI.
- **Bronze Duo/Haris — durable assault with recovery and command:**
  - **Bronze:** required native HI, up to two in family.
  - **Ment CoC / Sekudroid / Vidocq FTO:** economical protection/utility options.
  - **Parvati Wildcard:** recovery-focused support when the price is justified.
  - **Santiago FTO:** selected *linked* profiles only; its **Combat Jump** profiles cannot also be in this Fireteam.
- **Betatrooper family — climb to favorable lanes with technical support:**
  - **Betatrooper:** required for its Duo/Haris/Core.
  - **Sekudroid:** adhesive/control and repairable team body.
  - **Epsilon:** visored ranged solution.
  - **Trade-off:** movement efficiency does not make an expensive HI block immune to hackers.
- **RoadBot + Oko Road Duo:** **RoadBot** counts as a Lawkeeper-required source and **Oko** adds Sensor/FO/Repeater; supports rapid two-body scoring.
- **Varangian + Ioann Bann Duo:** Ioann counts as Varangian in this family; durable assault plus Smoke, but no new pooled Regular Order from Ioann's Irregular training.

### Unusual access, gaps and trade-offs

- **Lots of modular Fireteams:** **one Core, one Haris and effectively unlimited Duos**, but *Security*, *Nyokas*, *Betatroopers*, *Bronzes*, *Varangians* and *Road* have different source and FTO requirements.
- **Conventional Smoke, Eclipse and White Noise all available, via distinct carriers:**
  - **Varangian / Ioann Bann:** normal Smoke.
  - **Epsilon MSV2:** fires through conventional Smoke.
  - **Bluecoat Disco 16:** limited-use projected Eclipse, including against MSV.
  - **Cyberghost HD+ 21/0.5:** White Noise through a working Repeater/Pitcher network.
- **Distinct command/network engine:** **Saladin LT/CoC** offers Minelayer(2) Repeaters; **Ment CoC 14** is cheaper succession insurance and cannot be assumed to share Saladin's equipment.
- **Zeta is the only standard TAG family; Firebat is a VH:** **Wrecker** is *not* available in Starmada, so a TAG-heavy army has fewer distinct chassis choices.
- **Important non-access:** no Vanilla **Gamma**, **Moonraker**, **Team Sirius** or **Razor**. **Šarko** partially fills the camouflaged-midfield gap at greater cost.
- **Specific link restrictions matter:** **Nyoka FTO**, **Vidocq FTO** and **Santiago FTO** cannot be replaced by superficially similar non-FTO loadouts. The [N5.3 Starmada reference](../../sectorials/o-12/starmada.md) determines legal membership and points.

### Starmada in five decisions (the "why")

1. **Start with the link's job, then buy its members.** Starmada's 20–40+ point units can starve an army of Orders. Choose a primary plan before buying every toolbox:
   - **Security Core:** economical defense and Order support.
   - **Betatroopers / Sekudroids:** climbing mobility and specialist delivery.
   - **Bronzes / Nyokas:** heavier assault and lane control.
   - **RoadBot + Oko Duo:** objective mobility.
   - **Hector:** command and combined-arms package.
   - **Zeta:** TAG-centric strategy.
   
   These replace Vanilla-specific **Gamma, Omega, Wrecker and Moonraker** options; do not import those unavailable units.
2. **Security is the adjustable economy/defense Core.** A legal Security Haris/Core must retain at least one **Kappa or Bluecoat** source member. Build layers from eligible options:
   - **Kappas:** cheap Regular Orders and specialists.
   - **Bluecoats:** Sixth Sense, Discover and glue.
   - **Raveneye:** mines and deployment-zone control.
   - **PSI-Cop / Vidocq / Jackboot:** optional technical, visor and shooting roles.
   - **Bronze / Parvati:** premium push or recovery.
   
   Do not buy five expensive HI by habit or infer eligibility solely from a Wildcard/FTO label.
3. **Put *mobility* where it saves Orders.** Spend movement efficiencies on scoring or favorable angles:
   - **Betatrooper + Sekudroid:** Climbing Plus/Remote Presence mobility with adhesive/ARO support and a repairable structure.
   - **Betatrooper + Epsilon:** alternative team when the MSV gun opens the route.
   - **RoadBot + Oko Duo:** 20–24pt fast Paramedic with a 15/18pt Sensor/FO/Repeater for objective reach.
   
   Route around towers and walls, not through prepared enemy ARO lanes.
4. **Nonlethal control creates the attack.** Combine different disabling threats to force Dodges, Resets and inefficient movement:
   - **Bluecoat:** Heavy Riotstopper / adhesive.
   - **Sekudroid:** adhesive threats.
   - **Bronze:** E/M and PARA close combat.
   - **Raveneye:** E/M mines.
   - **Cyberghost:** Pitcher and HD+ White Noise.
   - **Shona / Varangian:** smoke and close-range pressure.
   - **Šarko:** mines and WildParrot.
   
   These are complementary setup pieces; repeating one premium gun is not equivalent.
5. **Protect mission capacity after the fighting piece falls.** Starmada has fewer cheap Regular bodies than Vanilla and no Moonraker Minelayer. Plan a backup scoring lane rather than relying on a 60–73pt hero/TAG:
   - **Oko:** economical Sensor/FO.
   - **RoadBot:** mobile Paramedic.
   - **Šarko:** camouflaged midfield specialist.
   - **Kappa / PSI-Cop:** alternate mission support.
   
   The June 2026 comparative criticism focused on this opportunity cost and the need to protect your surviving Orders.

### TAG, Vehicle and *all* REMs in Starmada

**Roster boundary (current Starmada options):**
- **Available flagship:** Zeta TAG.
- **Available Vehicle:** Firebat.
- **Not available:** Wrecker TAG, StormBot, Moonraker or Gamma from other O-12 rosters.
- **Shared tactical comparisons:** Zeta, Firebat, Oko, RoadBot and Peeler insights may transfer; other armies' AVA and Fireteam permissions never do.

**Practical platform shells:**
- **Zeta shell:** Zeta Lt/NCO + Ment/Cho CoC when appropriate + Lambda/Parvati/Yudbot engineering + cheap Road/Oko scorer + denial pieces. Challenge: too many points in support for one TAG; do the Order math first.
- **Mobility shell:** Beta Spitfire/KHD + **one Sekudroid** (or linked Epsilon for MSV problem) and a separate RoadBot/Oko Duo; obstacles become alternate routes, not dead ends.
- **Network shell:** use only network elements that create a relevant Spotlight / White Noise / Oblivion route:
  - **Cyberghost HD+ Pitcher:** establishes active projection.
  - **Kyttä (7), Oko (15–18), FuzzBot Baggage (8):** distinct Repeater or reload infrastructure.
  - **Separate Hacker/KHD protection:** protects the investment.
  - **Millicent (optional):** Guided follow-through if the list can reliably produce Targeted.
- **Cheap defense shell:** 13–17pt Bluecoats and 12pt Raveneyes near objectives + 19pt Peeler; avoid standing a 40+pt Bronze in an easy long-range ARO unless that trade is intentional.

### Real disagreements, with reasons

| Question | View A | View B / counterargument | Use-case distinction |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zeta: excellent or inefficient?** | [Loss of Lieutenant N5 TAG panel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dZo_gQWQYSY) argues **S-tier** for O-12: HMC, Climbing Plus, high resilience and ZetBot make it a flagship, with O-12's Lt support. Historic WIP12 Starmada players also built Zeta-focused lists. | [Tactical Awareness Starmada N5](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1DKpabLoqyE) is unusually negative: **no Tactical Awareness**, 70–73pts and few cheap Orders make it much worse value *specifically in Starmada*. | Parent-faction TAG grade ≠ Starmada-specific list efficiency. Compare scoring/Orders to a Bronze/Betatrooper core. |
| **Are Bronzes the faction's signature or a trap?** | Tactical Awareness N5 enthusiasts field Bronze HMG/Red Fury/Paramedic backed by Ment and Parvati: durability, CC control, high BTS, recovery and multiple legal teams create a robust push. | Infinity the Dante guest and 2026 Shepherd/Jordan critique highlight **expense**: two Bronzes or a stacked premium Haris can cost as much as a second independent attack group and sacrifice midfield presence. | Start with **one** Bronze and cheap support; add a second only if the mission rewards that much resilience. |
| **Šarko: essential midfield or overpriced?** | Tactical Awareness and Infinity the Dante say Šarko's new Camouflage/Infiltration/WildParrot fills a historic Starmada weakness; it can threaten objectives with KHD/FO. | June 2026 Robert Shepherd/Jordan call it a **costly Zero-like 22–24pt troop** with BS11 and short guns; not a strong value-per-point attacker. | Useful for a planned classified/objective/trap; weak as generic gunfighting filler. |
| **Firebat: tactical flyer or wasteful luxury?** | Aerial Mimetism(-6), NCO and E/M/adhesive toolkit can create angles most grounded pieces cannot. | The June 2026 pair rate its **35–36pt/no-Cover cost** very poorly relative to other factions' flyers; mobility alone does not earn its points. | Only pay for it when there is a specified first-turn route and NCO plan. |
| **Casanova / Andromeda high-variance aggression** | Some hosts like decoys, marker pressure and aggressive infiltration as ways to force defense reactions unavailable to passive Security links. | Tactical Awareness N5 hosts call Casanova **too expensive/too unreliable**, and Andromeda's risk/fragility hard to justify; a credible camo specialist/cheap smoke screen can do more predictable work. | Choose based on deployment zone, mission and failure tolerance; do not build the list assuming a high-risk infiltration success. |
| **Big Betatrooper/Epsilon Core or small team?** | Strong Betatrooper/linked Epsilon gunfighter; a Doctor plus Sekudroid protects expensive assets and reaches difficult angles. | WIP12's experienced Starmada guest dislikes committing a **heavy-HI Core** ("eggs in one basket"), preferring flexible Haris/Duos; later reviewers also note Beta and Epsilon still cost real points. | A Duo/Haris preserves more independent Orders/backup scoring; a Core is justified when its sustained push is the central win condition. |
| **Oko/RoadBot game-breaking?** | Tactical Awareness and historic WIP12 praise rapid objective access, multiple climbing routes and efficient Duo repositioning. | June 2026 comparison calls it a **sensible, modest** Duo rather than dominant: fragile WIP12/13 button presses still lose to control and good ARO positioning. | Strong *scoring infrastructure*, not a replacement for your main attack/defense. |
