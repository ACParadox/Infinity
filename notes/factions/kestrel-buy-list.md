## Whole-unit skips and conditional extra bodies

- **No default buy:** Fennecs, Fireflies, Jackals, Scarecrows, Yamabushi, Griffin, team Crux, Bipandra, Knauf, Tech-Bee, doctor, engineer, Warcor, Palbots, Mulebots and all shared Dronbot/Armbot chassis. You already have functional army architecture; only a specific simultaneously fielded profile/weapon/peripheral justifies a duplicate.
- **Proxy rather than purchase:** Agent Maximus, Dart, Sāchā, Ruby Monday and Priya Harper until repeated lists establish a need. Some are effective niche specialists, not universally bad units.
- **Character model priority follows play frequency:** Quinn / Emily for Wildcard-counts-as; Sforza if the FTO Wildcard and viral/adhesive combination enters lists. Avoid buying an Outrage multi-character box solely for Emily.
- **No 2nd Maximus TAG to represent Agent Maximus**; one Maximus rule, dramatically different silhouettes and troop types. Likewise **no 2nd Joan** for Kestrel; Joan is **not on the Kestrel standard roster** in the pinned reference.
- **Older and special sculpt warning:** don't pay a premium for original Sandtrap Yamabushi promo, old Kaldstrøm/Icestorm sculpts, exclusive Maximus or **a newer repackage** solely because it has a new box name.
