# Onyx Contact Force — N5/N5.3 tactics, TAGs, vehicles and REMs

_Last researched: 2026-10-22. Evidence: YouTube MCP transcripts of Tactical Awareness S3E14 (Mar 2025), Robert Shepherd's older Onyx focus, and Concilium Watch's army-building discussion; Podcast MCP transcripts of **Irregular Order #56 (Oct 17, 2026)**, MetaChemistry #38 (2022), WIP12 E176 Vanilla Combined (Jan 2025) and Loss of Lieutenant E142 N5 TAG tier list (Apr 2025). **Quick legality/price guardrails:** **Unidrons Core/Haris** require a Unidron (1–5), and **Nexus/Kerr-Nau Wildcards belong to the Unidron context only**. **Nourkias Wildcard** is legal specifically in Umbra Samaritan, Umbra Legate or Xeodron contexts—not freely in every Onyx team. **Xeodrons form Duo/Haris**, **Overdrons form Duo**, and **Sphinx and Caskuda are standalone TAGs** with no current standard team access. The current roster calls the airborne TAG **Caskuda**; some uploaded/community sources spell it *Cascuda*. Overdron options are **51/1.5 HRL(+1B), 59/1.5 Plasma Sniper(+1B), and 62/1.5 HRMC**, so the uploaded HRMC/Plasma debate excludes a legitimate lower-cost alternative.

## Executive take: the role structure

Onyx is the **Combined Army's robotic combined-arms / mobile TAG + Umbra sectorial**. The good lists combine a hard-to-remove REM/order backbone, a premium *problem solver*, and a separate mission plan. The faction's unusual strength is that **many supporting pieces are simultaneously Orders, weapons, specialists and recoverable REMs**.

**Default sequence:** (1) pick a safe or fighting Lieutenant; (2) decide whether the main hammer is an **Overdron / Xeodron + Nourkias / active Umbra group / airborne Cascuda**; (3) add **Unidron, Nexus and/or Q-Drone** defence; (4) enable attacks with **Bit & KISS, Sensor, EVO and a reachable Engineer**; (5) add one alternate route, a hidden ARO, and mission specialists; (6) check SWC, hacking protection and Orders before buying another TAG.

- **Where Onyx excels:** plasma/K1 ammunition; Albedo anti-MSV long-range threat; N5 **Unidron + Engineer** attrition; multiple **Super-Jump** routes; serious repeaters/KHD tools; active TAG pressure; Noctifer uncertainty.
- **What it lacks:** dependable standard Smoke; many cheap infiltrating scoring bodies; consequence-free losses of expensive linked models; immunity to hacking. An all-visible expensive TAG brick lets the opponent tailor every response. A hidden Noctifer or airborne Fraacta/Ko Dali changes that calculation.
- **Order economy:** a multi-TAG list can have many *extra* Tactical Awareness/NCO resources yet still have few freely assignable Regular Orders. Count **who may spend each Order and on what**. TAGs do not capture every scenario objective.
- **SWC is a real constraint:** Unidron guns/TinBots, TAG main weapons, Noctifer and Umbra weapons can all consume SWC. Never decide on your last SWC-intensive gun before buying the actual ARO/network package.

## Build archetypes (not current Army legality exports)

### A. REM attrition: Unidron + Nexus + repair

**Choose:** an efficient **Unidron-anchored** Core/Haris with Plasma/K1 guns and an eligible **Nexus Engineer/Hacker Wildcard**; Q-Drone and R-/M-Drone provide **independent**, unlinked coverage; add one premium independent attacker.

**Why it works:** each Unidron can score or threaten, REM Presence/Dogged make it inconvenient to remove, and a nearby Engineer forces the attacker to spend Orders *again*. Some 2026 Onyx podcast hosts regard Unidrons as unusually cost-effective linked shooters and defence.

**Watch out:** every defensive piece may be hackable; active gunfights without modifiers or range advantage still fail. Take only as many team members as the link bonus/movement plan warrants. A passive 5-model core cannot substitute for a successful primary attack.

### B. Overdron + follow-up attack

**Choose:** one Overdron heavy gun, a cheap command/support package, Bit & KISS or relevant hackers, and a compact Umbra/Xeodron or Fraacta assault follow-up.

**Why:** the Overdron forces premium MSV AROs off the table using Albedo and long-range fire. The *second* piece then crosses safely and does the mission.

**Watch out:** Albedo does not negate cover, range, templates or every return-fire situation. It can become an expensive long-lane gun into armies with no MSV. Include a second angle rather than spending all Orders re-fighting the same impossible FTF.

### C. Nourkias + Xeodron mobile team

**Choose:** Nourkias plus a Xeodron in a **Xeodron Duo/Haris with at least one native Xeodron**; Nourkias has conditional Wildcard access in this context. Keep a **Nexus Engineer independently positioned** for recovery—**Nexus is not a Xeodron Wildcard in the pinned chart**.

**Why:** mobile Super-Jump Duo can exploit roofs, different heights and short engagement windows; hacker/CC and K1/MULTI weapons address different defensive layers. Command/NCO/Tactical Awareness resources potentially make activation unusually efficient.

**Watch out:** do **not** count the expensive TAG as unhackable or the Umbra as invincible; a hacking trap or close template can neutralize both expensive units. A Nexus Engineer only earns its cost if it can keep up or use a viable recovery route.

### D. Cascuda insertion + Overdron suppression (experimental)

The Oct 2026 Irregular Order review explicitly builds **Overdron HRMC + Cascuda + Bit & KISS + E-Drone**, economical Imetron/Ikadron/R-Drone Orders, Nexus command/Unidron support and a hidden scoring specialist.

**Why:** Overdron removes safe opposing AROs, Bit & KISS pressures hackers that could trap Cascuda, EVO raises the chance of successful insertion, and Cascuda attacks from a nonfrontal axis.

**Counterargument from the hosts:** spending E-Drone just to improve a single Combat Jump may not repay its cost; Cascuda is an exciting **specialized, setup-heavy** tool, not a general better Xeodron. Ask whether the extra chance of a successful landing is worth the support model.

### E. Multiple heavy TAGs: gear-check skew

Can field several Xeodrons, perhaps Overdron plus support. Threatens opponents who lack enough hard anti-armor/hacking. However, a low number of specialists, fixed attack vectors and high SWC/points concentration can make an attractive but brittle tournament list.

**Recommendation:** start with **one heavy platform and one *different* attack vector**, not three interchangeable gun TAGs, unless the mission and terrain explicitly reward the skew.

## TAGs, Vehicle and REM deep dive

### TAG hierarchy by job (not absolute ranking)

**Current cost floor:** don't overlook **Overdron Heavy Rocket Launcher (+1B) at 51 points/1.5 SWC**, a lower-entry-cost Albedo/TacAware ranged-platform alternative to the 59-point Plasma Sniper and 62-point HRMC; the cheaper gun changes long-lane pressure and coverage, so it is not a straight substitute.

| Platform | Best target / table condition | Avoid / prepare |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overdron HRMC** | Long firelane, hard ARO removal, particularly versus MSV pieces through Albedo; lots of dice compensate for moderate BS | Enemy has no MSV or denies lanes; a close attack or hacker threatens it. Engineer access matters. |
| **Overdron Plasma Sniper** | Long-range suppressive/ARO pressure, strong multi-save threat and template/damage characteristics against troops vulnerable to Plasma | Fewer attack dice/range situations may make HRMC the safer active gun. **Both preferences appear in the transcripts.** |
| **Xeodron MULTI or K1** | Mobile TAG/Haris or Duo that can change firing angle, exploit K1 against armored targets and operate alongside Nourkias | Weak to hacking including Total Control; moderate raw BS/ARM compared with flagship TAGs. |
| **Sphinx** | Terrain-rich flanks and weak order generators unreachable to visible platforms; hidden deployment makes opponent guard unexpected spaces | No compelling AP solution, expensive opportunity cost, repeater zones, Sensor/high-access opponents. |
| **Cascuda** | Enemy DZ/rear corner with exploitable landing and attack path after ARO/hacker clearance | Cannot count on landing for free; risky insertion and cost of EVO/Bit/Overdron support. |

**Vehicle terminology:** Xeodron, Overdron, **Caskuda** (sometimes spelled “Cascuda” in media), and Sphinx are discussed as **TAGs**, not generic Vehicle (VH) profiles. Research did **not** establish a separate Onyx-only Vehicle profile. Do not treat a large mini or Remote Presence as the Vehicle troop type. Verify any VH/vehicle additions in the current Army catalogue.

### Cross-faction lessons you can actually transfer

- **Vanilla Combined (WIP12 E176)**: Overdron **HRMC + Med-Tech** is praised because Albedo/MOV/REM Presence let it win lanes and be repaired. Cheap Q-Drone/Ikadron defensive staples transfer well. Vanilla has *other premium threats* that often push Onyx-type pieces out of its lists; do not infer that Onyx gets all of Vanilla's order/Fireteam options.
- **Morats**: Q-Drone/Ikadron/Baggage/T-Drone are shared tools; **Morat Warhorse** and cheap Daturazi Smoke **do not** transfer to Onyx broadly. Morat Pitchers/close-range assault change the job of shared drones.
- **Shasvastii**: Sphinx/Noctifer/Malignos ambiguity transfers, but SEF builds around denser Camo/Impersonation. Onyx tends to blend **visible robotic armor with just enough off-table threat** to keep enemy deployment honest.
- **Next Wave / other Combined**: shared Nexus, hackers or REMs are a useful profile benchmark; do not assume Next Wave's specific Fireteam charts or new squads are permitted in Onyx.

## Explicit opposing opinions / testable tradeoffs

1. **Is Onyx mainly a REM faction?** The 2026 Irregular Order hosts disagree in usage: one values **Unidron + Engineer** as the standout structure; the other often skips Unidrons for Umbra/TAG aggression. Both styles are credible. Pick based on whether the mission rewards **durable multi-order defence** or **rapid independent attack**.
2. **Overdron weapon choice:** March 2025 Tactical Awareness strongly praises the Plasma Sniper, including its ability to crack difficult AROs with saves and Albedo; 2025 Vanilla and 2026 Onyx reviewers often prefer **HRMC** for Burst and general-purpose lane clearance. HRMC is the safer broad hammer; Plasma Sniper can be excellent for a specific lane/target profile.
3. **Does Albedo justify Overdron automatically?** An early-2025 host calls it close to an auto-include; others argue that if nobody fields relevant MSV, much of the unique value disappears. **Albedo is a matchup bonus, not a universal defensive modifier.**
4. **Is Nourkias worth it over Samaritan?** Newer reviewers tend to pay the premium for stronger combinations of melee, hacking, marker/command utility and TAG linking; the basic Samaritan remains a real alternative when the points differential buys a useful specialist or extra Order.
5. **Sphinx polarizes:** MetaChemistry 2022 praised its ceiling; Loss of Lieutenant 2025 and Irregular Order Oct 2026 emphasize **lack of AP, cost and alternative platforms**. Opposing players still rate hidden/mobility pressure very highly. Deploy on a favorable table with a specific target itinerary; otherwise prefer Xeodron, Overdron or Cascuda + support.
6. **Cascuda versus Sphinx:** Cascuda is a cheaper, better-armed **arriving-from-above** problem but depends on a successful Jump and safe hacker environment; Sphinx hides in initial deployment and uses terrain and Camo to choose attacks over multiple turns. Not interchangeable.
7. **Kerr-Nau versus new Nexus:** Kerr-Nau's linkable Plasma/KHD may justify his premium; Oct 2026 commenters say updated Nexus improvements make the character less efficient than before. Choose for his actual weapon, hacking and team benefits.
8. **Heavy Suryat / Rodok support:** Suryat offers Warhorse/ARO sturdiness but can consume a huge fraction of a list when linked; Rodok mobility/Engineering and smaller Unidron shells can better support the main robots. Do not overpay for a heavy Fireteam in an army already rich in expensive guns.
9. **Pure Core vs small links:** A 5-Unidron Core can be efficient but a 3-person Haris/Duo plus independent attacker spreads risk, moves through terrain more easily and often leaves more Orders for specialists. Team bonuses must be priced against body count and the vulnerability of moving a cluster.

## Opponent counterplay / how to prevent it

- **Against enemy hacking:** choose positions outside repeaters, pressure enemy hackers with **Bit & KISS / Nourkias**, place Firewalls, and ensure an Engineer/Reset plan. This is the critical failure mode for TAG-heavy Onyx.
- **Against enemy Smoke:** without reliable MSV in the selected list, smoke can simply block the Q-Drone/Overdron gunline. Buy the appropriate MSV or use Sensor, flanks, repeater zones and non-LoF AROs rather than blindly expecting the gun to see through it.
- **Against Camo:** **M-Drone Sensor** finds targets more economically than risking expensive TAG/Legate AROs to Discover. Pair it with a ready gun or templates; Sensor without follow-up isn't scoring.
- **Against missiles/templates and speculative attack:** do not bunch all Unidrons, Nexus and Engineer into one convenient attack; durability/repair works only if the support survives.
- **Against heavy long-range AROs:** Overdron uses favorable range and Albedo; if that fails, leverage Noctifer, Ko Dali/Fraacta or a TAG's Super-Jump to alter the angle.
- **Against objective-heavy missions:** keep a hidden Malignos/Shrouded or linked Nexus/Unidron specialist **and enough assignable Regular Orders**. A TAG that survives but cannot score is not the whole plan.
- **Against budget collapse:** if the central TAG fails its first attack, there must still be a backup weapon, a mission-runner and recoverable defence. Do not invest every Order into trying the same FtF again.

## Vision control — role/value update (23 October 2026)

- **Different answer to MSV:** **Overdron Albedo** attacks the visor directly but does **not** create a shared Zero Visibility zone. Onyx lacks the dependable cheap Dāturazi Smoke package that Morats/Vanilla CA exploit. Its Noctifer/Repeater/hidden threat and Umbra-led tactical solutions must be planned by actual roster.
- **Counterpoint:** a supported Overdron can remove several MSV AROs, but Albedo adds little versus enemies without visors; a hidden threat/cheap REM may preserve more attack Orders. See [vision tier guide](../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).
