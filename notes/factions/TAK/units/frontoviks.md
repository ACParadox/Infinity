# Frontoviks, Separate Assault Battalion — TAK (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A — excellent compact-Fireteam role compression.** User preference: do not build around five-member Cores. Prioritize Duos/Haris and preserve points for independent TAK marker pieces. The **26/0 Engineer** is the default Frontovik, followed by the **28/0.5 MSV1 AP Sniper** for a compact defensive link and the **30/1 AP Spitfire** for an economical active gun. The **19/1.5 HRL** is a points-cheap but SWC-intensive niche alternative. One Frontovik is usually sufficient; additional models are optional.

## Why the chassis works

- **Mimetism (-3):** imposes a shooting penalty on eligible opponents. In a linked firing position it combines well with cover, range and appropriate Fireteam bonuses. MSV or other counters can remove the Mimetism advantage.
- **Warhorse:** remains Regular in Loss of Lieutenant and Retreat, cannot enter Isolated State, and ignores opposing BS Attack (-X) skill MODs. This is not immunity to all negative MODs or all states; Mimetism, Cover, range, Surprise Attack and other applicable effects remain relevant. It does not prevent the army entering Loss of Lieutenant if the Frontovik LT dies.
- **Assault Pistol:** active B4, +3 at 0–8"; provides all options with a serious close-range fallback. Not a direct template.
- **T2 Rifle:** B3, +3 at 8–16", two Wounds lost per failed T2 save on ordinary susceptible targets, Anti-materiel and Suppressive Fire. Not AP; the weapon is less efficient against heavily armoured opponents than the AP Spitfire.
- **Regular order and modest footprint:** a useful S2 team member that contributes an Order even when not shooting or scoring.
- **Weakness:** W1/BTS0, no marker state or extra Wound layer. Do not confuse Warhorse with NWI or general ammunition immunity. A failed save can end the gun's contribution immediately.

## Profile analysis

### MSV1 AP Sniper — 28/0.5 (A)

The preferred defensive Frontovik for a compact Veterans Duo/Haris or Line Kazak Haris; do not build a five-member Core just for this gun. AP Sniper gives long positive range (16–48"), AP against armour and B2 active; the low SWC leaves room for TAK's independent attack pieces. MSV1 removes ordinary Mimetism (-3), reduces Mimetism (-6) to -3, and permits shooting through Smoke with the applicable -6 penalty; it does not see through Eclipse or replicate MSV2. Standard marker/targeting requirements still apply.

Mimetism, Warhorse and Fireteam modifiers make the sniper harder to remove than a naked line infantry ARO. The sniper is also a viable active-turn secondary gun against an exposed long-range target; avoid sacrificing it to an enemy's ideal HMG lane. Keep an option to go prone/hide or decline unfavorable exposure. MSV1 belongs to this option only.

- **Vystrel Neurocinetics AP Sniper (26/0.5):** stronger dedicated reactive alternative thanks to Neurocinetics, Sapper and STR2/Remote Presence; different Gunnery architecture, S4 base, hackable REM and repair requirements. Frontovik is more mobile as an ordinary S2 linked secondary gun and has MSV1.
- **TankHunter PAC (36/1.5):** hidden hard-target threat rather than an exposed persistent linked gun; costs more and competes for premium SWC.
- **Frontovik HRL (19/1.5):** saves 9 points but costs 1 additional SWC, lacks MSV1 and uses shorter effective range.

**Default:** choose the sniper only when the list needs a distinct MSV1 long-range ARO or secondary gun. With smaller links and an existing Vystrel/Gunnery shell, the Engineer is the better default.

### Engineer — 26/0 (A+)

Five points over the base T2 Rifle buys Engineer, Deactivator, Panzerfaust and D-Charges, all without SWC. WIP13 is adequate rather than exceptional. It remains a credible infantry attacker, Classified runner and a limited anti-heavy threat; can clear eligible states from teammates, repair STR targets, and perform Engineer objectives. D-Charges add Sabotage and eligible anti-materiel objective work; the Panzerfaust is a disposable AP+EXP weapon, not a sustained primary gun. Deactivator addresses eligible enemy deployables.

The best Frontovik for a moving Veterans Haris or Ratnik-led Mobility team. Engineer does not heal ordinary Wounds or restore a Veteran Kazak's NWI; carry a Doctor for biological recovery if needed. A servant is not a fourth Fireteam member; verify Peripheral/Fireteam activation restrictions before planning repairs. Warhorse preserves the Engineer's own pooled Order against Isolation but does not extend its protection to teammates.

- **Dozer (13/0):** much cheaper for static artillery repair or Line Kazak/Gunnery support.
- **Irmandinho (8/0):** cheap independent Engineer/Smoke/trade option, but Irregular and not a Veterans Fireteam member.
- **Veteran Kazak FO (36/0):** more resilient forward specialist, complementary rather than interchangeable with Engineer.
- **Strelok FO (17/0):** cheaper forward marker specialist if only button-pushing is required.

Pay 26 points when several of the Engineer's jobs are relevant, not just for a generic rear repair model.

### AP Spitfire — 30/1 (A)

B4 AP and +3 from 8–24" on a BS12/Mimetism platform. Particularly strong in an equivalence-rich Veterans or Line Kazak Fireteam when it can reach midrange safely. A good active gun with a 15-point saving over a Veteran Kazak AP HMG, but it lacks the Veteran's durability and HMG reach. The Assault Pistol covers 0–8".

- **Veteran Kazak AP HMG (45/1.5):** premium durable repeatable gun at longer range.
- **TankHunter AP HMG (33/1.5):** strong independent Camo/Surprise BS13 long-range option.
- **Spetsnaz HMG (36/1.5):** independent Camo/Marksmanship shooter against covered infantry.

Choose the Frontovik when the list already wants a moving Fireteam and an economical AP attacker. Do not build a large Core solely to make it into a top-end gunfighter; one Wound remains one Wound.

### Heavy Rocket Launcher — 19/1.5 (B+)

Cheapest Frontovik, saves nine points against the sniper but consumes three times its SWC. B2 active, +3 at 16–32", Continuous Damage and optional circular impact-template blast provide an anti-cluster threat. The impact template is not an automatic direct template. Useful as a cheap linked long-lane guard, secondary attack piece or Veterans equivalence body. No MSV1; less suitable against Mimetism or smoke. Use when points are tighter than SWC or the template is important, rather than as an automatic alternative to the sniper.

### Paramedic — 23/0 (B)

Only two points over the T2 Rifle for general Specialist access and MediKit utility. Useful if a team needs a button-pusher with credible firepower. Not a Doctor; MediKit relies on the relevant recovery roll and can kill the target. A **112 FTO Doctor (11/0)** is far cheaper in Veterans and is often the better recovery purchase, but does not count as Veteran and lacks the Frontovik's gun/Warhorse/Mimetism. A **Kazak Doktor (12/0)** counts as Line Kazak in that family; a **Konduktor FTO Paramedic (10/0)** is another Line Kazak team option.

### T2 Rifle — 21/0 (C+)

Useful as a 0-SWC Veteran-counting body in a Veterans team or Ratnik-counting member in Mobility. It is expensive for a mere order generator, especially in Line Kazaks. The 2-point Paramedic or 5-point Engineer upgrades normally give more worthwhile jobs; the cheaper HRL is not a free substitution because of its 1.5 SWC.

### Lieutenant — 21/0 (C)

Same weapons as the base option, one Lieutenant Order. Warhorse preserves its own Regular status but does not provide extra Lieutenant Orders, succession, or special protection from dying. A possible decoy among identical T2 Rifle models in a Veterans shell; avoid exposing the command model simply to use its gun.

- **Line Kazak LT (9/0):** budget hidden command by ordinary deployment, with cheaper decoys.
- **Voronin LT (22/0):** Counterintelligence, +1 Command Token and Line Kazak-counting Wildcard; no TAK Strategos.
- **TankHunter CoC Minelayer (25/0):** worthwhile insurance for an aggressive LT.
- **Veteran Kazak LT FO (36/0):** more robust active and scoring LT.

## Fireteam access and recommended packages

| Family | Legal types | Frontovik restriction | Equivalence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Line Kazaks | Core / Haris | Max 1; at least one native Line Kazak | Line Kazak |
| Veterans | Core / Haris / Duo | Max 5; Veteran Kazak and/or Frontovik required-choice | Veteran |
| Mobility | Haris / Duo | Max 2; Dynamo and/or Ratnik required-choice | Ratnik |

TAK allows one Core, one Haris and unlimited Duos. A Frontovik cannot anchor Line Kazaks without a native Line Kazak, or Mobility without a Dynamo/Ratnik. Counts-as Ratnik is only Fireteam equivalence, not HI classification or a Tactical Awareness Order. A Dynamo and Frontovik are not equivalent for bonuses.
