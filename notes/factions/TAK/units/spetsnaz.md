# Kazak Spetsnazs — Tartary Army Corps (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A+ overall.** The **HMG is S-tier for efficient first-contact gunfighting**, not a sustained all-comers apex. The **AP SMG and Boarding Shotgun Parachutists are A-tier** and offer a distinct flank threat; the **AP Sniper is B-tier** as an inexpensive-SWC concealed long-range counter. One HMG and one Parachutist is an attractive combination, but neither is compulsory.

## HMG — why its opening is exceptional

B4, PS5, favorable 16–32 inch range. Marksmanship removes the target's Partial Cover/Nanoscreen BS penalty, **not its saving-roll bonus**. Mimetism(-3) and the active-turn Surprise Attack(-3) impose separate penalties on appropriate opposing rolls; your own Cover adds another -3 to opposing BS when applicable. Against an ordinary covered BS13 opponent, both in positive range with the Spetsnaz in Cover, first-contact numbers can be BS15 versus BS7. After revealing, the same example becomes 15 versus 10. Enemy Mimetism, visors, Sixth Sense/Combat Instinct where applicable, range and other effects change that comparison. Surprise Attack can recur after legally returning to Camouflaged State, but requires additional Orders; Decoy is not regenerated.

Decoy adds a second apparent Camo marker at deployment, not another gun or Order. Use the marker shell to conceal the real attack axis. Do not confuse Camouflage with Hidden Deployment or casually reveal the HMG in a low-value ARO. White Noise, Albedo and Reflective affect Marksmanship users as specified in the current rules, even without a visor. Marksmanship does not remove enemy Mimetism or shoot through Smoke.

**Current ranking:** #8, S, Value/Mod-Stacking in the consolidated [gunfighters ranking](../../../list-building/gunfighters-ranking.md), below TankHunter AP HMG (#7, S). The older TAK overview's #1 historical MOD-stacking and separate value placements are not comparable to this merged ranking. Keep the distinction between efficient opening and sustained apex shooting.

TankHunter is the more universal budget main gun against TAG/HI; Spetsnaz is preferable when covered light/medium ARO removal is the key problem. Veteran is a better sustained attacker, despite ranking #17 A+ in the merged list. Spetsnaz is conventional non-Hackable-by-type MI (not immune to Spotlight or other applicable effects), but one failed engagement can remove a 36-point investment. Against very high ARM, AP is materially better than normal HMG with Shock.

## AP Sniper — useful but not a default ARO

32/0.5, B2 active, PS5 AP, favorable 16–48 inch ranges. One SWC cheaper than HMG, longer positive range and AP ammunition; Marksmanship/Surprise work on active attacks. It lacks Neurocinetics, Total Reaction, Fireteam bonus dice and a second wound. Reactive attacks have B1 and no Surprise Attack. AP and Shock do not become DA/EXP merely because it is a sniper.

Take it as a concealed counter-sniper where extreme distance or low SWC matters, not as a permanently exposed lane guard. The Vystrel Neurocinetics AP Sniper (26/0.5) is usually the superior dedicated defensive purchase; TankHunter PAC supplies more frightening reactive ammunition. The Spetsnaz sniper's real distinction is active long-range precision and marker deception. HMG is usually more efficient inside its positive band.

## Parachutists — TAK's distinct airborne axis

Both cost **27/0**, add D-Charges and ordinary Grenades (not Smoke), and have the same CC21/MA2, Marksmanship, Mimetism, Courage, Shock and Stealth package. They are **ordinary Parachutist**, not Parachutist (Deployment Zone), Combat Jump, Camouflage or Surprise Attack profiles. A legal edge outside the enemy DZ can threaten rearward targets after arrival; actual enemy DZ insertion requires a different rule.

Arrival uses a Long Skill and the trooper's own Order; off-table it contributes no Regular Order to the pool. No Partial Cover during the entry Order; enemy AROs apply. Decoy(1) applies when entering with normal deployment/coherency restrictions, and is useful for deception but does not make an exposed entry automatically safe. The first Attack cancels Decoy and there is no Camo reset. Choose a safe edge and ensure the combat group has follow-up Orders.

The Boarding Shotgun does not have a direct-template mode in the current weapon chart. The AP SMG has no +1B modifier in the November backup; both options have Decoy(1), despite older update commentary suggesting other values. **Default to AP SMG** for B3 and Suppressive Fire; choose Boarding Shotgun for the +6 band, stronger per-hit saves and specific very short-range fights. At close range the AP SMG's extra die and the shotgun's greater accuracy are a matchup-dependent tradeoff, not an absolute dominance. Neither has a direct template gun; Grenades are an impact template and offer speculative fire. Silenced Pistol (AP+Shock versus halved BTS, Silent) is an alternative against suitable targets. MA2/AP CCW can threaten an isolated armored piece, but W1 makes prolonged melee or unfavorable trades risky.

D-Charges enable relevant demolition/mission tasks; neither profile is a Specialist for generic console activation. The mission and opponent's flank screen determine the value of the threat. A Dynamo or Carmen supplies a cheaper ground-pressure vector; a Scout or Strelok supplies on-table objectives/marker presence. None reproduces actual off-table edge uncertainty. TAK lacks Vanilla's Mirage-5 and Van Zant, so the Spetsnaz fills a meaningful roster niche without claiming their deeper deployment capabilities. The current [DZ attacker ranking](../../../list-building/dz-attackers-ranking.md) lists both profiles jointly at **#71, A**, Value/Airborne/Mod-Stacking.

The [TAK research synthesis](../tak-listbuilding.md#transcript-verified-review-and-remmobility-deep-dive--2026-10-19) records a real disagreement: older Tactical Awareness favored HMG saturation, whereas the later WIP12/Swiss Miss discussions questioned paying repeatedly for one-wound non-AP guns. These are attributed historical opinions, not newly sampled tournament statistics. Current consolidated rankings support one excellent HMG without forcing multiples. Build around enough usable Orders, protected command, midfield specialists/mines and a separate anti-armor answer.

**Collection:** 2 dedicated models owned, AVA4. Two are sufficient for the normal HMG + Parachutist configuration, subject to loadout representation/proxy rules. No purchase priority for a third or fourth unless deliberately building a saturation list.

**Final selection rule:** Choose HMG when cover removal and first-contact efficiency matter; TankHunter AP HMG for armor and Veteran Kazak for sustained attacks. Take one AP SMG Parachutist when a separate flank threat is useful, or Boarding Shotgun for a close-range route. AP Sniper is mission/table dependent. Do not buy four merely because AVA permits it.
