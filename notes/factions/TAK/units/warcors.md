# Warcors — Tartary Army Corps (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A− overall — exceptional budget ARO, not an automatic inclusion in TAK.** Take **0–1**; default to the 360º Visor. Three points and a spare Combat Group slot buy a credible non-lethal speed bump, but TAK's unusually inexpensive native Regular Orders make the opportunity cost of an Irregular slot more important than in many other sectorials.

## What three points buy

Flash Pulse uses WIP13 rather than BS9, has B1, +3 range at 8–24", PS7 and one BTS save on an ordinary hit. Before opposing rolls or other MODs, WIP16 in positive range means an 80% chance of a successful single die; this is not an 80% chance of stunning a shooting opponent. See [weapon chart](../../../../rules/4_Ammunition%20and%20Weaponry/Weapon_Chart.md).

A failed save inflicts Stunned: no Attacks and −3 to rolls other than saves, normally until the States Phase of the Player Turn in which it was caused. The target may still Move, Dodge, Reset or perform legal non-Attack mission skills. See [Stunned State](../../../../rules/8_Game%20States%20and%20Glossary/Stunned_State.md).

- A successful stun can end the offensive contribution of a premium enemy gunfighter for the turn, regardless of its remaining wounds.
- Even without stunning anyone, forcing a separate attack, detour, or smoke setup may be enough return for three points.
- High BTS, cover, Mimetism and high-Burst attackers reduce the reliability of the effect. It is a speed bump, not the primary ARO.
- No lethal ranged weapon, no marker, no preplaced mine, no specialist or Repeater utility; ARM0/BTS0/W1 is easily removed.

## 360º Visor versus Sixth Sense

- **360º Visor (default):** constant full facing for legal LoF, including enemy movement rather than only attacks against the Warcor. Good for cross-lanes and rear approaches; does not see through walls or Smoke.
- **Sixth Sense:** allows reactions to attacks directed at the Warcor outside normal facing, ignores the −6 visibility MOD when attacked through a Zero Visibility Zone under the applicable rules, defeats Stealth, and removes most negative Dodge/Reset MODs. Does not grant unconditional 360º LoF against movement and **does not generally cancel Surprise Attack BS/WIP shooting MODs in N5**. Its Dodge/Reset benefits are separate.

See [360º Visor](../../../../rules/5c_Equipment/360_Visor.md), [Sixth Sense](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Sixth_Sense.md) and [Surprise Attack](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Surprise_Attack.md).

| Konduktor FTO Chain Rifle/LSG/Shock Mine | 7 / 0 | Regular | +4 points for pooled Order, lethal close defense, carried mine and Line Kazaks Fireteam eligibility |
| Irmandinho Chain Rifle/Smoke | 8 / 0 | Irregular + Impetuous | +5 points for mobile Engineer, Smoke and trade threat; not a pooled Regular Order |
| Line Kazak FO | 10 / 0 | Regular | +7 points for WIP13 Flash Pulse, Rifle, Forward Observer, specialist and link access; normal facing |

Prioritize an adequate Regular Order pool and the necessary mission specialists before filling a spare slot with a Warcor. At the troop limit, replacing the Warcor with a Regular model is often worth the small points difference. TAK has no Wallace-style Inspiring Leadership conversion package in the documented roster. A Command Token can convert an available Irregular Order under the usual rules, but this is not a native pooled Order or a free recurring conversion.

## Deployment and synergy

Use a secondary lane in Partial Cover, preferably in the positive range band, rather than exposing the Warcor to a trivial first attack from the main long-range enemy gun.

- **Vystrel / Tankhunter / Frontovik:** layer Flash Pulse coverage with the established lethal ARO piece. Ideally an attacker cannot remove both from one favorable position; do not assume a simultaneous Flash Pulse makes the other attack Face to Face.
- **Scout / Strelok / Konduktor:** markers and mines make alternate routes expensive after the enemy avoids the Warcor. The carried mine of the 7pt Konduktor is not a predeployed Minelayer mine.
- **Spetsnaz / Veteran Kazak:** protect the counterattack plan by making access to the deployment zone less efficient.
- **Line Kazaks Fireteam:** position the Warcor to cover a side or rear approach; it cannot join the Fireteam.

Journalist provides +3 to allied Guts Rolls and −3 to enemy Guts Rolls within its Zone of Control under the [Journalist rules](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Journalist.md). Minor situational benefit for holding a lane; do not spend Orders moving an otherwise useful ARO solely to deliver this aura.

## Selection and collection

- **Take one:** three points remain, a slot is available, Regular economy is already sufficient, and a meaningful secondary lane or flank needs coverage.
- **Skip:** the list is slot-capped, short on pooled Orders or needs a lethal close defender or another mission specialist more than a Flash Pulse.
- **Profile choice:** 360º Visor by default; Sixth Sense only for a deliberate defensive plan against Stealth or attacks through Smoke.

The [owned TAK inventory](../../../../inventory/tak.md) records one shared Warcor, filling AVA1. No purchase required.

**Bottom line:** premium effectiveness per point, modest effectiveness per slot. The Warcor complements TAK's layered defense but should not displace the cheap Regular backbone or the lethal ARO anchor. It remains outside the global Regular-cheerleader and template/mine-DZ-defender rankings, in keeping with the existing guides.
