# Dynamo Regiment of Kazak Light Cavalry — TAK (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A-tier overall; A+ value on the 14-point SMG/Panzerfaust, A on the 16-point Paramedic, B+ on the AP Spitfire.** A Dynamo is primarily a fast Regular-order toolbox that develops the board, delivers smoke and launches mines. The cheap profiles are particularly attractive because they do not compete heavily with TAK's premium guns. Two cheap Dynamos form an excellent 30-point Mobility Duo.

## Verified profiles

The [TAK reference](../../../../sectorials/ariadna/tartary-army-corps.md#dynamo-regiment-of-kazak-light-cavalry) lists unit ID 1134, AVA 5, LI, Regular + Impetuous, MOV 20–10 cm (8–4"), CC15, BS12, PH12, WIP13, ARM1, BTS0, W1, S4. Shared: Motorcycle, Mine Dispenser (Shock), Dodge (+1"), Dogged, Courage, Impetuous, Mimetism (-3), Limited Cover, Smoke Grenade Launcher, Boarding Pistol and CCW.

## Why the cheap Dynamo is so good

- **Order efficiency:** a Regular Order for the pool plus optional independent Impetuous activation. An unlinked bike can use the latter to advance and shoot smoke or a mine when legal, retaining its Regular Order. It is not a second unrestricted order.
- **Mobility:** up to 12" on a normal Move-Move Order; excellent for lateral repositioning, ground-level objectives and flank pressure.
- **Smoke:** BS12 Smoke Grenade Launcher creates crossings without relying on a separate warband. Respect range bands, LoF and MSV.
- **Mine Dispenser (Shock):** creates a persistent threat at range rather than needing to physically reach a corner. All three profiles carry it.
- **Dogged:** can keep fighting through an otherwise Unconscious result if eligible; not a second wound, and the model dies at the end of the turn if Dogged is used. Preserve a specialist rather than automatically choosing Dogged.
- **Defensive profile:** Mimetism helps in Face-to-Face rolls, but Limited Cover prevents the usual -3 BS modifier from Partial Cover; the saving-roll benefit can still apply. S4, ARM1, W1 and no Prone make exposure expensive.
- **Motorcycle restrictions:** no Climb, ladders, upward Jump or Cautious Movement; ground routes must accommodate the base. It is LI, not VH, and not a conventional hackable HI/TAG attack piece.

## Profile breakdown

### 14/0 SMG + Panzerfaust — A+

Best default. This is a 14-point Regular Order with both inexpensive close-range pressure and a limited-use AP+EXP ranged threat. The Panzerfaust gives a cheap piece a credible chance to trade into hard targets, but its low Burst and ammunition limits make it a poor substitute for a primary gun. The smoke and mine dispenser are often worth more than its shooting.

Use it to develop a flank, screen a primary attack, deliver mines, challenge a corner or trade when the payoff warrants it. It can also protect the Paramedic in a Duo by taking the dangerous lead. If no lane is available, preserve the Regular Order.

### 16/0.5 Boarding Shotgun Paramedic — A

For 2 points and 0.5 SWC over the basic profile, gain a fast WIP13 mission specialist and MediKit, replacing SMG/Panzerfaust with Boarding Shotgun. Excellent for distant ground-level objectives and late-turn scoring. The shotgun gives AP close-range fire and a template option, but cannot substitute for a longer-range active gun.

MediKit recovery is useful but not guaranteed; do not confuse the Paramedic with a Doctor. Avoid treating the only mobile specialist as a disposable first-turn attacker. A pure Dynamo Duo's +1 SD also applies when firing a MediKit as a BS weapon.

### 25/1 AP Spitfire — B+

BS12, Mimetism (-3), AP and high movement make a credible secondary gun. It offers a substantially better sustained medium-range active attack than either cheap profile; an L2 Duo gives the AP Spitfire four normal Burst dice plus one selection die, not Burst 5.

The premium is 11 points/1 SWC over the SMG or 9 points/0.5 SWC over the Paramedic. No extra wound or superior protection accompanies it. TAK already has strong long-range active guns, so select this when the list specifically needs a ground-mobile AP attack axis rather than another utility body. Avoid facing elite long-range AROs head-on.

## Mine Dispenser — defining utility

The [N5.3 weapon rules](https://infinitythewiki.com/Mine_Dispenser) give a BS-based Targetless weapon, B1, Disposable (2), Double Shot and Speculative Attack; range MODs are 0 at 0–8", +3 at 8–16", -3 at 16–24", then -6 through 48". A normal unopposed shot in the +3 band is BS15 before other modifiers. A legal speculative shot uses the additional -6 MOD and a Long Skill. An L2 Fireteam grants +1 SD to eligible normal BS Attacks but not Long Skills; the selection die does not consume another disposable use.

- Launch a mine to obstruct a ground-level route, cover an objective approach or make an opposing model's later activation awkward.
- Apply the Drop Bears BS-mode placement restrictions: successful placement, legal supported surface, valid trigger geometry and no automatic damage on placement. A mine is not an immediate second attack.
- Do not place mines so that friendly models prevent detonation, or create an attractive template opportunity against friendly Camo troops. A mine cannot trigger through Total Cover or where its template catches a friendly model.
- Baggage from a suitable Traktor/Kuryer can replenish disposable weapons when the applicable requirements are met; not worth an elaborate detour in every game.
- The mine is Shock, not AP or E/M. It is best against light models; the Panzerfaust/AP Spitfire provide separate anti-armour tools.
- The Kuryer speculative-fire combination described in TAK list-building is situational: a pre-existing mine can complicate the target's later choices, but actual trigger/ARO legality determines whether the pressure works.

## Mobility Fireteam options

Dynamo and Ratnik form the required-choice set. Dynamo max 2 per Mobility team; Ratnik max 3. Frontovik max 2 (counts as Ratnik), 112 Motorized max 1, Ioann Bann max 1. TAK permits one Haris and unlimited Duos. Wildcards do not independently satisfy the required-choice condition.

| Package | Pts/SWC | Level | Verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| SMG + Paramedic | 30/0.5 | L2 | **Default** — two Regular Orders, smoke, four total mine uses, anti-armour deterrent, specialist |
| SMG + SMG | 28/0 | L2 | Strong budget flank package; no specialist |
| Paramedic + Paramedic | 32/1 | L2 | Mission-specific redundancy; costly SWC for two support bikes |
| AP Spitfire + SMG | 39/1 | L2 | Mobile assault plus utility |
| AP Spitfire + Paramedic | 41/1.5 | L2 | Mobile gun and scoring; worthwhile if both roles needed |
| SMG + Paramedic + 112 Motorized | 49/0.5 | L2 | Two medical models; competing for the sole Haris; usually prefer Duo |
| AP Spitfire + Paramedic + 112 Motorized | 60/1.5 | L2 | Deliberate attack/scoring Haris; not automatic |

L2 means BS Attack (+1 SD), not +1 Burst. A third mismatched trooper does not make the two Dynamos L3. Only the Leader performs the relevant active-turn attack; one order does not fire both bikes' dispensers. Activating an Impetuous member independently breaks that member's participation (or cancels the team if it is the Leader); do not count on two free independent activations while retaining an intact Duo. Rejoining follows the Fireteam Integrity/States Phase rules. Both bikes still retain Limited Cover.

A Ratnik or Frontovik may be useful in Mobility for a heavier gun/recovery package, but slower foot troops can reduce the bikes' actual movement advantage. Keep the Dynamo Duo compact and independent unless the Haris has a clear mission.

## Alternatives in TAK

- **Irmandinho:** cheaper Irregular smoke/Engineer and expendable support; Dynamo provides a Regular Order, ranged mine deployment and faster ground movement.
- **Strelok / Scout:** forward Camo specialists start closer to objectives and are less exposed; Dynamo has speed for cross-table or late objectives and smoke.
- **Carmen & Bâtard / Dog-Warrior:** aggressive trading and close-range pressure; Dynamo better for Regular economy and ranged deployable control.
- **Veteran Kazak / TankHunter / Spetsnaz:** stronger dedicated shooting roles; cheap Dynamo complements their attack rather than replacing it.
- **Kuryer:** long-range artillery and pre-game area denial; Dynamo develops mobile pressure and can complement it rather than duplicating it.

## Selection rule

Start with one SMG Dynamo or the 30/0.5 SMG + Paramedic Duo. Prefer an independent bike when the free Impetuous activation and independent positioning matter more than shared movement and +1 SD. Select AP Spitfire only for an explicit medium-range attack plan. Two bikes are enough for most lists; AVA5 does not mean five are desirable, especially with multiple other S4 pieces on the table.

This agrees with the repository's presenter synthesis: Shepherd favors the cheap mixed Duo; Swiss Miss values the AP Spitfire; WIP12 questions the gun premium and cautions against bike congestion and mine placement near Camo. The tiers here are my TAK-specific analysis, not presenter rankings or global ranking edits.
