# Dozers, Field Engineers — Tartary Army Corps (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: B+ overall; A− in a dedicated artillery/recovery architecture.** Take **0–1** normally. The Dozer is TAK's cheapest Regular Engineer, and its real niche is combining repair, technical objectives and native-equivalent Fireteam support. The **14pt Blitzen** is the default all-purpose option; **14pt Deployable Cover** or **15pt Armed Turret** can be better in a prepared defensive list. The 13pt basic Rifle is viable when every point counts. Do not automatically take one just because a cheap Traktor is present.

## Profiles and priorities

**ID 256; AVA 2; Regular LI; MOV 10–10 cm (4–4"), CC14, BS11, PH11, WIP13, ARM1, BTS0, W1, S2.** Shared Courage, Engineer, Deactivator and GizmoKit. All options carry Rifle, D-Charges, Pistol and CCW.

| Opt | Additional equipment / weapon | Pts / SWC | Rating | Preferred use |
|---:|---|---:|---|---|
| 3 | Blitzen | 14 / 0 | **A−** | Default flexible pick; E/M deterrent at medium-long range |
| 4 | Deployable Cover | 14 / 0 | **A− / build-around** | Defensive geometry for artillery and firing positions |
| 5 | Armed Turret (Rifle) | 15 / 0 | **A− / build-around** | Extra persistent reactive layer after deployment |
| 1 | None | 13 / 0 | **B** | Cheapest Regular Engineer and pure-composition anchor |

The weapon upgrades do not improve the Dozer's own statline. Its ordinary Rifle is for emergency self-defense, not sustained active gunfighting.

## Profile distinctions

- **Blitzen (14/0):** for one point over the basic profile, buy a B1, Disposable (2), nonlethal E/M weapon with +3 range from 16–32". It threatens Isolation and, on applicable targets, Immobilized-B; two half-BTS saves make a hit consequential. It is useful as a backup ARO or a situational active shot, especially in a Gunnery team with a Special Die. BS11, limited ammunition and no shooting MODs mean this is a deterrent rather than an apex anti-heavy attacker. It can be resupplied by eligible Baggage support.
- **Deployable Cover (14/0):** the N5.3 upgrade is especially relevant: Disposable (2), Perimeter placement wholly within ZoC, S3 scenery. Choose Cutting Foam (opponents cannot claim its cover) or Vitroferro (eligible contact saves +6 instead of +3, but no enemy -3 BS MOD; ARM/BTS plus MODs capped at 12). The latter is not ordinary cover stacked on top of itself. Place after spending an Order; not Minelayer or free deployment. Useful when an S4 gun lacks suitable geometry, or when changing a firing position after deployment; check actual LoF, S3 obstruction and Partial Cover requirements. Do not spend Orders building redundant cover on dense tables.
- **Armed Turret (15/0):** Disposable (1), Non-Reloadable, placed using Perimeter/Place Deployable within ZoC. The separate stationary S2 turret has BS10, ARM2, BTS3, STR1, 360° Visor and Total Reaction, with a Rifle and PARA CCW (-3). A real extra reactive problem against enemy Models, but no ARO against Markers, no Order, no Fireteam membership and no free pregame placement. Excellent when an otherwise idle support model can establish a crossfire or guard a blind corner; inferior when the opponent can simply outrange or Deactivate it. The turret does not inherit the Dozer's Fireteam dice.
- **Basic Rifle (13/0):** take only when the extra capability has no useful deployment location/Order budget or the last point is needed elsewhere. Still has full Engineer, D-Charges, Deactivator and GizmoKit utility.

## Repair and technical objectives

TAK has no standard TAG or VH. The repair case is mostly its artillery REMs, not organic infantry.

| Repair target | Cost / SWC | Why repair it? |
|---|---:|---|
| Vystrel Neurocinetics AP Sniper | 26 / 0.5 | Restore a strong 2 STR, Remote Presence long-range ARO |
| Vystrel Neurocinetics Portable Autocannon | 32 / 1.5 | Recover expensive anti-heavy lane control |
| Kuryer Katyusha / Uragan | 15 / 1 or 18 / 1 | Recover useful artillery / reactive pressure, not already expended mines |
| Traktor Mul Baggage / Guided artillery | 5 / 0, 9 / 1 or 14 / 1 | Restore an Order, Baggage or a relevant artillery tool; rarely worth a long repair trip for the 5pt body alone |

WIP13 Engineer succeeds on **65%** of unmodified rolls; one Command Token reroll on a Remote Presence target raises success to **87.75%**, two to **95.71%**, conditional on spending those Tokens. Remote Presence allows a single success to restore an unconscious REM to operational status from either Unconscious level (not necessarily full STR). Failed final repairs inflict a Wound; a fully destroyed REM cannot be restored.

The Dozer's GizmoKit is a valuable ranged fallback when contact is dangerous, but the listed artillery has PH9: a successful hit still gives only a **45%** patient recovery roll, with failure causing a Wound. Prefer WIP13 contact repair whenever safely available. A Fireteam Special Die can improve the ranged application, not the patient's PH roll.

Other meaningful work:
- Clear eligible states such as Isolated, Immobilized and Targeted from key troops. This does not require a STR target. A Veteran Kazak's VITA wounds are not repairable by ordinary Engineer.
- Engineer objectives, D-Charges and applicable Classifieds provide real utility even in an organic-heavy list; reach and survivability are the limitations.
- Deactivator can remove revealed deployables with a Normal WIP Roll (WIP19 at 0–8", WIP16 at 8–16", ignoring Cover/Mimetism). It requires LoF or ZoC, cannot target Camouflage Markers, and does not prevent a deployable triggering in the same Order. Do not assume the Dozer is safe from enemy fire while doing this.
- Deploy behind total cover with a short path to a likely downed REM. Avoid placing the Engineer where the same enemy attack that removes the REM also removes its repair support.

Rules: [Engineer](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Engineer.md) · [Remote Presence](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Remote_Presence.md) · [GizmoKit](../../../../rules/5c_Equipment/GizmoKit.md) · [Deactivator](../../../../rules/5c_Equipment/Deactivator.md).

## Remontnikbot — excellent if unlinked

The **2pt Remontnikbot V-5 Elektronik** (AVA1, S1, Mimetism -3, Peripheral (Servant)) gives a safe second repair approach. An independent 14pt Dozer with it costs **16/0** and generates one Regular Order, not two. The Engineer uses its own WIP13, not the bot's WIP12, when the bot reaches a casualty. There is no operating-distance limit; Engineer can affect only one target per Order. The Dozer can stay protected while the bot approaches a vulnerable lane.

**Critical trade-off:** neither the Servant nor its Controller can be in a Fireteam. A linked Dozer must be purchased without the bot assigned to it; do not combine the Servant package with a Gunnery Duo or Line Kazak Core. The Servant does not itself become an independent Engineer objective specialist. See [Peripheral](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Peripheral.md) and [N5.3 update](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Peripheral_N5.3_Update.md).

## Alternatives — which Engineer to choose?

| Profile | Pts / SWC | Orders / Fireteams | Assessment |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Irmandinho Chain Rifle | 8 / 0 | Irregular + Impetuous; no link | Smoke, PH14, Booty, AP CCW and D-Charges; superb expendable pressure/technical scorer. No Cover and forward trading make it less reliable as protected repair insurance |
| Dozer Rifle / Blitzen | 13–14 / 0 | Regular; Line Kazaks / Gunnery | Cheapest pooled Engineer; better backline support; lacks smoke and a serious active gun |
| Frontovik Engineer | 26 / 0 | Regular; Line Kazaks / Veterans / Mobility | BS12, ARM2, Mimetism -3, Warhorse, T2 Rifle, Panzerfaust and D-Charges. Much better forward fighting specialist and native Veterans/Ratnik equivalent; expensive to leave behind artillery |
| Traktor Mul Baggage | 5 / 0 | Regular; Gunnery | Not an Engineer; cheap composition and Order provider. Choose instead if no repair or Engineer objectives are needed |

**Dozer vs Irmandinho:** the extra 5–7 points buy a pooled Regular Order, safer stationary role and Fireteam access, rather than better WIP. If the Engineer is mainly a cheap forward D-Charge/Smoke piece, the Irmandinho wins. If the Engineer is responsible for multiple valuable backline REMs, the Dozer generally wins.

**Dozer vs Frontovik:** the 12–13pt saving funds another cheap unit or upgrade. Frontovik earns the premium when the Engineer must advance with a Veteran or Ratnik, survive contested movement and fight. Do not put a Dozer in a Veterans or Mobility team: it has no such access.

## Fireteam packages

All four profiles count as **Gunnery** (Duo/Haris, max one Dozer) or **Line Kazak** (Core/Haris, max one Dozer). Gunnery needs at least one Traktor Mul, Kuryer or Vystrel; Line Kazaks need at least one actual Line Kazak. A Servant controller is excluded from every package below.

| Legal starting module | Total pts / SWC | FT level | Assessment |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Vystrel Neuro AP Sniper 26 + Traktor Baggage 5 | 31 / 0.5 | 2 | Benchmark: cheapest useful linked sniper; no Engineer |
| Vystrel Neuro AP Sniper 26 + Dozer 13 | 39 / 0.5 | 2 | Eight-point premium for repair and scoring; useful if local recovery matters |
| Vystrel Neuro AP Sniper 26 + Dozer Blitzen 14 | 40 / 0.5 | 2 | Default linked recovery Duo with alternate anti-tech threat |
| Vystrel Neuro AP Sniper 26 + Dozer Cover 14 + Traktor Baggage 5 | 45 / 0.5 | 3 | Pure recovery/geometry Haris, reload support and extra pooled Order; uses the sole Haris slot |
| Vystrel Neuro AP Sniper 26 + Dozer Turret 15 + Barsuk HFT FTO 10 | 51 / 0.5 | 3 | Layered long/short range and deployable defense; turret needs an Order to place |
| Line Kazak FO 10 + Barsuk HFT FTO 10 + Dozer Blitzen 14 + Frontovik AP Sniper 28 | 62 / 0.5 | 4 | Pure Core with BS bonus, ARO gun, corner guard and two specialists |
| Above Core + Kazak Doktor 12 | 74 / 0.5 | 5 | Both medical and technical recovery, Sixth Sense; take only if the large shell fits the rest of the list |

The first two modules show the main opportunity cost: a Dozer costs 8 points more than a 5pt Traktor for the same FT2 shooting bonus. If fielding two widely separated Gunnery Duos, an **independent Dozer + Servant** often has better coverage than assigning the sole Engineer to one Duo. A Dozer already in a Line Kazak team can leave it to repair outside the team's practical movement/positioning envelope, subject to normal Fireteam rules. The team does not grant extra Engineer rolls or improve WIP for contact repairs.

## Community perspective and practical verdict

The repository's [transcript research](../tak-references.md) documents the genuine disagreement: Swiss Miss is skeptical of the Dozer versus a 5pt Traktor die-provider or the other Engineers, while the broader artillery architecture discussed by Shepherd and MetaChemistry values making the opponent clear defensive pieces repeatedly. The latter is a tactical rationale for support, not a claim that those presenters independently ranked each current Dozer loadout.

**My assessment:** the Dozer is not an obligatory tax. One is excellent when the recovery plan, scoring mission or extra deployable is genuinely useful. Its cheap profile and one-point upgrades make it better than a generic backline Engineer, but the model is still a slow W1 LI and competes with TAK's exceptionally cheap utility troops.

## Quantities and collection

- **0:** no meaningful repair target, or Irmandinho/Frontovik already fills Engineer coverage.
- **1:** default for a multi-REM list, technical mission or deliberate Line/Gunnery recovery shell. Choose Blitzen for general use, Cover for geometry, Turret for an extra reactive lane.
- **2:** niche, split repair zones or separate Fireteam shells; only one Remontnikbot available. Usually spend the second slot on a different capability instead.

[Owned TAK inventory](../../../../inventory/tak.md) records **one Dozer and no dedicated Remontnikbot**; an existing ALEPH Yudbot is the recorded S1 proxy option. One Dozer is sufficient; no second Dozer or dedicated Servant purchase is necessary unless the same model is needed elsewhere or the proxy is unacceptable.

**Bottom line:** take a 14pt Blitzen Dozer for general-purpose Regular Engineer support, or swap to the Cover/Turret option when you have a specific defensive setup in mind. For a REM-heavy army, the unlinked 16pt Dozer + Servant package is often the most practical configuration; for a simple Gunnery link, the 5pt Traktor remains the value benchmark.
