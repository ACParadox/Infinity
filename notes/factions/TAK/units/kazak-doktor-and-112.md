**Verdict:** Motorized 112 **A−** as a mobile mission toolbox; Kazak Doktor **B+** overall / **A** in a Line Kazak Core; foot 112 **B** overall / **A−** as budget Veterans recovery when composition permits. Normally take **0–1** Doctor, not one of each. Select the profile for its delivery route, Fireteam and independent mission utility rather than for medical skill differences.

## Kazak Doktor — B+ / A in Line Kazaks

The default Doctor for a Line Kazak-based team. At 12 points, it costs three more than a basic Rifle Kazak or one more than a Line Kazak Paramedic. It adds WIP13 contact recovery and retains ranged MediKit, Rifle range flexibility and the native equivalence needed for Fireteam bonuses.

- Best alongside a Frontovik ranged gun or a Veteran Kazak in the Line Kazak family; the Doctor moves with the gun instead of spending separate Orders catching up.
- ARM1 and normal Partial Cover are useful relative to the motorized medic, but not a reason to expose a one-VITA support model.
- With a cheap Line Kazak Lieutenant shell alone, the premium is not automatically justified; an FO is cheaper for objectives, and reviving disposable troops rarely justifies multiple Orders.
- Cannot join Veterans or Mobility teams. A native Line Kazak remains mandatory for the Line Kazaks family; the Doktor's equivalence does not erase that row.

## 112 on foot — B / A− when Veterans recovery is needed

The cheapest Doctor and a good independent budget specialist when no link is planned. At 11 points the FTO has Courage, PH12 and a Light Shotgun; it exchanges the Doktor's Rifle and ARM1 for close-range utility and Veterans access.

- Use for an expensive Veteran/Frontovik package if a recoverable casualty is likely to be accessible.
- Its key drawback is composition: it does not count as Veteran. A Veteran Kazak + 112 Duo is L1, while two Veteran-equivalent gunfighters + 112 Haris is L2 instead of L3.
- The Doctor is attractive when the 10–15+ points saved over another Frontovik or Veteran-equivalent member matter more than the lost bonus. It is not automatically the best filler just because it is cheapest.
- In a marker-heavy list without an active infantry Fireteam, choose it over the Doktor if you only need the cheapest Regular Doctor; choose the Doktor for Rifle flexibility if that is more valuable.

## 112 Motorized — A−

The 7–8 point premium is for an entirely different delivery and utility package, not better Doctor WIP. A full Move-Move can cover 12 inches instead of 8, and a first Move of 8 inches can reach a casualty or objective before the second Short Skill. Smoke Grenades and the launcher offer two delivery methods. This model generates a Regular Order without an Impetuous Order, so it can stay protected until needed.

- Best as a late-game objective runner, mobile rescue piece and smoke provider even when nobody needs healing.
- Its smoke can help approach casualties or bypass conventional firing lanes; do not assume it defeats MSV or other visibility counters.
- Mimetism (-3) partially compensates for Limited Cover. Limited Cover removes the enemy's normal -3 BS cover modifier, not the +3 saving-roll benefit of qualifying Partial Cover. It does not stack full cover BS protection with Mimetism.
- S4, inability to go Prone, climb or use ladders, and route congestion are meaningful drawbacks. No alternate dismounted profile is listed in the checked TAK Army unit. ARM0 and one VITA make this a poor attack piece.
- Can join Dynamo or Ratnik Mobility teams, but is not equivalent to either. Speed is particularly useful alongside Dynamos; a slow Ratnik limits the distance the pair can move while remaining coherent. The Doctor is still legal independently.

## Medical mechanics and priority targets

WIP13 means a 65% unmodified contact Doctor success chance for each of the three; failure kills the Unconscious target. Command Token rerolls require an eligible Cube target; do not budget for universal rerolls. Doctor also cancels Stunned as specified in the rules. Ordinary Doctor cannot heal merely wounded active troops or STR REMs.

MediKit instead uses the patient's PH (or special MediKit PH value); a ranged application first requires a BS hit, and a contact MediKit requires no user roll. A Veteran Kazak has MediKit (PH=15), so contact MediKit is 75% versus ordinary Doctor's 65%; ranged delivery is conditional on hitting. L2 Fireteam +1 SD can improve ranged MediKit hit reliability but does not improve the patient's PH or the Doctor's WIP.

- **Veteran Kazak:** primary high-value target; Shock Immunity and NWI give opportunities for recovery only if it actually reaches Unconscious without being killed. MediKit PH15 is an important option.
- **Frontovik / expensive TankHunter or Spetsnaz:** worthwhile if recoverable and the rescue route is efficient; one-VITA targets may die directly to Shock.
- **Ratnik:** high-value VITA platform; consider whether it actually remains Unconscious after Dogged/Transmutation interactions rather than assuming it can always be recovered.
- **Vystrel, Kuryer, Traktor Mul:** STR platforms, use an Engineer/GizmoKit instead.
- **Disposable Line Kazaks:** recover if needed for Fireteam composition, mission points or an imminent useful Order; otherwise spend Orders on the mission.

## Motorized Doctor versus Dynamo Paramedic

| Profile | Pts / SWC | Preferred role |
|---|---:|---|
| Dynamo Paramedic | 16 / 0.5 | Mine Dispenser (Shock), Dogged, ARM1, BS12, Impetuous and matching Dynamo composition; better pressure and cheaper in points |
| 112 Motorized | 19 / 0 | Doctor WIP13, Smoke Grenades in addition to launcher, non-Impetuous Regular specialist; better deliberate rescue and zero-SWC utility |

For pure Mobility pressure, a matching Dynamo Duo with its Paramedic is usually preferable. For independent recovery, Doctor-specific objectives or if SWC is tight, the 112 is more attractive. Do not buy both merely to duplicate healing.

## List selection and ownership

- **Line Kazak Core/Haris:** Kazak Doktor first.
- **Veterans:** foot 112 if recovery and point savings justify the composition tradeoff; otherwise no Doctor or an independent medic.
- **Mobility / objective-heavy mission:** Motorized 112 if independent movement, smoke and Doctor utility justify 19 points; compare Dynamo Paramedic.
- **Marker-heavy / artillery-centric:** take zero Doctors unless mission or a specific recoverable VITA package warrants one. Prioritize Engineer for artillery recovery.
