# Wardrivers — Tartary Army Corps (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: B+ overall — situational tech piece; A− for the Disco Baller profile.** Take **0–1**. The Wardriver is primarily an Eclipse-delivery and mission-coverage purchase, not the foundation of a competitive hacking network. The 16/1 option is normally worth the extra two points over 14/1, but neither is mandatory.

Both have Pistol and CCW. Neither has a KHD, HD+, Pitcher, native Repeater, Deactivator, forward deployment or Minelayer. Zero Pain is a program upgrade, not a Firewall.

## Disco Baller — the main reason to take one

TAK already has abundant ordinary Smoke but limited means of bypassing MSV gunfighters without defeating them directly. The Disco Baller's Eclipse template obstructs LoF even for MSV, allowing protected movement after deployment, not during the Order that places the device.

- Use the device to cross a dangerous lane, reach a console, approach close combat, or disengage from an unfavorable ARO piece.
- Targetless deployment uses **BS11**, not WIP13 or PH10. The placement is a Normal Roll; enemy shooting against the exposed Wardriver is not opposed by this deployment.
- The most attractive range is **8–16" (+3)**. Speculative Attack can place it without LoF when a valid trajectory exists but costs a Long Skill, B1 and −6 BS.
- Disposable (2); deployment is not guaranteed. The template deactivates at the start of the States Phase. A surviving deployed ball may be reactivated by an allied Deactivator carrier, requiring the appropriate LoF/ZoC and roll.
- Eclipse does not prevent hacking, close combat, or other legal attacks that do not require LoF. It is a routing tool, not invulnerability.

| Range | Direct single-die placement | Speculative placement |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0–8" | BS11 (55%) | BS5 (25%) |
| 8–16" | BS14 (70%) | BS8 (40%) |
| 16–24" | BS8 (40%) | BS2 (10%) |
| 24–32" | BS5 (25%) | BS below 1 (impossible) |

These are unmodified single-die Normal Roll baselines from the [weapon](../../../../rules/4_Ammunition%20and%20Weaponry/Disco_Baller.md) and [Speculative Attack](../../../../rules/3_Combat/Speculative_Attack.md) rules, not a full Double Shot simulation. Direct placement may use the weapon's Double Shot trait under its applicable conditions. Do not assume two Speculative attempts will succeed: at 8–16", two independent B1 attempts have a 64% chance of at least one success, consuming both uses if both are needed.

### Synergies

- **Irmandinho:** Smoke and mobile Engineer/Deactivator support can exploit and later reactivate a placed ball. Use ordinary Smoke when MSV is not the obstacle.
- **Dog-Warrior / Carmen & Bâtard / Pavel:** Eclipse may provide the approach that normal Smoke cannot, enabling close pressure against a visor defender. Their attacks must still satisfy the normal requirements.
- **Dozer / Frontovik Engineer / Traktor Mul Baggage / Ratnik:** eligible Deactivator carriers can reactivate a useful surviving ball. The 5/0 Traktor is an inexpensive existing support option, not a reason to buy a second unit solely for Eclipse.
- **Scout / Strelok:** protected console or extraction routes for a forward specialist. Do not unnecessarily reveal a useful marker when the Wardriver could open the route first.

## Hacking — defensive specialist, not cyber supremacy

The HD provides Carbonite, Oblivion, Total Control and Spotlight, plus Zero Pain. This gives TAK a local anti-HI/REM/TAG threat and Hacker-specific mission eligibility that its other specialists cannot reproduce.

- **Sixth Sense:** Stealth is ineffective against the Wardriver; a legal Hacking ARO can punish otherwise quiet movement inside its Hacking Area.
- **Zero Pain:** B2 in Active or Reactive Turn, imposes −3 WIP on opposing Comms Attacks and can cancel them in a Face to Face Roll. This is useful self-defense, not a way to wound enemy Hackers or shield a friendly Ratnik. It does not replace a Killer Hacker or passive Firewall.
- **WIP13/BTS3/ARM0/W1:** adequate budget support; no premium cyber attributes, and direct shooting is dangerous. Enemy Repeaters can expose the Wardriver to Hackers who would otherwise have few hacking targets in TAK.
- **No native network projection on the profile:** local Hacking Area and any actually available allied Repeater coverage determine its reach. Avoid moving into a hostile network merely to create an offensive hacking contest.
- **Cybermine:** carried deployable, not a predeployed mine. On a hit, two PS5 BTS Saves; a failed save causes Stunned or Immobilized-B against Hackers/Hackable targets. Evaded by Reset rather than Dodge, with −3 WIP. Useful local order tax but no lethal damage or Repeater function. See [Mines](../../../../rules/4_Ammunition%20and%20Weaponry/Mines.md).

The existing [Hackers ranking](../../../list-building/hackers-ranking.md) already treats Wardriver HD14/1 as a lower-return, unranked Hacker due to SWC and TAK's weak Repeater ecosystem. This assessment preserves that judgment: the higher local rating comes from the separate Eclipse utility.

## Artillery and objectives

- **Wardriver Disco + Traktor Mul Katyusha:** 25/2 total. Cheap theoretical Spotlight-to-Guided combination, but the Wardriver must actually reach Hacking Area and the target must fail an AP PS5 Spotlight save. Not a long-range Spotlight network or reliable default offensive package.
- **Wardriver Disco + Kuryer Katyusha:** 31/2 total. More expensive artillery alternative with Kuryer's own non-Guided utility. Choose Kuryer for its independent benefits, not because the Hacker is compulsory.
- **Scout FO / Strelok FO / Line Kazak FO:** alternative Targeted sources and mission specialists without buying a Hacker. Forward starting position or lower cost often makes these more efficient for ordinary buttons and Targeted setup.
- **Kazak Doktor / Dozer / Irmandinho:** alternative technical specialists where medical or engineering Classifieds matter. They cannot replace Hacker-specific eligibility.

Spotlight is B2 active and B1 reactive normally; it requires a failed Saving Roll rather than automatically assigning Targeted. See [Spotlight](../../../../rules/3_Combat/Spotlight.md). The Wardriver's primary mission advantages are rare Hacker access and protected movement, not superior generic objective delivery.

## Opportunity cost and deployment

One SWC is substantial in a 300-point 6-SWC TAK list. It can consume the budget for a long-range ARO, artillery piece or premium active gun. Prioritize the established attacker/ARO/marker shell, then buy the Wardriver if a particular mission or deployment problem justifies it.

Deploy in Total Cover near a useful lane, with the option to approach a safe firing position and with physical protection from airborne/close attackers. Preserve the Regular Order until its special abilities are needed. Do not expose an ARM0 model for an unnecessary Flash Pulse ARO.

**Take 16/1** when a key MSV crossing, Eclipse-supported close-pressure plan or Hacker-specific objective justifies it. **Take 14/1** only when Hacker access is needed, the two points matter, and Eclipse is unlikely to be used. **Take zero** in ordinary lists where strong independent guns, Camo specialists and inexpensive Smoke already solve the mission.
