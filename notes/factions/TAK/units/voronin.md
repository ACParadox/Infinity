# Colonel Voronin — Tartary Army Corps (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A+ Lieutenant; B+/B non-Lieutenant.** The default command/economy choice for a balanced TAK list with a meaningful primary Order pool. Take 0–1; prefer T2 Boarding Shotgun for close-range lethality or Light Shotgun (+1B)/Flash Pulse for defensive flexibility. The 18pt non-LT is a specialist countermeasure, not ordinary cheap Order filler.

## Command engine — the main reason to take him

- **Strategos L1:** converts the single Special LT Order to a Regular Order in his own Combat Group and permits two reserve Troopers (plus their Peripherals) rather than one during deployment. Put him in the group that most benefits from the flexible Order. No double counting with NCO; no extra LT Order beyond the one converted.
- **Counterintelligence:** if the opponent chooses to strip Orders from the first player, reduces the loss from two to one. Alternatively raises the first-turn Command Token limit from one to two if that restriction is chosen. Conditional, not a guaranteed recurring extra Order; ordinary stripping requires more than ten counted Orders. Both non-LT options retain this skill.
- **Lieutenant (+1 Command Token):** normally five rather than four starting tokens; a finite extra resource for converting Irregular Orders, reorganizing groups, reforming teams or other legal uses. Not LT (+1 Order).
- **WIP14:** useful initiative roll and reliable Sensor/Flash Pulse. Two reserve deployments are particularly valuable when coordinating a Camo attack piece and a defensive gun or adapting to the opponent's revealed positions.

If he survives all three active turns, Strategos can make three LT Orders available to other troops that might otherwise be unspent; Counterintelligence may preserve one more on the first turn. These are not four extra Orders versus a list that already spends its LT Orders effectively. No Strategos L2 free group transfer.

## Counter-infiltration and close defense

- **Guard + MA2:** CC22 becomes CC25, with opponent -3 in a qualifying opposed roll, and AP CCW PS=5. Guard permits CC Attack inside ZoC and LoF without silhouette contact, including in ARO. It is normally B1; MA2 does not add dice. The opponent may also use relevant CC skills through Guard; it cannot target non-Unconscious Aerial troops. It does not establish Engaged State at a distance.
- **Sensor:** WIP14+6 = 20 for the normal area sweep against Camouflaged and Hidden Deployment enemies within ZoC, without LoF or range/Mimetism penalties. Prevents enemy Camouflage re-entry in ZoC and boosts ordinary Discover against Camo. Not a mass anti-Impersonation tool or a replacement for a shooter. Prefer sweeping from behind safe terrain.
- **Combat Instinct and Sixth Sense:** surprise/Stealth counterplay and reactive awareness; neither provides unconditional 360-degree LoF or immunity to attacks. Guard still requires LoF. Sixth Sense is already native, so FT5 does not grant him a new personal capability.
- **Light Shotgun (+1B) / Flash Pulse:** better Burst for short firefights and a WIP14 ranged nonlethal ARO option. **T2 Boarding Shotgun:** different ammunition and stronger wound conversion on susceptible targets; select based on desired defense rather than combining the two profiles.
- **Weakness:** BS11, ARM0/BTS0/W1, S4. A normal long-range attacker can kill him easily; avoid exposing the LT merely to exploit Guard. Guard does not make him a premium attack piece. Do not use him as a general button pusher; no dedicated Specialist skill on any of the four options.

See [Guard](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Guard.md), [Sensor](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Sensor.md), [Strategos](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Strategos.md) and [Counterintelligence](../../../../rules/5b_Special%20Skills/Counterintelligence.md).

## Lieutenant alternatives

| Alternative | Pts / SWC | Assessment |
|---|---:|---|
| Voronin LT | 22 / 0 | Best general command economy, extra reserve, anti-Camo utility; visible, fragile S4 |
| Line Kazak LT + Rifle lookalike | 18 / 0 | Two bodies and two Regular Orders, concealed identity, 4pts cheaper; lacks Voronin's flexible LT Order and command skills |
| TankHunter LT Minelayer | 25 / 0 | Camo shell and mine/weapon utility, useful when hiding identity is more important |
| Scout LT | 27 / 0 | Infiltrating marker command, but risks occupying or exposing a valuable forward attacker |
| Vassily LT FO | 35 / 0 or 1 | Camo, Decoy (2), WIP14, AP/T2 gun, FO; costly active command |
| Veteran Kazak LT FO | 36 / 0 | Durable active specialist; choose if the Veteran is meant to use the personal LT Order |
| Veteran Kazak LT AP HMG | 45 / 1 | Durable primary gun, saves 0.5 SWC vs standard; exposes LT to retaliation |

**Voronin LT + TankHunter CoC Minelayer = 47/0.** The TankHunter's mine, Camo and Panzerfaust make it productive succession insurance. Prefer the 22pt LT alone if he can be well protected and the 25pt successor displaces a vital specialist or gun. CoC does not transfer Strategos to the successor. By comparison, Veteran LT FO + TankHunter CoC = 61/0; Veteran AP HMG LT + CoC = 70/1. These are alternatives, not simultaneous LTs.

The 18pt non-LT is worth considering with an aggressive Veteran LT if Counterintelligence and Sensor specifically matter; avoid taking him merely because he is a Wildcard. A Line Kazak LT plus non-LT Voronin costs 27/0 and sacrifices Strategos and the bonus token, but keeps two Regular bodies and potentially hides the actual LT. If the desired utility is mobile midfield Sensor, compare the 26pt Antipode Assault Pack, which performs a very different pressure role.

## Fireteam use

Voronin is a **global Wildcard, max 1**, counts as Line Kazak for composition; may join other eligible TAK families while respecting native minima/required-choice entries. Not equivalent to Veteran, Ratnik or Gunnery. Do not add him to an otherwise illegal team or treat the Line Kazak equivalence as satisfying the requirement for an actual Line Kazak. Prefer independent deployment or a defensive Line Kazak team; avoid dragging the LT into an active Veterans/Mobility/Gunnery team's danger zone.

The three-body teams can be Core or Haris; four/five-body teams require Core. FT2 supplies +1SD, not +1 retained hit; FT4 boosts BS. Voronin's native Sixth Sense makes full composition valuable mainly for his allies. [Report #127](../../../battle-reports/121-130-n5-battle-report-tactical-analysis.md#L204) documents a five-model Voronin/three Line Kazaks/Frontovik team in a coached game; not evidence that an exact reconstructed list won a tournament.

## Quantities, rankings and collection

- **1:** Recommended to own and a frequent LT choice, especially for Camo and multi-attacker lists.
- **0:** Reasonable in very cheap hidden-LT builds, or an active Veteran/TankHunter architecture.
- The [inventory](../../../../inventory/tak.md) already records one dedicated model; AVA filled. No further purchase.
- Existing global [LT ranking](../../../list-building/LT-ranking.md) places Voronin at **#7, A+** among command LTs. Retain this ranking; the verified Strategos correction validates its application to TAK. Local ratings are tactical judgments, not global reorders.

**Bottom line:** Voronin is an excellent command multiplier who happens to be a strong close-range deterrent and Sensor carrier. Keep him alive to fund the rest of the army; the 22pt profile's Strategos is the defining advantage over the 18pt non-LT.
