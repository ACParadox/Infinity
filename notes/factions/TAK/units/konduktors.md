# Konduktors, Transtartaric Railway Troops — TAK (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: S overall; S+ for the 7pt FTO, S for the 12pt infiltrator, A− for the 10pt Paramedic, C for the 16pt Marksman Rifle.** Usually take one or two. The 7pt profile is one of the best native Regular cheap guards in the game; the 12pt profile buys genuinely different forward-deployed pressure. AVA2 is shared by all four profiles, so choose the job before filling the slots.

## Verified profiles

ID 1844, AVA2, Regular LI, MOV 10–10 cm (4–4"), CC15, BS10, PH11, WIP12, ARM1, BTS0, W1, S2. No shared special skills or equipment. All four options cost 0 SWC.

## 7/0 Chain Rifle/Light Shotgun/Shock Mine FTO — S+

The best default: a native Regular pooled Order, small S2 footprint and large direct-template Chain Rifle for only seven points. The Light Shotgun has B2 and +6 within 8 inches, giving BS16 before opposing modifiers; it is not a direct-template weapon in N5. The Shock Mine provides persistent defence once deployed; there is **no Minelayer**, so it is not a free initial mine. No specialist or anti-armour ammunition.

- Position independently in Total Cover behind corners, near an exposed approach to a protected LT, artillery, or specialist. Present a live close-range trade threat, not an exposed long-range ARO.
- Two can cover different approaches, so an enemy cannot automatically remove both with one attack. Avoid clustering around valuable troops.
- Place a mine when it materially protects an approach or objective; do not routinely spend an Order laying a mine merely because it is available.
- Preserve the Regular Order when no favourable trade is available. It is an economy model first and a disposable defender second.
- Costs 2 fewer points than a plain Line Kazak; unlike the latter it does not satisfy the native team requirement or count as Line Kazak for Fireteam level.

## 12/0 Infiltrating Konduktor — S

For five more points, exchange the cheap DZ profile's equipment and Fireteam access for Infiltration, one-use Camo, Rifle, Chain-colt and Flammenspeer. Still a Regular Order and zero SWC. One-use Camouflage normally gives a starting marker without Mimetism, Decoy or Surprise Attack on this profile. Once that use ends it cannot re-camouflage; the FAQ permits an unused one-use skill to be used later if initially deployed as a model. Infiltration can start safely in the friendly half outside the opponent's DZ; crossing into the enemy half requires the appropriate roll and carries risk.

The Chain-colt is a small direct template that does not rely on BS10 to hit; the Rifle is B3 but is not a premium duel weapon. The Flammenspeer is Disposable (2), B1, Continuous Damage, with an AP single-target hit mode or normal-ammo circular impact-template mode, +3 from 16–32 inches. It is a credible threat against armour and clustered targets, not a Panzerfaust-equivalent or sustained attack gun. Against elite shooters the low BS and lack of Mimetism are significant constraints. This option has **no mine at all**, no Minelayer, no specialist and no Fireteam access.

Deploy it so its marker creates uncertainty near Streloks, Scouts and their actual mines; make the opponent spend Orders identifying and removing an inexpensive obstacle. Do not expose it just to fire a speculative-looking low-odds Flammenspeer shot; the weapon has no Speculative Attack trait. Reveal when the trade is worthwhile or when the opponent threatens something more valuable. An infiltrating Beasthunter or Scout provides a different ceiling; the Konduktor's advantage is economy and cheap marker density rather than repeated combat.

## 10/0 FTO Paramedic — A−

A cheap Regular WIP12 mission specialist with Rifle and MediKit. Unlike the 7pt profile, it has no Chain Rifle, shotgun or mine. It can contribute to a same-unit pair in a mixed Line Kazak Fireteam. A useful pick if a mission needs a Paramedic specifically or the list has exactly 10 points and needs recovery plus a button pusher.

Compare the **10pt Line Kazak FO** (WIP13, Flash Pulse, native team anchor), **11pt Line Kazak Paramedic** (WIP13 and native equivalence), and **12pt Kazak Doktor** (WIP13 Doctor, MediKit and Line Kazak equivalence). The 10pt Konduktor saves one or two points relative to the dedicated medical options; it costs a scarce Konduktor slot and may weaken Fireteam level. Prefer the Doktor for reliable contact recovery and a larger pure link; prefer a forward specialist such as Strelok FO (17/0) for distant objectives. MediKit uses the patient's PH (or applicable specific MOD), not the Paramedic's WIP; it is not the Doctor skill.

## 16/0 Shock Marksman Rifle FTO — C

BS12 and B3 Shock Marksman Rifle give decent unopposed shooting in the 8–24 inch band, zero SWC and Suppressive Fire where legal. This can fill an unusual SWC-starved midrange role, but costs nine points more than the basic guard while losing templates and mines. W1/ARM1, no Mimetism, no MSV, no AP and no specialist. In a mixed team it cannot count as Line Kazak. In most lists a 19pt Barsuk FTO AP Marksman Rifle (ARM3, Shock Immunity, BS Attack Shock, counts as Line Kazak) is a more useful investment for three more points; premium Spetsnaz/TankHunter/Frontovik/Veteran guns should handle serious duels. Do not buy this instead of the cheap Konduktor by default.

## Fireteam composition — important distinction

The Line Kazak chart permits Core/Haris (not Duo), requires at least one **native Line Kazak**, and allows up to two Konduktor FTOs. The three FTO loadouts count as Konduktors, **not** as Line Kazaks. Two different FTO loadouts still share the same unit identity. The infiltrator cannot join. Fireteam level follows the largest equivalence group, not physical headcount; FT2 grants +1 SD on eligible BS Attacks, not extra Burst. No independent Konduktor Duo or Gunnery/Veterans/Mobility access.

| Package | Pts/SWC | FT level | Assessment |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Line Kazak Rifle 9 + 2 cheap FTO 7 each | 23/0 | L2 | Minimum-cost three-body team; independent guards often better |
| Line Kazak FO 10 + cheap FTO 7 + Paramedic FTO 10 | 27/0 | L2 | Two specialists and three Regular Orders; limited gun |
| Line Kazak FO 10 + Barsuk HFT FTO 10 + cheap FTO 7 | 27/0 | L2 | Close defence and scoring; FO/Barsuk equivalent |
| Line Kazak FO 10 + Frontovik MSV1 AP Sniper 28/.5 + cheap FTO 7 | 45/.5 | L2 | Budget sniper/scoring Haris; 48/.5 Barsuk version is L3 |
| Line Kazak FO 10 + Rifle 9 + Barsuk HFT FTO 10 + Frontovik AP Sniper 28/.5 + cheap FTO 7 | 64/.5 | L4 | Four Line Kazak equivalents; cheap fifth body does not confer Sixth Sense |

The 23pt Haris consumes TAK's sole Haris slot; do not buy it solely for the label. Keep the scarce slot for a Gunnery, Mobility or Veterans team if that contributes more. A same-equivalence Barsuk or other Line Kazak family member may be worth the extra points when it unlocks higher bonuses. These are legal chart constructions, not claimed tournament results.

## Opportunity cost and default AVA choices

| Two-slot combination | Pts/SWC | Recommendation |
|---|---:|---|
| 7 + 7 | 14/0 | Best pure order economy and DZ protection |
| 7 + 12 | 19/0 | **Default balanced choice** for typical Camo/counterattack TAK |
| 12 + 12 | 24/0 | Strong deliberate midfield shell; obtain home defence elsewhere |
| 7 + 10 | 17/0 | Budget scoring/recovery when a Paramedic fills a real mission gap |
| Any 16pt option | varies | Only explicit zero-SWC midrange requirement |

The 5pt Traktor Mul is cheaper for a bare Regular Order, but it is S4, Hackable and lacks the immediate Chain Rifle guard role. A 10pt Barsuk FTO is tougher and provides a large template and same-family link value; the 7pt Konduktor is cheaper. A 12pt Konduktor and a 17pt Strelok FO are complementary; the Strelok has reusable Camo, Decoy and specialist access but not the same loadout or deployment skill. For marker saturation, a 15pt Camo Beasthunter offers stronger close and anti-armour pressure at the cost of an Irregular Order. Scouts provide true mine traps and stronger weapons at higher cost.
