# Streloks — Tartary Army Corps (Infinity N5.3)

**Verdict: A+ overall — core midfield infrastructure.** FO and SMG are S-tier value; Minelayer and K-9 are A+; T2 MMR is A. These are local tactical grades, not global ranking changes or measured tournament win rates. A pair of cheap Streloks is a strong default, not a mandatory tax.

## Forward Observer — 17/0

The default mission piece. One point buys FO and Flash Pulse compared with the 16-point SMG profile, but exchanges SMG and Chain-colt for the AP Boarding Shotgun. WIP13, Camo, FD and Decoy make a protected early or late scorer. BSG B2, PS6, AP and +6 at 0–8 inches (BS17 before modifiers); it has **no direct-template mode**. Flash Pulse offers WIP-based nonlethal ARO and FO covers its applicable mission tasks. Preserve marker state when scoring later is more important than taking an opportunistic shot. One wound, no exceptional WIP or durable objective holding; don't force elite MSV gunfights. This profile is #9 S+ in the current consolidated global specialist ranking (row labelled Vanilla Ariadna; same profile exists in TAK). The overview's separate historical #8 Value placement is not the current merged rank.

## SMG + Chain-colt — 16/0

Excellent inexpensive Regular Order and area-denial marker. Normal-ammunition SMG B3 PS7 and Suppressive Fire; Chain-colt PS7 direct template ignores BS Mimetism penalties, and is useful against difficult close opponents. AP Mines deter armored movement as well as light targets. Mine is carried, **not predeployed**; placing one ordinarily costs an Order. Decoy is not a second unit, and not regenerated when recamouflaging. No generic Specialist. Pick over FO when a direct-template threat matters more than another scoring piece; both are excellent. The global midfield-defenders note currently describes it as 'Infiltrating'; actual skill is FD(+20), not Infiltration.

## Boarding Shotgun Minelayer — 17/0.5

One real Camo marker, one Decoy, one initial mine (subject to legal deployment, coherency/placement requirements). The extra predeployed AP Mine is an order-efficiency advantage, not an additional Regular Order. A lethal BSG reveal punishes a close approach; unlike SMG variant this has no direct-template gun, though mines remain templates. The cost is SWC and losing the FO/Flash Pulse. At equal points FO is preferable when mission scoring matters, Minelayer when immediate defensive layering matters. Scout E/M Minelayer(2) 28/0.5 covers deeper space, places two E/M mines and has a stronger long gun; Strelok is cheaper and offers lethal AP mine pressure rather than E/M state denial. Good in a defensive shell, not automatic in SWC-heavy lists.

## K-9 Specialist Operative — 26/0

Controller retains SMG, Chain-colt, AP Mines and shared Strelok skills; gains Specialist Operative and included Synchronized K-9. **One Regular Order in total**, not two. Compared with basic SMG, +10 points buys specialist status and a second body. Compared with FO, +9 points buys the specialized package but trades away FO, Flash Pulse and BSG.

**K-9:** MOV15-15, CC20, BS10, PH15, WIP13, ARM0, BTS0, W1, S5; Camouflage, Surprise Attack(-3), FD(+20), Dodge(+5), Mimetism(-3), Sensor, Stealth, Climbing Plus. The controller has Decoy(1); the Antipode does not. Sensor helps reveal eligible markers in its zone, with relevant WIP bonus, without requiring ordinary LoF to each marker. It is not a permanent MSV aura and does not cancel Mimetism on subsequent gunfights. Climbing Plus and high PH/Dodge are useful for accessing and surviving awkward terrain. Synchronized activation allows both bodies to develop together, subject to legal declarations, coherency and peripheral rules; not two independent specialists or independent Order generators. Large silhouette, one wound and controller dependence limit aggressive use; guard the controller and don't assume the dog can always take an independent route.

The TAK-specific profile has Specialist Operative at 26/0, versus Vanilla's 25/0 non-specialist K-9 in the pinned references. Its strongest use case is scoring plus a marker-hunting tool in a list without another convenient Sensor. Particularly valuable into Camo-heavy Ariadna or Shasvastii. Against opponents with few markers, FO plus saving nine points is often better. Distinct toolbox/unique capability candidate, not an automatic global ranking insertion.

## T2 Marksman Rifle — 24/0

BS12, B3, PS7, T2, +3 at 8–24 inches, Anti-materiel, Suppressive Fire. Surprise Attack and Mimetism improve the opening; Cover and range matter, and visors/other counters can reduce the advantage. T2 punishes multi-wound targets on failed saves but is not AP: very high ARM remains a problem. No specialist, no direct-template gun. Costs 50% more than base SMG and 7 more than FO. Strong secondary no-SWC gun, not the primary attacker. Scout FO has better forward deployment, AP Ohotnik and mission role for 4 more points; TankHunter AP HMG is a superior armor solver when SWC and points allow. T2 is attractive against vulnerable multi-wound targets when a zero-SWC midrange threat is desired.

## Comparison with TAK midfield alternatives

| Alternative | Cost | Distinct role |
|---|---:|---|
| Strelok FO | 17/0 | Budget reusable Camo scorer + Decoy |
| Scout FO | 28/0 | Infiltration, AP Ohotnik, D-Charges, stronger shooting and classified flexibility |
| Scout E/M Minelayer(2) | 28/0.5 | Deeper two-mine anti-heavy denial; not FO |
| Konduktor infiltrator | 12/0 | Cheap true Infiltration, Camo(1 Use), template pressure; not reusable Camo |
| Beasthunter Camo | 15/0 | Irregular HFT/Panzerfaust threat and carried AP Mine; not Regular specialist |
| Barsuk Camo | 11–12/0 for cheap options | Low-cost one-use marker and disposable attack; not reusable Camo |
| Strelok K-9 | 26/0 | Mission scoring plus included synchronized Sensor platform |

Streloks complement, rather than replace, Scouts: use the cheaper forward shell to preserve a Scout's Infiltration advantage and stronger gun for a task worth paying for. A template/mine fork is better than contesting elite long-range shooting. Enemy Sensor/MSV, templates, mine clearance and high armor all matter. A marker that costs the opponent Orders to clear has provided value without killing anything; avoid putting the entire midfield in one template or relying only on passive roadblocks.

Deploying more markers is not equivalent to deploying more troops. Budget for usable attack Orders, a protected LT and heavy-armor answers. Typical count **1–3**, with 2 a particularly strong starting point; four is a deliberate saturation build rather than baseline.

**Collection:** 1 dedicated Strelok owned; AVA4. A second is a useful purchase for FO + SMG or two FO; third optional if regularly building Camo saturation. No default need for four. K-9 requires one S5 dog from the existing shared four-figure pool, not a new purchase by default. Account for any dogs already allocated to Bâtard or Antipode Assault Pack. Do not change ownership records.

## Final selection rule

FO is the default; SMG if template defense is important; Minelayer if predeployed AP denial justifies 0.5 SWC; K-9 if Sensor and a scoring controller solve a concrete matchup problem; T2 MMR if an extra 0-SWC gun is needed. No profile is Skip. Global rankings and faction overview remain unchanged pending a separate ranking decision.
