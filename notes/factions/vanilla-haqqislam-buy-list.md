## Skips and cross-sectorial model reuse

Do **not** extrapolate availability from Haqq product branding: Tarik and Al Fasid are Ramah, not this vanilla roster; Shujae/Hussein/Áyyār are Hassassin but not vanilla. Source audits do not establish a universal entire-unit never-take. **Extra Irregular-only copies without Saladin/order plan** and duplicate gunfighter profiles are weaker buys than the first useful smoke, camo and specialist bodies.

**Proxy first** for low-play-frequency characters, extra expensive TAGs, optional aircraft and second attack pieces; do not buy all AVA models to complete the collection. Special/limited editions are collector options rather than required army rules.
