# Svalarheima's Winter Force (WinterFor / SWF) — N5.3 tactical guide

**Research date:** 2026-10-19. **Scope:** actionable list-building, unit and Fireteam choices, actual SWF TAG/REMs, and cross-PanO Vehicle coverage. **This is not a Military Orders guide.**

**Factual source:** [current SWF roster, loadouts and canonical Fireteam chart](../../sectorials/panoceania/svalarheima-s-winter-force.md), status `current`, Army **7.26246.158** (data 2026-09-03), N5.3 + FAQ. Video/podcast commentary is opinion; historic costs, profiles and link assertions do **not** supersede the reference. Source judgments are attributed below. Particular care: **Blade FTO is linkable now; Pathfinder TacAware is not a SWF Wildcard; no SWF Vehicle (VH) entries; Jotum is the only TAG**.

## In one minute

- **Identity:** unusually direct PanO with excellent long-range shooting **and** fast close assault; MSV/Mimetism/Albedo, Super-Jump/Climbing Plus and a very durable Jotum. Combine firepower with the specialist or melee piece that will *take the objective you just cleared*, rather than spending the whole first turn shooting (Shepherd/Lockie, ITS17).
- **Strong starting skeleton:** 10-point Fusilier LT (or protected Hospitaller LT) + useful cheap Orders/Fugazis; **Karhu Feuerbach/NCO** or **Knight of Justice Missile Launcher** covering the approach; **Vargar FO + Disco Baller** and Shona/Hawkins/Hannibal for assault; **Hidden Locust or forward Uma** for objectives; then choose **Jotum or distributed specialists/Fireteams**.
- **Strengths:** Karhu NCO makes the LT Order useful; Vargar Albedo(-6) and Disco provide an answer to visual defenses; Hawkin's specialist shooting/close assault combines roles; Locust can now **Hidden Deploy**; Fusilier/Alpha/Beta teams let serious guns travel with support.
- **Weaknesses:** no **Combat Jump**; only an unusual Blade-Ops Parachutist option; no **Vehicle / Redeye**; Pathfinder TacAware **cannot join links**, unlike some other PanO sectorials; many premium MSV/mimetic shooters (Karhu, Nisse, Vargar) are **one Wound**. Several TAG/HI/REM plans are hackable, and a Jotum list can run short of Orders for multi-objective missions.
- **The central dissent:** **Swiss Miss** regards Jotum as a near-automatic S-tier monster; **Loss of Lieutenant #142** debated it down around high-B/low-A relative to other TAGs as AP proliferates; **Shepherd** regards it as excellent but *not compulsory* when ITS17 makes specialist/order efficiency decisive.

## TAG, Vehicle and REM research

### TAG: Jotum is strong, but the ranking genuinely splits

**The roster contains Jotum only** (AVA2): **82/1.5** regular, **82/2** Lieutenant; BS15, **ARM10/BTS9/W3**, S7, MULTI HMG, Heavy Flamethrower, Panzerfaust, Tactical Awareness, Remote Presence, critical immunity and ECM Guided(-6).

- **Swiss Miss (N5 tier list): S-tier.** Extreme armor can leave an opponent without suitable AP/EXP/CC tools; forcing several Orders to inflict damage and then **repairing** it breaks enemy tempo. Think of two Jotums as threatening but **not** a universal list recipe.
- **WIP12 Lockie/DaBoarder (2024, N4-era transcript): resilient positional bully.** Its value is *not* only standing at range shooting. Identify and kill the opponent's genuine anti-TAG pieces, then advance into useful table space and force low-power guns to fail; BTN/WIP13 gives strong resilience to nonlethal threats. Movement via its S7 body is often better than its reputation. All pre-N5 rules claims are historical.
- **Loss of Lieutenant #142 (Apr 2025 N5 TAG comparison): high-B / contested A, not S.** Multiple speakers initially rate it well but debate lowering it relative to Cutter and versatile cheaper PanO TAGs: AP prevalence reduces the practical advantage of ARM10, and the gun lacks some offense efficiencies/angles of alternatives. Heavy Flamethrower is a real defensive plus versus close attackers. The transcript contains open debate, not a unanimous fixed grade.
- **Shepherd/Lockie N5 ITS17: extremely good, optional.** Still worth 82 points when toughness itself wins the match, but multi-location/objective ITS17 missions can favor **Karhu + Hawkins/Vargar + Locust/Uma** with more specialists and faster coverage. **Foxbringer 2026** likes one Jotum, says two leave too few useful pieces and notes a linked KoJ ML can force a dangerous FtF despite extreme armor.
- **Tradeoffs:** no native SWF TAG Fireteam, not the same tool as a Tikbalang (Mimetism/Climbing Plus in **other** PanO rosters) or Dragão (HRMC in **other** PanO rosters). Those cross-faction comparisons are about opportunity cost **between sectorials**, not additional SWF legal picks. Armor is bypassed by sufficient AP, E/M, hacking, positioning and CC; screening and repair matter. **Machinist 17/Palbot3**, **Karhu Engineer25**, **Blade FTO Engineer23**, **Tech-Bee9** provide different repair/utility layers. Tech-Bee TAGCom buffs defensive/repair-related rolls **not the TAG's BS**.

### Vehicles (VH): none in SWF

- **No SWF Vehicle-class unit**, including **no Redeye**. Do not transpose Redeye from **Vanilla PanO/Kestrel/NCA** battle reports or podcasts into this sectorial.
- **Cross-PanO opponent preparation:** [Shepherd: N5 Aerial Vehicles](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N6TYjMgU4ZM) argues **Redeye** needs expensive clearing guns as a partner: super-jump/high-angle attacks punish null deployment but Spitfire alone cannot clear every hard ARO and SWC competes with real high-power guns. [Dice Gods: Vehicles/Flyers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QscqX3hlUZU) takes the opposing view that Redeye's price, speed, Mimetism and tall attack angles are potentially *too* efficient and may force defensive list changes; anti-flyer counters and roofs must be considered. **Both YouTube MCP transcripts checked**. These are **not** SWF army-building options; expect them as opponents and preserve elevated ARO/defense coverage.

## Dissent to remember

1. **Mandatory Jotum?** Swiss Miss S-tier, Lockie WIP12 control-bully; Loss of Lieutenant debates high-B/A due to AP prevalence; Shepherd says skip if ITS17 objectives demand too many specialist Orders. **Test versus mission + anti-armor opposition**.
2. **Karhu vs ORC vs Nisse?** Foxbringer favors Karhu's Mimetism/MSV1 avoiding hits over ORC W2; ORC supporters value predictable wounds and TinBot; Nisse MSV2 is stronger into particular smoke/visual opponents but loses versatility/armor. **Choose what attack MODs you're solving**.
3. **Vargar/Disco vs Bøyg?** Shepherd likes inexpensive flexible FO Vargar far more; Swiss/WIP12 admit Bøyg's durable ML or Mk12 opens other arcs. **Pay 44 only when W2/ARM5 makes a difference**.
4. **Locust vs Nøkken / Uma?** Later Shepherd values hidden, prepositioned specialists and 27pt Camouflage Uma for ITS17; early Dante skeptical of Uma and rated Locust > exposed Nøkken. **Build for the mission, not just a gun profile**.
5. **Pathfinder vs Peacemaker/Bulleteer?** Shepherd still runs unlinked Pathfinder for FO/Sensor/extra Order; Swiss Miss rates it low *because it cannot link*, preferring Peacemaker (control) or Bulleteer (attacks); Dante is cautious on Peacemaker where hackers are scarce.
6. **Blade FTO vs 30pt Parachutist/Decoy?** Shepherd/Swiss Miss love FTO Engineer/TacAware linked utility; Foxbringer (2026) judges 20pt bare FTO and 30pt independent versions inefficient next to cheaper/hidden mission tools.
7. **Melee choices:** Swiss Miss prefers linked Shona/Valkyrie to Gunnar/Liang Kai; Shepherd argues scenario scoring makes Hawkins and a melee escort more valuable than a pure enemy-DZ murder run. **Pick your required objective before your preferred CC model**.
8. **Older presenter vs current Fireteam data:** Dante's N5 initial video incorrectly remains true for old link-access discussion; 2026 pinned roster specifically allows **Blade FTO in Fusiliers**. WIP12 2024 uses N4 rules, not current costs/keywords. Protect current facts from automated transcript errors.
