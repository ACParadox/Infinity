## Costing requirements for ALL sectorials

Before publishing **any** 'full completion price', ensure every missing unit identity has one assigned purchased product or a clearly accepted no-cost physical proxy with correct silhouette; group boxed sets across multiple identities once; include multi-figure support/TAG/warband peripherals when mandatory; apply scenario acquisitions, avoid/no-buy policy, no duplicate owned inventory. If any identity remains unpriced/unproxied, label the result **'priced subtotal, full price unknown'** with the explicit unpriced identities. Do not use a named starter/core-box total as though it is a full completion price. **Do not silently subtract unseen planned-but-not-bought boxes from the physical inventory.**
