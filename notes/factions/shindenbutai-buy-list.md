## Owned-miniature proxy shortlist (same silhouette, class and identifiable shape)

Follow [the exact adopted JSA proxy rules](../miniature-proxy-guidelines.md): **correct silhouette including base diameter *and* silhouette height**, prefer LI↔LI, MI↔MI, HI↔HI, SK↔SK, REM↔REM, TAG↔TAG. Similar Japanese armour or ninja look is preferable to generic unrelated models, particularly for bikes, TAGs, dogs or remotes. **Do NOT use sculpts of a unit *also selectable in this same target army* as stand-ins for another unit**, even if today's army list leaves the donor out. Always account for *one physical body per fielded model*, including a separate S1 servant/ancillary, bike/dismounted form or Koala where the profile requires representation. Declare proxies to opponents and check TO policy.

**No unconditional whole-unit ban.** Directly criticised loadouts: *Senku HMG as main gun* (not useful FO/Commlink line bodies), *Aïda non-FTO ambush* versus Raiden HRL (the FTO Viral/minelayer remains distinctive), and unplanned Jizamurai fireteam tax. **Strong proxy rule:** every suggested donor must match base **and** height, the correct class/visual archetype, and not already be a selectable unit under the same force's name. In Ōban use outsider Shinden figures as donors only where they are not legal themselves; in Shinden don't proxy absent Ninjas with Kurayami/Nokizaru because they are themselves legal. One physical Yáoxiè, Yudbot or Yojimbo does not become multiple simultaneous figures. These lists are not inventory updates.
