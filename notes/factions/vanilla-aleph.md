# Vanilla ALEPH — N5.3 tactical and list-building research

**Reference archive:** [Vanilla ALEPH source recordings, cross-references, battle reports and purchasing audit](references/vanilla-aleph_refs.md). **Authoritative N5.3 roster:** [Vanilla ALEPH reference](../../sectorials/aleph/aleph.md).

## Overview

**What Vanilla ALEPH does exceptionally well:** it combines extremely efficient **Posthuman / cheap-Order / REM infrastructure** with tools usually spread across different sectorials: Greek **normal Smoke and close combat**, powerful **MSV2/MSV3 gunfighters**, elite WIP15 hacking, Hidden/Infiltrating specialists, and either a premium TAG or a top-tier aerial Vehicle. It wins by choosing *several independent routes* into the mission rather than relying on a single linked attack team.

### Whole-game standouts — apex versus points value

**Top 3 / Top 5 / Top 10 are editorial candidates *within the specific job named*, not measured pick-rate rankings, tournament statistics, or claims that these are the best units overall.** **Apex** measures the capability ceiling; **value** measures that capability for the *complete* points/SWC/Order investment. These shortlists draw on the repository's [multi-body](../list-building/peripheral-synchronized-multibody-apex-value.md), [TAG](../list-building/tags.md), [Vehicle](../list-building/vehicles-vh.md), [toolbox](../list-building/jack-of-all-trades-toolbox-units-apex-value.md), [hacker](../list-building/hackers-and-killer-hackers.md), and [cheap-Order](../list-building/cheerleaders-and-order-generators.md) comparisons.

**Other high-quality roles without forced whole-game ranks:**
- **Shukra CoC 21/0:** Counterintelligence and succession insurance for a risky Marut/Asura Lieutenant.
- **Myrmidon 16/0:** cheap normal Smoke, Mimetism(-6) and a credible melee screen.
- **Dasyu FO 29/0 / KHD 31/0:** Hidden Deployment, Mimetism(-6) and mission reach at a significant cost.
- **Parvati 36/0 / Sophotect 25/0:** different premium recovery packages; the cheap Posthuman Mk.1 often makes a second premium repairer unnecessary.

### Particularly powerful synergies — but no standard Vanilla Fireteams

**Vanilla ALEPH has no normal Core, Haris or Duo Fireteam chart in the pinned N5.3 roster.** An FTO label, a unit named a "Team", or an OSS/Steel Phalanx chart does **not** grant Vanilla linking. Its standout *non-Fireteam* combinations are:

- **Marut + 16pt Myrmidon Smoke:** exceptionally high anti-Mimetism shooting ceiling, paid for with smoke/setup Orders and substantial TAG points.
- **Posthuman Mk.1 Engineer + Mk.4 HMG / Mk.5 FO:** repair, long-range attack and scoring spread across the table without buying separate troop slots or separate Regular Orders.
- **Pilot-X + one/two RacerBots + Danavas HD+ (optional):** fast Repeater placement plus a high-WIP Pitcher/White Noise Hacker; the full two-bot Pilot-X package costs **39/0.5**, before Danavas.
- **Marut or Maximus + Apsaras TAGCom + Engineer:** same-Combat-Group printed TAG support and a genuine recovery plan, *not* a Fireteam bonus.

### Unusual access, meaningful absences and costs

- **All three kinds of vision control exist, on different profiles:**
  - **Normal Smoke:** Myrmidon, Penthesilea and Hippolyta; friendly MSV can exploit it against suitable opponents.
  - **Eclipse / projected vision denial:** Kinnara Parachutist Eclipse and the **Yadu / Claire Disco Baller** profile options; this is **not** ordinary smoke-shooting.
  - **White Noise:** HD+ options such as Danavas and Asura, against the appropriate visors/Marksmanship situation.
- **Unusual combined visor access:** **Marut MSV2** plus **Asura MSV3**, with Greek Smoke available in the *same Vanilla army*.
- **Command and recovery:** **Apsaras cheap LT**, **Shukra CoC + Counterintelligence**, efficient Posthuman engineering, and optional TAGCom support.
- **Heavy platform choice:** **Marut** and **Maximus** are TAG choices; **Tarkshya is VH, not a third TAG**. **Agent Maximus and TAG Maximus cannot be selected together.**
- **Main price for flexibility:** no legal standard Fireteam bonuses, many Hackable REM/TAG investments, expensive premium models and competing Orders/SWC. A broader roster does not mean a single 300pt list can buy every strong module.

## One-minute faction assessment

- **Core identity:** A **Posthuman/cheap-order/REM infrastructure** like OSS, plus **Myrmidon smoke, Hippolyta/Penthesilea CC, Greek MSV/ARO, K2 flank utility**, all selectable together with elite hacking and a premium TAG. **No normal Fireteams**, so the cost is losing the link bonuses/support combinations that make OSS more concentrated.
- **Best generic skeleton (choose the mission-specific options):**
  - **Apsaras LT 20/0**, or another legal Lieutenant.
  - **Posthuman Mk1 Engineer 13 + Mk4 HMG 30**, with optional **Mk5 FO 13** or **Mk2 Hacker 22**.
  - **Netrod 6** (AVA3) for cheap Orders.
  - **Lamedh 7** (AVA2) for Flash Pulse/Repeater duty.
  - **Probot Baggage 8** for support and reloads.
  - **Smoke delivery** if fielding **Marut** or another MSV attack piece.
  - Spend the remainder on the mission's extra attacker, hacker or midfield package.
- **Vanilla's additional smoke/CC access:**
  - **Myrmidon 16pt:** efficient normal Smoke.
  - **Penthesilea 28pt:** Smoke GL, monofilament CC and specialist bike.
  - **Hippolyta 37pt:** Smoke, close combat and vertical assault.
  - **Marut:** can smoke-shoot using MSV instead of relying on an unobstructed BS15 gunfight. Smoke still costs Orders and can fail against enemy MSV, templates or counter-hacking.
- **Strengths:** dense Regular Order floor, very efficient one-slot G:Jumper support/firepower, the elite BS15 TAG with smoke, WIP15 hackers and specialists, cheap sensor/repeater bots, many independent surgical strikes, and unusual redundancy.
- **Weaknesses:** big hackable REM/TAG population; no link-based Sixth Sense/extra dice; many one-STR robots and relatively vulnerable hidden/deployed LT; cramped protected S3/S4/S7 bases; inefficient overinvestment in character-heavy lists; high SWC if buying Guided + several long guns.
- **Why Vanilla rather than OSS:** Shepherd/Jordan (June 2026) lean Vanilla for **smoke + Greek melee**: sometimes a fast Penthesilea attack solves a repeater fortress better than another hacker or REM. **Why OSS instead:** Fireteam specialists/bonuses, more concentrated Asura/Optimates building and some different AVAs/loadouts; not every Vanilla unit is a downgrade or upgrade.

**Vision-control implications:**

- **Vanilla signature:** **16pt Myrmidon normal Smoke + Marut MSV2**, or Hippolyta/Penthesilea Smoke with Asura/other visor attacker. The smoke carrier is cheap compared with the Marut; the **whole package** still risks huge active-turn investment. **Asura HD+ White Noise** selectively blocks enemy visors/Marksmanship, not ordinary guns.
- **Against strong MSV lanes:** ordinary Smoke does not guarantee passage; build a different gun/marker angle, Eclipse profile where legal, or White Noise where reachable. This is a principal advantage over OSS, which uses Yadu/Claire Disco and Kinnara Eclipse instead of broad cheap Smoke. [Apex/value tiers](../list-building/vision-control-units-apex-and-value.md).

### Best value in the first 100–140 points of a list

1. **Posthuman Mk1 Engineer 13 + Mk4 HMG 30**: high-value repair plus strong BS14/ARM5 heavy gun for *one G:Jumper troop slot*. Third Proxy often **Mk5 FO 13** for objectives or **Mk2 Hacker 22** for Hidden specialist; some 2026 hosts intentionally cut the third when other midfield needs win.
2. **Netrods 6 each (max 3)**: efficient Regular orders and Warhorse. **Must Combat Jump in deployment**; plan landing/deployment risk and do not treat them as ordinary freely placed DZ Cheerleaders.
3. **Lamedh 7 + Probot 8**: cheap network/Flash Pulse order and Baggage order. The first Baggage bot supports reloads (Pitchers, mine dispensers, other Disposable gear). Extra big bots only where hiding spots permit.
4. **16pt Myrmidon**: pays for itself whenever smoke creates safe Marut/Agêma/Asura gunfights or protects the mission path; less attractive if list has neither MSV nor a use for CC/vision denial.
5. **Pilot-X full team 23+8+8 = 39pts / .5 SWC**: three order-generating bodies in one Combat Group, very fast two-bot forward Repeater and PARA pressure. Restriction: **RacerBots cannot deploy independently or in another Combat Group**. No Fireteam bonus from the “Team” name.

**Do not blindly maximize AVA:** Vanilla allows **Riksha/Dawon 5**, Dikpala Total, Netrods 3, Zayin 2, unlike some OSS entries; more legal bots do not imply more safe DZ slots or spare orders.

## Characters and specialists that make Vanilla different

- **Penthesilea 28/0:** fast Specialist/Smoke Grenade Launcher/Monofilament bike; solves a hostile hacking-repeater fortress by **taking a physical flank** and, in a Marut list, can create smoke. **Disagreement:** Shepherd values her highly, but using her exclusively as a “smoke taxi” wastes the fast attack and scoring she was purchased for; sometimes the **16pt Myrmidon** is the better smoke source.
- **Hippolyta 37/0:** W2 Mimetism(-6) high-CC Super-Jump + Smoke, answers close threats and vertical cover cheaply compared with buying another giant robot. Costs more than Myrmidon and competes with TAG support points.
- **Myrmidons 16–30:** very useful **16pt Chain Rifle Smoke** baseline; Hacker **29/.5** if mission/hack goals demand. More than one is plausible with an MSV-focused Marut/Agêma gun plan. Unlike older N4 sources **there is no ordinary Vanilla Fireteam**.
- **Midfield choices — buy the mission's required jobs, not all five:**
  - **Naga FO 25/0:** economical forward specialist.
  - **Dasyu FO 29/0**, viral minelayer **30/.5**, or Hidden MSR **34/1.5:** expensive but meaningful HD/BS/Mimetism after the update; older blanket dismissals are outdated.
  - **Dart 42/.5 with K-9:** dangerous independent midfield fork at a premium.
  - **Thyreos FO 22/0:** cheap forward scoring.
  - **Samsa 26/0:** forward Plasma specialist with Decoy.
- **Agêma 27/1.5:** Hidden MULTI Sniper or Camo/Decoy Missile, MSV2. **Vanilla** grants access to this anti-Mimetism ARO without needing the Steel Phalanx link; expensive one-STR investment and must be placed where the enemy spends orders to solve it.
- **Apsaras LT 20/0 versus Deva LT 20+:** This is the most important vanilla list-construction improvement. Apsaras supplies a cheap, useful hacker/RemDriver LT, with Firewall but one wound; Deva is safer against dedicated KHD alpha and offers Devabot/Sensor variants. Vanilla **Apsaras RemDriver 20/1** (non-LT), unlike OSS **20/0**. **TAGCom Apsaras 18/0** improves Marut/Maximus without link assumptions.
- **Danavas 22/.5 HD+ Pitcher:** still the most straightforward WIP15 ranged-network projection and White Noise plan if Asura isn't in list; **Pilot-X** is a different axis (fast bot-delivered Repeaters, Zero Pain), not proof Danavas has become obsolete. More Pitchers means more Baggage payoff, but forward Repeaters can expose own REMs.
- **Shukra CoC 21/0:** Counterintelligence and continuity are valuable behind an expensive Lieutenant; a 21pt insurance policy is not free. **Hawkwood CoC 40/0** trades cost for fighting ability. Make LoL decisions *before* buying every extra REM.
- **Recovery specialists — compare actual roles:**
  - **Parvati 36/0:** durable combined Doctor/Engineer.
  - **Sophotect 25/0:** WIP15/NWI Doctor/Engineer, optional turret.
  - **Posthuman Mk1 13/0:** efficient WIP15 Engineer once G:Jumper is funded.
  - **Artalis 23/0:** NCO/K1 Engineer and frontline utility.
  - **Johnny 36+:** durable defensive specialism.
  - Avoid two premium repairers for one REM unless distant mission requirements warrant them.

**Asura toolbox versus distributed specialist packages:**

- **Asura HD+ 63/.5** is an apex solo gun+WIP15 hacker+MSV3+scorer: buy when combat, cyber and objective work lie on one approach.
- **AP Spitfire61/1.5** is a separate non-hacker gun profile.
- The counterview (2026 Shepherd/LoL) is that no Mimetism forces fair gunfights and 63pts could buy multiple independent specialists.
- **Parvati/Sophotect/Posthuman** instead compress recovery and scoring for less, but do not acquire Asura gun power.

## Keep disagreements instead of averaging them

1. **Vanilla vs OSS:** June 2026 Shepherd/Jordan lean **Vanilla**, since **smoke plus MSV Marut / independent Penthesilea or Hippolyta** adds routes OSS cannot buy, while many post-rework OSS bodies are good even without links. May Tactical Awareness is very enthusiastic about **OSS's FT chains, Sharvara and expensive specialists**. Both acknowledge early post-update sample size and that optimized mission lists may shift verdict.
2. **Posthuman third proxy:** Extremely strong efficiency case for Mk1 + Mk4; Shepherd generally likes Mk5 FO/Mk2 specialist added. Jordan sometimes stops at **two proxies** to afford Naga/Dart or other mission-specific pieces. **Mk4**, not Mk5, has the HMG (video transcription sometimes confuses them).
3. **Asura hackers:** May LoL strongly prefers **HD+** over AP-Spitfire for hacking/mission versatility; June Shepherd values her **elite protection** but observes that trading damage upgrade for a special die means some BTS6 hackers tank hits. Vanilla **LT variant is Spitfire only**; don't transpose OSS LT hacking profile.
4. **Marut vs Maximus:** Marut is the natural smoke/MSV/Strategos build; old LoL ranking disliked older Maximus. **May 2026** commenters changed their evaluation following AP HMG/Thunderbolt and defensive upgrades. ## Practical turn / deployment checklist

1. **Choose one high-end fighter + alternate route.** A Marut/Maximus needs smoke/repeater strategy and Engineer; **Tarkshya**, Garuda, Penthesilea, Kinnara or midfield specialists should score where the TAG cannot. Pick route before investing in a second expensive character.
2. **Establish LT/CoC and support.** If **20pt Apsaras LT**, hide it and consider KHD/Impersonator attack vectors; if **90pt Marut LT**, treat it as a significant assassination objective and plan Shukra CoC where needed. Warhorse Netrods mitigate some LoL order consequences but **don't eliminate LT risk**.
3. **Build real combat groups.** Spend Netrods/flashbots/probots to reach usable pools, then assign **regular orders** to your intended attackers and AD arrivals; Tactical Awareness, Impetuous and Lieutenant orders aren't universally transferable Regular group orders.
4. **Deploy smoke and MSV with a plan:** enemy MSV/Albedo/White Noise can reverse your advantage, and smoke sources are easily lost. **Eclipse** is for safety/path creation, **not** smoke-shooting.
5. **Repair geometry:** Mk1/Yudbot or Sophotect must realistically reach Zayin, Rudra, Marut or Tarkshya Operator. A valuable remote that dies in unreachable open table space has no functional “repair engine”.
6. **Avoid repeaters as gifts.** Place Pilot-X RacerBot repeaters so your hackers can act without giving enemy KHD free access to the unprotected backline; clear enemy network with Myrmidon/Penthesilea or other unhackable/low-hackability routes when necessary.
7. **Choose ARO by task:** 19/0 Zayin to tax routes, 27/1.5 Agêma to appear at an unexpected angle, 20/1.5 Riksha HRL for recoverable area pressure, or **16/1.5 Samekh Guided** only as an actual Targeted follow-up, not a “free” static solution.
8. **Budget mission bodies:** 7pt+8pt support REMs, Mk5 FO, Garuda FO, Thyreos FO, Naga and Penthesilea are not decorative. A list with great kills but no endgame scoring can still lose.
