# JSA — consolidated Vanilla, Ōban and Shindenbutai collection buy carts (N5.3)

**Date:** 2026-10-30. **Authoritative committed `main` N5.3 Army `7.26246.159` (verified 2026-10-16):** [Vanilla JSA, 43 roster entries](../../sectorials/jsa/jsa.md), [Ōban, 35](../../sectorials/jsa/oban.md), [Shindenbutai, 36](../../sectorials/jsa/shindenbutai.md). Read each of the detailed, per-unit buy/owned/proxy/skip audits: [Vanilla](vanilla-jsa-buy-list.md), [Ōban](oban-buy-list.md), [Shinden](shindenbutai-buy-list.md). These are buy plans, **not inventory modifications**.

**Ownership and objectives:** You already own most Shinden combat models: Senku×3, Raiden, Jizamurai, Hatamoto, Nokizaru, Teishin, Kurayami, Shizoku, Shurayuki, Mechazoid, Kyojin×2, Yuriko, Tanuki×2, Yamabushi×2, **both Yojimbo mounted/foot sculpts (mutually exclusive profiles)**, **2 Yáoxiè Rui Shi/Lù Duān shared chassis**, and Warcor. [Inventory and pool accounting](../../inventory/shindenbutai.md) remains the source. Missing useful first identities: Keisotsu, Ryūken, Kempeitai, Tankō, Domaru, Shikami, Tokusetsu, Metsuke. Strong counts to own physically: **2+ Keisotsu, 2 Domaru, 3 Tankō shared (max 2 in Vanilla), 2+ Jizamurai, 3+ Tanuki and 2 Hatamoto**. None of this requires buying an entire duplicate Sandtrap box. No AVA-completion or every-weapon rule.

**Important correction to old project notes:** **Tankō Zensenbutai are legal in current pinned Vanilla JSA (AVA2) AND Ōban (AVA3)**. The earlier three JSA buy lists contained obsolete/incorrect “Ōban-only” statements, which were corrected on 2026-10-30; the existing source-backed Vanilla Tankō Disco recommendation was right. Tankō are **not** a Shindenbutai standard unit. Likewise **Yojimbo is Ōban/Shinden-only**, **Kuroshi Rider and Yuan Yuan are Ōban-only**, **Tanuki is Shinden-only**, and **Shinden Sandtrap named models are often Vanilla legal but not necessarily Ōban legal**.

**Price caution:** Cheap USD amounts are **U — user-saved historical Miniatures Market and Ultimate Toy Zone quotations**, NOT verified live checkout offers or shipping origin. [JSA USD source snapshot](jsa-miniatures-market-prices-usd.md), [UTZ USD snapshot](ultimate-toy-zone-infinity-prices-usd.md). **USD 1 = CAD 1.3985 (2026-10-29)**, each product independently rounded. **V** Canadian catalogue price in CAD at **Tistaminis (Hamilton ON)** or **401 Games (Toronto/Vaughan ON)**, and stock can change. Some Miniatures Market product URL pages previously returned 404. Taxes (GST/QST), shipping, FX conversion charges, duties and brokerage excluded. **Do not treat an archive quote as an orderable cart**.

## Existing models, robot proxies, and mandatory model counts

- **Generic robots:** The ordinary four **Robio/Atom/Garon/Uran Aibōt** normal-looking drone roles do **not** need their official packs when suitable **four owned PanO Dronbots S3** represent them clearly. Baggage **Pangguling** from existing **S4 Mulebot**, servant **Yáozăo S1** from **Palbot/Yudbot**. **Two owned Yáoxiè chassis TOTAL** can serve Rui Shi or Lù Duān in different lists—never count 2 each or simultaneously duplicate a physical chassis.
- **Owned shared human units:** 1 Warcor, Yuriko Oda, Shinden named figures, existing 2 Yamabushi and Yojimbo mounted/foot, existing TAG Mechazoid. **Yojimbo in Ōban/Shinden only**; Shinden **Tanuki** are not legal as themselves in Vanilla/Ōban; Shinden **Kyojin** legal in Vanilla but not Ōban. Only reassign a physical miniature to its **exact named currently legal JSA roster**. Any proposed purchase is not owned until confirmed.
- **Occasional human-role donors:** Spare **Kestrel Fennec S2 LI** for third Keisotsu/extra Moderator-like line troops where appropriate, **OSS Naga S2 SK** for missing Ninja/Oniwaban/Saito (provided it is not already fielded), free **Caledonia Cameronian or McMurrough S6** for Ioann Bann S6 and suitable **Penthesilea S4 motorcycle** for occasional Aragoto. Do **not** proxy a same-army selectable Kurayami as Ninja, Yojimbo as Aragoto, or S6 TAG as S7 Triphammer. Choose the identical **Silhouette/base/height**, armor/physical archetype, and make all simultaneously fielded roles visually distinct.
- **Physical multi-part rules:** 1 Neko is a character even if sold singly and in Booster. Two Yojimbo physical forms exist but **only one Yojimbo character identity** can be fielded. JSA Support's two Yáozăo are servant peripherals and **do not produce extra ordinary trooper Orders**; O-Yoroi's **Kobaruto** and Triphammer's **TurtleMek** are separate ancillary models when using applicable profiles. No user inventory counts confirm either ancillary; don't assume them owned.
- **Profile/whole-unit skips:** Avoid the poorly rated **Genbutai BSG as a generic close trader** compared with Domaru/Tankō; **Genbutai Engineer/NCO/mission-specialist profiles remain valid**. Proxy unused Sōhei/Kyoshō, a second Shikami/Oniwaban/extra Shinobu/Saito, expensive LE and obsolete sculpts, extra TAGs and one of every Yojimbo/hatamoto weapon pose; **Vanilla Tankō Disco is not a skip or an illegal troop**. Don't buy extra Shinden Sandtrap/Beyond/Expansion Beta when their unit identities are already owned, and never pay collector premium for an unrelated **Kuge Delegate HVT or Kaizoku Spec-Ops preorder exclusive** as though it were a normal roster troop.

[401 Ōban Alpha](https://store.401games.ca/products/infinity-jsa-oban-expansion-pack-alpha) CAD52.95 **sold out** versus [Tistaminis](https://tistaminis.com/collections/infinity-jsa) CAD45 low stock.
