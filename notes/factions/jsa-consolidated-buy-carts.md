- Bought **two Aragoto S4 motorcycles** are usable in Vanilla and Ōban, but **not four physical bikes** for Ōban AVA4. To test an Ōban four-Aragoto list, arrange two distinct additional appropriate S4 motorcycle proxies such as free **TAK Dynamos/Penthesilea**; **don't use Yojimbo** as a proxy in Ōban because Yojimbo is himself legal there. Dismounted bike states must have appropriate S2 figures where necessary.
- **Shinobu/Karakuri are actually selected to buy**; a generic OSS Naga or HI donor is no longer an instruction to avoid buying their first physical models. Extra Oniwaban, Ninja, Saito, rare named skirmishers, extra Shikami/Aragoto beyond demonstrated lists and collector/LE variations remain proxy-first.
- **Alternative acquisition:** old **JSA Expansion Alpha** (2 Ninjas, Shikami, Shinobu, Oniwaban) contains the wanted Shinobu but *duplicates Shikami* from Start Here and adds other ninjas; consider it only if actually obtainable cheaply **instead of** Shinobu single, not in addition. **JSA Essentials Army Pack 7** (3 Keisotsu, Kempeitai, Ryūken, Oniwaban, Daiyōkai) overlaps multiple planned items; replace/deduplicate rather than add a whole starter on top.
