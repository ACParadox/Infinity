# Infinity N5.3 — Overall Faction & Sectorial Rankings (1–45)

*Last reviewed: 13 November 2026. Includes all 45 Vanilla factions, sectorials and NA2 armies in the repository's N5.3 roster set.*

## What this ranking means

This is a **qualitative, editorial competitive-strength ranking**, **not** measured faction win rates, an official Corvus Belli tier list, or a statistical prediction of a particular matchup. It preserves the **user-provided tier boundaries and exact left-to-right order within each tier** as the complete #1–#45 ranking. The accompanying one-line explanations are qualitative context informed by the faction guides and external discussion, not independent justification for changing those positions. Adjacent positions, especially in the same tier, are close calls.

The primary contemporary discussion is Robert Shepherd with Jordan (*Loss of Lieutenant*) in their [2026 all-factions comparison](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1dW3i9dpI34) (published **13 June 2026**, video title says “July 2026 Mazebreaker Update”). This is cross-checked against their [October 2025 N5 tier review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHIXG1moDZw), *Loss of Lieutenant* [Episode 132 — N5 Faction Tierlist](https://lossoflieutenant.podbean.com/e/episode-132-n5-faction-tierlist/) (**24 December 2024**; older first impressions), and our N5.3 [sectorial capability matrix](sectorial-capability-access-matrix-n5-3.md), [individual listbuilding guides](factions/) and [battle-report mission analysis](battle-reports/cross-report-mission-performance-by-sectorial.md). The content creators do **not** necessarily endorse the exact positions below.

## Parent-faction rankings — common strengths across armies

This is a **separate #1–#11 ranking of the parent factions/army families**, judging what works **repeatedly across most of their constituent Vanilla and sectorial rosters**, rather than how strong their single best army is. It considers shared offensive and defensive tools, mission play, points/Order efficiency, breadth of reliable plans and the typical floor across the family. It is **not** a mean of the #1–#45 placements or a statistically measured tournament ranking. The ranks in parentheses are cross-checks against the unchanged detailed table below; **Next Wave belongs to Combined Army**, not O-12. Parent-family tiers are comparative within this table, not a recalculation of constituent army tiers.

JSA is above O-12 because its constituent entries are more consistently competitive even though O-12 has several excellent individual units. Tohaa is a single-army case; NA2 is an especially loose collective, so ranks #10–11 are less directly comparable to the other parent factions.

**Whole-game standout-unit percentages:** Each column is the number of **distinct standard roster units** appearing at least once at rank **#1–3**, **#1–5**, or **#1–10**, respectively, in a qualifying broad-role Apex or Value ranking, divided by the army's standard logical roster-unit count (the `Roster units` column). Values are cumulative and rounded to one decimal. A qualifying profile validates its *unit*; alternate loadouts, Apex and Value placements, multiple role lists, and repeated table entries never give additional units. A compound/Peripheral package is assigned to its parent roster unit rather than separately counting its attached bodies. Optional Spec-Ops, Team-Ops and Reinforcements are excluded from the roster denominator.

**Included broad roles:** Active-turn gunfighters, TAGs, Vehicles, ARO/long-range and deployment-zone/midfield defense, mission specialists/button pushers, hackers and Repeater/Pitcher network projection, warbands/cheap trading, command/Lieutenant/Order economy, non-hacking anti-TAG/HI, vision control, airborne/enemy-DZ breakthroughs, Doctors/Engineers/recovery and objective holding/zone domination. The **same qualifying role** and the exact locally available N5.3 unit/profile must support its rank; a higher placement in another, excluded role cannot be borrowed.

**Excluded narrow/overlapping roles:** Force multipliers, dedicated close-combat assassins (including specialist contact/CC subrankings), indirect/speculative/Guided fire, classified-objective runners, rare or unique capabilities, jack-of-all-trades/toolbox, Mimetism/BS MOD stacking, Nanoscreen/Albedo and other attribute-only or anti-tech subrankings. The excluded rankings alone never qualify a unit; another eligible broad-role placement can still qualify it.

**Interpretation:** This is **documented qualifying-unit density**, not a measured tournament-strength statistic. The source `factions/` guides consolidate profiles and categories differently and do not necessarily capture every eligible unit. Review source unit names, specific profiles and Fireteam/Peripheral eligibility before treating close percentages as meaningful. Counts reviewed **16 November 2026**.

## Best Camouflage / Shell Games — Top 10 armies (N5.3)

**Scope (17 November 2026):** This is a **separate playstyle ranking**, including Vanilla, sectorial and NA2 armies from the pinned N5.3 roster set; it does **not** change the overall #1–#45 table. Here, **shell games** mean compelling uncertainty over *which Camo markers conceal real troopers, Decoys or mines; what those troopers can do; and where unrepresented Hidden Deployment/Impersonation threats might emerge*. The order emphasizes **quality and danger of the concealed threats, density/redundancy, deployment reach, and ability to turn uncertainty into wasted enemy Orders or favorable ambushes** rather than the sheer count of Camo profiles. These positions are an **editorial synthesis**, not measured tournament win rates or a Top 10 endorsed by the creators. **What the media actually supports:** [Robert Shepherd — TAK faction focus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eM5cM_Y18vk&t=407s) explicitly calls the Camo/Decoy shell game among the game's best; [MetaChemistry #141 — TAK (4 May 2026)](https://open.spotify.com/episode/5Jndc3fD91n84MZ9WZxM4l) and [WIP12 #203 — TAK (15 April 2026)](https://open.spotify.com/episode/0fLvDDDa2baw7lxt3J4qo9) independently emphasize sheer marker saturation, dangerous hidden identities and layered defense. [Tactical Awareness S3E12 — Shasvastii (March 2025)](https://open.spotify.com/episode/0oGd3tdSkCtTp2Yi99sRc5) examines its Camo, Hidden Deployment, Speculo and Decoy toolbox. **Category winners:** **TAK** for visible Camo/Decoy/mine saturation; **Hassassin Bahram** for completely hidden ambush threats; **Shasvastii** for the broadest *integrated* Camo + Hidden Deployment + Impersonation package. ## Best Gunfighting — Top 10 armies (N5.3)

Compare a plausible, legal **300-point N5.3 list's ability to win and sustain shooting engagements**: quality of active-turn gunfighters (including TAGs), credible long-range/ARO defence, MSV/Mimetism/Marksmanship/Albedo and weapon/ammunition answers, durability, points/SWC value, plus Fireteam and Order economy that let the guns work repeatedly. This is **not** a ranking of overall mission strength or of a single unit's theoretical maximum. **Editorial synthesis** of the linked faction reviews, podcasts and [active-turn gunfighter rankings](list-building/active-turn-gunfighters-apex-and-value.md), **not** a community-voted Top 10, tournament win-rate calculation, or a creator-endorsed ordering. The first three have especially direct media support; positions 4–10 require more roster-level judgment. The overall #1–#45 table above remains unchanged.

## Best Close Combat / Close Assault — Top 10 armies (N5.3)

**Scope (17 November 2026):** Separate **CC *and/or* short-range (0–16in) attack** playstyle comparison, including Vanilla, sectorials and NA2. Approximate editorial priorities: **30% melee quality** (dueling, assassinations, anti-heavy damage and redundancy), **30% practical close-range offense** (templates, shotguns, Viral/Plasma and multi-modal threats), **25% delivery and survival** (Smoke/Eclipse, Hidden Deployment/Impersonation, movement, Fireteams, defensive durability), **15% points and Order efficiency**. A melee expert unable to reach a victim does not automatically win the category. These are **judgment weights, not statistically computed scores or win rates**. The order is our synthesis of linked creator reviews, podcasts and pinned N5.3 roster/unit rankings; **no creator published or endorsed this full Top 10**. The existing overall rankings above remain unchanged.
