# Infinity N5.3 — Overall Faction & Sectorial Rankings (1–45)

*Last reviewed: 19 November 2026. Includes all 45 Vanilla factions, sectorials and NA2 armies in the repository's N5.3 roster set.*

## What this ranking means

This is a **qualitative, editorial competitive-strength ranking**, **not** measured faction win rates, an official Corvus Belli tier list, or a statistical prediction of a particular matchup. It retains the prior user-provided tier boundaries and ordering **except for the explicitly requested 19 November 2026 reassessment of #17–20**. The accompanying one-line explanations are qualitative context informed by the faction guides and external discussion, not independent justification for changing those positions. Adjacent positions, especially in the same tier, are close calls.

The primary contemporary discussion is Robert Shepherd with Jordan (*Loss of Lieutenant*) in their [2026 all-factions comparison](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1dW3i9dpI34) (published **13 June 2026**, video title says “July 2026 Mazebreaker Update”). This is cross-checked against their [October 2025 N5 tier review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHIXG1moDZw), *Loss of Lieutenant* [Episode 132 — N5 Faction Tierlist](https://lossoflieutenant.podbean.com/e/episode-132-n5-faction-tierlist/) (**24 December 2024**; older first impressions), and our N5.3 [sectorial capability matrix](sectorial-capability-access-matrix-n5-3.md), [individual listbuilding guides](factions/) and [battle-report mission analysis](battle-reports/cross-report-mission-performance-by-sectorial.md). The content creators do **not** necessarily endorse the exact positions below.

## Parent-faction rankings — common strengths across armies

This is a **separate #1–#11 ranking of the parent factions/army families**, judging what works **repeatedly across most of their constituent Vanilla and sectorial rosters**, rather than how strong their single best army is. It considers shared offensive and defensive tools, mission play, points/Order efficiency, breadth of reliable plans and the typical floor across the family. It is **not** a mean of the #1–#45 placements or a statistically measured tournament ranking. Parent-family tiers are comparative within this table, not a recalculation of constituent army tiers.

**Whole-game standout-unit percentages:** Each column is the number of **distinct standard roster units** appearing at least once at rank **#1–3**, **#1–5**, or **#1–10**, respectively, in a qualifying broad-role Apex or Value ranking, divided by the army's standard logical roster-unit count (the `Roster units` column). Values are cumulative and rounded to one decimal. A qualifying profile validates its *unit*; alternate loadouts, Apex and Value placements, multiple role lists, and repeated table entries never give additional units. A compound/Peripheral package is assigned to its parent roster unit rather than separately counting its attached bodies. Optional Spec-Ops, Team-Ops and Reinforcements are excluded from the roster denominator.

**Included broad roles:** Active-turn gunfighters, TAGs, Vehicles, ARO/long-range and deployment-zone/midfield defense, mission specialists/button pushers, hackers and Repeater/Pitcher network projection, warbands/cheap trading, command/Lieutenant/Order economy, non-hacking anti-TAG/HI, vision control, airborne/enemy-DZ breakthroughs, Doctors/Engineers/recovery and objective holding/zone domination. The **same qualifying role** and the exact locally available N5.3 unit/profile must support its rank; a higher placement in another, excluded role cannot be borrowed.

**Excluded narrow/overlapping roles:** Force multipliers, dedicated close-combat assassins (including specialist contact/CC subrankings), indirect/speculative/Guided fire, classified-objective runners, rare or unique capabilities, jack-of-all-trades/toolbox, Mimetism/BS MOD stacking, Nanoscreen/Albedo and other attribute-only or anti-tech subrankings. The excluded rankings alone never qualify a unit; another eligible broad-role placement can still qualify it.

## Best Board Control & Area Denial — Top 10 armies (N5.3)

**Scope (17 November 2026):** A separate **300-point N5.3 playstyle ranking** across Vanilla, sectorial and NA2 armies, not a modification to the overall #1–#45 strength order. Board control means **denying or taxing practical approach routes and objective positions**, with mines (including E/M, PARA and Viral), Jammers, Repeaters and live hackers, disposable/perimeter weapons, deployable turrets, Dazers, Peripheral screens and forward blockers. Favor **effective Turn-0 coverage, overlapping physical and electronic threats, points/Order efficiency, persistence after the first clearance action, and redundancy across multiple flanks**. A preplaced device counts only on the precise legal Minelayer loadout; a carried mine/Pitcher/Deployable Repeater requires the appropriate later action. Hacking denial needs an active Hacker and relevant targets, so pure Repeater density is not enough. These are qualitative judgments, not tournament win rates or a creator-endorsed consensus Top 10; see the current [midfield defender](list-building/midfield-defenders-and-speed-bumps.md) and [network projection](list-building/network-projection-repeaters-pitchers-apex-value.md) role rankings.
