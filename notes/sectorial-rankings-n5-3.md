# Infinity N5.3 — Overall Faction & Sectorial Rankings (1–45)

*Last reviewed: 13 November 2026. Includes all 45 Vanilla factions, sectorials and NA2 armies in the repository's N5.3 roster set.*

## What this ranking means

This is a **qualitative, editorial competitive-strength ranking**, **not** measured faction win rates, an official Corvus Belli tier list, or a statistical prediction of a particular matchup. It uses the **user-provided tier boundaries as the baseline**; the **numeric order within each tier is a new comparative judgment**, prioritizing (1) all-comers mission coverage, (2) list-building ceiling and efficient Orders, (3) offense/defense and counterplay options, then (4) consistency against varied opponents. Adjacent positions, especially in the same tier, are close calls.

The primary contemporary discussion is Robert Shepherd with Jordan (*Loss of Lieutenant*) in their [2026 all-factions comparison](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1dW3i9dpI34) (published **13 June 2026**, video title says “July 2026 Mazebreaker Update”). This is cross-checked against their [October 2025 N5 tier review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHIXG1moDZw), *Loss of Lieutenant* [Episode 132 — N5 Faction Tierlist](https://lossoflieutenant.podbean.com/e/episode-132-n5-faction-tierlist/) (**24 December 2024**; older first impressions), and our N5.3 [sectorial capability matrix](sectorial-capability-access-matrix-n5-3.md), [individual listbuilding guides](factions/) and [battle-report mission analysis](battle-reports/cross-report-mission-performance-by-sectorial.md). The content creators do **not** necessarily endorse the exact positions below.
