## N5 rules (vs old N4 rules):
Keep in mind these facts, but don't mention the old rules. These are the N5 rules (vs the old rules):
- Fireteam bonus level 2 give an extra Special Dice (usable in active or reactive turn) instead of + Burst.
- Fireteam bonuses are easier to get but less powerful in general.
- Cover give +3 MOD to the Saving Throw (SR), not an ARM bonus, so AP ammo doesn't help against cover.
- Berserk isn't a normal roll, but is a Face-to-Face roll.
- Combat Instinct allows the user ignores Surprise Attack MODs, not Sixth Sense
