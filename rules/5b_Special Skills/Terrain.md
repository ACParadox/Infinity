# Terrain

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Optional 

Some units receive additional training to navigate and fight effectively on a certain type of terrain

- The Type of Terrain of the Terrain area must match the Type of Terrain printed on the user's Troop Profile.

- The user is granted a +1 inch bonus to the first value of their MOV Attribute.

- This bonus only applies when declaring a Skill with the Movement Label in the Terrain area.

- The user may move normally through Terrain areas of their Terrain Type without applying any of the Movement restrictions provided by that Terrain area.

- If the user's Terrain Special Skill lists several Terrain Types between brackets on their Unit profile, the player must choose one of them when placing the Trooper on the gaming table.

- Users with Terrain (Total) apply these Effects to all Types of Terrain available in the rules (Aquatic, Desert, Mountain, Jungle, or Zero-G).


# [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
## Terrain Skill General Interaction Notes
NOTE: Terrain (Total) doesn't mean +1 MOV all the time, but +1 MOV on all special terrain types.

### Interaction with Difficult Terrain
- Troopers normally entering a Difficult Terrain zone immediately end their movement for that Order and must spend a new Order and apply -1" to MOV to continue. 
- A Trooper with matching Terrain ignores these Difficult Terrain penalties and may continue moving without the automatic movement stop or MOV penalty.
- This creates clear tactical asymmetry when the battlefield includes large Difficult Terrain sections.

### Interaction with Movement Skills
- Skills with the Movement Label (Move, Climb, Jump, Cautious Movement, Berserk, etc.) all benefit from the +1" bonus **only while inside the matching Terrain zone**.
- Entering Terrain during the first half of an Order may cause the +1" to apply only to part of the movement path; players need to measure carefully.

### Interaction with Mixed Terrain Zones
- Some custom or scenario terrain may include a Type plus additional characteristics (Visibility, Saturation, Difficult Terrain).  
  Terrain gives no special protection from Visibility MODs or Saturation, only from Movement-related penalties and provides MOV bonuses.

### Interaction with Fireteams
- Only the member(s) with Terrain gain the benefit.  
  Mixed Fireteams may split movement efficiency: members with Terrain move faster or with fewer penalties, causing potential spacing or coherency challenges.

### Interaction with Deployment Skills
- Terrain does not change deployment unless combined with Skills that reference Terrain (rare).  
  Standard Deployment, Infiltration, and Forward Deployment do not inherently change based on Terrain presence.

# [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
## Strategic and Tactical Considerations

### When Terrain Is Valuable
- Any table with large patches of Difficult Terrain such as Jungle, Desert, Mountain, or Zero-G.
- Scenarios emphasizing area control (Domination, Frontline, Supremacy) where movement efficiency inside zones matters greatly.
- Against opponents lacking the matching Terrain, letting the user outmaneuver them.

### When Terrain Is Less Valuable
- Extremely dense urban boards with negligible special zone placement.
- Competitive tables designed around symmetry may minimize heavy Special Terrain zones.
- Missions focusing more on close-range gunfights or hacking rather than mobility.

### Pros of Terrain Skill
- Free +1" MOV effectively compresses Orders, letting the user cover more ground.
- Immunity to Difficult Terrain movement penalties is extremely impactful in large zones.
- Enhances objective play: reaching Consoles, moving Supply Boxes, or repositioning Specialists.

### Cons of Terrain Skill
- Irrelevant if no matching Terrain appears on the table.
- Dependent on table setup agreements between players.
- Less effective for static or primarily long-range troopers.

# [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
## Advice for Table Builders and Scenario Designers

- Adding Special Terrain zones increases the tactical relevance of Terrain and Terrain (Total).
- Mixing Terrain Types across quadrants makes different troops shine in different sections.
- Be mindful that too much Difficult Terrain can disproportionately benefit units with Terrain, potentially skewing balance.
