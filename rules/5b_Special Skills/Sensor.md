# Sensor

Special Skills 
 SHORT SKILL
Attack, Optional, Zone of Control. 

Hyper-developed senses and sophisticated tracking technology are the two sides of the Sensor coin. Either way, the bottom line is the same: the ability to uncover hidden threats in one's immediate surroundings.

- By declaring Sensor, the user may make a Normal WIP +6 Roll (without applying Range or Mimetism MODs) to simultaneously Discover all enemy Troopers, Weapons or pieces of Equipment in the Hidden Deployment or Camouflaged States, who are inside the user's Zone of Control.

- The user does not need LoF to a target, or to designate a target at all.

- Enemies with the Camouflage Special Skill cannot regain Camouflaged State inside the Zone of Control of the user.

- Sensor also grants its user, automatically and without making Rolls or spending Orders, a +6 MOD to WIP when declaring Discover against Camouflage Markers.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
• Sensor is one of the strongest anti-Hidden Information tools in N5 because it Discover-checks ALL valid targets inside ZoC with a single WIP+6 Roll. Since it is a Normal Roll, enemies cannot oppose it unless a rule specifically allows it (such as Sixth Sense allowing AROs only when attacked, which Sensor is not). 

• Sensor does not require LoF and does not require choosing a specific target. This makes it ideal for sweeping behind walls, under catwalks, around corners, and into smoke-blocked areas, provided the ZoC extends there.

• Hidden Deployment is explicitly canceled by Sensor. 

• Sensor bypasses the usual Discover step-by-step limitation (no “one attempt per trooper” sequencing). All eligible enemies are checked simultaneously with one Roll, which makes Sensor extremely order-efficient.

• Sensor ignores Range and Mimetism MODs. This is particularly relevant against units relying on Mimetism (-6) or extreme-range positioning. Since Discover MODs can otherwise accumulate heavily, this immunity to negative MODs gives Sensor unique reliability.

• Enemies with Camouflage cannot regain Camouflaged State if inside a Sensor user's ZoC. This creates persistent anti-recloaking zones that can force Camo specialists to withdraw if they wish to re-enter Marker State.

• Sensor provides an automatic +6 WIP bonus to standard Discover Rolls against Camouflage Markers (no Order, no Roll, always active). This can turn weak-WIP troops into extremely reliable spotters.

• Sensor provides no benefit against Impersonation beyond standard Discover bonuses (it does not grant MODs vs Impersonation and cannot mass-Discover IMP-1/IMP-2). It is strictly limited to Camouflage and Hidden Deployment.

• Sensor does not contest Stealth. 

• Sensor is best used in combination with units that can move safely into ZoC: fast Remotes, camo-hunters, or pieces with high mobility. 

• Sensor can be valuable in scenario play. Because many Classified Objectives involve interacting with the HVT or enemy specialists, eliminating Hidden Deployment threats early can prevent ambushes or area denial.

• Sensor does not break Marker State by itself until it succeeds. Declaring Sensor does nothing if the Roll fails, and no penalties apply to the enemy.

## Compared to a standard Discover:
  – Sensor is a Normal Roll, so the enemies cannot oppose it.
  – Sensor covers all eligible targets at once.
  – Sensor uses WIP+6 ignoring many MODs and lacks LoF requirements.
  – A standard Discover is more restrictive but can be done as an ARO, whereas Sensor may not always be available as an ARO due to Stealth blocking ZoC-based reactions.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
• Pros:
  + Extremely order-efficient mass revealing.
  + Ignores CAMO/Mimetism/Range Discover penalties.
  + Reveals Hidden Deployment—unique capability.
  + Creates a “no-recloaking” zone.
  + No LoF requirement; perfect for tight or blocked terrain.
  + Synergizes with fast mobile platforms

• Cons:
  - Requires entering ZoC, which may be dangerous.
  - Provides no direct attack—specialists must follow up.
  - Does not counter Impersonation directly.
  - Single Roll: if failed, entire sweep fails.

• General tactical advice:
  – Use Sensor early in the turn to break open the opponent’s shell game.
  – Combine Sensor with fast movement or coordinated repositioning.
  – Supporting attack pieces should be ready to immediately exploit revealed targets.
  – Prevent enemy infiltrators or skirmishers from re-entering Camo by locking them inside your ZoC.
  – Consider pairing Sensor units with attack REMs, warbands, or hackers who can capitalize on revealed enemies.
