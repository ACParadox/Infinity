# Warhorse

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Optional 

This soldier has survived years of active duty in the thick of the fight and has picked up more than a few tricks along the way.

- The user is not affected by the Loss of Lieutenant situation and remains Regular.

- The user is not affected by the Retreat! situation, remains Regular, and does not enter Retreat! State, acting normally until the end of the game.

- The user cannot enter Isolated State (caused by Ammunition, Hacking Programs, scenario special rules, etc.).

The Special Skill BS Attack (-X) has no effect against Troopers with the Warhorse Special Skill.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Interaction Notes
- Warhorse troopers continue contributing a Regular Order even if they are the last element of the army with no Lieutenant on the table.

### Warhorse vs Retreat!
- Functions identically to Courage in this context, but is superior because it also preserves Regular status, not just activation freedom.  

### Warhorse vs Isolated
- Prevents entering Isolated outright.  
- This is massive: Isolated normally removes a Trooper from the Order Pool and forces them to rely solely on their own Irregular Order.  
- Warhorse troopers remain fully operational even when struck by **E/M ammo**, **Jammers**, **Isolation-causing Hacking programs**, or **scenario effects** that inflict Isolation.  
- Note that this does NOT grant immunity to other IMM/Null states; only Isolated.

### Warhorse vs BS Attack (-X)
- All BS Attack (-3), (-6), etc. modifiers applied by enemy Special Skills are ignored.  
- This includes skills that impose negative FTF MODs through BS Attack labels.  
- It does NOT ignore Mimetism, Surprise Attack, Range penalties, or Cover; only the “BS Attack (-X)” type of MOD.

---

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Comparison With Similar Skills

### Warhorse vs Courage
**Similarities:**  
- Both ignore Retreat!.  

**Differences:**  
- Warhorse also ignores Loss of Lieutenant (Courage does not).  
- Warhorse prevents entering Isolated; Courage does not.  
- Warhorse negates BS Attack (-X) penalties; Courage does not.  
- Warhorse guarantees the Trooper stays Regular; Courage does nothing about Order type.  
**Conclusion:** Warhorse is strictly superior.


## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Pros and Cons

### Pros
• **Complete immunity to Isolated**, one of the most crippling states in Infinity.  
• **Unaffected by Retreat!** — continues fighting normally in endgame collapse situations.  
• **Unaffected by Loss of Lieutenant** — maintains Regular status and command stability.  
• **Ignores BS Attack (-X) penalties**, improving odds in certain firefights.  
• Provides psychological and tactical reliability: Warhorse units remain predictable even as the army falls apart.

### Cons
• **No immunity to other states** (Immobilized, Possessed, Targeted, Stunned, etc.).  
• **Does not protect against hacking effects other than Isolation**—programs dealing Damage or imposing IMM still work.  
• **Rule value is meta-dependent**: Warhorse is strongest in scenarios where Retreat, Loss of Lieutenant, or E/M weaponry are common.


## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Tactical Applications

### 1. Anchor Positions and Order Reliability
Warhorse Troopers are exceptional for holding key zones because they remain Regular even if the rest of the army collapses. Perfect for fireteam leaders, TAG pilots, or midfield anchors.

### 2. Anti-Hacking and Anti-E/M Resilience
E/M Mines, E/M Grenades, Jammers, and powerful Hackers cannot force Isolation, allowing aggressive play against factions that rely on Isolation as a control tool.

### 3. Crisis-Proof Late Game
Scenarios where you expect heavy losses—Annihilation, Domination, Firefight—benefit enormously from Warhorse units who continue scoring, moving, and fighting in the final rounds.
