# Journalist

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Optional 

The presence of a Journalist on the battlefield, transmitting only information authorized by Intelligence, always praises the work of the troops, catapulting some of them into the spotlight as heroes of the mass media, or just enhancing their reputation amongst their commanding officers.

- Troopers within the Zone of Control of any allied Troopers with the Journalist Special Skill apply a +3 MOD to their Guts Roll.

- Similarly, Troopers within the Zone of Control of any enemy Troopers with the Journalist Special Skill apply a -3 MOD to their Guts Roll.

## JOURNALIST AND CAMPAIGN RULES:

- If playing a campaign (rules available in the Infinity Daedalus Fall book), at the end of a scenario the player may: Apply a +3 MOD to their CUBEVAC Rolls. Apply a +3 MOD to their Promotion Roll.

- Restriction: In order to be able to apply these Campaign Effects, at the end of the scenario the Trooper possessing this Special Skill must be on the game table in a non- Null State.

- Restriction: If a player has multiple Journalists on the game table, their MODs to Guts Rolls and also to Campaign Rules are not cumulative.

- Apply a +3 MOD to their CUBEVAC Rolls.

- Apply a +3 MOD to their Promotion Roll.

## Interaction Notes

### Journalist and Guts Rolls
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- The +3 / –3 modifiers from Journalist affect only **Guts Rolls**, not Morale, Courage, or Dodge.
- Allied Journalist presence acts similarly to making your troops “more stable,” but unlike Courage, it **does not bypass** the Guts Roll—it only modifies the WIP Roll.
- Enemy Journalist presence can make your models *more likely to fall back* by imposing a –3, particularly important for units without Courage or Religious Troop.
- Religious Troops ignore Guts Rolls entirely by rule, but since they may *optionally force a WIP roll* to withdraw, the Journalist MOD would affect only that optional roll.

## Tactical Commentary

### Offensive Use
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Push a Journalist into the enemy’s ZoC bubble to destabilize their front line—units more likely to fail Guts and fall back, breaking suppressive fire or leaving objectives.
- Works best against armies with few Courage/Religious units (e.g., some merc forces).

### Defensive Use
- Keep a Journalist mid-table behind cover; friendly troops receive +3 to stay in place, which is valuable for:
  - Maintaining Suppressive Fire lanes  
  - Holding objectives  
  - Preventing involuntary retreat from valuable positions  

### Area Control
- Journalist effectively creates a 8" zone where morale is altered—similar to soft crowd-control.
- Good when combined with:
  - Defensive fireteams lacking Courage  
  - Troops staking objectives who want to stay in place
