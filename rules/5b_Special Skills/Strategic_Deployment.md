# Strategic Deployment

Special Skills
 DEPLOYMENT SKILL
Optional 

This Special Skill allows its owner and the Troopers belonging to their same Fireteam to apply the Forward Deployment Special Skill.

- During the Deployment Phase, the owner of this Special Skill must be the first member of the Fireteam to be deployed on the game table.

- The owner of this Special Skill must be deployed as the Team Leader of the Fireteam.

- During the Deployment Phase, first the owner of this Special Skill, and then the rest of their Fireteam, can be deployed as if they have the Forward Deployment (+4") Special Skill.

- If a scenario or tournament rule provides the Forward Deployment (+4") Special Skill to any member of the Fireteam, the owner of Strategic Deployment and the rest of the Fireteam can be deployed as if they have the Forward Deployment (+8") Special Skill.

# [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Purpose of Strategic Deployment
Strategic Deployment is a **Fireteam-wide deployment extender**, enabling the entire Fireteam to deploy farther forward on the table **as long as the bearer is the first deployed and is the Team Leader during deployment**.  
It effectively turns one specialist Trooper into a **force multiplier**, repositioning the start line of the entire Fireteam.

**Practical effect:**  
You cannot deploy the Fireteam leader far forward and then later decide to rearrange the order or structure of the Fireteam. Everything must be decided up front.
---

## Interaction: Strategic Deployment + Minelayer
- Minelayer follows Forward Deployment restrictions.  
- A Fireteam member with Minelayer can place a Mine up to the FD limit granted by Strategic Deployment.

## Interaction: Deployment Constraints
Strategic Deployment **does not override**:
- Exclusion Zones
- Terrain restrictions
- Scenario-specific deployment bans
- “Totally inside Deployment Zone” rules for Troopers without FD

It only extends lawful FD deployment *for the Fireteam*.

---

# [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Comparison With Similar Abilities

## Strategic Deployment vs. **Forward Deployment**
**FD** (individual)  
- Applies to one Trooper.  
- Does not require Fireteam membership.  
- Usually granted natively or by scenarios.
**SD:**
- Moves an entire Fireteam up the table.  
- Saves points over buying multiple FD units.  
- Strong synergy with close-range or mid-range teams.


## Strategic Deployment vs. **Infiltration / Airborne Deployment**
These are *superior* deployment skills with higher impact:
- Infiltration allows deployment across the midline.  
- AD allows off-table arrival.

**Strategic Deployment is weaker than both**, but:
- Works on multiple Troopers at once.  
- Is guaranteed and doesn’t require rolls.  
- Keeps Fireteams functioning and together.

# [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Tactical Advantages

## Benefits
- **Midfield pressure immediately**: FD (+8") Fireteams start closer to objectives.  
- **First-turn efficiency**: Shorter distances reduce Orders spent on movement.  
- **Board control**: Strong early angles and trades.  
- **Synergy with durable link leaders** that can push forward safely.

## Risks
- **Counter-deployment vulnerability**:  
  Moving your whole link forward gives the opponent obvious targets.
- **Template exposure**:  
  FD (+8") zones often contain fewer safe deployment spots.  

# [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Good Strategic Deployment Fireteams

**Ideal traits for exploiting SD:**
- Close or mid-range weapons (shotguns, breakers, marksman rifles).  
- Durable or high-BTS models benefiting from early positioning.  
- Troopers with Smoke or White Noise support.  
- Heavy short-range specialists (Engineers, Doctors, FOs).

---

# [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Deployment Tips
- Place the SD bearer somewhere central so the team spreads efficiently.  
- Use terrain edges to limit how many enemy AROs can punish the advanced position.  
- Avoid bunching: Forward-deploying Fireteams often clump due to lack of space—spread them as much as coherency permits.  
- If expecting strong ARO pieces, deploy *just* inside FD (+4”) rather than maxing out.
