# Aerial

> **N5.3 change (September 2026):** Troopers with the **Aerial** Special Skill **do not activate Weapons with the Boost Trait**.

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Obligatory 

Troopers possessing this Special Skill have flight capabilities. While in combat, Aerial Troopers stay as close to the ground as possible to avoid being an easy target.

- Restriction: While this Skill is active, the Trooper cannot be in Prone State or Engaged State.

- Restriction: While this Skill is Active, the Trooper is never in Silhouette contact with other Models, Objectives, scenery items, or game elements, and vice versa, even if physically touching them.

- Restriction: Troopers with this Special Skill cannot declare Cautious Movement.

- Troopers with the Aerial Special Skill do not activate Weapons with the Boost Trait.

Unconscious State Automatic Special Skills and Automatic Equipment have no effect while in Unconscious State.

Aerial and Guard:

The Guard Special Skill may not be used against Troopers with the Aerial Skill, unless they are in Unconscious State.

The aerial movement of Troopers with this Skill is represented using the Super-Jump and Terrain (Total) Special Skills, which are listed in their Unit Profiles. Similarly, the fact that these Troopers are harder to hit due to their higher speed and the height they can fly at is represented by Mimetism, or other Special Skills.

In addition, it is impossible to enter Silhouette contact with these Troopers unless they are in Unconscious State, so if they are not Unconscious any Wounds they have received can only be removed by using a GizmoKit.

## FAQs

## Aerial — tactical interaction notes

Aerial is an official Infinity N5 Special Skill. The notes below discuss common tactical consequences of the actual rule rather than using “aerial” as an informal category.

### ARO interactions
- Aerial Troopers can create unusual LoF angles through Super-Jump and related movement tools on their profile.
- Their inability to enter Silhouette contact while Aerial is active changes how they interact with melee threats, objectives, scenery, and some reactive weapons.
- Under N5.3, they do not trigger Weapons with the Boost Trait.

### Partial Cover and elevated lines
- Cover is still determined normally from the relevant LoF and scenery interaction; being Aerial does not by itself remove Partial Cover.
- Elevated or unusual firing angles can nevertheless change whether the scenery actually provides Partial Cover to either participant.

### Recovery and Silhouette contact
- Because an active Aerial Trooper cannot enter Silhouette contact with other Models, normal Silhouette-contact recovery interactions may be unavailable while the Skill is active.
- The rule text specifically notes GizmoKit as the way to remove Wounds from a non-Unconscious Aerial Trooper when Silhouette contact cannot be established.

### Practical notes
- Aerial movement can open firing lanes unavailable to ground-bound pieces, but may also expose the Trooper to additional AROs.
- Check the Trooper's actual Unit Profile for Super-Jump, Terrain, Mimetism, and other abilities that represent its flight characteristics; do not infer additional rules from the Aerial label alone.
