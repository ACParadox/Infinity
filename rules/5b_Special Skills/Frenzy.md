# Frenzy

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Obligatory, States Phase. 

The Trooper's urge to kill is barely contained. Once he gets a first taste of blood, he will become a whirlwind of death.

- Directly inflict at least one Wound on an Enemy Trooper, or cause them to enter Dead State.

- In the States Phase of the Player Turn where the Requirements have been met, the Trooper gains the following Special Skills and retains them for the remainder of the game: Impetuous Limited Cover

- When Frenzy Troopers gain the Impetuous Special Skill, they cancel any Marker State they are in ( Camouflaged, Impersonation...), and any other States that say so. These Troopers cannot re-enter those States.

- Impetuous

- Limited Cover

The Requirements for this Special Skill demand that the Frenzy Trooper must inflict the Wound directly on the target, or cause them to enter Dead State. The Trooper’s Deployable Weapons are not taken into account for the fulfillment of the Requirements.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

### FRENZY — INTERACTIONS AND UNOFFICIAL EXPLANATIONS

General Understanding
Frenzy is a delayed drawback that turns a trooper Impetuous after they inflict a Wound or kill an enemy. The key impact is losing the ability to retain Marker States and becoming subject to Impetuous movement or Limited Cover penalties. Frenzy is usually priced as a points discount on the profile, making units cheaper but riskier.

### FRENZY —  INTERACTIONS WITH OTHER RULES

• Marker States (Camouflaged, Impersonation, Holoecho, etc.)
  Frenzy cancelling into Impetuous means that once the trooper becomes Impetuous, they are forced out of any Marker State and cannot re-enter it. This makes Frenzy a major drawback for units relying on stealth or surprise.

• Fireteams
  Not real relation with fireteam other that if the troops activated during Impetuous Phase, he comes out of the Fireteam (or break the FT if the FT leader)

• Limited Cover
  Gaining Limited Cover means the trooper no longer benefits from the full Cover bonuses in the Active Turn. They still get Cover in the Reactive Turn. This matters most for gunfighters who normally rely on high burst in the Active Turn.

• Retreat!
  Frenzy-triggered Impetuous is disabled during Retreat, so Frenzy has no effect at that stage of the game.

• Deployable Weapons and Requirements
  Deployables do not count toward fulfilling Frenzy requirements. Only direct attacks causing a Wound or Dead State matter.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### FRENZY — TACTICAL CONSEQUENCES

• When to “Allow” Frenzy to Trigger
  You control when you inflict a Wound. A Frenzy troop can purposely avoid finishing an enemy in the Active Turn if staying in Cover next round is more important than the kill.

• Exposing the Trooper
  Once Frenzy triggers and the model becomes Impetuous, they may be forced to move aggressively if the Impetuous Order is used. On some profiles, you may choose NOT to activate them during the Impetuous Phase to avoid danger.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### FRENZY — COMPARISONS WITH OTHER RULES

Frenzy vs. Impetuous (Native Impetuous)
• Frenzy: delayed, only after the first Wound inflicted.
• Impetuous: always active, no discount after the first kill.
Pros Frenzy: You can play cautiously early game;
Cons Frenzy: Once active, you’re stuck with Limited Cover and Impetuous the rest of the game.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### FRENZY — PROS / CONS OF FRENZY AS A TROOP TRAIT

Pros
• Lower point cost for the same stats and gear.
• Does not activate until after causing a Wound, letting you maintain Cover early.

Cons
• Huge penalty for solo pieces relying on Cover in the Active Turn.
• Bad synergy with Marker States—losing Camouflage or Impersonation can cripple the troop’s role.
• Movement restrictions (if Impetuous Order is used) may expose the model.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### FRENZY — USAGE AND RECOMMENDATIONS

• Attack Pieces:
  Evaluate when to finish a target. If exposing yourself in next turn is dangerous, delay the kill.

• Camouflage / Impersonation Units:
  Avoid Frenzy unless you intend to break your Marker State anyway. Losing Marker State permanently is a steep cost.

• Long-Range Shooters:
  Limited Cover in Active Turn is very punishing. Consider using terrain, smoke, or careful movement to compensate.

• Cheap Irregular Attackers:
  Once Frenzy activates, combine the Impetuous Order with their Irregular Order for high activity at the cost of safety.

### FRENZY — SUMMARY
Its biggest downsides relate to loss of Marker States and loss of Active Turn Cover. Proper management—deciding when to get the kill—turns Frenzy from a liability into an economy advantage, especially for Fireteam-based lists.
