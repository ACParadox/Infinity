# Religious Troop

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Obligatory 

The beliefs and teachings of their leaders have made these soldiers into loyal warriors with an unshakable faith. The ardor of these zealots is enough to help them stand firm in battle even when their comrades have given up hope.

- The user automatically passes all their Guts Rolls without requiring a Roll. This means that the user is forced to hold their position and cannot withdraw or seek cover after surviving an Attack.

- However, if the player wants the user to withdraw or gain Cover after surviving an Attack, then they may make a WIP Roll. If the Roll is a success, the user can apply the Effects of failing the Guts Roll.

- The user is not affected by the Retreat! situation and does not enter the Retreat! State, acting normally until the end of the game.

- Restriction: In a Retreat! situation, the user cannot voluntarily abandon the game table unless the scenario rules indicate otherwise.

- The user is affected by the Loss of Lieutenant situation, including in a Retreat! situation.


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Comparisons & Similar Rules

### Religious Troop vs. Courage  
**Similarity:** Both modify Guts behavior.  
**Difference:**  
- *Courage* gives the option to ignore Guts and stay.  
- *Religious* prohibits fallback and removes choice.  
**Pros Religious:** More predictable in that they never retreat.  
**Cons Religious:** Cannot reposition defensively when wanted.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Pros and Cons of Religious Troop

**Pros**
• Steadfast defenders — excellent on suppressive fire or on ARO duty.  
• Never retreat from key firelanes; maintain board control.  
• Predictable behavior for tactical planning.  
• Can remain guarding objectives even under intense fire.

**Cons**
• Cannot retreat when it would be safer.  
• Vulnerable to being pinned in poor range bands or LOF.  
• Combined with Impetuous or Frenzy, often overextends into danger.  
• Fireteam members may break formation if forced to stay while others reposition.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Tactical Use Cases

• **Ideal for Static ARO Units**  
Troops holding rooftops, long firelanes, or objective zones benefit: they stay exactly where you expect, even under heavy fire.

• **Risky When Advancing**  
Any unit that moves forward risks being locked in place by its own Religious nature.

• **Strong on High-ARM/High-BTS Platforms**  
These units can afford to resist staying exposed longer.

• **Pairs Well with Smoke, MSV, and Range Dominance**  
Since they won’t retreat, ensure they fight on your terms.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Common Traps and Mistakes

• **Improper Firelane Choice**  
A Religious Trooper left in a disadvantageous position *cannot fix its own exposure* via Guts.  
This is a primary cause of unnecessary losses.

• **Fireteam Anchors Facing Multiple Attack Vectors**  
Religious models sometimes break coherency because they cannot reposition even when the Fireteam wishes to withdraw after Resolution.
