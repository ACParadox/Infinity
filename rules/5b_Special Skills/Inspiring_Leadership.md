# Inspiring Leadership

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Obligatory 

This Special Skill ties the behavior of entire armies to the user's discipline and state of mind.

- If the user belongs to a Fireteam where all members have the Regular Training Characteristic listed in their Unit Profiles, apply a +2 inch bonus to the Team Leader’s Zone of Control when making Coherency Checks for the Fireteam.

- Restriction: This bonus is not cumulative with any bonus to the Team Leader’s Zone of Control.

- If the user is the Lieutenant of the Army, is on the game table, and not in a Null State ( Unconscious, Dead, Sepsitorized …), the following effects are applied: All Troopers in the Army List that provide Orders: Possess the Regular Training Characteristic. Have the Courage Special Skill. Troopers with the Religious Troop Special Skill don’t apply this Effect. You may declare a Coordinated Order that includes the user by spending only the Special Lieutenant Order, without expenditure of Command Tokens or extra Orders. The army is not affected by the Retreat! rule, and never enters a Retreat! situation.

- If the user becomes the Lieutenant of the Army (for example via the Chain of Command Special Skill or after a Loss of Lieutenant situation), the previous Effects of Inspiring Leadership apply immediately after the user has been appointed as Lieutenant.

## 1. Fireteam Interaction
- Inspiring Leadership provides +2" to the Fireteam Leader’s Zone of Control for Coherency *only if* the user is in the Fireteam and *only if* all Fireteam members have Regular Training. This effectively makes maintaining coherency easier, especially for larger Fireteams.
- This bonus is not cumulative with any other ZoC increases (for example from Fireteam: Duo bonuses, special equipment, or scenario effects).
- Inspiring Leadership’s Fireteam bonus is independent from its Lieutenant effects. A non-Lieutenant Inspiring Leadership model still provides the Fireteam ZoC bonus.

## 2. Order-Related Effects
- When the Inspiring Leadership user is the Lieutenant and active on the table, every Trooper that generates an Order becomes Regular and gains Courage, unless they have Religious Troop (Religious Troops ignore these benefits).

- This can dramatically alter the army’s efficiency by converting Irregular models into Regular ones, greatly increasing the Order Pool.

- Because the “Regular Training Characteristic” effect is applied to all Troopers that provide Orders, this can cause Order reallocation across Combat Groups during the Tactical Phase.

- Coordinated Orders including the Lieutenant can be declared by spending only the Lieutenant Special Order. This effectively allows a “free” Coordinated Order each Turn without consuming Command Tokens.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
## 3. Retreat! Interaction
- While the Inspiring Leadership Lieutenant is alive, deployed, and not Null, the army never enters Retreat!. This includes preventing automatic Retreat! at the start of the Active Turn.
- If the Lieutenant with Inspiring Leadership enters a Null State during the Tactical Phase, the army immediately loses the immunity and the standard Retreat! check applies.

## 5. Interactions with Religious Troop
- Religious Troop never receives the Courage effect nor the conversion to Regular Training. They retain their Religious behavior in Guts and Orders.
- A Fireteam containing Religious models can still benefit from the +2" ZoC bonus if all members have Regular Training *printed* in their profiles; the Religious restriction only affects the global Lieutenant-based effects.

## 7. Practical Tips & Example Uses
- Armies with many Irregular models benefit greatly, often gaining 3–6 extra Regular Orders per Turn.
- Combined with a defensive Lieutenant position, Inspiring Leadership can anchor an entire list’s order economy.
- The +2" Fireteam coherency bonus subtly enhances wide sweeps, room clearing, or multi-level movement inside buildings.
- Using the “free” Coordinated Order early in the Turn allows repositioning multiple pieces with minimal Order expenditure.

## 8. Risks and Limitations
- The army becomes extremely dependent on the Lieutenant’s survival; a single unlucky Crit can collapse order efficiency.
- Coherency bonus does not help teams containing Airborne Deployment, Marker State, Decoy, or Suppressive Fire models, because these cannot be Fireteam members according to Fireteam rules.

- Coordinated Orders still obey the rule that Fireteam members cannot participate in a Coordinated Order.
- Religious Troops remain Irregular unless they already have Regular Training printed; Inspiring Leadership cannot convert them.
