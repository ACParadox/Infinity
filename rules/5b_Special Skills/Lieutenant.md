# Lieutenant

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Private Information, Obligatory. 

The user of this Special Skill is the commanding officer of the combat force deployed by the player.

- The user of this Special Skill has a Lieutenant Special Order.

- If, during the Tactical Phase of the Turn, the Lieutenant is in Isolated State or any Null State ( Unconscious, Dead, etc.) or has not been deployed on the gaming table, the Loss of Lieutenant rule will be applied.

- This Special Skill remains active even when its owner is in a Null State, until a new Lieutenant is appointed. Once that happens, the owner of this Special Skill loses it.

- When a new Lieutenant is appointed (due to Loss of Lieutenant, Chain of Command...), they gain the Lieutenant Special Skill, with no additional abilities.

- In the Unit Profile, this Special Skill may have additional abilities listed. Some examples are: +1 Command Token. The player that takes this Trooper as their Lieutenant will have one extra Command Token for the match. +1 Order. This indicates that this Lieutenant has two Special Lieutenant Orders each Turn instead of one.

- +1 Command Token. The player that takes this Trooper as their Lieutenant will have one extra Command Token for the match.

- +1 Order. This indicates that this Lieutenant has two Special Lieutenant Orders each Turn instead of one.

- The presence of a Trooper with the Lieutenant Special Skill in the player's Army List is obligatory.

- The player's Army List cannot include more than one Trooper with this Special Skill.

## Lieutenant - Mnemonica

- The user may transfer his own WIP Attribute value and the Mnemonica Special Skill to another Trooper in the same army. That Trooper must be on the battlefield in the form of a Model or Marker and have either a Cube or the REM Troop Type. If the user has the Lieutenant Special Skill this is also transferred, including any additional abilities and MODs such as (+1 Order).

## Lieutenant - NCO

- Allows the user to replace any Special Lieutenant Orders with Tactical Orders and to use them.

## Lieutenant - Strategos

- In order to benefit from this Level of Strategos, the user must be the army's Lieutenant.

- At the beginning of your Active Turn, during your Order Count, replace any Special Lieutenant Orders with Regular Orders, adding them to the Order Pool of the Lieutenant's Combat Group.


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Lieutenants with extra abilities—such as *Lieutenant (+1 Order)*—are especially impactful because they grant additional economy without requiring more Troopers. However, these profiles often cost more points or have drawbacks (e.g., being more obvious, or only available on high-value targets). Balancing the advantage of increased Orders with the danger of a predictable Lieutenant is a core part of Infinity list building.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Some factions rely heavily on aggressive Lieutenants that contribute actively (e.g., heavy infantry or TAG Lieutenants), while others prefer hidden or bunker-style Lieutenants who never leave safety. Aggressive Lieutenants can be devastating but risky—once exposed, they can be isolated or killed, causing immediate Loss of Lieutenant.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Using Chain of Command dramatically changes Lieutenant risk management. With Chain of Command, you can afford to run an aggressive Lieutenant—or even intentionally expose or sacrifice them—because the backup will immediately take over and avoid Loss of Lieutenant. Without Chain of Command, losing the Lieutenant at a critical moment can cost the entire game.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Lieutenant interactions to consider:  
- **Isolation effects** (e.g., *Oblivion*) can cause a pseudo-Loss of Lieutenant, as an Isolated Lieutenant does not contribute their Order and triggers Loss of Lieutenant during the next Tactical Phase.  
- **Mnemonica** allows Combined Army players to circumvent Lieutenant hunting almost entirely by transferring both WIP and Lieutenant status to a safer Trooper.  
- **Camouflage and Holomask** are some of the strongest defensive tools for Lieutenants because they obscure identity and positioning.  
- **NCO** creates flexibility because it lets your list benefit from the Lieutenant Order without putting the Lieutenant at risk.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Comparison: **Bunker Lieutenant vs. Active Lieutenant**  
- *Bunker Lieutenant (hidden, protected)*  
  - **Pros:** Harder to assassinate, minimizes risk of LoL, enables stable game plans.  
  - **Cons:** Lieutenant Order often goes unused unless NCO is present; less flexible; predictable placement.  
- *Active Lieutenant (frontline, attacking)*  
  - **Pros:** Direct offensive value, can leverage powerful profiles, excellent for early pressure.  
  - **Cons:** High assassination risk; losing them can collapse your Turn; demands Chain of Command for safety.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Choosing a Lieutenant is less about raw stats and more about overall army plan: offense, defense, deception, and redundancy. Well-built lists have either (1) a very safe Lieutenant, or (2) a reliable way to recover when the Lieutenant is lost. Poor Lieutenant planning is one of the most common causes of losing games by Turn 2.
