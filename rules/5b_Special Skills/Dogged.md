# Dogged

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Optional 

Some soldiers show such utter disregard for their own lives that even the most crippling injuries will not keep them from reaching their goal.

- The Trooper enters Unconscious State, and his player declares that he is activating the Dogged Special Skill, whether in the Active or Reactive Turn.

- The Unconscious State of Troopers using the Dogged Special Skill has the following special rules: Instead of placing an Unconscious Token next to the Trooper, place a Dogged Token. To activate Dogged, the player must announce its use when the Trooper enters Unconscious State. Allows the Trooper to ignore the effects and Null Label of Unconscious State, treating it as Normal State instead, but only for the rest of that Player Turn. If a Trooper activates this Special Skill, then in the States Phase of that Player Turn the Trooper automatically enters Dead State and is removed from the game table. If the Trooper receives another Wound, they enter Dead State directly and are removed from the game table.

- Once activated, Dogged prevents the Trooper from being healed (by Special Skills or Equipment such as Doctor, MediKit, Regeneration...).

- Troopers with Remote Presence, the STR Attribute, and Dogged may activate this Special Skill when they enter any level of Unconscious State, but not if they are already in Unconscious State.

- This Special Skill does not interfere with any Automatic Special Skills or Automatic Equipment, which will function normally.

- Instead of placing an Unconscious Token next to the Trooper, place a Dogged Token.

- To activate Dogged, the player must announce its use when the Trooper enters Unconscious State.

- Allows the Trooper to ignore the effects and Null Label of Unconscious State, treating it as Normal State instead, but only for the rest of that Player Turn.

- If the Trooper receives another Wound, they enter Dead State directly and are removed from the game table.

Shock Ammunition cancels the Unconscious State of Troopers using this Special Skill, and they pass directly to Dead State.


### [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]  
### Interaction Notes & Clarifications

**Dogged vs. Shock Ammunition**
- Shock normally sends 1-Wound VITA troops directly to Dead, skipping Unconscious.
- If a Trooper would normally *enter Unconscious* from the hit (e.g., multi-Wound troops or STR models), they can still activate Dogged.
- 1-Wound VITA troops killed outright by Shock **cannot** activate Dogged—because they never enter Unconscious State.

**Dogged vs. Continuous Damage**
- If a failed Continuous Damage Saving Roll causes you to enter Unconscious, you *may* activate Dogged.
- Additional failed rolls cause more Wounds, so a Dogged Trooper failing multiple rolls goes Dead immediately.

**Dogged vs. Doctor / Engineer / Healing**
- Once Dogged is activated, the Trooper **cannot be healed**, even this same Order.

**Dogged vs. Regeneration**
- Regeneration cannot function because Dogged prohibits healing and the Trooper dies in the States Phase anyway.

**Dogged vs. Remote Presence**
- STR/Remote Presence Troopers have two Unconscious levels; they may activate Dogged upon entering either level.
- Once activated, they die in the States Phase exactly like VITA troops.

**Dogged vs. Coup de Grâce**
- Per Unconscious rules, Coup de Grâce cannot be used against Dogged units. They must be killed by normal attacks.

**Dogged vs. ARO**
- Dogged works identically whether triggered in Active or Reactive Turn.
- Because it lasts only until the end of the Player Turn in which it was activated, a Trooper that activates Dogged in the opponent’s turn will die at the end of *their* States Phase—before you get your next Active Turn.

**Dogged vs. “Cannot be activated if already Unconscious”**
- If a Trooper is already Unconscious (e.g., dropped earlier in game and healed to Unconscious again), they cannot retro-activate Dogged. It must happen the *instant* Unconscious is entered.

### [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]  
### Practical Tactical Applications

**Best Uses**
- Push last-inch objectives: grab, activate, or contest for one critical Order.
- Trading pieces: survive long enough to unload a final Burst on an exposed threat.
- ARO denial: force opponent to spend more Orders to finish the Dogged model.
- Suppressive Fire anchor: enter Unconscious, declare Dogged, remain in Suppression until the end of the Turn.

**Cautions**
- Dogged Troopers are “living on borrowed time”: they *will* die at the next States Phase.
- Reactively activating Dogged guarantees the Trooper dies before your next active Turn.
- Vulnerable to single additional points of damage—no margin for error.

---

### [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]  
### Pros / Cons of Dogged

**Pros**
- Strong short-term durability spike.
- Enables suicide missions, button-pushing, and trading.
- Allows the Trooper to act at full performance for the rest of the Turn.
- Works even on STR/Remote Presence troops.

**Cons**
- Guaranteed death at States Phase.
- Cannot be healed once activated.
- Any extra Wound causes immediate death.
- Poor synergy with units designed to be durable over multiple turns.
- Reactive activation often means no more Active Turn for the Trooper.

---

### [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]  
### Comparison: Dogged vs. No Wound Incapacitation (NWI)

**Similarities**
- Both let you ignore the effects and Null label of Unconscious.
- Both allow acting normally after taking the “last” Wound.

**Differences**
- **Duration**:  
  - Dogged lasts *one Player Turn* and then kills you.  
  - NWI lasts until healed or you suffer another Wound.
- **Healing**:  
  - Dogged forbids healing.  
  - NWI can still be healed to return to full Unconscious/Wound cycle.
- **Cost**:  
  - Dogged units are cheaper in points.  
  - NWI units cost more because they provide lasting durability.
- **Risk**:  
  - Dogged = guaranteed self-destruction.  
  - NWI = persistent extra life until killed.

**Which is better?**
- Dogged is best for aggressive, disposable warbands and attack pieces.  
- NWI is best for expensive elite units who need sustained presence.
