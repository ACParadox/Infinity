# Exrah

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Obligatory

Special Skill specific to Exrah Troopers that determines what happens when these aliens enter Unconscious State.

- If a Trooper with this Special Skill enters Unconscious State, they automatically pass directly to Dead State, without receiving an extra Wound, and must be removed from the game table.

- Therefore the Trooper cannot be healed in any way (through the use of Special Skills or Equipment such as Doctor, MediKit, Regeneration...).

- This Special Skill remains active even when its owner is in a Null State.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

## Rule Summary (Official Text Recap)
Troopers with the Exrah Special Skill:
- Immediately go to **Dead State** upon entering **Unconscious State**.
- Cannot be healed or recovered by **Doctor, MediKit, Engineer, Regeneration**, or any similar rule.
- This effect still applies even while the trooper is in any **Null State**.

Exrah is absolute in its prohibition of recovery. Any effect that would normally:
- Cancel Unconscious,
- Restore Wounds/Structure,
- Allow a roll to stand back up,
…is automatically invalid because Exrah **converts Unconscious directly into Dead**.

This notably overrides:
- **Doctor**  
- **MediKit** (both user-rolled and target-rolled versions)  
- **Engineer** when applied to STR models  
- **Regeneration**  
- **No Wound Incapacitation** won’t trigger if it requires “entering Unconscious” before activating, because Exrah replaces that step.

### Interaction: Dogged / No Wound Incapacitation
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Many Special Skills that “activate when entering Unconscious” cannot activate for Exrah.  
Because Exrah skips Unconscious and proceeds straight to Dead:
- **Dogged** cannot trigger.  
- **NWI** cannot trigger.  
- Anything requiring the activation step “as you enter Unconscious” simply never gets an opportunity.

## Comparisons to Similar Race/Traits

### Exrah vs **Shasvastii**
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- **Shasvastii** convert Unconscious into a special embryo state that stays on the table, counts for points, and preserves scoring.  
- **Exrah** remove the model outright, losing all presence and scoring utility.
**Pros (Exrah):** None mechanically.  
**Cons:** Far more fragile; cannot protect objective points; cannot stall enemy scoring.


## Strategic Notes — Playing With or Against Exrah

**For Exrah Players**
- Treat Exrah units as “glass cannons”—commit when advantage is overwhelming.  
- Avoid attrition trades; every wound is catastrophic.  
- Use positioning, range dominance, and disposable movement pieces to protect them.

**Against Exrah**
- Any successful wound can permanently remove key pieces.  
- Shock ammo provides no special benefit—they already die on Unconscious.  
- Focused fire and forcing multiple Saving Rolls is extremely effective.
