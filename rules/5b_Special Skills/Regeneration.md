# Regeneration

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
States Phase, Optional. 

This Special Skill represents the capacity of certain Troopers to heal from damage they have received, by removing Wounds.

- Automatic during the States Phase, if the player decides to use this Special Skill.

- During the States Phase, the user makes a Normal PH Roll: If the Roll is a success, the Trooper removes 1 Wound, cancelling the Unconscious State if applicable. If the Roll fails, the Trooper receives 1 additional Wound.

- This Special Skill remains active even when its owner is in a Null State.

- If the Roll is a success, the Trooper removes 1 Wound, cancelling the Unconscious State if applicable.

- If the Roll fails, the Trooper receives 1 additional Wound.

If the Trooper receives a Wound, the player places a Regeneration Token beside the Model, as a reminder to perform the check in the next States Phase.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

General Concept
Regeneration allows a Trooper to attempt to remove 1 Wound in the States Phase by making a PH Roll; success heals, failure inflicts a Wound. This can cancel Unconscious. Because failure causes damage, the skill is a risk-reward mechanic.

## INTERACTIONS

• With Unconscious:
  Regeneration is one of the few ways to self-revive. Since it is Automatic and allowed even in Null States, a Trooper can attempt to stand back up without outside help.
  However, failure while Unconscious usually sends the Trooper to Dead, making this still risky.

• With Shock Ammunition:
  Shock that kills a 1-VITA Trooper immediately sends them to Dead; such a Trooper never becomes Unconscious, so Regeneration cannot trigger on following States Phases. Troopers with more than 1 VITA or protection from Shock (e.g., Immunity) retain access to Regeneration.
  Practical effect: Regeneration is drastically less useful on 1-Wound VITA Troops unless they also have Immunity (Shock).

• With Healing Skills (Doctor / Engineer / MediKit / GizmoKit):
  These work before the States Phase if an Order is spent. Regeneration is “free” but risky; healing attempts are more controlled but cost Orders.
  Using a Doctor/Engineer before the States Phase prevents the need for a Regeneration check at all.

• With States that allow Automatic Skills:
  States like Retreat!, Targeted, Sepsitorized do not prevent Automatic Skills. Regeneration continues to function.

## TACTICAL PROS

• Free self-healing:
  Regeneration costs no Orders, making it valuable on troops far from support or in difficult positions.

• Threat of revival:
  Opponents may over-invest to ensure a kill, changing their target priority or spending more Orders to finish off a Regenerating unit.

• Particularly strong on:
  – High-PH troops.
  – Multi-Wound units.
  – Troopers with Immunity (Shock).

## TACTICAL CONS

• High risk of instant death:
  Failing the PH Roll while Unconscious normally kills the Trooper. Low-PH models have very poor odds.

• Shock vulnerability:
  Any 1-Wound VITA Trooper is effectively denied use of Regeneration if hit by Shock.

• Predictable timing:
  Regeneration only triggers in the States Phase, so you cannot rely on it to stand up mid-turn for ARO or board control.

## COMPARISONS

Regeneration vs. NWI:
• Regeneration offers actual healing but risks death.
• NWI provides guaranteed activity for one extra wound but does not heal.
• Regeneration is stronger if it succeeds, weaker (and deadlier) if it fails.

Regeneration vs. Dogged:
• Dogged guarantees one more Turn of performance but guarantees death at the States Phase.
• Regeneration gambles on survival beyond the current turn.
• Dogged is safe but temporary; Regeneration is risky but long-term.

Regeneration vs. Doctor/Engineer:
• Doctor/Engineer: controlled, requires Orders, requires proximity.
• Regeneration: free, automatic, but risky.
• Use Regeneration when support models are unavailable; use Doctors/Engineers when reliability is needed.

Regeneration vs. Protheion:
• Protheion heals on offense via CC; Regeneration heals passively in States Phase.
• Protheion is more reliable in melee dominance; Regeneration is defensive and automatic.

## ADDITIONAL UNOFFICIAL CLARITY

• Automatic use is optional:
  You decide each States Phase whether to attempt Regeneration. Skipping an attempt avoids risking death.

• Enemy finishing attacks:
  Because Regeneration threatens revival, opponents often perform Coup de Grâce or Shock attacks to deny it; this pressure is a strategic effect.
