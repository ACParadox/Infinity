# Sixth Sense   

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Optional 

Troopers with this Special Skill have an uncanny ability to sense imminent threats. This Sixth Sense is either an instinctive skill, or the result of a pseudo-AI designed for threat detection and appraisal that senses immediate dangers. Troopers with Sixth Sense are able to perceive the aggression level and hostile intent of their enemies without needing to see them, allowing them to respond swiftly to any attack.

- Allows the user to respond to Attacks (and only Attacks) directed at them by enemies outside their LoF. For the purposes of drawing Line of Fire to the attackers, the user has a 360° LoF arc, and if they are in Engaged State, they can draw LoF to attackers outside their Close Combat.

- If the user is the target of a BS Attack through a Zero Visibility Zone, they ignore the -6 MOD from the resulting Poor Visibility Zone.

- If the user declares Dodge or Reset, no negative MODs are applied, with the exception of the -6 PH MOD for Immobilized-A State, the -3 WIP MOD for Immobilized-B State, and the -9 WIP MOD for Isolated State.

The Stealth Special Skill is not effective against Troopers with Sixth Sense.

## Sixth Sense FAQs

Q: How do Sixth Sense, Zero Visibility Zones and Multipectral Visor Level 1 interact?

A: If the Trooper with Multispectral Visor Level 1 and Sixth Sense is the target of a BS Attack through a Zero Visibility Zone, they ignore the -6 MOD for drawing LoF through the Zero Visibility Zone.

## 1. Key Interaction Notes

### 1.1 Responding to Attacks from Outside LoF
Sixth Sense grants the user a 360º reaction arc **only when they are the target of an Attack**.  
This means:
- You cannot use it to ARO against movement or non-attack skills outside your LoF.
- You only gain the 360º LoF for the purpose of declaring your reacting Skill, not for Discovering or declaring unrelated AROs.

**Practical effect:** A trooper may Dodge or shoot back even when attacked from behind, but not when an enemy simply moves behind them without attacking.

---

### 1.2 Interactions with Stealth
Stealth is **ineffective** against Sixth Sense.

This means:
- Entering or acting in the user’s Zone of Control will always provoke AROs from the Sixth Sense user.
- The Sixth Sense user may choose to Dodge even if they had no LoF and the enemy used Stealth to avoid it.

### 1.3 Zero Visibility Zones (Smoke, Eclipse, White Noise)
Sixth Sense users **ignore the -6 Poor Visibility penalty** when attacked **through Smoke** (or other Zero Visibility Zones), but **only when they are the target of the BS Attack**.  
They still cannot draw LoF normally to attack into or through a Zero Visibility Zone unless another rule grants that capability.

**Meaning:**
- Sixth Sense does NOT allow attacking through Smoke.
- It only improves reaction rolls and removes the attack penalty when a Sixth Sense user is attacked through Smoke.

---
### 1.4 Dodge and Reset MOD immunity
Sixth Sense removes negative MODs to Dodge or Reset, except those specific to Immobilized-A, Immobilized-B, and Isolated.

This does **not** increase values; it simply removes penalties such as:
- Suppressive Fire Dodge penalties  
- Poor Visibility Zone Dodge penalties (when applicable)  
---

### 1.5 Engaged State and 360º LoF
If Engaged, a Sixth Sense user may react to attacks from outside the Close Combat.  
This includes:
- Dodging  
- Resetting  
- Declaring certain legal CC Skills when permitted  

They **cannot** attack the distant shooter with a BS Attack if Engaged unless another rule specifically allows breaking engagement or performing a specific BS Attack.

---
## 2. Comparison With Similar Abilities

### 2.1 Sixth Sense vs. Combat Instinct

**Pros of Sixth Sense:**
- 360º ARO arc **against attacks**
- Ignores Smoke penalty when attacked
- Removes negative Dodge/Reset penalties
- Counters Stealth inside ZoC

**Pros of Combat Instinct:**
- Protects against Surprise Attack  
- Useful even in Active Turn  
- Always active regardless of LoF or ZoC

**Summary:**  
Sixth Sense is broader defensively but Reactive-only and attack-triggered.  
Combat Instinct is narrower but always applicable.

---

### 2.2 Sixth Sense vs. 360º Visor
**360º Visor:** Always provides 360º LoF for all purposes.  
**Sixth Sense:** Provides 360º LoF **only when reacting to an Attack**.

**Pros of 360º Visor:**
- Works in Active and Reactive turns
- Works against movement, Discover, and all ARO triggers

**Pros of Sixth Sense:**
- Provides Dodge/Reset MOD immunity
- Allows ignoring Smoke penalties when attacked
- Counters Stealth

**Summary:**  
360º Visor is comprehensive situational awareness.  
Sixth Sense is conditional but adds powerful reaction benefits.

---

## 3. Common Interaction Cases (Unofficial Clarification)

### Case A — “Can a Sixth Sense user Discover through Smoke?”
**No.** Sixth Sense does not grant LoF through Smoke.  
It only removes the penalty **when being attacked**.

### Case B — “Enemy moves in my back arc without attacking. Do I get AROs?”
**No.** Sixth Sense is not triggered without an Attack.

### Case C — “Enemy uses Stealth and ends movement inside ZoC.”
Sixth Sense user **can ARO**, because Stealth does not work against them.

### Case D — “Attacker shoots from Smoke at max range. Do I avoid the -6 Smoke penalty AND range MODs?”
You ignore only the Smoke penalty.  
Range, Cover, Mimetism, and all other MODs apply normally.

---

## 4. Pros and Cons Summary

### Advantages
- Strongest reactive anti-ambush tool.
- Excellent defense against Smoke shenanigans.
- Punishes Stealth approaches.
- Reliable Dodging and Resetting without MOD penalties.

### Disadvantages
- Provides no benefit unless **attacked**.
- Provides no help in the Active Turn (except for negated Dodge penalties from AROs).
- Does not allow you to ignore Smoke when attacking.
- Less universally useful than a 360º Visor.

---

## 5. Tactical Notes (Unofficial)

- Sixth Sense is best on static defensive pieces (e.g., missile launchers, snipers, or heavy infantry covering fire lanes).  
- In Fireteams, Level 5 Bonus granting Sixth Sense vastly increases area denial and makes Smoke assaults much riskier for your opponent.  
- Sixth Sense does **not** replace careful positioning—being attacked from multiple arcs can still overwhelm a model due to burst advantage.
