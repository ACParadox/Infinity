# Guard

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Optional 

Troopers with this CC Special Skill go into combat with an escort unit.

- The target of the CC Attack must be in LoF and ZoC.

- Allows the user to perform a CC Attack but with no need to reach or be in Silhouette contact with the target.

- Both the user and the target can perform Skills and apply MODs and CC Special Skills that require Silhouette Contact. For example: they can apply Martial Arts, Natural Born Warrior, D-Charges, or the +1B MOD for each Allied Trooper engaged in CC.

Aerial and Guard:

The Guard Special Skill may not be used against Troopers with the Aerial Skill, unless they are in Unconscious State.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

## General Concept
Guard is a Close Combat Special Skill that allows a Trooper to perform CC Attacks at range (LoF + ZoC) without needing Silhouette contact. This makes it function like a “ranged melee” attack that still counts as entering Close Combat for the purposes of applying CC-related MODs and Special Skills.

It represents a trooper fighting with a guardian creature, drone, or remote escort able to project melee threat beyond physical contact.

### Guard vs. Standard CC
- Standard CC requires Silhouette contact; Guard breaks this restriction.
- Guard still uses **CC Attack** and all associated MODs, Burst bonuses from allies in Silhouette contact, and CC Special Skills.
- Unlike normal CC, Guard can be declared from outside Silhouette contact but **never beyond ZoC**, even if LoF exists at longer distances.

### Guard and Martial Arts / Natural Born Warrior
- These Special Skills say they apply when both user and target are in Silhouette contact, but Guard explicitly allows both the user and the target to apply Skills requiring Silhouette contact.
- Therefore MA/NBW fully apply during a Guard CC Attack.
- **Implication:** Guard is effectively a form of “pseudo-contact,” enabling all CC interactions even though the models are not physically touching.

### Guard and Multiple Troopers in CC
- Standard rules grant +1B if allies are in Silhouette contact with the target.
- Because Guard does not create Silhouette contact, **the Guard user does not generate any allies’ +1B bonus unless those allies are actually in Silhouette contact**.
- The target still counts as in CC contact with anyone physically touching them, allowing the target to benefit from +1B from their own allies.
- **Result:** Guard users may be disadvantaged compared to a true “dogpile” melee.

### Guard and ARO
- Guard can be declared in ARO as long as the target is in LoF + ZoC.
- If the attacker is outside Silhouette contact, ARO CC Attacks are normally not permitted; however, Guard **specifically permits CC Attack without Silhouette contact**, so Guard ARO CC is legal.
- This provides a powerful deterrent bubble extending to the user’s ZoC.

### Guard vs. Stealth
- Stealth prevents AROs unless LoF exists.
- Guard *requires LoF + ZoC*, so:
  - If the Stealth user is in ZoC but not in LoF, Guard cannot ARO.
  - If the Stealth user is in ZoC **and** in LoF, Stealth does not prevent ARO, so Guard can ARO normally.

### Guard vs. Aerial
From rules:
> “The Guard Special Skill may not be used against Troopers with the Aerial Skill, unless they are in Unconscious State.”

- **Guard cannot target Aerial Troopers** unless they are Unconscious.
- Even if the Aerial Trooper is in ZoC + LoF, Guard is suppressed.
- This reflects that Aerial troopers cannot be considered in Silhouette contact; Guard’s extra range does not override this protection.

## USE-CASE NOTES (Pros and Cons)

### Pros of Guard
- **Ranged threat in close quarters:** Can deter enemies from entering ZoC.
- **Powerful ARO tool:** Allows CC in ARO at distance, something few skills can do.
- **Synergizes with MA/NBW:** Fully compatible despite no contact.
- **Can protect against melee specialists:** Enemies cannot safely stop outside Silhouette contact to avoid CC.

### Cons of Guard
- **Limited to ZoC:** Maximum 8" threat range; often effectively 2–4" due to LoF requirements.
- **Does not generate allies’ +1B bonuses:** Unlike true CC contact fights.
- **Shut down completely by Aerial units.**
- **Does not bypass Stealth’s ZoC restriction:** Must have LoF to Stealth users.

## COMPARISON WITH SIMILAR SKILLS

### Guard vs. Close Combat (Standard)
**Guard Advantages**
- Does not require base contact.
- Can project CC threat through small openings.
- Works as ranged ARO.

**Standard CC Advantages**
- Enables +1B from allied troopers in Silhouette contact.
- Engaged State protection from enemy shooting.

## TACTICAL USES (Unofficial)

- Ideal for protecting narrow corridors or objectives.
- A great deterrent to melee specialists who want to avoid Face to Face CC attacks.
- Best used on models with high CC and MA/NBW, turning them into “proximity mines.”
- Pair with Sensor or perimeter weapons to force enemies into LoF + ZoC for Guard AROs.
- Avoid relying on Guard against Aerial troops or models with high mobility and Stealth.
