# Forward Observer

Special Skills 
 SHORT SKILL / ARO
BS Attack, Optional 

This Special Skill characterizes Troopers who move before the bulk of their own forces and take positions beyond the established allied lines.

- The user must be able to draw LoF to the target.

- The use of Forward Observer is a form of Attack with a BS Weapon with the BS Weapon (WIP) Trait, and instead of forcing the target to make a Saving Roll, causes them to enter Targeted State.

- Place a Targeted Token (TARGETED) beside the target of a successful Forward Observer attack.

## Forward Observer FAQs

Q: Do Skills that let you perform a 'BS Attack Roll' (for example Intuitive Attack and Speculative Attack), or have the BS Attack Label, count as a BS Attack for MODs? For example, if the Trooper has 'BS Attack (SR-1)', 'BS Attack (+1 SD), or 'BS Attack (AP)'.

A: Yes. Note that +1 SD MODs don't apply to Long Skills.

## Forward Observer and Targeted State 
- The Trooper or game element ("Trooper" from now on) suffers a successful Attack or Effect using an Ammunition, Hacking Program or a game condition or Scenario Special Rule, capable of causing this state.

- Any Trooper declaring a BS Attack, Comms Attack, or Discover against a Trooper in Targeted State gains a +3 MOD to the Attribute used to perform that Skill.

- Troopers in this state must apply a -3 WIP MOD to their Reset rolls.

- Restriction: While in this state, Cautious Movement may not be declared.

- Restriction: A Trooper in this state may not use the Stealth Special Skill.

- This state does not interfere with Automatic Special Skills or Automatic Equipment, which will function normally.

- The affected Trooper successfully passes a Normal or Face to Face Reset Roll, applying a -3 WIP MOD.

- A Trooper with the Engineer Special Skill (or an equivalent Skill) may cancel the state by spending one Short Skill of an Order while in Silhouette contact with the affected Trooper, and passing a Normal WIP Roll (or the Roll specified by the Special Skill or Scenario).


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Forward Observer (FO) is a non-lethal Special Skill used to inflict the Targeted State on an enemy. It functions as a WIP Attack, requires Line of Fire, and can be declared in ARO. FO is one of the main non-hacker methods to put targets into Targeted State, enabling Guided Attacks and various mission objectives.

## Forward Observer INTERACTIONS
------------

**Forward Observer vs. Targeted State**
Forward Observer places the target into the Targeted State on a successful WIP Attack. This gives enemies a -3 to Reset and grants +3 BS MOD to attacks against them, including Guided Attacks. While Spotlight can affect any target in Hacking Area without LoF, Forward Observer requires LoF but does not rely on Hacking Area.

**Forward Observer vs. Mimetism and Cover**
As a BS Attack using WIP, all standard visibility modifiers apply. Mimetism, Cover, and poor ranges penalize the FO roll. FO is best applied at short range or from units with high WIP.

**Forward Observer vs. SymbioMate**
A SymbioMate user completely ignores a Forward Observer Attack because it is a non-BTS/ARM effect that is prevented by Immunity (Enhanced). A successful FO hit on a target protected by a SymbioMate does not apply Targeted.

**Forward Observer in ARO**
In ARO, FO can be used to punish reckless attackers. If the active trooper is visible long enough, FO can convert a reactive opportunity into a later Guided strike. Since FO is a Face to Face roll, the attacker can oppose it with BS Attack or Dodge.

**Forward Observer vs. Dodge**
Dodge opposes FO like any other BS/WIP Attack. If the enemy Dodges successfully and you fail, the Targeted State is not applied. If you succeed and they fail, Targeted applies as normal.

**Forward Observer vs. Guided Attacks**
Forward Observer is one of the primary enabling tools for Guided Attacks. After a Trooper has been Targeted, Guided weapons receive automatic +3 BS and can shoot without LoF.

## Forward Observer COMPARISON WITH SPOTLIGHT
-------------------------

**Similarities**
- Both cause the Targeted State.
- Both can be used in ARO.
- Both synergize with Guided Attacks and BS Attack bonuses.

**Differences**
- FO requires LoF; Spotlight does not.
- FO uses WIP vs. standard shooting MODs (Cover, Mimetism, Vis mods).
- Spotlight uses AP BTS rolls; FO has no Saving Roll.
- Spotlight requires a Hacker; FO does not.
- Spotlight can be done from behind total cover via Repeaters; FO cannot.

**Pros of Forward Observer**
- Works on any Specialists with FO, not limited to Hackers.
- Can be done without Repeaters or Hacking Area.
- No saving roll required after success; the effect is immediate.
- Works on non-Hackable troopers equally well.

**Cons of Forward Observer**
- Requires LoF, exposing the operator to enemy fire.
- Suffers from BS-like modifiers that hinder success (Cover, Mimetism, Smoke).
- Often easier to avoid by Dodging.

## Forward Observer TACTICAL NOTES
--------------

**Optimal Use**
- Use FO from units with high WIP and good positioning tools (Infiltration, Camouflage, Forward Deployment).
- Use FO in the active turn to set up Guided Missile strikes on entrenched enemy models.
- Use FO in ARO to punish brash movement or attacks where the opponent must expose themselves.

**Risk Management**
- FO operators are usually light infantry with little durability; prioritize staying in Cover and using range bands favoring WIP Attacks.
- Coordinate FO with other troopers to minimize exposure and maximize chance of success.

**Synergy**
- Camouflaged or Hidden Deployment FO models excel at surprising targets with low-penalty shots.
- Fireteams with FO members can use link bonuses to improve WIP rolls.
- Pairs well with missile REMs or TAGs carrying Guided weapons.

**Scenario Utility**
- FO counts as a Specialist Troop for many missions and is required by some Classified Objectives.
- Useful for triggering objective scoring without relying on hacking infrastructure.
