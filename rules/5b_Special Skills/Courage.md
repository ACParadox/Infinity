# Courage

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Optional 

This trooper is unafraid of death and ready to lay down his life in the execution of his duty.

- Courage allows its user to choose to automatically pass any Guts Rolls.

- Troopers with the Courage Special Skill are unaffected by Retreat! situations, so they cannot enter Retreat! State and act normally until the end of the game.

- However, even in a Retreat! situation, Troops possessing Courage are affected by Loss of Lieutenant.


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

### Courage vs Guts Rolls
Courage gives the user the choice:  
- Automatically pass the Guts Roll (stand in place)  
- Or choose to roll if they want the *option* to withdraw (rarely done)

This makes Courage more flexible than Religious Troop, which forces holding position unless a WIP roll is passed to withdraw.

### Courage vs Stun Ammunition
Rules note:  
- Stun causes the target to automatically fail the subsequent Guts Roll unless they have Courage or an equivalent. 

### Courage vs Religious Troop
**Key difference:**  
- Courage = optional pass  
- Religious = automatic pass but optional withdrawal via WIP roll

### Courage and Retreat! Situation
Rules note:  
- Troopers with Courage do not enter Retreat! State and act normally. 

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
This is a good benefit:  
- They remain Regular (unless the LO Lt clause transforms them).  
- They can still attack, move freely, and play normally.

But note:
- They **still suffer the Loss of Lieutenant** effects. 

### Courage and Suppressive Fire
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Courage is excellent for models holding suppressive fire lanes.  
Because they can choose to ignore Guts Rolls, they avoid being forced out of suppression range or behind cover.

## Pros and Cons of Courage

### Pros
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Complete control over Guts behavior.  
- Excellent synergy with defensive roles and Suppressive Fire.  
- Prevents forced moves from Stun or E/M-triggered Guts Rolls.  
- Immunity to Retreat! State.  
- No WIP roll required to control Guts outcome.

### Cons
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Does not protect from Loss of Lieutenant effects.  
- Does not prevent entering other states (Immobilized, Targeted, Stunned) — only affects Guts behavior afterward.  
- Sometimes withdrawing is good, but Courage does *not* allow forced fall-back; the player must willingly choose to roll Guts, which can be forgotten.


## Comparison: Courage vs Religious Troop

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

Courage:
- Optional pass.
- Player can choose to fall back by making a Guts Roll normally.
- Greater tactical flexibility.
- Prevents Retreat! effects.

Religious Troop:
- Automatic pass (forced hold).  
- Can fall back only with a successful WIP roll.  
- Sometimes worse for survivability when taking sustained fire.  
- Also prevents Retreat!, but still suffers Loss of Lieutenant.

**Conclusion:**  
Courage is generally better for tactical flexibility, especially in competitive play. Religious Troop provides thematic discipline but demands more careful positioning.

## Tactical Uses

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Anchor points: Courage units are ideal for holding key angles without fear of being displaced.  
- Suppression anchors: They maintain fire lanes even after taking fire.  
- TAGs/HI: Particularly strong when combined with high ARM, allowing them to tank hits without repositioning.  
- Order efficiency: Courage units require fewer orders to maintain board control positions.
