# Chain of Command

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Optional, Private Information. 

The owner of this Special Skill is the next in line in the command echelon to the officer in command, being able to replace him if necessary during the combat operation.

- This Special Skill can only be used at the start of the player's Loss of Lieutenant check.

- The player's Lieutenant must be in the Isolated State or any Null State.

- The user must be on the game table as a Model or as a Marker.

- The user cannot be in the Isolated State or any Null State.

- The user automatically becomes the new Lieutenant of the Army List, gaining the Lieutenant Special Skill with no extra abilities.

- The Loss of Lieutenant check is successful this Turn.

- Even if the previous Lieutenant recovers from the Isolated or Null State, the user will continue to be the Lieutenant.

- If the user has Chain of Command (Non Specialist) listed on their Unit Profile, the user is not a Specialist Troop in those scenarios where Chain of Command grants that category.

### Interaction Note
Related rules: Chain of Command, Loss of Lieutenant, Open and Private Information.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

**Core Rule Summary (for reference only):**  
Chain of Command automatically triggers at the start of the Loss of Lieutenant check. The Trooper with Chain of Command becomes the new Lieutenant and the army avoids entering Loss of Lieutenant for that Turn. 

---
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### How Chain of Command Interacts with the Game

**1. Loss of Lieutenant Timing**  
- If your previous Lieutenant is Dead, Unconscious, Isolated, or off-table, the Chain of Command user immediately becomes the new Lieutenant.  
- This prevents the army from becoming Irregular for the Turn.

**2. Order Pool Stability**  
Because it prevents entering Loss of Lieutenant, Chain of Command protects:  
- Regular Order Pools  
- Fireteam stability (since Irregular Orders can break flow)  

**3. Private Information**  
The identity of your Chain of Command trooper is Private Information. Your opponent only learns who it was when they are forced to publicly take the Lieutenant role.

---
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### Pros and Cons of Chain of Command

**Pros:**  
- **Prevents LoL:** Keeps your entire army Regular even after losing your Lieutenant.  
- **Enables Aggressive Lieutenant Play:** Since the army does not collapse into Irregular, you can risk pushing an offensive Lieutenant like a Gunfighter Lt or +1 Order Lt.  
- **Stabilizes Fireteams:** Especially valuable in Sectorials where the Lieutenant is embedded in a Fireteam Core.

**Cons:**  
- **Points and Slot Tax:** Chain of Command profiles tend to be more expensive or less optimal in raw stats.  
- **Predictable Targeting:** Experienced opponents can deduce likely Chain of Command candidates based on faction and profiles.  

---
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### Chain of Command Tactical Use Cases

- **Aggressive Lt Strategy:** Use your Lt offensively—CoC guarantees that the rest of the army stays functional.  
- **Protecting High-Value Fireteams:** Losing the Lt inside a Fireteam often disrupts command tempo; CoC prevents this collapse.  
- **Late-Game Objective Pushing:** A hidden or backline CoC model can step in as Lt with minimal risk and enable a final turn of coordinated movement.

---
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### Chain of Command Downsides and Counterplay (Opponent Perspective)

- **Hunting the CoC:** Knowing a faction’s common CoC profiles lets you target them to force true Loss of Lieutenant.  
- **Lt Bait:** Opponents may use a Lt aggressively, expecting CoC—set traps to kill both Lt and the likely CoC holder.  
- **Isolation Attacks:** CoC doesn’t trigger if the CoC model is *already Isolated* when the Lt dies.
