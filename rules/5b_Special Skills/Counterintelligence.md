
# Counterintelligence

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Optional, Private Information. 

This Special Skill allows its owner to partially counteract the effects of the Command Token Strategic Use

- This Special Skill counteract the effects of the Strategic Use of a Command Token by reducing to one the number of Orders the adversary can nullify.

- Alternatively, this Special Skill counteracts the limit on Command Token use imposed by the adversary due to the Strategic Use of a Command Token, increasing it to two.


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
*** Reference ***
 Strategic Use of a Command Token: 
 
 - Procedural: The player who has the second Player Turn may make Strategic Use of their Command Tokens during the Tactical Phase of their opponent's first Turn to perform one of the following actions: 
    - Remove two Regular Orders for this Turn from their opponent's Order Pool. The player may choose which Combat Groups these Orders correspond to. This option may only be performed during the opponent's Tactical Phase, right after the Order Count, but only if the number of Orders ( Regular + Irregular + Tactical ) is higher than ten. The Orders of Troopers in Hidden Deployment State, or off table via Airborne Deployment Skills, are not counted for this limit. 

    - Prevent the adversary from using more than one single Command Token during that first Turn. 

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
The Special Skill *Counterintelligence* is traditionally a list-building and Tactical Phase rule that affects the enemy’s ability to reduce your Order Pool. In Infinity, it is generally considered a defensive support skill.

## Counterintelligence Interaction Notes
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Counterintelligence typically interacts with the opponent’s use of Command Tokens at the start of the game, preventing or reducing the usual penalty of “Order stripping”.

- Most commonly, if the opposing player uses a Command Token to reduce your first-turn Order Pool, the reduction is limited (usually to –2 Regular Order instead of up to –2).

- This interaction only matters in missions or game setups where the opposing player has the right to reduce Orders at the start of the game.


## Counterintelligence Advantages
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Helps maintain a stronger first-turn tempo by preserving a key Regular Order.
- Especially valuable for alpha-strike lists that depend on early momentum.
- Helps mitigate the disadvantage of going second in missions where your opponent tries to dampen your first turn.

## Counterintelligence Disadvantages
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Takes up points on a trooper that might provide no in-game effect if:
  - The opponent chooses not to strip Orders.
  - The mission format does not allow Order stripping.
  - You win the roll and choose going second.
- Provides no direct combat or reactive benefit.

## Tactical Uses
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Ideal for lists planning strong first activations (alpha strikes, long-range elimination, hacking offensives).
- Good for elite armies where each Order is high value (e.g., TAG lists, elite HI lists).
- Helps mitigate the weakness of low-Order lists when going second.
- Strong choice in tournaments where many players opt to strip Orders by default.
