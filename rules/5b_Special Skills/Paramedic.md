# Paramedic

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Obligatory 

Paramedics provide their unit with first aid treatment on the field.

- Paramedics are equipped with MediKits to recover their Unconscious comrades.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
## UNOFFICIAL COMMENT — Core Function
A Paramedic is effectively a **Specialist Troop** whose support capability is limited to the **MediKit**. Unlike a Doctor, Paramedics do *not* heal with a WIP roll in base contact; they rely exclusively on the MediKit’s PH roll, which is often worse and more lethal to the patient.

Because of this, Paramedics are usually “budget specialists”: cheaper than Doctors, more dangerous to their patients, but still able to perform mission objectives that require a Specialist Troop.

## UNOFFICIAL COMMENT — Rules Interactions

### 1. **Paramedic vs MediKit Interaction**
The Paramedic skill does not give bonuses to the MediKit.  
Its only effect is: **you carry a MediKit.**

Because the MediKit:
- **Does not use WIP**  
- **Uses the target’s PH**, often 10–12  
- **Kills the target on failure**  

the healing chance is very dependent on the patient, not the Paramedic.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### 2. **Paramedic vs Doctor (Comparison)**
**Doctor (from rules):**  
Requires silhouette contact, uses **WIP**, and kills the patient only on a failed WIP.  


**Differences (UNOFFICIAL COMMENT):**

**Doctor – Pros**
- Uses WIP (often 13–15) → **high success rate**.  
- Can apply Command Token re-rolls.  
- Can cancel some States (Stunned, etc.).

**Doctor – Cons**
- Must be in silhouette contact → exposed to danger.  
- More expensive.

**Paramedic – Pros**
- Can heal from range (8–16”).  
- Cheaper specialist.  
- Can attempt healing without exposing themselves.

**Paramedic – Cons**
- Relies on patient PH (often bad).  
- Frequently kills the target.  
- Provides no rerolls and no ability to cancel states.

### 3. **Paramedics in Fireteams**
Fireteam bonuses do affect MediKits, because the MediKit is a **BS Attack**:
- SD dice is used for ranged use of Medikit  (weapons chart shows B1)  
- Also this means Fireteam Paramedics can gain **BS bonuses**, but this also only improves the *hit* roll, not the *PH roll*.

**Fireteam Paramedics are often attractive** because they’re cheap, count as Specialists, and can sit safely inside the link.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### 4. **Paramedic & Classified Objectives**
The Classified Objectives list confirms Paramedics qualify for:  
- **HVT: Inoculation**  
- **Combat Support**  
- **Nanoespionage**  

Unofficial comment: This makes Paramedics valuable in ITS missions with high Classified pressure.

### 5. **Paramedic and PH (X) Profiles**
Some troops have **PH (X)** listed beside MediKit.  

This is extremely important:  
Certain troops (e.g. Aleph, some HI) have **PH 12 but PH(14) for MediKit**. These troops drastically increase Paramedic viability.

### 6. **MediKit Failure Kills the Target**
This is one of the **harshest consequences** in the healing system.  
If the target is low-PH (PH 10 or less), using the MediKit is often a **coin-flip to kill them**.  
This is tactically relevant when:
- The unconscious trooper is holding an Objective Room, or  
- The enemy could score points by finishing them off.  

You may choose **not** to attempt healing to avoid creating easy enemy points.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

### Pros
- Cheap Specialist Trooper.
- MediKit can be used safely at range.
- Often present in Fireteams at low cost.
- Qualifies for several ITS Classifieds.

### Cons
- Lethal healing due to PH roll.
- Cannot heal Structure (STR) targets.
- Cannot cancel States like a Doctor.

## UNOFFICIAL COMMENT — Tactical Notes

1. **Use Paramedics for objectives, not healing.**  
Their greatest value comes from being a Specialist, not a medic.

2. **Heal only high-PH troopers.**  
Healing success rises dramatically at **PH 13+**, especially with PH(X) profiles.

3. **In Fireteams, use them safely.**  
Thanks to BS bonuses, they hit MediKit shots reliably — but the target still risks death.

4. **Late-game triage.**  
Sometimes killing your own unconscious trooper (accidentally via MediKit failure) denies the enemy a point. Rare but real tactical use.

5. **Don’t waste Orders trying repeated healing.**  
Each attempt risks death; unlike Doctors, there is **no reroll mechanic** without Command Tokens (and even then, only Doctors get reroll benefits from Cubes).

6. **Great filler in Fireteams.**  
Paramedic upgrades are often the cheapest way to “specialize” a generic line trooper.

## UNOFFICIAL COMMENT — Overall Evaluation
The Paramedic is a **situational Specialist** whose healing ability is unreliable but occasionally useful. Their true strength is **mission utility and low cost**, not battlefield triage. They should be viewed as “specialist shooters who happen to carry a risky medical tool.”
