# Combat Instinct

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Optional 

Troopers with this Skill have a special talent for sensing danger, which enables them to react swiftly to any surprise attack.

- The user ignores Surprise Attack MODs from attackers.

The Stealth Special Skill is not effective against Troopers with Combat Instinct.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
## Reference: Stealth
- The user of this Special Skill must be in his Active Turn.
- The user can declare Cautious Movement inside the Zone of Control and Hacking Areas of enemy Models and Markers.
- If the user declares Cautious Movement, Idle, or a Basic Short Skill with the Movement Label, they only grant AROs to enemies that have LoF to them.
- If any other Skill is declared, AROs are granted normally.
- If the user enters Silhouette contact with an enemy without entering their LoF, and declares any of those other Skills, that enemy can only declare CC Attack, Dodge, Reset, or those Skills that can be used in Engaged State.

## Reference: Surprise Attack
- To use this Special Skill, Troopers must be in Marker form (for example, Camouflaged, Impersonation, Decoy, Holoecho...) or in Hidden Deployment at the start of the Order in which they are activated.
- The user must declare a CC Attack, BS Attack, or Comms Attack.
- The user of this Special Skill must be in their Active Turn.
- This Attack imposes an additional negative MOD, as shown in round brackets in the Unit Profile [Surprise Attack (-3), Surprise Attack (-6)...]. This MOD only applies to targets of the Attack and applies to any Face to Face Roll that those targets perform in ARO.

Related rules: ARO, Face-to-Face, Partial Cover.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

Combat Instinct is a defensive Special Skill whose two major functions in N5 are:  
1. **Ignore all Surprise Attack MODs**, and  
2. **Nullify the benefits of Stealth** when an enemy tries to approach or activate within your ZoC without granting AROs.

Because Stealth and Surprise Attack are the primary tools used by ambush units (Camo, Impersonators, Holoecho units, CC assassins), Combat Instinct directly counters both of their signature advantages. This greatly changes how AROs are generated, how Face-to-Face Rolls occur when ambushed, and how Partial Cover interacts with defensive positioning.

### Combat Instinct Practical Interactions

#### **Against Stealth**
- Stealth **no longer restricts AROs** against the Combat Instinct user.
- Any time a Stealth user activates inside the Combat Instinct user’s Zone of Control, **even with movement-only Skills**, the Combat Instinct user **may declare an ARO**.
- This negates one of Stealth’s most powerful features: the ability to bypass ZoC AROs.

#### **Against Surprise Attack**
- All Surprise Attack penalties (BS, CC, or Comms Attack) are **ignored**.
- The attacker loses both angles of their ambush advantage:  
  - No Stealth bypass of ZoC AROs, and  
  - No Surprise Attack MOD.

#### **ARO Timing**
- Combat Instinct **does not provide 360° LoF** or out-of-LoF AROs (unlike Sixth Sense).  
- But if the opponent is in ZoC and activates, **an ARO is generated even without LoF** (because Stealth fails).  
- The ARO options remain limited by LoF as normal (e.g., Dodge is always allowed; BS Attack requires LoF).

#### **Face-to-Face Rolls**
- Without the Surprise MOD, FtF odds heavily shift toward the defender.  
- Combined with Partial Cover, the defender often becomes extremely difficult to overwhelm with a single alpha-strike order.


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### Combat Instinct vs Hacking
Hacking Programs are Comms Attacks, and Surprise Attack applies to them normally.
A Trooper with Combat Instinct ignores the Surprise Attack MOD from hacking attacks.
Stealth does NOT suppress AROs against a Combat Instinct trooper—this includes hacking-based AROs like Reset.

Since Stealth units are the primary counter to dense Repeater coverage, Combat instinct hackers counters that.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### Stealth Breakdown with Combat Instinct Applied

**Normal Stealth Effects** (summarized from rules):
- Avoids granting AROs inside ZoC unless LoF is drawn.
- Allows silent approach.
- Limits ARO options when entering Silhouette contact from behind/hidden angles.

**Combat Instinct Cancels All of the Above:**
- AROs **are granted** even without LoF.
- The defender can declare **full ARO options they normally would** (subject to LoF requirements).
- Close-combat assassins cannot “walk into base” without provoking reaction.

### Combat Instinct Tactical Applications

- **Defensive anchors**: Units with Combat Instinct punish close-range infiltrators and melee assassins.  
- **Anti-Camo zone control**: Camo markers cannot safely maneuver near them.  
- **Protecting exposed firelanes**: The defender can still Dodge or Reset freely when ambushed.  
- **Stacking with Cover**: Cover + full ARO access + no Surprise MOD often results in flat or favorable FtF odds.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### Combat Instinct Pros and Cons Summary

**Pros**
- Cancels Surprise Attack MODs entirely.  
- Cancels Stealth effects inside ZoC.  
- Grants reliable ARO presence against silent approach units.  
- Strong vs melee infiltrators, impersonators, and camo alpha strikes.

**Cons**
- Does not grant 360° LoF (unlike Sixth Sense).  
- Provides no visibility benefits.  
- Still vulnerable if unaware or attacked from outside ZoC.
