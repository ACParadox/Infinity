# Marksmanship

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Obligatory 

Users of this Special Skill are exceptional sharpshooters, adept at hitting their target's weakest spots and making every shot count.

- The user may only benefit from this Special Skill when he declares a BS Attack.

- The user may ignore the negative BS MODs imposed by Partial Cover, and Nanoscreen.

Troopers with the Multispectral Visor piece of Equipment or with the Marksmanship Special Skill are affected by:

- White Noise Zones.

- The Reflective Trait.

- The Albedo piece of Equipment.

The Assisted Fire Hacking Program grants Marksmanship to a REM.

---
## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Key Interactions

### 1. **Cover Ignoring: What exactly is ignored?**
Marksmanship ignores:
- The **–3 BS MOD** for Partial Cover.
- The **–3 BS MOD** from Nanoscreen (which grants a *virtual* Cover MOD).

Marksmanship **does NOT** ignore:
- The **+3 SR** bonus for being in Partial Cover (rules do not state ARM is ignored).
- Any **Visibility Zone MODs**.
- **Mimetism** penalties.
- **Range MODs**.
- Other defensive MODs like Surprise Attack.

**Practical takeaway:**  
Marksmanship boosts hit probability but does *not* increase damage output directly (unlike AP or Shock).

### 2. **Interactions with Multispectral Visors and White Noise**
Troopers with Marksmanship count as “MSV-like” for the purposes of:
- **White Noise Zones**  
  → White Noise blocks LoF against MSV users *and* Marksmanship users.  
- **Reflective Trait** and **Albedo**  
  → These anti-visor defenses affect Marksmanship users the same way they affect MSV.

**Implication:**  
Marksmanship grants *offensive benefits* but also exposes the user to *anti-visor tech drawbacks*.
---

### 3. **Marksmanship vs. Skills that Modify Cover**
Comparison of cover-affecting abilities:

**Marksmanship**
- Ignores negative BS MODs from Partial Cover/Nanoscreen.
- Does **not** ignore the ARM bonus.

**Pros/Cons Summary**

**Marksmanship Pros**
- Greatly increases hit chance.
- Universal benefit to all BS Attacks.
- REMs can get it via Assisted Fire.

**Marksmanship Cons**
- Still must chew through ARM (including Cover SR mods).
- Vulnerable to White Noise / Albedo like MSV.
- Provides no help with other defensive layers (Mimetism, Range bands).

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Tactical Use Cases

### *Best Uses*
- **Long-range firefights** where Cover is guaranteed.  
- **High-Burst weapons** (HMGs, Spitfires, Multi-Rifles in Active Turn).  
- **REM gunfighters** under Assisted Fire.  
- **Units relying on raw BS stat instead of Mimetism**.

### *Synergy Highlights*
- **Marksmanship + BS Attack (+1B)** from Fireteam Core Leader  
  → Very strong active-turn shooters.  
- **Marksmanship + Targeted State (via Spotlight)**  
  → Ignores Cover MOD + gets +3 from Targeted.  
  → Extremely reliable high-BS shots.
- **Marksmanship + MSV** 
  → Further reduces mods of Mimetism, and already vulnerable to MSV-counters anyways

### *Weaknesses to Expect*
- **White Noise** zones completely shut you down.  
- **Reflective** and **Albedo** bypass your advantage.  
- Camo/Mimetism-heavy opponents still stack negative MODs you cannot ignore.


## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] When to Prioritize Marksmanship
Choose units with Marksmanship when:
- You expect heavy use of Cover on dense tables.
- You lack MSV but still need strong long-range firepower.
- You rely on REM gunfighters for order efficiency.

Avoid relying on it when:
- Opponent uses **White Noise** or **Albedo**.
- You need damage amplification (consider AP or heavier ammo types).
- Opponent stacks strong MODs *other than* Cover (i.e., Mimetism –6).
