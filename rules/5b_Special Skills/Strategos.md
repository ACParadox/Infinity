# Strategos

> **N5.3 change (September 2026):** **Strategos L2** was aligned with L1. L2 includes the two-reserve-Trooper deployment benefit and conversion of Special Lieutenant Orders into Regular Orders, in addition to its free Combat Group transfer during the Tactical Phase.

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Optional 

This Special Skill identifies professional strategists, individuals with superior analytical minds and a wealth of knowledge on the art of war and its psychological implications.

## STRATEGOS L1 
- In order to benefit from this Level of Strategos, the user must be the army's Lieutenant.

- During the Deployment Phase, you may set aside two Troopers and their Peripherals to deploy after your opponent instead of the usual one.

- At the beginning of your Active Turn, during your Order Count, replace any Special Lieutenant Orders with Regular Orders, adding them to the Order Pool of the Lieutenant's Combat Group.

## STRATEGOS L2 
- In order to benefit from this Level of Strategos, the user must be the army's Lieutenant.

- During the Tactical Phase of the Active Turn, in the Executive Use of Command Tokens step, the user of this Special Skill may move a Trooper to a different Combat Group without expending a Command Token.

- During the Deployment Phase, you may set aside two Troopers and their Peripherals to deploy after your opponent instead of the usual one.

- At the beginning of your Active Turn, during your Order Count, replace any Special Lieutenant Orders with Regular Orders, adding them to the Order Pool of the Lieutenant's Combat Group.

## INTERACTION NOTES

### Strategos vs. NCO
- If Strategos is active, **there is no Lieutenant Order left for NCO to use**, since it has already been converted into a Regular Order.
- NCO therefore loses its primary function in an army where the Lieutenant has Strategos.

**Pros of Strategos vs. NCO:**
- Order becomes flexible and usable by any Trooper in the Combat Group.  
- No need to expose the NCO to spend it.

**Pros of NCO vs. Strategos:**
- NCO keeps the Lieutenant Order “hidden” when used, avoiding giving away the Lieutenant’s Combat Group.  
- Useful when the Lieutenant is not intended to reveal Strategos, or if Strategos is not available.

## COMPARISON WITH SIMILAR ORDER-MODIFYING SKILLS

### Strategos L1 vs L2
- N5.3 explicitly gives L2 the L1 reserve and Lieutenant-order benefits; L2 additionally allows the player to move a Trooper to a different Combat Group without expending a Command Token. 
This can be very useful if the main Combat Group has been reduced and the secondary Group can transfer a Trooper to it (or vice-versa).

### Strategos vs. NCO
- **Strategos:** Converts Special Lieutenant Orders into Regular Orders in the Lieutenant's Combat Group.  
- **NCO:** Uses the Lieutenant Order through the NCO rules.

**Strategos Pros:**  
- More flexible Regular Order economy in the Lieutenant's group.  
- Does not force a specific NCO to spend the order.  

**Strategos Cons:**  
- Requires the Strategos user to be the Lieutenant.  
- Can reduce the value of bringing NCO specifically to exploit Lieutenant Orders.

## TACTICAL NOTES

### When Strategos Is Most Effective
- Armies that value reserve advantage, flexible Lieutenant-order conversion, and—at L2—free Combat Group restructuring.

### Common Strategos Plays
- Use the two-model reserve to protect or counter-deploy key pieces.
- Use the L2 transfer to repair order-group structure after casualties without spending a Command Token.
