# Infiltration

Special Skills
 DEPLOYMENT SKILL
Superior Deployment, Optional. 

The user of this Special Skill may deploy outside the limits of their Deployment Zone thanks to their training in infiltration techniques.

- During the Deployment Phase, the Trooper may be deployed anywhere in the player's half of the table.

- It also allows the user to deploy in the opponent's half of the game table by making a successful Infiltration Roll.

- The user cannot use Infiltration to deploy inside the enemy Deployment Zone.

## Infiltration Roll

- An Infiltration Roll is required to infiltrate into the enemy's half of the table. This is a Normal Roll with PH -3. If a Trooper has Infiltration listed in its Unit Profile with a PH value between brackets, the PH-3 Infiltration Roll is made using that PH value.

- The Infiltration Roll must be made after placing the user on the game table, along with any Deployable Weapon or piece of Equipment they can deploy.

- Infiltration (ReRoll PH=X). Any failed Infiltration Roll can be rerolled, but applying the PH Attribute indicated between brackets to the PH-3 Roll. The player must accept the result of this last Roll.

- Failed Infiltration Roll. If the user fails the Roll, they must be placed within their player's Deployment Zone, in contact with the edge of the game table. Additionally, after failing this Roll, the user loses the option to deploy in a Marker State or Hidden Deployment State and is always deployed as a Model. Any Deployable Weapons and Equipment that were deployed alongside them are removed from the game table.

Troopers cannot be deployed in contact with enemy and neutral Models, Markers, or Tokens, or in contact with any mission objective, no matter the Special Skills they have.

A Trooper with the Infiltration, Camouflage, and Minelayer Special Skills, and Shock Mines, decides to deploy in the Enemy's half of the table. They must follow these steps:

- Place both Camouflaged (-3) Markers in the Enemy's half of the table following the Minelayer rule.

- Check that the Markers are in Zone of Control of each other.

- Make the Infiltration Roll, which in this case has a Success Value equal to their PH Attribute -3.

If the Infiltration Roll is failed, the Trooper is placed in Model form within their player's Deployment Zone, touching an edge of the game table and losing the Shock Mine which is removed from the game table.

If the Trooper has the Infiltration (PH=18) Special Skill, the Success Value for their Infiltration Roll (PH-3) is 15 (18-3=15).

## Infiltration FAQs

Q: If a Trooper with Camouflage (1 Use) deploys as a Model, can the Trooper enter Camouflaged State during the game? Does this change if the Trooper fails an Infiltration Roll?

A: Yes, the Trooper can enter Camouflaged State later in the game. If the Trooper attempted to deploy as a Marker and failed the Infiltration Roll, they have used the Camouflaged State and cannot enter it later in the game.

# INFILTRATION — INTERACTIONS & COMMENTARY

## Core Mechanics (Summary from Rules)
- Troopers with Infiltration may deploy on the enemy half of the table.
- If a Roll is required (usually PH -3 unless specified otherwise), failure forces the Trooper to deploy in the owner’s Deployment Zone, touching a table edge.  

- Camouflage interactions: if deploying as a Marker and the Roll is failed, the Camouflaged State is considered **used** and cannot be regained 

- Hidden Deployment + Infiltration: If the Infiltration Roll is failed, the Trooper appears normally in the Deployment Zone, losing Hidden Deployment

## GENERAL INTERACTION NOTES
### Infiltration + Camouflage/Marker States
- Deploying as a Camouflage (or Camouflaged -3) Marker on the enemy half requires the Infiltration Roll.  
- Failure cancels the ability to start in Camouflaged State; you deploy as a model in your DZ and cannot re-enter Camouflaged State later

This makes risky infiltration rolls especially punishing for Camo troops—losing the Marker State can significantly reduce survivability and mission effectiveness. Use Infiltration Rolls sparingly on essential Camo specialists unless you are comfortable accepting a huge early-game penalty.

### Infiltration + Hidden Deployment
- HD Troopers must roll before the game begins; on success, they keep HD and infiltrate.  
- On failure, the model appears on table in the DZ, losing HD entirely.

This is a high-risk/high-reward play. A successful infiltration HD placement is extremely powerful: the Trooper begins truly hidden deep in enemy territory. But a failure erases one of the strongest defensive tools in the game.

### Infiltration + Minelayer
- Minelayer relies on being able to deploy normally in the area allowed by the Trooper’s deployment method.  
- When Minelayer is used with Infiltration and the Roll fails, the deployable is removed 
 
Minelayer makes failed infiltration even more costly—losing a Mine can reduce area control severely. Only attempt infiltrating Minelayers when the payoff is decisive.

### Infiltration vs Fireteams
- Troopers with the Infiltration Special Skill **cannot** be part of a Fireteam 


## PROS & CONS OF INFILTRATION

### PROS
- **Forward Starting Position:** Immediate pressure on objectives, consoles, and midfield.
- **Order Efficiency:** Starting closer means fewer Orders spent reaching mission-critical areas.
- **Threat Projection:** Enemy must account for midfield attack vectors immediately.
- **Synergy with Camo/HD:** Deep deployment plus strong defensive states creates potent early-game threats.

### CONS
- **Risk of Failure:** Failed Rolls teleport the Trooper back to the DZ in model form, often losing Camo/HD and/or deployables.
- **Exposed Positioning:** If deploying without Marker/HD, infiltrated models can start dangerously close to enemy ARO pieces.
- **Fireteam Incompatibility:** Cannot leverage FT order efficiency or bonuses.

## INFILTRATION VS FORWARD DEPLOYMENT (+4"/+8")
### Key Differences
- **Distance Gained:**  
  - Infiltration can get *much* farther (entire enemy half) than FD ever can.

## TACTICAL RECOMMENDATIONS
- **Only roll when the payoff is vital.** For many Camo specialists, a failed Infiltration damages the entire game plan.
- **Use extreme positions when success is impactful.** High-value flanks, backline angles, or objective choke points.
- **If you roll, plan for failure.** Have backup plans—alternate specialists, support pieces, or deployment redundancy.


## SPECIAL TRICK: “Soft Infiltration Threat”
Instead of rolling, place the infiltrator safely on the center line **where no roll is required**.  
This preserves states and equipment while still projecting midfield presence.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Many experienced Infinity players avoid rolling entirely with expensive infiltrators unless the table or mission strongly rewards it.
