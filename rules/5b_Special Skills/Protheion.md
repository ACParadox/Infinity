# Protheion

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
CC Special Skill, Optional. 

Users of this CC Special Skill can remove Wounds or increase the value of their Vitality Attribute whenever they inflict Wounds in Close Combat.

- In the Active or Reactive Turn, the Trooper must reach or be in Silhouette contact with the target and declare a CC Attack.

- The target must have the VITA Attribute.

- For each Wound they inflict on the target of the CC Attack (See FAQs.), or Coup de Grâce they perform, the user removes 1 Wound they have received. If the user hasn't received any Wounds, they increase their VITA Attribute value by 1.

- Restriction: The value of the user's VITA Attribute may only be increased by a maximum of two points.

- If the user's VITA Attribute is higher than its original value, the player must place a Power-Up 1 or Power-Up 2 Token to indicate the current value of the Trooper's VITA Attribute.

- When multiple Wounds are inflicted in the same Order, the user follows this sequence: First, they use Wounds they have inflicted to remove their Wounds, if they have any. Then they use any remaining Wounds they have inflicted to increase the value of their VITA Attribute, never increasing it beyond the maximum of two points. Finally, any Wounds inflicted on them during this Order are applied.

- First, they use Wounds they have inflicted to remove their Wounds, if they have any.

- Then they use any remaining Wounds they have inflicted to increase the value of their VITA Attribute, never increasing it beyond the maximum of two points.

- Finally, any Wounds inflicted on them during this Order are applied.

## Protheion Examples

A Trooper with the Protheion Special Skill enters Close Combat against an enemy, who declares CC Attack as their ARO. The Trooper with Protheion wins the fight and the enemy fails their Saving Roll, receiving a Wound and entering Unconscious State. When the Order concludes, the Trooper with Protheion places a Power-Up 1 Token next to his base, showing that his VITA Attribute is now 2.

During the next Order, the Trooper with the Protheion Special Skill declares a CC Attack against the Unconscious enemy, performing a Coup de Grâce so that the target automatically passes from Unconscious to Dead State, without performing a Saving Roll. At the end of the Order, the Trooper with Protheion places a Power-Up 2 Token next to his base, showing that his VITA Attribute is now 3.

A Trooper with the Protheion Special Skill and a VITA Attribute of 2, who has received a Wound in a previous combat, uses his DA CC Weapon to CC Attack an enemy who has a VITA Attribute of 2. The Trooper with Protheion wins the Roll, forcing the enemy to perform 2 Saving Rolls due to his DA CC Weapon. The enemy fails both Saving Rolls, receives 2 Wounds, and enters Unconscious State. Thanks to his Protheion Special Skill, the Trooper removes the Wound he had previously received, and increases the value of his VITA Attribute by one, placing a Power-Up 1 Token next to his base. If, in a later Order, he performs a Coup de Grâce against the Unconscious enemy, he increases his VITA Attribute by another point and replaces the Power-Up 1 Token with a Power-Up 2 Token. The Trooper with Protheion now has a VITA Attribute value of 4 and the enemy is removed from the game table.

Let’s say that the Trooper had not received any Wounds and still inflicts two Wounds on his enemy. And, during the same Order, a different enemy opens fire on him from a distance and inflicts one Wound. In this case, the Trooper first increases his VITA value by two by inflicting two Wounds on the enemy in Close Combat, and then he receives the Wound caused by the enemy shooter. At the end of the Order, he has a VITA Attribute value of 4 represented by a Power-Up 2 Token, and one Wound.

## Protheion FAQs

Q: What happens if the target of a Protheion user fails more Saving Rolls than are needed for it to enter Dead State? For example, if an Unconscious target fails two or more Saving Rolls.

A: Any additional failed Saving Rolls will have no effect, so will not benefit the Protheion user.



**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Protheion is a Close Combat Special Skill that lets the user heal themselves or increase their VITA Attribute by inflicting Wounds in Close Combat. The effect triggers only from Wounds caused in CC, and the gained Attribute increases remain until lost through further damage.

## General Behaviour and Use Cases

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Protheion turns CC engagements into a resource engine. The user becomes progressively harder to kill by absorbing enemy vitality. Even against a single target, a successful multi-Wound CC Attack can create a strong snowball effect.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
The Protheion FAQ clarifies that excess failed Saves past the point the target would enter Dead State do *not* grant further VITA gains. Only the Wounds that "matter" before entering Dead State count.

## Interaction: Protheion vs. Unconscious Targets

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Protheion can still heal or grow when attacking an Unconscious target, but only for the Wounds the target can legally lose. Once it would reach Dead, any additional failed Saves do not help.

**Pros**
- Reliable and safe VITA farming.
- No Face to Face Roll from the victim.
- Great for setting up a “super-Caliban” or similar mid-game tank.

**Cons**
- Order-intensive.
- Leaves you exposed to enemy fire if performed in the wrong position.

## Interaction: Protheion vs. Shock Ammunition

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Because Shock sends the target straight to Dead, you can only gain VITA from the first Wound (if any) inflicted before the Shock effect applies. Protheion users can still benefit if they deal multiple Wounds in a single CC Attack, but once Dead is reached, all additional failures are ignored.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Against Shock-vulnerable enemies, Protheion farming is limited compared to targets with multiple Wounds or STR.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
High-Burst CC from Martial Arts or Vorpal-type weapons can dramatically increase Protheion potential when multiple hits land.
