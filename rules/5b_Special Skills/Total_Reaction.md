# Total Reaction

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Optional 

Troopers with this Special Skill have higher than normal offensive reaction capabilities in ARO.

- When performing a BS Attack ARO, the user may use the full Burst (B) of their weapon, including any Burst MODs.

- AROs must choose one of the Troopers activated by the Order as their target.


## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
### Overview
Total Reaction (TR) turns a trooper—most commonly a REM—into a **full-Burst ARO platform**. Instead of firing B1 like normal in ARO, they fire their **full weapon Burst**, making them a strong deterrent in long firelanes.

---
## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
### Core Interactions

### **1. Total Reaction vs. Enhanced Reaction (Supportware)**
Enhanced Reaction (Supportware) grants **Burst 2 in ARO** instead of full Burst, but can apply to many REMs.
- Total Reaction gives **B = full weapon burst**, which is often higher than 2.

**Conclusion:**  
Enhanced Reaction is only for REMs that **do not** have Total Reaction.


### **2. Total Reaction vs. Suppressive Fire**
Suppressive Fire (SF) puts Burst to **B3**, limits Range, and applies -3 to enemy attacks, but require an order to activate.

**Comparison:**
- **Total Reaction (in ARO):** fires **full Burst**, no range restrictions beyond weapon profile.  
- **Suppressive Fire (in ARO):** fires **B3**, imposes **–3** to enemy BS, but only effective **within 24”** and require an order to activate.

**Which is better?**  
- At long range: **Total Reaction** (full Burst, long-range weapons).  
-  **Suppressive Fire** can be stronger in some cases due to the -3 debuff

**Important:** A TR trooper **can enter Suppressive Fire**—it simply overrides TR because SF sets Burst.

---

### **5. Total Reaction and Coordinated Orders**
Since ARO must target **one** activated trooper:
- Against 4-model Coordinated Order, TR still fires full Burst **at only one target**.
- This makes Coordinated Orders an effective way to neutralize them.

---

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
### Strengths (Pros)
- **Full Burst in ARO**—one of the strongest reactive abilities in Infinity.
- Excellent at locking down long firelanes.
- Forces enemy to invest real resources: smoke, hacking, Coordinated Orders, or long-range gunfighters.
- Very Order-efficient defender.

---

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
### Weaknesses (Cons)
- Typically **low ARM**, **low BS**, or both—easily defeated by high-end shooters.
- Still vulnerable to:
  - Smoke + MSV
  - Hacking if it is a REM
- Can be bypassed by careful movement or terrain.

---

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
### Total Reaction Comparisons With Similar Abilities

#### **Total Reaction vs. Neurocinetics**
- **TR:** Full Burst **in ARO**, normal Burst in active turn.  
- **Neurocinetics:** Full Burst **in ARO**, but **Burst 1** in active turn.

**TR is strictly superior** unless the unit also wants to use high-burst weapons in the active turn.
---

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
### Total Reaction Practical Tactical Notes
- Deploy TR covering firelanes **with Cover**, or it dies quickly.
- Pair with:
  - **Fairy Dust** (if REM) for +3 BTS vs hacking.
  - **EVO Assist** (Assisted Fire) for **Marksmanship** on many TR REMs—but only if the unit is not already using another Supportware.
- Expect enemies to use:
  - Smoke or White Noise
  - Coordinated Orders
  - Long-range guns with strong mods
- TR is best as a **speed bump**, not a true “unkillable turret.”
