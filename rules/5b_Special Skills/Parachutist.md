# Parachutist

Special Skills 
 LONG SKILL
Airborne Deployment (AD), Optional, Private Information. 

The owner of this Special Skill has been trained to parachute or fast-rope from heliborne vehicles immediately before combat, in order to find an alternative point of ingress onto the battlefield, and to flank the enemy forces

- This Trooper is not required to deploy during the Deployment Phase, remaining off-table until their player decides to deploy them during their Active Turn.

- While off-table, the Trooper will not add their Order to the Order Pool during the Tactical Phase. However, they may enter the table using their own Order to use this Special Skill, when the player chooses.

- By using this Special Skill, the player cannot place the Trooper in Prone State, or in Silhouette contact with enemy and neutral Models, Markers, or Tokens, or in contact with any scenario objective.

- The player may deploy the Trooper on any surface of the game table, as long as that surface's size is equal to or larger than their base and is in contact with the edge of the table.

- They may not be deployed within the enemy Deployment Zone.

- Once the Trooper is placed on their entry point, the Reactive Player declares all their AROs.

- Restriction: During the Order this Special Skill is used, the Trooper cannot benefit from Partial Cover.

- This Special Skill can be used during the Impetuous Phase, which requires removing both the Trooper's Impetuous Token and their own Order.

- While off-table, Troopers with this Special Skill can be selected to deploy as part of a Coordinated Order. However, in this situation they will lose their own Order.

### Parachutist (Deployment Zone)

- Parachutist (Deployment Zone) allows the Trooper to deploy within the enemy Deployment Zone.

Troopers cannot be deployed in contact with enemy and neutral Models, Markers, or Tokens, or in contact with any mission objective, no matter the Special Skills they have.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

## GENERAL FUNCTION
Parachutist provides a controlled form of Airborne Deployment that does not require a PH Roll and does not risk scattering. The unit enters from any table edge (unless they have the Deployment Zone version) during its own activation, spending its own Order. This makes it safer but less flexible in reach compared to Combat Jump.

## INTERACTION NOTES

• Order Economy Interaction
Parachutists do not contribute their Order while off-table. They also must spend their own Order to walk in. 

• ARO Timing Interaction
Once placed at the table edge, all enemy AROs are declared immediately. This means the unit can be shot at before taking its first Short Skill. Because it cannot benefit from Partial Cover during its entry Order, this initial exposure is dangerous. Terrain and LoF planning are critical.

• Prone and Contact Restrictions
Parachutists cannot enter Prone and cannot enter Silhouette contact with enemy models, markers, tokens, or objectives when deploying. This often denies certain “perfect” entry corners. The player must always verify the selected surface is legal in size and not within enemy Deployment Zone unless using the Deployment Zone version of the skill.

• Impetuous Interaction
Parachutist can be used during the Impetuous Phase, but this costs both the Impetuous Token and the model’s own Order. This is a niche play, but sometimes valuable if the timing of a flank entry is vital before Regular Order expenditure.

• Coordinated Order Interaction
Parachutists can enter the table as part of a Coordinated Order, even though they begin off-table. However, in such a group entry, each participating Parachutist loses its own Order for the turn. This is primarily useful for synchronized multi-edge pressure or objective grabs but is expensive in Orders.

• Peripheral Interaction
If a Parachutist has Peripherals without Airborne Deployment, those Peripherals deploy normally during the Deployment Phase and remain Disconnected until the Controller arrives. This forces careful list-building consideration, as the Parachutist will re-connect them only after moving within range.

• Airborne Deployment Label Interaction
Parachutist has the Airborne Deployment Label. Any rule that enhances or penalizes AD applies to it. This includes enemy abilities that modify AD deployment zones or terrain effects that force placement considerations. Unlike Combat Jump, Parachutist is not directly affected by Controlled Jump’s ±3 MODs, because Parachutist does not involve a Roll.

## COMPARISON: PARACHUTIST vs COMBAT JUMP

### Advantages of Parachutist
• Safe entry: No PH Roll, no chance of failure.
• Guarantees arrival exactly where declared along the edge.
• No risk of revealing Hidden Information via failed rolls.
• Ideal for flank specialists, button-pushers, or close-range gunfighters.

### Disadvantages of Parachutist
• Restricted to table edges (unless Deployment Zone version).
• Cannot land in the enemy Deployment Zone unless possessing (Deployment Zone).
• No Partial Cover on the entry Order, making it vulnerable to AROs.
• Often requires long movement paths after arrival compared to Combat Jump.
• Requires spending the unit’s own Order to deploy.

### Advantages of Combat Jump
• Can land anywhere on the table (except illegal areas) with a PH Roll.
• Can bypass long approach paths and deploy directly into midfield or backfield.
• Can threaten objectives or firelanes unreachable to Parachutists.

### Disadvantages of Combat Jump
• Risk of failed PH Rolls if trying to deploy farther than midfield.
• Countered by Controlled Jump, imposing a -3 to land.
• More vulnerable to mishaps.

## MATCHUPS AND TACTICAL CONSIDERATIONS

• Against High-ARO Armies
Parachutist is safer than Combat Jump because it avoids AD landing rolls into heavy ARO zones. However, its entry Order vulnerability means edge lanes must be scouted for safe approach.

• Against Corner-Campers
Parachutists excel at bypassing enemies that rely on frontal defensive arcs. Walking in behind or at an angle can force opponents to waste Orders repositioning.

• Mission-Specific Play
Parachutists are strongest in missions where:
- Side objectives exist near the table edges.
- Spread table control is rewarded.
- Mid-game insertion is more important than Turn 1 alpha strikes.

• Timing
Parachutist units often function best on Turns 2 or 3. This keeps them safe from early alpha strikes and allows opportunistic arrival after enemy forces commit to central zones.

• Synergy with Suppressive Fire Breakers
A Parachutist entering behind an enemy team can dismantle suppressive fire setups by approaching from outside their firing arcs.

• Synergy with Smoke or White Noise
If smoke can be placed near the board edge, it can greatly reduce the risk of the entry AROs. This requires coordination with friendly troopers already on-table.

• Anti-Camo Use
Parachutists are useful for hunting backfield camo units, mines, or skirmishers who think they are safe behind terrain or deployment lines.

• Defensive Use
A Parachutist can be held off-table until an enemy AD troop or infiltrator threatens your backfield. Deploying to intercept them can protect your Lieutenant or vital assets.

## VERSION: PARACHUTIST (DEPLOYMENT ZONE)

### Differences
• Can deploy inside the enemy Deployment Zone.
• Still must enter from the table edge unless otherwise specified.
• Dramatically increases offensive pressure.
• Requires extremely precise timing and usually a coordinated setup to avoid lethal AROs.

### Pros of (Deployment Zone)
• Enables assassination runs.
• Can neutralize enemy support pieces quickly.
• Forces the opponent to defend full width of DZ edges.

### Cons of (Deployment Zone)
• Usually more Order-intensive.
• Very high ARO danger due to proximity to enemy models.
• Still cannot contact enemy models or objectives during deployment.

### COMMON MISTAKES

1. Forgetting the “no Prone on entry” rule.
2. Assuming the model gets Cover during the arrival Order.
3. Attempting to deploy too close to enemy figures or objectives.
4. Forgetting the model’s Order is not in the pool until it arrives.
5. Ignoring vertical surfaces: any valid surface touching the board edge is legal, including rooftops.
6. Forgetting the model’s Order is used to deploy using parachutist and not an order from the Order pool.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
SUMMARY
Parachutist is a stable, low-risk Airborne Deployment method used to exploit flanks, create pincer movements, and open new attack vectors. It trades Combat Jump’s explosive reach for consistency and safety. Players who value reliability and flexible mid-game activation will find Parachutist exceptionally powerful in mission play.
