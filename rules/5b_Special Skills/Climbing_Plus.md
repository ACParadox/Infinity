# Climbing Plus

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Movement, Optional. 

Troopers with Climbing Plus are particularly gifted in or equipped for climbing and vertical relocation.

- At the end of the movement, Troopers must be able to place themselves on a surface that is equal or larger than their base.

- With this Special Skill, the Climb Long Skill is turned into a Basic Short Skill, if declared as the first Basic Short Skill of the Order.

- Climbing Plus also allows the user to move on vertical surfaces as if performing normal Movement on a horizontal surface when declaring Move or Dodge, or during movement due to a failed Guts Roll. The General Movement Rules and Moving and Measuring chapter must be applied.

- The Climbing Plus Special Skill allows its user to perform other Basic Short Skills or Short Skills (e.g. Move + BS Attack ) while moving on a vertical surface, or if hanging from a height (see Trooper Activation ).

- Restriction: The Trooper can only declare Climb as a Basic Short Skill once per Order. If the Trooper then declares Move as their second Basic Short Skill, this uses the second value of their MOV Attribute.

- Restriction: As with the Climb Skill, Troopers using Climbing Plus do not benefit from Partial Cover MODs while they are on a vertical surface.

- For visualization purposes, Troopers using this Skill move with at least half of their base's underside in contact with the vertical surface, as indicated in the Requirements of the Move and Climb Skills.

- During the Reactive Turn, Climbing Plus allows its owner to react in ARO, if applicable, while being on a vertical surface.

- If the Trooper is or becomes Impetuous (due to having the Frenzy Skill or due to any other rule), they will be able to use Climbing Plus in the Impetuous Phase.

Troopers cannot be deployed on vertical surfaces during the Deployment Phase or during the game.

- When declaring the use of Climbing Plus, the complete and exact trajectory that Trooper will perform must be specified so the rival player may declare all their AROs.

- Troopers have a 360° LoF during their entire trajectory.

## Climbing Plus Examples

Spending an Order, the Trooper declares Move as the first Skill of their Order. Thanks to their Climbing Plus Special Skill, as the Trooper moves their first two inches and reaches the obstacle, they may continue their movement on the vertical surface, as if it was a horizontal surface.

Likewise, with the second Skill of their Order, they can choose to keep moving, or can declare any other Short Skill or Basic Short Skill such as BS Attack, Discover, etc.

Since there are no enemies in sight, the Trooper keeps moving.

With a second Order, they declare Move + Move, so they climb down the obstacle and advance until they have used all their movement.

## Climbing Plus FAQs

Q: Can you vault while Climbing or Jumping?

A: Yes. Climb and Jump follow the General Movement Rules, including vaulting.

### Interaction Note
Related rules: ARO, Face-to-Face, Partial Cover.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

Climbing Plus fundamentally changes how a Trooper interacts with vertical surfaces:
- It turns **Climb from a Long Skill into a Basic Short Skill** if used as the first Skill of the Order
- It allows **Move, Dodge, and Guts movement on vertical surfaces as if on horizontal ground**
- The Trooper may **declare other Short Skills (BS Attack, Discover, etc.) while on a vertical surface**
- Vertical movement still requires **declaring the exact trajectory**, granting ARO opportunities along the entire path
- Troopers have **360º LoF during the entire trajectory**
- Troopers **cannot claim Partial Cover while on a vertical surface**
- Unlike normal Climb, **Climbing Plus Troopers *may declare AROs* while on a vertical surface**

---
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### How Climbing Plus Interacts with ARO

**Rules involved**: ARO resolution, LoF arcs during movement, declaring Skills while on vertical surfaces.

- Because the trooper traces an exact path and has **360º LoF during movement**, Climbing Plus enables surprise-angle AROs or Active Turn attacks from positions that would normally lack LoF.
- Enemies may declare AROs **at any point** along the declared trajectory, just as with normal Move.
- The Climbing Plus user **may declare AROs while on a vertical surface**, unlike standard Climb which forbids all AROs because Climb is a Long Skill.

**Practical effect**:  
A Climbing Plus trooper can “round corners” or “creep up” vertically and still shoot, Dodge, Reset, or respond meaningfully in Reactive Turn. Without Climbing Plus, a model hanging from a wall becomes defenseless and easy prey.

---
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### Climbing Plus and Partial Cover

Climbing Plus rule explicitly states:  
- **Troopers using Climbing Plus do not benefit from Partial Cover while on a vertical surface**
**Reason**: Climbing and vertical-hanging positions are inherently “exposed” in Infinity.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### Comparisons: Climbing Plus vs. Normal Climb vs. Super-Jump (Pros & Cons)

**Climbing Plus vs. Climb**
- **Both cannot claim Cover** on vertical surfaces.  
- **Climbing Plus can declare ARO**, Climb cannot (Climb is a Long Skill).  
- **Climbing Plus allows Move, Dodge, BS Attack**, etc. on vertical surfaces; **Climb forbids anything but Climb** while on the wall.  
- **Climbing Plus requires trajectory declaration**, same as Climb.  
**Result**: Climbing Plus is essentially “free movement mode” on walls; Climb is extremely restrictive and often order-inefficient.

**Climbing Plus vs. Super-Jump**
- Both allow **Short Skill attacks during movement** (e.g., Move/Climb + BS Attack vs. Jump + BS Attack).
- Both provide **360º LoF during trajectory** (Super-Jump explicitly, Climbing Plus implicitly by rule)
- Both **cannot claim Partial Cover** while performing their movement Skill.
- **Major difference**:  
  - Super-Jump allows *parabolic* or *diagonal* leaps—including repositioning to non-adjacent surfaces.  
  - Climbing Plus is **restricted to vertical surfaces** and requires the base to remain in contact.
- **Super-Jump is more burst-mobility aggressive**; Climbing Plus is safer for gradual elevation changes.

**Pros of Climbing Plus**:
- Safest vertical mobility in the game (can Dodge, Shoot, Reset on walls).  
- Very strong for peek-and-shoot maneuvers on rooftops.  
- Works during Guts Rolls automatically.

**Cons of Climbing Plus**:
- No Cover on walls.  
- Predictable movement path (must be declared fully).  
- Slower elevation changes than Super-Jump in some terrain layouts.

---
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### Climbing Plus Strategic Notes (Unofficial)

- **Attack from unexpected angles**: Climbing Plus often enables “broken” fire lanes by approaching targets from nearly invisible wall routes.
- **Reactive dominance**: Because the Trooper retains ARO ability while vertical, climbing onto walls in Reactive can create nasty crossfires that force enemies to break LoF.
- **Avoid committing to long climbs**: The trooper is vulnerable to Normal BS Attacks from long-range ARO pieces because they lack Cover.
- **Use Dodge to advance vertically**: Dodge on a wall gives additional safer vertical repositioning when under fire.
