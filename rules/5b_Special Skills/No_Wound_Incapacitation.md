# No Wound Incapacitation

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Optional 

Troopers with this Special Skill have a higher tolerance to Wounds or combat damage.

- The Trooper enters Unconscious State, and his player declares that he is activating the No Wound Incapacitation Special Skill, whether in the Active or Reactive Turn.

- The Unconscious State of Troopers using the No Wound Incapacitation Special Skill has the following special rules: Instead of placing an Unconscious Token next to the Trooper, place an NWI Token. To activate No Wound Incapacitation, the player must announce its use when the Trooper enters Unconscious State. Allows the Trooper to ignore the effects and Null Label of Unconscious State, treating it as Normal State instead. If the Trooper receives another Wound, they enter Dead State directly and are removed from the game table. This Special Skill does not interfere with Automatic Special Skills or Automatic Equipment, which will continue to function normally.

- The user of the No Wound Incapacitation Special Skill can be healed (using Special Skills or Equipment such as Doctor, MediKit, Regeneration, Protheion...), removing one Wound, going back to Normal State, and removing the NWI Token.

- If the Roll to heal the user of the No Wound Incapacitation Special Skill fails, the Trooper automatically enters Dead State and is removed from the game table.

### No Wound Incapacitation and Remote Presence:

- Troopers with Remote Presence, the STR Attribute, and No Wound Incapacitation may activate this Special Skill when they enter any level of Unconscious State, but not if they are already in Unconscious State.

### No Wound Incapacitation and Doctor/Engineer:

- Troopers with No Wound Incapacitation and the Doctor or Engineer Special Skills can use these Skills on themselves, applying the corresponding Effects.

Shock Ammunition cancels the Unconscious State of Troopers using this Special Skill, and they pass directly to Dead State.


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

No Wound Incapacitation (NWI) is a rule that lets a Trooper ignore entering the Unconscious state when they lose their final Wound. Instead, the Trooper remains on the table, active and functional, but in a precarious condition. This often acts like “a virtual extra Wound,” though it does not actually provide additional durability beyond postponing the transition to a Null State.

## INTERACTIONS & COMMON RULE COMBINATIONS
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

- **Doctor / Healing:**  
  A Trooper in NWI cannot be healed or restored by Doctor or MediKit because they are *not* Unconscious.  
  - This is beneficial for Factions lacking good Doctors (e.g., Ariadna), but a drawback for factions that rely heavily on medical recovery.
  
- **Shock Ammunition:**  
  If the Trooper **is not Shock Immune**, Shock ammo bypasses NWI completely.  
  - A Trooper reduced to 0 Wounds by a Shock weapon goes immediately to Dead instead of entering NWI.
  - Therefore, NWI models without Immunity (Shock) must play cautiously around weapons with Shock, Viral ammo, or CC Weapons with Shock.

- **Immunity (Shock):**  
  The best synergy for NWI.  
  - Immunity (Shock) allows the Trooper to enter NWI rather than being killed outright.
  - Many premium or elite Troopers combine these two traits to function almost like pseudo-2-Wound units.

- **Courage / Veteran / Religious Troops:**  
  NWI and these Skills pair naturally since staying conscious at 1 functional Wound greatly benefits from ignoring Guts Rolls.  
  - Helps maintain aggressive positions even after entering NWI.

- **Remote Presence:**  
  RPs with multiple Structure values often outperform NWI because they can be repaired. NWI Troopers cannot.  
  - Compared with Remote Presence + Engineer support, NWI is simpler but less sustainable.

- **Fireteams:**  
  Entering NWI does **not** break Fireteam membership because the Trooper is still active (not in a Null State).  
  - This can preserve bonuses at crucial moments.

- **States:**  
  NWI is a dangerous place to suffer Continuous Damage (ex Fire).  
  - Failing a Save in this state usually kills the model outright.

## HOW NWI PLAYS ON THE TABLE
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

- Acts as a buffer: allows Troopers to take risks normally reserved for multi-Wound units.
- Encourages last-activation aggression: models in NWI are often committed to suicide-run attacks because healing is impossible anyway.
- Increases reliability for mission-focused units: specialists with NWI can press buttons even after being wounded.

However:
- You cannot repair or heal the NWI layer.
- Once you enter NWI, the Trooper is effectively on a timer — any hit may kill them.

## COMPARISON: NWI vs. 2 WOUNDS
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

**NWI (1W + NWI):**
- Pros:
  - Often cheaper than actual 2W.
  - Keeps working at full capacity.
  - Does not require additional markers or rules overhead.

- Cons:
  - Cannot be healed.
  - Vulnerable to Shock if not immune.
  - Vulnerable to Continuous Damage.
  - No benefit against multiple simultaneous hits — if you take more than 1 Wound at once, you may skip NWI and go straight to Dead.

**Real 2W Trooper:**
- Pros:
  - Can heal up to Wound 1.
  - Survives Shock without needing Immunity.
  - More resistant to burst damage.
  - Stronger synergy with defensive mechanics and support.

- Cons:
  - More expensive in points.

**Summary:**  
NWI mimics a second wound but lacks the ability to be healed and has more counterplay. It is budget durability, excellent for elite skirmishers, midfield specialists, and solo attackers.

## COMPARISON: NWI vs. DOGGED
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

These are often confused because both let a Trooper keep fighting after losing their last Wound.

**NWI:**
- The Trooper remains normal and functional.
- Can survive indefinitely (unless killed).
- Does not impose a time limit.
- Costs more than Dogged (typically).
- Can be invaluable for mission running.

**Dogged:**
- Time-limited: Trooper dies at the end of the Turn.
- Cannot cancel Dogged once activated.
- Often cheaper.
- Provides short-term burst aggression but cannot survive after the push.

**Summary:**  
- *NWI = endurance/mission stamina.*  
- *Dogged = suicide run power spike.*  

Both may be balanced by Shock vulnerability.

## PROS & CONS: STRATEGIC OVERVIEW
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

**Pros**
- Great for maintaining tempo in midfield objectives.
- Provides “free” risk-taking during late Turn activations.
- Less dependent on support units than multi-Wound platforms.
- Remains operational even after taking damage that would otherwise remove a model.

**Cons**
- Shock weapons remain a hard counter.
- Cannot be brought back to full Wounds.
- Vulnerable to burst damage and Continuous Damage.
- Makes expensive specialists fragile compared to true 2W pieces.
