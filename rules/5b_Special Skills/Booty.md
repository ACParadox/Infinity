# Booty

Special Skills
 DEPLOYMENT SKILL
Optional 

Soldiers with this Special Skill have an uncanny ability to find equipment and gear during their field operations and take some of it as a keepsake. As a consequence, each of them carries a different extra weapon or piece of equipment into battle.

- Booty allows a roll on the Booty Chart, after placing the Trooper in Model form on the table, in order to determine that Trooper's extra item.

- The item obtained via the Booty Chart is added to the Trooper's gear and does not replace any of the Trooper's weapons or pieces of Equipment.

## FAQs

Q: During your main part of the Deployment Phase, can you reposition Troopers, Deployables, etc?

A: In their main Deployment Phase step, players can reposition Troopers, Deployables, etc. As soon as a Roll or measurement is made for those game elements, their position is fixed, and they cannot be repositioned during the Deployment Phase.

For example, you can reposition a Trooper as many times as you want, but after you create a Fireteam and adjust the placement of the Fireteam members during the Coherency Check, you can no longer reposition any member of that Fireteam.

### Interaction Note
Related rules: Deployment, Weapons, Equipment.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
#### Booty Interaction Considerations

- **Deployment Timing**  
  Booty is resolved *after placing the model on the table*, meaning you must commit to the trooper's deployment position before knowing what item you get. This affects risk-assessment: aggressive forward placement may backfire if you roll a non-weapon or non-defensive result.

- **Skill and Equipment Layering**  
  Booty results that grant equipment stack with the trooper's native skills unless specifically prohibited by Negative Feedback (NFB) or similar labels. None of the Booty items inherently conflict with common skills (e.g., Mimetism or Martial Arts).  
  Example: A trooper with Mimetism (-3) that rolls “Mimetism (-6)” gains the new value; the rules do not specify merging or stacking. The usual interpretation is to replace the profile value with the new one.

- **TAG-Only Results**  
  Several Booty entries provide one result for TAGs and an alternate result for “Other Troop Types,” such as:  
  - “TAG: BS Attack (Shock). Other Troop Types: MULTI Rifle.”  
  This avoids granting inappropriate TAG upgrades to non-TAG troops and ensures every trooper gains something meaningful.
 
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
#### Booty Pros and Cons of Booty (Outcome-Based)

**Pros**
- Potential high-value bonuses (Mimetism-6, MSV1, HMG, MOV 8-4).
- Unexpected weapon loadouts complicate enemy planning.
- Cheap units can spike into disproportionately strong pieces depending on the roll.
- Defensive boosts (ARM+4, ARM+2, total extra weapons) can anchor flanks.

**Cons**
- High variance: some results (e.g., Grenades, +1 ARM) may not fit the trooper’s intended role.
- Must commit to deployment *before* knowing the Booty outcome.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
#### Booty Comparisons with Similar Mechanics

**Booty vs. MetaChemistry**
- *Similarity:* Both are random-roll special skills that provide variable improvements.  
- *Difference:*  
  - Booty grants **equipment / weapons**.  
  - MetaChemistry grants **stat or movement buffs** or temporary abilities.  
- *Pros of Booty:* More direct, reliable utility via new equipment; can drastically change weapon loadout.  
- *Pros of MetaChemistry:* Often improves survivability or mobility continuously without being consumable.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
#### Booty Tactical Notes

- **If you roll MSV1 or Mimetism-6:** the trooper's role shifts to “firelane controller” or hard-to-hit reactive piece. Repositioning is not allowed post-roll, so plan deployments with potential line-of-fire utility in mind.

- **If you roll Panzerfaust / MULTI Sniper / HMG:** consider adjusting your reactive posture to exploit long-range weaponry unexpectedly.

- **If you roll ARM bonuses:** position the trooper to contest objectives or hold exposed corners, leveraging durability.

- **If you roll CCWs (DA/EXP/Mono):** treat the trooper as an ambusher; combine with Engage routes or close-range movement options to threaten heavy units.

- **MOV 8-4:** dramatically improves objective grabbing; pre-plan potential paths so you can immediately exploit this if rolled.
