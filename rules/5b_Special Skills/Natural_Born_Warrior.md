# Natural Born Warrior NBW

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
CC Special Skill 

Troopers with this CC Special Skill are superb fighters, able to cancel the negative MODs that their enemies try to impose on them in Close Combat.

- The user must be the target of a CC Attack and must declare a CC Attack.

- In the CC Face to Face Roll, the user ignores all negative MODs imposed by the opposing Trooper.

A Natural Born Warrior who is the target of a CC Attack from a Trooper with Surprise Attack (-3), Martial Arts Level 3, CC Attack (-3), and PARA CC Weapon (-3) and declares CC Attack themselves does not suffer any of the negative MODs normally caused by these Skills and Weapons in the Face to Face Roll.

# Natural Born Warrior
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

Natural Born Warrior (NBW) is a Close Combat Special Skill that allows a Trooper to ignore *all negative MODs imposed by an opponent* during a CC Face to Face Roll, but only if:
- The NBW user is **the target of a CC Attack**, and  
- The NBW user **declares a CC Attack**.

Because of these requirements, NBW is situational and reactive in nature, but extremely powerful when it triggers.

---
## INTERACTION NOTES
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

### Martial Arts
- NBW *does not cancel* Martial Arts bonuses; it cancels the **negative MODs** an MA user imposes on the opponent.
- Example: An enemy using MA3 usually gives a **–3 CC** or **–3 opponent CC**. NBW cancels these penalties completely.

### Surprise Attack
- NBW cancels **the negative MOD inflicted on the NBW user** by CC Surprise Attack (e.g., –3 or -6)

### CC Attack (-3) Traits
- Any CC weapon imposing a negative MOD (e.g., CC Attack (-3), PARA CCW (-3)) has its penalty ignored by NBW.

### Multiple Combatants
- NBW does **not** cancel the –3 for being attacked by multiple enemies in CC (because that penalty is not imposed by an “opposing Trooper,” but by the *situation*).  
- NBW does **not** modify Burst bonuses or penalties from extra friendly/enemy combatants.

## PROS & CONS
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

### PROS
- **Shuts down most negative MOD stacking** in CC (MA, Surprise Attack, CC Attack (-3), certain PARA weapons).
- Provides **high reliability** in reactive CC, making the NBW user hard to bully in close range.
- Excellent defensive skill for mid-tier CC specialists who otherwise fold under MOD pressure.

### CONS
- **Only works when targeted by a CC Attack**
- **Does not remove environmental or situational MODs**, such as outnumbering penalties.
- **Does not reduce opponent bonuses**, only cancels penalties placed on the NBW user.

## COMPARISON WITH SIMILAR CC SKILLS
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

### Natural Born Warrior vs Martial Arts
**Difference:**  
- MA provides both offensive bonuses (+CC, +Damage, etc.) and imposes penalties.  
- NBW provides defensive cancellation of opponent-imposed penalties only.

**Pros of NBW:**  
- Completely stops the opponent’s negative MODs.

**Pros of MA:**  
- Provides powerful offensive bonuses and additional MOD stacking.

**Typical Use:**  
- NBW is better for **being attacked** in CC.  
- MA is better for **initiating** and killing efficiently.

## TACTICAL APPLICATION
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

- NBW models excel at **holding objectives**, **protecting corners**, and **counter-charging enemies** who rely on MOD stacking.  
- Use NBW units to **engage enemy melee specialists defensively**, forcing even MA5 units to fight “fair.”  
- NBW does *not* give offensive boosts, so pair NBW models with high native CC or strong CCWs for maximum lethality.
