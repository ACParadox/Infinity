# Mnemonica

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Optional 

Mnemonica is a capability displayed only by EI Aspects, further proof of the terrible sophistication of VoodooTech. It allows the Aspect to jump onto an allied vessel's Cube upon destruction of its physical form.

- In order to activate this Special Skill, the user must enter a Null State.

- The user may transfer his own WIP Attribute value and the Mnemonica Special Skill to another Trooper in the same army. That Trooper must be on the battlefield in the form of a Model or Marker and have either a Cube or the REM Troop Type. If the user has the Lieutenant Special Skill this is also transferred, including any additional abilities and MODs such as (+1 Order).

- The new WIP value replaces the host's original WIP value.

- If the Mnemonica Trooper was also the army's Lieutenant, the host will become the new Lieutenant.

- Once activated, this transfer is automatic at the end of the Order in which the user entered the Null State.

- You must identify the Model or Marker that is the new host, but not whether the Lieutenant Special Skill has been transferred, as that is Private Information.

- After the transfer is complete, the original user, now in Dead State, is removed from the game table.

- If the new host enters a Null State, the original user's WIP value, Mnemonica Special Skill and Lieutenant Special Skill may be transferred again to another ally who fulfills the same requirements.

- In addition, Troopers with Mnemonica are never considered to have a Cube.

Isolated State is not a Null State, so does not trigger Mnemonica.

# Mnemonica — Interaction Notes & Commentary (Infinity N5)

## Core Concept
Mnemonica allows certain EI/Combined Army units (e.g., Aspect units, EI-linked TAGs) to transfer their **WIP value** and the **Mnemonica Special Skill** to another eligible Trooper **when the user enters a Null State**.  
It is a unique resilience mechanic that overlaps in function with Chain of Command, but behaves differently and has distinct tactical implications.

## Valid Transfer Targets
A valid recipient must:
- Be on the battlefield **as a Model or Marker**.
- Possess a **Cube** *or* be a **REM Troop Type**.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
This grants flexibility:
- Even an Unconscious REM can receive the transfer (still a Model on the table).  
- Hidden Deployment markers are allowed (they are Markers).  
- Camouflage/Impersonation markers are allowed.

Illegal targets:
- Troopers without a Cube and not REM (e.g., many Morats, Taigha, Dogs, Ariadna units).  
- Off-table units (Airborne Deployment not deployed yet).  
- Peripherals (markers not representing a Trooper Model/Marker, unless specifically considered Troopers).
---

## Interactions with Lieutenant Status
Because Mnemonica can transfer WIP and the Skill itself, the new Trooper may then become (or remain) **Lieutenant**, depending on Army List legality.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Mnemonica is *not the same as Chain of Command*.  
It:
- Does **not** automatically appoint a Lieutenant.
- Does not occur **during** the Loss of Lieutenant check—only when the user enters Null.
- Can *preemptively* avoid LoL **if** the Aspect is killed before the check.

This often leads to timing situations:
- If your Lieutenant dies during the opponent’s turn and triggers Mnemonica, your new Mnemonica host will carry the transferred WIP into your Tactical Phase and avoid LoL.
- If your Lieutenant is Isolated instead of killed, Mnemonica does nothing and you suffer Loss of Lieutenant.

---

## Interaction: Mnemonica vs Chain of Command

### Similarities
- Both can prevent or respond to Loss of Lieutenant.
- Both allow a new Lieutenant to arise without Order cost.

### Differences (UNOFFICIAL COMMENT)
- **Trigger Timing:**  
  - Mnemonica: **When entering Null State**.  
  - CoC: **At the Loss of Lieutenant check**, independent of unit dying.
- **Effect:**  
  - Mnemonica transfers **WIP and the Skill itself**.  
  - CoC simply **appoints a Lieutenant**.
- **Target Restrictions:**  
  - Mnemonica requires Cube/REM and Model/Marker on table.  
  - CoC only works on the CoC model, no transfer choice.
- **Resilience:**  
  - Mnemonica gives you multiple possible backup hosts.  
  - CoC is a single point of failure.

### Pros of Mnemonica
- High WIP transfer, potentially creating a much stronger Lieutenant.  
- Can jump onto Hidden Deployment units without revealing them.  
- Works on REMs (non-vulnerable to many effects).  
- Can protect order efficiency even after Lieutenant death.

### Cons of Mnemonica
- Requires the user to enter a **Null State**, meaning the powerful Aspect/TAG must be destroyed for the effect to occur.  
- Does **nothing** against Isolated or certain “non-lethal” Lt-denial tools.  
- Vulnerable to opponents controlling timing (e.g., killing Lt *after* your Loss of Lieutenant check).  
- Can accidentally place WIP on a suboptimal target if few valid recipients remain.

---
Mnemonica is a powerful, flexible, and uniquely Combined Army tool that enhances command resilience but relies on correct timing and awareness of Null vs Isolated states. Its ability to transfer high WIP to stealthy or durable units gives significant strategic depth, making it one of the most tactically rewarding mechanics in the game—when properly understood and planned around.
