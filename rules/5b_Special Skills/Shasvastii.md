# Shasvastii

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Obligatory 

Racial Special Skill that represents the characteristic behavior of Shasvastii combatants when they fall Unconscious.

- The Trooper enters Unconscious State, and his player declares that he is activating the Shasvastii Special Skill, whether in the Active or Reactive Turn.

- The Unconscious State of Troopers using the Shasvastii Special Skill (usually referred to as 'Shasvastii-Embryo') has the following special rules: Instead of placing an Unconscious Token next to the Trooper, place a Shasvstii-Embryo Token. To activate Shasvastii, the player must announce its use when the Trooper enters Unconscious State. During the game, the Trooper is counted when calculating Victory Points for the Retreat! situation, and for any other rules or conditions required by the scenario. At the end of the game, the Trooper's Unconscious State loses these special rules, so they are not counted when calculating Victory Points.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

## Core Behavior
Instead of entering standard Unconscious, the Trooper becomes a Shasvastii-Embryo. During the game, the Embryo still counts for Victory Points (including Retreat! checks and any scenario that counts surviving troopers), but at the end of the game these extra protections turn off and the trooper no longer counts as surviving unless brought back to Normal State.

## Interactions

### Shock Ammunition
Shock Ammunition cancels the Unconscious State and sends Shasvastii directly to Dead.  

### Scenarios That Count Victory Points (e.g., Domination)
Shasvastii specifically continue to count for Victory Points while in the Embryo state during the game, which matters for scenarios like Domination.  
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
This effectively gives Shasvastii models “sticky” presence in zones. A defeated Shasvastii can still deny a quadrant or secure one until game end. Smart opponents must confirm kills with Shock or expend an extra Order to finish them off. This forces the enemy to invest resources, helping Shasvastii maintain board control even while losing firefights.

### Interactions with Retreat!
Shasvastii Embryos still count as alive for Retreat! calculations.  
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
This often delays or prevents Retreat! for Combined Army lists using Shasvastii units. In some cases, it allows a battered force to keep operating normally for an extra turn because many “dead” Shasvastii still contribute their point value until actually removed from the table.

### Doctor/Engineer, Regeneration, etc.
Shasvastii remain Unconscious (Embryo) and therefore are eligible for healing effects that cancel Unconscious.  

## Tactical Notes

### Board Control After Being “Killed”
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Shasvastii can continue to deny objective zones, block enemy domination, or anchor scoring even when defeated. Deploying them aggressively can force the opponent into bad trades simply to clean up bodies.


### Closing out Games
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Shasvastii shine in missions that score every Round. They continue scoring after being “killed,” forcing the opponent to expend extra Orders to confirm kills. In the final Round, reviving an Embryo can swing multiple points at once.
