# Immunity

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Obligatory 

Troopers with this Special Skill have an extraordinary innate or artificial capacity to withstand specific types of damage. There are several Immunities categories.

This Special Skill is activated when one of the following Requirements is met:

- The Trooper must make a Saving Roll.

- The Trooper is affected by the State indicated in their Immunity.

This Special Skill has different categories shown between brackets in the Unit's Profile, which indicates to which type of Ammunition or Effect it is applied. The most common are:

- Immunity (Trait): The Trooper ignores the special effects of the listed Trait.

- Immunity (Ammunition): The Ammunition listed between brackets is treated as Normal (N) Ammunition. Its special effects, and any MODs to the Attribute and number of the Saving Rolls are ignored.

- Immunity (ARM): Any Ammunition that requires a Saving Roll using the ARM Attribute is treated as Normal (N) Ammunition. Its special effects, and any MODs to the Attribute and number of the Saving Rolls are ignored. If the Weapon, Skill, or piece of Equipment used in the Attack has Traits, the Trooper is completely immune to any of those Traits that cause States, refer to inflicting Wounds, or reduce the Attributes of the target. For example, State: Dead, Continuous Damage, or ARM=0.

- Immunity (BTS): Any Ammunition that requires a Saving Roll using the BTS Attribute is treated as Normal (N) Ammunition. Its special effects, and any MODs to the Attribute and number of the Saving Rolls are ignored. If the Weapon, Skill, or piece of Equipment used in the Attack has Traits, the Trooper is completely immune to any of those Traits that cause States, refer to inflicting Wounds, or reduce the Attributes of the target. For example, State: Dead, Continuous Damage, or BTS=0.

- Immunity (Enhanced): A Trooper with this Special Skill has both Immunity (ARM) and Immunity (BTS).

- Immunity (State): the Trooper is completely immune to any Comms Attack effect or special rule effect that causes the listed State, for example Possessed (POS). The Trooper cannot enter that State.

- Immunity (Critical): The Trooper will not make the additional Saving Roll caused by Critical hits.

This Special Skill does not apply against Comms Attacks, with the sole exception of Immunity (State).

Also, Immunity does not apply against the Non-Lethal and State: Stunned Traits, which are always applied.

## Immunity and Critical Hits

If a Trooper suffers a Critical hit with the Ammunition, effect, Attribute, State, etc. which it is immune to, unless they have Immunity (Critical), they will have to make the additional Saving Roll, but ignoring any Special Effects of the Ammunition, since it is treated as Normal (N) Ammunition.

Vulnerability A Trooper with Immunity (Enhanced) and Vulnerability (Viral) cannot apply the effects of Immunity (Enhanced) (treating all Ammunition types as Normal Ammunition and ignoring their special effects) to any weapon with "Viral" in its name.

Example 1 A Trooper with Immunity (ARM) is hit by a Missile Launcher. Thanks to their Immunity (ARM), instead of making three Saving Rolls due to the EXP Ammunition, they only have to make one Saving Roll using their ARM Attribute, since the hit is treated as being made with Normal Ammunition.

Example 2 A Trooper with Immunity (ARM) is hit with a Monofilament CC Weapon. Thanks to their Immunity (ARM), they only make one Saving Roll, ignoring the Weapon's ARM=0 and State: Dead Traits, so they do not reduce their ARM to 0 and the Dead State is not applied if the Saving Roll is failed.

If the impact is a Critical, the Trooper makes two Saving Rolls, ignoring the Weapon's ARM=0 and State: Dead Traits.

Example 3 A Trooper with Immunity (Enhanced) suffers an impact from a Plasma Rifle. We must remember that Immunity (Enhanced) avoids the special effects of any ammunition, treating it as Normal Ammunition.

Plasma weapons use Normal Ammunition, but their Saving Roll is ARM + BTS, so the Trooper with Immunity (Enhanced) must make a Saving Roll against ARM and also a Saving Roll against BTS.


## Immunity and Combined Saving Roll

Example 4 A Trooper with Immunity (BTS) is hit by a Flash Pulse. Thanks to their Immunity (BTS), the hit is treated as being made with Normal Ammunition. However, the Non-Lethal Trait of the Weapon, indicating that it does not inflict Wounds, is applied. In addition, the State: Stunned Trait is applied if the Trooper fails their Saving Roll.

## Immunity General Interaction Notes

- Immunity only activates when:
  - The Trooper must make a Saving Roll.
  - The Trooper is affected by a State listed in their Immunity (e.g. Immunity (POS)).

- - Immunity turns the affected Ammunition or Trait into **Normal (N)** for the purpose of:
  - Ignoring special effects (extra rolls, special States, exceptions to ARM/BTS, etc.)
  - Ignoring any Attribute MODs or roll-multipliers that that ammunition normally causes.

- When hit by something they are *immune to*, a Trooper **still suffers Critical extra rolls** unless they have Immunity (Critical).

## Immunity Notes on Each Immunity Type

### Immunity (Trait)
- Cancels only the *special effects* of the listed Trait—does not convert the Ammunition into Normal.
- Useful when the Trait is the real threat (e.g., ignoring Continuous Damage).

### Immunity (Ammunition)
- Treats that specific Ammunition Type as Normal.
- Great against DA, EXP, AP, or similar high-impact ammo types.
- Does not protect against Traits attached to the weapon unless those Traits require the Saving Roll that is being replaced.

### Immunity (ARM)
- Converts *any* ARM-based Saving Roll to Normal:
  - Affects all Ammunition requiring ARM (including DA, EXP, AP, etc.).
  - Ignores Traits of the weapon that cause States or reduce ARM.
- Does *not* protect against BTS-based weapons.

### Immunity (BTS)
- Same as above, but for BTS.
- Particularly valuable against Flash Pulse, Viral weaponry, and many Hacking-like effects that force BTS Rolls.

### Immunity (Enhanced)
- Grants both Immunity (ARM) and Immunity (BTS).
- Very strong general-purpose protection.
- Still does not protect against ammo types that do not force ARM or BTS Rolls (e.g., PARA).

### Immunity (State)
- Prevents entering the specified State entirely.
- Works even though Immunity usually does not function against Comms Attacks.

### Immunity (Critical)
- Cancels the extra Saving Roll from Critical hits.
- Does *not* cancel the hit; only cancels the extra roll.

## Immunity Important Interactions

### Immunity vs. Critical Hits
- A Trooper immune to the relevant ammo still rolls the **extra Critical Saving Roll**, unless they also have Immunity (Critical).
- The extra roll is treated as Normal (N) as well.

### Immunity vs. Continuous Damage
- If the Trooper has Immunity to the ammo type or Trait causing the Continuous Damage, they completely ignore the follow-up automatic hits.
- Especially relevant for Fire, Plasma (traits), or other weapons with Continuous Damage.

### Immunity vs. Plasma
- Plasma uses Normal Ammunition but forces **ARM + BTS** Rolls.
- Immunity (Enhanced) strips Plasma of its special effect and forces **one ARM Roll and one BTS Roll**, both as Normal (N).
- Immunity (ARM) or Immunity (BTS) provides partial protection—only half of the dual-roll is simplified.

### Immunity vs. Monofilament
- Monofilament normally forces BTS and sets ARM to 0, with Dead State on failure.
- Immunity (ARM) ignores this entirely and treats it as Normal (N) ARM Roll.
- A dramatic example of how Immunity can completely change a weapon’s lethality.

### Immunity vs. AP / DA / EXP
- Immunity (ARM) or (Ammunition) removes all special effects:
  - DA becomes one roll.
  - EXP becomes one roll.
  - AP becomes one roll using full ARM.

### Immunity vs. E/M
- E/M forces BTS Rolls with half BTS and applies Isolation (and IMM-B for HI, TAG, REM, VEH).
- Immunity (BTS) converts it to Normal (N), so:
  - No halving of BTS.
  - No Isolation.
  - No IMM-B.

### Immunity vs. State: Possessed
- Immunity (POS) prevents entering the Possessed State entirely.
- Works despite being a Comms Attack because it is a *State* Immunity.

## Immunity Common Tactical Implications

- Immunity often determines target priority. Troops with Immunity (Enhanced) or (ARM) can shrug off weapons that normally dominate the table.
- Attackers should switch to weapons with Traits that Immunity does NOT stop—Comms Attacks, PARA, or Stunned are common counters.
- Remember that even with Immunity, the *base* ammunition still deals damage—Immunity never prevents the normal N ammunition effect.
