# MetaChemistry

Special Skills
 DEPLOYMENT SKILL
Optional 

This Special Skill provides its user with random advantages and Special Skills that represent a plethora of combat drug treatments and biotechnological augmentations

- Immediately after placing the Trooper with MetaChemistry in Model form on the table, you may roll once on the MetaChemistry Table to determine that Trooper's extra MOD or Special Skill.

- Special Skills obtained via the MetaChemistry Chart are added to the Trooper's Skills, and do not replace any of them.

## MetaChemistry FAQs

Q: During your main part of the Deployment Phase, can you reposition Troopers, Deployables, etc?

A: In their main Deployment Phase step, players can reposition Troopers, Deployables, etc. As soon as a Roll or measurement is made for those game elements, their position is fixed, and they cannot be repositioned during the Deployment Phase.

For example, you can reposition a Trooper as many times as you want, but after you create a Fireteam and adjust the placement of the Fireteam members during the Coherency Check, you can no longer reposition any member of that Fireteam.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Timing & Deployment Interactions
- MetaChemistry **always triggers at deployment**—specifically *after placing the model* but before the player finalizes any effect that would lock its position (such as Fireteam coherency checks during placement).
- You **cannot reposition the model after the roll** if any roll or measurement has already been made for the unit during deployment.
- If the Trooper deploys using skills like **Hidden Deployment**, **Combat Jump**, or **Parachutist**, the MetaChemistry roll still happens **when the Trooper enters the table as a model**, not when reserved off-table.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Comparison Notes — MetaChemistry vs. Booty
Both MetaChemistry and Booty are “random advantage” generation skills.  
**Differences:**  
- MetaChemistry always grants *Self-buffs / special movement / survivability traits*; Booty modifies *equipment and weapons*.  
- MetaChemistry activates **automatically** when the model is placed. Booty requires rolling at placement as well, but usually affects gear loadout.  
- MetaChemistry results are generally **consistent in usefulness**, while Booty is **higher variance**, containing both weak and extremely strong results.  

**Pros of MetaChemistry vs Booty:**  
- More predictable spread of upgrades.  
- Fewer “dead” results.  
- Always beneficial to the unit’s core performance.  

**Cons of MetaChemistry vs Booty:**  
- Less dramatic power spikes; MetaChemistry rarely gives something as impactful as ODD, EXP CCW, or Panzerfaust.  
- Movement and PH boosts may be irrelevant depending on mission or Fireteam usage.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Which MetaChemistry Results Are Strongest?
**Top-tier results (unofficial evaluation):**  
- **No Wound Incapacitation (15)** — Stable survivability boost; combines well with Doctors and Engineers.  
- **Dogged + Immunity (Enhanced) (17)** — Extremely high pressure in gunfights; enables reckless trading.  
- **MOV 8-4 + Climbing Plus (19)** — Massive mobility, particularly for solo attackers or suppression breakers.  
- **+3 PH + Regeneration (20)** — Great for Dodge-heavy troops or high-risk melee units.

**Mid-tier results:**  
- **Super-Jump (4-5)** — Excellent, but situational depending on the table.  
- **Climbing Plus (8-10)** — Always helpful, but less impactful on naturally mobile units.  
- **MOV 6-4 + Super-Jump (18)** — Good but limited by Fireteam considerations.

**Lower-tier but still useful:**  
- **+3 PH (1-3)** — Solid for Dodging and Discover+Shoot sequences.  
- **+1 ARM + Immunity (ARM) (16)** — Good durability but less dramatic.


## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Tactical Advice & Pros/Cons
### Pros
- 100% chance of gaining a beneficial upgrade.  
- Some results dramatically increase survivability or mobility.  
- Very strong on solo attack pieces or disposable shock troops.  
- Many results synergize with high-risk playstyles (Dogged, Regeneration).

### Cons
- Randomness makes planning more difficult.  
- Movement upgrades often wasted in Fireteams.  
- Durability boosts may have limited impact if the Trooper is not used aggressively.  
- Dogged and NWI results can shift your plan but may encourage overly risky play.
