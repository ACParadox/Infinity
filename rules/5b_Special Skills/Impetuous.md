# Impetuous

> **N5.3 change (September 2026):** The Impetuous restrictions were clarified: an Impetuous Trooper cannot enter **Marker States or any other State whose rule prohibits it**. During an Impetuous activation, declaring **Move, Jump, or Climb cancels Prone**, requires the applicable full movement, and the Trooper cannot enter Prone at the end of that movement.

Special Skills
AUTOMATIC SKILL
Impetuous Phase, Obligatory.

Impetuous Troopers are always eager to engage in combat: the closer and the nastier, the better.

- During the Tactical Phase, place an Impetuous Token next to every Trooper with this Skill. Remove it when that Trooper is activated in the Impetuous Phase or at the end of the Impetuous Phase if unused.
- During the Impetuous Phase, each Impetuous Trooper may be activated once without spending an Order from the Order Pool. Removing the Impetuous Token counts as spending an Order on that Trooper and follows the Order Expenditure Sequence.
- Impetuous activations allow only the Skill combinations listed by the Impetuous rule: Move + CC Attack, Move + BS Attack, Move + Dodge, Move + Idle, Move + Move, Jump, Climb, Berserk, Skills with the Airborne Deployment Label, and Skills that specifically say they can be used during the Impetuous Phase.
- When declaring a Skill with the Movement Label during the Impetuous Phase, the Trooper must move the full applicable MOV distance and apply the Impetuous movement priorities: first enter Silhouette contact with an Enemy if possible; otherwise move toward the Enemy Deployment Zone without doubling back, using the complete movement and ending as close to the Enemy Deployment Zone as possible. If the Trooper cannot end closer, it performs Idle. Once inside the enemy Deployment Zone, it can move normally while remaining inside it.
- A shorter movement is allowed only where the Impetuous rule permits it, such as reaching Silhouette contact with an Enemy or being constrained by Special Terrain / a required Jump or Climb.
- **N5.3:** When declaring Move, Jump, or Climb in the Impetuous Phase, the Trooper cancels Prone State, moves the full applicable value, and cannot enter Prone State at the end of the movement.
- **N5.3:** Impetuous Troopers cannot enter Marker States (Camouflaged, Impersonation, etc.) or any other State whose own rule prohibits it.
- Players in Retreat! do not carry out the Impetuous Phase during their Turn.

## Impetuous and Frenzy

When the Frenzy requirements are met, apply Frenzy's current rules for gaining Impetuous and Limited Cover in the appropriate States Phase.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
The N5.3 wording matters because the prohibition is not an open-ended list invented by the Impetuous rule. Marker States are explicitly barred, and other States are barred when their own rules say so. Also, an Impetuous Move/Jump/Climb cannot be used to remain or finish Prone.
