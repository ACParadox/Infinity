Impetuous Phase, Obligatory. 

Impetuous Troopers are always eager to engage in combat: the closer and the nastier, the better.

- During the Turn's Tactical Phase, the player will place an Impetuous Token next to every Trooper with this Special Skill. This Token will be removed when the Trooper is activated, or at the end of the Impetuous Phase for any Trooper that has not been activated.

- During the Turn's Impetuous Phase, the player may activate each Impetuous Trooper once, without spending an Order. However, removing the Impetuous Token to activate the Trooper counts as spending an Order on them, applying the Order Expenditure Sequence normally.

- Restriction: Impetuous activations only allow a fixed set of Skill combinations: Move + CC Attack. Move + BS Attack. Move + Dodge. Move + Idle. Move + Move. Jump. Climb. Berserk. Skills with the Airborne Deployment Label. Those Skills that specify they can be used during the Impetuous Phase.

- Restriction: In the Impetuous Phase, when declaring a Skill with the Movement Label, the Trooper must always move the full corresponding MOV value, attempting to perform the first of these options that the Trooper can complete: Enter Silhouette contact with an Enemy Trooper during this move. Go towards the Enemy Deployment Zone without doubling back from the movement's starting position, applying the following priorities: 1. Use their complete MOV value. 2. End their movement as close to the Enemy Deployment Zone as possible. 3. If they cannot end their movement closer to the enemy Deployment Zone, the Trooper performs an Idle. Once inside the enemy Deployment Zone, the Trooper can move normally as long as they stay inside it.

- Troopers may only move a shorter distance if they reach Silhouette contact with an Enemy or a Special Terrain area hinders their Movement or forces them to declare Jump or Climb.

- In the Impetuous Phase, when declaring Move, Jump, or Climb, the Trooper must cancel Prone State, move the full corresponding MOV value, and cannot enter Prone State at the end of the movement.

- Restriction: Impetuous Troopers cannot enter Marker States ( Camouflaged, Impersonation...), or any other States that say so.

## Core Behaviour Commentary
Impetuous provides a *free activation* in the Impetuous Phase, but with strict limitations:
- Only specific Skills are allowed (Move + BS Attack, Move + CC Attack, Move + Dodge, Move + Idle, Move + Move, Jump, Climb, Berserk, etc.).
- Movement must use the *full MOV value* and must prioritise either:
  1. Reaching Silhouette contact with an enemy, or  
  2. Moving directly toward the enemy Deployment Zone (without doubling back).
- The Trooper cannot enter or remain Prone during Impetuous movement.
- Impetuous Troopers lose access to any State that prohibits Impetuous (e.g., Camouflaged, IMP-1/IMP-2, Suppressive Fire).

This makes Impetuous an aggressive, often forced behaviour: the Trooper moves forward unless a legal movement path toward the DZ or enemy contact is blocked.

## Impetuous - Interactions With Other Rules

### Impetuous - Fireteams
A Trooper **activated during the Impetuous Phase automatically leaves a Fireteam**.  
This includes the Fireteam Leader (triggering Fireteam cancellation).  
Practical notes:
- Players generally **avoid** Impetuous activations for Fireteam members unless the Fireteam is already broken or the member is irrelevant.
- This is one of the most punishing interactions for Warbands in mixed Fireteams.

###  Camouflage / Impersonation
Becoming Impetuous **cancels**:
- Camouflaged State
- Impersonation-1 / Impersonation-2

If the Trooper *gains* Impetuous (e.g., from Frenzy), they immediately lose these States and cannot re-enter them.
If the Trooper *already is* Impetuous, they **cannot** enter these Marker States at any time.

### Impetuous - Frenzy
Frenzy converts into **Impetuous + Limited Cover** the *States Phase after inflicting a Wound*.  
Notes:
- They immediately cancel Camouflage/Impersonation.
- They stop receiving Partial Cover (Limited Cover rule).
- Frenzy creates mid-game Impetuous units that were not Impetuous at deployment.

### Retreat!
In Retreat!, **the Impetuous Phase is skipped**.  
Impetuous Troopers **do not** receive free activations.

### Movement Edge Cases
- If the Trooper reaches Silhouette contact with an enemy during *any* legal path, they must stop—even if undesirable.
- If the Trooper cannot end the movement closer to the enemy DZ, they must Idle.
- Move is *mandatory* unless completely impossible.

### Peripherals
If the Controller becomes Impetuous, the Peripherals also become Impetuous and obey the same restrictions.

### Prone
Impetuous movement **always cancels Prone**, and the trooper cannot become Prone at the end of its Impetuous move.

### Airborne Deployment
Skills with the Airborne Deployment label may be used during the Impetuous Phase.  
This includes Parachutist, but still requires spending the Trooper’s own Order in addition to removing the Impetuous Token.

## 3. Tactical Pros & Cons

### Pros
- **Free Orders**: An extra activation each turn can dramatically improve order efficiency.
- **Fast board pressure**: Forced forward motion is advantageous for Warbands, melee troops, and templates.
- **Threat Projection**: Forces opponents to respond to aggressive early positioning.
- **Synergy with Smoke / Templates**: Move + BS Attack during Impetuous is often used for Smoke Grenades, Chain Rifles, etc.

### Cons
- **Forced Movement**: The Trooper may move into firelanes or bad positions unwillingly.
- **No Marker States**: Makes the Trooper vulnerable to ranged fire and removal.
- **Cannot stay Prone**: Harder to safely approach objectives or rooftops.
- **Fireteam Breakage**: Impetuous Troopers cannot safely belong to Fireteams unless their Impetuous activation is skipped.

## Impetuous - Common Decision Points (Why to Cancel or Use Impetuous)

### Reasons to *Use* an Impetuous activation:
- Safe Smoke / Speculative fire entry.
- Advancing toward midfield objectives.
- Moving into chain rifle range.
- No enemy AROs watching the path.
- Trooper is cheap and expendable.

### Reasons to *Avoid* an Impetuous activation:
- Strong enemy ARO pieces covering the movement path.
- The move would break a Fireteam.
- The Trooper would move off an objective or critical position.
- The Trooper would be exposed with no cover.
- Using the Irregular order strategically later is more valuable.

## Comparison: Impetuous vs Frenzy

**Impetuous (native)**  
- Present at deployment.  
- Cannot ever enter Marker States, Suppressive Fire, etc.  
- Always aggressive from the beginning.

**Frenzy (conditional Impetuous)**  
- Gains Impetuous only after inflicting a Wound.  
- Can start the game conservatively (e.g., benefiting from cover).  
- Becomes more aggressive *mid-game*, often unpredictably.  
- Forces the player to plan threats: “Is it worth triggering Frenzy this turn?”

**Pros of Native Impetuous**  
- Predictable from Turn 1.  
- Free order every turn.  

**Cons of Native Impetuous**  
- Hard to integrate into Fireteams.  
- No Marker States ever.  

**Pros of Frenzy**  
- Can start the game safe and defensive.  
- Only becomes Impetuous once it has already contributed meaningfully.  

**Cons of Frenzy**  
- Loss of Cover can be very damaging for mid-cost units.  


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
## Impetuous - Practical Tips
- Use Impetuous movement when **no enemy LoF** covers the route.  
- Check whether “toward the enemy DZ” allows you to sidestep into a safe corridor.  
- Consider smoke to cover the forced movement.  
- Don’t forget you may **choose not to activate** an Impetuous Trooper.
