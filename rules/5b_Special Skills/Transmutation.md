# Transmutation

Special Skills
AUTOMATIC SKILL

This Special Skill represents the user's ability to transform into a different type of Trooper with its own Attributes and Skills.

- The user must meet the condition indicated between brackets next to the Skill.

- Different types of Transmutation are characterized by the cause that triggers the Trooper's transformation, which forces or allows them to use their second Unit Profile.

- When the Trooper transforms, the Model corresponding to the first Unit Profile is replaced with the Model corresponding to the second Unit Profile. If there is no alternative Model, place a Transmuted (TRANSMUTED) Token (or the corresponding Token if there is a specific one) beside the user, indicating their new profile.

- The VITA / STR Attribute points of a Trooper possessing this Special Skill are shared among all of their Unit Profiles. So, any Wounds received by one of the Unit Profiles also apply to the other Unit Profiles.

- Similarly, States are also shared by the different Unit Profiles, if applicable.

### Transmutation (X)

- A Trooper with this Skill is deployed with their first Unit Profile.

- The value listed between brackets in the Unit Profile after the name of this Skill specifies how many Wounds the Trooper must receive before changing to their second Unit Profile.

- The Trooper and their initial Unit Profile must be replaced by the second Unit Profile—and the Model representing it—at the end of the Order in which the initial Unit Profile has received at least as many Wounds as the value between brackets after the name of this Skill.

### Transmutation (Escape System-X)

- This works the same way as Transmutation (X), but when the Unit Profile and Model are replaced, an Eclipse Special Ammo Circular Template must be placed, centered on the Model.

- The number of Wounds required to change Unit Profiles is listed in the name of the Skill in brackets after "Escape System".

Update PDF 5.2, October 2025

- If the Trooper is in Possessed State when they change to their second Unit Profile, their Possessed State is canceled.

### Transmutation (Hatching)

- The Trooper with this Skill is deployed with its first Unit Profile, but using a Seed-Embryo Marker instead of a Model.

- If the Trooper has any Skill that allows it to deploy in Marker form, it uses that Marker instead of the Seed-Embryo Token. The Camouflage Marker of a Troop with Transmutation (Hatching) has a Silhouette (S) value of 2. If the Trooper is Discovered or it reveals itself, the Marker is replaced with the Seed-Embryo Token.

- Similarly, if the Trooper has any Deployment Skill with the Airborne Deployment Label, then it will deploy as a Seed-Embryo Token, and it will apply the rules of its Airborne Deployment Skill as usual.

- The Trooper will change to its second Unit Profile and replace the Marker or the Seed-Embryo Token with the Model that corresponds to its Developed Form whenever: It declares a Skill with the Movement Label as the first Short Skill of its Order. When this happens, its second "Developed Form" Unit Profile is used for the entire Order Sequence. It performs a successful Dodge ARO. The Developed Form profile is applied before the Dodge move is performed.

- It declares a Skill with the Movement Label as the first Short Skill of its Order. When this happens, its second "Developed Form" Unit Profile is used for the entire Order Sequence.

- It performs a successful Dodge ARO. The Developed Form profile is applied before the Dodge move is performed.

### Transmutation (Auto)

- The Trooper may deploy using any of their Unit Profiles.

- By performing any Skill with the Movement Label, a Trooper with Transmutation (Auto) may switch between the different Unit Profiles listed in their Unit Profile. This change always happens at the beginning of their Movement, applying the new Unit Profile for the entire sequence of that Movement.

- This will affect the distance the Trooper can move across the game table, depending on the MOV Attribute of the chosen Unit Profile.

### Transmutation (WIP)

- The Trooper with this Skill is deployed with its first Unit Profile.

- When a Trooper activates, before declaring its Order or ARO the Trooper may immediately change to its second Unit Profile by passing a Normal WIP Roll. When this happens, its second Unit Profile is applied for the rest of the current Player Turn. At the start of the States Phase the Trooper changes back to their first Unit Profile.

- If the WIP Roll is failed, the Trooper continues to use its first Unit Profile, but may try again before each of their Orders or AROs.

- If a MOD is listed after the Skill in the Unit Profile, that MOD applies to these WIP Rolls.

Game Sequence and Transmutation (X)/(Escape System-X):

If the number of Wounds received is equal to or higher than the value between brackets after the name of this Skill, the Unit Profiles must be changed after making the Guts Roll.

## Transmutation Examples

A Trooper with Transmutation (2) receives three simultaneous hits. He makes his three Saving Rolls and fails two of them, so he receives 2 Wounds. The Trooper changes to his second Unit Profile and replaces his current Model with the alternative one, placing his Wounds next to it. Thus, the Trooper will remain active on the table, but he still has 2 Wounds. If there is no alternative Model, the Transmuted Token is used to indicate the change of Unit Profile.

A Trooper takes multiple hits and receives 2 Wounds. Since this is the number indicated in his Transmutation (Escape System-2) Skill, the player replaces the Model of the TAG with that of its Operator and places the Eclipse Special Ammo Circular Template centered on the Operator.

If the Trooper is in Stunned and Immobilized-B States when the Unit Profile and the Model are changed, the Trooper retains both State Tokens.

A Trooper with Transmutation (WIP-3) is activated in an ARO. Before declaring the Skill it will perform, its player makes a WIP -3 Roll, and it is successful. Having passed the Roll, he may use the Trooper’s second Unit Profile for this ARO and for the rest of the Player Turn.


## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
Transmutation is one of the most complex transformation mechanics in Infinity because it modifies **unit profiles mid-game while preserving the same Wounds and States**. These interactions create tactical depth but also several edge-case considerations. The notes below highlight these interactions and give unofficial guidance for consistent adjudication.

## INTERACTIONS & EDGE CASE NOTES

### 1. **Shared Wounds Across Profiles**
**Rule basis:** Wounds are shared across all profiles.  
**Effect:**  
- A “fresh” second profile **never starts at full health**; it inherits the exact remaining Wounds.  
- If a trooper transmuted early (e.g., due to Escape System), its second profile may already be near death.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
This often makes Transmutation feel more like a *mode shift* than an extra “health bar.” Players should not think of Transmutation as extra durability—it's *flexibility*, not extra wounds.

### 2. **Shared States (Includes Negative States)**
**Rule basis:** States are shared.  
**Effect:**  
- IMM-A, IMM-B, Targeted, Isolated, Stunned, etc. **all persist between forms.**  
- When a profile changes, **the new form inherits all active State Tokens.**

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
This means Transmutation cannot be used defensively to “shake off” negative States. In particular, a trooper stuck in IMM-B remains stuck regardless of profile.

### 3. **Timing of Profile Changes**
Different Transmutation types specify different moments when the transformation occurs:

- **(X) / Escape System-X** — Triggers **after the Order in which sufficient Wounds are suffered, after Guts.**  

- **(Hatching)** — Triggers when first Movement Skill is declared or on successful Dodge.  

- **(Auto)** — May trigger at the start of any Movement.  
- **(WIP)** — May trigger at activation before declaring the Order.

### 4. **Model Replacement & Facing**
“Replace the model… place it facing any direction.”  

**Impact:**  
- The new form can reorient its facing for free.  
- This can break LoF arcs or avoid back-shots.

### 5. **Escape System-X Eclipse Template**
Placing a **free Eclipse Circular Template** on transformation can:
- Break LoF against incoming attacks on the following Orders.
- Protect linked models.
- Block enemy vision during follow-up activations.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
This is a massive tempo swing. The TAG becomes harder to finish off after losing its outer shell.

### 6. **Deploying in Marker States (Hatching)**
“Hatching” allows deployment as Seed-Embryo or Camouflage Marker (if possessing a Camo-type deployment ability).  
(Silhouette value becomes S2 even if normally smaller.)  

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
This allows some Shasvastii to essentially “double mask” themselves—appearing first as a broad S2 marker, then “emerging” into their real profile.


### 7. **Interactions with Healing (Doctor/Engineer/Regeneration)**
Because Wounds are shared, any healing effect:
- Benefits both forms equally.
- May allow the Trooper to “backtrack” from a transformed state only for (WIP) profiles, not for (X) or (Auto).

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Some players mistakenly believe they can “heal back into” the first profile. **This is not supported by N5 rules** except for Transmutation (WIP), which automatically reverts during the States Phase.

## COMPARISON OF TRANSMUTATION TYPES (PROS/CONS)

### **Transmutation (X)**
**Pros:**  
- Simple and predictable.  
- Strong “two-mode” identity.  

**Cons:**  
- Offers no defensive benefits.  
- Still shares States, so IMM-B “locks” both forms.


### **Transmutation (Escape System-X)**
**Pros:**  
- Eclipse is extremely powerful.  
- Excellent survivability spike.  

**Cons:**  
- Still dependent on suffering Wounds.  
- Vulnerable to Shock (if not protected by Immunity).


### **Transmutation (Hatching)**
**Pros:**  
- Strong deployment advantage.  
- Hard to target early.  
- Dodge-triggered transformation is flexible.

**Cons:**  
- Vulnerable to Discover chains.  
- Seed-Embryo form cannot act meaningfully until transformation.


### **Transmutation (Auto)**
**Pros:**  
- Maximum mobility flexibility.  
- Functions like adaptable “stances.”

**Cons:**  
- Requires careful measurement to avoid invalid activations.  
- Enemies may exploit predictable profile-switch moments.


### **Transmutation (WIP)**
**Pros:**  
- Player-controlled activation.  
- Reverts automatically—great for cycling abilities.  

**Cons:**  
- Can fail the WIP roll at inconvenient times.  
- Requires planning across Player Turn boundaries.


## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT — GENERAL TACTICAL GUIDANCE]

- **Transmutation is best viewed as toolbox design**, not durability enhancement.  
- **Always consider the enemy’s ability to force State Tokens**; these will carry across profiles.  
- Players should discuss model swapping to avoid accidental measurement or information leaks.  
- For tournament clarity, it is recommended to keep **both profile cards visible** to the opponent at all times once the first transformation occurs.
