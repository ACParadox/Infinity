- This Special Skill grants its user a Tactical Order in addition to the one provided by their Training Characteristic (Regular or Irregular).

- During the Order Count step, if the user is on the game table as a Model or Marker, the player places a Tactical Order beside the user.

# Tactical Awareness  
(from: Tactical_Awareness.md)

## Official Rule Text (Quoted)

This Special Skill defines a higher knowledge of the tactical environment allowing its user a greater operational capability, providing him with an extra Tactical Order.  


- This Special Skill grants its user a Tactical Order in addition to the one provided by their Training Characteristic (Regular or Irregular).  


- During the Order Count step, if the user is on the game table as a Model or Marker, the player places a Tactical Order beside the user.  


- If the Trooper is placed on the game table during the Orders Phase, a Tactical Order must be placed next to the Trooper who has this Special Skill.  


- If the Trooper with this Special Skill is a member of a Fireteam and is designated as the Team Leader, this Order can be used to activate the Fireteam.  


Additional order context:  
Tactical Orders are special Orders kept by the Trooper which generates them for their own use, though they may also be spent for advanced rules and for Fireteams.  


Tactical Orders are placed during Order Count alongside Regular, Irregular, and Lieutenant Orders if the Trooper is on-table and not in a Null State.  


---

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]

Tactical Awareness (TA) is essentially a **built-in extra Order** that behaves similarly to an Irregular Order but with key differences. It only belongs to the TA Trooper, but can be used in special ways (like Fireteam activation).

This makes TA one of the most efficient order-generation skills in the game, especially for high-cost units such as TAGs and elite Heavy Infantry.

---

## Tactical Awareness Interactions Notes  
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

- **Fireteams:**  
  TA Orders *can* be spent to activate a Fireteam **only when the TA Trooper is the Team Leader**. This makes TA especially potent providing an extra activation without drawing from the group’s Regular Orders.

- **Hidden Deployment / AD / Off-table:**  
  TA only generates its Order **once the model is on the table**. A Trooper entering from Parachutist or Hidden Deployment will place their TA Order immediately upon arrival, even mid-turn.

- **Null States:**  
  If the Trooper is Unconscious or otherwise Null during Order Count, the TA Order is **not generated**.

- **Command Token stripping:**  
  TA Orders **count toward the “total Orders” number** used for Strategic Turn 1 order stripping (the “more than 10 Orders” limit).  
  This can make a TA-heavy list more vulnerable to losing two Regular Orders on Turn 1.

- **NCO:**  
  TA Orders remain separate from Lieutenant Orders.  
  NCO cannot use a TA Order, and TA cannot be converted to Lieutenant Order or vice versa.

- **Irregular Troopers:**  
  TA works on both Regular and Irregular troops.  
  An Irregular trooper with TA will have **two personal Orders**: their Irregular + their TA.

## Comparison: Tactical Awareness vs. Irregular vs. Lieutenant Orders  
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

**Tactical Order (from TA):**

- Belongs only to its Trooper.  
- Cannot be transferred to the Order Pool.  
- Can activate Fireteams (only if TA user is Team Leader).  
- Very predictable: always available as long as the model is on-table & not Null.  
- Cannot be converted or replaced by NCO.

**Irregular Order:**

- Belongs only to its Trooper.  
- Can be converted to Regular using a Command Token.  
- Cannot activate Fireteams unless the Irregular model is the Team Leader (but still only if the Skill allows it).

**Lieutenant Order:**

- Belongs only to Lt.  
- Visible information.  
- Can be used by NCO.  
- Cannot activate Fireteams unless the Lieutenant is the Team Leader.

**Pros of Tactical Awareness:**

- Provides additional Orders without SWC/points overhead beyond profile cost.  
- Highly synergistic with elite or centerpiece units (TAGs, elite HI).  
- Extremely useful in Fireteam structures.

**Cons of Tactical Awareness:**

- Order is *only* available if the model is physically on-table.  
- Does not add flexibility to Order Pool.  
- Makes Turn 1 order-stripping more likely vs. strategic Command Token usage.  
- TA model being killed early removes not just a strong unit but also an Order source.

## Tactical Usage Notes  
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

- **TAGs with Tactical Awareness** become significantly more efficient—often functioning as a “pseudo-Lieutenant” without the risk.  
- **Forward pieces** (e.g., fast HI, attack REMs) can push deeper while still retaining the Orders needed to escape danger or re-position.  
- **Fireteams with TA Leaders** gain an extra team-wide activation every turn, a powerful tempo advantage.  
- **High-threat TA units** draw attention—protecting them early can preserve a crucial Order engine.  
- **Multiple TA units** stack very well in terms of action economy, but increase vulnerability to Turn-1 Order stripping strategies.
