# Non-Hackable

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Obligatory 

Troopers that have technological systems that are so rudimentary that they are not susceptible to Hacking attempts.

- The user of this Special Skill cannot be the target of Hacking Attacks whose Requirements require the target have a specific Unit Type ( HI, REM, TAG, etc.).

- This Special Skill remains active even when its owner is in a Null State.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

## Clarified Interpretation of the Official Rule
The Non-Hackable Special Skill prevents a Trooper from being targeted by **Hacking Attacks whose Requirements specify that the target must be a particular Unit Type**, such as HI, REM, TAG, or Hacker.

### Non-Hackable vs. Specific-Type Hacking Attacks
Blocked completely:
- Possession (requires TAG)
- Oblivion if it requires Hackable or specific types (depending on profile wording)
- IMM-A/B-causing programs that specify HI/REM/TAG as valid targets

Allowed (still works):
- Spotlight (works on any Trooper)

### Non-Hackable vs. Comms Attacks (Not Hacking)
Non-Hackable only protects against **Hacking Attacks**.  
It does **not** prevent Comms Attacks that are not Hacking, such as:
- Jammer (Isolated)
- Pheroware Tactics (Eraser, Endgame)
- Cybermines (Stunned on Non-Hackable targets)

Thus: Non-Hackable ≠ Comms immunity.

### Non-Hackable and Null States
- The protection remains active even when the Trooper is Unconscious or Dead.
- Hackers **still cannot** target an Unconscious Non-Hackable unit with type-restricted programs.
- Spotlight still works, because Spotlight has no Unit Type requirement.

## Pros and Cons

### Pros
- Full protection from the most dangerous Hacking Attacks (Possession, Immobilization, Isolation) when they target specific types.
- Greatly reduces TAG/REM/HI vulnerability against hackers.

### Cons
- Does not stop Spotlight or any general-purpose hacking.
- Does not stop Comms Attacks (Jammer, Eraser, Cybermines, etc.).
- Can create a false sense of total anti-hacking immunity if misunderstood.


## Tactical Notes
- TAGs with Non-Hackable can push through Repeater nets far more safely, but may still be Spotlighted for guided missiles.
- Warbands and low-tech assault pieces become even more resilient against hacking-heavy factions.
