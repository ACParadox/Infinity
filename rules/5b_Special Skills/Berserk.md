# Berserk

Special Skills 
 LONG SKILL
Attack, CC Attack, CC Special Skill, Movement, Optional. 

A berserk Trooper is overcome with bloodlust or such single-minded determination that all concern for personal safety is cast aside in an attempt to annihilate the opponent.

- The target must be within the user's LoF when declaring this Skill.

Allows the user to declare Move + CC Attack as a single Long Skill, with the following special effects:

- The Move can be as far as both values of his MOV Attribute added together to reach Silhouette contact with the target.

- If, when measuring to find where the Trooper can move, they cannot reach Silhouette contact with the target, the Trooper will perform an Idle and therefore will neither Move, nor perform the CC Attack.

- If a MOD appears between brackets, for example Berserk (+3), that MOD is applied to the CC Attack.

- This CC Special Skill may be declared without being in Silhouette contact with a target.

- Although Berserk is a Long Skill, when performing it the user may still apply any (+1 SD) MOD they have.

As Berserk is a Long Skill, when it is declared the player must indicate both the route of the Basic Short Skill Move and the target of the CC Attack before his opponent declares any AROs.

## Fireteams and Berserk

If the Fireteam Leader declares the Berserk Skill, only the Fireteam members who have that Skill will be able to declare and perform it, the rest of the Troopers will declare and perform an Idle.

### Interaction Note
Related rules: ARO, Face-to-Face, Partial Cover.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

### Berserk Rules Interactions

**Berserk and ARO**
- Declaring Berserk grants ARO normally.
- If the target chooses CC Attack, Dodge, or BS Attack, the attack affects the outcome of the user’s CC Attack. This creates a Face-to-Face Roll.
- If the target declares Idle or a Skill that does not affect the attacker, the Berserk CC Attack is a Normal Roll.

**Berserk and Face-to-Face**
- Any opposing Skill that can cancel or oppose the CC Attack produces a Face-to-Face Roll.
- Berserk still applies any listed CC MODs such as Berserk (+3).

**Berserk and Partial Cover**
- CC Attacks ignore Cover, so Cover does not affect the Berserk Roll.

**Berserk and Movement**
- The user moves up to both MOV values added together.
- If the user cannot reach Silhouette contact, the entire declaration becomes Idle, and the opponent receives a free ARO.
- Berserk counts as having the Movement Label for effects such as Difficult Terrain, but it is still a Long Skill.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### Comparisons

**Berserk vs. Standard Move + CC Attack**
- Standard Move + CC Attack:
  - Costs two Short Skills.
  - Usually produces a Face-to-Face Roll.
  - Provides more control over positioning.
- Berserk:
  - Costs a full Long Skill.
  - Extends threat range significantly.
  - Still produces a Face-to-Face Roll
  - Doesn't allow switching to another skill after first skill (ex Dodge instead)
  

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### Berserk Pros and Cons

**Pros**
- Greatly extended threat range.
- Can break stalemates by reaching enemies behind obstacles.
- Useful against models that rely on distance.
- MODs from Berserk (+3) help win F2F CC Rolls.

**Cons**
- Declaring Berserk locks the user into the Long Skill sequence.
- Opponents can oppose with CC Attack, Dodge, or BS Attack, generating Face-to-Face Rolls.
- If the movement fails, the entire action becomes Idle and the opponent gets a free ARO.
