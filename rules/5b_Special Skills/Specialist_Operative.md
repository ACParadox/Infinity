# Specialist Operative

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Optional 

The user of this Special Skill can accomplish missions and objectives when playing scenarios, even if he does not have one of the roles specified as a Specialist Troop in the scenario.

- Playing a mission or a scenario, the user of this Special Skill is considered to be a Specialist Troop and may use the special rules the mission or scenario specifies for this type of Trooper.

- The user of this Special Skill will be considered to be a Specialist Troop, even if he doesn't fulfill any of the defining characteristics of this type of Trooper (not being an Engineer, Doctor, Hacker, etc.) in the scenario rules.

# [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]

Specialist Operative is the most generic way for a Trooper to count as a “Specialist Troop” in missions.  
It grants **no specific action**, **no bonuses**, and **no extra equipment**.  
It only grants the **permission** to perform mission objectives that require *a Specialist Troop*.

Unlike Doctor/Engineer/Forward Observer/Hacker/Paramedic, it does not include any functional skill attached to objective interaction—it only satisfies the “you must be a Specialist Troop” requirement.


# [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
## Pros and Cons of Taking a Specialist Operative

### Pros
- Universally qualifies for Specialist Troop-only objectives.  
- Often cheaper SWC and points than “specialized” specialists.  
- Safe from anti-Hacker tech; low liability to enemy Comms Attacks.  
- Good filler for missions where you need multiple objective grabbers.

### Cons
- No bonus for interacting with Consoles (unlike Hackers).  
- Cannot complete role-specific objectives (Doctor/Engineer/FO/Hacker-specific tasks).  
- Offers no extra combat capability—purely utility.  
- Becomes less effective in Classified-heavy formats where more specific roles are required.

# [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]


### Good Synergies
- Fireteams: they benefit from Burst bonuses to attacks while still being objective-capable.  
- Durable bodies (HI with Specialist Operative) make excellent objective holders.  
- Hidden Deployment + Specialist Operative (if available on a unit) can produce late-game surprises.

# [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
## Summary
Specialist Operative is a “universal key” for objective access but provides **no extra mission bonuses** and **no unique actions** beyond simply meeting requirements.  
Think of it as a *generic role tag*, not a functional skill.  
It is strongest when used as a cheap, reliable, low-risk objective runner and weakest when specific Specialist sub-types are required.
