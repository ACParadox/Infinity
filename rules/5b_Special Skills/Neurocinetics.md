# Neurocinetics

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Obligatory 

A Trooper with this Special Skill possesses enhanced response implants, which provide him with a better reaction capability.

- During the Active Turn, the Burst (B) value of all the BS Weapons of this Trooper is reduced to 1.

- During the Reactive Turn, Neurocinetics allows the Trooper to use the whole Burst (B) value of their BS Weapons against a single target.

- Any MOD to B only applies in the Reactive Turn.

## General Interaction Notes
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Neurocinetics effectively turns a trooper into a “reactive turret,” often comparable to Total Reaction but with key differences (detailed later).
- The skill encourages players to position the model aggressively for ARO dominance, because its Active Turn performance is intentionally crippled.
- Burst is only increased in ARO if the weapon profile (or bonuses) provides it. Neurocinetics does *not* give extra Burst; it only *allows* full Burst in the Reactive Turn.
- The “single target” clause means the trooper **cannot split Burst** even if the weapon normally allows it.

---

## Interaction with Fireteams
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Fireteam Burst SD bonuses apply **both in active and Reactive Turn**, so improves the ARO of Neurocinetics trooper even more. 

---

## Interaction with Suppressive Fire
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- A trooper in Suppressive Fire has a fixed **Burst 3**, but only in Active Turn (on declaration) and in Reactive Turn while in the Suppressive Fire State.  
- Neurocinetics **does not** override the Suppressive Fire rules.  
- In the **Active Turn:** Neurocinetics forces **B1**, so the model **cannot benefit from Suppressive Fire** offensively.  
- In the **Reactive Turn:** The model may declare a BS Attack with **B3 from Suppression**, but **cannot use the weapon’s native full Burst**, because Suppressive Fire replaces the profile’s Burst.  
- Therefore, Neurocinetics and Suppressive Fire are **situationally compatible** but not synergistic; you choose one mode or the other for ARO.

---

## Interaction with Direct Template Weapons
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Neurocinetics is also applied in ARO with DTW weapons.

---

## Interaction with MODs to Burst (+1B Weapons, Traits)
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Since Neurocinetics states “**Any MOD to B only applies in the Reactive Turn**,” all printed bonuses (e.g., Combi Rifle (+1B), Multi Rifle (+1B), profiles with B4/B5) become fully active **only in ARO**.
- In Active Turn, even a B5 HRMC is **reduced to B1**.

---

## Comparison: Neurocinetics vs Total Reaction
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

**Similarities:**  
- Both enable high-Burst ARO attacks.  
- Both encourage anchoring a fire lane.

**Differences (Neurocinetics → Total Reaction):**
- Neurocinetics **cannot split Burst**; Total Reaction may split.  
- Neurocinetics **reduces Active Turn to B1**; Total Reaction allows full Burst in Active Turn.  
- Neurocinetics benefits from **all Burst MODs** in Reactive Turn; Total Reaction uses the printed Burst without needing MODs.  
- Neurocinetics is often found on **expensive elite shooters**; TR is typically on **REM platforms** with lower stats.

**Pros of Neurocinetics vs TR:**  
- Can reach extremely high Reactive Burst (B5+ with the right weapon).  
- Works on tougher or more elite troops.

**Cons vs TR:**  
- Terrible in Active Turn (locked to B1).  
- No splitting Burst in ARO.  
- Requires careful placement, as the model often does little outside ARO duty.

## Pros & Cons of Neurocinetics (General)
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

**Pros:**  
- Exceptional lane-denial capability.  
- High Burst with powerful weapons in ARO.  
- Synergizes with long-range, high-PS weapons (e.g., HRMC, Missile Launcher profiles with extra Burst).  
- Forces opponents to deal with it using Smoke, coordinated orders, or bypass maneuvers.

**Cons:**  
- Severely limited Active Turn (B1).  
- Vulnerable to template trades (your high Burst provides no protection).  
- Vulnerable to coordinated attack saturation.  
- Cannot provide flexible ARO responses due to single-target restriction.  
- Requires very careful deployment: poor positioning means wasted points.

---

## Tactical Notes
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Place Neurocinetics troopers to watch long, open firelanes where the opponent cannot easily move multiple attackers into view simultaneously.  
- Avoid short-range or corner-engagement positions where templates or shotguns can neutralize your advantage.  
- Consider screening with Mines, repeaters, or camo units to discourage enemy close-range approaches.  
- Use command tokens and reserves to ensure your opponent doesn’t counter-deploy cheaply.  
- Because Active Turn output is poor, consider pairing the unit with strong attack pieces elsewhere in the list.
