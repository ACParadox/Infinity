# Doctor

Special Skills
 SHORT SKILL
Optional 

Troopers with this Special Skill have the medical know-how to stabilize their unconscious comrades while in the field, saving their lives and returning them to active duty.

- Troopers using this Special Skill must be in Silhouette contact with their target.

- The target must have the Vitality Attribute and be in Unconscious State.

- By passing a Normal WIP Roll, the target's Unconscious State is cancelled, by removing 1 single Wound from those it has received, unless a rule or Skill specified otherwise. If the WIP Roll fails, the target automatically enters the Dead State and is removed from the game table.

- Troopers may be recovered from the Unconscious State as many times as necessary, as long as the Doctor passes the WIP Roll.

- A Trooper with the Doctor Special Skill can use it on themselves, to cancel any states that can be canceled by Doctor. However, this cannot be done if the Trooper is in a Null State.

- This Skill may present the following variations in the Unit Profile: Doctor (2W): Allows the target to remove 2 Wounds when their Unconscious State is cancelled. Doctor (ReRoll WIP=X ): Once per Order, any failed Roll with this Skill may be rerolled, but applying the WIP Attribute indicated between brackets. The player must accept the result of this last Roll. This reroll does not provide a new ARO to the enemy.

- Doctor (2W): Allows the target to remove 2 Wounds when their Unconscious State is cancelled.

- Doctor (ReRoll WIP=X ): Once per Order, any failed Roll with this Skill may be rerolled, but applying the WIP Attribute indicated between brackets. The player must accept the result of this last Roll. This reroll does not provide a new ARO to the enemy.

Even if the player failed the roll granted by Doctor (ReRoll WIP=X), they could still apply the Operational Use of the Command Tokens to repeat a Doctor’s WIP Roll.

However, the WIP Attribute value provided by the Doctor (ReRoll WIP=X ) Skill does not apply when repeating this Roll.

Doctor can be used to cancel these States:

## Doctor - Unconscious State

- A Wound is removed from the Trooper, leaving them with less Wounds than their VITA / STR Attribute value.

- Doctors can remove Wounds from Troopers with the VITA Attribute, while Engineers can remove Wounds from Troopers with the STR Attribute. In both cases, the Wound is removed by performing the Doctor/Engineer Special Skill while in Silhouette contact with the affected Trooper, and passing a Normal WIP Roll (or the Roll specified by the Special Skill or Scenario).

## Doctor - Stunned State (target does not need to be Unconscious)

- To cancel this state, Troopers with the Vitality (VITA) Attribute require a Doctor while Troopers with the Structure (STR) Attribute require an Engineer.

- A Trooper with the Doctor/Engineer Special Skill (or an equivalent Skill) may cancel this state by spending one Short Skill of an Order while in Silhouette contact with the affected Trooper and passing a Normal WIP Roll (or the Roll specified by the Special Skill or Scenario).

## Doctor - Command Tokens: Operational Use and Cubes

If the target has a Cube, Command Tokens can be expended to reroll failed Doctor Rolls.

## Doctor - Technorganic

- The Unconscious State of the Trooper can be cancelled by using either the Doctor or Engineer Skill, or the MediKit or GizmoKit pieces of Equipment, regardless of whether they have the VITA or STR Attribute.

## Doctor - Order Expenditure Sequence

If a Trooper receives and removes Wounds or States in the same Order, the positive effect is always applied first, followed by the negative effect.

# Doctor — Interactions and Unofficial Commentary


## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]

# Interactions Notes

## Doctor and Unconscious State
- Doctors can return a Trooper with the Vitality (VITA) Attribute from Unconscious to active by passing a WIP Roll.  
- Failure kills the target.  
- Troopers can be healed repeatedly as long as the Doctor continues to succeed.  

This is a high-risk, high-reward action. Healing a critically important unit is often worth the Order and risk, but attempting to revive low-value units is rarely necessary unless for mission scoring or order efficiency.

## Doctor and Stunned State
Doctors cancel Stunned for VITA Troopers with a successful WIP Roll in Silhouette contact.  
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
This is often overlooked. Many players forget Doctors can remove Stunned, which can turn a disabled attack piece back into a threat.

## Doctor and Shock Ammunition
Shock prevents Unconscious and sends the target directly to Dead, making the Doctor unusable.  
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Units vulnerable to Shock are dramatically harder to support. Consider Immunity (Shock) or NWI units if you rely heavily on Doctors.

## Doctor (ReRoll WIP = X)
Allows a built-in reroll once per Order.  
Does not stack with Command Token rerolls for Cube targets.  
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Doctor (ReRoll) dramatically increases the reliability of reviving expensive units. It reduces the need for Command Token expenditure in many cases.

## Doctor (2W)
When successfully healing, the target recovers 2 Wounds instead of 1.  
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Particularly strong on multi-Wound Heavy Infantry and Characters. Restoring two wounds can bring back a severely damaged powerhouse.

## Doctor and Cubes
Troopers with Cubes allow Command Token rerolls on failed Doctor Rolls.  
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
This is often the distinction between a tournament-grade force and a fragile one. Cube-equipped HI become extremely order-efficient to heal.

## Doctor and Technorganic Troops
Technorganic rules allow either Doctor or Engineer to revive a trooper regardless of VITA/STR.  
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
This expands flexibility and reduces the need to bring both roles in certain factions.

## Doctor with Peripheral (Servant)
Doctors can heal through Peripherals as long as the Servant reaches the target.  
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
This is one of the strongest support mechanics in the game. It allows the Doctor to stay safely behind cover while projecting healing through the board.

## Doctor and Engaged State
Doctors cannot be used on a Trooper who is Engaged.  
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
A common error: you cannot revive a soldier in an ongoing CC. You must extricate them first.

# Comparisons

## Doctor vs Paramedic
Paramedics use a MediKit (WIP Roll at range or PH-3 on the patient).  
**Differences:**  
- Doctor uses his own WIP directly in contact.  
- Paramedic can heal from range but use the target's PH.  
- A good Doctor can be safer but must reach the target. 

**Pros/Cons:**  
Doctor: Reliable, but positional.  
Paramedic: Flexible, but lethal to fragile units.  

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Paramedics are good for cheap Specialists; Doctors are best for high-value models.
---

## Doctor vs Engineer
Doctors heal VITA; Engineers heal STR and remove states.  
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Both roles are essential. The choice depends on whether your army primarily uses HI (VITA) or REM/TAG (STR). Many missions reward having both.


## Doctor vs Regeneration
Regeneration allows self-recovery at the start of the active turn.  
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Regeneration is unreliable and cannot revive other models. Doctors are more controllable and tactically flexible.


## Doctor vs Protheion
Protheion *steals* Wounds through CC; Doctor *restores*.  
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Protheion is offensive sustain; Doctor is defensive sustain. They rarely compete directly in list building since they serve different functions.

# Pros and Cons Summary

## Pros
- High reliability for recovering key units.  
- Enables objective play as a Specialist Troop.  
- Supports durable units (HI, Characters).  
- Peripheral (Servant) devices greatly extend influence.  
- Variants (2W, ReRoll) dramatically improve efficiency.

## Cons
- Requires reaching Silhouette contact.  
- Failure kills the target.  
- Vulnerable to Shock removal.  
- High Order investment in dangerous zones.  
- Limited impact if the enemy uses a Shock-heavy list.


# Strategic Notes

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
- Consider deploying your Doctor behind cover, with the Servant as the active piece.  
- Use Smoke or suppressive fire screens to approach downed models safely.  
- Always assess the value of the model you're attempting to revive—sometimes it is better to let a unit die than risk further casualties or Order loss.  
- Healing heavy hitters (HI, TAG Pilots, elite troops) is often a game-changing play.  
- Keep an eye on mission scoring: many ITS missions give classified objectives to Doctors.

---

# Example Use Scenarios

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
- A Heavy Infantry link leader goes down. A Doctor with a Servant can revive them safely from behind cover, restoring command and fireteam integrity.  
- A Specialist falls Unconscious mid-mission. Reviving them may be necessary to access consoles or complete objectives.  
- Stunned state removal can restore an attack piece in time for a critical push.
