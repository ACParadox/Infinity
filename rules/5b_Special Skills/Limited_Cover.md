# Limited Cover

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Obligatory. 

For a variety of reasons, the Trooper cannot benefit from the BS MODs for being in Partial Cover.

- Attacks against this Trooper do not apply the -3 BS MOD for Partial Cover.

Keep in mind that the most severe restriction always applies. If a No Cover Trooper also has Limited Cover due to another rule or Skill, the No Cover Skill will still apply.

Frenzy

- In the States Phase of the Player Turn where the Requirements have been met, the Trooper gains the following Special Skills and retains them for the remainder of the game: Impetuous Limited Cover

- Impetuous

- Limited Cover

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Limited Cover is a Special Skill that removes only the *BS Attack MODs* normally granted by Partial Cover.  
The Trooper still benefits from the *Saving Roll* MOD (+3 ARM/BTS) normally provided by Partial Cover, unless another rule explicitly removes that too.  
This distinguishes Limited Cover from the **No Cover** Skill, which removes *all* Partial Cover benefits.

## INTERACTIONS

**Saving Roll MODs**  
Unless another rule applies (such as No Cover, or some special terrain variant), a Trooper with Limited Cover may still apply the +3 ARM/BTS Partial Cover bonus against attacks that force Saving Rolls.

**Stacking and Conflicts with Other Skills**
- If a Trooper possesses both **Limited Cover** and **No Cover**, then **No Cover** takes precedence (being the more restrictive).  
- If a piece of scenery or equipment modifies cover effects (e.g., **Deployable Cover: Vitroferro**, which changes the –3 BS MOD to 0 and increases the ARM bonus), the *most restrictive effect* always applies, but Limited Cover still removes the –3 BS MOD entirely for the Trooper that has it.

**Hacking, Comms Attacks, and Traits**
Limited Cover only affects the BS Attack MODs of Partial Cover.  
It does *not* affect:
- Comms Attacks (which ignore Cover BS MODs by rule),
- CC Attacks,
- Template Weapons (which ignore the –3 BS Attack MOD but still allow the +3 ARM bonus),
- Visibility Zone MODs,
- Range MODs,
- Mimetism.

## LIMITED COVER PROS AND CONS OF LIMITED COVER

### **Advantages**
- **Cost-efficient**: Usually found on inexpensive, aggressive units; the “drawback” can reflect a discount in points.
- **Synergizes with strong ARM**: If a unit’s survivability comes from ARM/BTS rather than dodging or MOD stacking, keeping the +3 ARM can still be relevant.

### **Disadvantages**
- **Loses the primary defensive utility of Cover**: The missing –3 to enemy BS makes the Trooper much easier to hit.
- **Anti-gunfight**: Units with Limited Cover should avoid direct firefights unless leveraging other modifiers (Mimetism, Range, Suppression, Smoke).
- **Does not synergize with “peek-and-shoot” play**: Trading from Partial Cover is much weaker.

---

## LIMITED COVER vs NO COVER (COMPARISON)

### **Limited Cover**
- **LOS MOD removed**: Cannot apply the –3 to enemy BS.
- **ARM/BTS MOD remains**: Can still apply +3 ARM/BTS.
- **Partial Cover still “exists”** for other interactions involving scenery.

### **No Cover**
- Removes **all** Partial Cover benefits (no –3 BS MOD *and* no +3 ARM/BTS).
- Overrules Limited Cover when both occur.

**Summary:**  
Limited Cover = “Half-Cover.”  
No Cover = “No Cover at all.”

---

## LIMITED COVER TACTICAL USE AND RECOMMENDATIONS

- **Use terrain to completely block LoF** when possible; Limited Cover does not protect you from BS Attacks the way normal Cover does.
- **Avoid long-range duels**; you cannot stack the full defensive MOD suite.
- **Pair with camouflage, marker states, or high Burst** to compensate for the lack of defensive BS MODs.
- **Advance under Smoke, Eclipse, or Zero Vis Zones** if possible—these grant defensive MODs regardless of Limited Cover.
- **Consider aggressive attack roles**: Many Limited Cover units are meant to push forward and trade rather than hold ground.
