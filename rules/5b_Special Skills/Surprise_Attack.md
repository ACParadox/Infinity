# Surprise Attack

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Obligatory 

The user of this Special Skill is able to catch their adversaries unaware by declaring Attacks that are difficult to avoid.

- To use this Special Skill, Troopers must be in Marker form (for example, Camouflaged, Impersonation, Decoy, Holoecho...) or in Hidden Deployment at the start of the Order in which they are activated.

- The user must declare a CC Attack, BS Attack, or Comms Attack.

- The user of this Special Skill must be in their Active Turn.

- This Attack imposes an additional negative MOD, as shown in round brackets in the Unit Profile [Surprise Attack (-3), Surprise Attack (-6)...]. This MOD only applies to targets of the Attack and applies to any Face to Face Roll that those targets perform in ARO.

- If a specific Attribute is listed between brackets in the Unit Profile, such as Surprise Attack (CC-6), the negative MOD only applies to that Attribute of the targets of the Attack. This MOD is not cumulative with other MODs imposed by Surprise Attack, for example if the Trooper has both Surprise Attack (-3) and Surprise Attack (CC-6).

- Restriction: This Skill cannot be used again until the Trooper returns to Marker form.

## Surprise Attack - Biometric Visor

- Users of a Biometric Visor ignore Surprise Attack MODs from attackers benefitting from Impersonation or Holoecho States, if they have LoF to the attacker. In addition, they also ignore Surprise Attack MODs from CC Attacks from those attackers even if they don't have LoF to the attacker.

## Surprise Attack - Combat Instinct

- The user ignores Surprise Attack MODs from attackers.

Multispectral Visor

- Users of a Multispectral Visor L3 ignore Surprise Attack MODs if they have LoF to the attacker. In addition, they also ignore Surprise Attack MODs from CC Attacks even if they don't have LoF to the attacker.

## Surprise Attack -  FAQs

Q: If multiple Troopers use Surprise Attack against the same target, for example via a Coordinated Order, or a Controller and Peripheral, do the Surprise Attack MODs stack with each other?

A: No. Each Trooper's Surprise Attack MOD will be applied separately.

# Surprise Attack — Unofficial Interaction Notes and Commentary

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
Surprise Attack is a one-time burst of penalty MODs applied when a Marker-state Trooper attacks. It is a central tool for alpha strikes, assassination pieces, and surgical engagements. It rewards careful positioning, timing, and order efficiency.

## Condition & Timing Clarifications

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Surprise Attack can only be used if the Trooper **starts the Order in a Marker State or Hidden Deployment**. If the Trooper is revealed *during* the order (e.g., by Discover or by declaring a Skill that reveals them), the Surprise Attack effect still applies because the check is at the start of the Order.
- Once used, it cannot be used again until the Trooper **returns to Marker State**. This is why camouflage loops and re-entering Marker State are tactically valuable.

## Interaction: Discover + Surprise Attack

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- If the attacker begins the Order in Marker form and declares an Attack, enemies may declare **Discover ARO**, but the Surprise MOD still applies to any **Face to Face Roll** resulting from their declared ARO (e.g., BS Attack, Dodge, Reset).
- Discover itself is *not* a Face to Face Roll against the Surprise Attack Roll (Discover is never FtF against Attacks), so the Surprise MOD does not apply to the Discover roll.

**Key point:**  
Surprise Attack only affects **Face to Face rolls**, not Discover rolls. A Discover+ARO piece cannot overcome Surprise Attack with WIP bonuses.

---

## Interaction: Sixth Sense & Combat Instinct

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- **Sixth Sense no longer cancels Surprise Attack in N5.**  
- Troopers with **Combat Instinct** ignore all Surprise Attack MODs, regardless of LoF or CC.
## Interaction: Visors (MSV & Biometric)

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- **MSV3**: Ignores Surprise Attack MOD **if the MSV3 user has LoF**; also ignores CC Surprise MODs without LoF.
- **Biometric Visor**: Ignores Surprise Attack **only** from attackers benefitting from *Impersonation or Holoecho*.
- **MSV1/MSV2**: Provide **no protection** against Surprise Attack.  

**Practical difference:**  
- MSV3 stops **all** Surprise Attacks.  
- Biometric Visor stops **only specific sources** (Impersonators / Holoecho users).  
- MSV1/2 stop **none**.

## Interaction: Attribute-Specific Surprise Attack (e.g. CC-6)

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Profiles like **Surprise Attack (CC-6)** apply their MOD only to **that Attribute**.  
  So a CC-focused assassin may impose CC-6 but **not** impose a BS or WIP penalty on the enemy.
- Attribute-specific versions do **not** combine with the generic Surprise Attack (-3 or -6). You choose the one printed on the unit profile (the rules specify non-cumulative).

**Tactical effect:**  
These specialized versions are generally *stronger* in duels but offer narrower usage.

---

## Interaction: Dodge ARO

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Surprise Attack applies normally against **Dodge**, since Dodge opposes Attacks in FtF.
- Dodge penalties stack with states like Immobilized-A (PH-6).
- Troopers with Sixth Sense can Dodge without some penalties, but Sixth Sense does **not** remove Surprise Attack.

## Interaction: Marker State Reveal Timing

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- When the user declares an Attack, they reveal *after* declaring it. Surprise Attack still works because the requirement was met at the **start** of the Order.
- If the Trooper loses Marker State before declaring an Attack (e.g., failed Cautious Movement, forced Reset, triggered a Mine earlier), they **cannot** use Surprise Attack.

## Tactical Use Cases (Pros & Cons)

### Pros
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Large immediate swing in FtF odds (especially Surprise Attack -6).
- High leverage for assassination, hitting key AROs, or breaking defensive positions.
- Very order-efficient: one MOD affects all rolls in the FtF exchange.
- Synergizes with Camouflage, Impersonation, Holoecho, Hidden Deployment.
- Great with low-Burst but high-impact weapons (shotguns, SMGs in good range, melee).

### Cons
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Single-use per Marker State cycle; requires planning to reset.
- Template AROs bypass its value entirely.
- Hard-countered by MSV3 and Combat Instinct.
- Requires Active Turn; provides no benefit in Reactive.
- Cannot be used after Trooper is revealed unless re-entering Marker State (often slow or order-intensive).

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- **Camo Sniper engages an ARO piece:** Surprise Attack improves odds dramatically in the first Order, often enough to remove a key piece safely.
- **Impersonator (IMP-1) entering melee:** Surprise Attack synergizes with CC Attack and Martial Arts, allowing you to neutralize backline targets who rely on weak PH/Dodge values.
- **Holoecho Harassment:** Holoecho allows for deceptive positioning and Surprise Attacks on targets who believe they are engaging a harmless decoy.
