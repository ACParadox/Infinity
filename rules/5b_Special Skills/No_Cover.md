# No Cover

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Obligatory. 

For a variety of reasons (agility, size, training...), the Trooper cannot benefit from any of the MODs granted by Partial Cover.

- Troopers with this Special Skill do not benefit from Partial Cover MODs.

Keep in mind that the most severe restriction always applies. If a No Cover Trooper also has Limited Cover due to another rule or Skill, the No Cover Skill will still apply.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

No Cover removes all benefits a Trooper would normally receive from Partial Cover:
- No -3 MOD applied to enemy BS Attacks.
- No +3 ARM/BTS to Saving Rolls.
This applies even if the Trooper would otherwise qualify for Limited Cover or other forms of modified cover. The most restrictive rule always applies, so No Cover overrides them.

## Total Cover
No Cover does NOT prevent Total Cover.  

## Template Weapons
Direct Template Attacks already ignore Partial Cover for BS Attack MODs.  
However, the *Saving Roll* bonus from Partial Cover would normally apply if the target is behind cover. No Cover prevents that +3 ARM/BTS.

## Tactical Pros & Cons

### A. Pros
- Usually represents high mobility or aggression; the Trooper may have compensating advantages such as better MOV, Mimetism, wounds, or equipment.
- No Cover Troopers often shine in open-field aggression, relying on speed or native MODs rather than positional bonuses.

### B. Cons
- Significantly more vulnerable in gunfights: no -3 to enemy BS and no +3 ARM/BTS on Saves.
- Severely punished by Template Weapons due to losing the ARM bonus.
- Requires careful positioning: Total Cover is crucial because Partial Cover is useless.
- Becomes disproportionately vulnerable to high-Burst weapons at range.

## Comparison with Similar Skills

### LIMITED COVER
- LIMITED COVER: Only some kinds of Partial Cover apply or apply only in specific conditions.  
- NO COVER: No Partial Cover ever applies.  
Pros/Cons: Limited Cover Troopers can sometimes still use terrain defensively; No Cover Troopers cannot at all.

### SAPPER
- SAPPER creates a Foxhole granting special Cover bonuses.  
- NO COVER users cannot benefit from these bonuses and cannot become Foxhole models.  
Difference: Sapper relies on a specialized Cover state; No Cover completely forbids Cover use.

### Play Tips (Unofficial)
- Rely on long movement, smoke, or Mimetism instead of Cover.  
- Avoid long-range firefights unless you have superior MODs or Burst.  
- Use Total Cover aggressively: slice angles so only one enemy can fire.  
- Consider using Suppressive Fire situationally, but remember you're easier to hit.  
- Deploy in positions where terrain blocks LoF naturally rather than relying on Cover modifiers.

### Summary
No Cover is a major defensive drawback in Infinity N5. It removes the single most common defensive modifier in the game. Troopers with No Cover must play as high-mobility, angle-controlling pieces that rely on speed, stealth, or brute resilience instead of positional defense.
