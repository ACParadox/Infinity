# Morpho-Scan

Special Skills
 SHORT SKILL
Comms Attack, No Roll, Optional, Zone of Control. 

Morpho-scan allows the user to replicate the Attribute values of an enemy model in Zone of Control.

- The target must be an enemy Model possessing the Vitality Attribute and be in the user's Zone of Control.

- Morpho-scan has no effect on enemy Markers, or enemy Troopers possessing the STR Attribute.

- This Skill doesn't require a Roll.

- Morpho-scan replaces the MOV, CC, BS and PH Attribute values of its user with the ones of its target.

- Morpho-scan imposes a MOD of -9 to its target if they declare a Reset ARO to prevent it, no matter what kind of Troop it is (LI, MI, HI...), and even if the Morpho-scan is declared outside of its LoF.

- Once this Special Skill is used successfully, having replaced the owner's Attribute values with those of its target's, it cannot be used again during the game.

## MORPHO-SCAN — INTERACTIONS, TRICKS, PROS/CONS, AND COMPARISONS

Interactions:
- Morpho-Scan does not require LoF. As long as the target is inside ZoC and meets requirements, the user may perform the Skill even from behind total cover.
- Because it requires no Roll, the only reactive defense is declaring Reset. The target suffers a -9 MOD to that Reset, making success unlikely unless the target has very high WIP.
- The replacement of MOV, CC, BS, and PH means that all future Rolls that reference these Attributes (attacks, Dodge, movement distances, etc.) use the copied values.
- Morpho-Scan does not interact with States; it does not remove, add, or modify any. The user keeps its original WIP, ARM, BTS, and others.
- The copied Attributes replace, not modify, the user’s own. Any equipment, Skills, or MODs that reference the replaced Attributes apply normally to the new values.
- The Skill cannot be attempted on Markers (Camouflage, Impersonation, Holomask, etc.) nor on units with the STR Attribute (Remotes, TAGs, etc.). This severely narrows viable targets.
- A Fireteam member using Morpho-Scan still benefits from Fireteam bonuses, which apply to the *resulting* Attributes (for example, BS bonuses apply to the cloned BS).
- If the user already has high Attribute values, the Skill may be disadvantageous; once used, it cannot be undone for the rest of the game.

### Unofficial Interpretation Notes:
- If the target’s Attributes suffer temporary MODs (e.g., from States or Skills), the copied Attributes reflect the *current* printed values, not temporary modifications. If your playgroup needs a ruling, a practical house rule is: Morpho-Scan copies the base Attributes shown in the Unit Profile, ignoring current temporary penalties or bonuses.

Pros:
- The result can elevate a mediocre trooper into a top performer if the chosen target has great CC, BS, MOV, or PH.
- Excellent for turning a cheap skirmisher or mid-range trooper into a powerful attacker for the remainder of the game.
- Works without risk, as no Roll is required.
- Can be done from total safety, encouraging safe attribute harvesting.

Cons:
- Extremely narrow targets: cannot affect Remotes, TAGs, or any STR-based unit, nor Marker-state enemies.
- Single-use: if the copied profile is not helpful in the current board state, you’re stuck with it for the whole game.
- Requires ZoC positioning, exposing the user to Templates or direct AROs when approaching.
- Does not improve WIP, ARM, or BTS—limiting durability and utility of highly risky engagements.

### Tactical Advice:
- Use Morpho-Scan early: gaining superior attributes for the entire game amplifies the payoff significantly.
- Target high-stat specialists (elite shooters, melee monsters) whenever possible. A Morpho-Scan on an enemy Asura, Swiss Guard, or Knight can radically increase your model’s value.
- Consider the board plan: a long-range shooter with high BS is much more valuable if your unit has access to appropriate weapons.
- Use ZoC positioning through corners, smoke, or Stealth allies to enable safe scanning opportunities.
