# Peripheral — N5.3 / FAQ v0.1 Update

> **N5.3 change (September 2026):** This file records the N5.3 Peripheral (Cyberplug) wording and September 2026 Peripheral FAQ rulings. If older wording in `Peripheral.md` conflicts with this file, use this update.

Official reference: https://infinitythewiki.com/Peripheral

## Peripheral (Cyberplug)

> **N5.3 change:** Peripherals (Cyberplug) have a **Connected Profile** and an **Autonomous Profile**, chosen by the player at the start of the Order/ARO.

- Only Troopers with the Cyberplug Special Skill may have Peripherals (Cyberplug).
- There is no operating-distance limit between a Peripheral (Cyberplug) and its Controller.
- At the **start of the Order/ARO**, determine which profile each Cyberplug is using.
- While using the **Connected Profile**, the Peripheral is activated by the same Order as its Controller and declares the same Short Skills or Long Skill. The **Controller performs Idle instead of the declared Skill**, subject to the Doctor/Engineer exception in the Peripheral rules.
- While using the **Autonomous Profile**, the Peripheral can be activated with Orders from the Order Pool and can declare its own AROs independently of the Controller. Place an Autonomous Token beside it.
- A Controller may have a maximum of **two** Peripherals (Cyberplug).
- A maximum of **one Cyberplug may use the Connected Profile** at a time. Any other Cyberplug uses its Autonomous Profile.
- When deploying two Cyberplugs, one begins using its Connected Profile and the other its Autonomous Profile.

## Controller unable to perform the declared Skill

> **N5 FAQ v0.1 clarification:** In the Active Turn, if a Peripheral's Controller cannot perform a Skill because it is Stunned, Immobilized, Engaged, or otherwise fails the Skill's Requirements, the Peripheral may still perform that Skill if it can.

The Controller and all its Peripherals declare the same Skill. Any individual game element that cannot legally perform it executes **Idle** instead, while the others act normally.

## Peripheral (Ancillary) and Killed scoring

> **N5 FAQ v0.1 clarification:** An **undeployed Peripheral (Ancillary) does not count as Killed** for scenario objectives.

A Peripheral (Ancillary) only counts as Killed if it was **deployed during the game** and either:

- entered **Dead State**, or
- is in a **Null State at the end of the game**.

## Fireteam interaction

> **N5.3 change:** Peripheral Controller status also has explicit Fireteam-integrity consequences. See `../../6_Command and Fireteams/Fireteam_Integrity.md`: the Fireteam is cancelled if its Leader becomes a Peripheral Controller; another member that becomes a Controller leaves the Fireteam.
