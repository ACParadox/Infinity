# Triangulated Fire

Special Skills
 LONG SKILL
Attack, Optional. 

These detection systems can also aid in the precise triangulation of the target, resulting in more effective target acquisition.

- The user must have LoF to the target.

- Allows the user to declare a BS Attack against a target without applying any MODs to the Roll ( Range, Cover, Special Skills such as Mimetism...).

- The only exception are MODs that affect Burst (B), which are applied normally.

Triangulated Fire does not allow the user to hit a target farther away than the weapon’s Maximum Range. For example, a Combi Rifle can never hit a target at a distance of more than 48 inches.

## FAQs

Q: Do Skills that let you perform a 'BS Attack Roll' (for example Intuitive Attack and Speculative Attack), or have the BS Attack Label, count as a BS Attack for MODs? For example, if the Trooper has 'BS Attack (SR-1)', 'BS Attack (+1 SD), or 'BS Attack (AP)'.

A: Yes. Note that +1 SD MODs don't apply to Long Skills.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
**Triangulated Fire is a Long Skill.**  
This is extremely important because:

- It **cannot** be used in ARO.  
- It **cannot** be combined with any second Short Skill in the same Order.  
- It **breaks Fireteam Coherency** for all members except the Leader (because Long Skills activate only the user).  
- It **prevents movement**, so positioning must already be ideal.

Triangulated Fire produces **flat BS rolls**, removing all positive and negative BS MODs. Only Burst modifiers can apply.

Because it is a Long Skill, its use is always intentional and usually tactical rather than opportunistic.

---
## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
**Practical Effect:**  
Triangulated Fire is best used from a safe, already-established firing position — ideally from cover, height, or with friendly smoke/MSV support (while still maintaining LoF).

---

### Triangulated Fire vs. Range Bands  
All Range MODs are ignored.  
Because it is a Long Skill, you cannot move to optimal positioning — you fire from your current position at flat BS.

### Triangulated Fire vs. Mimetism  
All levels of Mimetism are ignored.  
This is one of the strongest reasons to use a Long Skill on your turn, as you do not get this power in ARO anyway.

### Triangulated Fire vs. Cover  
Cover’s -3 BS penalty is removed.  
Cover’s ARM/BTS bonus remains.

Because Triangulated Fire is a Long Skill, you can’t combine it with a Move to reach a better angle to remove Cover — but the BS penalty is gone regardless.


### Triangulated Fire vs. Fireteam Bonuses  
- **Burst Bonus (SD)** applies (allowed).  
- Using a Long Skill removes other members of a Fireteam from activation unless this model is the Fireteam Leader.

**Practical:**  
Triangulated Fire in a Fireteam often breaks Fireteam integrity for that Order.

---

### Triangulated Fire vs. Zero Visibility Zones  
Triangulated Fire ignores BS MODs, but **does not grant LoF through smoke**.  
ZVZ still blocks LoF unless you have MSV appropriate to the type of zone.

## Triangulated Fire Strategic Impact

### Pros (specific to Long Skill use)
- Allows shooting through heavy MOD stacks without any pre-setup (no need to Discover, Debuff, or reposition).  
- High-value option when stationary in a fortified, safe location.

### Cons (specific to Long Skill use)
- You cannot Move + Shoot — you must already have LoF.  
- You cannot Slice the Pie; the enemy may gain multiple AROs if they have LoF.  

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]

## Triangulated Fire Closing Notes
Triangulated Fire as a **Long Skill** requires preparation and positional advantage.  
It trades mobility and tactical flexibility for extreme reliability against MOD stacks.  
Used from safety or elevation, it becomes an excellent tool to nullify enemy defenses or bad range bands.
