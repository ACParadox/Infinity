# Number 2

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Optional 

The owner of this Special Skill is the next in line in the command echelon to the Team Leader of a Fireteam, being able to replace him if necessary during combat operations.

- The user must be part of a Fireteam.

- The Fireteam Leader of that Fireteam enters Isolated State or any Null State

- The user may automatically become the new Fireteam Leader of his Fireteam, placing the Fireteam Leader Token beside him.

- Even if the Fireteam Team Leader recovers from their Isolated or Null State, the Number 2 Trooper will continue to be the Fireteam Leader until the player decides otherwise.

# Number 2 — Unofficial Commentary and Interactions Notes

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

Number 2 is a Fireteam-related Special Skill.  
According to the rules:  
"When the Fireteam Leader of that Fireteam enters Isolated State or any Null State, the user may automatically become the new Fireteam Leader of his Fireteam, placing the Fireteam Leader Token beside him."  


This is a defensive skill that prevents automatic Fireteam cancellation when the Leader is removed, Isolated, or otherwise rendered inactive.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Number 2 exists to stabilize Fireteam integrity. Fireteams are prone to collapsing when the Leader is eliminated or suffers States like Unconscious or Isolated. Number 2 lets a designated Trooper "catch" the leadership slot automatically, without spending Orders or Command Tokens.

This is most valuable in high-risk Fireteams that frequently expose the Leader (Haris, aggressive Core link pushes, wildcard Leaders, etc.).

## How the Skill Interacts with Fireteam Rules

**Interaction:**  
Number 2 prevents cancellation *specifically* when the cause is loss of the Leader.

Number 2 *does not protect against Fireteam members leaving due to ARO mismatch*.  
It protects *only* the Leader loss condition.

## Tactical Notes (Unofficial)

### Advantages of Number 2
- **Fireteam Stability:** Prevents the Fireteam from collapsing during aggressive plays.  
- **Efficient Activations:** Avoids needing a Command Token to reform the Fireteam later.  
- **Preserves Bonuses:** Keeps the team in full Fireteam state for BS MODs, Burst bonuses, Sixth Sense (if Core), etc.  
- **Good for Haris:** Especially useful because Haris/Core links are often exposed and can quickly collapse.

### Disadvantages
- **Takes a Slot:** A Trooper with Number 2 may cost more points or displace another specialist.  
- **Doesn’t Stop Other Breaks:**  
  - Mismatched AROs  
  - Members entering Marker State  
  - Members entering Null or Isolated states  
  - Coherency breaks  
  Number 2 only covers *Leader* loss.

### When to Use It
- In Fireteams where the Leader is highly exposed (aggressive shooters, infiltrating wildcards, or units with strong BS weapons).  
- In Sectorials with expensive Fireteams that you cannot afford to reform often.  
- In Haris Teams that rely on the Leader for strong Burst bonuses.

## Comparison With Similar Fireteam-Stability Effects

### **Number 2 vs. Chain of Command (CoC)**  
- **Number 2:** Prevents Fireteam collapse by replacing the Fireteam Leader.  
- **CoC:** Replaces the *Lieutenant*, not the Fireteam Leader.  
- **Key Difference:** CoC does not protect Fireteam integrity; Number 2 does not protect command structure.
