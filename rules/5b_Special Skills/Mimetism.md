# Mimetism

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
NFB, Obligatory. 

This Special Skill represents the soldier's ability to conceal their position and move stealthily, making them difficult to target in combat.

- Any enemy declaring a BS Attack that requires LoF, or Discover, against the user of this Skill must apply a negative MOD to their Attribute, as shown in round brackets in the Unit Profile: Mimetism (-3), Mimetism (-6)...

- The Mimetism MOD does not apply to CC Attacks.

## Interactions with Other Rules

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
**Mimetism and Visibility Zones**  
- Mimetism stacks with Low/Poor/Zero Visibility Zone MODs unless a rule states otherwise.  
- Multispectral Visor modifies or removes these MODs (see MSV interaction below).

**Mimetism and MSV (Multispectral Visor)**  
- MSV1 reduces Mimetism (-3) to 0.  
- MSV2+ reduces Mimetism (-6) 
- Smoke interactions from MSV do not affect the application of Mimetism itself (Mimetism is not a LoF-blocking mechanic).

**Mimetism and Camouflage / Hidden Deployment**  
- Camouflaged Markers display their Mimetism value.  
- Discover rolls always apply the marker’s Mimetism MOD.  
- Mimetism is one of the main factors making Discover more difficult.  
- Sensor bypasses Mimetism for the Sensor roll (explicit in rules).

**Mimetism and Surprise Attack**  
- Both apply separate negative MODs to the enemy.  
- These MODs stack because they come from different skills: one always-on (Mimetism) and one triggered when declaring Surprise Attack.

**Mimetism and Direct Template Weapons**  
- Mimetism does not apply to attacks that do not require a Roll, such as Direct Templates.  
- This is a major counter to high-Mimetism models.

**Mimetism and CC**  
- MOD does not apply to CC Attacks, making close combat an effective counter.

## Pros and Cons of Mimetism Levels

### Mimetism (-3)
**Pros:**
- Solid general defensive layer.
- Forces common BS 12–13 shooters to degrade to effective BS 9–10.
- Cost-efficient; widely available.

**Cons:**
- Easily countered by MSV1+.  
- Direct Templates ignore the MOD.  
- Close combat ignores the MOD.  
- Less impactful against high-BS shooters or linked BS17+ attackers.

### Mimetism (-6)
**Pros:**
- Very powerful defensive modifier.  
- Significantly changes math: even BS 14 becomes 8 before other MODs.  
- Forces opponents with no MSV2+ to rely on Templates, Discover tricks, or high-risk FTF rolls.

**Cons:**
- More expensive in points.  
- MSV2 reduces it to only 0
- Vulnerable to Template bypassing.  
- May cause opponents to switch to non-LoF tools (Guided, Speculative Fire).

## Tactical Notes and Usage Examples

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- **Positioning matters:** Mimetism multiplies in value when supported by cover. A Mimetism (-6) trooper in cover often imposes a combined -9 to -12 in MODs, approaching the cap.  
- **Force bad angles:** Opponents may attempt to bypass Mimetism with Direct Templates—use spacing and elevation to avoid template traps.
- **Pair with Suppressive Fire:** Mimetism (-3) + Suppressive Fire (-3) creates a cumulative -6 to enemies shooting back.  
- **Anti-MSV play:** Avoid direct shootouts with MSV troopers; instead, force them into moving, Dodge AROs, or unfavorable ranges.
