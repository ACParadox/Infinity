# Stealth

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Optional. 

The user of this Special Skill has received special training with a focus on silent movement, the art of remaining undetected, and the capacity to strike an unsuspecting enemy with unrelenting close combat attacks from out of the blue.

- The user of this Special Skill must be in his Active Turn.

- The user can declare Cautious Movement inside the Zone of Control and Hacking Areas of enemy Models and Markers.

- If the user declares Cautious Movement, Idle, or a Basic Short Skill with the Movement Label, they only grant AROs to enemies that have LoF to them.

- If any other Skill is declared, AROs are granted normally.

- If the user enters Silhouette contact with an enemy without entering their LoF, and declares any of those other Skills, that enemy can only declare CC Attack, Dodge, Reset, or those Skills that can be used in Engaged State.

Stealth is not effective against Troopers with the Combat Instinct or Sixth Sense Special Skills, or against Deployable Weapons or pieces of Equipment.

Targeted State

- Restriction: A Trooper in this state may not use the Stealth Special Skill.

## FAQs

Q: Which Automatic Skills or Equipment have to be declared when in a Marker State?

A: Automatic Skills and Equipment that alter how an Order activates a Trooper, or restrict AROs, must be declared when used in Marker State. For example using Stealth.

Q: If several Troopers are activated at once (for example a Coordinated Order or a Fireteam) and generate AROs, can any of the AROs target Active Troopers that are using Stealth?

A: Yes, but the ARO will become an Idle if the Stealth user does not declare a Skill that allows AROs.

### Stealth vs Zone of Control AROs
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Stealth’s main power is eliminating ZoC AROs when using movement-based skills. This allows a Trooper to move through ZoC without provoking Reset, Dodge, or Hacking AROs. Enemies must *see* the Stealth user to react.

This is especially powerful for:
- Camouflage or Impersonation units slipping past Repeater networks.
- Close Combat specialists approaching without risking multiple Dodge/Reset AROs.

---

### Stealth vs Hacking Areas / Repeaters
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Stealth works even inside Repeater coverage: Hackers cannot ARO unless they have LoF. This is a core tool for assault units or CC specialists to bypass infowar defenses.

Caveat: **Deployable Repeaters do not trigger Stealth immunity**—but the Hacker connected to them still cannot ARO without LoF.

---

### Stealth vs Sixth Sense / Combat Instinct
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Sixth Sense and Combat Instinct **completely ignore Stealth**, making those units dangerous anchor points in an enemy line.  
Approaching such Troopers will always provoke AROs through ZoC even when performing movement-only short skills.

Stealth users must treat both traits as “no-go stealth holes” in the line.

---

### Stealth + Cautious Movement
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Stealth makes Cautious Movement significantly better: the user may declare it **within enemy ZoC**, which normally is forbidden.  
This allows safe disengagement or approach, provided the user stays out of LoF.
---

### Stealth entering Silhouette Contact
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
If a Stealth user reaches base contact without being seen and then declares a non-movement Skill (e.g., CC Attack), the enemy’s ARO options are restricted to:
- CC Attack  
- Dodge  
- Reset  
- Skills usable in Engaged  

This prevents the enemy from declaring BS Attack on the approach or at the moment of contact if LoF was never gained.  
Strong for CC assassins.

---

### Stealth in Coordinated Orders / Fireteams
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
FAQ: Enemies *may* declare ARO against any member of a group activation, even Stealth users. But if the Stealth user does not declare a Skill that allows AROs, the ARO becomes **Idle**.

Practical effect:
- Multi-model activations partially break Stealth.  
- The opponent can “try” AROs, but they usually waste them.

This is a tactical tool to burn enemy ARO opportunities intentionally.

---

### Stealth and Marker States
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Stealth must be declared (as per FAQ) when used in Marker State. This is purely informational: the opponent needs to know whether a ZoC ARO is suppressed.

This prevents “gotcha” play where a Marker silently uses Stealth without informing the defender.

---

### Stealth vs Deployables
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Deployable weapons/equipment completely ignore Stealth, meaning:
- Mines trigger normally.  
- Perimeter weapons trigger normally.  
- MadTraps/Koalas ignore stealthy movement.

Stealth bypasses *hackers*, not *hardware/repeaters*.

---

### Stealth and Targeted State
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
A Trooper in Targeted cannot use Stealth.  
This makes spotlighting (via Spotlight or Forward Observer) a powerful tool for revealing and pinning down infiltrators who rely on Stealth to penetrate defenses.

---

## Comparisons: Stealth vs Camouflage

### Similarities
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Both suppress or limit AROs.  
- Both reward careful movement and positioning.  
- Both allow approach without revealing or provoking enemies in ZoC.

### Differences & Pros/Cons

**Stealth Pros**
- Works even when not in a Marker State.  
- Works inside Hacking Areas.  
- Allows Cautious Movement inside ZoC.  
- Cannot be removed unless Targeted.

**Stealth Cons**
- Useless against Sixth Sense / Combat Instinct.  
- Does not prevent Discover attempts (Camouflage does).  
- Does not protect against Template weapons triggered by proximity.

**Camouflage Pros**
- Provides Marker State protection (must Discover first).  
- Strong defense against BS Attacks at range.  
- Enables Surprise Attack later.

**Camouflage Cons**
- Lost upon performing many actions.  
- Does not allow Cautious Movement in ZoC unless combined with Stealth.  
- Can be countered by Sensor, Triangulated Fire, MSV.

---

## Tactical Notes

### Best Uses of Stealth
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- CC specialists infiltrating through ZoC safely.  
- Hackable units maneuvering through Repeater nets.  
- Avoiding Reset spam from opponents trying to block your approach.

### Counters to Stealth
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Sixth Sense anchor units.  
- Combat Instinct skirmishers.  
- Targeting the Stealth unit (Spotlight or FO).  
- Deployable equipment like Mines and Koalas.  
- Forcing non-movement Skills with area threats (Templates, hacking, etc.).

### Synergies
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Stealth + Camouflage: ultimate approach package.  
- Stealth + Martial Arts: maximizing the CC threat while minimizing incoming AROs.  
- Stealth + Climbing Plus: 3D infiltration without provoking Reset in ZoC below/above you.  
- Stealth + Impersonators: devastating assassination runs.
