# FT Master

> **N5 FAQ v0.1 clarification (September 2026):** A Fireteam normally does **not** cancel merely because it loses a Trooper that was required to satisfy the Fireteam Chart's `Min` value when the team was created. **FT Master is a specific exception:** when FT Master lists a Unit in brackets, the Fireteam is automatically cancelled if the FT Master Trooper leaves the Fireteam while that Fireteam contains a member of the listed Unit.

Special Skills
AUTOMATIC SKILL
Obligatory

A Fireteam Master is capable of turning a handful of undisciplined troopers into a well-oiled war machine.

- The Trooper with this Special Skill must be part of a Fireteam.
- In the Order Count step of the Tactical Phase, if this Trooper is part of a Fireteam, all the members of that Fireteam are Regular.
- If all members of the Fireteam already have the Regular Characteristic on their Unit Profile, apply a +2 inch bonus to the Team Leader's Zone of Control when making Coherency Checks for the Fireteam.
- Restriction: This bonus is not cumulative with any bonus to the Team Leader’s Zone of Control.
- If a Unit name is listed in brackets after this Special Skill in the Trooper's Unit Profile, and the Trooper's Fireteam contains any members belonging to that Unit, the Fireteam is automatically canceled if the FT Master Trooper is no longer in the Fireteam.

Having an FT Master in a Fireteam does not prevent its members from suffering the effects of Loss of Lieutenant.

## N5 FAQ v0.1 interaction

The normal Fireteam `Min` requirement is checked when creating the Fireteam. Losing a member later does not retroactively invalidate formation. The FT Master cancellation clause above remains an explicit rule and therefore overrides that general persistence rule when its listed-Unit condition applies.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
FT Master's Regular conversion is checked in the **Order Count step of the Tactical Phase**. Do not treat losing the FT Master later in the Turn as retroactively removing Orders that were already generated during Order Count. Re-evaluate the Fireteam and FT Master effects at the appropriate rules timing.
