# FT Master

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Obligatory. 

A Fireteam Master is capable of turning a handful of undisciplined troopers into a well-oiled war machine.

- The Trooper with this Special Skill must be part of a Fireteam.

- In the Order Count step of the Tactical Phase, if this Trooper is part of a Fireteam, all the members of that Fireteam are Regular.

- If all members of the Fireteam already have the Regular Characteristic on their Unit Profile, apply a +2 inch bonus to the Team Leader's Zone of Control when making Coherency Checks for the Fireteam.

- Restriction: This bonus is not cumulative with any bonus to the Team Leader’s Zone of Control.

- If a Unit name is listed in brackets after this Special Skill in the Trooper's Unit Profile, and the Trooper's Fireteam contains any members belonging to that Unit, the Fireteam is automatically canceled if the FT Master Trooper is no longer in the Fireteam.

Having an FT Master in a Fireteam does not prevent its members from suffering the effects of Loss of Lieutenant.


# FT Master — Interactions & Unofficial Commentary

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
The primary practical purpose of **FT Master** is **order conversion**:  
**If this Trooper is part of a Fireteam, all members of that Fireteam are considered Regular.**  
This rule turns normally Irregular Troopers into Regular Order contributors **as long as they remain part of that Fireteam**.

This makes FT Master one of the strongest order-efficiency skills in the game, especially for Sectorials whose Fireteam-eligible units are cheap Irregular troops.

## Interactions Notes

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

### FT Master & Order Conversion  
- When any Trooper with FT Master is in a Fireteam, **every member of that Fireteam becomes Regular**, regardless of whether they were originally Irregular.  
- This applies even if some members do *not* have FT Master themselves; being in the same Fireteam is enough.  
- Converted Orders join the Combat Group's main Regular Order Pool, increasing total spending flexibility.

### FT Master & Cheap Irregular Troops  
- Units that are normally discounted due to being Irregular effectively become **budget Regular Troopers** when added to a Fireteam that includes at least one FT Master.  
- This allows significantly more **order density per point cost**, creating very efficient lists.

### FT Master & Fireteam Integrity  
- If the Trooper with FT Master leaves the Fireteam (Coherency break, State change, etc.), all Irregular members **instantly revert to Irregular**, and their Orders stop contributing to the Regular Pool.  
- Protecting the FT Master Trooper becomes strategically important.

## Pros & Cons of FT Master (Focused on Order Efficiency)

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

### Pros
- **Major Regular Order generation increase**: converts entire Fireteam into Regular.  
- **High efficiency with low-cost Irregular troops**: effectively “upgrades” them at no SWC or points cost.  
- **Significant flexibility in Order expenditure** within the Combat Group.  

### Cons
- **Relies on maintaining Coherency and Fireteam integrity**.  
- **FT Master model becomes a high-value target** for assassination or Isolating attacks.  
- Loss of FT Master mid-turn may create sudden order shortages next turn.  
- Requires adherence to Fireteam restrictions and Fireteam Chart limitations.

---

## Tactical Commentary

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- FT Master Fireteams shine as “order farms” while still acting as functional combat units.  
- Prioritize positioning to keep Coherency, since dropping the FT Master collapses the efficiency engine.  
- Protect against E/M, Hacking, Jammers, and other Isolation sources.  
- Consider splitting groups to maximize Fireteams that include FT Master and cheap members.  
- Combine FT Master with defensive anchor units for low-risk, high-order generation.
