# Electromagnetic (E/M) Ammunition

- After a successful attack using E/M Ammunition, the target must make two Saving Rolls per hit suffered, using half his BTS value (BTS/2 x 2).

- E/M Ammunition reduces the BTS value of its target to half of its original value, rounding up.

- A failed Saving Roll against E/M Ammunition causes the target to enter Isolated State, placing an Isolated Token next to it, preventing it from receiving Orders from the Order Pool.

- If, at the start of his following Active Turns, the Trooper is still Isolated, then he is considered to be Irregular and does not add his Order to that Turns Order Pool.

- If the target fails a Saving Roll and is Heavy Infantry ( HI ), a TAG, a Remote ( REM ), or a Vehicle ( VH ), then it also enters Immobilized-B State. Place an Immobilized-B Token (IMM-B) next to it in addition to the Isolated Token.

- A Trooper who receives a hit from E/M Ammunition and fails the Saving Roll must make the usual Guts Roll, if applicable. The exceptions to this are HIs, TAGs, Remotes, and Vehicles that, having failed the Saving Roll, are now Immobilized-B and cannot make Guts Rolls. Also, Troopers possessing the Courage Special Skill, or an equivalent, can ignore this rule.

- Critical hits with E/M Ammunition cause the target to make an additional Saving Roll, applying their halved BTS value.

- The exceptions to this are HIs, TAGs, Remotes, and Vehicles that, having failed the Saving Roll, are now Immobilized-B and cannot make Guts Rolls. Also, Troopers possessing the Courage Special Skill, or an equivalent, can ignore this rule.

### Interaction Note
Related rules: ARO, Face-to-Face, Partial Cover.

**Electromagnetic (E/M) Ammunition – Interactions and Unofficial Commentary**
#### Electromagnetic (E/M) - Simplified Explanation

E/M Ammunition is a **state-inflicting**, **BTS-targeting**, **double-effect** ammo type.  
It halves BTS, then forces the target to save. Failing means:

- **Isolated** (everyone)
- **+ Immobilized-B** (HI, TAG, REM, Vehicle)

Because these results remove Orders and Actions, E/M is among the strongest control tools in the game.
---

#### Electromagnetic (E/M) - Interaction Notes (Official Mechanics)

**1. E/M vs ARO**  
- The defender gets only **Burst 1** in ARO.  
- If both declare BS Attacks, the roll is **Face-to-Face** per normal rules.  
- If the E/M attack hits and the target fails BTS, the target suffers state effects immediately.

**2. E/M vs Face-to-Face**  
- Criticals work normally: the critical success forces an **additional halved-BTS Saving Roll**.  
- If any of those rolls fail, the target enters Isolated, and possibly Immobilized-B.

---

#### Electromagnetic (E/M) - Comparisons (Pros & Cons)

**E/M vs AP Ammunition**  
- **E/M Pros:**  
  - Inflicts devastating **States** (Isolated, IMM-B).  
  - Strongest against heavy units (HI/TAG/REM).  
  - Reduces BTS (usually lower than ARM).  

- **E/M Cons:**  
  - Does **not deal Wounds**.
  - Can be negated by high BTS .  
  - Some Troopers have **Immunity (BTS)** or high BTS.
  - Troops with Warhorse (lots of Combined Army) are immune to Isolated, so E/M doesn't affect them much.

**E/M vs PARA / Stun / Control Effects**  
- **E/M Pros:**  
  - Stronger and more final: IMM-B is far more restrictive than PARA or Stunned.  
  - Isolated cripples Order generation.  
- **E/M Cons:**  
  - PARA/Stun often works on a wider array of targets that E/M does not fully disable.  
  - PARA/Stun weapons often have templates or better Burst.

**E/M vs Hacking (Oblivion, Carbonite)**  
- **E/M Pros:**  
  - Does not require Hackable or LoF conditions specific to hacking.  
  - Affects non-Hackable troops by causing Isolated.  
- **E/M Cons:**  
  - Shorter range or riskier delivery (grenades, pistols, close-range weapons).  
  - Hacking can be projected via Repeaters; E/M weapons usually require direct LoF.

---

#### Electromagnetic (E/M) - Tactical Notes and Examples

- **Primary use:** disabling elite/expensive pieces (Knights, TAGs, REM-spam cores, Hackers).  
- **Best delivery:** intuitive/close-range gunfighters, grenades, or direct-template E/M.  
- **Follow-up attacks:** Isolated enemies are easier to isolate and destroy—no coordinated AROs and fewer options.  
- **E/M vs TAGs:** One failed roll can remove a 90-point model from the fight for several Orders.  
- **Beware Immunity (BTS):** Loses all special effects.  
- **Using E/M in ARO:** high-risk but great payoff—succeeding once can break a full attack run.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
E/M is one of the strongest toolbox effects in Infinity N5 because it bypasses many forms of durability and defensive tech. Against elite lists, punishing the Order economy is often more devastating than dealing wounds. Use E/M when you want to **disable**, not **damage**, and pair it with pieces that can exploit the resulting helpless target.
