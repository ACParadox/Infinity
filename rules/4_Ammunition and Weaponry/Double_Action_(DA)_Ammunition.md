# Double Action (DA) Ammunition

This Ammunition uses high-impact light caliber projectiles.

- After a successful attack using DA Ammunition, the target must make two Saving Rolls per hit suffered.

- Each Saving Roll failed against DA Ammunition inflicts 1 Wound on the target.

- The second Saving Roll is mandatory, even if the target fails the first one or falls Unconscious.

- Critical hits with Double Action (DA) Ammunition cause the target to make an additional Saving Roll.

For each Critical hit with DA Ammunition, the target must make 3 Saving Rolls: 2 due to the hit with DA Ammunition and 1 more due to the additional Saving Roll.

### Interaction Note
Related rules: Double Action (DA) Ammunition, Saving Rolls, Critical Hits, ARO, Face-to-Face, Partial Cover.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### Interaction Note

Double Action (DA) Ammunition represents a powerful kinetic impact that forces **two ARM/BTS Saving Rolls for every hit**, regardless of whether the first roll already drops the target. This makes DA one of the most reliable ways to push a target toward Unconscious or Dead States through sheer saving-roll pressure.

DA interacts strongly with ARO dynamics. Since Burst is generally reduced to 1 in ARO, many weapons that rely on DA do not suffer as heavily from the reactive Burst limitation compared to AP or Shock weapons that depend on multiple hits. A **single hit with DA in ARO still forces 2–3 rolls** (if Critical), making it disproportionately punishing.

Partial Cover still provides its +3 ARM/BTS to **each** Saving Roll the target must make. Thus DA’s efficiency scales inversely with defensive modifiers: the better the target’s ARM, Cover, and range control, the worse DA becomes compared to AP.

In a Face-to-Face Roll, DA does not increase your chances of winning the roll, but **dramatically increases the consequences** when you do. This makes DA especially valuable for high-BS platforms, Fireteam Leaders, and units that can reliably win FTF through MOD stacking.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

DA Ammunition sits between AP and EXP in the “wound pressure spectrum,” providing multiple saves like EXP but without attribute-halving effects. As a result, the best targets for DA are:

- **Low-to-mid ARM units** — they crack easily under multiple saves.  
- **ARO threats or midfield brawlers** where single-hit lethality matters.

Its comparative weaknesses include:

- Very low Burst profiles in the Active Turn where AP or Shock may offer more consistency.

### Double Action (DA) - Comparisons (Unofficial)
For the same Burst value, DA ammo is generally superior to AP or Shock.

### Double Action (DA) - Tactical Use (Unofficial)

Most MULTI weapon that have DA ammo reduce the Burst of the weapon to 1, making it ideal in ARO where Burst is maximum 1.

The exception is Sniper rifles which keep B2 for DA.
