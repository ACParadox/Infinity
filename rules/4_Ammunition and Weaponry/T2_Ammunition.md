# T2 Ammunition

A high-value ammunition that causes severe damage to the target.

- After a successful attack using T2 Ammunition, the target must make a Saving Roll per hit suffered.

- Each failed Saving Roll against T2 Ammunition inflicts 2 Wounds on the target.

- Critical hits with T2 Ammunition cause the target to make an additional Saving Roll. Failing this additional Saving Roll inflicts only 1 additional Wound on the target. It is important to indicate, before performing the Saving Rolls, which die corresponds to the Saving Roll from the hit and which die to the additional Saving Roll from the Critical effect.

### Interaction Note
Related rules: ARO, Face-to-Face, Partial Cover.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
**Simplified Explanation**

T2 Ammunition causes:
- 1 Saving Roll per successful hit.
- Each failed roll inflicts **2 Wounds** instead of 1.
- On a Critical:
  - The target makes 1 extra Saving Roll.
  - That extra roll inflicts **only 1 Wound** (not 2).

So:  
- Normal hit → fail = 2 Wounds.  
- Critical’s extra roll → fail = 1 Wound.  

This makes T2 extremely lethal vs 1-Wound troops and very threatening even to multi-Wound or high-ARM targets.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
**Interaction / Resolution**

**Saving Rolls**  
   - Roll ARM (or BTS if the weapon specifies) normally.  
   - For each failed roll from normal hits → apply 2 Wounds.  
   - For each failed Critical bonus roll → apply 1 Wound.

**States & Incapacitation**  
   - T2 interacts brutally with 1-W models: one failed save = straight to Dead.  
   - NWI, Dogged, and multiple-Wound models are not immune; 2-Wound hits can outright kill them without entering Unconscious.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
**Comparison With Similar Ammo Types**

DA vs T2  
- DA = 2 saves, 1 Wound per failed save → maximum 2 Wounds but requires multiple failed rolls.  
- T2 = 1 save, inflicts 2 Wounds immediately.  
**Pros T2:** More reliable lethality from a single failed save.  
**Pros DA:** Better chance a putting down a single wound unit.

EXP vs T2  
- EXP = 3 saves, 1 Wound each → very high ceiling but depends on multiple fails.  
- T2 = guaranteed 2-Wound punishment for a single failure.  
**Pros T2:** Consistent kill potential on one fail.  
**Pros EXP:** Better chance a putting down a single wound unit.

In general DA and EXP are better than T2 for single wound units (non-NWI and Dogged).

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
**Pros and Cons Summary**

Pros of T2:
- Extremely efficient at killing 1-Wound troops outright.
- High lethality vs Multi-Wound targets.

Cons of T2:
- Less effective vs high ARM targets compared to AP/EXP.
