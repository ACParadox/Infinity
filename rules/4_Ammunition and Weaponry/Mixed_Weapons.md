# Mixed Weapons

These weapons can be used as both BS Weapons and CC Weapons, so they have two different Modes.

- In CC Weapon Mode it: Uses the Trooper's CC Attribute. Has the CC Trait. Is a CC Weapon for all purposes.

- In BS Weapon Mode it: Uses the Trooper's BS Attribute. Has Range MODs. Is a BS Weapon for all purposes. If the weapon uses an Attribute other than BS to make BS Attacks, this will be listed in its Traits.

### Interaction Note
Related rules: ARO, Face-to-Face, Partial Cover.  
Topic: **Mixed Weapons**

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

Mixed Weapons occupy a unique space in Infinity N5 because they can be used as both BS Weapons *and* CC Weapons. This dual-mode functionality creates several interaction points worth noting.

---
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
#### How Mixed Weapons Interact with Core Rules

**1. Mixed Weapons - ARO interactions**  
- A Trooper armed with a Mixed Weapon can freely choose whether to respond in CC (using the CC Mode) or as a BS Attack (using the BS Mode) whenever melee contact occurs.  
- Because Mixed Weapons count as BS Weapons when used at range, any Trooper targeted at distance may ARO with the BS Mode—even if the weapon also has a CC Mode.  
- In Engaged State, the BS Mode cannot be used unless a very specific rule overrides the normal restrictions. Therefore: in most ARO situations while Engaged, the Mixed Weapon’s CC Mode is your only usable option.

**Pros:** flexibility to pick the correct mode in ARO; ability to deter both melee and ranged attackers.  
**Cons:** the range-mode is still limited by the usual BS Attack restrictions (LoF, range bands, MODs).
