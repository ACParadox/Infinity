# WildParrot

This mobile weapons platform has been designed to be deployed on the battlefield at a distance, acting as a Mine with E/M Special Ammunition.

- These weapons are placed with the Place Deployable or Intuitive Attack Common Skills, always applying the Deployable and Perimeter rule.

- Therefore, when players deploy this weapon, they place the WildParrot totally inside the Zone of Control of the Trooper, instead of placing it in Silhouette contact.

- Deployed WildParrots work like E/M Mines, except that a WildParrot Token or Model is placed instead of a Camouflage Marker.

Once deployed, WildParrots work like E/M Mines.

Related rules: ARO, Face-to-Face, Partial Cover, Deployable, Perimeter, E/M Ammunition, Direct Template.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

WildParrots function as a range-deployed E/M Mine.

Step-by-Step Resolution

You declare Place Deployable.
✔ No LoF required
✔ No roll required
✔ You place the WildParrot at the end of the Order, inside your ZoC.

If a Camouflaged Marker is inside the Trigger Area, you must declare Intuitive Attack instead.
– Requires a WIP Roll
– If failed: WildParrot is not placed and the Disposable use is spent.

### How WildParrots Interact With Core Mechanics

**Triggering**
WildParrots trigger like E/M Mines rather than like Boost-style Perimeter Weapons. They do **not** “move” toward the enemy. Instead, they detonate when the usual Mine trigger conditions are met (enemy enters trigger area, enemy interacts, etc.). The Perimeter rule only governs **placement**, not movement or Boost behavior. Unlike CrazyKoalas or MadTraps, WildParrots do *not* walk toward targets.

**ARO Generation**
Enemies do **not** get AROs against a WildParrot detonation. Mines and Deployables do not provoke AROs by detonating. However, an enemy may generate AROs against the **Trooper who is placing the WildParrot** if that Trooper was seen performing Place Deployable or Intuitive Attack.


**Partial Cover Interaction**
Partial Cover does **not** apply to Direct Template Attacks. WildParrot detonations, being Direct Template E/M attacks, ignore any Cover to ARM/BTS. The saving attribute is BTS/2 regardless.

**Effect Resolution**
A triggered WildParrot forces **two BTS Saving Rolls at BTS/2**. A single failure causes **Isolated**, and HI/REM/TAG/VH additionally become **Immobilized-B**. This can disable an entire attack piece in a single event.

### WildParrots Pros and Cons (Compared to E/M Mines, Koalas, MadTraps)

**Compared to E/M Mines**
Pros:
- Can be placed at distance inside ZoC instead of Silhouette contact (safer, more flexible).
- Not Camouflaged, but more flexible placement compensates.
- Works identically to E/M Mines once deployed.

Cons:
- No Concealed trait; enemy can see and potentially destroy it.
- Perimeter placement may force slightly less optimal hiding spots.

**Compared to CrazyKoalas (Boost)**
Pros:
- WildParrots deliver E/M, which is dramatically more punishing against HI/REM/TAG.

Cons:
- WildParrots **do not move** toward enemies; they lack Boost.
- Easier for opponents to avoid if they never enter the trigger radius.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### WildParrots Practical Interaction Examples

**Vs. advancing Heavy Infantry**
A HI entering a lane covered by a hidden WildParrot risks losing their entire activation: a failed BTS roll can cause Isolated and Immobilized-B simultaneously.

**Vs. skirmishers**
Low-BTS models fear Isolated; even a single failed roll can cut them off from Orders and hacking defenses.

**Vs. TAGs**
One WildParrot can neutralize a TAG for several Orders. TAGs usually have high BTS but halving it (BTS/2) makes these hits dangerous.

**Vs. shooters trying to clear it**
Direct shooting at a WildParrot is allowed (as a Deployable). However, if an enemy moves into trigger range while attempting to get a firing angle, it may detonate first.

---

### WildParrots Summary
WildParrots create a powerful area-denial threat using E/M effects but lack the predictive movement of Boost-type Perimeter weapons. Their strengths lie in flexible deployment, devastating status effects, and reliable triggering. Their weaknesses come from being visible and static, unlike Concealed Mines or mobile Koalas/MadTraps.
