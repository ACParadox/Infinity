# Disco Baller

A two-use Disposable BS support weapon that allows Speculative Attacks, uses the BS Attribute, and fires Disco Balls, light-emitting jamming devices.

- You do not need a target for the Disco Baller, simply choose the desired location and make the corresponding non Face to Face Normal BS Roll. If you pass the Roll, a Disco Ball is successfully deployed in the location, placing a Disco Ball Miniature or Token in the Conclusion step of the Order.

- After placing the Disco Ball, place an Eclipse Special Ammo Circular Template, centered on the Disco Ball.

- At the start of the States Phase, the Disco Ball deactivates. Remove the Circular Template.

- Deactivated Disco Balls can be reactivated using the Activate Disco Ball Skill.

- A deployed Disco Ball remains on the table until the end of the game, or until it is destroyed.

## Activate Disco Ball

- The Trooper must have the Deactivator piece of Equipment.

- The Disco Ball must be in the LoF or Zone of Control of the Trooper.

- Only allied Disco Balls can be Activated this way, never enemy Disco Balls.

- By making a successful WIP +3 Normal Roll, this Skill allows for the remote activation of an allied Disco Ball.

- The Range MODs of the Deactivator are applied to the Roll.

- If the Roll is successful, in the Conclusion Step of the Order place the Eclipse Special Ammo Circular Template, centered on the Disco Ball Model or Token.

- At the start of the States Phase, the Disco Ball deactivates. Remove the Circular Template.

- If the DiscoBall is destroyed remove the Circular Template from the game table.

## Disco Baller FAQs

Q: When using Place Deployable and declaring where the Deployable will be placed, what happens if a Trooper moves into that space before the Conclusion of the Order?

A: The Trooper that declared Place Deployable may place the Deployable in a different position (still following the Place Deployable rule), and if this is not possible, the Deployable will be lost.

This also applies to weapons that place a Deployable Weapon or Equipment on a successful Roll, such as Disco Ballers, Pitchers, and Drop Bears (BS Mode).


### Interaction Note
Related rules: ARO, Face-to-Face, Partial Cover.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

Disco baller are similar to eclise ammo, they create a zero visibility zone that cannot be pierced.

**Disco Baller - Core Interaction Themes**
The trooper using Disco Ball can movement to expose enemies piece-by-piece by moving around the disco ball zone, so it can provoke staggered ARO timings that limit coordinated responses, instead of triggering multiple AROs at once.

**AROs Against a Disco Baller**
The enemy’s BS Attack ARO is also a Normal Roll.


**Face-to-Face Dynamics**
If the Disco Baller uses an effect that does not require a roll, then opposing BS Attacks become Normal Rolls. Note that the eclipse effect is triggered later in the the order, so it won't affect the enemies shooting you in ARO.

**Pros and Cons of a Disco Baller vs. Similar Tools**
Comparing a Disco Baller to “visibility control units” like White Noise users or Intuitive Attack platforms:

*Advantages*
- Can force reactive decisions without committing to lethal engagement.
- Potentially affects multiple enemies simultaneously.
- Strong when combined with movement tricks that exploit LoF angles.
- Nothing sees through eclipse
- Protects units moving, especially forward CC or closer range bands.

*Disadvantages*
- Provides no guaranteed damage unless paired with attacks.
- Can be deactivated
- Disposable

## Disco ball Counters

### Deactivator
Deactivator can be used on a Disco Ball inside an Eclipse zone — but only from within ZoC (it usuall can be Lof or ZoC).

Because Eclipse blocks LoF completely, you cannot use a Deactivator from farther than 8″ away.

### Sixth Sense:
Sixth Sense does not let you see through Eclipse.
But it DOES help when someone shoots you through Eclipse:

You still cannot draw LoF, so you cannot shoot back.

But if you are hit, you can Dodge with no visibility penalties.
(Sixth Sense removes all negative MODs except immobilized/isolated penalties.)

This is defensive only.

### Hacking (Comms Attacks)

✓ Works normally if target is within ZoC or Repeater network.
Hacking ignores visibility entirely since it does not need LoF.

Relevant: All standard hacking programs (e.g., Oblivion, Spotlight, Trinity) function without issue.

### Intuitive Attack

Does not require LoF but requires template or direct template weapon and imposes WIP roll.

Eclipse does NOT prevent Intuitive Attack.

### Speculative Attack

“Speculative Attacks do not require LoF… do not apply any negative MODs for Visibility Zones, only the -6 MOD for Speculative Attack.”

✓ You may throw grenades / launchers through Eclipse freely.

### Some Direct Template Weapons (DTW)

✓ Work normally if the target is inside their Zone of Control and you see no need for LoF.

Requires placing the template so that target is inside the area, but does not require LoF.

### CC Attack
Close Combat works normally inside an Eclipse zone.
Eclipse prevents drawing LoF, but CC does not need LoF, only Silhouette contact.

**Disco Baller - Tactical Summary**
A Disco Baller plays best as a tempo manipulator: forcing bad AROs, denying Partial Cover, and shaping Face-to-Face odds. Its power is indirect—the more the opponent is pushed into suboptimal reactions, the more the Disco Baller amplifies the lethality of the *rest* of its force.

Use it early in a turn to open safe lanes for key attackers, or late in a turn to pin enemy ARO pieces in place while scoring objectives.
