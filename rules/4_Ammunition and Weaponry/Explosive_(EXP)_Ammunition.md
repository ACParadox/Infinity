# Explosive (EXP) Ammunition

Ammunition and Weaponry

A type of ordnance designed to cause massive damage to the target by detonating on impact.

- After a successful attack using Explosive (EXP) Ammunition, the target must make three Saving Rolls per hit suffered.

- Each Saving Roll failed against EXP Ammunition inflicts 1 Wound on the target.

- The three Saving Rolls are mandatory, even if the target fails one of them or falls Unconscious.

- Critical hits with Explosive Ammunition cause the target to make an additional Saving Roll.

For each Critical hit with EXP Ammunition, the target must make 4 Saving Rolls: 3 due to the impact with EXP Ammunition and 1 more due to the additional Saving Roll.

### Interaction Note
Related rules: ARO, Face-to-Face, Partial Cover.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

EXP Ammunition is one of the most punishing damage types in Infinity because each *single hit* forces **three independent Saving Rolls**. This drastically increases lethality whenever the attacker can reliably land a Face-to-Face success, especially with high-damage weapons like Missile Launchers.

Below are the major interaction considerations:

---

### How EXP Interacts with ARO
- **ARO Burst is 1**, so most EXP weapons in ARO suffer from low dice volume.  
  - A single failed Face-to-Face roll means no EXP impact at all.
- However, if the defender wins the F2F, even with only *one* hit, the target still takes 3–4 Saves.
- **High-Burst EXP weapons do not exist**, so EXP payoff in ARO relies on strong positioning, long-range Range Bands, and forcing unfavorable movement from the opponent.

**Pros (ARO)**  
- Massive punishment for opponents who lose a single F2F.  
- Strong area denial due to high threat per hit.

**Cons (ARO)**  
- Low Burst can make landing hits inconsistent.  
- Vulnerable to MOD stacking by the Active Player.

---

### How EXP Interacts with Face-to-Face Rolls
- Because EXP’s damage output triggers *after* the roll, the most important interaction is **simply winning the F2F**.

- When combining with **AP+EXP**, the defender halves ARM/BTS *and* still takes all 3–4 rolls. Very few enemies survive.

**Key takeaway:** Because Burst is typically 1, players must rely on strong modifiers (range, link bonuses, surprise, etc.) to secure a winning face-to-face roll.

---

### How EXP Interacts with Partial Cover
Partial Cover provides:
- **–3 BS MOD** for the attacker.
- **+3 ARM** for the defender (if the weapon uses ARM saves).

This is one of the most important interactions with EXP:
- +3 ARM applies **on all three saving rolls**, meaning Cover effectively adds +9 total ARM value across the sequence.
- Yet EXP remains brutally lethal, especially from high-Damage sources (e.g., DAM 14–15 Missile Launchers, DAM 16 Autocannons).

**Practical result:** Cover greatly improves survival, but EXP can still delete most 1-Wound units and seriously threaten even TAGs.

### Explosive (EXP) - Comparison to Similar Ammunition Types

#### **DA (Double Action) vs. EXP**
- **DA:** 2 Saves per hit  
- **EXP:** 3 Saves per hit  
- EXP is strictly more damaging, but DA can appear on weapons with higher Burst or more accessible platforms.

**Pros of EXP over DA**  
- Higher maximum damage per hit.  
- More punishing when Burst is low but damage must be guaranteed.

**Pros of DA over EXP**  
- More widely available, often found on higher-B platforms (BS attacks, CC, hacking).  
---

#### **AP vs. EXP**
- **AP:** One Save per hit, but ARM/BTS is halved.  
- **EXP:** Three Saves, no automatic ARM reduction.

**Pros of EXP**  
- Much higher total wound potential.  
- Particularly devastating vs. low-ARM targets (they cannot halve what they barely have).

**Pros of AP**  
- Better vs. high-ARM targets (TAGs/HI) because halving ARM is often more effective to win the FtF than forcing raw quantity of saves.

---

#### **AP+EXP Combined Ammunition**
This is the most lethal routine ammunition in the game.  
A single AP+EXP hit frequently deletes even heavily armored models.

---

### Explosive (EXP) - Tactical Considerations
- EXP weapons excel in **long-range domination**, punishing movement or peeking.
- Best used on units with:
  - long Range Bands (Missile Launchers)
  - high BS
  - Fireteam bonuses
- Avoid trading with them in the open—forcing templates, Smoke, or White Noise is often preferable.
- Against EXP, prioritize:
  - staying in Cover  
  - mods (Mimetism, range)  
  - forcing Normal Rolls whenever possible

---

### Summary
EXP Ammunition is a low-volume, high-impact mechanic: it converts *every* successful hit into a serious threat, especially when paired with high base Damage or AP combination modes. It is one of the strongest area-control and over-watch tools in the game but relies heavily on winning the Face-to-Face roll due to usually low Burst.
