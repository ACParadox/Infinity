# Armor Piercing (AP) Ammunition

A type of ammunition designed to penetrate even the toughest armor plating.

- After a successful attack using AP Ammunition, the target must make a Saving Roll per hit suffered, using half his ARM or BTS value (ARM/2 or BTS/2) as specified by the weapon being used.

- AP Ammunition reduces the ARM or BTS value of its target to half of its original value, rounding up. Consequently, any ARM or BTS above 0 cannot be reduced below 1.

- Each Saving Roll failed against AP Ammunition inflicts 1 Wound on the target.

- Critical hits with AP Ammunition cause the targets to make an additional Saving Roll, applying their halved ARM or BTS value.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
## Simplified Explanation

AP ammo weakens the target’s defense by cutting their ARM/BTS in half (rounding up) before they make their Saving Rolls.
It does not increase damage, but rather reduces effective protection, which drastically improves lethality against armored or BTS-based defenses.

It always inflicts 1 Wound per failed Save, unless paired with another ammo type (like DA, EXP).

Criticals simply add one extra Saving Roll, still using the halved ARM/BTS.

AP ammunition works by cutting the base ARM or BTS value of the target in half (rounding up).

After you have the reduced base value, you then add any modifiers such as:
- Partial Cover (+3 ARM or BTS)
- TinBots
- Other situational MODs to Saving Rolls
Then perform the Saving Roll(s).

AP does not alter damage—only the defensive Attribute.

### Step-by-Step Resolution / Interaction

1. Attack hits (normal or Face-to-Face success).

2. Check weapon profile: If it lists AP, apply ARM/2 or BTS/2 (depending on weapon).

3. Round up (e.g., ARM 5 → 3).

4. Target makes the listed number of Saving Rolls (typically 1).

5. Apply +3 Partial Cover to SR

6. For each failed Save, target takes 1 Wound.

7. If attacker rolled a Critical, target makes one additional Save using the same halved ARM/BTS value.

## Interaction Note

AP vs. ARM/BTS Boosts (e.g., Cover, TinBots, SymbioMate)

SymbioMate explicitly overrides ARM/BTS values to ARM 9 / BTS 9 regardless of AP (treating AP as Normal), so AP is ignored there.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
## Order of Operations: Does Cover Apply Before or After AP?

AP halves base ARM, then you add Cover.
Example: ARM 3 → AP halves to 2 → Cover makes 5.

## Pros & Cons of AP Ammunition
### Pros

Best vs. high ARM/BTS targets (HI, TAGs, REMs, Vehicles).

Scales extremely well with higher Damage weapons (HMG, Autocannon).

More reliable than Shock or Stun against multi-Wound models.

Works against both ARM and BTS (depending on weapon).

### Cons
Low benefit vs. lightly armored troops (ARM 0–1 halving does little).

Less dramatically impactful than DA/EXP vs. single targets (those inflict multiple saves).

T2 can produce more total Wounds; Shock can eliminate Unconscious states—AP does neither.

## Comparison Table
Ammo Type	Effect	Best Use	Weakness
AP	Halves ARM/BTS	Crack heavy armor efficiently	Lower impact vs light troops

DA	2 Saves	Stress multiple-Wound targets	Ineffective vs high ARM unless damage is high

EXP	3 Saves	Maximum burst damage	Overkill for light/medium targets

Shock	Cancels Unconscious	Cleanup infantry	Does nothing vs high ARM

T2	Inflicts 2 Wounds on fail	Kill multi-W units	Single Save only; no effect on STR targets

## Tactical Notes and Examples
### When to Choose AP Mode

Your target has ARM 4+ or BTS 6+.

You're firing a MULTI Rifle, HMG, or AP Marksman Rifle in good range bands.

Opponent is using SymbioMate? → AP is ignored (treat as Normal), so choose Shock or DA instead.

### When AP is less effective

Shooting Warbands or Line Infantry (ARM 0–1).

When Shock Mode denies Unconscious and secures kills more easily.

Target relies on BTS → consider Breaker (AP vs BTS) or Viral (multiple saves).
