# N5 FAQ v0.1 — Fired Deployable Placement

> **N5 FAQ v0.1 clarification (September 2026):** A Deployable fired with the applicable BS Attack mechanic **cannot be placed on a vertical surface**. Its Token must be **fully supported by the horizontal surface beneath it**.

This ruling applies to affected weapons/equipment including:

- **Pitcher**
- **Drop Bears**
- **Disco Baller**
- **Mine Dispenser**

Do not place these fired Deployable Tokens vertically on walls, façades, pillars, or other vertical scenery, even if the firing solution would otherwise reach that point.

This is an authoritative local FAQ override for the related weapon/equipment pages if an older generated page omits the September 2026 clarification.
