# D-Charges

Demolition (or simply D) Charges is the generic name for any number of explosive charges. As a weapon, they have been designed to demolish structures, objectives and terrain alike, but they can also be used in Close Combat.

- To use D-Charges in Demolition Mode, the user must be in Silhouette contact with a Building or Scenery Structure, or with an Enemy Model in an Immobilized or Null State (except Sepsitorized or Possessed ).

- To use D-Charges in CC Mode, the user must be in Silhouette contact with an enemy Trooper.

- D-Charges in Demolition Mode: Are placed using the Place Deployable Short Skill. Restriction: Cannot be used in ARO. Detonate during the Resolution Step of the Order, without requiring a Roll.

- D-Charges in CC Mode: Follow the CC Combat rules.

- The detonation of D-Charges doesn't affect the user.

- Both Modes of using this weapon share the Disposable (3) Trait. The user may use it just 3 times max, no matter which Mode they are using.

- The Improvised Trait gives a CC-6 MOD to the user.

- Are placed using the Place Deployable Short Skill.

- Restriction: Cannot be used in ARO.

- Detonate during the Resolution Step of the Order, without requiring a Roll.

- Follow the CC Combat rules.

### Interaction Note
Related rules: ARO, Face-to-Face, Partial Cover, CC Attack, Place Deployable, Anti-materiel.
---
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
**D-Charges - Simplified Explanation**

D-Charges are extremely destructive **AP+EXP** weapons that can be used either as:
1. **Demolition Mode** — placing an explosive on scenery or on an immobilized/null target. It does **not** require a roll and **cannot** be used in ARO. Note that destroying scenery is only used in certain scenarios, as scenery usually can't be destroyed.

2. **CC Mode** — used directly as a Close Combat Weapon. This uses all normal CC attack rules, including Face-to-Face interactions.

Because CC Attacks are never affected by Partial Cover, and because D-Charges in CC Mode suffer **Improvised (–6 CC)**, they are very strong but risky unless assisted by high CC stats or MODs such as Martial Arts.

---
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
**D-Charges - Step-by-Step Interaction Resolution**

1. **Declaring Demolition Mode**
   - User must be in Silhouette contact with valid scenery or an enemy in **Immobilized or Null state** (not Possessed/Sepsitorized).
   - Declare **Place Deployable**.
   - No AROs involving Face-to-Face are possible against the placement itself.
   - The D-Charge detonates in the Resolution Step, automatically, and cannot harm the user.

2. **Declaring CC Mode**
   - Must be in Silhouette contact with a trooper.
   - D-Charges become a CC Weapon with **AP+EXP**, causing 3 ARM saves per hit (4 on crit).
   - Since it is a **CC Attack**, opponents may declare CC Attack or Dodge; these become **Face-to-Face** if they oppose the action.
   - Partial Cover never applies.
   - The user applies **Improvised (–6 CC)** unless other modifiers override or compensate.

3. **ARO Restrictions**
   - **Demolition Mode cannot be used in ARO**.
   - **CC Mode may be used in ARO**, because it is simply a CC Attack.

4. **Guard and D-Charges**
   - Guard allows performing CC Attacks without touching the enemy.
   - D-Charges still function because Guard explicitly states they can use “D-Charges” as if in Silhouette contact.

5. **Anti-materiel interactions**
   - D-Charges always have **Anti-materiel**, so they can destroy scenery/objectives capable of being destroyed.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

D-Charges sit in a unique niche: part deployable explosive, part ultra-lethal CC Option. This creates many interesting interactions:

**D-Charges - Pros**
- Strongest anti-materiel option in most lists.
- AP+EXP in CC Mode is one of the most lethal melee profiles in the game.
- Ignores Partial Cover because all CC Attacks ignore Cover.
- Can be combined with Martial Arts, Berserk, Guard, or CC Surprise MODs for devastating effect.
- Auto-detonation in Demolition Mode means guaranteed scenery destruction if you can reach it.

**D-Charges - Cons**
- **–6 Improvised MOD** is painful without CC buffs or high native CC.
- Cannot use Demolition Mode in ARO.
- Requires base contact in most situations (except with Guard).
- Disposable (3) limits repeated mission use for objectives such as Sabotage.
- Using CC Mode exposes you to enemy CC specialists.

**D-Charges - Tactical Notes**
- Combine with **Immobilize** effects to ensure legal and safe Demolition placement on tough enemy models like TAGs.
- Engineers often benefit most because they need them for Classifieds such as **Sabotage**.
