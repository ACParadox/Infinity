# Eclipse Ammunition

Eclipse Ammunition is a variant of Smoke Ammunition designed to block the effect of Multispectral Visors.

- Eclipse Ammunition works in exactly the same way as Smoke Ammunition, but the Zero Visibility Zone it generates also affects Multispectral Visors, preventing them from drawing LoF through the Zone, regardless of their Level.

- Performing an Attack with a weapon with Eclipse Ammunition allows the user to make a Face to Face Roll against all enemy Attacks that require a Roll and LoF, and whose LoF passes through the Reflective Zero Visibility Zone generated by the Template.

- If a Trooper with a Multispectral Visor is the target of a BS Attack into, through, or out of this Reflective Zero Visibility Zone, their Multispectral Visor cannot reduce the MODs of the resulting Poor Visibility Zone.

The Area of Effect of a Template may affect Allied Troopers as long as the Template lacks the PS Attribute and causes no State.

Multispectral Visors are affected by the Reflective Trait, so Troopers equipped with this piece of Equipment also perform Face to Face Rolls against weapons with Eclipse Ammunition.

Area of Effect of Special Terrain Zones In Infinity, the Area of Effect of a Special Terrain zone is the area in where its special effects are applied. Any Trooper in Silhouette contact with a Special Terrain zone, or whose base or Silhouette Template is covered at least partially by a Special Terrain zone, is equally affected by the Effects of the zone.

### Eclipse Ammunition - Simplified Explanation

Eclipse Ammo is a Smoke-type visibility tool whose primary advantage is that it **completely nullifies Multispectral Visors of all levels**. While normal Smoke can be countered by MSV2 and MSV3, Eclipse cannot — no MSV can see through it.

Like Smoke, Eclipse:
- Creates a Zero Visibility Zone.
- Allows a Face-to-Face roll when thrown/shot as a response to BS Attacks whose LoF crosses the template.
- Is non-lethal and blocks LoF.

- Eclipse Ammunition generates a Zero Visibility Zone (see: Special Terrain, Visibility Conditions) the size of a Circular Template and with infinite height.

- The Eclipse Template remains on the table until the start of the States Phase.
Smoke Ammunition is a non-offensive ammunition, so it does not require an enemy or, in fact, any trooper at all as a target, and can be thrown at any point on the table.

- Performing an Attack with a weapon with Eclipse Ammunition allows the user to make a Face to Face Roll against all enemy Attacks that require a Roll and LoF, and whose LoF passes through the Reflective Zero Visibility Zone generated by the Template.

- Critical hits with Eclipse Ammunition have no additional effect, other than winning the Face to Face Roll, if applicable.

- Any enemy in the Area of Effect of the Smoke Template that declares a Dodge ARO will make a Normal Roll, not a Face to Face Roll against the Eclipse user.

Its added effect:
- **MSV troopers cannot see through it** and therefore cannot ignore or mitigate visibility penalties.

### Eclipse Ammunition - Interaction Resolution (Step-by-Step)

1. **Declaring Eclipse Attack**
   - The Active trooper declares BS Attack with Eclipse (usually Burst 1).  
   - Template is placed as with Smoke.

2. **ARO Generation**
   - Enemies that can see the active trooper declare AROs normally (BS Attack, Dodge, etc.).
   - MSV troopers can still declare AROs before the template is resolved.

3. **Face-to-Face Mechanics**
   - If an enemy BS Attack’s LoF crosses the Eclipse template area, the Eclipse attack is **Face-to-Face** with all such attacks.
   - This applies **even against MSV** due to the Reflective trait.

4. **Outcome**
   - If the Eclipse wins the Face-to-Face, the template lands and **blocks LoF for everyone**, including MSV users.
   - Once placed, no trooper may draw LoF into/through/out of the zone.
   - MSV cannot downgrade the zone to Poor Visibility—Eclipse explicitly prevents this.

### Comparison: Eclipse vs Smoke vs White Noise

**Eclipse**
- Blocks *all* LoF, including MSV.
- Best for approaching elite MSV ARO pieces (Kamau, linked MSV2 snipers).
- Reflective: MSV shooters entering FTF suffer the same LoF interaction as non-MSV.

**Smoke**
- Standard tool; MSV2+ can see through it and shoot without penalty.
- Cheaper and more common, but inferior vs MSV.
- Can be combined with your own MSV2 to shoot at non-MSV targets.

**White Noise**
- Blocks LoF **only from MSV troopers**.
- Does NOT block LoF for non-MSV troopers.
- Generated by Hackers, not usually by thrown weapons.
- More positional control but narrower application.

**Eclipse Ammunition -  Pros/Cons Summary**

**Eclipse Pros**
- Absolute LoF block; MSV cannot counter it.
- Enables safe movement vs top-tier ARO units.
- FTF protection on deployment.

**Eclipse Cons**
- Cannot use in ARO like Smoke Grenade Launcher B2 profiles.
- Fewer units have access to it 

**Discoball Pros**
- Can be reactivated with a Deactivation
- More common then Eclipse ammo
- Better range than Eclipse Grenades

**Discoball Cons**
- Only appears at end of order so cannot use in FtF.
- Disposable only

**Smoke Pros**
- Widely available.
- Often higher Burst when fired from launchers.

**Smoke Cons**
- Weak vs MSV.

**White Noise Pros**
- One-sided: protects your MSV shooter while punishing theirs.
- Does not block LoF for your non-MSV units.

**White Noise Cons**
- Requires a Hacker and network projection and order investment.
- Does not protect from non-MSV AROs.

### [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]

- Eclipse is **the strongest anti-MSV “smoke” tool** in Infinity.  
- It is often the *only* reliable way to move against linked MSV2 snipers without taking high-risk Face-to-Face rolls.
- Timing and positioning are critical—throwing Eclipse from **total cover** is ideal.
- Eclipse is especially valuable in missions requiring midfield button-pushing or close-range dominance.
- A common tactic: use Eclipse to cross fire lanes, then drop Smoke further upfield to sustain protection without spending more rare Eclipse uses.
