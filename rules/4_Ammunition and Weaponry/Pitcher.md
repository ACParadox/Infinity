A support BS Weapon that shoots Deployable Repeaters, a range amplifier for Hackers.

- A successful BS Roll allows players to place a Deployable Repeater Token (REPEATER) at the point of impact.

## FAQs

Q: When using Place Deployable and declaring where the Deployable will be placed, what happens if a Trooper moves into that space before the Conclusion of the Order?

A: The Trooper that declared Place Deployable may place the Deployable in a different position (still following the Place Deployable rule), and if this is not possible, the Deployable will be lost.

This also applies to weapons that place a Deployable Weapon or Equipment on a successful Roll, such as Disco Ballers, Pitchers, and Drop Bears (BS Mode).

### Interaction Note
Related rules: ARO, Face-to-Face, Partial Cover, Speculative Attack, Targetless, Deployable, Repeater.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

The Pitcher is a BS Weapon that creates a Deployable Repeater at the point of impact. Because it is **Speculative Attack**, **Targetless**, **Indiscriminate**, and **Non-Lethal**, its interactions differ noticeably from other deployable-placing tools (Drop Bears, FastPandas, Mine Dispensers).

### Repeater
Repeaters extend the Hacking Area of all Hackers in the same Army List.
    Hackers within the Zone of Control of an enemy Repeater may use it to apply Hacking Programs against any enemy Hacker, but applying Firewall MODs (-3).
    There can be no reaction against a Repeater that is being used by an enemy Hacker, only against the Hacker, if possible.

The Trooper benefiting from the Firewall also applies a MOD of +3 to their Saving Rolls (SR) against the Comms Attack.

Only the MODs of a single Firewall can be applied, even if the Trooper benefits from more than one. In the case of the Trooper benefitting from more than one Firewall, their player will decide which one to apply.



**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
**Pitcher - Rules interactions:**

- **ARO:**  
  Declaring a Pitcher is a **BS Attack**, so it **always grants AROs** to enemies with LoF to the active model (even though the attack itself does not require LoF or a target).  
  Enemies cannot react to the *impact point*, only to the *attacking trooper*.

- **Face-to-Face:**  
  Pitcher shots are **almost always Normal Rolls** because:  
  - Targetless weapons **cannot be opposed directly**.  
  - Opposing Skills like Dodge or BS Attack do **not interact with the Pitcher’s impact**, because no target is being attacked.  
  Only the *attacking trooper* may be affected by enemy AROs (e.g., being shot), and these do **not** oppose the Pitcher Roll unless they directly cancel the active skill (e.g., forcing Guts Movement after resolution, but that happens after impact).

- **Partial Cover:**  
  Does not apply to Pitcher because no target is being attacked.  
  The **attacking trooper** may still benefit from Cover against incoming AROs.

- **Speculative Attack MODs:**  
  Pitcher uses the normal Speculative Attack rule: **–6 MOD** to the BS Roll.  
  This is applied when firing at a point without LoF.

- **Deployable Repeater placement:**  
  On success, place the Deployable Repeater Token at the point of impact.  
  If a model moves into that space before the Conclusion of the Order, the Repeater may be relocated following Deployable rules; if impossible, it is lost.

- **Interactions with Camouflage:**  
  Targetless weapons ignore Camouflage restrictions; they **can be placed inside Trigger Areas**, because the Repeater has no Trigger Area.  
  Unlike Drop Bears (BS Mode), the Pitcher is **not restricted** by enemy Camo within any zone.

- **Repeater activation:**  
  The Repeater becomes active **after the Order concludes**, enabling Hacking through it immediately for subsequent Orders.

**Pitcher Pros/Cons**
- **Pros:**  
  - Excellent tool for projecting hacking threat quickly and from safety.  
  - Only long range network projection available (up to 24–32 inches, with penalties).  
  - Can be placed without moving the model.  
  - Difficult for the enemy to stop (Normal Roll).  

- **Cons:**  
  - Requires a BS Roll.  
  - Disposable (2).  
  - AROs against the attacker may kill or suppress him.
  - A failed Roll wastes scarce Disposable uses.

### Pitcher - Practical usage tips

- Against Fireteams, a well-placed Pitcher can force entire groups into bad choices (e.g., risk Oblivion or Reset).  
- Use Pitchers to **extend ZoC for Spotlight missions**, enabling consistent scoring opportunities.  
- Deploy early in the turn to reshape the hacking map before committing attack pieces.
