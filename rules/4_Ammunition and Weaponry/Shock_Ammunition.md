# Shock Ammunition

This Ammunition is designed to kill rather than simply incapacitate enemy combatants.

- After a successful attack using Shock Ammunition, the target must make a Saving Roll per hit suffered.

- Each Saving Roll failed against Shock Ammunition inflicts 1 Wound to its target.

- Critical hits with Shock Ammunition cause the target to make an additional Saving Roll.

- If the target has the VITA Attribute, with a value of 1, any failed Saving Rolls against Shock Ammunition will also cancel any type of Unconscious State the target is in, or has entered during this Order, and the target passes directly to Dead State.

Shock Ammunition affects Unconscious State and any Special Skills that affect a Trooper's Unconscious State:

- Unconscious

- Shasvastii and Shasvastii-Embryo

- Dogged

- No Wound Incapacitation

All their special effects are cancelled by Shock Ammunition and those Troopers pass directly to the Dead State.

The Explode Special Skill does not activate if the Trooper passes directly to Dead State.

### Interaction Note
Related rules: Shock Ammunition, Unconscious State, VITA, Dogged, No Wound Incapacitation, Shasvastii, Explode.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

Instead of allowing a Trooper to enter (or remain in) the Unconscious State, Shock forces them directly into the Dead State. 

### How Shock Interacts With Common States & Skills
- **Normal Unconscious**  
  Shock bypasses the Unconscious State entirely. A single failed Save is enough to kill any 1-Wound (W1) Trooper lacking special rules or Immunity.
  
- **VITA 1 Troopers**  
  Troopers with VITA=1 are guaranteed to die to any failed Shock save, even if they have multiple Unconscious levels or other special rule interactions.

- **Dogged / No Wound Incapacitation (NWI)**  
  Both skills rely on entering the Unconscious State. Since Shock cancels Unconscious, these skills *cannot activate* if the Trooper is killed directly.  
  This makes Shock the most efficient counter to Dogged/NWI heavy factions and profiles.

- **Shasvastii & Shasvastii-Embryo**  
  Their survival mechanics also require entering Unconscious. Shock cancels this and removes the model outright.

- **Explode**  
  Shock can completely prevent Explode from activating. If the Trooper dies immediately (no Unconscious), the Explode trigger condition is never met.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### Pros of Shock Ammunition
- Extremely efficient against:
  - Dogged
  - NWI
  - Shasvastii
  - VITA=1 Troopers
  - Any 1-Wound unit
- Prevents healing or revival attempts (Doctor/Paramedic).
- Great on Direct Template Weapons (e.g., Chest Mines, CrazyKoalas, Drop Bears) because a single failed save kills outright.
- Excellent finishers for skirmishers and midfield pieces.

### Cons of Shock Ammunition
- Much less effective vs:
  - Multi-Wound Targets (2+)
  - ARM-stacked units (since Shock does not modify ARM)
  - Troopers with **Immunity (Shock)** or **Immunity (ARM/BTS)**  
- Does not provide additional damage (unlike DA or EXP).
- Provides no extra effect vs. structures or STR-based targets unless they rely on Unconscious (many REMs do not).
- Does nothing if the enemy wasn't going to be healed, still out of game and not giving orders to order pool.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### Shock Ammunition Strategic Uses & Tips
- Use Shock to clean up Dogged/NWI heavy factions (Haqqislam, Morats, Ariadna, etc.).
- Use Shock on midfield / objective pieces to ensure they cannot be revived.
- Mines with Shock (e.g., Drop Bears, CrazyKoalas) create zones where Dogged or NWI units cannot safely engage.
- Combine AP+Shock (e.g., certain MULTI weapons) to punch through armor *and* deny survival skills.
