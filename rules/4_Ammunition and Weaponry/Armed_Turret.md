# Armed Turret

The Armed Turret is a Deployable Weapon designed to be deployed on the battlefield to react to the enemy.

- These weapons are placed on the game table using the Place Deployable Common Skill, applying the Perimeter and Deployable rule. Therefore, when players deploy this weapon, they place it totally inside the Zone of Control of the Trooper, instead of placing it in Silhouette contact.

- The Armed Turret is a Deployable Weapon, declaring a BS Attack or CC Attack against any Order declared by an active Enemy Model (but not a Marker) within its LoF or in Silhouette contact.

- The Armed Turret's BS Weapon is listed in brackets after the weapon's name in the Trooper's Unit Profile.

- The Armed Turret must be removed from the gaming table when it suffers a number of Wounds equal or higher than its STR Attribute value.

- Restriction: The Armed Turret is stationary and cannot move.

- Due to the Deployable Trait, this weapon does not activate other Deployable Weapons or Equipment.

## Armed Turret - Simplified Explanation

An Armed Turret is a stationary, automatic ARO platform placed as a Deployable.

It:
- Automatically declares BS Attack or CC Attack as an ARO whenever an enemy Model (not Markers) declares any Order in LoF or Silhouette contact.

- Has 360º Visor → LoF in all directions.

- Has Total Reaction → full Burst in ARO with its listed weapon.

- Is STR 1 → any successful damaging hit that causes a wound will destroy it.

- Does not move and does not trigger other Deployables.

It behaves like a “mini REM turret” but is a deployable weapon, not a Trooper.

## Armed Turret - Step-by-Step Interaction / Resolution
Interaction: Armed Turret + ARO

### Enemy Model (not Marker) declares an Order.

If the Turret has LoF or Silhouette contact, it automatically declares BS Attack (or CC Attack if in Silhouette contact).

Because of Total Reaction, the Turret uses full Burst in ARO.

Rolls are Face-to-Face if the active Model shoots back or imposes a Face-to-Face effect.

If the attacker uses a non-opposing skill (Move, Idle, etc.), the Turret’s attack is Normal Rolls.

### Interaction: Armed Turret + Face-to-Face Rolls

Works normally: if both sides declare attacks with LoF, rolls are FtF.

If the active Model throws Smoke that blocks LoF, Smoke may cancel the attack at Resolution (normal Infinity timing).

###  Interaction: Armed Turret + Partial Cover

The Turret does benefit from Partial Cover if 1/3 of its silhouette is obscured and the attacker draws LoF through scenery.

The Turret does not have Mimetism or any extra defense beyond ARM 2/BTS 3, and STR 1.

Because the Turret is stationary, opponents can easily peek corners to deny it Cover or approach from angles where it has no physical concealment.

### Interaction: Armed Turret + Markers

Cannot attack Markers.

It will not reveal Camo/Impersonation units unless they reveal themselves by attacking the Turret.

Interaction: Armed Turret + Deployables / Mines

“Due to the Deployable Trait, this weapon does not activate other Deployable Weapons or Equipment.”
→ So it cannot trigger Mines, Perimeter weapons, etc.

## Armed Turret - How Armed Turrets play on the table

They act as cheap, disposable ARO speedbumps.

Their offense is meaningful only if the weapon profile is strong (e.g., HMG variant) — many Turrets have low-PS, mid-quality guns.

Their BS 10 means they rely heavily on Burst in ARO from Total Reaction to land hits.

STR 1 means they die to one failed save, so they are unreliable under focused fire.

### Armed Turret - Pros

360° coverage with no facing worries.

Full Burst ARO makes even a weak weapon statistically dangerous.

Excellent at defending backfield, objectives, DZ flanks, or punishing careless moves.

Immune to being bypassed by Camo until the Camo unit reveals itself.

### Armed Turret - Cons

Extremely fragile (STR 1).

BS 10 struggles against high-MOD shooters or Cover.

Stationary → opponent picks the optimal engagement range/angle every time.

Cannot contest camo infiltrators until they reveal.

Vulnerable to E/M, templates, or even Deactivator.

## Armed Turret - Comparisons with similar pieces
Item	Strengths	Weaknesses
Armed Turret	Full-Burst ARO, 360º Visor, Deployable anywhere in ZoC	BS 10, STR 1, stationary

Perimeter Weapons (Koalas etc.)	Auto-hit triggers, great area denial	One-use, no ranged threat

Mines	Automatic triggers, bypass defense	Template only, no continuous ARO

REM Total Reaction bots	Mobile, BS 11–12, multiple STR, real troop	Much more expensive SWC/points

### Key difference:
Armed Turrets are denial pieces; TR REMs are real gunfighters.

##  Armed Turret - Tactical Advice

Place Turrets on rooftops or behind hard cover so they get Partial Cover.

Use them to tax Orders—even if they die, they force the opponent to deal with them.

Combine with Repeaters or Mines so opponents must solve multiple layers of defense.

Turrets are great midfield traps for enemy warbands or fast skirmishers.

Don't rely on them as your only ARO piece — they’re expendable tools, not anchors.

# Armed Turret - Step-by-Step: Effective Counters to Armed Turrets
## A. Use a Deactivator (hard counter)

1. Rules-backed and extremely efficient.

2. You only need LoF or ZoC.

3. Make one Normal WIP Roll.

4. If passed → Turret is removed immediately.

The Turret cannot stop you with Face-to-Face because the roll is Normal.

### Why it works:
Turret has no MODs, no resistance, and cannot interact meaningfully with a Deactivator user.

This is the single most order-efficient, rules-clean counter.

## B. Attack with Visual MODs (Mimetism, Smoke Shooting, Nimbus, White Noise)

Turret has no Mimetism, no MSV, no special defenses.
It’s BS 10 → anything that adds MODs crushes it.

### Strong approaches:

- Mimetism (-6) shooters

- Smokes + MSV2 for clear FtF

- White Noise if the Turret’s weapon has MSV (rare, but safe option)

This forces the Turret to shoot back at BS 10 – 6 – Cover – Range, often needing 1s or 2s.

## C. Armed Turret - Use Templates (Direct or Impact)

Because the Turret is STR 1 and stationary:

A Light Flamethrower or Chain Rifle that reaches it will kill it.

Turret must choose:

- Shoot back (Face to Face with the template)

- Or dodge on awful PH (PH --) → Actually, Turret has PH “--” (no stat) so it cannot Dodge.

Important: Because the Armed Turret does declare CC Attack or BS Attack, but has no Dodge skill and no PH, it cannot declare Dodge.

Templates are brutally effective.

## D. Armed Turret - Outrange It

Most Turrets have short- to mid-range weapons.

Fight from 24–32″ with weapons the turret is MODded against.

Its BS10 collapses under range bands.

### Typical winning matchups:

- Snipers

- Missile Launchers

- Feuerbach

- HMGs from strong platforms

## E. Use Camo or Hidden Deployment

The Turret cannot react to Markers: Approach safely as a Marker.

What this enables:

- Close-range template delivery

- Deactivator approach

- Short-range assassin shots

Bypassing its ARO until the moment you choose to reveal

Camo completely strips Turret of purpose until you decide to engage.

## F. Attack via Hacking or E/M

If the Turret has a REM-type statline and BTS 3, E/M can be effective:

- E/M Grenades

- Oblivion/Carbonite if the Turret is hackable (depends on its classification in your faction lists)

It only has STR 1, so entering IMM-B or Isolated is often enough to neutralize it.

## G. Engage from Total Cover (Speculative Fire, Intuitive Attack, Targetless)

Turrets rely completely on Line of Fire.

Attacks that do not require LoF break them:

- Speculative Grenades

- Speculative E/M grenades

- Targetless weapons (Drop Bears, Pitchers — though these don’t damage directly)

- Intuitive Attack (if applicable)

The Turret gets no FtF, only Normal Rolls.

## Armed Turret - Tactical Notes – What Works Best (UNOFFICIAL COMMENT)
### Top-tier counters (very order-efficient)

Deactivator (Auto-kill)

Camo/HD to Walk Up + Template

Mimetism (-6) shooter at good range

Speculative grenades / E/M

### Mid-tier counters (trade pieces)

Chain Rifle or Flamethrower warbands (cheap, lethal)

Long-range weapons outranging turret

### Weak counters (work, but wasteful)

Face-to-Face gunfighting without MODs

Trying to tank hits (not great against Total Reaction)
