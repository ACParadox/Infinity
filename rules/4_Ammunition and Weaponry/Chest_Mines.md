# Chest Mines

- This weapon has two Modes of use: In BS Weapon Mode, Chest Mines function as a Direct Template Weapon, not applying the Mines rule. In CC Weapon Mode, Chest Mines function as a CC Weapon, not applying the Mines rule. If the user wins the Face to Face Roll, the Chest Mine will affect all enemy Troopers in Silhouette contact with its bearer. This weapon cannot be used in CC if there are allied troopers in Silhouette contact with its bearer.

- The Disposable (2) Trait is shared between all Modes. The bearer of this weapon has only two uses available, no matter which Modes are used.

- The detonation of a Chest Mine doesn't affect its bearer.

## Chest Mines - Simplified Explanation

Chest Mines are not deployable weapons and are not mines in rules terms.

Despite the name, the rules are explicit:
They do not use the Mine rules.
They cannot be placed on the table as Deployables.
They do not create Trigger Areas.
They do not detonate automatically.

So “Chest Mine” is just a name. Mechanically, it behaves like a built-in template blast that the trooper releases manually, never like a deployable mine.

Chest Mines are short-range burst explosives a trooper detonates on themselves, but with two distinct modes:

### 1. BS Mode

Functions as a Direct Template weapon, like a Chain-colt but with Shock and Double Shot (B2).

No Mines rules: no triggering, no Concealment, no Deployable.

Can be declared normally as a BS Attack or using Intuitive Attack against Markers.

### 2. CC Mode

Functions as a CC Weapon (with +3 MOD).

If you win the Face-to-Face, all enemies in Silhouette contact take the template’s effect.

Cannot be used if an ally is also in Silhouette contact.

Shock + ARM save.

Both modes have Disposable (2), and do not harm the user.

## Chest Mines - Step-by-Step Interaction Focus
Interaction: Chest Mines vs ARO / Face-to-Face

#### BS Mode

Declaring a Direct Template BS Attack normally produces a Face-to-Face only if the target declares an Attack affecting the outcome (BS Attack, CC Attack, etc.).

Against Dodge ARO: Normal roll (template auto-hits unless Dodge succeeds).

#### CC Mode

Always a Face-to-Face Roll against any CC Attack or BS Attack declared into CC.

On win: ALL enemies in base contact are hit.

On loss: no effect; you spend one Disposable use.

### Interaction: Partial Cover

Template weapons ignore Partial Cover for both modes (cover never applies to Direct Templates).

In CC Mode, cover is irrelevant because CC ignores cover by default.

### Interaction: Shock Ammunition

In both modes, Shock applies: 1 Wound → straight to Dead for non-NWI/STR models.

### Interaction: Intuitive Attack (BS Mode only)

You may attack Camouflage/Marker states directly using WIP roll.

### Interaction: Movement into Silhouette Contact

CC Mode cannot be used if an ally is in contact.

BS Mode can template allies if they are caught in the template, since it does NOT obey the restriction of Mines (Mines never trigger if ally would be hit).

## Chest Mines - Comparison With Similar Systems

## Pros of Chest Mines:

Immediate, reliable damage tool.

BS Mode gives Burst 2 Shock small template—rare and strong.

CC Mode punishes multi-engagements.

Works even in CC.

## Chest Mines - Cons:

Very short range.

Only two uses.

No area denial like real Mines.

## Chest Mines - Tactical Notes and Examples
### When to Use BS Mode

Enemy is approaching from a corner with low ARM: drop a double Shock template.

Engage Marker states aggressively using Intuitive Attack.

Use when enemies are spread in a small cluster.

### When to Use CC Mode

You expect to win CC and want to splash damage to multiple base-contact enemies.

You need guaranteed hits in CC without worrying about Burst.

### Counters / Weaknesses

Multi VITA troops reduce Shock’s killing power.

Enemies with high Dodge or CC may negate CC Mode.

Disposable limit means opponents can bait them out.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

Chest Mines function almost like portable suicide-mines, but without the risk to the bearer. They reward aggressive trading, close-range ambushes, and punishing enemies who swarm a single trooper. Compared to standard Mines, they offer burst damage rather than area denial—they are a tactical weapon, not a strategic one.

Their strongest niche is when a trooper with Chest Mines can force close engagements, especially with bonuses (MetaChemistry, high PH for dodging into position, etc.). The BS Mode's Burst 2 Shock template is unusually potent and often underestimated.
