## Trench-Hammer

> **N5.3 change:** Trench-Hammer is **no longer Disposable (3)** in either BS Mode or CC Mode.

| Mode | PS | B | Ammo | Save Attr. | Save Rolls | Traits |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Trench-Hammer — BS Mode | 6 | 1 | DA | ARM | 2 | Anti-materiel, BS Weapon (PH), `[*]` |
| Trench-Hammer — CC Mode | 6 | 1 | DA | ARM | 2 | Anti-materiel, CC, `[*]` |

`Disposable (3)` must not be applied to either mode under N5.3.
