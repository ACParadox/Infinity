# Stun Ammunition

A type of ammunition devised to incapacitate its target without killing it.

- After a successful attack with Stun Ammunition, the target must make one Saving Roll per hit suffered.

- A failed Saving Roll against Stun Ammunition causes the target to enter Stunned State. Place a Stunned State Token (STUNNED) next to it.

- Additionally, failing the Saving Roll causes the target to automatically fail the subsequent Guts Roll for having survived an Attack, unless he has the Courage Special Skill or an equivalent.

- Critical hits with Stun Ammunition cause the target to make an additional Saving Roll.

### Interaction Note
Related rules: ARO, Face-to-Face, Partial Cover.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
**Stun Ammunition – Interaction Notes**

**Rules basis:**
- “A failed Saving Roll against Stun Ammunition causes the target to enter Stunned State.”
- Stunned State:
  - “Troopers in this state cannot declare Attacks.”:contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}
  - “Troopers in Stunned State must apply a MOD of -3 to any Roll they perform, except Saving Rolls.”:contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}
- Ammunition Summary:
  - “STUN | … | Non-Lethal, State: Stunned”

- Restriction: Troopers in this state cannot declare Attacks.

- Moreover, Troopers in Stunned State must apply a MOD of -3 to any Roll they perform, except Saving Rolls.

- Automatic Special Skills and Equipment continue to work, but the Trooper must still respect all declaration restrictions.

- To cancel this state, Troopers with the Vitality (VITA) Attribute require a Doctor while Troopers with the Structure (STR) Attribute require an Engineer.

- A Trooper with the Doctor/Engineer Special Skill (or an equivalent Skill) may cancel this state by spending one Short Skill of an Order while in Silhouette contact with the affected Trooper and passing a Normal WIP Roll (or the Roll specified by the Special Skill or Scenario).

- The Stunned State is automatically canceled at the start of the States Phase of the Player Turn in which it was caused.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

### How Stun Ammunition interacts with AROs
Stun is **non-lethal**, so it does not inflict Wounds, but the resulting **Stunned State** shuts down future *Attacks*, including ARO Attacks.  
A Trooper currently in Stunned State:
- **Cannot declare BS Attacks, CC Attacks, Hacking Attacks**, or any Skill with the *Attack* Label.
- **May still declare Dodge or Reset**, because they are not Attacks.
- Still generates ARO normally, but its options are reduced to non-attack declarations.

Practical outcome:
- A Reactive Trooper hit and failed against Stun will lose the ability to ARO-shoot on subsequent Orders until the State cancels.

---
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### How Stun Ammunition interacts with Face-to-Face Rolls
- The Face-to-Face calculation is **unchanged**; Stun Ammo does not modify the attack roll mechanics.
- If the Stun-using Trooper wins the FtF and inflicts a hit, the target makes **one Saving Roll** (per hit) using the weapon’s listed **ARM or BTS**.
- If the target fails, it enters **Stunned**.  
- Being Stunned **after** the FtF does *not* retroactively alter the FtF result; it only affects future Orders.

Key point:
- Stun Ammo is excellent for **disabling overwatch pieces**: even one good FtF result can remove their ability to continue attacking.

---
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

Stun has **no special exception** to bypass Partial Cover.

---
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### Comparison With Similar Ammunition

**Stun vs PARA (Paralysis)**
- PARA imposes **IMM-A** on failed PH-6 saves, and prevents all skills and ARO except dodge. 
- Stun prevents **only Attacks**, but the target can still move normally and do other skills.
- Stun stay one turn only, PARA stays until cancelled.
- PARA is stronger than Stun in general, and stays longer.

**Stun vs E/M**
- E/M is more severe against HI/REM/TAG/VH, inflicting **Isolated** and possibly **IMM-B** on HI/REM/TAG/VH.
- Stun stay one turn only, E/M states stay until cancelled.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### Pros and Cons Summary

**Pros**
- Completely removes the target’s ability to Attack (AROs included).  
- Non-lethal—safe for scenarios requiring capture.  
- Excellent for neutralizing ARO pieces.
- Good in ARO to shut done main active turn gunfighters
- Works against all Troop types; no Hackable restriction.

**Cons**
- Requires a failed single Saving Roll—somewhat unreliable, especially vs high ARM/BTS.  
- Canceled at the start of the target’s next States Phase.  
- Does not impede movement or Order generation.

---

### Tactical Use Notes (Unofficial)
- Stun is best employed by **burst-rich guns or high-BS platforms** to reliably break enemy overwatch.  
- Use Stun to disable key enemy gunners before committing a high-value attack piece.  
- Stun on a template (e.g., some deployables/mines) can neutralize multiple overwatch models with a single Order.  
- A stunned enemy Hacker or ARO missile bot becomes harmless until the next States Phase.

---

Stun Ammunition is one of the most underrated control tools in Infinity N5. It allows you to dictate tempo by removing enemy attack options without overcommitting to lethal trades. It is best viewed as a method of *asymmetric risk reduction*: you spend Orders to reduce the chances of being punished by return fire on future Orders. Use it on linked ARO snipers, missile REMs, or TAGs you don't want to kill yet. Pair Stun with Smoke, White Noise, or movement-based attacks for layered disruption.
