# Mine Dispenser

A Disposable BS Weapon that shoots Mines and allows its user to perform Speculative Attacks using their BS Attribute.

- In game terms, this weapon uses the same rules as Drop Bears in BS Mode, and the same Effects are applied when placing the Mine Token.

## Mine Dispenser FAQs

Q: When using Place Deployable and declaring where the Deployable will be placed, what happens if a Trooper moves into that space before the Conclusion of the Order?

A: The Trooper that declared Place Deployable may place the Deployable in a different position (still following the Place Deployable rule), and if this is not possible, the Deployable will be lost.

This also applies to weapons that place a Deployable Weapon or Equipment on a successful Roll, such as Disco Ballers, Pitchers, and Drop Bears (BS Mode).

**Mine Dispenser – Interaction Notes & Commentary**

The *Mine Dispenser* is a BS Weapon that fires Deployable Weapons (Mines). Instead of placing a Mine by spending a Short Skill, the user shoots a projectile that attempts to place a Mine at range. This creates a unique hybrid between a ranged BS Attack and the Place Deployable skill, with several tactical implications.

---

### How Mine Dispenser Interacts With Core Rules

**ARO**
- Shooting with a Mine Dispenser in **BS Mode** is a BS Attack (Targetless, Speculative Attack allowed) and therefore generates AROs normally if the acting Trooper is seen.  

- AROs will usually be **BS Attacks** or **Dodges**.  

- Since Mine Dispenser in BS Mode cannot detonate during the same Order, the defender cannot be harmed by the mine within that Order. They may still Dodge to avoid being hit by other attacks but **the Mine itself imposes no immediate threat**, reducing the pressure on ARO choices.  

- ARO Dodge does **not** oppose the placement unless the Dodge specifically affects LoF or movement—this is a *Normal Roll* for the Mine Dispenser user, unless an enemy BS Attack or other opposing skill makes it **Face-to-Face** (see below).

**Face-to-Face**
- If the Mine Dispenser is used against an enemy with LoF and that enemy chooses to BS Attack back, the roll becomes Face-to-Face.
- Because the *Mine Dispenser* roll is trying to *place* a Mine rather than inflict a Saving Roll, a success by the defender cancels the Mine placement entirely.
- ARO Dodge does **not** oppose the placement unless the Dodge specifically affects LoF or movement—this is a *Normal Roll* for the Drop Bear user, unless an enemy BS Attack or other opposing skill makes it **Face-to-Face**. 

**Partial Cover**
- The Mine Dispenser uses BS range and Cover modifiers.
- Partial Cover grants the target the usual –3 to the attacker’s BS and +3 ARM in case the Dispenser uses a mode that deals damage (rare, depending on profiles).  
- Even when only placing a Mine, Cover still applies as a BS MOD and may determine success/failure of the placement.

### Mine Dispenser Tactical Interaction Notes – Pros & Cons

**Mine Dispenser Pros**
- **Long-range Minelaying:** Safely seeds the board with Mines from outside Direct Template range.
- **Works from around corners:** You can shoot the projectile into position without exposing yourself to the target of the future Mine.
- **Area control during Active Turn:** Excellent for locking down corners, rooftops, and objectives.
- **Punishes enemy movement next turn:** Even if the Mine is placed during F2F and the enemy cancels the placement, you can try again from safety.

**Mine Dispenser Cons**
- **Face-to-Face vulnerable:** Enemies with LoF can prevent Mine placement simply by beating your roll.
- **BS/Range dependent:** If firing into poor range bands, the chances of placing the Mine drop sharply.
- **Blocked placement:** If the landing point is invalid (ineligible surface, too close to other Mines, etc.), the attempt fails.
- **Not instant damage:** Unlike a direct weapon, it does not harm the enemy immediately—only creates a future threat.

### Mine Dispenser Comparisons to Similar Tools

**Mine Dispenser vs. Direct Template Mines (normal placement)**
- **Dispenser advantages:** Can place Mines at range, safer, more flexible angles.
- **Normal placement advantages:** Cannot be prevented with Face-to-Face rolls; guaranteed placement if requirements are met.

**Mine Dispenser vs. E/Maulers/Other Deployables**
- **Dispenser advantage:** Ranged delivery.  
- **Other deployables:** Often stronger effects (E/M, AP+Shock), but require risky proximity.

**Mine Dispenser vs. Throwing Grenades / Speculative Fire**
- **Dispenser advantage:** Creates a persistent threat zone rather than a one-time attack.  
- **Grenade/Speculative Fire advantage:** Immediate damage, less predictable for opponents.

### Strategic Uses Mine Dispenser

**Locking down retreat paths:**  
Fire a Mine into an alley the enemy *must* cross next turn.

**Shaping enemy movement:**  
Use the Dispenser early to create no-go zones around objectives.

**Supporting Coordinated Orders:**  
After enemies Dodge or move under coordinated pressure, a long-range Mine placement forces further tough decisions.

**Layering traps:**  
Combine a Dispenser-placed Mine with a Hidden Deployment model watching the same corner—forcing Dodges that expose them to a shot.

---

### Mine Dispenser - Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Shooting into bad range bands:** The Mine will almost always fail to land.  
- **Firing while exposed to strong ARO pieces:** They cancel your placement and may injure you.  
- **Trying to place too close to your own troops:** If the Mine would violate placement requirements, it simply fails.

---

### Summary
The *Mine Dispenser* is a precision area-control weapon that rewards careful positioning and Active Turn pressure. It is best used to create safe approach routes, punish enemy aggression, and seed the board with threats without exposing the user to danger. While less reliable in direct face-offs, its ability to project deployables at range makes it one of the strongest board-shaping tools in Infinity.
