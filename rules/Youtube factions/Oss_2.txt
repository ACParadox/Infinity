The KHDs like Assura and Satra are amazing with Cyber Mask as roll compression units that can do missions
as they are moving towards objectives and hacking through repeaters with second short skill.

Chapter 4: Weaknesses
Now for the weaknesses of operation subsection.
Despite having access to an MSV3 unit,
you do not have the possibility to shoot through smoke by your own actions. The absence of smoke in this sectoral is a
big down you need to work with. And the only visibility control you have is an expensive YU trooper profile you can't afford to duo in most lists.
Units in this sectoral are really efficient but do cost accordingly. This makes lists harder to round out without
resorting to cheap cheerleaders. You don't have any cheap left tenant options either. Most of them are troopers you
wouldn't want two of in any list. This makes having a decoy or chain of command for safety a burden on your points. You
could go for an obvious lieutenant in certain missions, but it's usually a thing to avoid. Most factions cover this
floor easily by having a cheap low 10 points lieutenant and a double to use as a decoy. Torch Light gets the cheapest
chain in command in the game, but there's no such thing as cheap here. I only hire the best in my forces.
The midfield options are on the weaker side comparatively to their cost. Dart is good in offense, but rather bad in
defense and would be better off as a hidden deployment trooper rather than a camouflage one. Andromeda, on the other end, can actually serve well in defense,
but is fairly expensive and fragile.
The Dashu is your hidden deployment troop, but is paying for a lot of skills it doesn't need. He's just too
expensive. The usual trooper you would use for defense is the Naga mine layer,
but I would only take him if the mission strongly encourages you to contest the midfield at deployment. OSS has better defensive tools for the cost than them.
Although they have among the strongest whip hackers in the game, I think not having BTS6 or access to a Tinbot
firewall minus three is a great failure in the design of this sectoral as a hacking apex predator. Their hackers are really banking on that one extra whip,
which is only going to make a difference 5% of the time. Higher BTS or Tinbot's firewall would make them more resilient in all instances rather than sometimes.
I find hacking options like a moderator hacker in Bikunin paired with a tinbot firewall minus 6 riot girl more
resilient despite being whip 13 instead of 15. Same with light brigade with the
waver rider and psychop both6 that can be paired with a silverstar tinbot firewall minus three. What OSS is
better at is pressing buttons. Their hackers are vulnerable to an alpha strike of pitches and KHDs.
