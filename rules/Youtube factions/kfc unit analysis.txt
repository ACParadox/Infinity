KCF wants to:

Outgun with rock-paper-scissors style
Use CC or hidden hacker vs hacking
Move in midfield with resilient guns
Create defensive foothold
Use deployable to reduce maneuverability
High-quality doctors/eng to counter-balance lack of redundancy
Take objectives as necessary or in late game

KCF are afraid of:

Gunfighter mismatch
Hackers being scalped
Pushed out of midfield
Their vision control killed
Hidden element being read
Running out of time
