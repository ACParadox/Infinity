Map Building
---
9-4-Scatter:
divide table in 3x3 (18 inch) squares
9 largish buildings, each square with 1 building (8x8 to 12x12 or clumps of smaller)
No interiors with roof you can get on.
Vary roof heights
+1 objective room in middle if required
Rotate building 45 degrees
Leaves lanes for TAGs (but note they can vault)
Bias tall buildings in center
Give only one DZ a good sniper perch
Put cover on edges of table too
Add stairs, catways and ladders
No long range sight lanes to objectives
Add rooftop clutter/cover
Building clumps 2-3 inch from each other (to let TAGs pass but keep cover)
4 scenery pieces (4x4 to 5x5), put in the 4 intersection lines made by 9 buildings
Could be parked/crashed vehicles and such
Scatter terrain to have cover every 4-6 inch (60-80) pieces
3 big buildings in middle
Put smaller terrain in intersection
Some inside deployments
Mix of small, medium, large. Large with roofs, parapets and recesses
Asymmetry is king (not for new players)
No dead ground
Plan the deployment zone
No DZ to DZ firelanes

2-3 ‘corridors’ across the table.
