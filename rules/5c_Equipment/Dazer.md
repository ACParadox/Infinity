# Dazer

Equipment

The Dazer is Deployable Equipment that creates a Terrain Zone in its Zone of Control, which hinders movement for other Troopers.

- This piece of Equipment generates a Difficult Terrain area of infinite height in its Zone of Control.

- Troopers with the Terrain (Total) Special Skill ignore the effects of the Dazer and benefit from their MOD when declaring Skills with the Movement Label.

- A deployed Dazer remains on the table until the end of the game, or until it is destroyed.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
*General Deployable Interactions*
- As a Deployable, the Dazer follows all rules found under “Place Deployable”.
- Must be placed:
  • In Silhouette contact during Active Turn  
  • Totally within the user's Zone of Control if treated like other Deployables with similar listing  
  (Matches its profile trait: Zone of Control)
- Cannot be placed on vertical surfaces.
- Placed at the *conclusion* of the Order.
- Enemy may not react to the Dazer’s placement—only to the trooper placing it.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
*Destruction and Targeting*
- The Dazer has STR 1 and no ARM/BTS; any successful damaging hit destroys it.
- It may be removed by a successful enemy Deactivator attack.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Create an area of Difficult Terrain: 

“Troopers must apply the Difficult Terrain rules when their Silhouette is inside or in contact with a Difficult Terrain area and they declare a Skill with the Movement Label such as Move, Cautious Movement, Climb, Jump, or the Berserk Special Skill.”

“Difficult Terrain also affects Troopers who make Silhouette contact with it during an Order.”

“Entering Silhouette contact with a Difficult Terrain area immediately and automatically ends the Trooper's Movement, regardless of its type, for the rest of the Order.”

“If the Trooper chooses to continue to move through the Difficult Terrain area, they must declare a new Order and apply a MOD of –1 inch to both values of their MOV Attribute, or to the value specified by any Skill involved, such as Jump.”


## UNOFFICIAL PROS & CONS


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Pros:
- Low-cost area control tool if given a zone-affecting role.
- Disposable (3) allows multiple placements, shaping multiple corridors.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Cons:
- Very fragile—any attack destroys it.
- Requires proximity (ZoC) for placement, limiting board reach.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Dazer vs Mines (UNOFFICIAL COMPARISON)
- Mines threaten enemies directly (template attack); Dazers would provide *area manipulation* instead.
- Mines require specific trigger conditions; Dazer would be *persistent*.
- Mines are inherently lethal; Dazer is expected to be non-lethal control.

Pros of Dazer:  
- Persistent board shaping  
- Doesn’t require LoF triggers  

Cons of Dazer :  
- Provides no damage  
- Easier for enemy to ignore 

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Use multiple Dazers to stack environmental pressure if your group assigns movement penalties.
- Place them around objectives: even mild area effects force extra Orders from opponents.
- Combine with Mines or Perimeter Weapons for layered zones of denial.
- Placing Dazers early helps shape enemy approach vectors without exposing your troopers.
