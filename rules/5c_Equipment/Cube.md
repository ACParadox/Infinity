# Cube

Equipment

This indicates that the Trooper has an automated memory recording and storage device, commonly known as a Cube.

Troopers with a Cube are easier to heal. Additionally, Cube-wearing casualties can potentially be recovered for use in future missions.

- When using the Doctor Special Skill (or other Special Skills or pieces of Equipment that explicitly say so) to heal a Trooper with a Cube, you can expend Command Tokens to re-roll a failed WIP Roll.

- When using the Doctor Special Skill (or other Special Skills or pieces of Equipment that explicitly say so) to heal a Trooper with a Cube 2.0, you can expend Command Tokens to re-roll a failed WIP Roll.

- The user applies a +2 MOD to their Saving Roll when making a Saving Roll against an Attack with Sepsitor or Sepsitor Plus.

# Cube - Sepsitor

- The target must be a Trooper with a Cube, or a piece of Equipment that performs a similar function. [...]

- If the target fails the Saving Roll, it enters Sepsitorized State.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

## INTERACTIONS

### 1. Cube and Sepsitor
- Only **Cube-wearing Troopers** can enter **Sepsitorized State**.
- Troopers **without Cube** are immune to Sepsitor and therefore cannot be taken over.
- REMs and some non-biological units also cannot be Sepsitorized unless explicitly stated.

**Practical Impact:**  
Units without Cubes gain a major advantage against CA Sepsitor weapons: they cannot be “mind-hacked”.

### 2. Cube and Doctor/Healing Mechanics
- Troopers with **Vitality (VITA)** and a Cube revived using a **Doctor** can expend Command Tokens to re-roll a failed WIP Roll.


### 3. Cube and Mnemonica
- Mnemonica requires the receiving model to be **Cube-equipped _or_ a REM**.
- If the target does not have a Cube and is not a REM, it cannot inherit a Mnemonica transfer.

**Implications:**  
Cube-bearing troops expand the viable pool of Mnemonica hosts.  
Morat units (no Cubes) cannot receive Mnemonica transfers, limiting Combined Army options.

### 4. Cube and Scenario Effects
Some missions include rules like:
- “Extract a Cubed trooper”
- “Recover a data-core from a Cube”
- “Extra Objective Points for carrying a Cube”

**Takeaway:**  
Cube occasionally becomes a scenario-specific vulnerability or advantage.

## PROS AND CONS SUMMARY

### Pros
- Enables Command token use to reroll WIP Doctor rolls.
- Enables Mnemonica transfers.
- Enables specific mission scoring or interactions.
- Under standard play, Cube has minimal drawbacks unless facing Sepsitor or mission rules.

### Cons
- Vulnerability to Sepsitor and similar advanced weapons.
- In Combined Army matchups, Cube becomes a serious liability.
- Scenario-specific penalties may target Cubes.

## TACTICAL NOTES (UNOFFICIAL)

- If facing Combined Army, **list composition should consider Cube vulnerability**. High-value Cube troopers may require screening or deployment caution.
- Troopers with Cube + high WIP make excellent **Mnemonica hosts** when available.
- Against factions without Sepsitor (most of the game), Cube is an advantage for the revive reroll with Command Token.
