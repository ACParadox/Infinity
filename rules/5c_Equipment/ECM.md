# ECM

Equipment

Electronic Countermeasures cover all systems carried by Troopers, Vehicles and TAGs with the goal of disrupting and deactivating enemy guided projectiles, Comms Attacks etc. depending on the type of ECM they are equipped with.

- In order to use this Automatic Equipment, its owner must be the target of the Attack type listed in round brackets.

- ECM imposes a series of negative MODs on the Enemy when they attempt to Attack the Trooper that owns it.

- Both the affected Attack type and the MOD that it imposes are listed in the Unit Profile of the Trooper.

# ECM Equipment — Interactions and Unofficial Commentary

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

- **ECM (Hacking -3)** — Any Trooper attempting to declare a **Hacking Program against the user** applies an additional **–3 WIP MOD**.

- ECM stacks with **Firewall bonuses** if the user is also benefiting from a Firewall source, but:  
  - **Firewall MOD** applies to the *attacker’s roll* (–3 or –6)  
  - **ECM –3** applies independently  

## Pros and Cons of ECM

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

### **Pros**
- Always-on, no range or facing requirements.  
- Significantly degrades enemy hacking efficiency.  
- Stacks with Firewall, forming extremely strong hacking resistance.  
- Particularly valuable on high-value models: TAGs, REMs, HI.

### **Cons**
- Does not help against:
  - **Comms Attacks** that are *not* Hacking Programs  
  - **Non-hacking control tools** (E/M, PARA, Smoke/Eclipse, etc.)  
- Provides no Saving Roll protection (unlike Firewall’s +3 BTS).

---

## Tactical Notes

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

- ECM makes enemy hacking unreliable enough that opponents may avoid hacking entirely, altering the decision space.  
- When paired with **Firewall**, ECM creates one of the most difficult defensive profiles for Hackers to penetrate.  
- TAGs or REMs with ECM are more confident pushing through Repeater networks.  

## Summary

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

ECM (Hacking –3) is a universally active, low-complexity defensive modifier that greatly improves survivability against Hacking Programs. On its own it is moderate protection; combined with Firewall it becomes one of the strongest hacking defenses available, especially for TAGs and REMs.
