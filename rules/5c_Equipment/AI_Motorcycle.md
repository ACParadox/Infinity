# AI Motorcycle

Equipment

An AI Motorcycle is a Motorcycle that, when its rider dismounts, becomes a Peripheral (Synchronized) Remote.

- Units equipped with an AI Motorcycle have two different Unit Profiles, applying the Transmutation (Auto) rules to change between them: Use the Mounted Trooper Profile when the Trooper is riding his or her AI Motorcycle, applying the rules for the Motorcycle piece of Equipment. When changing to this Profile, the Dismounted Trooper Model must be in Silhouette contact with the Peripheral Model. Replace the Peripheral Model with the Mounted Trooper Model, then remove the Dismounted Trooper Model (See Replacing Models and Markers ). Use the Dismounted Trooper Profile when the Trooper dismounts and moves on foot, applying the rules for Peripheral (Synchronized) to the AI Motorcycle. When changing to this Profile, replace the Mounted Trooper Model with the Peripheral Model, then place the Dismounted Trooper Model in Silhouette contact with it.

- There are two different sets of weapons listed, the first one is for the Mounted Trooper Profile, and the second one is for the Dismounted Trooper Profile.

- Restriction: If the Peripheral (Synchronized) Remote is in Isolated, IMM, or any Null State, the Trooper cannot change to its Mounted Trooper Profile.

- Restriction: The Mounted Trooper Profile cannot benefit from any Supportware affecting the Peripheral (Synchronized) Remote, so it is canceled for that Trooper at the moment the Mounted Trooper Profile is applied.

- Use the Mounted Trooper Profile when the Trooper is riding his or her AI Motorcycle, applying the rules for the Motorcycle piece of Equipment. When changing to this Profile, the Dismounted Trooper Model must be in Silhouette contact with the Peripheral Model. Replace the Peripheral Model with the Mounted Trooper Model, then remove the Dismounted Trooper Model (See Replacing Models and Markers ).

- Use the Dismounted Trooper Profile when the Trooper dismounts and moves on foot, applying the rules for Peripheral (Synchronized) to the AI Motorcycle. When changing to this Profile, replace the Mounted Trooper Model with the Peripheral Model, then place the Dismounted Trooper Model in Silhouette contact with it.


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Core Idea**

An AI Motorcycle is a Motorcycle whose dismounted chassis becomes a *Peripheral (Synchronized) Remote*.  
When mounted → behaves as a Motorcycle Trooper.  
When dismounted → the rider becomes a normal Trooper on foot, and the bike itself becomes an independent Peripheral REM that must remain synchronized.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Interaction Notes

### 1. AI Motorcycle + Peripheral (Synchronized)
- The dismounted bike follows all **Peripheral (Synchronized)** rules:
  - Must maintain Coherency with its controller.  
  - Acts with the same Order declaration as the rider.  
  - Cannot act independently.
- Unlike a normal bike left behind, this REM is an active game element:
  - Provides LoF, can contest area, can be targeted, and can be disabled.

### 2. AI Motorcycle + States (Isolated / IMM / Null)
- If the bike-REM enters **Isolated**, **IMM**, or **Null**, the rider **cannot remount**.  
- This creates a key vulnerability: hacking or E/M attacks can permanently deny the mounted profile.

### 3. AI Motorcycle + Supportware
- Supportware (e.g., Assisted Fire, Enhanced Reaction) applies to the bike while dismounted.  
- When the rider mounts again, **Supportware immediately ends** for that Trooper.

### 4. AI Motorcycle + Transmutation (Auto)
- The swap between mounted/dismounted counts as **Transmutation**, but with stricter placement rules:
  - You need Silhouette contact to switch profiles.
  - This prevents abusive repositioning tricks.

### 5. AI Motorcycle + Movement Restrictions
While mounted, the rider suffers ALL Motorcycle restrictions:
- Cannot go Prone  
- Cannot Climb or use ladders  
- Cannot Jump upward  
- Cannot Cautious Move  

Dismounting temporarily removes all these restrictions, which is a common tactical trick.

### 6. AI Motorcycle + Order Efficiency
- The REM cannot generate Orders.  
- But it *does* impose tactical pressure by creating additional vectors of ARO, body-blocking, or corner-checking.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Pros and Cons

### Pros
- **Two profiles** → high flexibility (speed vs precision).  
- **Dismounted REM** provides a free Peripheral unit:
  - Extra body for AROs, corner peeks, objective guarding.  
- **Supportware access** (while dismounted) gives powerful REM buffs.  
- Ability to bypass Motorcycle movement restrictions by dismounting mid-turn.  
- Excellent for missions requiring **area occupation**—two bodies for one slot.  
- Unexpected board control; opponents must account for two separate threats.

### Cons
- Vulnerability to **hacking, E/M, IMM**, especially on the bike-REM.  
- If the REM becomes Isolated/IMM/Null → **no remounting for the rest of the game**.  
- Mounted profile loses all Supportware effects automatically.  
- Requires careful cohesion management; Peripheral order rules can restrict movement.  
- Dismounted REM is STR 1 → fragile and easily removed.  
- Mounted Troopers still suffer all normal Motorcycle restrictions.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Tactical Uses

### 1. Early-Game Speed → Midfield Control Anchor  
Start mounted for speed, dismount near objectives, and use the REM as a **bodyblocker** or ARO point.

### 2. Supportware Spike  
Apply Supportware to the REM (Assisted Fire, Enhanced Reaction), then hold ground with the rider.  
Strong for defensive positioning—but remember: if you remount, Supportware ends.

### 3. Safe Climbing / Ladder Zones  
Dismount before vertical movement to bypass Motorcycle restrictions.

### 4. Sacrificial REM for Information  
The REM can corner-check, bait mines, or force AROs without risking the main Trooper.

### 5. Anti-Hacking Play  
Keep the REM behind total cover while the rider advances.  
If the REM gets E/M’d, the rider loses the mounted profile permanently—protect it.

### Which Is Better?
- **AI Motorcycle** is stronger in objective play, mid-board control, and layered tactics.  
- **Standard Motorcycle** is more reliable, less hackable, and fewer moving parts.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Overall Assessment

AI Motorcycles are one of the most tactically flexible mobility tools in Infinity N5.  
They reward careful state management and positioning but punish sloppy exposure to hacking.  
Players who exploit the REM’s utility (Supportware, body blocking, synchronized actions) get massive value out of a single Trooper slot.
