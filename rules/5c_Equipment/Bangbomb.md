# Bangbomb

Equipment

Users of Bangbombs often throw them at their own feet while dodging enemy fire, because, when the Bangbombs hit the ground and detonate, they create a curtain of reflective particles that enhance the flash of the blast, making it easier for the user to dodge and evade enemy attacks.

- The user declares a Dodge.

- The user applies a MOD to their Dodge Rolls. This MOD is listed in brackets after the name of this piece of Equipment in the user's Unit Profile.

- Restriction: This Dodge MOD only applies against Attacks declared by enemy Troopers who are within the user's LoF or ZoC.

- Restriction: This Dodge MOD does not apply against Attacks made with Template Weapons.

During their Active Turn, the player activates a Trooper with BangBomb (+4), declaring Move as the first Skill of the Order. Two enemy Troopers in LoF declare their AROs, both BS Attack, one with a Combi Rifle and the other with an E/Marat, a Template Weapon.

The Active Trooper then declares Dodge as the second Skill of the Order. Thanks to BangBomb (+4), they have a MOD of +4 to their Dodge Roll against the enemy with the Combi Rifle. Since the Active Trooper has PH 13, their Success Value is 17 (13+4). As the BangBomb MOD does not apply against Attacks with Template Weapons, their Success Value to Dodge the Template is 13.

Dice are rolled, and the Active Trooper gets a 14. This is enough to win the Face to Face Roll against the enemy with a Combi Rifle, who rolls a 9.

However, the E/Marat Template hits the Active Trooper. As the Bangbomb MOD does not apply against Template Weapons, the Trooper would have needed to roll a 13 or less.

By failing to Dodge the Template, the Trooper is unable to move up to 2 inches, even though he succeeded against the Combi Rifle Attack.

# Bangbomb — Interaction Notes & Commentary

Bangbomb is a Dodge-enhancing piece of Equipment that applies a positive MOD to the user’s Dodge Rolls, with two key restrictions:
• The MOD only applies against enemy Attacks declared from inside the user’s LoF or ZoC.  
• The MOD does NOT apply against Template Weapons.

## 1. INTERACTIONS WITH CORE RULES

### Interaction with **Dodge**
Bangbomb requires the user to declare a Dodge.  
• The Dodge Roll receives the Bangbomb MOD **only** versus Attacks coming from LoF/ZoC.  
• Dodge against Template Weapons ignores the Bangbomb MOD entirely.  
• If the Dodge succeeds against some attacks but fails against a Template, the movement portion of Dodge is lost (because Template hits cancel Dodge movement).

### Interaction with **Line of Fire & Zone of Control**
Since the MOD only applies when the attacker is within LoF or ZoC:  
• Attackers outside both (e.g., long-range snipers without ZoC overlap) cannot be Dodged with the Bangbomb MOD.  
• Positioning so enemies must enter your ZoC or LoF (e.g., corner-guarding) maximizes the benefit.

### Interaction with **Face to Face Rolls**
• The Bangbomb MOD modifies the user’s PH during the F2F Dodge.  
• It does **not** impose any negative MODs on the attacker—it only increases your own SV.  
• Multiple enemy attacks may be opposed in the same Dodge, but only those coming from within LoF/ZoC grant the bonus.

### Interaction with **Template Weapons**
Explicit restriction: **No Bangbomb bonus**.  
• The user still Dodges normally using raw PH.  
• Mixed opposition (e.g., rifle shot + template) results in the Dodge opposing the rifle with increased SV (PH+MOD), but the Template is resolved against raw PH.  
• Failing the Template Dodge negates Dodge movement.

---

## 2. TACTICAL APPLICATIONS

### Strengths
• Excellent at surviving Face to Face gunfights at close-mid range.  
• Best when forcing enemies to engage within ZoC (e.g., around corners).  
• Synergizes with troopers who already have good PH values.  
• Provides Active Turn survivability when provoking AROs deliberately (e.g., Move → Dodge).

### Weaknesses
• Useless when targeted by Direct Templates (e.g., Chain Rifle, E/Marat).  
• Ineffective against long-range attacks outside LoF/ZoC (especially Snipers, Missile Launchers).  
• Requires declaration of Dodge, which limits your action economy and prevents returning fire.


## 4. COMPARISONS WITH SIMILAR DEFENSIVE TOOLS

### Bangbomb vs. **Dodge (+X)** as a Unit Skill
**Similarities:**  
• Both increase Dodge-roll SV.  
**Differences:**  
• Dodge(+X) applies universally; Bangbomb has restrictions (LoF/ZoC, no Templates).  
• Dodge(+X) is always active; Bangbomb requires the declaration of Dodge and uses Equipment.

**Pros of Bangbomb:**  
• Often provides a larger bonus (e.g., +4).  
• Can be balanced onto units not typically high-PH.

**Cons of Bangbomb:**  
• Worse than Dodge(+X) when dealing with Templates or long-range non-LoF threats.

---
## 5. PROS AND CONS SUMMARY

### **Pros**
• Huge Dodge bonuses in favorable engagement geometry.  
• Excellent anti-gunfighter defense.  
• Great for objective runners and corner defenders.  
• Can allow aggressive Active Turn movement while mitigating risk.

### **Cons**
• Entirely ineffective against Templates.  
• Requires declaring Dodge—no returning fire.  
• Positional dependency (must engage inside LoF/ZoC).  
• Does not affect long-range confrontation.

## 6. PLAYSTYLE RECOMMENDATIONS

• Use Bangbomb troops to pressure short-range zones and channels.  
• Combine with terrain to force enemies into LoF/ZoC rather than long fire lanes.  
• Avoid template-heavy enemies or position to deny template lines.  
• Best used on fast troopers who can Move → Dodge repeatedly.
