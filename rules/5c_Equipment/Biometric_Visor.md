# Biometric Visor

Equipment

This piece of Equipment has been conceived to reduce the effects of the different impersonation and disguise technologies.

- The user ignores the WIP -3 MOD imposed by the Impersonation-1 State.

- If the user passes a Discover Roll against a Trooper in Impersonation-1 State, that Trooper's Impersonation State is canceled and their IMP-1 Marker is replaced with the corresponding Model.

- Users of a Biometric Visor ignore Surprise Attack MODs from attackers benefitting from Impersonation or Holoecho States, if they have LoF to the attacker. In addition, they also ignore Surprise Attack MODs from CC Attacks from those attackers even if they don't have LoF to the attacker.


## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]  

- **Against Impersonation-1 (IMP-1)**
  - The visor removes the –3 Discover penalty. This effectively raises the odds of revealing or denying a Speculo/Impersonator during the approach.
  - After a successful Discover against IMP-1, the target immediately loses IMP-1 and becomes a model, removing its strongest layer of deception.

- **Against Surprise Attack from Impersonation or Holoecho**
  - The visor fully cancels the Surprise Attack MOD, both **with LoF** (ranged) and **without LoF in CC**, making it a powerful defensive tool.

- **Does NOT help against:**
  - Camouflage or Mimetism penalties.
  - Regular Surprise Attack from *non-Impersonation / non-Holoecho*.  
  - Discovering HoloMask (still Private Information, uses normal Discover).
  - Attacking troopers with Holoechoes


## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]  
### Comparison: Biometric Visor vs Multispectral Visor

**Biometric Visor**
- Focused on impersonation/disguise technologies.
- Cancels:
  - IMP-1 Discover penalty.
  - Surprise Attack MOD from Impersonation or Holoecho.
- Does **not** affect visibility, smoke, camouflage, or mimetism.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]  
### Pros & Cons of Biometric Visor (Equipment-Specific)

**Pros**
- Extremely strong against IMP-1 threats (Speculo, Fidays, etc.).
- Eliminates Surprise Attack penalties from these threats, making assassination runs much safer to challenge.
- Improves Discover reliability against IMP-1, which normally can be very hard to reveal.
- Works even in melee, protecting from CC Surprise MODs regardless of LoF.


## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]  
### Tactical Considerations & Use Cases

- Place Biometric Visor troops close to:
  - Your Lieutenant.
  - High-value specialists.
  - Firelanes where IMP units tend to infiltrate.

- In close quarters, the visor’s CC Surprise immunity makes assassination attempts less reliable for the attacker.

- Even one Biometric Visor model can make entire deployment zones less hospitable for impersonators.

- Because it does not help in regular firefights, try to field it cheaply unless expecting impersonation-heavy factions.
