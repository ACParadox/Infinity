# X-Visor

Equipment

This piece of Equipment increases its user's accuracy at difficult ranges.

- This piece of Equipment alters the Range MODs of the user's BS Weapons, Special Skills, and Equipment from -3 to 0 and from -6 to -3.

- X-Visor benefits also apply to Common Skills with Range bands, such as Discover and Suppressive Fire.


## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
The X-Visor is a pure accuracy stabilizer: it smooths out the harsher Range penalties on long-range or awkward-range weapons and skills.  
It does **not** improve positive ranges, and it does **not** affect MODs unrelated to Range (Cover, Mimetism, Surprise Attack, Visibility Zones, etc.).

---

# Interactions Notes

## 1. X-Visor + Suppressive Fire
Suppressive Fire imposes strict Range bands (best at 0–16", heavy penalties past that).  
- X-Visor can **soften** the long-range penalties: a **-6 becomes -3**, making 24–32" suppressive fire more threatening.  
- Does **not** remove the Range cap (you still cannot shoot beyond the weapon's normal maximum).  
**Unofficial Insight:** This makes X-Visor units excellent long-lane suppressive anchors.

## 2. X-Visor + Discover
Discover uses Range MODs.  
- The X-Visor improves long-range Discover attempts, changing:  
  • -3 → 0  
  • -6 → -3  
**Unofficial Insight:** This is one of the biggest hidden strengths of X-Visor troopers: reliable long-range Discover against Camo pieces.

## 3. X-Visor + Template Weapons
- Direct Template Weapons (DTWs) cannot use “range bands” in normal template mode → **no benefit**.  
- Impact Templates fired as BS Attacks use range bands → **X-Visor *does* help**.

## 4. X-Visor + Speculative Fire
- Speculative Fire imposes a flat -6.  
- This is **not a Range MOD**, so X-Visor **does not** modify it.

## 6. X-Visor + Weapons with Already-Good Range Bands (HMG, MULTI Sniper)
- X-Visor mostly impacts the **worst** range bands.  
- Snipers often don’t need help, but shooting at closer-than-ideal ranges may benefit (e.g., -3 → 0).

## 7. X-Visor + MSV (Multispectral Visor)
- These two do not interact; MSV handles Mimetism/Visibility, X-Visor handles bad Range MODs.  
- Together they create an extremely consistent gunfighter across multiple conditions.

# Pros and Cons (Unofficial)

## Pros
- **Reliable long-range accuracy**, turning bad shots into viable ones.  
- **Buffs Discover** and **Suppressive Fire**, two skills that benefit enormously from Range smoothing.  
- Great on **mid-range burst weapons** (Combi, SMG, Multi Rifle) by extending their functional range.  
- Always-on, no activation order required.

## Cons
- Provides **no benefits** against Visibility Zones, Cover, Mimetism, or other common MODs.  
- Does not improve already-positive Range bands.  
- Less impactful on weapons already optimized for long range (Sniper Rifles, Feuerbach).  
- Still cannot fix the flat -6 from Speculative Fire.

---

# Tactical Uses (Unofficial)

## 1. Midfield Firefights at Awkward Range
Units with Combi Rifles or Multi Rifles become dangerous at 24–32", forcing opponents to respect longer sightlines.

## 2. Superior Long-Range Discover
X-Visor troops can reliably Discover Camo markers across the table, breaking defensive setups.

## 3. Enhanced Suppressive Fire “Zone Control”
Suppressive Fire reaching out to 32" with only -3 MOD turns many X-Visor units into exceptional area denial pieces.

## 4. Flexible Skirmishers & Fireteam Pieces
In Fireteams, X-Visor becomes even more potent: consistent Range means better odds to win FTF rolls, especially when Burst increases.

---

# Summary (Unofficial)
The X-Visor is **a consistency tool**, not a raw power increase.  
It is strongest on weapons with mid-range falloff and on troops that need to operate effectively at multiple engagement distances.  
Its biggest hidden strengths are **long-range Discover** and **extended Suppressive Fire**.
