# SymbioMate

Equipment

The SymbioMate is a single use piece of Equipment that allows its owner, in whose Unit Profile the SymbioMate is listed, to assign it to a Trooper in the same Army List, called the user, in order to provide additional combat advantages.

- The SymbioMate must be used when the Trooper is forced to make a Saving Roll.

- A SymbioMate provides its user with ARM and BTS values of 9—replacing the ARM and BTS values of the user's Unit Profile —and it also provides the Immunity (Enhanced) Special Skill. In addition, the SymbioMate can be used against Comms Attacks.

- When a Trooper with a SymbioMate is forced to make any Saving Rolls against ARM or BTS, they perform those Saving Rolls with an ARM/BTS Attribute value of 9, applying the Immunity (Enhanced) Special Skill if applicable. Any other Saving Rolls against ARM or BTS during the same Order also apply the ARM and BTS values of 9, and the Immunity (Enhanced) Special Skill.

- Against Comms Attacks, players apply the ARM and BTS values of 9 but not the Immunity (Enhanced) Special Skill.

- This is a single-use piece of Equipment, the SymbioMate will be removed from the game table at the end of the Order in which it was used.

## SymbioMate Examples

During the Deployment Phase, the Tohaa Player assigns two of their Kaeltar Specialists' SymbioMates to a Gorgos and a Sakiel, two Troopers with the Transmutation (X) Special Skill.

During the game, the Gorgos receives, in the same Order, a successful hit with DA Ammunition, and a successful Attack with Forward Observer. As the Gorgos possesses a SymbioMate they are forced to use it. Thanks to the Immunity (Enhanced) Special Skill, the TAG treats the DA hit as Normal Ammunition, applying an ARM value of 9, and is not affected by the Forward Observer Attack. At the end of the Order, the player must remove the SymbioMate from the game table.

Let's suppose the Gorgos received two successful hits from a weapon with the Continuous Damage Trait and also an Attack with a Hacking Program. The Gorgos is forced to use the SymbioMate, so the TAG ignores the Continuous Damage Trait (thanks to the Immunity (Enhanced) Special Skill) applying an ARM Attribute value of 9. However, the Gorgos must perform a Saving Roll against BTS for the Hacking Attack, using the Attribute value of 9 provided by the SymbioMate. At the end of the Order, the player must remove the SymbioMate from the game table.

If the Gorgos was in Partial Cover, it would apply the +3 MOD to its Saving Roll, if applicable.

Later, the Kaeltar Specialist who is the owner of the Sakiel's SymbioMate passes to Dead State. However, even though the Kaeltar model is removed from the game table, the player will not remove the Sakiel's unused SymbioMate.

In the next Game Round, during a heroic action, the Sakiel suffers two successful hits with AP Ammunition and one Critical hit with DA Ammunition, all of them in the same Order. As the Sakiel has a SymbioMate, he must use it, so he treats all three hits as Normal Ammunition due to the Immunity (Enhanced) Special Skill, performing a total of four Saving Rolls with an ARM value of 9—one Saving Roll for each hit and an additional Saving Roll due to the Critical. At the end of that Order, the player must remove the SymbioMate.

If the Sakiel suffers a successful Attack with a weapon loaded with PARA Ammunition, he cannot apply the Immunity (Enhanced) Special Skill as PARA Ammunition does not force Saving Rolls against the ARM or BTS Attributes.

## Overview
SymbioMates are one-use defensive equipment issued by Kaeltar Specialists and assigned during Deployment. Their power comes from two effects that apply when their user is forced to make a Saving Roll:
- ARM 9 and BTS 9 for the rest of the Order.
- Immunity (Enhanced), which converts any ammunition requiring ARM/BTS Saves into Normal Ammunition and cancels Traits such as Continuous Damage.
- Special case: Against Comms Attacks, ARM/BTS become 9 but Immunity (Enhanced) does not apply.

A SymbioMate must be spent the first time the user is required to make *any* Saving Roll in an Order.

---

## Key Rules References (for context)
- Must be used when the user is forced to make a Saving Roll.  
- Grants ARM 9 and BTS 9, replacing the user's own values.  
- Grants Immunity (Enhanced) for ARM/BTS-based effects.  
- Treats DA, AP, EXP, Continuous Damage, etc. as Normal Ammunition while active.  
- Does **not** grant Immunity (Enhanced) versus Comms Attacks.  
- Removed from the table at the end of the Order in which it is used.  
- Unused SymbioMates persist even if the Kaeltar owner dies.

---

## Interactions with Common Game Effects

### SymbioMate vs. DA, AP, EXP, T2, K1, Viral
When the SymbioMate activates, all of these are treated as Normal Ammunition (unless they do not require ARM/BTS Saves):
- AP loses its ARM-halving effect.  
- DA/EXP lose extra Saves; only one Normal Save per hit is made.  
- T2 loses its 2-Wound effect; fails become normal 1-Wound results.  
- Continuous Damage is ignored entirely.  
- Viral becomes Normal ARM Saves instead of BTS vs multiple rolls.

**Effectively, the SymbioMate compresses lethal spike damage into a few ARM 9 rolls.**

---

### SymbioMate vs. PARA Ammunition
PARA does not force ARM/BTS Saves and therefore does not trigger Immunity (Enhanced). The SymbioMate cannot convert its effects.

## Pros and Cons (Unofficial)

### Pros
- Extremely strong damage mitigation for a single Order — often completely nullifies alpha-strike attempts.  
- Converts deadly ammo types into harmless Normal Saves.  
- Immunity to many state-causing attacks that rely on "successful Attack" rather than ARM/BTS Saves.  
- Works in both Active and Reactive turns.  
- Does not require LoF or declaration; simply auto-triggers.

### Cons
- One-use only; timing control is limited because it must trigger on the first required Save in an Order.  
- Offers no protection against effects that do not use ARM/BTS Saves (PARA, some scenario or exotic effects).  
- Does not prevent the effects of Comms Attacks beyond providing BTS 9.  
- Can be baited out by a cheap attack before a stronger one arrives.
---

## Tactical Notes (Unofficial)

### Optimal Usage
- Assign to high-value pieces expected to take heavy fire (Gorgos, high-threat Tohaa attack pieces).  
- Best used to absorb an enemy alpha strike or force your opponent to waste Orders "stripping" the Mate before committing serious resources.  
- Excellent on units that intend to push into open firelanes or objectives on the first turn.  
- Combine with Partial Cover (+3 Save) for astonishing durability: ARM 9 + Cover often equals ARM 12 vs Normal ammunition.

### Baiting and Counterplay
- Opponents may intentionally trigger the Mate with minimal attacks, then follow up with lethal fire after the Mate is gone.  
- Use positioning to make the first attack likely to be something meaningful rather than disposable.

### Order Economy Impact
A SymbioMate can “tax” the opponent’s Orders by forcing them to spend more resources on an attack run than usual. Even if the model survives with no damage, the lost Orders can be decisive.
