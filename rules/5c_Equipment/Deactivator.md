# Deactivator

Equipment

This device is capable of deactivating traps and automated weapons deployed by the Enemy.

- The target must be within the user's LoF or ZoC.

- A Deactivator can only target enemy Deployable Weapons and Deployable pieces of Equipment that have been deployed on the game table, but never Camouflage Markers.

- The user of Deactivator, by passing a Normal WIP Roll, may deactivate the enemy Deployable Weapon or Deployable piece of Equipment ( Mines, FastPandas, WildParrots, Deployable Cover, Deployable Repeaters, Armed Turrets, etc.).

- Restriction: Passing the WIP Roll does not prevent the enemy Deployable Weapon or Deployable piece of Equipment from triggering during the current Order.

- The WIP Roll only applies MODs for Range, but not those from Special Skills (for example Mimetism ), or Cover.

- If the user passes the WIP Roll, the enemy Deployable weapon or Deployable piece of Equipment is removed from the game table.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

The Deactivator is a powerful utility tool that removes enemy Deployable Weapons and Equipment on a **Normal WIP Roll**, using **BS rangebands**, but with no Cover/Mimetism modifiers applied. It is the most order-efficient way in the game to erase troublesome area-control pieces while staying relatively safe.

## 1. Interaction Notes (Unofficial)

### Deactivator vs AROs
- Declaring **Deactivator** is neither a BS Attack nor a Comms Attack, so enemies may ARO as normal if they see the user.
- An enemy may declare:
  - **BS Attack** → creates a **Face-to-Face** Roll against the user’s Deactivator WIP roll.
  - **Dodge** → **Normal Roll**, does **not** oppose the Deactivator roll.
  - **Reset** → irrelevant (Deactivator is not Comms Attack).
- If the Deactivator succeeds, the Deployable is removed *even if the user is wounded in the same Order*, because removal happens in Resolution when the WIP success applies.

### Deactivator vs Visibility / MODs
- Only **Range MODs** apply (as explicitly stated in the rules).
- **Mimetism**, **Cover**, **Surprise Attack**, and similar defensive MODs **do not apply**.
- This makes Deactivator unusually reliable at close range (+6 at 0–8").

### Deactivator vs Camouflage Markers
- Explicit restriction: **cannot target Camouflage Markers**.
- Mines in Camouflage state must be revealed (usually by triggering or via Intuitive Attack/Discover + shoot) before a Deactivator can target them.

### Deactivator vs “Triggers During Order”
- Passing the roll **does not prevent** the Deployable from triggering earlier in the same Order.
- This matters for:
  - Mines
  - WildParrots (E/M Mines)
  - CrazyKoalas / MadTraps (Boost)
- Timing: If you move into a Mine’s trigger area, the Mine may detonate **before** the Deactivator removes it.

### Deactivator vs Perimeter Weapons
- It may remove:
  - **CrazyKoalas, MadTraps**
  - **WildParrots**
  - **FastPandas**
- But these are dangerous to approach:
  - Boost (“Koalas/MadTraps”) triggers before your WIP roll resolves.
  - You must position outside Boost range or behind total cover before Deactivating.

### Deactivator vs Armed Turrets / Deployable Turrets
- Deactivator removes Turrets on a single successful WIP Normal Roll.
- Turrets shoot back if they have LoF (Face-to-Face).
- Walking up from Total Cover → declaring Deactivator → winning the roll removes the Turret with no damage roll required.

### Deactivator and disco ball
- Can reactivate discoballs, helping reduce load on this disposable resource

## 2. Practical Edge-Case Interactions (Unofficial)

### 2.1 Can you Deactivate through a Repeater?
- No. Deactivator is not a Comms Attack. It requires **LoF or ZoC** of the Trooper itself.

### 2.2 Can you Deactivate while Engaged?
- Deactivator has no “No LoF” or CC permissions.
- If Engaged, you typically cannot draw LoF to targets, and ZoC may not include the Deployable.
- Even if within ZoC, you are still performing a non-CC skill in base contact with an enemy → usually illegal unless explicitly allowed.
- Recommended: treat as **not permitted** unless FAQed otherwise.

### 2.3 Can you Deactivate Multiple Deployables?
- No. Burst 1.
- Each Order removes one target at most.

### 2.4 Removing Deployables placed during the same Order
- Deployables placed with Place Deployable appear **at the Conclusion step**.
- You cannot Deactivate something that appeared **after** the Resolution step in which you used the Deactivator.

## 3. Tactical Commentary – Pros & Cons (Unofficial)

### **Pros**
- **Most order-efficient counter to Deployables.**
- Ignores defensive MODs like Mimetism and Cover.
- Massive +6 at 0–8": extremely accurate at close range.
- Can be used through Eclipse/Smoke via ZoC.
- Removes high-value pieces:
  - Mines, E/M Mines, WildParrots
  - Koalas/MadTraps
  - Deployable Repeaters
  - Armed Turrets
  - Deployable Cover variants
- Safe if used from Total Cover (no LoF → no ARO).

### **Cons**
- Requires LoF or ZoC → must approach the target.
- Cannot affect Camouflage Markers (which are often mines)
- Does not stop a Deployable triggering **during that same Order**.
- Vulnerable to Face-to-Face returns from Turrets or enemy shooters.
- Usually on Engineers or Specialist support troops (fragile carriers).

## 4. Comparison with Similar Tools (Unofficial)

### **Deactivator vs Shooting the Deployable**
**Deactivator Advantages**
- Work in ZoC without LoF (through Total Cover)
- Auto-remove on one successful roll (no ARM/BTS Saves for target).
- Ignores Cover/Mimetism.
- Does not risk scattering (unlike grenades).
- Works on high-ARM objects like Turrets with equal ease.

**Shooting Advantages**
- Can delete a Deployable **without entering trigger area**.
- Weapons may outrange the Deactivator beyond 16–24".
- Safer against Boost weapons because you can shoot from outside ZoC.

## 5. Tactical Usage Examples (Unofficial)

### Clearing Mines safely
Approach from Total Cover → declare Deactivator on a visible mine around the corner → success removes it with zero risk.

### Killing Armed Turrets
Walk a Camo/Marker close to the Turret → reveal + Deactivator at +6 range band.

### Clearing Eclipse/Smoke Zones before attacking
A Trooper inside Eclipse can Deactivate within ZoC to remove a Disco Ball or mine without LoF.


## 6. Summary (Unofficial)

The Deactivator is:
- **The strongest anti-deployable tool in Infinity N5**
- **A high-accuracy, low-risk, clean removal mechanism**
- Best when used from:
  - Total Cover
  - Inside ZoC but outside trigger ranges
  - Post-reveal on Camo Deployables

Its limitations (cannot target Camouflage, does not prevent same-order triggers) define its tactical boundaries, but within those limits it remains vastly superior to all alternatives for clearing enemy area control.
