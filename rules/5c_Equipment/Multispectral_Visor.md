# Multispectral Visor

Equipment

This piece of Equipment was designed to combat the effects of different military concealment and camouflage technologies.

## MULTISPECTRAL VISOR LEVEL 1 
- This Visor reduces the MODs of Mimetism (-3), and of Low Visibility Zones, to 0.

- This Visor reduces the MODs of Mimetism (-6), and of Poor Visibility Zones, to -3.

- Additionally, it allows the user to draw LoF through Zero Visibility Zones, applying a -6 MOD to any Skill that requires LoF. (See FAQs.)

- Troopers equipped with this piece of Equipment do not make Face to Face Rolls against weapons using Smoke Ammunition.

## MULTISPECTRAL VISOR LEVEL 2  
- This Visor reduces MODs from Mimetism and from Visibility Zones to 0.

- In addition, it allows its user to draw LoF through Visibility Zones without having to apply that Zone's MODs to any Skill that requires LoF.

- Troopers equipped with this piece of Equipment do not make Face to Face Rolls against weapons that use Smoke Ammunition.

## MULTISPECTRAL VISOR LEVEL 3

- This Visor reduces MODs from Mimetism, and from Visibility Zones, to 0.

- In addition, it allows its user to draw LoF through Visibility Zones without having to apply that Zone's MODs to any Skill that requires LoF.

- Troopers equipped with this piece of Equipment do not make Face to Face Rolls against weapons that use Smoke Ammunition.

- When performing Discover against a target in Camouflaged State, the user's WIP Roll is automatically a success.

- Users of a Multispectral Visor L3 ignore Surprise Attack MODs if they have LoF to the attacker. In addition, they also ignore Surprise Attack MODs from CC Attacks even if they don't have LoF to the attacker.

- Additionally, in the Active Turn the user may perform a BS Attack on a Camouflage Marker (CAMO) in LoF without making a previous Discover Roll, but they must apply the Mimetism MOD indicated on the Marker. However, the Cancellation clauses for Camouflaged State are applied as usual.

## IMPORTANT
Troopers with the Multispectral Visor piece of Equipment or with the Marksmanship Special Skill are affected by:

- White Noise Zones.

- The Reflective Trait.

- The Albedo piece of Equipment.


## FAQs

Q: How do Sixth Sense, Zero Visibility Zones and Multipectral Visor Level 1 interact?

A: If the Trooper with Multispectral Visor Level 1 and Sixth Sense is the target of a BS Attack through a Zero Visibility Zone, they ignore the -6 MOD for drawing LoF through the Zero Visibility Zone.


# Multispectral Visor — Interactions & Unofficial Commentary

# [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
# Multispectral Visor — Interaction Notes

## 1. Interaction with Smoke, Eclipse, and White Noise

### Smoke
- MSV2+ can draw LoF through Smoke as if it were not a Zero Visibility Zone.  
- This is the classic “Smoke + MSV2 shooting” combo:  
  • Active trooper with MSV2 fires out of Smoke with no penalty.  
  • Opponent inside/outside Smoke suffers the normal Visibility penalties.  
- MSV1 can see through Smoke, but get a -6 MOD.

### Eclipse
- Eclipse is the **hard counter** to *all* MSV levels.  
- MSV1/2/3 cannot draw LoF through Eclipse at all.  
- MSV cannot downgrade Eclipse to a Poor Visibility Zone.  
- This makes Eclipse the preferred tool against MSV ARO pieces and linked MSV snipers.

### White Noise
- White Noise blocks LoF **only** for MSV-equipped troopers.  
- Non-MSV units can shoot through normally.  
- This is a powerful asymmetric tool: your non-MSV troopers get free shots vs enemy MSV shooters.

## 2. Interaction with Camouflage, Mimetism, and Discover

### Auto-success Discover (MSV3)
- MSV3 grants **automatic success** when Discovering a Camouflaged State target.  

### MSV vs Mimetism
- MSV1 cancels Mimetism (-3).  
- MSV2+ cancel stronger Mimetism values such as (-6).  
- Because Smoke does not interact with Mimetism, these effects stack in MSV user’s favor.

### Shooting at Camouflage Markers (MSV3)
- MSV3 allows BS Attack directly against a Camo Marker in LoF.  
- This reveals the target immediately by applying normal cancellation rules.  
- Strong counter to midfield Camo “roadblocks” such as Minelayers or infiltrated skirmishers.

---

## 3. Interaction with Surprise Attack

- MSV3 ignores Surprise Attack MODs with LoF.  
- Also ignores Surprise Attack MODs from CC attacks *even without* LoF.  
- MSV1 and MSV2 do **not** provide Surprise Attack immunity.

---

## 4. Interaction with Reflective Traits and Eclipse

- MSV troopers are explicitly affected by Reflective Zero Visibility zones.  
- When an enemy throws Eclipse in FtF, MSV trooper must still roll FtF even though they cannot see through Eclipse.  
- Reflective effects ensure MSV cannot “cheat through” these special zones.

# Comparisons: MSV Levels

## MSV1
**What it does well**
- Cancels Mimetism (-3) — solid accuracy boost vs most skirmishers.  
- Cheap and often on line troops or mid-cost soldiers.

**Limitations**
- Can see through Smoke only with -6 MOD.  
- Provides no Surprise Attack protection.

**Use Cases**
- Budget counter-Camo option.  
- Good for Discover+Shoot sequences.  
- Works well in midfield brawls where enemies have Mimetism (-3).

---

## MSV2
**What it adds over MSV1**
- Can see through Smoke → enables powerful Smoke-shooting tactics.  
- Cancels strong Mimetism (-6).  
- Much more dangerous in long-range duels.

**Pros**
- Turns Smoke into an offensive weapon.  
- Dominates visibility-mod reliant units (Camo, Mimetism-6 snipers).

**Cons**
- Vulnerable to White Noise and Eclipse.  
- Often found on expensive profiles that attract enemy attention (Albedo use)

**Use Cases**
- Pair with Smoke throwers to create safe FtF engagements.  
- Defensive ARO pieces (e.g., MSV2 snipers) become difficult to approach.

---

## MSV3
**Extra abilities**
- Ignore Surprise Attack MODs (with/without LoF in CC).  
- Can directly *shoot at* Camo Markers in LoF.  
- All benefits of MSV2.

**Pros**
- Best anti-Camo tool in the game
- Eliminates many alpha-strike tricks that rely on Surprise Attack.  
- Very strong defensive presence.

**Cons**
- Very rare and usually very expensive.  
- Still helpless against Eclipse and White Noise.  
- Shooting a Camo Marker may waste an Order if the Marker’s position is non-critical.

**Use Cases**
- Hold lanes against impersonators, ninjas, surprise shooters.  
- Dominate midfield and high-value objectives by stripping Camo advantage.

# [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
# Pros / Cons of MSV in General

**Pros**
- Hard counters Mimetism-heavy factions.  
- Enables Smoke-shooting tactics (MSV2/3).  
- MSV3 shuts down Surprise Attack and allows pre-emptive shots on Camo.

**Cons**
- White Noise neutralizes MSV entirely.  
- Eclipse ignores MSV completely.  
- Vulnerable against Troopers with Albedo.
- MSV troops are typically premium units and high-value targets.  
- Does not protect against Direct Templates or non-LoF attacks.

# [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
# Tactical Notes

• **MSV2 + Smoke is one of the strongest active-turn tools** in Infinity. Use it to dismantle enemy ARO networks safely.  
• When fighting enemy MSV2 snipers, **use Eclipse** to bypass them completely.  
• Against White Noise hackers, reposition non-MSV units to exploit one-sided LoF.  
• MSV3 is an excellent anchor for objective missions where Camo pieces attempt to stall scoring.  
• Remember: **MSV does not help against Eclipse**, so plan alternative movement tools.
