# Albedo

Equipment

An Albedo jams Multispectral Visors and targeting systems, preventing its bearer from being the target of an Enemy Trooper carrying that piece of Equipment.

- Any enemy with a Multispectral Visor or the Marksmanship Special Skill who declares a BS Attack requiring LoF, or Discover, against the bearer of this piece of Equipment must apply a negative MOD to their Attribute, as shown in round brackets in the Unit Profile: Albedo (-3), Albedo (-6)...

- The Albedo MOD does not apply to CC Attacks.

## How Albedo Works (Rules Summary)
From the rules:
- Any enemy with **Multispectral Visor** or **Marksmanship** must apply the negative MOD listed in the profile (e.g., Albedo –3 / –6) when declaring:
  - **BS Attack requiring LoF**
  - **Discover**
- Does **not** affect CC Attacks.
- Applies even against Marksmanship (treated as MSV-like in N5 interactions).

## Interactions With Other Rules and Skills

### 1. **Albedo vs. Multispectral Visors**
- MSV users take a **penalty** when attacking or Discovering the Albedo bearer.
- Albedo strongly counters **MSV ARO pieces** (Snipers, linked MSV2 shooters).

### 2. **Albedo vs. Marksmanship**
- Marksmanship counts as MSV-like; therefore, the negative Albedo MOD applies.
- Albedo bypasses the benefit of ignoring Cover MODs, making Marksmanship shooters still suffer Albedo’s penalty.
- Marksmanship users cannot avoid this effect unless attacking with CC.

### 4. **Albedo vs. Template Weapons**
- The MOD only applies to **BS Attacks requiring LoF** and **Discover**.
- **Direct Templates do not require LoF**—they are *not affected* by Albedo.
- Impact Templates *still require LoF* to place the template, so they are affected.

### 5. **Albedo and Decoy / Holoecho**
- Decoy duplicates State Tokens and “effects of Skills that impose MODs” such as Albedo.
- Replica Decoys gain Albedo’s defensive effect.

**Use case:**  
- Create multiple Albedo-bearing markers to overload Discover attempts.

## Comparison Section

### Albedo vs. Mimetism
**Similarities:**
- Both impose negative MODs to enemy BS Attacks.
- Both help win Face-to-Face Rolls.

**Differences:**
- **Mimetism works against everyone**, MSV negates it.
- **Albedo works only vs. MSV / Marksmanship**, but MSV cannot negate it.

**Pros (Albedo):**
- Hard-counter to MSV sniper AROs  
- Works even against Marksmanship  

**Cons (Albedo):**
- Useless against enemies without MSV or Marksmanship  
- No effect vs. Templates  
- Does not stack as universally as Mimetism

## Tactical Use Cases

### Best Uses
- Breaking strong **MSV ARO pieces** on rooftops and firelanes.  
- Pushing objectives past MSV guards.  
- Protecting Camo units from Discover spam.  
- Early-game alpha strikes against Marksmanship REMs or Fireteams.

### Optimal Loadouts for Albedo Bearers
- High-Burst weapons (Spitfire, HMG, Red Fury).  
- Camouflaged or Mimetism platforms.  
- Skirmishers who need to cross MSV-threatened midboards.

## Counters to Albedo
- **Non-MSV shooters** (Albedo does nothing to them).
- **Direct Template Weapons** up close.
- **CC specialists**, because Albedo does not apply in CC.

# Summary
Albedo is a specialized anti-visor defense that punishes MSV and Marksmanship users, excels at cracking open MSV-based ARO towers, and synergizes extremely well with White Noise and Marker States. Its limitations (no effect vs. non-MSV foes or Templates) mean it is best used as a **targeted countermeasure**, not a universal defensive layer.
