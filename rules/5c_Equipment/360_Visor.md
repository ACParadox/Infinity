# 360º Visor

Equipment

This piece of Equipment expands the user's LoF arc.

- This type of Visor provides its user with a 360˚ LoF arc, rather than the usual 180˚ arc.


## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Interactions

### ARO generation
- The Trooper may **declare AROs against any enemy** that activates anywhere in its 360° perimeter, provided normal LoF and visibility requirements are satisfied.
- This removes a common weakness where a model can be approached from behind without allowing ARO.

### Marker States
- Marker State Troopers still have 360° LoF by rule (“Markers have 360° LoF”), so the Visor offers no additional benefit until revealed.
- Once revealed, the Visor prevents back-arc exploitation.

### Suppressive Fire
- A Trooper in Suppressive Fire normally retains only **180°** LoF for ARO.
- A 360º Visor gives full **360°** LoF even while in Suppressive Fire.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Comparison with Similar Abilities

### 360º Visor vs. Sixth Sense
**360º Visor**
- Constant 360° LoF for all purposes.
- Works in all situations.
- Helps prevent being approached from behind.

**Sixth Sense**
- Only grants reactive 360° LoF when the user is being **attacked**.
- Does not prevent an enemy from safely walking through the user’s rear arc.
- Does not apply outside reactions to Attacks.

**Pros of 360º Visor over Sixth Sense**
- Works proactively and passively at all times.
- Extremely strong on static ARO pieces.
- Protects against Surprise rear flanks.

**Cons**
- Does not protect versus Smoke, White Noise (if MSV), or Zero Vis Zones.

---
### 360º Visor vs. Stealth
Stealth suppresses AROs from Zone of Control unless the enemy has Line of Fire to the Stealth user.

A Trooper with 360º Visor has full 360° LoF, meaning they always have a facing that includes the Stealth user if the model is visible.

---

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Pros and Cons of 360º Visor

### Pros
- Total protection from back-arc approach.
- Superb for guarding multiple firelanes.
- Counter to smoke-walking or template-walking behind you (still requires LoF, but protects arc).
- Pairs extremely well with Suppressive Fire since you cover a full arc.

### Cons
- Provides no MODs—purely arc coverage.
- Does not counter Smoke, Zero Visibility, or White Noise (for MSV users).
- Offers no benefit against attackers outside LoF in Zero-Vis zones.

---

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Tactical Notes

### Best used on:
- **ARO anchors** watching multiple angles.
- **Objective holders** who cannot reposition easily.
- **Sectors vulnerable to flanking**, such as rooftops or long corridors.
- **REM and HI gunners** who benefit from wide control arcs.

### Counterplay
- Use Smoke or Zero-Vis Zones.
- Attack from total cover to block LoF rather than relying on back arc.
- Engage from multiple vectors to force bad MOD trades.
- Apply White Noise if the Visor is on a MSV-equipped model (rare, but possible).

### Examples
- A Fusilier without a 360º Visor can be completely bypassed by walking behind them.
- A Trooper with 360º Visor in Suppressive Fire can defend an entire room without needing to face any door.
- Skirmishers attempting to template from behind must still respect LoF and face a valid ARO.
