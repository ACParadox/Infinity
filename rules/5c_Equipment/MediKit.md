# MediKit

Equipment

Special Equipment that allows recovery of Models with the VITA Attribute from the Unconscious state.

- A MediKit's target must be an Allied Model with the VITA Attribute and in Unconscious State, unless certain scenario rules say otherwise.

- A MediKit can be used in one of two ways, each one with their own Requirements: To use it remotely, the user must have LoF to the target. To use it as a piece of contact equipment, the user must be in Silhouette contact with the target.

- To use it remotely, the user must have LoF to the target.

- To use it as a piece of contact equipment, the user must be in Silhouette contact with the target.

- Used remotely, a MediKit is a Non-Lethal BS Weapon. If the user passes a BS Attack Roll, applying the corresponding MODs (for Range, Cover, etc.), the target performs a single PH Roll.

- A MediKit can also be used in Silhouette contact. To do so, the user must spend one Short Skill without needing a Roll, and their target performs a PH Roll.

- In both cases, by passing the PH Roll, the target may remove one Wound and cancel their Unconscious State. If the PH Roll is failed, the target automatically enters Dead State and is removed from the game table.

- The target of a MediKit does not perform a Saving Roll.

- A MediKit can be used as many times as necessary to cancel a Trooper's Unconscious State, as long as they pass the corresponding PH Roll.

- If, due to a Special Skill or rule, a Trooper receives more than one successful hit or use of MediKit during the same Order, if any of the corresponding PH Rolls is successful, the Trooper removes a Wound and cancels their Unconscious State. In this case, even if the Trooper passes every PH Roll, they only remove one single Wound.

- The user can apply the MediKit on themselves, to cancel any states that can be canceled by this piece of Equipment. However, this cannot be done if the user is in a Null State.

- If a Trooper lists MediKit in their Unit Profile with a PH value between brackets, the Trooper will use this PH value when a MediKit is used on them.

## MediKit - GizmoKits work on Models with the STR Attribute.

- The Unconscious State of the Trooper can be cancelled by using either the Doctor or Engineer Skill, or the MediKit or GizmoKit pieces of Equipment, regardless of whether they have the VITA or STR Attribute.

# MediKit — Unofficial Interaction Notes and Commentary

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

## Core Function

The MediKit is a tool that attempts to return an Unconscious allied Model with the **VITA** Attribute back to active gameplay. Its resolution is extremely binary:  
- **Success:** The target passes a **PH Roll** → removes 1 Wound → Unconscious is cancelled.  
- **Failure:** The target automatically **enters Dead State**.

---

## Interaction Notes

### 1. MediKit as a BS Attack
When used at range, the MediKit is:
- A **BS Attack**.
- **Non-Lethal** (the BS Attack cannot kill—only the PH failure kills).
- Affected by:
  - Range Modifiers (+3 at 0–8", 0 at 8–16", –6 at 16–24").
  - Cover (-3 to BS).
  - Fireteam bonuses (BS MODs; Special Dice).

However:
- Improving the BS Roll **does not improve the PH Roll**.  
- A perfect hit still leaves the patient at the mercy of their own PH.

This means Fireteam Paramedics excel at *hitting* with the MediKit, but the *success* is still entirely dependent on the target.

### 2. MediKit vs. Doctor (Comparison)

**Doctor:**
- Uses Doctor's **WIP Roll**, typically 13–15.
- Only available in Silhouette contact.
- Failure also kills the target, but they may reroll via:
  - Doctor (ReRoll)
  - Command Token (if target has Cube)

**MediKit:**
- Uses **target’s PH Roll**, often far lower.
- Can heal from **range**.
- Cannot benefit from WIP or Command Token rerolls (PH Rolls only).
- Much easier to deliver safely, but far saltier in outcomes.

**Pros of MediKit:**
- Can be used without risking the healer.
- Great for difficult-to-reach casualties.
- Can combine with Fireteam bonuses for hit consistency.

**Cons of MediKit:**
- Low-PH troopers often face ~40–50% death chance.
- Cannot cancel States like Doctor can.
- No rerolls unless scenario provides something unusual.

---
### 3. MediKit and PH (X) Profiles

Some troops have a modified PH when being healed: **PH(X)**.

These profiles significantly increase survival odds and make MediKits much more reliable.  
A force with multiple PH(13–15) troops benefits disproportionately from Paramedics.

---
### 4. Multi-Hit MediKit Interactions

“When a Trooper receives more than one successful hit from a MediKit… if any PH Roll is successful, the Trooper removes one Wound.”

This means:
- Multiple PH Rolls do **not** stack healing.
- More hits only improve the statistical chance of **getting at least one success**, never more than +1 Wound restored.


### 6. MediKit and Technorganic / GizmoKit Interactions

Rules specify:
- Technorganic troops may be revived by **either MediKit or GizmoKit**, regardless of VITA or STR Attribute.
- This makes Technorganic profiles uniquely flexible.

GizmoKit, by contrast:
- Works on STR models.
- Has **no death penalty on failed PH**, unlike MediKit.

## Tactical Commentary

### When to Use a MediKit
Use a MediKit when:
- The target has **high PH**.
- You need a **Specialist** or strong Trooper back online immediately.
- Recovering a wounded trooper safely from distance is preferable to risking a Doctor.

### When NOT to Use a MediKit
Avoid MediKit use when:
- The target’s PH is 10 or lower (high kill chance).
- You can safely bring in a Doctor with a strong WIP.
- The trooper’s death would yield Objective points to the opponent.

---

## Pros and Cons Summary

**Pros**
- Safe delivery (ranged healing).
- Cheap equipment—often on budget Specialists.
- Fireteams boost BS accuracy.
- Useful for high-PH elite troops.
- Can revive Technorganic troops.

**Cons**
- Failure = **Dead State**.
- Low-PH troops are extremely risky to heal.
- Cannot cancel non-Unconscious States.
- No rerolls; success relies solely on target PH.

---

## Overall Assessment

The MediKit is a **high-risk, situational revival tool**.  
It rewards players who:
- Understand probability,
- Position Paramedics safely,
- Prioritize high-PH units for healing,
- Treat healing as a tactical option—not a guarantee.

Viewed properly, the MediKit is not a Doctor substitute.  
It is a **tactical gamble** that can restore tempo or catastrophically backfire.  
Use it with intention, not hope.
