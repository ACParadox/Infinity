# Deployable Repeater

Quantronic Combat (Hacking)

Equipment

This is a hacking range amplifier designed for deployment onto the battlefield.

- When the player declares the Place Deployable Common Skill, the Trooper places a Deployable Repeater Token (REPEATER) on the game table.

- The Deployable Repeater is a piece of Equipment that contains a Repeater.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
## Interaction Notes for Deployable Repeaters

### 2. **No Trigger Area → Very Few Placement Restrictions**
- Because Deployable Repeaters lack a Trigger Area, they avoid the main limitations that Mines, Koalas, or other deployables face.  
- **Camouflage markers do NOT prevent placement** (unless scenario-specific restrictions apply).

### 3. **Activation Timing**
- The Repeater becomes active at the **end of the Order** in which it is placed.  
- This means:
  - You **cannot** place a Repeater and hack through it in the **same** Order.  
  - You **can** hack through it starting on **your next Order**.

### 4. **Hacking Through Enemy Repeaters**
- When hacking *through an enemy Repeater*, Firewall MODs apply:
  - **Attacker:** suffers **–3 WIP**  
  - **Defender:** gains **+3 to Saving Rolls**
- Only **one** Firewall applies even if the target has multiple possible sources.

### 5. **Deployable Repeaters vs. Pitcher Repeaters**
- A Pitcher creates a Deployable Repeater, but with the following distinctions:
  - Pitcher requires a **BS Attack** and a roll.  
  - Standard Deployable Repeater uses **Place Deployable**, requiring **no roll**, but must be in Silhouette contact (no long-range projection).  
- Both produce **identical functional Repeaters** once placed.

### 6. **Stealth Interactions**
- Stealth prevents Hacking AROs unless the hacker has LoF, **even if the Stealth unit moves within Repeater range**.  
- Repeaters do **not** disable Stealth or provide special detection.

### 7. **Destroying Deployable Repeaters**
- STR 1, ARM 0, BTS 0, Size 1  
- Vulnerable to:
  - BS Attacks
  - Templates
- As Equipment with a profile, they can always be targeted normally.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
## Pros & Cons of Deployable Repeaters

**Pros**
- Creates hacking bubbles exactly where needed.  
- No roll required → extremely reliable.  
- Bypasses Camouflage placement restrictions.  
- Provides the cheapest way to extend hacking area mid-mission.  
- Excellent for enabling:  
  - Guided missile plays  
  - Spotlight pressure  
  - Lockdown of movement corridors  
  - Disrupting TAGs and HI

**Cons**
- Must be placed in Silhouette contact (no ranged projection, unless using a pitcher).  
- Easily destroyed due to low ARM/BTS/STR.  
- Requires Order expenditure and often exposes the carrier.  
- Hackers gain Firewall protection when you attack them through **their** Repeaters.  
- Provides **no zone denial** — unlike Mines, Repeaters do not trigger and do not impede movement by themselves.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
## Comparison With Similar Tools

### Deployable Repeater vs. **Pitcher**
**Deployable Repeater**
- Short-range (touch-based) placement.  
- No Roll required.  
- Safer for precision positioning.  
- Guaranteed to appear unless blocked by terrain or space.

**Pitcher**
- Long-range projection (8–24+ inches).  
- Requires BS Attack roll; affected by range and modifiers.  
- Generates AROs because it is a BS Attack.  
- Can bypass dangerous zones by shooting around corners.

**Pros of Deployable Repeater**
- Certainty, reliability.  
- Quiet placement with minimal risk.

**Pros of Pitcher**
- Threaten targets far away.  
- Enables Guided/Spotlight networks at distance.

---
### Deployable Repeater vs. **FastPanda**
**Deployable Repeater**
- Static once placed.  
- Requires Silhouette contact, no roll.  
- Stays forever until destroyed.  

**FastPanda**
- Placed anywhere within the user’s **ZoC**.  
- Still static, despite Perimeter label.  
- Disposable (1).  
- Allows placement around corners or onto rooftops within ZoC.

**Pros of Deployable Repeater**
- Can be replenished (Disposable 3).  
- Highly predictable and safe placement.

**Pros of FastPanda**
- Extreme positional flexibility.  
- Can be placed behind terrain without exposing the user.


## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
## Tactical Uses and Interactions

- **Objective denial:** Place a Deployable Repeater on or next to consoles to threaten hackers/HI/TAGs approaching them.
- **Mid-game hacking trap:** Move a light specialist near an enemy firelane, drop a repeater, retreat — then engage with hackers from safety.
- **Corner threat:** Place behind a wall so enemies rounding the corner enter your hacking area unexpectedly.
- **TAG hunting:** Combine with Spotlight → Missile or Oblivion chains to freeze/disable big attack pieces.

### Counter-play
- Enemies will often:
  - Shoot the repeater immediately.  
  - Use a Warband or cheap paramedic to clear it.  
  - Move a Stealth unit through it safely.  
  - Send in a non-hackable unit.

Counter this by:
- Placing multiple Repeaters.  
- Covering the Repeater with mines, Koalas, or overlapping LoF.  
- Positioning them in hard-to-reach terrain.
