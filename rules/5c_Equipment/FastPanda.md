# FastPanda

Equipment

The FastPanda is a self-propelled hacking range amplifier designed for short-range remote deployment to key areas of the battlefield.

- When the player declares the Place Deployable Common Skill, the user of this piece of Equipment can deploy the FastPanda Model totally inside of his Zone of Control rather than in contact with his Silhouette.

- The FastPanda is a piece of Equipment that contains a Repeater.

- A deployed FastPanda remains on the table until the end of the game or until it is destroyed.

FastPandas apply the Deployable and Perimeter rule, placing the weapon or piece of Equipment completely inside the ZoC of the Trooper, instead of in Silhouette contact.

# FastPanda — Interactions and Unofficial Comments

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
The key strength of FastPanda is speed: it can instantly project a Repeater forward without having to spend movement to get into a dangerous area. However, it must be placed within the Requirements of its declaration and cannot be moved after deployment. It is a temporary projection of hacking influence, not a mobile or durable asset.

## FastPanda — Rules Interactions (Unofficial Commentary)

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
**Interaction with Hacking Area:**
- Once deployed, the FastPanda counts as a Repeater for all purposes.
- This immediately extends the user’s Hacking Area and can enable ARO hacking, Supportware, or Spotlight from advantageous positions.
- Because FastPanda creates a Repeater *instantly*, the Active Trooper can threaten enemy Hackable Troopers in the same Order if they move into range after the FastPanda is placed.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
**Interaction with Pitchers:**
- FastPanda is an alternative to Pitchers for many armies lacking B2/B3 Deployable repeaters.
- FastPanda is automatic and cannot miss, but is much shorter ranged since it is placed, not fired.

## FastPanda Compared to Similar Tools

**FastPanda vs. Pitcher**
- FastPanda:  
  - **Pros:** Automatic success, no range bands, instant and safe.  
  - **Cons:** Short placement distance, Disposable with fewer uses.
- Pitcher:  
  - **Pros:** Very long-range Repeater projection, can be used from total safety.  
  - **Cons:** Can miss; range penalties matter; requires BS rolls;

**FastPanda vs. Deployable Repeater**
- FastPanda:  
  - **Pros:** Very fast to apply; placed instantly without needing to travel to the spot; forces immediate hacking threats.  
  - **Cons:** Does not remain stealthily hidden; usually fewer uses.
- Deployable Repeater (via Move + Place):  
  - **Pros:** Can be delivered exactly where desired if the Trooper can reach the area.  
  - **Cons:** Costs more Orders, exposes the model to danger, delays activation of the hacking threat.

## Unofficial Tactical Notes

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- FastPandas shine in **alpha strikes**: deploy one, then immediately use hacking programs such as Spotlight, Carbonite, or Oblivion in subsequent Orders.
- Excellent for **mission play**, especially for assassinations or denying key zones by threatening heavy infantry or REMs with immobilization.
- Because FastPanda deployment does not require a roll, it is ideal for **consistency-heavy game plans** where you cannot risk a failed Pitcher shot.
- Strong deterrent: opponents must consider that any advance into the Panda’s radius opens them to Hacker AROs.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Common tactical pattern:
1. Trooper activates.  
2. Deploy FastPanda into a safe forward position.  
3. Hacker(s) in the backline immediately gain access to the target zone.  
4. Follow up with Spotlight for guided attacks, or Oblivion to shut down key enemy pieces.

## Weaknesses (Unofficial Commentary)

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Easily removed by enemy attacks (BS, Templates, etc.).  
- Cannot be repositioned once deployed; poor if the battle flow shifts.  
- Limited uses—conserve them for decisive exchanges.  
- If deployed too early, opponents may simply avoid the zone and waste your investment.

# Summary

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
FastPanda is a fast, reliable hacking projection tool. Compared to Pitchers, it trades range for guaranteed placement. Compared to Deployable Repeaters, it trades stamina and flexibility for immediate effect. It rewards clever positioning and timing, especially when supporting aggressive hacking strategies or coordinated area denial.
