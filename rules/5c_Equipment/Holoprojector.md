# Holoprojector

Equipment

A nanotechnological tridimensional pseudo-solid image emitter, conceived to hide the identity of its user or to deceive the adversaries regarding the number of enemies they have to face.

- This piece of Equipment allows its bearers to deploy in Holoecho State when they are placed on the table.

- In game, it allows the user to enter Holoecho State.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

## GENERAL FUNCTION (Unofficial Summary)

Holoprojector allows a Trooper to:
• Deploy in **Holoecho State** (three holograms: one real, two decoys).  
• Re-enter Holoecho State in-game (Long Skill, outside LoF).  

Holoecho State ends the moment the bearer performs actions that could reveal identity:
• Declaring an Attack, Look Out!, or any Skill requiring a Roll.  
• Declaring a Long Skill (except Cautious Move).  
• Being Discovered, taking a hit, entering Silhouette contact, or making a Saving Roll.  
• Becoming Impetuous or entering Retreat!  
• Breaking Coherency at the start/end of an Order.

------------------------------------------------------------
## INTERACTIONS WITH GAME MECHANICS

### ARO Interactions
• Each Holoecho provides ARO triggers independently. Reactive troopers pick **one** echo to target, but different defenders may pick different ones.
• A delaying ARO is allowed—if the bearer **reveals** with the second Short Skill. If not, the delayed ARO is lost.
• Holoechoes activate Mines, Koalas, and similar equipment. This lets you "sweep" enemy deployables.

### Discover Interactions
• Each Holoecho must be Discovered separately.  
• A discovered decoy disappears but does **not** reveal the bearer unless the bearer is itself Discovered.  
• Mass Discover attempts can drain enemy Order efficiency.

### Fireteam Interactions
• A model in Holoecho State **cannot be part of a Fireteam**.  
• Entering Holoecho State immediately removes the bearer from the Fireteam.  

### Hidden Deployment
• A Trooper may begin the game in both Hidden Deployment and Holoecho State.  
• You must secretly note positions of all three Holoechoes.  
• Upon revealing, a Coherency Check is required—out-of-Coherency echoes vanish.

### Deployables and Equipment
• Holoechoes **cannot replicate deployables** (Koalas, Mines, FastPandas, etc.).  
• They **can replicate non-token equipment** like TinBots, and State markers (Prone, Unloaded).

### Silhouette, Movement, and Interactions
• Decoys cannot interact with scenery (vaulting, ladders, etc.), but copy the movement declaration.  
• All three must maintain Coherency at order start/end—failure cancels the state.

## UNOFFICIAL TACTICAL EXPLANATIONS

### What Holoprojector Is Best At
• **Absorbing AROs:** Decoys can soak templates, ARO bursts, or attacks that would otherwise kill the real trooper.  
• **Forcing inefficient enemy Orders:** Multiple Discover rolls needed; cautious enemies will slow down dramatically.  
• **Approaching dangerous ARO pieces safely:** Use decoys to test LoF, trigger Mines, or bait out templates.  
• **Obscuring your deployment:** Even experienced players struggle to identify the real model early.

### Common Tactical Patterns
• **“Template Fishing”:** Present decoys within template range to bait out a risky enemy template attack.  
• **“AROsplitting Trap”:** When an opponent divides ARO fire across echoes, real survival chances spike.  
• **“Mine Sweeping”:** Move decoy close enough to trigger a deployable without risking the real troop.  
• **“Delayed ARO Punish”:** Enemies that delay will lose the ARO entirely if you do not break Holoecho.

### Weaknesses
• Vulnerable to Sensor
• Cannot be used while Impetuous or in Retreat!.  
• Holoecho breaks immediately once you attack—so it provides **no combat advantage**, only approach/survival advantage.

------------------------------------------------------------
## PROS AND CONS

### PROS
+ Powerful deception and opponent misallocation of resources.  
+ Effective ARO mitigation—forces opponents to shoot decoys.  
+ Excellent for maneuvering across fire lanes.  
+ Cheap capability on many mid-cost Skirmishers/Light Infantry.  
+ Strong synergy with missions requiring early aggressive movement.

### CONS
– Provides no direct combat bonuses once revealed.  
– Susceptible to Sensor and coordinated Discover attempts.  
– Requires strict Coherency management; mistakes instantly break the state.  
– Cannot operate inside Fireteams.  

------------------------------------------------------------
## COMPARING HOLOPROJECTOR VS HOLOMASK (Unofficial)

### What They Share
• Both provide deception and Private Information.  
• Both cancel when the bearer takes hits, makes Rolls, or becomes Impetuous.  
• Both can be combined for “deep impersonation”.

### Key Differences
Holoprojector:
• Creates **multiple bodies** (3 echoes).  
• Best for approaching, disruption, soaking fire, and sweeping deployables.  
• Breaks when attacking or performing any rolled skill.

### Holomask:
• Creates **identity disguise** but does not multiply bodies.  
• Works inside **Fireteams** (as long as not revealed).  
• Superior in long-term deception and list concealment.

### Pros of Holoprojector vs Holomask
+ Provides physical redundancy (decoys).  
+ Much better for ARO manipulation and advance.  
+ Creates strong early-game misreads.

### Cons of Holoprojector vs Holomask
– Cannot stay active once combat begins.  
– No Fireteam compatibility.  
– Less useful in long-term bluffing; more of a one-turn tool.

------------------------------------------------------------
## WHEN TO USE HOLOPROJECTOR
• When crossing exposed fire corridors.  
• When approaching MSV or high-burst ARO towers.  
• When you want to hide the real threat among multiple false ones.  
• When attacking deployable-heavy opponents (Nomads, Haqq, Ariadna).  
• When playing first turn and needing safe early board control.

## ADVANCED TRICKS (UNOFFICIAL)

• **Reverse Psychology Reveal:** Sometimes intentionally breaking Holoecho early convinces opponents the “wrong model” was real.  
• **Echo Body Blocking:** Use decoys to create movement obstacles or cover angles for allies.
