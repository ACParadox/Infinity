# HoloMask

Equipment

A nanotechnological tridimensional pseudo-solid image emitter, conceived to hide the identity of its user and make it resemble another trooper in its army.

- During the Deployment Phase, it allows the user to deploy in HoloMask State.

- In game, it allows the user to enter HoloMask State.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

## INTERACTIONS AND HIDDEN DETAILS

**Entering HoloMask**
- The Equipment allows deploying in HoloMask State and re-entering the State later using a Long Skill while outside all enemy LoF.  
- The State requires you to place the *imitated Model* instead of the real one.

**Restrictions on What Can Be Imitated**
- The imitated Trooper must be from the same Faction/Sectorial and have the same Silhouette.  
- HoloMask **cannot** copy:
  - Hacker/Hackable status when entering an enemy Hacking Area. You must reveal that you are *not* hackable when entering an enemy Hacking Area.
  - Deployable Equipment represented by Tokens/Models (TinBots, Koalas, Mines, FastPandas, SymbioMates, etc.).
  - Special Skills, weapons, or equipment of the imitated Trooper; only the *appearance* and *declared weapon option* are duplicated.

**Revealing Conditions**
HoloMask is cancelled whenever the bearer:
- Declares an Attack, Look Out!, or any Skill requiring a Roll.  
- Declares any Long Skill except Cautious Movement.  
- Enters Silhouette contact with an enemy.  
- Makes a Saving Roll.  
- Is Discovered.  
- Becomes Impetuous (e.g., Frenzy) or enters Retreat!.

Cancellation always replaces the fake model with the real one at the **end of the Order**, even if the revealing action occurs at the end of that Order.

**Fireteam Interactions**
- A HoloMasked member of a Fireteam remains disguised when the team declares Short Skill combinations such as Move + BS Attack, as long as the HoloMasked trooper *itself* performs Idle (for not being Team Leader or not attacking).  
- However, actions that require *that trooper* to roll (e.g., Dodge, Reset, Attack) automatically reveal it.

**Private Information**
- Presence and identity of HoloMask troopers are always Private Information until revealed.  
- When revealed, full Open Information must be provided immediately.

**Hacking Interaction**
- Even if imitating a Hacker/Hackable model, you must declare your *real* non-Hackable status when entering a Hacking Area. This often betrays disguised Heavy Infantry or REM imitations.

**Discover Interaction**
- Enemies must succeed a Discover Roll to reveal a HoloMasked model.  
- However, they may still intuitively suspect a disguise based on unusual deployment or Fireteam composition.


## TACTICAL INTERACTIONS (UNOFFICIAL)

- **Lieutenant protection:** One of the strongest defensive tools for Lieutenants—your opponent may misallocate entire Order pools hunting the wrong target.
- **Fireteam deception:** Excellent for blending a vulnerable model into a Fireteam, imitating a cheaper or tougher profile.
- **Baiting Discover:** Forces opponents to spend Orders verifying identities rather than attacking.
- **Counter-hacking mind games:** Pretending to be a Hacker can bait enemy Hackers into exposing themselves; the reveal moment (Hacking Area entry) then disrupts their plans.
- **Objective trickery:** Since the real profile is used, a Specialist disguised as a non-Specialist can reach objectives uncontested.

 
## PROS AND CONS OF HOLOMASK

**Pros**
- Exceptional deception tool: hides identity, profile, and role.
- Forces opponents to spend Orders on Discover or repositioning.
- Protects high-value targets such as Lieutenants, Hackers, or gunfighters.
- Allows convincing imitation of common units to avoid drawing attention.
- Works during Deployment and can be regained mid-game.

**Cons**
- Reveals on any roll, including Dodge, Reset, or Saving Rolls—easy to break unintentionally.
- Cannot copy Hackable status or deployables, often giving away the disguise.
- Fireteam interactions can accidentally reveal the user if they participate in rolled actions.
- Careless deployment may create inconsistencies that alert experienced opponents.
- Loses effectiveness late game when fewer models remain and identities become more obvious.


## COMPARISON — HOLOMASK VS HOLOECHO (UNOFFICIAL)

**Similarity**
- Both are deception tools and both rely on Private Information.  
- They may be **combined** (officially supported) when the trooper has both pieces of Equipment.

**Differences**
- HoloMask is *identity deception*; HoloEcho is *quantity deception*.  
- HoloMask replaces the model with another; HoloEcho creates duplicates.  
- HoloMask is vulnerable to Discover; HoloEcho also collapses from being hit, discovered, or breaking Coherency.  
- HoloMask allows blending into Fireteams; HoloEcho users cannot remain in Fireteams.

**Pros of HoloMask over HoloEcho**
- Subtle, harder to detect.  
- Works in Fireteams (until forced to roll).  
- Can disguise role (e.g., Specialist identity).

**Pros of HoloEcho over HoloMask**
- Provides real defensive value: attacks may hit decoys.  
- Forces AROs to choose one target.  
- Protects against alpha strikes or precision shooters.

**Cons of HoloMask compared to HoloEcho**
- Provides no additional survivability—only deception.  
- Once revealed, the disguise is gone permanently.

**Cons of HoloEcho compared to HoloMask**
- Obvious hologram behavior gives away the presence of a Holoprojector unit.  
- Requires Coherency and collapses easily.  
- Cannot be part of Fireteams.

  
## COMMON MISTAKES AND CLARIFICATIONS (UNOFFICIAL)

- **You must declare real Hackable status when relevant.** Forgetting this is the most common rules mistake with HoloMask.  
- **You do *not* gain the imitated unit’s Skills or Equipment.** Only appearance and declared loadout.  
- **Being attacked does not reveal you—only making a Saving Roll does.** The opponent may shoot you with no reveal until you are forced to save.  
- **Template weapons do not reveal you unless a Saving Roll is forced.**  
- **Fireteam Dodges reveal the user**, even if the rest of the Fireteam Dodges safely.


## BEST USES (UNOFFICIAL)

- Protecting Lieutenants by imitating a common line trooper.  
- Hiding Hackers or Specialists until the exact moment they must act.  
- Creating false Fireteam compositions to mislead the opponent’s threat assessment.  
- Drawing enemy forces into bad fights by pretending to be a weaker unit.  
- Using psychological pressure: opponents often overthink disguised models and misallocate Orders.
