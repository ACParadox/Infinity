# Baggage

Equipment

This piece of Equipment reflects the supplies the player's combat force has available.

- An Allied Trooper in a Non- Null state and inside the Zone of Control of this Trooper may perform the Reload Common Skill to either cancel their Unloaded State, or to regain all expended uses of their Disposable weapons or pieces of Equipment. This effect may only be applied to a single Allied Trooper per Order.

- In the States Phase, each Allied Trooper inside the Zone of Control of this Trooper may automatically cancel their Unloaded State, or regain all expended uses of their Disposable weapons or pieces of Equipment.

- Recovering the use of Disposable weapons and pieces of Equipment does not affect any weapons and pieces of Equipment that were already deployed on the gaming table.

Non-Reloadable Unloaded State cannot be cancelled for Disposable weapons or pieces of Equipment with the Non-Reloadable Label or Trait, and their expended uses cannot be regained.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
## INTERACTION NOTES:

- Interaction with Disposable (X):
  Disposable (X) items regain all uses at once when Reload is applied. A Trooper who has spent multiple types of Disposable weapons (for example, both Panzerfaust and Drop Bears) regains all of them simultaneously. Baggage does not re-arm items already placed on the table (such as deployed Mines or Perimeter weapons).

### PROS:

- Portable, passive support piece that keeps Disposable weapons relevant throughout the game.
- Excellent for armies relying on Panzerfausts, Mines, E/Maulers, Pitchers, Adhesives, or any attrition-oriented tools.
- Allows aggressive models to expend resources early with reduced opportunity cost.
- States Phase automatic reload means no Order expenditure if timing is convenient.

### CONS:

- Requires proximity (ZoC) and the Baggage trooper must stay alive and Non-Null.
- Does not restore deployed items, which can limit some strategies.
- Baggage models often have minimal combat stats, making them vulnerable.
- Provides little benefit to units that don’t use Disposable equipment.

## TACTICAL NOTES:

- Keep Baggage near midfield or behind main attack elements to enable efficient re-arming without endangering the carrier.
- Combine with high-impact Disposable ARO weapons (Panzerfaust, Flammenspeer) to maintain continuous threat projection.
- Synergizes extremely well with repeaters such as FastPandas, as Pitcher carriers can regain uses for sustained hacking pressure.
- Also useful for Discoballer
- Avoid overexposing Baggage models — losing them early drastically reduces your long-game toolset.
