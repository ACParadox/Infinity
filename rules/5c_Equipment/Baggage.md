# Baggage

> **N5.3 change (September 2026):** Baggage can be carried by a **Trooper or game element**. Allied Troopers may Reload while inside the ZoC of that non-Null Baggage source.

AUTOMATIC EQUIPMENT
Obligatory, No Roll.

This piece of Equipment reflects the supplies the player's combat force has available.

- An Allied Trooper in a non-Null state and inside the Zone of Control of this Trooper or game element ("Trooper" from now on) may perform the Reload Common Skill to either cancel their Unloaded State, or to regain all expended uses of their Disposable weapons or pieces of Equipment. This effect may only be applied to a single Allied Trooper per Order.
- In the States Phase, each Allied Trooper inside the Zone of Control of this Trooper may automatically cancel their Unloaded State, or regain all expended uses of their Disposable weapons or pieces of Equipment.
- Recovering the use of Disposable weapons and pieces of Equipment does not affect any weapons and pieces of Equipment that were already deployed on the gaming table.

Non-Reloadable Unloaded State cannot be cancelled for Disposable weapons or pieces of Equipment with the Non-Reloadable Label or Trait, and their expended uses cannot be regained.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
The important N5.3 wording change is that the Baggage source does not have to be a Trooper; it can be another allied game element carrying Baggage. Disposable uses are restored on the owning Trooper, not on already-deployed copies left on the table.
