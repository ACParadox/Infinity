# Motorcycle

Equipment

Motorcycles are single-person light transports that offer great maneuverability at top speeds. For game purposes, Motorcycles are not a Vehicle (VH).

- Restriction: Troopers mounted on a Motorcycle cannot enter Prone State.

- Restriction: Troopers mounted on a Motorcycle cannot declare Climb or use ladders.

- Restriction: Troopers mounted on a Motorcycle cannot Jump vertically or diagonally upward, they can only Jump horizontally and vertically or diagonally downward.

- Restriction: Troopers mounted on a Motorcycle cannot declare Cautious Movement.

A Trooper on a Motorcycle may drive up ramps and stairs as long as they respect the General Movement Rules. However, they cannot use ladders.

## General Behavior
Motorcycle Equipment grants a Trooper a high-mobility profile with increased first-Move value, but usually imposes restrictions on Cover and sometimes on silhouette or weapon availability depending on the specific edition/army profile. Troopers with this Equipment often have the option to **Dismount**, changing to an alternate profile with a different Movement value, silhouette, and mobility limitations.

## Cover Interaction
Motorcycles typically cannot claim Cover while mounted. This is a major defensive limitation and affects both Active and Reactive turns. The rider must evaluate when to stay mounted for speed versus when to dismount to gain defensive bonuses.

**Pros (Mounted):**
- High Movement allows wide flanking, fast repositioning, and objective reach.
- Great for pushing into midfield before the opponent can react effectively.

**Cons (Mounted):**
- Lack of Cover significantly increases risk when exposed to AROs.
- Sometimes harder to hide due to larger silhouette.

## Dismounting Interaction
Dismounting generally replaces the Trooper with a different profile. This allows:
- Access to Cover.
- Access to terrain that bikes cannot enter.
- Finer movement control or repositioning in tight spaces.

However:
- Losing the speed bonus reduces reach.
- Switching states may require careful timing so that the Trooper is not stranded.

## Template Weapons and Vulnerability
Motorcycles being unable to claim Cover makes them more vulnerable to:
- **Direct Templates:** No Cover means full-damage saves.
- **Large blast areas:** Harder to hide due to silhouette and mobility pathing.

But the high mobility makes them excellent at:
- Approaching template-wielding enemies from outside their ZoC.
- Crossing firelanes quickly.

## Impetuous and Motorcycle Units
Many motorcycle units are **Impetuous**, increasing their Order efficiency but forcing movement toward the enemy. This synergizes with speed but increases risk of being forced into bad lines of fire.

**Pros:**
- "Free" movement is amplified by high speed.
- Excellent early-game pressure.

**Cons:**
- Harder to remain hidden while mounted.
- Can require careful positioning to avoid being forced into an unfavorable Impetuous path.


## Tactical Uses
- **Opening Turn Pressure:** Motorcycle units excel at aggressive first-turn engagement and board control.
- **Fast Objective Runners:** They can reach objectives before most troops.
- **Flank Attackers:** Speed allows them to hit isolated targets or exposed linchpins.
- **Zone Scoring:** High mobility helps rapidly reposition into scoring areas.

## Counterplay Considerations
Enemies can exploit:
- Their inability to take Cover.
- Predictable Impetuous movement paths.
- Vulnerability to templates.

Positioning, suppressive fire lanes, and mines are effective methods of slowing or eliminating motorcycle troops.
