# Nanoscreen

Equipment

This is a device that generates a thick cloud of nanobots that protects its user against ranged Attacks.

- The user is the target of a BS Attack.

- A -3 BS MOD is imposed on BS Attack Rolls against the user, and a +3 MOD applies to the user's Saving Rolls against BS Attacks.

- Restriction: These MODs do not apply to BS Attacks with the Comms Attack Trait.

This device cannot be used against CC Attacks.

## INTERACTION NOTES

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Nanoscreen and Template Weapons  
Template Weapons ignore the -3 Cover MOD to attacks but do not ignore the +3 Cover MOD to Saving Rolls unless the template is placed in a way that would ignore terrain-based Cover.  
Since Nanoscreen is not terrain-based, the Trooper always gains the +3 to Saving Rolls against Template attacks.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Nanoscreen while Prone  
Entering Prone does not disable Nanoscreen. The Trooper continues to receive Cover MODs even in the open.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Nanoscreen and Surprise Attack  
Nanoscreen does not protect against Surprise Attack MODs. It only applies the MODs that Cover would provide.

## PROS AND CONS OF NANOSCREEN

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Pros  
- Always-on Cover MODs regardless of terrain availability.  
- Very strong in open-field reactive positions.  
- Excellent synergy with Mimetism and long-range weapons.  
- Works whether standing or Prone.  
- Works against Template Weapons for Saving Rolls.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Cons  
- Does not create actual terrain; no LoF blocking or silhouette protection.  
- Does not help against attacks that bypass BS modifiers (e.g., many CC interactions, some Hacking).  
- Can create a false sense of safety; enemies may engage with high-Burst weapons and overwhelm the cover MODs.  
- Provides no benefit against attacks that do not roll to hit (certain states and effects).

## TACTICAL NOTES

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Nanoscreen Troopers excel as gunfighters in mid- to long-range duels.  
Place them in elevated or open positions where opponents cannot deny their line of fire without exposing themselves.  
Pair with Suppressive Fire to produce a highly punitive -6 MOD for attackers.  
When combined with Mimetism, Nanoscreen often forces attackers into -9 MOD situations before range is considered, making them ideal ARO pieces.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Avoid letting enemies approach too close.  
Nanoscreen is much less helpful against Close Combat, Direct Template Weapons (for hit MODs), or non-BS attacks.  
Keep distance and force ranged engagements.
