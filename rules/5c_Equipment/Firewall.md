# Firewall

> **N5.3 change (September 2026):** Firewall is **AUTOMATIC EQUIPMENT**.
>
> **N5 FAQ v0.1 clarification (September 2026):** If a Trooper's Hacking Device provides Firewall, the Firewall MODs apply to **any Comms Attack** against that Trooper while the Hacking Device is active. They do not apply while that Hacking Device is disabled, for example because the Trooper is Isolated or in a Null State.

Quantronic Combat (Hacking)

AUTOMATIC EQUIPMENT

Obligatory

Some Troopers or Hacking Devices have pieces of Equipment which provide extra protection against Comms Attacks. These defenses are codified in the Firewall rule, which applies a series of MODs to hinder the Attack and enhance the target's protection.

- Any enemy that declares a Comms Attack against a Trooper benefitting from a Firewall must apply a negative MOD to his WIP Attribute, as indicated between brackets: Firewall (-3), Firewall (-6)...

- A Trooper benefiting from a Firewall also applies a MOD of +3 to their Saving Rolls (SR) against Comms Attacks.

- Troopers can only benefit from one Firewall at a time. If a Trooper can benefit from more than one Firewall, their player will decide which one to apply.

The Firewall in a Comms Attack is the equivalent of Cover in a BS Attack, it hinders the Attack and aids the target’s Saving Roll.

Although the MOD to the Attack varies (-3, -6…), the MOD to the Saving Roll is always fixed (+3), unless a specific rule indicates otherwise.

Examples of Tinbot Types TinBot: Firewall (-3) Indicates that, when the owner of the TinBot is targeted by a Comms Attack, the Enemy applies a -3 Firewall MOD.

## FAQ v0.1

Q: If a Trooper's Hacking Device has a Firewall, when do the Firewall MODs apply?

A: The Firewall MODs apply to any Comms Attack made against the Trooper, unless their Hacking Device has been disabled, for example by being in Isolated State or any Null State.


## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
## What Firewall Represents in Gameplay
Firewall is the Quantronic equivalent of **Cover for Hacking**:  
it imposes offensive penalties on the attacker (–3 or –6 to WIP) while giving the defender a **+3 Saving Roll MOD** against Comms Attacks.
---

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
## Typical Interactions

### Firewall stacking limitations
- Even if a Trooper could benefit from several Firewall sources, **only ONE Firewall** is chosen and applied.  
- The owning player chooses which available Firewall to apply.

### Firewall and Repeater networks
- Hacking interactions can provide Firewall through specific rules such as Repeaters or Hacking Devices. Check the exact source rule rather than assuming all network positions provide the same Firewall value.


## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
## Comparative Analysis of Firewall Sources

### 1. **TinBot Firewall**  
**Pros:**  
- Common defensive source in Fireteams.  
- Passive while active.  

**Cons:**  
- Only as strong as the Firewall value printed for that source.  
- Protection can disappear when the rule providing it is disabled.

### 2. **Supportware / other Firewall sources**  
**Pros:**  
- Can protect vulnerable Hackable Troopers across a wider area or category, depending on the exact rule.  

**Cons:**  
- Requires the corresponding Skill/Equipment and remains subject to its cancellation conditions.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
## Pros and Cons of Firewall Overall

### **Pros**
- Strong defensive modifier against Comms Attacks.  
- Combines an attacker WIP penalty with a +3 Saving Roll MOD for the defender.  
- Only one Firewall applies, making its interaction straightforward once the available sources are identified.  

### **Cons**
- Does nothing against attacks that are not Comms Attacks.  
- Cannot stack multiple Firewalls.  
- Protection can disappear when the Equipment or rule providing it is disabled.  

---

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
## Common Interaction Questions

### **Q: Does Firewall help against Spotlight?**  
**A:** Yes. Spotlight is a Comms Attack, so the Firewall WIP MOD applies; apply the Saving Roll benefit only where the attack actually calls for a Saving Roll.

### **Q: Does Firewall work against Guided Attacks?**  
**A:** No. A Guided BS Attack is not itself a Comms Attack. Other dedicated defensive rules may apply separately.

### **Q: Does Firewall protect Hackers themselves?**  
**A:** Yes, whenever the Hacker is a Trooper benefitting from an active Firewall source.

---

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]
## Tactical Application Notes

- Firewalls materially change the odds of hacking interactions, especially around valuable Hackable Troopers.
- When evaluating a hacking attack, identify the exact active Firewall source and use only one Firewall.
- Under FAQ v0.1, a Firewall supplied by the target's Hacking Device applies broadly to Comms Attacks against that Trooper until the Device is disabled.
