# Idle

Common Skills
 BASIC SHORT SKILL
No Roll 

Common Skill that allows the user to execute no action at all.

- A Trooper that declares Idle performs no action. As such, its declaration just activates the Trooper, potentially generating AROs.

- Whenever a Trooper that received an Order in the Active Turn chooses not to perform an action with one of the two Short Skills of that Order, that Trooper is considered to declare Idle.

- In the Resolution Step of the Order, if a Trooper is found to have not met the Requirements of a declared Skill, they instead perform an Idle. In this situation: The ammunition of Disposable weapons or pieces of Equipment is spent. If the Trooper is in Marker form, it is revealed, and its Model is placed where the Marker was.

- The ammunition of Disposable weapons or pieces of Equipment is spent.

- If the Trooper is in Marker form, it is revealed, and its Model is placed where the Marker was.


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Idle has no Requirements and no Effects. However, it is still a declared Skill, and this matters a lot for Order structure, ARO timing, and interactions with other Skills.

A very common rules interaction is Move + Idle. This is the only way to perform “just Move” in an Order. Declaring Move as the first Short Skill and Idle as the second Short Skill is fully legal and frequently optimal. 

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
A classic “gotcha” interaction is declaring BS Attack without valid Line of Fire or declaring a Hacking Program without the target being in Hacking Area. When Requirements are checked, the Trooper does not perform the intended Skill and instead performs Idle. Any Burst, weapon choice, or ammo declaration is lost, and the Order effectively becomes weaker than intended.
