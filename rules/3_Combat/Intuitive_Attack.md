# Intuitive Attack

Combat Module

Common Skills
 LONG SKILL
BS Attack 

Intuitive Attack represents those times when a soldier perceives movement through the corner of his eye and instinctively sprays the area with his weapon in the hopes of unearthing a hidden enemy.

- The user must employ a BS Weapon with the Intuitive Attack Trait.

- The target of the Intuitive Attack (or Main Target if using a Template Weapon ) must be outside the user's LoF due to a Zero Visibility Zone, or be in a State that would normally prevent Attacks from being performed against them without previously being Discovered, such as the Camouflaged State.

- The user may perform a single BS Attack Roll against the target. The Burst (B) of this BS Attack will always be 1 regardless of the actual B value of the Weapon or any MODs applied to the B.

- In order to make an Intuitive Attack, the user must pass an unmodified WIP Roll. MODs from Partial Cover, Special Skills, pieces of Equipment or any other source do not apply to this Roll.

- If the target reacts by declaring an Attack or Dodge, his reaction is simultaneous and resolved by a Face to Face Roll, if applicable.

- If the user fails his WIP Roll, he cannot attempt to make another Intuitive Attack against the same target until his next Active Turn.

- If more than one enemy would be affected by the Intuitive Attack, the shooter must choose only one of them as the Main Target.

- If the WIP Roll is a Critical, it counts as a Critical against the Main Target of the attack, and only the Main Target. Against other targets affected by the Intuitive Attack, it is a successful Roll instead of a Critical.

- An Intuitive Attack must be declared in order to place a Deployable Weapon on the gaming table if there is an enemy Camouflaged Marker (CAMO) inside its Trigger Area. This restriction does not apply if there is a valid, non-camouflaged enemy Model inside the Trigger Area. If the WIP Roll is failed, the Deployable Weapon is not placed on the table and, if it also has the Disposable Trait, one use must be considered spent.

Trooper A declares an Intuitive Attack against the Camouflaged Marker [CAMO].

The Marker [CAMO] has two options:

- Declare nothing and not reveal itself unless Trooper A passes the required WIP Roll, in which case it will be hit by the template and be forced to make the corresponding Saving Roll, which would reveal it.

- Declare Dodge, BS Attack, etc. and oppose Trooper A’s WIP Roll with its ARO.

In this situation, if Trooper B was affected by the Template, his Roll would oppose Trooper A’s WIP Roll.

## FAQs

Q: Do Skills that let you perform a 'BS Attack Roll' (for example Intuitive Attack and Speculative Attack), or have the BS Attack Label, count as a BS Attack for MODs? For example, if the Trooper has 'BS Attack (SR-1)', 'BS Attack (+1 SD), or 'BS Attack (AP)'.

A: Yes. Note that +1 SD MODs don't apply to Long Skills.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Intuitive Attack is often misunderstood as a “Discover replacement”, but it is not. It is an Attack declaration that happens to use WIP as a gate check. Passing the WIP Roll does not Discover the target first; it directly enables the Attack to resolve normally. This distinction matters because effects that trigger “on being Discovered” do not apply, and because failure locks you out of retrying that same target until your next Active Turn.

A very common mistake is applying MODs to the WIP Roll. The WIP Roll for Intuitive Attack is always unmodified: no Cover, no Mimetism, no Visibility Zones, no Surprise, no Range. 
The MODs are applied after the WIP roll, when the attack is now permitted.

The Burst is always 1, even in the Active Turn and even if the weapon normally has higher Burst or Fireteam bonuses. Fireteam Burst bonuses (because it's a long skill, SD don't apply), +B from Skills, and similar effects are ignored. This makes Intuitive Attack better used with powerful low-burst weapons.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
When used against Camouflaged Markers, the reactive player has a meaningful choice. If they do nothing, they risk being hit if the WIP Roll succeeds. If they Dodge or Attack, they reveal and enter a Face to Face Roll. This creates a psychological pressure point where the Active Player can force reveals on unfavorable terms.

Failing the WIP Roll is more punishing than it looks. You cannot attempt another Intuitive Attack against the same target until your next Active Turn, even if another trooper tries. This effectively “protects” the camo marker for the rest of the turn and can stall an attack run if you rely on a single Intuitive-capable piece.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Intuitive Attack is mandatory when placing Deployable Weapons if a Camouflaged Marker is inside the Trigger Area. This is an easily missed restriction. You cannot “sneak” a mine past a camo marker without rolling WIP, and failure both cancels the placement and still consumes a Disposable use. This makes careless mine placement surprisingly costly.

Impersonation is explicitly protected from Intuitive Attack. IMP-1 and IMP-2 markers cannot be Intuitively Attacked at all; you must Discover first. Players often assume Intuitive Attack bypasses all marker defenses, but Impersonation is the hard exception.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Zero Visibility Zones enable Intuitive Attack but do not otherwise interact with it. You still do not apply Visibility MODs to the WIP Roll, and the attack can be declared even without LoF. This makes Intuitive Attack a direct counter to smoke or eclipse-based defense when using suitable weapons.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Strategically, Intuitive Attack is best used as an order-efficiency tool, not a primary gunfighting method. One successful Intuitive template weapon can remove  threats, mines, or hidden roadblocks with a single Order, whereas repeated Discover + Shoot sequences are far more order-intensive.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
High-WIP warbands, specialists, and midfield skirmishers gain disproportionate value from Intuitive Attack. Units that already want to be close (chain rifles, nanopulsers, E/M templates) can convert WIP into guaranteed pressure against camo-heavy defenses.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Tactically, Intuitive Attack pairs extremely well with board control. Forcing enemies into camo states near objectives, chokepoints, or corners allows you to trade a single Order for area denial removal, especially late in the turn when Discover attempts are risky.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
A subtle but important interaction: because Intuitive Attack is still a BS Attack, effects that modify BS Attacks apply to the Attack roll itself, not the WIP Roll. Ammo traits, States inflicted, and special ammunition all work normally once the WIP gate is passed.
