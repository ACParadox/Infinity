# Spotlight

Quantronic Combat (Hacking)
 SHORT SKILL / ARO
Comms Attack, Non-Lethal. 

- In the Active Turn, this Program's Burst of 2 allows the user to perform two WIP Rolls, which can be distributed among one or more targets as usual.

- Each successful Roll, due to AP Ammunition, forces the target to make a Saving Roll against their halved BTS, with PS 5.

- Any failed Saving Roll causes the target to enter Targeted State, placing a Targeted State Token (TARGETED) by them.

- A Critical with Spotlight forces its target to perform an additional Saving Roll, applying their halved BTS value.

- The range of this Program is the Hacker 's Hacking Area.

The target of this Hacking Program does not need to have the Hackable Characteristic.

The target of a Hacking Program or a Comms Attack can declare a Reset, regardless of their Troop Type (LI, MI, HI, etc.), and even if this Hacking Program or Comms Attack is performed from outside their LoF.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
A common mistake is forgetting that Spotlight is a **Comms Attack** and not a BS or CC Attack. This matters for ARO eligibility, interaction with Marker States, and effects that specifically reference Attacks vs Comms Attacks. 
Troopers affected by Spotlight do not suffer damage directly, but the resulting **Targeted State** dramatically changes how other attacks interact with them.

Spotlight is the only Hacking attack that can target all types of units, which keeps hacker relevant versus armies with small amount of Hackable units.


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Spotlight can be declared in **ARO**. This allows reactive players to impose the Targeted State without winning a gunfight. Even a sacrificial Hacker can trade itself to mark a key enemy piece, enabling devastating follow-up attacks later in the Turn or even by other Troopers.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Targeted State does not require Line of Fire to exploit. Once a Trooper is Targeted, **Guided Attacks**, indirect fire, and certain mission effects can affect the target regardless of LoF. This makes Spotlight especially oppressive against units that rely on strong positioning, total cover, or visual defenses.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Spotlight bypasses many defensive layers indirectly:
- Mimetism, Cover, and poor Range Bands become irrelevant for Guided Attacks.
- High ARM or BTS is often less relevant when multiple Normal Rolls are applied repeatedly.
Because of this, Spotlight is often stronger against elite, expensive models than against cheap warbands.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
One easily missed interaction is that **Reset** is often the only meaningful defense against Spotlight in ARO. If the target lacks good WIP or is already under pressure from other hacking effects, Spotlight can land surprisingly reliably even through repeat attempts.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Spotlight synergizes extremely well with **Order efficiency**. Spending one Order to Spotlight can translate into multiple Orders of value later, especially if the opponent is forced to:
- Spend Orders Resetting
- Reposition the affected Trooper
- Accept losses from Guided or easier attacks

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Against Marker-heavy armies, Spotlight acts as a *post-discovery punishment*. Even if the opponent successfully advances under Camouflage or similar protections, once revealed they can be immediately punished without needing favorable fire lanes.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Spotlight strongly rewards **networked play**. A backline Hacker can mark a target that a completely different Trooper exploits. This decoupling of risk (Hacker) and reward (attacker) is one of the core strategic strengths of the program.


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
From a tactical perspective, Spotlight is often best used:
- Against a powerful unit that will likely take many BS shots to hit.
- Reactively to deter aggressive advances
- On high-value, low-WIP Troopers who cannot easily Reset
- In conjunction with Guided Missile
- On a unit with Camouflage, Targeted State's MOD help Discover it
- Targeted State disables Stealth

It is usually inefficient to Spotlight expendable warbands unless mission objectives specifically reward it.
For hackable units, it's often preferable to use Carbonite or Oblivion.


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
In summary, Spotlight is not a damage program—it is a **force multiplier**. Its true value lies in how it amplifies other rules, weapons, and objectives, and how it converts a single successful roll into sustained strategic pressure across the Turn or the entire Round.
