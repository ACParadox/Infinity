# Enhanced Reaction

Quantronic Combat (Hacking)
 LONG SKILL
Supportware, No Roll. 

- The target must be a REM from the same Army List as the user and must be inside the user's Hacking Area.

- Enhanced Reaction grants the target REM a Burst value of 2 in ARO.

- When the user activates this Program, which does not require a Roll, place a Supportware Enhanced Reaction Token (Sup: Enhanced Reaction) beside the user, and beside the REM.

- Once activated, this Program's range covers the entire game table.

SUPPORTWARE

- A Trooper can benefit from the effects of only one Program with the Supportware Label at a time.

- Each Hacker can only sustain one active Supportware Program at a time.

- A Hacker can cancel an active Supportware Program by declaring another Program and expending the corresponding Order or ARO.

- A Supportware Program is automatically cancelled if the target of the Supportware is targeted by a new Supportware Program, or if the Hacker running the Program enters Isolated State or any Null States.

During their Active Turn, a Player that has two Troopers with EVO Hacking Devices on the table, declares Fairy Dust with one of them and chooses REMs as the Troop Type that will benefit from this Supportware.

For tactical reasons, they decide to spend the last Order on the other Trooper with an EVO Hacking Device to declare Enhanced Reaction on a REM located in the vanguard.

By designating a REM that was already benefitting from Fairy Dust, the new Supportware Program deactivates the Fairy Dust Program and the corresponding Token is removed from the table.

A Supportware Program is automatically cancelled if the target of the Supportware is targeted by a new Supportware Program, or if the Hacker running the Program enters Isolated State or any Null States.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Supportware: Enhanced Reaction is often misunderstood as a general Burst bonus. It is not. Its effect is extremely narrow: it only modifies the Burst value of the target REM when declaring a BS Attack in ARO. It does nothing in the Active Turn and does not interact with any non-BS AROs such as Dodge, Reset, or Guts Rolls.

Because ARO Burst is normally fixed at B1, players sometimes forget to apply Enhanced Reaction at all, or incorrectly apply it in the Active Turn. Always remember that this Supportware exists solely to break the default ARO Burst restriction for REMs.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Enhanced Reaction stacks with effects that add Special Dice but does not convert them into Burst. If the REM benefits from a rule that grants +1 Special Die, that die is resolved normally in ARO alongside the increased Burst from Enhanced Reaction. However, the Supportware itself does not grant Special Dice, only raw Burst.

This distinction matters in Face to Face Rolls, because Burst increases the number of opposing dice, while Special Dice improve result selection but do not increase cancellation power in the same way.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Enhanced Reaction is cancelled when another Supportware program is successfully applied to the same target, or when the Hacker maintaining it enters a Null State or otherwise loses the ability to sustain Supportware. This creates a subtle but important vulnerability: attacking the Hacker can indirectly neutralize a powerful ARO piece without ever engaging it directly.

This interaction is often overlooked by newer players, who focus exclusively on killing the REM instead of removing the Supportware source.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
A tactical nuance: Enhanced Reaction strongly favors high-quality REM weapons with good range bands and ammo types, rather than cheap defensive pieces. A Burst increase in ARO is far more impactful on a REM with strong BS, good range coverage, and access to Cover than on a lightly-armed perimeter defender.

As a result, the program rewards deliberate lane control and overwatch positioning rather than reactive trading at short range.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Enhanced Reaction synergizes extremely well with layered defenses. When combined with other ARO tax elements such as Repeaters, Mines, or difficult terrain, the opponent is forced to face a higher-dice Face to Face Roll while already suffering MOD pressure. The value is not just in inflicting wounds, but in Order drain.

Even if the REM fails to hit, forcing multiple Face to Face Rolls against increased Burst can rapidly exhaust enemy resources.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
From a strategic perspective, Enhanced Reaction is best viewed as a tempo control tool rather than a killing tool. Its purpose is to slow advances, protect firelanes during the early game, and force inefficient attack vectors. Over-investing in keeping the REM alive late game can be a mistake once its area denial role has been fulfilled.

In many games, the correct play is to accept that the REM will eventually be removed, as long as it has already consumed enough Orders to justify the Supportware investment.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Finally, remember that Enhanced Reaction is predictable Open Information once declared. Experienced opponents will plan around it with smoke, hacking, White Noise, or indirect attack methods. Its true strength lies in forcing those counters early, shaping the flow of the turn before the first Order is even spent.

Also, since it needs to be applied first before being active, this means it won't protect against a first-turn alpha strike.
