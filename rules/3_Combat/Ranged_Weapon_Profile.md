# Ranged Weapon Profile

Combat Module

In Infinity, every Weapon has its own game profile and some of them even have special usage rules. This chapter describes how to read such a profile, and each Weapon is detailed individually in the Weapons Chart. The MULTI Rifle will be used as an example to explain how to read a BS Weapon profile.

## MULTI Rifle

| Name | 8" | 16" | 24" | 32" | 40" | 48" | 96" | PS | B | Ammo | Save Attr. | Save Rolls | Traits |

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| MULTI Rifle (Anti-materiel Mode) | +3 | +3 | -3 | -3 | -6 | -6 | -- | 7 | 1 | DA | ARM | 2 | Anti-materiel |

| MULTI Rifle (AP Mode) | +3 | +3 | -3 | -3 | -6 | -6 | -- | 7 | 3 | AP | ARM/2 | 1 | Suppressive Fire |

| MULTI Rifle (Shock Mode) | +3 | +3 | -3 | -3 | -6 | -6 | -- | 7 | 3 | SHOCK | ARM | 1 | Suppressive Fire |

MULTI Weapons are an example of Weapons that have more than one firing mode. Each firing mode has a Burst (B) and different Ammunition associated with them. The player must decide which firing mode to use each time they declare a BS Attack.

The MULTI Rifle in AP Mode can use AP Ammunition and B3. This means that if the player declares BS Attack, they will roll 3 dice with AP Ammunition. Conversely, if they decide to use Anti-materiel Mode they will have just 1 die available, but will use DA Ammunition.

## Profile Key

Range indicates the Weapon's reach in inches. In this case the MULTI Rifle has a reach of 0 to 48 inches, this reach is divided in different ranges that imply different MODs to the BS Attack Roll, as explained in the Range Modifier section.

Possibility of Survival (PS) indicates the destructive capability of the Weapon. The lower the PS value, the more powerful the Weapon.

Burst (B) is the number of dice the Active Player must roll when declaring a BS Attack. Remember that in the Reactive Turn the value of B is 1, unless modified by a rule or Skill.

The Ammunition column indicates what Type of Ammunition the Weapon uses. If two Types of Ammunition are shown linked by a "+" it means both Types are combined.

The Saving Roll Attribute column indicates which Attribute or Attributes ( ARM, BTS, PH, ARM+BTS, etc.) any Saving Rolls are made against to avoid the hit's damage or Effects. As with Ammunition, saving against different Attributes or their combination may be necessary (see Possibility of Survival (PS) ). The Attribute can also be shown with MODs, usually negative (-3, -6), or it can directly show the value for that Attribute (ARM=0).

The Number of Saving Rolls (SR: No) column indicates the number of Saving Rolls that need to be made for every hit received.

Traits are special features some Weapons and pieces of Equipment possess. They are all explained in the Labels and Traits Section. An asterisk (*) in this section indicates that the Weapon or piece of Equipment has extra rules in the corresponding section of the rulebook.

A weapon that uses AP + DA Ammunition indicates that the target must perform 2 Saving Rolls for each hit received (due to DA Ammunition), with the corresponding Saving Roll Attribute halved (due to AP Ammunition).

Although in the Traits section the [***] indicates that it is a weapon or piece of Equipment that has additional explanation in the Skills and Equipment section of the rulebook.



**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Range Bands are evaluated at the exact point from which the Attack is declared. If the attacker moves and shoots, the chosen firing point along the movement path determines the Range MOD. This allows deliberate “range fishing”: stepping forward or backward a few millimeters to flip a −3 into a +3, even if the final model position is different.

Strategically, this makes micro-positioning during Move + BS Attack extremely powerful, especially with weapons that have narrow optimal bands (e.g. sniper-class weapons).

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Burst listed in a Ranged Weapon Profile represents the *maximum dice available in the Active Turn only*. In ARO, Burst is reduced to 1 unless explicitly modified by a Skill or Equipment. Players sometimes mistakenly apply weapon Burst values in ARO or forget that Special Dice bonuses do not increase Burst.

Tactically, high-Burst weapons are order-efficient in the Active Turn but gain very little defensive value in ARO unless paired with Fireteam bonuses or specific reactive skills.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Dividing Burst between multiple targets is declared upfront and all shots originate from the same point. If later checks reveal that some dice do not meet Requirements (invalid LoF, incorrect range, etc.), those dice are simply lost.

This makes speculative or over-ambitious Burst splitting risky. A common expert tactic is to assign the minimum Burst needed to secondary targets and keep the majority focused on the primary Face to Face Roll.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Ammunition Types listed in the weapon profile fundamentally change the value of each successful hit. Critical effects, number of Saving Rolls, and interaction with Cover are all driven by Ammunition, not by the base weapon.

A frequent mistake is assuming AP affects Cover. In N5, Cover modifies the Saving Roll directly, not ARM, so AP does not reduce the Cover bonus. This makes multi-save Ammunition (e.g. forcing multiple Saves) often more reliable than armor-reduction effects.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Save Attribute and number of Saving Rolls are part of the weapon profile, not optional details. Some weapons bypass ARM entirely and target BTS, which can completely invert matchup expectations.

Strategically, this encourages list-building with mixed damage vectors. A force relying only on ARM-based weapons may struggle against high-BTS targets, while a single specialist weapon can dramatically shift engagements.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Traits on Ranged Weapon Profiles are easy to overlook but often define advanced use cases. Traits like Suppressive Fire, Direct Template, Deployable, or Non-Lethal fundamentally alter how the weapon interacts with Orders, AROs, and States.

Expert play involves selecting weapons not only for lethality but for *board control*. For example, a weapon with Suppressive Fire is often chosen for its end-of-turn area denial rather than its raw Damage.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Weapons using PH or WIP instead of BS still follow all normal BS Attack rules unless explicitly excluded. Range MODs, Cover, Mimetism, and Face to Face mechanics all apply normally, with only the Attribute swapped.

This is frequently misunderstood and leads to illegal MOD stacking or missed penalties. Tactically, these weapons shine when mounted on Troopers with strong secondary Attributes, enabling unexpected attack vectors.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Maximum Burst is capped at 6, regardless of modifiers. Extra Special Dice do not count toward this cap. This prevents extreme dice pools but still rewards quality over quantity.

From a tactical perspective, once you approach the Burst cap, improving MODs (range, visibility, enemy penalties) is more impactful than chasing additional dice.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Ranged Weapon Profiles should always be read in conjunction with the Order Expenditure Sequence. Declaring weapon, ammunition, targets, Burst division, and firing point is mandatory at declaration, not resolution.

Experienced players use this transparency to pressure opponents into suboptimal AROs, especially when the declared profile implies lethal ammunition even if the actual odds are slim.
