# White Noise

Quantronic Combat (Hacking)
 SHORT SKILL
NFB 

- To use this this Hacking Program, the Hacker must be the Active Trooper.

- The White Noise Hacking Program generates a White Noise Zone the size of a Circular Template and with infinite height.

- On a successful Normal WIP Roll, the Hacker may place the Circular Template representing the White Noise Zone inside their Hacking Area at the end of the Order.

- The Circular Template must be totally inside the Hacking Area.

- The effects of this Program persist until the start of the States Phase, at which point you must remove the Circular Template.

- While the Circular Template is on the game table, the Program's NFB Label applies to the Hacker, so they cannot use or apply any other Skill, piece of Equipment, or Hacking Program that has the NFB Label, for example Mimetism, Albedo, Holoprojector etc.

- The range of this Program is the Hacker's Hacking Area.

Troopers possessing a Multispectral Visor or Marksmanship cannot draw LoF through a White Noise Zone.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
White Noise creates a **White Noise Zone**, which functions as a **Zero Visibility Zone** but **only** for Troopers equipped with **Multispectral Visors (any Level)**, **Marksmanship**, or other equipment explicitly affected by White Noise.  

This selective nature is the single most misunderstood aspect of White Noise: it does **not** block Line of Fire universally.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Because the White Noise Zone is still a Zero Visibility Zone for affected Troopers, those Troopers:
- Cannot draw LoF **into**, **out of**, or **through** the Zone.
- Cannot declare BS Attacks or other LoF-dependent AROs through it.
- Are effectively blind to targets protected by the Zone, even at point-blank range, if LoF would cross the Zone.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Troopers **without** Multispectral Visors or Marksmanship:
- Are completely unaffected by White Noise.
- Can draw LoF normally through the Zone.
- Can declare BS Attacks and AROs as if the Zone did not exist.

This creates asymmetrical fire lanes where some Troopers can shoot freely while others are entirely shut down.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
White Noise does **not** impose MODs; it is binary:
- Either the Trooper is affected and cannot see at all.
- Or the Trooper is unaffected and ignores the Zone completely.

This means abilities like Mimetism, Cover MODs, or Range Bands are irrelevant if the Trooper cannot draw LoF in the first place.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
White Noise does not stop:
- Movement through the Zone.
- Dodge declarations (if valid by other means, such as templates or ZoC).
- Non-LoF attacks that do not require visibility.

However, affected Troopers moving into a White Noise Zone may find themselves unable to declare meaningful AROs afterward.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
A key interaction: **Templates**
- Direct Template Weapons can still be declared **if** their Requirements are met without drawing LoF.
- However, many Template declarations still require LoF to the target point; White Noise can indirectly prevent legal template placement for affected Troopers.

This often causes table disputes and should be clarified before rolling dice.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
White Noise is especially powerful against:
- MSV ARO pieces relying on long fire lanes.
- Linked Fireteams using MSV gunners.
- Snipers anchoring deployment zones.

By selectively disabling these pieces, White Noise can open attack routes without needing smoke.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Unlike Smoke:
- White Noise does **not** protect MSV users.
- White Noise actively **punishes** MSV reliance.

This makes White Noise a soft counter to lists that over-invest in visual superiority.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Strategic use: **Order Efficiency**
White Noise often trades a single Hacking Order for:
- Multiple denied AROs.
- Forced repositioning by enemy MSV Troopers.
- Safe movement for key attack pieces.

This makes it one of the most order-efficient hacking tools when properly positioned.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Strategic use: **Fireteam Disruption**
If a Fireteam’s main gunner has MSV:
- White Noise can neutralize the Team’s offensive and defensive output without breaking the Fireteam.
- Non-MSV members may still ARO, but often with inferior weapons or positioning.

This creates a false sense of safety for the Fireteam owner.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
White Noise Zones are often strongest when placed:
- Slightly forward of objectives.
- Across diagonal fire lanes rather than straight corridors.
- In positions that force MSV Troopers to either idle or move blindly.

Overextending the Zone can reduce its impact by allowing enemies to simply go around it.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Common mistake:
Players assume White Noise is “Smoke for hackers.”  
It is not.

Smoke:
- Blocks LoF universally.
- Is bypassed by MSV.

White Noise:
- Blocks LoF selectively.
- Specifically targets MSV and similar abilities.

Treat it as a **precision denial tool**, not a general defense.
