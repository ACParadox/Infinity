# Fairy Dust

Quantronic Combat (Hacking)
 LONG SKILL
Supportware, No Roll. 

- Only HIs, REMs, TAGs, or VHs from the same Army List as the user may be chosen as targets.

- Fairy Dust allows the Player to choose a single Troop Type (HI, REM, TAG, or VH) that will benefit from Firewall (-3). Every Trooper of the Hacker 's Army List that belongs to the chosen Troop Type applies the corresponding MODs every time they suffer a Comms Attack.

- When the user activates this Program, which does not require a Roll, place a Fairy Dust Token (SUP: FAIRYDUST) beside them.

- This Program's range covers the entire game table.

SUPPORTWARE

- A Trooper can benefit from the effects of only one Program with the Supportware Label at a time.

- Each Hacker can only sustain one active Supportware Program at a time.

- A Hacker can cancel an active Supportware Program by declaring another Program and expending the corresponding Order or ARO.

- A Supportware Program is automatically cancelled if the target of the Supportware is targeted by a new Supportware Program, or if the Hacker running the Program enters Isolated State or any Null States.

During their Active Turn, a Player that has two Troopers with EVO Hacking Devices on the table, declares Fairy Dust with one of them and chooses REMs as the Troop Type that will benefit from this Supportware.

For tactical reasons, they decide to spend the last Order on the other Trooper with an EVO Hacking Device to declare Enhanced Reaction on a REM located in the vanguard.

By designating a REM that was already benefitting from Fairy Dust, the new Supportware Program deactivates the Fairy Dust Program and the corresponding Token is removed from the table.

A Supportware Program is automatically cancelled if the target of the Supportware is targeted by a new Supportware Program, or if the Hacker running the Program enters Isolated State or any Null States.


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Fairy Dust is a *global, passive defensive modifier* rather than a targeted buff. A common mistake is thinking it must be “applied” to individual Troopers like Assisted Fire or Enhanced Reaction. Instead, once declared, *every Trooper of the chosen Troop Type in the Army List* benefits simultaneously, regardless of position, Orders spent, or proximity to the EVO Hacker.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Fairy Dust only applies when a Trooper *suffers a Comms Attack*. It does nothing against:
- BS Attacks (including Guided)
- CC Attacks
- Template weapons
- E/M weapons that do not use a Comms Attack
This makes Fairy Dust a **meta-dependent defense** rather than a universal protection layer.

The Firewall granted by Fairy Dust does not stack with other Firewall sources. If a Trooper has access to multiple Firewall providers (TinBot, Fairy Dust, enemy Repeater penalties), only a single Firewall applies. Players must be careful not to assume cumulative MODs.

Fairy Dust applies equally to Hackers and non-Hackers of the chosen Troop Type. A Heavy Infantry Hacker protected by Fairy Dust gains the same benefits as a non-Hacker HI when targeted by Oblivion, Spotlight, or Total Control.

Because Fairy Dust has table-wide range and requires no Roll, it am order-efficient defensive programs. One Order can meaningfully alter every future hacking interaction until cancelled.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Supportware exclusivity is a frequent source of rules errors. If *any* Trooper benefiting from Fairy Dust becomes the target of another Supportware Program, Fairy Dust is immediately cancelled for *all Troopers*, not just that model. This includes common EVO actions such as Enhanced Reaction or Assisted Fire.

Multiple EVO Hackers do not allow overlapping Fairy Dust effects. Each Hacker may sustain only one Supportware Program, and each Trooper may benefit from only one Supportware at a time. Running two EVOs provides redundancy and flexibility, not stacking protection.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
If the EVO Hacker running Fairy Dust enters Isolated or any Null State, Fairy Dust immediately ends. This creates a *single-point-of-failure* vulnerability: protecting the EVO Hacker is as important as protecting the units benefiting from Fairy Dust.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Fairy Dust is especially strong in lists with:
- Multiple HI Fireteams
- TAG-centric strategies
- REM-heavy gun platforms (although this means Assisted Fire or Enhanced Reaction cannot be applied too)
In these cases, Fairy Dust functions as an *army-wide tax* on enemy hacking Orders, often forcing opponents to abandon hacking lines entirely.

Against low-Burst hacking programs, the combination of Firewall MODs and the +3 BTS bonus often flips favorable hacking math into unreliable territory. This is particularly impactful against Oblivion and Spotlight spam strategies.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Strategically, Fairy Dust encourages *aggressive board presence* by otherwise hack-vulnerable units. TAGs and HI can advance into midfield hacking zones with reduced risk, forcing the opponent to rely on kinetic solutions instead of infowar.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Because Fairy Dust is declared as a Long Skill and does not require LoF or range checks, it is often best activated early in the Turn before committing valuable units. Delaying Fairy Dust until after exposure frequently results in preventable losses.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Psychological impact matters: experienced opponents often overestimate Firewall protection and avoid hacking altogether. This indirect pressure can be as valuable as the numerical MODs, shaping enemy movement and Order expenditure.

In summary, Fairy Dust is not a reactive trick or a situational buff—it is a *strategic posture choice*. When used correctly, it reshapes the hacking game for an entire Turn and forces opponents to fight on less favorable terms.

Also, since it needs to be applied first before being active, this means it won't protect against a first-turn alpha strike.
