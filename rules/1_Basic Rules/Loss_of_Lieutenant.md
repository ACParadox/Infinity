# Loss of Lieutenant

Despite their training and their constant connection with Mission Control, losing a team leader to enemy fire or system failure can wreak havoc on the discipline of soldiers, creating a moment of confusion in which everyone is left to their own devices, without a coordinated tactical approach. This situation is usually temporary, and order reigns again once the next rung in the chain of command steps up or Mission Control chooses a new leader.

- If, during the Tactical Phase of the Active Turn, the Active Player lacks a Lieutenant because they did not deploy it on the game table (as a Model or as a Marker) or the previous Lieutenant is currently in a Null ( Unconscious, Dead, Sepsitorized...) or Isolated State, then a Loss of Lieutenant situation is automatically declared.

- While an army is in a Loss of Lieutenant situation, all Troopers in its Army List are Irregular.

- At the end of the player’s Turn, they must appoint a new Lieutenant, at no cost, which cancels the Loss of Lieutenant situation. The new Lieutenant gains the Lieutenant Special Skill, with no additional abilities. The identity of this new Lieutenant is still considered Private Information.

Troopers whose Unit Profile has the Irregular Characteristic or the REM Troop Type cannot be appointed as Lieutenant.

Chain of Command

- This Special Skill can only be used at the start of the player's Loss of Lieutenant check.


- The user automatically becomes the new Lieutenant of the Army List, gaining the Lieutenant Special Skill with no extra abilities.

- The Loss of Lieutenant check is successful this Turn.

Mnemonica

- In order to activate this Special Skill, the user must enter a Null State.

- The user may transfer his own WIP Attribute value and the Mnemonica Special Skill to another Trooper in the same army. That Trooper must be on the battlefield in the form of a Model or Marker and have either a Cube or the REM Troop Type. If the user has the Lieutenant Special Skill this is also transferred, including any additional abilities and MODs such as (+1 Order).

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
A Loss of Lieutenant (LoL) situation occurs automatically at the start of the player’s Tactical Phase if **no Lieutenant is deployed on the table**, or the previous Lieutenant is in a **Null** or **Isolated** State. Remember that **Hidden Deployment counts as “not deployed,”** so a Lieutenant in Hidden Deployment will trigger LoL.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Loss of Lieutenant does **not** happen immediately when the Lieutenant enters a Null or Isolated State. It is only checked at the Tactical Phase of the player’s next Turn. This creates cases where a Lieutenant can die during the opponent’s turn, but the army still functions normally for the remainder of that Round.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
While in Loss of Lieutenant, *all Troopers in the Army List become Irregular*.  
This means:
- Regular Order Pool becomes **zero** until a new Lieutenant is appointed.
- Troopers now all generate Irregular Orders only.
- Tactical Orders (Tactical Awareness) still exist, because they are generated by specific skills, not by Regular/Irregular status.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
At the end of the player’s Turn, a new Lieutenant is appointed “for free.” This cancels Loss of Lieutenant automatically. However, the newly appointed Lieutenant’s identity remains **Private Information**, just like the original Lieutenant.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Troopers with **the REM Troop Type** or who have the **Irregular Characteristic** **cannot** be appointed as the new Lieutenant. This can create difficult edge cases if most remaining Troopers are REMs.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Loss of Lieutenant only affects **Orders**, not AROs. A Trooper that becomes Irregular still has full ARO functionality.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
A Trooper that is Isolated during the LoL check also cannot be appointed Lieutenant, because Isolated Troopers generate only Irregular Orders and cannot participate in the Regular Order Pool.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
If a Trooper with **Chain of Command (CoC)** is on the table, CoC triggers **immediately at the start of the Loss of Lieutenant check**, preventing Loss of Lieutenant entirely.  
This means:
- The Order Pool is not disrupted.
- The Turn proceeds as normal.
- CoC activation is **not optional**; the skill automatically promotes the CoC Trooper.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
If multiple CoC Troopers exist you must reveal privately which one is used, but only one becomes Lieutenant.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
**Mnemonica** interacts uniquely with Loss of Lieutenant.  
Because Mnemonica transfers WIP and the Lieutenant Special Skill *at the moment the user enters a Null State*, you can:
- Save the Lieutenant role before the Tactical Phase check.
- Prevent Loss of Lieutenant as long as the transfer target is valid (on the table, Cube or REM, not in Null).
This can occur during *either player’s* turn.

Mnemonica can preserve **extra Lieutenant abilities** (e.g., Lieutenant +1 Order), unlike Chain of Command, which only grants a “basic” Lieutenant with no extras.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
It is possible for an army to enter a situation where **no eligible model remains to be appointed Lieutenant**.  
In this case:
- Loss of Lieutenant persists indefinitely.
- All Troopers remain Irregular every Turn.
- The game does not end automatically; this is a soft-lock on Order Pool efficiency, not an end-state.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
A Lieutenant in a Marker State (Camouflaged, Impersonation, etc.) does not reveal itself when the army enters or exits Loss of Lieutenant. Lieutenant identity is always Private Information unless explicitly revealed by a rule.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Loss of Lieutenant does not affect Fireteams directly, but because all members become Irregular:
- Fireteam members cannot contribute Regular Orders.
- Fireteams still function normally as long as someone spends their own Irregular Order or provided Tactical Orders.
This often makes Fireteam repositioning slow and order-starved.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
A Trooper in Hidden Deployment cannot be appointed Lieutenant at the end of the Turn unless it first appears as a Model or Marker. Private Information does not override the requirement of being physically present on the table.
