# Open and Private Information

Information about Army Lists in an Infinity game can be either Open or Private.

Consider any piece of information on a player's Army List that is not explicitly Private as Open and knowable to all. You must share Open Information about your Army List while you deploy your Models, Markers and Tokens during the Deployment Phase, and also any time your opponent asks during the game.

Private Information is information you can keep to yourself that your opponent cannot ask about. Your Private Information remains secret until a specific game event forces you to disclose it.

You are required to write down or record all Private Information before the game starts to show your opponent if necessary.

These pieces of information are Private in Infinity:

- Your TroopersCost and SWC.

- The presence of Troopers possessing the Combat Jump or Parachutist Special Skill.

- The presence of Hidden Deployment Troopers.

- The presence of troopers disguised by HoloMask.

- The identity of Troops with Chain of Command or Counterintelligence.

- The contents of your Markers.

- The identity of your Lieutenant.

When the game ends, an Army List 's Private Information becomes Open Information.

## FAQs

Q: Which Automatic Skills or Equipment have to be declared when in a Marker State?

A: Automatic Skills and Equipment that alter how an Order activates a Trooper, or restrict AROs, must be declared when used in Marker State. For example using Stealth.


## INTERACTION: PRIVATE INFORMATION THAT BECOMES OPEN WHEN REVEALED

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
A common mistake is thinking that you can “declare anything” while in a Marker
state and only reveal afterward. This is false. Requirements are checked at
Resolution, but illegal declarations lead to Idle, revealing the Trooper and
possibly wasting Disposable equipment.

## INTERACTION: DISCOVER + SHOOT AND PRIVATE INFORMATION TIMING

Discover does NOT reveal Private Information unless it succeeds.
If Discover fails:
- The Marker remains a Marker.
- No Open Information changes.
'
If Discover succeeds:
- The Trooper is revealed immediately.
- All Open Information for that Trooper becomes public.
'
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Remember that failing Discover does *not* entitle the discovering player to
know anything new (weapons, skills, cost, SWC, etc.). They only know the
opposite: “Whatever that Marker is, it still has a legal Marker state.”

'
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Because the opponent cannot know how many Hidden/AD Troopers you have,
the Order Pool may look “too small.” This is intentional. Concealment of the
real force size is a key strategic tool.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Some interactions reveal *only* identity, not profile details.
Example: A Camo Marker is discovered and becomes a Model.
The opponent learns:
- What Trooper it is
- All Open Information (weapons, stats, visible equipment)
But does NOT learn:
- Hidden SWC or points cost
- Whether it has Chain of Command
- Whether it is the Lieutenant


## INTERACTION: LIEUTENANT IDENTITY AND DECLARING THE LT ORDER

The Lieutenant identity is Private Information.
However:
- Spending the Lieutenant Special Order is Open Information.

This creates a subtle rules interaction:
If a player spends the Lieutenant Order, the opponent will know which unit is the lieutenant, because Lieutenant Orders are only for the Lieutenant 's own use.
The exception to this is: 
1) Using NCO to transform the Lieutenant Order to a Tactical Order for the NCO trooper.
2) Using Strategos to convert the Lieutenant Order to a Regular Order.
3) Decide not to spend the Lieutenant Order to avoid revealing him.


## INTERACTION: HOLOMASK AND OPEN INFORMATION

HoloMask allows a Trooper to present false Open Information.
'
Example:
- The Trooper pretends to be a Fusilier.
- All Open Information is replaced with the fake profile’s information.
- Private Information includes the truth: the Trooper is something else.
'
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
A tricky nuance is that the fake Trooper’s SWC and cost are considered Open
Information — but only for the fake identity. The real Trooper's values remain
Private until the HoloMask breaks. This means your public army list math may
appear "wrong," and that is expected in Infinity.


## INTERACTION: CHAIN OF COMMAND AND REVEALING PRIVATE INFORMATION

Chain of Command is Private Information unless it triggers.
When it triggers (Loss of Lieutenant):
- The Chain of Command Trooper becomes the new Lieutenant.
- This identity does NOT become Open Information.
'
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Many players mistakenly believe that using Chain of Command reveals the model.
It does not. The skill operates entirely in the background.

## INTERACTION: COST AND SWC REMAIN PRIVATE UNTIL GAME END

Even when a Trooper is revealed during the battle, its Cost and SWC remain
Private Information until the game ends.
'
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
This means opponents cannot verify list legality mid-game. Infinity assumes
trust and post-game validation rather than real-time auditing.


## INTERACTION: PRIVATE INFORMATION DURING DEPLOYMENT (MINELAYER, DECOY, HOLOECHOS)

When deploying effects that depend on ZoC or Coherency measurements:
- The opponent MUST look away.
- The measurement is legal even though the Marker contents are private.
'
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
This protects hidden identities (e.g., Minelayer vs non-Minelayer profiles)
and ensures that deployment deception tools function correctly without
leaking unintended information.


## INTERACTION: REVEALING PRIVATE INFO AT GAME END

When the game ends, all Private Information becomes Open:
- Costs
- SWC
- Hidden Deployment unit identities
- Lieutenant identity
'
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
This allows players to confirm legal lists, validate scoring, and learn from
the opponent’s hidden elements for future games.


## SUMMARY (UNOFFICIAL)

Open and Private Information is not only about secrecy but about timing.
Infinity's gameplay heavily relies on deliberate information control,
and most misunderstandings come from confusing "identity" with
"declared actions" or assuming that revealing a model reveals all info.
'
Correct handling of information flow is essential for fair, strategic,
and rules-compliant play.
