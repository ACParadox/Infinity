# Trooper Activation

Players use Orders to activate Troopers and have them perform actions in the form of Skills ( Move, Jump, CC Attack...). Depending on their complexity and the in-game time taken, Skills are divided into:

- Basic Short Skills: These skills are Move, Discover, and Idle. They can be declared twice, or can be combined with a different Basic Short Skill in the same Order. For instance, you could use an Order to Move and Discover. They can also be combined with a Short Skill.

- Short Skills: Most of the Skills available in Infinity are Short Skills. They can only be combined in an Order with a Basic Short Skill. They cannot be declared twice or be combined with other Short Skills.

- Long Skills: These Skills employ one whole Order and cannot be combined in any way.

You can check the Orders and AROs Reference Chart.

Therefore, spending an Order allows the activated Trooper to declare a maximum of two Skills, choosing from one of the following combinations and always declared in the order shown:

- 1 Basic Short Skill + 1 Basic Short Skill.

- 1 Basic Short Skill + 1 Short Skill.

- 1 Long Skill (which activates the Trooper and expends the whole Order).

Even if declared one after the other, both Skills in a single Order are performed simultaneously. For example, if you declare Move plus BS Attack, (the Short Skill that Troopers use to fire their weapons), you can make the BS Attack at any point during the declared movement route, and not necessarily at the beginning or end of that route.

The type, status, and expenditure of any Order ( Regular, Irregular, Tactical, the Lieutenant Order, which Combat Group it belongs to, whether it has been spent or not...) is Open Information. Players must place the Tokens for each and every one of their Orders in a visible place on the gaming table, and they must inform their opponent every time they spend an Order to activate a Trooper, even if it is the Lieutenant Order.


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
A Trooper is considered “activated” the moment the Active Player declares
which Trooper will spend the Order (Order Expenditure Sequence, Step 1).
This activation is what grants enemies the *first* opportunity to declare AROs.


# COMMONLY MISUNDERSTOOD INTERACTIONS


## 1. ACTIVATION VS. DECLARATION OF SKILLS
When a Trooper activates, he also declares the trooper's first skill. Even if the Trooper has NOT moved, attacked, or
generated any measurable position-based effects (e.g. a Idle) AROs are still triggered.

## 2. MULTIPLE OR REPEATED ACTIVATIONS

A Trooper may be activated multiple times in a turn as long as Orders remain.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Each activation is independent. A Trooper who already moved or shot earlier
in the turn does NOT “retain” any special restrictions unless a rule places them
in a State or imposes a per-turn limit. Reset opportunities, ARO triggers, etc.
occur freshly each activation.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
A Trooper activating *as a Marker* still generates AROs if the enemy meets a
trigger condition, but restrictions apply on what the enemy can declare.
Example: Against a Camo Marker, the enemy cannot declare BS Attack as a first
ARO unless they have discovered it or are using a legal alternative (e.g.
intuitive attack, templates, special rules).

## 5. ACTIVATION AND PERIPHERALS

Peripherals activate *together* with their Controller when the Controller spends
an Order, copying the same declared Short Skills.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Activating the Controller activates ALL attached Peripherals even if some of
them cannot perform the declared Skills (those simply perform Idle). This often
catches players off guard when a Peripheral unintentionally gives AROs.

## 6. ACTIVATION AND FIRETEAMS
---------------------------
Fireteams check Coherency at activation after the first declared Short Skill.
Members failing Coherency drop from the team and are NOT activated.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
You do not activate “the whole Fireteam automatically.” You activate the
Team Leader, then apply Fireteam rules. Members out of Coherency are not
activated nor do they gain Fireteam bonuses or generate additional ARO targets.

## 7. ACTIVATION AND ARO WINDOWS

Activation opens two distinct ARO declaration windows:
  • After Skill 1 is declared.
  • After Skill 2 is declared.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
If a Trooper activates in LoF but declares Skill 1 as Idle or a non-movement,
you still get an ARO window RIGHT THEN. The second window opens only if
the second Skill generates new ARO opportunities (movement, etc.).

## 8. ACTIVATION AND ORDER TYPE VISIBILITY
----------
Orders being spent (Regular, Irregular, Lieutenant, Tactical) are Open
Information.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Because activation always reveals the type of Order spent, players often gain
indirect information about who might be the Lieutenant or which troopers have
Tactical Awareness—even if they cannot identify the exact model.

## 9. ACTIVATION DURING LOSS OF LIEUTENANT
----------
All Troopers become Irregular during Loss of Lieutenant, but activation rules
for spending Orders remain unchanged—only the kind of Orders available changes.

## 10. ACTIVATION AND “ALL AT ONCE” TIMING
----------
All Effects (attacks, movement interactions, etc.) resolve simultaneously.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Declaring a Skill from the starting point of movement vs. the ending point
does not alter the fact that AROs occur *concurrently*. This prevents players
from trying to “see if a Discover succeeds” before choosing whether to shoot.


## SUMMARY OF KEY TAKEAWAYS
• Activation ALWAYS precedes Skill declaration.
• Activation, regardless of Skill declared, ALWAYS creates potential ARO triggers.
• Activation is separate each time an Order is spent.
• Marker States, Stealth, and Fireteam rules heavily modify activation timing.
• Activation triggers Coherency checks.
• Two ARO windows always exist per Order (after Skill 1 and after Skill 2).
• All effects resolve simultaneously, even When declared at different points.
