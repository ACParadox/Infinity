# Isolated State

Game States and Glossary

- The Trooper or game element ("Trooper" from now on) suffers a successful Attack or Effect using an Ammunition, or a game condition or Scenario Special Rule, capable of causing this state.

- Restriction: Troopers in Isolated State cannot receive Orders from their Order Pool.

- Restriction: If, at the start of their following Active Turns, the Trooper is still Isolated, then during that Turn they are Irregular and do not add his Order to their Order Pool.

- Restriction: The Irregular Order of an Isolated Trooper cannot be transformed in a Regular Order.

- Restriction: While in Isolated State all the Trooper's Skills and pieces of Equipment with the Comms Attack or Comms Equipment Labels or Traits ( Hacking Device, Repeater...) are disabled. In addition, all their Hacking Programs are disabled. Other Automatic Special Skills and Equipment continue to work in this state.

- If the Isolated Trooper is the army's Lieutenant, then at the beginning of the player's next Active Turn the army enters a Loss of Lieutenant situation unless the state is canceled before then.

- Restriction If a Trooper with the NCO Special Skill is in Isolated State, they cannot replace Special Lieutenant Orders with Tactical Orders or use them either.

- If a Peripheral or a Peripheral's Controller enters Isolated State, the Peripheral automatically enters Disconnected State.

- Troopers in this state must apply a -9 WIP MOD to their Reset rolls.

- Restriction: A Trooper in Isolated State cannot be part of a Fireteam. If a member of a Fireteam enters Isolated State they automatically stop being part of the Fireteam. In addition, remember that if the Isolated Trooper is the Team Leader the Fireteam will break.

- Restriction: Troopers in Isolated State cannot take part in a Coordinated Order.

- A Trooper with the Engineer Special Skill (or an equivalent Skill) may cancel the state by spending one Short Skill of an Order while in Silhouette contact with the affected Trooper and succeeding at a Normal WIP Roll (or the Roll specified by the Special Skill or Scenario that caused the state).

## Cancelling Isolated State 

- The affected Trooper may cancel this state via a successful Normal or Face to Face Reset Roll, applying the -9 WIP MOD provided by this state.

- If the army is in a Loss of Lieutenant situation as a result of the Lieutenant being in Isolated State, Canceling the Lieutenant's Isolated State does not cancel the Loss of Lieutenant situation.

## Isolated State and Oblivion

- Any failed Saving Roll causes the target to enter Isolated State, placing an Isolated State Token (ISOLATED) by them.

## Isolated State and Electromagnetic (E/M) Ammunition

- A failed Saving Roll against E/M Ammunition causes the target to enter Isolated State, placing an Isolated Token next to it, preventing it from receiving Orders from the Order Pool.

# Basic Interaction Pattern

Isolated is primarily an **Order-economy attack**.  
Instead of harming the target directly, it cuts them off from their Order Pool and often removes their coordinating abilities.

Key practical effects:
- The trooper cannot receive Orders from the pool.
- If still Isolated at the start of their next turn, they become Irregular and do not add a Regular Order.
- Their Irregular Order **cannot** be transformed into a Regular Order.
- All **Comms Attack / Comms Equipment** (Hacking Device, Repeater, etc.) are disabled.
- They immediately leave any **Fireteam**.
- They suffer **–9 WIP** to Reset, making self-recovery unreliable.
- Can be cleared by **Engineer** with a Normal WIP Roll in base contact, or by **Reset**.


# Common Causes (Rules-Supported)
- Failing a Saving Roll vs **Oblivion**  (Hacking Program).
- Failing a Saving Roll vs **E/M Ammunition**.
- Scenario effects that specify Isolated.


# Frequently Missed Interactions

## 1. Fireteams Break Immediately
The moment a Fireteam member becomes Isolated:
- They leave the Fireteam instantly.
- If they were **Team Leader**, the Fireteam breaks.
This often disrupts positioning, movement plans, and Fireteam AROs for the rest of the turn.

## 2. NCO Cannot Use Lieutenant Orders While Isolated
Even if the Lieutenant is fine, an Isolated NCO:
- Cannot convert LT Orders into Tactical Orders.
- Cannot spend Tactical Orders at all.
This removes a major efficiency tool from many lists.

## 3. Hackers Become “Silent”
Because Comms Equipment and Hacking Programs are disabled:
- Hackers in Isolated cannot declare Hacking Programs.
- Repeaters they carry are inactive.
This dramatically reduces the reach of a hacking network.

## 4. Peripherals Auto-Disconnect
If a Controller enters Isolated, all their Peripherals become **Disconnected**.  
This can instantly neutralize warbands, Servant bots, or special profiles.

## 5. Reset Is Hard (–9)
Even high-WIP units often reset on an 8 or lower.  
Engineer becomes the realistic method of clearing Isolated unless many Orders are spent.

# Comparisons with Similar States

## **Isolated vs Immobilized-B**
- *IMM-B* restricts movement and attacks but does not touch Orders.
- *Isolated* removes Orders but does not directly restrict physical actions.
- E/M can inflict **both** on HI/TAG/REM targets, making them unable to fight *and* unable to fuel other pieces, with Reset becoming WIP MOD -12

**Pros of Isolated:**
- Cuts opponent’s Order cycle.
- Disrupts Fireteams and tactical tools (NCO, hacking).
- Reduces Reset MOD by -9, making follow-up hacking (like Carbonite for IMM-B), much easier.

**Cons of Isolated:**
- Does not physically pin a target; mobile pieces may still attack once (or more with Tactical Awareness).
- Target can also still ARO.

# Tactical Notes

## 1. Best Uses for Isolated
- Neutralizing an active turn piece: with Isolated it will only be able to use a single order (2 with TacAware) .
- Setting up for Carbonite to completely cut off unit.
- Removing key Order generators (Regular LI, midfield specialists, Lieutenant candidates).
- Breaking Fireteams before engaging them.
- Disabling a Hacker net by isolating the one unit carrying the Repeater or the Hacker himself.

## 2. Common Delivery Methods
- **Oblivion** through Repeaters (safer, long-range).
- **E/M templates or grenades** (riskier, higher payoff).
- **E/M close-range weapons** (cheap and devastating vs elite troops).

## 3. Using Isolated as Soft Removal
An Isolated trooper with no Engineer support may effectively be stuck for the rest of the game:
- They contribute no Orders.
- They provide no hacking utility.
- Their Fireteam is crippled even if they remain physically alive.

## 4. Engineer as Counter-Tech
Because Isolated is so crippling, Engineers become essential:
- A single WIP roll often restores full function to expensive units.
- Servant bots expand repair coverage safely.
- Factions without reliable Engineers suffer disproportionately vs E/M-heavy opponents.

# Example Situations (Unofficial Scenarios)

- **Breaking a Fireteam Core:** Using Oblivion on a Team Leader often destroys the entire defensive ARO plan.
- **Isolating a TAG:** The TAG still has STR and weapons, but without Orders may be unable to attack at all in active turn.
- **Isolating a Lieutenant:** Even if the state is cleared later, **Loss of Lieutenant still happens** if they began the next turn Isolated.

---

# Summary
While it deals no damage, its attack on the opponent’s Order economy and coordination structure often decides games.  
It is best used to **cripple expensive units**, **break Fireteams**, or **shut down hackers and hacking networks**, and is countered primarily by **Engineer** support.
