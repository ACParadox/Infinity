# Prone State

Game States and Glossary

- During the Deployment Phase, players may deploy one or more of their Troopers Prone by placing a Prone Token beside them.

- A Trooper may change its status from Normal to Prone, and vice versa, at either the start of their Movement or at the end of their Movement, by performing any Short Skill or ARO with the Movement Label (except for Jump ) or as a result of a failed Guts Roll. However, this will affect their Silhouette and the distance they can move around the game table.

- If a Trooper enters Unconscious State, it automatically enters Prone State unless it has the Motorcycle piece of Equipment, or it is a VH or TAG, Troop Types that cannot enter Prone State.

- Prone Troopers have a Silhouette (S) value of X, that is, equivalent to their base's height and width.

- While in this state, Troopers halve the distance they can move when performing any Skill with Movement Label ( Move, Dodge...), or when moving as the result of a failed Guts Roll.

- This state does not affect Automatic Special Skills or Automatic Equipment, except when explicitly stated in their description.

- A Trooper may change its status from Normal to Prone, and vice versa, at either the start of their Movement or at the end of their Movement, by performing any Short Skill or ARO with the Movement Label (except for Jump ) (See FAQs.) or as a result of a failed Guts Roll. However, this will affect their Silhouette and the distance they can move around the game table.

- This state is automatically canceled when the Jump Common Skill is declared.

- Canceling a Trooper's Unconscious State automatically cancels their Prone State as well.

General Movement Rules

- When performing a Short Skill or ARO with the Movement Label (except for Jump), or when moving after failing a Guts Roll, a Trooper may enter or leave Prone State at either the start of their Movement or the end of their Movement, at no cost. However, if they move in Prone State, this will reduce the distance they can move.

## Prone Restrictions Chart

| Restrictions Chart |

| --- |

| Troopers mounted on Motorcycles cannot go Prone, declare Cautious Movement, Climb, use ladders, or perform an upwards Jump. |

## Prone FAQs

Q: Which Skills and AROs allow a Trooper to enter or cancel Prone State?

A: A Trooper can enter or cancel Prone State when performing any Skill or ARO with the Movement Label (except Jump and Berserk).

Q: If a Trooper enters Prone State at the end of their move, how does this affect the distance they can move?

A: It doesn’t. They move normally and then finish the end of the move prone.

## What Prone Does (Summary)
- Reduces the Trooper’s Silhouette to its base's height and width.
- Movement is halved while prone (the Trooper’s first MOV value becomes 2" unless otherwise stated).
- Prone Troopers may not be in base contact with scenery in a way that would count as being on a vertical surface.
- Entering and cancelling Prone is done by declaring a Short Skill with the Movement label (including in ARO, but except Jump) or through a movement caused by Guts or Dodge.

## Frequently Missed Interactions

### **1. Line of Fire When Prone**
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Prone Troopers still have a **full 180° LoF arc**, but their silhouette is **shorter**, which commonly causes situations where:
  - They can see *out* from behind a low obstacle while enemies cannot see *in* due to angle differences.
  - They lose LoF to enemies at elevation unless the enemy’s silhouette is tall enough to be visible over cover.

This creates interesting “micro-duel” situations, especially with S2 troops behind crates or low barriers.

---

### **2. Prone and Templates**
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Being Prone does not provide **any defensive MODs** against templates.
- However, silhouette reduction can drastically change whether the template physically reaches the model.
- Direct Template Weapons often fail to reach a Prone target hiding behind a mid-height obstacle even when the activating model is right next to it.

---

### **3. Prone While Engaged**
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- A Trooper **may be forced Prone** by some effects (e.g., Unconscious) while in base contact.
- Normally, you cannot voluntarily enter Prone while Engaged.
- The silhouette reduction **does not break the engagement**, but can change which enemies have LoF into the fight.

---

### **4. Going Prone Does NOT Break Marker States**
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- If a Marker (Camouflage, Holoecho, etc.) declares Change State to go Prone, the Marker **remains a Marker**.
- However, the silhouette shrink may cause the Marker to drop out of LoF *after* the Order, retroactively denying AROs for the second Skill.

---

### **5. Cancelling Prone and ARO Windows**
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Cancelling Prone requires declaring **a Short Skill** and therefore generates AROs if declared within LoF.
- Be cautious: standing back up often exposes the Trooper to additional AROs before it can shoot or re-enter cover.

A common tactical mistake is cancelling Prone as the **first** Short Skill rather than the second.

## Tactical Uses of Prone

### **1. Safest way to hide specialists**
Prone behind partial cover allows a Specialist to stay alive long enough to accomplish late-game objectives.

### **2. Control your LoF footprint**
By shrinking your silhouette, you can:
- prevent multiple angles of AROs,
- avoid triggering mines,
- control enemy approach lanes.

### **3. Strong rooftop dominance**
A Prone sniper on a rooftop:
- cannot be easily template-killed from street level,
- can stand up and shoot when desired,
- often forces opponents to climb (an Order tax).

### **4. Critical for avoiding blast/template splash**
The reduced height changes many blast angles, particularly for:
- Missile Launchers,
- Smart Missiles,
- Impact Templates.
