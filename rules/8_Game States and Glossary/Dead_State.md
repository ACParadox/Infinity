# Dead State

Game States and Glossary

If a target receives as many Wounds as the value of its VITA / STR Attribute, it enters Unconscious State.

On the other hand, if the target receives more Wounds than the value of its VITA/STR Attribute, it enters Dead State.

- A Trooper or a game element ("Trooper" from now on) receives more Wounds than their VITA / STR Attribute value.

- A Trooper in the Unconscious State receives more Wounds.

- The Trooper is removed from the game table and their Combat Group.

- The Trooper ceases to generate Orders in successive Player Turns.

- The Trooper does not count towards the Player's Victory Points.

- Dead State is irreversible and cannot be cancelled, unless the rules specific to the mission or scenario being played indicate otherwise. In that case, follow the procedure stated in the rules.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  

## Key Interactions & Notes

- **Order Contribution Ends Instantly**  
  A Trooper entering Dead no longer contributes Orders. This is important when resolving simultaneous effects; if something gives a positive effect and a Dead result in the same Order, rules mandate the positive effect is applied first, then negative — but Dead removes the Trooper from the table entirely.

- **Fireteam Consequences**  
  When a Fireteam member dies, the team immediately recalculates bonuses.  
  **[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]** A Fireteam may drop below size thresholds mid-Order and this affects active Burst bonuses or Special Dice availability.

- **Dead vs. Unconscious**  
  Some effects bypass Unconscious and jump straight to Dead (e.g., certain Ammo or States).  
  **[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]** This is tactically relevant because no Doctor/Engineer intervention is possible, and the model cannot serve as an ARO blocker or body to deny movement.

- **Dead and Peripherals**  
  Controllers dying generally causes their Peripherals to leave the table.  
  **[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]** This makes killing the controller often more efficient than fighting the peripheral itself.

- **Dead During Engaged**  
  If a model dies in CC, it is removed, potentially freeing both participants.  
  **[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]** Many CC pieces rely on tying up enemies; killing the tarpit can open LoF lines instantly, often enabling follow-up shots.

## Comparison: Dead vs. Unconscious vs. Sepsitorized

**Dead**
- Removed immediately.  
- No recovery possible.  
- Allows movement/LoF to open instantly.  
**Pros/Cons:** Clean resolution; extremely punishing.

**Unconscious**
- Can be healed; marker remains on table.  
- Still blocks movement/LoF.  
**Pros/Cons:** More interactive; tactically abusable as a smoke blocker.

**Sepsitorized**
- Trooper changes sides instead of dying.  
- Remains on table as an active threat.  
**Pros/Cons:** Most disruptive state, creating new vectors of attack.
