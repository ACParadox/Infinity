# Immobilized-A State

Game States and Glossary

- The Trooper or game element ("Trooper" from now on) suffers a successful Attack or Effect using an Ammunition, Hacking Program or a game condition or Scenario Special Rule, capable of causing this state.

- Troopers in Immobilized-A State cannot declare any Skill or ARO, except Dodge, applying a -6 PH MOD.

- Automatic Special Skills and Equipment continue to work, but the Trooper must still respect all declaration restrictions.

- Troopers in the Immobilized-A state State continue to provide Orders for their Order Pool.

- The affected Trooper may cancel this state via a successful Normal or Face to Face Dodge Roll, applying the -6 PH MOD provided by this state.

- A Trooper with the Engineer Special Skill (or an equivalent Skill) may cancel the Immobilized-A state by spending one Short Skill of an Order while in Silhouette contact with the affected Trooper, and passing a Normal WIP Roll (or the Roll specified by the Special Skill or Scenario).

## Immobilized-A  and Paralysis (PARA) Ammunition

- If the target fails the PH -6 Roll, it enters Immobilized-A State. Place an Immobilized-A Marker (IMM A) next to it.

Replacing Game Elements

## Immobilized-A  Example

Let's suppose a Trooper is in Immobilized-A state.

Then, the Active Trooper spends an Order, declares Move + BS Attack, and uses their Spitfire against the Immobilized-A Trooper.

In this state, the Immobilized-A Trooper may only declare Dodge as their ARO, with a -6 MOD.

Therefore, a Face to Face Roll of 4 Spitfire shots is made against 1 Dodge Roll.

The Immobilized-A Trooper will apply the following MODs to the Dodge Roll:

- -6 for Immobilized-A.

With a PH Attribute of 10, the Success Value (SV) will be 10 - 6 = 4.

The Active Trooper will apply the following MODs to the BS Attack Roll:

- +3 for Range.

- -3 for Cover.

With a BS Attribute of 13, the Success Value (SV) will be 13 + 3 - 3 = 13.

Resolution: The Immobilized-A Trooper loses the Face to Face Roll, so he suffers 3 Spitfire hits, since the 4th (with a result of 14) is a miss for the Active Trooper.

However, if the Immobilized-A Trooper wins the Dodge Roll, then they not only would dodge the incoming hits, but also cancel their Immobilized-A State.


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Overview**  
IMM-A is the physical immobilization state (as opposed to IMM-B which is the electronic/digital immobilisation). Immobilized-A as a state in which the Trooper **cannot declare any Skill or ARO except Dodge**, and that Dodge suffers **–6 PH**. This makes IMM-A extremely restrictive and tactically punishing.

## **[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Key Interaction Notes**

- **Troopers in IMM-A cannot declare Attacks, Move, Reset, or any Special Skills** — *only* Dodge (with –6).  
  This includes:
  - No BS Attack  
  - No Hacking  
  - No Reset (important!)  
  - No CC Attack  
  - No Discover  
  - No Move, Jump, Climb, Cautious Movement  
  - No re-entry into Marker States  
  - *No AROs*, except Dodge (–6)

- **Automatic Skills still work**, but the Trooper must obey declaration limits.  
  Examples:
  - Mimetism still applies  
  - Sixth Sense still applies  
  - TinBot firewall bonuses still apply  
  - Martial Arts or Natural Born Warrior do *not* matter because the Trooper cannot declare CC Attack

- IMM-A **does not remove the Trooper’s Order**. They continue generating Regular/Irregular Orders normally.

- The Trooper may **Dodge to cancel IMM-A**, applying the –6 PH penalty.  
  - Dodge may be Normal or Face to Face.
  - The Dodge movement, if successful, is allowed (Dodge movement is part of resolving a Dodge, not a declared Skill).

- An **Engineer** can cancel IMM-A with a successful WIP roll while in Silhouette contact.

## **[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Tricky Rules Interactions**

### **Fireteams**
- If a Fireteam is forced to declare a Skill they cannot (e.g., Move, BS Attack), the IMM-A member becomes **Idle** automatically.

### **Suppressive Fire**
- A Trooper in Suppressive Fire entering IMM-A:
  - Immediately drops Suppressive Fire because they cannot declare BS Attack.
  
### **Engaged State**
- A Trooper in Engaged State that enters IMM-A:
  - Still cannot declare CC Attack (illegal).
  - Can only declare Dodge (–6).  
  - Opponents may repeatedly CC Attack them with no possibility of return attack.

### **Hacking Area & AROs**
- IMM-A **does not block being targeted** — only declarations.  
- Hackers can freely:
  - Oblivion  
  - Carbonite  
  - Spotlight  
  - Total Control (if valid target type)

The IMM-A Trooper can only respond with Dodge at –6 (and not Reset, so it doesn't help against hacks).

## **[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Frequently Misunderstood Cases**

- **Reset does NOT cancel IMM-A**  
  Reset cannot be declared at all while in IMM-A.  
  This is a major break from earlier editions.

- **IMM-A Troopers are not helpless turrets**  
  They cannot attack, but they CAN:
  - Dodge templates  
  - Dodge out of LoF  
  - Dodge *and clear the state*

- **Immobilized-A does not stack with Isolated**  
  They apply independently:
  - Isolated = Order restriction  
  - IMM-A = declaration restriction  
  A Trooper may be both, but cancelling one does not cancel the other.

## **[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Tactical Notes**

**For the owning player:**
- Prioritize Engineers; a fast Engineer is often mandatory support.  
- IMM-A Troopers holding zones still count for scoring (if not Null).

**For the opponent:**
- IMM-A targets are ideal for:
  - CC specialists  
  - Direct Templates  
  - Hacking follow-up  
  - Objective denial (pin them away from zones)
- Use IMM-A to shut down TAGs or REMs long enough to maneuver.
