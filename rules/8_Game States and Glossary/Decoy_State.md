# Decoy State

Game States and Glossary

- Automatic when deployed for Troopers with the Decoy Skill.

- When deploying, this state allows the user to deploy up to two Decoy Markers (DECOY-1 / DECOY-2) in addition to the Trooper, within Coherency of the user's Model and respecting the general conditions of the Deployment rules.

- The number of additional Decoys that the Trooper may place during their Deployment is indicated between brackets: Decoy (1) or Decoy (2).

- If the user deploys in Marker form instead of as a Model, for example in Camouflaged or Impersonation States, the player places (CAMO) and (IMP) Markers on the table instead of the Decoy Markers.

- The Trooper with this Special Skill is represented by up to three Decoys: the Decoy 1 Marker, the Decoy 2 Marker and the actual Model, or Marker if the user has that choice. The player must note in secret which of these Decoys represents the real user of this Special Skill, with the others being mere replicas.

- The replicas do not hide a Trooper, Weapon or piece of Equipment and they remain static on the gaming table.

- DECOY-1 and DECOY-2 Markers are placed on the gaming table when a Trooper is replicated in Model form, so they must be considered as Models for LoF purposes and may be targeted.

- A replica Decoy must be automatically removed from the gaming table as soon as: It is Discovered. It is hit by a successful Attack. In this case, the replica Decoy does not make the Saving Roll and is simply removed. An enemy Model enters Silhouette contact with it. The Trooper with this Special Skill reveals themself or is Discovered.

- The real Decoy user is revealed, removing all the replicas and placing the Trooper in its real position, whenever: The Decoy user declares an Attack, Look Out!, or any Skill that requires a Roll, other than Combat Jump. The Decoy user declares a Long Skill of any kind, other than Cautious Movement, Parachutist, or Combat Jump. The Decoy user fails an Infiltration Roll or a Combat Jump Roll. An enemy Model enters Silhouette contact with the Decoy user, or vice-versa. The Decoy user is Discovered. The Decoy user is forced to make a Saving Roll. The Decoy user becomes Impetuous (due to the Frenzy Special Skill or any other effect) or enters Retreat! State.

- If the user was hidden as a Decoy Marker, the player must replace the Marker with the corresponding Model, facing in any direction the player chooses.

- When replacing the Marker with the Model, the player must provide their adversary with the Trooper's complete Open Information.

## Decoy State - Decoy

- Allows its user to deploy in Decoy State when they are placed on the table.

Decoys, weapons, Equipment and States: The replicas cannot replicate those Deployable weapons or pieces of Equipment represented by a Token or Model ( TinBots, FastPandas, SymbioMates, Mines deployed by the Minelayer Special Skill...) the Decoy user could have.

However, the replicas duplicate all the State Tokens ( Prone, Unloaded...) the Decoy user has, and the Effects of Skills that impose MODs on the opponent ( Albedo, Mimetism...).

Decoy and Fireteam: A Trooper using Decoy cannot be part of a Fireteam. If a member of a Fireteam is deployed using Decoy, then it will automatically cease being part of the Fireteam.

Decoy and Coordinated Orders: Troopers using Decoy cannot be part of Coordinated Orders.

Decoy and Airborne Deployment: When deploying a Trooper using Decoy and a Skill with the Airborne Deployment Label, the player must perform a Coherency Check as if it were the Deployment Phase, even though this is during the game.

## Decoy State - FAQs

Q: During your main part of the Deployment Phase, can you reposition Troopers, Deployables, etc?

A: In their main Deployment Phase step, players can reposition Troopers, Deployables, etc. As soon as a Roll or measurement is made for those game elements, their position is fixed, and they cannot be repositioned during the Deployment Phase.

For example, you can reposition a Trooper as many times as you want, but after you create a Fireteam and adjust the placement of the Fireteam members during the Coherency Check, you can no longer reposition any member of that Fireteam.

Q: In the Deployment Phase do you have to check ZoC when a Trooper uses Minelayer, or Coherency when deploying Decoys or Holoechoes?

A: Yes, but your opponent must look away so that you can check the ZoC/Coherency without revealing Private Information.


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Overview**
Decoy State creates static, targetable replicas during Deployment. These replicas exist solely to mislead opponents regarding the real Trooper’s position. Unlike Holoecho or Camouflaged State, Decoys cannot move, cannot perform actions, and do not protect the real trooper beyond information denial.

Decoy is strongest during turn 1 positioning and long-range deception, but offers no mid-game survival benefit once the active trooper is revealed.

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Key Interaction Notes & Common Misunderstandings**

• **Decoys are always considered “Models” for LoF purposes**, even though they are not Troopers.  
  This means they can be:  
  – Targeted normally  
  – Drawn LoF to/from  
  – Used to force enemy movement choices  

• **Decoys do NOT hide a real trooper, weapon, or equipment.**  
  They are empty shells. This confuses some players who expect Decoy to mimic Hidden Deployment or Holoecho rules.

• **Decoys are STATIC.**  
  They cannot move. They do not follow the real trooper. They do not reposition.

• **All replicas are removed immediately the moment any cancellation trigger happens**, including simple Discover.  
  This is a major difference from Camouflaged State, which requires a successful Discover roll and still leaves a functioning trooper afterwards.

• **Decoy cancellation often happens “earlier than expected.”**  
  Because the real user is revealed by:  
  – Declaring *any* Skill requiring a Roll (other than Combat Jump)  
  – Declaring *any* Long Skill (other than Cautious Movement / AD variants)  
  – Being forced to make a Saving Roll  
  – Becoming Impetuous automatically  
  Decoy State almost never survives beyond the first meaningful Order.

• **Decoy cannot replicate Deployables.**  
  Mines, Koalas, etc. **are never duplicated**, which prevents “fake minefields.”

• **Decoy prevents Fireteam membership and Coordinated Orders.**  
  A common oversight—taking a Decoy profile means the model is not an eligible fireteam body.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Practical Strengths of Decoy**

• Excellent during Deployment to disguise a sniper nest, missile position, or firelane threat.  
• Very strong when the table has multiple good vantage points — Decoys multiply uncertainty.  
• Harshly punishes opponents who prematurely commit long-range attacks.  
• Creates genuine hesitation in alpha-strike factions.  
• Simple and low-overhead—no marker management or mid-game resets.
• If trooper and decoys are camouflaged adding camouflaged mines add uncertainty to the mix

---

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Weaknesses of Decoy**

• Provides **no ongoing protection** after the first reveal.  
• Easily eliminated by Discover or incidental movement.  
• No Mimetism, no Surprise Attack, no ARO restriction—unlike Camouflaged State.  
• Completely static and predictable.  
• Limited to Deployment Phase — no regeneration during the game
