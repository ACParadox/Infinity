# Labels

> **N5.3 change (September 2026):** The **Null** label now explicitly identifies **Dead, Disconnected, Possessed, Sepsitorized, and Unconscious** as Null States.

Game States and Glossary

Labels describe defining aspects of Skills, weapons, and pieces of Equipment for quick reference.

### Airborne Deployment (AD)
This label encompasses all Skills related to Airborne Infantry. Any rule mentioning Airborne Deployment affects Skills with this Label.

### Assignable (Transmutation)
When deploying its owner during the Deployment Phase, and only then, this weapon or piece of Equipment can be assigned to Troopers who possess Transmutation (X) and are present on the game table as a Model, subject to the Assignable rules. A Trooper cannot receive more than one weapon or piece of Equipment of the same type with this Label.

### Attack
The use of this Special Skill or piece of Equipment is considered an Attack. Attacks cannot be declared against allies or Neutral elements unless a rule says otherwise.

### BS Attack
The use of this Special Skill or piece of Equipment is a form of BS Attack.

### CC Attack
The use of this Special Skill or piece of Equipment is a form of CC Attack.

### CC Special Skill
This Skill can only be used when the user is in Silhouette contact with the target. MODs may combine with other CC Special Skills unless a rule states otherwise.

### Comms Attack
The use of this weapon, Special Skill, or piece of Equipment is a Comms Attack. It is opposed with Reset rather than Dodge where the rules specify, and Partial Cover does not apply to Comms Attacks.

### Comms Equipment
This Equipment is vulnerable to certain Hacking Programs and Ammunition.

### Hackable
A Trooper, Peripheral, weapon, or piece of Equipment with the Hackable Characteristic or Label can be targeted by applicable Hacking Programs.

### Hostile
A game element with its own Attributes that belongs to neither player's Army List. It is treated as an Enemy by all players and can declare and receive Attacks.

### Marker
This Label indicates that a Trooper, Weapon, or piece of Equipment can be represented by a Marker rather than a Miniature or Token.

### Movement
The use of this Skill or Equipment is a form of Movement.

### Negative Feedback (NFB)
The use of a Special Skill or piece of Equipment with this Label is incompatible with another active rule carrying the same Label/Trait. Activating one NFB effect cancels/overrides another as specified by the NFB rules.

### No LoF
This Skill does not require LoF to the target.

### Non-Reloadable
Unloaded State cannot be cancelled for Disposable weapons or pieces of Equipment with this Label, and their expended uses cannot be regained unless a specific rule says otherwise.

### No Roll
This weapon, Special Skill, or piece of Equipment works automatically and does not require a Roll. Fixed results are shown in brackets where applicable.

### Null
A Game State with this Label indicates that, while in that State, the Trooper does not provide an Order or Victory Points to the player. **Dead, Disconnected, Possessed, Sepsitorized, and Unconscious are Null States.**

### Obligatory
Use of this Special Skill or Equipment is compulsory and cannot be waived.

### Optional
Use of this Special Skill or Equipment is optional and can be waived.

### Private Information
Whether Troopers have rules with this Label is Private Information until the rules require disclosure or the game ends.

### States Phase
This piece of Equipment or Skill can only be used during the States Phase unless stated otherwise.

### Superior Deployment
This Label encompasses Skills that allow Troopers to deploy outside their Deployment Zone. Rules referring to Superior Deployment affect all Skills with this Label.

### Supportware
Hacking Programs designed to support Allied Troopers or interfere with enemy Troopers.

## FAQs

Q: Which Skills and AROs allow a Trooper to enter or cancel Prone State?

A: A Trooper can enter or cancel Prone State when performing any Skill or ARO with the Movement Label, except Jump and Berserk.

Q: Do Skills that let you perform a BS Attack Roll, or have the BS Attack Label, count as a BS Attack for MODs?

A: Yes. Note that +1 SD MODs do not apply to Long Skills.

Q: Does a Trooper in Disconnected State or Unconscious State prevent an enemy from re-entering Marker State, or cancel their Cautious Movement?

A: No.
