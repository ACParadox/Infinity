# Traits

Game States and Glossary

Traits are the special features of certain weapons and pieces of Equipment.

Traits are commonly given to Common and Special Skills, or to specific effects that make these rules more unique. Some are pretty obvious, and their names describe what their effects are, nevertheless, a list of the different Traits is provided to make them easier to identify:

Click on the Trait name to see a list of all items with that Trait.

Most Traits are found in the Weapon Chart.

### Traits - Anti-materiel

This weapon's Special Ammunition can affect structures and pieces of scenery.

### Traits - ARM = 0

This weapon or piece of Equipment reduces the ARM Attribute of the Target to 0 when a Saving Roll is required.

### Traits - ARO

This weapon or piece of Equipment is only usable in ARO.

### Traits - BioWeapon

This weapon applies the combination of DA + Shock Special Ammunition to targets with the Vitality (VITA) Attribute.

### Traits - Boost

A weapon possessing this Trait applies the Boost rule, activating when an enemy Model declares or executes an Order or ARO in its ZoC.

### Traits - BS Weapon (PH)

This weapon can make BS Attacks, but uses the PH Attribute in place of BS. When using this weapon, all rules and MODs that affect the Trooper's BS affect their PH Attribute instead.

### Traits - BS Weapon (WIP)

This weapon can make BS Attacks, but uses the WIP Attribute in place of BS. When using this weapon, all rules and MODs that affect the Trooper's BS affect their WIP Attribute instead. The BS Attack (Shock) Skill cannot be used with weapons with this Trait.

### Traits - BTS = 0

This weapon or piece of Equipment reduces the BTS Attribute of the Target to 0 when a Saving Roll is required.

### Traits - Burst (B)

This indicates the number of dice the player must roll when using the weapon or piece of Equipment.

### Traits - Burst: Single Target

This weapon can only choose a single target for all shots of the Burst.

### Traits - CC

This weapon can be used when making CC Attacks.

### Traits - Concealed

This weapon or piece of Equipment uses the effects of Camouflaged State. Refer to the description of the weapon or Equipment for more details. Camouflage Markers concealing a weapon, or a piece of Equipment have a Silhouette (S) value of 2.

### Traits - Continuous Damage

After failing a Saving Roll, the target receives a Wound and will have to keep making Saving Rolls until it passes a Saving Roll or until it enters Dead State. A Critical hit with a weapon with this Trait forces the target to make an additional Saving Roll. This extra Saving Roll doesn't apply the Continuous Damage Trait.

### Traits - Deployable

This weapon or piece of Equipment may be deployed on the battlefield, thus becoming an element independent of its carrier. Deployable weapons and pieces of Equipment have their own profiles and their own Attributes, and they may be chosen as a target during the game. If a Deployable item enters Unconscious State, it automatically passes directly to Dead State, without receiving an extra Wound, and must be removed from the game table. Weapons and pieces of Equipment with this Trait do not activate other Deployable Weapons or Equipment.

### Traits - Direct Template

This weapon or piece of Equipment uses the Direct Template rules, firing the Template indicated in brackets.

### Traits - Disposable (X)

This weapon or Equipment has a limited amount of ammunition or uses, and one is expended every time you declare its use, regardless of the success or failure of the Roll involved, or if a Skill has been declared illegally. The profile indicates, through a figure in brackets after the Disposable trait, the number of uses for that weapon or Equipment. Once all the available uses are expended, the Trooper, or the item if the Trooper has several Disposable weapons or Equipment, will be in Unloaded State.

Each increment in the Burst (B) value applied to weapons with this Trait due to any MOD uses up a Disposable use. For example, a Trooper with a Panzerfaust (a two-use weapon with B 1) and BS Attack (+1B) must make a BS Attack with B 2, consuming in a single Order the two available uses in the Panzerfaust, so the weapon is unloaded, and the player must place an Unloaded Token next to the Trooper.

Burst (B) MODs cannot raise the B value higher than the remaining available uses.

In addition, those weapons possessing the Disposable Trait and different Modes (such as Mixed Weapons, D-Charges...) share the number of uses provided by Disposable between all Modes.

Idle

- In the Resolution Step of the Order, if a Trooper is found to have not met the Requirements of a declared Skill, they instead perform an Idle. In this situation: The ammunition of Disposable weapons or pieces of Equipment is spent.

- The ammunition of Disposable weapons or pieces of Equipment is spent.

### Traits - Double Shot

In the Active Turn, this weapon or piece of Equipment can apply a +1 MOD to its Burst value. If it also possesses the Disposable (2) Trait, this Burst MOD can only be applied if both uses remain unspent. Applying this MOD will expend both uses, putting the Trooper in Unloaded State.

### Traits - Impact Template

This weapon or piece of Equipment places a Template on the point of impact, firing the Template indicated in brackets.

### Traits - Improvised

This weapon or piece of Equipment is being used in a manner it was not designed for, making it difficult to use. Therefore, the Improvised Trait imposes a MOD of -6 to the corresponding Attribute of the user.

### Traits - Indiscriminate

This weapon or piece of Equipment is usable or deployable even if there is a Camouflage and Hiding Marker in its Area of Effect, and even if there is no valid target nearby.

### Traits - Intuitive Attack

This weapon can be used to make Intuitive Attacks.

### Traits - Non-Lethal

No matter the ammunition type, this weapon, piece of Equipment or Hacking Program uses, it doesn't inflict Wounds on the target, or doesn't require the target to make a Saving Roll when hit.

Even if a Skill, rule or MOD adds or combines other ammunition types, the Non-Lethal Trait always take precedence, so this weapon, piece of Equipment or Hacking Program never inflicts Wounds on the target.

### Traits - Non-Reloadable

Unloaded State cannot be cancelled for Disposable weapons or pieces of Equipment with this Trait, and their expended uses cannot be regained.

### Traits - Perimeter

When declaring Place Deployable, the player applies the Deployable and Perimeter rule, placing the weapon or piece of Equipment completely inside the ZoC of the Trooper, instead of in Silhouette contact.

### Traits - Prior Deployment

You must place this weapon or piece of Equipment on the game table during the Deployment Phase.

### Traits - Reflective

The effects of this weapon or piece of Equipment are also applied to Troopers who have the Marksmanship Special Skill or are equipped with a Multispectral Visor of any Level, or with any other piece of Equipment that specifies the same.

### Traits - Silent (X)

If you use this weapon or piece of Equipment to make an Attack while inside the target's ZoC and outside their LoF, the target must apply the MOD between brackets to any Face to Face Dodge Roll they make against the Attack. This MOD is cumulative with other Dodge MODs.

### Traits - Speculative Attack

This weapon can be used to make Speculative Attacks.

### Traits - State

This weapon or piece of Equipment causes its target to enter a specific Game State. The Game State it causes will be indicated in the profile of the weapon, Program, or piece of Equipment.

### Traits - Suppressive Fire (SF)

This weapon allows the user to enter Suppressive Fire State, substituting its usual profile with the SF Mode profile.

### Traits - Target (Attribute)

This weapon or piece of Equipment is only effective against targets with the Attribute indicated between brackets, either VITA or STR. If the target does not have the indicated Attribute, they do not need to make the Saving Roll, as this weapon or piece of Equipment has no effect on them.

### Traits - Targetless

This weapon utilizes a Special Ammunition capable of firing without designating an enemy as a target. In the Reactive Turn, LoF to the Active Trooper is required when using a Targetless weapon.

### Traits - Zone of Control (ZoC)

The range of this weapon or piece of Equipment is equivalent to the user's Zone of Control (8 inches).

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
## Anti-materiel
Anti-materiel allows a weapon to destroy scenery, certain mission objectives, and other items that specify vulnerability to Anti-materiel.  
One of the most commonly overlooked interactions is that Anti-materiel does **not** provide any additional effect on Troopers unless another rule explicitly says it works against them.  


## BioWeapon
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
BioWeapon attacks typically reference Target’s **BTS** and often ignore specific immunities unless those immunities explicitly protect against Biological attacks.  
This commonly interacts with States like **Immune: Total** or **Dogged/NWI**, because some BioWeapons bypass these entirely (depending on the ammo type the weapon uses).  
A frequent misunderstanding is believing BioWeapons are always Non-Lethal—many are absolutely lethal. Only the specific ammo defines lethality.

## Boost
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Boost is characteristic of certain Perimeter Weapons. It allows an automatic move toward a triggering enemy, normally leading to an automatic attack.  
The key interaction: Boost triggers when an enemy enters the weapon's ZoC and LoF if applicable—meaning careful pathing can avoid making contact.  
Boosted movement is not optional. Once triggered, the Perimeter Weapon must follow through, often leading to accidental chain-reactions with other Boost-capable items or triggering mines.

## Concealed
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
A Concealed weapon or item cannot be targeted until it is revealed by its own activation or by a rule that specifically exposes Concealed elements.  
This can confuse newer players who mistake Concealed for Camouflage—it is NOT a Marker State and does not benefit from Surprise Attack.  
Similar to Hidden Deployment, but tied to equipment rather than a Trooper.  
Strong tactically because opponents cannot preemptively remove the item before it activates.

## Continuous Damage
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
After failing a single Saving Roll, the model continues making additional Saves until one succeeds.  
Frequently misunderstood: Continuous Damage does **not** stack multiples if several weapons with this Trait hit at once—each source resolves separately, but the Trait itself does not multiply its own effect.  
This Trait punishes low ARM/BTS troops disproportionately, and synergizes particularly well with template attacks that are likely to hit multiple models.

## Deployable
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Deployable items exist on the table independently of their owner once placed.  
Their alignment always counts as belonging to the owner’s Combat Group.  
One tricky interaction: Deploying a Deployable does **not** generate AROs unless the Skill used to place it normally would (Place Deployable is usually allowed without ARO if no movement or LoF is involved).  
Deployables often conflict with **Prior Deployment** and **Disposable (X)**—running out of uses limits your ability to replace lost Deployables.

## Disposable (X)
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Weapons or Equipment with Disposable have limited uses, tracked across the entire game, not per encounter.  
Losing a declaration due to failed Requirements **still consumes** one use—this is a common mistake.  
Combines problematically with high Burst weapons: if Burst is above 1, the Disposable count must allow firing the whole Burst. If not, some dice are simply lost.

## Double Shot
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Double Shot adds a second hit when a successful attack is made, often making low-Burst weapons significantly more threatening.  
This is not Burst +1—it's an extra effect **after** a hit is confirmed.  
Particularly deadly when combined with AP or high-Damage ammunition.  
Players frequently forget that Double Shot still requires a successful Roll; it is not automatic.

## Improvised
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Improvised weapons generally impose penalties or reduced effectiveness compared to standard weapons.  
Most often seen when a Trooper uses a fallback mode due to being unarmed or using Environmental rules.  
Pros: Always available.  
Cons: Usually terrible stats and no useful Traits; intended as an absolute last resort.

## Indiscriminate
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Indiscriminate weapons affect Allies, Enemies, and Neutrals alike.  
A frequent oversight is forgetting that **Indiscriminate** still requires a legitimate target—it does not bypass the Targetless requirement unless also possessing Targetless.  
Be cautious when combining with Impact or Circular Templates; friendly casualties can cost Objective Points.

## Non-Lethal
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Non-Lethal weapons cannot cause Wounds but may apply debilitating States like Stunned or Immobilized.  
Common misconception: Non-Lethal means safe for friendlies—this is false; allies hit by Non-Lethal attacks still suffer State effects.  
Strong for disabling high ARM models that are difficult to kill, but useless against mission objectives requiring destruction.

## Non-Reloadable
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Weapons that cannot be Reloaded even when a Trooper has access to Reload Skill or a Baggage unit.  
Often paired with Disposable, forcing careful resource management.  
A notable trap: some players believe Command Tokens or Engineers can restore Non-Reloadable gear—this is incorrect.

## Perimeter
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Perimeter items orbit their owner and are activated with the owner’s Order.  
Their most misunderstood rule: Perimeter items do not block LoF and do not provide Cover.  
Interactions: Perimeter + Boost creates zone denial; Perimeter + Disposable means once Boost expends the item, it’s gone forever.

## Prior Deployment
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Items with Prior Deployment must be placed during the Deployment Phase.  
If the deploying Trooper is kept as a Reserve Model, this may delay or even prohibit legal placement—poor sequencing can invalidate the item entirely.  
Often confuses players when combined with Minelayer: Minelayer still checks ZoC conditions even if the Mine also has Prior Deployment.

## Silent (X)
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
### Traits – Silent (X)

If you use this weapon or piece of Equipment to make an Attack while inside the target's ZoC and outside their LoF, the target must apply the MOD between brackets to any Face to Face Dodge Roll they make against the Attack. This MOD is cumulative with other Dodge MODs.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Silent (X) is often misunderstood as “no ARO allowed,” but in N5 this Trait specifically affects FtF rolls made by the target when the attacker is inside the target’s ZoC but outside their LoF.  

This creates a powerful interaction with Stealth and Marker States, because:
- If Stealth prevents the target from gaining an ARO, Silent (X) never comes into play (no Dodge is rolled).  
- If Stealth is broken (intentionally or unintentionally), Silent (X) penalizes the Dodge FTF Roll, making it harder to avoid hits.

Silent (X) does **not** suppress AROs from *other* enemy Troopers who have LoF to the attacker; only the attacked Trooper is affected, and only if they attempt to Dodge.

A common mistake:  
Players often think Silent (X) applies when shooting from outside ZoC — it does not. The condition is specifically **inside ZoC AND outside LoF**.

Pros:  
- Strong for isolation picks and safe attacks from behind corners.  
- Great synergy with melee or short-range assassins who frequently operate in ZoC but outside LoF.  

Cons:  
- Does nothing against Alert!, Reset, or BS Attack AROs from enemies who *do* have LoF.  


## Targetless
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Targetless weapons do not require a Trooper or scenery element as a target—you select a point on the table instead.  
This allows attacks into Zero Visibility Zones, behind walls, or through smoke without needing LoF.  
However, AROs are still triggered normally by the **Skill declaration**, not by the point of impact.  
The downside: You cannot make Face to Face Rolls with Targetless attacks, so enemies can freely shoot back.
