# Unconscious State

Game States and Glossary

If a target receives as many Wounds as the value of its VITA / STR Attribute, it enters Unconscious State.

On the other hand, if the target receives more Wounds than the value of its VITA/STR Attribute, it enters Dead State.

- A Trooper or game element ("Trooper" from now on) receives as many Wounds as their VITA / STR Attribute value.

- Restriction: Troopers in this state cannot declare Orders or AROs.

- Unconscious Troopers automatically enter Prone State unless they are a Troop Type unable to do so.

- Troopers in this state do not contribute Orders during the Tactical Phase.

- Automatic Special Skills and Automatic Equipment have no effect while in this state.

- The Trooper does not count towards the Player's Victory Points.

Update PDF 5.2, October 2025

- Troopers in Unconscious State still belong to their Combat Group.

- A Wound is removed from the Trooper, leaving them with less Wounds than their VITA / STR Attribute value.

- Doctors can remove Wounds from Troopers with the VITA Attribute, while Engineers can remove Wounds from Troopers with the STR Attribute. In both cases, the Wound is removed by performing the Doctor/Engineer Special Skill while in Silhouette contact with the affected Trooper, and passing a Normal WIP Roll (or the Roll specified by the Special Skill or Scenario).

- Other Special Skills and pieces of Equipment allow the removal of Wounds as indicated in their rules.

- Canceling Unconscious State automatically cancels Prone State as well.

- The Unconscious State of Troopers using the Shasvastii Special Skill (usually referred to as 'Shasvastii-Embryo') has the following special rules: [...]

- During the game, the Trooper is counted when calculating Victory Points for the Retreat! situation, and for any other rules or conditions required by the scenario.

## Unconscious and Remote Presence

- Troopers with Remote Presence and the STR Attribute have two distinct levels of Unconscious State.

- When the Trooper receives as many Wounds as their STR Attribute value, they enter Unconscious State like any other Trooper. However, if the Trooper receives one Wound more than its STR value, they do not enter Dead State as usual, but instead enter a second level of Unconscious State, signified by a second Unconscious Token (UNCONSCIOUS)...

## Unconscious and MediKit

- A MediKit's target must be an Allied Model with the VITA Attribute and in Unconscious State, unless certain scenario rules say otherwise. [...]

- In both cases, by passing the PH Roll, the target may remove one Wound and cancel their Unconscious State. If the PH Roll is failed, the target automatically enters Dead State and is removed from the game table.

## Unconscious and GizmoKit

- The target of a GizmoKit must be an Allied Model with the STR Attribute. [...]

- In both cases, by passing the PH Roll, the target removes a Wound. This cancels Unconscious State if applicable. If the PH Roll is failed, the target receives a Wound instead of removing it, entering Unconscious or Dead State if applicable.

## Unconscious and Regeneration

- During the States Phase, the user makes a Normal PH Roll: If the Roll is a success, the Trooper removes 1 Wound, cancelling the Unconscious State if applicable. If the Roll fails, the Trooper receives 1 additional Wound.

- If the Roll is a success, the Trooper removes 1 Wound, cancelling the Unconscious State if applicable.

- If the Roll fails, the Trooper receives 1 additional Wound.

## Unconscious and Shock Ammunition

- If the target has the VITA Attribute, with a value of 1, any failed Saving Rolls against Shock Ammunition will also cancel any type of Unconscious State the target is in, or has entered during this Order, and the target passes directly to Dead State.

## Unconscious and CC Attack > Coup de Grâce

- Coup de Grâce: When declaring the CC Attack, if the Enemy Target is in Unconscious State (including if it is represented by a Shasvastii-Embryo Token), then, without needing to perform a roll, the target automatically goes from Unconscious to Dead State without the possibility of making a Saving Roll. Coup de Grâce cannot be used against a Trooper who has activated the Dogged or No Wound Incapacitation Special Skill. Therefore, the CC Attack is resolved normally.

## Core Concepts Players Often Miss
Unconscious State is a **Null State** triggered when a trooper has taken Wounds equal to its VITA/STR value.  
While Unconscious:
- The trooper cannot declare Orders or AROs.
- Automatically becomes Prone, unless unable to do so.
- Provides **no Automatic Skills or Equipment**; these shut off entirely.
- Contributes **no Orders** and **no Victory Points**.
- Remains in its Combat Group.

Many tactical interactions revolve around the fact that Unconscious = the model still exists on the table and can be healed, repaired, or finished.

---

## Interactions Most Frequently Confused

### 1. **Prone and Line of Fire**
Unconscious troopers are forcibly Prone:
- They often cannot be seen over terrain that would normally block a S prone trooper.
- However, template weapons or intuitive attacks may still reach them.

**Misconception:**  
Players often assume an Unconscious trooper cannot block movement or LoF.  
**Reality:** They still occupy their Silhouette and block movement unless a rule states otherwise.


### 2. **Spending Orders on a Trooper That Became Unconscious Mid-Order**
If a trooper enters Unconscious during resolution, any undeclared Skills cannot be declared afterward, and the Order ends.  
This matters for multi-Skill actions where the return fire knocks the model out before resolution finishes.

---

### 3. **Automatic Skills Turn Off**
Automatic Skills like Mimetism, Courage, Stealth, Dodge (+X"), or ARM/BTS bonuses granted by Automatic Equipment do **not** work while Unconscious.

Typical consequences:
- An Unconscious trooper with Mimetism-6 gives **no defensive MOD** against coup-de-grâce fire.
- A trooper with Automatic Dodge movement cannot apply it after being dropped.

---

### 4. **Healing vs. Killing Misunderstandings**
#### Doctor / MediKit (VITA)
- Successful roll: Remove 1 Wound and cancel Unconscious.
- Failure: Target instantly dies

#### Engineer / GizmoKit (STR)
- Successful roll: Remove 1 Wound, cancel Unconscious if applies
- Failure: Target **takes** a Wound; might cause Dead State if STR exhausted

**Differences matter tactically:**  
- MediKit is lethal on failure; GizmoKit is risky for STR 1 troopers.  
- Remote Presence grants a second level of Unconscious before Dead, greatly improving survivability.

---

### 5. **Shock Ammunition**
Shock bypasses Unconscious for **1-Wound VITA models**, sending them straight to Dead State.
This eliminates many healing opportunities and is especially impactful for:
- Light infantry
- Warbands
- VITA 1 specialists

---
### 6. **Special Skills That Modify Unconscious**
#### Dogged
- Trooper ignores Unconscious for the rest of the Turn, treating state as Normal instead.
- Automatically dies at end of Turn.

#### No Wound Incapacitation (NWI)
- Trooper ignores the effects permanently, treating Unconscious as Normal State
- Still dies if reduced below 0 Wounds or by Shock.

#### Shasvastii (Embryo)
- Enters a special version of Unconscious; still counts for Retreat! and scoring conditions.

#### Remote Presence (Two-Stage Unconscious)
- STR models with RP enter **Unconscious 1**, then **Unconscious 2**, before Dead.
- Makes Engineers drastically safer and more efficient when repairing.


### 7. **Coup de Grâce**
- If the target is in any Unconscious State, the CC Attack decrees **automatic Dead**.
- Cannot be used against Dogged or NWI models still ignoring Unconscious.


### 8. **Unconscious and Marker State Interactions**
Unconscious bodies do **not** count as a threat for Marker State mechanics.


## Tactical Notes
**Leaving an Enemy Unconscious Is a Trap**  
Unconscious enemy specialists or attack pieces may return to the board at an inopportune time.

---

## Common Mistakes
- Forgetting Automatic Skills are OFF while Unconscious.
- Thinking NWI is “extra wounds”—it is just a modified form of Unconscious behavior.
- Leaving Unconscious specialists near objectives without finishing them.

---

## Final Summary
Unconscious State is a central, high-impact rule that intersects with:
- Wound system  
- Healing tools (Doctor/Engineer, MediKit/GizmoKit)  
- Ammunition types (Shock, DA, Viral, etc.)  
- Tactical scoring  
- Special survivability Skills (Dogged, NWI, RP, Shasvastii)

Understanding when to **heal**, **ignore**, or **finish** an Unconscious model is a vital skill that dramatically shapes Infinity N5 gameplay.
