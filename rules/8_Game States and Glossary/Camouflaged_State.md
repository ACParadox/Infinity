# Camouflage

> **N5.3 change (September 2026):** The Camouflaged State restriction now explicitly limits which AROs may be **declared** against a Camouflaged Marker: Discover, Dodge, Look Out!, or Reset. This replaces older wording framed around which AROs could be “performed” or executed.
>
> **N5 FAQ v0.1 clarification (September 2026):** If an Attack hits a Camouflaged Marker but the actual game element represented by that Marker is in **Total Cover** from the Attack, an Attack that would force a Saving Roll reveals the Marker, but the underlying game element is not hit because it has Total Cover.

Special Skills
 AUTOMATIC SKILL
Optional 

This Special Skill represents the soldier's ability to conceal their position and move stealthily.

- During the Deployment Phase, it allows the user to deploy in Camouflaged State.

- In game, it allows the user to enter Camouflaged State.

# Camouflaged State

- Automatic during the Deployment Phase for Troopers with the Camouflage Skill.

- During the Active Turn, Troopers with the Camouflage Skill may only return to this state by spending a Long Skill, while outside the LoF of enemy Markers or Troopers.

- In Camouflaged State, the Player does not place the Model on the table, but instead places a Camouflaged Marker.

- Camouflaged Markers can possess the Mimetism Skill, so Camouflaged Markers indicate the MOD level that Mimetism applies, if they have it.

- Restriction: You cannot enter Silhouette contact with an Enemy Camouflaged Marker.

- Restriction: You cannot declare Attacks against Camouflaged Markers, it is necessary to Discover that Marker first (Discover + Attack maneuver), unless otherwise specified by a rule or Skill.

- To reveal a Camouflaged Marker, a Discover Roll must be passed, applying MODs listed for Discover, including the Marker's Mimetism, if they have it.

- If the Discover Roll is successful, the Camouflaged Marker is replaced with the corresponding Model, facing in any direction its player chooses.

- A Trooper that fails a WIP Roll to Discover a Marker cannot attempt to Discover the same Marker until the next Player Turn. Note that a Trooper that has been revealed, and re-entered Camouflaged State again, does not count as the same Marker.

- A Camouflaged Marker has a LoF of 360˚.

- A Camouflaged Marker has the same Silhouette (S) value as the Trooper it represents. However, the Camouflaged Marker of a Weapon or piece of Equipment in Camouflaged State has a Silhouette (S) value of 2.

- This state does not interfere with Automatic Special Skills or Automatic Equipment, which will remain functioning normally.

- Restriction: A Trooper in Camouflaged State cannot be part of a Fireteam. If a member of a Fireteam enters Camouflaged State they automatically stop being part of the Fireteam.

- Restriction: The only AROs that can be declared against a Camouflaged Marker are: Discover, Dodge, Look Out! or Reset.

- When a Trooper in Camouflaged State is activated, each Reactive Trooper can delay the declaration of their ARO until the second half of the Active Trooper's Order has been declared. In this case: If the Trooper in Camouflaged state reveals themselves with the second half of their Order (by declaring a BS Attack, moving into Silhouette contact with an enemy...), the delaying Trooper can declare their ARO. If the Trooper in Camouflaged state does not reveal themselves, the delaying Trooper loses their right to declare an ARO.

- If the Trooper in Camouflaged state reveals themselves with the second half of their Order (by declaring a BS Attack, moving into Silhouette contact with an enemy...), the delaying Trooper can declare their ARO.

- If the Trooper in Camouflaged state does not reveal themselves, the delaying Trooper loses their right to declare an ARO.

## Camouflaged - Camouflaged State is canceled whenever:

- The Camouflaged Marker declares an Attack, Look Out!, or any Skill that requires a Roll. The Camouflaged Marker declares a Long Skill of any kind, other than Cautious Movement. The Camouflaged Marker enters Silhouette contact with an enemy Model. The Camouflaged Marker is Discovered. The Camouflaged Marker is forced to make a Saving Roll. The Trooper is or becomes Impetuous (due to having the Frenzy Special Skill or any other effect), or enters the Retreat! State.

- When Camouflaged State is canceled, the player must replace the Marker with the corresponding Model, facing in any direction the player chooses.

- When replacing the Marker with the Model, the player must provide their adversary with the Trooper's complete Public Information.

- The Camouflaged Marker declares an Attack, Look Out!, or any Skill that requires a Roll.

- The Camouflaged Marker declares a Long Skill of any kind, other than Cautious Movement.

- The Camouflaged Marker enters Silhouette contact with an enemy Model.

- The Camouflaged Marker is Discovered.

- The Camouflaged Marker is forced to make a Saving Roll.

- The Trooper is or becomes Impetuous (due to having the Frenzy Special Skill or any other effect), or enters the Retreat! State.

## Camouflaged - Camouflage

- During the Deployment Phase, it allows the user to deploy in Camouflaged State.

- In game, it allows the user to enter Camouflaged State.

## Camouflaged - Concealed Trait
Camouflage Markers concealing a weapon, or a piece of Equipment have a Silhouette (S) value of 2.

## Camouflaged - Transmutation (Hatching) 
The Camouflage Marker of a Troop with Transmutation (Hatching) has a Silhouette (S) value of 2.

## Camouflaged - Multispectral Visor Level 3 
In the Active Turn the user may perform a BS Attack on a Camouflage Marker (CAMO) in LoF without making a previous Discover Roll, but they must apply the Mimetism MOD indicated on the Marker.

## Camouflaged - Sensor 
By declaring Sensor, the user may make a Normal WIP +6 Roll (without applying Range or Mimetism MODs) to simultaneously Discover all enemy Troopers, Weapons or pieces of Equipment in the Hidden Deployment or Camouflaged States, who are inside the user's Zone of Control.

## Camouflaged - ARO > All At Once

All AROs are also simultaneous. This means that, if a Camouflage Marker declares a Move, the opponent can declare Discover with Troopers that have LoF to it, but they cannot declare Discover with one of the Troopers and BS Attack with the rest, waiting for the Discover Roll to be successful (see: Camouflaged State ).

Cancellation of Camouflaged State is applied to the entirety of the declared Order, even if the Skill revealing the Camouflaged Trooper is performed at the end of the Order.

## Camouflaged - ARO against a Marker:

- The only AROs that can be **declared** against a Camouflaged (CAMO) Marker are Discover, Dodge, Look Out!, or Reset.

- Against a Marker, the enemy can delay declaring their ARO until the Marker declares the second Short Skill of its Order.

- In this case, they may declare an ARO only if the Camouflaged Marker reveals themselves with the second Short Skill of their Order. If the second Short Skill of the Order does not reveal the Marker, the Reactive Trooper loses their right to declare an ARO.

Camouflaged Markers (CAMO) may be indirectly affected by Template Weapons if another non-Camouflaged Enemy Trooper is designated as the Main Target.

## Camouflaged - Examples

Step 1: A Camouflaged Caliban declares Move as their first Skill, moving until they reach Cover.

Step 2: The Fennec decides to delay declaring his ARO against the Marker.

Step 3: The Caliban declares BS Attack as the second Skill of their Order: He indicates that he will use his Boarding Shotgun in Burst Mode to shoot at the Fennec from Cover, which is the closest point.

Step 4: The Fennec declares BS Attack with his Combi Rifle, before the Caliban reaches the Cover.
Remember that the cancellation of the Camouflaged State applies to the entirety of the Order.

Resolution: By declaring BS Attack as the second Skill of their Order, the Caliban has revealed himself for the entire Order, which includes his movement towards the point in Cover.

The distance between the points where the Skills were declared is measured, and the Face to Face MODs are calculated.

## Camouflaged - FAQs

Q: Which Automatic Skills or Equipment have to be declared when in a Marker State?

A: Automatic Skills and Equipment that alter how an Order activates a Trooper, or restrict AROs, must be declared when used in Marker State. For example using Stealth.

Q: If a Trooper with Camouflage (1 Use) deploys as a Model, can the Trooper enter Camouflaged State during the game? Does this change if the Trooper fails an Infiltration Roll?

A: Yes, the Trooper can enter Camouflaged State later in the game. If the Trooper attempted to deploy as a Marker and failed the Infiltration Roll, they have used the Camouflaged State and cannot enter it later in the game.

Q: Does a Trooper in Disconnected State or Unconscious State prevent an enemy from re-entering Marker State, or cancel their Cautious Movement?

A: No.

### N5 FAQ v0.1

Q: What happens if an Attack hits a Camouflaged Marker but the actual game element represented by it has Total Cover from that Attack?

A: If the Attack would force a Saving Roll, the Marker is revealed. The underlying game element is not hit because it has Total Cover from that Attack.


# Camouflaged State — Unofficial Interaction Notes & Commentary
# ============================================================

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Core Concept
Camouflaged State (CAMO) protects a Trooper or game element by restricting the AROs that may be declared against its Marker and by requiring Discover before most direct attacks, unless a specific rule says otherwise.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Key Rule Interactions (General)
• Enemies may only **declare** the following AROs against a CAMO Marker: Discover, Dodge, Look Out!, Reset.  
• They may delay AROs where the Camouflaged State rules allow it.  
• A CAMO Marker has 360° LoF.  
• You cannot enter Silhouette contact with an enemy CAMO Marker.  
• You cannot normally attack a CAMO Marker without Discover unless a specific rule permits it.  
• Cancellation conditions include declaring an Attack, declaring a Skill that requires a Roll, declaring most Long Skills, entering Silhouette contact, being Discovered, being forced to make a Saving Roll, becoming Impetuous, or entering Retreat!.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] N5 FAQ v0.1 — Total Cover case
A Camouflaged Marker being a valid visible marker target does not remove Total Cover from the actual concealed game element. If an Attack against the Marker would force a Saving Roll, the Marker can be revealed while the actual element remains unharmed because it was in Total Cover.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Timing Pitfalls
• CAMO cancellation applies to the entirety of the declared Order when the rules say the Trooper reveals during that Order.  
• A successful Discover replaces the Marker with its Model as specified by the rule.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Discover Interaction Notes
• A Trooper that fails Discover on a Marker cannot attempt Discover again on that same Marker until the next Player Turn.  
• If the same Trooper later re-enters CAMO, it is not treated as the same Marker for that restriction.  
• All AROs are simultaneous; Reactive Troopers cannot condition later ARO declarations on whether another Discover Roll succeeds.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] MSV3 Interaction
• MSV3 can permit BS Attacks directly against CAMO Markers under its own rule, applying the indicated Mimetism MOD.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Sensor Interaction
• Sensor provides an efficient way to Discover Camouflaged and Hidden Deployment game elements in its area under the Sensor rule.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Mines & Template Weapons
• Templates may affect a CAMO Marker indirectly where another legal Main Target causes the Template to cover the Marker.  
• Check the current Mine/Deployable rules for their interaction with Markers rather than assuming a Discover requirement.

## [UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] Tactical Use
• CAMO is useful for safe movement, forcing Discover decisions, hiding identity, and setting up Surprise Attack where the Trooper has the relevant Skill.  
• Sensor, direct Marker-interaction rules, and incidental Templates are important counters.
