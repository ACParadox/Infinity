# Sepsitorized State

Game States and Glossary

- A Cube -wearing Trooper failed a Saving Roll against a Sepsitor Attack.

- Restriction: If a Trooper enters Sepsitorized State they automatically stop being part of their Combat Group.

- Restriction: Troopers in this state cannot be activated or receive Orders from their player's Order Pool. While they are Sepsitorized, Troopers are considered to be enemies by the rest of their owner's Troopers and are considered to be allies by the Troopers of the player that caused this game state.

- Restriction: Troopers in this state do not contribute Orders during the Tactical Phases of either player.

- Troopers in this state may be activated and receive Orders from the Order Pool of the Combat Group of the Trooper who caused them to enter the Sepsitorized state. A Sepsitorized Trooper does not count towards the maximum number of members the Combat Group may contain.

- This state does not interfere with Automatic Special Skills or Automatic Equipment, which will remain functioning normally.

- Restriction: A Sepsitorized Trooper does not count towards either Players' Victory Points.

- Restriction: A Trooper in Sepsitorized State cannot be part of a Fireteam. If a member of a Fireteam enters Sepsitorized State, they automatically stop being part of the Fireteam.

    
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Sepsitorized State represents total domination of a Trooper’s mind by the EI. The Trooper immediately becomes *Enemy to its former owner* and *Ally to the Sepsitor user* (Combined Army). The original controller loses all control: Orders, AROs, LoF declaration, etc. This makes Sepsitorized one of the most powerful and disruptive States in Infinity.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] – Order Control Interactions**
- A Sepsitorized Trooper *stops contributing its Order to its original Combat Group* because it is no longer an Ally.
- The Trooper also *does not contribute an Order to the Combined Army player*, as the State does not grant them a new Order. They can only be activated by spending *Regular or Irregular Orders* of the controlling player, just like possessing an enemy TAG via Hacking.
- If the Trooper was the Lieutenant, the original owner immediately enters *Loss of Lieutenant* on their next Tactical Phase. Sepsitorized counts as a Null State for that purpose.
- The new controller does **not** gain a Lieutenant Order even if the Sepsitorized Trooper had the Lieutenant Skill.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] – Skills & Equipment That Stop Working**
- Automatic Skills that require “Allied Trooper” conditions (e.g., providing bonuses to allies, Fireteam benefits, Coordinated Orders participation) immediately stop working.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] – Fireteam Interactions (tricky and often missed)**
- Becoming Sepsitorized immediately *removes the Trooper from its Fireteam*. This may break bonuses or dissolve the team entirely.
- If the Team Leader becomes Sepsitorized, the Fireteam loses its Leader and may be forced into a broken formation, removing Fireteam Bonuses immediately.
- The now-enemy Trooper might still be standing among other Fireteam members, potentially creating devastating ARO crossfire opportunities for the new controller.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] – States & Cancellation Interactions**
- Sepsitorized is not affected or cancelled by *Doctor*, *Engineer*, or *Reset*.
- *Possessed State* and *Sepsitorized State* share similarities: both flip alignment and both allow the new player to activate using their own Orders. However:
  - Sepsitorized cannot be cancelled except by the Trooper entering a Null State (Unconscious or Dead).
- If the Trooper enters Unconscious and then is healed (Doctor/Regeneration), the Trooper returns to the table still **Sepsitorized**, because the State does not specify cancellation on recovery.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] – ARO Interactions**
- Because alignment flips, the original owner must now treat the Trooper as an *Enemy* for LoF, ARO, template interactions, and restrictions.
- The new controller’s models may declare AROs in support of the Sepsitorized Trooper.
- *Zone of Control ARO triggers* now treat the Sepsitorized Trooper as if it always belonged to the new controller.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] – Command Token Interactions**
- The original owner cannot move the Trooper between Combat Groups, cannot reform a Fireteam around it, and cannot include it in Coordinated Orders.
- The new controller can include it in Coordinated Orders, provided it meets all normal eligibility requirements.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] – Similarities and Differences: Sepsitorized vs Possessed**
Similarities:
- Both change the Trooper’s alignment.
- Both allow the controlling player to activate the unit with their own Orders.
- Both prevent the original player from using the model’s skills, orders, or AROs.

Differences:
- *Cancellation*: Possessed can be ended via Total Control; Sepsitorized can only end via the Trooper entering a Null State.
- *Profile*: Possessed uses some of the "Possessed profile" attributes instead of the Troopers'.
- *Common targets*: Sepsitors mostly target LI/MI/HI without BTS 6 immunity; Possession is often restricted to TAGs.
- *Recovery consequences*: Healing a Unconscious Sepsitorized Trooper keeps them under enemy control; 

Pros of rules design:
- Sepsitorized creates dramatic board-swinging moments.
- Maintains the target’s full statline, making it an extremely potent control tool.

Cons:
- Difficult bookkeeping for new players.
- Creates rare corner cases where Fireteams break at unexpected times or Orders become ambiguous.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] – Practical Tactical Notes**
- Never leave key pieces alone in Sepsitor threat range without Supportware or Repeater coverage.
- Troopers with high ARM and short-range weapons (e.g., Boarding Shotguns) are especially dangerous when turned against you.
- If you cannot prevent the Sepsitor attack, position so that a Sepsitorized Trooper has no meaningful LoF or movement lanes toward your main force.
- If facing the Combined Army, use expendable units to screen valuable models from template or direct-Sepsitor angles.
