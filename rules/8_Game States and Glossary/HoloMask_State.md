# HoloMask State

Game States and Glossary

- Automatic when deployed.

- During the Active Turn, Troopers may only return to this state by spending a Long Skill, while outside the LoF of enemy Markers or Troopers.

- While in HoloMask State, players don't place the HoloMask bearer's Model but the Model of any other Trooper (known as the imitated Model), declaring which weapon option it is duplicating from the imitated Model's Unit Profile.

- Restriction: The imitated Model must be a Trooper from the bearer's faction or Sectorial Army and must have the same Silhouette value as the bearer. However, the player will use the real Unit Profile of the HoloMask bearer, just as it appears on their Army List.

- Restriction: Troopers in HoloMask State cannot replicate the Hacker / Hackable status of the imitated Trooper. For example, even if they are imitating a Hacker or a Hackable Trooper, if they enter or are in the enemy's Hacking Area, the player must declare their lack of Hacker/Hackable status.

- Restriction: A Trooper in HoloMask State cannot replicate those Deployable weapons or pieces of Equipment represented by Tokens or Models ( TinBots, FastPandas, SymbioMates, Mines deployed with the Minelayer Special Skill...) that the imitated Model has.

- In order to Discover a Trooper in the HoloMask State, the Enemy must pass a Discover Roll.

- If the Discover is successful, replace the imitated Model with the real Trooper's Model, facing the same direction as the imitated Model.

- This state does not interfere with Automatic Special Skills or Automatic Equipment, which will remain functioning normally.

## Cancelling HoloMask

A Trooper's HoloMask State is canceled, replacing the imitated Model with the real Model, whenever:

- The Trooper in HoloMask State declares an Attack, Look Out!, or any Skill that requires a Roll.

- The Trooper in HoloMask State declares a Long Skill of any kind, other than Cautious Movement.

- The Trooper in HoloMask State enters Silhouette contact with an enemy Model.

- The Trooper in HoloMask State is forced to make a Saving Roll.

- The Trooper in HoloMask State is Discovered.

- A Trooper in HoloMask State is or becomes Impetuous (due to the Frenzy Special Skill or any other effect) or enters Retreat! State.

In addition:

- Whenever HoloMask State is canceled, replace the imitated Model with the real Model, facing the same direction as the imitated Model, at the end of the Order in which the state was cancelled.

- When replacing the imitated Model with the real one, the player must provide their adversary with the Trooper's complete Open Information.

## HoloMask

- During the Deployment Phase, it allows the user to deploy in HoloMask State.

- In game, it allows the user to enter HoloMask State.

Cancellation of HoloMask State is applied to the entirety of the declared Order, even if the Skill revealing the Trooper is performed at the end of the Order.

The presence of Troopers in HoloMask or Holoecho State is Private Information. This includes any Special Skills, weapons, and pieces of Equipment they have.

## HoloMask Examples

A Trooper in HoloMask State who is imitating a different Trooper with Mimetism (-3) is the target of an Enemy BS Attack. Even though their Special Skills, weapons and pieces of Equipment are considered Private Information, the HoloMasked Trooper cannot apply the Mimetism (-3) Special Skill of the other Trooper. This means that during the Resolution step of the Order, when MODs are applied, the HoloMasked Trooper's player must inform his opponent that there is no Mimetism MOD to apply.

A Fireteam Core includes a Trooper in HoloMask State who is imitating another Fireteam member. The Fireteam declares a Move + BS Attack Order. The HoloMasked Trooper, who is not the Team Leader, moves and provides support to the Team Leader. As they perform an Idle, the HoloMasked Trooper is not revealed, and so remains in HoloMask State.

However, in the following Order, the Team Leader declares Dodge, so all the Fireteam members make a PH Roll. In this situation, the HoloMasked Trooper is revealed automatically.


## HoloMask State — Interactions & Unofficial Commentary
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

### Core Concepts
HoloMask is an identity-disguise State. The trooper places the *model of another legal trooper profile* instead of its real miniature, respecting:
- Same Silhouette requirement.  
- Same Faction/Sectorial requirement.  
- Real profile is always used for gameplay (WIP, ARM, weapons, BS, Skills, etc.).

It is canceled by:
- Declaring Attacks, Look Out!, Long Skills (except Cautious Move), or *any* Skill requiring a Roll.  
- Entering Silhouette contact.  
- Being forced to make a Saving Roll.  
- Being Discovered.  
- Becoming Impetuous or entering Retreat!  
(All per official cancellation list.)

### Interaction Notes (Unofficial)
**1. Saving Rolls are the most common accidental reveal trigger.**  
Players often forget that even a **template scattering onto you** or taking a **Normal Roll hit** forces a Saving Roll and therefore reveals the disguise.

**2. HoloMask does not protect against information revealed by required game declarations.**  
Even though the imitated model is placed on the table, the real unit’s Hackability must be disclosed if it enters Hacking Area. This is a frequent giveaway.

**3. Automatic Skills and Equipment always function on the real profile.**  
The disguise has *no effect* on underlying automatic abilities such as Immunities, Martial Arts Level, or ARM/BTS values.

**4. Discover against HoloMask is a standard Discover Roll.**  
Discovered → Replace the imitated model with the real one, facing the same direction.  
This is often forgotten, leading some players to incorrectly re-face the model.

**5. Fireteam Interaction is subtler than players expect.**  
HoloMask is legal inside Fireteams **until** a roll is made by the disguised member.  
- Moving/idling with a Fireteam = safe.  
- Fireteam **Dodge** = forces a PH roll → reveals immediately.  
- Fireteam **Reset** = forces a WIP roll → reveals immediately.

**6. HoloMask does not copy non-token equipment or Skills.**  
Examples frequently misunderstood:  
- The disguised model showing Mimetism does **not** grant the trooper Mimetism.  
- The disguised model carrying a Deployable Device (Mine, Koala, Panda, etc.) cannot cause that device to appear or exist.

**7. Psychological interaction with Discover sequencing.**  
Because HoloMask looks like a normal model, opponents may skip Discover opportunities in situations where a Camo/Impersonation Marker would certainly provoke one. Skilled players exploit this inertia.

**8. Interaction with Surprise Attack.**  
HoloMask does **not** provide Surprise Attack.  
The trooper must actually have that Skill—and must also be in a legal State to use it (HoloMask is *not* a Marker State and does not qualify).

**9. Interaction with States that modify Identity Visibility.**  
Becoming Impetuous ends HoloMask immediately, which particularly impacts Frenzy units.  
This is a key reason why HoloMask is rarely seen on Frenzy profiles.

## Frequently Misplayed Edge Cases
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

**• When exactly does revelation occur?**  
HoloMask is canceled for the *entire declared Order*, even if the revealing Skill is declared at the end. The replacement of the model happens at the **end of the Order**, not mid-declaration.

**• Can an opponent demand open information early?**  
No. HoloMask, its presence, and the real profile are Private Information until a reveal condition occurs.

**• Does being targeted reveal you?**  
No. Only rolling a dice (Attack, Dodge, Reset, Save) or fulfilling another cancellation clause reveals you.

## Comparisons — HoloMask vs Other Deception States
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

### HoloMask vs Camouflage
- HoloMask: identity deception.  
- Camouflage: visibility denial (Marker State).  

**Pros of HoloMask:**  
- Functions in Fireteams.  
- Can mimic Specialists/weaker units to mislead.  
**Cons vs Camouflage:**  
- Does not impose Mimetism MODs.  
- Does not restrict targeting; enemy may shoot freely without Discover.


## Tactical Applications (Unofficial)
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

**1. Lieutenant Protection**  
Disguising your Lieutenant as a generic Line Infantry forces opponents to spend Orders confirming identity.

**2. Fireteam Composition Deception**  
You can imply a different Fireteam role (e.g., show a “Paramedic” while actually being a Hacker).

**3. Baiting Enemy Attack Runs**  
Presenting a “weak” trooper may lure opponents into bad trades, discovering too late that the disguised model is a gunfighter.


## Common Pitfalls
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Failing to match Silhouette is a rules violation and immediate giveaway.  
- Forgetting to declare non-Hackable status when inside Hacking Area.  
- Using illegal weapons/equipment of the imitated profile (e.g., dropping a Mine).  
- Attempting Surprise Attack from HoloMask.  
- Overly “perfect” disguises: missing obvious fireteam bonuses, visible actual gear, or faction mismatches ruin the bluff.
