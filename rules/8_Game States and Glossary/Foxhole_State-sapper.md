# Sapper

Special Skills
 DEPLOYMENT SKILL / LONG SKILL
Optional 

This Special Skill allows its user to deploy in a fortified emplacement that provides some in-game advantages.

- The space in which the user activates Foxhole State must have a height and width equal or greater than the Silhouette Template of this State.

- Otherwise, the Trooper will not be able to enter Foxhole State and will perform an Idle instead.

- In the Deployment Phase, the user may deploy in Foxhole State, placing a Foxhole Token beside them.

- In the Active Turn, a Trooper can use this Long Skill to enter Foxhole State and place a Foxhole Token beside them.

# Foxhole State

Game States and Glossary

- Automatic during the Deployment Phase for those Troopers with the Sapper Special Skill.

- During the Active Turn, Troopers may only activate this state by spending a Long Skill and placing a Foxhole Token beside them.

- The Trooper has a Silhouette Attribute value of 3 (S3). If the Trooper's Unit Profile has a higher Silhouette Attribute value, they use that value while in this state.

- The Trooper has Partial Cover in a 360˚ arc, from all directions and angles.

- The Trooper has the Mimetism (-3) and Courage Special Skills.

- Foxhole State is a fixed position which doesn't allow its user to perform any type of movement, including movement granted by a successful Dodge Roll.

- The Trooper enters Prone State.

- A Trooper in Foxhole State in the Active Turn may automatically cancel this state at the beginning of their movement by declaring a Skill with the Movement Label. The player must announce he is Canceling Foxhole State when declaring the Skill. By doing so, the state is canceled at no cost and the Trooper can carry out its Movement using its regular MOV and S values. In the same way, in the Active Turn, the Trooper may automatically cancel this state at the beginning of a Dodge move.

- When Canceling Foxhole State, the Trooper loses all the advantages provided by it, the Foxhole State Token is removed from the game table and the Trooper recovers his MOV and Silhouette values.

# Foxhole State - Sapper

- In the Deployment Phase, the user may deploy in Foxhole State, placing a Foxhole Token beside them.

- In the Active Turn, a Trooper can use this Long Skill to enter Foxhole State and place a Foxhole Token beside them.

The space in which the user of this Special Skill activates Foxhole State must have a height and width equal or greater than the Silhouette Template of this state.

## Entering and Leaving Foxhole
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
- Entering Foxhole usually requires declaring the appropriate Skill (e.g., Sapper) as part of an Order.  
- You cannot enter Foxhole in ARO unless a specific rule grants it.  
- Leaving Foxhole typically happens automatically when the Trooper performs movement that is incompatible with staying stationary, or when they voluntarily cancel the State.

**Common misunderstanding:**  
Players often assume Foxhole is maintained even while performing Move. It is not: movement normally cancels Foxhole unless explicitly permitted by a rule.

---

## Effects on Silhouette and LoF
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
- Foxhole reduces the Trooper’s effective Silhouette for LoF and Cover calculations, usually treated as a smaller Silhouette footprint.  
- This creates powerful defensive angles but can also reduce the Trooper’s firing elevation or ability to shoot over obstacles.

**Tricky Interaction:**  
If a Trooper in Foxhole is replaced by a Marker (Camouflage, Holoecho, etc.), determining the “reduced Silhouette” often raises questions. The State is attached to the Trooper, not the Marker, but the Marker uses its own Silhouette. Raw rules frequently fail to directly describe this interaction, so players must rely on TO rulings or house rulings.

---

## Foxhole / Sapper and Cover
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
- Entering Foxhole almost always grants Partial Cover, even if the Trooper appears visually exposed from some angles.  
- This is a *special rule*, not the normal Cover requirement.  
- AP ammo does **not** reduce this defensive benefit in N5 because Cover applies to Saving Throw MODs, not ARM.

**Comparison: Foxhole vs. Regular Cover**  
- Regular Cover requires obscuration and being in contact with terrain.  
- Foxhole grants Cover by State, not by geometry.  
- Advantage: You retain Cover even if your bodily position becomes exposed.  
- Disadvantage: You often lose mobility and must break the State to move.

---

## Foxhole / Sapper and Fireteams
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
- Fireteam members can enter Foxhole, but the Team Leader’s actions may cause members to break Foxhole if they need to move to maintain Coherency.  
- If one member enters Foxhole and others must stay in LoF lanes, Foxhole may be lost immediately when the Fireteam declares a movement-based Order.

**Tip:**  
Using Foxhole inside a Fireteam is most effective only when the Team Leader remains stationary or when the Foxhole unit is *not* the Team Leader.

---

## Foxhole / Sapper Pros & Cons Summary
**Pros**
- Grants reliable Cover regardless of scenery.  
- Reduces Silhouette, creating harder LoF angles for opponents.  
- Excellent for long-range reactive pieces.
- Gives Mimetism -3

**Cons**
- Limits mobility; movement cancels the State.  
- Poor synergy with Fireteams that must reposition frequently.  
- Can reduce firing arcs or visibility depending on terrain height.

---

## Foxhole / Sapper Commonly Missed Rules Interactions
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- If a Trooper is forced to move by Guts Roll, Foxhole is immediately lost before movement is applied.  
- Engineers cannot “repair” Foxhole because it is a State, not a Structure or piece of Equipment.

---

## Foxhole / Sapper Practical Tactics
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Use Foxhole to lock down firelanes when you expect sustained shooting.  
- Break Foxhole only when repositioning meaningfully; do not “waste” Orders re-entering Foxhole unless you gain a critical defensive advantage.  
- Consider pairing Foxhole units with TinBots or defensive Supportware to increase resilience.
