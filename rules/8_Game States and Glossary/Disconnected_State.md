# Disconnected State

Game States and Glossary

- The Controller of this Trooper is in Isolated State or any Null State.

- The Peripheral fails a Coherency Check.

- The Peripheral is subject to a successful Attack or Effect of an Ammunition, Hacking Program, condition, or Scenario Special Rule that specifies that it causes this state.

- The Peripheral is in Isolated State.

- Restriction: The Peripheral cannot be activated or declare Orders or AROs.

- Automatic Special Skills and Automatic Equipment have no effect while in this state, with the exception of the Peripheral Special Skill.

- Restriction: The Peripheral does not count towards the Player's Victory Points.

- This state is automatically canceled if the Controller of this Peripheral recovers from the Isolated State or Null State which activated the Disconnected State.

- If this state was caused by an Attack, a piece of scenery, or a Scenario Special Rule applied to this Trooper, then a Trooper with the Engineer Special Skill (or an equivalent Skill) may cancel this state by spending one Short Skill of an Order while in Silhouette contact with the affected Trooper, and passing a Normal WIP Roll (or the Roll specified by the Special Skill or Scenario).

- If this state was caused by failing a Coherency Check, it is automatically canceled by passing a Coherency Check at the end of an Order or ARO.

## Disconnected State FAQs

Q: Does a Trooper in Disconnected State or Unconscious State prevent an enemy from re-entering Marker State, or cancel their Cautious Movement?

A: No.



**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
The **Disconnected State** ony affects **Peripherals**, and certain equipment that requires a Controller. While simple in concept (“you lose functionality until reconnected”), it has a number of edge-case interactions players often miss.

# GENERAL FUNCTION

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
A Trooper in **Disconnected**:

- Cannot declare any Skills except those explicitly allowed by rules (normally: *Idle* until reconnected).  
- Loses the ability to benefit from Supportware or Control skills.  
- Is still considered **on the table** (not Null) for scoring zones and Victory Points unless a mission states otherwise.  
- Retains its silhouette, LoF, and can still be targeted normally.

---

# CAUSES OF DISCONNECTED

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Typical ways a Trooper becomes Disconnected:

- The Controller (for Peripherals) becomes **Isolated**, **Null**, or otherwise loses the connection.  

Think of Disconnected as “the lights are on but nobody’s driving.”

---

# CANCELLING DISCONNECTED

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
A Trooper is normally reconnected via:

- **Engineer** skill (WIP roll).  
- **Controller restoring control** (ending Isolated, standing up from Unconscious, etc.).  
- Sometimes **States Phase automatic re-link** if mission rules specify.

A key nuance:  
If the Controller recovers mid-turn (e.g., Doctor or Engineer heals them), **the Peripheral does not automatically recover** unless the rule explicitly states it. Some require a new connection check or Engineer action.


# INTERACTIONS WITH PERIPHERALS

### Interaction with Coherency:
- Peripherals have to maintain Coherency when activated.  
- When Disconnected, they **cannot activate**, so Coherency is not checked for them.  
- They may remain totally out of ZoC with no consequence until reconnected.


**Pros/Cons comparison**

### Disconnected  
+ Hard shutdown; easy to track.  
− Extremely punishing: no activation, no orders, no self-recovery.

### Immobilized  
+ Less severe; the Trooper can still Reset or declare non-movement, non-attack skills.  
− Still cripples offensive capability.

### Isolated  
+ Trooper can still act, just inefficiently.  
− Devastating for Order-hungry REM or HI lists.

# COMMON TRICKY SITUATIONS

### **1. Can a Disconnected Peripheral ARO?**  
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
No. Disconnected Troopers cannot declare AROs unless a special rule explicitly overrides the state.

---

### **2. Does a Disconnected Trooper block Line of Fire?**  
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Yes. It remains a physical model on the table.

---

### **3. Does a Disconnected Trooper count for scoring zones?**  
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**  
Yes, unless the mission or ITS packet says otherwise. It is not a Null state.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**

- Keep Controllers safe; Peripherals are order-efficient but brittle when link is lost.  
- When fighting REM-heavy armies, targeting Controllers or applying Isolated often yields better payoff than shooting the REMs themselves.
