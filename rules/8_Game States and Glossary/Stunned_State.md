# Stunned State and Stun

Game States and Glossary

- The Trooper or game element ("Trooper" from now on) suffers a successful Attack or Effect using an Ammunition, Hacking Program, or a game condition or Scenario Special Rule, capable of causing this state.

- Restriction: Troopers in this state cannot declare Attacks.

- Moreover, Troopers in Stunned State must apply a MOD of -3 to any Roll they perform, except Saving Rolls.

- Automatic Special Skills and Equipment continue to work, but the Trooper must still respect all declaration restrictions.

- To cancel this state, Troopers with the Vitality (VITA) Attribute require a Doctor while Troopers with the Structure (STR) Attribute require an Engineer.

- A Trooper with the Doctor/Engineer Special Skill (or an equivalent Skill) may cancel this state by spending one Short Skill of an Order while in Silhouette contact with the affected Trooper and passing a Normal WIP Roll (or the Roll specified by the Special Skill or Scenario).

- The Stunned State is automatically canceled at the start of the States Phase of the Player Turn in which it was caused.

## Stunned/Stun and Stun Ammunition

- A failed Saving Roll against Stun Ammunition causes the target to enter Stunned State. Place a Stunned State Token (STUNNED) next to it.

## Stunned/Stun and Cybermines

- Troopers who are hit by a Cybermine must make two Saving Rolls against BTS, with PS 5. If the target fails either Saving Roll, it enters Stunned State.

- However, any Troopers with the Hackable Characteristic ( HI, REM, TAG, VH...) and Hackers who fail either Saving Roll enter Immobilized-B State instead of Stunned State.

## 1. Core Effects Recap (from rules)
- Troopers in Stunned State **cannot declare Attacks**.  
- Apply **–3 MOD to all Rolls except Saving Rolls**.  
- Automatic Skills/Equipment still function, but declaration restrictions remain.  
- Canceled at the **start of the next States Phase** of the player whose Trooper was stunned.  
- Can also be canceled by **Doctor (VITA)** or **Engineer (STR)** via WIP roll in Silhouette contact.

These core effects create the unique role of Stun as a **soft-disabling**, **short-duration** control state.
---

## 2. Stun vs Common Game Interactions

### 2.1 Stun vs ARO
**Key point:** Stunned Troopers **cannot Attack**, even in ARO.  
They may still declare non-Attack AROs:
- Dodge  
- Reset  
- Look Out!  
- Engage (only if allowed and considered non-Attack in your edition; if ambiguous, treat as house-ruling)  

**Impact:**  
Stun cripples long-range ARO pieces such as snipers, missile launchers, TAGs, or heavy infantry gun platforms. They remain present on the table but *do nothing threatening* for one turn.

---

### 2.2 Stun vs Face-to-Face Rolls
Stunned Troopers may still oppose Rolls **as long as their declaration is legal**.

For example:
- A Stunned Trooper may Dodge against enemy fire: this produces a legal Face-to-Face roll.
- They cannot declare BS Attack or CC Attack, so they cannot Face-to-Face an attacker using those Skills.

Because Dodge is still legal, Stun does **not** completely shut down defense, but it **eliminates offensive FTF options**.

---

### 2.3 Stun vs Suppressive Fire
Entering Stunned State immediately **cancels Suppressive Fire**, because the Trooper can no longer declare Attacks and therefore cannot maintain the State.

This is one of the strongest tactical applications of Stun:  
**turning off suppressive fire at range without killing the model.**

---

### 2.4 Stun vs Hacking and Comms Attacks
Stun does **not** prevent:
- Declaring Reset  
- Declaring Hacking Programs *unless* the Program is considered an Attack (most are)

Since many Hacking Programs have the **Attack** label, Stun effectively disables most hacking actions.  
However:
- Support programs or non-Attack Quantronic abilities remain usable.

---

### 2.5 Stun vs Courage and Guts Rolls
Rules note: failing a Stun save causes the trooper to **automatically fail the subsequent Guts Roll unless they have Courage or equivalent**.

**Meaning:**  
Courage is disproportionately powerful against Stun.  
Stunned + forced Guts failure can move defensive ARO pieces out of position—*unless they have Courage*.

---

### 2.6 Stun vs Cybermines
Cybermines cause:
- **Stunned State** on most units  
- **Immobilized-B instead of Stunned** when the target is HI, REM, TAG, Vehicle, or a Hacker

Thus Cybermines invert Stun into a much stronger effect on Hackable units.

---

## 3. Comparisons With Other Disabling States

### 3.1 Stun vs Immobilized-A
**Stun**
- Applies –3 MOD  
- No Attacks  
- Still can Move and Dodge/Reset normally  
- Auto-clears next States Phase  
- Can be repaired by Doctor/Engineer depending on STR/VITA

**Immobilized-A and B**
- Target can only **Dodge or Reset**  
- Cannot Move   
- Clears only on a **successful Dodge or Reset** or Engineer

**Pros of Stun:**  
- Easier to apply and short duration  
- Strong against shooters  
**Cons:**  
- Does not restrict movement , only stops attacks 
- Dodge remains strong at full PH

## 4. Tricky or Frequently Misplayed Points

**1. Stunned Troopers MAY Dodge and Reset.**  
Many players mistakenly believe Stunned = no Rolls at all; the restriction is specifically “cannot declare Attacks”.

**2. Stun removes both Active and Reactive Attacks.**  
This makes it unique among States because it simultaneously stops:
- Outgoing attacks on your turn  
- ARO threats on the opponent's turn

**3. Stun does *not* prevent movement.**  
Troopers can Move, Climb, Jump, and retreat as needed.

**4. Stun auto-clears on the affected player’s next States Phase.**  
This means applying Stun during your opponent’s turn is **less impactful** than applying it during your own.

**5. Stun is Non-Lethal but still impacts Guts Rolls.**  
Especially important when displacing key defenders.

---

## 5. Tactical Uses

### Use 1 — Breaking ARO Pieces
Throwing Stun at:
- Missile Launchers  
- Snipers  
- TAGs in ARO  
instantly turns them off.

### Use 2 — Opening Safe Movement Paths
Stunned enemies cannot retaliate, letting you:
- Push Specialists  
- Advance Fireteams  
- Reposition vulnerable assets

### Use 3 — Softening a Target Before Assault
Because Stun applies –3 MOD to all non-Save Rolls, it reduces:
- Dodge odds  
- Reset odds  
- Technical Skill Rolls (Hacking, Sensor, etc.)

## 6. Summary
Stun is a **short-duration**, **non-lethal**, **attack-preventing** state ideal for:
- Turning off ARO platforms  
- Softening targets for follow-up  
- In ARO, stopping a main active turn attack piece

It is weaker than IMM-B or Isolated but significantly **more order-efficient** and **reliable at range**, especially when delivered via:
- Stun Grenades  
- Stun Pistols  

Stun shines as a **tempo tool**, letting you advance safely without needing to kill your opponent’s models outright.
