# Immobilized-B State

Game States and Glossary

- The Trooper or game element ("Trooper" from now on) suffers a successful Attack or Effect using an Ammunition, Hacking Program or a game condition or Scenario Special Rule, capable of causing this state.

- Troopers in Immobilized-B State cannot declare any Skill, Attack or ARO, except Reset applying a -3 WIP MOD.

- Automatic Special Skills and Equipment continue to work, but the Trooper must still respect all declaration restrictions.

- Troopers in the Immobilized-B state continue to provide Orders for their Order Pool.

- The affected Trooper may cancel this state via a successful Normal or Face to Face Reset Roll, applying the -3 WIP MOD provided by this state.

- A Trooper with the Engineer Special Skill (or an equivalent Skill) may cancel the Immobilized-B state by spending one Short Skill of an Order while in Silhouette contact with the affected Trooper, and passing a Normal WIP Roll (or the Roll specified by the Special Skill or Scenario).

## Immobilized-B and Carbonite

- Any failed Saving Roll causes the target to enter Immobilized-B State, placing an Immobilized-B State Token (IMM-B) by them.

## Immobilized-B and Electromagnetic (E/M) Ammunition

- If the target fails a Saving Roll and is Heavy Infantry ( HI ), a TAG, a Remote ( REM ), or a Vehicle ( VH ), then it also enters Immobilized-B State. Place an Immobilized-B Token (IMM-B) next to it in addition to the Isolated Token.

## Immobilized-B and Cybermines

- However, any Troopers with the Hackable Characteristic ( HI, REM, TAG, VH...) and Hackers who fail either Saving Roll enter Immobilized-B State instead of Stunned State.


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Immobilized-B represents a full tactical lockout: the Trooper cannot perform any Skill except **Reset**, and may only declare it at **–3 WIP**. It is one of the most restrictive non-Null States in the game and is usually inflicted by Hacking Programs (e.g., Carbonite), EM Ammunition, or scenario effects.


## Declaration Restrictions

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
A Trooper in Immobilized-B:

- Cannot declare **Move**, **Dodge**, **BS Attack**, **CC Attack**, **Discover**, **Idle**, **Look Out!**, **Reset** (allowed), or any other Skill.
- May **only** declare **Reset**, and must apply **–3 WIP** to it, both as ARO and Active Skill.
- Automatic Skills still function (e.g., Mimetism, Sixth Sense, Martial Arts MODs that apply automatically), but do not override the declaration ban. For example, a Mimetism trooper still imposes Mimetism MODs, but cannot Dodge or Attack.

Players often forget that the *only legal declaration* is Reset, even in situations where Dodge would seem allowed (e.g., reacting to a Template). IMM-B disables Dodge completely.

## ARO Interactions

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Immobilized-B replaces all ARO options with **Reset –3**.  
Even being attacked by a Template does not permit Dodge — Reset is still the only option.

A Reset ARO suffers –3 from the State, and may also take additional MODs (e.g., Surprise Attack –3, Cybermask MODs, or TinBot firewall bonuses/penalties when Resetting against Hacking). Don't forget the Isolation penalty to Reset stacks with this also.



## Cancellation Methods and Timing

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Two cancellation paths exist:

1. **Self-cancellation:**  
   Declare **Reset** (Normal or Face to Face), succeed at WIP –3.  
   • If multiple enemies attack, only effects targeting the Resetting Trooper are opposed; Reset does not protect allied models (per N5.2 Dodge/Reset FAQ).

2. **Engineer:**  
   A Trooper with Engineer may cancel IMM-B with a **Short Skill**, making a **Normal WIP Roll** while in Silhouette contact.  
   • Engineer bonuses, rerolls, GizmoKit modifiers, or scenario-specific rolls may apply.

## IMM-B and Face to Face Rolls

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Reset **can** be Face to Face when opposing:
- BS Attacks (because Reset affects the trooper’s ability to resist effects if target of the attack)
- Hacking Attacks (most common)
- CC Attacks (though IMM-B Troopers cannot declare CC Attack, they can still declare Reset in CC)

However, Reset does **not** oppose:
- Template weapons that do not target the IMM-B Trooper directly (e.g., being secondarily caught in a blast)
- Attacks against *other* targets in the same order (per Dodge/Reset FAQ)

## IMM-A vs IMM-B — Differences, Pros, Cons

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
IMM-A and IMM-B are often confused.

**IMM-A:**  
- Total Skill lockout except Dodge.  
- Usually caused by Adhesive weapons, physical immobilization.

**IMM-B:**  
- Total Skill lockout except Reset.  
- Typically hacking/EM related immobilization.

## Immobilized-B and States Stacking

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
IMM-B stacks with:
- Isolated  
- Targeted  
- Immobilized-A  

If IMM-B is applied to a Marker:
- It immediately becomes a Model due to suffering an Attack/Effect.
- IMM-B then applies normally.

Stacking IMM-B with Targeted is especially devastating since the IMM-B Trooper cannot Move, Dodge, or Seek Cover.
And Stacking IMM-B with Isolated means target has an additional -9 to Reset so the max -12 MOD to Reset, making it pretty much taken out of the game unless there's an Engineer.

## Practical Tactical Notes

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- IMM-B is best used to stall enemy attack pieces or lock down midfield skirmishers.
- Engineer protection dramatically increases durability against hacking-based lists.
- IMM-B pairs extremely well with follow-up attacks since the victim cannot dodge or counter.

For the victim:
- Reset in ARO whenever possible — even low odds accumulate over repeated Orders.
- Consider positioning around Engineers before advancing high-value targets.
- Troopers with high WIP (e.g., Aleph, Nomads) are more resilient to IMM-B through Reset spam.
