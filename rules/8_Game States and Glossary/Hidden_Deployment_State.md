# Hidden Deployment

Special Skills
 DEPLOYMENT SKILL
Optional, Private Information 

Some Troopers have a superior capacity to blend in with their environment, which allows them to go completely unnoticed by the Enemy.

- During the Deployment Phase, it allows the user to deploy in Hidden Deployment State.

# Hidden Deployment State

Game States and Glossary

- Automatic in the Deployment Phase for Troopers with the Hidden Deployment Skill.

- The Hidden Deployment State is a special form of deployment that allows the user to deploy during the Deployment Phase without placing a Model or Marker on the battlefield.

- In your Deployment Phase, write down the position of your Hidden Deployment Troopers in as much detail as possible (make sure to specify whether the Trooper is in Cover, Prone, etc.) in order to show your opponent when their State is canceled and their position revealed.

- Until a Trooper's Hidden Deployment State is canceled, that Trooper is considered not to be on the game table at all. Consequently, such a Trooper does not affect LoF, is not affected by Template Weapons, etc. However, the player must consider the written down position of the Trooper in Hidden Deployment, to check whether they may declare AROs.

- While in Hidden Deployment State, the Trooper will not add their Order to the Order Pool during the Tactical Phase. They can only use this Order themselves, when placing the Trooper on the gaming table during the Active Turn.

- Although the Trooper in Hidden Deployment is not considered to be on the table, if the Trooper has the Infiltration Special Skill and makes an Infiltration Roll, the Roll must be made once their position is written down. If the Roll is a success, the Trooper infiltrates and does not lose the Hidden Deployment State. If the Roll is failed, the Trooper's Model is placed within their player's Deployment Zone, always in contact with the edge of the game table.

- If a Trooper deploys in Hidden Deployment State and in Holoecho State, the player must write down the positions of the three Holoechoes on the game table and which of them is the real Trooper. When the Trooper's Hidden Deployment State is canceled you must perform a Coherency Check. Any Holoechoes that are out of Coherency have their Holoecho State cancelled.

- When a player moves a Hidden Deployment Trooper into a different Combat Group, they must inform their opponent that the Trooper exists. The Troopers's information remains Private, until the Trooper is revealed.

- Troopers in Hidden Deployment State can be selected to be part of a Coordinated Order. However, in this situation they will lose their own Order.

## Cancelling Hidden Deployment State

- Once a Troopers' Hidden Deployment State has been cancelled, they cannot regain it.

- If the Roll is a success, the Trooper infiltrates and does not lose the Hidden Deployment State.

- If the Roll is failed, the Trooper's Model is placed within their player's Deployment Zone, always in contact with the edge of the game table.

- The Hidden Deployment State is canceled if the user is Discovered by means of a Special Skill that explicitly allows it (for example Sensor ).

- The Hidden Deployment state is automatically canceled whenever the Trooper declares an Order or ARO.

- The Trooper's Model or Marker is placed on the game table, in the position you wrote down, facing in the direction of your choice.

- If the Trooper has a Skill that allows them to deploy in a Marker State (for example Camouflage ), they will be able to keep that State as long as: They declare Cautious Movement or any Basic Short Skill that does not require making a Roll (except Look Out!). They delay their ARO against a Marker. They prevent an Enemy Trooper from entering a Marker State.

- If the Trooper in a Marker State declares a Short Skill, Long Skill, or ARO other than those listed above, their Marker State is also cancelled. In this case, place the Model that represents the Trooper in the position you wrote down, facing in the direction of your choice.

- If a Model or a Marker, ally or enemy, occupies the same space as a Trooper in Hidden Deployment and this Trooper declares an Order or ARO, they are revealed and are placed as close as possible to their written down position. They cannot be placed in Silhouette contact with the Model or Marker.

- The Hidden Deployment State is canceled if the Trooper becomes Impetuous (due to a game situation) or enters Retreat! State.

- When the Model is placed on the battlefield, the player must provide their adversary with the Trooper's complete Open Information.

- They declare Cautious Movement or any Basic Short Skill that does not require making a Roll (except Look Out!).

- They delay their ARO against a Marker.

- They prevent an Enemy Trooper from entering a Marker State.

### Hidden Deployment

- During the Deployment Phase, it allows the user to deploy in Hidden Deployment State.

If a Trooper in Hidden Deployment wants to prevent another Trooper in LoF from returning to a Marker State, they must place the corresponding Model or Marker on the table at the moment the opponent declares their intention to return to Camouflaged, Impersonation etc. State

# Hidden Deployment State — Interactions, Notes, and Unofficial Commentary

## Core Understanding
Hidden Deployment allows a Trooper to exist on the table *only on paper* until revealed. The Trooper does not contribute an Order to the Order Pool, cannot be targeted, does not block LoF, and cannot be affected by any attack or template until the State is cancelled. 
Despite “not being on the table,” the controlling player must always check the written position for LoF/ZoC when determining whether the Trooper may declare an ARO.

---

## Key Interactions and Edge Cases

### 1. ARO Mechanics
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- A Trooper in Hidden Deployment may declare an ARO *if and only if* the written position would allow one.
- Declaring any ARO immediately cancels Hidden Deployment and places the model on the table.
- Revealing to ARO often results in **Normal Rolls** because the opponent usually cannot see the attacker before the reveal (common with long-range ambush snipers), aka opponent is using a Long Skill or the hidden Troopers sees the enmy on a second move only.

---

### 2. Declaring Orders in Active Turn
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Declaring any Order also cancels Hidden Deployment.
- The Trooper spends *its own Order*, which it did not contribute to the pool earlier.
- If placing the Model at the written location is illegal due to another Model/Marker occupying the space, the Trooper is placed as close as possible without entering Silhouette contact.

---

### 3. Interaction With Marker States (Camouflage, Impersonation, Holoecho)
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- If the Trooper has a Skill that allows deployment in a Marker State (e.g., Camouflage), it may reveal *as a Marker* upon canceling Hidden Deployment.
- It keeps the Marker State only if it performs:
  - Cautious Movement  
  - Short Skills that do not require Rolls (except Look Out!)  
  - Delaying ARO against a Marker  
  - Preventing enemies from re-entering a Marker State
- Any Roll, Attack, Long Skill (except Cautious Movement), or Silhouette contact cancels that Marker State.

*This makes Hidden Deployment + Camouflage function as a layered stealth tool: Hidden → Camo → Model.*

---

### 4. Interaction With Sensor
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Sensor is one of the few effects that can *explicitly Discover and cancel* Hidden Deployment.
- Sensor is a **Normal WIP+6 Roll**, ignores Mimetism and Range, and reveals *all* eligible targets inside ZoC.
- Hidden Deployment Troopers revealed by Sensor must immediately place their Model at their written location.

---

### 5. Interaction With Infiltration and Superior Deployment
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- A Trooper with Hidden Deployment + Infiltration must still roll if deploying outside its half.
- The roll occurs *after writing down the location*.
- Failure:
  - Trooper is placed in the player’s Deployment Zone at the table edge.
  - Hidden Deployment and Marker States are lost.
  - Any deployables placed with Minelayer during Hidden Deployment are removed (if applicable).

---

### 6. Interaction With Coordinated Orders
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- A Hidden Deployment Trooper may participate in a Coordinated Order.
- If it does, it loses its personal Order (the one it would otherwise use to reveal itself).
- Declaring its part in the Coordinated Order cancels Hidden Deployment and places the Model on the table.

---

### 7. Hidden Deployment and Combat Groups
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- If moved between Combat Groups, the opponent must be informed that a Hidden Deployment Trooper exists.
- All other information (profile, location) remains Private until revealed.

---

### 8. Preventing Enemy Return to Marker States
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- If an enemy declares the Long Skill to return to a Marker State, a Hidden Deployment Trooper with LoF may reveal *at that moment* to prevent it.
- The reveal interrupts the enemy skill if LoF exists from the written location.


### 9. Impetuous / Retreat! Forcing Cancellation
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- If the Trooper becomes Impetuous due to Frenzy or game effects, or enters Retreat!, Hidden Deployment is immediately canceled.
- This is rare but important—e.g., Mnemonica transferring WIP to a Hidden Deployment Trooper can accidentally force a reveal if that Trooper later becomes Impetuous.

---

## Common Misunderstandings and Pitfalls
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Declaring *any* Order or ARO, even a harmless one, cancels Hidden Deployment.
- Hidden Deployment cannot be regained under any circumstances.
- You must always write down the facing and exact position; vague notes cause disputes.

## Tactical Notes (Unofficial)
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Best used for ambush snipers, midfield specialists, and mission-critical assassins.
- Ideal counter to aggressive Turn 1 pushes—extremely punishing Rolls.
- Combine with:
  - **Crossfire traps**
  - **Objective denial**
- Beware enemy Sensor troops or fast units that can flood ZoC.
- Hidden Deployment is strongest in missions where:
  - First-turn survivability is essential.
  - High-value targets cross open firelanes.
  - Delaying Order economy is acceptable for powerful reveal timing.
