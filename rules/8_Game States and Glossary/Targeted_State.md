# Targeted State

Game States and Glossary

- The Trooper or game element ("Trooper" from now on) suffers a successful Attack or Effect using an Ammunition, Hacking Program or a game condition or Scenario Special Rule, capable of causing this state.

- Any Trooper declaring a BS Attack, Comms Attack, or Discover against a Trooper in Targeted State gains a +3 MOD to the Attribute used to perform that Skill.

- Troopers in this state must apply a -3 WIP MOD to their Reset rolls.

- Restriction: While in this state, Cautious Movement may not be declared.

- Restriction: A Trooper in this state may not use the Stealth Special Skill.

- This state does not interfere with Automatic Special Skills or Automatic Equipment, which will function normally.

- The affected Trooper successfully passes a Normal or Face to Face Reset Roll, applying a -3 WIP MOD.

- A Trooper with the Engineer Special Skill (or an equivalent Skill) may cancel the state by spending one Short Skill of an Order while in Silhouette contact with the affected Trooper, and passing a Normal WIP Roll (or the Roll specified by the Special Skill or Scenario).

## Targeted and Spotlight

- Any failed Saving Roll causes the target to enter Targeted State, placing a Targeted State Token (TARGETED) by them.

## Targeted and Forward Observer

- The use of Forward Observer is a form of Attack with a BS Weapon with the BS Weapon (WIP) Trait, and instead of forcing the target to make a Saving Roll, causes them to enter Targeted State.

## Core Effects Recap
A Trooper becomes Targeted when affected by an Attack or Effect capable of causing the state.  
While Targeted:
- Enemies get **+3 to BS Attack, Comms Attack, and Discover** against the target.  
- The Targeted Trooper suffers **–3 WIP to Reset**.  
- Cannot declare **Cautious Movement**.  
- Cannot use **Stealth**.  
- Automatic Skills still work.  
- State cancels on a successful **Reset (–3 WIP)** or by **Engineer** WIP roll.

## Key Interactions Often Missed

### 1. **Reset Timing and Face-to-Face**
Targeted modifies Reset, but does not change when Reset can be declared.
- Reset versus **Guided** is F2F (WIP vs WIP).  
- Reset versus **Spotlight** (Active Turn) is F2F, even though Spotlight requires no LoF.  
- Reset versus **Forward Observer** is F2F (WIP vs WIP BS Attack).  
- Reset can cancel Targeted in **either** Active or Reactive turn.

**Common mistake:** Players believe Reset vs Guided is Normal; it is explicitly F2F.

---

### 2. **Guided Attacks and Targeted**
Guided Attacks benefit from:
- **+3 from Targeted**  
- Ignoring **Cover** and **Mimetism**, and usually many visibility MODs  
- Template Dodge is **–3** without LoF

This combination makes Targeted the single strongest enabler for Guided weapons.

If the target chooses:
- **Dodge** → must apply –3 for no LoF to template source  
- **Reset** → applies –3 for being Targeted  
Both defensive options are statistically weak.

---

### 3. **Targeted and Discover**
The +3 MOD also applies to **Discover**, making Targeted a powerful tool to strip camouflage.  
Discover gains:
- +3 from Targeted  
- All regular Discover modifiers still apply  
This makes a Targeted Camo Marker significantly more vulnerable to reveal.

---

### 4. **Targeted and Stealth Suppression**
The Targeted State removes Stealth entirely.  
This means:
- Entering ZoC will always trigger AROs  
- Hacking AROs occur if the target is Hackable  
- Even non-Hackable targets may suffer ZoC AROs such as Dodge

This is one of the least noticed tactical consequences of Targeted.

---

### 5. **Targeted and Template Weapons**
A Trooper that becomes Targeted mid-Order (for example through **FO in ARO**) may face a Guided missile later with a guaranteed template even if they hide in Total Cover.  
Guided ignores LoF entirely.

## Comparison: Spotlight vs Forward Observer vs Other Targeting Tools

### **Spotlight**
Pros:
- No LoF required because it's a Hacking attack
- Can work through Repeaters  
- Often used from complete safety  
- Can Target units behind Total Cover  
- Hacking Area coverage creates large zones of influence  

Cons:
- Requires a **Hacker**  
- Opposed by Reset (F2F)  
- Some units have strong BTS or Firewall support  

---

### **Forward Observer**
Pros:
- Works on any FO specialist  
- Functions in ARO  
- No Saving Roll required—success immediately applies Targeted  
- Works equally on Hackable and non-Hackable troopers  

Cons:
- Requires LoF → exposes user  
- Suffers BS-like modifiers: Mimetism, Cover, Range  
- Easier for enemy to Dodge than BTS-defend  
- FO can be negated by Immunity (Enhanced) effects (e.g., SymbioMate)

---

### **Guided Missiles (as a Targeted consumer)**
Pros:
- +3 BS, ignores Cover and Mimetism  
- Attacks from anywhere on table  
- Forces the target into very poor defensive choices  
- Deviates threat away from direct firefights  

Cons:
- Order intensive  
- Can be cancelled using a **Reset spam** (still uses up orders though)
- Requires set-up (Spotlight or FO)  
- Not effective vs high-PH Dodge armies in open maps

## Tactical Uses and Advice

### **1. Pinning Dangerous Enemies**
Tagging MSV snipers, TAGs, or ARO anchors with Targeted forces them to either:
- Waste Orders Resetting  
- Hide  
- Eat a Guided missile

Targeted is a tempo weapon more than a raw-damage tool.

---

### **2. Breaking Defensive Camouflage Networks**
Camo skirmishers that are Targeted become easier to Discover and kill.  
Spotlight through Repeaters is particularly strong for clearing midfield zones.

---

### **3. Punishing Overextension**
FO in ARO is extremely dangerous for enemy attackers:
- If they expose for too long or peak incorrectly, they risk being Targeted  
- Even if they win the firefight, being Targeted afterward can doom them next Order cycle

---

### **4. Bait-and-Strike Tactics**
Force an enemy to remove Targeted by Reset, consuming Orders.  
During that time:
- Position long-range shooters  
- Reposition Repeaters  
- Push objectives  

Targeted can indirectly win games without ever launching a missile.

---

## Pros and Cons of Using Targeted (Strategically)

### **Pros**
- Enables Guided fire (most effective missile delivery)  
- Gives universal +3 to many types of attacks  
- Strips Stealth and restricts movement  
- Forces enemy Order expenditure  
- Works on Camo/TO/Marker states via Discover buffs  

### **Cons**
- Targeted can be wiped by a single successful Reset  
- Enemy may have multiple Engineers  
- Requires setup (Spotlight, FO, etc.)  
- Cybersec-heavy armies resist Spotlight efficiently  
- Guided attack requires a Guided weapon, which might not always be in available.

---

## Summary
Targeted State is a “force multiplier” states.  
Its value lies not only in enabling Guided Attacks, but in:  
- Disrupting movement and stealth  
- Enhancing Discover  
- Taxing the enemy’s Order pool  
- Creating long-term positional pressure  

Used correctly, Targeted allows a player to dominate the tempo of the game without necessarily relying on high-damage options.
