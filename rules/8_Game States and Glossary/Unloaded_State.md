# Unloaded State

> **N5.3 change (September 2026):** Unloaded can be cancelled via Reload or the States Phase while inside the ZoC of an allied **Trooper or game element** with Baggage, provided the Baggage source is in a non-Null State.

Game States and Glossary

## Activation
- The Trooper or game element ("Trooper" from now on) has entirely depleted the ammunition of a weapon or piece of Equipment with the Disposable Trait.

## Effects
- The Trooper can no longer use the relevant weapon or piece of Equipment.

## Cancellation
- The Trooper performs the Reload Common Skill, which does not require a Roll, while inside the Zone of Control of an Allied Trooper or game element with the Baggage piece of Equipment, and that Baggage source is in a non-Null State.
- In the States Phase, the Trooper is inside the Zone of Control of an Allied Trooper or game element with the Baggage piece of Equipment, and that Baggage source is in a non-Null State.
- Some scenarios and missions have Scenario Special Rules that allow cancellation of this state. In this case, the player must meet the conditions specified by those rules.
- Canceling this state returns all weapons and pieces of Equipment with the Disposable Trait that the Trooper possesses to their full capacity.
- Restriction: Weapons and pieces of Equipment with the Non-Reloadable Trait cannot cancel this state, unless a scenario or special rule says otherwise.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
Unloaded is tied to the depleted Disposable weapon/equipment. N5.3 broadens the valid Baggage source from only an allied Trooper to an allied Trooper **or game element**.
