# Unloaded State

Game States and Glossary

- The Trooper or game element ("Trooper" from now on) has entirely depleted the ammunition of a weapon or a piece of Equipment either with the Disposable Trait.

- The Trooper can no longer use the relevant weapon or piece of Equipment.

- The Trooper performs the Reload Common Skill, which does not require a Roll, while inside the Zone of Control of an Allied Trooper with the Baggage piece of Equipment. The Trooper with Baggage must be in a non- Null State.

- In the States Phase, the Trooper is inside the Zone of Control of an Allied Trooper with the Baggage piece of Equipment. The Trooper with Baggage must be in a non-Null State.

- Some scenarios and missions have Scenario Special Rules that allow cancellation of this state. In this case, the player must meet the conditions specified by those rules.

- Canceling this state returns all weapons and pieces of Equipment with the Disposable Trait that the trooper possesses to their full capacity.

- Restriction: Weapons and pieces of Equipment with the Non-Reloadable Trait cannot cancel this state, unless a scenario or special rule says otherwise.


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
It appears whenever a Trooper expends all uses of a **Disposable** weapon or piece of Equipment. Once the final use is spent, the Trooper enters **Unloaded State** *for that item only*. The Trooper is **not** globally Unloaded — only that specific weapon/equipment option is affected.

---

### **[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] — Key Interaction Notes**

- **Unloaded applies per-weapon, not per-Trooper.**  
  A Trooper who has fired all Panzerfaust shots is *Unloaded (Panzerfaust)* but may still fire a Light Shotgun, Nanopulser, etc.

- **Reload only restores items with the Disposable Trait.**  
  Reload does *not* replenish Mines (Deployable, not Disposable) or items that are single-use by design (e.g., certain special scenario gear). This is one of the most commonly confused points.

### **[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] — Tactical Advice**

- **Disposable weapons should be treated as “burst spikes.”**  
  Use them only when the target, range, and odds make the shot valuable.

- **Avoid declaring risk-heavy attacks with your last use.**  
  A miss can remove a major threat piece from your long-range toolkit.

- **Combat groups with strong support benefit more from Reload opportunities.**  
  In lists expecting prolonged fire lanes, consider protecting routes back to the Deployment Zone.

- **In Fireteams, try to avoid “accidental unloading.”**  
  Burst increases can tempt overuse — monitor ammunition closely and keep a spare attack vector.

### **[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT] — Example Scenarios**

- A Fusilier fires the second (final) Panzerfaust shot:  
  → Immediately becomes *Unloaded (Panzerfaust)*.  
  → May still ARO with Combi Rifle.

- A Ye Mao with +1SD on a Disposable weapon:  
  → Rolls extra die, discards one.  
  → **Only one** Disposable use is consumed.

- A Trooper declares Speculative Fire with the final grenade:  
  → Becomes Unloaded even if the roll fails.
