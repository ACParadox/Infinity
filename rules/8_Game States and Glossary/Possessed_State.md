# Possessed State

Game States and Glossary

- The Trooper or game element ("Trooper" from now on) suffers a successful Attack or Effect using an Ammunition, Hacking Program or a game condition or Scenario Special Rule, capable of causing this state.

- Restriction: Troopers in this state cannot be activated or receive Orders from their player's Order Pool. While they are Possessed, Troopers are considered to be enemies by the rest of their owner's Troopers and are considered to be allies by the Troopers of the player that caused this game state.

- Troopers in this state do not contribute Orders during the Tactical Phases of either player.

- Troopers in this state may be activated and receive Orders from the Order Pool of the Combat Group of the Trooper who caused them to enter the Possessed state. A Possessed Trooper does not count towards the maximum number of members the Combat Group may contain.

- Possessed Troopers must use the Possessed Trooper profile instead of their own.

Note* Use the Trooper's original Attribute values.

- This state does not interfere with Automatic Special Skills or Automatic Equipment, which will remain functioning normally.

- A Possessed Trooper does not count towards either Players' Victory Points.

- Restriction: A Trooper in Possessed State cannot be part of a Fireteam. If a member of a Fireteam enters Possessed State they automatically stop being part of the Fireteam.

- In the owning player's Tactical Phase, during the Executive Use of Command Tokens step, the player can automatically cancel this state by spending 1 Command Token.

- The Possessed State is automatically canceled if the owning player successfully affects the Possessed Trooper with the Total Control Hacking Program.

## Possessed and Total Control

- Any failed Saving Roll causes the target to enter Possessed State, placing a Possessed State Token (POS) by them. If this Program is used against a TAG in Possessed State, any failed Saving Roll cancels the target's Possessed State, causing them to enter Normal State, and the Possessed State Token (POS) is removed.

## Possessed and Transmutation (Escape System-X)

- If the Trooper is in Possessed State when they change to their second Unit Profile, their Possessed State is canceled.


**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- When a Trooper enters Possessed, its alignment temporarily changes: the Trooper becomes *Enemy* to its original owner and *Ally* to the controller who caused the State. This overrides normal allegiance for all game purposes (AROs, Attacks, templates, hacking, etc.).
- The new controller may activate the Possessed Trooper normally using Orders from *their* Order Pool, unless the Possessed rules explicitly restrict certain actions.
- The TAG’s own weapons, skills, Burst values, and equipment remain the same unless specifically removed by the State.

---

## ORDER & ARO INTERACTIONS
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- **Orders:** A Possessed TAG does **not** contribute an Order to the opponent’s Order Pool. The opponent must spend *their own* Orders to activate it. A common misconception is that the TAG “switches combat groups”; it does not.
- **ARO Eligibility:** The Possessed TAG can react to the original owner’s Troopers as if they were enemies. Conversely, those Troopers may attack it as normal.
- **Fireteam Interactions:** If the Trooper was originally in a Fireteam, it immediately breaks coherency and leaves the team when Possessed. It never counts as a Fireteam member while under enemy control.

---

## WEAPON & SKILL USE
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Possessed models can use *all* BS Weapons, CC Weapons, and Equipment unless specifically restricted.  
- **Caveat:** Any optional skills or modes the original player did not reveal (e.g., hidden traits on some Equipment) might become Open Information once the model is under enemy control; this should follow the rulebook’s general “replacement model” and Open/Private Information protocols.

---

## GUTS, STATES & DAMAGE RESOLUTION
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- A Possessed TAG behaves like an enemy Trooper for the purposes of Guts Rolls, entering Unconscious, applying Fire, Immobilized, and other States.
- If the TAG drops to Unconscious, it **remains Enemy** until the State is cancelled. At that moment it reverts to the owning player but is still Unconscious unless repaired.

**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
**Pros of Possessed (as an effect):**
- Allows enemy to weaponize a high-value asset (often a TAG).  
- Creates psychological and positional pressure.

**Cons / Limitations:**
- Does not give an extra Order in the opponent’s pool.  
- Can be cancelled with the right specialists.  
- Some of the Possessed trooper attributes can be less than the original unit's.
---


## TACTICAL NOTES (UNOFFICIAL)
**[UNOFFICIAL COMMENT]**
- Using Possessed offensively is strongest when:
  - The TAG is already positioned near vulnerable enemy assets.
  - The opponent lacks Engineers or unprotected repeaters.
- As the original owner:
  - Keep Engineers safe and nearby.  
  - Avoid overextending TAGs into networks of enemy hackers.
